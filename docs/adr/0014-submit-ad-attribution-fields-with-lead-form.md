# Submit ad attribution fields directly with the lead form

The ads landing-page form posts `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign/utm_term/utm_content`, `source_page_path`, `consent_at`, and `consent_version` directly in the `/contacts/submit` payload (alongside the existing `visitor_id`), instead of relying only on the asynchronous `/visitors/track` association keyed by `visitor_id`. Per-keyword attribution completeness is the campaign's acceptance metric ("which keyword produced this lead"), and an async side-channel can lose the association when the lead submits faster than the tracking request. The CRM is expected to tolerate the additional fields (confirmed 2026-09-14); the existing `/contact` form keeps its current payload until attribution is aligned site-wide in a separate change.
