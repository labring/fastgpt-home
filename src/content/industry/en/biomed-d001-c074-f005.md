# Multiturn Conversation and Prompts for Biopharmaceutical Equipment Clinical Trial Pre-screening

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Biopharmaceutical equipment data originates from technical manuals, operating guides, maintenance records, calibration reports, and equipment operation logs provided by equipment manufacturers. These documents are typically in PDF, Word, Excel, or XML formats. Technical manuals and operating guides are updated infrequently, usually with new equipment models, potentially every few years. Maintenance records and calibration reports are generated based on equipment usage frequency and maintenance cycles, typically quarterly or annually. Equipment operation logs are generated in real-time, recording sensor data, operating parameters, and error codes. Document structures are complex, containing numerous diagrams, specialized terminology, and abbreviations. Fields and units are highly standardized, such as pressure units `psi` or `bar`, temperature units `℃` or `K`, flow units `L/min` or `mL/s`, and various parameters specific to bioreactors, chromatography systems, and other equipment.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Multiturn Conversation and Prompts

The complex structure and specialized terminology of equipment documentation require the multiturn conversation system to have robust semantic understanding. The system must accurately identify specific equipment models, parameter names, and their relationships. Data sources with varying update frequencies necessitate knowledge base support for multi-version management and the ability to distinguish document recency. For example, for equipment operation questions, the latest operating guide should be prioritized. For historical fault diagnosis, past maintenance records are relevant. Integrating real-time operation logs requires the system to process high-frequency structured data and link it with unstructured document content. Additionally, standardized fields and units demand explicit unit conversion rules in prompt design to prevent misinterpretation due to unit confusion. In multiturn conversations, users may frequently shift focus from equipment parameter inquiries to troubleshooting and then to consumable ordering. The system must maintain contextual coherence and dynamically adjust retrieval strategies.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for This Value |
| :----- | :------- | :----------- |
| `maxContext` | `8` | Ensures the model effectively covers continuous user queries about equipment parameters, fault symptoms, and solutions in multiturn conversations. |
| `Chunk size` | `500 characters` | Balances semantic integrity of long documents with model processing efficiency, preventing crucial information truncation. |
| `Recall count` | `10` | Increases the probability of retrieving relevant information from a large volume of equipment documents, covering more potential answers. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75` | Ensures the precision of retrieved content, especially for equipment documents with many specialized terms and abbreviations. |
| `Rerank result count` | `3` | Prioritizes displaying the most relevant few items from retrieval results, reducing user effort in filtering. |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Accommodates the size of large equipment technical manuals and maintenance record files, ensuring successful file uploads. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   The model repeatedly asks for information already provided in the conversation or fails to understand specific equipment models. This occurs when the `maxContext` parameter is set too low, leading to context loss, or when equipment model entities are not effectively recognized in the knowledge base.
*   When a user asks for specific parameter unit conversions, the model provides incorrect or inconsistent values. This happens when unit conversion rules are not explicitly defined in the prompt, or when the knowledge base lacks a corresponding unit conversion mapping table.
*   The system fails to associate equipment operation log data with fault diagnosis manuals, leading to ineffective recommendations. This is due to a lack of mapping between key fields in operation logs and fault codes in knowledge base documents during the data preprocessing stage.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration

*   Construct multiturn conversations including equipment models, specific parameters, and unit conversions. Verify the system's ability to accurately understand and provide consistent answers.
*   Upload equipment documents in various formats and sizes. Observe file processing status and knowledge base construction to ensure all critical documents are correctly parsed.
*   Simulate the entire process for typical equipment fault scenarios, from describing fault symptoms to obtaining solutions. Check if the system accurately retrieves relevant maintenance records and operating procedures.
