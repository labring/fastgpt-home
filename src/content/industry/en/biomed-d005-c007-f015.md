# Deploying and Upgrading GMP Compliance Systems

## Data Characteristics for This Category

GMP compliance data originates from internal quality management system documents, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), inspection procedures, validation reports, deviation records, change control documents, and regulatory texts. These documents are typically stored as PDFs, Word files, or scanned images, with varying degrees of structural organization. Regulatory texts are periodically published or revised by regulatory bodies. Internal SOPs and procedures are revised based on production needs, technological updates, or regulatory changes, with revision cycles ranging from months to years. Documents often contain specialized terminology, charts, flowcharts, and tables. Fields and units strictly adhere to pharmaceutical industry standards, such as batch numbers, expiration dates, test results (e.g., percentage content, impurity limits), and equipment calibration dates. Units are precise, down to micrograms, milliliters, and hours.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Deployment and Upgrades

The complexity and rigor of GMP compliance data impose specific requirements on knowledge base deployment and upgrades. The specialized nature of regulatory texts and SOP documents demands that tokenization and embedding models possess strong domain-specific understanding to prevent semantic deviations from improper tokenization. Documents containing charts and tables require the content extraction module to have multimodal parsing capabilities to ensure information completeness. The unpredictable update frequency necessitates that the knowledge base supports incremental updates and version management during upgrades to avoid redundant imports and data loss. For example, when new regulations are issued or SOPs are revised, the knowledge base should quickly identify and update affected sections. The strictness of fields and units requires accurate recognition and processing of numerical values and units during Q&A to prevent compliance risks due to unit conversion or recognition errors. The need for historical traceability also requires the knowledge base system to support version rollback for audits or investigations.

## Configuration Guidelines

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----------------- | :---------------- | :-------- |
| `Chunk size` | `500–800 characters` | GMP documents are logically dense. Shorter segments risk cutting off context, while longer segments may introduce irrelevant information, affecting recall accuracy. |
| `Recall count` | `8–12 entries` | Ensures coverage of multiple relevant clauses in complex compliance questions while avoiding excessive noise. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75–0.85` | Compliance Q&A demands high accuracy. A high threshold filters out irrelevant recall results. |
| `Rerank result count` | `3–5 entries` | After re-ranking, a small number of the most relevant results are returned, improving user reading efficiency and answer accuracy. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | GMP documents often contain many pages or complex formats, requiring longer parsing times to prevent timeouts. |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `200 MB` | Scanned or high-resolution compliance documents are often large, requiring support for uploading large files. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   **Symptom**: The system returns compliance clauses that do not match actual requirements, or the answer contains confusing specialized terminology that should not be present.
    **Cause**: The tokenizer or embedding model is not optimized for the biomedical domain, leading to misunderstandings of specialized terminology.
*   **Symptom**: After uploading a PDF file, some table or flowchart information is lost and cannot be retrieved.
    **Cause**: The content extraction module lacks the ability to parse complex multimodal content, failing to correctly extract chart and table information from the PDF.
*   **Symptom**: After a knowledge base upgrade, Q&A results for some older SOP versions become inaccurate, or new SOP versions do not fully cover the content of older versions.
    **Cause**: Inadequate version control and incremental updates during the upgrade process lead to data overwriting or duplication, affecting knowledge consistency.

## How to Verify Configuration

*   Select typical GMP regulations or SOP documents. Test their upload and parsing completeness, especially for pages containing charts and tables.
*   For specific compliance questions, use different phrasings. Observe if the recall results are stable and cover key clauses. Check the effectiveness of `Recall count` and `Similarity threshold`.
*   Simulate an SOP update scenario. Upload the revised document. Verify if the knowledge base correctly identifies and updates relevant content while retaining the ability to query older versions (if applicable).
*   Examine numerical values and units in Q&A results. Ensure numerical accuracy and correct unit identification. Compare with the original document.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against specific samples.
