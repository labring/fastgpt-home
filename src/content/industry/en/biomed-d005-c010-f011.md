# Document Parsing and Chunking for IVD Diagnostic Reagent Regulations

## Data Characteristics

IVD diagnostic reagent regulations and SOP documents originate from regulatory standards, internal quality management system files, product manuals, and operating procedures. Update cycles typically align with regulatory revisions, product lifecycles, or internal audit schedules, usually quarterly or annually, with some urgent changes occurring more frequently. Document structures are highly standardized. Common formats include PDF, Word, or scanned images, often containing numerous tables, diagrams, and flowcharts. Text content is highly specialized, involving medical terminology, chemical components, units of measurement (e.g., `mg/dL`, `IU/L`), and batch information, demanding extreme accuracy. Documents frequently include metadata such as version numbers, effective dates, and revision histories.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Document Parsing and Chunking

The standardized structure of IVD diagnostic documents requires parsers to accurately identify chapter titles, paragraphs, and table content, preventing content confusion. The presence of specialized terminology and units of measurement means that context integrity must be maintained, preventing over-chunking from causing semantic loss. Tables and flowcharts in documents require enhanced PDF parsing capabilities to extract structured information; otherwise, critical steps, parameters, or decision criteria may not be effectively indexed. The update frequency and version number characteristics demand that the document parsing process supports incremental updates and can associate content changes between different versions, ensuring question-answering results are based on the latest and correct regulations. Given the high accuracy requirements, any parsing error could lead to non-compliant question-answering.

## Configuration Strategy

| Configuration Item                 | Recommended Value       | Rationale                                                                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Chunk Length`                     | `800–1200 characters`   | Ensures the completeness of professional terms and context in IVD regulations and SOPs, reducing the risk of semantic loss.                |
| `Overlap Length`                   | `100–200 characters`    | Improves the relevance between paragraphs, capturing cross-paragraph information, especially for processes or multi-step instructions.       |
| `Enable Enhanced PDF Parsing`      | Checked                 | Effectively identifies and extracts complex layout content such as tables and flowcharts commonly found in IVD documents.                    |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`       | `600 seconds`           | Handles large SOPs or guideline documents, preventing parsing timeouts due to large file sizes or complex structures.                        |
| `maxContext`                       | `4000 characters`       | Accommodates situations in IVD diagnostic reagent question-answering where longer background information may be needed, providing more comprehensive answers. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   **Symptom:** Table content appears garbled or missing in the parsed knowledge base. **Reason:** Enhanced PDF parsing was not enabled or configured, preventing correct identification and extraction of table structured data from PDFs.
*   **Symptom:** When a user asks about an operating procedure, the answer is incomplete or disjointed. **Reason:** The `Chunk Length` was set too small, splitting a complete operating procedure into multiple discontinuous knowledge blocks, leading to context loss.
*   **Symptom:** After uploading a document, there is a long period of unresponsiveness or a `504 Gateway Timeout` error. **Reason:** The `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameter was set too short, and large or complex IVD files could not be parsed within the default time.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration

*   Upload an IVD diagnostic reagent manual or SOP document containing complex tables and flowcharts. Check if the parsed knowledge blocks correctly display table data and process descriptions.
*   Ask a question about a specific key operating procedure within a regulatory document. Verify if the answer completely and accurately presents all details of the procedure, including relevant parameters and units.
*   Attempt to upload an updated regulatory document. Observe if the system can identify version differences and provide question-answering services based on the latest version.
*   Check parsing logs to confirm no parsing errors or timeout warnings occurred due to complex file structures or size limitations.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against your own samples.
