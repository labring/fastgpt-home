# Deployment and Upgrade for Attenuated Inactivated Vaccine Regulations

## Data Characteristics

Data for attenuated inactivated vaccine regulations and SOP documents primarily originates from regulatory files published by the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) and the Pharmacopoeia Commission, guidelines from industry associations, and internal quality management system documents. These documents are typically in PDF, Word, or scanned image formats. Update frequency is relatively low, generally quarterly or annually, with additional updates for major policy changes or technical breakthroughs. Document structure is chapter-based, including fixed modules such as introduction, definitions, responsibilities, operating procedures, quality control, and record requirements. Fields involve vaccine batch numbers, production dates, expiration dates, storage conditions, and test results. Units strictly follow national standards, such as `IU/mL`, `℃`, and `h`.

## Constraints on Deployment and Upgrade

The data characteristics of attenuated inactivated vaccine regulation documents impose specific constraints on FastGPT's deployment and upgrade. First, the semi-structured nature of documents and the presence of scanned images require stable and reliable OCR and document parsing capabilities for accurate text extraction. The low update frequency means significant document import during initial deployment, but subsequent incremental update pressure is low, focusing mainly on identifying revisions and version management. Second, strict field and unit requirements necessitate attention to entity recognition and numerical extraction precision during knowledge base construction to support accurate question answering. During system upgrades, ensure compatibility between the existing knowledge base's semantic indexing and vector embedding models to prevent recall accuracy degradation due to model updates. Furthermore, compliance requirements mandate strict validation for every knowledge base update and model adjustment to guarantee the authority and accuracy of question answering results.

## Common Pitfalls

*   Some document content is missing or garbled after knowledge base import. This is due to insufficient text recognition capability of the document parser for complex tables or images, or incorrect OCR language pack configuration.
*   The system returns "no relevant content found" after a user query. This might be because of an unreasonable knowledge base segmentation strategy, leading to key information being split, or the vector model not fully capturing the semantics of specialized terms.
*   Question answering accuracy decreases after upgrading FastGPT. This is due to adjustments in the new version's embedding model or retrieval algorithm, causing compatibility issues with the vector representations of the old knowledge base.
