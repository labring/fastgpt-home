# Workflow Orchestration for Pharmaceutical E-commerce Regulations

## Data Characteristics in this Domain

Regulatory and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) documents in pharmaceutical e-commerce primarily originate from national and local drug administration agencies, as well as internal corporate compliance manuals, operational procedures, and training materials. These documents are typically stored in formats such as PDF, Word, and Excel. Content covers various aspects, including drug procurement, storage, sales, distribution, quality management, and adverse event reporting.

Document update frequency is high. Regulatory files may be revised several times a year, and internal SOPs require regular updates to adapt to policy changes and business developments. Document structures often include chapters, clauses, and annexes. Fields may involve drug generic names, batch numbers, production dates, expiration dates, storage conditions, and transport temperatures. Data strictly adheres to national standard units, such as Celsius (°C) for temperature and milligrams (mg) or International Units (IU) for dosage.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Workflow Orchestration

The high update frequency of pharmaceutical e-commerce regulatory documents requires the knowledge base synchronization mechanism within the workflow to support efficient incremental updates. This ensures the timeliness and accuracy of question-answering results.

Diverse document formats, especially scanned PDFs, demand advanced document parsing capabilities. Accurate OCR recognition is crucial to avoid missing key information. Documents contain extensive specialized terminology and legal provisions, requiring models to possess precise semantic understanding to prevent misinterpreting user queries.

The strictness of fields like drug batch numbers and expiration dates means that information extraction and comparison within the workflow must be exact. Any deviation could lead to compliance risks. To address these characteristics, workflow orchestration needs to pre-configure various parsing strategies and validation steps to handle different document types and data sensitivities.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item         | Recommended Value    | Rationale                                                                                               |
| :------------------------- | :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Chunk size` (Segment Length) | `500–800 characters`       | Ensures the completeness of regulatory clauses, preventing truncation of critical information.            |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count)   | `Top 5 entries`            | Balances recall precision with computational resource consumption, covering the main relevant clauses.  |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.75–0.85`          | Pharmaceutical regulatory Q&A demands high accuracy, avoiding interference from low-relevance content.  |
| `Rerank result count` (Reranked Return Count) | `Top 3 entries`            | Further refines results, prioritizing the most relevant core clauses.                                    |
| `Max Conversation Turns` | `5 Turns`               | Limits multi-turn conversation depth, preventing deviation from the topic or entering invalid loops.    |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds`             | Handles parsing of large PDF files, preventing processing failures due to timeouts.                     |

## Common Pitfalls

*   The knowledge base search in the workflow returns empty results. This may occur if document parsing fails, leading to incomplete knowledge base content or incorrect indexing.
*   After a user query, the system fails to display a pre-configured submission button. This typically indicates an incorrect configuration in the conditional judgment node within the workflow, preventing the triggering of subsequent UI interaction components.
*   Answer content includes outdated or revised regulatory information. This happens when the knowledge base synchronization mechanism fails to process the latest versions of regulatory documents in a timely manner.

## Verification Steps

*   Upload the latest version of a national drug administration regulation document. Check if the knowledge base content is complete, free of garbled characters, and allows specific clauses to be retrieved via keyword search.
*   Simulate a user query about drug procurement processes. Observe if the workflow accurately identifies the intent and triggers relevant SOP Q&A or information extraction nodes.
*   In a test environment, use queries containing expired batch numbers or incorrect storage conditions. Verify if the workflow's validation mechanism correctly identifies and flags potential risks.
