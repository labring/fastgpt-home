# Hospital Operations Policy Deployment and Upgrade

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Hospital operations policy data primarily originates from internal hospital management systems, regulatory documents published by administrative departments, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) developed by various departments based on practical needs. Data update frequencies vary. Fundamental policies, such as human resource management guidelines, might be revised annually. Infection control guidelines and equipment operating procedures, however, may be updated quarterly or monthly due to national standards or the introduction of new equipment. Document structures typically exist as PDFs, Word files, or internal knowledge base pages. Content includes numerous hierarchical headings, tables, flowcharts, and specialized terminology. Fields and units involve department names, job responsibilities, process steps, approval authorities, timelines (e.g., "Approval Deadline: 3 working days"), drug dosage units, and equipment parameter units, all demonstrating a high degree of professionalism and standardization.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Deployment and Upgrade

The data characteristics of hospital operations policies impose specific constraints on deployment and upgrade. First, the complex document structure and multiple formats require the knowledge base platform to have robust document parsing capabilities. Accurate recognition of tables and nested lists within PDFs and Word documents is crucial to ensure semantic integrity. Second, the varying update frequencies necessitate a deployment solution that supports incremental updates and version management. This avoids full re-indexing with every update, which reduces system load. The presence of specialized terminology and flowcharts means that text segmentation must carefully consider contextual relevance to prevent key information from being fragmented. Furthermore, numerical information within fields, such as time and dosage, must be accurate during RAG retrieval to avoid incorrect operational guidance. This places higher demands on the selection of text embedding models and retrieval strategies.

## Configuration Recommendations

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for Recommendation |
| :----- | :------- | :----------- |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Hospital policy documents are often large, containing images and tables, requiring support for uploading large files. |
| `Chunk size` (Chunk Length) | `800–1200 characters` (characters) | Policy document paragraphs are long. Chunks that are too short risk losing context, while chunks that are too long affect retrieval accuracy. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `Top 8 entries` (top 8) | Ensures coverage of various aspects of the policy, improving the comprehensiveness of answers. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | Calibrate based on actual measurements | Prevents over-generalization or omission of highly relevant policy clauses. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` (seconds) | Parsing large PDF documents can be time-consuming, preventing parsing failures due to timeouts. |
| `maxContext` | `3000 Tokens` | Policy Q&A requires a longer context length to ensure answer accuracy. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   Slow response from chat interface calls, with API calls returning after more than `30 seconds` (seconds): This may be due to high load on the text embedding model or unoptimized database query indexes.
*   `npm install` reports a `chakra-ui/react` error: This is typically caused by a mismatch between the local Node.js environment version and project dependency requirements, or npm cache issues.
*   `curl` test returns a `500` error code: This is often due to backend service configuration errors, missing environment variables, or abnormal database connections. Check service log files.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   Upload a PDF policy document containing complex tables and flowcharts. Verify that the content is correctly recognized and segmented after document parsing.
*   Perform an incremental update on an SOP document with updated content. Confirm that the system processes only the changed parts and can retrieve the latest information.
*   Select at least 5 policy questions covering different business scenarios for testing. Compare the system's answers with the original policy text to evaluate accuracy and completeness. Set an accuracy threshold based on actual business needs.
*   Simulate high-concurrency call scenarios. Monitor interface response times to ensure that response speed meets requirements under expected load. Set an upper limit for response time based on business peak periods.
