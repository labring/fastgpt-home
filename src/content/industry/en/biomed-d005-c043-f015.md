# Gene Therapy AAV Regulations: Deployment and Upgrade

## Data Characteristics for this Category

Gene therapy AAV (adeno-associated virus) related regulations and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) documents typically originate from technical guidelines published by regulatory bodies, quality management standards from industry associations, and internal enterprise-developed SOPs for production, quality control, and clinical research. These documents are primarily in PDF or Word format. They feature rigorous structures, extensive specialized terminology, flowcharts, tables, and references.

Update frequency is relatively low, usually annually or biennially, occurring mainly when policies, regulations, or technologies change. Document content covers viral vector construction, cell bank establishment, production processes, quality testing standards (e.g., AAV titer, purity, empty/full capsid ratio, genomic integrity), preclinical toxicology evaluation, clinical trial design, ethical review, and data management and statistical analysis. Fields and units are highly specific. For example, AAV titer is commonly expressed in `vg/mL` (viral genomes per milliliter), purity in `%` (percentage), and production batch numbers follow strict naming conventions.

## Constraints on Deployment and Upgrade

The rigor and specialization of AAV regulatory documents require FastGPT to accurately parse complex tables and diagrams during knowledge base construction. It must also understand the contextual relationships of specialized terminology.

Low update frequency, coupled with broad impact, means the knowledge base needs comprehensive version control and incremental synchronization during updates. This ensures seamless transitions between new and old versions and the ability to compare differences.

Unique units of measurement and fields in the documents, such as `vg/mL`, demand that the underlying model can recognize scientific units, preventing misinterpretation as ordinary text. Furthermore, since these documents are foundational for enterprise compliance, the deployment environment must meet high-security requirements to prevent data leakage.

Parsing flowcharts and nested tables in large PDF files places high demands on the file processing module's `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` and `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` parameters. This prevents parsing failures or upload timeouts due to file complexity.

## Three Common Mistakes

*   **Symptom:** After uploading a large AAV regulatory PDF file, the interface displays "File upload failed" or "Parsing timeout."
    **Reason:** `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` or `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameters are set too low. They cannot handle the transmission and processing requirements of complex documents.
*   **Symptom:** In knowledge base Q&A, queries about professional values like AAV titer or purity yield inaccurate or missing results.
    **Reason:** The text segmentation strategy did not fully consider the integrity of specialized terminology and units, leading to related information being fragmented during segmentation.
*   **Symptom:** After Docker deployment, the PostgreSQL container repeatedly restarts and fails to launch properly.
    **Reason:** PostgreSQL data volume mounting permission issues or insufficient resource limits in the Docker Compose configuration. This prevents the database from persisting or being allocated enough memory.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration

*   Upload an AAV regulatory PDF file containing complex charts and lengthy text. Observe if the upload and parsing processes complete smoothly, without timeouts or error messages.
*   Query the knowledge base for professional fields such as AAV titer `vg/mL` and purity `percentage`. Verify that the returned results accurately include these key information and units, and check if their context is complete.
*   Create test questions covering the differences between new and old regulatory versions. Verify that the knowledge base correctly identifies and distinguishes changes between different versions of regulations. Confirm the authority of the answers.
