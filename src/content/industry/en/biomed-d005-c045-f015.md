# Deployment and Upgrade for Academic Promotion Policies

## Data Characteristics
Academic promotion policies in the biomedical field typically use data sources such as internal compliance manuals, guidance documents from medical affairs departments, post-marketing surveillance (PMS) reports, clinical trial ethics review documents, and industry association codes of conduct. These documents exist as PDFs, Word files, or internal knowledge base pages. Content structure varies, but they usually contain specialized terminology, regulatory clauses, approval processes, and medical data. Compliance policies and industry guidelines may update once or twice a year. Specific medical promotion plans and clinical data may revise quarterly or monthly based on new drug launches, research progress, or market feedback. Common fields in documents include drug name, indications, promotion activity type, approval level, approver, and compliance review points. Units may involve dosage, frequency, and approval timelines.

## Constraints on Deployment and Upgrade
The highly specialized and compliance-sensitive nature of academic promotion policy data imposes specific requirements on FastGPT's deployment and upgrade. First, parsing large PDF and Word documents requires robust text extraction capabilities, especially for complex tables and text within images. Second, the periodic nature of document updates necessitates an efficient incremental update mechanism. This ensures knowledge base timeliness and avoids compliance risks from outdated policies. Third, policy Q&A demands extremely high accuracy. Incorrect information can lead to severe consequences, requiring stricter context management and retrieval strategies. Furthermore, medical terms and abbreviations in the data require the model to accurately understand their semantics during embedding and retrieval. For deployment environments, data security and access control are paramount due to the involvement of core internal policies. This usually requires on-premise or private cloud deployment solutions, integrated with existing enterprise identity authentication systems.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item         | Recommended Value | Rationale                                                                                             |
| :------------------------- | :---------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE`     | `500 MB`          | Supports uploading large compliance manuals or integrated documents with multiple reports.              |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600`             | Provides ample parsing time for policy documents with complex structures and extensive text.            |
| `Chunk size`                 | `800–1200 characters`   | Balances the integrity of policy clauses with model context window limits, avoiding critical information fragmentation. |
| `Similarity threshold`               | `0.75–0.85`       | Improves retrieval precision, ensuring only highly relevant policy clauses are matched, reducing the risk of incorrect answers. |
| `Rerank result count`             | `Top 5 entries`         | Ensures the large language model processes enough relevant policy snippets for accurate answers.        |
| `maxContext`               | `32000 token`     | Provides a longer context window to understand details and related clauses when dealing with complex policy questions. |

## Common Pitfalls
*   Uploading a large policy document results in a request failure or parsing timeout. This usually occurs because `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` or `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` are set too low for the volume and complexity of biomedical documents.
*   The AI's answer contains compliance errors or omits critical clauses after a user query. This might stem from an outdated knowledge base or a `Similarity threshold` set too low, leading to the retrieval of irrelevant or peripheral information.
*   After on-premise deployment, integration with enterprise LDAP or SSO systems fails, preventing users from logging in or accessing the system. This usually happens because `AUTH_TYPE` or related authentication configurations are not correctly aligned with the enterprise's identity authentication service.

*   Upload a recent academic promotion policy PDF file that includes complex tables and multiple pages. Confirm successful file parsing and preview its content via the FastGPT interface.
*   Ask multiple questions about specific clauses, approval processes, or compliance points within this policy document. Check the AI's answers for accuracy, completeness, and timeliness, ensuring consistency with the latest policy.
*   Simulate user logins with different permission levels. Verify that users can only access authorized knowledge base content, confirming data isolation and access control policies are effective.
*   Check log outputs for frequent `408 Request Timeout` or `500 Internal Server Error` messages, especially those related to file parsing and knowledge base updates.
