# Workflow Orchestration for Home Healthcare Regulations

## Data Characteristics in this Category

Regulatory and SOP documents in home healthcare typically originate from quality management system files, product manuals, after-sales service handbooks from medical device manufacturers, and regulatory guidelines. These documents update at a relatively stable frequency, usually aligning with product iterations, regulatory revisions, or internal quality audit cycles, for example, annually or biennially. In special cases, temporary updates occur based on urgent notices. Document structures are primarily PDF, Word, or scanned images, containing numerous charts, flowcharts, and technical parameters. Fields and units often involve medical device models, serial numbers, batches, production dates, expiration dates, operating procedures, error codes, measurement units (e.g., mmHg, mmol/L, ℃), and calibration parameters. This information is frequently nested within complex tables or unstructured text descriptions.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Workflow Orchestration"

The complexity of home healthcare regulatory documents places specific demands on workflow orchestration. First, diverse file formats, especially scanned images and PDFs with complex charts, require robust document parsing capabilities within the workflow. OCR technology integration is necessary to accurately extract non-textual information. Second, although document update frequency is not high, each update might involve critical operational steps or parameter adjustments. This necessitates workflow support for version management and incremental updates to ensure knowledge base timeliness. The specificity of fields and units, such as numerical ranges for particular medical parameters and unit conversions, requires standardization after information extraction to avoid ambiguity. Furthermore, due to the strictness of operating procedures, the workflow must precisely match user queries with specific steps in SOPs, ensuring the accuracy and actionable nature of question-answering results.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----- | :------- | :----------- |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `200 MB` | Home healthcare documents often contain many images and charts, leading to large file sizes. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Parsing complex PDFs and scanned images is time-consuming; this prevents timeouts. |
| `Chunk size` | `800–1200 characters` | Preserves complete operational steps or logical paragraphs in SOPs, avoiding semantic fragmentation. |
| `Recall count` | `Top 5 entries` | Ensures coverage of multiple relevant operational steps or regulatory clauses a user might need. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75` | Guarantees high relevance between recall results and regulatory/SOP content, improving accuracy. |
| `Rerank result count` | `3 entries` | Filters for the most relevant and contextually coherent results, enhancing answer quality. |

## Three Common Pitfalls

*   Phenomenon: After document upload, some table content or flowcharts are not correctly recognized as text. Reason: The workflow does not integrate or incorrectly configures OCR parsing modules for complex layout documents.
*   Phenomenon: A user queries specific operating steps for a medical device, but the returned results are incomplete or do not match the latest SOP version. Reason: After document updates, the knowledge base fails to perform timely version synchronization or incremental indexing, leading to outdated information.
*   Phenomenon: When dragging components in the workflow orchestration interface, the interface responds slowly or even freezes. Reason: The workflow contains computationally intensive nodes or loops, leading to front-end rendering performance bottlenecks, especially when variable values change frequently.

## How to Verify Configuration

*   Upload a home healthcare SOP document with complex tables and flowcharts. Check the document preview and segmented content to confirm all critical information (including values within tables and text in diagrams) is correctly extracted and recognized.
*   For recently updated regulatory documents, upload new versions and conduct question-answering tests. Verify that the Q&A results reflect the latest content and check the knowledge base's version management records.
*   Perform tests using query statements containing specific medical parameters (e.g., units, numerical ranges). Check if the returned results accurately match and provide answers with correct units and field information.
*   Simulate user queries under high concurrency scenarios. Monitor workflow execution logs and response times to confirm system stability under high load and that Q&A accuracy is unaffected.
