# Model Integration and Configuration for Rehabilitation Equipment Regulations

## Data Characteristics for this Category

Rehabilitation equipment regulations and SOP documents primarily originate from quality management system files of medical device manufacturers, regulatory documents issued by national and local drug administrations, and internal equipment operation procedures of medical institutions. These documents are updated quarterly or annually, typically when regulations are revised or products are iterated. Document structures are hierarchical, containing extensive technical details, operating steps, safety specifications, and maintenance requirements. Content often includes specific rehabilitation equipment models, serial numbers, technical parameters (e.g., power, frequency, treatment modes), calibration cycles, and fault codes. Units are frequently international standard units (e.g., watts, hertz, millimeters, Celsius) and specific medical measurement units (e.g., joules/cm², milliamperes).

## Constraints on Model Integration and Configuration

The technical detail and strict regulatory nature of rehabilitation equipment documents require high accuracy and low hallucination from the model during knowledge retrieval to avoid misleading information. Frequent mentions of equipment models and technical parameters necessitate the model's ability to accurately identify and understand these entities, distinguishing differences between various models. The hierarchical structure and long text nature of the documents pose challenges for text segmentation strategies, requiring semantic integrity to be maintained. Furthermore, regular updates to regulatory files demand convenient incremental updates and version management for the knowledge base to ensure the model always provides Q&A services based on the latest valid regulations. Accurate identification of specific fields and units directly impacts the usability of Q&A results, requiring the model to have strong numerical and unit comprehension capabilities.

## Configuration Strategy

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----------------- | :---------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Segment Length` | `500–800 characters` | Balances semantic completeness and retrieval efficiency, preventing long paragraphs from diluting key information. |
| `Segment Overlap` | `50 characters` | Ensures contextual continuity and minimizes loss of important information due to segmentation. |
| `Retrieval Count` | `Top 5–8 entries` | Covers relevant information from multiple angles but avoids introducing excessive irrelevant content that burdens the model. |
| `Similarity Threshold` | `0.75–0.85` | Filters out low-relevance results, improving retrieval accuracy and reducing model hallucination. |
| `Reranked Return Count` | `Top 3 entries` | After reranking, focuses on the most relevant information, enhancing final answer quality. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Accommodates parsing time for large SOP documents, preventing processing failures due to timeouts. |

## Three Common Pitfalls

*   The model often confuses equipment models or technical parameters, or provides incorrect numerical values when answering questions involving them. This occurs because document segmentation fails to maintain an effective correspondence between equipment and parameters, or the embedding model's understanding of specialized terminology is insufficient.
*   Users receive "404" or "No relevant information found" error responses after asking questions. This can be due to a mismatch between the model name or API address in the OneAPI configuration and the actual deployment, or because the embedding model used does not support the specific language encoding.
*   The model provides results that do not align with the latest regulations when handling time-related institutional questions, such as calibration cycles or maintenance frequencies. This happens when the knowledge base is not updated promptly, or the document version management mechanism is ineffective.

## How to Verify Configuration

*   Select several representative rehabilitation equipment SOP documents, upload them to the knowledge base, check if parsing completes normally, and look for `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` errors in the logs.
*   For the uploaded documents, design test questions covering equipment models, technical parameters, operating steps, and safety specifications. Verify if the model can accurately retrieve relevant segments and check the completeness and accuracy of the retrieved segments.
*   Prepare a set of comparative questions that include both pre- and post-update regulations. Test whether the model can provide answers based on the latest version of the regulations, and manually compare the accuracy and timeliness of the answers to determine the effectiveness of the knowledge update mechanism.
