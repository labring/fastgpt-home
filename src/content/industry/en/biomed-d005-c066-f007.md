# Workflow Orchestration for Tender Bidding and Listing Regulations

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Tender bidding and listing regulation data in the biopharmaceutical sector originates from regional drug and medical device centralized procurement platforms, official medical insurance bureau websites, and internal enterprise management systems. This data updates frequently, sometimes weekly or even daily, especially with new procurement batches, price adjustments, or policy changes. Document types are diverse, including PDF policy documents, Excel product listing inventories, and Word tender announcements and evaluation rules. Beyond basic product names, specifications, and manufacturers, data fields include registration certificate numbers, medical insurance payment codes, listed prices, procurement cycles, negotiation rules, distribution areas, and restrictions. Price and quantity units can vary by region and category.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Workflow Orchestration

The high update frequency of tender bidding data requires automated triggers and rapid response capabilities in workflows to ensure real-time information. Diverse document formats and unstructured content necessitate workflow integration of document parsing and structured extraction modules. For example, table data in PDFs needs precise identification, and policy clauses in Word documents require semantic understanding. Field and unit discrepancies challenge data standardization and validation steps within the workflow, demanding clear conversion rules and error handling mechanisms. Additionally, regulation inquiries often involve cross-referencing multiple information sources. Workflows must support multi-knowledge base retrieval and information integration to ensure comprehensive and accurate answers, preventing the omission of critical restrictive clauses or regional differences.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----- | :------- | :----------- |
| `maxContext` | `6 entries` | Tender bidding regulation inquiries often involve contextual relationships. This quantity covers most multi-turn dialogue scenarios, ensuring complete understanding of user intent. |
| `segmentLength` | `800–1200 characters` | Policy documents and detailed rules typically contain extensive clauses. This length helps maintain the semantic integrity of individual knowledge blocks, improving recall quality. |
| `recallCount` | `top 8 entries` | Ensures coverage of relevant regulatory clauses from different sources or dimensions, enhancing answer comprehensiveness. |
| `similarityThreshold` | `0.78–0.85` | Balances recall precision and recall rate, avoiding interference from irrelevant clauses while not missing potentially relevant information. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Provides sufficient parsing time for large PDFs or documents with complex tables, preventing timeout failures. |
| `rerankReturnCount` | `top 3 entries` | After reranking, the three most relevant pieces of information typically support an accurate and concise answer. |

## Three Common Pitfalls

- The workflow loses context in multi-turn dialogues, preventing subsequent questions from inheriting previous information. This occurs if the `maxContext` parameter is set too low or not passed correctly.
- After a customer inquiry, the AI platform fails to accurately call a specific product knowledge base. This can be due to inaccurate tag identification by the classification module or ineffective guidance from "C-link prompts."
- The text content extraction plugin errors or returns an empty value, with logs showing `Failed to extract content`. This usually happens when the `chatHistory` parameter is configured improperly, failing to provide sufficient context for the plugin to make a judgment.

## How to Verify Configuration

- Conduct multi-turn dialogue tests for typical tender bidding regulation questions. Check if context is continuously understood correctly and evaluate the coherence and accuracy of answers.
- Upload and parse tender bidding documents in various formats (PDF, Excel, Word). Verify if the workflow successfully extracts key information and check the completeness of extracted fields and the correctness of units.
- Simulate user questions containing typos or vague descriptions. Observe if the workflow can still recall correct regulatory clauses through fuzzy matching or semantic understanding, and evaluate the reasonableness of the `similarityThreshold` for recall.
- Check workflow logs to ensure no `timeout`, `parsing failed`, or `knowledge base call exception` errors, especially when processing large or complex documents.
