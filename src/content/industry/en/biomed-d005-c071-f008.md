# Tool Calling and Plugins for Cleanroom Management Regulations

## Data Characteristics

Cleanroom management regulation data primarily originates from internal quality management system documents within pharmaceutical companies. These include SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) annexes, validation protocols, and risk assessment reports. Documents are typically in PDF, Word, or internal knowledge base page formats. Content covers personnel conduct, equipment operation procedures, environmental monitoring standards, material flow paths, and emergency handling processes.

Update frequency is relatively low, usually annually or every few years, driven by regulatory revisions or internal process optimizations. Document structure is rigorous, containing extensive terminology, numbering, diagrams, and cross-references. Common metadata fields include "Document Number," "Version Number," "Effective Date," and "Revision History." Units involved include physical quantities such as differential pressure (Pa), airborne particulate counts (particles/m³), and temperature/humidity (°C/%RH).

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Tool Calling and Plugins

The rigor and high density of specialized terminology in cleanroom management regulation documents necessitate high-precision entity recognition and contextual understanding for tool calling. This prevents misinterpretation of regulatory clauses. The prevalence of cross-references and version traceability requirements in documents demands that tools handle complex inter-document relationships and provide accurate version information during queries.

The low update frequency means real-time knowledge base requirements are relatively relaxed, but robust historical version management and change traceability capabilities are essential. Furthermore, physical units in documents require tools to correctly handle unit conversions during parameter passing and result parsing, preventing misjudgments due to unit inconsistencies. Understanding diagram content may require integrating visual question answering or more sophisticated document parsing plugins.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item     | Recommended Value              | Rationale                                                                                                                                                                |
| :--------------------- | :----------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `maxContext`           | `2000–3000 Tokens`             | Cleanroom SOP paragraphs are often long and contain detailed procedures. A longer context window ensures complete semantic understanding.                                |
| `chunkSize`            | `800–1200 characters`          | Given the integrity of regulatory documents, longer chunk sizes help preserve the complete semantics of a paragraph, reducing information loss due to truncation.         |
| `overlapSize`          | `100–200 characters`           | Appropriate overlap ensures contextual continuity between adjacent chunks, especially when handling complex logic or cross-references spanning paragraphs.                 |
| `top_k`                | `top 5`                        | Regulatory queries typically require high accuracy. Recalling more relevant items increases the probability of hitting the correct answer and supports multi-angle verification. |
| `similarity_threshold` | Calibrated by measurement, `0.75–0.85` suggested | Cleanroom regulation Q&A demands high accuracy. The threshold must ensure recall while filtering out irrelevant low-similarity content.                                   |
| `tool_retries`         | `3 times`                      | External tools (e.g., unit conversion, version query services) may fail due to network fluctuations or temporary service overload. Multiple retries improve success rates. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   Calling an external plugin returns a `connect ECONNREFUSED` or `Timeout` error. This may be due to an incorrect plugin service address configuration or the service not being started, preventing FastGPT from establishing a connection.
*   The model exhibits unit confusion or numerical errors when answering cleanroom environmental parameters. This appears as generated results with values inconsistent with the original regulations. This may be due to incorrect parsing of unit metadata in the document or the external tool failing to perform unit conversion.
*   Queries for specific document numbers or version numbers return empty or inaccurate results. This may be due to the knowledge base index not containing complete metadata, or the search plugin failing to precisely match complex query conditions.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   For multi-turn Q&A covering different document numbers, version numbers, and revision dates, verify if the model accurately identifies and cites the correct version of regulatory clauses. Check if the returned citations point to the correct documents.
*   Select parameters with units from the regulations, such as differential pressure, temperature/humidity, and airborne particulate counts. Ask about related management requirements and check if the model's returned values and units are consistent with the original text or have been correctly converted.
*   Simulate typical cleanroom management scenarios involving anomaly handling, such as "How to handle a cleanroom differential pressure alarm?" Verify if the model can call relevant SOPs and provide process-compliant answers.
