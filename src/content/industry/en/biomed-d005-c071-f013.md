# Knowledge Base Retrieval for Cleanroom Management Regulations

## Data Characteristics

Cleanroom management data in the biopharmaceutical industry originates from internal Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), batch production records, validation reports, training manuals, and regulatory documents. These documents are typically in PDF, Word, or scanned image formats, with varying degrees of structure. SOP documents are detailed, containing operational steps, parameters, and precautions. They are updated annually or when regulations or processes change. Batch production records include environmental monitoring data and equipment operating parameters for specific production processes, often in tabular format, with high update frequency tied to each batch. Fields and units are industry-specific, such as air cleanliness levels (ISO classes), differential pressure (Pa), temperature and humidity (°C, %RH), and microbial limits (CFU/m³).

## Constraints on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

The diverse structure of cleanroom management documents requires specific knowledge base chunking strategies. Long SOP texts need effective segmentation to prevent context loss. Tabular data requires special handling to maintain semantic integrity. The high update frequency of batch production records and regulatory documents necessitates an efficient knowledge base update mechanism for timely retrieval results. Industry-specific fields and units, such as "differential pressure 15 Pa" or "ISO Class 5," require the model to accurately understand and match them, potentially involving pre-training or fine-tuning for specific terminology. Retrieval accuracy is critical; any misinterpretation or recall error can lead to production compliance issues. Therefore, a high recall precision is required, avoiding generalized matches.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----- | :------- | :----------- |
| `Chunk size` | `800–1200 characters` | Balances SOP text completeness with information density per chunk, preventing overly long or short contexts. |
| `Chunk Overlap Length` | `100 characters` | Ensures sufficient contextual overlap between paragraphs, improving semantic coherence. |
| `Recall count` | `Top 5 entries` | Considers retrieval efficiency and result coverage, reducing interference from irrelevant information. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75` | Guarantees high relevance between recall results and query intent, lowering the risk of false positives. |
| `Rerank result count` | `3 entries` | Refines the final information presented to the user while maintaining accuracy. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Addresses the parsing requirements for large PDF or Word documents, preventing processing failures due to timeouts. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   Knowledge base retrieval steps take too long, for example, `4～5s`. This often results from an excessively large knowledge base or improper retrieval model configuration. Optimizing indexing strategies or selecting more efficient retrieval models may be necessary.
*   Content in uploaded `.xlsx` batch production record tables is not effectively indexed by the knowledge base, leading to empty results when querying related data. This occurs because the default file parser may not correctly extract structured information from multi-row tables.
*   When querying specific cleanroom management terms, such as "differential pressure requirements," the recall results contain many irrelevant general texts. This indicates that the knowledge base's embedding model has an insufficient understanding of specialized terminology or that the chunking strategy failed to effectively isolate professional content.

## Verification of Configuration

*   For cleanroom management SOP documents, submit queries about specific operational steps. Verify that the recall results include complete operational procedures and key parameters.
*   After uploading an `.xlsx` file containing cleanroom environmental monitoring data, query for temperature, humidity, or differential pressure for a specific batch. Confirm that the returned results accurately extract numerical values from the table.
*   Simulate common daily work questions, such as "What is the microbial limit standard for an ISO Class 7 cleanroom?" Check if the recalled items accurately point to relevant regulations or standard sections. Confirm that the similarity threshold effectively filters out irrelevant general text.
