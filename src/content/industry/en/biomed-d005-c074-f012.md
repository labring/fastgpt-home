# Model Integration and Configuration for Biopharmaceutical Equipment Regulations

## Data Characteristics

Biopharmaceutical equipment regulations and SOP documents are typically PDFs, Word files, or scanned images. Data sources include operation manuals from equipment suppliers, internal quality management system documents, and regulatory guidelines from compliance departments. These documents have a relatively low update frequency, occurring mainly after equipment introduction, process changes, regulatory updates, or annual audits. Document structures usually include a table of contents, detailed operating procedures, troubleshooting guides, maintenance schedules, safety precautions, and appendices. Field and unit specifications are strict. Equipment parameters often involve pressure (kPa, psi), temperature (℃, ℉), flow rate (L/min, mL/s), and time (s, min, h). Documents extensively use specialized terminology and abbreviations.

## Constraints on Model Integration and Configuration

The low update frequency of biopharmaceutical equipment regulation documents allows for longer batch processing cycles during model training or knowledge base construction, reducing pressure for real-time updates. The complex document structure and extensive specialized terminology demand higher accuracy in text segmentation and entity recognition to prevent critical information loss or misunderstanding. Scanned documents, in particular, require high-quality OCR preprocessing to ensure text content integrity and readability. The strictness of fields and units means the model must accurately understand and generate numerical information with correct units during Q&A, preventing safety or operational risks due to unit confusion. Additionally, these documents are often lengthy, requiring models with large context windows and strong long-text processing capabilities.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| :----------------- | :---------------- | :-------- |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Regulation documents may contain many images or complex layouts, resulting in large file sizes. |
| `Chunk size` | `800–1200 characters` | Ensures each segment contains sufficient context while avoiding excessive length that could reduce model processing efficiency, adapting to dense specialized terminology. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75–0.85` | Regulatory Q&A demands high accuracy, requiring retrieval of highly relevant segments to reduce the risk of incorrect answers. |
| `Rerank result count` | `Top 5 entries` | Ensures the model receives sufficiently diverse yet highly relevant reference information, improving answer comprehensiveness. |
| `maxContext` | `32k tokens` or higher | Biopharmaceutical equipment documents are often lengthy, requiring a larger context window to handle complex questions and long texts. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Processing large PDFs or OCR-identified scanned documents requires significant time, preventing parsing timeouts. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   The model exhibits logical errors or omits critical steps when answering equipment operation procedures. This occurs because text segments are too short or too few retrieval results are provided, preventing the model from acquiring complete operational flows.
*   Processing progress stalls or errors occur after uploading large PDFs or scanned documents. This usually happens because `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` is set too low, insufficient for file parsing and OCR recognition time.
*   When users query specific parameters (e.g., "sterilization temperature"), the model returns numerical values without units or with incorrect units. This occurs because the association between values and units was not adequately identified and labeled during knowledge base construction, or the model failed to correctly reproduce them during generation.

## Verification Steps

*   Upload several typical equipment operation manuals and SOPs. Check if files are successfully parsed and segments are reasonable.
*   Ask complex questions regarding key operational procedures, troubleshooting steps, and safety regulations. Verify the accuracy and completeness of the model's answers, checking if all necessary steps or precautions are included.
*   Randomly select parameters with units from documents and query them. Confirm that the numerical values returned by the model are accurate and units are correct.
*   Simulate equipment failure scenarios and ask questions. Evaluate whether the model can provide reasonable diagnostic suggestions and solutions based on knowledge base content, and compare them with actual documentation.
