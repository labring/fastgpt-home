# Deployment and Upgrade for Biopharmaceutical Equipment Regulations

## Data Characteristics

Data for biopharmaceutical equipment regulations and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) originate from technical documentation provided by equipment vendors, internal quality management system documents from pharmaceutical companies, and compliance guidelines issued by regulatory bodies. These documents are typically in formats like PDF, DOCX, and XML. Content includes equipment operation manuals, maintenance specifications, calibration and validation records, troubleshooting guides, and risk assessment reports. Data update frequency is relatively stable, primarily occurring during equipment procurement, upgrades, or regulatory policy adjustments. Document structures are often highly standardized, featuring clear section titles, charts, parameter lists, and flowcharts. Fields and units are strictly specialized, for example, pressure units like `bar` or `psi`, temperature units like `°C`, and flow rates like `L/min`, often accompanied by specific equipment models and batch information.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Deployment and Upgrade"

The specialized nature and standardized structure of biopharmaceutical equipment regulation documents require the knowledge base to accurately extract text from specific paragraphs, tables, and figures during data parsing to ensure semantic completeness. Documents contain numerous technical terms and acronyms, necessitating dedicated glossaries or domain models for precise vectorization and retrieval. Update frequency is relatively low, but content changes have significant impact. Therefore, the knowledge base's incremental update mechanism must support managing and tracing specific document versions to prevent contamination from outdated information. Document size can also be substantial, requiring higher limits for file upload size and parsing timeout. The strictness of fields and units means retrieval results must clearly present numerical information from the original text to avoid ambiguity. High compliance requirements mandate rigorous validation of the deployed knowledge base to ensure accuracy and consistency of Q&A results.

## Three Common Mistakes

*   Receiving `File too large` or `Parsing timeout` errors when uploading large PDF documents: This usually indicates that `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` or `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameters are set too low for the actual needs of biopharmaceutical equipment documents.
*   Retrieval results contain a large amount of irrelevant or low-relevance content: This may stem from `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) being set too low, leading to an overly broad recall range and failing to effectively filter out non-core regulatory clauses.
*   Encountering a `MongoServerError: The dollar ($) p` error when importing a knowledge base backup file: This typically occurs due to incompatibility between the FastGPT version and the MongoDB database version, preventing correct parsing of data structures or query syntax.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   Upload a PDF file of an equipment operating procedure containing complex tables and figures. Verify that the file uploads successfully, is parsed correctly, and that content segmentation is reasonable.
*   Ask questions related to specific equipment models and fault codes. Check if retrieval results accurately point to corresponding troubleshooting sections or maintenance specifications and present key parameters from the original text.
*   Compare different versions of regulation documents and perform incremental updates. Confirm that the knowledge base correctly identifies and updates changed parts, and that old version information is not erroneously overwritten.
