# Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall for Monitoring Equipment Regulations

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Monitoring equipment regulations and SOP documents originate from internal quality management, equipment, and procurement departments within medical institutions. Some content may reference national or industry standards. These documents update infrequently, typically annually or in response to regulatory changes, excluding emergencies. Document structures primarily include regulations, operating procedures, maintenance manuals, and emergency plans. Most are PDF or Word text files, containing numerous tables, images, and flowcharts. Text content often involves equipment models, serial numbers, calibration dates, fault codes, alarm thresholds, operating steps, and various technical parameters. Fields may include units like "mmHg," "kPa," "bpm," and "℃," with explicit specifications for precision and error ranges.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

The low update frequency of monitoring equipment regulation documents means real-time requirements for the knowledge base are not stringent after import. However, it may necessitate tracking and managing historical versions. Complex tables and flowcharts within documents challenge the knowledge base's parsing capabilities; plain text retrieval might miss critical information. Extensive technical parameters and units require the retrieval system to identify and differentiate values and units for precise matching. Furthermore, the specificity of equipment models and fault codes demands effective handling of such proper nouns during indexing to prevent recall rate degradation due to improper tokenization. Descriptions of precision and error ranges require retrieval results to provide sufficient context for engineers to make accurate judgments.

## Configuration Guidelines

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `Chunk size` (Segment Length) | `500–800 characters` | Ensures each segment contains sufficient context, covering complete operating steps or regulatory clauses. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `8–12 entries` | Balances retrieval efficiency and coverage, increasing the likelihood of recalling relevant regulations or SOPs. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.75–0.85` | The medical field demands high accuracy; a low threshold may introduce irrelevant information, while a high one risks omissions. |
| `Rerank result count` (Reranked Return Count) | `5 entries` | Re-sorts initial recall results to further enhance relevance and reduce engineer screening time. |
| `Parsing Strategy` | `Smart segmentation, with table structure parsing enabled` | Addresses common tables and flowcharts in documents, ensuring data within tables is effectively indexed. |
| `Metadata Extraction` | `Equipment model, SOP number, revision date` | Utilizes structured information within documents to aid retrieval, improving accuracy and traceability. |

## Three Common Mistakes

1.  Error: Retrieval results contain numerous irrelevant general medical terms, but specific equipment model regulations are not recalled.
    Reason: The knowledge base failed to effectively identify unique model codes or abbreviations for monitoring equipment during tokenization, leading to overly coarse or fine-grained indexing.
2.  Error: When querying for the handling procedure corresponding to an alarm code, the returned results lack critical numerical ranges or unit information.
    Reason: Document parsing failed to correctly extract values and units from tables or lists, treating them as independent text and resulting in context loss.
3.  Error: After importing SOP documents containing many charts, the recall rate for relevant content is extremely low or incomplete.
    Reason: The knowledge base's parsing capabilities could not effectively process text embedded in images or the logical relationships in flowcharts, indexing only a small amount of surrounding text.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration

*   Select 5–10 typical monitoring equipment fault scenarios or operational queries. Check if the recall results include the original regulation text, operating steps, and key parameters.
*   Randomly select multiple SOP documents containing complex tables or flowcharts. Verify if the knowledge base can recall specific values within tables or process nodes through specific queries.
*   Query for different equipment models or SOP version numbers. Confirm that retrieval results differentiate and prioritize the latest or most relevant documents.
*   Simulate engineers' actual questioning methods. Test if the knowledge base can still recall accurate and complete regulatory information with colloquial or non-standard queries.
