# Multiturn Conversations and Prompts for mRNA Vaccine Registration Dossier Preparation

## Data Characteristics for This Category

mRNA vaccine registration dossiers primarily include clinical trial reports, non-clinical study reports, manufacturing process and quality control documents, and stability study data. This data typically exists in various formats such as PDF, Word, and Excel. PDF documents are prevalent and often contain numerous charts, graphs, and scanned images. Data update frequency is high during the clinical trial phase and stabilizes once the submission phase begins, mainly involving supplementary materials. Document structures adhere to regulatory body guidelines, such as ICH M4E, with strict hierarchies and section divisions. Fields involve dosage units like `μg`, time units such as `days`, `weeks`, `months`, `years`, and complex biological indicators like `antibody titer` and `T-cell response`.

## Constraints on "Multiturn Conversations and Prompts" Imposed by These Characteristics

Charts, graphs, and scanned images within PDF documents challenge text extraction, requiring enhanced OCR capabilities to ensure data completeness. Strict document structures demand that recall understands contextual hierarchies to avoid erroneous cross-section associations. High data update frequency, especially in early clinical trials, requires the knowledge base to support rapid updates and version management, ensuring multiturn conversations are based on the latest information. Complex biological indicators and specialized terminology place higher demands on the large model's understanding and generation capabilities, necessitating precise prompt guidance to focus the model on factual answers. In multiturn conversations, users may refer to earlier data versions, requiring the system to locate and cite historical information.

## Common Pitfalls

*   Multiturn conversations terminate midway, returning incomplete answers. This occurs due to large model API connection timeouts or internal workflow configurations not covering all branches.
*   AI response word limits fail, producing output exceeding expectations. This typically happens when the word limit instruction in the prompt has insufficient weight and is diluted by other instructions.
*   After a user selects a plugin, the prompt repeatedly triggers in subsequent conversations. This occurs when the plugin trigger logic is not associated with the single session state, lacking effective context management.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration

*   Upload multiple mRNA vaccine dossier PDFs containing scanned images. Check the consistency between the OCR-extracted text in the knowledge base and the original documents.
*   Ask multiturn questions about specific biological indicators or experimental data. Verify that the AI's answers accurately cite specific values and units from the documents.
*   Simulate users asking questions about the same document at different times. Confirm the system can cite the latest data version and explain data update circumstances.
