# Conversation Logs and Auditing for Structured Analysis of Deviation and CAPA R&D Documents

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Deviation and Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) documents originate from production quality management systems in biopharmaceutical companies, such as those in pharmaceuticals and medical devices. These documents have a relatively low update frequency. They are typically recorded and approved after an event occurs, resulting in a final version. The document structure is primarily unstructured text, covering event descriptions, root cause analyses, corrective actions, preventive actions, and verification results. Common fields include deviation number, occurrence date, impact level, responsible department, CAPA plan completion date, and actual completion date. Impact levels are often graded (e.g., minor, moderate, severe), and date fields are critical for chronological analysis.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Conversation Logs and Auditing"

The unstructured nature of Deviation and CAPA documents requires conversation logs to precisely record user query intent, extracted key information, and system responses for traceability and auditing. Due to the infrequent document updates, there is a high demand for historical version queries, requiring logs to support linked queries for specific document versions. Key fields like impact level and completion date require close attention during auditing. Log records must therefore include the extraction and usage of these fields. The auditing process may involve cross-departmental collaboration, necessitating logs that track different users' queries and interactions with the same deviation or CAPA record, and identify potential errors or non-compliant operations. Conversation log retention periods must meet regulatory requirements, typically several years.

## Configuration Guidelines

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| `LOG_LEVEL` | `INFO` or `DEBUG` | Records detailed user interactions and system processing for easier troubleshooting. |
| `maxContext` | `6000 characters` | Ensures full capture of deviation or CAPA descriptions in user queries, preventing information truncation. |
| `PARSED_LOG_RETENTION_DAYS` | `1825 days` | Complies with biopharmaceutical industry regulations requiring quality records to be retained for at least 5 years. |
| `AUDIT_LOG_ENABLED` | `true` | Enforces audit logging, recording all user access and operations on Deviation and CAPA documents. |

## Three Common Pitfalls

*   Auditing difficulties arise from missing critical business fields in logs, due to not explicitly specifying required metadata fields in the configuration.
*   User deletion of conversation content also deletes backend logs, violating auditing requirements. This occurs when the logging system is not decoupled from conversation records, allowing frontend operations to affect backend audit data.
*   Querying conversation records for specific historical document versions fails because conversation logs are not linked to document version information, preventing accurate traceability.

## Verification Steps

*   Execute a series of queries involving different deviation numbers and impact levels. Check if the logs fully record these key fields.
*   Attempt to delete user conversations. Then, check backend audit logs to confirm that deletion operations do not affect already recorded conversation log entries.
*   Simulate queries for archived deviation or CAPA documents. Verify that logs correctly link to document version information.
