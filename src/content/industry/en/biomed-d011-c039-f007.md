# Workflow Orchestration for Compliance-Related Private Domain Consultation Conversion

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Compliance-related data in the biopharmaceutical industry originates from regulatory documents and guidelines published by official agencies like the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) and the National Health Commission. It also includes internal compliance review documents, product specifications, and clinical trial reports. This data typically exists as PDFs, Word documents, or structured databases. Regulatory policy documents are usually updated quarterly or annually. Internal company materials are updated periodically based on product lifecycles or market changes. Regulatory documents often have hierarchical structures, including chapters, clauses, and annexes. Content is rigorous and rich in specialized terminology. Fields may include drug names, indications, contraindications, adverse reactions, dosage and administration, and approval numbers. Specific units, such as `mg`, `ml` for dosage and `days`, `weeks` for time, are common.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Workflow Orchestration

The highly specialized and rigorous nature of compliance data demands extreme accuracy in information extraction. Semantic deviations or omissions of critical information are unacceptable. The periodic updates to regulatory documents mean knowledge bases require regular synchronization. The workflow must support incremental updates or version management to ensure consultations use the latest regulations. Complex document structures challenge document segmentation and vectorization strategies within the workflow. Fine-grained processing is necessary to maintain contextual integrity. The presence of specialized fields and units requires intent recognition and entity extraction components in the workflow to accurately identify and understand this information. For example, the system must distinguish between a `5mg` dosage and `5 minutes` of time to avoid misunderstandings that could lead to non-compliant responses. These constraints dictate specific configuration requirements for data preprocessing, knowledge base construction, and Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) within the workflow.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Suggested Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `Chunk size` (Segment Length) | `500–800 characters` (characters) | Ensures contextual integrity of regulatory clauses while preventing information overload from excessively long segments. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `Top 5–8 entries` (top 5–8 items) | Balances the relevance of regulatory clauses, improving recall accuracy and reducing interference from irrelevant information. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.78–0.85` | Increases the threshold for highly specialized terminology to ensure a high semantic match in recalled content. |
| `Rerank result count` (Rerank Return Count) | `Top 3 entries` (top 3 items) | Streamlines the final output while maintaining accuracy, focusing on the most relevant compliance clauses. |
| `maxContext` | `3000 Tokens` | Provides a sufficiently long context window to handle complex multi-turn conversations that may arise during regulatory consultations. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` (seconds) | Handles the parsing of large regulatory files and complex tables, preventing file processing failures due to timeouts. |

## Three Common Pitfalls

*   Workflow conversations fail despite the model testing normally and showing response logs. This can occur if the parsing path for the model's return value is incorrectly configured in the workflow, preventing subsequent components from receiving valid data.
*   A specific node in the workflow, such as "Question Optimization," triggers its prompt frequently. This happens when the system `prompt` and the node `prompt` conflict or overwrite each other, preventing the node from executing according to its intended logic.
*   After invoking the workflow, the conversation log is empty. This may be due to an execution error within a workflow component without a robust error handling mechanism configured, leading to a process interruption with no clear feedback.

## How to Verify Configuration

*   Simulate typical compliance consultation scenarios. Verify that the workflow's output accurately cites regulatory clauses and that the cited clause numbers and content match the original text.
*   Review the workflow's logs. Ensure that the input and output of each component meet expectations, particularly verifying that the `similarity score` of knowledge base retrieval results meets the set threshold.
*   For regulatory updates, upload new versions of regulatory documents and execute the knowledge base synchronization process. Verify that the workflow correctly identifies and updates relevant compliance clauses, ensuring consultations are based on the latest data.
