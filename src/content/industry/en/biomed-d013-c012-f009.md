# Source and Traceability for SMO Quality Documents

## Data Characteristics

Site Management Organizations (SMOs) play a critical role in biopharmaceutical research. SMO quality documents primarily include Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), training records, institutional qualification files, ethical review approvals, investigator brochures, subject informed consent form templates, and clinical trial protocols. These documents originate from internal SMO development, sponsor provision, or regulatory agency releases. Update frequency is relatively high, especially for SOPs and study protocols, which may be revised based on project progress, regulatory updates, or internal process optimization. Documents are typically in PDF or Word format, with standardized content and fixed formatting. Fields often include version numbers, effective dates, revision history, approvers, and document numbers. Some documents contain specific biomedical parameters such as drug dosage, trial duration, and visit schedules, with units strictly adhering to international standards.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Source and Traceability"

The standardization and high update frequency of SMO quality documents demand stringent requirements for source accuracy and traceability. Documents contain extensive critical operational details and medical parameters. Any citation error can lead to severe compliance risks or trial deviations. Therefore, citations must precisely pinpoint specific paragraphs in the original text and trace back to the document's specific version and effective date. This ensures information consistency and timeliness. Furthermore, common non-textual content like images, tables, and attachments requires the system to handle complex document structures, ensuring these elements are correctly parsed for citation. When exporting data for backup, the system needs to support fine-grained differentiation of document types and business categories to prevent loss of specific document version information due to bulk export.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| `Chunk size` (Segment Length) | `500–800 characters` (characters) | Balances semantic completeness for long documents and retrieval efficiency for short documents, suitable for long texts like SOPs. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `8–12 entries` (items) | Covers a broader range of potentially relevant paragraphs, considering the specialized and interconnected nature of SMO documents. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.75–0.85` | Ensures precision of recalled content, avoiding irrelevant or low-relevance content confusion. |
| `Rerank result count` (Rerank Return Count) | `4–6 entries` (items) | Reduces redundant information presented to the user while maintaining accuracy. |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Accommodates SMO documents that may contain numerous images and attachments. |
| `maxContext` | `3000 Tokens` | Ensures the complete context can be processed by the model, especially for complex process descriptions. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   Answer results lack original source links or version information for citations. This occurs when the knowledge base segmentation strategy is too coarse and does not retain sufficient metadata.
*   Some specialized terms or table content are not correctly cited. This happens when the text processing model's ability to parse complex document structures is insufficient, leading to critical information loss.
*   When exporting the knowledge base, filtering by document type or business category is unavailable. This is because the data export function's granularity only supports the entire knowledge base level and does not offer refined options.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   Conduct question-and-answer tests using typical SOP documents. Check if the results include original source links and specific paragraph citations. Verify if the cited content matches the original document version.
*   Upload trial protocols containing complex tables and diagrams. Verify if the system correctly identifies and cites data points in tables or diagram descriptions to confirm parsing capabilities.
*   Attempt a knowledge base backup export. Confirm if export options support fine-grained filtering by document type, file number, etc. Check if the exported files contain complete metadata.
*   Simulate asking questions about a revised document. Confirm that the system returns the latest version as the source and that older content is no longer prioritized.
