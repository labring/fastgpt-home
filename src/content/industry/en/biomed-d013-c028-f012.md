# Model Integration and Configuration for Molecular Diagnostics Quality Documentation

## Data Characteristics in This Category

Molecular diagnostics quality documentation primarily includes reagent kit inserts, instrument operation manuals, performance verification reports, batch production records, calibration and quality control records, SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), and related regulatory compliance documents. These documents typically originate from manufacturers, internal laboratory generation, or regulatory bodies. Update frequency is relatively stable, usually occurring a few times a year during product upgrades, regulatory adjustments, or internal process optimizations. Document structure is highly standardized, often using section numbering, tables, and flowcharts. Fields and units are highly specialized, such as "Ct value," "Limit of Detection (LOD)," "linear range," "specificity," "sensitivity," and "copies/mL," and strictly adhere to metrological unit specifications.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Model Integration and Configuration"

The high standardization and specialized fields in molecular diagnostics documents require the model to effectively identify and maintain semantic integrity within tables and flowcharts during text segmentation. This prevents critical information loss due to fragmentation. The highly specialized fields and units mean the model needs a strong understanding of these domain-specific terms to accurately extract and associate information during Q&A, avoiding irrelevant answers or unit confusion. The relatively stable update frequency reduces the need for frequent full knowledge base updates. However, during incremental updates, the model must correctly recognize the correlation and differences between new and old document versions. The prevalence of long texts, such as detailed performance verification reports, challenges the model's context window and vectorization strategy. Overly short segmentation can lead to semantic fragmentation, while overly long segmentation may dilute core information.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `Chunk size` | `800-1200 characters` | Balances the integrity of tables and paragraphs in molecular diagnostics documents, preventing critical information from being cut. |
| `Chunk overlap` | `100-200 characters` | Ensures semantic continuity between paragraphs, especially when dealing with process steps or parameter lists. |
| `Vector Model` | `text-embedding-ada-002` or `bge-large-zh-v1.5` | Provides more accurate semantic representation for understanding specialized terms and long texts. |
| `Recall count` | `8-12 entries` | Increases the coverage of relevant document snippets, addressing complex queries that may involve multiple knowledge points. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75-0.85` | Balances recall and accuracy, preventing interference from irrelevant content while ensuring professional terminology matching. |
| `maxContext` | `8000 Token` | Adapts to the characteristics of long report-type texts in molecular diagnostics, reducing semantic understanding deviations caused by insufficient context. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   When processing long texts like performance verification reports, the model's output may seem relevant but is actually off-topic. This typically occurs because `Chunk size` is set too short, splitting critical contextual information and preventing the model from obtaining complete semantics.
*   After uploading SOP documents containing numerous charts, the model cannot accurately answer questions involving processes or data. This happens because the document parser fails to correctly identify and extract text information from charts, leading to missing knowledge base content.
*   When retrieving batch production records, the model fails to return expected results, or even prompts "503 Current Group default For Model text-embedding." This may indicate a vector service call failure or improper `Vector Model` configuration, preventing documents from being correctly vectorized or queries from being executed.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   Select a reagent kit insert containing complex tables and specialized terms. Ask questions about key parameters or operating procedures within it. Check if the model's output is accurate, complete, and includes correct units.
*   Upload a recent regulatory compliance document. Query it for specific testing methods or quality standards. Observe if the model can correctly cite original passages and assess the precision of the cited content.
*   For an SOP document containing multi-step processes, ask questions about specific step order or anomaly handling. Verify the logical consistency of the model's answer and the correctness of the process to confirm its understanding of flowcharts or step descriptions.
