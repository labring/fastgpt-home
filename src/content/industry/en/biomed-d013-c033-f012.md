# Model Integration and Configuration for Medical Affairs Quality Documentation

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Medical affairs quality documentation primarily includes clinical study protocols, ethics approval documents, informed consent forms, investigator brochures, clinical trial reports, drug package inserts, post-market safety reports, and various regulatory compliance statements. These documents often originate from pharmaceutical internal R&D departments, Contract Research Organizations (CROs), regulatory bodies, or academic institutions. Document update frequency is relatively low, typically released periodically with clinical trial progress, regulatory updates, or product lifecycle stages. Document structure is highly standardized, often following international guidelines such as ICH GCP, FDA, or EMA templates. They contain extensive specialized terminology, dosage units (e.g., mg/kg, IU), time units (e.g., weeks, months, years), and complex table and chart data.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Model Integration and Configuration"

The standardized structure and specialized terminology of medical affairs quality documents require high precision from the model during text embedding and retrieval. The low document update frequency means that after initial large-scale vector database construction, incremental update pressure is minimal, but historical document version management is crucial. Dosage and time units within documents require the model to accurately identify and differentiate numbers from units, preventing ambiguity during information extraction or summarization. Complex table and chart data demand higher capabilities from the document parser to effectively extract structured information. Furthermore, regulatory compliance requirements necessitate considering data privacy and compliance during model integration, potentially requiring private deployment or local large language models, and avoiding the generation of inaccurate regulatory interpretations in model responses.

## Configuration Guidelines

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Documents like clinical trial reports can contain many images and charts, leading to large file sizes. |
| `Chunk size` (Chunk Length) | `800 characters` (characters) | Medical documents have strong contextual relevance; longer chunks help maintain semantic integrity. |
| `Recall count` (Retrieval Count) | `Top 8 entries` (top 8) | Ensures sufficient relevant information snippets are covered during specialized knowledge retrieval. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.78` | Ensures high relevance of retrieval results to medical queries, filtering out general information. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` (seconds) | Large file parsing takes longer; sufficient timeout is needed to prevent task failure. |
| `maxContext` | `4096 tokens` | Complex medical questions require the model to process a longer context for accurate answers. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   Model responses contain confusing dosage units or numerical errors. This occurs because the document parser fails to accurately distinguish numbers from units, or the model does not strictly adhere to original numerical values during generation.
*   Processing large PDF files times out after upload, leading to file import failure. This occurs because the `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` configuration is too short to cover the parsing time for large documents.
*   Local large language model API calls return `Invalid API key` or `Unauthorized` errors. This occurs because the `API_KEY` or `BASE_URL` configuration does not match the actual authentication information of the deployed local model service.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration

*   Upload a medical document containing complex tables and charts. Check if the parsed text content is complete and well-structured, paying special attention to whether table data is correctly converted into readable text.
*   Ask questions related to clinical research for specific diseases. Verify the document snippets cited in the model's answers, ensuring their professionalism and accuracy meet medical standards.
*   Test medical queries of varying lengths and complexities. Observe the model's response speed and content quality, ensuring accurate and well-organized answers are provided within an acceptable response time.
