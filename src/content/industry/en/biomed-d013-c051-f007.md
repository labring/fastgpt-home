# Workflow Orchestration for Market Access Quality Documents

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Market access quality documents primarily cover regulations, guidelines, technical requirements, and internal SOPs, batch production records, and inspection reports for drug and medical device product registration, production licenses, and quality system certifications. Data sources include public documents from national and international regulatory bodies, industry association standards, and internal quality management systems. Regulations and guidelines may update quarterly or annually. Internal company documents update weekly to annually, depending on production and quality management needs. Document structures typically include standardized section titles and fixed fields (e.g., product name, registration number, batch number, production date, expiry date, test items, results, limits, approver, approval date). Documents often contain charts, images, and scanned copies. Field units strictly follow regulatory requirements, such as `mg/mL`, `IU`, `pH`, and `°C`.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Workflow Orchestration"

The regulatory dependence of market access documents requires workflows with high-fidelity structured parsing capabilities. This ensures accurate extraction of key information and comparison against regulatory requirements. The periodic nature of document updates demands workflow support for scheduled triggers and incremental updates, avoiding redundant processing of unchanged data. Documents containing numerous charts and scanned copies necessitate OCR integration within the workflow. Post-processing and validation of OCR results are also required. The presence of fixed fields means information extraction modules in the workflow must precisely identify and extract field values, such as `registration number` and `production batch number`. The strictness of units constrains data validation steps in the workflow to recognize and verify unit correctness, for example, numerical range validation for `pH` values. Real-time requirements for document content drive the need for rapid indexing after data ingestion, supporting immediate retrieval and question answering.

## Configuration Recommendations

| Configuration Item | Suggested Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Market access documents are complex, containing extensive specialized terminology and charts, leading to longer parsing times. |
| `maxContext` | `8000 tokens` | Ensures complete coverage of key sections of typical regulations or SOPs, providing sufficient context for question answering. |
| `Chunk size` (Segment Length) | `500 characters` | Balances semantic completeness and retrieval efficiency, preventing overly long segments from introducing irrelevant information. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `Top 10` | Guarantees coverage of multiple relevant regulatory clauses or technical requirements during retrieval. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | Calibrate based on actual measurements | Requires balancing recall and precision against specific document sets and query types, avoiding irrelevant information. |
| `Rerank result count` (Reranked Return Count) | `Top 5` | Focuses on the most relevant regulatory basis or technical details, improving the accuracy of the final results. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   After uploading a file, the file storage path does not match expectations. This prevents subsequent workflow steps from locating the file. The cause may be incorrect specification of the target file storage path or the use of relative paths in the workflow configuration.
*   The workflow experiences prolonged unresponsiveness or freezing when processing documents with numerous tables or scanned copies. This typically occurs due to the OCR engine taking too long to process complex images or insufficient resource allocation.
*   Information extraction steps return empty or incorrectly formatted field values, for example, `registration number` missing numbers or letters. The cause may be that regular expressions or structured extraction rules do not match the actual document format.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration

*   Upload a market access document containing typical structured information (e.g., tables, specific fields). After workflow processing, check if key fields (e.g., `batch number`, `expiry date`) are correctly extracted and populated.
*   Submit a complex query about the document content. Verify the accuracy and completeness of the returned results and check if the cited source document passages are correct.
*   Monitor workflow execution time through system logs or monitoring dashboards. Ensure no timeout errors occur when processing large files and check for normal resource utilization.
