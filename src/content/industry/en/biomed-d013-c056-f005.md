# Cardiovascular Quality Documentation: Multi-turn Conversations and Prompts

## Data Characteristics

Cardiovascular disease quality documentation originates from clinical trial reports, drug inserts, treatment guidelines, and post-market surveillance data. These documents update infrequently, typically with new drug approvals, guideline revisions, or large-scale clinical data releases. Update cycles can range from months to years. Document structures are complex, containing extensive specialized terminology, dosage units, pharmacokinetic parameters, and statistical data. For example, drug inserts detail indications, dosage and administration (e.g., `mg/kg`, `QD`, `BID`), contraindications, adverse event rates (e.g., `1/1000`), and interactions. Clinical trial reports include baseline characteristics of subjects, primary and secondary endpoint data, statistical analysis methods, and P-values. Documents often mix international units and common clinical units, such as blood pressure in `mmHg` and heart rate in `bpm`.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Conversations and Prompts

The complexity of cardiovascular quality documentation demands high accuracy in multi-turn conversations and prompt design. Specialized and ambiguous terms require precise semantic understanding from the model to avoid misdiagnosis or misuse of information. For example, "heart failure" can refer to cardiac insufficiency but might colloquially mean myocardial infarction. Dosage unit and numerical precision are critical; minor deviations can lead to severe consequences. Therefore, conversations require repeated confirmation of values and units. Documents update infrequently but contain authoritative content. This means the model must recall the latest validated knowledge to avoid providing advice based on outdated guidelines. Multi-turn conversations need to track disease status, medication history, and patient characteristics within the context to ensure generated responses align with cardiovascular diagnosis and treatment logic. The system must also handle user inquiries about the efficacy and risks of specific treatment plans.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Suggested Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `maxContext` | `8` | Ensures the model retains sufficient cardiovascular diagnosis and treatment context in multi-turn conversations, such as patient history and past medications, while preventing overly long contexts from diluting critical information. |
| `Chunk size` | `500-800 characters` | Cardiovascular document paragraphs are often long and contain multiple pieces of information. This length helps maintain semantic integrity and prevents key information from being truncated. |
| `Recall count` | `Top 5 entries` | Given the specialized and precise requirements of cardiovascular documents, recalling too many items can introduce noise. A small number of highly relevant items helps the model focus. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.78-0.85` | Medical document terminology often has high similarity. This range helps filter out highly relevant paragraphs, avoiding generalized recall. |
| `Rerank result count` | `Top 3 entries` | Further refines recall results, ensuring the model ultimately processes a small amount of the most relevant and authoritative information, reducing the probability of misinterpretation. |
| `LLM_MODEL_NAME` | `gpt-4o` | Complex medical terminology understanding and logical reasoning require advanced language model support. |

## Common Mistakes

*   Numerical or unit confusion in conversations, such as mistaking `mg` for `g`. This occurs because prompts do not explicitly require the model to strictly validate numbers and units.
*   The model forgets early patient medication information in multi-turn conversations, leading to inappropriate advice. This can happen if `maxContext` is set too low, resulting in an insufficient context window to cover the entire treatment process.
*   When a user queries contraindications for a specific drug, the model returns general, unrelated information. This happens if the `Similarity threshold` is set too low, failing to precisely filter document segments related to the specific drug.

## How to Verify Configuration

*   Conduct multi-turn conversation tests, gradually increasing the number of turns and information complexity. Observe whether the model can accurately recall and utilize early information after the `maxContext` turn.
*   Input queries containing cardiovascular specialized terms, dosage units, and numerical values. Check if the numerical values and units in the model's response strictly match the original document and if it correctly handles unit conversions.
*   Simulate user questions about a specific drug's indications, contraindications, and adverse reactions. Verify if the document segments recalled by the model are highly relevant and provide authoritative, up-to-date information. Determine the qualification threshold by comparing against the latest treatment guidelines.
