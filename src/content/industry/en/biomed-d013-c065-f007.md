# Workflow Orchestration for Quality Documentation in Nursing Management

## Data Characteristics in This Category

Quality documentation in nursing management primarily includes regulations, operational procedures, nursing records, risk assessment forms, training materials, and inspection reports. Data sources are diverse, covering Hospital Information Systems (HIS), Electronic Medical Records (EMR), nursing documentation systems, and scanned paper documents. Update frequency varies by document type; operational procedures and regulations may be revised every six months to a year, while nursing records and risk assessment forms are generated in real-time or daily. Document structures typically include standardized fields such as title, version number, revision history, scope, responsibilities, process steps, and appendices. Field units are often text descriptions, dates, times, numerical values (e.g., body temperature in Celsius, blood pressure in mmHg), and boolean values (e.g., qualified or not).

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on "Workflow Orchestration"

The diverse sources and update frequencies of nursing management quality documents require flexible data ingestion capabilities in workflow orchestration. Real-time or daily updates of nursing records and risk assessment forms necessitate triggered or scheduled tasks to ensure knowledge base timeliness, for instance, by synchronizing data periodically via API. Documents contain numerous standardized fields and specialized terminology, requiring workflows to focus on accurate named entity recognition and field extraction during document parsing to ensure information is correctly indexed. Furthermore, the presence of document revision history and version numbers demands specific knowledge update strategies within workflows to manage new and old versions, preventing information confusion. Numerical and boolean fields may require specific filtering or aggregation logic during retrieval, and workflow design must include corresponding processing nodes.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `Chunk size` (Chunk Size) | `500 characters` (500 characters) | Paragraphs in nursing management documents are often long; 500 characters balance completeness and retrieval granularity. |
| `Recall count` (Recall Count) | `Top 8 entries` (Top 8 entries) | Ensures coverage of multiple perspectives, especially when user queries involve several operational procedures. |
| `Similarity threshold` (Similarity Threshold) | `0.75` | For documents with extensive specialized terminology, a high threshold improves recall precision and reduces irrelevant information. |
| `maxContext` | `3000 tokens` | Ensures the model can process lengthy nursing process descriptions and background information. |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` (600 seconds) | Handles large PDF regulations or training manuals, preventing parsing timeouts. |
| `RECALL_STRATEGY` | `Hybrid Search` | Combines keyword and vector search to address diverse user queries, including precise and fuzzy searches. |

## Three Common Mistakes

*   Knowledge base query results are empty or inaccurate because knowledge base categorization is too broad, leading to relevant documents not being accurately indexed.
*   Code execution nodes in the workflow fail to retrieve knowledge base references because the knowledge base recall results were not correctly mapped to code variables during node configuration.
*   API-created workflows fail at runtime with a `Redis connection error` because the `REDIS_URL` environment variable is incorrectly configured or the Redis service is unreachable.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration

*   Upload typical nursing management documents, such as an operational procedure and a risk assessment form. Ensure document parsing completes without errors and content is fully displayed in the knowledge base preview.
*   Simulate user queries involving operational procedures, regulations, or common risk management issues. Verify the workflow recalls relevant document snippets and generates expected responses.
*   Check workflow logs to confirm all node execution statuses are successful, especially data synchronization and knowledge base update nodes, ensuring no timeouts or error messages.
*   For documents containing numerical fields, verify through queries that the workflow can correctly extract and interpret these values, for example, by querying the normal range for a specific indicator.
