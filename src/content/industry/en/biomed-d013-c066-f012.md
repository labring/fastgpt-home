# Model Integration and Configuration for Tender Bidding Quality Documents

## Data Characteristics for This Category

Tender bidding data in the biopharmaceutical industry primarily originates from centralized procurement platforms for drugs and consumables at various levels, public procurement information platforms of medical institutions, and internal bidding management systems of enterprises. Data update frequency depends on policy releases, procurement cycles, and market changes, typically quarterly or annually. However, some urgent procurement information may be released in real-time. Document structures are predominantly PDF, including tender announcements, procurement documents, product lists, technical parameters, commercial terms, quality standards, and compliance certificates. Fields include generic drug names, dosage forms, specifications, manufacturers, registration certificate numbers, batches, expiry dates, winning bid prices, procurement quantities, and quality inspection report numbers. Units strictly adhere to national pharmacopoeia and industry standards, such as milligrams (mg), milliliters (ml), tablets, vials, and boxes, with clear requirements for numerical precision.

## Constraints from These Characteristics on Model Integration and Configuration

Tender bidding documents combine highly structured and semi-structured data. This requires models to have high accuracy in text recognition and information extraction. For example, product lists and technical parameters in table format within PDF documents require the model to accurately identify rows and columns and extract corresponding fields. Frequent policy updates and procurement adjustments mean the knowledge base must support rapid content updates and version management to ensure the model responds based on the latest data. The extensive use of specialized terminology and units in documents challenges the model's vocabulary understanding and unit conversion capabilities, such as recognizing "10mg/tablet" and associating it with "10 milligrams per tablet." Additionally, compliance requirements mandate that the model precisely identify key regulatory clauses and certification information when processing quality standards and certification documents, avoiding misinterpretations or omissions.

## Configuration Recommendations

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `500 MB` | Tender bidding documents, especially quality certification files with many images and scanned copies, can have large individual file sizes. |
| `maxContext` | `8192 token` | Ensures the model can process longer procurement documents and detailed technical parameter descriptions. |
| `Chunk size` | `500 characters` | Balances semantic completeness of text and retrieval efficiency, avoiding the splitting of critical information. |
| `Recall count` | `Top 8 entries` | In tender bidding scenarios, more contextual information is typically needed for comprehensive judgment. |
| `Similarity threshold` | `0.75` | Improves recall precision, filtering out document segments irrelevant to the query. |
| `Rerank result count` | `3 entries` | From a high recall volume, re-ranking selects a small number of the most relevant pieces of information for output. |

## Common Pitfalls

*   Model answers contain inconsistent product specifications or pricing information. This typically results from outdated knowledge bases or the model failing to correctly identify the latest version when processing multiple document versions.
*   After uploading a large PDF document, the system displays a "file processing timeout" error. This may be due to `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` being set too low, not allowing enough time for document parsing and vectorization.
*   The model fails to recognize specific fields within tables in documents, such as "winning bidder" or "registration certificate number." This often occurs because the model lacks optimization for recognizing such semi-structured data during training or fine-tuning.

## Verification Steps

*   Upload a tender bidding PDF document containing complex tables and multiple pages. Check if the file processing status is "successful" and review the number of vectorized segments.
*   Query the model about specific drug names, specifications, and winning bid prices from the document. Verify if the model can accurately extract and output this information from the document and compare the output with the original document data.
*   Simulate a compliance review scenario by asking questions about product quality standards or certification qualifications. Confirm if the model can cite relevant clauses or certificate numbers from the document and manually verify if the citations are correct and complete.
