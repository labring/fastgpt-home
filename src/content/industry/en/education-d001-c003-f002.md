# Context and Token Budgets for Kindergarten Healthcare and Meal Management: Find Supporting Evidence

## What this category's data looks like

This page focuses on kindergarten health care and meal management. The materials include purchase orders with ingredient names and specifications, retention sample records, menus, disinfection records, and absence cause registrations. Purchase orders and menus are updated monthly, retention sample records are generated by meal batch, disinfection records are filled in by disinfection period, and absence cause classifications are summarized daily. When entering the business basis query, purchase orders and menus are used to check category consistency, retention sample records are used to trace back to batches and times, disinfection records are used to check periods, and absence cause registrations are used for classification verification. Each material plays the role of a source that can be traced back to the original text within the function, and answers need to point to specific entries.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This part is executed by application settings (response limit, etc.), which determine the length of a single answer and the range of context that can be accommodated. The basis entries that can be traced back to the original text are then completed by the model. Entry and authorization are handled by the publishing service and file permissions, while the business output is still answered by the model based on these.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Response limit | Matches the answer length required by the page; the slider position determines the range of context that can be accommodated in a single answer | Check the switch and slider position in application settings, and use `maxResponse` to mark this limit |
| Ingredient names and specifications | The categories in purchase orders and menus can be matched item by item, and specifications do not conflict with retention sample batches | Extract purchase order and menu entries for the same meal, compare ingredient names and specifications, and record the `quoteMaxToken` budget |
| Retention sample time and batch | The time and batch in retention sample records can be traced back to the corresponding documents | Take retention sample records and purchase orders by meal, check batch numbers and retention sample times, and record the number of array entries separately |
| Disinfection period | Disinfection records cover each disinfection period, and absence cause classifications do not contradict the records for the day | Take disinfection records and absence registrations by date, check whether the periods are continuous, and use `maxContext` to mark the context range |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: documents and retention sample batches do not match. Cause: purchase orders are summarized daily, retention sample records are generated by meal batch, and the two sets of numbers are not aligned, so the corresponding entry cannot be found when tracing back.
- Symptom: menus and purchased categories are inconsistent. Cause: menus are arranged weekly, purchase orders are recorded by actual arrival, and categories and specifications are misaligned in update rhythm, causing basis entries to contradict each other.
- Symptom: disinfection records are missing periods. Cause: disinfection records are filled in by disinfection period, absence cause classifications are summarized daily, and when a period is missing, it is impossible to determine whether disinfection covered that day.

## How to verify the setup

This function acceptance: ask a question that requires a long answer, see whether the ending is complete, and check whether `maxResponse` matches the answer length required by the page.

Business test: ask a basis question that spans materials, and see whether the answer gives a traceable source.

- Check the response limit switch and slider position in application settings, and confirm that they match the answer length required by the page.
- Extract the purchase order, menu, and retention sample record for the same meal, and check whether ingredient names, specifications, batches, and times can be traced back item by item.
