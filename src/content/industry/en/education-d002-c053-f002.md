# Context and Token Budgets for College Admissions Health Examination Organization: Compare Documents and Locate Differences

## What this category's data looks like

During the admissions season, college admissions physical examination organization centrally processes three types of materials: physical examination forms, re-examination applications, and restricted-major notifications. Physical examination forms come from various examination stations and are aggregated by batch; their fields include name, candidate number, physical examination conclusion, restriction code, date, and physician signature. Re-examination applications are submitted by candidates or middle schools; their fields include application reason, original examination date, re-examination items, and receipt. Restricted-major notifications are generated by the admissions office based on the conclusion; their fields include major name, restriction basis, notification date, and candidate acknowledgment. The three types of materials update at different rhythms: physical examination forms roll forward with batches, re-examination applications enter immediately on a case-by-case basis, and notifications are written back in bulk according to admission milestones. When materials enter the verification function, physical examination forms serve as the baseline ledger, while re-examination applications and notifications serve as objects to be verified, each providing name, quantity, date, and signature fields for item-by-item comparison and discrepancy localization.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This part is executed by application settings (response limit, etc.), which output the length of a single answer and the range of context that can be accommodated. The "discrepancy list" is then completed by the model. The response limit uses a slider to control the length of a single answer; if set too low, the answer will be truncated, and if set too high, consumption will increase. The context range is jointly defined by `maxContext` and `maxResponse`, and the quoted content budget is controlled separately by `quoteMaxToken`.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical examination form | The name, quantity, and date of the same record are consistent between the physical examination form and the re-examination application | Check name, candidate number, physical examination conclusion, restriction code, date, and physician signature, and compare field by field |
| Re-examination application | The application reason, original examination date, re-examination items, and receipt are complete, and the version is synchronized with the ledger | Check the application reason, original examination date, re-examination items, and receipt, and inspect for missing signatures or receipts |
| Restricted-major notification | The major name, restriction basis, notification date, and candidate acknowledgment are consistent with the conclusion | Check the major name, restriction basis, notification date, and acknowledgment, and confirm items whose versions are not synchronized |
| Context and token | The length of a single answer matches the range of context that can be accommodated and the page requirements | In application settings, check the response limit switch and slider position, and record `maxContext`, `quoteMaxToken`, and `maxResponse` |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: The name or candidate number of the same candidate does not match between the physical examination form and the re-examination application. Cause: The ledger fields were not used to backfill during entry, and the discrepancy was not localized.
- Symptom: The major name in the restricted-major notification is not synchronized with the version of the physical examination conclusion. Cause: The notification was generated in bulk based on the old conclusion and was not written back with the re-examination result.
- Symptom: The re-examination application lacks a signature or receipt, and the notification lacks candidate acknowledgment. Cause: Receipts were not fully collected at the material circulation nodes, and missing items were not marked during verification.

## How to verify the setup

This function's acceptance: ask a question that requires a long answer, see whether the ending is complete, confirm that the response limit matches `maxResponse` and the page requirements, and that the answer does not break off in the middle of a sentence.

Business test: use one real record to find fields inconsistent with the ledger, write them into the discrepancy list, and after confirmation by the reviewer, write them back.

- In application settings, check the response limit switch and slider position, and compare the usage changes between two runs
- Check the tokens of the quoted text against `quoteMaxToken`, record the number of array items separately, and do not compare it for equality with the budget
