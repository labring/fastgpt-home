# Knowledge Base Retrieval for Kindergarten Play Activity Organization: Handle Questions and Collect Missing Information

## What this category's data looks like

This page addresses the scenario of question acceptance and missing-item follow-up for kindergarten game activity organization. The materials involved include observation records, area plans, material placement lists, activity objectives, and reflection-to-next-week plans. The correspondence between observation records and area plans determines the judgment of activity objective achievement; material placement quantities are used to identify material shortages; reflection-to-next-week plans are used to detect plans that have not been updated. When these materials enter the knowledge base retrieval service, each serves as a candidate fragment source, participating in semantic or full-text recall, and after merging, re-ranking, filtering, and upper-limit truncation, forms the cited content. The update cadence is: observation records are added after each activity, area plans and material placement quantities are updated weekly, and reflection-to-next-week plans are summarized monthly. Field characteristics include activity name, area, objective, material name and quantity, observation points, reflection entries, and next-week adjustments. Acceptance records and the completed follow-up questions are generated by subsequent nodes or manually based on candidate fragments and similarity scores.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is executed by the knowledge base retrieval service (retrieval node and search test), outputting candidate fragments and similarity scores (after mode, merging, re-ranking, filtering, and upper-limit truncation). Acceptance records and the completed follow-up questions are then completed by subsequent nodes or manually.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation records | Correspond to area plans, and activity objective achievement has a basis | In the search test, test once each with synonymous questions, check whether the candidate fragments cover the observation points and objective entries, and record the number of candidates and scores |
| Material placement list | Material placement quantities match activity needs, and material shortages can be identified | Retrieve with questions about material names and quantities, compare candidate differences when `searchMode` is semantic, full-text, or hybrid, and see whether fragments with insufficient quantities are recalled |
| Reflection-to-next-week plan | Plans that have not been updated can be followed up on, and reflection entries enter the candidates | Retrieve with questions about reflections and next-week adjustments, compare the top few rankings before and after enabling `usingReRank`, and check changes in `similarity` scores |
| Area plan | When objectives and observations do not match, missing-item follow-up can be triggered | Test with a question missing key information to see whether it first asks a follow-up question before answering; check the `limit` number of cited texts and content completeness |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: the same question cannot be recalled when rephrased. Cause: question optimization does not cover coreference resolution and expansion, or the retrieval mode does not match the material form.
- Symptom: there are many candidates, but the ones ranked at the top are all irrelevant. Cause: after multi-path merging, no re-ranking secondary sort is performed, or the similarity filtering threshold does not match the citation upper-limit truncation.
- Symptom: the answer lacks key paragraphs, and material shortages are not identified. Cause: material placement quantity fragments do not enter the candidates, or the correspondence between observation records and area plans has not been updated.

## How to verify the setup

Feature acceptance: test once each with synonymously rewritten questions, compare candidates and scores, then toggle re-ranking on and off to inspect the ranking of the top few.

Business test: ask a question missing key information and see whether it first asks a follow-up question before answering.

- In the knowledge base search test, switch `searchMode` and compare the number of candidates and `similarity` scores across the semantic, full-text, and hybrid modes.
- Enable and disable `usingReRank`, record changes in the order of the top few; separately record the `limit` number, and check the tokens budget of the cited text.
