# Multi-Turn Conversation and Prompts for International Student Admissions and Enrollment Records: Draft Materials and Hand Over Responsibilities

## What this category's data looks like

In the international student admissions and enrollment management scenario, materials mainly consist of international student admission brochures, admission letters, and visa application forms. Admission brochures are usually drafted by the admissions office before project launch, and their fields include project name, training level, application deadline, fee description, and signature/seal position; admission letters are issued by the admissions review step after the admission decision, and their fields include student name, passport number, admitted major, enrollment date, and responsible person's signature; visa application forms are filled out with assistance from student affairs or the international office after admission, and their fields include personal information, study plan, proof of funds, and receipt column. The three types of materials have different update rhythms: brochures are adjusted by annual project, letters are generated by admission batch, and visa application forms change with embassy and consulate requirements. When materials enter the multi-turn dialogue and prompts feature, the model, based on the memory turns and prompt configuration in the application settings, refers to the prior context range and answer constraints to draft issuable drafts and handover records; external entry points and authorization are handled by the publishing service and file permissions. Field characteristics are concentrated in names, quantities, dates, signatures, and version numbers, and cross-material consistency is the core verification point.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is executed by the memory turns and prompt configuration in the application settings, and the output refers to the historical message range and answer constraints. "Issuable material drafts and handover records" are then completed by the model. Memory turns control how many previous rounds of dialogue can be referenced at most when answering; if the value is too small, the prior context cannot be used, and if the value is too large, irrelevant content is brought in and consumption increases.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International student admission brochure | Fields are complete: project name, training level, application deadline, fee description, signature/seal position; dates and signatures are aligned | Check the memory turns value in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive questions, and confirm that the second question can carry forward the reference from the first question; check whether the brochure fields in `messages` are complete |
| Admission letter | The name, quantity, and date of the same record are consistent between the brochure and the admission letter; version and signature are synchronized | Compare the usage records of two runs to confirm the consumption change after adjusting the turns; check the version number and signature column of the admission letter draft in the session corresponding to `chatId` |
| Visa application form | Personal information, study plan, proof of funds, and receipt column are complete; dates are consistent with the admission letter | Run a trial with two consecutive questions and confirm that the second question references the passport number and major from the first question; check whether the visa fields in `variables` are aligned with the admission letter |
| Handover record | The responsible person is clear, version and date are consistent, and missing items are blocked | Draft one according to the template and complete one handover, then check the receipt column and receipt; confirm that the memory turns match the prior context range |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: The same student's name spelling, passport number, or enrollment date does not match between the brochure and the admission letter. Cause: The two materials were drafted separately without sharing the same record fields, and the memory turns were too small, so the prior context information was not referenced.
- Symptom: The visa application form references the major name or deadline from an old version of the admission letter. Cause: The version was not synchronized, the prompt did not constrain consistency of the version number and date, and the model referenced outdated historical messages.
- Symptom: The handover record lacks a signature or receipt, and the next step cannot sign off. Cause: The answer constraints did not require outputting the signature column and receipt field, and the responsible person was not specified in the draft.

## How to verify the setup

Feature acceptance: Use two consecutive follow-up questions. The first question gives the student name and passport number, and the second question uses a reference to ask about the admitted major and enrollment date. See whether the second question carries forward the reference from the first question; check whether the historical message range and answer constraints in `messages` match the memory turns setting.

Business test: Draft one according to the template and complete one handover, and see whether missing items are blocked.

- Check the current memory turns value in the application settings and confirm that it matches the prior context range required by the question; compare the usage records of two runs to confirm the consumption change after adjusting the turns.
- Run a trial with two consecutive questions, and check whether the names, quantities, dates, and signatures of the material fields in `chatId`, `messages`, and `variables` are consistent, and whether missing items are blocked.
