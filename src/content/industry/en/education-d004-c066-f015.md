# Knowledge Base Retrieval for Initial Review of Academic Journal Submissions: Draft Materials and Hand Over Responsibilities

## What this category's data looks like

In the initial review stage of academic journal submissions, material drafting and responsibility handover revolve around three types of materials: submitted manuscripts, author information forms, and plagiarism check reports. Submitted manuscripts come from author submissions, and updates change with revised versions; fields include title, authors, affiliation, abstract, keywords, main text, and attachments. Author information forms are filled in by authors and checked by the editorial office; fields include name, order, affiliation, contribution statement, and signature. Plagiarism check reports are generated by the detection system; fields include detection date, duplication rate range, comparison sources, and receipt. When these three types of materials enter knowledge base retrieval and recall, they respectively serve the roles of source-text basis, responsible-party evidence, and compliance evidence, providing material for subsequent nodes to draft signable material drafts and handover records.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is executed by the knowledge base retrieval service (retrieval node and search test), outputting candidate chunks and similarity scores (after pattern, merge, re-rank, filter, and upper-limit trimming). Signable material drafts and handover records are then completed by subsequent nodes or manually.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submitted manuscript | Title, authors, affiliation, date, and signature must be consistent with the author information form | In the search test, retrieve with synonymous questions, check candidate chunks and scores, and record the number of candidates |
| Author information form | Name, order, affiliation, contribution statement, and signature must be consistent with the manuscript | Switch `searchMode` to semantic, full-text, or hybrid, and compare whether candidates cover both materials |
| Plagiarism check report | Detection date, duplication rate range, comparison sources, and receipt must be synchronized with the manuscript version | Compare the ranking of the top few results before and after enabling `usingReRank`, and check the quoted text after `similarity` filtering |
| Handover record | Responsible person, version, and date must align with the fields of the three types of materials | After trimming by `limit`, check the tokens of the quoted text, and record the number of array items separately |

## Three common errors

- Phenomenon: The name, affiliation, and date of the same record do not match between the manuscript and the author information form. Cause: Drafting was not based on the same version, and retrieval candidates were not compared field by field.
- Phenomenon: The plagiarism check report version is not synchronized, and the handover record still cites the old detection date. Cause: The version and date were not updated with the revision, and the receipt was not checked before handover.
- Phenomenon: The handover record lacks a signature or receipt, so the next step cannot be accepted. Cause: The responsible person and date fields were not fully filled in, and missing items in the drafting template were not blocked.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance of this function: test once each with synonymously rewritten questions, compare candidates and scores, then toggle re-ranking on and off to observe the ranking of the top few results, and record the number of candidates and scores.

Business test: draft one copy according to the template and complete one handover to see whether missing items are blocked.

- In the search test, switch retrieval modes and check whether candidates include both semantic and full-text results, and record the number of candidates and scores.
- Compare the order of the top few results before and after enabling re-ranking, and check whether the quoted text retains key chunks after similarity filtering and upper-limit trimming of quotes.
