# Conversation Records and Traceability for New Employee Onboarding and Training: Maintain Document Versions and Changes

## What this category's data looks like

This category of data for enterprise new-hire onboarding training involves three core types of materials: the onboarding training plan, the course sign-in sheet, and the job description. The onboarding training plan comes from the human resources department's annual plan and is updated by batch; its fields include course name, class hours, instructor, and date. The course sign-in sheet comes from the training site and is generated by session; its fields include trainee name, department, sign-in time, and signature column. The job description comes from the hiring department and is updated as positions are adjusted; its fields include position name, responsibilities, qualification requirements, effective date, and approval signature. When these three types of materials enter the conversation logs and audit function, they serve respectively as conversation reference sources: the training plan corresponds to course arrangement Q&A, the sign-in sheet corresponds to attendance confirmation Q&A, and the job description corresponds to responsibility clarification Q&A. In terms of update cadence, the training plan is adjusted quarterly, the sign-in sheet is archived by session, and the job description changes with organizational changes. Field characteristics require that the name, quantity, date, and signature of the same record remain consistent across the plan, sign-in sheet, and description, that the version number and reference are updated in sync, and that old versions remain traceable.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is performed by the conversation record interface (by application and session dimension), which outputs the session message list and total record count, including role and reference information. The "version ledger and change records" are then completed manually or by an external system.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding training plan | Course name, class hours, instructor, and date are consistent with the fields in the sign-in sheet and job description | Pull pages by application and session dimension, verify the `chatId` source, and compare the total record count total with response `code` of 200 |
| Course sign-in sheet | Trainee name, department, sign-in time, and signature column match the training plan roster | Open a single record to verify the role field and reference information, and check the correspondence of `dataId` |
| Job description | Position name, responsibilities, qualification requirements, effective date, and approval signature are in sync with the plan reference | Sample one record to verify the reference and elapsed-time fields in `value`, and confirm that the version number and reference are updated in sync |
| Conversation record interface | The total record count is the number of message records, distinct from the business material version ledger | Pull two pages in succession to compare the offset and total count, and confirm that each message has an identified role and the reference fields are complete |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: the quantity for the same trainee name does not match between the training plan and the sign-in sheet. Cause: the two materials are registered separately, and cross-checking by the same record key is not performed, resulting in misalignment of names and dates.
- Symptom: the job description has been updated, but the training plan reference still points to the old version. Cause: the version is not synchronized, the reference relationship is not refreshed as the content changes, and although the old version is traceable, the current version is not unique.
- Symptom: the sign-in sheet lacks a signature or receipt, making attendance impossible to confirm. Cause: after on-site registration, the signature column is not returned, so during audit only message records can be seen, and the business ledger lacks evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance of this function: page through using the total record count total to retrieve all records, sample one record to verify the role, reference information, and time, and confirm response `code` of 200 and that `_id`, `dataId` and `value` are queryable.

Business test: change one piece of content, see whether the version number and reference are updated in sync, and compare the name, quantity, date, and signature in the training plan, sign-in sheet, and job description.

- Pull pages by application and session dimension, pull two pages in succession to compare the offset and total record count, and confirm that the role field matches the original conversation and the reference and elapsed-time fields are complete.
- Open a single record to verify the number of references and the reference text tokens; record the array count separately, and do not compare the number of references with the reference content budget as equal.
