# Conversation Records and Traceability for Corporate Compliance and Safety Training: Track Progress and Check Statistics

## What this category's data looks like

Enterprise compliance and security training involves three types of materials: compliance training courseware, security operating procedures, and exam records. Courseware is iterated according to the pace of regulatory updates, containing chapter numbers and effective dates; procedures are managed by position and equipment version, containing operating steps and signature fields; exam records are generated by session, containing personnel, scores, and receipts. When these three types of materials enter the conversation logs and auditing feature, courseware and procedures are cited as knowledge sources on the question side, and exam records are retrieved as verification evidence on the answer side. Field characteristics center on four items: date, name, quantity, and signature; the update cadence is quarterly for courseware, semi-annual for procedures, and per session for exams. The role of the materials is an evidence source, not the ledger itself; progress tables and statistical criteria must be registered based on them manually or by an external system.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is executed by the conversation record interface (by application and session dimension), which outputs a session message list and the total number of records, including role and citation information. The "progress table and statistical criteria description" is then completed manually or by an external system. The interface only retrieves conversation evidence and does not write business ledgers; the number of messages and the number of sessions are not the same quantity, and the total number of records is the number of message records.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance training courseware | Whether the name, effective date, chapter number, and signature field are consistent with the exam records | Pull the session message list, locate the session by `chatId`, and verify the courseware name and date in the cited text |
| Security operating procedures | Whether the version number, operating steps, quantity, and receipt match the courseware | Page through to retrieve all records, compare the citation information in `dataId` and `value`, and confirm the versions are in sync |
| Exam records | Whether personnel, session, score, date, and signature are consistent with the courseware and procedures | Page through to retrieve all records by the total record count total, sample one record to verify the role, citation information, and time; response `code` of 200 is normal |
| Conversation record interface | The number of records matches the total, and each message has an identified role | Pull two consecutive pages and compare the offset and total, open one record and verify the citation and elapsed-time fields |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: The name, quantity, and date of the same record do not match between the courseware and the exam records. Cause: The two materials are registered separately and are not verified against the same criteria.
- Symptom: The procedure version is not in sync, and old-version steps are still cited. Cause: After the courseware is updated, it is not synced to the procedures, and the cited text points to the old version.
- Symptom: Exam records lack a signature or receipt. Cause: Signatures were not fully collected at registration, and the exported records lack citation fields.

## How to verify the setup

Feature acceptance: Page through to retrieve all records by the total record count total, sample one record to verify the role, citation information, and time; response `code` of 200 is normal; verify the message identifiers and content fields `_id`, `dataId` and `value`.

Business testing: Use one week of records to verify the totals, and check whether the criteria and exceptions are clearly stated; the number of citations is the array length, `quoteMaxToken` is the citation content budget, and the two are not compared for equality; if a citation upper limit is involved, check the tokens of the cited text, and record the array count separately.

- Pull records by application and session identifier, pull two consecutive pages and compare the offset and total record count, and confirm that the pagination parameters match the total.
- Open one record and verify the role field, citations, and elapsed time, confirm that message roles correspond to the original conversation, and retain citation information when exporting.
