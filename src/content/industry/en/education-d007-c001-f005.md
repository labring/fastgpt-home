# Multi-Turn Conversation and Prompts for Daily Kindergarten Care Management: Trace Exceptions and Review Results

## What this category's data looks like

This page focuses on exception tracing and manual review in kindergarten childcare daily life management. The materials come from three types of records generated by the daily operation of the kindergarten: daily life schedule, morning and afternoon health check records, and child health observation records. The daily life schedule provides the timeline and activity framework, the morning and afternoon health check records carry the health status at entry and noon, and the child health observation records supplement all-day behavior and emotional clues. The update rhythm is daily filling, weekly summary, and monthly archiving. Field characteristics include child name, date, activity segment, health indicators, exception description, and signature field. When the materials enter the multi-turn dialogue and prompt function, the daily life schedule serves as the time baseline, and the morning and afternoon health check records and child health observation records serve as the exception evidence chain. Together, the three support the generation of the exception list and review conclusions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This step is executed by the memory turns and prompt configuration in the application settings, outputting the reference historical message range and answer constraints. The exception list and review conclusions are then completed by the model. Memory turns control how many previous dialogue turns are referenced at most when answering. If the value is too small, the preceding text cannot be used; if the value is too large, irrelevant content is brought in and consumption increases.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily life schedule | Whether the names, dates, and signatures are consistent with the morning and afternoon health check records and child health observation records | Compare the activity segment names and dates item by item, check whether the signature field is complete, and confirm version synchronization |
| Morning and afternoon health check records | Whether the quantities, dates, and signatures match the daily life schedule and child health observation records | Check the child names and exception quantities, check date continuity, and confirm missing signatures or receipt status |
| Child health observation records | Whether the names, quantities, and dates are consistent with the daily life schedule and morning and afternoon health check records | Compare exception descriptions with the original records, check signatures and receipts, and confirm version non-synchronization issues |
| Memory turns | Whether it matches the preceding text range required by the question | Check the current value in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive question turns, and compare the usage records of the two runs |

## Three common errors

- Phenomenon: The names, quantities, and dates of the same record do not match between the daily life schedule and the morning and afternoon health check records. Cause: The two materials are filled in by different personnel at different times, lacking a cross-check step.
- Phenomenon: The version of the child health observation records is not synchronized, and the old and new versions exist at the same time. Cause: After updating, they were not uniformly archived, and the multi-turn dialogue referenced expired materials.
- Phenomenon: The morning and afternoon health check records lack signatures or receipts, and the responsible person cannot be confirmed during exception tracing. Cause: The signature process is not closed-loop, and the review step does not enforce verification of the signature field.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance of this function: Ask two consecutive follow-up questions, see whether the second question inherits the reference from the first question, and check whether the references to `chatId`, `messages`, and `variables` within the historical message range are accurate.

Business test: Create an exception record, see whether the source material can be traced, and confirm that the exception is supported by original records and the reviewer's signature.

- Check the current value of memory turns in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive question turns, and compare the usage records of the two runs.
- Compare the fields, dates, and signatures of the daily life schedule, morning and afternoon health check records, and child health observation records item by item, and confirm that the exception list and review conclusions are supported by original records.
