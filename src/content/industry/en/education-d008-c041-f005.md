# Multi-Turn Conversation and Prompts for Corporate Compliance and Safety Training: Manage Authorized Access and External Services

## What this category's data looks like

Materials in the enterprise compliance and security training category include three types: compliance training courseware, security operating procedures, and exam records. Courseware comes from the compliance department and is revised according to the pace of regulatory updates; its fields include course name, applicable position, effective date, version number, and signature field. Procedures come from the security management department; their fields include operating steps, risk level, issuer, and receipt date. Exam records come from the training system; their fields include examinee, exam paper name, score, exam date, and invigilator signature. When these three types of materials enter the authorized access and external services function, they play different roles: courseware and procedures serve as externally visible controlled documents, determining visitor identity and visible scope; exam records serve as restricted materials, open only to parents or superiors, and are not visible beyond authorization. In terms of update cadence, courseware is synchronized with regulatory changes, procedures are reviewed quarterly, and exam records are generated by session. Field characteristics require that the name, quantity, and date of the same record in courseware, procedures, and exam records must be consistent, version numbers synchronized, and signatures or receipts complete; otherwise, the authorization scope and visible scope of the external entrance will deviate.

## Configuration and implementation checks

This part is executed by the memory turns and prompt configuration in the application settings, outputting the referenced historical message range and answer constraints. The externally available entrance and authorization scope are determined by the publishing service and file permissions; the model only processes filtered materials, while the publishing service and file permissions handle identity and file authentication. Memory turns control how many previous dialogue turns can be referenced at most when answering; if the value is too small, prior context cannot be used, and if the value is too large, irrelevant content is brought in and consumption increases.

| Object | Check | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance training courseware | The courseware name, applicable position, effective date, and version number are consistent between the courseware and exam records, and the signature field is complete | Check the memory turns value in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive questions, and check whether the second round inherits the reference from the first round |
| Security operating procedures | The operating steps, risk level, issuer, and receipt date are consistent between the procedures and the receipt, and are not visible when the version is not synchronized | Access once with a restricted identity, see whether the visible scope matches the setting, and check the session record corresponding to `chatId` |
| Exam records | The examinee, exam paper name, score, and exam date are consistent between the exam records and the receipt, and are not visible beyond authorization when the signature or receipt is missing | Compare the usage records of two runs and check whether the fields in `messages` and `variables` are consistent with the materials |
| Memory turns | Matches the prior context range required by the question; if the value is too small, prior context cannot be used, and if the value is too large, irrelevant content is brought in and consumption increases | Check the current value in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive questions, and compare the usage records of two runs |

## Three common errors

- Symptom: The name, quantity, and date of the same record do not match between the courseware and exam records. Cause: The two materials were not synchronized to the same version, and the field sources differ.
- Symptom: The procedure version is not synchronized, and the external entrance still displays the old version. Cause: The publishing service and file permissions were not updated with the version, and the authorization scope was not recalculated.
- Symptom: The exam record lacks a signature or receipt but is still open to parents or superiors. Cause: The restricted identity and visible scope matching were not verified, resulting in visibility beyond authorization.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance for this function: ask two consecutive follow-up questions, see whether the second question inherits the reference from the first question, and check the historical range and answer constraints in `chatId`, `messages`, and `variables`.

Business test: access once with a restricted identity, see whether the visible scope matches the setting, ensure it is not visible beyond authorization, and check whether the material fields, dates, and signatures are consistent.

- Check the current memory turns value in the application settings, run a trial with two consecutive questions, and compare the usage records of two runs.
- Access the external entrance once with a restricted identity, and check whether the names, quantities, dates, versions, and signature receipts of the courseware, procedures, and exam records are consistent.
