# Petition Matter Handling: Service Consultation and Reply - Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
This category’s data primarily originates from the national petition information system, local petition systems, the 12345 citizen hotline platform, grassroots governance platforms, and historical petition archives. Data is updated daily.
Daily documents include petition registration forms, acceptance notices, transfer and assignment notices, handling opinions, review and re-examination applications, and resolution plans.
Data fields cover petitioner information, appeal content, acceptance numbers, handling process nodes, and more. All content is sourced from public business specifications, with no fictional statistical values.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Multi-source data sources require forms to support cross-system data import and export, to ensure data consistency across different platforms.
Daily update frequency requires daily calibration of form synchronization mechanisms, to prevent outdated data from affecting handling progress.
Multi-type document structures require forms to support dynamic field switching, to match document formats for different handling stages.
Statutory handling procedures require complete operation logs to be retained during interaction steps, to meet traceability needs for review and re-examination.
Pre-emptive appeal screening requires form validation rules to trigger before submission, to reduce the risk of duplicate or unauthorized hierarchy petition requests.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- When configuring `formInput`, fields are empty after importing cross-system data. Cause: Field mapping does not follow the standard format of the data source, leading to incorrect parsing of cross-system data.
- After selecting a business entity via the `userSelect` component, subsequent handling processes do not trigger. Cause: Correct event callbacks are not bound, leading to interrupted interaction links.
- After configuring a third-party data endpoint, the corresponding configuration information is not displayed in the interface. Cause: The configuration synchronization switch is not enabled, leading to endpoint information not being synchronized to the interaction interface.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Import standard petition test data, check whether the `formInput` fields cover the preset business content, and adjust mapping rules until they match.
- Select preset business entity options, verify that the interaction process proceeds normally according to the configuration, and adjust event bindings until the link is complete.
- After configuring a third-party data endpoint, check whether the corresponding configuration information is displayed synchronously in the interaction interface, and enable the synchronization switch until the information displays normally.
- Submit a test form, confirm that the validation rules and log retention function trigger normally, and adjust the trigger timing until it meets business requirements.
