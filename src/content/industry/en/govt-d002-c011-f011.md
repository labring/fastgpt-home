# Service Inquiry and Reply for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category includes
Data for this category is sourced from social insurance management systems, personal benefit records, labor relations and work injury identification systems, and the electronic social security card platform. The update frequency is daily. Associated daily documents include benefit application forms, payment duration certificates, retirement approval forms, work injury recognition materials, and unemployment insurance benefit application materials. Data fields cover payment duration, benefit calculation standards, qualification review criteria, and more. Units include months, yuan, and person-times. Most document structures combine structured forms and supporting materials. Cross-domain data from multiple systems is required to complete verification.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Multiple cross-domain data sources require the form interaction link to support field linkage verification across multiple data sources, to prevent inconsistencies across systems. Daily update frequency means the interaction interface must pull the latest personal benefit records and benefit calculation parameters in real time, to ensure the accuracy of inquiry and reply results. Daily documents include structured forms and supporting materials, so forms must support segmented uploads and required field verification, and match the dedicated field requirements of different benefit types. The professional nature of qualification criteria and benefit standard fields requires the interaction link to provide clear field descriptions and compliance prompts, to reduce user input errors.

## Three common misconfigurations
- A `quote type error` appears in the debug preview interface, with a prompt indicating abnormal variable format. The cause is that variable references are not configured according to the field formats specified in business documents, and the correct reference rules for fields such as payment duration and benefit standards are not followed.
- Form nodes cannot be reused by other business links, and cross-branch jump calls are not supported. The cause is that the node reuse rules for `userSelect` are not correctly configured, only single-link binding is set, and cross-branch call permissions are not enabled.
- After enabling input guidance and configuring a custom thesaurus, the debug interface shows an error or no response. The cause is that the custom thesaurus content does not match the standard terminology in business documents, causing thesaurus validation to fail and triggering an abnormal interrupt.
