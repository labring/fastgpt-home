# Service Consultation and Response for Medical Institution Supervision and Public Health Inspection: Document Parsing and Chunking

## Data Overview for This Category
Data for this category primarily comes from health supervision information systems, medical institution electronic registration systems, complaint and reporting platforms, and law enforcement inspection records, with a monthly update frequency. Commonly processed documents include supervision inspection forms, medical institution practice licenses, rectification notices, supervision opinions, penalty case files, and others. Documents include structured fields such as institution qualification numbers, inspection times, rectification requirements, and penalty bases. Some on-site inspection records are unstructured text without fixed layout formats.

## Constraints on Document Parsing and Chunking
Monthly batch-updated documents include structured license certificates and unstructured on-site records. Parsing tools must support mixed-format documents. Unstructured on-site inspection records have no fixed layout. Staff must accurately identify core field boundaries to avoid information omission. Penalty case files include multi-page related materials. Chunking processes must retain law enforcement event association logic between documents to prevent inability to associate the same item after splitting. Data errors may trigger practice risks or penalty deviations. Chunking processes must ensure key information such as qualification numbers and rectification requirements remains complete.

## Configuration Settings
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the paragraph length of documents such as penalty case files and rectification notices, and retains the integrity of core law enforcement information |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by paragraph + title | Matches structured titles such as supervision opinions and rectification requirements in documents, avoiding information fragmentation caused by cross-title splitting |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Compatible with PDF documents such as medical institution practice licenses with stamps and fixed formats, to accurately extract text content |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 100 MB | Covers the upload requirements of single multi-page penalty case files and batch inspection records, preventing large files from being blocked |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 600 seconds | Adapts to the time consumption of batch document parsing, preventing timeout failures of large-volume law enforcement record parsing tasks |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Custom mode | Allows adjusting chunking rules for different document types such as inspection forms and penalty case files, adapting to the mixed document structure of this category |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The symptom is that the chunk ID cannot be copied on the knowledge base page. The cause is that the copy interaction component is not configured in the default interface, and only static text content is displayed.
- The symptom is that document chunks after custom chunking are automatically deleted after being stored in the knowledge base, leading to disorder of the preset index order. The cause is that the system enables document chunk deduplication logic by default, and the setting has not been adjusted for the need to retain the law enforcement event association order of this category.
- The symptom is that the table format of on-site inspection records is lost in the parsing results, and is scattered into unstructured paragraphs. The cause is that custom parsing rules for structured inspection forms are not configured, and the default parsing logic cannot recognize table-structured content.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Upload a single medical institution practice license PDF, and verify that the parsed text includes complete core fields such as institution qualification number and validity period.
- Upload a multi-page penalty case file, confirm that adjacent document chunks after chunking belong to related content of the same law enforcement event, and no cross-event splitting occurs.
- View the knowledge base upload task log, confirm that the uploaded file does not exceed the set size limit, and no timeout error occurs in the parsing task.
- Test the custom chunking rules, verify that duplicate document chunks are retained or deduplicated according to business needs, and match the preset index order requirements.
