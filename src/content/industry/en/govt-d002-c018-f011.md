# Service Consultation and Reply for Social Insurance Registration and Payment Verification: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from social insurance management systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and public security and civil affairs shared data.
The update frequency for this data is daily.
Daily processing includes documents such as social insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, staff addition and removal forms, social insurance premium verification notices, and personal insurance benefit records.
Fields covered include personnel identity identifiers, labor relationship certificates, payment bases, and the number of insured persons, among other items.
Basic measurement units include yuan and individuals.
Data links to cross-department shared subject and personnel information.

## Constraints on forms and interactions
Forms must support multi-source information linkage verification. This prevents cross-system data inconsistency, per cross-department data source requirements.
Forms must pull the latest insurance registration and payment data in real time after submission. This ensures verification results match the current day’s system status, per the daily update frequency requirement.
Forms must split modules by business scenario. This avoids excessive fields on a single page that lead to operational errors, per field coverage across multiple business documents.
Forms must include built-in identity verification steps. This reduces manual verification time and errors, per data linked to identity and labor relationship information.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: A 400 error is returned after entering specific social insurance-related consultation content. Cause: No field validation rules are configured for `formInput`, which causes the model to trigger invalid parameters.
- Phenomenon: Entering "what about 350" returns results unrelated to social insurance registration and payment. Cause: No question optimization rules for social insurance professional consultations are configured, which causes the model to deviate from the business scope.
- Phenomenon: Users cannot select the preset insured personnel list. Cause: Unit insured personnel data is not synchronized to the `userSelect` option library, resulting in empty options.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Submit test content that includes personnel identity information. Check whether `formInput` automatically populates associated fields.
- Select preset unit insured personnel options. Confirm that `userSelect` options match the unit’s insured personnel list.
- Enter social insurance registration and payment-related consultations. Check that the number of returned results aligns with the single search limit setting.
- Upload a test image and enter relevant consultation content. Confirm that the correct multimodal processing logic is triggered.
