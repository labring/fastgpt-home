# Government Centralized Payment and Reimbursement Audit Inquiry and Response: Vector Models and Indexes

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from government centralized payment systems, unified budget management systems, electronic invoice platforms, and contract management systems, with a daily update frequency. Daily processed documents include payment applications, fund use plans, invoices, contracts, acceptance receipts, reimbursement approval forms, and payment vouchers. Data fields include budget indicator codes, economic classification codes, single payment amounts, approval workflow nodes, contract numbers, invoice verification codes, and more. Most individual documents are structured and semi-structured business forms.

## Constraints on vector models and index links
The data characteristics of this category impose clear constraints on the vector model and index link. Multi-source and scattered data sources require indexes to support unified access and association of cross-system data. The daily update frequency requires adaptation to incremental index update logic to avoid resource consumption from full reindexing. Mixed structured and semi-structured business documents require vector models to support field-level semantic encoding. Strongly related business fields such as budget indicators and payment amounts require that vector recall can accurately match business association relationships to support subsequent compliance audits.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A call error occurs when deploying and configuring the vector model. The interface displays timeout or invalid parameters. Cause: Correct keys and access addresses are not configured for cloud vector model interface authentication, or the model's input format requirements are not matched.
- Phenomenon: Multi-system business document formats cannot be recognized when adding indexes. The index status remains failed. Cause: Chunk rules adapted to the mixed structured and semi-structured documents of this category are not configured, leading to chunking failure.
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant historical payment vouchers appear in recall results, with insufficient matching accuracy. Cause: The similarity threshold is set unreasonably, or field-level vector encoding configuration is not enabled, leading to semantic matching deviation.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Upload a single business document of this category, such as a reimbursement approval form, and verify that chunked content covers complete business units without obvious semantic fragmentation.
- Initiate a simulated payment audit inquiry, and verify that recalled results include associated fields such as matching budget indicators and contract numbers.
- Review index update logs to confirm that daily incremental update tasks execute normally without abnormal full reindexing prompts.
- Test the vector model call interface to confirm that returned vector data formats match platform requirements, with no parameter overlimit errors.
