# Service Consultation and Reply for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data is sourced from the enterprise registration fully electronic system, the national enterprise credit information publicity system, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database, with a daily update frequency. Daily associated documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and more. Data fields include entity name, residence address, shareholder identity information, license type, processing status, and others. Each single piece of data is associated with multiple supporting documents, and no additional fictional statistical values are included.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Multiple data sources and daily updates require that the latest data from the corresponding systems must be called synchronously during multi-turn dialogue to verify consultation content, and avoid citing expired information. There are various types of associated documents, so it is necessary to clearly guide submitters to provide the core fields of the corresponding documents during dialogue, and avoid confusing the information boundaries of different documents. The fields include specific content related to subject qualification and business access, so prompts must limit the scope of dialogue to the registration and licensing processing stages, and not extend to other matters. At the same time, it is necessary to adapt to high-frequency consultation scenarios such as verifying names, residences, and shareholder materials, and guide submitters to provide information step by step according to the processing flow.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Context | 1200–1500 previous characters | This category has multiple associated document types, so core consultation and reply content must be retained to avoid context overflow affecting reply accuracy |
| Question Optimization | Enabled, calibrated based on actual testing | Convert scattered colloquial consultations into standard registration and licensing business terminology, adapting to high-frequency consultation scenarios |
| `chatId` | Bound to the unified social credit code identifier of the market entity | Distinguish the consultation history of different entities, and avoid confusing the processing progress of different submitters |
| `variables` | Preset common field variables for registration and licensing | Quickly call residence information database and enterprise credit information data to fill in dialogue context |
| Single Reply Limit | Top 3 business guidelines | Consultations in this category mostly involve step-by-step guidelines, so avoid outputting excessive redundant content that causes submitter confusion |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant designated reply plugin content appears in the dialogue, causing the reply to deviate from registration and licensing business. Cause: The historical records generated by the plugin are not filtered in `messages`, causing non-business content to be mixed into the context.
- Phenomenon: Newly deployed systems cannot start a dialogue, and the interface prompts a key configuration error. Cause: The FastGPT call key is not correctly bound on the system configuration page, resulting in missing API call permissions.
- Phenomenon: A version update description pops up every time a new conversation is opened, interfering with normal consultation processes. Cause: The configuration item for automatically popping up update prompts when starting a new conversation is not disabled in the system settings.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Initiate a test consultation for name verification, and check that the dialogue context only contains registration and licensing related content, with no irrelevant plugin records.
- Call the `variables` preset variables, verify that the corresponding data from the residence information database can be quickly retrieved to complete residence address verification.
- Switch different test entity identifiers, confirm that after `chatId` is bound, the chat records of different entities do not interfere with each other.
- Check the system settings, confirm that the configuration item for automatically popping up update prompts when starting a new conversation is disabled.

The configuration values listed above are common starting points, and should be evaluated against available local samples.
