# Administrative Registration and Licensing for Market Entities: Service Consultation and Reply - Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category comes from the full electronic enterprise registration system, the national enterprise credit information public disclosure system, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database, and is updated daily. Commonly processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and more. Fields cover subject qualification information, residence addresses, license types, shareholder identity information, and other categories. Each business scenario uses a dedicated field combination, and there is no universal cross-category fixed field structure.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Forms and Interactions
Daily data updates require form interactions to support real-time pulling of the latest verification data, such as enterprise name duplicate checks and residence information matching, to avoid registration rejection caused by outdated information. Multiple types of business documents require forms to distinguish scenarios including establishment, change, and license applications, and automatically adapt to the corresponding field groups to reduce manual configuration errors. Name verification, residence verification, and shareholder material review are the most time-consuming steps. Interactions must add real-time verification feedback to reduce the number of user corrections. Cross-system data synchronization must also be supported, to ensure that submitted form data can be synced to the full electronic enterprise registration system for follow-up processes.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` | Support bulk upload of multiple documents and automatic field extraction | This category needs to process multiple types of business documents such as establishment registration applications and articles of association, and bulk upload can improve processing efficiency |
| `userSelect` | Preset business scenario options (establishment/change/license) and bind corresponding field groups | Field differences vary significantly across different business scenarios, preset options can reduce user configuration errors |
| Real-time verification trigger delay | 800–1200 milliseconds | Avoid frequent verification interfering with user input, while providing timely verification feedback |
| Single document upload limit | 50 MB | Matches the conventional size range of business documents for this category |
| Bulk upload document count limit | Up to 10 | Balances single-processing efficiency and system load |
| Error prompt copy | Use business-specific terminology, such as "Duplicate name, please change" | Improve user understanding efficiency for the business scenario |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A "fields not matched" error is returned after form submission. Cause: Uploaded articles of association, shareholder resolutions, and other documents are not bound to the field mapping rules configured in `formInput`, causing the system to fail to extract corresponding business data.
- Phenomenon: Form fields do not switch synchronously after selecting a business scenario. Cause: The association relationship between `userSelect` options and field groups is not configured correctly, leading to incorrect verification logic being triggered.
- Phenomenon: Uploaded residence certificates and other documents are not used for consultation and reply. Cause: Upload data from `formInput` is not connected to the context window, causing the large language model to fail to read business document content.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Upload multiple types of business documents such as establishment registration applications and articles of association, and check whether corresponding business fields are automatically extracted.
- Select different business scenario options, and check whether the form field group switches synchronously.
- Enter an enterprise name, and check whether real-time verification is triggered and corresponding prompts are returned.
- Submit test data, and check whether the preset business verification process is triggered.
