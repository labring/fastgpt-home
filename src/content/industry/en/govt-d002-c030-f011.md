# Construction Engineering Quality and Safety Supervision: Service Consultation and Reply - Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
The service subjects for this category are housing and urban-rural development quality and safety supervision stations, construction units, supervision units, and construction units. Data comes from the engineering quality and safety supervision system, on-site real-name management platform, supervision logs, and test reports. The update frequency is weekly. Routine documents include supervision plans, supervision inspection records, hidden danger rectification notices, quality problem handling forms, and completion acceptance supervision reports. Fields cover supervision subjects, project information, inspection items, rectification requirements, acceptance conclusions, and other content. These records only document on-site and material information according to business processes, with no additional custom statistical values.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Data sources are scattered and updated weekly. Forms must support associated calls for multi-source data to avoid duplicate entry. Diverse document types require forms to adapt to field switching for different business scenarios. For example, call inspection record fields during supervision inspections, and hidden danger notice fields during rectification closure. Weekly data updates require form context caching to synchronize the latest data regularly, preventing expired information from being called. The on-site inspection link requires quick access to test reports and supervision logs. Forms must support fast retrieval and loading of large text content to avoid interaction lag. Missing core business fields can lead to quality or safety accidents. Required field validation must cover key content such as rectification requirements and acceptance conclusions to ensure process compliance.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: The number of retrieved results exceeds expectations, including a large number of non-core business materials. Cause: No reasonable single retrieval limit is set, and no restrictions are imposed to only call specified category documents such as supervision inspection records and rectification notices.
- Phenomenon: A prompt of receiving message address verification failure appears after form submission. Cause: No publicly accessible interaction callback address is configured, and link legitimacy verification is not completed.
- Phenomenon: The large model output content mixes redundant information unrelated to business. Cause: Retrieval results are not filtered according to business scenarios, and no restrictions are imposed to only use the top 2 core documents as background knowledge.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Submit a test form, switch between different business document templates, and confirm that form fields are automatically updated with the template.
- Trigger a retrieval operation, and check whether the returned document types match the current business scenario.
- Simulate the rectification closure process, and check whether a validation prompt is triggered when required fields are empty.
- Test long document loading, and confirm that there is no obvious lag during the interaction process, and the loading speed meets business expectations.
