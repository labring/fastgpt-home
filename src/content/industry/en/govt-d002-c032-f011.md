# Service Consultation and Response for Investment Project Approval and Post-Evaluation: Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category centers on the full lifecycle of investment projects. Typical sources include state-owned assets supervision investment management systems, enterprise ERP and project management systems, industry market data, audit and financial statements. Data is updated quarterly. Daily associated documents cover annual investment plans, project proposals, feasibility study reports, due diligence reports, asset appraisal reports, post-investment evaluation reports, and more. Fields cover core business information such as project number, investment amount, construction period, risk control opinions, and others. The main participating entities are central enterprise strategic investment departments, project companies, state-owned assets supervision departments, and external consulting and evaluation institutions. Business processes must align with specific requirements for central enterprise board investment decisions, negative lists, and post-investment evaluation.

## Constraints on Forms and Interactions from These Characteristics
The data characteristics of this category directly constrain the design logic of forms and interactions. The need for multi-source data synchronization requires forms to support cross-system field association, to avoid errors caused by manual entry. The quarterly update frequency requires that the form submission and data synchronization rhythm matches the quarterly cycle, and high-frequency submission restrictions should not be set. The field differences across multiple types of documents require forms to flexibly configure exclusive fields for different business stages. For example, the approval stage requires a feasibility study approval document number, while the post-evaluation stage requires post-launch operation data. Strict business compliance requirements mean that forms must include built-in role permission verification, allowing only specified entities to submit or modify data. At the same time, required field verification and risk control prompts must be added to prevent state-owned asset losses or accountability due to missing information. Differences from government investment approval processes require forms to adapt to specific links of central enterprise board investment decisions, and do not need to include exclusive approval fields for government ends.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: The rearrangement function cannot select a model adapted to specified industry reports, and the interface only displays general model options. Cause: The model adaptation rules associated with `formInput` were not configured based on the business document characteristics of this category, resulting in the model selection range not covering the text processing requirements of industry reports.
- Issue: After submitting the form, a prompt indicating invalid username appears, and identity verification cannot be completed. Cause: The role range configured in `userSelect` does not include the entity type to which the current user belongs, and the system verification logic does not match the participating entity list for this category.
- Issue: More than 10 minutes pass after form submission, and the synchronization success status is still not displayed. Cause: A reasonable timeout period was not set, and cross-system data retrieval time exceeded the default threshold, resulting in unhandled request timeouts.
