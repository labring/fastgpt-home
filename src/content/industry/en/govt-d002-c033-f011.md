# Bidding and Procurement Compliance Management: Service Consultation and Reply - Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for this category comes from central state-owned enterprise (SOE) and public sector procurement platforms, public resource trading platforms, supplier databases, contract management systems, and audit issue repositories, and is updated daily. Daily documents include procurement plans, requirement specifications, bidding documents, bid evaluation records, contracts, acceptance documents, and supplier complaint materials. Fields cover procurement methods, budget amounts, supplier qualifications, scoring rules, performance clauses, and similar content. There is no fixed unified unit, and the field format must match the corresponding business documents.

## What constraints these characteristics place on forms and interactions
The data for this category comes from scattered sources and is updated daily, requiring form interactions to support rapid retrieval and verification of multi-source data. There are diverse document types, so the system must adapt to field extraction and matching rules for different documents. Sensitive content related to compliance reviews requires data permission isolation during the interaction process. It is necessary to connect to business systems of multiple departments such as procurement and audit, so forms must support cross-system data linkage. The consequences of errors include bid rigging and audit rectification, so pre-compliance check prompts must be added during the interaction process.

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: No matching content is returned after submitting a `formInput` field. Cause: The field validation rules for the corresponding procurement documents are not configured, so the input content cannot trigger data recall.
- Symptom: After calling a node in the workflow, the final output contains unintended intermediate step content. Cause: The output switch of intermediate nodes is not turned off, causing redundant content to be included in the final result.
- Symptom: A timeout error occurs during form interaction. Cause: The timeout threshold is set too short and does not adapt to the response duration of cross-system procurement data retrieval.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Submit `formInput` content that includes procurement plan fields, and check whether document data from the corresponding source can be recalled.
- Select the procurement method option in `userSelect`, and confirm that the pop-up options cover the procurement types commonly used by central SOEs and public sector entities.
- Simulate cross-system data retrieval operations to verify that the response duration of the interaction link meets business requirements.
- After configuring department role permissions, check that non-authorized roles cannot access the interaction content of sensitive procurement documents.
