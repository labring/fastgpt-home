# Pollutant Discharge Permit Management Inquiry and Reply: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from the national pollutant discharge permit management information platform, enterprise environmental protection ledgers, online monitoring systems, and environmental statistics systems. Update frequency is quarterly. Daily business documents include pollutant discharge permit application forms, implementation reports, self-monitoring plans, ledger records, change applications, and permit copies. Fields cover emission limits, ledger numbers, monitoring data, change items, and more. There is no unified fixed unit; fields must match the industry technical specification fields of the corresponding documents. Service subjects include pollutant discharge permit management positions in ecological environment departments, environmental protection departments of pollutant discharge enterprises, and third-party environmental service institutions. Data use must comply with regulatory requirements.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the "Forms and Interactions" Link
Multiple data sources with quarterly updates require form interactions to support mounting multiple knowledge bases and syncing updates quarterly, to avoid using outdated permit limits or ledger data. Diverse document types with fields that must match industry technical specifications require forms to preset field verification rules for corresponding business scenarios, to ensure input content complies with pollutant discharge permit management business requirements. Data use involves regulatory compliance, requiring the interaction link to limit data source scope, to prevent non-compliant data from being used for inquiry replies. Multi-subject participation business scenarios require forms to support presetting different interaction processes based on user roles, to adapt to the different inquiry needs of management positions, enterprise positions, and third-party institutions.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When `formInput` cannot extract pollutant discharge permit related fields, directly return no matching results without triggering the preset prompt. Cause: The null value verification and specified reply logic of `formInput` are not configured, so it is impossible to guide users to supplement valid information.
- Phenomenon: Do not switch the corresponding knowledge base according to the business type, and cannot accurately match inquiry content for different scenarios such as pollutant discharge permit application and implementation report. Cause: The association rule between `userSelect` options and knowledge base mounting scope is not bound, so it is impossible to filter data sources according to business scenarios.
- Phenomenon: The returned inquiry results include ecological environment content unrelated to pollutant discharge permit management, exceeding business boundaries. Cause: No filtering rules for result display are configured, and no restrictions are set to only display document types related to pollutant discharge permit management.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Submit simulated input related to pollutant discharge permit change application, check whether `formInput` triggers the required verification and the corresponding prompt text.
- Select the "Implementation Report Submission" option in `userSelect`, check whether the search results of the corresponding knowledge base are automatically switched.
- Simulate a timeout request, check whether the preset timeout prompt text is returned.
- View the knowledge base sync log, confirm that the sync cycle meets the quarterly update requirement.
