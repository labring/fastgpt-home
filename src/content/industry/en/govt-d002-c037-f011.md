# Government Information Application-based Disclosure: Service Consultation and Reply - Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
This category of data originates from government information disclosure platforms, official agency archive systems, document management systems, departmental business ledgers, and relevant regulations. Daily processed documents include government information disclosure application forms, supplementary notice letters, third-party opinion solicitation letters, reply letters, delivery receipts, and administrative reconsideration response materials. Data updates have no fixed cycle, and are generated based on individual applicant requests and internal agency business workflows.

Data fields are structured around core elements such as applicant information, application content, information target, disclosure attribute judgment, and relief channels. There is no unified standardized unit, and matching must be completed based on specific business scenarios. For example, the applicant information field includes name, contact information, identification number, and similar items.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Data for this category is scattered across multiple systems and has no fixed update cycle. This requires the forms and interactions link to support cross-system data pulling and real-time verification. Due to the diverse types of processed documents and significant differences in their fields, forms must support custom field groups to adapt to different business scenarios.

The exclusive attributes of individual requests require forms to bind a unique association between applicants and application content to avoid information confusion. At the same time, since the consequences of errors include delayed replies and administrative reconsideration corrections, the interaction link must add confirmation prompts for key steps to prevent operational mistakes.

## Three Common Mistakes
- A "Permission denied by system" error pops up after triggering voice input. This occurs because the application has not been granted voice input permissions at the system level, and the permission verification logic for voice input has not been adapted in the `formInput` configuration.
- Malformed Human instruction text appears during tool calls. This happens because the form interaction link did not correctly encapsulate user input content, causing the context format for tool calls to fail to meet requirements.
- Status confusion occurs when multiple interaction pages are opened on a single device. This occurs because each interaction session is not bound to a unique applicant identifier, causing session data to interfere with each other.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Submit a simulated government information disclosure application, check whether the form automatically verifies required fields and pops up corresponding prompts.
- Trigger the voice input function, confirm that the system permission verification works normally and that input content can be correctly synchronized to the form input box.
- Generate simulated sessions for different applicants, confirm that form data for each session does not interfere with each other.
- Load business templates, confirm that the form can automatically match the field structure for the corresponding scenario.
