# Matter Consultation and Reply for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
This category’s data mainly comes from enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, law and regulation repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. Daily processed documents include compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, major issue legal opinions, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation results. Data is updated monthly. Core documents cover structured forms and unstructured analysis reports. High time-consuming steps in business processes include identifying compliance risks, reviewing contracts and major issues, and investigating violation clues. The data links to the legality judgment of business operations, and directly correlates to risk consequences such as legal disputes, administrative penalties, asset losses, or reputational damage.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the knowledge base retrieval and recall link
Monthly updated data sources require knowledge base indexing tasks to match the monthly scheduling cycle. This avoids frequent indexing consuming system resources. Documents from multiple sources include structured forms and unstructured analysis reports. Semantic association logic varies significantly across different documents. Retrieval must adapt to context retention rules for each document type. The data directly links to business compliance judgments. The relevance and completeness of retrieval results directly affect risk identification and decision quality. Matching accuracy of recalled content must be strictly controlled. Duplicate indexing of documents across systems must also be avoided. Documents cover multiple scenarios such as compliance review and risk investigation. Retrieval must prioritize recalling entries strongly related to current consultations. This prevents irrelevant content from interfering with decision-making.

## How to set configurations
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | `0.75–0.85` | Meets compliance relevance requirements for documents in this category, avoids recalling irrelevant content while covering core compliance clauses |
| `limit` | `Top 8–12 entries` | Adapts to the multi-entry association characteristics of legal compliance documents, balances context length and recall coverage |
| `searchMode` | `Semantic Retrieval` | Meets semantic matching needs for unstructured compliance documents, compensates for accuracy shortcomings of keyword retrieval |
| `usingReRank` | `Enabled` | Improves sorting priority of highly relevant documents, prevents core compliance opinions from being overshadowed by low-relevance content |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | `Enabled` | Expands compliance-related semantics of user consultations, covers synonyms and industry terms, and improves recall completeness |
| `collectionIds` | `Configured by business system grouping` | Matches document classification for multiple data sources in this category, avoids duplicate indexing and retrieval confusion across systems |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Segmentation count displayed after uploading a single document does not match the final ready segmentation count, with duplicate segments appearing. The cause is that `collectionIds` are not configured by business system grouping, leading to duplicate indexing of cross-system documents.
- No matching content is retrieved after entering a compliance-related consultation. The cause is that `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` is not enabled, and compliance-related semantics of user consultations are not expanded, resulting in overly narrow recall scope.
- Core compliance opinions do not appear in the top positions of retrieval results after reranking. The cause is that the `similarity` threshold is set too low, and low-relevance content interferes with the sorting logic.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Properly Set
- Upload a single compliance review form, check that the segmentation count matches the actual document content, with no duplicate segments.
- Enter a consultation related to compliance risks, check that retrieval results include compliance documents from the corresponding business system.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, verify that highly relevant legal opinions appear in the top positions of retrieval results.
- Review the `collectionIds` configuration, confirm that only business system data sources for this category are associated.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
