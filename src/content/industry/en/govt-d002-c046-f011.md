# Service Consultation and Response for Social Assistance and Minimum Living Standard Verification: Forms and Interactions

This content is configured based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data for this category mainly comes from the low-income population dynamic monitoring platform, resident family economic status verification system, and cross-department shared data from public security, human resources and social security, medical insurance, housing and urban-rural development, and tax authorities. The update frequency is once per month.

Daily documents include minimum living security application forms, family economic status verification reports, household survey forms, democratic appraisal records, public notice materials, and assistance approval forms. Core fields cover family income, total property, member identity and relationship, etc. Common units include yuan, person, household, and similar.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Decentralized cross-department data sources require form interactions to support multi-field linkage verification, to avoid inconsistent family information entry. The monthly update frequency requires form configuration to include trigger logic for regular synchronization of verification data, ensuring the latest data is called during consultations.

Multiple document types and complex field structures require forms to be split into modules by business link, to reduce user entry burden. Family economic status and member relationship are core verification items. Interactions must force association with corresponding supporting material upload entrances, to lower the risk of incorrect or missed assistance.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: No dedicated civil affairs options appear in the knowledge base selection dropdown, making verification data unavailable for retrieval. Cause: The dedicated low-income population dynamic monitoring and family economic status verification knowledge bases for this category have not been bound.
- Phenomenon: No verification prompt appears after submitting the `formInput` field, and family income or property information is missing. Cause: The mandatory association verification rule for `formInput` has not been configured, and the corresponding supporting material upload entrance has not been bound.
- Phenomenon: After streaming calls return segmented data, complete consultation response results cannot be assembled. Cause: The receiving logic for streaming results has not been correctly configured, and returned segmented content has not been assembled as required.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- The form configuration page can be accessed to check whether business modules such as basic family information and income and property verification have been split.
- The `userSelect` dropdown can be tested to confirm that preset role options including Civil Affairs Assistance Section and Subdistrict Civil Affairs Office are included.
- A test form can be submitted to check whether the `formInput` field triggers verification prompts, and submission is blocked when core information is missing.
- The test interface can be called to confirm that the returned results include content related to low-income population dynamic monitoring and family economic status verification.
