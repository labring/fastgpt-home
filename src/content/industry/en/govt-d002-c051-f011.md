# Business Consultation and Reply for Road Transport Business Licensing: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
The data sources for this category are the road transport administration system, the national public service platform for dynamic information of road transport vehicles, and the vehicle and practitioner database. Data is synchronized daily for updates. Business documents include five categories: business license application, vehicle technical file, driver qualification material, safety production management system, and road transport business license. Fields cover business premises address, vehicle technical parameters, practitioner qualification number, safety production management system items, and more. All documents must comply with the fixed format requirements of government approval.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions phase
Since data sources come from multiple government systems and are updated daily, forms must support real-time pulling of the latest vehicle and practitioner qualification status to avoid submitting outdated data. Business document structures are fixed and fields are clearly defined, so forms must be split into modules by document type, matching the required fields of each document category. The consequences of errors are directly tied to licensing approval results, so the interaction phase must strictly validate field legitimacy to prevent license rejection due to incorrect information. Additionally, this category focuses on business access qualifications, so forms must strictly correspond to legal licensing classifications and must not omit core access condition fields.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Content transcribed from voice input cannot be automatically filled into the corresponding form fields. Cause: The field mapping rules for `formInput` are not configured, and the matching relationship between transcribed text and business fields is not verified.
- Phenomenon: The API cannot retrieve the initial configuration information of the form. Cause: The API access permission of the form is not enabled, and the correct data source associated fields are not bound.
- Phenomenon: The original multi-file classification structure is lost after importing the backed-up knowledge base. Cause: Files were not split by business document type during export, and only a single CSV format backup was generated.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Submit a test road transport business license application form, verify whether preset validation prompts are triggered for required fields.
- Call the specified API interface, confirm that initial configuration information such as the form opening remarks and required fields can be returned.
- Upload test vehicle technical files and practitioner materials, verify whether the form automatically matches the corresponding business fields.
- Export and re-import the knowledge base, confirm that the multi-file classification structure is fully retained.
