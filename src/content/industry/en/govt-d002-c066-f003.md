# Administrative Inquiry and Reply for Advanced Chemical Materials: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompts
FastGPT v4.17.0

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Administrative inquiry data for advanced chemical materials mainly comes from product filing information published by industry associations, public product specifications from enterprises, national standards and industry specification documents, and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS). There are two types of data update cycles: new product filing information is updated quarterly, national and industry standard documents are updated irregularly along with policy adjustments, and internal enterprise documents are updated monthly. The document structure includes fields such as product name, grade, core performance parameters like tensile strength, heat distortion temperature, density, etc., with clear unit identifiers, application fields, and compliance descriptions.

## Constraints Imposed by Data Characteristics on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Configuration
The data characteristics of advanced chemical materials impose multiple constraints on multi-turn dialogue and prompt configuration. First, performance parameters with clear units in documents require multi-turn dialogue to retain unit information from the context, to avoid confusion between parameters of different material grades. Second, compliance content and filing information require prompts to forcibly associate document source fields, to ensure replies comply with industry regulations. Third, data sources with different update cycles require regular refreshes of knowledge base chunks, to avoid using expired filing information. Finally, long technical documents require that conversation contexts are not truncated excessively, otherwise associated information of key parameters will be lost.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Advanced chemical material documents are mostly technical text with parameters. Excessively long chunks reduce retrieval accuracy, while excessively short chunks destroy the integrity of parameter context |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents performance parameters and compliance clauses from being truncated across chunks, and ensures coherence of cross-chunk information |
| Maximum Context | 4000–6000 tokens | Adapts to context retention for information such as grades and parameters repeatedly queried by users in multi-turn dialogue |
| `variables` | Bind product grade and application scenario variables | Enables precise retrieval for specific subcategories, reduces irrelevant retrievals |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 results | Core parameters of advanced chemical materials are concentrated in the first few retrieval results. Too many results will disrupt dialogue logic |
| Question Optimization | Enabled | Helps users clarify vague technical inquiries, such as converting vague statements into specific parameter queries |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- A 500 error is returned when uploading technical documents for advanced chemical materials. The cause is that the documents contain a large number of performance parameters with special symbols, and parsing fails due to incorrect escape handling during chunking.
- After connecting multiple AI dialogue modules in a workflow, the final output includes all dialogue results from all modules. The cause is that the context truncation rule for `messages` is not configured, causing all historical dialogue to be included in the final output.
- Response delay exceeds 3 seconds when initiating an advanced chemical materials inquiry conversation for the first time, with normal speeds for subsequent conversations. The cause is that knowledge base chunks are not preloaded, and the first request triggers chunk loading and retrieval processes.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a typical advanced chemical material product specification, initiate a multi-turn dialogue, and verify that parameter units retained in the context are free of errors.
- Configure `chatId` to bind session identifiers, initiate two independent sessions, and verify that contexts between different sessions do not interfere with each other.
- Trigger a multi-turn dialogue, check that the final output only retains the required final round of results, with no redundant historical dialogue content.
- Adjust the maximum context configuration, and verify that the response delay for the first conversation meets expected requirements.
