# Administrative Consultation and Response for Advanced Chemical Materials: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The administrative consultation data for advanced chemical materials primarily comes from industry standard specifications, enterprise R&D test reports, product specification sheets, safety data sheets, and compliance filing documents. Updates to this data are triggered on demand as new products enter production, industry standards are revised, or compliance requirements change, with no fixed cycle. A single document typically includes fields such as product grade, CAS registry number, physical and chemical parameters, production process parameters, and safety compliance clauses. Parameter units cover professional chemical units including MPa, g/cm³, ℃, kg/m³, and some documents include product photos or screenshots of test reports.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall Link
Professional parameters and unique identifiers demand precise matching during retrieval, to prevent irrelevant documents from being included due to fuzzy recall. Long documents contain multiple sets of continuous parameters; overly short chunking splits complete performance test data, reducing retrieval accuracy. On-demand data updates require an indexing strategy that supports incremental updates, to avoid excessive resource usage from full indexing operations. Compliance clauses and safety data have priority, so retrieval results must prioritize compliance-related documents. Non-advanced chemical material knowledge base data must be isolated to prevent cross-category interference. Images attached to documents are bound to their parameters, so retrieval results must display associated corresponding resources.

## How to Set the Configuration
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Advanced chemical material parameters have high precision requirements, to avoid low-match irrelevant documents appearing in retrieval results |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | A single document contains multiple sets of professional parameters; too many results will exceed the context window and harm response coherence |
| `searchMode` | Precise matching | Match unique identifiers such as product grades and CAS numbers, to avoid fuzzy recall of non-target documents |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Reorder initial recall results by relevance, to optimize the sorting priority of professional parameters |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically expand synonyms for professional terms, such as matching "tensile strength" with "pull strength", to cover different expression formats of queries |
| `collectionIds` | Exclusive collection ID for advanced chemical materials | Isolate knowledge base data from other categories, to ensure only documents from this category are recalled |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Symptom: The knowledge base returns an incorrect format, and variable content fails to load correctly. Cause: Non-standard variable formats such as `[{datasetId: xxx}]` are used incorrectly, and FastGPT variable reference rules are not adhered to.
- Symptom: The knowledge base continues to display the indexing status after upload, and retrieval cannot be completed. Cause: Chunking parameters for long documents are not adjusted, and incremental indexing is not enabled, resulting in excessive time consumption due to backlogged full indexing tasks.
- Symptom: Image resources in the knowledge base do not display in retrieval results. Cause: Embedding configuration for in-document images is not enabled, or the unique identifier of the corresponding document is not bound to the image, so resources fail to associate and display.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- View the `collectionIds` configuration of the knowledge base collection, confirm that only the exclusive dataset for advanced chemical materials is associated, launch a cross-category test retrieval, and verify that non-target documents are not returned.
- Launch a test retrieval that includes professional parameters such as CAS number and tensile strength, check that the `similarity` score of the returned results falls within the preset 0.75–0.85 range.
- Upload a test image to the corresponding knowledge base collection, confirm that the image resource displays normally in the response after retrieval is initiated.
- View indexing task logs in FastGPT v4.17.0, confirm that uploaded documents complete indexing within 1 hour, and do not remain in the indexing status for an extended period.
