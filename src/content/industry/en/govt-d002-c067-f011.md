# Fertilizers and Pesticides: Service Inquiries and Responses - Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Fertilizer and pesticide service inquiry data mainly comes from the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs pesticide registration announcements, fertilizer filing database, and compliance documents submitted by production enterprises. Data update rhythm adjusts with the frequency of new registration product submissions. New pesticide registration information is synced monthly, and fertilizer filing information is updated quarterly. The document structure includes fields such as product common name, registration certificate number, production enterprise name, applicable crop range, active ingredient content, recommended application dosage, pre-harvest interval, and others. Active ingredient content units are mostly grams per liter or mass percentage, while application dosage units are mostly kilograms per mu or milliliters per plant.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Because fertilizer and pesticide data includes highly compliance-focused fields and multi-unit measurement requirements, forms must be split into modules by business scenario to avoid user input confusion. Dynamic updates of official registration data require forms to pull the latest filing information in real time during interaction, to prevent returning expired compliance content. Association checks exist between fields. For example, applicable crops must match the reasonable range of corresponding dosages, and association checks must be triggered synchronously during input. Additionally, multi-unit usage scenarios require forms to provide unit switching options, automatically converting input value measurement standards to ensure the accuracy of inquiry responses.

| Result cache duration | 3600 seconds | Balances data timeliness and interface call frequency, avoids frequent requests to official interfaces |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: When configuring `formInput`, historical record variables are introduced. After submission, a validation failure prompt pops up, and the next step of the workflow cannot be entered. Cause: Historical record variables are not configured as allowed input sources, and form validation rules block non-preset input content.
- Phenomenon: After adding a speech recognition component, the interface still prompts that the browser does not support speech input, and the recording function cannot be invoked during testing. Cause: Speech input adaptation parameters for `userSelect` are not configured, or the interaction trigger logic for browser permission authorization is not enabled.
- Phenomenon: After setting plugin initial values, global variables are not updated synchronously during runtime. Cause: Initial values are not bound to the update trigger node of global variables, and variables are not synchronized upon form submission.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Enter a valid fertilizer or pesticide registration certificate number, verify whether the form automatically associates public information of the corresponding product.
- Switch the unit of measurement option in the form, confirm that the input application dosage value automatically completes unit conversion.
- Submit an application dosage that exceeds the reasonable range, verify whether the form triggers the corresponding compliance validation prompt.
- Wait for the configured cache duration, then re-query the same product, confirm whether updated filing data is loaded.
