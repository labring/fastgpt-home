# Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation: Service Consultation and Response - Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the data for this category looks like
Data primarily comes from local emergency management departments’ hazardous chemicals storage and transportation service guides, corporate compliance filing documents, daily service consultation archive records, and safety inspection logs for storage and transportation links. Compliance documents are updated quarterly to annually to align with national and local hazardous chemical regulatory policies. Service consultation records are updated in real time with daily operations. Internal corporate storage and transportation documents are updated irregularly with process optimizations. Most documents are in PDF format, containing long sections of regulatory text, structured tabulated material lists, emergency response diagrams, and question-and-answer pairs. Fields include storage and transportation volume, approval time limit, responsible entity, and others. Units include tons, cubic meters, and working days.

## Constraints on Document Parsing and Chunking
Compliance documents contain long sections of regulatory text and professional terms. Chunking must retain contextual associations of terms to avoid breaking semantic integrity. Tabulated material lists and approval time limit tables, if incorrectly split, will prevent subsequent question-and-answer matching between materials and corresponding requirements. Emergency response diagram images require OCR recognition first; valid text cannot be extracted for chunking otherwise. Real-time updated service consultation records often have large individual file sizes, so support for large file uploads and parsing is required. Professional terms such as critical quantity and flame arrester must retain adjacent context during chunking to avoid semantic misunderstanding.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When uploading a single hazardous chemicals compliance PDF document that exceeds the configured threshold, an "offset out of range" error appears after the parsing progress reaches 90%. Cause: The `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` parameter is not adjusted to a value suitable for large files, so the system cannot process file content beyond the preset upper limit.
- Phenomenon: After chunking question-and-answer pair documents for service consultations, complete question and answer combinations cannot be matched via retrieval. Cause: The `chunkSplitter` parameter is not configured according to the document structure, and fixed character count splitting is used incorrectly, leading to a single question-and-answer pair being split into two different chunks.
- Phenomenon: Chunking results differ between service consultation documents uploaded via API and identical documents uploaded directly through the platform. Cause: The `chunkSettingMode` and `chunkSplitter` parameters are not uniformly configured, resulting in inconsistent chunking rules across the two upload channels.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a single hazardous chemicals compliance PDF document that meets business requirements, and confirm that the parsing progress completes fully without timeout or offset errors.
- Select a service consultation question-and-answer pair document, and confirm that the complete question and answer content appears in the same retrieval result after searching.
- Upload the same document via API and directly through the platform respectively, compare the chunking results, and confirm that the chunking rules are consistent across both methods.
- Parse a storage and transportation material list document containing tables, and confirm that the table content is fully extracted and retains its structure, with no disordered content.
