# State-owned capital operation company service consultation and reply: Forms and interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Service consultation and reply data for state-owned capital operation companies mainly comes from the official policy repository of the state-owned assets supervision system, internal operation ledgers, business forms submitted by subordinate enterprises, and supervision reports. Policy data is stored in a structured manner by issuing authority, document number, effective date, and scope of application. Subordinate enterprise declaration data includes fields such as enterprise name, registered capital, business type, and declaration amount. The units for registered capital and declaration amount are uniformly ten thousand yuan. Time fields use the YYYY-MM-DD format. Supervision report data is updated monthly or quarterly. Policy document data is synchronized in real time. Internal ledger data is updated daily.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Real-time updates to state-owned assets supervision policies require interaction components to support fast retrieval of the latest policy content, to avoid returning expired information. Standardized declaration fields for subordinate enterprises require form input components to strictly match the field rules of supervision submissions, and no non-standard custom fields are allowed. The requirement for a uniform unit of ten thousand yuan requires clear unit prompts in the interaction interface, to prevent mixed use of input data units. For scenarios with multiple subordinate enterprise declarations, user selection components must support batch selection and fuzzy search, to improve declaration operation efficiency. Cross-system data retrieval requires reasonable configuration of concurrency and timeout parameters, to avoid interaction failures caused by insufficient interface carrying capacity.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: A connection error appears when multiple chat interaction pages are opened at the same time. Cause: No reasonable concurrency limit and timeout parameters are configured for the internal interfaces of the state-owned assets supervision system, leading to concurrent requests from multiple pages exceeding the interface carrying limit.
- Phenomenon: Unformatted Human <Instruction> instruction text appears on the interface during tool calls. Cause: No system instruction filtering rules are configured in the `formInput` component, leading to internal call instructions being directly returned to users.
- Phenomenon: Variable references fail when selecting a knowledge base, and the selected knowledge base cannot automatically associate corresponding business fields. Cause: Business variables are not bound to the preset options of the `userSelect` component, leading to variables failing to map correctly to knowledge base filtering conditions.
