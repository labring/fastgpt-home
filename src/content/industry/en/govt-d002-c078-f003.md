# Municipal Water Services Inquiry and Response: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
Data sources for municipal water services inquiry include real-time pipe network monitoring systems of water service operators, offline work order databases, water quality testing ledgers, water supply payment records, and government service guideline documents. Update rhythms vary by data type: real-time monitoring data is updated every 5 minutes, work orders and payment records are updated daily, water quality testing reports are updated in batches, and service guidelines are updated quarterly. Document structures fall into three categories: structured data tables (fields include pipe network number, water pressure, turbidity, pH value, with corresponding units MPa, NTU, and dimensionless respectively), unstructured service documents (handling processes and response standards grouped by business modules), and semi-structured work order conversation records.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
The high-frequency update of real-time monitoring data requires multi-turn dialogue to support dynamic refresh of associated data, to avoid returning outdated information. Water service data includes multiple structured fields with specific units, so prompts must clarify field meanings and units to prevent unit mix-ups in responses. Most service inquiries are single-business closed-loop scenarios, so multi-turn dialogue must bind session identifiers to associate historical work orders and avoid cross-session information interference. Semi-structured work order records require the dialogue to identify whether a user’s question is linked to a submitted work order, to enable accurate responses for scenarios such as progress inquiries.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind business work order number as session identifier | Associate historical work order information in multi-turn dialogue to avoid cross-session context confusion |
| `messages` | Retain the most recent 15 conversation turns | Adapt to the short conversation characteristics of water service inquiries and reduce redundant information interference |
| `variables` | Pre-set pipe network area, water quality indicators, work order status variables | Adapt to structured fields of water service data to quickly locate corresponding data sources |
| Maximum context length | 6000–10000 token | Cover multi-turn dialogue of single-round inquiries and associated water service documents to prevent key information truncation |
| Knowledge base refresh interval | Calibrated based on actual testing: refresh real-time data every 5 minutes, refresh static documents every 12 hours | Distinguish update rhythms of real-time monitoring data and static service documents to ensure retrieval accuracy |
| Query optimization | Enabled | Adapt to vague expressions common in water service inquiries, optimize phrases such as "low water pressure" into pipe network water pressure retrieval conditions for the corresponding area |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base retrieval returns no results during dialogue, but normal recall works on the knowledge base test page. Cause: The current session's `variables` are not bound, so restricted conditions such as water service business area and work order number are not included during retrieval.
- Phenomenon: Uploaded files cannot be read during dialogue after local deployment, while the knowledge base upload process works normally. Cause: Temporary storage path permissions for file parsing are not configured, so the dialogue link cannot access uploaded copies of water service documents.
- Phenomenon: Vector index construction progress stalls. Cause: The index update frequency of real-time monitoring data and static documents is not distinguished, using unified batch index tasks leading to excessive resource occupation and task blocking.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
This verification process is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Initiate a test multi-turn conversation related to water service business, check whether historical conversation content is correctly retained without abnormal truncation.
- Call the pre-set `variables` to verify whether retrieval results are linked to corresponding water service business fields and data.
- Adjust the knowledge base refresh interval to verify whether retrieval results of real-time monitoring data and static documents match the current update rhythm.
- Upload test files related to water service business, initiate a conversation to verify whether file content can be normally read and recalled.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
