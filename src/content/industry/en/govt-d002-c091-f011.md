# Building Construction General Contracting Service Consultation and Reply: Forms and Interaction
This document is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The service consultation data for building construction general contracting mainly comes from official service guidelines released by housing and urban-rural development departments, project approval documents, construction progress reporting records, completion acceptance filing materials, and internal process forms of general contractors. Data update rhythm has two categories: Official service guidelines are updated quarterly along with policy adjustments, while project-specific data is submitted and updated in real time along with construction progress. The document structure is divided into three categories by service items: project establishment application, progress reporting, and completion acceptance. Each document includes fixed fields such as project number, construction unit, construction scope, floor area (unit: ㎡), contract amount (unit: ten thousand yuan), approval status, and others.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Forms and Interaction Link
The quarterly updates of official service guidelines require forms to support regular synchronization of the latest reporting specifications, and avoid using expired field requirements. The real-time requirement for project-specific data requires forms to support association with existing information of the current project, reducing repeated filling. Fixed units (㎡, ten thousand yuan) for fields require forms to have built-in unit verification logic to block non-compliant inputs. The distinction between multiple service items requires forms to be displayed by item category, avoiding confusion of fields from different processes and improving reporting efficiency.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: When configuring `formInput` to reference knowledge base documents, search results include approval documents from non-building construction projects, and cannot accurately match the current service item. Cause: No screening parameter based on project number is set, resulting in the search scope covering all knowledge base documents.
- Phenomenon: When adding team collaboration members, entering the username of the corresponding project leader shows as invalid. Cause: The real-name personnel information of the building construction project is not bound to the system user permissions, and the verification logic does not match the exclusive account within the project.
- Phenomenon: After submitting the form, the associated historical approval data is not automatically filled into the corresponding fields. Cause: The linkage trigger timing of `userSelect` is not configured correctly, resulting in data synchronization not being triggered.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Open the form for the corresponding service item, check whether the preset fields are consistent with the service guidelines released by the housing and urban-rural development department, and whether the unit markings meet the requirements.
- Enter any project number, verify whether the associated project information such as construction unit and construction scope is automatically brought out.
- Simulate a period of inactivity, check whether the draft is automatically saved and the draft content is complete.
- Submit a test form, check whether the historical approval data of the current project can be correctly associated and filled into the corresponding fields.
