# Service Consultation and Reply for New Energy Development: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for new energy development service consultation and reply mainly comes from the government energy project approval system, site survey records, grid connection approval documents, and user-submitted service consultation tickets. The data update rhythm changes with the project phase: updated weekly during the project preparation phase, updated daily during the approval phase, and archived after completion. The document structure is divided into four modules: service items, required materials, processing procedures, and consultation replies. Fields include item code, processing time limit (unit: working days), material list (with format requirements), consultation channels, and project-specific land use and environmental impact assessment related fields.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on the forms and interactions workflow
The data fields for new energy development service consultation are numerous and change dynamically with the service item. This requires forms to load corresponding fields based on the service item selected by the user, avoiding redundant display from fixed forms. Data updates occur at high frequency, so the knowledge base synchronization frequency during interactions must match the business rhythm to prevent returning outdated approval rules or material requirements. Material lists include format requirements, so format verification and prompts must be added during interactions to reduce invalid material submissions from users. The specific fields for different projects vary greatly, so dynamic expansion of form items must be supported to adapt to different types of new energy development projects such as wind power and photovoltaic.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- When service consultation questions are output in the chat window, the prompt "No knowledge base selected" appears. The cause is that the new energy development project approval knowledge base is not bound to the corresponding item in `userSelect` for the current interaction scenario.
- Knowledge base referenced variables cannot be selected in the code running node. The cause is that the context linkage configuration between forms and knowledge base is not enabled, causing the variable pool to not load the corresponding knowledge base content.
- The number of consultation result entries returned after submitting the form does not match expectations. The cause is that the number of displayed result entries is not configured correctly, or the exclusive knowledge base data range for the corresponding item is not associated.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
When the wind power project approval item is selected, verify that the form automatically loads the corresponding land use and environmental impact assessment related material fields.
When the knowledge base synchronization task is triggered, check that the background log completes the data update during the scheduled early morning period.
When material information that does not meet the format requirements is entered, verify that the corresponding format verification prompt pops up.
When a user consultation question about new energy project approval is simulated, confirm that the returned results include exclusive approval rule content.
