# Nuclear Power Plant Operations: Service Consultation and Response — Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Consultation data for nuclear power plant operations comes from on-site operation logs, equipment maintenance manuals, national nuclear safety regulatory documents, and on-site emergency response procedures. Update frequency: real-time synchronization of daily operation alerts and operation records, weekly updates to equipment parameter manuals, and monthly iterations of emergency response documents. Documents are organized in three tiers: equipment classification, alert code, and operation step. Fields include unique equipment identifiers, threshold parameters, and operation step numbers. Most units use industrial standard units such as MPa, ℃, hours, and similar.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The structured fields and specialized unit requirements for nuclear power plant operation data require that multi-turn dialogue bind unique equipment identifiers as context anchors, to avoid parameter confusion across devices. Real-time updated operation data requires that the context window be time-limited, to prevent retrieval of expired alert and operation records. The document structure organized by equipment classification requires that classification filtering instructions be added to prompts, to ensure retrieved documents match the equipment being consulted. Context transfer during multi-turn dialogue must retain fields such as alert codes and threshold parameters, to avoid responses generated by prompts deviating from on-site execution standards.

## Configuration Settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Context | Previous 10 turns of dialogue + 800–1200 characters | Matches the single-block length of nuclear power plant operation documents, prevents expired data from being included due to excessive context overload |
| `messages` | Passed in session order, retain equipment ID and alert code fields | Ensures consistent context anchors for multi-turn dialogue, aligns with the structured characteristics of nuclear power data |
| Single Search Limit | Top 3 retrieved results | Matches the equipment-classified structure of nuclear power plant documents, avoids retrieving documents from unrelated equipment |
| `variables` | Bind the currently consulted equipment ID and threshold parameters | Forces prompts to filter documents from the corresponding classification, improves response accuracy |
| Question Optimization | Enable automatic completion of equipment-specific fields | Adapts to the unit and field requirements of nuclear power data, reduces input format errors |
| `chatId` | Bind unique session identifier, do not reuse across sessions | Ensures context isolation for multi-turn dialogue, avoids content confusion across different consultations |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After increasing the chat history storage limit, multi-turn dialogue still fails to retain context, and response content is unrelated to prior consultations. Cause: Historical fields are not passed in session order within the `messages` parameter, or the equipment ID anchor in `variables` is not bound, leading to retrieval of unrelated documents.
- Phenomenon: The dialogue interface returns non-streaming full results, which does not match the expected streaming output. Cause: The streaming output switch is not enabled in the dialogue configuration, or session parameters are not passed correctly.
- Phenomenon: After clicking a custom trigger button, it does not jump to the corresponding dialog box, or the preset process cannot be triggered. Cause: The parameters of the trigger button are not bound to the preset instructions in `variables`, causing the prompt to fail to recognize the trigger condition.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate multi-turn consultations that include equipment IDs and alert codes, verify that each round of responses is associated with the parameters and rules of the current equipment.
- Check the `chatId` parameter of the session, confirm that `chatId` values for different sessions are not duplicated to avoid context confusion.
- Trigger the custom trigger button, verify that preset equipment fields and consultation instructions are automatically filled.
- Check the `messages` field returned by the interface, confirm that historical conversation content is correctly passed in order.
