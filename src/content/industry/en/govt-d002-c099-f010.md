# Service Consultation and Response for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Business data for oil and gas exploration and development primarily comes from internal oilfield exploration databases, drilling construction logs, trial production reports, and industry standard documents. Data update rhythms fall into three categories: drilling operation data is updated in real time alongside construction progress, block evaluation reports are updated upon completion of exploration phases, and trial production operation data is updated via monthly aggregated summaries. Individual documents include fields such as exploration block code, drilling operation number, well logging curve raw data, oil layer thickness, formation pressure, and permeability. Units include industry-specific petroleum measurement standards such as meters, megapascals, and millidarcys. Some long documents, such as well logging analysis reports, have substantial length.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
Oil and gas exploration and development business data characteristics impose multiple constraints on the knowledge base retrieval and recall link. First, the data contains a large volume of oil and gas industry-specific professional terms and structured business fields. Retrieval must prioritize precise semantic matching to avoid irrelevant results interfering with service consultation responses. Second, drilling and trial production data types are updated in real time or at high frequency. Recall results must cover newly uploaded documents to ensure the timeliness of consultation content. Third, individual document lengths vary significantly. Long well logging reports contain extensive content. When performing chunk processing, balance must be maintained between context integrity and retrieval efficiency to avoid disrupting the logical connections of professional content. Fourth, some data is tied to specific operation numbers or blocks. Retrieval must support filtering results by business dimensions to accurately match the specific scenarios of user consultations.

## How to Set the Configuration
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range/Method | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Oil and gas exploration data has high professionality; this threshold range filters low-relevance broad matches and retains content with precise semantic matching |
| `limit` | Top 8–12 results | Single exploration documents are professional and lengthy; excessive recall results will exceed the context window. This range covers key relevant information |
| `searchMode` | Semantic Retrieval | Matches semantic associations of professional terms, adapting to professional consultation scenarios in the oil and gas exploration field |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Performs secondary sorting of initial recall results by semantic relevance, improving the display priority of high-value professional documents |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Supplements industry terms for exploration-related professional questions to optimize the semantic matching effect of retrieval queries |
| `collectionIds` | Bind exclusive knowledge base collections for oil and gas exploration and development | Isolates business data to avoid retrieving irrelevant administrative documents |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: No optional content appears in the reference variable dropdown after selecting a knowledge base. Cause: The exclusive knowledge base collection specified by `collectionIds` is not correctly bound, or structured metadata fields have not been generated within this knowledge base.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval of a single professional exploration document takes more than 30 seconds. Cause: The `limit` setting is too high, and `usingReRank` is not enabled, causing the system to load excessive long text content.
- Phenomenon: Documents inserted via the API cannot be retrieved. Cause: The correct `collectionIds` parameter is not included in the API request, or the document was not uploaded in accordance with the field specifications for oil and gas exploration data.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Properly Set
- Access the FastGPT knowledge base management interface, and verify whether bound `collectionIds` include the exclusive knowledge base collection for oil and gas exploration and development.
- Submit a professional exploration consultation question, and confirm that the single retrieval return duration falls within a reasonable range.
- For any retrieved result, confirm that the reference variable dropdown loads the corresponding structured field content.
- Upload a test exploration document via the API, then confirm that the document appears in the recall results following retrieval.
