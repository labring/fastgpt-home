# Oilfield Engineering Services Inquiry and Reply: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
Inquiry and reply data for oilfield engineering services primarily comes from on-site construction logs, equipment operation and maintenance ledgers, regional compliance service guidelines, and client demand coordination records. Data is updated per single construction node or daily. Each document includes fields such as operation area number, equipment model, operation duration, approval process node, and compliance clauses. Some on-site inquiry records include speech-to-text transcripts. Field units follow standard conventions: operation duration uses hours, approval nodes use serial numbers. Most documents are structured PDF or editable table formats.

## Constraints for Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The inquiry and reply data for oilfield engineering services includes structured fields such as operation area, equipment model, and approval node. Multi-turn dialogue must retain cross-turn operation context to avoid repeated inquiries about basic information. Data updates dynamically with construction nodes. Prompt engineering must support real-time calls to the latest compliance clauses and operation requirements, and cannot rely on static knowledge base caches. Some inquiry records have colloquial transcripts. Prompt engineering must adapt to colloquial intent recognition, while standardizing the mapping of structured field units and formats to avoid parameter confusion in dialogue. The work process involves multi-level approval. Multi-turn dialogue must guide users step-by-step according to approval nodes to ensure all necessary links are covered.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: When viewing dialogue history without a login status, associated on-site operation images fail to load. Cause: `chatId` is not bound to the work order number, causing dialogue history to fail to match corresponding resource links.
- Phenomenon: After clicking the dialogue opening quick button, consultation content matching the current operation is not automatically populated. Cause: The preset prompt for the quick button does not inject variables such as operation area and equipment model from `variables`, so it cannot adapt to specific scenarios.
- Phenomenon: Response timeout occurs during multi-turn dialogue. Cause: The number of documents recalled per single dialogue is not limited, loading excessive construction logs and compliance clauses increases model inference and data call latency.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate an inquiry that includes operation area and equipment model, verify that corresponding variables are automatically populated without manual input.
- Trigger the dialogue opening quick button, verify that consultation options matching high-frequency oilfield engineering service scenarios are displayed.
- View dialogue history without a login status, verify that associated operation document links load correctly.
- Initiate multi-turn cross-node service inquiries, verify that previous round operation information is retained in context, with no repeated inquiries required.
