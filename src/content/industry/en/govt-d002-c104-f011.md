# Coal Chemical Industry Service Consultation and Reply: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
Data for coal chemical industry service consultation and reply comes from internal work ledgers of central and local state-owned coal chemical sector, and coal industry item databases of local government service centers. Data syncs for government item changes once every quarter. The data uses a structured table format, including fields such as item code, item name, handling level, application material details, legal handling time limit, and consultation contact information. The unit for handling time limit is working days. The unit for application materials is copies or physical original documents. Some materials require industry-specific identifiers such as environmental impact assessment approval numbers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "forms and interactions" link
Fixed structured data fields require forms to strictly match preset items. Do not add non-essential fields arbitrarily, to avoid incompatibility with ledger data formats. The quarterly update feature means dropdown options in forms cannot call dynamic interfaces in real time. Data sources must be synchronized periodically in batches. Special fields and units require the time selection component to filter legal holidays. Only working days may be selected. The upload control must match the format and naming rules of coal chemical industry approval materials. The consultation contact information field for cross-departmental services must match the official number of the local government service platform. No custom numbers are allowed.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: The `formInput` control dialog always displays when nested into other workflows. Cause: The pop-up trigger rule was not adjusted in the nested configuration, and the default pop-up display logic of the independent workflow was retained.
- Phenomenon: Radio options do not match the latest coal chemical industry service items. Cause: The data source of `userSelect` was not updated quarterly, and expired ledger data was used.
- Phenomenon: Submission fails after uploading coal chemical industry approval drawings. Cause: Correct file format and size limits were not set, and the uploaded file exceeded the preset single file limit.
