# Matter Handling Consulting and Reply for Telecommunications Engineering Construction: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for telecommunications engineering construction matter handling consulting and reply mainly comes from industry construction specifications, acceptance standards, construction approval process documents, operation manuals, and historical consultation records from offline service windows. Industry specifications are updated every 1 to 2 years. Historical consultation records are stored in real time. Document structures include fields such as item code, handling process, required materials, approval time limit, and charging standards. Some documents are structured tables. Historical consultation records are unstructured question-and-answer text. Units include workdays, copies, yuan, square meters, and other engineering-related measurement standards.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the knowledge base retrieval and recall workflow
Dense professional terminology in documents requires retrieval to balance semantic matching and exact matching. This avoids recall failure caused by terminology differences. Mixed data sources of structured process documents and unstructured historical records require retrieval modes to support both full-text retrieval and semantic retrieval. This covers different types of consultation questions. Low data update frequency but long single document length requires chunking to retain process integrity. This avoids splitting step logic. Real-time synchronization of historical consultation records requires retrieval services to have low-latency pulling capabilities. This ensures availability of the latest consultation data.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The symptom is a `Connection error` error returned during retrieval. The cause is that the correct knowledge base storage path or remote service access permissions are not configured, preventing normal pulling of exclusive documents for telecommunications engineering construction.
- The symptom is that the number of retrieval results is far lower than expected. The cause is that the `similarity` value is set too high, filtering out valid matched results containing professional terms.
- The symptom is that the output content does not mark the knowledge base source. The cause is that the retrieval result traceability configuration is not enabled, or the exclusive `collectionIds` collection is not bound.
