# Administrative Inquiry and Response for Metal Material Processing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Terms

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
The data for metal material processing administrative inquiries and responses is primarily sourced from national standards for ferrous and nonferrous metal industries, internal enterprise process specifications, quality inspection logs, and government administrative service guidelines. Data updates occur every six months to one year in line with industry standard revisions, and are also synchronized when internal enterprise process adjustments or government service process updates take place. Individual documents are split by process segments such as smelting, rolling, and heat treatment. Core fields include material grades, process parameters (units: ℃, MPa), required materials for administrative procedures, processing time limits, and some documents include quality inspection qualification judgment rules.

## Constraints for Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The segmented structure of industry standards and process data requires multi-turn dialogue to progress questions through linear process nodes such as smelting, rolling, and heat treatment. Jumping between nodes will cause context misalignment.
Fixed unit rules for material grades and process parameters require prompts to enforce returned results use standard industry units such as ℃ and MPa.
The data update frequency and linear nature of administrative procedures require multi-turn dialogue context to only retain valid information for the current node and prior nodes. Historical data from before expired process adjustments must be filtered. Knowledge bases must be regularly synchronized with the latest standard and specification versions.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Dialogue returned results use inconsistent units, including both Fahrenheit and Celsius, or megapascals and pounds per square inch. Cause: The prompt does not enforce use of standard units for the metal material processing industry, leading to chaotic result formatting.
- Phenomenon: Multi-turn dialogue context is disorganized, with redundant content spanning multiple process nodes. Cause: `messages` are not sorted in ascending order of dialogue time, leading to context logic misalignment with the linear order of administrative procedures.
- Phenomenon: The material grade confirmed in a previous question cannot be used as prompt input for the current dialogue. Cause: The `variables` field is not used to bind key parameters from the previous session, and automatic transfer of context parameters is not implemented.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a query that includes a material grade and process segment, verify that returned results comply with industry standard unit requirements.
- Initiate a series of consecutive multi-turn dialogues progressing through process nodes, verify that context arrangement order matches the linear logic of administrative procedures.
- Attempt to transfer parameters confirmed in a previous dialogue to the current dialogue, verify that parameters are correctly loaded.
- Test inquiry scenarios of varying complexity, verify that the timeout setting matches the thinking and data retrieval duration for the corresponding scenario.
