# Service Inquiry and Response for Metal Material Processing: Model Access and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Service inquiry data for the metal material processing division of central and state-owned enterprises is mainly sourced from internal process specification documents, quality inspection reports, customer inquiry ledgers, and national and industry metal material standard documents. The data update rhythm is divided into three categories: customer inquiry ledgers are updated in real time, process specification documents are revised quarterly, and industry standard documents are updated annually. A single document usually includes material grade, mechanical performance indicators such as tensile strength and elongation, processing technology parameters, inquiry question categories, and standard reply templates. Units involve professional measurement standards such as MPa, mm, and %, and the structure is fixed with clear field boundaries.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on model access and configuration?
The metal material processing division of central and state-owned enterprises has dense technical terms, strict unit requirements, and data includes real-time updated inquiry records and long-text process documents. These characteristics impose multiple constraints on model access and configuration. First, the strict requirements for technical terms and units mean the model must have domain semantic alignment capability, and the embedding model must be configured to accurately recognize metal material-related technical terms. Second, real-time updated customer inquiry data requires adaptation to fast context loading, and the context window refresh logic must be adjusted. Third, long-text process documents and standard clauses require the model's context length configuration to support full reading of single documents, avoiding truncation of key parameters.

## How to Set the Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Adapts to the total length of process documents and multiple inquiry records in metal material processing scenarios, avoiding truncation of key parameters and context information |
| `maxTokens` | 4000 token | Limits the output length of single-round responses, matches the concise reply requirements of service inquiries, and avoids generating redundant content |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 2000 token | Limits the length of quoted document fragments, ensures complete extraction of process parameters and standard clauses, and reduces interference from irrelevant information |
| `temperature` | 0.3–0.5 | Maintains the professionalism and accuracy of responses, avoiding non-standard content that does not meet industry standards |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Official access address of the corresponding model service provider | Ensures connectivity for model calls, and adapts to professional large models required for metal material processing scenarios |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Enterprise-specific authorization key | Ensures the security of internal inquiry data calls, preventing unauthorized access |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After configuring a speech model and initiating an inquiry, the interface displays "Speech service not responding". Cause: No speech model that supports recognition of metal material technical terms is selected, or `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` is not configured as the exclusive access address of the speech model.
- Phenomenon: After configuring the Doubao indexing large model and testing the call, a "404 page not found" error is returned. Cause: The address filled in `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` does not match the actual deployment address of the model, or the permission of the corresponding embedding model is not enabled in FastGPT v4.17.0's model channel management.
- Phenomenon: After entering a process-related inquiry question, the model's recognized question category does not match actual needs, and recognition is inflexible. Cause: Fields are not organized in the format of the background knowledge for metal material processing question categories, and structured input using "material grade + process type + question type" is not used, resulting in the model being unable to match semantic associations.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a test inquiry that includes specific metal material grades and process parameters, and check whether the reply returned by the model accurately includes the corresponding parameters and units.
- View the FastGPT model call log, confirm that the verification status of `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is passed, and there are no 404 or 401 errors.
- Test the multi-round continuous inquiry scenario, check whether the context window can retain the material grade and process information from previous inquiries, without truncation or loss.
- Initiate a voice inquiry test, confirm that the voice-to-text and reply voiceization processes work normally, and there are no recognition errors for technical terms.
