# Ledger Summary and Report Generation for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## Data Profile for This Category
Daily processed documents for this category include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, domicile certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials. Data sources cover the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the domicile information database. The update frequency is daily. Most document structures combine structured forms and unstructured text. Core fields include entity name, unified social credit identifier, domicile, license type, registration status, and no additional custom units of measurement. Field formats vary slightly based on system docking requirements.

## Constraints on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The data characteristics of this category impose multiple constraints on the retrieval and recall process. Daily updated data sources require the retrieval pipeline to support high-frequency incremental knowledge base synchronization, to avoid recalling expired registration or license information. The mixed structure of multiple document types requires separate retrieval logic for structured forms and unstructured text, to ensure recall accuracy for content in different formats. Core fields are key items for regulatory business work; retrieval must prioritize matching entity identification fields to prevent irrelevant results from interfering with subsequent ledger summary and report generation. Decentralized multi-system data sources require integrating multi-source data to build the knowledge base, and cross-source association rules must be configured to ensure the integrity of ledger data.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | `0.72–0.80` | Matches the accuracy requirement for core market entity fields, to avoid recalling irrelevant registration and license data |
| `limit` | `Top 8 entries` | Controls the number of results retrieved per call, adapts to the batch processing needs of ledger summary, and avoids context overload |
| `searchMode` | `vector+keyword` | Balances retrieval for structured fields and unstructured text, adapting to the mixed-format document data of this category |
| `usingReRank` | `Enabled` | Improves the sorting priority of highly relevant results, corrects result deviations from basic retrieval, and meets the strict requirements of regulatory business |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | `Enabled` | Automatically optimizes retrieval queries by supplementing entity identification keywords, improving recall matching for decentralized data sources |
| `collectionIds` | `Configured exclusively for registration and licensing datasets` | Limits the retrieval scope, only calls knowledge base collections related to market entity registration and licensing, avoiding interference from cross-category data |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results include expired canceled entity data, and core fields are missing. Cause: The daily incremental synchronization configuration is not enabled, and the knowledge base has not synchronized the latest updated data from the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.
- Phenomenon: The number of retrieval results is far lower than expected, and license application materials for specified entities cannot be recalled. Cause: `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` is not enabled, and keywords such as entity name and unified social credit identifier are not automatically supplemented, making it impossible to match content from decentralized data sources.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results mix non-registration and licensing documents, such as food sampling records. Cause: `collectionIds` is not configured correctly, and the retrieval scope covers knowledge base collections from other categories, leading to the recall of irrelevant data.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Manually trigger a retrieval, check that the source of the recalled results only includes documents related to market entity registration and licensing, to confirm that the `collectionIds` configuration is correct.
- Enter a retrieval query containing a unified social credit identifier, check that the retrieval results prioritize matching registration and license data for the corresponding entity, to confirm the reasonableness of the `similarity` threshold.
- View the knowledge base synchronization logs, confirm that the daily incremental synchronization task is executing normally, and verify that frequently updated data is correctly loaded.
- Compare the sorting of retrieval results before and after enabling `usingReRank`, confirm that the reranking function is active, and improve the priority of highly relevant results.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
