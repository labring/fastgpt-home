# Emission Permit Management: Ledger Summary and Report Generation - Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
This category handles daily documents including emission permit application forms, implementation reports, self-monitoring plans, ledger records, change applications, and permit copies. Data sources include the national emission permit management information platform, enterprise environmental protection ledgers, online monitoring systems, and environmental statistics systems. Updates occur quarterly. Documents include fields such as fixed permit limits, emission boundaries, and monitoring data, with units for emission concentration, emission volume, and other metrics. Service subjects include environmental protection department emission permit management staff, enterprise environmental protection departments, and third-party environmental protection service institutions.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Vector Models and Indexes
Multi-source data requires indexes to support structured tables, text reports, monitoring data and other formats. Configuration must adapt to length differences across formats.
Quarterly update frequency requires incremental index scheduling logic to adapt to batch data processing. This avoids single-index overload.
Structured content such as fixed permit limits and emission boundaries requires index matching accuracy to meet regulatory requirements. This prevents omission of key information.
Differences in document structures require chunking strategies to adapt to text of varying lengths. This ensures the integrity of key fields.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- CSV file chunking error after version upgrade. Phenomenon: After importing CSV-format enterprise environmental protection ledgers, the interface displays abnormal chunking logs and fails to generate valid index chunks. Cause: The system adjusted CSV field parsing rules after the upgrade, and did not adapt to the multi-field nested structure of emission permit ledgers.
- Knowledge base index stuck and incomplete. Phenomenon: The index progress bar stays in the middle position for a long time, and the background logs display timeout errors. Cause: The single processing data volume was not adjusted, and did not adapt to the large volume of ledgers and monitoring data updated quarterly.
- Irrelevant documents returned in search results. Phenomenon: When searching for "permit limits for a certain industry", environmental impact assessment approval documents are returned, and no content related to emission permits is matched. Cause: The similarity threshold was set too low, recalling document content outside the permit scope.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Upload a single copy of the emission permit copy, view the chunking preview function, confirm that the chunked content completely covers the original document, and no key fields are truncated.
- Trigger an incremental index task, wait for the index to complete, check that the progress bar displays 100% and there are no abnormal error logs.
- Enter questions such as "verify a certain emission limit", check that the source documents of the returned results all belong to emission permit-related files, with no cross-category documents.
- Adjust the similarity threshold, test recall results under different thresholds, confirm that they meet the accuracy requirements of business audits.
