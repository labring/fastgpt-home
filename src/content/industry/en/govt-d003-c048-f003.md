# Administrative Licensing Approval Ledger Summary and Report Generation: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Data sources for administrative licensing approval scenarios include the national integrated government service platform item database, administrative licensing item list, departmental business approval system, and electronic certificate database. The update frequency is daily. Daily processed documents cover service guides, application forms, commitment letters, acceptance notices, correction notification letters, administrative licensing approval decisions, license certificates, and others. Data fields include item name, applicant subject, acceptance number, approval status, handling conditions, and others. The structure and fields of each document must comply with the fixed format of legal approval procedures.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering" Link
Daily updated data sources require the dialogue process to pull the latest item list and certificate data in real time, to avoid using expired information. Fixed structures across multiple document types require prompt engineering to accurately match the field rules of different documents, ensuring the accuracy of material verification and opinion drafting. Electronic certificate data retrieved across departments requires multi-turn interaction to confirm source and validity, preventing information inconsistency. Strict legal approval requirements mandate that the dialogue process retains complete interaction traces for subsequent traceability and verification.

## Three Common Errors
- Knowledge base association errors occur during formal dialogue, but no issues appear in preview. The cause is that knowledge base access permissions configured in the formal environment have not been synchronized, or there is an abnormal `chatId` binding.
- Dialogue failure occurs because the total character count of associated knowledge bases exceeds the limit. The cause is failing to calculate the total number of Chinese characters in the associated knowledge bases in advance, and not matching the platform's limit requirements.
- The default submission question does not load automatically on the dialogue interface. The cause is failing to enable the default question preset function in the dialogue configuration, or the configuration parameters are not taking effect.

## How to Verify the Configuration is Complete
- Manually trigger a multi-turn approval dialogue, and verify that the historical interactions retained by the `messages` parameter fall within the preset 18-round range.
- Import multiple documents related to administrative licensing approval, and check that the number of recalled documents in a single session is 7, covering core retrieval needs.
- Submit a test approval application, and verify that the preset approval item fields in `variables` are correctly bound to the prompt engineering.
- Test associating up to 8 knowledge bases, and confirm that the total character count does not exceed 1000 MB, with no errors occurring.
