# Ledger Summary and Report Generation for Road Transport Business Licensing: Workflow Orchestration

## What the data for this category looks like
This category's data is sourced from the road transport administration system, the national public service platform for dynamic information of road transport vehicles, and the vehicle and practitioner database. The update frequency is daily. Daily processed documents include business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, and road transport business licenses. Data fields include license number, vehicle license plate number, practitioner qualification number, business scope, business site address, and others. All content is taken from official business systems, with no additional custom statistical fields.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "workflow orchestration" link
This category's data is multi-source and updated daily. Workflows must support multi-data source scheduled pull nodes to avoid information lag caused by manual synchronization. There are various types of daily documents, so differentiated text extraction rules need to be configured for different documents such as business license applications and vehicle technical files to ensure accurate extraction of core fields. The verification of vehicles, personnel and safety systems is the most time-consuming part of the business. Workflows must split this link into independent orchestratable nodes to facilitate subsequent adjustments and optimization of process efficiency. At the same time, errors in license processing will directly lead to enterprises being unable to operate or facing penalties. Workflows must configure failure retry and exception notification links to ensure timely intervention when process exceptions occur.

## How to set up the configuration
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale for This Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled Trigger Cycle | 2:00 AM – 4:00 AM daily | Business data is updated daily. Executing during non-peak hours avoids occupying resources of core business systems |
| `variables` Configuration | Bind license number and vehicle license plate fields from historical sessions | Multi-turn conversations need to retain historical application information to avoid repeated user inquiries |
| Text Extraction Rules | Configure exclusive field mappings for different documents | Extract vehicle license plate and annual inspection validity period from vehicle technical files; extract qualification number and validity period from driver materials |
| Retry Count and Interval | 2-3 times, 60 seconds between each attempt | Address temporary network fluctuations or system delays during single data pulls |
| `nodeId` Association Configuration | Bind identifiers of verification nodes and exception notification nodes | Automatically trigger the notification link when a process exception occurs to ensure timely intervention |
| Context Window | First 6 chat records | Cover key information such as historical applications and qualification verification required for business conversations |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The workflow cannot retain previously selected business categories and historical information when executing multi-turn conversations. Cause: Core fields from historical sessions are not bound in `variables`, causing the context state to reset with each conversation.
- Phenomenon: The text content extraction node returns empty or incorrect fields. Cause: Differentiated extraction rules are not configured for different documents such as business license applications and vehicle technical files, resulting in failure to match preset business fields.
- Phenomenon: The scheduled ledger summary workflow fails to execute. Cause: Reasonable retry count and interval are not set. The process is interrupted after a single data pull times out without automatic retry.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Manually trigger the workflow, check whether corresponding documents are pulled from multi-source data, and verify that the extracted fields match business requirements.
- Simulate a multi-turn conversation scenario to confirm whether the historical session fields bound in `variables` are correctly passed, ensuring that conversation context is not lost.
- Trigger a temporary exception scenario, such as disconnecting the data source connection, to verify whether the workflow executes the retry and exception notification process.
- View the scheduled task log to confirm whether the daily update process is automatically executed according to the preset cycle.
