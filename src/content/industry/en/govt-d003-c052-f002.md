# Ledger Summary and Report Generation for Department Final Accounts and Financial Reports: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data originates from the unified budget management system, centralized treasury payment system, accounting system, and asset management system. Updates occur once per year. Commonly processed documents include department final account statements, balance sheets, income and expenditure final account statements, final account explanations, and government financial reports. Data fields cover revenue and expenditure details, asset and liability balances, budget execution difference explanations, and more. Units are typically standard financial units such as ten thousand yuan or yuan. Most document structures consist of structured tables paired with text explanations.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector model and indexing link
Large-scale annual batch updates require vector indexes to support one-time large-capacity data imports and incremental update adaptation. Data from multiple systems has format differences, so vectorization processing for mixed content including structured tables and plain text explanations must be supported. The rigor of financial documents requires vector matching to align with business terminology, preventing retrieval results from deviating from financial data logic due to semantic ambiguity. Additionally, the annual fixed-cycle update rhythm requires index configuration to support regular synchronization and version management, ensuring consistency across different batches of data.

## Three common mistakes
- Rate limit exceeded errors occur during vectorization, with logs indicating too high request frequency. The cause is failure to limit the number of concurrent threads for single imports, triggering the rate limit of the vectorization interface when batch importing annual data.
- Knowledge base retrieval speed is slow, with poor performance under the same model configuration. The cause is failure to adjust the chunking strategy based on the structured characteristics of financial documents, resulting in the need to load excessive redundant text fragments during retrieval.
- The index status remains stuck in "indexing" and cannot become ready. The cause is failure to correctly configure the incremental update trigger rules, leading to repeated submission of index tasks and task backlog.
- Index entries in the dataset automatically increase. The cause is failure to turn off the automatic incremental synchronization switch, resulting in repeated import of processed historical data during annual system updates.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Perform a one-time small-batch vectorization import of financial documents, check task progress and error logs, and confirm there are no rate limit exceeded prompts.
- Retrieve specified financial report fields, verify the relevance and loading speed of retrieval results, and adjust the configuration until it meets business expectations.
- Trigger an incremental synchronization task, check the change in the number of entries in the dataset, and confirm there is no repeated import.
- Check the index version management configuration, confirm that historical data and newly imported data can be correctly distinguished during annual updates.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
