# Ledger Summary and Report Generation for Advanced Chemical Materials: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The ledger data for advanced chemical materials comes primarily from Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), batch reports from quality inspection departments, raw material purchase and storage records, and industry compliance documents. There are three update cycles for this data:
- Production ledgers synchronize batch data daily
- Quality inspection reports are updated with each batch of product output
- Industry standard documents are updated once per quarter

Single ledger documents are mostly structured tables or text with clear field labels. They include fields such as raw material grade, batch number, production cycle, qualification rate, and unit energy consumption. Common units include tons, kilograms, hours, and percentage.

## Constraints on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall Posed by These Characteristics
Advanced chemical materials datasets have three key traits: high structured data share, varied update frequencies, and unique identifier fields. These traits create multiple constraints for retrieval and recall:
1. The high share of structured fields requires prioritizing exact field matching during retrieval, to avoid irrelevant data being included in ledger summaries via generic text matching.
2. Daily updated batch data and quarterly updated standard documents coexist, so an incremental synchronization mechanism is needed to ensure the timeliness of recalled data.
3. Unique identifiers such as batch numbers and raw material grades are core to retrieval, and must be set as high-weight matching items.
4. Energy consumption and output fields that use multiple units require automatic unit alignment during retrieval, to reduce matching deviations.

## Configuration Settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0, adapted to the retrieval needs of advanced chemical materials ledger scenarios:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Advanced chemical ledgers have strict requirements for field accuracy. A value too low introduces irrelevant batch data, while a value too high may miss valid matching items. Adjust based on actual testing. |
| `limit` | Top 10 entries | A single ledger summary involves up to 10 associated batch data entries. Exceeding this number increases context redundancy and reduces report generation efficiency. |
| `searchMode` | vector+keyword hybrid mode | Supports both structured field matching and text semantic recall, adapting to the mixed retrieval needs of structured data and unstructured analysis reports in ledgers. |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Chemical data has strong field relevance. Reranking improves the sorting priority of high-matching batch data, preventing critical information from being pushed to lower positions. |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Queries for ledger summaries often require association with raw material grade aliases or industry-wide common abbreviations. Extended queries cover matching results for more synonymous expressions. |
| `collectionIds` | Bind three dedicated collections: production ledgers, quality inspection reports, and industry standards | Isolate knowledge base collections by data type, to avoid cross-category data interfering with retrieval accuracy. |

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: Calling the knowledge base interface returns empty results with no relevant ledger data. Cause: The application is not bound to the `collectionIds` of the corresponding knowledge base, and shared access permissions are not configured during cross-team deployment. This prevents the application from reading the target dataset.
- Phenomenon: Long-text batch analysis reports are split into text chunks that are too short or too long, making it impossible to match complete semantics during retrieval. Cause: Chunking rules are not adjusted for the structured fields of chemical ledgers, and default chunking parameters do not adapt to the semantic integrity of long fields.
- Phenomenon: After importing a single QA-formatted Markdown document, retrieval cannot accurately match the corresponding relationship between questions and answers. Cause: The question field is not set as a retrieval priority field, and `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` is not enabled. This leads to semantic matching deviations.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Check the knowledge base collection binding status, confirm that `collectionIds` includes the three target collections: production ledgers, quality inspection reports, and industry standards.
- Initiate a test query by entering a specific batch number, and verify that the returned fields and units match expected values.
- Adjust the `similarity` parameter range, compare the number of retrieval results under different values, and confirm that matching accuracy meets ledger summary requirements.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, view the sorted results after reranking, and confirm that high-priority batch data appears at the top.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
