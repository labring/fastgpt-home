# Fertilizer and Pesticide Ledger Summary and Report Generation: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Fertilizer and pesticide ledger data primarily comes from a company’s internal ERP production system, quality inspection laboratory system, sales outbound system, and the regulatory filing platform of agricultural and rural affairs departments. Production ledgers are updated daily, recording single-batch raw material inputs, process parameters, and finished product outputs. Sales ledgers are updated in real time alongside outbound operations, including flow direction, quantity, and customer information. Regulatory filing data is synchronized quarterly, covering product registration and compliance inspection information. Most individual ledger documents are structured records, containing fields such as production batch number, raw material name, input quantity, quality inspection indicator values, and outbound date. Some also include full text of quality inspection reports.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexing link
Differences in multi-source data formats require the indexing layer to support cross-system field alignment. This prevents embedding deviations caused by inconsistent field names or units. Differences in update frequencies across data sources require configuring layered index refresh strategies. These strategies distinguish between real-time updated sales ledgers, daily updated production ledgers, and quarterly updated filing data. The mixed presence of structured fields and unstructured text requires vector models to adapt to both numerical features and text description embeddings. This ensures that summary reports can associate quality inspection results with production records. The length of individual ledgers falls within a relatively short range. This requires avoiding excessive chunking that causes context breaks, while also ensuring that long-text quality inspection reports can be fully embedded.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | The text length of individual production ledgers and quality inspection reports mostly falls between 500-1000 characters. This range covers complete business information |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents breaks in production batch and quality inspection item information across chunks, and ensures contextual integrity of associated fields |
| `maxToken` | 4096 token | Adapts to the text length of single-batch ledger summary reports, and matches the input limits of mainstream vector models |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.78 | Distinguishes between same-category raw material ledgers and similar batch records, reducing false matches for non-target ledgers |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Covers production and sales ledgers from the past 7 days, meeting data source requirements for regular summary reports |
| `defaultToken` | 2048 token | Serves as the baseline length for default embedded context, adapting to the average text length of ledger fields |

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: A 503 status code is returned when calling the vector model, with a prompt that no available channel exists for the text-embedding-v3 model under the current default group. Cause: Channel permissions for the corresponding model are not configured, or resource quotas for the default group are exhausted.
- Symptom: Unit-inconsistent raw material ledger records appear in retrieval results. Cause: Units for raw material input quantities and finished product output quantities are not unified during the index preprocessing stage, leading to deviations in numerical feature vector embeddings.
- Symptom: Some recent sales ledgers are not retrieved after incremental index updates. Cause: Index trigger rules matching the real-time update rhythm of sales ledgers are not set, and only full index updates are executed on a fixed schedule.

## How to confirm correct configuration
- Upload a single production ledger and quality inspection report. Check if the embedded text blocks cover all complete fields, with no critical information truncated.
- Trigger an incremental index update. Verify that the updated retrieval results include the latest sales outbound records, matching the preset update time range.
- Adjust the similarity threshold. Validate whether the matching accuracy of retrieval results meets the business requirements for ledger summary.
- View the model call logs in FastGPT v4.17.0. Confirm that the `defaultToken` and `maxToken` configurations do not exceed the input limits of the selected vector model.

Note: The values listed above are common starting points. They should be evaluated against the reader’s own samples.
