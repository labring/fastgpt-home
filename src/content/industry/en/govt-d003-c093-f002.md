# Ledger Summary and Report Generation for Government and Enterprise IT Services: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Ledger data for government and enterprise IT services comes from internal business support systems. These systems include modules for project management, financial accounting, asset operations, government services, and more. Updates are primarily triggered by business nodes, with daily full or incremental synchronization as a supplementary method. Documents primarily take the form of structured tables or semi-structured text. Fields include ledger ID, responsible entity, execution progress, budget amount, deadline, and others. Units include items, ten thousand yuan, percentage, calendar days, and more. The length of a single record ranges from tens of characters to thousands of characters.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Vector Models and Indexing
The structured nature of government and enterprise ledgers requires vector models to support multi-field associated encoding. This prevents damage to the integrity of business logic from single-text splitting. Frequently updated business data requires indexes to support incremental synchronization. This reduces resource consumption and time costs associated with full index rebuilding. Compliance requirements for internal network deployment restrict use of external vector services. Local-deployed open-source models should be prioritized. Ledger records have wide variation in length. Chunking rules must be adjusted flexibly to retain complete business association information.

## How to Set Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the total field length of single ledger records for government and enterprise services, avoids splitting that breaks business field integrity |
| Chunk Overlap Length | 50–100 characters | Retains business context across chunks, prevents key associated information from being broken |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85 | Adapts to the structured characteristics of ledger data, filters redundant results with low matching degrees |
| `maxToken` | 4096 | Adapts to the input length limit of the bge-large model, ensures complete vector encoding |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Meets multi-source associated query needs during ledger summary, balances recall accuracy and performance |
| Reranking | Enabled | Optimizes recall accuracy after long document chunking, improves relevance for report generation |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Fields are lost or segments become disorganized when importing structured ledger documents. Cause: Chunking rules are not adjusted for structured ledger documents. Default chunk parameters break the integrity of business fields.
- Phenomenon: Index loading fails with out-of-vram error when multiple 3090 GPUs are deployed on the internal network. Cause: The GPU device used by the vector model is not specified. Default single-card occupation leads to underutilized resources.
- Phenomenon: 404 error is returned when configuring the baidu embedding-v1 model. Cause: Model forwarding rules are not configured correctly, or the model identifier does not match platform requirements.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Successful
- View the vector model run logs for FastGPT v4.17.0. Confirm that the specified bge-large model has been loaded, and vram usage matches expectations for single-card or multi-card configurations.
- Import a single complete structured ledger document. Check that core business fields such as ledger ID and responsible department are not lost in the chunked text.
- Run an associated query test. Confirm that the number of recall results matches the configured single search limit, and similarity scores fall within the set threshold range.
- Test the incremental update process. After importing new ledger data, confirm that the new data can be indexed normally and participate in subsequent summary queries.
