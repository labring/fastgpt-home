# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Property, Asset and State-owned Capital Management: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category's data looks like
Data for this category originates from state-owned assets supervision information systems, enterprise asset management systems, property right exchange systems, appraisal agency reports, and financial statements. The update frequency is monthly. Daily documents include property right registration forms, asset appraisal reports, property right transfer plans, on-site transaction announcements, and asset disposal approval documents. Data fields include asset ownership number, appraisal value, transaction approval status, on-site transaction compliance flag, and other items. Units include measurement units such as asset book original value and appraisal net value. Document structures mostly combine structured forms and unstructured reports.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
The monthly update requirement means multi-turn dialogue systems must support regular synchronization of data sources such as state-owned assets supervision information systems to avoid outdated data. Documents contain mixed structured form and unstructured report data, so multi-turn dialogue must retain key context such as asset ownership number and approval status to prevent loss of critical information across conversation turns. Differences in fields across data sources require prompt engineering to clearly distinguish between units such as book original value and appraisal net value and policy matching rules, to ensure retrieval results match actual asset status. High demand for compliance review means multi-turn dialogue must support multi-turn follow-up questions, gradually verifying policy basis in the transaction approval process to ensure alignment with regulatory requirements.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: Attachments uploaded after mirror packaging and deployment cannot be recognized, with no error prompt. Cause: Binding rules for configuration file link variables were not synchronized during deployment, leading to incorrect attachment parsing paths.
- Issue: Asset fields bound via `variables` appear empty during conversation. Cause: Field binding rules were not clearly defined in the prompt, leading to variables not being correctly associated with asset numbers.
- Issue: AI dialogue after the judgment node in advanced orchestration cannot access the initial user question. Cause: The user question node in the initial `messages` was not passed to the AI dialogue step.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a test question that includes an asset ownership number, confirm that retrieval results returned by the dialogue include policy documents for the corresponding asset.
- Upload a property right registration form and an asset appraisal report, confirm that the system correctly identifies fields in the documents and associates them with `variables` variables.
- Enable multi-turn dialogue, conduct three consecutive follow-up questions about policy basis for asset disposal, confirm that historical context is retained correctly.
- After deployment, upload an attachment, confirm that results returned by the system match those from the local development environment.
