# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Infectious Disease Information Reporting and Monitoring: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for this category include the Chinese Center for Disease Control and Prevention Information System, medical institution HIS systems, infectious disease reporting systems, and laboratory testing systems. The update frequency is daily. Daily processed documents include infectious disease reporting cards, epidemic analysis reports, case correction records, clustered epidemic reports, and epidemiological investigation reports. Data fields include basic patient information, diagnosis results, reporting time limits, correction status, reporting institution codes, and more. All fields must comply with unified format requirements for statutory infectious disease reporting, with no additional custom statistical indicators.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on deployment and upgrade
The data sources for this category are scattered and updated daily. During deployment, it is necessary to connect to interfaces from multiple heterogeneous systems. During upgrades, interface compatibility must be ensured to avoid interrupting daily data synchronization. There are various daily document types, and the field structures of different documents vary greatly. During deployment, it is necessary to configure applicable retrieval rules for different document types to ensure matching accuracy for policy basis Q&A. Statutory reporting requirements are strict. During deployment, it is necessary to build in compliant verification logic. During upgrades, the verification rule version must be updated synchronously to prevent risks of missed or delayed reporting. Additionally, data update frequency is high. During deployment, it is necessary to configure a reasonable incremental synchronization mechanism to avoid excessive system resource usage from full synchronization.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: Unable to create the database normally after deployment, with error prompts for connection timeout or insufficient permissions. Cause: Authentication information for `MONGODB_URI` or `PG_URL` is not configured correctly, or the database service has not opened the corresponding port.
- Symptom: The number of results when retrieving policy basis is abnormally low, and complete regulatory documents cannot be matched. Cause: The `DB_MAX_LINK` configuration is not adjusted, resulting in insufficient database connections and failure to pull full index data.
- Symptom: Retrieval results cannot be associated with the latest correction records, and Q&A content is outdated. Cause: No daily incremental synchronization task is configured, or the trigger frequency of the synchronization task does not match the daily update rhythm of this category.
