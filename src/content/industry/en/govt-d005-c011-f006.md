# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this data category looks like
This data category originates from social insurance handling systems, personal benefit records, labor relations and work injury identification systems, and the electronic social security card platform. It is updated daily. Daily processed documents include benefit application forms, payment period certificates, retirement approval forms, work injury identification materials, and unemployment insurance benefit application materials. Data fields cover payment periods, benefit calculation parameters, qualification review criteria, and more. The format follows official business system output standards, with no additional custom extended fields.

## Constraints on citation sources and traceability
Multi-source data updated daily requires the citation traceability workflow to support cross-system data association verification, ensuring cited content matches the latest business data. Most daily processed documents are structured forms, so avoid breaking form logic when splitting content, and preserve complete cited material. Fields focus on core parameters for benefit calculation and qualification review, so traceability citations must accurately align with the business definitions of corresponding fields, and cannot deviate from specific verification scenarios. Data directly links to benefit payments, so citation errors lead to direct consequences. Strictly control the confidence and source credibility of cited content.

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: Cited content does not appear in the answer body, or the citation field is empty. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled, or the `responseData` return format is not configured correctly.
- Symptom: The large model cannot receive context content after setting `quoteMaxToken` beyond the preset threshold. Cause: The system context limit is exceeded, so recalled cited content cannot be spliced normally.
- Symptom: Garbled characters or unrecognizable content appear during retrieval after uploading CSV-format business documents. Cause: The correct character encoding was not specified during upload, so the document content cannot be parsed correctly.

## How to confirm configurations are correctly set
- Initiate a test query related to benefit verification, and check if the answer body includes complete cited original text and source path.
- Adjust `quoteMaxToken` to different value ranges, verify that context splicing works normally with no truncation or loss.
- Upload a standard business document of this category, confirm after retrieval that cited content fully covers the document's core fields.
- Check system logs to confirm that the citation source identifier matches the actual data source.
