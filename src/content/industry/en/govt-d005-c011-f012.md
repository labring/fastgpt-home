# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data primarily comes from social insurance management systems, personal benefit records, labor relations and work injury identification systems, and the electronic social security card platform. The update frequency is daily. Daily associated documents include benefit application forms, payment period certificates, retirement approval forms, work injury identification materials, and unemployment insurance benefit application materials. Data fields cover payment duration, benefit calculation basis, qualification determination conditions, and more. The category includes two types of content: structured form fields and unstructured supporting documents. There is no fixed uniform single-document length standard.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the deployment and upgrade phase
Access requirements for multiple system data sources require configuring adaptation rules for multi-source data synchronization to avoid data conflicts or omissions. The daily update frequency requires setting a reasonable incremental synchronization cycle during deployment to ensure the timeliness of policies and data. Document types include structured forms and unstructured supporting materials, so parsing rules compatible with both types of documents must be configured to ensure complete field extraction. The accuracy of fields related to benefit calculation directly affects determination results. Data verification logic must be configured during deployment to avoid subsequent risks caused by incorrect association of policy bases.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Request timeout error appears during conversation. Cause: No reasonable timeout parameters set. Multi-system data synchronization or knowledge base retrieval time exceeds the preset threshold.
- Phenomenon: Unable to call tools to complete policy basis matching. Cause: Not upgraded to FastGPT v4.17.0, or proxy tools configured incorrectly, not adapted to the calling logic of the current version.
- Phenomenon: Retrieved policy clauses do not match the data to be verified. Cause: Document fields and policy basis mapping rules not configured correctly, leading to incorrect content association.

## How to confirm proper configuration
- Check the connection status of multi-source data sources, confirm that all social insurance-related systems can be accessed normally.
- Trigger an incremental synchronization task, verify the integrity of synchronized data fields and update time.
- Initiate a simulated benefit verification query to confirm that the retrieval results include corresponding policy bases and data associations.
- Check system operation logs to confirm no frequent connection timeouts or data parsing errors.
