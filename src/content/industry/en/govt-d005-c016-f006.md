# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Medical Insurance Expense Settlement Audit: Citation Sources and Traceability
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from the national medical security information platform, hospital HIS and medical insurance interfaces, medical insurance catalog and coding library, and cross-provincial medical treatment settlement platform. The update frequency is daily.

Daily processed documents include medical insurance settlement lists, expense details, medical record homepages, prescriptions, rejection lists, and settlement reconciliation statements. Data fields include diagnosis and treatment item and drug and consumable codes, expense details, insured person identity identifiers, settlement amounts, and more. Both single and bulk data must match official coding libraries to complete compliance verification.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" link
Daily updated official coding libraries and business documents require traceability to link to the latest version of medical insurance policies and catalogs, to ensure the timeliness of audit basis. Field differences across multiple types of business documents require traceability to match corresponding data sources by document type. For example, prescriptions must link to medical insurance prescription review specifications, and settlement lists must match national medical insurance settlement standards.

When processing bulk data, avoid duplicate traceability. Also ensure that traceability results for single settlement data correspond one-to-one with business documents. Failure to do this may lead to fund payment deviations or rejection disputes.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Traceability results returned by the conversation interface do not include original text access portals. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled, and original text links are not embedded in returned content.
- Traceability results truncate key clauses of policy texts. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` value is too small, failing to cover core audit basis of full policy texts.
- Traceability results returned during bulk settlement audit do not match the target documents. Cause: Data sources are not filtered by business document type, resulting in irrelevant medical insurance policy content being linked.

## How to verify a complete configuration
- Initiate a policy Q&A for medical insurance expense settlement audit, check if returned results include the specific original text clauses corresponding to the `detail` field.
- Check if the returned `responseData` includes identifiers such as original text links, document types and update dates.
- Test multiple different types of business documents, confirm that returned traceability results match the business scenario of the current document.
- Verify the single recall limit setting, confirm that the number of returned traceability results matches the preset value.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
