# Q&A on Policy and Regulatory Basis for Enrollment Registration and Payment Verification: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and public security and civil affairs data sharing channels. The update frequency is daily. Commonly involved documents include enrollment registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification forms, and personal benefit records. Data subjects include social insurance handling agencies, human resources and social security bureaus, tax departments, and employer personnel and finance departments. Most documents are structured tables, with fields such as personnel identity, labor relationship, payment base, verified amount, and others.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Vector Models and Indexes Link
Daily updated data requires indexes to support incremental synchronization, to avoid excessive cluster resource usage from full indexing. The dense field nature of structured documents requires retaining key information integrity during chunking, to avoid splitting associated fields across chunks. The field alignment requirement for multi-source data requires indexes to support grouping by business dimension, ensuring that only the dataset for the corresponding category is called during retrieval. Additionally, data for this category is directly linked to personal benefits and payment compliance. This requires the confidence level of retrieval results to match business standards, with no field missing or matching errors allowed.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Matches the field density of structured documents for this category, avoids splitting key associated information |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retains associated fields such as personnel identity and payment base across chunks, improves retrieval coherence |
| `defaultToken` | 1024 token | Adapts to the average length of single document chunks, balances embedding accuracy and call costs |
| `maxToken` | 8000 token | Matches the context length requirement for policy Q&A, covers complete regulatory basis content |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Filters low-match irrelevant documents, ensures relevance of retrieval results to policy basis |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8 entries | Controls the number of returned results, avoids excessive redundant information interfering with business judgments

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Index tasks remain in a queued state for extended periods. The cause is that incremental index trigger rules are not configured. Full synchronization of data from multiple systems each day consumes large amounts of cluster resources, preventing new tasks from executing promptly.
- Irrelevant category document content appears in retrieval results. The cause is that a dedicated index group is not created based on the business dimension of enrollment registration and payment verification, leading to retrieval calls to the full dataset.
- Embedding model call returns an error. The cause is that call parameters for Alibaba multimodal-embedding-v1 are not configured correctly, and the `defaultToken` setting and interface permissions are not verified.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- View index task logs in FastGPT v4.17.0, confirm that the incremental index trigger frequency matches the daily data update rhythm.
- Launch a policy consultation test for enrollment registration or payment verification, check that retrieval results only include document fields from this category.
- Adjust the similarity threshold, observe changes in retrieval result matching accuracy, confirm that the threshold setting meets business requirements.
- Verify the embedding model call link, confirm that Alibaba multimodal-embedding-v1 returns normal results.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
