# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data primarily comes from social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. Daily documents include insurance registration forms, premium base declaration forms, personnel increase/decrease forms, social insurance premium verification notices, and personal benefit records. Fields cover core content such as personnel identity identifiers, labor relationship status, premium base, and unit registration information. The dataset updates daily, and centers on association information between personnel and units across the full insurance participation and premium payment process.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the knowledge base retrieval and recall link
Daily updated data sources require the knowledge base synchronization frequency to match the update rhythm, to avoid recalling expired data. Data from multiple system sources has field heterogeneity, so the retrieval link must associate field identifiers corresponding to each system to reduce cross-source data confusion. Form-based documents have a structured format but many field dimensions, so retrieval must precisely match core fields such as personnel identity and premium base to avoid irrelevant recalled content. The dataset focuses on the insurance participation and premium payment link, so retrieval must strictly limit scope and exclude non-target text.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Accurately match policy content related to insurance participation and premium payment, filter low-relevance non-target text |
| `limit` | Top 8–12 results | Cover policy basis for multiple scenarios including personnel adjustments and base declaration, avoid insufficient or excessive recalled entries |
| `searchMode` | Hybrid vector search + keyword search | Adapt to structured fields of form-based documents and keyword characteristics of policy text, improve retrieval accuracy |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled, retain top 5 results after re-ranking | Perform secondary sorting on recalled results, prioritize displaying core policy clauses and optimize information acquisition efficiency |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Expand query term scope, cover synonymous expressions of "insurance registration" and "premium verification" to match query needs across different scenarios |
| `collectionIds` | Bind exclusive knowledge base collections for this category | Isolate policy documents from non-target categories, avoid recalling content unrelated to insurance participation and premium payment |

This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Retrieval responses time out, exceeding 10 seconds. Cause: The retrieval mode is not optimized for this category’s multi-system data sources, and an overly large recall range increases computational load.
- Phenomenon: Recalled results include policy text related to benefit verification. Cause: The exclusive knowledge base collection for this category is not bound, confusing policy documents from the insurance participation and premium payment link with those from the benefit verification link.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results fail to match policy requirements related to personnel identity. Cause: `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` is not enabled, so synonymous query terms for personnel identity verification are not covered.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Check the binding configuration of the knowledge base collection, confirm it only includes documents related to insurance registration and premium verification for this category.
- Trigger a test query, verify that response time meets business scenario requirements, and adjust configuration items to match actual load.
- Verify the field coverage of recalled results, confirm core information such as personnel identity and premium base is included.
- Verify that the re-ranking function is active, confirm core policy clauses have higher sorting priority than secondary content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
