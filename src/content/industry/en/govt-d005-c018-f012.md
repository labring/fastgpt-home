# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Social Insurance Enrollment and Payment Verification: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this page covers
- Client industry: Social insurance enrollment and payment verification
- Business direction: Policy and regulatory basis Q&A
- Capability area: Deployment and upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category comes from social insurance management systems, tax social insurance collection systems, market entity registration information, and public security and civil affairs data sharing. The update frequency is daily. Commonly involved documents include enrollment registration forms, payment base declaration forms, staff change forms, social insurance payment verification slips, and personal benefit records. Data fields include the unit's unified social credit code, staff ID number, payment base, verified amount, enrollment status, and others. Different documents have different field structures, and some data requires cross-system cross-verification.

## Constraints during deployment and upgrade
Multi-source cross-system data sources require configuring multiple trusted database connections during deployment to adapt to different types of government data storage formats. The daily update frequency requires configuring stable scheduled synchronization logic during deployment to avoid data lag that affects the accuracy of policy Q&A. Diverse document types with sensitive information require configuring encrypted storage and access permission control during deployment, while adapting to parsing rules for different documents. Government data compliance requirements require strictly following data security specifications during deployment to avoid sensitive data leaks.

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: When deploying across machines, running FastGPT on machine A and connecting to a model or data source on machine B, creating a new application throws a connection timeout error. Cause: Cross-machine network access permissions are not configured, causing `PG_URL` or `MONGODB_URI` to fail to connect to government system databases.
- Phenomenon: After enabling the thinking switch, policy and regulatory basis Q&A results always display the full thinking process without folding. Cause: The `FE_DOMAIN` parameter is not configured correctly, causing the frontend script to fail to load the folding logic for the thinking process.
- Phenomenon: After submitting a policy query, the returned policy basis content is missing or incomplete. Cause: The `DB_MAX_LINK` setting exceeds the concurrent connection limit of the government database, causing partial data query failures.

## How to verify successful configuration
- Use the system's built-in data source testing tool to verify that `PG_URL` and `MONGODB_URI` can correctly pull basic data on staff enrollment and payment verification.
- Upload a local enrollment registration form document to test whether the system can correctly parse document fields and associate corresponding policy basis content.
- Trigger a manual synchronization task to verify that data updates meet the daily update frequency requirement.
- Enable the thinking switch, submit a query about enrollment registration policy, and confirm that the folding logic for the thinking process works correctly.
