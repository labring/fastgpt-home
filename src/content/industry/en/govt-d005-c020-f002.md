# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Territorial Spatial Planning Compilation and Implementation Evaluation: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for this category mainly comes from the territorial spatial basic information platform, natural resource survey and monitoring data, population and economic statistics, and remote sensing images. Commonly processed documents include territorial spatial overall plans, detailed plans, special plans, planning implementation evaluation reports, and public opinion summaries. Most documents have structures that include spatial layout descriptions, constraint indicator explanations, implementation progress records, and multi-party opinion texts. The update frequency is once per year.

## What constraints these characteristics place on the vector models and indexes module
The annual update frequency of this category means vector indexes do not need frequent incremental updates, and batch rebuilding can be carried out to match the annual planning cycle. The multi-source and heterogeneous document types, including short-text public opinions and long-document planning reports, require a chunking strategy that balances the semantic integrity of short texts and the logical coherence of long documents. The official nature of the data sources requires that index recall results must accurately match the original policy and regulatory texts, to avoid semantic deviations affecting planning approval.

## Three common misconfigurations
- An error of the format `{"error":{"code":"Invalid` occurs during testing. The cause is that when accessing a multimodal Embedding model, the input format of the text and spatial association data of documents in this category is not correctly configured, causing the model to fail to recognize non-standard input content.
- After rebuilding the index for the knowledge base, some policy provisions in planning reports are not correctly recalled. The cause is that a reasonable overlap length is not set during chunking, leading to truncation of the context of long paragraphs and loss of associated information for key policy provisions.
- After upgrading to a new version, old vector database data cannot be directly imported into the new index. The cause is that the vector dimension definitions of the old and new versions are inconsistent, and no dimension alignment processing is performed on the old data.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Upload a single typical planning report, check whether the chunked text fragments retain complete policy provisions and logical structure.
- Input policy-related test questions, verify whether the number and relevance of recall results meet the preset configuration requirements.
- Perform a vector database migration operation, confirm that old data can be normally retrieved after being imported into the new index, with no error messages.
- Call the API interface to initiate a retrieval request, confirm that the format and fields of the returned results meet expectations.
