# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Centralized Treasury Payment and Reimbursement Audit: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What Data for This Category Includes
Core data for this category comes from the centralized treasury payment system, unified budget management system, electronic invoice platform, and contract management system. Daily processed documents include payment applications, fund appropriation plans, invoices, contracts, acceptance reports, reimbursement approval forms, and payment vouchers. Each individual document typically contains structured fields such as budget index code, economic classification code, document number, and contract amount, plus unstructured attachments. Data is updated daily. The lifecycle of single payment-related data spans from fund appropriation plan application to final payment voucher generation, covering the full execution cycle.

## Constraints on the Citation Sources and Traceability Link
Multi-source data access requires the traceability link to adapt to field formats and data structures of different systems, to avoid cross-system matching discrepancies. Daily update frequency requires traceability indexes to support incremental synchronization, ensuring that the latest fund appropriation plans, payment vouchers and other data can be quickly retrieved. The characteristic that a single payment is associated with multiple types of documents requires traceability results to be accurately bound to specific payment applications, invoices or contracts, to avoid generalized matching. Additionally, the severity of potential errors requires that traceability results can be directly linked to original business documents, to meet audit traceability requirements.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Setting too small a value for `quoteMaxToken` causes complete policy basis text to be truncated, and the citation fragments returned in the interface are incomplete. This occurs because the total text length of multiple documents associated with a single payment for this category is not accounted for.
- Setting the knowledge base sync frequency to hourly or full real-time sync causes excessive system resource usage and interface timeout errors. This occurs because the daily update frequency of data for this category is not matched, and unnecessary data synchronization is performed excessively.
- Attempting to directly pass HTTP return content from non-business systems as policy basis into the Q&A process causes traceability results to fail to link to original business documents, and the citation source field returned in the interface is empty. This occurs because the designated business system data source for this category is not used, and field information of corresponding business documents is not bound.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a policy Q&A for a simulated payment application, check if the returned results include business fields such as corresponding budget index codes and document numbers, to confirm that the `responseData` configuration is active.
- View the knowledge base sync logs, confirm that incremental sync is triggered at 2:00 AM daily, and there are no abnormal full sync records, to confirm that the sync frequency configuration is correct.
- Search for policy questions related to a completed payment voucher, check if the returned results include fragment citation markers from original documents, to confirm that the `detail` configuration is active.
- Adjust `quoteMaxToken` to different values, observe changes in the total token count of returned citations, to confirm that the configuration parameter is active.
