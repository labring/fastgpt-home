# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Land Use Rights, Mining Rights, and State-Owned Property Transactions: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category's data looks like
The data sources for this category are the national public resource trading platform, natural resource trading systems, property right exchange systems, and state-owned asset supervision systems. Updates are synchronized daily. Daily documents include transfer announcements, trading guidelines, bid application documents, margin vouchers, transaction confirmation documents, listing result announcements, and property right transfer specifications. Each document typically includes fields such as target ownership number, trading node information, official links, margin amount, and bid qualification requirements. Units involved include square meters, ten thousand yuan, and other resource asset-related measurement standards.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on source citation and traceability
Multi-source data synchronization requires that traceability must link to the official source identifiers of the corresponding systems to avoid confusion from cross-system citations. The daily update rhythm requires the traceability chain to support real-time or near-real-time document version verification, ensuring that the returned policy basis is the latest valid version. Documents include clear official link fields, which can directly serve as traceability anchors. Reference to draft documents that have not completed public disclosure must be avoided. Fields such as ownership and margin in transaction documents are highly correlated. When citing, the referenced content must fully match the target information, otherwise disputes such as invalid transactions or asset loss may arise.

## How to configure settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Documents in this category have relatively long content, so sufficient tokens must be reserved to fully present policy basis and traceability information |
| `responseData` | Enable automatic official link extraction | Documents in this category come with built-in official source links, which can directly serve as traceability anchors without additional external address splicing |
| `detail` | Enable full source traceability | Specific issuing authority and issuing time of the referenced document must be clearly marked, to comply with compliance requirements for transaction review |
| Single search limit | Top 5 results | Transaction-based policy basis requires precise matching of target information, to avoid excessive irrelevant results interfering with review and judgment |
| Context timeout threshold | `600 seconds` | Complete chain of long document retrieval and traceability must be ensured, to avoid traceability process interruption due to timeout |

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Symptom: The traceability information returned by the AI only displays document fragments, without including the official original link. Cause: The official link extraction configuration corresponding to `responseData` is not enabled. Only the document text content is captured, making direct access to the original trading platform page impossible.
- Symptom: The number of traceable documents returned by the search exceeds expectations, and includes draft documents that have not been officially disclosed. Cause: The single search limit is not set, or draft transaction documents are not filtered, resulting in non-compliant documents being included in the traceability scope.
- Symptom: The policy basis generated by the AI does not match the actual target ownership information, and the traceability chain times out and interrupts. Cause: The value of `quoteMaxToken` is too small to fully load the ownership-related content of long documents, or the context timeout threshold is set too low, resulting in incomplete long document retrieval.
