# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Student Aid and Subsidy Review: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data primarily comes from the national student aid management information system, school student status systems, civil affairs low-income and special hardship data, and fiscal payment systems. Commonly processed documents include family economic hardship assessment forms, aid application forms, scholarship and grant review forms, disbursement lists, and public notice materials. Data updates follow a semester cycle. Most documents contain student status identifiers, family economic status descriptions, qualification determination fields, and disbursement detail entries, with no additional custom statistical fields.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexing link?
Multi-system linked data sources require indexes to support cross-field associated matching, to ensure consistency verification of student status, low-income, and fiscal data. The semester-level update rhythm means indexes must adapt to batch incremental updates, avoiding additional time costs from full reconstruction. Documents include both structured forms and unstructured policy texts, so separate chunking strategies must be adapted for both types of content to ensure the integrity of policy clauses and the relevance of form fields. Policy texts related to qualification determination require precise recall, to avoid review bias caused by fuzzy matching. Disbursement list data requires reasonable retrieval weight settings to ensure the accuracy of associated retrieval.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | This category of documents includes policy texts and structured forms. This range balances the integrity of policy clauses and the relevance of form fields |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Policy texts have logical connections across paragraphs. This overlap length prevents key information from being truncated during chunking |
| Single Search Limit | Top 5 results | Review scenarios require precise matching of policy clauses and application materials. Too many recall results will increase the workload of manual verification |
| Maximum Context | 3000–4000 tokens | Policy basis Q&A requires integrating multiple policy text fragments. This range covers a complete policy logic chain |
| Timeout Threshold | 600 seconds | Adapts to the time consumption requirements of multi-system data retrieval, avoiding index interruption caused by call timeouts |
| Reranking Priority | Prioritize policy text weights | Policy basis Q&A requires prioritizing matching policy clauses to ensure the accuracy of review basis |

## Three common errors
- Default indexes generated after manually inserting a knowledge base disappear after several hours: Index persistence storage is not configured. System-generated temporary indexes are not written to the persistent directory, and are lost after restart or scheduled cleanup.
- After connecting qwen3-embedding-8b, the file status consistently shows "indexing": The embedding model's call address and key are not correctly configured in FastGPT v4.17.0, or the model response timed out.
- Retrieval results include irrelevant policy clauses: The overlap length setting is insufficient, causing cross-paragraph policy association logic to be split, making it impossible to match complete semantics during vector recall.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Manually insert a policy document, check if a corresponding entry is generated in the index list, to confirm that the persistent configuration is effective.
- Call the embedding model interface, confirm that the returned vector data format meets FastGPT requirements, to verify model compatibility.
- Submit a review question related to aid qualification, check that the number of retrieval result recall entries matches the single search limit configuration.
- Wait for one semester update cycle, check if the incrementally updated index includes newly imported semester data, to confirm that the update logic is working properly.
