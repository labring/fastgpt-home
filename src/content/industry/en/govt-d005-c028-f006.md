# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category comes primarily from four official systems: the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. The update frequency is daily.
Documents processed daily include establishment registration application forms, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials. The overall structure primarily uses structured form fields paired with explanatory text. Fields include subject name, residence address, shareholder information, license type, application number, and similar items. Only basic character and number units are included, with no complex measurement dimensions.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the source citation and traceability link
The official nature of data sources requires that traceability be precisely bound to specified systems, to avoid introducing non-compliant data sources. The daily update rhythm requires that the retrieval link pull the latest data in real time, and cannot rely on expired cache. The structured nature of documents requires that traceability distinguish between field-level citations and full-text citations, to match different business verification scenarios. At the same time, error consequences are directly linked to entity registration qualifications, so cited policy basis must be completely accurate, with no mismatches or omissions.

## How to configure settings

| `responseData` | Only return associated data source entries | Must clearly mark the cited source system or document type, to meet traceability verification requirements for regulatory scenarios |
| `detail` | Full citation of document fragments | Must display original policy fragments for manual verification, to match compliance review needs in market supervision scenarios |
| Maximum number of recalled entries | `Top 3–5 entries` | Policy basis for this category has a clear hierarchy. Too many recalled entries will disrupt judgment efficiency for core basis |
| Data source filtering rules | Restrict to enterprise registration/credit publicity systems | Only retrieve data from specified official systems, to avoid introducing non-compliant external reference content |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Citation traceability results do not include data source system names, and fields are empty. Cause: Data source filtering rules are not configured, leading to retrieval of external data not specified for this category.
- Phenomenon: Policy citation fragments in generated responses are truncated, and cannot fully display basis content. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` value is too small, failing to cover core clauses of policy documents.
- Phenomenon: After the variable reference function is configured, the temperature parameter setting button disappears and cannot be adjusted. Cause: The advanced parameter configuration switch is not enabled in the variable reference module of FastGPT v4.17.0, causing related setting items to be hidden.
