# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Urban Environmental Sanitation and Urban Management Law Enforcement Cases: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data is primarily sourced from urban management law enforcement platforms, urban operation grid platforms, video surveillance, and public complaint work orders, and is updated daily. Daily documents include inspection records, case filing approval forms, on-site photos, rectification orders, penalty decisions, and case closure reports. Core fields cover case unique identifiers, types of illegal acts, responsible party information, on-site evidence collection content, rectification and penalty execution status. Some documents include multimedia evidence materials.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Citation Sources and Traceability
Data sources are scattered and include unstructured on-site materials. This requires the citation traceability process to associate homologous case data across different platforms, and avoid cross-source information conflicts. The daily update frequency requires the traceability logic to support incremental synchronization, ensuring that the latest rectification or penalty records are included in the citation scope in a timely manner. Diverse document types including on-site photos and other non-text content require matching corresponding data association rules to ensure that the complete evidence chain of the case can be fully presented during citation. Additionally, law enforcement scenarios have high requirements for evidence accuracy. The traceability process must strictly match the correspondence between policy basis and case documents, and avoid law enforcement disputes caused by incorrect citations.

| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | Matches the average length of a single core law enforcement document for this category, ensuring complete citation of policy basis and case details |
| `responseData` | Enable associated field mapping | Supports binding unique identifiers such as case numbers and penalty document numbers, enabling precise traceability of cross-platform data |
| `detail` | Enable multi-source data verification | Adapts to association rules for multi-source data including inspection records, complaint work orders, and law enforcement documents |
| Number of citations displayed | Top 3 core documents | Aligns with the business logic of prioritizing core evidence display in urban management scenarios |
| Context window length | 6000–8000 characters | Accommodates the complete content of multiple associated documents, avoiding truncation of critical policy basis |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- A 400 parameter format error occurs when viewing knowledge base citations in chat responses. The cause is that the field mapping rules for `responseData` are not configured correctly, making it impossible to extract valid traceability identifiers.
- Redundant search process content is forced to appear in responses. The phenomenon is that redundant search input and response fields appear at the end of the answer. The cause is that only citation content related to policy basis and case documents is not filtered, and unnecessary intermediate process information is retained.
- Citation content exceeds the expected length. The phenomenon is that a single citation exceeds the actual character range of the document. The cause is that the value of `quoteMaxToken` is set too large, and it does not match the actual length characteristics of documents in this category.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Input policy-related questions about urban management law enforcement cases, and check whether the returned results include traceable unique identifiers such as case numbers and penalty document numbers.
- View the citation module in the response, confirm that only content related to policy basis and case documents is displayed, and there are no redundant search process fields.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, test whether citations of different lengths can display core content completely without truncation or overflow.
- Import a simulated inspection record and penalty decision, verify that multi-source data association citation works normally, and ensure that case information from different sources can be correctly associated.
