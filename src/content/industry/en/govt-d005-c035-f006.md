# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Pollution Discharge Permit Management: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category covers all documents related to the entire post-issuance management process of pollution discharge units, including pollution discharge permit application forms, implementation reports, self-monitoring plans, ledger records, change applications, and permit copies. Data sources include the National Pollution Discharge Permit Management Information Platform, corporate environmental protection ledgers, online monitoring systems, and environmental statistics systems. The update frequency is quarterly. Data fields include permit limits, discharge outlet information, monitoring data, implementation status, and more. All fields follow unified specifications from the ecological environment department, with no additional custom units.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Citation Sources and Traceability
Dispersed data from multiple sources requires precise labeling of the platform or ledger type each cited content belongs to during traceability, to avoid mixing information from different systems. The quarterly update feature requires traceability information to be checked for timeliness, ensuring cited permits or implementation data are currently valid. There are various daily document types, and field logic differs across documents. It is necessary to match the structure of the corresponding document during traceability, ensuring cited content corresponds to business links such as application, implementation, and monitoring. The post-issuance supervision attribute of pollution discharge permits requires traceability information to be fully traceable, retaining the acquisition path of original data to meet compliance verification requirements.

## How to Configure Settings

| Maximum Context | 800–1200 characters | Matches the average length of a single pollution discharge permit document, to avoid truncating critical permit limits or monitoring data |
| `quoteMaxToken` | First 300 tokens | Limits the length of cited passages, ensuring traceability information is concise and covers core policy basis |
| `responseData` | Enabled | Forces return of original fields of citation sources and affiliated platform information, to meet compliance verification needs |
| `detail` | Enabled | Displays specific fields of cited content and corresponding document types, to facilitate verification of business logic |
| Maximum Single Search Count | Top 5 entries | Matches the quarterly updated data volume, avoiding introduction of redundant or expired non-current permit information |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Search returns content unrelated to pollution discharge permit policies, and does not associate correct document sources. Cause: Failed to limit the search scope to the pollution discharge permit-specific knowledge base, or failed to configure document chunking rules that match business fields.
- Phenomenon: Traceability information does not display data source platforms or document types, with corresponding fields empty. Cause: Did not enable the `responseData` configuration item, and did not force return of source association information.
- Phenomenon: Cited passages are too long, containing redundant non-policy basis content. Cause: Did not set the `quoteMaxToken` parameter, or set its value outside a reasonable range.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Initiate a query about pollution discharge permit limits, check if the returned result associates source information of the corresponding document, to confirm that the `responseData` configuration is active.
- View the length of cited passages, confirm whether it meets the display needs of the business scenario, and adjust the `quoteMaxToken` value to a reasonable range.
- Test a query unrelated to non-pollution discharge permit content, confirm that no irrelevant results are returned, to verify that the search scope restriction configuration is active.
- Cross-check the document type in the traceability information, confirm that it matches the business link involved in the query, such as implementation reports or self-monitoring plans.
