# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Government Service Item Catalog and Service Guide Maintenance: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
The data for this category mainly comes from official authoritative sources such as the national basic catalog of government service items, administrative licensing item list, and local government service item databases. Daily maintenance documents include the basic catalog of government service items, implementation lists, service guides, standard material catalogs, flowcharts, and frequently asked questions. The data update cycle is usually monthly. Each individual document contains standardized fields such as item code, acceptance conditions, application material list, and service process. Some documents must meet the unified format requirements of the superior catalog.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the deployment and upgrade link
The monthly update frequency requires enabling incremental data synchronization during deployment, to avoid resource usage and time consumption caused by full re-import. The structural differences between multiple document types require compatibility with different field parsing rules during upgrades, to avoid field matching errors or data loss. The format constraints of official data sources require configuring unified format verification logic during deployment, to ensure imported data meets the standard requirements for government affairs items. At the same time, the time-consuming nature of the data verification link requires adjusting related resource allocation during upgrades, to ensure the stability of the maintenance process and avoid affecting the update progress of daily government service items.

## How to configure the settings
This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0, and the following configuration items are formulated based on the storage and synchronization needs of government affairs data.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PG_URL` | `postgresql://username:password@host:5432/fastgpt_v4?sslmode=require` | Adapts to the storage and query of structured government affairs item data, and meets the format requirements of official data sources |
| `REDIS_URL` | `redis://:authpassword@redis-host:6379/1` | Caches frequently called government affairs item metadata to reduce database access pressure |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | `20–30 connections` | Matches the data volume scale of monthly incremental updates, to avoid overloading the database connection pool |
| `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` | `32-bit random encryption string` | Ensures download permissions for service guide attachments, preventing unauthorized access to sensitive government affairs documents |
| `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` | Fill in according to the local private object storage service address | Stores unstructured government affairs documents such as flowcharts and standard material catalogs, to meet data storage compliance requirements |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Symptom: A 404 error is returned when calling the model after deployment. Interface tests pass, but the Q&A process fails. Cause: `PG_URL` or `REDIS_URL` is not configured correctly, causing abnormal connections to the vector database or cache service, and failing to load the Q&A context for government affairs items.
- Symptom: Imported government service guide documents do not trigger document recall, and Q&A results only return general content. Cause: The `DB_MAX_LINK` parameter is not set reasonably. Insufficient connection pool count causes incremental synchronized documents to not be fully loaded into the knowledge base.
- Symptom: The system password cannot be modified after Docker deployment. An error indicating permission issues is prompted after attempting a reset. Cause: The `ROOT_KEY` parameter is not configured correctly, causing background permission verification to fail, and preventing password modification operations.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Correct
- Execute the database connection test command to verify the connectivity of the `PG_URL` and `REDIS_URL` configurations, and confirm there are no connection timeout or permission errors.
- Upload a test service guide document to trigger the incremental synchronization process, and check if the background log shows that the document was parsed successfully.
- Access the frontend address configured in `FE_DOMAIN`, attempt to log in to the backend and modify related configurations, and verify that permission verification takes effect.
- Call the model to initiate a question related to government service items, and confirm that the recalled content includes fragments of the locally uploaded service guide document.
