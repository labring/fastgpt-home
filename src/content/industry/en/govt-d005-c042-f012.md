# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for this category comes from enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, law and regulation repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. Daily generated documents include compliance risk lists, legal review opinions for contracts, legal opinions on major matters, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation results. Data update frequency is monthly. Most document structures contain fields such as subject information, issue description, compliance basis, and handling suggestions. There is no unified fixed unit, and fields are adjusted based on document type and business scenario.

## Constraints imposed on deployment and upgrade phases
The monthly data update requirement mandates configuring a stable incremental synchronization link during deployment, to ensure uninterrupted updates for the compliance risk database and law and regulation repository. Multiple types of structured documents require support for multi-field parsing and indexing, to avoid failure to associate compliance bases caused by extracting only plain text. Scenarios where the Q&A system participates in business review early require the recall accuracy of the system to match the timeliness of business decisions. Sufficient concurrent processing resources must be reserved during deployment. Data sources involve multiple internal systems, so the upgrade phase must verify compatibility of cross-system data synchronization, to avoid data loss or format errors. Since the consequences of errors are severe, additional log retention and exception alert configurations must be added during deployment and upgrade phases, to quickly locate compliance-related Q&A deviations.

## Configuration settings
This page applies to the deployment and upgrade process of FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MONGODB_URI` | Connection string pointing to an enterprise compliance-specific MongoDB instance, configured with read-write separation | Stores unstructured documents such as compliance risk lists and legal opinions. Read-write separation prevents resource contention between business queries and synchronization tasks |
| `REDIS_URL` | Configured as an enterprise internal cache instance, with key expiration time set to 3600 seconds | Caches frequently accessed law and regulation entries and compliance bases, adapting to the monthly update data source cycle |
| `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` | Endpoint address pointing to an enterprise internal object storage service | Stores attached files such as compliance review forms and violation report investigation results, complying with internal enterprise data storage specifications |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | Set to 20–30 | Adapts to the concurrent connection requirements of monthly batch synchronization, preventing synchronization interruptions caused by exhausted database connections |
| `ROOT_KEY` | Generate a key of required complexity in accordance with enterprise internal permission management rules | Ensures the security of system background management permissions, matching the strict permission control requirements of legal and compliance departments |

## Three common configuration errors
- Symptom: Returns a `400 Bad Request` error when calling an external model, with an authentication failure prompt. Cause: `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` or `ROOT_KEY` is not configured in the parameter format required by the model, causing the request to fail authentication checks.
- Symptom: Cannot load the compliance document directory after upgrading the version, with a path does not exist prompt. Cause: The mapping configuration between `FE_DOMAIN` and internal file storage was not updated synchronously, causing the frontend to fail to locate the storage path of compliance documents.
- Symptom: Monthly data synchronization task times out, and synchronization progress stalls. Cause: `DB_MAX_LINK` is configured with too low a value, which cannot support the concurrent connections required for batch synchronization, leading to exhaustion of the database connection pool.

## How to verify successful configuration
- Execute a manual synchronization task for compliance data, check the database connection logs, and confirm that the connection status of `MONGODB_URI` and `REDIS_URL` is normal.
- Initiate a test query for a compliance risk list, verify that the returned results include matching compliance bases and document sources, and confirm that the recall logic is working.
- Check the system background exception alert configuration, confirm that the threshold setting for `DB_MAX_LINK` meets the concurrent requirements of the current synchronization task, and no connection exhaustion alerts are triggered.
- Restart the service after upgrading the version, access the frontend address corresponding to `FE_DOMAIN`, confirm that the compliance document list loads normally, and no path error prompts appear.
