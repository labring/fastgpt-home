# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Administrative Licensing and Approval: Tool Calling and Plugins

## What the data for this category looks like
This category's data comes from the national integrated government service platform item database, administrative licensing item list, departmental business approval systems, and electronic certificate repository. The update frequency is daily. Daily documents include service guides, application forms, commitment letters, acceptance notices, correction notification letters, administrative licensing decision letters, and license certificates. Data is bounded by statutory licensing items, and includes standardized fields such as item setting basis, handling conditions, and application material list. No extra custom statistical fields are included.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the tool calling and plugins workflow
The daily data update requirement means tool calling must connect to real-time data sources, and avoid using static cached old item content. The multi-type document structure requires plugins to adapt to parsing different formats of approval documents, to ensure core fields such as setting basis and material requirements can be extracted. The characteristic of being bounded by statutory licensing items requires that tool calling must accurately bind the unique identifier of the corresponding item, to prevent cross-item matching errors. The high standardization of data fields can reduce parsing redundancy, but the field definitions of the item database must be strictly followed to avoid matching failures caused by custom fields.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: Unable to locate the plugin configuration entry in the system interface, and uploaded plugins do not appear in the available tool list. Reason: The plugin file was not placed in the FastGPT-specified plugin storage directory, or the service was not restarted to load the plugin.
- Phenomenon: The policy basis returned during tool calling does not match the administrative licensing item queried by the user. Reason: `toolChoice` was not configured to the correct trigger mode, or the item unique identifier was not accurately mapped.
- Phenomenon: Exported plugin configuration cannot be imported again, or parameters are lost after import. Reason: OPENAI V3 format schema metadata was not retained, or parameter definitions were not synchronized after modifying the configuration.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Enter the FastGPT plugin management page, confirm that the administrative licensing and approval tool is enabled and its status shows normal.
- Input a test question containing a specific administrative licensing item name, trigger tool calling, and check whether the returned parameters include the item unique identifier.
- After calling the tool, verify whether the returned policy basis content is consistent with the public content in the national integrated government service platform item database.
- Simulate a timeout scenario, confirm that the tool call is completed within 600 seconds and returns valid results.
