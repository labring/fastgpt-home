## Common Configuration Errors
- The symptom is that the model recalls multiple irrelevant documents and cannot accurately locate the specified performance evaluation report. The cause is that no recall rules for specific documents have been configured, leading the recall scope to cover the entire knowledge base.
- The symptom is an interface returning a 429 status code, with current-limiting errors triggered during concurrent calls. The cause is that the call frequency for the fiscal expenditure performance evaluation scenario has not been adapted, and reasonable concurrency control has not been set in the configuration.
- The symptom is generated responses containing policy basis deviations and insufficiently rigorous expression. The cause is that `temperature` is set too high, causing the model's generated content to deviate from the rigor requirements of official documents.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Initiate a query targeting a specific performance evaluation report, and verify whether the recall results only include relevant content from the target document.
- Conduct a multi-concurrency test, and confirm that no current-limiting errors are triggered during interface calls.
- Generate a test response, and verify whether the rigor of the content meets the requirements of policy basis.
- Check whether the configurations for `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` and `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` match the official information provided by the platform.
