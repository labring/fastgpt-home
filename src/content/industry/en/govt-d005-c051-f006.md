# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Road Transport Business Licensing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Typical entities include transportation bureau approval departments, road transport management agencies, and transport enterprises. Daily documents cover business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, and road transport business licenses. Data sources include the road transport administration system, the national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and the vehicle and personnel database. The update frequency is daily. Document fields include application number, vehicle identification code, personnel qualification number, safety system clauses, and similar items. Structure varies significantly across different document types, with a core focus on road transport business licensing access and ongoing compliance requirements.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Citation Sources and Traceability Link?
Multi-source data updated daily requires the traceability link to prioritize retrieving the latest content, to avoid citing expired license or policy documents. Citing expired materials may lead to errors in business verification. Diverse document types with distinct structures require filtering search results by document type, to ensure cited content is strongly relevant to road transport business licensing application and verification scenarios. Core fields center on qualifications and compliance requirements. Traceability must accurately match policy basis with specific document content, rather than citing irrelevant clauses broadly. Business errors have serious consequences: the traceability link must ensure the accuracy of cited sources, otherwise it may cause license rejection, enterprise operational disruption, and similar issues.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After setting the maximum number of citations to top 5, the output includes irrelevant traffic law enforcement policy documents. Cause: Failed to limit the retrieval data source to the road transport business licensing exclusive knowledge base, mixing in policy content from other categories.
- Phenomenon: After binding the knowledge base to the reference variable, the application returns a parameter error during runtime. Cause: Failed to correctly match the knowledge base ID with the data source range configured for the application, or the variable format does not meet system requirements.
- Phenomenon: Retrieved cited content does not include the latest vehicle dynamic data. Cause: Failed to configure data update checks to trigger once per day, resulting in citation of expired business documents.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Initiate a policy consultation regarding a road transport business license change, check that all cited documents in the output come from the specified road transport business licensing knowledge base.
- Review the token length of cited content, confirm it does not exceed the `quoteMaxToken` configuration value, and that content is complete without truncation.
- Simulate the timing of business data updates, initiate a similar consultation, check that the system can retrieve the latest policy and license materials.
- Enter a low-relevance question such as "How to apply for a catering business license", confirm the system does not return irrelevant road transport-related cited content.
