# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Department Final Accounts and Financial Reports: Tool Calling and Plugins

This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data comes from the budget management integrated system, centralized treasury payment system, accounting system, and asset management system. The update cycle is annual. Daily produced documents include department final account statements, balance sheets, income and expenditure final account statements, final account explanations, and government financial reports. The document structure covers core fields such as revenue and expenditure details, asset and liability details, and budget execution difference explanations. Units mostly use yuan or ten thousand yuan, and must strictly follow unified fiscal caliber specifications.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "tool calling and plugins" link?
The annual update cycle requires that tool calling be limited to the dataset for the corresponding year, to avoid cross-cycle data confusion. Mixed input of multiple types of financial documents requires plugins to support both structured report field extraction and semantic parsing of unstructured explanatory text. The multi-system data source feature requires tool calling to connect to interface permissions of specified systems to ensure data source compliance. The high error risk in the final account link requires that tool call results include data traceability information to facilitate subsequent verification and audit.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Tool calling cannot parse Word-format final account explanation documents. Cause: No Word-format document parsing plugin is configured, resulting in failure to correctly extract unstructured text.
- Phenomenon: Tool calling logic in the workflow does not meet expectations; the model does not independently select tools. Cause: `toolChoice` is not configured to `auto` mode, resulting in a mismatch between tool calling rules and business processes.
- Phenomenon: No notification response occurs after configuring the DingTalk webhook plugin. Cause: Correct interface signature and permission verification parameters are not configured, resulting in the system being unable to connect to the DingTalk notification channel.
