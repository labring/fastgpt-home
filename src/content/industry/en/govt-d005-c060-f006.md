# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Warehousing and Logistics: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Warehousing and logistics policy and regulatory basis data primarily comes from specialized warehousing industry policies issued by the National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Transport, and Ministry of Commerce, local warehousing management regulations, and standard documents from national logistics industry associations. Update frequency follows this rhythm: national policies are revised 1 to 2 times per year, local policies are updated quarterly, and industry standards release revised versions annually. Each policy is structured as a single document, including fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable warehousing scenarios, clause details, and compliance requirements. Single document lengths typically range from 500 to 2000 characters.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on citation and traceability workflows
Warehousing logistics policy data includes clear applicable scenarios, effective dates, and territorial requirements, which create three core constraints for traceability. First, filter recall results based on the warehousing scenario associated with the query to prevent incorrect citation of cross-scenario policies. Second, match policies at the appropriate level based on the region where the query originates, to ensure traceability results align with territorial management rules. Third, include metadata such as policy document number and issuing authority in traceability displays. Returning only clause content fails to meet compliance verification needs. Additionally, policies are regularly revised or abolished, so the latest policy list must be synchronized to avoid citing invalid content.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: Citation policy document information is not shown in the official chat interface, but displays correctly on the debug page. Cause: The formal page citation synchronization setting is not enabled, and traceability configurations are only completed in the debug environment.
- Phenomenon: Returned traceability information only includes clause content, and does not show metadata such as policy document number and issuing authority. Cause: The configuration to return metadata is not enabled, and only plain text content is extracted.
- Phenomenon: Recalled policy clauses do not match the current warehousing scenario, such as citing hazardous chemical warehousing policies for general warehousing scenarios. Cause: Recall results are not filtered based on the applicable scope of the warehousing scenario, leading to incorrect recall of cross-scenario policies.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Initiate a query related to warehousing policies, and check if returned results include policy metadata such as document number and issuing authority.
- Switch to the officially released chat interface, and confirm that cited policy document information matches the content shown on the debug page.
- Adjust the single search limit value, and verify that the number of returned citations falls within the configured range.
- Trigger the traceability view action, and confirm that the complete original policy text and associated metadata can be retrieved normally.
