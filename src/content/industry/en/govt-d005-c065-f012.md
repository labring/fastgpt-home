# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Survey and Design: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the survey and design sector is primarily sourced from official announcements released by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, provincial and municipal housing and urban-rural development departments, national standards and industry specifications for the survey and design sector, and local construction management measures. Data updates trigger irregularly alongside policy revisions and the release of new specifications, with no fixed cycle.

## Constraints Imposed on Deployment and Upgrade by These Characteristics
The long-form, multi-field nature of survey and design policy data, plus its irregular update schedule, creates clear constraints for the deployment and upgrade phase.
First, most individual policy entries are thousands of characters long. Custom vector chunking configuration is required to avoid truncating core clauses.
Second, data updates trigger irregularly alongside policy revisions. The upgrade process must support incremental synchronization, eliminating the need for a full re-import of the entire dataset each time.
Third, the data includes multiple metadata fields such as policy document number, effective date, and applicable region. Corresponding indexes must be configured in the database to ensure efficient filtering and querying.
Additionally, trusted access rules must be configured during deployment for officially sourced data to prevent unauthorized modification.

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: A `connection timed out` error appears during Docker Compose deployment, with logs showing a failed connection to MongoDB. Cause: The host address and port for `MONGODB_URI` were not configured correctly, or the local MongoDB service is not running.
- Phenomenon: Historical content still displays in queries after clearing conversation records. Cause: Session data stored in Redis cache was not cleaned synchronously, or corresponding service instances were not restarted.
- Phenomenon: The number of matching results returned by policy Q&A is far lower than expected. Cause: The `DB_MAX_LINK` parameter was not set correctly, leading to insufficient database connections and an inability to pull full matching data.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Execute the `docker ps` command to confirm that dependency service containers such as MongoDB and Redis are running.
- Log into the FastGPT platform, import a survey and design policy PDF document, and check for successful storage and generation of corresponding vector data.
- Submit a query related to survey and design policies, and confirm that returned results include correct metadata fields such as policy document number and effective date.
- Review platform backend logs to confirm that database connection count does not exceed the `DB_MAX_LINK` configuration value, with no connection overflow errors.
