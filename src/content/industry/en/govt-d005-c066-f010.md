# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Advanced Chemical Materials: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What this category’s data looks like
Data for policy and regulatory basis of advanced chemical materials mainly comes from industrial guidance documents, access standards, subsidy policies and industry specification documents released by the State Council ministries and local industry and information technology departments. The update schedule synchronizes with the official release of policies. Most documents are in PDF or WORD format. Their structure includes release document number, issuing authority, effective date, clause entries, applicable sub-categories and core quantitative requirements. Fields include unique policy identifier, issuing entity, effective time, applicable advanced chemical materials sub-categories and core clause content. Units involve document numbering, date formats, production capacity indicators and similar items. This page is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on knowledge base retrieval and recall
Advanced chemical materials policy documents are mostly long texts, with clauses tied to document numbers and effective conditions. When chunking, avoid splitting cross-clause content, otherwise complete policy basis cannot be associated during retrieval. Policies are categorized by effective time for different applicable scenarios, so retrieval must add effective time filtering constraints to ensure only currently valid policies are recalled. Policies for specific sub-categories have exclusive clauses, so retrieval must match the applicable advanced chemical materials sub-category field to narrow the recall scope. Large policy files have a high number of chunks, which may cause vectorization anomalies, and update frequency fluctuates with policy releases, so dynamic index triggering logic must be adapted.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When uploading an advanced chemical materials policy PDF larger than 10MB, some chunks show vectorization anomalies after chunking, with a "vectorization failed" prompt displayed in the interface. Cause: Policy documents are mostly dense long texts, and the number of chunks exceeds the default processing threshold, leading to abnormal semantic vector generation for some chunks.
- Phenomenon: After batch uploading multiple advanced chemical materials policy files and restarting the server, the relevant knowledge base status shows "not ready" with no automatic indexing progress. Cause: Index tasks triggered by batch uploads are not persistently stored, so incomplete indexing queues are not restored after server restart.
- Phenomenon: Standard images embedded in imported advanced chemical materials policy documents cannot be displayed in retrieval results. Cause: Image links in the documents are local paths, which have not been uploaded to the storage service associated with the knowledge base, so resources cannot be loaded during retrieval.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Correct
- A single advanced chemical materials policy file larger than 10MB may be uploaded, then the number of chunks and vectorization status checked to confirm no abnormal prompts.
- A retrieval targeting a specific policy clause may be initiated, with verification that the applicable scope and policy effective time of the recalled results match the configuration requirements.
- More than 3 policy files may be batch uploaded, the service restarted, then the knowledge base status checked to confirm automatic indexing tasks start normally.
- Embedded policy attachment images in retrieval results may be checked to confirm they load and display correctly.
