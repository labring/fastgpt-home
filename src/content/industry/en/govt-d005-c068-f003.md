# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Pharmaceutical Distribution: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for the pharmaceutical distribution sector comes primarily from official announcements of the National Medical Products Administration, Ministry of Commerce, medical insurance bureaus of various provinces and cities, and industry regulatory authorities. Data updates happen in real time as policies are released, with no fixed cycle. Document structures include fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, scope of application, and core clauses. Some local policies add regional implementation details. Field units typically include document number codes, date formats, and clause serial numbers. Individual document lengths vary widely, from hundreds to thousands of characters.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Wide variation in the length of pharmaceutical distribution policy documents and their regional differences create requirements for multi-turn dialogue. The dialogue must continuously track policy application scenarios from user follow-up questions, to avoid losing context. Policy update frequency is not fixed. Prompts must explicitly require the model to only reference currently effective clauses, to prevent recall of expired content. Users often ask for implementation details of specific clauses during multi-turn interactions. Prompts must guide the model to link back to the policy scope of the initial query, to avoid straying from exclusive regulatory requirements for pharmaceutical distribution. Regional policies also require passing user location parameters during dialogue. This ensures recalled content matches accurately.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Context | First 8 turns of dialogue + 1200–1500 characters of knowledge base recall content | Pharmaceutical distribution policy documents are relatively long. Retaining multi-turn dialogue scenario information prevents context overflow from harming answer accuracy |
| Question Optimization | Enabled, calibrated based on actual testing | User follow-up questions often involve refinements of policy clauses. Optimization enables precise recall of corresponding content and improves question-answer matching accuracy |
| `chatId` | Bound to the unique identifier of the current session | Multi-turn dialogue policy reference context must be tracked to ensure consistent policy references within the same session |
| `messages` | Retain the most recent 10 interactions in session order | Pharmaceutical policy Q&A requires coherent scenario descriptions. Retaining recent interactions avoids interference from old dialogue with current query logic |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 recall results | There are a large number of pharmaceutical policy documents. Filtering the most relevant clauses avoids information overload and ensures answers focus on core content |
| `variables` | Inject policy application region parameters for the current session | Pharmaceutical policies have regional differences. Injecting region parameters enables accurate matching of policy implementation requirements for the user’s location |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Guidance prompts do not take effect, and the model does not focus on the exclusive policy scope of pharmaceutical distribution. Cause: No policy type parameter is injected via `variables`, and the prompt does not explicitly limit the Q&A scope.
- Phenomenon: Returned results include expired policy document numbers, leading to insufficient answer accuracy. Cause: No knowledge base regular update trigger mechanism is configured, resulting in recalled policy documents not being synchronized to the latest version.
- Phenomenon: Answers are truncated after multi-turn dialogue, and key clauses are not fully displayed. Cause: The number of retained `messages` is not limited, and excessive session history exceeds the model’s context window.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate an initial query, enter "Drug storage management regulations for pharmaceutical distribution enterprises", and verify that the returned content includes currently effective policy clauses with no expired content.
- Initiate 3 consecutive follow-up questions, such as "Regional implementation details for a certain area", "Penalty standards" in sequence, and verify that the session context is correctly retained, with coherent answers linked to the initial query.
- View the knowledge base update log to confirm that policy documents are automatically updated according to release time, with no omissions or delays.
- Test the `chatId` binding function in FastGPT v4.17.0, and verify that multi-turn interaction context within the same session is not lost.
