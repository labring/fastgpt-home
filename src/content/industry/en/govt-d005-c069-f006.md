# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation: Source Citation and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for hazardous chemicals storage and transportation policy and regulatory basis comes from public regulations, national standards, and local regulatory rules issued by official regulatory departments including the Ministry of Emergency Management, Ministry of Transport, and Ministry of Ecology and Environment. Update frequency follows regulatory policy adjustments. National standards are revised every 3 to 5 years. Departmental regulations and local rules are released as needed. Document structure includes fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable storage and transportation scenarios, technical parameters, and compliance requirements. These fields use industry-specific units, such as flash point (℃), storage volume (tons), and transportation time limit (hours).

## Constraints for source citation and traceability
Hazardous chemicals storage and transportation policy data comes from multi-department official documents. Different documents have different effective times and applicable regions. Traceability requires precise matching of document numbers and issuing entities to avoid cross-level policy confusion. Policy documents include industry-specific units such as flash point and storage volume. Recalled content must fully retain field units to ensure accurate compliance statements. Policy update frequency follows regulatory adjustments. The knowledge base must synchronize latest documents regularly, and mark effective dates during traceability to avoid citing expired or invalid compliance requirements. A single policy document may include multiple storage and transportation scenario requirements. Fragments must be split by scenario to prevent cited content from mismatching current storage and transportation links.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The number of context entries displayed on the interface does not match the number actually sent to the calling API. Cause: The mapping relationship between `quoteMaxToken` and single recall count was not configured correctly, leading to abnormal truncation logic.
- Phenomenon: Document fragments exceeding the preset length in the knowledge base are cited normally. Cause: The split threshold was not set according to the structure of hazardous chemicals policy documents, so long fragments were not properly truncated and exceeded context window limits.
- Phenomenon: Traceability information such as policy document number and issuing authority is not displayed in cited content. Cause: The `detail` configuration item was not enabled, so the returned results only contain compliance text and lack official traceability identifiers.
