# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
The policy data for this category mainly comes from power generation equipment industry policies and national standard announcements issued by the National Energy Administration and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, as well as specialized standard documents from the China Electrical Equipment Industry Association. Updates are completed within 7 working days after a policy is officially released or revised. The document structure is divided into three categories: original policy texts, official interpretations, and supporting implementation rules. Fields include policy number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable equipment type, and core clause summary. Units use standard official document formatting and date formats.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
The policy number and applicable equipment type fields require tracking specific power generation equipment categories mentioned by users during multi-turn dialogue, to avoid mixing policies across scenarios such as thermal power, wind power, and nuclear power.
The effective date field requires embedding timeliness verification logic in the dialogue flow, to ensure only valid and non-repealed clauses are called.
The three-category document structure requires prompts to explicitly prioritize retrieving official interpretation content, to reduce the reading burden of long original texts for users.
The update rhythm requirement restricts the dialogue system to regularly synchronize the latest policy library, to avoid retrieving expired policy documents.
The long text nature of original policy texts requires segmenting and truncating retrieved content during multi-turn dialogue, to adapt to context window limits.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Markdown table output shows truncated content, with `...[hide X char` displayed at the end. Cause: No context truncation rules adapted for long texts are configured, causing the system to automatically truncate reply content that exceeds the window limit.
- Phenomenon: `insufficient_quota Current group upstream load is saturated, please try again later` error is returned. Cause: No limit is set on the number of concurrent requests per session, or no reasonable timeout retry mechanism is configured, causing peak requests to exceed the system load limit.
- Phenomenon: Conversation records are not saved to the database after the client closes the SSE connection. Cause: `chatId` is not correctly bound and session persistence logic is not configured, and the context write operation for the `messages` array is not completed when the request is interrupted.
