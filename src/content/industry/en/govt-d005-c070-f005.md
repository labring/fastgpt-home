# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Tool Calling and Plugins

This configuration is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like

Power generation equipment manufacturing policy and regulatory basis data is mainly sourced from specialized equipment manufacturing policies, industry standard documents released by the National Energy Administration and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, as well as supporting implementation rules issued by local industry and information technology departments. Data is updated simultaneously with the official release of policies, with no fixed update cycle. The document structure includes fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable equipment categories (such as thermal power steam turbines, wind turbine towers), core clauses, quantified technical indicators, etc. Some fields come with standard units such as megawatts, tons, and years.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the tool calling and plugins workflow

The fixed structure of official policy documents requires that tool calling must accurately filter by fields such as document number and effective date, to avoid returning irrelevant content. The segmented applicable equipment category field requires that plugins must support associating policy data by equipment type, to improve query targeting. The lack of a fixed update cycle requires that the tool calling link must support regular synchronization of official data sources to ensure content timeliness. Quantified technical indicators with standard units require that the result processing step of tool calling must retain unit information to avoid parameter confusion.

## Three Common Misconfigurations

- Issue: Tool call returns unstable results, with some key clauses missing or duplicated. Cause: The result segment extraction rule is not configured, and long documents are truncated or loaded repeatedly, leading to deviations in subsequent processing.
- Issue: mysql database reports an error when executing multi-line queries. Cause: The number of query statements per batch is not limited; some deployment environments do not support combined execution of too many statements, triggering database permission or syntax restrictions.
- Issue: Tool call returns non-official policy interpretation content. Cause: The official domain range for web scraping tools is not restricted, and third-party non-authoritative content is mixed in, affecting answer accuracy.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct

- Initiate a query that includes a specific power generation equipment type and a known policy document number, and check whether the content returned by the tool call includes the core clauses of the corresponding policy.
- Test the execution of a multi-line SQL combination statement including insert and select, and confirm that there are no errors and the returned results meet expectations.
- Check the tool call logs to confirm that only policy content from specified official domains such as the National Energy Administration and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology is scraped.
- Adjust the tool call timeout threshold to verify that queries for lengthy policy documents can be completed normally.
