# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the power generation equipment manufacturing sector comes from official public documents of the National Energy Administration, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, plus industry standards released by the China Electricity Council. Data updates take effect immediately when official policies are released. Industry standards are revised every 1 to 2 years.
Document structure includes fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, release date, applicable equipment type (for example, steam turbine units, generator components), compliance clauses, and associated penalty clauses. Field units use industry-standard expressions like clause numbers and applicable unit power ratings. This document is written based on the configuration logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the citation and traceability process
Policy data for power generation equipment manufacturing has clear industry segmentation tags and precise clause positioning. The traceability process must bind unique identifiers such as policy document numbers and issuing authorities. This avoids vague traceability caused by only citing body content.
Segmented fields for applicable equipment types require retrieval to associate corresponding equipment category parameters. This filters irrelevant policy content and reduces traceability noise.
Policy updates occur at a relatively high frequency. The knowledge base synchronization cycle must match official release rhythms to ensure traceability content timeliness.
Documents include attachments and layered clauses. The traceability process must support associating attachment content and accurately locating specific clause positions.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- The globally configured datasetid in the workflow fails to take effect in the knowledge base retrieval node, and the corresponding field is empty. This occurs when the global variable mapping is not bound in the data source configuration of the retrieval node, and only the global variable is set in the global variable panel without associating it with the node parameter.
- The AI output policy content includes non-original decorative expressions, which is inconsistent with the original knowledge base text. This happens when the `responseData` configuration is not enabled, causing the large model to perform secondary processing on the retrieved original text.
- The token count passed to the retrieval node exceeds the preset threshold, leading to workflow timeout or errors. This is caused by not configuring `quoteMaxToken` to limit the token length of a single traceability entry, resulting in full-length long documents being passed to the large model.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Enter the configuration panel of the knowledge base retrieval node, check whether the global variable is correctly mapped to the data source parameter, trigger a test retrieval, and confirm whether the data source identifier of the returned result matches the preset value.
- After enabling the `responseData` configuration, initiate a test Q&A, and check whether the returned content is completely consistent with the original knowledge base text, without any additional decorative statements.
- View the workflow operation log, confirm that the token count passed by the retrieval node does not exceed the preset value of `quoteMaxToken`, with no abnormal truncation or excessive consumption.
- Adjust the single search limit parameter, initiate multiple rounds of test Q&A, and confirm that the number of returned policy clauses meets the configuration requirements.
