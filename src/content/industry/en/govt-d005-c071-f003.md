# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Groups: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

This configuration is written for FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for power generation groups comes from official management documents issued by group headquarters and industry supervision policy documents released by superior competent authorities. Data updates adjust with new policy releases and internal system revisions, with no fixed cycle. Most document structures are official public documents with document numbers, effective dates, chapters and clauses. Fields include document number, issuing entity, effective time, applicable scope, and specific clause content. Units are primarily clause serial numbers and date formats, with no universal quantitative statistical units.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Multi-Turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The structured clauses and long text nature of official documents requires accurate location of specific clauses during multi-turn dialogue, avoiding vague general responses. The lack of a fixed update cycle for policies requires dialogue contexts to track the currently referenced policy version, preventing confusion between cross-version clauses. The presence of the applicable scope field requires prompts to clearly define the dialogue’s retrieval scope, only calling the group’s internal systems and corresponding industry policies. Long document splitting must retain clause context to avoid logical breaks that harm multi-turn dialogue coherence. Multi-turn dialogue must also associate retrieval results from historical conversations, ensuring previously referenced document information can be traced during user follow-ups.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: An uncaught exception prompt pops up in the workflow AI dialogue component. Cause: The configuration file was not correctly associated with the policy library storage path and model interface during private deployment, resulting in failure to load specified data.
- Phenomenon: Parsing failure is returned after uploading policy files in dialogue. Cause: The input parameters of the document parsing tool were not correctly bound in the workflow, causing the system to fail to recognize the file address.
- Phenomenon: Previously referenced policy clauses cannot be traced during multi-turn dialogue. Cause: The `messages` field was not used to store context, resulting in loss of historical association information for the dialogue session.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate an inquiry referencing specific policy clauses, and verify that the response includes accurate document numbers and clause content.
- Conduct consecutive multi-turn follow-up questions, and verify that the system can trace previously referenced policy document information.
- Upload a test internal policy document for the group, and verify that the workflow can parse it normally and include it in the retrieval scope.
- Check the binding parameters of the configuration items, confirm that `variables` are associated with the dedicated policy library ID to avoid cross-library retrieval.
