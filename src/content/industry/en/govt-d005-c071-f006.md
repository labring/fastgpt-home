# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Groups: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Power generation group policy and regulatory basis data mainly originates from official red-headed documents released by the group, power industry regulatory rules issued by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, and public notices from the National Energy Administration and local energy regulatory agencies.
Updates sync with policy release dates, with no fixed cycle. Updates trigger when policies are revised or new documents are published.
Documents are structured text, with fields including document name, document number, issuing body, effective date, clause number, and specific content. Clauses are labeled by article, paragraph, and subparagraph levels. Single entry length varies widely, ranging from tens to thousands of characters.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability link
The structured nature of policy data requires traceability to exact clauses, not just document names. Otherwise, the requirements of power generation group compliance reviews cannot be met.
No fixed update cycle means traceability systems must support on-demand knowledge base synchronization, to avoid retrieving expired policy documents.
Wide variation in single entry length requires chunking and retrieval steps to preserve clause hierarchy. This ensures cited text blocks map to exact clause positions.
Document numbers and effective dates in metadata are core compliance review basis. Traceability steps must return this information to ensure responses meet compliance review requirements.

| `responseData` | Include document name, document number, effective date | Policy traceability requires core metadata to meet compliance review information requirements |
| `detail` | Enabled | Matches the structured characteristics of policy documents to accurately locate specific clauses |
| Knowledge base sync frequency | Manually triggered per policy release node | Power generation group policy updates have no fixed cycle; on-demand sync avoids expired data |
| Single retrieval limit | Top 3–5 entries | Policy-related questions usually link to a small number of core documents, avoiding redundant information interfering with responses |

## Three Common Mistakes
- No traceability information is shown at the end of responses. This occurs because the citation display switch is not enabled, so the system does not return traceability data.
- Cited text blocks only show document names, and do not include core metadata such as document numbers and effective dates. This occurs because metadata fields are not configured for return, and only document name information is extracted.
- When calling the chat interface, the number of returned citation lists cannot be controlled, and all matching results are returned by default. This occurs because the single retrieval limit parameter is not set, leading to excessive redundant information being returned.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Complete
- Submit a question related to power generation group policies, and check whether metadata such as document name, document number, and effective date is attached at the end of the response.
- Check whether cited text blocks are labeled with specific clause numbers, to confirm the retrieval hierarchy meets expectations.
- Configure a knowledge base search node in the workflow, pass a custom knowledge base name variable, and test whether the node can correctly call the target knowledge base.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` parameter, and verify that the length of the returned cited text matches the set value range.
