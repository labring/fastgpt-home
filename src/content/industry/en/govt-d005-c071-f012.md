# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Generation Groups: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
Power generation group policy and regulatory basis data mainly comes from internally released official management measures and implementation rules, as well as industry compliance policy documents issued by the National Energy Administration and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission. The data update rhythm is full synchronization within 1 to 3 business days after a major policy is released, plus monthly routine verification and updates. Each single document usually includes fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, scope of application, clause details, and attachment notes. Clause content is mostly structured chapter entries, with no unified fixed units. Some fields need to match internal group-specific identifiers such as unit numbers and business classifications.

## Constraints imposed on deployment and upgrade workflows
Power generation group policy data characteristics impose clear constraints on deployment and upgrade workflows.
Multi-source heterogeneous data sources require cross-data-source synchronization adapters to be configured during deployment, to avoid format conflicts between internal documents and external public policies.
Long-text, structured document structures require chunking rules adapted to complete semantic units during vector database import, to prevent clause fragmentation.
Low-frequency but critical update requirements require incremental synchronization configuration support during upgrades, to reduce resource consumption from full re-imports.
Matching requirements for dedicated fields require database indexes to cover internal group business identifier fields, to improve retrieval accuracy.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: Only 127.0.0.1 can access the service within the local area network, and 192.168.0.111 cannot open the page. Cause: `FE_DOMAIN` is not correctly configured as a local area network address. Frontend resources are bound to the local loopback address by default, so they cannot provide local area network access externally.
- Phenomenon: The number of retrieved policy clauses after vector import far exceeds the preset range, leading to high answer redundancy. Cause: The single search limit configuration is not adjusted. Default parameters for general scenarios are used, and the multi-clause association characteristic of power generation group policy documents is not adapted.
- Phenomenon: After deployment is complete, the default promotion QR code is displayed at the bottom of the page, and it cannot be hidden through conventional configuration. Cause: The default promotion component is not overwritten in the frontend static resource configuration, and the platform’s built-in open source display link is not closed.

## How to verify successful configuration
- Access the configured `FE_DOMAIN` address, confirm that the frontend page loads normally, with no resource loading failure error prompts.
- Upload an internal group policy document, trigger the vector import process, and check that corresponding data entries and embedded vectors are generated in the database.
- Initiate a query related to group policies, confirm that the number of returned results matches the preset single search limit configuration, with no abnormal error logs.
- View deployment logs, confirm that the database connection pool and Redis cache service have normal connection status, with no timeout or connection failure prompts.
