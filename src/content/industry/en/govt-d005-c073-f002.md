# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Industrial Funds: Vector Models and Indexing

## What this category of data looks like
State-owned industrial fund policy and regulatory basis data primarily comes from official public documents issued by state-owned assets supervision and administration commissions, finance departments, and local state-owned assets supervision institutions. It also includes internal system documents such as fund contribution agreements and management charters. Document formats are mostly PDF or official web pages, with wide variations in total length. Fields include document number, issuing authority, effective date, clause hierarchy, original text body, and more. Clauses are layered and marked by article, paragraph, and sub-paragraph. Data updates have no fixed cycle, and are triggered by the release of new national and local policies or adjustments to internal fund systems.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexing link
The wide length range of individual documents requires chunking strategies adapted to both short and long texts, to avoid single chunks exceeding model limits. The rich metadata fields require clear specification that vector modeling only targets the main text content, to avoid metadata interfering with retrieval accuracy. Data updates have no fixed cycle, so incremental indexing trigger logic must be configured to only sync updated files, reducing resource consumption from full indexing. Policy documents have hierarchical and repealed relationships, so metadata tags must be associated during indexing to facilitate filtering invalid content during subsequent retrieval.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: After enabling the reranking model, no changes are observed in online recall test results. Cause: The associated switch for the reranking model was not enabled synchronously in the knowledge base indexing configuration, or the reranking model's call parameters do not match the dimensions of the main vector model.
- Phenomenon: An error during indexing prompts parameter overlimit. Cause: The chunk length exceeds the range allowed by `maxToken`, leading to failure in generating single-chunk vectors.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results include repealed policy documents. Cause: No metadata filtering rules were configured, and the effective and repealed status tags of policy files were not associated.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Access the knowledge base indexing configuration page in FastGPT v4.17.0, verify that the set values for chunk length and overlap length match the preset plan.
- Initiate a single test retrieval, check whether the number of returned results conforms to the single search limit configuration.
- After enabling the reranking model, compare the sorting differences of retrieval results before and after enabling to confirm the reranking logic is active.
- Upload a test policy document to verify that incremental indexing can normally sync updated content.
