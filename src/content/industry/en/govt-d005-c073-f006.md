# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Industrial Funds: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
State-owned industrial fund policy and regulatory basis Q&A data draws from official normative documents released by state-owned asset supervision and administration departments and finance departments. It also uses internal system documents such as fund charters and annual management measures. The platform syncs updates within 1-3 business days after a new policy or regulatory revision launches. Each single document includes fields such as document number, issuing body, effective date, clause level and applicable scenario. Units cover document numbering, currency units, time units and other categories. Some documents contain nested attachment clause structures.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Citation Sources and Traceability Link
The traceability process must verify the official authority of the issuing body, since data sources include official normative documents and internal fund rules. It must ensure cited content is not altered or replaced. Documents include effective dates and clause levels. Users must annotate corresponding information when citing, to avoid outputting expired policy content or content that does not match the scenario. The nested attachment structure requires precise matching of attachments and main clauses during traceability, to prevent misplaced extracted content. The update cycle requirement restricts knowledge base sync frequency. Corresponding entries must be updated promptly after policy release, to ensure answers use the latest valid policy basis.

## How to Set the Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Basis for This Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Similarity Threshold | `0.75–0.85` | Policy documents for state-owned industrial funds have dense professional terminology. A higher threshold filters irrelevant content and ensures accurate citation matching |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `8000–12000 token` | Single policy documents are lengthy. This range covers complete clauses and associated attachment content, avoiding truncation of core basis |
| Maximum Number of Citations | Top 3–5 | Core policy basis is usually concentrated in 3-5 official documents. Too many entries lead to redundant answers and scattered focus |
| `detail` | Enabled | Metadata such as document number and issuing body must be displayed to provide complete traceability verification basis for users |
| `responseData` | Return Full Metadata | Fields such as effective date and issuing body of policy data are core information for traceability, and cannot be omitted |

## Three Common Mistakes
- When configuring `quoteMaxToken`, only fixed preset options are available, and custom ranges cannot be set. The phenomenon is that the interface only provides discrete preset values such as 100 and 900, and custom values between 300 and 800 cannot be entered. The reason is that some versions of the interface do not offer a custom input box, only retaining preset options.
- An error occurs when trying to cite policy content returned via HTTP as a knowledge base source. The phenomenon is that the system prompts that unstructured HTTP response content cannot be parsed. The reason is that the HTTP returned content is not preprocessed into a standard document format, and metadata fields such as document number and issuing body are not associated.
- A large number of low-match citations are still output after setting the minimum relevance to 1. The phenomenon is that irrelevant policy clauses appear in the answer. The reason is that `detail` verification is not enabled or the issuing body field is not associated, so the system cannot filter non-core matching results.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Set Correctly
- Check the citation list at the end of the answer, and confirm each citation includes metadata such as document number, issuing body and effective date.
- Input a policy-related question for testing, and verify that the cited documents are the latest released valid versions, with no expired policy content.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, observe the length change of the cited content in the answer to verify that the configuration takes effect.
- Check the knowledge base sync log, and confirm that newly released policy files have been synced and updated within 1-3 business days.
