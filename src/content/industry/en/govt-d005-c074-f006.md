# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Asset Management Companies: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
State-owned asset management policy and regulatory basis data is primarily sourced from state-owned capital supervision documents released by the national state-owned asset supervision commission and the ministry of finance, implementation rules issued by local state-owned asset supervision departments, internal state-owned asset management measures, and annual supervision review reports of companies. Data is synchronized in real time when new policies are released, and calibrated on a regular monthly basis. A single document typically includes fields such as issuing authority, document number, release date, applicable scenarios, and core clauses. The content primarily consists of structured policy texts, with no complex nested formats.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Citation Sources and Traceability Link
Policy documents from multiple departments require the traceability link to associate metadata such as issuing authority and document number, to avoid confusion between same-name clauses from different files. The monthly calibration update rhythm requires the traceability link to synchronously verify the latest version identifier of documents, to prevent recalling expired and invalid policy texts. Structured document fields require the chunking process to retain non-content metadata such as document number and release date, to ensure complete display of source identifiers during citation. The rigor of policy texts requires that returned traceability content strictly matches the clause hierarchy of the original document, with no arbitrary truncation or alteration of expressions.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The context citation list returned after retrieval is empty, or fails to match policy documents in the knowledge base. Cause: Metadata fields associated with issuing authority and document number are not configured, so structured policy documents cannot be matched during vector retrieval.
- Phenomenon: Only plain text content is displayed in context citations, without identifiers such as document number and release date, and the format has no clear separation. Cause: Metadata synchronization configuration is not enabled, and non-content fields are not retained during chunking, so source identifiers cannot be extracted during the traceability link.
- Phenomenon: The retrieved policy document version is expired, inconsistent with currently effective supervision requirements. Cause: The knowledge base vector library is not updated according to the monthly calibration rhythm, and expired old policy documents are retrieved.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a state-owned asset management policy document, initiate a targeted query, and verify that the returned context citations include source identifiers such as issuing authority and document number.
- Adjust the chunk length parameter and check the document chunking preview, confirm that the chunking units do not split complete policy clause expressions.
- Initiate a query involving multi-department supervision basis, check whether the number of retrieved results matches the preset single search limit.
- Trigger the traceability link, confirm that the returned cited content does not exceed the length range supported by the model.
