# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Capital Investment Companies: Workflow Orchestration

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
State-owned capital investment company policy and regulatory basis data primarily comes from regulatory policies officially released by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, supporting notices from local state-owned assets supervision authorities, and capital operation and industrial investment policy manuals formulated by group headquarters. There is no fixed update cycle. Updates are synchronized when major state-owned assets policies are issued, group business scope is adjusted, or internal policies are revised. Documents follow standardized structures, including fields such as release document number, issuing unit, effective date, clause number, and applicable business segment. Most use standard official document formatting. Policy levels fall into three categories: national, provincial, and group level.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Workflow Orchestration
The classification of policy levels requires workflows to first complete policy priority verification, to avoid returning expired or low-level invalid policies. The lack of a fixed update cycle requires configuring dynamic data source refresh trigger rules for workflows, to adapt to temporarily updated policy documents. The clausal structure of documents requires knowledge base chunks to be split at single-clause granularity, to avoid context confusion across clauses. The clear applicable business segment field requires adding a business classification node at the start of the workflow, to match user questions to the policy knowledge base of the corresponding segment, narrowing the recall scope and improving accuracy.

## How to Configure the Workflow
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy Level Filtering | Sort by national level > provincial level > group level, take the top 3 entries | State-owned capital investment company business must comply with state-owned assets supervision level requirements, and superior regulatory policies take priority |
| Context Window Limit | First 8000 tokens | Policy document clauses are relatively long, to avoid exceeding the model's context carrying limit |
| Variable Binding | Bind the policy document number field via `variables` | Facilitates subsequent verification of policy effective dates and implementation scope |
| Node ID Configuration | Set `nodeId` to policy_check_01 | Facilitates workflow debugging and log tracing, quickly locating abnormal nodes |
| Single Knowledge Base Recall Limit | Top 5 entries | The number of policy documents for state-owned capital investment companies is relatively concentrated; excessive recall will lead to redundant context |
| Effective Date Verification | Match policy effective/expiration dates against the current date | Ensure returned policies are in active implementation status, avoiding invalid content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The workflow returns a tool call failure with a 400 status code. Cause: The policy parameters in `variables` were not correctly bound to the tool call node, causing the tool to fail to obtain the required policy document number.
- Phenomenon: After the workflow processes multiple knowledge base results, the context history is not cleared, leading to confusion in subsequent question-and-answer sessions. Cause: No context clearing step was configured after the unified logic processing node, and the historical conversation before multi-round classification was incorrectly retained.
- Phenomenon: Previously uploaded policy documents are loaded during document parsing, leading to redundant parsing results. Cause: No filtering rule for only processing the currently uploaded file was configured in the document parsing node, and the historical file list was loaded by default.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Trigger a workflow test, input a question containing a policy document number, and check if returned results preferentially match policy documents of the corresponding level.
- View workflow logs to confirm that the policy field in `variables` has been correctly passed to subsequent nodes.
- Upload a test policy document, trigger the parsing process, and check that only the currently uploaded document is loaded and parsed.
- Run a test case that includes unified logic processing, and check that the context history is correctly cleared after the step.
