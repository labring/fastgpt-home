# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Capital Investment Companies: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for state-owned capital investment companies mainly comes from regulatory documents issued by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, local state-owned assets supervision institutions, internal compliance manuals of companies, and supporting industry implementation rules. Data updates dynamically alongside regulatory policy releases, with no fixed cycle. Each individual document contains fields including policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, clause content, and scope of application. Clauses are structured in tiers of articles, paragraphs, and sub-paragraphs. Document content is mainly normative text, with no complex embedded structured tables. The word count of individual documents varies widely.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation and traceability process?
The characteristics of state-owned capital investment company policy data impose three core constraints on the citation and traceability process. First, most data comes from official regulatory documents and internal compliance materials. Citations must accurately match policy document numbers, issuing entities, and effective dates to avoid confusion between different levels of state-owned assets supervision rules. Second, data updates dynamically with regulatory policies. The traceability process must bind to the current version of the knowledge base to ensure cited content aligns with the latest policies. Third, documents mainly consist of plain text clauses with clear tiered structure. The system requires accurate positioning to specific articles, paragraphs, and sub-paragraphs. No additional processing of embedded attachments is needed, but the system must adapt to the wide word count range of individual documents to avoid truncating critical basis content.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Redundant knowledge base file citation links appear in conversation outputs, and even links to non-policy documents. Cause: The Q&A chain is not bound to a dedicated state-owned capital investment policy knowledge base, or the citation range set to only return document numbers and clause positions is not configured.
- Phenomenon: Cited policy clauses do not match current latest regulatory requirements, and expired state-owned assets policy content appears. Cause: The configuration to bind context to the knowledge base version is not enabled, or associated data of the Q&A chain is not refreshed after the knowledge base is updated.
- Phenomenon: Cited policy snippets are too long or too short, failing to fully display the contextual logic of clauses. Cause: The max citation token count is set incorrectly, failing to adapt to the wide word count range of individual state-owned capital investment company policy documents.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Submit a question targeting a specific state-owned capital investment policy clause. Check that the output only includes matching policy document numbers and clause positions, with no additional redundant file links or full document content.
- Check the length of the output citation snippets. Confirm that they do not exceed the preset token count range, and that no critical clauses are truncated.
- Update a policy document in the dedicated policy knowledge base. Submit the same question, and confirm that the cited content is updated to the latest version.
- Adjust the citation matching threshold. Submit a low-relevance question, and confirm that the output filters out unrelated policy snippets, only returning precisely matched content.
