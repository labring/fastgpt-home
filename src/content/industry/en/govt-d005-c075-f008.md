# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Capital Investment Companies: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What this category of data looks like
The data for policy and regulatory basis Q&A for state-owned capital investment companies primarily comes from official documents issued by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, local state-owned assets supervision departments, and internal group investment and operation management systems, annual supervision guideline documents. The update rhythm follows new policy releases and adjusted regulatory requirements, with no fixed cycle. Core regulatory documents are updated alongside annual state-owned assets work priorities. Most documents are long-form official texts, containing structured fields such as document numbers, issuing units, effective dates, clauses and articles. Some files include high-definition scanned attachments or multi-page tables. The overall structure has clear hierarchical levels, and most content uses clause-based formatting.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the document parsing and chunking stage?
The structured characteristics of state-owned assets policy documents require the parsing stage to preserve metadata such as document numbers and effective dates. This prevents loss of policy source traceability during Q&A. Long-form text and multi-attachment structures increase parsing time, so the system must support processing large files and multi-page content. The lack of a fixed update cycle requires the parsing workflow to support incremental updates and batch processing, to avoid re-parsing already processed files. The clause-based formatting structure requires chunking to preserve semantic integrity. Randomly splitting independent clauses will disrupt context coherence during Q&A.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Most state-owned assets policy documents use clause-based long-form text. This range balances context integrity and chunk granularity, preventing semantic fragmentation caused by overly long single chunks |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by title + paragraph | Documents divide clauses using clear title hierarchies. Splitting by title preserves independent semantic units for each clause, and aligns with the structural characteristics of policy documents |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 300–600 seconds | Large state-owned assets policy documents may include multiple attachments or multi-page scanned content. This duration covers parsing time for most files, preventing parsing timeout errors |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 1000 MB | State-owned assets policy documents may include bulk attachments or high-definition scanned files. This upper limit supports large file upload requirements for most scenarios |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Many state-owned assets policy documents exist as scanned PDFs. Custom parsing optimizes OCR recognition accuracy and metadata extraction precision |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual configuration | Chunking parameters must be adjusted for different types of policy documents such as official issuances, detailed rules, and reports. Manual configuration adapts to diverse document structures |

## Three Common Errors
- Uploading state-owned assets policy files fails to trigger parsing, and document content is not called during Q&A. This occurs when `customPdfParse` is not enabled, or when `chunkSettingMode` does not match the document type, leading the system to incorrectly determine that the file does not require parsing.
- FastGPT reports a PDF parsing timeout, but the PDF parsing service returns a success status. This happens when `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` is set too short, failing to cover the parsing cycle of large documents with multiple attachments.
- Workflows cannot read uploaded Excel policy attachment content. This occurs when the table parsing plugin for document parsing is not enabled, or when chunking configurations do not adapt to Excel column field structures, leading parsed text to be unavailable for workflow calls.
