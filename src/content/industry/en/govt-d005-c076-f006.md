# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for State-owned Capital Operation Companies: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
The data for policy and regulatory basis Q&A for state-owned capital operation companies comes from three sources: state-owned capital supervision policies released by the state-owned assets regulatory authority, internal group operation management systems, and supporting documents issued by local state-owned assets regulatory departments.
Data synchronization follows this schedule: new policies are synchronized within 1 to 3 days after release by the regulatory authority. Internal group documents are updated in real time when revised.
Documents are structured official formal papers. They include fixed fields such as policy document number, issuing entity, effective date, clause number, and applicable scope. Each single clause has complete content, with a unified standard format.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on source citation and traceability
Structured fixed fields require traceability to link to metadata like document numbers and effective dates. This ensures the official nature and timeliness of cited sources.
The high update frequency requires regular incremental synchronization tasks. This prevents knowledge base content from lagging behind latest policies.
Complete single clause content requires sufficient context space. This ensures cited content can be fully carried.
The regulatory attribute of policies requires strict citation scope limits. Only officially released compliant data sources are allowed. Non-official content must be excluded.

## How to set the configuration
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 characters | A single policy clause has complete content. This range fully carries cited content and avoids context overflow |
| `responseData` | Return the document number, effective time, and issuing entity of the citation source | Policy traceability needs clear official source identification and timeliness information. This complies with state-owned capital supervision compliance requirements |
| `detail` | Enabled | Full cited clause content must be displayed to ensure policy basis matching accuracy |
| Single search limit | Top 3 results | The state-owned capital policy system has clear hierarchies. Too many citations cause redundant answers and reduce reading efficiency |
| Synchronization frequency | Daily incremental synchronization | The state-owned assets regulatory authority has a high policy update frequency. Daily synchronization keeps knowledge base content consistent with official requirements |

## Three common misconfigurations
- The answer cites policy documents, but generated content does not match knowledge base clauses at all. The cause is a too-small `quoteMaxToken` value. This truncates recalled cited content, making it unable to match generation logic.
- Only text datasets appear in traceability results. Policy datasets in other formats are not recalled. The cause is unenabled recall switches for corresponding format datasets, and unadapted structured field extraction rules for state-owned capital policy documents.
- Dynamically binding knowledge base search parameters fails to return corresponding cited documents. The cause is incorrect association of dynamic parameters with the knowledge base search node. This leaves the search scope unable to cover target policy documents.

## How to confirm the configuration is correctly set
- Enter the knowledge base management page. Check the synchronization log to confirm policy documents updated per daily incremental synchronization rules.
- Initiate a test question about state-owned capital policies. Check if the answer includes metadata fields like citation source document number and effective time.
- Test policy datasets in different formats. Confirm all supported formats are included in the recall scope.
- Check the answer's citation module. Confirm cited content length matches the configured `quoteMaxToken` value.
