# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Urban Water Utilities: Workflow Orchestration

## What the data for this category looks like
Policy data for urban water utilities comes from official policy documents issued by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development and the Ministry of Water Resources, covering topics such as urban water supply guarantee, sewage treatment and discharge, and pipe network operation and maintenance. It also includes internal management documents from local urban water investment and water supply enterprises. Updates sync with official release dates, with regular monthly calibration. Most documents follow a structured, chapter-style official format, containing fields such as document number, effective date, applicable scenarios, specific clauses, and supporting implementation rules. Most content is text, with standardized numbering and date formats.

## Constraints on workflow orchestration
The official document structure of urban water utility policy data, combined with the multi-local, multi-department source characteristics, creates multiple constraints for workflow orchestration. Original clause traceability nodes must be added in the workflow to ensure Q&A content can be directly linked to official original texts. Regional routing branch nodes must be configured to adapt to policy differences across different cities. Regularly triggered data source verification nodes must be bound to avoid calling expired policy documents. Standardized field extraction rules must be preset to adapt to fixed field formats such as document numbers and effective dates in policy documents.

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: After calling a sub-workflow to complete user selection, subsequent nodes of the parent workflow do not trigger execution. Cause: Result return binding is not configured on the user selection node of the sub-workflow. The parent workflow cannot receive execution status signals, so it cannot continue running.
- Phenomenon: Policy metadata fields extracted by the workflow are empty, preventing traceability and verification. Cause: Standardized extraction rules are not preset via `variables`, and unstructured policy document fragments are parsed directly, leading to field matching failures.
- Phenomenon: The number of results returned after workflow execution exceeds the expected range. Cause: No regional routing branch is configured, and policy knowledge bases of multiple localities are retrieved simultaneously, resulting in redundant return results.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- The workflow is manually triggered, a test scenario corresponding to the target locality is selected, and returned results are checked for linkage to policy documents of that region.
- Workflow node logs are reviewed, and policy metadata fields extracted via `variables` are confirmed to display normally.
- Data source configuration grouping rules are inspected, and policy knowledge bases of different regions are confirmed to be correctly bound to corresponding branch nodes.
- A data source verification task is triggered, and synchronization node updates are confirmed to complete per the preset cycle.
