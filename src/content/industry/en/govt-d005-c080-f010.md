# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Urban Investment Platforms: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Policy and regulatory basis data for urban investment platforms mainly comes from internal operation management systems, special urban investment policies issued by local finance and housing and urban-rural development departments, and normative documents from superior state-owned assets supervision institutions. Updates are completed within 1 to 3 working days after official policy release, with incremental updates as needed.
Document structure includes fields such as document number, issuing authority, release date, applicable scope, and main content. Document numbers follow the standard format of "XX Finance Preparatory [202X] XX Number". Release dates use the YYYY-MM-DD format. Main content consists of long texts spanning several thousand characters.

## Constraints Imposed on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The characteristics of urban investment platform policy data impose multiple constraints on the retrieval and recall process.
Official source requirements mandate that recall results strictly match original text to avoid generalized deviations. Long main text requires chunking to preserve contextual relevance, preventing disruption of policy logic. Structured fields (document number, issuing authority) require prioritizing key identifiers during retrieval to improve accuracy. Irregular update cycles require retrieval configurations to support dynamic filtering of currently valid knowledge bases, avoiding recall of outdated or invalid policy documents.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Meets the rigor requirements for urban investment policies, prevents irrelevant policies from being included in recall results |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Controls context length per round of Q&A, adapts to display needs for long policy documents |
| `searchMode` | Exact Match | Adapts to exact retrieval requirements for structured fields such as policy document numbers and issuing authorities |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Optimizes relevance ranking after long document chunking, improves display priority of core policy fragments |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Supplements policy-related keywords to expand retrieval coverage scope |
| `collectionIds` | Bind exclusive knowledge base ID for urban investment policies | Isolates knowledge base data from other categories, prevents mixing of retrieval results |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A prompt stating "No knowledge base selected" pops up in the chat window after triggering Q&A. Cause: The urban investment policy-exclusive knowledge base collection is not bound in the session configuration, or the bound `collectionIds` parameter points to an incorrect knowledge base ID.
- Phenomenon: When entering an unreferenced policy-related question, the system directly returns that it cannot answer, without triggering generalized model inference. Cause: The `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` configuration is not enabled, or `searchMode` is set to exact match only, retaining no generalized inference logic.
- Phenomenon: Policy documents cannot be classified and managed by document number or issuing department during import. Cause: The folder function in the knowledge base is not used correctly, and urban investment policy documents of the same batch or type are not categorized into corresponding folders, making accurate filtering of target files impossible during retrieval.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Enter the FastGPT v4.17.0 knowledge base management page, check that the knowledge base corresponding to `collectionIds` only contains urban investment policy-related files, with no redundant data from other categories.
- Initiate a test Q&A, enter a known urban investment policy document number, confirm that the recall result includes an accurate fragment of the corresponding file, and that the similarity matches the preset threshold.
- Adjust the `similarity` parameter to 0.8, enter a vaguely worded policy question, confirm that the relevance of the recall result meets expectations, with no irrelevant entries included.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, compare the result ranking before and after reranking, confirm that core policy fragments of long documents are displayed first.
