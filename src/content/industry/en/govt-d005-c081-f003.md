# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Basic Chemical Industry: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

This page is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Basic chemical industry policy and regulatory basis data primarily comes from industry compliance documents issued by the Ministry of Emergency Management, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, as well as local implementation rules released by provincial-level industrial and information technology departments.
Data update timing is adjusted alongside new policy releases, with no fixed cycle.
A single policy document is lengthy, with fields including document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable production scenarios, specific compliance clauses, and supervision and inspection requirements.
Clauses follow an article, paragraph, sub-paragraph hierarchy. Some documents include quantitative production capacity and emission indicator requirements.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Basic chemical industry policy documents are lengthy and have clear hierarchical structures.
Multi-turn dialogue flows must retain complete context to connect different compliance scenario questions raised by users in sequence, and avoid disconnected clause associations.
Policy clauses use a strict article, paragraph, sub-paragraph hierarchy. Prompt configurations must specify clear field extraction rules to ensure returned content aligns with the correct policy hierarchy.
Some policies include quantitative production capacity and emission indicators. Dialogue flows must match indicator units and applicable production links, and avoid confusing general descriptions with formal compliance requirements.
Policy updates have no fixed cycle. The system must support dynamic loading of newly released policy documents to ensure Q&A content meets current regulatory requirements.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Context Retention Limit | First 10–15 turns of dialogue | Basic chemical industry policy documents have complex hierarchies. Sufficient context must be retained to associate questions about different clauses |
| Session ID Binding | Bind `chatId` | Used to distinguish policy Q&A sessions of different users, and avoid context interference |
| Historical Message Stitching | Stitch in the order of `messages` | Ensure accurate association of prior policy scenario questions during multi-turn dialogue |
| Variable Injection Configuration | Inject policy effective date from `variables` | Used to verify the timeliness of Q&A content and match current policy requirements |
| Dialogue Timeout Threshold | 600 seconds | Basic chemical industry policy documents are lengthy. Retrieval and generation require extended processing time |
| Prompt Rule Configuration | Explicitly specify extraction of article, paragraph, sub-paragraph hierarchy | Match the hierarchical structure of basic chemical industry policy documents, and avoid chaotic returned content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: Cannot retrieve specific content from prior questions during multi-turn dialogue, leading to incorrect answers associated with clauses. Cause: `chatId` is not correctly bound, or historical messages are not stitched in the order of `messages`, so context is not properly passed.
- Issue: Generated dialogue content exceeds the policy clause hierarchy, and non-compliant clause general descriptions appear. Cause: Prompt configurations do not explicitly specify extraction rules for article, paragraph, sub-paragraph hierarchy, and do not match the structure of basic chemical industry policy documents.
- Issue: Dialogue response time exceeds 600 seconds, but only 1 group is shown in model call grouping. Cause: No reasonable dialogue timeout threshold is set, and no adaptation processing is done for long-document policy retrieval, leading to generation process timeouts that are not correctly counted.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Launch three progressive questions. For example, first ask about compliant production requirements for a type of basic chemical product, then follow up with corresponding storage clauses, then ask about penalty basis. Confirm if the system can associate the product scenario from prior questions.
- View session logs. Confirm that `chatId` remains consistent across each dialogue, and the `messages` list fully records historical questions and replies in conversation order.
- Test calls to policy clauses with quantitative indicators. Confirm if the system returned content matches the policy's effective date and applicable authority.
- After setting the timeout threshold, launch a Q&A request for a long-document policy. Confirm if the system completes the response within the preset time with no abnormal interruptions.
