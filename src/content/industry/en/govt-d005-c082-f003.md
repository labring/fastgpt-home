# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Basic Telecommunications Operations: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What This Type of Data Looks Like
The policy and regulatory basis data for basic telecommunications operations is primarily sourced from official regulatory documents and industry standard specifications released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, as well as compliance management documents internal to basic telecommunications enterprises. Data updates are adjusted synchronously with the release of new policies. Document structures include fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, scope of application, and original text of clauses. Each individual document is stored as a separate policy file, with content mostly consisting of structured clauses and explanatory text.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering Workflows
The characteristics of basic telecommunications policy data impose multiple constraints on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering workflows. First, policy data includes clear structured fields such as policy document number and effective date. Multi-turn dialogue must associate the time node of user questions in context management, to ensure returned policy content matches the time of the user’s question and avoid referencing expired clauses. Second, individual policy documents can be lengthy. Multi-turn dialogue context windows must limit the length of valid content, to avoid loading redundant non-clause text that disrupts core question-answering logic. Additionally, policy update frequency is adjusted synchronously with regulatory requirements. Prompt engineering must guide the system to prioritize calling the latest knowledge base entries, while automatically filtering expired policy content during multi-turn dialogue, to ensure compliance and timeliness of question-answering.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The interface displays "No permission to operate this conversation record". Cause: The `chatId` parameter is not configured correctly, causing abnormal session association logic that fails to match the current user’s session permissions.
- Symptom: Multi-turn dialogue returns policy clauses that do not match the user’s question time. Cause: The question time and policy effective time parameters in `variables` are not passed, causing the system to be unable to screen valid policy content.
- Symptom: Response content includes subjective interpretations not present in the original policy text. Cause: The prompt template does not clearly limit the response basis, causing the system to generate content beyond the scope of the policy.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- A query including a known policy document number can be initiated. Verify that the returned content includes the corresponding clauses and document number, to confirm that the context association logic operates correctly.
- A cross-turn dialogue can be initiated. Verify that the system retains the association logic of historical questions, to confirm that the session identifier configuration is correct.
- The knowledge base update log can be checked. Verify that the session context automatically refreshes to the latest policy content, to confirm that the automatic refresh rule is active.
- The prompt template can be modified, then a query initiated. Verify that the returned content strictly follows the original policy text, to confirm that the prompt configuration is active.
