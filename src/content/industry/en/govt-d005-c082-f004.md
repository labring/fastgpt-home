# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Basic Telecommunications Operations: Workflow Orchestration

## What the data for this category looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for basic telecommunications operations mainly comes from Ministry of Industry and Information Technology regulations, State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council SOE compliance documents, telecommunications industry standards, and operator internal compliance manuals. Data updates are aligned with the release date of industry policies, with synchronization completed within 1 to 3 working days after major policy adjustments. The document structure includes standardized fields: unified document number, issuing authority, effective date, clause number, and applicable business scenario. The document number uses the format "Ministry of Industry and Information Technology Order No. XX" or "Ministry of Industry and Information Technology Notice [XXXX] No. XX", and the effective date uses the YYYY-MM-DD standard format.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "workflow orchestration" link
The unified document number and effective date fields of the data require the workflow to be configured with a field filtering node to only recall currently effective policy content, avoiding reference to expired clauses. The applicable business scenario field requires the workflow to be bound with business classification tags for basic telecommunications operations, ensuring recalled content matches the current consulting communication service type. Frequently updated policies require the knowledge base synchronization cycle of the workflow to align with industry update rhythms to ensure content timeliness. The fixed document structure requires chunking nodes to split by clause granularity, retaining the contextual association between clause numbers and associated document numbers to avoid information fragmentation.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: The workflow returns empty results when calling the RAG knowledge base and MCP tools. Cause: The `variables` binding for business scenario tags is not configured, causing the recalled policy documents to not match the currently consulted communication service.
- Phenomenon: The identity authentication option cannot be found in the workflow sharing configuration. Cause: The "Advanced Configuration" panel is not enabled in the workflow editing page, and authentication settings are hidden within this panel.
- Phenomenon: The database connection plugin takes significantly longer to execute than older versions. Cause: No reasonable node timeout duration is set, or no field filtering rule is configured, resulting in recall of a large number of irrelevant policy documents and increased parsing time.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Manually trigger the workflow, check whether the effective date of the returned policy documents is later than the current date, to confirm that the field filtering rule is effective.
- View the workflow node logs to confirm that the business scenario tags bound by `variables` are correctly passed to the RAG recall node.
- Test consultations for different communication service types to verify that the recalled policy documents match the corresponding business scenarios.
- View the knowledge base synchronization records to confirm that the most recent synchronization time aligns with the preset synchronization cycle.
