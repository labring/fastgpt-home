# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Basic Telecommunications Operations: Deployment and Upgrade

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
The data for policy and regulatory basis of basic telecommunications operations primarily comes from official announcements from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, industry supervision rules, internal compliance documents of operating entities, and compliance requirements released by local communications administrations. Data updates are triggered synchronously with revisions to regulatory policies, with no fixed cycle. Synchronization must be completed before core policies take effect. Documents are mainly structured original policy texts, including fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable business scenarios, and compliance clauses. Some explanatory documents include implementation step instructions.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Deployment and Upgrade Phase
Dispersed data sources require configuring multiple data source access rules during deployment, to support data synchronization from official websites, internal document libraries, and regulatory systems.
No fixed update cycle requires configuring incremental synchronization mechanisms during upgrades, to avoid full synchronization consuming excessive computing and storage resources.
Documents include structured fields such as policy document number and effective date. Metadata extraction rules must be configured during deployment, to ensure precise filtering by field during retrieval.
The high proportion of long documents requires adapting parameter configurations for long text chunking and vector storage during deployment, to prevent vector storage overflow or reduced retrieval accuracy.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: After starting the service, accessing the frontend page returns 404 no body. Cause: The `FE_DOMAIN` parameter is not configured correctly, causing a mismatch between the frontend callback address and the backend service address, preventing correct request routing.
- Symptom: The number of returned results when retrieving policy documents is much lower than expected. Cause: The `DB_MAX_LINK` parameter is not set correctly. Insufficient connection pool causes some retrieval requests to be discarded, preventing complete matching results from being obtained.
- Symptom: Duplicate entries appear when incrementally synchronizing policy data. Cause: No metadata unique identifier verification rule is configured, causing the same policy document to be synchronized multiple times and generate duplicate data.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Start the FastGPT v4.17.0 backend service, execute the health check interface, and confirm that core parameters such as `DB_MAX_LINK`, `PG_URL`, and `REDIS_URL` have no syntax errors, and the service starts without errors.
- Manually trigger a policy data synchronization task, check whether the corresponding document copy is generated in the object storage, and whether correct metadata fields such as policy document number and issuing authority are written to the database.
- Initiate a retrieval request for a specific policy, verify that the returned results include the expected policy content and metadata fields, and match the actual policy text.
- Access the frontend configured `FE_DOMAIN` address, confirm that the page loads normally, and there are no permission verification related error prompts.
