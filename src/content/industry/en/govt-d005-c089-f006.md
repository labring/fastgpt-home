# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Construction Machinery: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What data for this category looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the construction machinery field mainly comes from official announcements and normative documents issued by the national Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, Ministry of Transport, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, as well as local housing and urban-rural development and transport authorities. The update rhythm is adjusted irregularly following industry policy revisions. The document structure includes document number, issuing body, effective date, applicable construction machinery categories such as excavators, cranes, rollers, core execution clauses and violation penalty rules. Fields covered include policy number, applicable scope, execution standards, effective time. Units are typically pieces, hours, emission standard levels, and job site area tiers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" link
The issuing body and document number of policy documents are core mandatory traceability fields. Precise association with corresponding fields is required during retrieval to avoid citing content without official basis. Construction machinery policies are segmented by equipment category, so knowledge base chunks must be split by applicable models. Otherwise, retrieved content may not match the query scenario. Policy updates have no fixed cycle, so a task to regularly sync official channels must be configured to ensure the timeliness of traceable content. Some policies have effective time limits, so time filtering rules must be added during retrieval to avoid citing expired clauses.

## Three common mistakes
- Unable to return cited content after calling external release channels: `responseData` is not configured to enable traceability fields, or knowledge base sync is incomplete resulting in missing indexes.
- Retrieved policies do not match the queried construction machinery category: Knowledge base chunks are not split by equipment type, leading to incorrect retrieval of cross-category policies.
- Generated answer is unrelated to the cited content and logs show retrieval exceptions: Question optimization configuration does not match the retrieval logic, causing the retrieval scope to deviate from the scope of policy documents.

- Call the interface of the external release channel to verify that the returned content includes complete traceability information fields.
- Adjust the queried construction machinery category to confirm that retrieved policies only match the corresponding equipment type, with no cross-category redundant content.
