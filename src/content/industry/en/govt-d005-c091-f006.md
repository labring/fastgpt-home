# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Building Construction General Contracting: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for building construction general contracting mainly comes from official public documents released by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, housing and urban-rural development departments of various provinces and cities, and industry associations. The data update rhythm follows industry policy adjustments, with no fixed cycle. Updates are synchronized immediately after the release of newly issued general contracting management measures, qualification standards, valuation specifications, and other documents. Most documents are structured text or parsable PDF formats. Fields include policy document number, issuing entity, effective date, clause level, applicable region, and contracting scope. The primary formats are document number (e.g., Jianshi [2024] XX No.) and date (YYYY-MM-DD).

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" link
The standardized fields of building construction policy data require precise matching of specific clauses and identifiers during traceability, rather than relying solely on keyword recall. A policy’s effective date and applicable region determine the validity of citations. Corresponding fields must be associated during the traceability process to avoid citing expired or cross-region invalid clauses. The non-fixed update rhythm requires the knowledge base to regularly synchronize official documents to ensure cited content is the latest valid version. The structured document structure requires traceability rules to adapt to clause levels, ensuring compliance and accuracy of citations.

## Three common mistakes
- Interface displays citation identifiers, but the answer does not associate with the building construction policy knowledge base content: The `responseData` parameter is not configured correctly, and knowledge base citation fields are not included in the response output.
- Only text dataset citations are matched, and policy documents in other formats are not called: Adapted traceability rules are not configured for structured policy documents, and only the recall logic of text datasets is enabled.
- Cited policy clauses do not match the applicable scope of the current project: The effective date and applicable region fields of the policy are not associated during the traceability process, resulting in citations of expired or cross-region policy content.
