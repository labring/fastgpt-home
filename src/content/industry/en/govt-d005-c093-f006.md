# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Government and Enterprise IT Services: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category's Data Looks Like
Data for this category comes from official policy documents and regulatory standards published on national and local government public disclosure platforms and official websites of industry competent departments. The update rhythm adjusts dynamically with policy releases. New policies are ingested into the knowledge base within 1 to 7 working days after taking effect.
Each single document includes fixed fields: document number, issuing authority, release date, effective date, main text, attachments, and more. The main text is unformatted or uses basic formatted long text. Attachments are mostly in PDF or Word format. The size range of single documents is calibrated through actual testing.

## Constraints Imposed on Citation Sources and Traceability
The official source attribute of policy documents requires that traceability must match the issuing authority and document number fields to ensure citation authority. The dynamic update rhythm requires that the traceability link must regularly synchronize incremental policy data to avoid returning abolished or not yet effective documents.
The fixed document structure supports automatic extraction of citation metadata by fields, with no need for additional parsing of non-standard formats. Long text and attachment formats require that traceability must support cross-document association, and adaptation must be made for parsing time of large-volume attachments.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Suggested Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Search Limit | Top 3-5 entries | Policy documents are mostly authoritative sources. A single citation piece of information is sufficient to support traceability; too many entries will increase response latency |
| Maximum Context | 8000-12000 token | Policy texts are relatively long. Sufficient context must be retained to accurately match associated policies |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1000-1500 characters | Limit the length of cited content to avoid redundant response content, and adapt to the length of citation fragments in policy documents |
| Maximum Knowledge Base Citations | Top 2-4 documents | Government and enterprise policies are mostly scattered across multiple documents. A small number of precise documents can cover query requirements |
| `responseData` | Include metadata fields | Complete policy citation information must be returned, including document number, issuing authority and other content |
| `detail` | Enable | Detailed policy traceability information must be returned, covering all metadata fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, and more |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Citation fragments in the response content are truncated, or a prompt indicating overly long citation content appears. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` setting value is too small to accommodate complete policy citation metadata and main text fragments.
- Phenomenon: No citation source information is included in the returned results. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled, or the knowledge base containing policy documents is not bound.
- Phenomenon: Returned citation sources include documents that are not the target policy. Cause: The single search limit is set too high, which retrieves a large number of irrelevant policy documents, leading to confusion in traceability matching.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a query targeting a known policy document number, and check whether the returned result includes corresponding metadata such as document number and issuing authority.
- View the knowledge base synchronization log in FastGPT v4.17.0, confirm that the latest policy documents have been ingested into the knowledge base, and the traceability configuration is associated with this knowledge base.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, and verify whether the citation fragment length in the response content changes with the configuration.
- Call the interface and view the `responseData` field, confirm that it contains complete policy citation and traceability information.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
