# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Government and Enterprise IT Services: Model Integration and Configuration

## What this category of data looks like
Data for policy and regulatory basis Q&A for government and enterprise IT services mainly comes from official policy documents, industry regulatory notices, and telecommunications industry standard documents released by the State Council, central ministries and commissions, and local governments. The data update rhythm is adjusted irregularly with the release of new policies, with no fixed cycle. A single document usually includes fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, clause number, scope of application, and specific clause content. Clauses are unitized as "articles". The date format is YYYY-MM-DD. Some documents are attached with policy interpretation attachments. The total length varies greatly, ranging from hundreds of words to tens of thousands of words.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the model integration and configuration link
The wide range of policy document lengths and lack of fixed formats require models to support long context loading and precise clause matching during integration. Irregular data update cycles require the knowledge base update process to be flexible, enabling quick synchronization of the latest policy content. Structured fields including policy document number and effective date require the retrieval link to support precise filtering by fields, to avoid interference from irrelevant content. Additionally, compliance requirements in government and enterprise scenarios require that the model call link complies with network isolation and data security specifications, with higher precision requirements for proxy address and key configuration.

## How to set the configuration
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in the government and enterprise exclusive network proxy address, in the format `https://[government and enterprise exclusive domain]/v1` | Adapt to the government and enterprise network isolation environment, and ensure the model call link complies with regulations |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Use the exclusive API key allocated by the government and enterprise | Meet data security and permission control requirements, and avoid unauthorized access |
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | Accommodate context information from multiple policy documents, and avoid truncation of key clauses |
| `maxTokens` | `2000–3000 token` | Ensure that the answer can cover policy clause details, and meet strictness requirements |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `1500–2000 token` | Fully retain the core content of original policy text citations, and ensure citation accuracy |
| `temperature` | `0.1–0.3` | Reduce answer randomness, and ensure the strictness and consistency of policy Q&A |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Clicking the model test button returns a 404 status code (no body). Cause: `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` is not correctly configured as the government and enterprise exclusive proxy address, or the permission for `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` has not been activated.
- Phenomenon: Only reranking models appear in the channel model list, and other language models cannot be selected. Cause: The model interface whitelist configuration under the government and enterprise network environment has not been completed, or the proxy address does not cover the access path of the target language model.
- Phenomenon: The retrieved policy clauses have low matching accuracy. Cause: The value of `maxContext` is too small, and the context of the target policy document is not fully loaded, causing the model to fail to associate the correct policy entries.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Navigate to the FastGPT model configuration page, verify that `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` exactly match the official parameters allocated by the government and enterprise.
- Run a single-round model test interface, confirm that the returned results contain clear citations of original policy text, with no formatting errors or missing content.
- Import an archived policy document for retrieval testing, confirm that the number and scope of returned matching entries meet the expected configuration.
- Confirm that the currently deployed FastGPT version is v4.17.0, ensuring all configuration parameters are compatible with the current version specifications.
