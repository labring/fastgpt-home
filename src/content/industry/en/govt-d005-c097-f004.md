# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Nuclear Power Operations: Workflow Orchestration

This document is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0 workflow orchestration configuration.

## What this category of data looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for nuclear power operations mainly comes from official nuclear safety guidelines and industry standards issued by the National Nuclear Safety Administration and the National Energy Administration, as well as operating procedures and safety management documents internal to nuclear power operation enterprises. Data update rhythm follows regulatory policy revisions and on-site condition adjustments. Core compliance-related files are updated quarterly, and internal operating rules are optimized as needed. The document structure includes dedicated fields such as issuing authority, effective date, clause number, compliance requirements, and applicable scenarios. Clauses take "article" and "paragraph" as the smallest expression unit, and some detailed rules come with quantitative parameters for operating steps.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the workflow orchestration link
The dedicated fields and update characteristics of nuclear power operation policy data impose three constraints on workflow orchestration. First, a metadata association node must be configured to bind dedicated fields such as document numbers and effective dates, ensuring that retrieval results match current compliance requirements. Second, a version verification link must be set up to automatically filter revoked outdated policy documents, avoiding the output of obsolete compliance basis. Third, since clauses take "article" and "paragraph" as the smallest unit, the workflow must support precise positioning to the content of the corresponding clause, improving the accuracy of Q&A responses and avoiding returning irrelevant full documents.

## Three common mistakes
- When creating a question classification node, using the initialized default AI model to perform run, save or publish operations triggers a parameter verification failure prompt on the interface. The reason: Nuclear power policy documents contain professional terms such as nuclear safety guidelines and operating permits, and the context adaptation capability of the initialized model is insufficient to correctly recognize the dedicated terms, triggering a verification error.
- After the workflow text content extraction component runs, the returned result JSON field is empty. The reason: No metadata extraction rules are configured, or the extraction rules do not match the dedicated fields of nuclear power policy documents such as clause numbers and compliance requirements, resulting in no valid data in the extracted content.
- The specified reply component's output content is not displayed in the workflow global variable history. The reason: The history storage configuration for `variables` is not enabled, or the output of the specified reply component is not bound to the global variable, resulting in data not being written to the history.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Manually trigger the workflow run, check whether the retrieval results include policy documents issued by regulatory authorities, and verify whether the document source and effective date meet the configuration requirements.
- View the workflow operation log, confirm that the version verification node has filtered expired documents, and that the metadata extraction component has successfully extracted document numbers and clause numbers.
- Check the global variable storage panel, confirm that `variables` has saved the retrieved document version and source information.
- Input the document number of a revoked policy for testing, confirm that the workflow automatically filters the content and prompts an exception.
