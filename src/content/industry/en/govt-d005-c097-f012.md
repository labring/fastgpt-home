# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Nuclear Power Operations: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Nuclear power operation policy and regulatory basis data is mainly sourced from official documents released by the National Energy Administration, the National Nuclear Safety Administration, and industry authoritative standard institutions. The update rhythm is adjusted in real time as new policies and specifications are issued, with at least 2 annual routine updates. Most documents are in official document format, including fields such as policy document number, issuing entity, effective date, applicable operation scenarios, and clause entries. Clauses are layered by chapter, section, article, paragraph, and sub-paragraph. The length of a single document ranges from thousands to tens of thousands of characters. Some documents include attached drawings and tables, with formats mostly PDF or Word. Core fields have no redundancy, but format requirements are strict.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the deployment and upgrade phase
The structured long text feature of nuclear power policy documents requires the deployment phase to adapt to precise chunking logic for layered clauses and embedded tables, to avoid retrieval deviations caused by cross-clause or cross-table splitting. Policy updates have no fixed cycle and must strictly match effective dates, so the upgrade phase needs to support incremental synchronization mechanisms to prevent service interruptions caused by full updates. Strict format requirements for official documents and attachments require configuring format verification rules during deployment to filter invalid documents. The high density of professional terminology requires reserving a configuration entry for custom term mapping to ensure retrieval matching accuracy.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: When deploying with docker compose, only the fastgpt container fails to start, with no obvious error logs. Cause: The `MONGODB_URI` or `REDIS_URL` parameters are not configured correctly, causing the service to fail to connect to dependent database and cache components.
- Phenomenon: Uploaded PDF nuclear power policy documents with tables cannot be chunked normally, and table content is missing from search results. Cause: Structured document chunking rules are not enabled, and the default chunking logic cannot recognize the structured layout of tables.
- Phenomenon: After modifying the configuration file locally and redeploying, the service does not load the new configuration. Cause: The docker image was not rebuilt, or the local configuration file was not mounted to the corresponding directory in the container, causing the modifications to take no effect.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Run the `docker compose ps` command, confirm that the fastgpt container status is Up, with no abnormal exit prompts.
- Upload a test nuclear power policy document containing tables and layered clauses, wait for chunking to complete, enter policy clause keywords in the search interface, confirm that corresponding accurate results are returned.
- Use the configured `ROOT_KEY` to call the administrator interface, verify that the permission verification logic is normal, with no permission errors.
- Log in to the specified `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` storage bucket, confirm that the uploaded test document has been successfully stored to the corresponding path.
