# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Civil Aviation Airports: Workflow Orchestration

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The data sources for civil aviation airport policy and regulatory basis materials include official regulations and notices published by the Civil Aviation Administration of China, internal operating specifications and safety manuals of airport groups, and guiding documents released by industry associations. Update frequency follows these rules: Civil Aviation Administration regulatory documents are synchronized in real time based on their issue date. Internal airport specifications are updated on demand every quarter. The document structure uses a single file with multiple chapters, and includes fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable positions, and clause content. Clause numbers use Arabic numerals followed by the word "clause". The effective date format is YYYY-MM-DD.

## Constraints for Workflow Orchestration
Policy data is scattered across two sources: the Civil Aviation Administration’s official website and the airport intranet. Configure workflows with dual data source access rules to avoid missing retrieval results. Account for differing update frequencies between the two data sources by setting incremental sync trigger conditions. These triggers only synchronize newly added or modified files. Align text extraction nodes with the structured nature of civil aviation policy documents. Require these nodes to accurately match clause number and effective date fields, and only extract content within valid effective periods to avoid returning expired policy clauses. Address the relatively long document length by setting reasonable timeout and chunking rules for retrieval and extraction stages.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Irrelevant content is returned during workflow debugging. The cause is failure to bind user input to the `variables` parameter, resulting in incorrect passing of retrieval keywords.
- Workflow node errors prevent jump completion. The cause is failure to correctly set the `nodeId`, causing branch logic to fail to identify the target node.
- Valid policy clauses cannot be extracted via text extraction. The cause is failure to configure extraction rules based on clause numbers, leading to misidentification of irrelevant content via only keyword matching.
