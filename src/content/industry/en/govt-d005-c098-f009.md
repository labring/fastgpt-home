# Policy and Regulatory Reference Q&A for Civil Aviation Airports: Model Access and Configuration

This configuration is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for civil aviation airport policy and regulatory reference include civil aviation administration public regulations, airport internal operation management systems, and normative documents from local civil aviation regulatory authorities. Update cycles fall into two categories: regulations issued by the national civil aviation administration are updated every six months to one year. Internal airport systems are updated on demand alongside adjustments to business such as flight support and security checks.

Document structure includes document number, issuing entity, effective date, scope of application, specific clauses and supporting implementation rules. Fields include document type, applicable scenario, and clause number. Common units include person-times, hours, square meters and other units typical of airport operations.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on model access and configuration
Data sources for civil aviation airport policy and regulatory documents are scattered, covering both public regulations and internal systems. This requires configuring classification index rules for multi-source knowledge bases during model access, to ensure accurate matching of corresponding data sources during retrieval.

Update cycles are inconsistent: public regulations have longer update cycles, while internal systems are adjusted on demand. A flexible triggerable knowledge base synchronization mechanism must be configured to avoid retrieving outdated content.

Documents include clear clause numbers, applicable scenarios and unit fields. Context retention of these identifiers is required during model calls. Scene filtering rules must also be configured to ensure retrieved content matches the airport business scenario in the user's query. Long text clauses are common, which imposes constraints on the model's context carrying capacity.

## How to set the configurations

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | Individual clauses in civil aviation policy documents are mostly 500-2000 tokens long. Sufficient context must be retained to ensure complete association of clauses |
| `maxTokens` | `4000–6000 token` | Responses must cover policy interpretation and reference citations, to avoid exceeding the model's output limit |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `1500 token` | Policy clauses must be fully referenced as supporting evidence, to avoid truncation of critical content |
| `temperature` | `0.1–0.3` | Policy Q&A requires precision. Reducing randomness ensures responses align with source documents |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Exclusive access address provided by the service provider | Ensures stable model call links |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Exclusive key assigned by the service provider | Secures call permission |

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: Model avatar fails to load normally in the conversation interface. Cause: The `avatar` field is added directly to the third-party model provider's configuration file, instead of being set via FastGPT's built-in avatar configuration module.
- Phenomenon: Retrieved policy documents do not match the airport business scenario in the user's query. Cause: No classification retrieval rules are configured for the knowledge base, leading to unfiltered retrieval of both national civil aviation administration general regulations and airport internal specialized systems.
- Phenomenon: Speech transcription function cannot be called normally, no transcription result is returned. Cause: No compatible non-Whisper speech recognition model is selected, and correct speech recognition access parameters are not configured.

## How to confirm configuration is complete
- Submit a test query with the input "Policy basis for airport security checks". Verify that retrieved documents include relevant civil aviation administration regulations and airport internal systems, to confirm the retrieval scope matches the configured requirements.
- Review the reference fragments in the model's output. Confirm that referenced policy clauses are not truncated, and verify that the `quoteMaxToken` configuration is effective.
- Check that the model avatar displays normally in the conversation interface, to confirm the avatar configuration path complies with FastGPT requirements.
- Trigger a knowledge base synchronization operation. Verify that the update log shows the synchronization progress of corresponding policy documents, to confirm the update mechanism is configured correctly.
