# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Oilfield Engineering Services: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for oilfield engineering services mainly comes from oil and gas industry compliance documents issued by the National Energy Administration, internal engineering service management specifications of state-owned oil enterprises, and oilfield site operation compliance manuals.
The data update schedule triggers immediate updates when major policies are revised. It completes full data refresh on a quarterly basis for routine updates.
Most documents are in structured clause format. They include issuing body, policy document number, effective date, applicable operation links such as drilling, fracturing, cementing, and compliance requirements.
Fields include policy unique identifier, applicable region, operation type, original clause text. Some documents include quantitative indicator references for field operations.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexing link
The structured policy documents and metadata characteristics of this category impose multiple constraints on the vector models and indexing link.
First, documents include clear effective dates and applicable operation links. Indexing must support metadata filtering by release time and operation type. This prevents recalling expired or mismatched policy content.
Second, the length of single policy clauses varies widely. Some clauses include quantitative site indicators. Vector models must adapt to different text lengths. This avoids information loss caused by long text truncation.
Third, data update frequency is high. Incremental indexing trigger logic must support the mixed schedule of quarterly refresh and immediate updates. This ensures the timeliness of retrieval results.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the typical length of policy clauses. Prevents single chunks from including unrelated content across operation links |
| Overlap Length | 100–200 characters | Retains policy context across chunks. Ensures the integrity of clause logic |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Filters low-match non-compliant policy content. Ensures accuracy of retrieval results |
| Single Retrieval Limit | Top 8 entries | Controls the number of returned results. Fits the short context requirements of compliance Q&A |
| `maxToken` | 8000 | Adapts to the total length limit of policy documents. Prevents truncation of key compliance clauses |
| `defaultToken` | 500 | Adapts to the typical encoding length of single policy clauses |
| Reranking | Enabled | Improves the ranking priority of high-match results. Meets the accuracy requirements of compliance Q&A |

## Three common errors
- Issue: Index creation prompts "parameter configuration error". Cause: Chunk parameters are not set according to the text length supported by the vector model. Chunk length exceeds the model's encoding limit.
- Issue: Retrieval results from cross-index models cannot be displayed merged. Cause: Vector encoding dimensions and preprocessing rules of different models are not unified. This leads to incompatible vector spaces.
- Issue: Retrieval results do not link to the applicable operation links of the policy. Cause: Metadata such as operation type and applicable region are not included in the index filtering configuration. This causes recall of mismatched policy content.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Check the index creation logs for FastGPT v4.17.0. Confirm that the chunked text length does not exceed the vector model's encoding limit. Confirm that `defaultToken` and `maxToken` settings meet the length requirements of policy documents. Confirm that metadata fields are correctly associated.
- Run a test retrieval. Input a query that includes operation type and effective time. Verify that the results filter out mismatched policy content.
- Compare index results from different batches. Confirm that the index automatically refreshes after data updates. Confirm that vector encoding for new policies works correctly.
- Test cross-model mixed retrieval (if configured). Confirm that vector results from different models can be displayed normally.
