# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Oilfield Engineering Services: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
Oilfield engineering services policy and regulatory basis data primarily comes from industry compliance documents issued by the National Energy Administration, internal engineering management specifications of petroleum and petrochemical groups, and exclusive compliance manuals for oilfield projects.
Data updates follow quarterly or special policy release schedules.
Single documents typically include fields such as document number, issuing entity, effective date, applicable engineering scenarios, clause number and specific content.
Clauses are organized by individual article, with effective dates using standard date formats.
The overall structure is layered by policy category and specific engineering scenarios.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Decentralized data sources require multi-turn dialogue to integrate compliance content across different levels and entities. Prompts must clearly specify retrieval scope and priority.
Frequent policy updates require real-time validation of policy validity during multi-turn interactions, to avoid referencing expired clauses.
The layered document structure and clear field requirements mean multi-turn dialogue must link specific clause numbers and effective dates to ensure reply accuracy.
Long individual document lengths limit the maximum context length that can be loaded in a single pass, so historical interaction content must be filtered appropriately.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Incorrect offset configuration when retrieving conversation record lists, resulting in returned data volume not matching expectations. Cause: Offset is not clearly defined as the number of existing conversations to skip, and it is mistakenly used as a page number parameter.
- Phenomenon: After modifying global variables in the same conversation, subsequent interactions do not use the new values. Cause: The variable fields bound by `variables` are not updated during context transfer for multi-turn dialogue, only the initially passed values are retained.
- Phenomenon: Unable to distinguish between user questions and AI replies from different turns in the same conversation. Cause: `chatId` is not bound to conversation records to implement session isolation, leading to mixed data from different conversations.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a test question, and check if the full historical interaction content is retained in the conversation context panel.
- Modify the global variable value, then initiate a new question, and confirm that the updated variable content is used in the reply.
- Call the conversation record list interface, adjust the offset parameter, and verify that the returned conversation data is the expected subsequent entries.
- Check the context length parameter in the configuration panel, and confirm that its value covers the maximum possible content volume for a single round of interaction.
