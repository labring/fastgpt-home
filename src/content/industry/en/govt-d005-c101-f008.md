# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Port Shipping: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the data for this category looks like
Port shipping policy and regulatory basis documents mainly originate from official releases by the Ministry of Transport, direct maritime administrations, local port administrations, as well as internal work rules and safety specifications of port groups. Update frequency adjusts irregularly alongside policy revisions and new regulation releases. Common formats are docx and pdf. Some documents include nested tables, chapter numbering, and effective date markings. Core document fields include policy document number, applicable port area scope, effective time, operational requirements, and penalty clauses. Units cover meters, tons, hours, rate percentages, and other professional measurement standards.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on document parsing and chunking
The multi-format, nested table, and specialized field characteristics of port shipping policy documents create multiple constraints for parsing and chunking. Nested table parsing must retain row and column structure to avoid content loss or misalignment. Chapter numbering and effective dates are core metadata, and must be fully retained within chunks without being split across multiple blocks. Batch documents updated irregularly require support for fast upload and batch parsing, while accommodating format differences across sources. Some documents have long paragraphs spanning pages, so chunking logic must avoid breaking the integrity of individual policy details.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Port shipping policy documents mostly contain individual port area operational details or single rate tables. This range preserves content integrity |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by title + paragraph | Policy documents have clear chapter and clause titles. Splitting by title avoids invalid cross-chunk chunks |
| `customPdfParse` | Enable MinerU adaptation mode | Adapts to third-party parsing services, resolves parsing loss issues with complex nested tables |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 300–600 seconds | Large policy compilation documents take longer to parse. This range prevents task interruption from mid-run timeouts |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 1000 MB | Supports batch upload of multiple policy documents, meets batch update needs for regional policies and group regulations |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual configuration | For differentiated policy documents across port areas, chunk parameters can be flexibly adjusted to adapt to regional details |

## Three Common Mistakes
- No parsing results appear in the knowledge base after uploading a docx document, or content displays incorrectly. Cause: Some docx documents use non-standard embedded styles or encrypted formats, and the basic parsing engine cannot fully recognize the document structure.
- Table content from uploaded policy documents is split across multiple chunks. Cause: The `chunkSize` setting is too small, and the automatic adjustment logic for table boundaries is not adapted, causing the table to be forcibly split.
- Out-of-memory error occurs after deploying the MinerU parsing service, or GPU resources cannot be recognized. Cause: The CUDA device is not specified in the docker startup parameters, and the memory usage of the parsing service is not restricted, and it does not match the current graphics card driver version.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Upload a port navigation safety policy PDF document containing nested tables, check if the parsed markdown format fully retains the table's row and column structure and header information.
- Upload a port policy compilation document exceeding 500MB, confirm that the parsing task does not trigger a timeout error, and all chapter content is correctly chunked.
- Adjust the `chunkSize` parameter to 600 characters and 1500 characters, compare the chunking results, confirm that it can flexibly adapt to the length requirements of different documents.
- Check the system operation logs, confirm that the MinerU service has successfully mounted GPU resources, and no prompts of insufficient memory or device recognition failure appear.
