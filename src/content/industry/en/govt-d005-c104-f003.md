# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Coal Chemical Industry: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Coal chemical industry policy and regulatory basis data comes from modern coal chemical industry policies released by the National Energy Administration and the National Development and Reform Commission, internal compliance manuals of central and state-owned enterprises, and local coal chemical industry planning documents.
Updates trigger when new policies are released. Regular maintenance follows a quarterly synchronization cycle.
Each document includes fields such as document number, issuing authority, release date, applicable scenarios, clause details, and penalty notes. Document numbers follow the format `[Abbreviated Issuing Authority] 〔XXXX〕 XX Number`. Release dates use the YYYY-MM-DD format. Clauses are marked with "Article X".

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Single coal chemical policy documents are relatively long. Multi-turn dialogue must retain previously cited clause content. The context length for multi-turn dialogue must be adapted for long text scenarios.
Policy update frequency is high. The knowledge base synchronization cycle must match the policy release pace. This avoids retrieving outdated content.
There are many specialized terms in coal chemical policies. Prompts must clearly restrict the retrieval scope to coal chemical-related policies. Prompts must also require returning specific fields such as document number and release date.
Coal chemical policies vary across different regions. Corresponding documents must be accurately matched using regional parameters provided by user input.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Calling the `api/v1/chat/completions` interface without returning coal chemical policy results with specified tags. Phenomenon: Interface returns results that do not match the preset provincial coal chemical policy tags. Cause: No knowledge base tag parameter was passed in the `variables` field.
- Unable to associate and retrieve uploaded coal chemical policy PDF attachments during multi-turn dialogue. Phenomenon: The system prompts that relevant data cannot be found when attachment content is mentioned in the conversation. Cause: Automatic attachment chunking configuration was not enabled. Long text was not fully indexed as a result.
- Empty results returned after consecutive multi-turn dialogue interactions. Phenomenon: Responses are empty for conversations on the 4th turn or later. Cause: Maximum context length was set too small. Previously cited policy clause information was not retained.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Correct
- When calling the `api/v1/chat/completions` interface, check if the `variables` field includes parameters such as knowledge base tags and project region. Confirm that the parameter format meets the requirements of FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Enable test dialogue. Upload a coal chemical policy attachment. Input "Please extract the document number of this file". Verify whether the system returns the correct document number content.
- Enable multi-turn dialogue. Input 3 consecutive questions about different coal chemical policies. Verify whether the system retains the context association from previous conversations.
- View the knowledge base synchronization log. Confirm that the update frequency of coal chemical policy documents matches the configured synchronization cycle.
