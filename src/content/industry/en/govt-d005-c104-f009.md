# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Coal Chemical Industry: Model Access and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
The data sources for coal chemical industry policy and regulatory basis are mainly official documents and industry standards issued by the National Energy Administration, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and provincial development and reform commissions. Updates are adjusted irregularly alongside the release of new policies. Documents mostly exist in PDF or official web page formats, with structures including document numbers, issuing authorities, effective dates, scope of application, and specific clauses. Fields cover project approval requirements, energy consumption limits, pollutant emission limits, and similar content. Units mostly use common industry metrics such as ten thousand tons/year and tons of standard coal/ten thousand yuan. This page is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "model access and configuration" workflow
Coal chemical industry policy and regulatory documents are mostly long-form texts with fixed effective dates and application scopes. Models must retain full clause integrity when called, to avoid truncating critical approval or compliance requirements. Different policy documents have significant format differences, including plain text and embedded tables, so multi-format parsing logic must be supported. Policy updates follow no fixed cycle, so knowledge base synchronization must align with official release rhythms, and model configuration must support incremental updates. Policy clauses involve professional measurement values, so accurate numerical extraction and context association must be ensured to avoid compliance judgment deviations.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: After locally deploying the Qwen14B 32K model, uploading policy files causes parsing timeout and a request failure prompt. Cause: The `maxContext` parameter was not adjusted to adapt to long-text policy files, exceeding the model's context window and causing call interruption.
- Phenomenon: Model calling token consumption exceeds expectations, leading to abnormal billing or resource usage. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` parameter was not configured to limit the total token count of referenced fragments, causing redundant policy original text to be loaded repeatedly.
- Phenomenon: After uploading a policy PDF with embedded images, policy clauses in the images cannot be extracted. Cause: The `vision` parameter was not correctly configured, and embedded images in coal chemical industry policy documents are mostly scanned or non-standard formats, making it impossible for existing parsing models to accurately recognize text content.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Upload a single coal chemical policy PDF with more than 10 pages, check whether the parsing completion status is normal, and confirm there are no timeout or error prompts.
- Initiate a policy clause query, verify whether the reference fragments returned by the model match the uploaded policy original text, and confirm that context association is accurate.
- Switch the deployed model type, verify whether API calls can be completed normally, and confirm that the parameter configuration matches the model requirements.
- Check the system logs to confirm that the token consumption range conforms to the settings of the configured `maxTokens` and `quoteMaxToken` parameters.
