# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Coal Transportation and Sales: Model Access and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Coal transportation and sales policy and regulatory basis data mainly comes from the National Energy Administration, provincial coal industry management departments, transportation authorities, and internal compliance documents of relevant state-owned enterprises. Data updates follow the pace of policy revisions, with no fixed cycle. Core documents are stored in official document format, including fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable region, and transportation and sales clause terms. Some files include quantitative indicators such as compliant coal transportation tonnage and loading and unloading rate standards. Units include tons, kilometers, percentages, and others.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on model access and configuration
Coal transportation and sales policy documents have complex structures, including multi-level clauses and dedicated fields. When accessing the model, key identifiers such as document numbers and effective dates must be retained to avoid loss of core compliance basis in retrieval results. Data updates have no fixed cycle, so a synchronization trigger mechanism matching the source data must be configured to keep the knowledge base content consistent with the latest policies. Some documents include quantitative transportation and sales indicators, so the model must accurately identify and associate corresponding units and numerical values when called. Policies cover multiple transportation and sales links including railways, ports, and pitheads. The retrieval scope must be limited to coal transportation and sales related clauses to avoid interference from irrelevant policy content on answer accuracy.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- A 500 error is returned after calling the model. Investigation finds that `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` or the interface address is not configured correctly, causing the request to fail to reach the model service normally.
- A 429 exception is returned when concurrent requests exceed the set threshold. No concurrency restriction logic is configured in FastGPT, and the interface concurrency threshold of the cloud service provider is not matched.
- The policy clause document number or effective date is missing in the model's answer. This occurs because the `maxContext` value is too small, truncating the context fragment containing the key identifier.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Submit a test request containing specific transportation and sales policy questions, and check whether the returned result includes complete policy document numbers and effective dates to confirm that the context configuration meets expectations.
- Simulate multi-round concurrent requests, observe whether abnormal returns occur, and adjust the concurrency restriction configuration to match the threshold of the accessed model.
- Check the knowledge base synchronization log to confirm that the update time of policy data is consistent with the revision time of the source data, verifying that the synchronization logic is normal.
- Test different types of transportation and sales policy questions to confirm that the model only returns coal transportation and sales related policy content without interference from irrelevant field policies.
