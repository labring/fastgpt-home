# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Coal-Electric Integration: Model Access and Configuration

## What this category of data looks like
The policy and regulatory basis data for coal-electric integration comes primarily from publicly available normative documents and industry standard materials issued by the National Energy Administration, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and provincial-level energy authorities. Data updates follow policy release timelines, with no fixed cycle. Updates are synchronized immediately after new policies are issued. Individual documents typically include fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable scenarios, and core clauses, organized by article, paragraph, and subparagraph levels. Some documents also include supporting interpretation materials.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the model access and configuration link
Policy documents are mostly long texts with clear hierarchical structures. The model needs to accurately identify clause levels and associated content, which places clear requirements on context window handling. Structured fields such as policy document numbers and effective dates require precise matching, so the vector model needs to meet requirements for structured information encoding. Non-fixed update data sources mean the configuration needs to support dynamic switching of knowledge bases, and regular validation of data source validity is needed. Chunking of long documents needs to adapt to clause hierarchies to avoid breaking context associations, while ensuring that recalled policy clauses accurately correspond to the scenarios covered by user queries.

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: Calling a multimodal model to process images in policy documents returns format errors, with a prompt indicating unsupported image format. Cause: The image was not converted to PNG or JPG format as required, and the image URL or base64 encoded content was not correctly included in the request.
- Phenomenon: After configuring the `maxContext` parameter, recalled policy clauses still show context fragmentation. Cause: Chunking was not adapted to the clause hierarchy of policy documents, and long documents were directly truncated at fixed lengths, leading to loss of clause association relationships.
- Phenomenon: The model access function fails to restart normally after changing the FastGPT service password. Cause: Relevant service processes dependent on model invocation were not restarted at the same time; only restarting the main service resulted in configuration changes not being synchronized and applied.
