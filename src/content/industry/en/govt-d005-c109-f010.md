# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Power Grid Enterprises: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Policy and regulatory basis data for power grid enterprises primarily comes from the National Energy Administration, local energy regulatory authorities, and official internal corporate documents. Data updates align with the pace of policy releases, and immediate synchronization occurs when new industry standards or internal enterprise rules are issued. Most documents follow formal official formats, including fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, and clause entries. Clauses follow a hierarchical structure of articles, paragraphs, and subparagraphs. The length of individual documents ranges from hundreds to tens of thousands of words.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall Workflows
Policy documents have a wide range of lengths. Logical connections of core clauses must not be broken when chunking long documents. Single-batch recall volume must fit the context limits of long texts. Documents include clear hierarchical fields, so retrieval must prioritize precise matching of clause hierarchies, and cover metadata dimensions such as document number and effective date. Data update cadence aligns with policy changes, so incremental synchronization of the knowledge base must match policy release cycles to avoid recalling outdated content. Each document has clear clause hierarchies, so recall results must retain the original clause attribution to ensure the accuracy of cited basis during Q&A.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Matches the rigor requirements of power grid policy documents, filters low-relevance general results |
| `limit` | Top 10 entries | Adapts to the single-batch recall volume for power grid policy documents, avoids excessive redundant content |
| `searchMode` | Precise matching | Prioritizes precise matching of clause hierarchies, meets the rigor needs of policy Q&A |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Reorders recall results by relevance to optimize result ranking after long document chunking |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically supplements synonymous expressions related to policies, covering professional terms across different terminology standards |
| `collectionIds` | Bind exclusive policy knowledge base collection | Isolates power grid enterprise policy data from other categories to avoid cross-category interference |

This chapter’s configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After uploading a PDF policy document, search tests return no results and the interface displays an error. Cause: Document headers and footers (document number, page number) are incorrectly identified as invalid content, or the file was not correctly bound to the exclusive policy knowledge base collection.
- Phenomenon: After the knowledge base is split into multiple chunks, the number of query results is far lower than expected. Cause: The `limit` parameter value is too low, failing to recall enough relevant chunks, or chunking breaks the hierarchical connection of policy clauses.
- Phenomenon: Q&A performance when using a local model is poorer than when using an API model. Cause: The `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` feature is not enabled, so synonymous expressions for professional terms related to power grid policies cannot be covered, leading to insufficient relevance of recall results.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a test power grid policy document, run a search test, and check if the number of returned results matches the `limit` parameter setting.
- Check the similarity scores of recall results, confirm that the scores fall within the preset range of the `similarity` parameter.
- Compare recall rankings with and without `usingReRank` enabled, confirm that the reordering function works correctly.
- Verify the incremental sync function: After uploading a new policy file, the new content can be retrieved via search test.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
