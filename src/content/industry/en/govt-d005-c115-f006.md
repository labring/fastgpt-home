# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Rail Transit Equipment: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for rail transit equipment is primarily sourced from industry standards, compliance requirements, and special policy documents released by the National Railway Administration and the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. The data update rhythm adjusts irregularly alongside national standard revisions and new industry rule releases, with no fixed cycle. Each individual document includes fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable equipment type, and technical parameter requirements. Technical parameters are mostly labeled with specialized units such as axle load, traction power, and operating speed. Document content is divided into clauses and compliance terms by chapter, and some files include adaptation scope instructions for multiple vehicle types. This page is organized based on the configuration logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the citation source and traceability link
The specialized fields and hierarchical structure of rail transit equipment policy data require precise matching of equipment application scope and effective date during citation traceability, to avoid mixing policies across periods and vehicle types. The presence of specialized parameter units requires that traceability results fully retain original markings, without unauthorized modification or omission. Irregular update cycles mean the knowledge base must regularly sync the latest policy documents. Otherwise, traceability results may reference revoked old regulations. The multi-vehicle adaptation document structure requires combining the specific equipment type of the query to filter recalled content during retrieval, preventing irrelevant policies from being included in the citation list.

## Three common errors
- Phenomenon: During mixed retrieval, recalled policy documents do not match the current query's rail transit equipment type, and irrelevant content is output. Cause: No citation source filtering rule configured based on equipment application scope, resulting in simultaneous recall of cross-vehicle policies.
- Phenomenon: After configuring citation variables or knowledge base IDs, the output result remains unchanged and an error occurs during application. Cause: Failed to correctly bind access permissions to the rail transit equipment-specific policy knowledge base, or the variable format did not match system requirements.
- Phenomenon: After adjusting the similarity threshold and citation upper limit parameters, the number of recalled citations remains fixed and cannot increase. Cause: Knowledge base chunks are not divided separately by policy document number or effective date, leading to repeated recalls or inability to accurately match query conditions.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Initiate a policy query targeting a specific rail transit equipment type, check the citation list in the output result, confirm that each citation includes metadata such as policy document number and issuing authority.
- Switch queries for different types of rail transit equipment, verify that recalled policy documents only contain content within the corresponding application scope.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, check whether the total length of cited content changes with the parameter.
- Test historical conversation scenarios, confirm that previously generated policy citation records can be correctly retrieved and traced.
