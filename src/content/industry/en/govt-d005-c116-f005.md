# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Telecommunications Engineering Construction: Tool Calling and Plugins

## What the data for this category looks like
Telecommunications engineering construction policy and regulatory basis data is primarily sourced from national telecommunications engineering construction specifications and management measures issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, as well as local implementation rules issued by provincial and municipal communications administrations. The data update schedule is adjusted in tandem with new policy releases, with no fixed cycle. Document structures uniformly include fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable scenarios, and enforcement clauses. Applicable scenarios are categorized by sub-scenarios including base station construction, pipeline laying, communication network operation and maintenance, etc. Field units use standard formats such as "Article X" and "XX Province/Autonomous Region". ## What constraints these characteristics impose on the tool calling and plugins workflow
Because policy data includes cross-regional local implementation rules, tool calling must support precise filtering of knowledge base entries by region to avoid invoking inapplicable policies. The chapterized structure of policy documents requires plugins to extract content by locating clause hierarchies; direct extraction of unrelated fragmented text is not supported. The presence of the effective date field requires tool calling to verify policy timeliness and exclude expired clauses. Cross-departmental policy association requirements mean plugins must support retrieving documents from multiple sources simultaneously, which increases the complexity of the calling chain.

## Three common misconfigurations
- The symptom is a "format error" returned by the plugin node. The cause is that text extraction rules are not configured per the chapter structure of telecommunications engineering policy documents, resulting in extracted content that does not match the preset format.
- The symptom is that knowledge base retrieved policy entries do not match the target region. The cause is that the region filtering switch is not enabled, leading to retrieval of inapplicable policy rules across provinces and cities.
- The symptom is that the plugin node returns no results during workflow execution. The cause is that the batch calling parameter for `functionCall` is not configured, making simultaneous retrieval of policy documents from multiple sources impossible.
