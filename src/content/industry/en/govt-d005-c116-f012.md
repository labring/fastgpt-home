# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Telecommunications Engineering Construction: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Telecommunications engineering construction policy and regulatory basis data comes from normative documents, industry standard documents released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, and local telecommunications industry authorities, plus compliance filing materials for telecommunications engineering construction projects. Most documents are available in PDF or web format. Their structure includes fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable engineering scenarios, clause details, and penalties. Field units mostly use standardized formats like "Article X", "YYYY-MM-DD", "square meters", "kilometers". Updates follow no fixed cycle, and are synchronized with new policy releases or industry standard revisions.

## Constraints imposed on deployment and upgrade links
Telecommunications engineering construction policy data has numerous structured fields, originates from dispersed sources, and has no fixed update cycle. This creates three constraints for deployment and upgrade workflows:
1.  Configure a database that supports storage of complex structured fields, to ensure efficient verification and retrieval of fields such as document number and effective date.
2.  Reserve a configuration entry for incremental data synchronization during the upgrade phase, to adapt to non-fixed-cycle policy updates.
3.  Support isolated configuration of multi-region data sources, to accommodate differentiated requirements for telecommunications engineering policies across different regions.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After local deployment, accessing the page prompts an invalid certificate and fails to establish an HTTPS connection. Cause: The certificate path generated by mkcert is not bound in the `FE_DOMAIN` configuration item, or the domain name is not pointed to the locally deployed service address.
- Phenomenon: After offline server deployment, the AI proxy reports an error, and the log shows `get tiktoken dial tcp lookup`. Cause: `PG_URL` or `REDIS_URL` is not configured correctly. This prevents the service from connecting to dependent database and cache components, and fails to initialize the token counting module.
- Phenomenon: After deploying Sealos on Alibaba Cloud, the Deepseek test returns a configuration error. Cause: Structured fields of telecommunications engineering policy documents are not correctly mapped to model input parameters, or the `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` configuration is incorrect, leading to failed document parsing.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Access the domain configured in `FE_DOMAIN`, verify that the HTTPS connection works normally and the certificate status is valid.
- Log in to the database console pointed to by `PG_URL`, check whether structured data tables related to telecommunications engineering policies exist.
- Start the AI proxy service, check that the log contains no connection error prompts of the `dial tcp lookup` type.
- Upload a telecommunications engineering construction policy document, verify that core fields such as document number and effective date can be correctly extracted after parsing.
