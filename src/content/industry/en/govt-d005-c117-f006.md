# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Communication Towers and Infrastructure: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the communication towers and infrastructure sector is primarily sourced from public regulations, industry standards, and local supporting documents issued by official departments including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development. Data is updated on a quarterly basis, with synchronizations tied to the release of new industry norms. Documents follow standard official document formatting, including fields such as policy document number, issuing authority, effective date, scope of application, and specific clauses. Clause content is split by chapter and section numbering, using formats like "Article X" and "Clause X". The length of individual clauses typically falls between 500 and 800 characters.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability workflow
Fixed fields from official sources require accurate matching of identifiers such as policy document numbers and issuing authorities during traceability. Valid traceability cannot be completed without this matching. The quarterly update frequency means the knowledge base synchronization cycle must align with this timeline. This prevents citing expired policy content. The standard official document chapter structure requires retaining chapter and section numbering and clause attribution during citation. Traceability results cannot be linked to specific policy clauses without this data. The length limit for individual clauses requires reserving sufficient token space for citations. Truncating critical content will result in incomplete traceability information.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: `quote type error` is returned after invocation. Cause: The format of citation fields specified in `responseData` was not configured correctly. Non-permitted field names or non-compliant field formats were entered.
- Symptom: Only a single option appears in the citation source dropdown menu. Cause: The `detail` configuration was not enabled. Only a single core field was returned, and multi-clause citation content was not expanded.
- Symptom: Redundant non-policy citation content is automatically displayed in responses. Cause: The recall scope for `responseData` was not limited. Document content outside the communication tower policy sector was recalled.

## How to confirm correct configuration
- Submit a test inquiry regarding communication tower policies. Check whether the response includes traceability fields such as policy document numbers and issuing authorities.
- Verify that the length of cited content in the response falls within the range specified by `quoteMaxToken`. No truncation should occur.
- Check whether the citation source dropdown menu displays multiple relevant policy clauses. The menu should cover the policy scope involved in the test inquiry.
- Confirm that citation display can be toggled on or off via configuration. This should align with business requirements.
