# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Metal Material Processing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Policy and regulatory basis data for metal material processing originates from industrial policies, environmental protection standards, production licensing specifications, and quality supervision requirements issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, and local industry authorities. The data updates in real time alongside official policy releases, with no fixed publication cycle. Each individual policy document includes structured fields such as document number, issuing authority, effective date, scope of application, core clauses, and violation penalties. The scope of application covers segmented processing categories including hot-rolled steel plates and non-ferrous metal profiles. Fields use standard official document formatting, with no custom units added.

## Constraints on Citation Sources and Traceability
Metal material processing policy data is scattered across official channels of multiple departments. The traceability link must accurately connect to the original document links of the corresponding issuing authority, and avoid citing unofficial interpretive content. Policies update in real time alongside official releases. Synchronization trigger rules must be configured to ensure cited content always matches the latest effective version. Each individual document includes structured fields such as document number and effective date. These key details must be displayed synchronously during citation to enhance credibility. The scope of application covers segmented processing categories. The retrieval phase must limit matching to the specific processing scenario corresponding to the current conversation, to prevent invalid citations of cross-category policies.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Issue: After exporting a workflow and importing it to another environment, the associated plugin for citation sources fails to load normally. Cause: Dependent configurations including associated knowledge base sources were not selected during export, so the new environment cannot recognize the required policy data source synchronization plugin.
- Issue: When displaying citations at the end of a response paragraph, an error message "No permission to operate this conversation record" appears, and citation content cannot be hidden or modified. Cause: Permission scopes for conversation records were not configured, so the front end cannot properly pull associated data from citation sources.
- Issue: When specifying line breaks using \n in response prompts, the actual output retains the literal \n text instead of rendering line breaks. Cause: The Markdown rendering switch for response content was not enabled, so special characters were not parsed into line break formatting.
