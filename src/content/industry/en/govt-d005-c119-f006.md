# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Iron and Steel Smelting: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the iron and steel smelting sector comes from industry standards and safety regulations issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, plus internal smelting operation specifications from relevant central state-owned enterprises.
Industry standards are revised annually. Internal specifications are synced quarterly.
Each policy document includes its number, issuing authority, effective date, applicable smelting processes such as blast furnace ironmaking and converter steelmaking, clause number, and original text.
Fields include unique policy identifiers, applicable scenario tags, and original clause text. Clause serial numbers and document numbers serve as core identifying units.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" workflow
Policy data includes clear document numbers and effective dates. The traceability workflow must accurately match document identifiers and time ranges to avoid citing expired clauses.
Documents are tagged with applicable smelting processes. Retrieval must filter out irrelevant policies by process to ensure cited content aligns with current operating scenarios.
Policy update frequency is fixed. The knowledge base sync cycle must match this rhythm. Otherwise, traceability will use invalid policies.
Clause hierarchy is clearly defined. Citations must target specific clauses, not just document names. Otherwise, compliance traceability requirements will not be met.

## Configuration Settings

| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Iron and steel smelting policy clauses are lengthy. Sufficient tokens must be reserved to fully output cited original text and markers |
| `responseData` | Includes original text snippets and source metadata | Must return information such as policy document number and issuing authority to meet compliance traceability requirements |
| `detail` | Enabled | Enables return of citation metadata to ensure the interface can output cite-related fields |
| Single search limit | Top 6 results | The number of iron and steel smelting policy documents is moderate. Excessive results increase output redundancy and harm reading experience |
| Knowledge base sync cycle | Once per quarter | Aligns with the update rhythm of industry policies and internal specifications |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: Garbled citation marks appear at the end of conversation outputs, which are later restored to standard quotation marks. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` configuration value is too small. This truncates citation markers in the original text, preventing complete output.
- Phenomenon: When retrieving a question not present in the knowledge base, the system still returns references to knowledge base files. Cause: Matching degree verification logic is not configured, and low-matching retrieval results are not filtered.
- Phenomenon: After connecting the knowledge base to the tool call module in a workflow, the output includes no citation source information. Cause: The citation traceability switch is not enabled in the tool configuration, and the `detail` configuration item is not associated.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Submit a query about a specific iron and steel smelting policy clause. Check if the output includes source information such as policy document number, issuing unit, and effective date.
- Submit a query that does not exist in the knowledge base. Check if the output includes no citation markers.
- View the interface response data for the conversation request. Confirm that cite-related metadata fields are present.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` value, then test whether long policy clause outputs have no garbled characters.
