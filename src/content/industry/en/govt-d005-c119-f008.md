# Policy and Regulatory Basis Q&A for Iron and Steel Smelting: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What this category of data looks like
Policy and regulatory basis data for the iron and steel smelting field mainly comes from official policy documents released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and the China Iron and Steel Industry Association, as well as internal production compliance documents of central and state-owned enterprises. Document update rhythm is adjusted irregularly with the release of new national standards and industry regulations. Internal system documents are updated quarterly or annually. Document structures include document numbers, issuing authorities, effective dates, clauses and subclauses. Most documents include structured tables such as capacity indicators, energy consumption limits, and pollutant emission requirements. Fields include policy numbers, clause content, and quantitative indicators. Quantitative indicators mostly use industry-specific units such as kg standard coal/ton and m³/hour.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the document parsing and chunking link?
Structured tables in policy documents will be broken apart by default chunking. Complete table rows or blocks must be preserved. Special units are tied to quantitative indicators, so chunking must not split associated content. Irregularly updated documents require incremental parsing to avoid repeated full processing. Clause hierarchies of long documents must be retained to match user queries for specific policy clauses. These characteristics require the document parsing link to prioritize preserving format and structured content. Chunking rules must adapt to industry-specific document structures, rather than using generic plain text chunking logic.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After uploading multi-column iron and steel policy Excel attachments, chunking results are scattered and chaotic. Cause: Custom table parsing is not enabled, and default character-based chunking breaks the association between table rows and units.
- Phenomenon: When batch uploading policy documents, previously parsed files are repeatedly loaded and parsed. Cause: The full parsing switch is not turned off, and the system defaults to reprocessing all uploaded files.
- Phenomenon: After uploading scanned policy image files, the model cannot recognize policy text within the images. Cause: The OCR parsing module is not configured, and FastGPT does not support direct parsing of image format documents by default.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Complete
- Upload an official iron and steel industry policy PDF, and verify that the parsed text retains the document number, clause hierarchy and table structure.
- Upload an Excel policy attachment with a capacity indicator table, and check that chunking results are split by complete rows or table blocks.
- Upload a single policy attachment, and confirm that the knowledge base only adds parsing results for the currently uploaded file, with no repeated loading of historical files.
- Upload a policy compilation file over 100 MB, and confirm that the parsing task does not trigger a timeout error.
