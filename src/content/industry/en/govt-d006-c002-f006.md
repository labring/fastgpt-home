# "One-Stop One-Event Handling" Joint Office Document and Material Drafting: Citation Source and Traceability

Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category typically comes from the national government service platform’s "One Event" special zone, local integrated government service platforms, electronic certificate repositories, and departmental approval systems. The update frequency is daily.
Commonly generated and used documents include joint office item lists, one-time notice sheets, joint office application forms, material circulation sheets, parallel approval opinions, and completion receipts.
Data fields usually include item name, associated departments, material details, handling nodes, and circulation status. There is no unified fixed unit, and adjustments are made based on the specific content of each individual document.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Citation Source and Traceability Link
Data sources are scattered and updated daily. This requires the citation and traceability link to support real-time synchronization verification across multiple data sources, and avoid citing expired joint office item rules.
There are diverse daily document types and large differences in field structures. It is necessary to configure dedicated source matching rules for different document types to ensure that traceability information corresponds one-to-one with document content.
The cross-department data circulation feature requires that the specific department system from which the material comes be clearly marked during traceability, while adapting to the field naming differences of different systems to ensure the readability and compliance of traceability information.

## How to Configure the Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | Adapts to the average content length of documents related to joint office items, ensuring that cited content is complete and does not exceed the model's context limit |
| `responseData` | Enable source item binding | It is necessary to associate cited document content with specific data sources to meet traceability compliance requirements in government affairs scenarios |
| `detail` | Enable detailed traceability mode | It is necessary to display the department system, update time and other information corresponding to the cited content, in line with the traceability standards of government services |
| Single search limit | Top 6–10 items | The number of associated departments involved in joint office items usually falls within this range, ensuring coverage of core source data |
| Data source synchronization frequency | Daily synchronization | Matches the daily update rhythm of data in this category, ensuring the timeliness of cited content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Generated joint office documents do not include cited local knowledge base content, but corresponding entries exist in the traceability list. Cause: The content binding configuration corresponding to `responseData` is not enabled. Only traceability information is stored, and the content is not injected into the generation result.
- Phenomenon: The department system displayed in the citation traceability information does not match the actual data source. Cause: Dedicated field mapping rules are not configured for different departmental approval systems, leading to incorrect source matching.
- Phenomenon: The generated cited content exceeds the preset length, causing the model response to be interrupted. Cause: The value set for `quoteMaxToken` is too large, exceeding the context threshold supported by the model.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a test request for joint office item drafting, and check whether the generated document body includes the cited joint office material details.
- View the traceability list attached to the generated result, and confirm that each cited content is marked with the corresponding department system and update time.
- Initiate a query by simulating a cross-department joint office scenario, and check whether the number of search results matches the configured single search limit.
- Test the data source retrieval delay scenario, and confirm that the system triggers a reasonable error prompt when the delay exceeds the preset threshold.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
