# Document and Material Drafting for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data for this category primarily comes from social insurance administration systems, personal benefit records, labor relations and work injury identification systems, and electronic social security card platforms. Data is updated daily. Daily processed documents include benefit application forms, payment period certificates, retirement approval forms, work injury identification materials, and unemployment benefit application materials. Most document structures mix structured forms and unstructured supporting materials. These include participant identity identifiers, payment cycle records, and qualification-related fields. Some fields are measured in months or individual instances.

## How These Characteristics Impose Constraints on Vector Models and Indexing
This category’s data is multi-source, heterogeneous, and updated daily. This requires indexing pipelines to support incremental synchronization, avoiding the time cost of full reindexing. Daily documents mix structured forms and unstructured text. The `maxToken` setting must match the total length of documents to prevent critical business information from being truncated during vectorization. Documents include fields with specific units. Indexes must retain field metadata to avoid confusion between different types of business data during matching. Since benefit verification directly relates to fund payments and public interests, retrieval and recall accuracy and timeliness must align with business processing rhythms to avoid delays disrupting workflow.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The interface only displays a single vector model selection entry, and independent embedding models cannot be configured for different business datasets. Cause: The multi-vector model grouping function is not enabled, and the system uses a unified global embedding model configuration by default.
- Phenomenon: Indexing tasks remain stuck in the "indexing" state with no progress updates or error messages. Cause: The number of documents processed in a single batch exceeds the system’s default limit, or incremental indexing is not configured, leading to excessively long full reindexing times.
- Phenomenon: Documents generated with a specific vector model locally cannot be recalled normally on the server. Cause: The vector model embedding dimensions used locally and on the server are inconsistent, leading to mismatched vector spaces that prevent similarity calculation.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Correct
- Review the vector model’s embedding dimension parameters to confirm they match the dimensions of the called API or local model, and align with the vectorization requirements of business data.
- Trigger a small-batch test document indexing task, and check whether task logs include matching records for chunk length and overlap length to confirm the configuration has taken effect.
- Retrieve a known business document keyword, and verify that the similarity scores of returned results fall within the preset threshold interval.
- Check incremental indexing synchronization logs to confirm that daily updated data is correctly identified and added to the index.
