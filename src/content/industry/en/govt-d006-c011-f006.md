# Document and Material Drafting for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Source Citation and Traceability

## What data for this category looks like
This category's data originates from social insurance management systems, personal rights and interests records, labor relations and work injury verification systems, and the electronic social security card platform. The update frequency is daily. Daily produced documents include benefit application forms, payment duration certificates, retirement approval forms, work injury verification materials, and unemployment benefit application materials. Document fields include insured person identity identifiers, payment duration, benefit verification results, qualification review conclusions, and other relevant fields. Some fields must match unique traceability identifiers generated by systems. Field units include common business units such as years, yuan, and months.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on source citation and traceability
Multi-source data means citation traceability must associate content from different business systems. Cross-system citations must clearly identify the corresponding data source. The daily update frequency requires cited content to match the latest system data to ensure timeliness. Document structures are complex with diverse fields. Citations must retain complete information for specific business fields, and must not lose critical business details. The business logic centered on insured person information requires cited content to precisely match data for the corresponding insured person. This prevents mixing benefit verification results of different insured persons, and reduces risks of incorrect or missed benefit distribution.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: Excessive cited content in returned results causes cluttered interface display. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is not configured, leading to cited content exceeding reasonable length.
- Phenomenon: Red error prompts appear in cited content. Cause: Data sources are not correctly bound, unable to match social insurance management system data for this category.
- Phenomenon: During multi-round workflow testing, only the first question loads knowledge base citations, subsequent questions have no cited content. Cause: Insured person identifier matching rules across questions are not configured, subsequent links cannot associate data for the corresponding insured person.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- A single insured person identity query is initiated, and cited content in returned results is checked for matches to fields including that insured person's payment duration and benefit verification results.
- The source identifier of cited content is reviewed, and confirmation is made that it comes from specified data sources including social insurance management systems and personal rights and interests records.
- The `quoteMaxToken` configuration is adjusted, and whether the length of returned cited content falls within the set range is verified.
- Multi-round business processes are tested, and whether subsequent workflow links can normally load and display cited content is confirmed.
