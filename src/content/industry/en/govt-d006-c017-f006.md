# Document and Material Drafting for Medical Institution Supervision and Public Health Inspection: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category originates from health supervision information systems, medical institution electronic registration systems, complaint reporting platforms, and law enforcement inspection records, with a monthly update frequency. Daily processed documents include supervision inspection forms, medical institution practice licenses, rectification notices, supervision opinions, penalty case files, and more. The practice license includes structured fields such as institution name, permitted scope, and validity period. Supervision case files hold unstructured content like on-site inspection details and rectification requirements. This data covers the full workflow of medical institution qualification, on-site law enforcement, and rectification tracking, with all fields directly tied to practice compliance.

## Constraints for Citation Source and Traceability
The monthly update frequency requires the knowledge base used for citation traceability to align with this rhythm, to avoid citing expired license or inspection information. Document types span structured qualification files and unstructured law enforcement case files, so retrieval rules must balance accurate field matching and full retention of long text content. Errors here directly relate to medical institution penalties and practice risks, so citation traceability must clearly mark the specific type and generation time of the source document, to ensure compliance can be traced. Additionally, data sources involve cross-system coordination, so cross-system consistency of cited information must be maintained.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Covers core content of long-text documents such as supervision case files and rectification notices, preventing key law enforcement information from being truncated |
| `responseData` | Enabled | Used to return the knowledge base document ID, name, and source system information for citations, meeting traceability requirements |
| `detail` | Enabled | Displays specific fields of the cited document, such as institution name, inspection date, and license validity period, improving traceability credibility |
| Number of citations displayed | Top 3–5 entries | Health sector documents are content-concentrated; too many citations lead to redundant results, while too few fail to cover the necessary basis for compliance checks |
| Knowledge base synchronization frequency | Monthly synchronization | Matches the monthly update rhythm of business data for this category, ensuring the timeliness of cited information |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The conversation return result does not include the cited knowledge base document ID, only displays the document name. Cause: The `responseData` configuration item is not enabled, or the citation data return field is not correctly configured.
- Phenomenon: The cited content of long-text supervision case files is truncated, and rectification requirement details cannot be fully displayed. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` setting value is too small, failing to reserve enough tokens to carry complete law enforcement document content.
- Phenomenon: Search citation results include non-law-enforcement medical institution registration information that does not match the current supervision scenario. Cause: Targeted retrieval rules for health sector documents are not set, and full knowledge base content is mistakenly included in the citation scope.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Complete
- Initiate a query targeting a medical institution practice license, confirm that the returned result includes the cited document name and source system information at the bottom.
- Call the FastGPT v4.17.0 conversation interface, check the returned JSON field to confirm that it includes the citation entry ID and detailed information corresponding to `responseData`.
- Trigger a query for a complete supervision case file, confirm that the cited content is not truncated and falls within the range set by `quoteMaxToken`.
- Manually trigger a knowledge base synchronization task, confirm that the synchronization cycle matches the monthly setting, and expired data is not included in the current citation scope.
