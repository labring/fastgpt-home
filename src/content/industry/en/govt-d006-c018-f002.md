# Document and Material Drafting for Social Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Vector Models and Indexes

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Typical data subjects for this category include social insurance handling agencies, human resources and social security bureaus, tax authorities, and personnel and finance departments of employing units. Relevant entities daily generate documents including social insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification notices, and personal benefit records. Data sources cover social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. Data is updated daily. Most documents are structured forms, with fields including personal identity information, labor relationship identifiers, payment bases, and unit registration information.

## Constraints Imposed on Vector Models and Indexes
Structured form documents in this category have dense fields and cross-system associated data. Vector chunking must preserve the contextual integrity of key fields. Daily updated data volumes require incremental indexing strategies to avoid performance losses from full index rebuilding. Unified embedding of multi-source heterogeneous data must support field formats from different systems. Accurate retrieval results are required to prevent underpayment, missed payments, or incorrect benefit records caused by matching deviations.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Timeout errors occur in pgvector index queries. Cause: No incremental indexing strategy is configured for daily updated data. Frequent full index rebuilding leads to insufficient performance.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results omit key personnel information from personnel change forms. Cause: Chunk length settings exceed the reasonable range of form fields, truncating core field content.
- Phenomenon: Token count values do not match actual document content. Cause: Incorrect boundary configuration for `maxToken` leads to overflow deviations in token counting for long documents.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Review the running logs of embedding tasks. Confirm that all target documents have completed chunking and embedding, with no truncation or failure prompts.
- Run a simulated retrieval test. Verify that returned results include core document types for this category, such as social insurance registration forms and payment base declaration forms.
- Validate the token statistics function. Confirm that token counts for single documents match actual content lengths, with no abnormal overflow.
- Trigger a daily incremental update task. Confirm that newly generated social insurance data can be correctly synchronized to the index.

The values provided here are common starting points. They should be evaluated against local samples.
