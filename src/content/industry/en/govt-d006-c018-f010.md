# Document and Material Drafting for Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. The update frequency is daily. Commonly generated documents include insurance registration forms, premium base declaration forms, staff adjustment forms, social insurance premium verification forms, and personal benefit records. Document fields include the unified social credit code of the entity, resident ID number of personnel, premium base, verification date, personnel labor relationship status, and others. There is no unified fixed format template, and some documents require cross-system association and filling.

## Constraints for Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
Daily updated data sources require the retrieval system to support incremental synchronization and incremental recall, to avoid full scanning consuming resources. Multiple types of business documents and cross-system field association require retrieval to balance semantic matching and exact matching of key fields, to prevent errors in identity information matching. Document drafting requires reference to multiple associated documents, so recall results must cover sufficient reference entries while avoiding redundant information interference. The presence of sensitive fields requires retrieval results to undergo permission filtering, only displaying compliant content to authorized users.

## Configuration Settings
This guide is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0. The following configurations are adapted for this category scenario:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Documents for insurance registration and premium verification require accurate matching of personnel identity and business rules. This range filters out irrelevant results with low matching degrees. |
| `limit` | Top 10 entries | Document drafting needs sufficient reference materials while avoiding excessive redundant information interfering with the drafting process. |
| `searchMode` | Hybrid retrieval | Balances semantic matching and exact matching of key fields such as resident ID numbers and unified social credit codes to improve retrieval accuracy. |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled, using GPU acceleration | Insurance-related documents have high requirements for matching accuracy. Reranking improves the relevance of top results, and GPU acceleration reduces response latency. |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Optimizes colloquial handling demands entered by users into standardized retrieval terms to match more accurate business documents. |
| `collectionIds` | Fill in the exclusive dataset ID for insurance registration and premium verification | Filters business documents from other categories to avoid recalling content unrelated to this scenario such as benefit verification. |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: An error occurs when clicking the knowledge base module after default deployment with docker compose, and the interface displays the prompt "Data source not configured". Cause: The default configuration does not mount the storage volume of the local knowledge base dataset, and initialization of the exclusive dataset is not completed.
- Symptom: Retrieval results include content unrelated to insurance registration, mixed with benefit verification documents. Cause: `collectionIds` is not configured to filter the exclusive dataset, resulting in recall of business documents from other human resources and social security categories.
- Symptom: Retrieval response times out, with no results returned after more than 20 seconds. Cause: GPU acceleration for `usingReRank` is not enabled, and the recall range of `searchMode` is not restricted, leading a large number of irrelevant documents to participate in matching calculations.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Enter the FastGPT dataset management page, confirm that the exclusive dataset for insurance registration and premium verification has been created and associated, and the unique identifier of the corresponding dataset has been filled in `collectionIds`.
- Initiate a simulated retrieval, enter a business demand related to insurance registration or premium verification, and check whether the returned results only include business documents of this category with no irrelevant content.
- View the retrieval log to confirm that the `similarity` parameter is in effect, filtering out results with matching degrees lower than the set range.
- Test the response time of multiple retrievals, adjust the `limit` or `usingReRank` configuration to adapt to the real-time requirements of the handling scenario.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
