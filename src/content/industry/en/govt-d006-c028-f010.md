# Document and Material Drafting for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
Core documents for this category include standardized form-based documents such as establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, and food business license applications. Data comes primarily from the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. Data updates occur daily. Document structures are fixed, with clear fields including entity name, residence, shareholder information, and licensing matters. No complex nested content exists. All fields align with legal requirements for market entity registration.

## Constraints on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
Daily data updates require the knowledge base synchronization mechanism to support high-frequency incremental updates. This prevents the recall of expired registration data. Fixed form-based document structures require retrieval to match field keywords precisely. This avoids recalling irrelevant content through vague matches. Data from multiple sources has consistent formatting, but users must distinguish between raw data exported from systems and standardized document templates. This prevents confusion of retrieval scopes. A single retrieval must cover associated content such as pre and post registration and licensing conditions, and correction requirements. This ensures retrieval results support judgments during the material verification link.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for This Setting |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Adapt to the keyword matching accuracy of form-based documents, filter low-relevance non-registration documents |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Cover multiple types of results such as templates, preconditions, and correction requirements required for registration and licensing, to avoid missing key information |
| `searchMode` | Keyword matching priority | Adapt to the precise retrieval requirements of fixed fields, prioritize recalling documents that match legal fields |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Reorder the initial recall results by business relevance to ensure that core content such as pre and post licensing conditions ranks first |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically expand synonymous expressions related to registration, such as associating "residence certificate" with "business place certificate" to cover documents with different expressions |
| `collectionIds` | Bind a dedicated registration and licensing knowledge base collection with a total capacity not exceeding 1000 MB | Limit the retrieval scope to the dedicated data of this category to avoid mixing in other market supervision documents |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Retrieval returns no results, with a page prompt stating no matching content was found. This occurs when the `similarity` threshold is set too high, filtering all documents that meet business requirements. This falls under the knowledge base retrieval failure issue category.
- Retrieval results include non-registration documents, such as materials from other market supervision businesses. This occurs when `collectionIds` is not bound to a dedicated dataset, leading to unintended retrieval of content from other business knowledge bases.
- Retrieval results lack the latest licensing application templates, failing to match current business needs. This occurs when the knowledge base incremental training task does not trigger normally. This falls under the knowledge base training abnormality issue category.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Access the knowledge base management page in FastGPT v4.17.0. Confirm whether `collectionIds` is bound to the dedicated market entity registration and licensing dataset.
- Run a retrieval with the keyword "residence certificate". Check that the number of returned results matches the value set in the `limit` configuration.
- Test a retrieval for "pre-licensing conditions". Confirm whether `usingReRank` is enabled, and that core business-related results appear first.
- Trigger an incremental synchronization task. Verify that the data update time listed in system logs matches the update time of business data.

The values provided here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against applicable local samples.
