# Document and Material Drafting for Government Procurement Projects: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category includes official documents such as procurement requirements, procurement plans, bidding documents, tender documents, bid evaluation reports, winning bid announcements, procurement contracts, and responses to inquiries. Data sources include the China Government Procurement Network, local government procurement networks, the fiscal integration system, the procurer’s budget system, and supplier credit records. Data is updated daily. Data fields cover procurement budgets, supplier qualifications, bid evaluation rules, contract terms, and more. It strictly adheres to relevant business rules for government procurement. Document structures follow official templates, and include unified business identifiers and process node fields.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on the "Citation Source and Traceability" Link
Data sources for this category are scattered across multiple systems including fiscal supervision, procurement agencies, procurers, and suppliers. When conducting citation and traceability, it is necessary to connect to multi-source heterogeneous data. Official verification rules for each source must be clarified to ensure content validity. Documents are updated daily, so cited content must be the latest published valid version. Expired announcements or invalid documents must not be used. Document fields strictly follow government procurement business rules. During traceability, the unique identifier of the corresponding document must be associated to ensure cited fragments match the procurement scenario. When sensitive data such as supplier credit is involved, traceability must comply with regulatory requirements, and non-public business information must not be disclosed.

## Configuration Settings
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1200–1800 token | Government procurement document fragments are of moderate length. This range covers core business content and avoids truncating key information |
| `responseData` | Enable source association mode | It is required to associate the official source identifier of the document, which meets the traceability requirements of regulatory verification |
| `detail` | Enable detailed traceability | Display the source document type and release time of the cited fragment, which facilitates business personnel to check timeliness |
| Single search limit | Top 8 entries | Government procurement-related data is updated daily. The top 8 entries cover recent valid documents and avoid introducing expired content |
| Reranking toggle | Enable | Government procurement terminology is highly professional. Reranking improves the recall accuracy of relevant fragments |

## Three Common Errors
- After enabling reranking, citation fragments may appear empty, and no matching content will appear in search results. This occurs because the similarity threshold of the reranking model is set too high, filtering out valid but low-relevance fragments that fit government procurement business scenarios.
- Plugins may fail to be recognized in imported or exported workflows. This occurs because the plugins referenced in the workflow have not completed authorization or configuration in the new environment, and the docking keys for government procurement systems have not been synchronized.
- Responses may display "No permission to operate this conversation record" and citation cannot be canceled. This occurs because the cited document fragment comes from an unauthorized internal system, the system blocks display of non-public data, and the whitelist for public data sources has not been configured.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Submit a test request for procurement requirement preparation, and check whether the identifier and release time of the corresponding source document are displayed at the end of the response.
- After importing and exporting a workflow, verify whether the plugin configuration is associated with the keys and permissions of government procurement-related data sources.
- After adjusting the reranking and search limit parameters, verify whether the number of cited fragments under different keywords matches the expected recall range.
- Check the source field of cited fragments in the system log to confirm whether it matches the preset official data source address.

The values listed above are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader’s own samples.
