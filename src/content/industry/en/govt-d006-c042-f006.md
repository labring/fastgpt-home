# Document and Material Drafting for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
The core data for this category comes from enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, legal and regulatory repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. Daily drafted documents include compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, legal opinions on major matters, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation reports.
The data update cycle is monthly. A single document typically includes fields such as the subject of the matter, compliance basis, review conclusion, and associated identifiers. Different document types have varying formats and fields, but all must be linked to specific business scenarios and compliance clauses.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on citation sources and traceability
The data for this category comes from scattered sources and is linked to business compliance risks. Citation and traceability must accurately match entries from the original systems, to avoid associating incorrect compliance bases or case information.
The monthly update frequency requires that the knowledge base synchronization strategy covers regular incremental data pulls, to prevent expired compliance bases from being used. Diverse document types correspond to different field structures, so it is necessary to ensure that recalled content matches the current drafting document scenario, and general field mapping rules cannot be directly reused.
At the same time, citation errors in compliance documents may lead to major legal risks. The traceability link must retain complete citation trails to facilitate subsequent compliance reviews and traceability.

## How to set the configuration
The following configurations are formulated based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum citation token count | 800–1200 token | Compliance documents require complete citation of regulatory clauses and review opinions, to avoid truncating key compliance bases |
| Return citation details | Enabled | Core information of citation sources must be displayed in generated documents to facilitate compliance reviews |
| Return raw data | Enabled | The traceability link requires association with document entries from original systems, to match content from compliance risk databases |
| Single recall limit | Top 6 entries | Citations in compliance documents should not be excessive, to avoid confusing bases for different compliance matters |
| Knowledge base synchronization cycle | Monthly | Matches the monthly update cycle of this category’s data, to ensure cited compliance bases are up to date |
| Citation matching threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Compliance bases require high matching accuracy, to avoid recalling irrelevant compliance clauses |

## Three common mistakes
- After calling an external API to generate compliance documents, the `detail` and `responseData` fields in the returned results are empty. Cause: The return raw data and return citation details configuration items are not enabled, or the citation data return function is not activated for the release channel.
- After adjusting `quoteMaxToken` to above 2000, the generated compliance documents do not contain any citation content, and the recall log shows that compliance documents were successfully matched. Cause: The token threshold is too high, making it impossible for the model to effectively integrate citation content, or the recalled compliance documents exceed the system processing limit.
- The generated compliance documents cite expired compliance bases. Cause: The knowledge base synchronization cycle is set too long, and compliance risk databases and legal and regulatory repositories are not updated monthly.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Manually trigger a compliance document drafting process, and check whether the generated result includes the title and core content of the citation source.
- Call the external API interface, and check whether the returned data contains the `detail` and `responseData` fields, to confirm that citation data has been returned normally.
- Temporarily adjust the knowledge base synchronization cycle to daily, and verify whether newly added compliance data can be normally recalled and linked to the generated result.
- Adjust `quoteMaxToken` to the boundary values of the range, and check whether key compliance clauses are not truncated in the generated content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
