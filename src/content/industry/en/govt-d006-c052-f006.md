# Document and Material Drafting for Department Final Accounts and Financial Reports: Citation Sources and Traceability

## Data Characteristics for This Category
Core data for this category comes from the budget management integration system, centralized treasury payment system, accounting system, and asset management system. The update cycle is once per year. Daily produced documents include department final account statements, balance sheets, income and expenditure final account statements, final account explanations, and government financial reports. These follow unified fiscal system specifications, and include fixed fields such as revenue and expenditure detail subjects, corresponding amounts, asset and liability entries, and budget execution difference explanations. Compiling a complete annual single document requires associating raw data from multiple systems.

## Constraints on Citation Sources and Traceability
The annual centralized update means the final account compilation phase handles historical data from multiple systems and current summary reports at the same time. This requires strong batch association capabilities for traceability. Fixed document structures and field specifications demand precise matching of specific entries such as subjects and amounts during citation traceability. This prevents caliber errors caused by generalized matching. Associating data sources across multiple systems requires the traceability link to connect raw data entries from different systems simultaneously. This ensures accurate account statement verification. Errors directly lead to final account returns and audit risks. This means traceability results must meet the strict credibility and accuracy requirements of fiscal management.

## Configuration Settings
The following are configuration recommendations for FastGPT v4.17.0:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500–2000 token | Adapts to the length requirements of single-paragraph citations in final account reports, and avoids truncating key subjects and data entries |
| `responseData` | Return only associated fields | Matches the requirement for final account reports to accurately correspond to revenue and expenditure, asset and other modules, and filters irrelevant content |
| `detail` | Enabled | Ensures system sources and specific data entries are returned during traceability, meeting the traceability needs of audit verification |
| Single search limit | Top 6 entries | Covers the citation needs of multiple reports summarized from subordinate units, and avoids missing associated data |
| Maximum context | 8000–10000 characters | Accommodates complete associated data blocks of annual final accounts, and ensures the integrity of multi-document joint retrieval |
| Chunk overlap length | 150 characters | Prevents fixed fields from being truncated by chunking, and ensures complete subject and amount combinations can be matched during traceability |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The search results only return 1 document citation, and the interface prompts "Knowledge Base Citation (1 Entry)". Cause: The single search limit configuration is not adjusted. The default value limits the number of recalls, and cannot cover multiple subordinate unit reports after summarization.
- Phenomenon: After importing the final account report, the citation field is empty, and the corresponding system data cannot be matched. Cause: The chunk length is set too small. Revenue and expenditure subjects and amounts are split into different chunks, making it impossible to associate complete fields during traceability.
- Phenomenon: The model test prompts an error. After forced operation, the traceability result has no system source description. Cause: The `detail` parameter is not enabled, and the associated field mapping for `responseData` is not configured. This makes it impossible to extract data source information.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a single annual final account statement, trigger the citation traceability process, and check whether the returned citation list includes the source identifier of the corresponding system.
- Import multiple final account statements from subordinate units, and verify whether the number of recalled citations matches the single search limit setting.
- Adjust `quoteMaxToken` to a smaller value, and verify whether long paragraph citations are fully retained or truncated as required.
- Check the system logs to confirm that fields from corresponding data sources such as the budget management integration system and centralized treasury payment system are associated during the retrieval process.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
