# Document and Material Drafting for Pharmaceutical Distribution: Vector Models and Indexing

## What the data for this category looks like
Document data in the pharmaceutical distribution sector mainly comes from pharmaceutical purchase and sales contracts, cold chain transportation records, pharmaceutical business compliance reports, inventory ledgers, qualification certification documents, and similar materials. Data update frequencies vary: purchase and sales contracts are generated in real time with transactions, inventory ledgers are updated daily, and compliance reports are generated quarterly or during special inspections. Most documents are a mix of structured and semi-structured data. Fixed fields include generic drug names, production batch numbers, qualification numbers of buyers and sellers, transportation temperature thresholds, compliance clause numbers, and more. Units involve pieces, boxes, batch numbers, degrees Celsius, and similar units.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexing link
The mixed structured nature of pharmaceutical distribution documents requires indexes to support both field-level precise matching and full-text vector retrieval. Frequently updated inventory ledger data requires incremental indexing capabilities to avoid excessive time spent on full reindexing. Standardized units and identifiers for fixed fields must be retained during chunking to prevent loss of critical information during cross-chunk matching. Long documents such as special compliance reports require appropriate chunking strategies to avoid splitting key compliance clauses.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk length | 800–1200 characters | Aligns with the core content length of single sections in pharmaceutical purchase and sales contracts and compliance reports, preventing splitting of critical information such as drug batch numbers and compliance clauses |
| Overlap length | 100–150 characters | Retains critical identifiers such as drug batch numbers and transportation temperatures across chunks, preventing content breaks during vector matching |
| Similarity threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Filters low-relevance generic text, ensuring retrieval results focus on content related to pharmaceutical compliance and transactions |
| Single retrieval limit | Top 10 entries | Controls the number of returned contexts, avoiding context overload during long document drafting |
| `maxToken` | 8000 | Meets the context splicing requirements for long pharmaceutical documents, and complies with FastGPT general parameter specifications |
| `defaultToken` | 500 | Used for basic vector generation of single-chunk text, balancing vector accuracy and computational overhead |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Index reindexing takes more than 24 hours, and the interface shows no updates to the reindexing status. Cause: Incremental indexing is not configured for frequently updated inventory ledger data in pharmaceutical distribution, and full indexing is used to traverse all historical documents.
- Phenomenon: Parameter errors are returned when adding indexes in bulk, and some required fields are empty. Cause: Required parameters such as drug batch numbers and qualification numbers of buyers and sellers are not passed in accordance with the fixed field format of pharmaceutical documents.
- Phenomenon: The number of vector retrieval results does not match expectations, either too many or too few. Cause: The configuration logic of independent single-document indexing and global unified indexing is confused, and the aggregation rules for indexes within a collection are not clarified.

## How to confirm that the configuration is correct
- Upload a single typical pharmaceutical purchase and sales contract, check the chunk preview interface, and confirm that chunks do not split critical fields such as drug batch numbers and transportation temperatures.
- Initiate a document drafting request, check the retrieved context list, and confirm that the matching results include relevant compliance clauses and inventory records.
- Add a new cold chain transportation record, wait 10 minutes, and check the index status to confirm that incremental updates have been completed.
- Check the FastGPT v4.17.0 system logs to confirm that the `maxToken` and `defaultToken` configurations have taken effect.
