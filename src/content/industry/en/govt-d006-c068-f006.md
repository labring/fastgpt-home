# Document and Material Drafting for Pharmaceutical Distribution: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
The data sources for pharmaceutical distribution mainly include business qualification documents filed with the National Medical Products Administration, pharmaceutical company purchase and sales ledgers, cold chain logistics temperature and humidity records, scanned copies of pharmaceutical business licenses, and similar materials. The data update rhythm is daily synchronization of same-day purchase and sales data, and monthly updates of business qualifications and filing information. The document structure is divided into two categories: structured purchase and sales ledgers, which include fields such as enterprise name, generic drug name, batch number, purchase and sales date, quantity, and similar fields; unstructured qualification documents, mostly in PDF or scanned format. Fields and units include filing number, business category, piece, box, degree Celsius, cubic meter, and similar units.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "citation source and traceability" link
The structured fields of pharmaceutical distribution data are clear and include the unique identifier batch number, which requires that the traceability link must bind this field to avoid matching errors. The coexistence of multiple types of documents requires that the traceability link supports both structured field matching and unstructured text association. The high-frequency updated purchase and sales data requires that indexing tasks match the update rhythm to avoid referencing expired data. The unstructured nature of qualification documents requires that the traceability link supports associating and binding OCR results with structured data to ensure that compliance audits can be implemented.

## Three common mistakes
- A red error prompt appears in the interface indicating invalid citation. The cause is that the drug batch number is not set as the unique matching key, resulting in recalled data failing to be accurately bound to the drafted content.
- Subsequent workflow nodes cannot call knowledge base citation variables. The cause is that the pharmaceutical distribution-specific datasetid is not correctly bound in the global variables, or the variable scope does not cover the knowledge base retrieval node.
- Only the first query triggers knowledge base citations, and subsequent queries have no citation results. The cause is that the context reset logic is not configured in the workflow, causing residual information from previous nodes to interfere with subsequent retrieval matching.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- When drafting a test document, check the citation module below the generated content to confirm that it includes pharmaceutical distribution-specific fields such as drug batch numbers and purchase and sales order numbers.
- Enter the knowledge base management page and check that the update frequency setting of the indexing task matches the daily synchronization and monthly update rhythm of pharmaceutical distribution data.
- In the workflow debugging interface, manually enter different drug batch numbers to verify that each retrieval can recall corresponding purchase and sales ledgers and qualification documents.
- Check the log module of FastGPT v4.17.0 to confirm that the return status code of cited data is 200, with no timeout or missing data prompts.
