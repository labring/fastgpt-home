# Document and Material Drafting for Hazardous Chemical Storage and Transportation: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Business data for hazardous chemical storage and transportation originates from three primary sources: internal enterprise storage and transportation ledgers, real-time monitoring systems for tank farms and pipelines, and compliance documents released by emergency management departments. Update frequencies vary significantly: storage and transportation ledgers are updated daily, real-time monitoring data is refreshed per minute, and compliance documents are updated quarterly or when policies are adjusted, per regulatory requirements.

Document structures include three categories: storage and transportation work orders (containing hazardous chemical name, CAS number, storage volume, transportation route), safety data sheets (MSDS, containing flash point, storage temperature threshold), and work permit forms. Core fields must match fixed formats required by regulations. Units include tons, cubic meters, degrees Celsius, kilometers, and others.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Professional fields and update frequencies for hazardous chemical storage and transportation impose multiple constraints on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering configurations.
First, professional fields such as CAS number and flash point must strictly align with compliance standards. Multi-turn dialogue must prompt for missing fields incrementally to prevent the generation of non-compliant documents.
Second, the minute-level update requirement for real-time monitoring data means dialogue context must limit data to recent periods to avoid calling outdated information.
Third, the fixed format requirement for compliance documents means prompts must clearly list required fields and output structure.
Fourth, dispersed data sources must use bound variables to ensure the latest business and compliance data is called during dialogue.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Generated hazardous chemical storage and transportation documents do not strictly follow the preset JSON format, with incorrect field order or missing fields. Cause: Format verification rules are not embedded in the system prompt, and the requirement to match the preset field structure for output is not clearly stated.
- Phenomenon: Multi-turn dialogue tasks run slower in workflows than in standalone debug mode. Cause: The context range of `messages` is not limited, and too much historical conversation data is loaded each time it is called, increasing the model's context processing time.
- Phenomenon: The automatically generated reply signature time is empty when a previous conversation session is reopened. Cause: The `chatId` parameter is not bound, and the conversation context is not persistently stored, making it impossible to read historically configured time variables.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- A test conversation may be initiated, with a vague hazardous chemical name entered. Verify that the system prompts for complete professional fields such as CAS number and storage volume.
- Review the historical turns of the conversation context, confirm that only limited range of conversation content is loaded, with no redundant old data.
- The session may be saved, reopened, and verified to confirm the signature time is correctly displayed as the current date, and historical conversation content is not lost.
- A multi-turn dialogue task may be triggered, and verify that the generated document includes the latest storage and transportation data from the bound `variables`.
