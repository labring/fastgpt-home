# Document and Material Drafting for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
The data used for document and material drafting in power generation equipment manufacturing mainly comes from project-level design drawings, component process documents, factory quality inspection reports, and industry compliance standard documents. Data is updated per project batch. The size of a single project’s document collection ranges from dozens to hundreds of copies. The document structure consistently includes fields such as project number, component model, technical parameters, inspection date, and compliance clauses. The units for technical parameter fields are mostly megawatts (MW) and operating hours. Material grades must match national standard grade codes.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on citation sources and traceability
Documents for power generation equipment manufacturing often involve compliance requirements. Quoted standards or parameters must match the currently valid version. Otherwise, the compliance validity of the document will be compromised. Individual documents are lengthy, and contain a large number of professional terms and model codes. Improper interception of quoted fragments will make traceability information unable to correspond to specific components. There are slight differences in document field formats across different projects. Without unified mapping, citation matching errors will occur. Additionally, data update frequency fluctuates with project progress. A knowledge base that is not updated in time will lead to expired citations.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Unrelated random citation IDs appear in generated documents. Cause: The dedicated knowledge base source for power generation equipment is not bound, causing the large language model to call irrelevant general data to generate forged citations.
- Phenomenon: The output content contains the phrase "Citation Marker: [1]". Cause: The marker prefix configuration returned by the knowledge base is not disabled, and the original retrieval identifier is directly spliced without format cleaning.
- Phenomenon: Generated compliance documents reference expired industry standards. Cause: No knowledge base version filtering rules are configured, causing recall of outdated documents with lower update frequency.
