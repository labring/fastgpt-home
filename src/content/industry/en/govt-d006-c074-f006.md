# Document and Material Drafting for State-owned Asset Management Companies: Source Citation and Traceability

## What Data in This Category Looks Like
Data for document and material drafting for state-owned asset management companies comes from business documents including state-owned asset ownership registration ledgers, property rights transfer approval documents, asset valuation reports, and regulatory filing receipts.
Data updates trigger immediately when individual asset transactions or ownership changes occur. Routine existing data synchronizes quarterly.
Each document typically includes fields such as asset number, ownership entity, transaction amount, and approval document number. Field types cover text, date, and numerical values. Units use specialized business identifiers such as ten thousand yuan, square meter, and piece.

## Constraints on Source Citation and Traceability Processes
Data sources cover the full business process and include precise identifiers such as asset numbers and approval document numbers. Traceability links must bind specific business fields to complete matching. They cannot rely solely on general text similarity retrieval.
The real-time nature of data updates requires traceability configurations to support real-time synchronization of incremental knowledge base data. This prevents old documents from being incorrectly associated with new business scenarios.
The multi-field structure of individual documents increases single-document length. Core business identifiers must be retained during chunk splitting. Otherwise, traceability results cannot accurately point to the corresponding asset transaction link.
Regulatory compliance requirements for official documents raise the accuracy threshold for traceability. Cited content must fully correspond to fields in the original document.

| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Ensures core business fields across chunks are not truncated, maintaining the integrity of traceability matching |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 300–500 tokens | Limits the length of cited content, matching the compliance length requirements for single citations in regulatory documents |

| `responseData` | Include document title, asset number, approval document number | Ensures traceability results return business-specific identifiers, enabling quick location of original documents |
| `detail` | Enabled | Displays context of original cited passages, meeting traceability verification needs during compliance reviews |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant document fragments are returned even when search results have no connection to the user’s question. Cause: Precise matching rules are not configured for the exclusive fields of business documents. Only general text similarity is used, resulting in mismatches caused by a large number of repeated fields.
- Phenomenon: Asset numbers or approval document numbers are not displayed in traceability results. Cause: Core business fields are not retained during chunk splitting, or the configured `responseData` does not return the specified fields, leading to missing traceability information.
- Phenomenon: Cited content exceeds compliance length and is rejected by regulators. Cause: No reasonable upper limit is set for `quoteMaxToken`, resulting in cited passages that are too long to meet compliance requirements for business documents.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a test document that includes an asset number and approval document number. Submit a query related to that asset. Confirm whether the returned traceability results include the exclusive fields of this document.
- Adjust configurations related to citation length. Submit a long-text citation test. Confirm that the length of returned cited passages meets business compliance requirements.
- Upload multiple documents of the same business type. Submit cross-document retrieval queries. Confirm that matching rules filter out low-correlation results.
- Check knowledge base synchronization settings. Confirm that the synchronization cycle for new business documents adapts to the real-time requirements of business updates.
