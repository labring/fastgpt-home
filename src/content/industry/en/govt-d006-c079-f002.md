# Document and Material Drafting for Urban Gas Utilities: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for urban gas document and material drafting primarily originates from on-site daily inspection logs, pipeline operation and maintenance archives, user installation and safety inspection documents, safety hazard rectification notices, and emergency response records.
Update rhythm follows these rules: Daily inspections are synced daily, monthly operation archives are filed monthly, and major rectification documents are submitted immediately.
Documents use a mixed structure of structured fields and free text. Structured fields include station number, inspection time, hazard level, rectification deadline, and similar items. Free text includes on-site descriptions, treatment suggestions, and similar content. Common units include meters, cubic meters, hours, levels, and similar units.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Vector Models and Indexes Workflows
Mixed-structure documents require chunking logic to separate structured metadata and free text content, to avoid excessive truncation of metadata that causes semantic loss.
Daily incremental update data requires lightweight incremental indexing without full reconstruction, to reduce system resource usage.
Document lengths vary significantly: short rectification notices (hundreds of characters) to long emergency plans (thousands of characters). This requires adaptive variable-length chunking configurations.
Industry-specific fields and units must retain identifiers during vector modeling to prevent semantic confusion.
In high-traffic retrieval scenarios, control token consumption per batch to avoid resource overload.

## How to Set Configurations
The following configurations are based on the interface logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retain contextual association between adjacent chunks, avoid semantic breaks in pipeline records and rectification notices |

## Three Common Mistakes
1.  Phenomenon: After switching the knowledge base vector model, the indexing progress panel shows no updates, and the interface cannot roll back to the original model. Cause: The incremental indexing task of the old model was not closed, causing conflicting indexing processes between the new and old models and occupying system resources.
2.  Phenomenon: When using the Doubao embedding model, a large number of results unrelated to gas stations appear in retrieval results, with insufficient relevance. Cause: The vector model normalization configuration was not enabled, and the unnormalized output characteristics of Doubao embedding were not adapted, leading to deviations in vector similarity calculation.
3.  Phenomenon: Single retrieval logs show token consumption exceeding 40,000, far exceeding the expected setting. Cause: The `maxToken` limit was not set, or the chunk length was configured too large, causing too many redundant document chunk contents to be included in a single batch retrieval.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- View the real-time progress panel of indexing tasks, confirm that the update frequency of incremental indexing matches the submission rhythm of daily inspection and operation and maintenance documents.
- Submit a simulated retrieval request, check whether the chunk length and token consumption of returned results conform to the `maxToken` limit set in the configuration.
- After switching the vector model, verify that the indexing process starts normally and the indexing task of the old model has stopped.
- Compare retrieval results under different thresholds, confirm that the similarity filtering results meet the semantic differentiation requirements of gas industry documents.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
