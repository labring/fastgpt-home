# Document and Material Drafting for Urban Gas: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Documents and materials for the urban gas sector are primarily sourced from business processes including daily operations, installation approval, safety inspections, and contract management. Data updates are triggered by business events, with no fixed cycle. Document formats include multi-page PDFs, structured spreadsheet documents, and paragraph-style text files. Fields cover gas usage, inspection points, installation information, contract terms, and more. Units include cubic meters, hours, megapascals, and others. Some documents feature mixed layouts of handwritten annotations and structured forms.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Document Parsing and Chunking Step
Parsing processes for urban gas documents must first distinguish between table and paragraph content, to avoid merging table cells with main body text into chunks. Chunking tools must preserve the integrity of individual business information for business documents with no fixed update cycle, preventing splitting of key business node data such as installation applications and inspection records. Chunking must retain the binding relationship between numerical values and their units for fields with specific units, to avoid separating values from their corresponding units. For long cross-page documents, parsing processes must preserve contextual association across cross-page paragraphs, to avoid splitting the same inspection record into different chunks.

## Three Common Mistakes
- After configuring custom delimiters based on line breaks, chunks may still merge multiple paragraphs or split single paragraphs. The cause is failing to prioritize matching custom delimiters for `chunkSplitter`, or setting `chunkSize` beyond the paragraph length threshold.
- An error with specified information is returned when processing urban gas PDF documents after deployment. The cause is failing to enable the `customPdfParse` parameter, or setting `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` too short.
- The binding relationship between numerical values and units may be lost after chunking. The cause is setting `chunkSize` too small, splitting gas usage values with units and their corresponding units into different chunks.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a single urban gas inspection PDF document, check whether the parsed text retains the layout structure of tables and paragraphs.
- Upload a single installation application document, adjust `chunkSize` to 800 characters, verify that the chunked text does not split the complete business information of a single inspection record.
- Upload a multi-page urban gas document, check whether the parsed chunks retain the contextual association of cross-page paragraphs.
- Test uploading a document that meets the `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` limit, confirm that no timeout error occurs during parsing.
