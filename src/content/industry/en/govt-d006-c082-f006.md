# Document and Material Drafting for Basic Telecommunications Operations: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Document drafting for basic telecommunications operations relies on multiple types of business data. Sources include real-time operation logs, monthly billing statements, customer service tickets, network topology documents, and spectrum usage reports.
Operation logs update hourly, and include fields such as timestamp, device ID, alert level, and processing result. Billing statements update per calendar month, and include fields such as billing cycle, account ID, call duration, and cost amount.
Tickets and topology documents update as needed. Tickets include ticket number, user request, and processing progress. Topology documents include node ID, bandwidth parameters, and coverage area.
All business data have unique identifier fields. Text length varies widely: short single alert entries (hundreds of characters) to long monthly network-wide analysis reports (tens of thousands of characters).

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Citation Source and Traceability
The hourly update cadence of operation logs requires the knowledge base to support incremental synchronization. Otherwise, cited content may lag behind the latest business status.
The multi-field structure of billing statements requires that unit information such as amount and duration be retained during citation. Otherwise, traceability results cannot verify business rationality.
The on-demand update feature of tickets and topology documents requires the system to accurately match the latest version identifiers of documents. This avoids citing expired content.
The wide range of document lengths requires chunking strategies to balance the completeness of short texts and the readability of long texts. Otherwise, key traceability identifiers such as device ID or account ID may be truncated.
Additionally, the uniqueness of business data requires precise field matching during recall. This ensures that recall results correspond to specific business resources, avoiding vague matching that causes traceability confusion.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Aligns with the per-chunk information density of documents such as operation logs and tickets, avoids splitting complete alert entries or ticket workflows |
| Chunk Overlap Length | 150–200 characters | Retains key traceability identifiers such as device ID and account ID across chunks, prevents loss of unique matching fields after chunking |
| Single Recall Limit | Top 6 entries | Matches the multi-source association characteristics of telecommunications operation businesses, avoids recalling too many irrelevant billing or topology documents |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1200–1500 tokens | Adapts to the citation display needs of long texts, fully presents complete processing flows of operation logs or detailed content of billing statements |
| `detail` | Enabled | Retains complete metadata fields of citation sources, facilitates verification of key traceability information such as device ID and ticket number |
| `responseData` | Enabled | Forces return of associated identifiers of citation sources in responses, meets both interface call and manual traceability requirements |

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: In non-tool call mode, generated documents do not cite operation log content from the local knowledge base, and only use general training data. Cause: The local operation knowledge base is not set as the priority recall source, or the mandatory association switch for knowledge base recall is not enabled.
- Phenomenon: In generated billing analysis documents, citation entries only show document titles and do not include key information such as billing cycle and account ID. Cause: The `detail` parameter is not enabled, only document text is extracted without retaining metadata fields, making it impossible to match specific resources during traceability.
- Phenomenon: After calling the chat interface, the returned results do not include unique identifiers of citation sources, making it impossible to correspond to specific knowledge base document entries. Cause: The `responseData` parameter is not configured to return metadata fields, the interface only returns response text without carrying citation association information.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Generate a rectification proposal based on operation logs, check if the citation list at the end of the response includes metadata fields of the corresponding documents.
- Call the FastGPT v4.17.0 chat interface, check if the returned results include associated identifier fields of citation sources.
- Switch to non-tool call mode, generate an analysis document based on billing statements, confirm that citation entries fully display information such as billing cycle and account ID.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` parameter value, verify if the length of the generated citation text adapts to the typesetting requirements of the document.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
