# Document and Material Drafting for Commodity Trading: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

This document was written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like

The document data for commodity trading primarily comes from spot market quotation systems, port customs clearance documents, trade contract ledgers, and logistics waybills. Data synchronizes with each transaction batch or on a daily basis. A single document usually includes fields such as contract number, product category, transaction quantity, benchmark unit price, delivery location, settlement terms, and more. Units are mostly physical measurement and pricing units such as tons, yuan per ton, cubic meters, and others. Some cross-border trade documents also include customs codes and exchange rate conversion items.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Bring to Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

The high-frequency, multi-batch updates of commodity trading data require multi-turn dialogue to associate the latest transaction ledgers and quotation data in real time, avoiding the use of expired information. The numerous fields in documents, along with professional measurement and compliance clauses, require prompts to clearly define field scope and unit rules, preventing the generation of content that does not comply with trade specifications. The large volume of transaction data linked to a single document requires reasonable restriction of the multi-turn dialogue context window, avoiding content truncation or errors caused by exceeding the model's processing limits. For documents in cross-border trade scenarios, adaptation to compliance formats of different regions is needed, and prompts should gradually guide the supplementation of necessary clauses for the corresponding regions.

## Three Common Mistakes

- An empty return of associated transaction data when calling the dialogue interface. Cause: No dedicated knowledge base filter condition configured for the current trade category, causing the model to fail to retrieve the corresponding commodity trading ledger and quotation data.
- Context truncation errors during multi-turn dialogue. Cause: No restriction set for historical dialogue turns or character length, resulting in input interaction history exceeding the processing limit of FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Incorrect measurement units in generated trade documents. Cause: No binding of standard measurement rules for commodities in the prompt, causing the model to generate content that does not comply with industry general specifications.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration

- Initiate a test dialogue, input "Draft a steel procurement contract", and confirm the returned context only includes the last 8–12 rounds of interaction history.
- Review the variable binding configuration, confirm it includes specific parameters such as measurement units and contract clause templates for the current trade category.
- Pass a preset dialogue identifier, confirm dialogue contexts of different transaction batches are correctly distinguished, with no information confusion across batches.
- Wait 24 hours, confirm expired dialogue contexts have been automatically cleaned up, with no redundant historical data remaining.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
