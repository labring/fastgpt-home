# Document and Material Drafting for General Contracting of Building Construction Projects: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for document and material drafting for general contracting of building construction projects mainly comes from project bidding documents, construction logs, change visas, acceptance reports, material procurement lists, and similar sources. Data is updated by phase according to project progress. Core fields include project number, section scope, construction area, material specification, duration node, acceptance standards, and similar items. Most documents are in PDF, CAD drawing, or Excel spreadsheet formats, and units involve engineering-specific units such as cubic meters, square meters, tons, and working days.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the deployment and upgrade link
The document data for general contracting of building construction projects has large volume, numerous fields, and diverse formats. This requires adaptation to indexing and storage of large batches of data during deployment. Multi-source data is scattered across systems of different project departments, so multi-source access rules must be configured to achieve unified pulling. Documents contain a large number of engineering-specific fields and units, so additional field mapping rules must be configured to ensure the accuracy of retrieval and generation. Data update frequency fluctuates with project progress, so a flexible timed synchronization mechanism must be set up to adapt to update rhythms at different stages.

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: The model’s returned results forcefully display thought processes, and they cannot be hidden via configuration. Cause: The thought process switch for model access was not configured correctly, resulting in the default return of full thought content during calls.
- Symptom: Uploaded CAD drawings and Excel material lists for building construction projects cannot be indexed normally. Cause: The parsing adaptation for engineering documents was not enabled, and the default parsing rules cannot recognize drawing layers and table structured fields.
- Symptom: Database connection timeout errors occur during cross-machine deployment. Cause: The value range of `DB_MAX_LINK` was not configured correctly, resulting in insufficient connections or exceeding the database’s bearing limit.
