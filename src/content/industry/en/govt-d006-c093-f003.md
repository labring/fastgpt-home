# Document and Material Drafting for Government and Enterprise Informatization Services: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for document and material drafting for government and enterprise informatization services comes primarily from internal compliance manuals, project declaration templates, operation and maintenance work order archives, and contract approval filing documents of government and enterprise clients in the communications industry.
Compliance documents receive monthly updates. Daily work orders and project materials are entered in real time alongside business processes.
Each individual document includes fields such as document number, issuing unit, effective date, main body paragraphs, and attachment list. Corresponding data types for these fields are string, text, date, character, and list format.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering Workflows
The monthly update rhythm of compliance documents requires that multi-turn dialogue prompts be synced with the latest compliance terms on a regular basis. Context caching must prioritize retaining the most recent version of regulatory content to avoid referencing outdated standards.
Real-time entry of business materials requires that conversation contexts quickly associate with the latest work order and project data, to prevent generated content from conflicting with current business status.
Documents include structured fields such as document number and effective date. Multi-turn dialogue must accurately distinguish the business meaning of different fields. Prompt engineering must clearly specify extraction and verification logic for structured fields, to avoid mixing up field values.
Long main body paragraphs are measured in characters. The total length of stitched multi-turn context must be controlled to avoid exceeding model processing limits.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind to a dedicated ID for the business scenario | Ensures multi-turn dialogue context is tied to specific business processes for government and enterprise informatization, preventing cross-scenario context confusion |
| `messages` | Retain the most recent 20 turns of dialogue | Meets the long-context requirements of government and enterprise documents, while controlling the load of single-round requests to avoid timeouts |
| `variables` | Inject fields such as current business document number and effective date | Matches the structured field characteristics of documents, ensuring generated content is linked to accurate business parameters |
| Maximum context length | 8000–12000 characters | Matches the long-paragraph structure of government and enterprise documents, preventing truncation of critical compliance terms and business data |
| Single-round recall count | Top 8 entries | Meets the multi-field association requirements of government and enterprise documents, avoiding excessive irrelevant content that disrupts document generation |
| Question optimization toggle | Enabled | Optimizes user input question wording for professional terminology in government and enterprise documents, improving model understanding accuracy |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: A Request Timeout error occurs during dialogue, with the interface returning a 504 status code. Cause: Failure to bind a dedicated `chatId` leads to overly large multi-turn context cache, exceeding the load limit of interface requests.
- Symptom: The generated document has reversed document number and effective date field values. Cause: Failure to inject clear structured field parameters via `variables`, and failure to specify the corresponding business scenario for fields in prompt engineering.
- Symptom: Generated compliance terms reference outdated documents from before the monthly update. Cause: Failure to regularly sync the latest compliance documents, and failure to limit the retention turns of `messages`, leading to old version rules being stored in the context cache.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Initiate a test dialogue that includes document number and effective date, check whether the generated content accurately links to the injected business field parameters.
- View the conversation context record, confirm that only the specified number of turns of dialogue content are retained, with no redundant information from cross-business scenarios.
- Trigger a compliance term generation request, check whether the returned content matches the current latest version of compliance documents.
- Initiate multiple consecutive dialogue requests, confirm that no timeout errors occur and the interface runs stably.
