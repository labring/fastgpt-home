# Document and Material Drafting for Government and Enterprise IT Services: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data used for document and material drafting in government and enterprise IT services comes primarily from internal operation and maintenance manuals, compliance documents, bidding document templates, and industry standard documents of government and enterprise customers in the communications industry.
Data updates occur monthly or quarterly, matching the regular adjustment cycle for industry policies and standards.
Documents use a structured format, with fields including document number, release date, scope of application, and clause-based body text.
Clause content is organized by articles, paragraphs, and sub-paragraphs. Individual document lengths range from thousands to tens of thousands of characters.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Citation Source and Traceability
Structured document formats require traceability to pinpoint specific clause numbers. Displaying only the file name does not meet compliance review requirements for government and enterprise documents.
Stable update cycles require regular synchronization of data sources. This prevents citing expired policies or industry standard content.
The scope of application field attached to each document requires verification before citation. Confirm that the scenario of the currently drafted document matches the document’s scope of application, to avoid compliance risks from cross-scenario citations.
Long single-clause content requires reserving sufficient token space. This ensures complete cited content is retained, and key compliance information is not lost due to truncation.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant fields such as "knowledge base search", "input", or "response" appear in generated documents. Cause: The traceability display switch for tool calls in the workflow is not disabled. Original tool call input and output content is retained by default.
- Phenomenon: Cited traceability results do not include specific clause numbers, only file names are shown. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled, and the field extraction function for structured documents is not activated.
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base variables cannot be selected as citation sources in the workflow. Cause: The government and enterprise document data source is not added to the list of referable global variables, or the association between variables and the data source is not configured.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Manually trigger a document drafting task, and check whether irrelevant tool call log fields are present in the generated content.
- View the traceability module of the generated document, and confirm that each citation includes structured fields such as clause number and release date.
- Navigate to the global variable configuration page, and confirm that the government and enterprise document data source is included in the list of referable variables.
- Temporarily adjust the value of the `quoteMaxToken` parameter, and verify that the length of the generated cited content matches the expected setting.
