# Nuclear Power Operations: Document and Material Drafting – Vector Models and Indexing

## What data for this category looks like
Data related to nuclear power operations document and material drafting originates from four primary sources: equipment maintenance procedures, daily inspection records, safety review reports, and operation logs. Data update rhythms vary significantly. Daily inspection records are generated and updated daily. Equipment maintenance procedures are revised and archived quarterly. Safety review reports are submitted and archived monthly. Document structures include fields such as chapter numbers, unique equipment identifiers, operation steps, and exception levels. Some specialized documents include quantitative parameters like pressure and temperature, using standard industrial units including MPa, hours, and counts.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on vector models and indexing?
The above data characteristics impose multiple constraints on vector model and indexing configuration. First, varied update rhythms require an indexing update strategy that combines incremental embedding and full embedding. This strategy adapts to the high-frequency updates of daily inspection records and the low-frequency revisions of quarterly procedures. Second, coexisting structured fields and specialized terminology require a chunking strategy that balances semantic completeness and field relevance. This avoids splitting cross-device associated content into separate chunks. Finally, the length difference between long-text safety reports and short-text inspection records requires chunking parameters with flexible adjustment space. This adapts to embedding needs for different document types.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: After switching the knowledge base vector model, the system displays stalled indexing progress, and the original model configuration cannot be restored. Cause: No batch re-embedding operation was performed, the new model has not completed full data vectorization, and the original configuration was not automatically reset by the system.
- Phenomenon: After configuring the Doubao embedding interface, a 404 page not found error is returned during testing. Cause: The interface access domain name or key permissions were not configured correctly, causing the request to not be recognized by the target service.
- Phenomenon: After batch re-embedding multilingual documents, the recall effect of specialized terms does not meet expectations. Cause: The vector model selection or chunking strategy was not adjusted for multilingual scenarios, leading to deviations in semantic alignment.
