# Document and Material Drafting for Civil Aviation Airports: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Civil aviation airport document data sources include internal operation manuals, air traffic control coordination documents, flight dispatch logs, passenger service specifications, and safety audit records.
Update cycles vary by document type: safety compliance documents are reviewed and updated quarterly, daily scheduling logs are synced daily, and passenger service guidelines are adjusted monthly.
Documents use a chapter-based structure, with standardized fields including document number, effective date, applicable scenario, operating rules, and responsible entity.
Standard operation-related units include minutes, kilograms, passenger trips, and others.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Citation Source and Traceability
Scattered multiple data sources require the traceability link to associate cross-system file metadata to ensure citation integrity.
Fixed chapter-based structures and standardized fields require precise matching of identifiers such as document number and effective date during citation to avoid traceability confusion.
Different update cycles require incremental synchronization mechanisms to adapt to daily, monthly, and quarterly update periods, preventing citation of expired content.
Operation-related unit fields require unit information to be recorded during traceability to ensure scenario matching of cited content.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: Unparsed `\n` characters appear in the response content, and the expected line break effect is not achieved. Cause: The line break format is not configured in the Prompt, or the input `\n` is not properly escaped.
- Phenomenon: An error prompt "No permission to operate this conversation record" pops up during citation traceability. Cause: Access permissions for traceability data are not configured, or permission configurations are not synchronized when importing across environments.
- Phenomenon: After exporting a workflow and importing it to another environment, plugins referenced in the workflow cannot be called normally. Cause: Plugin dependency configurations are not included when exporting the workflow, and plugin instances are not rebound when importing across environments.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Draft content for an airport safety inspection procedure, and check whether the document number and effective date of the corresponding file are attached at the end of the returned content.
- Test citation of a long-text compliance document, and confirm that the content length of a single citation meets the preset limit of `quoteMaxToken`.
- Export and re-import the workflow, and check whether the referenced plugin configuration is complete and can be called normally.
- Input test text containing `\n`, and confirm that the line break effect in the response content works correctly.
