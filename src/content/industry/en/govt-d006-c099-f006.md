# Document and Material Drafting for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development: Citation Source and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Core data for oil and gas exploration and development comes from on-site exploration reports, drilling operation logs, production test monitoring records, reserve assessment documents, and similar sources. The data update rhythm is adjusted according to the project phase: real-time updates at operation nodes during the exploration phase, and batch synchronization on a monthly or quarterly basis during the development phase. Most documents are a mix of structured and semi-structured content, including professional fields such as well number, porosity, permeability, and others. Common units include percentage, millidarcy (mD), cubic meter, and more. The length of a single document ranges from thousands to tens of thousands of characters, often accompanied by supporting chart attachments.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Citation Source and Traceability?
The characteristics of long documents and multiple fields require precise positioning of the source paragraphs of professional content during traceability, to avoid generalized matching that leads to citation misalignment. Multi-batch updated data requires the traceability function to distinguish document versions from different time nodes, to avoid citing outdated exploration or production test data. In scenarios where a single document is large in length, it is necessary to limit the total number of citation tokens recalled in a single operation, to prevent exceeding the model context window. At the same time, the strict matching of professional terms and units requires that the traceability configuration be aligned with the field definitions of the data, to ensure that the cited content is consistent with the original text expression.

## Configuration Settings
This document is adapted for FastGPT v4.17.0. The following configurations are based on the interface and parameter logic of this version:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Single paragraphs of oil and gas exploration and development documents are relatively long, so sufficient tokens need to be reserved to accommodate cited paragraphs while avoiding exceeding model context limits |
| `responseData` | Enable original link and paragraph positioning | It is necessary to clearly display the source document and specific location of the cited content, to meet the compliance traceability requirements of oil and gas professional scenarios |
| `detail` | Enable professional field matching | Oil and gas data includes professional fields such as well number and permeability, so precise matching of field content is required to improve citation accuracy |
| Multi-turn context recall limit | First 3 turns of conversation context | Questions in oil and gas exploration scenarios are often associated with previous operation parameters, retaining 3 turns of context can ensure coherent follow-up question logic |
| Guest share citation permission | Calibrated based on actual testing | Needs to be configured according to project access permission requirements, balancing public access and data security |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: When accessing via a guest share link, the citation original text function appears gray and unclickable. Cause: The citation permission switch in the guest scenario was not enabled in the application configuration, causing the share link to fail to load source data.
- Phenomenon: After continuous follow-up questions, the cited content is irrelevant to the previous question, and the traceability paragraph does not match. Cause: Multi-turn context association parameters were not configured, the model only recalled the knowledge base based on the current question, and did not associate exploration operation parameters from historical conversations.
- Phenomenon: No citation marker is displayed at the end of the answer, or the cited content is empty. Cause: The value of `quoteMaxToken` is too small, causing the cited paragraph to be truncated or unable to be extracted completely, or the professional field matching switch of the `detail` configuration item was not enabled.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Submit a question related to a single drilling log, check whether the corresponding citation source link and paragraph positioning are displayed at the end of the answer.
- Submit continuous follow-up questions, such as first querying the porosity of a certain well, then asking for the production test data of that well, check whether the answer associates the well number parameter from the previous question, and whether the cited content matches the corresponding document.
- Generate a guest share link, access it and check whether the citation original text function can be clicked normally to jump to the target.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, test whether long document citations are displayed completely, without truncation or loss.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
