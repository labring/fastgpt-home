# Document and Material Drafting for Coal Transportation and Sales: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
Coal transportation and sales business data mainly comes from five categories: waybills, purchase and sales contracts, settlement vouchers, monthly transportation plans, and safety and compliance records. Data sources include offline scanned paper documents, files exported from online transportation systems, and structured field data synced from ERP systems. Update cycles vary significantly: waybills are generated in real time with each batch of transportation, monthly plans are updated monthly, and contracts are updated when signed. Document structure primarily uses structured fields, including goods name, tonnage, departure station, arrival station, rail car number, settlement unit price, and other fields. Units include tons, yuan per ton, trips, and similar units, alongside unstructured contract clauses and transportation instruction text.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the knowledge base retrieval and recall link
The multi-type data and differentiated update cycles of coal transportation and sales create multiple constraints on the retrieval and recall process. Mixed-format documents (scanned PDF files, Excel structured tables, plain-text contracts) require the retrieval system to support both OCR recognition and structured field matching. Without this support, the retrieval system will miss valid content from scanned documents. Waybill data updated in real time at high frequency increases the frequency of incremental index synchronization. Improper configuration can lead to delayed recalled data. Features with multiple fields and units can also cause unit mismatch errors in single-semantic retrieval, requiring targeted field matching logic.

## Three common mistakes
- Scenario: Empty results are returned when scanning a PDF of a coal transportation waybill. Cause: OCR recognition configuration is not enabled, only the text layer of the PDF is recognized, and document content in images is not extracted.
- Scenario: Structured fields are truncated or semantically broken after knowledge base chunking. Cause: Confusing character and token units for chunk length, failing to adjust chunking rules based on the average length of coal transportation and sales documents.
- Scenario: Retrieval returns a large number of duplicated waybill settlement data. Cause: Index deduplication configuration is not enabled, and incremental index optimization is not performed for high-frequency repeated business documents, leading to multiple recalls of the same data.

## How to confirm the configuration is correctly set
- Upload a scanned PDF of a coal transportation waybill, initiate a query containing "rail car number", and check whether the retrieval results include the document content extracted via OCR.
- Initiate a query containing "settlement unit price", review the order of returned results, confirm that documents with high relevance are ranked first, and verify that the `usingReRank` configuration is active.
- Check the number of returned retrieval results, confirm that it matches the value set in the `limit` configuration, and verify that the retrieval quantity limit is active.
- Check the field matching status of retrieval results, confirm that structured fields such as "departure station" and "arrival station" are correctly identified, and verify that the hybrid configuration of `searchMode` is active.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
