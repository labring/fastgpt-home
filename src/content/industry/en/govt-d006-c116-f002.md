# Document and Material Drafting for Telecommunications Engineering Construction: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Telecommunications engineering construction documents and materials primarily originate from project initiation approval documents, construction organization design plans, route survey records, supervision weekly reports, completion acceptance reports, and other sources.
Update frequency aligns with project phases. Initiation phase documents are updated during project initiation. Weekly progress logs and material requisition records are updated during the construction phase. Full acceptance documents are generated during the acceptance phase.
Document structures include fixed fields: project number, section information, construction scope, equipment parameters, construction period requirements, acceptance standards, and more.
Engineering-specific units are used, including kilometers, port count, sets/units, and construction days.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Vector Models and Indexes Workflows
The structured fields and specialized units of telecommunications engineering documents require index processes to retain field metadata. This avoids confusing parameters from different sections during matching.
Phased document updates require indexes to support incremental updates. This avoids full scans that repeatedly process historical documents.
Document lengths vary widely. Some survey records are only hundreds of characters long, while construction organization designs can be tens of thousands of characters. This requires chunking rules that adapt to different text lengths.
The uniqueness of specialized fields requires indexes to prioritize matching identification fields such as project number and section. This improves matching accuracy.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Telecommunications engineering documents often contain structured fields and long paragraphs of construction descriptions. This range preserves context integrity |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents critical construction steps and project numbers from being truncated after chunking |
| `defaultToken` | 4096 | Meets the token capacity requirements of most single chunks of telecommunications engineering documents |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Handles extremely long acceptance reports or consolidated supervision logs |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.78 | Distinguishes between construction plans for similar sections and general engineering templates |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 results | Relevant matches for telecommunications engineering documents typically cluster around documents from the same section or construction type |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The interface continuously displays the "Indexing" status for more than 30 minutes with no progress updates. Cause: Incremental indexing mode is not enabled, and all telecommunications engineering documents from the project cycle are scanned in full. Excessive data volume causes extended processing time.
- Symptom: Search results are empty, or return irrelevant non-telecommunications engineering documents. Cause: Exclusive fields such as project number and construction scope are deleted during chunk configuration, preventing vectors from matching precise content.
- Symptom: Indexing fails after connecting a local m3e model, and the console returns a dimension mismatch error. Cause: The vector dimension generated by the model is not aligned with the storage dimension of the index database, preventing embedded vectors from being stored in the index database.
