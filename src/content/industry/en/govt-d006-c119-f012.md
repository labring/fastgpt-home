# Document and Material Drafting for Iron and Steel Smelting: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
The data used for iron and steel smelting document and material drafting primarily originates from on-site process cards, per-heat quality inspection reports, monthly compliance documents, and customer order contracts. Data sources cover execution and management links across the full smelting workflow. Update frequency varies by document type: process cards update in real time with each heat, quality inspection reports generate synchronously tied to production batches, and compliance documents are revised quarterly or annually. Document structures include standardized headers, core parameter fields, and approval process nodes. Fields often include smelting duration, batch numbers, material composition values, work area numbers, and similar items. Document lengths vary widely, ranging from on-site records of a few hundred characters to tens of pages of compliance manuals.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on deployment and upgrade
The multi-source heterogeneous data characteristics of iron and steel smelting scenarios require configuring multiple data source connections during deployment, to cover access from different sources including on-site production sites, management systems, and archived documents. Real-time generated heat quality inspection reports and daily production reports require avoiding peak production hours during upgrades, to prevent interruptions to core data links. The structured nature of long compliance manual documents requires configuring parsing rules adapted to the extraction logic of headers and parameter fields, to avoid splitting that damages key approval node information. Under intranet deployment constraints, correctly configure access endpoints for local services, and resolve account anomalies caused by permission changes after upgrades.

## Three common configuration errors
- Symptom: Connection failure is returned when calling a local MCP tool, logs show service address resolution failure. Cause: Local service endpoints were incorrectly filled with the generic `localhost:port` instead of the actual intranet service address of the iron and steel enterprise, leading to blocked cross-node access.
- Symptom: System login fails after upgrade, with a prompt indicating account or password verification failure. Cause: The original `ROOT_KEY` configuration was not retained during the upgrade process, the system permission verification logic was reset, and the permission association relationships of original accounts became invalid.
- Symptom: Empty results are returned when searching for smelting documents, with no matching content. Cause: `PG_URL` was not correctly configured to point to the dedicated index database, or database connection permissions were not granted to the FastGPT service.
