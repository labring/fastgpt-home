# Document and Material Drafting for Railway Operations: Workflow Orchestration

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Railway operation documents and materials are primarily sourced from internal business systems such as train dispatching logs, passenger service records, and equipment maintenance reports. Data updates follow a shift or calendar day cadence. Individual documents range from hundreds to tens of thousands of characters in length, with a fixed structure including fields such as operation number, execution time, line section, operation content, and responsible person. Common units include professional identifiers such as kilometers, hours, train numbers, and fault codes. This page was written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Workflow Orchestration Link
The fixed structured nature of railway operation documents requires that workflow orchestration precisely matches preset field extraction rules to avoid missing critical information such as operation numbers and line sections. The wide range of individual document lengths and high update frequency requires workflows to support chunked processing and scheduled triggering mechanisms to adapt to daily or shift-level data synchronization requirements. The specificity of professional fields and units requires content generation tools in workflows to adapt to railway operation-specific terminology to ensure outputs comply with industry standards. Some classified operation and maintenance documents also require permission verification nodes to control data circulation scope.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Railway operation documents are mostly structured long documents; this range balances context integrity and processing efficiency |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents chunking from splitting continuous content such as operation procedures and fault descriptions, ensuring context coherence |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 results | Critical information in railway operation documents is mostly concentrated in core fields; a small number of retrievals can cover requirements |
| `variables` | Bind operation number and line section variables | Adapts to the fixed fields of railway operation documents, improving the accuracy of generated content |
| `nodeId` | Configure based on the actual identifier of the tool call node | Ensures the order and permissions of tool calls in the workflow match the business logic of railway operations |
| Maximum Context | 8000–12000 tokens | Adapts to the processing requirements of long railway operation documents, avoiding truncation of critical business content |

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: Tool calls become unresponsive after the workflow runs, and the interface displays database connection failed. Cause: The database port and permission configuration of the railway operation business system are not adapted, or the data source node is not correctly bound in the workflow.
- Phenomenon: Generated railway operation documents do not use correct professional separators, and context breaks after long documents are chunked. Cause: Only general title separators are used during knowledge base chunking, and the exclusive chapter structure of railway documents (such as "train dispatch order", "maintenance report" and other dedicated title hierarchies) is not matched.
- Phenomenon: The access link generated after workflow publishing points to a localhost address and cannot be accessed externally. Cause: The default local service address of FastGPT was not replaced during deployment, and no public network domain name and port mapping were configured.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Trigger a workflow test task, check whether the generated document includes preset fields such as operation number and line section, and confirm that `variables` binding takes effect.
- View the workflow running log, confirm that the tool call node can normally connect to the business database, with no timeout or permission errors.
- Check the preview content after knowledge base chunking, confirm that long documents are separated according to the exclusive chapter structure of railway documents, with no context breaks.
- Copy the workflow publishing link, replace the local address with the public network domain name and access it, confirm that the workflow chat interface can be opened normally.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
