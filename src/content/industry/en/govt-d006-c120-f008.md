# Document Drafting for Railway Operations: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Documents and materials for railway operations come primarily from four data sources: train dispatch logs, passenger service records, freight operation documents, and line inspection reports. Update frequencies cover three categories: real-time dispatch orders, daily operation reports, and monthly statistical ledgers.
Document structures include table documents with fixed fields and segmented long-text logs. Core fields include train number, operation time, line mileage, and cargo weight. Units include kilometers, tons, minutes, and others.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Document Parsing and Chunking
If table documents with fixed fields are split using universal line breaks, a single freight operation record will be split into multiple chunks, losing the association between fields such as train number and weight.
If the time periods of long-text train logs are cut randomly, the contextual coherence of dispatch instructions will be broken.
If chunk boundaries cross numeric values and their corresponding units for multi-field numeric content with units, semantic breaks will occur during subsequent retrieval.
Frequently updated real-time dispatch documents require chunking logic optimized for fast parsing to avoid timeouts.

## Configuration Settings
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | `800–1200 characters` | Balances information density for both table fragments and long logs from railway operation documents, while maintaining contextual coherence |
| `chunkSplitter` | By paragraph + custom separator | Adapts to table line breaks and the segmented format of operation logs, avoiding splitting the association between core fields |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Preserves the fixed layout structure of railway operation PDF documents, preventing table content from being disordered |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | `1000 MB` | Meets the large-file upload requirements for single monthly freight statistical reports |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | `600 seconds` | Covers the full parsing process for large, multi-page train logs, avoiding timeout interruptions |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual configuration | Matches the fixed format of railway operation documents, preventing incorrect splits from automatic chunking |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After uploading freight documents in Excel format, multi-line content of a single record is merged into one chunk. Reason: The custom separator matching Excel row separation rules is not configured. The default chunking logic splits by paragraphs, and cannot recognize the boundary of single-row Excel data.
- Phenomenon: After uploading multiple train log PDFs, some documents show a parsing timeout status. Reason: The `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameter is not adjusted. The parsing duration of large, multi-page logs exceeds the default threshold.
- Phenomenon: When retrieving dispatch information for a specific train number, chunks containing complete operation records cannot be retrieved. Reason: The chunk boundary crosses core associated fields such as train number and operation time, destroying the semantic integrity of the data.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single typical railway operation document, check the chunk preview interface, and confirm that single operation records are not randomly split or merged.
- Upload multiple documents in different formats, check the parsing status list, and confirm there are no timeout errors or parsing failure markers.
- After adjusting chunk-related configurations, compare the chunk results before and after, and confirm they match the structural characteristics of the business documents.
- After importing test data, retrieve core business fields, and confirm that the chunk content contains complete combinations of associated fields.
