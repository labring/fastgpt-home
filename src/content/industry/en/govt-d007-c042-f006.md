# Declaration Materials and Case File Review for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category of data includes
Daily documents under this category cover compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, major issue legal opinions, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation reports. Fields include compliance item numbers, review opinion content, report clue IDs, regulatory citation entries, and more. Data sources are enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, legal and regulatory repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. Update frequency is monthly. Most documents combine semi-structured and unstructured formats, and some review opinions include exclusive numbers and associated business identifiers.

## Constraints on source citation and traceability
Multi-source, heterogeneous data sources require the system to support integration with multiple independent knowledge bases, covering internal business systems and external regulatory repositories. The monthly update cycle requires the indexing schedule to match data update frequency, to avoid citing expired content. Exclusive document field identifiers require precise association with corresponding business nodes during traceability, to ensure cited content directly relates to the declaration matter. The high stakes of errors require traceability information to be fully auditable, with no discrepancies between cited content and source documents. The time-consuming nature of review workflows requires efficient citation processes, to avoid delaying overall review progress.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Context | `8000–12000 token` | Matches the average length of a single compliance case file or review material, to avoid truncating critical compliance basis |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 entries | Covers multiple types of reference materials needed for compliance review, while controlling overall token consumption |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `3000–5000 token` | Limits the maximum length of a single cited content segment, to prevent redundant information from interfering with large model generation of compliance review opinions |
| `responseData` | Return only associated document metadata | Reduces data transmission volume, and improves loading and display speed of traceability information |
| `detail` | Enabled | Displays the source system, update time, and exclusive number of cited documents, to meet full information requirements for compliance traceability |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Filters low-relevance regulations or historical cases, to avoid invalid citations disrupting review logic |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- After calling the knowledge base module in a workflow, returned results contain no citation content, and fields are empty. This occurs because the `responseData` configuration item is not enabled, and the metadata return switch is not activated.
- Knowledge base queries return timeout status codes, and inference execution time exceeds normal ranges. This occurs because the `quoteMaxToken` value is not restricted, and an excessive amount of tokens is pushed to the large model in a single request, leading to inference delays.
- Generated review opinions cite irrelevant regulations or historical cases, with abnormal context relevance. This occurs because a reasonable similarity threshold is not set, and a large volume of unrelated document content is introduced.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
This guide is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Trigger a declaration material review request, and check whether returned results include the source system, update time, and exclusive number of cited documents.
- Check large model inference execution time, to confirm no timeouts exceeding normal ranges have occurred.
- Randomly select a reviewed case file, and verify whether cited content matches compliance items in the source document.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` value, and verify whether the length of returned cited content conforms to preset limits.

The values provided here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
