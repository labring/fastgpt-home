# Application Material and Case Review for Social Assistance and Minimum Living Security: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category comes from daily business documents of civil affairs bureau relief departments, sub-district and township civil affairs offices, community committees, and verification centers. These documents include minimum living security application forms, family economic status verification reports, household survey forms, democratic appraisal records, public notice materials, and assistance approval forms.
Data sources include low-income population dynamic monitoring platforms, resident family economic status verification systems, and cross-department shared data from public security, human resources and social security, medical insurance, housing and urban-rural development, and tax authorities. The update frequency is monthly.
Most documents are forms, tables, or mixed text-and-image structures. Fields include family income, property status, member relationships, approval progress, and other details. There is no unified fixed format. Some documents include handwritten annotations or scanned copies.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Document Parsing and Chunking
Cross-department shared data has diverse sources and inconsistent formats. The parsing module must support scanned documents, forms, tables, and mixed text-and-image documents.
Monthly batch-updated business documents require stable parsing processes that meet efficiency requirements and avoid timeouts or interruptions.
Form and table documents must accurately identify field correspondences. Chunking must not damage the integrity of core fields such as family income and member relationships.
For mixed text-and-image public notice materials, the association between original images and text must be retained. Directly converting image content to OCR text and losing the original image must be avoided.
For household survey forms with mixed handwritten annotations and printed text, the parsing logic must adapt to mixed content to ensure key information is not lost.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Issue: Importing PPT or DOC documents and enabling PDF enhancement results in no parsing effect. The interface shows parsing failure or empty content. Cause: The PDF enhancement function only supports PDF format documents. The corresponding parsing logic is not enabled for other office documents.
- Issue: Images in parsed documents are converted to OCR text, and the original image format cannot be retained. Cause: The image-text separation mode of `customPdfParse` is not configured correctly. Full OCR parsing is enabled by default.
- Issue: The sheet name of Excel-format family economic status verification reports is not extracted after parsing. Only the content of the default first worksheet is returned. Cause: The sheet recognition configuration for table documents is not enabled. Multi-sheet information is ignored by default.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a single minimum living security application form document, check the parsed chunking results, and confirm that core fields are not split.
- Upload a public notice material document that includes images, check if the parsed result retains the association between original images and corresponding text.
- Upload an Excel-format family economic status verification report, confirm that all sheet names are correctly identified and extracted.
- Upload batch documents updated monthly, monitor the parsing task duration and success rate, and confirm that no timeouts or failures occur.
