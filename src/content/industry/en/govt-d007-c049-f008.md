# Declaration Materials and File Review for Legality Review of Regulatory Documents: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The entities served by this category include judicial administrative departments, departmental legal affairs offices, and policy and legal positions in government offices. Daily processed documents include regulatory document drafts, drafting explanatory notes, legality review opinions, summary of solicited comments, and filing reports. Data sources include regulatory and rule databases, higher-level law databases, departmental policy document databases, and administrative regulatory document filing systems. Update frequency is irregular. Document structures include chaptered provisions, tabular summaries, comment feedback entries, and more. Core fields involve formulation authority, rights and obligation settings, review opinions, and other items. There are no unified fixed units; identification must be based on specific provision content. Unlike individual administrative law enforcement review, this category reviews abstract policy rules, with focus on formulation authority, procedures, and the legality of provisions. FastGPT v4.17.0

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Document Parsing and Chunking" Link
Documents contain structured provisions, unstructured explanatory content, and tabular summary materials. Parsing must retain hierarchical structures and table integrity. Avoid splitting key provisions or comment entries. Most data sources are official databases. Parsed content must strictly match higher-level laws and departmental policies. Parsing results must not omit core review content such as rights and obligation settings. The irregular update feature requires parsing workflows to adapt to both existing and incremental files in different formats. Because errors may lead to filing corrections or delayed policy release, parsing must ensure content integrity and contextual coherence. Chunking results must not take content out of context.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Matches the paragraph length of regulatory document provisions and drafting notes, avoids splitting complete legal provisions across paragraphs |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by title + paragraph | Adapts to the chapter structure of documents, retains contextual connections between titles such as "formulation authority" and "review opinions" and their corresponding content |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Processes files containing tabular summaries of solicited comments, retains row and column structure and content of tables |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 600 seconds | Addresses large-volume batch filing report files, avoids timeout errors caused by insufficient parsing duration |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual mode | Flexibly adjusts chunking parameters for different document types such as regulatory document drafts and comment summary sheets |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Parsed table content is lost, and the interface shows empty table datasets. This occurs because the `customPdfParse` configuration is not enabled. The default parsing logic does not retain table structures.
- Timeout errors are triggered when parsing large-volume filing reports, and the task status shows timeout. This occurs because the `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameter is not adjusted. The default duration is insufficient to complete parsing of complex files.
- Complete legal provisions are split during chunking, leading to broken context. This occurs because the `chunkSize` setting is too small, splitting regulatory document provisions across paragraphs into multiple independent chunks.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single regulatory document draft, review the parsed chunking results, and confirm that complete legal provisions are not split.
- Upload a file containing a tabular summary of solicited comments, confirm that the parsed results retain the row and column structure and content of the table.
- Upload a large-volume filing report file, confirm that no timeout error is triggered during the parsing task.
- Adjust the `chunkSize` parameter, compare chunking effects across different values, and confirm alignment with contextual requirements for business review.
