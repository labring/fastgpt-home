# Review of Submission Materials and Case Files for Legitimacy Review of Regulatory Documents: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
This category serves judicial administrative departments, departmental legal affairs offices, and policy and legal affairs positions in government offices. Daily processed documents include draft regulatory documents, drafting explanations, legitimacy review opinions, summaries of solicited comments, and filing reports.
Data sources include regulatory and rule databases, higher-level law databases, departmental policy document databases, and administrative regulatory document filing systems. Update frequency is irregular.
Data fields include document title, publication number, issuing entity, text content, review opinions, and more. The core focus is on the legitimacy of policy-making authority, procedures, and text content. There are clear differences in data structure between this category and individual administrative law enforcement review data.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on deployment and upgrade
Most data sources are exclusive databases on government intranets. Deployments require configuring dedicated secure access links to avoid compliance risks from cross-network access.
Data updates have no fixed cycle. Configurations must support incremental synchronization and on-demand refresh to ensure the timeliness of retrieved content.
Daily documents are long-text compliance files. Deployments must use retrieval logic tailored to abstract policy rules, and cannot reuse configurations designed for administrative law enforcement scenarios.
Review errors have serious consequences. Deployments must include log retention and verification rollback configurations to facilitate post-hoc verification of process compliance.
Deployments must also be differentiated from other government review categories to avoid retrieval matching deviations caused by generic configurations.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MONGODB_URI` | Exclusive connection string for government intranet-compliant databases | Data sources include regulatory and rule databases and other government databases. A dedicated connection is required to ensure access security and compliance |
| `REDIS_URL` | Address of a government cloud-exclusive Redis instance, configure connection timeout to 300 seconds | Retrieval results and review logs need to be cached. The timeout setting adapts to the average latency of government networks |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | 10–15 | Multiple government data sources need to be connected simultaneously. This range prevents connection exhaustion from disrupting review workflows |
| Single retrieval limit | Top 8 results | Regulatory document review needs to cover major higher-level legal basis. Excessive results increase manual review workload |
| Context cache duration | 7200 seconds | Adapts to the irregular update characteristics of data sources. Regular cache refresh ensures retrieval accuracy |
| FastGPT Version | FastGPT v4.17.0 | This version fixes connection stability issues in some government scenarios and meets compliance deployment requirements |

## Three Common Mistakes
- The symptom is that retrieval results fail to match the corresponding higher-level legal provisions. The cause is that a dedicated data source connection for the regulatory and rule database was not configured, and the indexing rules of a generic knowledge base were used instead.
- The symptom is an error occurs after running the initialization command during local deployment, prompting a database connection failure. The cause is that the `MONGODB_URI` parameter was not configured correctly, and a non-compliant public database address was used, or intranet access permissions were not configured.
- The symptom is that no log records exist for key steps in the review process. The cause is that the `REDIS_URL` parameter was not configured, leading to failed caching of verification records and inability to roll back the review workflow.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Run a data source connectivity test to verify that services corresponding to parameters such as `MONGODB_URI` and `REDIS_URL` can be accessed normally, with no permission errors or connection timeouts.
- Upload a draft regulatory document to trigger retrieval, and confirm that the returned results include matching content from the regulatory and rule database and higher-level law database, with no irrelevant non-compliant data source results.
- Check system logs to confirm that all key steps in the review process are recorded, including data source calls, retrieval matching, opinion summary, and other links.
- Test the incremental synchronization function by uploading a new policy document, and confirm that it can be automatically covered by the retrieval index without manually rebuilding the full knowledge base.
