# Road Transport Business License Application Materials and File Review: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
This category serves transportation bureau approval departments, road transport management agencies, and transport enterprises. Data is sourced from the road transport administration system, the national public service platform for dynamic information of road transport vehicles, and the vehicle and practitioner database, and is updated daily. Daily processed documents include business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, work safety management systems, road transport business licenses, and similar items. Fields cover business entity qualifications, vehicle technical parameters, practitioner qualifications, safety management specifications, site conditions, and similar items. There is no unified fixed format template, and verification must be conducted based on the actual submitted content of the applicant.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering" link
The data sources for this category are scattered and updated daily. Multi-turn dialogue must gradually guide users to submit different types of application materials in sequence, avoiding overburdening users by requesting all information at once. There are significant differences in fields across different documents, so prompt engineering must define verification rules separately for documents such as business license applications and vehicle technical files. Vehicle and practitioner data is updated in real time, so the latest data sources must be called during the dialogue process to verify qualification validity, preventing the use of expired information for review. At the same time, verifying vehicle personnel and safety systems is the core time-consuming link, so multi-turn dialogue must prioritize information completion and verification for this link.

## How to Configure the Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind the unique identifier of the current approval session | Used to associate dialogue history with file review progress, ensuring context consistency across multi-turn interactions |
| `messages` | Retain the most recent 20 rounds of dialogue content | Covers the complete interaction flow of application material submission and qualification verification, avoiding context loss |
| `variables` | Bind verification fields such as vehicle qualifications and practitioner qualifications | Used to dynamically call compliant data from the latest database during dialogue |
| Maximum context length | 8000–12000 characters | Adapts to the length of single-batch submitted road transport application materials, avoiding truncation of critical information |
| Timeout duration | 120 seconds | Matches the single-operation duration of approval personnel during daily verification, preventing unexpected session interruptions |
| Single search limit | Top 3 entries | Avoids excessive redundant historical information interfering with the current review process |
| Question optimization | Calibrated based on actual testing | Optimize user questions for road transport professional terminology, improving the accuracy of review Q&A |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Interactive lag occurs in the dialogue interface, with response delays exceeding 15 seconds. Cause: No reasonable timeout duration is set, causing sessions to occupy system resources for extended periods.
- Phenomenon: Returned content does not meet expectations after a question is asked, and does not match the road transport license review rules. Cause: The prompt engineering does not limit the review scenario, and does not clearly require responses based on transport administration system data, corresponding to poorly effective prompt engineering issues in the community.
- Phenomenon: Complete dialogue history cannot be traced back, and session records cannot be associated with files. Cause: The `chatId` configuration is not bound, leading to context breaks in multi-turn interactions, corresponding to issues where dialogue records cannot be exported or associated in the community.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Initiate a test dialogue, input "What materials are required to apply for a road transport business license", check that the returned content clearly lists required document types such as vehicle technical files and driver qualification materials.
- View the session management list, confirm that each test session has a unique identifier, and the complete interaction process can be traced back through the identifier.
- Configure `variables` to bind the vehicle identification code field, input the identification code of a certain vehicle, check that the latest technical parameters of the corresponding vehicle are automatically called in the dialogue.
- Simulate a session scenario with 100 seconds of no operation, check that no abnormal error occurs in the session, and the timeout trigger logic works normally.
