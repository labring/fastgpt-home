# Road Transport Business License Application Materials and File Review: Source Citation and Traceability

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Data for this category originates from the road transport administration system, the national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and the vehicle and practitioner database. The update frequency is daily.
Regularly processed documents include business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, work safety management systems, and road transport business licenses. Fields cover business entity information, vehicle parameters, practitioner qualifications, and safety system clauses. All data is organized around core elements of road transport business access and ongoing operation.

## Constraints Imposed on Source Citation and Traceability Workflow
Scattered data sources require clear labeling of the corresponding official system when citing, to avoid traceability confusion. Document types cover applications, files, qualifications and other categories. Core field weights vary across different documents, so targeted citation association is required.
The daily update frequency means cited content must match the latest data, and expired materials must not be used. Errors directly impact license approval, so a complete traceability chain must be maintained. Every cited item must be traceable back to its original data source and upload node.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Blindly increasing the value of `quoteMaxToken` during semantic retrieval, which triggers context overflow errors. This occurs because the upper limit is not configured based on the actual length of documents in this category, and only more cited content is prioritized.
- Duplicate content or training exceptions appear during knowledge base citation after uploading multiple road transport-related documents. This occurs because the knowledge base's automatic deduplication logic is not enabled, or file uniqueness is not verified during upload.
- Failing to clearly label the source system when configuring the cited content template, which prevents reviewers from tracing the original data source. This occurs because the template content is not adjusted to match the multi-data source characteristics of this category.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Upload a standard road transport business license application document, trigger retrieval, and review the returned cited content to confirm it includes metadata such as source system and document type.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, upload an ultra-long document, and verify normal truncation or complete return to confirm the parameter is active.
- Upload multiple documents of the same type, review the knowledge base citation results to confirm duplicate content has been automatically deduplicated.
- Open the dialogue component configuration interface, confirm that large model related parameters can be set normally, and that the cited template includes the {{id}} field.
