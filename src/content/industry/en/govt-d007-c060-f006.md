# Warehouse Logistics Declaration Materials and File Review: Source Citation and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Declaration materials and file review data for the warehouse logistics sector originates primarily from inbound receipts, outbound receipts, transportation dispatch orders, customs declarations, and internal approval files. Data updates trigger with business nodes: it syncs immediately after a single business task completes, and bulk data updates daily. Most documents use structured Excel, scanned PDFs, or structured JSON formats. They include fields such as logistics tracking numbers, warehouse zone numbers, cargo categories, inbound/outbound times, approval workflow nodes, and approver signatures. Units include pieces, tons, cubic meters, and date formats. This page’s configuration instructions apply to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Source Citation and Traceability?
The multi-type business documents and approval file characteristics of warehouse logistics impose three constraints on source citation and traceability.
First, structured fields make up a large share of the data. Accurately match unique identifier fields such as logistics tracking numbers and zone numbers to avoid misalignment of traceability results.
Second, data updates in real time with business nodes. Ensure citations link to the currently active version of the document and exclude historical drafts.
Third, approval files include multi-stage circulation materials. Traceability must link all supporting documents across the complete approval workflow, covering circulation materials beyond a single submitted file. Mixed-format documents (scanned files and structured tables) require separate adaptation for text extraction and field mapping rules, which increases the complexity of traceability matching.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Redundant citation links appear at the end of conversations and cannot be turned off. After FastGPT returns review results, it automatically attaches citation links with file names at the end. This does not meet internal reporting format requirements. The cause is failing to turn off the citation display switch in system settings, or failing to explicitly require hiding citation information in the prompt.
- Historical declaration materials cannot be linked in multi-round review conversations. Subsequent review requests cannot match the warehouse documents submitted in the first round, and traceability results only display the input materials of the current round. The cause is failing to enable the context retention configuration, or setting the context window too small, resulting in truncation of historical document information.
- Traceability results match irrelevant warehouse documents. Returned reference materials do not match the current cargo category and zone number of the declaration. The cause is setting the similarity threshold too high, or failing to configure dedicated search terms for the standardized fields of warehouse documents.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Effective
- Submit a standard warehouse inbound receipt as a test document, initiate a review query that includes the inbound tracking number, and check whether the returned results link the citation information of this document.
- Initiate two consecutive review queries, test whether the second round can link the historical materials submitted in the first round, and confirm that the context configuration is effective.
- Adjust the similarity threshold and initiate the same query, observe the change in the number of matching traceability results, and confirm that the threshold configuration is effective.
- Check the status of the citation display switch in system settings, and confirm that it meets the business format requirements.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
