# Pharmaceutical Distribution Declaration Materials and Case Review: Tool Calling and Plugins

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for pharmaceutical distribution declaration materials and case review primarily comes from internal enterprise GSP compliance ledgers, standardized declaration templates from drug regulatory authorities, cold chain transport monitoring logs, and purchase and sales vouchers. Data updates follow project batch and daily transaction rhythms. Declaration materials are archived per single approval project, while cases are updated daily or per batch. Document structures include fixed header fields (declaring entity, product category, batch number), structured compliance items (approval number, inventory quantity, transport temperature range), and attachments (qualification scans, test reports, Excel purchase and sales ledgers). Field units are mostly physical or compliance units such as pieces, boxes, and degrees Celsius.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the tool calling and plugin workflow
The multi-format attachments, fixed compliance fields, and batched update features of pharmaceutical distribution declaration materials and cases create clear constraints for tool calling and plugin configuration. Multi-format attachments require plugins to support mixed parsing of PDF, image, and spreadsheet documents to avoid missing key data from qualification scans or purchase and sales ledgers. Fixed compliance fields require tool calling to bind preset mapping rules, ensuring accurate extraction of mandatory compliance items like approval numbers during review, and only extracting preset compliance field content. Batched update data requires plugins to support indexing and filtering by batch number, ensuring that corresponding batch cases and declaration materials are called during review to avoid cross-batch data confusion.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: An error is reported during deployment after adding the Doc2X plugin, and the log shows "plugin initialization failed". Cause: The multi-format document parsing whitelist for pharmaceutical distribution scenarios is not configured, or the local parsing service relied on by the plugin does not support batch processing of mixed PDF and image attachments.
- Phenomenon: An error is reported when clicking input after referencing a plugin in the workflow, and the interface prompts "missing parameter". Cause: The fixed field mapping of pharmaceutical distribution declaration materials is not bound, and the tool call cannot match mandatory fields such as approval number and batch number.
- Phenomenon: Only simple information is returned after searching, and complete web link content cannot be read. Cause: The plugin's web content crawling rule is not configured, and the long text parsing switch is not enabled, which cannot adapt to the full reading requirements of long purchase and sales records in pharmaceutical distribution cases.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a declaration material PDF from a pharmaceutical distribution enterprise, trigger tool calling, and check whether the extraction results of corresponding compliance fields are automatically matched.
- Simulate a single long case review request, check whether the tool call is completed within the preset duration without timeout errors.
- Test the plugin's retry mechanism, actively disconnect the network and initiate a call, check whether it retries according to the preset number of times and returns to normal.
- Verify the web crawling function, enter the public notice link of the drug regulatory authority, and check whether complete approval document content can be extracted.
