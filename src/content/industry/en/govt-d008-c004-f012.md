# Business Process Guidance for Active Public Disclosure and Policy Publication: Deployment and Upgrade

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
The data sources for this category include government portal websites, document management systems, government information disclosure directories, policy document repositories, and press conference materials. Daily processed documents include policy documents, policy interpretation drafts, disclosure directories, information release approval forms, press releases, and website column update records. The data update frequency is daily, requiring synchronization of multiple types of structured and unstructured government documents. This covers the approval, release, and update stages of the entire policy publication process, and supports active public disclosure services for the public.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose During Deployment and Upgrade
Multi-source data sources require deployment to support import interfaces for different data sources such as document management systems and disclosure directories, to avoid data silos. Daily update frequency requires configuring a scheduled synchronization mechanism to ensure public content aligns with the update rhythm of the government intranet. Document types include structured forms such as approval forms and unstructured text such as policy interpretation drafts, requiring deployment to adapt to parsing rules for mixed documents. High compliance requirements for government public disclosure mean deployment must configure security parameters such as intranet isolation and encrypted storage to prevent sensitive information leaks, and must also support synchronized release configurations for multiple websites and new media accounts.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | `300–500` | Adapts to the associated query needs of multi-source government data, and prevents database connection exhaustion |
| `MONGODB_URI` | Exclusive address of the government intranet private cluster | Stores sensitive government data such as policy documents and approval forms, and implements intranet isolated storage |
| `REDIS_URL` | Address of the government cloud caching instance | Caches daily updated disclosure directory indexes, and reduces query pressure on the core database |
| `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` | Address of the government-unified cloud storage service | Stores multimedia attachments such as policy interpretation drafts and press releases, and complies with government storage specifications |
| `FE_DOMAIN` | Official domain name of the government portal website | Binds the access domain name of the process guidance page, ensuring it is mounted to official public channels |
| `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` | Encryption key compliant with government information security specifications | Protects the security of public file download links, and prevents unauthorized access |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Synchronization failure of the disclosure directory after deployment, with connection timeout shown in logs. Cause: `REDIS_URL` is not configured to point to the government cloud caching instance, causing directory indexes to fail to load quickly and synchronization requests to time out.
- After upgrading to FastGPT v4.17.0, an unclickable overlay appears on the login interface, making background configuration inaccessible. Cause: `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` is not properly generated and configured, triggering front-end permission verification exceptions and preventing the overlay from closing normally.
- Only some sections take effect during multi-website column synchronization, with an unexpected number of results. Cause: `DB_MAX_LINK` is not adjusted to a reasonable range, leading to insufficient connections during multi-source data association, and some disclosure directories fail to complete synchronization.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Run the daily synchronization script, and verify that the update time of the disclosure directory matches the update time of the government intranet.
- Access the process guidance page under the bound `FE_DOMAIN` domain name, and confirm that policy document links can jump normally and no permission error messages appear.
- View the background storage panel, and confirm that `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` has successfully mounted attachments such as policy interpretation drafts and press releases.
- Submit a test information release approval form, and confirm that the approval status can be synchronized to the government disclosure directory.

The values provided here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the specific samples of the deploying entity.
