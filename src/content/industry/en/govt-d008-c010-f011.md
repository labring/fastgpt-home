# Business Process Guidance for Highway Maintenance and Road Property Rights Management: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
Business data for this category typically originates from highway maintenance management systems, bridge and tunnel monitoring systems, road administration patrol systems, and traffic flow monitoring datasets. Update frequency is daily.
Daily business documents include maintenance plans, patrol logs, defect disposal orders, road construction permits, and road property damage compensation forms. Common fields include road section number, defect location, damage area, construction start and end time, and others. Common units are kilometers, square meters, and working days. The data structure adheres to standard highway maintenance formats.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on forms and interactions?
Business data for this category comes from multiple dispersed systems. Form interactions must connect to cross-system field mappings to ensure data consistency.
Daily updated business data has high real-time requirements, requiring timely synchronization of data after patrols and work order submissions.
Differences in fields across business documents mean form configurations must match exclusive fields for each business stage. For example, road construction permit forms must include construction plans, safety protection measures, and related content.
Errors can impact road safety and lead to financial disputes, so form required fields and validation rules must strictly align with core business handling fields to avoid missing critical information.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Scenario: No historical business data from maintenance systems is referenced after form submission. Cause: `formInput` is not bound to the corresponding business system fields.
- Scenario: No model options such as Tongyi Qianwen appear when selecting a domestic large model. Cause: Corresponding API keys and permissions have not been added in the FastGPT v4.17.0 model configuration.
- Scenario: Preset process guidance is not triggered when calling tools to generate work orders. Cause: The tool call permission configuration for `userSelect` has not been enabled.

## How to verify correct configuration
- Check if `formInput` is bound to standard fields from road administration patrol systems, and confirm the data mapping relationship is correct.
- Test that the number of matching results for `userSelect` is the top 5, to verify the configuration is active.
- Submit a simulated road construction permit form to check if required field validation rules are triggered.
- Select the Tongyi Qianwen model and initiate a test to confirm domestic large models can be called normally for form interactions.
