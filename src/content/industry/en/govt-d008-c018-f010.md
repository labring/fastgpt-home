# Business Process Guidance for Social Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What This Category of Data Entails
Data for this category originates from social insurance management systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. The update frequency is daily. Daily processed documents include insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification forms, and personal benefit records. Most documents contain fields such as personnel identity identifiers, insurance registration status, payment base, and verified amount. Data is generated based on basic information across the full insurance premium payment process, focusing on core associated information for personnel insurance registration and premium verification.

## Constraints Imposed on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The daily data update requirement means knowledge base recall timeliness must align with synchronization rhythms, to prevent use of expired personnel change or premium verification information. Multiple document types have high field overlap, so keywords must be accurately matched to the business scenario, to avoid retrieval results confusing content from the insurance registration and premium verification links. Cross-system source data has format differences, so field mapping must be unified prior to retrieval, to ensure recalled information can be directly used for business verification. Personnel identity-related fields serve as the core retrieval basis, so high-confidence identity identifier fields must be prioritized for matching, to reduce time spent during verification steps.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Expired personnel change information appears in retrieval results. Cause: No daily synchronized knowledge base update task is configured, and outdated historical document data is used.
- Images within documents cannot be displayed properly in responses. Cause: The image storage configuration for the knowledge base is not enabled, or uploaded images are not bound to the retrieval association of the corresponding document.
- Content from this category's knowledge base is accidentally introduced during regular conversation calls. Cause: No dedicated collection is configured via `collectionIds`, leading to global retrieval covering irrelevant knowledge bases.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Manually trigger knowledge base retrieval, check whether returned document types relate to insurance registration and premium verification.
- View the knowledge base synchronization log to confirm the daily update task has executed normally with no failed records.
- Test retrieval of documents containing images, confirm corresponding images load properly in responses.
- Switch to regular conversation mode, verify this category's knowledge base content is not automatically recalled.
