# Business Process Guidance for Enrollment Registration and Premium Payment Verification: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for this category comes from social insurance management systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared data from public security and civil affairs departments. The update frequency is daily. Daily business documents include enrollment registration forms, premium base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification notices, and personal benefit records. Fields cover personnel identity identifiers, labor relationship status, premium base ranges, personnel change information, and other relevant content. There is no unified fixed unit, and adjustments are needed to match specific business reporting scenarios. FastGPT v4.17.0 supports connecting to multi-source data interfaces and can directly synchronize the latest content from the above data sources.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "forms and interactions" link
Multi-source data sources require forms to support cross-data-source linkage verification, to avoid inconsistencies between identity or labor relationship information and external systems, and ensure the accuracy of reported content. Daily update frequency requires form interactions to support real-time pulling of the latest data, ensuring that the content reported by handling personnel matches the current system state and reducing subsequent reconciliation workload. The fixed structure of business documents requires form fields to correspond one-to-one with document headers, reducing the probability of reporting deviations. Time-consuming verification links require forms to have built-in real-time verification logic to intercept erroneous information in advance and reduce subsequent processing costs. The serious consequences of errors require interactions to provide clear error guidance, enabling handling personnel to quickly troubleshoot issues and avoid underpayment, overpayment, or incorrect personal benefit records.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` | Bind core field group for enrollment registration | Match FastGPT v4.17.0 form binding logic and the fixed reporting structure of business documents |
| `userSelect` | Associate unit/individual identity verification data source | Connect to the identity verification interface of the social insurance management system to enable real-time identity verification |
| Form validation rules | Real-time validation of field format, required fields, and maximum input length (200 characters) | Reduce the probability of subsequent reconciliation errors and comply with the field length requirements of business documents |
| Multi-source data synchronization interval | 1 minute | Match the real-time requirement of daily data updates to ensure the form displays the latest data |
| Error prompt configuration | Output clear guidance categorized by field | Reduce the troubleshooting cost for handling personnel and avoid operational errors caused by vague prompts |
| Timeout threshold | 30 seconds | Adapt to the response duration of multi-source data calls and avoid long wait times that affect processing efficiency |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- When configuring `formInput`, failing to bind the corresponding business fields results in empty field errors after submission. The cause is that the fixed reporting structure of business documents is not matched, so the system cannot identify required reporting items.
- Failing to enable multi-source data linkage for `userSelect` results in timeout and no response during the identity verification link. The cause is that the identity verification interface of the social insurance management system is not connected, so real-time identity verification cannot be completed.
- Failing to configure knowledge base reference binding in the code execution node results in the output result being unable to associate enrollment data. The cause is that the association logic between business data and the knowledge base is not established, so the required enrollment information cannot be called.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Submit a test form, check whether all required fields trigger real-time validation prompts, and verify that the form validation rules are in effect.
- View the data source synchronization log, confirm that multi-source data completes update synchronization within a 1-minute interval, and verify that the data real-time configuration is correct.
- Trigger the identity verification operation, confirm that the returned verification result matches the data of the social insurance management system, and verify that the association configuration of `userSelect` is in effect.
- Simulate incorrect input, check whether clear error prompts for the corresponding fields are output, and verify that the error prompt configuration meets expectations.
