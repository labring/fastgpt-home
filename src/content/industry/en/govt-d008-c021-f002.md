# Business Process Guidance for Centralized Treasury Payment and Reimbursement Audit: Vector Models and Indexes

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from centralized treasury payment systems, unified budget management systems, electronic invoice platforms, and contract management systems, and is updated daily. Commonly processed documents include payment applications, fund appropriation plans, invoices, contracts, acceptance receipts, reimbursement approval forms, and payment vouchers. It covers daily business data for budget unit finance, treasury payment center finance audit positions, and competent department finance audit positions, and includes information records for the entire process of budget application, review, and payment.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexes link
The multi-source and daily updated nature of the data requires indexes to support efficient incremental synchronization mechanisms, to avoid excessive time consumption caused by full indexing. There are diverse document types, including application, voucher, contract and other texts with different structures. The core business fields of different documents vary significantly, so retrieval logic needs to be optimized for high-frequency verification fields such as budget indicators and economic classifications. The business process involves compliance verification for fund payments, which has extremely high requirements for the accuracy of retrieval results. Any mismatch may trigger payment returns or compliance risks, so the recall and sorting logic of indexes needs to be adapted to the professional business scenarios of this category.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the information density per chunk of documents such as payment applications and reimbursement approval forms, avoiding overly scattered or redundant information within chunks |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retains key associated information between adjacent chunks, ensuring retrieval continuity for cross-chunk fields such as budget indicators and economic classifications |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Meets the compliance verification accuracy requirements of treasury payment business, filtering low-relevant invoice and contract data |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Controls the scale of retrieval results, adapting to the quick verification needs of finance audit positions |
| `maxToken` | 8000 | Adapts to the context length after multiple documents are spliced, meeting the retrieval and verification requirements for complete payment chains |
| `defaultToken` | 1024 | Adapts to the basic token limit for single document chunks, ensuring normal vectorization of basic text |
| Embedding Model | Ali-emb3 | Aligns with the built-in model selection for FastGPT v4.17.0 open source version, adapting to semantic understanding of Chinese financial and economic text |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Multiple duplicate results from the same document chunk appear during retrieval, and sorting is chaotic. Cause: No dedicated index weight is set for high-frequency business fields of this category, such as budget indicator numbers and payment application serial numbers. This leads to failure to sort by business relevance during multi-index retrieval.
- Phenomenon: Index tasks take longer than 600 seconds to complete, and a timeout status is displayed on the page. Cause: No incremental index strategy is configured. Full indexing is performed on daily updated full data, exceeding the processing limit for the data scale of this category.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results fail to match corresponding economic classification information, and empty fields are returned. Cause: The semantic alignment dimension of the Ali-emb3 model is not adapted to financial and economic text, leading to understanding deviation for professional terms such as budget indicators and economic classifications.

## How to confirm proper configuration
- View index task logs to confirm that incremental update tasks trigger daily, and no repeated execution records for full indexing appear.
- Enter keywords for a typical payment application, check the sorting and business relevance of retrieval results, and adjust the similarity threshold to a range that meets business verification requirements.
- Test the context length after multiple documents are spliced, confirm that the `maxToken` setting does not exceed the model's supported range, and avoid truncation of key business information.
- Check the field integrity of retrieval results, ensuring that core business fields such as budget indicators and economic classifications are correctly recalled.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
