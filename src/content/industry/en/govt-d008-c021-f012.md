# Business Process Guidance for Centralized Treasury Payment and Reimbursement Audit: Deployment and Upgrade

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category is sourced from centralized treasury payment systems, unified budget management systems, electronic invoice platforms, and contract management systems. The update frequency is daily. Daily processed documents include payment applications, fund appropriation plans, invoices, contracts, acceptance receipts, reimbursement approval forms, and payment vouchers. Core fields include budget indicators, economic classification, payment amounts, audit status, and more. The data is linked to multiple fiscal business systems, and a single data record must be matched with multiple types of business documents to complete compliance verification.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on deployment and upgrades
Since data is updated daily and linked to multiple fiscal business systems, deployment must ensure the stability of data synchronization to avoid interruptions affecting daily audit processes. Documents involve compliance verification, so storage and access must meet fiscal business security requirements. The audit link frequently calls multi-source data, so deployment must reserve sufficient query and computing resources. During upgrades, compatibility with original business system docking configurations must be maintained to avoid data matching logic failure due to version changes, and a rollback path must be reserved to address compliance risks.

## Three common misconfigurations
- After a major version upgrade, only 2 global variable configuration items are displayed. The cause is that upgrades were not performed in consecutive minor version increments; skipping compatible versions with configuration structure adjustments causes abnormal front-end rendering logic.
- An insufficient storage space error is triggered after uploading business documents during local deployment. The cause is that sufficient storage quota was not reserved based on the daily increase in reimbursement, invoice, and other document volumes, and the storage capacity corresponding to `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` was not properly configured.
- Upgrading directly to the latest version in a production environment causes data synchronization interruptions. The cause is that a compatible transitional version was not selected; upgrading directly across multiple major versions invalidates original docking logic, and no rollback path was reserved.

## How to confirm configurations are correct
- A database connection test is executed to verify that the connection pool configured via `PG_URL` can normally handle concurrent query requests.
- A test invoice document is uploaded to confirm that core fields such as amount and invoice date can be extracted normally after parsing.
- A manual data synchronization task is triggered, and logs are checked for no connection errors related to `REDIS_URL` or `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT`.
- The global variable configuration page is accessed to confirm that all preset fiscal business variables are fully displayed with no missing entries.
