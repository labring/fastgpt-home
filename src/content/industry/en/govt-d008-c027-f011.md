# Business Process Guidance for Engineering Construction Project Bidding: Forms and Interactions

Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data comes from national public resource trading platforms, local public resource trading platforms, engineering approval systems, and bidding supervision systems, and is updated daily. Daily processed documents include bidding announcements, qualification prequalification documents, bidding documents, bid documents, bid evaluation reports, successful bid candidate notices, and successful bid result announcements. Core fields cover bid section division, qualification requirements, bill of quantities, bid evaluation expert records, objection and complaint content, and other related items. These are maintained by industry authorities, public resource trading centers, and bidding agencies.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Daily data updates require form interactions to pull latest fields including bid section division and qualification conditions in real time, to prevent business processing guidance using expired information.
Multiple types of business documents require forms to adapt to field extraction rules for different scenarios. For example, the qualification requirement field in bidding documents and the result announcement field in successful bid notices require different interaction logic.
Diverse business subjects require forms to support role permission division, distinguishing editable and viewable ranges for bidding agencies, bidders, and supervision departments.
The risk of serious errors requires forms to add required field verification and format verification links, to reduce the risk of project suspension or accountability caused by incorrect information.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Basis for This Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form input verification rules | Match the field formats of bidding announcements and bidding documents | This category of business documents includes standardized fields, and input information must be consistent with officially published content |
| User role permission configuration | Three-level role permission division | Business participants are diverse, and form operation permissions must be distinguished for different roles |
| Real-time data synchronization interval | 600 seconds | Business data is updated daily, and a reasonable interval balances information timeliness and interface load |
| `formInput` timeout threshold | 30 seconds | Input verification during business processing must be responded to quickly, to avoid excessive user waiting time |
| `userSelect` option data source | Associate with the public resource trading platform interface, retrieve the top 20 valid options | Ensure dropdown options are the latest valid bid section, expert list, and other relevant information |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After submitting the form, a prompt "Invalid username" appears, and subsequent processes cannot be accessed. Cause: The real-name authentication interface of the public resource trading platform is not associated, and the verification logic does not match the username format required by the platform.
- Phenomenon: `userSelect` dropdown options load incompletely, only a small number of old bid section information is displayed. Cause: The data source associated with the latest public resource trading platform interface is not configured, and only local cached data is called.
- Phenomenon: The bill of quantities value entered in the form fails verification, and direct submission triggers process exceptions. Cause: Input verification rules matching the business document format are not configured, and valid numerical units and precision are not recognized.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Log in to the account corresponding to the role, and check whether the editable fields of the form and the permission configuration match the role's responsibilities.
- Trigger form input verification, enter test content that conforms to the business document format, and confirm that the verification logic takes effect normally.
- View the `userSelect` dropdown options, and confirm whether the latest public resource trading platform data is associated.
- Wait for the configured synchronization interval, refresh the page, and confirm whether the form fields have synchronized the latest bid section or qualification condition information.
