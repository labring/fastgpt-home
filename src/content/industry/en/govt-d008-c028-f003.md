# Business Process Guidance for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data is sourced from the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the Electronic Business License System, and the Residence Information Database, with a daily update frequency. Commonly processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials. The document structure primarily uses standardized business fields, including fixed entries such as entity name, residence address, shareholder information, and license type, with no custom non-standardized fields.

## Constraints imposed on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering links
The daily update feature of the data requires that the dialogue call the latest government data sources in real time, to avoid returning expired registration rules or licensing conditions. The standardized business document structure requires that prompts preset the guiding order of fixed fields, to avoid missing key information. The need to associate multiple data sources requires that multi-turn dialogue gradually integrate return results from different systems and complete verification step by step. The most time-consuming verification link requires that prompts pre-judge possible rejection points, actively prompt users to supplement materials, and reduce the probability of rejection in subsequent processes.

## Configuration settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Context window length | 8000–12000 token | Business documents are mostly multi-page application materials, so complete conversation history and material parsing snippets must be retained to avoid loss of key information |
| Question optimization toggle | Enabled | Users' colloquial inquiries need to be converted into standardized registration process queries to meet the rigorous expression requirements of government affairs business |
| Number of knowledge base entries retrieved per query | Top 3 entries | Registration and licensing require precise matching of preconditions and postconditions, to avoid redundant information interfering with judgment logic |
| `variables` configuration | Bind the `enterprise name`, `residence address`, and `license type` variables | Dynamically fill business-specific fields to enable personalized dialogue guidance |
| `messages` format configuration | Spliced in user-assistant turn order | Multi-turn dialogue must retain historical verification progress to avoid repeatedly asking for already collected business information |
| `chatId` configuration | Bound to the business handling serial number | Associate the context of the corresponding handling link to avoid confusing conversation content across applications |

## Three common misconfigurations
- The knowledge base interface call associated with the dialogue fails, returning empty results or a 404 status code. The cause is that the binding relationship between the knowledge base and the dialogue process is not correctly configured, or the correct parameters for calling the interface are not specified in the prompt.
- Large file document parsing times out, and the interface displays a processing timeout status. The cause is that the chunking configuration is not optimized for long government documents, resulting in overly large chunks or processing duration exceeding the limit (calibrated to 300 seconds based on actual testing).
- Repeatedly asking for the same business field in multi-turn dialogue, such as repeatedly asking for shareholder information. The cause is that the status of already collected fields is not clearly specified in the prompt, or the context window configuration is too small resulting in loss of historical information.

## How to verify successful configuration
- Initiate a simulated enterprise establishment registration inquiry, and check whether the dialogue automatically prompts for required business materials and mandatory fields.
- Upload a complete establishment registration application document, and verify whether core fields can be correctly extracted after parsing and used for dialogue guidance.
- View the interface call log to confirm that the `chatId`, `messages`, and `variables` parameters are correctly passed to the dialogue process.
- Simulate multi-round inquiry interactions, and check whether historical conversation information is fully retained, with no repeated asking of already confirmed business information.
