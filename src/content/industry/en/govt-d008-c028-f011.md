# Business Process Guidance for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for this category originates from four primary sources: the enterprise registration fully electronic system, the national enterprise credit information publicity system, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. Updates are performed daily.
Commonly processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, and food business license applications.
Document structures rely primarily on structured fields. These include clear business fields such as subject name, residence address, shareholder information, and license type. No extra redundant non-business fields are included.

## Constraints on forms and interactions
Multiple data sources and daily updates require form interactions to run real-time verification against the latest residence and shareholder information. This ensures entered content matches official database records.
Documents cover materials for multiple business types. Form interactions must support quick scenario switching to adapt to different processing needs, such as establishment registration and license applications.
Structured fields require forms to strictly match fixed fields from business documents. This prevents input errors that lead to subsequent application rejection.
Pre- and post-conditions vary widely across different license types. Interactions must dynamically display corresponding verification rules. This reduces the number of user correction submissions.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- When configuring `formInput`, fail to bind the standard field template from the enterprise registration system. Submissions trigger the error "entered information does not match business requirements". This occurs because only general form configurations are used, and the fixed business field format for this category is not matched.
- Fail to preset optional ranges for residence addresses via `userSelect`. This causes residence information verification to fail after user input. The root cause is failure to link to preset options from the residence information database, allowing free input that prevents verification logic from matching official data.
- When using a text content extraction node to process shareholder resolution documents, the system cannot extract shareholder name and capital contribution fields, returning empty values. This occurs because extraction rules are not configured for the document format of this category, and only general extraction templates are used.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Submit a simulated establishment registration application. Check that the form automatically verifies required fields with no missing prompts.
- Select a preset license type option. Confirm that the interface dynamically displays corresponding material requirements and verification rules.
- Upload a test shareholder resolution document. Confirm that the text extraction node correctly extracts shareholder name and capital contribution fields.
- Wait 600 seconds, then check the verification process. Confirm that no timeout error is triggered.
