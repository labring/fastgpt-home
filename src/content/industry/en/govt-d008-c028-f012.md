# Business Process Guidance for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Deployment and Upgrade

## What data for this category looks like
Daily documents for market entity registration and licensing business include establishment registration application forms, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials. Data sources are the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. The update frequency is daily. Data fields strictly match the business fields of official systems, covering entity name, residence address, shareholder information, license type, and other relevant fields. It includes both structured business data and unstructured document content, with no additional custom fields.

## What constraints these characteristics impose during deployment and upgrade
As the data sources are four official systems, deployment must comply with compliance requirements of official data interfaces, and ensure that data access is legally authorized. The daily update frequency requires incremental update tasks to be scheduled daily to ensure business data timeliness. The fixed format of daily documents requires preset chunking rules for corresponding documents during deployment, to match the fields and hierarchy of business documents. The business verification link takes a long time, so retrieval and matching logic must be optimized during deployment to adapt to field verification rules for material verification. Corresponding data storage and caching mechanisms must also be configured to ensure security and stability of business data, and avoid consequences such as registration rejection or fraudulent registration caused by abnormal data.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Configuration | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | 150–250 concurrent connections | Adapted to daily update request volume of market entity registration and licensing business, calibrated based on actual testing |
| `MONGODB_URI` | mongodb://admin:regpass@localhost:27017/market_reg?authSource=admin | Stores business documents and knowledge base data, matches structured storage requirements of official data sources |
| `REDIS_URL` | redis://:redispass@localhost:6379/1 | Caches retrieval results and incremental update tasks, ensures temporary storage of business data |
| `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` | http://127.0.0.1:9000 | Stores business document attachments such as articles of association and residence certificates, adapts to file storage requirements of business documents |
| `FE_DOMAIN` | https://xzsp.xxx.gov.cn | Configures frontend access domain name, adapts to business deployment scenarios of market supervision windows |
| `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` | 32-bit random encrypted string | Generates file access tokens to ensure secure access to business documents |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: After starting FastGPT v4.17.0, logs show `MONGODB_URI connection failed, with authentication failure error`. Cause: The username, password, and authentication source of `MONGODB_URI` are not correctly configured, causing the database to fail to connect normally.
- Symptom: Only 1 result is returned when searching for business documents, which cannot match documents required for the business. Cause: `DB_MAX_LINK` is configured too low. Concurrent connection volume is insufficient to support retrieval requests of business data, so enough business documents cannot be retrieved.
- Symptom: An error indicating file access denied is prompted when uploading business documents. Cause: `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` is configured incorrectly, or `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` is not configured with the correct storage endpoint, causing file storage to fail.

## How to confirm configuration is complete
- Run the `docker ps` command, check the running status of FastGPT-related containers, confirm all services start normally with no abnormal exits.
- Access the configured `FE_DOMAIN` address, confirm the frontend page loads normally with no resource loading failures or 404 errors.
- Upload a market entity registration-related document, confirm the file stores normally in the storage location specified by `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` with no permission errors.
- Trigger an incremental update task, check that the synchronization log has no abnormalities, confirm data is normally pulled from official data sources and synchronization completes successfully.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against respective deployment samples.
