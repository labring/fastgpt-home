# Business Process Guidance for Construction Engineering Quality and Safety Supervision: HTTP Interfaces and External Systems

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category originates from four sources: engineering quality and safety supervision systems, on-site construction real-name platforms, supervision logs, and test reports. Daily documents fall into five categories: supervision plans, supervision inspection records, hidden danger rectification notices, quality problem handling forms, and completion acceptance supervision reports. These documents are structured around the on-site construction supervision workflow, and include fields such as responsible entities, inspection content, rectification requirements, and acceptance criteria. The data is updated weekly, and covers daily business data for four types of stakeholders: housing and urban-rural development quality and safety supervision stations, construction contractors, supervision firms, and project owners.

## Constraints imposed on HTTP interfaces and external systems by these characteristics
The data sources for this category are scattered, requiring integration with multiple external systems. As a result, HTTP interfaces must support field mapping and data aggregation across multiple data sources. The data update frequency is weekly, so high-frequency calls have no practical business value. Interface call rates must align with this rhythm to avoid redundant requests. Daily document types are diverse, so interfaces must support parsing and transmission of business data in different formats. The most time-consuming steps in the business handling process are document verification and rectification tracking. Interfaces must support bulk retrieval of target documents to reduce manual operation costs. Additionally, data errors can lead to quality and safety incidents or suspension of acceptance. Interfaces must include complete validation and retry mechanisms.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Authorization` | `Bearer {API key assigned by FastGPT v4.17.0}` | Complies with FastGPT interface authentication specifications, ensures call security, and adapts to the permission isolation requirements of business data |
| `variables` | `{"supervision station ID": "example value", "target document types": ["supervision plans", "hidden danger rectification notices"]}` | Filters dedicated business data to avoid retrieving irrelevant documents, and adapts to the scenario-specific fields of construction engineering quality and safety supervision |
| `responseData` | `Return only document content, associated entity ID, and update time` | Reduces invalid data transmission and optimizes loading efficiency for conversation context |
| Request rate limit | `No more than 20 calls per 10 minutes` | Aligns with the weekly data update rhythm to avoid triggering platform rate limits |
| Retry count | `3 retries, 60-second interval` | Reduces call failure rates caused by temporary network fluctuations, and matches the serious consequences of business data errors |
| Document format adaptation | `Supports docx and pdf format parsing` | Covers common formats of daily business documents and adapts to data access from multiple sources |

## Three Common Misconfiguration Issues
- Symptom: Interface returns `401 Unauthorized`, or external business documents fail to load in conversations. Cause: The `Authorization` parameter is not configured correctly, or the API key is not bound to the dedicated construction engineering quality and safety supervision business scenario.
- Symptom: The `responseData` returned by the interface contains a large amount of non-target document content. Cause: The `variables` parameter is not used to specify the supervision station ID or target document types, resulting in retrieval of full business data.
- Symptom: Single interface calls time out, or hidden danger rectification records fail to be associated in conversations. Cause: A reasonable request rate limit is not set, high-frequency calls trigger platform rate limits, or field mapping rules for multi-system data sources are not adapted.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Call the test interface and confirm that the returned `responseData` only includes preset target business documents with no redundant content.
- Initiate an authentication test call to confirm that normal business data is returned with no authentication-related errors.
- Simulate the business handling process, pass the supervision station ID via the `variables` parameter to retrieve corresponding data, and confirm that the association relationship is correct.
- Initiate consecutive interface calls to confirm that no rate limit is triggered and returned data aligns with the weekly update rhythm requirements.
