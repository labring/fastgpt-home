# Business Process Guidance for Construction Engineering Quality and Safety Supervision: Forms and Interaction

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for this category originates from four sources: engineering quality and safety supervision systems, on-site construction site real-name management platforms, supervision logs, and test reports. The update frequency is weekly. Daily business documents include supervision plans, supervision inspection records, hidden danger rectification notices, quality problem handling forms, and completion acceptance supervision reports. Document structures cover core fields such as responsible entities, inspection items, rectification status, and acceptance verification standards. Field dimensions and filling requirements differ across documents. Adaptation is required for the entry and retrieval of multiple types of business materials.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interaction link
Multi-source data requires forms to support cross-system field pulling and integration. The `formInput` component implements field mapping to avoid repeated entry. Weekly update frequency requires the form's data source synchronization cycle to match the business rhythm, preventing the use of expired data. Multiple types of business documents require forms to support dynamic template switching, adapting to filling requirements for different business links. High-risk error consequences require forms to add required field verification and operation logging, to avoid quality and safety accidents caused by missing information. Time-consuming links such as on-site inspection materials and tracking rectification closed-loop require forms to support segmented submission and draft saving, reducing repeated operations.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: After the tool call extracts rectification information, the hidden danger rectification notice is submitted directly without triggering user confirmation. Cause: The interaction confirmation pop-up is not configured, or the pop-up trigger condition is not bound to the tool call node.
- Phenomenon: When a new message is sent in the middle of form filling, the form process is interrupted and the current filling progress cannot be restored. Cause: Draft saving and break-point continuation functions are not enabled, and message interception rules are not set to block non-form interaction process triggers.
- Phenomenon: After selecting the reorder model configuration item when creating an application, the configured reorder model does not take effect in form interaction. Cause: The call parameters of the reorder model are not correctly bound in the corresponding configuration item, or the configuration is not synchronized to the current application instance.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Switch between different business templates and verify whether form fields automatically adapt to the filling requirements of the corresponding link.
- Trigger the data source synchronization operation and verify whether the latest business data is pulled.
- Exit the page during form filling, re-enter the page and verify whether filled content is fully retained.
- Intentionally skip required fields when submitting the form and verify whether corresponding verification prompt pop-ups are triggered.
