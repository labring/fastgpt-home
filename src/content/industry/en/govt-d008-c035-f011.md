# Business Process Guidance for Pollution Discharge Permit Management: Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The business data associated with this category includes documents such as pollution discharge permit application forms, implementation reports, self-monitoring plans, ledger records, change applications, and permit copies. Data sources are the National Pollution Discharge Permit Management Information Platform, enterprise environmental protection ledgers, online monitoring systems, and environmental statistics systems. The update frequency is quarterly. Data fields cover industry technical specification matching items, permit limits, monitoring values, ledger record items, and more. These must strictly match the established formats of the ecological environment industry, with no additional custom unit requirements.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Forms and Interactions" Link
Multi-source data sources require forms to support automatic cross-system field mapping and verification, to reduce compliance errors from manual entry. The quarterly update frequency means the form's synchronization cache period must adapt to this rhythm, to avoid loading outdated permit limit or industry specification data. Multiple types of business documents correspond to differentiated field groups. Interaction modules must be split by document type, to let users focus on required items for their current processing scenario. Fields must match the ecological environment industry format. Forms must include corresponding verification rules to block non-compliant input content. At the same time, the data involves compliance and regulatory requirements. The interaction link must support full-process operation logging, to ensure data traceability.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: An error triggers when uploading business files such as pollution discharge permit application forms, while the text conversation function operates normally. Cause: File parsing rules for this category have not been configured, or the file parsing module has not adapted to the format requirements of pollution discharge permit documents.
- Phenomenon: An exception with empty fields occurs after the form is submitted, and no verification prompt is triggered. Cause: The built-in verification rules for `formInput` have not been enabled, causing inputs that do not meet industry formats to be skipped.
- Phenomenon: Form fields do not update synchronously when switching business document types. Cause: The `userSelect` trigger logic has not been configured, and the mapping relationship between document types and field groups has not been bound.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Import a sample pollution discharge permit application form. Verify that the form automatically loads the corresponding field group, and that a verification prompt triggers after permit limit values are entered.
- Switch to the change application document type. Confirm that the form fields synchronously update to the required items for change applications.
- Upload an enterprise environmental protection ledger file. Verify that data parsed from the file correctly fills into the corresponding form fields.
- Submit a test data record. Confirm that a full-process logging record is generated.
