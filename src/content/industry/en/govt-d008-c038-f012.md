# Business Process Guidance for Government Procurement Projects: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
Data sources for this category include the national government procurement website, local government procurement websites, the fiscal integration system, the procuring entity budget system, and supplier credit records.
Daily business documents cover all process nodes: procurement requirements, procurement plans, bidding documents, submission documents, bid evaluation reports, winning bid announcements, procurement contracts, and responses to queries.
Core fields include procurement entities, project progress, and document status.
Data update frequency is daily. No additional custom statistical fields are included.
All content complies with standard format requirements for government procurement business.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics During Deployment and Upgrade
Data sources are scattered and require integration with multiple official systems. Deployment requires configuration of multiple data source connection parameters to sync full business data.
The daily update frequency requires configuration of scheduled sync tasks after deployment to ensure data timeliness.
Structural differences across multiple types of business documents require the knowledge base parsing module to adapt field extraction rules for different document formats.
Additionally, errors in business processes can lead to serious consequences such as bid invalidation, valid complaints, and regulatory penalties. Deployment requires configuration of failure retry and alert mechanisms to ensure stability and compliance of business process guidance.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The model list does not update after modifying `config.json`. Cause: The FastGPT backend container was not restarted, so the new configuration was not loaded and took effect.
- Phenomenon: An error is returned when clicking the knowledge base module, with status code 500. Cause: `MONGODB_URI` or `PG_URL` was not configured correctly, resulting in a failed database connection and inability to read knowledge base data.
- Phenomenon: Business process guidance frequently times out after private deployment. Cause: `DB_MAX_LINK` was not adjusted to adapt to concurrent volume, resulting in exhausted database connections and inability to respond to high-frequency requests.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Run `docker compose logs backend` to view backend startup logs, confirm that there are no error messages for service connections configured via `MONGODB_URI` and `REDIS_URL`.
- Upload a standard procurement requirement document, verify that the knowledge base parses successfully, and that document metadata can be viewed and retrieved normally in the backend management interface.
- Manually trigger a scheduled sync task, confirm that procurement documents from the designated channels of the national government procurement website are successfully pulled and stored in the knowledge base.
- Generate a temporary file download token, verify via the front-end interface that the token can be parsed normally with no verification failure errors, confirming that the `FILE_TOKEN_KEY` configuration takes effect.
