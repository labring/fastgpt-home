# Business Process Guidance for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Deployment and Upgrade

## What this category of data looks like
The data sources for this category include enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, legal and regulatory repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. The update frequency is monthly. Daily documents include compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, legal opinions on major matters, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation reports. Most documents are structured as itemized lists or structured review reports. Fields cover core business information such as risk descriptions, review conclusions, and processing workflows. The data has relatively high business sensitivity.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | `10–20 connections` | Limits concurrent connections across multiple data sources to avoid overconsumption of enterprise-level database resources, and meets the low-concurrency stable access requirements of compliance-related business |
| `MONGODB_URI` | `mongodb://[compliance database username]:[compliance database password]@[enterprise intranet address]:[port]/compliance_db?authSource=admin` | Connects to compliance risk databases and case management systems, in line with connection specifications for internal non-public enterprise databases |
| `PG_URL` | `postgresql://[contract system username]:[contract system password]@[enterprise intranet address]:[port]/contract_db` | Connects to enterprise contract management systems, adapting to the reading requirements of relational business data |
| `REDIS_URL` | `redis://:[cache password]@[enterprise intranet address]:[port]/0`, cache validity period set based on actual testing | Caches frequently accessed legal and regulatory repository entries and compliance risk lists to reduce query pressure on core databases |
| `FE_DOMAIN` | `https://[enterprise compliance service intranet domain]` | Configures the frontend access address for business process guidance, adapting to enterprise internal domain management rules |
| `ROOT_KEY` | `Randomly generated 32-character non-sequential string` | Configures the super administrator permission key, only accessible to core management personnel of the compliance department, to prevent unauthorized modification of system configurations |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: Unable to modify the system password after deployment via Docker, with an insufficient permissions prompt displayed on the interface. Cause: The `ROOT_KEY` parameter is not configured correctly, or the system settings page is accessed without super administrator permissions.
- Symptom: After exposing a local deployment to the public internet via ngrok, iOS devices display a white screen when accessing, while macOS and Windows devices access normally. Cause: The `FE_DOMAIN` configuration does not adapt to cross-domain rules for mobile browsers, or static resource loading parameters are not configured correctly, resulting in failure to properly load frontend resources.
- Symptom: After upgrading to a new version, the original compliance data synchronization task fails to execute normally. Cause: The original connection configurations for `MONGODB_URI` and `PG_URL` were not retained during the upgrade, or the data source connection format in the new version was changed without corresponding adjustments.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Run the built-in data source connectivity test tool to confirm that the configurations for `MONGODB_URI` and `PG_URL` can normally pull compliance-related business data, and verify that the returned fields match those of daily business documents.
- Access the address configured for `FE_DOMAIN` to confirm that the frontend page loads normally, and test access from different terminal devices without abnormal errors.
- Check the connection status of `REDIS_URL` to confirm that the cache service can normally store and retrieve high-frequency business data.
- Verify the permission configuration for `ROOT_KEY` to confirm that only designated management personnel can access the system core settings page.

The values listed here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
