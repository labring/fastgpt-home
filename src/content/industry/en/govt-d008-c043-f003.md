# Business Process Guidance for Special Equipment Safety Supervision: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Terms

## What the Data for This Use Case Looks Like
Special equipment safety supervision scenario data sources include special equipment safety supervision systems, inspection and testing institution systems, user unit account books, and operator certificate databases. Data is updated on a monthly basis. Daily business documents include use registration certificates, periodic inspection reports, maintenance records, hidden danger rectification notices, accident reports, and operator certificates. Each document contains core fields such as unique device identifiers, inspection validity periods, maintenance unit information, and operator qualification numbers. Document field formats across different systems follow unified specifications. Full lifecycle business data for corresponding devices must be accurately associated during interactions.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Data from multiple system sources must be associated with unique device identifiers. Multi-turn dialogue must bind device numbers as context anchors to avoid mixing business data from different devices. The monthly update frequency requires prompts to clearly prompt for data timeliness checks, and avoid use of expired inspection or maintenance information. Standardized fields across multiple types of business documents require prompts to accurately specify fields to be extracted, reducing model extraction bias. The long duration of core business links requires multi-turn dialogue to guide step-by-step according to the registration, inspection, maintenance, and rectification processes, and avoid missing key verification nodes.

## Configuration Settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `messages` retention count | First 6 messages | The special equipment supervision business process includes multiple core links: device registration, inspection, maintenance, and rectification. 6 messages can cover complete interaction nodes |
| Context window length | 800–1200 characters | Single daily business document content mostly falls within this range, avoiding context overflow that affects model understanding |
| `variables` binding configuration | Bind device number and inspection validity period fields | Data sources include use registration certificates and periodic inspection reports. Accurate association of business data for corresponding devices is required |
| `chatId` anchor setting | Associate via unique device identifier | Ensure multi-turn interaction contexts for the same device are not confused, and avoid cross-device data confusion |
| Single call timeout | 120 seconds | Avoid unresponsive situations during multi-turn dialogue, and meet the interaction timeliness requirements of supervision business |
| Format verification rule | Enable business field matching | Errors in supervision business have serious consequences. Standard field structures of business documents must be forcibly matched |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Text extraction nodes return empty fields. This occurs because corresponding business document variables are not bound via `variables`, and core fields such as device numbers and inspection validity periods are not explicitly required in prompts.
- No response from knowledge base search during multi-turn dialogue. This occurs because the context window is configured too long, causing model processing timeout, or the upper limit of knowledge base calls per dialogue is not restricted.
- Generated rectification replies do not meet business requirements. This occurs because mandatory format verification is not configured, and standard field structures of hidden danger rectification notices are not explicitly required in prompts.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a simulated dialogue, input a device number, and verify that conversation history automatically associates registration and inspection data for the corresponding device.
- Trigger a text extraction node, and check whether returned fields include core business fields such as device numbers and inspection validity periods.
- Input rectification content that does not meet format requirements, and verify that the model prompts for format adjustments, or returns content matching the business document structure.
- Test six consecutive rounds of dialogue, and verify that the context correctly retains previous interaction device information and business nodes.
