# Business Process Guidance for Administrative Penalty Case Handling: Workflow Orchestration

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data comes from administrative law enforcement case handling systems, law enforcement recorder footage, the national enterprise credit information publicity system, and departmental supervision databases. The update frequency is daily. Commonly involved documents include on-site inspection records, interrogation records, evidence lists, administrative penalty prior notice letters, hearing notice letters, penalty decisions, and service receipts. Each document corresponds to fixed law enforcement links and field requirements, and must be used in association according to the case handling process, with no extra non-case-related data mixed in.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "workflow orchestration" link
Multi-source data access requires the workflow to be configured with multiple nodes to connect to different data sources separately, to avoid mixing different types of data. Daily updated data sources require a scheduled synchronization node to ensure the timeliness of case file data. Differences in fields and rules for different law enforcement documents require the workflow to bind exclusive `variables` according to document types, to prevent missing fields or rule conflicts. The fixed link order of the case handling process requires workflow nodes to be triggered in accordance with law enforcement logic in sequence, and the order cannot be adjusted arbitrarily.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Workflow dialogue display fails, but response logs exist in the model backend. The cause is that `variables` for required fields of law enforcement documents are not configured, resulting in missing parameters during node execution.
- Prompt words do not take effect in the expected order. The cause is that workflow nodes are not sorted according to law enforcement procedures, and the output of the preceding node is not correctly passed to the link bound by the subsequent `nodeId`.
- The question optimization component is placed incorrectly. The cause is that the component is placed after the legal clause matching node, which cannot standardize problem expressions in law enforcement scenarios in advance, leading to subsequent matching deviations.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Check whether `variables` cover all required fields of law enforcement documents, with no missing items.
- Verify that the law enforcement links bound by `nodeId` are completely consistent with the actual case handling process, with no reversed order or missing links.
- Simulate input in a law enforcement scenario, and confirm that the node trigger sequence conforms to case handling logic, with no abnormal interruptions.
- Test the timeout setting, and confirm that the preset failure handling process is triggered when the 600-second threshold is exceeded.
