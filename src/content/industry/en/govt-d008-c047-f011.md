# Business Process Guidance for Administrative Penalty Case Handling: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data comes from administrative law enforcement case handling systems, law enforcement recorder footage, the national enterprise credit information publicity system, and departmental supervision databases, and is updated daily. Core documents include on-site inspection records, questioning records, evidence lists, administrative penalty prior notice letters, hearing notice letters, penalty decisions, delivery receipts, and similar materials. Its structure centers on standardized core fields such as illegal fact determination, penalty basis, discretion application, and document delivery. There are no custom special units, and all fields comply with unified norms for law enforcement procedures.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Scattered data sources covering multiple systems require form interactions to support cross-source data pulling and real-time verification. Fixed statutory document structures require forms to be displayed in stages according to law enforcement procedures to avoid field confusion. Daily updated supervision data requires synchronous verification, requiring forms to support real-time data validation to prevent use of expired information. A traceable penalty evidence chain must be established, requiring forms to record the time and operating entity of each step to ensure process compliance and traceability.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: After submitting the administrative penalty case handling form, the system prompts "missing required fields" and cannot proceed to the next link. Cause: Mandatory fields such as illegal subject and illegal time are not set as required fields, or field format validity is not verified.
- Phenomenon: After uploading an MP4 file recorded by a law enforcement recorder, the attachment field displays as empty, or a "format not supported" error pops up. Cause: The attachment format whitelist is not configured, and common recording formats for law enforcement recorders such as MP4 and AVI are not allowed.
- Phenomenon: In the knowledge base search link, it is not possible to select only document folders corresponding to the law enforcement category, and returned results include non-law enforcement content. Cause: Knowledge base directories are not divided by law enforcement agency classification, and folder permission binding rules are not configured.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Open the administrative penalty case handling form page, check whether statutory document fields are displayed in stages according to the four links of inspection, interrogation, penalty, and archiving.
- Upload an MP4 format law enforcement recorder footage file, confirm that the upload function works normally and does not exceed the 100 MB single file limit.
- Use the `formInput` component to enter illegal subject information, verify that it can real-time link to public data from the national enterprise credit information publicity system.
- Select a law enforcement personnel account, confirm that only on-duty personnel accounts of the current law enforcement agency can be selected.
