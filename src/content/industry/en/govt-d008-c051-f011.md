# Business Process Guidance for Road Transport Business Licensing: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category’s Data Includes
Data for this category originates from the road transport administration system, the national public service platform for dynamic information of road transport vehicles, and the vehicle and practitioner database. The update frequency is daily. Associated daily documents include business license application materials, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, work safety management systems, and road transport business licenses. Data fields cover business entity qualifications, vehicle technical parameters, practitioner qualifications, site and safety system compliance, and other dimensions. No custom special units are configured, and all fields follow the unified standard format of the transport industry.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on the Forms and Interactions Link
Official system interfaces are required for accessing this data. Integrate with existing transport administration databases for real-time verification in the interaction link, to prevent users from re-entering already recorded information. Pull the latest qualification status in form interactions to align with the daily updated data source, ensuring application materials match existing archived data. Support uploading different material types in modules to meet the requirement of submitting multiple associated documents, and automatically verify field completeness for each document. Add associated verification in the interaction link to enforce the strong binding requirement between practitioner and vehicle data, preventing submission of personnel or vehicle materials not belonging to the enterprise. Strengthen pre-verification logic in the interaction link, as missing pre-verification will directly lead to rejection of the licensing application.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` | Enable long text segment verification, with a segment threshold of 800–1200 characters | Road transport license application forms include multiple long text sections; segment verification reduces single verification load |
| `userSelect` | Display preset options in pages, up to 15 items per page | There are many options for business scope, vehicle type, and similar categories; page display aligns with user browsing habits |
| Input guidance configuration | Enable in stages according to business process, only display fields for the corresponding stage in each stage | License applications are divided into stages including material submission, qualification verification, and audit follow-up; staged guidance reduces filling errors |
| Lexicon configuration | Load the transport industry-specific lexicon, synchronize updates daily | The data source is updated daily; the lexicon must match the latest licensing type and vehicle classification terminology |
| Voice input timeout | Set to 12 seconds for automatic termination | Avoids invalid content from overly long voice input, adapts to normal speaking speed for form filling |
| Interaction node trigger | Trigger verification at material upload nodes | Different types of licensing materials require verification at corresponding stages, aligning with business process logic |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Voice input on the non-logged-in page shows 00:00 duration and content fails to send. Cause: Voice input timeout configuration does not match the business scenario, and no valid duration threshold is set, causing the session to terminate abnormally.
- After enabling input guidance and configuring the industry lexicon, the expected guidance content does not appear in the conversation interface. Cause: The lexicon is not synchronized at the daily update frequency, or the input guidance is not bound to the field rules of the corresponding business stage.
- Interaction nodes do not trigger the verification process as expected. Cause: The interaction node configuration is not associated with the corresponding material upload stage, and the trigger logic does not match the business process steps.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Fill out a test road transport license application form, verify that required fields trigger verification after completion, and check that verification prompts match business field requirements.
- Call the voice input function, test that the duration threshold meets expectations, and confirm that input content can be displayed and submitted normally.
- View the lexicon configuration log, confirm that the industry-specific lexicon has been synchronized and updated daily, and that the conversation interface can recognize standard terminology.
- Trigger different material upload nodes, confirm that interactive verification starts at the corresponding stage, and that the verification logic aligns with business requirements.
