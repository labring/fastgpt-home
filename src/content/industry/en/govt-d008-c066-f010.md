# Business Process Guidance for Advanced Chemical Materials: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
Advanced chemical materials business data primarily comes from internal enterprise process manuals, advanced material grade parameter databases, compliance approval documents, and upstream and downstream collaboration process files. Updates occur on no fixed schedule, triggered by new grade project initiation, process adjustments, or compliance standard updates. Individual documents have clear structures, including fields such as grade name, physical performance parameters, production process steps, and approval requirements. Units include professional chemical measurement standards such as MPa, g/cm³, tons, and hours. Some documents exceed 5000 characters.

## Constraints on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The multi-field attribute of advanced chemical materials data requires retrieval to precisely match parameters and process keywords, to avoid mixing in irrelevant content. The no-fixed-update-cycle characteristic requires the retrieval system to support dynamic synchronization of the latest documents, otherwise recall content will lag. The combination of long documents and professional measurement units requires reasonable control of chunk length and recall count, to avoid context overload or missing professional information. The business process guidance scenario also requires retrieval results to link upstream and downstream steps, rather than only returning isolated parameter content.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75-0.85 | Advanced chemical material parameters have high precision requirements; this range filters low-similarity irrelevant content while retaining core matching results |
| `limit` | Top 6 entries | Single advanced chemical material-related documents have multiple chunks; limiting recall count avoids exceeding the large model context window while covering core process and parameter information |
| `searchMode` | Semantic retrieval + keyword hybrid mode | Balances the semantic understanding needs of process guidance and the precise keyword matching needs of parameter queries |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Semantic drift often occurs after long document chunking; re-ranking sorts chunks a second time to improve result relevance |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Business process guidance requires expanding question boundaries, for example, automatically associating "application material list" with "grade application" and other extended questions |
| `collectionIds` | Bind exclusive knowledge base collection for advanced chemical materials | Isolates data from different categories to avoid recalling irrelevant content from general chemical or other industry datasets |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base content remains unchanged for long periods, and newly released advanced material grades are not recalled. Cause: Static page auto-sync trigger rules are not configured, documents are only imported manually, and the no-fixed-update-cycle characteristic of advanced chemical materials is not accommodated.
- Phenomenon: Initial questions work normally, but answers become irrelevant after consecutive questions. Cause: Context association configuration is not enabled, or the `similarity` threshold is set too low, causing subsequent questions to recall irrelevant historical chunk data.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results include a large number of irrelevant general chemical documents, and do not focus on advanced material categories. Cause: Exclusive `collectionIds` are not bound, causing cross-category data to mix into recall results, interfering with the accuracy of business process guidance.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a new advanced material grade document, trigger the auto-sync task, and check if the corresponding chunk content is added to the knowledge base collection in FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Launch two consecutive related questions, for example, first ask "PETG production approval process", then ask "Materials required for approval", and check if the two answers are contextually linked and accurate.
- Adjust the `similarity` threshold to 0.8, launch a fuzzy matching question such as "High-strength engineering plastic grades", and check if recall results only include relevant advanced material parameter content.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, compare the recall result ranking before and after enabling, and confirm that highly relevant process steps or parameter chunks are displayed first.
