# Business Process Guidance for Pharmaceutical Distribution: Model Integration and Configuration

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Data comes primarily from the business systems of pharmaceutical distribution enterprises. It includes purchase orders, sales delivery notes, acceptance inspection receipts, drug qualification documents, and more. Updates are triggered in real time during business processing, and synced after each transaction is completed. Documents fall into two categories: structured forms and unstructured attachments. Structured fields include generic drug name, specification, manufacturer, batch number, transaction quantity, unit (box, vial, tablet), handler, transaction time, and other relevant fields. Unstructured attachments are mostly drug quality inspection reports and GSP compliance documents, with significant variation in length.

## Constraints on Model Integration and Configuration
The coexistence of structured forms and unstructured attachments requires models to support both field recognition and long-text processing. Real-time updated business data requires model retrieval and context loading to match the rhythm of business processing. Fields include specific units (box, vial, tablet), so the guidance content generated by the model must strictly adhere to these unit specifications. Long-form compliance documents can easily exceed the model’s context length limit, resulting in errors.

## How to Set Configuration
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Matches the average content length of pharmaceutical distribution business documents and attachments, to avoid truncation of core approval and process information |
| `maxTokens` | 2000–3000 token | Adapts to the response length required for business process guidance, ensuring complete step instructions and compliance notes are included |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500 characters | Matches the single retrieval content length of pharmaceutical distribution business documents, to avoid exceeding the model’s context limit |
| `temperature` | 0.3–0.5 | Ensures accuracy of process guidance, reducing generation of non-standard business steps |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Allows the model to automatically decide whether to call tools for document verification and qualification checking based on the business scenario |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in according to deployment environment | Adapts to local or cloud-deployed model service addresses, ensuring data transmission complies with industry compliance requirements for pharmaceutical distribution |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Returns no response when more than 60 concurrent requests are initiated from a shared page. Local model server CPU and memory usage do not reach the upper limit. Cause: Did not adjust the `maxContext` parameter to adapt to context usage from concurrent requests for the high-frequency document retrieval scenario of pharmaceutical distribution business, leading to resource exhaustion.
- Symptom: Returns a 400 error when uploading a drug quality inspection report that exceeds the context length limit. Cause: Did not add a file content preprocessing logic to the workflow, and the directly uploaded content exceeds the model’s supported context range.
- Symptom: The model cannot read the content of an uploaded drug purchase order, and the corresponding document content exists within the `<FilesContent></FilesContent>` tags. Cause: Confused the entry points for model variables and model service configuration, and did not complete parameter settings on the corresponding model service page of FastGPT v4.17.0.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Initiate a simulated pharmaceutical business processing request, and check whether the response content includes the standard process steps and field verification rules for the corresponding business.
- Upload pharmaceutical business documents of different lengths, verify whether context truncation or abnormal errors are triggered, and adjust parameters to meet business scenario requirements.
- Initiate multiple concurrent requests, monitor service response status, and confirm there are no abnormal or unresponsive situations.
- View model operation logs, confirm that the document content within the `<FilesContent>` tags is correctly loaded and used to generate responses.
- Verify that parameters such as `maxTokens` and `temperature` take effect correctly on the model service configuration page.
