# Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation Business Process Guidance: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Business process data for hazardous chemicals storage and transportation comes primarily from enterprise storage and transportation ledgers, hazardous chemicals business approval and filing documents, transportation route approval forms, warehouse inspection records, and emergency response manuals.
Data update frequency varies by business node: immediate archiving after a single approval is completed, weekly summary updates for daily inspection records, and daily synchronization for ledger data.
Documents fall into two categories: single business documents and batch reports.
Single documents include fields such as hazardous chemical name, UN number, storage location, transportation route, and approval time limit.
Batch reports include content such as storage volume, inspection temperature, and compliance check results.
Field units include tons, degrees Celsius, cubic meters, days, and others.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector indexing link
The characteristics of hazardous chemicals storage and transportation business data impose three core constraints on the vector indexing link.
First, single business documents contain strongly unique identifier fields such as UN number and storage location. Retain semantic integrity of fields during chunking, and avoid splitting associated information across fields.
Second, documents exist in two forms: short single documents and long batch reports. Adapt different chunking boundary rules to ensure accurate matching of business process scenarios during retrieval.
Third, data includes numerical fields with units and time-sensitive content. Indexing must retain the binding relationship between fields and units to avoid semantic fragmentation that affects retrieval accuracy.
Some data is updated immediately after business nodes. Support fast synchronization configuration for incremental indexing.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the average length of hazardous chemicals storage and transportation documents and reports, avoids splitting cross-field associated information |
| Overlap Length | 50–80 characters | Retains semantic connection between chunks, prevents key information such as UN number and storage location from being split into different chunks |
| `maxToken` | 8192 token | Adapts to the maximum context length of FastGPT v4.17.0 and Qwen3-Embedding-8B models, ensures long documents can be fully encoded |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85 | Matches the strong semantic features of hazardous chemicals business data, filters low-relevance retrieval results |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Balances retrieval efficiency and recall completeness, covers reference needs for common business processes |
| `defaultToken` | 1024 seconds | Configures the timeout period for indexing tasks, adapts to the encoding duration of batch reports |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The file status remains "Indexing" in the interface with no progress updates. The cause is incorrect configuration of the `maxToken` parameter. Long batch reports exceed the model context limit, causing the encoding task to fail to complete.
- Adding a new index model configuration with the same name overwrites the existing configuration. The cause is failure to assign independent identifier names for different models on the platform, using only the full model name as the unique identifier.
- Irrelevant content with separated UN number and storage location appears in retrieval results. The cause is excessively small chunk length settings, splitting business document chunks containing strongly associated fields and destroying semantic integrity.
