# Business Process Guidance for Power Generation Groups: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Templates

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Business process data for power generation groups is primarily sourced from internal business manuals, work order circulation systems, and public specifications released by power regulatory authorities. The data is split into major business categories such as grid connection applications, electricity bill calculation, and maintenance filings. Each document includes content such as business name, processing process nodes, required qualification documents, legal processing time limits, and consultation channels.

Fields include unique business ID, link serial number, number of materials, processing time limit (working days), and other items. The data is updated once every quarter to adapt to regular adjustments of business specifications.

## Constraints for Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Business data is split by category and includes multiple sequential steps. Multi-turn dialogue must proceed in the order of process nodes, rather than returning full process information directly. Direct full returns may confuse users about node order.

Data is updated on a quarterly basis, so prompt templates must be synchronized every quarter to match the latest business specifications.

The legal processing time limit (working days) field mandates that the time limit returned in dialogue responses strictly matches the value in the business documents. No arbitrary adjustments are permitted.

Each business link corresponds to a dedicated material list. Prompts must dynamically extract the matching materials based on the current dialogue link to avoid redundant information interfering with user judgment.

## Configuration Settings
These configuration items are based on the logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Maximum Context Length | 8000–10000 characters | Single business documents for power generation groups are mostly under 5000 characters. This range fully covers multi-turn dialogue history interactions and current business information |
| Question Optimization Switch | Enabled | Business terms for power generation groups are highly professional. Optimization converts colloquial user questions into standardized business expressions to improve matching accuracy |
| Single Return Content Limit | Top 3 link information | Business processing procedures have multiple nodes. Returning too much content at once causes information redundancy, so prioritize displaying core content for the current link |
| Variable Filling Rule | Use `variables` parameter to bind business-specific fields | Dynamically insert exclusive data such as business ID and processing time limit, avoiding hard-coded content becoming outdated as business updates |
| `chatId` Binding Rule | Bind user business application number | Power generation group business processing requires association with a unique business ID to avoid confusion between dialogue contexts of different applications |
| Timeout Threshold | 600 seconds | Business consultations may involve multi-link queries, so reserve sufficient time to complete complete interactions |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After calling the dialogue API with streaming output, clicking links in the returned content only overwrites the current page and does not jump. Cause: No jump trigger rule for dialogue links is configured, and the default streaming output content links are not bound to the new window opening logic.
- Phenomenon: The dialogue log details do not match the actual answer content, and fixed content is still displayed after multiple dialogues. Cause: The context update of the `messages` parameter is not handled correctly, and the latest historical dialogue fragments are not carried in each request, causing the model to reuse old answer caches.
- Phenomenon: Skipping business processing links and returning final results directly in multi-turn dialogue. Cause: The prompt does not clearly require guiding according to the process node order in the document, and does not restrict dialogue from advancing strictly by link.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a multi-turn dialogue containing business link details, and check whether the returned processing time limit matches the internal business documents.
- Call the API with the `chatId` parameter to initiate two different business consultations, and confirm that the contexts of the two dialogues do not interfere with each other.
- Enable streaming output for testing, click links in the returned content, and confirm that the jump behavior meets expectations.
- View the dialogue log, and confirm that the `messages` parameter of each interaction contains the latest historical dialogue content.
