# Business Process Guidance for Power Generation Groups: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Business processing data for power generation groups comes from internal ERP systems, work order ledgers of affiliated power plants, and offline business archive files. Data updates follow a set rhythm: core business fields are synced daily, and full archive historical data is updated weekly. The document structure of a single business data entry includes business ID, applicant entity, application type, submission time, required materials list, current processing stage, and contact information of the liaison.
Field formats have clear specifications: business ID is a pure numeric string, submission time uses standard timestamp format, liaison contact uses area code-prefixed mobile phone number format, and required materials list is multi-paragraph text.

## Constraints for Forms and Interactions
Data synced across multiple internal systems has inconsistent field formats. Forms must support custom field mapping rules to adapt to business data from different sources.
Daily updated business material lists require forms to pull the latest version when loaded, to avoid displaying canceled material options.
Fields with fixed formats such as business ID and mobile phone number need built-in validation logic to block non-compliant inputs.
Multiple business types have dynamic field requirements. Forms must support switching configuration items based on application type, to avoid redundant or missing fields from fixed setups.
Power generation group business involves internal permission control. Forms must connect to the role permission system to restrict visible fields and allowed operations for different positions.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Power generation group business materials (such as engineering approval forms, electricity application forms) uploaded via the form cannot be parsed normally, and the interface displays "unsupported document format". Cause: Professional document format parsing rules commonly used by power generation groups are not configured, only general office document formats are supported.
- Phenomenon: The form returns "field format error" after submission, but the input content complies with business specifications. Cause: The built-in input validation regular expression does not adapt to the long string format of power generation group business IDs, blocking legitimate inputs.
- Phenomenon: The form associated with the business knowledge base only returns a single matching result. Cause: The default recall logic only selects the single result with the highest similarity, and is not adjusted to support multi-source reference information required for business processing.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Submit a test data entry that complies with power generation group business specifications. Confirm that validation rules do not block legitimate inputs to verify the validation configuration is effective.
- Switch between different business application types. Confirm that the loaded material fields match the corresponding type to verify the dynamic configuration is effective.
- Upload professional documents commonly used by power generation groups. Confirm that they can be parsed normally and content can be extracted to verify the format rule configuration is effective.
- After submitting the form, check the recall results of the associated knowledge base. Confirm that the number of returned results meets the actual needs of business processing, then adjust the recall configuration of `userSelect` to an appropriate range.
