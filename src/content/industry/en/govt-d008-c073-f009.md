# Business Process Guidance for State-owned Industrial Funds: Model Access and Configuration

## Data Characteristics for This Category
Data related to business process guidance for state-owned industrial funds comes from sources including fund registration forms, project declaration materials, approval circulation records, capital allocation vouchers, policy compliance documents, and more. Updates are triggered by business nodes, and take effect immediately after project initiation, approval completion, capital allocation, and other steps are finished. Document structures include both structured fields and unstructured text. Structured fields include fund subject code, declared project number, approval level, capital amount (unit: 100 million yuan), and others. Unstructured content includes due diligence reports, process description documents, and more, with single-document lengths varying widely.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Model Access and Configuration
The data characteristics of state-owned industrial funds impose multiple constraints on model access and configuration. First, the mixed format of structured and unstructured data requires that model access adapts to parsing rules for multiple document types, to avoid missing key information in structured fields. Second, data updates have no fixed cycle, so a knowledge base update mechanism that supports incremental synchronization must be configured, to ensure the latest information about business processes is included in a timely manner. Third, fields have dedicated units and coding rules. When the model is called, the original format of the fields must be retained, to prevent unit confusion or information distortion. Fourth, business process documents have many nodes and complex logic. The context and reference length must be precisely controlled, to ensure the model can fully call process-related information.

## Configuration Settings

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Semantic retrieval recalls multiple relevant pieces of content, but the model returns no relevant information found. Cause: The `maxContext` setting is too small, and the recalled reference content is truncated so that it cannot be fully parsed by the model.
- Symptom: The model response takes too long, exceeding the real-time waiting threshold for business handling. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` setting is too high, resulting in too much reference content passed in a single time, exceeding the processing limit of the large model.
- Symptom: The latest business process is not updated after the knowledge base is synchronized. Cause: The structured field format of state-owned industrial funds is not adapted, and updated coding and amount information cannot be correctly identified during incremental synchronization.
