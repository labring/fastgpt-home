# Business Process Guidance for State-owned Asset Management Companies: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
The business data of state-owned asset management companies comes from stock asset filing data from the state-owned asset supervision system, internal business approval ledgers, property right registration archives, and asset disposal application forms. The update rhythm is real-time synchronization for major asset changes, and routine business ledgers are updated monthly. The document structure includes structured forms and unstructured approval instructions. Structured fields include asset number, ownership subject, approval node, and processing time limit, with units mostly being ten thousand yuan, unit, and working day.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
The coexistence of structured fields and unstructured documents requires multi-turn dialogue to guide users to specify asset numbers to narrow the search scope and avoid returning irrelevant data. Real-time updated major asset change data requires embedding data timeliness verification logic in prompts to ensure that the latest filing information is called. Hierarchical differences in approval nodes require multi-turn dialogue to filter approval process information beyond the permissions of the conversation initiator based on their role permissions, avoiding returning content that the user does not have permission to view. Monthly updated routine ledgers require specifying the data update cycle in prompts to prevent users from obtaining outdated business processing time limit information.

## How to configure the settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Context | 8000–12000 token | Adapts to the number of fields and approval process length of state-owned asset management company business documents, avoiding context overflow |
| `chatId` parameter | Bind unique session identifier | Enables context-aware management of multi-turn dialogue, ensuring consistent business guidance logic within the same session |
| `messages` parameter | Retain the most recent 10 rounds of conversation records | Avoids overly long context interfering with the guidance logic of the current business processing flow |
| `variables` parameter | Bind asset number and handler role variables | Enables dynamic calling of business data corresponding to permissions during multi-turn dialogue |
| Conversation citation toggle | Disable unnecessary citations | Matches the need for concise display of process information in business processing scenarios |
| Conversation opening quick buttons | Preset 3 high-frequency questions (such as "Asset disposal process", "Property right registration requirements", "Approval node query") | Simplifies the operation steps for users to initiate business consultations |

## Three common configuration errors
- The conversation returns the error "No permission to operate this conversation record". This occurs when the handler role variable is not bound in `variables`, causing the prompt to fail to filter approval process information that exceeds the user's permissions.
- The knowledge base answer automatically appends citation content at the end. This happens when the conversation citation toggle is not disabled, and full document citation display is enabled by default.
- The conversation quick buttons do not trigger the corresponding preset questions. This occurs when the preset questions for the quick buttons are not bound to high-frequency business processing scenarios, or the configured trigger logic is not associated with the context update of the `messages` parameter.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Initiate a test conversation that includes an asset number, check whether the returned approval process information matches the permission scope of the corresponding asset.
- Review the end of the answer content, confirm that unauthorized citation content does not appear.
- Click the quick buttons for the conversation opening, check whether the corresponding preset question is automatically sent and relevant process guidance is returned.
- Initiate a multi-turn conversation, check whether the most recent 10 rounds of records are retained and no logical confusion occurs.
