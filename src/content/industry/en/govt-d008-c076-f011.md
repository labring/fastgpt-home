# Business Process Guidance for State-owned Capital Operation Companies: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
Business data for state-owned capital operation companies originates from internal business systems including property rights management, equity operations, salary distribution, and synchronized data from state-owned assets supervision platforms. Update frequency varies by business type: real-time business data such as property rights transfer and capital increase and share expansion is updated in real time alongside operations. Periodic business data such as monthly salary and quarterly assessment is updated at the end of each month or quarter. Documents primarily use structured forms, including fields such as the enterprise unified social credit code, shareholding ratio, transaction amount (unit: ten thousand yuan), approval node ID, and others. Some businesses involve classified data, so data access permissions must be divided by job position.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
The large number of structured fields with clear format requirements means forms must be configured with field-level validation rules to block non-compliant input. The presence of classified data requires forms to include job-level permission controls, so different roles can only view fields within their authorized scope. The difference in update rhythms across businesses means form caching and data pulling strategies must match the update frequency of the corresponding business. Forms for multi-node approval business must be displayed step by step, to avoid excessive field loading at one time that increases operational complexity.

## Configuration settings
Configuration settings are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` validation configuration | Perform rule-based validation by field type: validate 18-digit format for unified social credit code, validate positive floating-point numbers for transaction amount | Match the structured field format requirements of business data |
| `userSelect` option configuration | Pull options from internal business interfaces; cache real-time business data for 1 hour, cache periodic business data for 24 hours | Adapt to the data update rhythm of different business types |
| Permission control configuration | Divide visible field scope by job position; classified fields are only accessible to state-owned assets supervision positions | Adapt to the classified nature of business data and job permission requirements |
| Form draft caching | Automatically clear drafts if not submitted for 30 minutes | Avoid expired data submissions, match the operational cycles of most businesses |
| Step-by-step interaction configuration | Split forms by approval node, only display required fields for the current node in each step | Simplify the complexity of form filling for multi-node business |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: The system does not display an error prompt after submitting a field value that does not meet format requirements or leaving a required field blank. Cause: Field-level validation rules for `formInput` are not configured, and input content is not verified to match the format of business fields.
- Phenomenon: Drop-down options in the form are not updated for a long time, and do not match the latest data from internal business systems. Cause: The cache duration setting does not match the update rhythm of business data, such as configuring a 1-hour cache for monthly updated business.
- Phenomenon: Job roles without permission can view classified business fields. Cause: The visible scope of form fields is not divided by job position, and the permission control configuration does not take effect.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Submit a field value that does not meet format requirements, check if the system displays a corresponding format error prompt.
- Modify test data in the internal business system, refresh the form page, check if the drop-down options are updated synchronously.
- Switch to a test role without permission, check if classified fields are hidden or cannot be edited.
- Stay on the form page for more than 30 minutes, check if unsaved drafts are automatically cleared.
