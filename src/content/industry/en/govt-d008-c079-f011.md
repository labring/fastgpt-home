# Urban Gas Service Application Process Guidance: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
Urban gas service application data mainly comes from business management systems of local gas operating enterprises. It covers all types of business forms including account opening, ownership transfer, installation application, payment change, gas supply suspension and resumption application. Data updates in real time according to individual business application nodes. The data uses structured form format, and includes fields such as user identity identifier, gas usage address, gas usage nature, application type, and processing progress. Field units typically include standard administrative division addresses, 18-digit ID card numbers, cubic meters, and working days, with no large volumes of unstructured redundant content.

## Constraints for forms and interactions
Structured business fields require form interactions to strictly match preset field formats, preventing system verification failures from non-standard inputs. Real-time updated business data means the interaction link must sync the latest service scope and processing rules, to ensure user-entered addresses and application types align with current valid requirements. Fixed business processes require forms to display step-by-step by processing node, preventing users from entering content for later steps early. The system must also verify that input content matches the current processing node. Specific unit and field rules trigger automatic format verification during interaction, including ID card number length checks and reasonable range validation for gas usage scale.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The symptom is that users can initiate business applications without filling all required form fields. The cause is that the required field verification rule for `formInput` is not configured, and interaction is not restricted to trigger only after the form is completed.
- The symptom is that extracted question-answer pair results do not match actual business fields. The cause is that splitting logic is not configured according to the structured data order of gas business, leading to reversed processing order of guidance prompts.
- The symptom is that model return results do not include the configured thinking process. The cause is that the trigger logic between the prompt and model output configuration is not aligned, and the requirement to generate thinking steps is not clearly stated in the prompt.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Fill in some required form fields, try to initiate interaction, confirm that the system intercepts the incomplete submission request, and the verification rules are configured according to business fields.
- Select a non-preset business type option, confirm that the system only opens preset business options, with no abnormal free input channels.
- After configuring the model thinking output switch, initiate a test, confirm that the returned result includes preset thinking steps, matching the configuration requirements.
- Wait more than 600 seconds before filling out the form again, confirm that the system does not automatically interrupt the interaction process due to timeout, and the duration configuration meets business needs.
