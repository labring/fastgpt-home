# Business Process Guidance for Urban Investment Platforms: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
Business process data for urban investment platforms is primarily sourced from official government service item lists, offline window service guides, and approval process node logs. Data is updated synchronously when policies are adjusted, and maintained quarterly during regular cycles. Individual business documents are split by service item, and include fields such as application requirements, material lists, process steps, processing time limits, and contact information for inquiries. Material lists note required quantities and format specifications. Process steps are listed in numerical order. Processing time limits are measured in working days.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
Urban investment platform business data is structured and split by individual service item. Multi-turn dialogue must bind a unique session identifier to prevent mixing context across different business items. Data fields include specific material quantities and format requirements. Multi-turn dialogue must confirm each corresponding field individually, and cannot simplify prompt content. Data updates rely on policy adjustments. Prompt engineering must regularly match the latest service item parameters to ensure accurate guidance content. Business process steps are numerous. Multi-turn dialogue must retain key information confirmed in earlier stages to avoid repeating questions about content the user already provided.

## Configuration Settings
These configurations apply to FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind to the specific service item number the user is handling | Urban investment platform business is split independently by service item. `chatId` must be used to distinguish context across different sessions |
| Context window | Retain the first 10 turns of dialogue or 8000 characters | Urban investment business has numerous process steps. Key information such as confirmed materials and application requirements must be retained from earlier stages |
| `variables` | Preload fixed fields for the current service item (such as service location, inquiry phone number) | Reduce repeated questions, and match the fixed parameter requirements of urban investment service guides |
| `messages` | Filter historical dialogue that does not belong to the current service item | Prevent mixing context with information from different business items, and improve the accuracy of dialogue guidance |
| Timeout setting | Automatically end the session after 600 seconds of no interaction | Urban investment business consulting typically focuses on a single service item. Excessively long context will interfere with current dialogue guidance |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Observation: Custom output generated via HTTP orchestration does not appear in dialogue results. Cause: No output mapping rule for this field was configured in `variables`, so the dialogue chain does not return custom content synchronously.
- Observation: Response delay occurs after multi-turn dialogue exceeds 15 turns. Cause: Context length for `messages` was not limited, so historical dialogue occupies excessive computing resources and exceeds the interface response threshold.
- Observation: The system mixes prior service requirements when the user consults different business items. Cause: No unique `chatId` was bound, so context from different sessions interferes with each other.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Initiate a consultation for a specific urban investment business, such as water supply installation. Confirm the system prompts for required material lists, service location, and other information in sequence.
- Switch to another business item, such as gas account opening. Confirm the system does not reuse prompt content from the earlier water supply installation consultation.
- Wait 600 seconds with no interaction, then initiate a new consultation. Confirm the session has reset and does not retain prior dialogue content.
- Review session logs. Confirm each dialogue is bound to a unique `chatId` identifier, with no residual context from cross-session interactions.
