# Business Process Guidance for Urban Investment Platforms: Tool Calling and Plugins

## Data characteristics of this category
Business processing data for urban investment platforms is primarily sourced from internal government service item databases, offline processing ledgers, and online reservation systems. It is updated synchronously with government service item update cycles and completed business batches.
Each individual business document includes fields such as business category, handling conditions, required material list, processing flow nodes, and consultation channels. Field units are mostly "item", "copy", and "working day". Document lengths fall mostly within the 300-1200 character range, with clear structure and no multi-layer nesting.

## Constraints on tool calling and plugins
Characteristics of urban investment platform business data impose multiple constraints on the tool calling and plugins workflow.
Data sources are scattered across multiple systems, so multiple plugins are required to connect to government service item databases, offline ledgers, and online reservation ports. This increases the complexity of plugin linkage configuration.
Field names and units are fixed. Tool calling must strictly match preset fields, otherwise data extraction exceptions will occur.
Document lengths are moderate and structure is clear, so no excessive splitting is needed. Dynamic field mapping must be configured during tool calling to adapt to field differences across different businesses.
Government data updates daily, so plugin call frequency should not be too high to avoid triggering interface rate limiting rules.

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: Call logs show data acquisition exception, returning `error` or status code 500. Cause: Preset fields are not bound by business type, and the data pulled by the plugin does not match the field requirements of tool calling.
- Phenomenon: Text extraction tool returns `400 Invalid JSON payload received. Unknown name` error. Cause: Extra parameters not included in the preset field list are passed during plugin calling, causing JSON format verification failure.
- Phenomenon: Call timeout or interface error occurs after upgrading the PgVector plugin. Cause: The plugin call frequency is not adjusted to adapt to the new interface response rhythm, leading to frequent rate limiting triggering and timeout.

## How to confirm correct configuration
- Initiate a process guidance request for a high-frequency business, check if the corresponding plugin is correctly triggered in the tool calling logs.
- View the data fields returned by the plugin, confirm that they fully match the preset business fields, with no extra or missing fields.
- Simulate the update scenario after business completion, wait 12 hours and call again, confirm that the data has been synchronized and updated.
- Check the context window configuration to ensure that process information in long business documents is not truncated.
