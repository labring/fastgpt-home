# Business Process Guidance for Basic Chemical Industry: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Basic chemical industry business process data mainly comes from internal enterprise process manuals, safety supervision filing documents, equipment maintenance work orders, and supply chain approval documents. Data updates are triggered on demand when new production capacity goes live, industry policies are adjusted, or business processes are iterated, with no fixed cycle. Most documents are in structured format, containing fields such as process nodes, approval authorities, handling time limits, and required material lists. Units include workdays, copies, tons and other business-specific identifiers.

## Constraints Imposed on Vector Models and Indexes
The structured characteristics of basic chemical industry business process data require that field association relationships be retained during indexing, to avoid losing the binding information between process nodes and approval requirements after splitting. When long documents account for a large proportion, it is necessary to limit the length of individual text chunks to ensure semantic integrity. Business data is updated on demand as business iterations progress, so flexible on-demand batch reindexing capabilities must be supported. The exclusive attributes of fields and units require the indexing model to adapt to chemical industry-specific terminology, to avoid semantic matching deviations. FastGPT v4.17.0’s indexing configuration must be adjusted specifically for these characteristics to ensure business relevance of retrieval results.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The indexing model dropdown menu is empty when creating a knowledge base, and preset indexing models cannot be selected. Cause: The indexing model has not been bound and configured in the model management interface, and only the model was added without enabling the indexing model call permission.
- Symptom: After uploading the same document, the number of chunks exceeds the preset value, with duplicate indexed fragments appearing. Cause: The document contains non-standard line breaks or hidden characters, triggering automatic secondary chunking by the system instead of following the initial configuration rules.
- Symptom: Batch vector model training cannot be triggered, and only single-file parameter adjustment followed by upload is supported. Cause: The batch indexing switch for the knowledge base has not been enabled, or the trigger permission for batch training has not been configured in the system settings.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a typical basic chemical industry process document, check the chunk preview interface to confirm that the chunk length falls within the preset character range.
- Check the enabled status of the indexing model in the model management interface to confirm that the target model has been bound to the vector configuration of the current knowledge base.
- Submit a business-related query, check the similarity scores of the retrieval results to confirm that the threshold setting meets business matching requirements.
- Trigger a batch indexing task, check the task logs for error prompts related to chunk anomalies or duplicate indexing.
