# Business Process Guidance for Basic Telecommunications Operations: Model Access and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Business process data for basic telecommunications operations mainly comes from internal business handling systems, online business hall knowledge bases, and standardized process manuals for offline outlets. The data update rhythm is as follows: package policies and marketing activities are adjusted quarterly or during temporary marketing cycles, while common user inquiry questions are updated monthly based on user feedback. Documents are organized by business category. Each business entry includes business name, handling path, required documents/ materials, handling time limit, charging standard, and consultation channel. Fields include numeric business codes, handling time limits measured in working days, and text-format material lists.

## Constraints on model access and configuration
Structured business process documents contain fixed fields and clear units. This requires precise matching of field formats and unit rules during model access, to avoid incorrect handling time limits or material requirements in outputs. Frequently updated business data requires flexible knowledge base synchronization channels to be reserved during configuration, ensuring content called by the model aligns with the latest policies. Individual business entries have long content and cover a wide range of categories. The model must control context length during recall and generation, to prevent exceeding the model's processing limit, and avoid recalling too many irrelevant business entries that interfere with judgment.

## How to set configurations
The following configuration items apply to FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | Basic telecommunications business process documents have long individual entry lengths, requiring space for multiple business entries and complete process steps |
| `temperature` | `0.1–0.3` | Business handling processes require strict accuracy, reducing the probability of randomly generated incorrect content |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `2000–3000 token` | Sufficient business process details must be recalled, without exceeding the total context length limit |
| `toolChoice` | `auto` | Automatically call the knowledge base recall tool to complete precise matching of business processes |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Operator's internal exclusive proxy address | Ensure data transmission security and comply with internal network access permission rules |
| `maxTokens` | `1500–2000 token` | Control single-round reply length to ensure users can fully read handling steps and requirements |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After model access, the test returns an "API authentication failed" error, and process guidance cannot be triggered. Cause: `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` was not filled correctly, or a token that does not match the operator's proxy service was used.
- Phenomenon: Garbled characters or formatting errors appear in generated handling steps. Cause: The `maxContext` value does not match the length of the business document, and context truncation destroys the encoding rules of structured fields.
- Phenomenon: Recalled business entries do not match user inquiries, with a large amount of irrelevant package marketing content. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` value is too large, recalling document content outside the current business category.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Call the test interface, enter any telecommunications business inquiry (such as "How to handle 5G package change"), and check whether the returned content includes accurate handling paths and required materials.
- View the knowledge base synchronization log to confirm that the latest package policies or activity content has been synchronized to the data source used for model calls.
- Check the API request log to confirm that each call carries a valid `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN`, with no authentication failure records.
- After adjusting the `temperature` parameter, compare the rigor of the two test replies to confirm that the parameter value meets business requirements.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
