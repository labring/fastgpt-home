# Commodity Trading Business Process Guidance: Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Business data for commodity trading originates primarily from internal business systems including internal trade contracts, warehouse receipts, logistics waybills, and customs declaration documents. Data updates trigger alongside business nodes. Synchronized updates take place during stages such as contract signing, goods delivery, and customs clearance.
Most documents follow a structured format, with core fields including goods name, specification model, transaction quantity, settlement unit price, delivery location, and transportation method. Common units use industry-standard metrics such as tons, cubic meters, and container quantities.

## Constraints on Forms and Interactions From These Characteristics
Commodity trading’s multi-field structured data requires forms to group content by business modules such as trade qualifications, goods information, logistics arrangements, and settlement information. This prevents operational confusion caused by stacking fields.
Industry-specific unit needs require interaction components to preload options including tons, cubic meters, and container quantities. This cuts down on manual input errors.
Dynamic data updates tied to business nodes require forms to support real-time field validity checks. This blocks input that does not match the current business state.
Cross-role collaborative business processes require form interactions to support permission hierarchies. Different roles can only edit fields in their corresponding modules.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Interface lag occurs when dragging form components. Normal operation resumes after deleting custom variables. Cause: No grouping configuration is applied to multi-field data for commodity trading. This leads to loading excessive redundant field data during rendering.
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base selection options do not appear in tool call configurations. Cause: The knowledge base association configuration for the `userSelect` component is not enabled, or the bound option library is not associated with the corresponding business knowledge base.
- Phenomenon: Field verification fails after form submission, with a prompt about unit format errors. Cause: Industry-standard unit options are not preset. This allows users to manually enter non-standard units, leading to verification failure.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Enter the form editing interface, confirm that core fields are displayed grouped by modules such as trade qualifications, goods information, and logistics arrangements.
- Check the interaction components for fields such as goods quantity and transportation method, confirm that they are bound to industry-standard unit option libraries.
- Manually enter a non-standard unit of measurement, verify whether the real-time verification function triggers a corresponding prompt.
- Call the `userSelect` component, confirm that preset trade subject or transportation mode options are available for selection.
