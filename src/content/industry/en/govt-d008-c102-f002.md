# Business Process Guidance for Refinery and Chemical Production: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Business handling data for refinery and chemical production is mainly sourced from production management systems, equipment operation and maintenance logs, safety operation procedure libraries, and electronic workflows for operation approval. Operation approval records are updated in real time. Operation and maintenance logs are synchronized per shift. Safety procedures are revised quarterly. Documents include structured fields and unstructured text. Structured fields include work permit number, equipment ID, operation area, pressure (MPa), temperature (℃), and other items. Unstructured text covers operation steps, risk prevention and control measures, and approval processes for hot work, confined space work, and other operations.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexes link
Mixed structured and unstructured data requires retaining business associations during chunking, and avoid splitting work permit numbers and their corresponding operation steps. Real-time updated operation approval records require indexes to support incremental synchronization without full index reconstruction. Field units specific to the industry require retaining semantics during vector modeling to prevent matching deviations caused by unit confusion. Long-process documents require chunk lengths adapted to business nodes, and avoid truncating complete operation steps across chunks. Additionally, business handling involves safety compliance, so the accuracy of recall results has higher requirements, and low-match results must be strictly controlled.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: The interface returns a 404 status code after the embedding model is configured. Cause: The interface address and secret key of Baidu embedding-v1 are not correctly configured, or the interface permission is not enabled.
- Phenomenon: The vector model deployed without a GPU environment cannot be added in the management interface. Cause: The vector model container is not run in CPU mode, or the container resource allocation is insufficient.
- Phenomenon: The exported knowledge base data cannot be split by business category. Cause: Knowledge base classification tags are not set in advance according to business types such as hot work and confined space work, and only the global knowledge base dimension is used for export.

## How to confirm the configuration is correctly applied
- Upload a typical refinery and chemical work permit document, and check whether the chunked text completely retains operation steps and equipment parameters.
- Initiate a business handling guidance query, and verify whether the similarity score of the returned results falls within the set threshold range.
- Test the incremental update function, submit new work permit data, and confirm that the index library automatically synchronizes updates.
- View the vector model operation logs, and confirm that there are no records of connection timeouts or parameter parsing errors.
