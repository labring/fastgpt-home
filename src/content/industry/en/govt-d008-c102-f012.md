# Business Process Guidance for Refinery and Chemical Production: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Internal enterprise MES manufacturing execution systems, OA approval management systems, and safety operation procedure manuals supply business process guidance data for refinery and chemical production.
Updates occur when production plans adjust or approval processes optimize.
Document structures include fields such as operation type, approval hierarchy, required materials, and time limit requirements.
Some fields use professional units: material usage uses tons as the unit, and operation duration uses hours as the unit.
Individual process documents can be up to several thousand characters long.

## Constraints on Deployment and Upgrade
Refinery and chemical business process data comes from heterogeneous internal enterprise systems.
Configure corresponding database connections during deployment to adapt to different types of business data storage.
Data fields include professional units and structured approval nodes.
Calibrate field mapping rules during deployment to avoid data parsing errors.
Process documents have long lengths, and update frequencies shift dynamically with production plans.
Maintain compatibility with old data formats during upgrade to prevent business process interruptions.
Some data involves production safety sensitive information.
Configure encryption parameters during deployment to secure data transmission and storage.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: After modifying the configuration file, the service on port 3005 cannot respond normally, and access returns a 502 error. Cause: The mapping relationship between `FE_DOMAIN` and the intranet access port is not configured correctly, causing communication interruption between front-end and back-end services.
- Symptom: After upgrading to FastGPT v4.17.0, cross-department refinery and chemical business process knowledge bases cannot be shared. Cause: The `ROOT_KEY` permission parameter is not configured correctly, resulting in unopened cross-department access permissions.
- Symptom: After local deployment and attempts to integrate with existing business systems, only API access is available, and embeddable front-end pages cannot be generated. Cause: `FE_DOMAIN` is not configured correctly, causing the front-end build script to fail to generate static resources required for embedding.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Access the business process page corresponding to the configured `FE_DOMAIN`, verify that the process node list for refinery and chemical business loads normally.
- Submit a simulated refinery and chemical business process application, verify that data can be correctly written to the databases corresponding to `MONGODB_URI` and `PG_URL`.
- Download a business approval attachment, verify that the link can open normally and has no permission exceptions.
- Restart the service, check that the cache service corresponding to `REDIS_URL` connects normally, with no timeout errors.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
