# Coal Chemical Industry Business Process Guidance: Model Access and Configuration

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Coal chemical industry business process guidance data mainly comes from the enterprise's internal production scheduling system, safety management and control platform, administrative approval process library, and the latest safety operation specifications released by the industry. Data update rhythm falls into two categories: production-related business process data is updated in line with daily production scheduling adjustments, while administrative approval-related processes are updated temporarily when enterprise systems change or new businesses launch. A single business document usually includes fields such as process ID, node name, operation time limit (unit: hour), required qualification document type, responsible department, etc. Some processes involving hazardous chemical handling will additionally include safety check items and emergency disposal steps. The overall length of documents mostly falls within the 1000–3000 character range.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Model Access and Configuration Link
The long-text characteristics of coal chemical industry business documents require that model access must adapt to a larger context capacity to avoid truncating key process steps during retrieval or generation. The structure with multiple fields and units requires the model to accurately identify and retain unit information from business data; otherwise, operational parameter errors will appear in guidance content. Frequently updated business data requires that the knowledge base synchronization configuration must support flexible adjustment of update frequencies to avoid guidance content lagging behind the latest process specifications. In addition, some processes involve hazardous chemical operations, which have extremely high requirements for the accuracy of generated content, so the random generation space of the model must be strictly limited.

## How to Configure the Settings
The following configuration items are formulated based on the interface and parameter logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Coal chemical industry business process documents usually include multi-node steps and associated safety specifications, requiring sufficient context capacity to avoid taking content out of context |
| `maxTokens` | 4000–6000 token | Business process guidance needs to output complete steps and precautions to avoid truncating key approval nodes or safety requirements |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500–2000 characters | Matches the single-node content length of coal chemical industry business documents, ensuring that referenced business rules are accurate and complete |
| `temperature` | 0.1–0.3 | Business processes must strictly follow specifications, reducing the risk of random generation and avoiding operational steps that do not meet industry requirements |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Automatically call knowledge base retrieval to match the operational requirements of specific business nodes, adapting to multi-branch coal chemical industry business processing workflows |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After restarting the FastGPT service or device, the configured model keys and knowledge base binding relationships are displayed as empty in the interface. Cause: Local configuration persistent storage for FastGPT is not configured. By default, configuration stored temporarily under Windows systems is cleared after restart.
- Phenomenon: When generating chunked vectors for coal chemical industry business documents exceeding 1024 tokens, vector accuracy degradation or generation errors occur. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` parameter is not adjusted to adapt to long texts, and the default token limit does not match the single-node content length of business documents.
- Phenomenon: Vague or incorrect operational time limit descriptions appear in generated business process guidance. Cause: The `temperature` parameter is set too high, causing the model to generate random content that does not comply with coal chemical industry process specifications.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a complete coal chemical industry business process document that includes all operational steps, perform a vector generation test, and check whether the generated vectors cover the core content of the document.
- Initiate a business process guidance conversation test, input questions for specific business scenarios, and verify whether the model can accurately reference process nodes and unit information from the knowledge base.
- View the parameter values on the model configuration page, confirm that the set `maxContext`, `maxTokens` and other parameters match the preset values.
- After restarting the FastGPT service, check whether the configured model and knowledge base association relationships are retained, and there are no abnormal error prompts.
