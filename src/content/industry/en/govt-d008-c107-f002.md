# Business Process Guidance for Coal Mine Production: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data related to coal mine production business process guidance originates primarily from mine scheduling approval ledgers, job site safety regulations, equipment maintenance records, and business application forms.
Three update cadences apply:
- Daily application and approval records update in real time alongside ongoing business operations
- Equipment maintenance logs update per work shift
- General safety regulations and process documents are revised quarterly or annually
Three core document structures are used:
- Structured forms with fields including application time, approver, equipment ID, and job duration
- Unstructured regulation text organized into sections with clause numbers
- Semi-structured maintenance logs with timestamps and device IDs
Field units use professional formats such as hours, operation counts, and identification numbers.

## Constraints for Vector Models and Indexes
Coal mine business data has multiple structural characteristics. Vector chunking must balance the relevance of structured fields and the semantic completeness of unstructured text. This avoids splitting critical approval information or technical terms.
Real-time updated business documents require indexes to support incremental synchronization. This prevents performance loss from full reindexing.
Long-form regulation documents need appropriate chunking and context stitching logic. This stops semantic breaks across chunks.
Content dense with technical terms requires embedding models with domain adaptation capabilities. Index recall thresholds must align with the precision needs of the business scenario. This prevents irrelevant data from interfering with process guidance.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Covers semantic units of short approval forms and long regulations in coal mine operations, avoids splitting technical terms and complete process steps |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Connects context between adjacent chunks, prevents fragmentation of cross-chunk job process descriptions |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.80 | Filters irrelevant equipment maintenance records, accurately matches semantic similarity for business process guidance |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Controls the number of returned results, avoids excessive redundant information interfering with business process guidance |
| `defaultToken` | 8192 | Adapts to the long-text input requirements of coal mine regulation documents, accommodates complete professional content |
| `maxToken` | 16384 | Supports context stitching of multiple approval forms and regulations, meets input requirements for complex business queries |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
1. Phenomenon: After replacing the embedding model, manually inserted knowledge base document indexes disappear automatically after several hours.
   Cause: Incremental index persistent storage is not configured. Full index tasks overwrite temporarily generated default indexes, and no historical index snapshots are retained.
2. Phenomenon: After uploading coal mine equipment maintenance logs, the file status remains "Indexing" for a long time with no progress updates.
   Cause: The `defaultToken` parameter is not adjusted. The batch embedding task is interrupted due to exceeding the model input limit of FastGPT v4.17.0, and no timeout retry mechanism is set.
3. Phenomenon: When searching for "fully mechanized mining face installation process", irrelevant gas drainage records are recalled.
   Cause: The chunk length is set too small. Complete job processes are split into multiple semantically incoherent fragments, leading to deviations in vector recall.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
A standard coal mine job site safety regulation document may be uploaded. Chunk preview content is checked to confirm that technical terms and complete processes are not split.
A batch embedding task may be triggered. Task logs of FastGPT v4.17.0 are checked to confirm there are no out-of-memory or timeout errors. The `maxToken` setting is verified to meet model requirements.
A simulated business query may be run. Typical process keywords are entered to check if the similarity scores of recalled results fall within the preset threshold range.
The knowledge base index status may be checked after a 12-hour wait. Manually inserted document indexes are confirmed to not disappear automatically. The incremental synchronization mechanism is verified to operate correctly.
