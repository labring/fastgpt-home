# Business Process Guidance for Power Grid Enterprises: Model Access and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for power grid enterprise business process guidance mainly comes from internal marketing business systems, customer service ticket repositories, and officially released electricity business handling guidelines. Data is updated in real time for ticket status, with full process documents updated daily. Each individual business data entry includes fields such as business ID, handling links, required material list, handling time limit, and consultation channel. The material list is stored as a structured list, the time limit field uses working days or hours as the unit, and some documents include cross-page process diagrams and attachment descriptions.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the model access and configuration link
The mixed characteristics of structured fields and unstructured documents in business data require model access configurations to support both structured retrieval and natural language parsing. Real-time synchronized ticket data requires configurations to support dynamic data updates, preventing the model from calling outdated business information. Longer process documents and multi-field structures require context processing configurations to cover complete link information, preventing key content from being truncated. The requirement to import multi-column structured Excel-format business data requires chunking configurations to support splitting data by row, avoiding damage to the association relationship between cross-column fields.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Power grid business process documents usually include multi-link content, requiring complete context information to avoid missing key links |
| `maxTokens` | 1500–2000 token | A single business guidance reply needs to be clear and complete, avoiding exceeding the interface response length limit |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 2000–3000 characters | Need to fully reference core content such as business material lists and handling time limits to ensure accurate referenced information |
| `temperature` | 0.2–0.4 | Business handling processes need to remain rigorous and accurate, reducing the randomness of model-generated content |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Automatically trigger knowledge base retrieval to match real-time updated business tickets and process documents |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Enterprise internal proxy service address | Ensure business data does not leave the enterprise intranet, complying with compliance requirements |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Service key uniformly allocated by the enterprise | Implement interface permission control to prevent unauthorized access |

## Three common mistakes
- Issue: After importing multi-column business handling Excel files, retrieval result fields are chaotic, and cross-column data is incorrectly merged. Cause: The data splitting rule was not configured to split by row, causing the system to disrupt column field associations during automatic chunking.
- Issue: Calling a custom proxy channel returns a 403 status code, and the interface request fails. Cause: The enterprise-specific `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` was not used, and a universal key was used, resulting in failed permission verification.
- Issue: The generated business guidance includes irrelevant business content, deviating from power grid handling process specifications. Cause: The `temperature` value was set too high, and the randomness of model-generated content exceeded the requirements for business rigor.
