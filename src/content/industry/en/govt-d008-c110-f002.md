# Business Process Guidance for Mining and Mineral Processing: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category’s data looks like
Data related to mining and mineral processing business process guidance primarily comes from mine production management systems, safety supervision platforms, business approval systems, and on-site operation logs. Data update rhythms fall into two categories: daily business approval documents are updated in real time. Safety inspection records and equipment operation logs are updated weekly or monthly. Document structure includes structured fields and unstructured attachments. Structured fields include mining license number, mining scale (unit: 10,000 tons/year), safety hazard level, and others. Unstructured attachments are mostly PDF and Word format materials such as approval process descriptions and mineral processing technical manuals. Single segment content lengths vary significantly.

## Constraints imposed on vector models and indexes by these data characteristics
The above data characteristics impose multiple constraints on the vector model and index link. First, mixed structured and unstructured document formats require indexes to support vectorization adaptation for multiple content types, avoiding long text truncation or loss of structured field semantics. Second, the combination of real-time and periodic update rhythms requires indexes to support both incremental update and full reconstruction modes, adapting to different data update frequencies. Third, field characteristics where business processes are tightly bound to mining scale require vector recall to retain field metadata, avoiding recall of business process documents for unrelated mining levels.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the typical length of single-segment approval processes and equipment descriptions in mining and mineral processing business documents, avoiding semantic breaks |
| Overlap Length | 100–200 characters | Retains business step context across chunks, preventing associated steps in mineral processing and mining workflows from being split apart |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85, calibrated via actual testing | Distinguishes process similarity across different mining scales and approval stages, improving recall precision |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8 results | Matches the context length of single calls for business process guidance, avoiding exceeding model input limits |
| `maxToken` | 4096 token | Adapts to the input limit of general embedding models, avoiding truncation of core business content during vectorization |
| `defaultToken` | 256 token | Serves as the default vectorization length for short fields such as mining license numbers and hazard levels, balancing precision and computational overhead |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Index construction gets stuck at over 90% progress without response, or fails directly. Cause: Did not adapt to the mixed format of mining and mineral processing documents, and did not enable multi-format chunking configuration, leading to timeout during large file chunking.
- Phenomenon: After replacing the embedding model, index construction succeeds but search tests return empty results or throw errors. Cause: Did not synchronously update the matching vector dimension parameter, causing vectors generated by the new model to not match the dimensions of the existing index.
- Phenomenon: When querying the vector database, only vector data is returned, and specific content of the original business document cannot be associated. Cause: Did not enable the vector and original document metadata binding configuration, only storing vector data without retaining original document identifiers.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single mining and mineral processing approval process PDF, check that the chunk preview splits long text into semantically complete paragraphs with no obvious content truncation.
- Replace the test embedding model, perform index reconstruction and run a search test, confirm that returned results match the business question as expected.
- View the vector storage metadata fields, confirm they include unique identifiers such as original document filename, business type, update time.
- Simulate a business process query, check that returned results include relevant documents such as corresponding approval processes and compliance requirements.
