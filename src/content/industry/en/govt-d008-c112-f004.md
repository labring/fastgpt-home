# Business Process Guidance for Grain Reserve Management: Workflow Orchestration

## What this category of data looks like
Grain reserve business data mainly comes from warehouse in-and-out ledgers, inventory count reports, grain quality inspection reports, and rotation plan documents. Data updates are triggered along with business nodes. In-and-out documents are generated in real time, inventory ledgers are updated daily, and quality inspection reports are synchronized upon completion of each grain batch. Most documents are structured tables containing fields such as warehouse area number, grain variety, grade, actual quantity, inbound batch number, shelf life, storage location, and more. Units uniformly use tons, kilograms, and batch numbers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the workflow orchestration link
The large volume of structured data and complex field dimensions require workflow orchestration to bind different business fields via `variables` to avoid missing critical parameters. Real-time generated in-and-out data requires workflow nodes to support trigger-based execution to reduce data latency. The association between grain batches and shelf life requires adding conditional branching nodes in the workflow to trigger rotation warning processes based on the shelf life threshold of inbound batches. Minor differences in location field naming across different warehouse areas require workflow configurations to support custom field mapping rules. Single business documents are moderately sized, but large data volumes during batch processing require workflow support for pagination reading nodes to avoid memory overflow.

## Configuration Settings
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger Mode | Triggered by business events | Matches the characteristic that grain reserve business data is generated in real time along with in-and-out operations and inventory counts |
| Variable Binding | Bind warehouse area number and grain variety fields from `variables` | Adapts to the multi-field business logic of structured ledgers |
| Branch Judgment | Triggered based on inbound batch shelf life threshold | Corresponds to the business rules for grain rotation |
| Field Mapping | Support custom warehouse area location fields | Resolves naming differences across different warehouse areas |
| Timeout Setting | 600 seconds | Adapts to the processing duration of batch inventory counts |
| Maximum Context | 800–1200 characters | Matches the average length of a single business ledger |
| Document Size Limit | 1000 MB | Adapts to the maximum size of a single batch grain quality inspection report |
| Single Processing Batch | First 20 batches | Adapts to the maximum processing volume of a single inventory count |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Workflow execution fails with a tool call error, returning empty fields. The cause is that required fields of in-and-out documents are not bound via `variables`, causing nodes to fail to obtain business data.
- Workflow repeats loading historical documents when processing multi-batch data. The cause is that document filtering rules are not configured, and the system is not restricted to only reading files associated with the current business batch.
- Subsequent processes are not correctly triggered after workflow branch judgment. The cause is that the shelf life threshold is not bound to the inbound date field in `variables`, causing the judgment logic to fail.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Trigger a test workflow, upload the in-and-out documents for the corresponding batch, and check whether nodes automatically start execution.
- View the workflow variable binding panel, confirm that all business fields have been associated with the `variables` parameter.
- Simulate grain batches with different shelf lives, verify whether the branch judgment node correctly triggers the corresponding process.
- Check the document filtering rules, confirm that only files from the current business batch will be loaded and parsed.
