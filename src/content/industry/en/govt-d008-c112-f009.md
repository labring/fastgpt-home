# Business Process Guidance for Grain Reserve: Model Access and Configuration

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources include structured documents and forms generated by business systems such as grain in-and-out ledgers, inventory count reports, rotation execution documents, and quality inspection reports. Updates are triggered according to business nodes. In-and-out data is updated in real time. Monthly inventory summary data is synchronized daily. Rotation plan documents are updated quarterly. Document structures are divided into two types: single business documents (such as inbound orders, which include fields including batch number, grain variety, storage bin number, and quality inspection grade) and summary reports (which include fields including storage location, grain variety, and beginning/ending inventory). Most field units are tons, kilograms, batch numbers, and standard date formats.

## Constraints Imposed on Model Access and Configuration
This page is written based on the features of FastGPT v4.17.0. Grain reserve business data includes three categories: real-time in-and-out documents, monthly summary reports, and quarterly rotation files. The field structures of different document types have significant differences. Physical measurement units such as tons and kilograms are widely used. Real-time data requires model access response latency to match the immediacy requirements of business processing. Excessive latency will reduce the efficiency of process guidance. Long-text rotation plan files may exceed the model's context limit, so content truncation or segmentation processing must be performed in advance. The presence of physical unit fields requires the model to accurately associate units with business values to avoid unit errors in the guidance process. The field differences between different documents also require the model to adapt to the field extraction logic of different business forms.

## How to Set Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | The average length of grain reserve summary reports and rotation files falls within this range, covering the context requirements of most business documents |
| `maxTokens` | `4000 token` | Responses for business process guidance need to be concise and clear; overly long responses will interfere with operational judgment |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `2000 token` | Prioritize quoting core fields of business documents to avoid diluting key business information with redundant content |
| `toolChoice` | `auto` | Business process guidance includes multiple types of tasks such as inventory query and rotation plan query; automatic tool selection can adapt to different scenarios |
| `temperature` | `0.1–0.3` | Business guidance requires accuracy and rigor; a lower temperature reduces the randomness of responses and avoids deviations in business logic |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A 400 error is returned directly when uploading grain quality inspection images while calling a vision model. Cause: The `vision` configuration item is not enabled, or the proxy service does not have call permissions for the vision model enabled.
- Phenomenon: No response occurs when the shared page concurrency exceeds 60, and the local model server resources have not reached the bottleneck. Cause: Reasonable concurrency current limiting parameters are not configured, causing the model service queue to backlog and unable to respond to requests in a timely manner.
- Phenomenon: A 400 error is triggered when uploading an overly long rotation plan file, and the <FilesContent> tag contains the complete file content. Cause: No file preprocessing logic is added to the workflow, and the overly long file is not truncated or segmented, exceeding the model's context limit.

## How to Verify That the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a single in-and-out ledger document, and check whether the model's response accurately extracts core business fields such as batch number and storage bin number.
- Initiate multiple concurrent process guidance requests, observe the service response status, and confirm that there are no abnormal interruptions or no-response situations.
- Upload an overly long rotation plan file, confirm that no context overflow error is triggered, and that key business nodes are correctly identified.
- Call the vision model and upload a quality inspection image, and check whether the model can correctly identify the quality inspection identification information in the image.
