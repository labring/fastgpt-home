# Railway Operations Business Process Guidance: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for railway operations business process guidance comes primarily from official ticketing rule documents, station business process manuals, train operation adjustment announcements, and online and offline business ledgers. Update frequency varies by content type: ticketing policy documents are updated quarterly, timetable documents are adjusted every six months, and temporary business notices are pushed in real time. Most individual documents are structured entries, containing fields such as business ID, processing location, required materials, processing time limit (unit: working days), and consultation channel. Some process documents include step-by-step operation instructions. The text length of these documents spans a wide range.

## Constraints on Vector Models and Indexing
Railway operation data has a wide range of text lengths, diverse field types, and inconsistent update cycles. These factors create multiple constraints for the vector indexing process.
First, fixed chunk lengths cannot adapt to all content when mixing long and short texts. Adjust chunking strategies for different document types.
Second, documents with structured fields such as business ID and train number require vector indexes to distinguish core business entities from explanatory text. This avoids retrieving irrelevant content.
Third, real-time updated temporary notices and low-frequency updated policy documents need differentiated index refresh cycles. This ensures data timeliness and retrieval efficiency.

## Configuration Settings
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Aligns with the structured entry length of railway business documents, prevents retrieval gaps caused by scattered single-block content |
| Overlap Length | 50–100 characters | Ensures contextual coherence of process steps, prevents chunking from breaking continuous operation instructions |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Matches the input limit of mainstream vector models, avoids exceeding model processing scope |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.80 | Filters low-match retrieval results, focuses on core business processes and material requirements |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8 entries | Controls the number of retrieval results, meets the concise needs of business consultation |
| `defaultToken` | 200 | Configures the default recalled context length, ensures complete display of process descriptions |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After deploying FastGPT locally, an error about abnormal parameter configuration appears when retrieving railway business documents. Cause: The corresponding value of `defaultToken` was not correctly filled in the environment variables, resulting in missing necessary context parameters during vector model invocation.
- Phenomenon: After importing railway business documents, retrieval results mix train timetables and process steps. Cause: Chunking rules were not set for the structured fields of railway documents, and different types of business data were indexed together, leading to off-target recall results.
- Phenomenon: The number of results returned when retrieving business process flows is far lower than expected. Cause: The similarity threshold was set too high, filtering out some content whose match level meets business consultation needs.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single railway business process document, perform a retrieval test, and check if the returned results include the target business’s steps and material requirements.
- View the vector index refresh logs, confirm that documents with different update frequencies (such as policy documents, temporary notices) complete index updates according to the preset cycle.
- Call the vector model interface, check if the returned context length matches the configured values of `maxToken` and `defaultToken`.
- Adjust the similarity threshold, compare changes in the number of retrieval results, and confirm that the threshold configuration meets the precision requirements of business retrieval.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
