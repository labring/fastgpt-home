# Business Process Guidance for Railway Operations: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Railway operations business processing data primarily originates from three document types: official operation manuals, real-time dispatch announcements, and counter service guidelines. Data update rhythms fall into two categories: fixed cycles and temporary triggers. Fixed routes and standard services are updated monthly. Temporary information such as additional trains and station adjustments is synchronized hourly.

Document structures use business categories as first-level directories. Each category includes four core fields: processing steps, required materials, time limit requirements, and special case handling. Field units include train numbers, minutes, passenger trips, document types, and similar metrics. Individual document lengths typically range between 800–1500 characters.

## Constraints Imposed on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The multi-source, batched update characteristics of railway operation data require the retrieval link to support limiting search scope by business collections, to prevent interference from irrelevant cross-business content. The mixed update rhythm of fixed and temporary information requires the recall mechanism to support both incremental synchronization and full refresh modes, to maintain real-time information timeliness.

The step-by-step document structure and multi-field design require retrieval matching to cover both keyword and semantic information, to avoid missing steps caused by keyword-only matching. The moderate length of individual documents requires retaining step integrity during chunking, to prevent splitting complete processing workflows into disconnected fragments.

## How to Configure
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `similarity` | 0.72–0.80 | Railway business processing workflows require a relatively high semantic matching threshold to filter out irrelevant similar content |
| `limit` | Top 10 entries | Railway business processes have multiple steps, retaining sufficient context while avoiding context overflow |
| `searchMode` | Semantic + keyword hybrid | Balances fixed business process keywords and semantic expressions in user queries |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Reranks chunked recall results to prioritize highly relevant content |
| `collectionIds` | Specified by business collections such as ticket sales, security checks, ticket changes, and similar categories | Clear business classification in railway operations allows narrowing search scope and reducing response latency by limiting collections |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Disabled | Railway business queries are mostly precise process inquiries, so no additional query semantic extension is needed |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Single retrieval takes over 6 seconds, and the interface shows a loading timeout prompt. Cause: `collectionIds` is not configured, so the search scope covers all business documents, leading to excessive matching calculation load.
- Phenomenon: No relevant results are found in the specified ticket sales business document collection. Cause: The `collectionIds` parameter is not filled correctly, or the parameter format does not meet system requirements.
- Phenomenon: Recall results include security check business content, even though the user's query only targets ticket refund processes. Cause: The `similarity` threshold is set too low, matching semantically similar but business-irrelevant document fragments.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- A ticket refund business query may be submitted, and retrieval results can be checked to confirm they only originate from the ticket refund business collection, verifying the `collectionIds` configuration is active.
- Single retrieval latency data may be reviewed, with latency confirmed to fall within a reasonable range, verifying the `limit` and `similarity` configurations are appropriate.
- A hybrid query combining process keywords and semantic descriptions may be submitted, and reranked results can be checked to confirm they follow business relevance order, verifying `usingReRank` is enabled.
- A business guideline document for a temporary additional train may be imported, a relevant query submitted, and successful recall of the new document confirmed, verifying the incremental update configuration functions correctly.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
