# Railway Operations Business Handling Process Guidance: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
Railway operations business handling data primarily comes from the national railway ticket sales and reservation system, on-site station business terminals, and online service platforms.
Data updates follow two rhythms: real-time and scheduled.
Ticket availability and order status are updated in real time.
Business process rules and service notices are synchronized weekly.
Individual business documents include fields such as passenger identity identifier, travel interval, business type, and handling voucher number.
Field units follow railway industry standards uniformly.
For example, travel mileage uses kilometers as the unit, handling duration uses minutes as the unit, and identity identifiers are 18-digit resident ID card numbers or Mainland Travel Permits for Hong Kong and Macao Residents numbers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on forms and interactions
Real-time updated ticket data requires form interactions to verify ticket availability in real time. This prevents submission of invalid change or refund requests.
Scheduled updated business rules require form configurations to support quick adjustments to verification logic. No overall process rebuild is needed.
Standardized field units require forms to use unified input format prompts. For example, travel intervals must be entered in the "departure station - arrival station" format to avoid unit confusion.
Fixed-length identity identifiers require forms to add format verification. This automatically filters non-compliant input content.
Different business types correspond to different required field combinations. Forms must support dynamic field display switching to reduce redundant user input.

## How to set configurations
The following configuration instructions are formulated for FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale for This Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` timeout threshold | 600 seconds | Covers the average waiting time from information entry to submission for railway business |
| `userSelect` option cache duration | 300 seconds | Reduces waiting overhead from repeated loading of station lists and business types |
| Form dynamic field trigger rules | Bound to business type dropdown options | Adapts to differences in required field combinations across different businesses |
| Input format verification rules | Preset according to railway industry standards | Matches fixed format requirements for identity identifiers and travel intervals |
| Number of pre-submission form verifications | Real-time verification for the first 2 times, delayed verification for subsequent attempts | Balances verification accuracy and system resource usage |

## Three common mistakes
- A 400 error occurs after form submission, triggered only when vague non-business questions such as "how about 350" are entered. The cause is that form input verification does not filter non-business vague questions, leading the model to attempt calling an unpreset business interface.
- Voice input fails to work normally, and the deployed speech-to-text model does not respond. The cause is that voice input channel binding is not enabled in the `formInput` configuration, and the speech-to-text result is not correctly connected to the form input stream.
- Form dynamic fields do not switch correctly. No corresponding required items appear after selecting a business type. The cause is that business type options are not correctly bound to dynamic field rules, so the interaction logic fails to trigger field updates.

## How to confirm configurations are set correctly
- Enter identity identifiers and travel intervals that comply with railway industry standards, then check if the form automatically triggers format verification prompts.
- Switch between different business type options, then confirm that required fields for the matching business are correctly displayed or hidden.
- Simulate a long period of inactivity on the form, then check if the `formInput` timeout prompt is triggered.
- Submit a test form, then confirm that the system returns verification results consistent with preset rules.
