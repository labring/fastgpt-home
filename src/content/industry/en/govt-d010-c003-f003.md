# Party-building Learning Q&A for Real Estate Registration: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

This document is written based on the FastGPT v4.17.0 configuration specification.

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data comes from the real estate registration information system, cadastral survey database, housing transaction online signing system, tax system, and electronic certificate repository, and is updated daily. Commonly processed documents include registration applications, ownership certificates, survey results, tax payment vouchers, real estate ownership certificates, and real estate registration books. Data fields cover rights subject, location, ownership status, survey coordinates, and more. Each document typically contains both structured registration information and unstructured attachments. Relevant policy documents on Party-building-led real estate registration services must also be linked as supplementary reference materials for Q&A.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
This category of data is updated daily, so multi-turn dialogue context must support real-time association with the latest synchronized registration data to avoid returning outdated information. Commonly processed documents include structured registration fields and unstructured attachments. Combined with the party-building learning Q&A scenario, prompts must clearly distinguish the logic for structured field queries, unstructured content interpretation, and Party-building policy adaptation. Errors in ownership verification can easily lead to property rights disputes, so multi-turn dialogue must gradually guide users to confirm core ownership information, reducing the risk of omissions in single-turn Q&A. Data from multiple systems must also clearly mark data verification rules in prompts to ensure consistency of called information.

## Three Common Errors
- Meaningless separators in the dialogue interface: Continuous `|` symbols appear during dialogue. The cause is failure to close intermediate thinking log output in debug mode, or failure to hide debug markers in the prompt.
- Multi-turn dialogue context loss: Subsequent questions cannot associate the previously mentioned real estate ownership certificate number. The cause is failure to correctly pass the `chatId` parameter, causing the system to fail to recognize the dialogue session.
- Excessive parsing time for large documents: Parsing a Word document over 10MB takes more than 30 seconds. The cause is failure to configure a reasonable chunking strategy, or failure to enable the parallel parsing switch for document preprocessing.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Correct
- Initiate a single-turn question about the Party-building-led real estate registration process. Verify that the returned content includes the latest registration policies and practical operation information, confirming that the data synchronization configuration is effective.
- Initiate consecutive multi-turn questions, mention the real estate ownership certificate number and location in sequence. Verify that subsequent questions can associate previously mentioned information, confirming that the `chatId` parameter configuration is correct.
- Upload a registration-related Word document under 10MB. Verify that parsing time meets expectations, confirming that chunking and parallel configuration are effective.
- View the dialogue log. Confirm that the `messages` field carries valid `variables` parameters, confirming that the variable binding configuration is correct.
