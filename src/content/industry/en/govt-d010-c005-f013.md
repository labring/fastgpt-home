# Party Building Learning Q&A for Property Rights, Asset and State-owned Capital Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category's data looks like
Data for this category primarily comes from state-owned asset supervision information systems, enterprise asset management systems, property rights exchange systems, valuation institution reports, and financial statements. Daily documents include property rights registration forms, asset valuation reports, property rights transfer plans, on-site transaction announcements, and asset disposal approval forms. Fields cover ownership identifiers, valuation parameters, transaction terms, approval status, and more. Data update frequency is monthly. Single document volume ranges from a few pages to dozens of pages.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding link
Multi-source heterogeneous data sources require precise association with data source permissions of corresponding systems during the sharing and embedding link, to avoid cross-system data leakage. The monthly update frequency means the knowledge base synchronization cycle must match the business rhythm. A cycle that is too short increases system load, while a cycle that is too long fails to synchronize the latest asset information in a timely manner. Complex document structures require the embedded Q&A system to retain business field integrity, and must not destroy core information such as ownership and transaction terms due to chunking. Additionally, the sensitivity of content related to state-owned assets requires strict access permission control for sharing and embedding, with only internal personnel authorized to obtain complete content.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The embedded page loads but does not return matching property right assets or party building Q&A data. Cause: The `shareId` is not bound to the exclusive knowledge base of the corresponding category, resulting in the calling of unrelated data sources.
- Phenomenon: Sharing links allow access to all sensitive content without logging in. Cause: The sharing permission verification switch was accidentally turned off, violating the security management specifications for state-owned asset data (corresponding to community questions about authentication changes).
- Phenomenon: Horizontal scroll bars or content truncation appear on the page after iframe embedding. Cause: The page container size of the state-owned asset system intranet was not adapted, and default embedding parameters were used directly.
