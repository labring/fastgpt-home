# Party Building Learning Q&A for Highway Maintenance and Road Asset and Right Management: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources include highway maintenance management systems, bridge and tunnel monitoring systems, road patrol inspection systems, and traffic flow monitoring data. Updates occur daily.
Daily documents cover maintenance plans, patrol logs, defect disposal orders, road-related construction permits, and road damage compensation documents. Fields include facility ID, defect type, disposal deadline, permit number, compensation amount, and similar metrics. Common units include kilometers, square meters, working days, and other standard units.
The structure of different document types varies significantly. Calls must be differentiated based on specific business scenarios.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
Daily updated high-frequency business data requires multi-turn dialogue contexts to synchronize latest records promptly. This prevents referencing outdated maintenance or patrol information.
The mixed structure of multiple document types requires prompts to clearly distinguish usage scenarios for each document type. This avoids confusion between cross-category data.
Specialized segmented fields for road administration and maintenance require the dialogue process to identify identifiers such as facility IDs and permit numbers. This ensures Q&A accurately matches business details.
Multi-turn dialogue must support tracing back via patrol log timelines. This adapts to progressive Q&A logic that combines business processes during party building learning.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Formula formatting displays correctly in debug preview, but only raw LaTeX code appears after publishing. Cause: Rich text rendering rules are not enabled in the publishing configuration, and only plain text output logic is retained.
- Phenomenon: The dialogue process stays stuck at the AI response stage after deployment, with no progress feedback. Cause: No timeout threshold is configured for cross-system data retrieval, causing business data pulling to time out without interruption.
- Phenomenon: AI dialogue content inserted in the workflow is appended to the final output result. Cause: The result output switch for intermediate nodes is not turned off, causing non-target content to be mixed into the final response.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Submit a test question that includes facility ID and defect type. Check whether the returned content only associates document fragments from the corresponding category.
- Initiate multi-turn progressive questions. Verify that the system automatically triggers follow-up questions based on context association.
- Simulate a cross-system data retrieval scenario. Confirm that the session timeout time meets business response requirements.
- Check the rich text rendering switch in the publishing configuration. Ensure that formula formatting displays correctly.
