# Party Building Learning Q&A for Medical Insurance Expense Settlement Review: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Includes
This category of data covers daily business documents from entities such as medical insurance handling institutions and designated hospital medical insurance offices. Documents include medical insurance settlement lists, expense details, medical record homepages, prescriptions, rejection lists, and settlement reconciliation statements.

Data sources include the national medical security information platform, hospital HIS systems and medical insurance interfaces, medical insurance catalogs and coding libraries, and cross-regional medical treatment settlement platforms. The update frequency is once daily.

Data fields include diagnosis and treatment item and drug and consumable codes, expense amounts, insured person information, settlement status, and more. Consistency verification must be completed by matching official coding libraries. The category supports single or batch processing of medical expense payment-related information.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the "Citation Sources and Traceability" Link
The multi-source daily update feature of this category of data requires citation traceability to be bound to real-time synchronized official data sources. This avoids citing expired or incorrect medical insurance catalogs and settlement data.

The diverse structure of business documents requires traceability systems to adapt to field association logic for different documents such as settlement lists and prescriptions. This ensures accurate traceability association between expense details and corresponding prescriptions or medical records.

The single or batch processing business model requires traceability to support source marking for single expense records and batch settlement tasks.

Under strict compliance requirements, the accuracy of traceability directly affects the credibility of review results. Every citation must accurately correspond to the original business document and data source.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Knowledge base citation errors appear in chat responses. The interface returns "citation data format exception". The root cause is that the `responseData` parameter is not configured according to the field structure of category documents, resulting in extracted settlement details and rejection list fields that do not match the expected format.
- Input and response citations from knowledge base searches are forcibly displayed in responses and cannot be hidden. The root cause is that the associated switch for the `detail` parameter is not configured correctly, causing the system to display all intermediate search content by default.
- Insufficient token count for citation sources leads to truncation of key codes. The traceability results are missing drug and consumable code fields. The root cause is that the value of `quoteMaxToken` is too low, failing to cover the complete content length of a single medical insurance settlement document.
