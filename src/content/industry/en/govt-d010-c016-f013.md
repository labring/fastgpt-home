# Party Building Learning Q&A for Medical Insurance Expense Settlement Audit: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for this category include the national medical insurance information platform, hospital HIS and medical insurance interfaces, medical insurance catalog and coding library, and cross-regional medical treatment settlement platform.
Daily business documents cover medical insurance settlement lists, expense details, medical record homepages, prescriptions, rejection lists, and settlement reconciliation statements.
Fields include diagnosis and treatment project codes, drug and consumable codes, expense amounts, insured person identity identifiers, settlement status, and more.
Data is updated daily. Corresponding documents are only generated according to actual business processes, with no fixed statistical threshold.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
The daily data update requirement means embedded Q&A applications must support daily synchronization of the latest medical insurance settlement documents and catalog data. This prevents use of expired information that reduces audit accuracy.
The multi-type associated field document structure means shared Q&A applications must limit calls to a specified set of business documents. This avoids mixing irrelevant content that disrupts audit judgments.
Permission differences across user groups mean embedded scenarios must adapt to identity control requirements, while ensuring access security for shared links.
The business complexity of medical insurance settlement audit also means embedded Q&A applications must support long-text associated queries. This prevents matching errors caused by insufficient context.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- The conversation icon on the embedded page does not display as customized, and the default icon is used instead. Cause: The conversation icon parameter was not configured correctly, or the application bound to the `shareId` did not complete icon upload.
- After embedding the FastGPT chat window in a Vue3 project, Q&A results returned by the model cannot be obtained. Cause: The cross-domain message event of the iframe was not listened to correctly, or the front-end project did not configure cross-domain permissions allowing the FastGPT domain.
- The shared link does not use identity verification, and unauthorized sites can directly access the party building Q&A content for medical insurance expense settlement audit. Cause: The shared link identity authentication function was not enabled, or the allowed embedding domain whitelist was configured incorrectly.

## How to verify successful configuration
- Open the shared link bound to the specified `shareId`, and verify that the conversation icon matches the design for medical insurance business scenarios.
- Send a test query on the embedded front-end page, such as "Common rejection reasons for medical insurance settlement audit". Confirm that accurate answers from associated documents are returned.
- Attempt to access the embedded Q&A application from a domain not added to the whitelist. Confirm that the application fails to load normally or prompts insufficient permissions.
- Wait 24 hours, then refresh the embedded page. Confirm that the latest updated medical insurance settlement-related document content is available.
