# Party Building Learning Q&A for Medical Institution Supervision and Public Health Inspection: Conversation Logs and Auditing

This document is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What does the data for this category look like?
Data for this category primarily comes from health supervision information systems, medical institution electronic registration systems, complaint reporting platforms, and law enforcement inspection records. Daily processed documents include supervision inspection forms, medical institution practice licenses, rectification notices, supervision opinions, and penalty case files. Data updates occur on a monthly basis. Core fields include institution qualification number, inspection time, rectification requirements, penalty results, and other relevant fields. The original field format of corresponding documents must be matched.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on conversation logs and auditing?
Data sources for this category are scattered and linked to multiple types of compliance documents. Conversation logs must link to unique identifiers such as institution qualification numbers and inspection record numbers, to ensure traceability to original law enforcement or regulatory data during audits. The monthly update frequency requires log retention periods to align with regulatory filing requirements. Logs must support filtering by law enforcement batches and rectification cycles. Field differences across document types are significant. Logs must mark the document category involved in the conversation, to avoid confusion between records from different business segments during audits. Errors in law enforcement and rectification links directly relate to institutional practice risks. Audits must fully record the complete conversation context and operation nodes.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: After configuring logs to save to a file, log files are not generated or have missing content. Cause: Incorrect read/write permissions for the log output path, or failure to enable the full recording switch.
- Symptom: The annotation field in conversation logs cannot link to the corresponding regulatory document number. Cause: Failure to bind `chatId` to the law enforcement inspection record number or institution qualification number, causing logs to be disconnected from business data.
- Symptom: Stuttering occurs when loading the party building learning Q&A knowledge base, and associated logs show batch processing-related errors. Cause: Failure to adjust conversation context loading parameters based on the average length of documents in this category, leading to excessive loaded document fragments in a single session exceeding system limits.
