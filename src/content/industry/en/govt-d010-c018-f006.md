# Party Building Learning Q&A for Insurance Registration and Payment Verification: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data covers core business information related to insurance registration and payment verification. Daily documents include insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification forms, and personal benefit records. Data sources include social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. The data is updated daily. Data fields include unified subject identifiers, personnel identity information, payment verification parameters, benefit record association items, and others. The format complies with government business standards, with no additional non-government standard fields.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" link
Data sources span multiple government systems. The specific source system of each citation must be clearly marked during the traceability link to avoid confusion over responsibilities and rights. The daily update feature requires cited content to be synchronized with the latest business data. Near-real-time data source pull logic must be configured to ensure compliance and accuracy of Q&A content. The fixed and standardized document structure and fields allow limiting citations to only extract government standard fields, reducing interference from non-business information. At the same time, the data is directly linked to personal benefits and payment compliance. Core fields such as complete subject identifiers and verification time must be retained during citation traceability; otherwise, subsequent business verification will be affected.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Unassociated citation content appears in conversations. Cause: No data source filtering rules are configured, resulting in recall of external unrelated social insurance business documents.
- Phenomenon: Cited content is truncated and cannot display complete verification information. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is set too low, truncating core fields such as payment base and verification date.
- Phenomenon: A large number of low-relevance personnel change records appear in mixed retrieval scenarios. Cause: The similarity threshold is set improperly; too high filters out effectively associated documents, while too low introduces irrelevant content.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Initiate a Party Building learning Q&A that includes the name of the insured unit, check the source annotations in the citation module, and confirm that only business documents of this category are displayed.
- Trigger a query containing payment verification content, check whether the cited content includes complete government standard fields with no redundant non-business information.
- Adjust the similarity threshold and initiate a test, observe changes in the number of citations, and confirm that the threshold takes effect.
- Check the system logs to confirm that the citation traceability information includes compliance fields such as data source systems and update times.
