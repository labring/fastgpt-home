# Party Building Study Q&A for Social Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Deployment and Upgrade

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data comes from social insurance management systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. The update frequency is daily.
Daily processed documents include social insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel change forms, social insurance premium verification notices, and personal benefit records.
Data fields include personnel identity identifiers, unified social credit codes of insured units, payment bases, verified amounts, benefit record numbers, and other standard human resources and social security business fields such as unit involved personnel count, amount, and date.

## Constraints for Deployment and Upgrade Processes
Daily updated batch business data requires sufficient concurrent connections and storage capacity during deployment, to avoid data synchronization timeouts or loss. Multi-source data docking must be compatible with interface formats of different government systems. Upgrade preparations must reserve data adaptation modules to respond to system iterations.
Accuracy requirements for core fields such as personnel identity and labor relations require completion of field mapping verification during deployment, to prevent business errors caused by data misalignment.
High-frequency reconciliation requirements with tax collection data require configuration of stable cross-system data synchronization links. Core reconciliation processes must not be interrupted during upgrades.
Additionally, the sensitivity of personal benefit record documents requires enabling data encryption storage configurations during deployment, to comply with government data security specifications.

## Configuration Guidelines
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MONGODB_URI` | Configure the address of a government intranet-exclusive MongoDB cluster, set connection pool size to `200 connections` | Support storage of daily batch social insurance registration, personnel change and other unstructured business data, ensuring connection stability under high concurrency |
| `PG_URL` | Connect to a PostgreSQL instance of the local social insurance management system, set connection timeout to `30 seconds` | Store structured business data such as payment verification notices and personal benefit records, complying with intranet access rules of government systems |
| `REDIS_URL` | Configure a government cloud cache instance, set memory quota to `1000 MB` | Cache frequently queried insured unit information, reducing call pressure on backend social insurance management systems |
| `DB_MAX_LINK` | `500 connections` | Adapt to high concurrent connection requirements of daily batch data synchronization, preventing service interruptions caused by connection exhaustion |
| `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` | Connect to the intranet address of a government cloud object storage service | Store document attachments such as social insurance registration forms and payment base declaration forms, complying with government data storage security specifications |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A `connection refused` error appears after starting the FastGPT container, and the configured PostgreSQL instance cannot be connected. Cause: `PG_URL` was not configured as the intranet address of the local social insurance management system, and a public network address was used instead, causing cross-network access failure.
- Phenomenon: Docker image pulling times out, and download cannot be completed even after configuring a domestic mirror source. Cause: A dedicated mirror acceleration address was not configured in the government intranet environment, and public network mirror sources cannot be accessed within the intranet.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval of party building study Q&A returns a large number of irrelevant social insurance premium data, and the number of results does not meet expectations. Cause: An independent knowledge base index was not configured for exclusive documents related to social insurance registration and premium verification, leading to insufficient recall accuracy caused by mixed data.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Execute a locally written batch data synchronization script, check whether the databases corresponding to `MONGODB_URI` and `PG_URL` can normally write and read business test data.
- Call the FastGPT built-in file storage interface, upload a test social insurance registration form, and confirm that the storage service configured via `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT` can normally receive and return files.
- After starting the FastGPT service, access the frontend access address configured via `FE_DOMAIN`, and check whether the interface loads normally and connects to the backend service.
- Simulate a daily batch data update task, run the data synchronization process, and confirm that the connection number configured via `DB_MAX_LINK` does not show exhaustion errors, and the synchronization process completes normally.
