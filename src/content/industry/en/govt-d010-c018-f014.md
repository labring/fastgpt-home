# Party Building Learning Q&A for Insurance Registration and Premium Verification: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents involved in this category include insurance registration forms, payment base declaration forms, personnel addition and reduction forms, social insurance premium verification forms, and personal benefit records. Data is sourced from social insurance handling systems, tax social insurance premium collection systems, market entity registration information, and shared public security and civil affairs data. The update frequency is daily. Data fields include personnel identity identifiers, insurance registration status, payment base, verified amount, and others. Units include person-times, yuan, months, and others. Cross-system integration is required to form a complete business workflow.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "conversation logs and auditing" link
Cross-system data sources require logs to fully record link identifiers for calls to each system, to avoid data tracing gaps. Daily updated high-frequency data requires logs to retain the latest associated records by conversation dimension, to prevent audit failure caused by historical data being overwritten. Structured fields of business documents need to have their field matching status recorded in logs synchronously, to facilitate checking processing results of time-consuming links such as personnel identity and labor relationship. The severity of error consequences requires logs to retain full-link operation traces, including detailed nodes of parameter verification and data comparison, to support problem tracing and responsibility determination.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log Retention Period | 30–90 days | Matches the monthly reconciliation and annual audit cycles of social insurance handling, covering the complete business verification workflow |
| `chatId` Binding Rule | Associate with the unified social credit identifier of the insured unit | Ensures each log is bound to a specific business entity, facilitating tracing of conversation records by unit or individual |
| `responseChatItemId` Association Rule | Bind to the corresponding business document ID | Enables precise mapping between conversation replies and documents such as insurance registration forms and premium verification forms |
| `detail` Log Switch | Enable full field recording | Retains detailed processes of personnel identity verification and base comparison, supporting detailed verification during audits |
| Single-Session Log Storage Limit | 100 MB (calibrated via actual testing) | Prevents storage and reading exceptions caused by oversized single logs, adapting to high-frequency business scenarios |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Input and output parameters are not displayed after adding a log auditing plugin to a workflow. Cause: The binding rule between `chatId` and business data is not configured correctly, causing the plugin to fail to recognize associated fields.
- Phenomenon: The copy button in conversation logs does not respond after being clicked. Cause: The `detail` log switch is not enabled, resulting in empty detailed record content for the copy target.
- Phenomenon: The FastGPT service fails to load after deployment. Cause: No disk quota is configured for log storage, causing service blocking due to excessive storage space occupied by conversation logs.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Log in to the FastGPT v4.17.0 backend, navigate to the log configuration page, and confirm that the `chatId` binding rule is associated with the unified social credit identifier field of the insured unit.
- Initiate a test conversation to trigger log generation, view the log details page, and confirm that `responseChatItemId` is bound to the test business document ID.
- Check the log details to confirm that detailed process records of personnel identity verification and base comparison have been retained after the `detail` switch is enabled.
- Check the system storage monitoring to confirm that single-session logs do not exceed the preset storage upper limit threshold.
