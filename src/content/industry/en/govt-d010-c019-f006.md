# Party Building Learning Q&A for "Double Random, One Public" Regulatory Inspections: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Typical entities are regulatory departments including market supervision, housing and urban-rural development, and ecological environment. Daily documents cover random inspection plans, inspection object lists, inspection item lists, on-site inspection records, rectification notices, and random inspection result announcements. Data sources are the "Double Random, One Public" regulatory platform, enterprise credit information disclosure system, industry regulatory systems, and law enforcement officer directory. Update frequency is typically monthly. Document fields include inspection batch number, unified social credit code, law enforcement officer number, inspection item entries, rectification requirements, and more. The structure varies significantly across document types. For example, random inspection plans focus on planning dimensions, while on-site records focus on details of individual law enforcement actions.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the source citation and traceability link
Dispersed multi-source data requires the traceability link to associate multiple knowledge bases, to ensure coverage of full-process regulatory documents. The monthly update rhythm requires the knowledge base synchronization cycle to match the update frequency, to avoid citing expired random inspection plans or rectification notices. Documents with different structures need to adapt to different retrieval logic, to ensure core fields such as inspection objects and law enforcement information are accurately recalled. The compliance attribute of regulatory law enforcement requires traceability to clearly mark document sources and generation times. Failure to do so will risk regulatory traceability failure. It is also necessary to avoid citing irrelevant data, which may cause repeated disturbances to enterprises or non-compliant extraction issues.

## Three Common Incorrect Configurations
- Enabling reranking results in empty citations. This manifests as zero results returned by knowledge base search, with no available fragments in the traceability link. The cause is an overly high reranking threshold, which filters out compliant but low-relevance regulatory data fragments that originally met requirements.
- Failing to set `quoteMaxToken` to match document length. This manifests as cited fragments being truncated, making it impossible to fully display inspection items or rectification requirements. The cause is that the parameter value does not match the actual content scale of regulatory documents.
- Failing to enable the `responseData` configuration. This manifests as answers not including document source identifiers, which cannot meet compliance requirements for regulatory traceability. The cause is that relevant settings for returning source data are not enabled.
