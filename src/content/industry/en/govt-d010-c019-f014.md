# Party Building Learning Q&A for "Random Inspection and Public Disclosure" Supervision: Conversation Logs and Audit

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from the "Random Inspection and Public Disclosure" supervision platform, enterprise credit information public disclosure system, industry supervision systems, and law enforcement officer directory. The update frequency is once per month. Daily generated documents include inspection plans, inspection target lists, inspection item lists, on-site inspection records, rectification notices, and inspection result public disclosures. Common data fields include inspection target identifiers, law enforcement officer information, inspection time periods, inspection item content, rectification requirements, and public disclosure status. No custom units are used, and all field formats follow supervision business standards.

## Constraints Imposed on Conversation Logs and Audit by These Characteristics
The monthly data update rhythm requires conversation logs and audit to complete archiving and verification on a monthly basis. This prevents loss of historical associated records due to data iteration. Data from multiple system sources requires logs to associate the unique identifier of each corresponding platform. This ensures traceability during cross-system calls. Diverse document types involve supervision public disclosure links. Audit must cover the full conversation chain from plan generation to result public disclosure. This prevents situations where inspection results are not synchronized to logs. The binding relationship between law enforcement officers and inspection targets must be retained in logs. This ensures that the compliance of the supervision process can be traced.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind to the unique identifier of the inspection task | Align with the task ID of the "Random Inspection and Public Disclosure" supervision platform, to directly associate conversations with specific inspection tasks |
| `responseChatItemId` | Associate the document ID of the on-site inspection record | Correspond to the unique system number of the on-site inspection record, to facilitate quick retrieval of corresponding business documents during audit |
| `detail` | Store the unified social credit code of the inspection target and law enforcement officer number | Fully retain core fields of supervision business, to meet traceability requirements for compliance audit |
| Log Archiving Cycle | 180 days | Match the monthly data update rhythm of this category, covering full logs of at least 6 update cycles |
| Audit Verification Limit | Top 20 conversation records | Adapt to the typical conversation rounds of a single inspection task, to avoid excessive occupation of audit resources |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Symptom: The interface prompts "No permission to operate this conversation record". Cause: `chatId` is not bound to the permission scope of the corresponding law enforcement officer, causing the audit link to fail to identify the legitimate operating subject.
- Symptom: Refreshing the conversation page displays a new conversation, but background logs still show historical records. Cause: The association logic between `responseChatItemId` and the front-end session is not configured correctly, causing the front-end session to fail to load the bound inspection task logs.
- Symptom: The login-free access conversation window has no historical records, but corresponding records exist in the system logs. Cause: No temporary identifier of the login-free user is retained in the `detail` field, causing session association failure.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- In FastGPT v4.17.0, enter the `chatId` of any inspection task, verify that the conversation log can directly associate with the inspection plan document on the supervision platform.
- Trigger a simulated inspection conversation, confirm that the `detail` field contains the unified social credit code of the inspection target and the law enforcement officer number.
- Refresh the front-end conversation page, verify that historical conversation records load normally, with no new conversation pop-up.
- View the system audit panel, confirm that all conversation records are associated with the corresponding `responseChatItemId` and business documents.
