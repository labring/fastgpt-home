# Party Building Learning Q&A for Centralized Treasury Payment and Reimbursement Review: Vector Models and Indexing

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category comes from centralized treasury payment systems, unified budget management systems, electronic invoice platforms, and contract management systems. It covers daily documents including payment applications, fund appropriation plans, invoices, contracts, acceptance receipts, reimbursement approval forms, and payment vouchers. Documents contain fields such as budget indicators, economic classifications, payment amounts, and approval process records. Data is updated daily. Individual business documents are linked to clear fund payment and compliance review nodes. They can also be associated with party building learning-related text content.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Vector Models and Indexing Link
Multi-source heterogeneous data sources require the vector embedding step to adapt to different formats of business documents and party building learning text. This avoids embedding bias across systems. The daily update frequency requires indexes to support incremental construction and scheduled synchronization. This prevents resource usage and delays caused by full reconstruction. Documents include clear business fields such as budget indicators and economic classifications. Indexes must associate corresponding metadata during recall to ensure recall results accurately match business scenarios. Individual businesses are linked to compliance review nodes. Index recall logic must align with the core needs of compliance verification, reducing interference from irrelevant content.

## How to Configure Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the average length of individual business documents and associated party building text for this category, balancing embedding accuracy and recall efficiency |
| Overlap Length | 100–200 characters | Prevents loss of cross-chunk business association information, such as continuous descriptions of budget indicators and coherent party building policy content |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Aligns with the high-confidence requirements of compliance verification for this category, filtering irrelevant recall results |
| `defaultToken` | 4096 | Adapts to the default input length of mainstream embedding models, covering the total length of individual business documents and associated party building text |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Meets the maximum length limit for context splicing in complex scenarios, covering the complete content of multiple associated documents |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Balances recall accuracy and system response speed, meeting the information acquisition needs of daily review |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The index model dropdown list is empty, and the corresponding embedding model cannot be selected. Cause: Environment dependencies for the local embedding model are not configured, or the FastGPT version in use does not support the corresponding model.
- Phenomenon: The number of vector recall results is far lower than expected. Relevant business documents cannot be matched. Cause: The chunk length is set too large, causing some long documents to fail full embedding. Or the similarity threshold is set too high, filtering valid recall results.
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant non-business party building content appears in search results. It is not linked to specific payment review nodes. Cause: The association between business fields and party building text is not bound during indexing. The recall logic does not align with scenario requirements.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Use the vector embedding tool in FastGPT v4.17.0 to upload a single typical business document and associated party building learning text. Verify that the embedding task runs without errors.
- Launch compliance questions related to party building learning. Check whether the corresponding business documents and party building content are included in the recall results.
- View the index synchronization logs. Confirm that the daily incremental update task runs normally, with no timeout or failure records.
- Adjust the similarity threshold. Verify that the number of recall results changes in line with expected adjustment logic.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the user's own samples.
