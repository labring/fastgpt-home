# Party Building Learning Q&A for Land Use Rights, Mining Rights and State-owned Property Rights Transactions: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What data for this category looks like
Data typically comes from national public resource trading platforms, natural resource trading systems, property right exchange systems, and state-owned assets supervision systems, with a daily update frequency. Commonly handled daily documents include transfer announcements, trading guidelines, bid application forms, margin payment vouchers, transaction confirmation letters, listing result announcements, and property right transfer specifications. Most documents contain fixed fields such as subject ownership, trading terms, amount, and process nodes. Some documents include structured content like plot coordinates and property right details.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the document parsing and chunking stage
Batch documents updated daily usually require stable parsing efficiency to avoid single-task timeouts disrupting the overall workflow. Multiple document types have distinct structural differences, so different chunking logic is typically used to preserve contextual connections. The parsing accuracy of core trading fields such as ownership and amount directly impacts the effectiveness of content for party building learning Q&A. Chunking that splits critical information must be avoided. Some documents include structured attachments, so the link between attachment content and main text must be maintained to ensure full association of policy requirements and trading scenarios during Q&A.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Uploading a 3 MB PDF results in an error, with the interface returning `{"detail":"Error message}`. The cause is incorrect configuration of `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` or `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, leading to large file parsing timeouts or exceeding upload limits.
- After setting a custom delimiter, chunking results either merge multiple paragraphs or split a single paragraph. The cause is that the `chunkSize` value does not match the document paragraph length, and chunking parameters were not adjusted based on the length of ownership clauses in trading documents.
- Deploying Marker via Docker results in PDF processing errors. The cause is incorrect configuration of environment variables related to `customPdfParse`, or incompatibility between the parsing tool and the current FastGPT version, leading to failed structured attachment parsing.

## How to confirm the configuration is properly set
- Use a service deployed with FastGPT v4.17.0, upload a single property right transfer specification under 1 MB, check the parsed chunking results to confirm that ownership clauses are not split.
- Batch upload three different types of trading documents, check that no parsing tasks time out, confirm that the `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` configuration is effective.
- Adjust the `chunkSize` parameter, compare the contextual integrity of chunking results to confirm that it meets the requirement of linking policies and trading scenarios in party building learning Q&A.
- After enabling `customPdfParse`, check whether structured fields in the PDF are correctly extracted, with no missing or misaligned content.
