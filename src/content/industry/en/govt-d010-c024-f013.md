# Party Building Learning Q&A for Student Financial Aid and Subsidy Review: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
The typical users of this category are education bureau financial aid centers, school student affairs offices or financial aid offices, and finance departments. Daily business documents include family financial difficulty assessment forms, financial aid application forms, scholarship and grant review forms, distribution lists, and public notice materials. Data sources include the national student financial aid management information system, school student status systems, civil affairs low-income and special hardship data, and finance payment systems. The data updates once per semester. The document structure primarily uses structured table fields, including core content such as student identity, family financial situation, financial aid eligibility, and distribution information, with no additional statistical percentage data.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding link
Business data from multiple systems requires embedded Q&A services to connect to a unified internal permission system, to prevent cross-system sensitive data leaks. The once-per-semester data update cycle requires the embedded knowledge base synchronization cycle to match the business update cycle, preventing the use of expired financial aid policies and list data. Structured document fields require embedded Q&A retrieval to accurately match table field information, avoiding result deviations from generalized retrieval. Consequences of errors include incorrect or missed financial aid distribution and student complaints. Embedded Q&A results must be traceable, ensuring every returned entry can be linked to the original business document. The business scenario targets internal staff, so embedded sharing must restrict access permissions, allowing only authorized personnel to use the service.

## Three common configuration errors
- Issue: After embedding a business system via iframe, the frontend cannot retrieve Q&A results returned by the model. Cause: The callback interface corresponding to `shareId` is not configured, or sharing callback permission is not enabled in the FastGPT backend.
- Issue: After deploying the open-source version, the embedded page allows unauthenticated public access. Cause: A public `shareId` was used directly for embedding, without enabling internal identity authentication configuration.
- Issue: A timeout error appears after the embedded page loads. Cause: No reasonable timeout period was set, and multi-system data retrieval time exceeded the default threshold, causing the request to terminate.

## How to verify the configuration is complete
- Open the embedded page of the business system, enter party building-related student financial aid policy questions, and confirm that accurate content from the corresponding knowledge base is returned.
- Use an unauthorized internal account to access the embedded page, confirming that identity authentication interception is triggered and the Q&A function cannot be used normally.
- View the call logs for the `shareId` in the FastGPT backend, confirming that the number of interface calls matches the business system's access volume.
- Simulate a semester data update, reload the embedded page, and confirm that updated policies and list content can be loaded.
