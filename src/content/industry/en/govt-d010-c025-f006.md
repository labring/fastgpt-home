# Party Building Learning Q&A for Safety Production Hazard Inspection and Governance: Quote Source and Traceability

## What data for this category looks like
This category's data comes from safety production supervision systems, enterprise hazard self-inspection systems, law enforcement inspection records, and accident statistics systems. The update frequency is daily. Daily documents include hazard inspection lists, hazard management ledgers, rectification notices, review records, and major hazard suspension and supervision orders. Data fields cover hazard ID, inspection time, rectification responsible person, rectification deadline, review result, and more. The data structure centers on hazard closed-loop management, with no additional statistical numbers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the quote source and traceability link
The daily update frequency of this category's data requires the quote traceability link to support scheduled or real-time data source synchronization, to ensure the timeliness of quoted content. The existence of multiple structured and semi-structured documents requires traceability to accurately match document types and corresponding fields, to avoid confusing hazard data from different links. Data from multiple sources requires clear marking of source systems, to ensure the traceability of quoted content. The existence of core fields such as hazard rectification and review requires traceability to link corresponding business node information; returning only the document itself cannot meet business needs. The serious consequences of business errors require the quote accuracy of the traceability link to meet business compliance requirements.

## How to configure settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | Adapts to the average content length of single hazard-related documents in this category, to avoid truncating key fields such as rectification deadlines and review results |
| `responseData` | Includes quote ID, document type, business node identifier | Matches the traceability needs of multiple types of business documents, to facilitate linking full-process information of hazard inspection, rectification, and review |
| `detail` | Enabled | Used to return complete document fields, covering core business information such as hazard ID and rectification responsible person |
| Single search limit | Top 5 entries | Aligns with the business scope of single-round hazard inspection, to avoid introducing redundant historical hazard data |
| Data source synchronization frequency | Daily | Matches the daily update rhythm of this category's data, to ensure quoted content is consistent with the latest business data |

## Three common configuration errors
- Garbled quote identifiers appear in conversation outputs, displayed as abnormal symbols. This occurs when the return format of `responseData` is not configured correctly, leading to abnormal escape logic for quote markers.
- After connecting the knowledge base, the tool call module cannot return quote information. This happens when the `detail` configuration item is not enabled, or when `responseData` is not specified to include core traceability fields such as quote ID and document type.
- For hazard questions that do not exist in the knowledge base, references to hazard-related documents are still returned. This is caused by not configuring question matching verification logic, leading to recall of irrelevant historical hazard data.

## How to confirm correct configuration
- Initiate a test question containing specific hazard inspection content, check whether the output includes the quote identifier and field information of the corresponding document.
- Trigger a knowledge base retrieval for a non-existent hazard question, confirm that no irrelevant hazard document references are returned.
- View the `responseData` field returned by the interface, confirm that it includes traceability-related content such as quote ID and document type.
- Simulate a daily updated hazard data scenario, confirm that quoted content is consistent with the latest rectification and review records.
