# Party Building Learning Q&A for Safety Production Hazard Inspection and Governance: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data originates from safety production supervision systems, enterprise hazard self-inspection systems, law enforcement inspection records, and accident statistics systems. It updates daily. Typical documents include hazard inspection checklists, hazard management ledgers, rectification notices, review records, and major hazard suspension and supervision orders. Documents include fields such as hazard ID, inspection subject, rectification responsible person, rectification deadline, and review status. All data is generated across the full hazard inspection, rectification, and closure workflow, with a clear structure tightly bound to business processes.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" Workflow
Daily updated hazard data means the embedded component’s cache duration should not be too long. This prevents display of expired inspection or rectification results.
Structured documents such as ledgers and notices require sharing and embedding to support filtering Q&A context by business fields like hazard level and rectification status. This aligns with the operation logic of business systems.
Internal data from multiple sources requires permission isolation. Embedding configurations must link to user identity verification in the business system, to block unauthorized access to sensitive hazard information.
Documents related to major hazards have high sensitivity. Sharing links must include permission settings or validity periods to prevent information leaks.

## How to Set the Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shareId` | Bind to the unique identifier of hazard records in the business system | Achieve precise association between Q&A content and specific hazard data |
| Sharing link validity period | 1 day | Match the daily update frequency of hazard data, avoid access to expired hazard information |
| Embedded window permission control | Verify the identity of business system users | Adapt to the permission isolation requirements for internal data from multiple sources |
| Custom sharing icon | Match the visual specifications of emergency management scenarios | Ensure visual consistency in government public affairs scenarios |
| Cache duration | 4 hours | Balance data timeliness and system load, adapt to the update rhythm of daily updated data |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- The sharing icon automatically reverts to the default style within a short time. Cause: The custom icon file format does not meet platform requirements, or the configuration was not saved after upload, leading to a reset.
- A script error appears when opening the embedded window with Internet Explorer (Trident kernel) browser. Cause: The embedded code does not support older browser syntax features, or compatible mode was not configured to activate.
- Business system users cannot associate hazard data with their corresponding permissions when starting Q&A. Cause: Identity verification logic was not configured, or the bound `shareId` is not linked to the business system user’s permission scope.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Copy the generated sharing link, embed it in the business system’s test environment, and confirm that Q&A content matching the corresponding hazard record is displayed.
- Adjust the sharing link validity period setting, and confirm the link cannot be accessed normally after exceeding the set duration.
- Upload the custom sharing icon, and confirm the sharing button icon in the embedded window updates to the target style.
- Access the embedded window using an unauthorized user identity, and confirm sensitive Q&A content related to major hazards is not visible.
