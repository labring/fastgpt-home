# Party Building Learning Q&A for Engineering Construction Project Approval: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category comes from engineering construction project approval management systems, construction drawing review systems, enterprise qualification databases and credit platforms. The update frequency is daily. Daily documents include construction permit applications, construction drawing review qualification certificates, quality and safety supervision procedures, and completion acceptance record forms. Most documents are structured approval forms and unstructured approval text. Fields cover approval nodes, participating construction unit information, qualification levels, acceptance results. Some documents include scanned attachments with no unified fixed format.

## Constraints on Vector Models and Indexing
This category’s data is scattered across multiple business systems. Daily incremental updates require indexing processes to support batch synchronization logic. Daily documents include structured forms and unstructured scanned files, so multi-format content extraction must be supported. Information such as approval nodes and participating units’ qualifications has strong correlations. Indexing must retain field-level associations to enable accurate Q&A. Daily update volume is stable, so indexing build time must be controlled to avoid disrupting normal approval business workflows. Additionally, the party building learning Q&A scenario requires linking approval business and party building-related content. Indexing must cover both business processes and party building policy requirements to prevent content fragmentation. Index accuracy must be strictly controlled to prevent issues such as unlicensed construction, construction delays, or failed completion acceptance caused by matching errors.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Index construction always gets stuck on the final set of tasks. This occurs when the batch synchronization logic for daily incremental updates is not adapted, causing the system to repeatedly retry unfinished incremental packages.
- Knowledge base creation fails and the process gets stuck at the indexing step. This happens when the multi-format parsing module is not configured correctly, making it impossible to process scanned attachments in construction permit applications.
- After adding a new embedding model, indexing completes but search tests throw errors. This is caused by failing to synchronously update the `defaultToken` parameter for model calls, resulting in context length exceeding the model’s supported range.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Upload a single completion acceptance record form, check that chunking results retain complete approval nodes and party building-related content with no content truncation.
- Trigger an incremental synchronization task, confirm that the indexing build progress proceeds normally with no long-term stagnation at any stage.
- Input party building-related approval Q&A for testing, verify that returned results include correct approval processes and participating unit qualification information.
- Call the newly added embedding model for search testing, confirm that matching results are returned normally with no error prompts.
