# Party Building Learning Q&A for Engineering Construction Project Approval: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category originates from the engineering construction project approval management system, construction drawing review system, enterprise qualification database, and public credit platform. The update frequency is daily. Daily documents include construction permit application materials, construction drawing review qualification certificates, quality and safety supervision procedures, and completion acceptance record forms. Data fields cover approval node status, participating construction unit qualifications, project leader information, material record numbers, and other relevant items. All fields come from standard output of official business systems, with no custom special units.

## Constraints on Citation Sources and Traceability
Data for this category mostly comes from official business systems and is updated daily. Citation and traceability processes must use the latest version of approval materials to avoid citing expired information. Daily documents cover the full engineering approval workflow. Material fields across different process links have clear corresponding relationships. During traceability, links must be established to specific approval nodes and material numbers to ensure citation accuracy. Fields such as participating construction unit qualifications and personnel information are sourced from a unified qualification database. During retrieval, field dimensions must be strictly matched to prevent cross-category confusion. Incorrect citations may delay approval processes, so the traceability link must be bound to the unique identifier of the original data.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The Q&A result only displays 1 knowledge base citation, and cannot show multiple approval material sources. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` or single search limit configuration was not adjusted, limiting the number of recalled documents.
- Phenomenon: An insufficient citation quota prompt is displayed when calling the knowledge base. Cause: The knowledge base synchronization task was not configured according to the daily update frequency, causing redundant old data to occupy too much quota.
- Phenomenon: The cited approval material fields are empty or cannot be traced back to the original system. Cause: The field binding configuration of `responseData` was not enabled, and the unique identifier field of the engineering approval system was not associated.

- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, then observe if the number of recalled documents matches the configured result.
