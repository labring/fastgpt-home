# Party Building Learning Q&A for Engineering Construction Project Bidding: Conversation Logs and Audit

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data comes from the national public resource trading platform, local trading platforms, project approval systems, and bidding supervision systems. Updates are released daily. Daily documents include bidding announcements, qualification pre-qualification documents, bidding documents, bid submission documents, bid evaluation reports, successful bid candidate public notices, and successful bid result announcements. Core fields cover section division, qualification requirements, bid evaluation expert records, objection and complaint content, bill of quantities, and other items. The data structure follows the engineering project construction and bidding law framework.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Conversation Logs and Audit Link
Daily data updates require conversation logs to be archived each day and linked to bidding documents matching the business date. Differentiated fields across multiple types of business documents require the audit link to match document types when extracting corresponding core information. For example, associate bid evaluation expert records from bid evaluation reports, and extract section division information from bidding announcements. Usage scenarios for regulatory entities require logs to support full-link traceability and prevent arbitrary modification. Strict accountability requirements for the bidding process require the audit link to retain all `chatId`, `responseChatItemId`, and `detail` parameters from conversation calls, ensuring every operation can be traced to a specific business link.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: GPT-4o-mini call error logs pop up during the party building learning Q&A link, and relevant information is not captured in the logs. Cause: Full recording of model call logs is not enabled in the conversation log configuration, resulting in error information not being written to the log chain.
- Symptom: Knowledge base Q&A pair extraction task gets stuck during training, and no relevant task records appear in the call logs. Cause: Task trigger recording for logs is not configured, resulting in training status not being synchronized to audit logs.
- Symptom: Global variable history records cannot be modified or traced, and audit work cannot associate past conversation content. Cause: History record tracking for the `detail` parameter is not enabled, resulting in incomplete retention of conversation context.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Launch a simulated party building learning-related bidding Q&A conversation, check whether the `chatId` bound to the current session exists in the logs.
- Check the log export function, confirm that structured files containing `responseChatItemId` and `detail` parameters can be exported.
- Trigger a simulated objection complaint handling process, verify that audit snapshots are generated at the corresponding business nodes.
- View the global variable history record module, confirm that the complete chain of conversations can be traced.
