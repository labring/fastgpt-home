# Party Building Learning Q&A for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents for this category include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials. Data is sourced from the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database, and is updated automatically daily. Data fields include entity name, residence address, shareholder information, license type, and other relevant items. Units mainly use Chinese characters and numeric codes. Most document structures are standardized tables or structured text.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability process
The data sources for this category are mostly official business systems. Citation traceability requires binding unique identifiers of official data sources to ensure authority. The daily update feature requires traceability mechanisms to synchronously link data version markers, preventing references to outdated registration and licensing content. The coexistence of standardized documents and unstructured text requires traceability to support multi-format original text positioning and field matching. Fields cover key information such as entity qualification, license type, and other critical details. Traceability requires accurate matching of exclusive content for corresponding entities to prevent confusion of registration and licensing information across different market entities.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Daily documents are mostly standardized tables and paragraph text; this length can cover complete registration and licensing clauses and entity information blocks |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents critical information from being truncated during chunking, ensuring traceability can locate complete residence certificate or shareholder resolution content |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Requires distinguishing similar entity names and license types to avoid incorrect traceability matching of documents from different market entities |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 results | Key information for this category is mostly concentrated in a small number of core documents; too many results increase the risk of traceability confusion |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 2000–2500 tokens | Must accommodate complete original text fragments from establishment registration applications or food business license applications to ensure complete traceability content |
| `responseData` | Enable original text fragments and data source markers | Must clearly return the source system and document type of referenced content to meet the traceability requirements of party building learning Q&A |
| `detail` | Enable traceability details | Must return specific locations of referenced content and data source metadata to facilitate verification of source authenticity |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After associating a knowledge base and launching a party building Q&A, the interface returns a string of red numeric errors. Cause: The dedicated data source for market entity registration and licensing is not correctly bound, or the `quoteMaxToken` value exceeds the system's allowed token limit.
- Phenomenon: After the Q&A outputs content, the corresponding referenced original text fragments and data source markers cannot be viewed. Cause: The `responseData` configuration item is not enabled, and the traceability data return function is not activated.
- Phenomenon: After associating multiple knowledge bases, traceability results match irrelevant market entity documents. Cause: The similarity threshold is set too low, or the single search limit is too high, leading to confusion of similar entity names and license type information.
