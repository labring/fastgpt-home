# Party Building Learning Q&A for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Forms and Interactions

## What data this category includes
Data sources for this category include the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. The update frequency is daily. Commonly processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and more. Core fields cover subject qualification information, business access conditions, license types, and material verification items. The data structure primarily combines structured form fields and unstructured document attachments.

## How these characteristics impose constraints on forms and interactions
The daily data update requirement means form interactions must support real-time calls to the latest residence information database and enterprise credit information data for pre-verification. Multiple types of document attachments require interactions to support differentiated parsing and adaptation, matching field extraction rules for different documents such as establishment registration applications and articles of association. Core fields related to subject qualification and business access judgment must use the `formInput` and `userSelect` components to complete format verification and option limitation, preventing invalid submissions. The correction and change process requires interactions to clearly display specific fields that need adjustment, reducing rejection risks.

| `formInput` | Set format verification for core fields, limit input length to 200–500 characters, calibrate verification logic based on actual testing | Subject name, residence address and other core required registration items. Format verification can filter invalid inputs in advance, matching the field requirements of business documents |

Preset options can reduce manual judgment errors, matching the classification rules of business processes |
| Attachment upload size limit | Maximum 1000 MB per file, total upload limit 5000 MB | Commonly processed documents such as articles of association and shareholder resolutions are mostly PDF or structured text. This range can cover common document sizes |
| Interaction timeout setting | 600 seconds | Data sources include calls to multiple systems, a longer timeout can avoid verification failures caused by network fluctuations |
| Field required verification | Force verification for shareholder information and residence certificate fields | Missing these fields will directly lead to registration rejection. Forced verification can improve submission pass rate |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: After configuring the `userSelect` option group, the license categories displayed on the frontend do not match current business requirements. Cause: The option source was not synchronized to the latest license classification data, resulting in options lagging behind regulatory requirements.
- Symptom: Parsing errors are triggered after uploading documents such as articles of association or shareholder resolutions, and an invalid file address prompt is returned. Cause: The system storage path after file upload was not correctly mapped in the workflow, and the frontend temporary address was directly used for parsing.
- Symptom: The `formInput` field does not trigger format verification, allowing submission of characters that do not meet requirements. Cause: The field verification rule was not enabled in the configuration, or the matching conditions of the verification logic were set incorrectly.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Enter the form configuration interface, and confirm that the configuration items of the `formInput` and `userSelect` components match business field requirements.
- Upload standard format documents such as articles of association and residence certificates, and confirm that the parsing process can normally extract fields without errors.
- Initiate a conversation interaction, and confirm that content is pushed in segments via streaming.
- Simulate submitting a form with missing required fields, and confirm that the system displays corresponding correction prompts.
