# Party Building Learning Q&A for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for party building learning Q&A related to market entity registration and licensing comes from four sources: the enterprise registration full-process electronic system, the national enterprise credit information publicity system, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database.
Daily processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and other related materials.
Data updates occur daily.
Data fields cover structured and unstructured content tied to subject qualifications and business permits, and strictly adhere to the field specifications of official systems.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" workflow
The data for party building learning Q&A related to market entity registration and licensing comes from scattered sources and updates daily.
Knowledge bases used for sharing and embedding must synchronize official system data daily. Without this sync, Q&A results will not reflect currently valid information.
Daily processed materials include multiple structured and unstructured documents. When embedding, limit retrieval scope to only registration and licensing-related content and party building learning materials. This prevents irrelevant information from being included.
Errors during data processing can lead to registration rejection or operational restrictions. Q&A results after embedding must exactly match official document content to ensure accuracy.
The total data volume is large. Control the number of retrieval results during embedding to avoid loading timeouts or page freezes.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: An embedded iframe page shows no content, but backend logs indicate normal interface execution. Cause: No cross-domain whitelist is configured for the embedded page, so the browser blocks FastGPT embedding requests.
- Phenomenon: After submitting a party building learning question about registration and licensing, the returned result does not match the corresponding document. Cause: The retrieval scope is not limited to registration and licensing-specific documents, so the search covers the full knowledge base.
- Phenomenon: The embedded page times out while loading. Cause: No reasonable single search limit is set, so too much data loads and exceeds the page's threshold.
