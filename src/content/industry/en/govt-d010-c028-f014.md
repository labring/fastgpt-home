# Party Building Learning Q&A for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for this category originates from the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the national enterprise credit information public disclosure system, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. It is updated once daily.
Daily processed documents include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar materials.
Core fields cover entity name, residence information, shareholder information, license type, registration status, and other relevant items.
All data are official application and filing materials from the business entity admission or change phase. They are used for name verification, license determination, correction review, and other business processes.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Conversation Logs and Auditing Workflow
Data is updated daily and includes official filing materials. Conversation logs must fully record the time of each query, associated document versions, and source identifiers. This ensures data timeliness can be traced during audits.
Core fields involve subject qualification and license type. Logs must also record the field names and values mentioned in conversations, to avoid information deviations during audits.
Data from different sources has varying formats. Logs must uniformly store the data source identifiers used for calls, to facilitate troubleshooting of cross-system call exceptions.
Conversations in the party building learning Q&A scenario cover policy explanations and handling guidelines. Logs must retain the complete Q&A chain, including user questions, knowledge base fragments called by the system, and final reply content.
Processes involving corrections and changes include multi-round interactions. Logs must record each round of interaction nodes, to facilitate backtracking of correction reasons and review progress.

## How to Configure the Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `detail` | Enable full field storage | Conversation logs must fully record all parameters and returned content during interactions, to meet audit traceability requirements |
| `chatId` | Bind the unique identifier of the conversation and the corresponding registration business | This links conversations to specific market entity registration applications, facilitating audits by business batches |
| `responseChatItemId` | Generate a unique identifier for each reply | This allows precise location of log entries for single-round interactions, to quickly troubleshoot abnormal replies |
| Log expiration time | 180 days | Meets audit retention cycle requirements for public service scenarios |
| Log export format | JSON structured format | Facilitates integration with audit tools of market supervision systems, enabling batch verification |
| Abnormal log trigger threshold | Calibrated based on actual testing | Must adapt to the update frequency and interaction complexity of data in this category, to avoid false positives or false negatives |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Conversation records remain in the log system after calling the delete conversation interface. Cause: The binding relationship between logs and `chatId` is not configured via the `detail` parameter, causing delete operations to fail to match complete log entries.
- Symptom: Knowledge base original document download links cannot be accessed via nginx proxy. Cause: Log configuration does not enable recording and verification of external link jumps, causing proxy rules to not cover access paths for knowledge base files.
- Symptom: Conversations return no results, and no clear error is shown in server logs. Cause: The `detail` parameter for logs is not configured to enable full request parameter recording, making it impossible to capture external network dependency exceptions during embedding model calls.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Complete
- Initiate a party building learning Q&A that includes a market entity name. Check whether complete interaction records binding `chatId` and business application number exist in the log system.
- Call the delete conversation interface, confirm that all log entries for the corresponding `chatId` are cleared synchronously.
- Trigger a knowledge base original document download, check whether the log records complete external link access paths and response statuses.
- Simulate a scenario where embedding model calls fail, confirm that abnormal logs are fully recorded and include all information from the `detail` parameter.
