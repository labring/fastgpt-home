# Party Building Learning Q&A for Construction Engineering Quality and Safety Supervision: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
This category’s data originates from engineering quality and safety supervision systems, on-site construction site real-name management platforms, supervision logs, and test reports. The update frequency is weekly. Daily documents include supervision plans, supervision inspection records, hidden danger rectification notices, quality problem handling forms, and completion acceptance supervision reports. Most documents combine structured forms and unstructured text. Fields cover hidden danger levels, rectification requirements, acceptance deadlines, and other relevant items. The length of individual documents varies widely, ranging from hundreds to thousands of characters.

## Constraints on Citation Sources and Traceability from These Characteristics
The weekly update requirement means traceability processes must support incremental synchronization and regular full verification. This prevents the use of expired supervision records. The mixed structure of multi-source data requires traceability systems to match both structured form fields and unstructured text content. This ensures accurate citation and positioning for documents such as hidden danger rectification notices and completion acceptance reports. The wide range of document lengths requires traceability text matching logic to adapt to content blocks of varying lengths. It must also associate original data identifiers from corresponding systems to maintain traceability credibility.

This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| `responseData` | Return only citation source metadata and original text snippets | Meets compliance traceability requirements for party building learning Q&A, clearly marks the type and source of cited documents |
| `detail` | Enabled | Used to display specific field information of cited documents, such as rectification deadlines for hidden danger rectification notices, improving traceability accuracy |
| Single search limit | Top 3 results | Matches the weekly updated document scale of this category, avoids retrieving excessive redundant content |
| Knowledge base incremental update cycle | Weekly | Aligns with the update frequency of data in this category, ensures cited content is the latest supervision records |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Setting too small a value for `quoteMaxToken` prevents Q&A pairs from being generated for some documents during Q&A generation. Raw text is displayed directly instead. This occurs because the value does not adapt to the wide length range of documents in this category, truncating core content used to split Q&A pairs.
- Not configuring multi-source associated retrieval limits simple workspace knowledge base applications to retrieving only one document per attempt. It cannot reference supervision records from multiple sources. This occurs because cross-document associated retrieval configuration is not enabled, and only a single data source is bound.
- Not enabling the `detail` configuration item restricts responses to only returning text snippets. It cannot display specific fields of cited documents, such as rectification deadlines for hidden danger rectification notices. This fails to meet precise traceability requirements. This occurs because the detail display configuration is not enabled, and traceability information for structured fields is lost.

## How to Confirm Correct Configuration
- Initiate a quality and safety supervision question related to party building. Check if the response marks the type and source of cited documents. Confirm that the `responseData` configuration is active.
- View the knowledge base update log. Confirm that documents from the most recent week, such as supervision plans and inspection records, have completed incremental synchronization. This aligns with the configured update cycle.
- Test documents of different lengths. Check if complete Q&A pairs are generated. Confirm that the `quoteMaxToken` setting adapts to document lengths.
- View the detail display area of the response. Check if specific fields of cited documents are shown, such as rectification requirements and acceptance deadlines. Confirm that the `detail` configuration is active.
