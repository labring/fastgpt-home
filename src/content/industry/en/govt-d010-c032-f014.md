# Party Building Learning Q&A for Investment Project Approval and Post-Evaluation: Conversation Logs and Audit

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category mainly comes from the state-owned assets supervision and administration commission’s investment supervision system, enterprise ERP and project management systems, industry market data, audit and financial statements. Commonly involved documents include annual investment plans, project proposals, feasibility study reports, due diligence reports, asset evaluation reports, and post-investment evaluation reports. The data update frequency is quarterly. Core fields include project approval number, investment scale, approval date, post-investment evaluation milestones, and others. Field units are adjusted based on the business scenario of the specific document, with no unified fixed format.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Conversation Logs and Audit Processes
The multi-source data and quarterly update characteristics of this category impose multiple constraints on the conversation logs and audit process. Multi-data sources cover internal business systems and external public industry data, requiring logs to be associated with specific data sources and called document fragments to meet the traceability requirements of state-owned asset audits. The quarterly update business rhythm requires log storage periods to match the business traceability period, to avoid premature deletion of key audit materials. The multi-stakeholder investment process requires logs to be divided into access permissions by role, to prevent unauthorized viewing of confidential project information, while ensuring that logs cannot be tampered with and fully retain full-link call records.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Conversation logs are missing, and historical call records cannot be retrieved. Cause: Log storage period is not configured, or the Docker configuration file does not correctly mount the log directory.
- Symptom: A specified agent cannot view conversation logs from other groups. Cause: Role-isolated permission settings are not enabled, or permission group configuration is incorrect.
- Symptom: Token statistics data does not match actual call volume. Cause: Token statistics by application dimension is not configured, or the statistical scope does not cover full-process calls.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Navigate to the log management page in the FastGPT backend, retrieve conversations using the specified project number, and confirm that records corresponding to the target business process are returned.
- Switch test accounts with different roles, verify that only conversation logs within the authorized scope are accessible, and that unauthorized access to records from other projects is blocked.
- View the token statistics panel, confirm that the statistical dimension aligns with the business requirements of the current application, and that call data can be viewed by application dimension.
- Navigate to the detail page of a single conversation, confirm that the `detail` field contains specific called document fragments and parameter information.
