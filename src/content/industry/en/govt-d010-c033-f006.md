# Party Building Learning Q&A for Tendering and Procurement Compliance Management: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data comes from enterprise procurement platforms, public resource trading platforms, supplier databases, contract management systems, audit issue databases, as well as compliance training materials related to party building learning and documents requiring disciplinary inspection, audit and party building work. It is updated daily. Daily documents include procurement plans, demand specifications, bidding documents, bid evaluation records, contracts, acceptance documents, and supplier complaint materials. Data fields cover procurement methods, budget basis, scoring methods, supplier qualification conditions, performance and acceptance status, etc., with no additional statistical figures.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability link
Data sources are scattered and cover two scenarios: procurement and party building learning, which requires citation traceability to associate unique identifiers from different platforms to avoid cross-source confusion. The daily update rhythm requires the traceability link to synchronize the latest procurement, audit and party building compliance data to ensure the timeliness of cited content. The diverse document types require traceability to match the field structures of different documents and accurately locate cited fragments. Strict compliance risk consequences mean that traceability must be able to trace back to the specific location of the original document, and forged citation IDs are not acceptable.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `2000–3000 token` | Matches the single-section citation length requirements of tendering, procurement and party building compliance documents, and avoids truncating key compliance clauses |
| `responseData` | Enable original document metadata return | Used to associate unique identifiers of original documents such as procurement plans, bid evaluation records and party building training materials, to support traceability |
| `detail` | Enable full citation details | Display the specific location of the cited fragment and the source document type, which meets the traceability requirements of audit and party building learning |
| Knowledge base dynamic binding | Bind corresponding procurement and party building compliance knowledge bases according to business scenarios | Adapt to the exclusive data scope of tendering and procurement compliance management, and avoid interference from irrelevant data |
| Citation source filtering | Only retain valid fragments of procurement and party building compliance documents | Eliminate non-compliance data and improve traceability accuracy |

## Three common errors
- When dynamically specifying a knowledge base, there are no optional options for the citation variable in the knowledge base search node. Cause: The knowledge base ID variable was not defined in advance in the workflow, or the variable type does not match the parameter requirements of the knowledge base selection component.
- Forged citation IDs appear in the answers generated by the large model. Cause: The `responseData` configuration was not enabled, so the system cannot generate real citation identifiers, or the knowledge base synchronization is delayed, resulting in missing original document identifiers.
- The output content includes the phrase "Citation Mark: [1]". Cause: The explicit output configuration of citation marks was not turned off, or the formatting rules for citation content were not configured correctly.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Initiate a query related to procurement compliance or party building learning, and check whether the answer is accompanied by the type and identifier of the source document.
- Check the FastGPT v4.17.0 workflow log to confirm that each query is associated with the correct knowledge base data, and there are no invalid cross-knowledge base citations.
- Test the scenario of dynamically binding knowledge bases, and confirm that the citation variable can normally select preset knowledge base parameters.
- Check the output content to confirm that undefined citation mark phrases do not appear.
