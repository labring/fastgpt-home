# Enrollment and Student Status Management: Party Building Learning Q&A and Conversation Logs & Audit

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources for this category include the national primary and secondary school student status management system, local enrollment platforms, household registration and property data, and school academic affairs systems. Update frequency is daily. Daily documents include enrollment application forms, student status information forms, transfer applications, enrollment plans, admission rosters, and student status change approval forms. Core fields focus on student identity, admission eligibility, and student status transfer status. Data is linked to multiple departmental business systems, with no unified single-document format. Cross-system consistency verification is required.

## Constraints Imposed on Conversation Logs and Audit
Because data updates daily and is linked to multiple departmental systems, conversation logs must bind specific business identifiers for precise traceability. Documents involve sensitive information such as student status and admission eligibility. The audit process must fully record the entire interaction flow, and avoid missing key verification steps. Errors in operations such as student status changes and admission results directly affect student enrollment and progression. Audits must allow traceability of policy explanations and qualification verification steps in conversations, to ensure every operation is traceable. Additionally, linkage of data from multiple systems requires logs to support field formats from different sources, to facilitate cross-system audit verification.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Conversation history records are not loaded, leading to failed context association. `chatId` and `responseChatItemId` are not correctly bound to business identifiers corresponding to student status or enrollment batches, causing historical conversations to fail to match.
- Audit logs do not fully record interaction details, making key verification steps untraceable. The `detail` parameter is not enabled to store complete conversation content, and only simplified response summaries are saved.
- FastGPT containers fail to start after Docker deployment, with no valid error logs. Docker Compose configuration has mounting directory permissions that do not adapt to local business data storage paths, or resource allocation is insufficient.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a conversation that includes student status information queries, and check whether logs contain records associating `chatId` with the corresponding student ID number.
- Open the audit log detail page, verify that complete conversation context and response content are stored, and confirm the `detail` parameter is active.
- Attempt to log in to the system with a regular administrator role, confirm that full audit logs cannot be viewed, and verify that permission configurations are active.
- Check the Docker container running status, confirm that the FastGPT container has no abnormal exits, and verify that deployment configurations are error-free.
