# Party Building Learning Q&A for Pollution Discharge Permit Management: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Data sources for this category include the national pollution discharge permit management information platform, enterprise environmental protection ledgers, online monitoring systems, and environmental statistics systems. Daily documents include pollution discharge permit application forms, implementation reports, self-monitoring plans, ledger records, change applications, and permit copies.
Data is updated quarterly. It includes fields such as permitted emission limits, discharge outlet information, monitoring frequency, and compliance status. There is no unified fixed unit, and values adjust based on the field requirements of specific documents.

## Constraints Imposed on the "Citation Sources and Traceability" Link
Data sources are scattered and drawn from multiple homologous systems. During traceability, unique document identifiers from corresponding platforms must be clearly matched to avoid cross-source data confusion.
The quarterly update requirement means valid document versions from the latest quarter must be prioritized for retrieval. This ensures content cited in party building learning Q&A meets current compliance standards.
Multiple document types exist. Citation logic must be differentiated for documents such as permit copies, implementation reports, and self-monitoring plans, to match the learning needs of corresponding positions.
No unified unit exists for fields. Original document field labels must be retained during traceability to ensure cited content accuracy.
