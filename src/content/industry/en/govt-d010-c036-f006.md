# Party Building Learning Q&A for Government Service Item Catalog and Service Guide Maintenance: Citation Source and Traceability

## What data for this category looks like
This category of data typically comes from the national basic catalog of government service items, administrative licensing item list, power and responsibility list, and local government service item databases, with a monthly update cycle. Daily maintained documents include the basic catalog of government service items, implementation lists, service guides, standard material catalogs, flowcharts, and frequently asked questions. Fields cover item codes, acceptance conditions, application materials, processing procedures, and other content. There is no unified fixed unit, and it needs to match the actual field specifications of local government services.

## Constraints on citation source and traceability
Monthly updated data sources require traceability to match the latest version of the catalog and list when tracing, to avoid inconsistent service handling standards caused by citing expired content. Multiple types of document structures and field differences require precise association with specific entries of corresponding documents during traceability. Generalized citation cannot meet the precise verification needs of government service items. The existence of unique identifier fields such as item codes provides a clear association basis for traceability, but complete source identification information must be included when citing to facilitate subsequent verification. At the same time, the requirement for consistent service handling standards means that the accuracy of traceability is directly linked to the compliance of online and offline service handling. Strict binding to specific document versions and source nodes is necessary.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Conversation interface does not return cited knowledge base ID: The phenomenon is that there are no structured fields related to sources in the interface return results. The cause is that the citation source return configuration is not enabled, or `detail` is not set to `true`, resulting in the interface not carrying the metadata fields required for traceability.
- Incorrect cited files output by the knowledge base: The phenomenon is that the returned cited documents do not match the current government service item in the Q&A. The cause is that the monthly updated data source version is not matched, and an expired government service item catalog is used as the recall source, resulting in incorrect associated documents.
- Failed parsing of traceability information in streaming returns: The phenomenon is that valid source identifiers cannot be extracted from streaming results. The cause is that structured fields returned by the `detail` parameter are not correctly identified, and unstructured streaming text is directly spliced without extracting the corresponding document name and item code.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Call the conversation interface, check whether the return results include source fields related to government service item documents, such as item codes and document names.
- Trigger a test conversation in the party building learning Q&A scenario, verify whether the returned citation information matches the current monthly updated government service catalog version.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, test whether long text citations are fully retained, and confirm compliance with the length requirements of government service documents.
- View the `detail` field returned by the interface, confirm that it includes complete traceability information such as knowledge base source and document identifier.
