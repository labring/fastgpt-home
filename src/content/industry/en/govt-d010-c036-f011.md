# Party Building Learning Q&A for Government Service Item Catalog and Service Guide Maintenance: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category's Data Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from the national basic catalog of government service items, administrative licensing item list, power and responsibility list, and local government service item databases. Daily maintained documents include the basic catalog of government service items, implementation lists, service guides, standard material directories, flowcharts, and frequently asked questions. Data is updated once per month. Typical maintenance entities include administrative approval reform oversight offices, government service data management bureaus, and departmental approval divisions. The data structure uses item codes as unique identifiers, and associates fields such as acceptance conditions, application materials, and service processes. Some items must also be associated with policy interpretation content related to party building learning.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When configuring the `formInput` form component, two channels using the same model are bound, and no priority rules are set. Cause: The system cannot identify a unique call chain, leading to random delays or errors during calls.
- Phenomenon: After the service guide form is submitted, the party building association field is empty. Cause: Mandatory verification for `userSelect` is not enabled, causing maintenance personnel to miss associating party building policy content.
- Phenomenon: A timeout error occurs when bulk importing item catalogs. Cause: The amount of data submitted in a single batch is not limited, exceeding the interface's carrying capacity.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Submit a test service guide entry, check if the `formInput` component correctly captures the party building association field and generates Q&A materials.
- Switch between the two configured model channels, verify that calls follow preset priority rules.
- Import a small amount of test data, check if the form triggers mandatory field verification prompts.
- Initiate a party building-related service consultation question, check if the guide content for the corresponding item is accurately recalled.
