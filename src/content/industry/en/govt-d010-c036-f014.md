# Party Building Study Q&A for Government Service Catalog and Service Guide Maintenance: Conversation Logs and Audit

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category primarily comes from the national basic catalog of government service items, administrative licensing item list, power and responsibility list, and local government service item databases. The update cycle is usually once per month. Daily maintained documents include the basic catalog of government service items, implementation lists, service guides, standard material directories, flowcharts, and frequently asked questions. Fields cover item names, acceptance conditions, application materials, handling procedures, etc. There are no unified fixed units for fields; they need to match the actual expression norms of local government services.

## Constraints Imposed on Conversation Logs and Audit
Monthly updated data sources require conversation logs to be linked to the corresponding version of government service catalogs, to prevent deviations in service handling standards for historical conversations caused by catalog updates. For mixed retrieval scenarios across multiple document types, audit logs must fully record the document source, matched fields, and matching results of each call, to ensure traceability of operations such as item comparison and material verification. The diversity of item fields requires logs to fully record the specific item codes and names involved in conversations, to facilitate subsequent consistency checks of service handling standards. At the same time, compliance requirements for government services determine that audit logs must support tamper-proof storage and targeted export by item dimension and time dimension.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Conversation log storage period | 30 days | Matches the monthly update frequency of government service catalogs, ensuring coverage of the complete monthly maintenance audit cycle |
| `chatId` binding rule | Associate with corresponding item code | Facilitates retrieval of corresponding conversation records by specific government service item, matching business verification scenarios |
| Audit log detail switch | Enable `detail` mode | Fully records retrieved documents, matched fields, and verification results in conversations, meeting compliance traceability requirements |
| Historical conversation loading limit | Top 20 entries | Adapts to the conversation volume of monthly maintenance, avoiding excessive data loading that affects operational efficiency |
| Log export format | CSV format | Facilitates batch import into verification tools for consistency checks of service handling standards |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Interface prompts "No permission to operate this conversation record". Cause: The permission binding rule between `chatId` and corresponding government service item is not configured, resulting in unauthorized users being unable to access conversation logs associated with specific items.
- Symptom: Refreshing the conversation page displays new conversations, while background logs can still view historical records. Cause: The local session storage configuration of `responseChatItemId` in FastGPT v4.17.0 is not enabled, causing front-end session cache loss, with only complete logs retained in the background.
- Symptom: Exported audit logs lack specific matched field information. Cause: `detail` mode is not enabled, only basic conversation time and user ID are recorded, without covering key audit information such as retrieved documents and matched fields.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Log in to the corresponding business account of FastGPT v4.17.0, attempt to retrieve historical conversations for a specific government service item, and confirm that corresponding records can be loaded normally.
- Trigger a government service item verification operation, view the audit log details, and confirm that complete information such as retrieved documents and matched fields has been recorded.
- Export audit logs for a specified time period, and confirm that the export format meets business archiving requirements.
- Check the log storage duration, and confirm that it can cover the audit needs of at least one monthly maintenance cycle.
