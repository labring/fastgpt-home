# Party Building Learning Q&A for Government Procurement Projects: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category’s data covers
This category of data covers daily business documents from procuring entities, financial department government procurement supervision offices, procurement agencies, and evaluation experts. Documents include procurement requirements, procurement plans, bidding documents, tender documents, bid evaluation reports, winning bid announcements, procurement contracts, and question reply documents. Data sources are China Government Procurement Network, local government procurement websites, financial integration systems, procuring entity budget systems, and supplier credit records. Update frequency is daily. Documents include dedicated fields such as procurement number, supplier name, and winning bid amount. Core business links include compiling procurement requirements, verifying supplier qualifications, and handling question and complaint matters.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability link
Daily updated data sources require the traceability system to support scheduled full synchronization of government procurement documents, to avoid outdated or unincluded content. Multiple types of business documents require corresponding dedicated source identifiers, to ensure accurate matching of corresponding files such as procurement requirements and winning bid announcements during traceability. The high compliance requirements of core business links mean that traceability content must include clear fields such as release channel and release date, to facilitate regulatory verification. Data from multiple systems must be uniformly integrated and labeled, to avoid source confusion.

## Configuration Settings
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | Adapts to the block length of government procurement documents, to avoid truncation of core fields such as procurement number and winning bid announcement release date |
| Citation source display limit | Top 3–5 entries | Focuses on core compliance basis in procurement scenarios, to avoid displaying excessive irrelevant content |
| `responseData` | Includes original document path and release date | Meets traceability requirements for government procurement supervision, to clarify specific release channels of cited content |
| `detail` | Enable complete original text snippets | Facilitates verification of specific clauses in documents such as procurement requirements and bid evaluation reports, to avoid out-of-context quoting |
| Similarity threshold | 0.75–0.85, calibrated via actual testing | Filters non-procurement unrelated documents, to ensure traceability content is strongly relevant to the procurement theme of party building learning Q&A |
| Scheduled synchronization frequency | 1 time per day | Matches the daily update rhythm of government procurement data, to ensure timeliness of traceability content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base search results are sorted unexpectedly, with non-highly relevant content displayed first. Cause: No reordering based on dedicated fields of government procurement documents such as procurement number, only relying on general similarity calculation.
- Phenomenon: Unconfigured local knowledge base citations appear in conversations. Cause: Global default knowledge base association is not turned off, or non-government procurement documents were accidentally imported during synchronization.
- Phenomenon: Key procurement fields are missing from cited content displayed in traceability. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is set too small, truncating core identifying information such as procurement contract number and winning bid announcement release date.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Trigger party building learning Q&A questions related to government procurement, check if displayed citation sources include designated data sources such as China Government Procurement Network and financial integration systems.
- Check the length of cited snippets, confirm that core fields such as procurement number and winning bid amount are not truncated.
- Simulate triggering non-government procurement related questions, verify that government procurement documents are not returned as citation sources.
- Check scheduled synchronization task logs, confirm that the daily automatic update process for government procurement data runs normally.
