# Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management Party Building Learning Q&A: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
This category’s data comes from enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, legal and regulatory repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. The update frequency is monthly. Daily processed documents include compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, major issue legal opinions, compliance review forms, and violation report investigation reports. Data fields cover compliance item numbers, associated business IDs, risk levels, processing progress, reporting subject information, and more. There is no fixed unified unit field, and adjustments are made based on the type of the corresponding document.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "conversation logs and auditing" link
Monthly updated data sources require that conversation logs be bound to compliance data from the corresponding cycle to avoid data mismatches from cross-cycle calls. Data from multiple system sources requires logs to associate business identifiers from different systems to ensure full chain traceability during audits. Conversation scenarios for official compliance documents require logs to fully retain context and not truncate key review opinions. Since error consequences involve major legal risks, logs must support full-chain export, and the storage cycle must match the archiving requirements of compliance reviews.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |
|---|---|---|
| `chatId` | Bind to the unique identifier of a single compliance business, with a single ID length not exceeding 128 characters | Ensure logs can accurately associate with the corresponding compliance documents and avoid confusion across businesses |
| `responseChatItemId` | Automatically generated by conversation turn, retain the first 5 historical records | Compliance audits require retaining key review conversation turns and avoid redundant logs occupying storage |
| `detail` | Enable full log storage, including user questions, model replies, and data source reference links | Meet full-chain traceability requirements for major compliance matter reviews |
| Conversation history retention duration | 12 months | Match annual archiving specifications for central enterprise compliance reviews |
| Automatic data source reconnection | Enable, with a retry interval of 600 seconds | Avoid log storage interruptions caused by offline compliance databases during monthly updates |
| Log export format | Support PDF and CSV formats | Meet document archiving and external inspection needs of compliance departments |

## Three common errors
- Symptom: When the same `chatId` is accessed multiple times, historical records from the first call are not loaded correctly, and only the current question content is displayed. Cause: The `chatId` is not bound to the compliance business ID, causing the historical context to not associate with the compliance data of the corresponding business.
- Symptom: The log page displays a database connection failure error, and complete conversation content cannot be viewed. Cause: The automatic data source reconnection mechanism is not configured, and automatic recovery is not triggered after the monthly updated compliance database goes offline, resulting in interrupted log storage.
- Symptom: Conversation logs only retain 1 round of historical records, making it impossible to trace the complete process of multi-round compliance reviews. Cause: The historical record retention turn count is set incorrectly, which does not match the multi-round review requirements of compliance audits.

## How to verify a complete configuration
- Enter the `chatId` bound to the compliance business ID, initiate multi-round conversations, and verify that logs correctly associate with the document links of the corresponding business.
- Verify that the `detail` parameter is enabled, and confirm that logs contain complete user questions, model replies, and data source reference information.
- Attempt to export conversation logs, and verify that PDF and CSV format export options are available.
- In the system monitoring of FastGPT v4.17.0, verify that data source connection status is normal and no offline alarms are present.
