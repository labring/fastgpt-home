# Administrative Licensing and Approval Party Building Learning Q&A: Forms and Interactions

This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like
This party building learning Q&A scenario revolves around administrative licensing and approval business. Data is sourced from the national integrated government service platform item database, administrative licensing item list, departmental business approval systems, and electronic certificate repository, and is updated daily. Daily documents include service guides, application forms, commitment letters, acceptance notices, correction notices, administrative licensing decision documents, license certificates, as well as party building-related government policy documents. Fields include item code, applicant subject qualification, submitted material details, legal handling conditions, key party building policy points, and all fields follow government service standard formats with no custom units.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "forms and interactions" link
This party building learning Q&A scenario combines administrative licensing and approval business. Data has a high degree of standardization and is updated daily. Form fields must strictly match legal fields from government service item databases and key party building policy points, with no arbitrary addition, removal, or custom non-standard items. The existence of multiple types of approval documents and party building policy files requires forms to support modular entry of different application materials and learning content, while linking electronic certificate retrieval interfaces to automatically fill compliant information. The clarity of legal handling conditions and party building policies requires real-time verification of submitted content against both during interaction, to avoid errors in subsequent processes due to mismatched information. Additionally, data sources are scattered across multiple systems, so forms must support cross-system data linkage to reduce manual entry costs.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The variable field bound to `formInput` is empty after submission. Cause: No variable mapping rule is configured, and no dynamic data source is linked to the government item database or party building policy repository.
- Phenomenon: The form input box cannot upload electronic certificates or party building learning material files, and a format error is returned upon submission. Cause: No format validation for government and party building documents is enabled in the file upload component, and no automatic verification logic is linked to the electronic certificate repository.
- Phenomenon: After switching the approval item type or party building policy category, the retrieval scope of the corresponding knowledge base does not automatically switch, and can only be implemented via manual configuration of a judge. Cause: The selected value of the `userSelect` component is not bound to the knowledge base association rule, relying on global retrieval leads to mismatched results.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Open the form preview page, select any legal administrative licensing item or party building policy category, and check whether the form fields match the standard format of the corresponding content.
- Submit a test application, check whether the corresponding knowledge base content can be automatically associated, and whether the retrieval results include handling conditions and key party building policy points.
- Upload government documents or party building learning materials, check whether format validation is triggered and correct error information is prompted.
- Switch the options of the `userSelect` component, confirm that the knowledge base retrieval scope is automatically updated without manual configuration adjustments.
