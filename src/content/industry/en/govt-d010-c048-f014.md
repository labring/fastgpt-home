# Administrative Licensing and Approval: Party Building Learning Q&A - Conversation Logs and Auditing
## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for the administrative licensing and approval scenario comes from the national integrated government service platform item database, administrative licensing item list, departmental business approval systems, and electronic certificate repository. The update frequency is daily.
Commonly involved documents include service guidelines, application forms, commitment letters, acceptance notices, correction notification letters, administrative licensing decision letters, and license certificates.
Data fields include approval item codes, applicant subject information, approval status, electronic certificate numbers, and more. All data is generated around the full process handling links of statutory licensing items.

## Constraints Imposed on Conversation Logs and Auditing
Dispersed data sources and daily updates require conversation logs to bind to the unique identifier of the corresponding approval item, to avoid cross-item confusion.
Diverse daily document types require audit entries to record specific associated document types and numbers, to facilitate tracing approval materials corresponding to Q&A.
The daily update frequency requires matching the daily archiving rhythm, to ensure the audit scope covers all approval-related conversations from the current day.
Approval results have legal effect. Logs must fully retain setting basis verification links involved in Q&A, to provide traceable complete records for subsequent reconsideration or litigation.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `chatId` | Bind to the administrative licensing approval serial number, use the unique identifier string of the corresponding approval item | Ensure one-to-one correspondence between conversation logs and specific approval items, enabling quick location of full-process conversations for a single approval during audits |
| `responseChatItemId` | Associate with the number of electronic certificates or approval documents, use the unique identifier of the corresponding document | Bind conversation output to actual approval documents, facilitating tracing of approval materials or final results corresponding to Q&A |
| `detail` | Enable full log storage, covering user questions, model responses, and intermediate invocation steps | Meet audit requirements for complete trace records, facilitating troubleshooting of issues such as overdue completion and model invocation failures |
| Log storage period | Minimum 180 days, set in accordance with general government audit retention requirements | Match the audit trace period for administrative licensing and approval, ensuring traceability of all past approval-related conversations |
| Log archiving trigger | Daily scheduled archiving, or archiving triggered when an approval item is completed | Match the daily data update rhythm, avoid redundant log storage, and facilitate audits by approval batches |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Symptom: Conversation returns empty. Server logs show the `message` field is missing. Cause: Full log storage for the `detail` parameter is not enabled, so intermediate model invocation steps are not recorded, making it impossible to locate the failure point.
- Symptom: When multiple users share a secret key, audit logs cannot distinguish approval conversations corresponding to specific operators. Cause: `chatId` is not bound to the user's approval account, only the global secret key is used to associate logs, leading to vague audit traceability.
- Symptom: After upgrading to FastGPT v4.17.0, conversation logs cannot be associated with approval document numbers. Cause: The binding rule for `responseChatItemId` is not updated, and it is not matched with the new approval document number field.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Initiate a test Party Building learning-related approval Q&A, check if the conversation log contains the bound approval serial number to confirm that the `chatId` configuration takes effect.
- Export logs for a specified approval item, check if the approval document number associated with `responseChatItemId` is included to confirm that the document binding configuration is correct.
- View the log storage duration, confirm that logs exceeding the set retention period have been archived to confirm that the storage period configuration meets requirements.
- Simulate a model invocation failure scenario, check if the log records complete intermediate steps to confirm that the `detail` parameter is enabled correctly.
