# Party Building Learning Q&A for Fiscal Expenditure Performance Evaluation: Conversation Logs and Audit

## What this category of data consists of
This category of data comes from the unified budget management system, project declaration materials, fund payment data, business system ledgers, and satisfaction surveys. The update frequency is annual. Daily documents include performance target forms, self-assessment forms, performance evaluation reports, supporting material lists, and rectification reports. Data fields cover project codes, fund amounts, performance indicator completion status, supporting material association relationships, rectification completion progress, and other items. There are no fixed unified units; values vary based on document type and business link.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the conversation logs and audit workflow
Since the data update frequency is annual, conversation logs do not require high-frequency refreshes. They must fully retain all year’s project-related question-and-answer interactions to ensure audit traceability. As data originates from multiple business systems, logs must associate fields such as project codes and fund identifiers from the corresponding systems to prevent cross-system data confusion. Daily document types are diverse, so logs must record the specific document types involved in interactions to facilitate subsequent verification of the correspondence between fund expenditures and performance targets. Consequences of errors include project scoring adjustments, budget revisions, and accountability measures. Logs must fully retain interaction details and cannot be arbitrarily deleted or lost.

## Three common configuration errors
- The `detail` field of conversation logs contains no running data after a workflow is called. The cause is that full interaction storage configuration is not enabled, and only short summaries are retained.
- After a user deletes a front-end conversation, background audit logs are deleted synchronously. The cause is that the permission boundary between user operations and audit logs is not distinguished, and the deletion operation is associated with the global identifier of `chatId`.
- Historical conversation records cannot be queried after entering a user identifier. The cause is that a retrieval index that associates user identities with `chatId` is not configured, resulting in failure to match corresponding conversations.

## How to confirm configurations are correctly set
- Initiate a question-and-answer interaction related to performance evaluation, and check whether the log contains complete content of the `chatId`, `responseChatItemId`, and `detail` fields.
- Attempt to perform a log deletion operation as a regular user, and confirm that audit logs cannot be modified or deleted.
- Enter a project code or user identifier to retrieve conversation logs of the corresponding dimension, and confirm that relevant content can be matched according to the configured rules.
- Wait for one synchronization cycle, and check whether logs have completed synchronization at the configured frequency with no data loss or omission.
