# Party Building Learning Q&A for Road Transport Business Licensing: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category's data looks like
Data for this category comes from the road transport management system, national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and vehicle and practitioner database. Daily documents include business license application materials, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, and road transport business licenses. The data update frequency is daily. Document structures are a mix of structured tables and paragraph text, with fields such as license number, vehicle license plate number, qualification certificate number, and safety system clauses. There is no unified fixed format.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector models and indexes link
High-frequency daily updated data requires indexes to support incremental synchronization, avoiding full reindexing that takes too long and impacts business response speed. Mixed structured tables and paragraph documents need to support both table field vectorization and paragraph semantic vectorization, avoiding loss of structured business information or long-text semantics with a single chunking method. Fields include short business identifiers and long system texts, requiring a balance between vector accuracy for short texts and chunking rationality for long texts. The party building learning Q&A scenario needs to associate policy clauses with licensing business, requiring indexes to match semantic associations across documents, avoiding the limitations of single-document recall.

## Configuration Settings

| Overlap Length | 50–100 characters | Connects semantic associations between chunks, adapts to cross content between vehicle information and system texts |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- A prompt reading "No available index model detected" appears after refreshing the page. The cause is failure to associate documents related to road transport business licensing with the party building learning Q&A knowledge base, or failure to save and activate the index model configuration.
- Table data cannot enable multi-vector support. The cause is failure to enable the multi-vector recognition switch of the knowledge base, or failure to process table fields according to structured chunking rules.
- An error occurs when testing access to a multimodal embedding model. The cause is failure to match the input token limit of the model, or failure to format request parameters according to model requirements, resulting in an invalid interface response.
