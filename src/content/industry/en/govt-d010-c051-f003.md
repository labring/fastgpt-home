# Party Building Learning Q&A for Road Transport Business Licensing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category's data looks like
Service subjects for this category include transportation bureau examination and approval departments, road transport management agencies, and transport enterprises. Daily data includes documents such as business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, and road transport business licenses.
Data sources are the road transport administration management system, the national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and the vehicle and personnel database. The update frequency is daily.
Data fields cover dimensions such as business entity qualifications, vehicle operation status, personnel employment qualifications, and safety management specifications. Field formats vary across different source systems.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
Multi-source heterogeneous data sources require the dialogue system to support associated recall across systems, to avoid inconsistent information.
The daily update frequency requires the dialogue link to retrieve the latest vehicle, personnel, and license status data in real time, and not rely on offline caches.
Different types of business documents correspond to different approval stages. Prompt engineering must distinguish scenarios such as application, modification, and annual inspection, to ensure that Q&A matches corresponding business logic.
At the same time, the consequences of errors in approval stages are clear. The dialogue system must ensure the accuracy of responses, and must not produce misleading content.

## How to set configurations
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum context length | 8000–12000 characters | Must accommodate content from multiple business license applications, vehicle technical files, and other documents, to avoid truncating critical approval information |
| Maximum single dialogue turns | 8 turns | Matches the multi-stage verification logic of road transport business license approval, to avoid meaningless repeated dialogue |
| Timeout setting | 60 seconds | Adapts to the standard response duration of government system data retrieval, to avoid dialogue interruptions |
| Dialogue history management | Retain the first 10 turns of recent `messages` | Focus on valid information from the current approval session, to reduce interference from redundant data |
| Variable binding | Bind the `variables` field | Automatically import basic information of the current approval's vehicles and enterprises, to improve Q&A accuracy |
| Session association | Bind the `chatId` field | Associate exclusive data from the current approval session, to avoid confusion across session data |

## Three common misconfigurations
- A crash occurs on the dialogue page or knowledge base page, with a blank screen or error prompt. This happens when a reasonable context length is not configured for road transport business licensing multi-source data, leading to exceeded memory usage limits.
- The set guide prompt does not take effect, and the AI response deviates from the topics of party building learning and license approval. This occurs when the prompt engineering does not bind exclusive fields for the corresponding business, and does not clearly define the Q&A scope.
- The dialogue application cannot retrieve road transport administration system data after being connected to plugins. This happens when `chatId` is not correctly associated with the current approval session, leading to data matching failures.

## How to confirm configurations are set correctly
- Initiate a multi-turn query containing keywords such as vehicle technical files and driver qualification materials, to confirm that the AI can retrieve corresponding document content.
- Test 8 consecutive rounds of dialogue, to confirm that the system does not crash or truncate context.
- Check whether the prompt template binds the `variables` field, to ensure that exclusive information for the current approval can be automatically imported.
- Verify the session association configuration, to confirm that dialogue data does not cross instances when switching approval sessions.
