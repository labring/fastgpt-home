# Party Building Learning Q&A for Road Transport Business Licensing: Forms and Interaction

## What does the data for this category look like?
Typical users of this category are transportation bureau approval departments, road transport management agencies, and transport enterprises. Daily business documents include business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, and road transport business licenses. Data is sourced from the road transport administration system, the national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and the vehicle and practitioner database. The update frequency is daily. The data structure covers all materials and dynamic information across the entire process of license application, verification, modification, and annual review, and does not include additional statistical content.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "forms and interaction" link?
The daily update feature of data requires form interactions to support real-time pulling of the latest documents, and avoid using outdated license-related content. Multi-source official data requires forms to support filtering and recalling content by data source category, to ensure that matched documents come from compliant channels. Multi-type business documents require forms to preset corresponding document options, narrow the query scope, and improve matching accuracy. The most time-consuming verification link feature requires forms to guide users to submit verification required information step by step, reducing invalid queries. The feature of serious consequences from errors requires forms to add pre-verification to filter irrelevant questions and avoid triggering incorrect business processes.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: When configuring the `userSelect` component, no preset document options for the category are set, and the recalled documents after submitting the query are unrelated to road transport business licensing. Cause: No optional values are set based on the core document types of the category, causing the system to match irrelevant knowledge base content.
- Phenomenon: A timeout error is triggered after submitting the query, with status code 504 returned. Cause: No reasonable failure retry count is set, and the single search limit is not defined, causing the system to pull too many document fragments beyond the response threshold.
- Phenomenon: When using variable references to select knowledge base documents, correct document content cannot be obtained, and there is no clear parameter filling description. Cause: No category-specific data source mapping rules are configured, and the data source paths of the road transport administration system and vehicle dynamic platform are not associated, resulting in no basis for parameter filling.

## How to verify that the configuration is complete
- Submit a test question, such as "How to verify driver qualification materials", check whether the recalled documents include category-specific content such as vehicle technical files and driver qualification materials.
- Try entering a non-license-related party building question, check whether it is intercepted by pre-verification, and confirm that the `formInput` verification rule takes effect.
- Switch between different `userSelect` options, check whether the recalled document types correspond to the selected options.
- Simulate temporary system fluctuations, submit a query and check whether failure retry is triggered, and confirm that the retry logic operates normally.
