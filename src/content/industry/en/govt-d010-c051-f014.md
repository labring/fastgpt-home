# Party Building Learning Q&A for Road Transport Business Licensing: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
This category’s data primarily comes from the road transport administration management system, the national road transport vehicle dynamic information public service platform, and the vehicle and practitioner database. It updates daily. Daily documents include business license application materials, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, safety production management systems, road transport business licenses, and other administrative approval standard fields. These fields cover operating entity qualifications, vehicle technical status, practitioner qualifications, safety management specifications, and similar content. There are no additional custom units. Field formats uniformly comply with transportation administrative approval requirements.

## What These Characteristics Impose on the Conversation Logs and Auditing Link
This category’s data comes from multiple sources and updates daily. Link conversation logs to the update timestamp of the corresponding data source to ensure traceable data timeliness during audits. Daily document types are diverse and follow administrative approval standard formats. Logs must record document type identifiers to facilitate classified auditing of interactive content across different approval stages. Entities involved include administrative approval departments and transport enterprises. Logs must distinguish between operating roles. Given the serious consequences of errors, audits must retain complete interactive links, including contextually linked approval data references. This avoids omitting conversation records from core stages such as verifying vehicle and personnel information and reviewing business scope.

## Configuration Settings
This configuration is formulated based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log storage period | 30–90 days | Matches standard government audit retention requirements, corresponds to the daily update frequency of category data, and ensures coverage of complete approval cycles |
| `chatId` binding rule | Bind to the unique identifier of a single road transport business license application | Ensures conversation logs are tied to specific approval matters, avoiding log confusion across applications |
| `detail` full log switch | Enabled | Covers interactive details of core stages such as verifying vehicle and personnel information and reviewing business scope, meeting complete traceability requirements for audits |
| `responseChatItemId` association field | Bind to the approval data entry corresponding to the reply | Ensures each reply can be traced back to specific data such as vehicle files and practitioner materials |
| Single log synchronization interval | 60 seconds | Adapts to the daily update rhythm of category data, avoiding retrieval delays caused by log backlogs |
| Context history retention limit | 10 conversation turns | Limits the historical length bound to a single `chatId`, avoiding loading excessive redundant data during audits |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: As more questions are asked in the same conversation window, audit log loading speed decreases, and the time to retrieve associated approval data exceeds 1 minute. Cause: The context history length bound to a single `chatId` is not limited, resulting in excessive historical session data being loaded during each audit, and logs are not split and stored by individual approval applications.
- Symptom: Database connection errors occur after configuring conversation logs, and logs cannot be written normally. Cause: No database connection verification mechanism is configured for log storage, and the synchronization strategy is not adjusted to match the traffic peak of daily category data updates, leading to log write interruptions.
- Symptom: Associated records of specific approval documents cannot be traced during audits, and only Q&A content is displayed in logs without document identifiers. Cause: The `detail` full log recording switch is not enabled, and the document type field is not written to logs.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Check the log storage configuration items to confirm that the storage period matches government audit retention requirements and corresponds to the category data update frequency.
- Initiate a test conversation and check whether the log contains `chatId`, `responseChatItemId`, and document type identifiers, confirming that the full log switch is enabled.
- Initiate conversations using different roles to confirm that logs are stored categorized by role, and operation records for corresponding entities can be retrieved individually.
- Simulate a conversation after data update to confirm that the data source timestamp associated with the log is correctly recorded.
