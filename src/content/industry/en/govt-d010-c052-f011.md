# Party Building Learning Q&A for Department Final Accounts and Financial Reports: Forms and Interactions
This document is written for FastGPT v4.17.0. It targets business personnel and technical engineers in central state-owned enterprises, government, and public service sectors who handle department final accounts and financial reports. It focuses on form and interaction configuration for party building learning Q&A scenarios.

## What data for this category includes
Data for this category comes from the budget management integrated system, centralized treasury payment system, accounting system, and asset management system. The update cycle is annual. Daily output documents include department final account statements, balance sheets, income and expenditure final account statements, final account explanations, and government financial reports. Data fields cover actual revenue and expenditure details, asset and liability balances, budget execution difference explanations, and more. Field units follow general fiscal standards, with no custom non-standard units.

## What constraints these data characteristics impose on forms and interactions
Annual concentrated update cycles require form interactions to support end-of-year bulk data import and verification, preventing overload from single-time processing. Multi-system data sources require form modules to adapt to parameter extraction logic for multiple data sources, ensuring consistency of cross-system fields. The serious consequences of errors require adding pre-verification in the interaction link, intercepting field values that do not meet fiscal specifications. The party building learning Q&A scenario requires form triggers to bind to user questions. When users mention party building learning questions related to final accounts, the corresponding form module activates automatically to link data and Q&A. It also accommodates data differences across budget units at different levels.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` | Bind core department final account fields to party building Q&A trigger logic | Automatically associate corresponding data fields when users ask party building-related final account questions, reducing manual input costs |
| `userSelect` | Pre-set 10-15 standardized difference explanation options | Cover common budget execution difference explanation scenarios, unifying the output format of Q&A interactions |
| Form timeout period | 600 seconds | Match the time limit for annual bulk final account data processing, preventing interaction interruptions |
| Verification rules | Enable required field verification and data format matching | Intercept field values that do not meet fiscal specifications, reducing the risk of final account statements being returned |
| Bulk import configuration | Support uploading CSV-format final account files | Adapt to end-of-year concentrated processing business scenarios, improving interaction efficiency |

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: Users send new messages before completing form interactions, and the process cannot proceed. Cause: No fallback interaction logic after form interruption is configured, and no timeout automatic reset or jump mechanism is set.
- Phenomenon: After configuring `userSelect` options, some party building Q&A results do not display preset options. Cause: The `userSelect` module is not bound to the party building Q&A trigger condition, causing the options to not be activated correctly.
- Phenomenon: Form fields are empty after importing final account files. Cause: No verification of the matching between file format and preset fields is performed, resulting in failed correct data parsing.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Simulate a question about department final account differences during party building learning, check if the corresponding form module is automatically activated.
- Upload a formatted final account test file, confirm if fields are correctly mapped to form components.
- Intentionally enter non-standard field values, check if preset verification prompts are triggered.
- Wait 600 seconds without completing form operations, confirm if the timeout reset logic is activated.
