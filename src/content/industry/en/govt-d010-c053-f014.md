# Party Building Learning Q&A for Department Budget Preparation: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What this category of data looks like
This category covers party building learning Q&A scenarios. Core data comes from the budget management integrated system, department staff establishment system, prior year final accounts, project library, Ministry of Finance budget preparation documents, and party building-related policy notices. The update frequency is annual. Documents processed include department budget drafts, project application forms, performance target statements, personnel expense calculation forms, and budget approvals. Data fields include budget year, unit code, project details, fund amount, performance indicators, and more. There is no unified fixed cross-entity format, and data must be organized according to the reporting unit's filling rules.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "conversation logs and auditing" link
This category of data is sourced from core fiscal business systems and party building policy content. The content links annual fund arrangements and party building learning requirements. Errors will result in budget returns, projects being unable to secure funding, and other adverse outcomes. Therefore, all interaction links must be fully retained in conversation logs, and arbitrary deletion is prohibited. The data update frequency is annual, so the log retention period must match the budget year span. Since most processed documents are structured tables and policy notices, logs must be linked to specific document fragments and field information. Identifiers such as `chatId` and `responseChatItemId` must also be recorded to facilitate audit tracing and verification of party building learning content.

This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: Prompt "No permission to operate this conversation record". Cause: Log access permissions are not configured according to budget management and party building learning roles, or the bound `chatId` is not associated with the corresponding unit code and learning batch, resulting in failed permission verification.
- Symptom: After upgrading the version and refreshing the conversation page, front-end conversation records disappear but can still be viewed in the back-end. Cause: Session cache persistence for `responseChatItemId` is not enabled in the configuration, or the session storage path is not bound to the budget year and party building learning batch dimensions, resulting in cache failure after version upgrade.
- Symptom: Call count statistics for party building classification questions are empty. Cause: The field extraction configuration for `detail` logs is not enabled, and the association information between classification tags and call counts is not recorded, resulting in missing statistical data.

## How to confirm the configuration is correctly set up
- Initiate a Q&A that combines party building policies and department budget drafts, check the back-end logs, confirm that `chatId` is bound to the current budget unit code, budget year, and party building learning batch.
- Verify accounts with different permission roles, confirm that only conversation logs within the corresponding jurisdiction and related to the party building learning batch can be viewed, with no unauthorized access.
- Check the log storage directory, confirm that the retention period meets the budget year and party building learning cycle span, and no automatic early cleanup rules are set.
- Trigger a call for a party building classification question, confirm that the association information between classification identifiers and interaction times is recorded in the `detail` log.
