# Party Building Learning Q&A for Flood Prevention, Disaster Mitigation and Emergency Duty: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category primarily comes from meteorological early warning systems, hydrological monitoring systems, emergency command platforms, grassroots disaster reporting systems, and video surveillance.
Daily stored documents include duty records, early warning notices, consultation minutes, rapid disaster reports, emergency response plans, and material allocation slips.
Data is updated daily. Each single record includes early warning level, monitoring point coordinates, rainfall amount, water level threshold, disposal node, reporting unit, and timestamp. Fields are attached with corresponding business units: for example, rainfall is measured in millimeters, and water level is measured in meters.

## What constraints this category of data imposes on conversation logs and auditing
This category of data has multiple sources and is linked to the entire emergency response workflow. Conversation logs must accurately bind corresponding monitoring data, documents, and conversation scenarios.
Daily updated data requires logs to be archived by duty cycles to avoid mixing cross-cycle data. The timeliness of emergency response requires that key conversation content not be lost in logs, otherwise audit tracing will be impacted.
Conversations from different business modules such as rainfall summary and material allocation must be distinguished by identifiers. This ensures rapid location of corresponding disposal links during audits.
Conversations accessed from multiple systems must have clear sources. This prevents mixing logs from different monitoring points.

## How to configure the system

| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind the unique ID of the emergency duty conversation, including regional code and date suffix | Distinguish flood prevention duty conversations from different regions and time periods, facilitating scenario-based archiving and auditing |
| `responseChatItemId` | Associate the document ID referenced in the conversation, bind the number of the corresponding early warning notice or rapid disaster report | Ensure rapid location of business documents corresponding to conversation content during audits |
| Log storage duration | 30–90 days, calibrated based on actual testing | Meet the audit tracing requirements of emergency duty, while balancing storage costs |
| Concurrent log write threshold | 1–2 calls per second | Match the daily data update frequency of this category, preventing log loss caused by high concurrency |
| `detail` | Enable full log recording, including fields such as rainfall data and water level thresholds | Cover key business information of emergency response, meeting the complete requirements of audit tracing |

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: The generated party building learning Q&A reply is not saved to the history record, and the reply content is empty when the conversation is reopened. Cause: `chatId` is not correctly bound to the unique identifier of the conversation, causing logs to not be stored grouped by duty conversations.
- Phenomenon: When the concurrent call volume reaches 2–3 calls per second, the workflow returns empty results. Cause: The concurrent log write threshold is set beyond the system's carrying capacity, causing key log entries to be lost.
- Phenomenon: Conversation logs from different monitoring points cannot be distinguished, making it impossible to quickly locate the corresponding disposal scenario during audits. Cause: The `detail` parameter is not configured to enable full field recording, and distinguishing identifiers such as regional codes are not added.

## How to verify the configuration is correct
- Initiate a party building learning Q&A conversation that includes rainfall queries. Check whether the `chatId` field in the session log matches the current duty ID.
- View the conversation log details. Confirm that the `detail` parameter is enabled and includes business fields such as early warning level and monitoring point coordinates.
- Simulate 1–2 concurrent calls per second. Check that all conversation content is fully saved in logs without loss.
- Check the log archiving duration. Confirm that it meets the audit tracing requirements of this category, and adjust according to actual business scenarios.
