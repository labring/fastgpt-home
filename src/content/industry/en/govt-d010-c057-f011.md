# Food Safety Supervision Sampling Party Building Learning Q&A: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
The data for this category comes from the food safety sampling and monitoring system, the State Administration for Market Regulation's sampling results query system, the laboratory information management system (LIMS) of inspection institutions, and enterprise account books. The update frequency is monthly. Daily documents include sampling plans, sampling slips, inspection reports, unqualified food verification and disposal reports, and risk control notices. Core fields cover sampling locations, inspection items, unqualified judgment basis, disposal measures, and more. All data is generated around sample inspection and unqualified product disposal processes, with laboratory test results as core evidence support.

## Constraints on forms and interactions
Monthly updated data requires the knowledge base associated with the form to support synchronization by natural month. This prevents data lag from affecting regulatory timeliness. The business logic centered on laboratory test reports requires the form to support uploading structured documents and extracting key fields. It also requires setting verification for core fields such as unqualified items and disposal measures. Fixed-format documents such as sampling slips and verification and disposal reports require form controls to preset standardized options. This reduces manual entry format errors. Delayed disposal of unqualified food will trigger regulatory risks. This requires form submission verification to cover core business fields and avoid missing key information.

## Configuration Settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0, and are adapted to the form and interaction requirements of this category.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` text input control | Enable mandatory verification mode, bind the sampling plan number field | Match the fixed numbering rules of sampling slips to avoid entry format errors |
| `userSelect` selection control | Preset three options: regulatory institutes, inspection institutions, food production operators | Cover typical business entities of this category and reduce custom input errors |
| Knowledge base synchronization cycle | Set by natural month, synchronization interval 30 days | Match the monthly update frequency of this category's data to ensure retrieval data timeliness |
| File upload format restriction | Only allow PDF and structured spreadsheet files | Adapt to common document formats of this category such as inspection reports and sampling slips |
| Retrieval result limit | Top 10 retrieval results | Balance retrieval efficiency and information integrity, avoid interference from redundant information |
| Form submission verification | Mark unqualified item field as required, associate knowledge base verification | Avoid delayed disposal of unqualified food caused by missing fields |

## Three Common Errors
- Phenomenon: Radio options in the form are not generated dynamically via parameters, and sampling items must be added manually one by one. Cause: The parameter configuration method of `userSelect` was not used correctly. Static input options were adopted, resulting in the need to manually update the form when adding new sampling items.
- Phenomenon: Expired historical sampling data appears in knowledge base retrieval results. Cause: The knowledge base synchronization cycle was not configured at a monthly frequency. This leads to delayed data updates that cannot match current regulatory requirements.
- Phenomenon: No verification is triggered after form submission, and a verification and disposal report without unqualified items appears. Cause: Mandatory verification rules were not set for core business fields. This ignores the regulatory risks that may be caused by missing information in this category.

## How to Confirm Configuration Completion
- Enter the form editing interface, check whether the `userSelect` control is bound to preset field options such as regulatory entities and unqualified items, with no custom garbled characters or missing items.
- Trigger the knowledge base synchronization operation, check the synchronization log to confirm that the latest synchronization has been completed, and the synchronization cycle matches the 30-day setting requirement.
- Upload a simulated inspection report, intentionally leave the unqualified item field blank, and check whether the form pops up a mandatory field verification prompt.
- Call the form control preview, retrieve sampling-related documents, and confirm that the returned results only include valid data from the past month.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
