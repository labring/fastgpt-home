# Party Building Learning Q&A for Warehousing and Logistics: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category's data looks like
Party building learning data in the warehousing and logistics sector mainly comes from internal party building ledgers, weekly branch study minutes, policy documents issued by superior party organizations, and study notes and reflections from frontline party members.
The update rhythm is not fixed. Superior policy documents are updated in batches quarterly. Internal study minutes are updated alongside weekly study activities at warehouse sites. Most documents are in PDF or Word format, with fields including title, issuing unit, release date, main body content, and affiliated warehouse site. Single document length ranges from hundreds to tens of thousands of characters.

FastGPT v4.17.0 supports the following configurations and validation logic.

## Constraints on the vector model and indexing link from these characteristics
The characteristics of warehousing and logistics party building data impose multiple constraints on the vector model and indexing link.
Wide range of document lengths, from short minutes with hundreds of characters to long policy documents with tens of thousands of characters. Flexible chunking strategies are needed to avoid chunk failure.
Fields including warehouse site and branch hierarchy require indexing to support filtering recall results by business dimension, to avoid matching irrelevant party building content across sites.
Uneven update rhythm, with batch updates of superior documents and sporadic updates of daily study minutes, requires incremental indexing to reduce repeated computing resource consumption.
Parsed multi-format original documents need unified standardized fields to ensure consistent input format for vector generation.

## How to set the configurations

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapt to the average length of warehousing party building documents, avoid overly fragmented or overly long chunks affecting vector quality |
| Overlap Length | 50–100 characters | Retain contextual association between chunks, avoid key party building knowledge points being split across two chunks |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85, calibrated via actual testing | Match the semantic similarity range for warehousing party building content, filter irrelevant general party building content |

| `defaultToken` | 150 token | Configure the minimum token threshold for chunks, filter invalid overly short chunk content |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: The exported knowledge base dataset.csv only contains the index field, no content field. Cause: The content extraction mapping rule after chunking is not correctly configured, leading to only index identifiers being generated during chunking, with no document main body content written to the corresponding fields.
- Phenomenon: The vector generation speed after document parsing fails to match business requirements, multiple 3090 GPUs are not being used effectively. Cause: The deployment configuration does not specify the target GPU device for vector model loading, only single GPU resources are called by default, resulting in idle multi-GPU computing power.
- Phenomenon: Recalled party building content after text import is irrelevant to frontline warehousing scenarios, matching results have obvious deviations. Cause: General default similarity threshold and chunking configurations are used, without adjusting parameters for the warehousing party building business scenario, leading to recall of irrelevant content.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Upload a party building study minute from a frontline warehouse branch, check the chunk preview module, confirm that the chunk results conform to the preset length and overlap rules.
- Launch a test Q&A focused on the party building theme, check the field list of returned results, confirm that the content field has been fully populated.
- View the server resource monitoring interface, confirm that the vector model loading device matches the preset configuration, and multi-GPU resources are called as configured.
- Adjust the test range of the similarity threshold, observe the change in the number of recall results, confirm that the configuration item has taken effect.

The values listed are common starting points, and should be assessed using local samples.
