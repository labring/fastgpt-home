# Party Building Learning Q&A for Warehousing and Logistics: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for warehousing and logistics party building learning comes primarily from internal party building materials uploaded by the enterprise’s party-mass work department. These materials include policy interpretations issued by superior authorities, branch study plans, and party building cases linked to warehousing business.
The update schedule follows this pattern: synchronize superior party building documents monthly, and update party building documents that combine branch study minutes and business every quarter.
Most documents are in Word or PDF format with structured tables of contents. Fields include document topic, issuing authority, effective date, and associated warehousing business scenarios. The length of a single document ranges from 5000 to 20000 characters.

## Constraints imposed by these data characteristics on knowledge base retrieval and recall
These data characteristics create clear constraints for the knowledge base retrieval and recall link.
First, documents are associated with warehousing business scenarios. Retrieval must use metadata filtering to avoid returning general party building content unrelated to warehousing business.
Second, update frequencies are uneven. This requires support for incremental index configuration, so newly uploaded party building documents combined with business can be retrieved quickly.
Third, single documents are lengthy. Sufficient context must be retained during chunking to prevent semantic fragmentation after splitting, which would harm recall accuracy.
Fourth, documents have structured formats. Retrieval should prioritize matching entries with clear business tags to improve precision.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for This Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Match precise semantics of warehousing-related party building content, filter general party building fragments unrelated to warehousing business |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Control the number of document fragments retrieved in a single request, adapt to the short-context interaction requirements of party building Q&A |
| `searchMode` | hybrid | Combine keyword and semantic retrieval, cover party building-specific keywords related to warehousing business |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Reorder retrieval results, prioritize content with higher relevance to warehousing scenarios |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically expand synonyms for warehousing business related to party building, improve recall coverage |
| `collectionIds` | Bind dedicated warehousing party building dataset | Limit retrieval scope, avoid mixing party building data from other categories |

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base retrieval takes a long time, with noticeable delays in returned results. Cause: `usingReRank` is not enabled, `searchMode` is set to pure semantic retrieval, and no business metadata filtering for warehousing party building is applied. This results in retrieval of a large number of document fragments unrelated to warehousing business.
- Phenomenon: Error logs indicating the specified model was not called appear during workflow operation. Cause: `collectionIds` is not correctly bound. The retrieval scope includes non-warehousing party building datasets, triggering invalid model call verification.
- Phenomenon: The table of contents of uploaded party building documents is automatically deleted. Cause: The automatic document formatting function is not disabled. The system mistakenly identifies chapter titles in warehousing party building documents as redundant directory structures.

## How to verify correct configuration
- Access the knowledge base management page of FastGPT v4.17.0. Check whether `collectionIds` is bound to the dedicated warehousing party building dataset.
- Run a test retrieval. Input a party building question related to warehousing business. Confirm that the `similarity` score of returned results falls within the 0.75–0.85 range.
- Upload a party building document with a structured table of contents. Check whether the original table of contents structure is retained after document chunking.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, compare the result order before and after reordering. Confirm that content related to warehousing business appears at the top of the list.
