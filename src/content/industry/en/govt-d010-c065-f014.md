# Party Building Learning Q&A for Survey and Design: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What data for this category looks like
Data for Party Building learning Q&A in the survey and design field comes from internal party building records, learning materials issued by superior Party organizations, and shared documents from industry party building co-construction. Superior documents are updated synchronously upon release. Internal learning materials are updated every quarter. Document structure includes title, main text paragraphs, issuing entity, release date, and applicable job tags. Fields include document unique identifier, content classification, and release time. Storage units are characters and GB.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the conversation logs and auditing link
Data sources are scattered across multiple channels. Conversation logs must be linked to the unique document identifier of each source to ensure traceability of original materials during audits. Update rhythms vary across sources. The auditing link must synchronously record the document version at the time of conversation triggering to avoid referencing expired content. Documents include job adaptation tags. Logs must record the job attribute of the conversation initiator to match the corresponding learning scenario. Documents are split by chapter. Logs must accurately record the specific hit chapter position to facilitate locating specific learning materials.

## How to configure
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `chatId` binding rule | Bind the globally unique identifier of the conversation session | Ensure complete traceability of conversation logs within a single session, meeting audit requirements for session integrity |
| `responseChatItemId` association method | Associate the unique identifier of each reply | Precisely locate the context and material source of a single Q&A, facilitating problem traceability |
| Audit log retention period | 180 days | Match the standard archiving cycle of party building archives for survey and design units, meeting compliance audit requirements |
| Log detail switch `detail` | Enable full-field recording | Cover information such as conversation initiator, triggered document, hit chapter, etc., adapting to multi-dimensional traceability requirements for audits |
| Log export format | CSV format | Adapt to the import requirements of most audit tools, facilitating compliant archiving |
| Log retrieval permission configuration | Divide by job hierarchy | Match the internal data permission rules of survey and design units, preventing sensitive log leaks |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Call logs return data acquisition failure errors, and conversation traceability information cannot be viewed. Cause: `chatId` is not correctly bound to the session identifier of the corresponding conversation, causing the log system to fail to match the original conversation data.
- Phenomenon: Specified conversation records cannot be cleared after Docker deployment. Cleanup commands are executed but log files remain. Cause: No `responseChatItemId` filter condition is specified, leading to accidental deletion of unrelated session logs, or temporary file storage path configuration error resulting in incomplete cleanup.
- Phenomenon: Historical conversation records cannot be retrieved after sharing the application via web page. Cause: Log detail `detail` recording is not enabled, and context association information of the conversation is not saved, making traceability of historical interaction data impossible after sharing.

- Log in with accounts of different job permission levels, verify that only conversation logs within the corresponding permission range can be viewed.
