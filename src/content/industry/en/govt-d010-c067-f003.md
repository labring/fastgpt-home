# Fertilizer and Pesticide Party Building Learning Q&A: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category's data looks like
Fertilizer and pesticide industry party building learning data comes mainly from the party building archive database of state-owned enterprise chemical sector segments, relevant policy compilations from agricultural and rural affairs authorities, and learning materials uniformly distributed by the industry party committee. Update frequency is monthly for industry regulatory policy documents, and quarterly for centralized party committee learning courseware. Document structure includes policy clauses, learning key points, and supporting Q&A questions. Fields include publishing entity, effective time period, applicable category, core requirements, and unit identifiers such as chapter numbers and question numbers.

## Constraints Imposed on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The data contains exclusive applicable requirements for different fertilizer and pesticide categories. Multi-turn dialogue must continuously track the specific category mentioned by the user, to avoid policy confusion across categories. Monthly updated policy documents require regular synchronization of the knowledge base. Prompt engineering must add effective time period verification for the latest policies, to ensure dialogue content complies with currently valid requirements. The structured Q&A bank must match the context category during multi-turn dialogue, to accurately retrieve corresponding questions. Long-text policy content must limit the associated scope in the prompt, to avoid irrelevant fragments interfering with answer accuracy.

## Configuration Settings
This configuration plan is based on FastGPT v4.17.0. The following settings are configured for the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link in the fertilizer and pesticide party building learning scenario:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `variables` configuration | Preset the current session's fertilizer/pesticide category | Ensure multi-turn dialogue always links to party building learning content for the corresponding category |
| `messages` history turn limit | 8–12 previous turns | Avoid information overload from overly long context, and adapt to the typical dialogue length of party building Q&A |
| `chatId` binding rule | Bind the unique identifier of the enterprise party building account | Implement permission isolation and ledger association for single-account sessions |
| Conversation record retention period | 30–90 days | Align with the regular retention cycle for party building materials of state-owned enterprises |
| Knowledge base permission scope | Only open fertilizer and pesticide industry party building materials | Match the exclusive learning data boundary for the category |
| Context window upper limit | 8000–12000 token | Adapt to the reading requirements of long-text policy documents |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: The page displays "Session storage exception" after the conversation record retention period is set outside the recommended range. Cause: The retention cycle does not match the fertilizer and pesticide party building scenario. The setting exceeds 180 days, which goes beyond the platform's default storage limit.
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant medical industry party building materials are returned when calling the knowledge base. Cause: The category-specific knowledge base permission scope is not configured. The system fails to limit retrieval to only fertilizer and pesticide industry party building data.
- Phenomenon: The console returns the error "The value of 'offset' is out of range" when executing long-text policy interpretation tasks. Cause: The context association scope for long-text chunking is not limited, causing chunk offsets to exceed the actual length of the document.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Launch a session and specify a specific fertilizer or pesticide category such as urea or herbicide. Check that the returned party building learning content only includes policies and Q&A for the corresponding category.
- View the session history list. Confirm that old sessions older than 30 days are not automatically cleaned up, matching the preset retention period.
- Call the `variables` preset field. Verify that the session automatically binds the currently mentioned category parameter.
- Test a long-text policy parsing task. Confirm that no offset errors occur and the returned content fully covers the specified document fragment.

The values provided here are common starting points. They should be evaluated against the target deployment's own sample data.
