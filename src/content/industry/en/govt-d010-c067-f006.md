# Fertilizer and Pesticide Party Building Learning Q&A: Quote Source and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data in the fertilizer and pesticide sector comes from party building databases of party committees and branches under the chemical sector of central and local state-owned enterprises engaged in fertilizer and pesticide production and R&D. It includes theme education materials, industry party building guidance documents, branch study notes, and other content. Updates follow a regular quarterly rhythm, with temporary supplements during major party building activities. Documents use a structured format with four core fields: title, issuing unit, release date, and main body content. Each main body block is typically 1000 to 2000 characters, with a small amount of professional terminology related to agricultural production included.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Quote Source and Traceability Link
Structured document fields require that traceability must return metadata such as title and issuing unit synchronously. Returning only main body content fails to meet the compliance traceability requirements of party building work in central and local state-owned enterprises. Main body content mixed with professional terminology means professional terms cannot be truncated when cutting citations, to avoid matching irrelevant fragments during traceability. The quarterly update rhythm requires the knowledge base synchronization cycle to align with the update rhythm, otherwise expired or unupdated party building materials may appear in citations. Exclusive study materials from different branches require traceability to match the affiliated unit tag, to prevent incorrect cross-branch citations.

## Configuration Settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 characters | Matches the common length of single main body blocks in fertilizer and pesticide party building documents, avoiding truncation of professional terms and core study points |
| `responseData` | Includes title, issuing unit, release date | Matches the structured fields of documents, ensuring complete and compliant traceability information |
| `detail` | Enabled | Allows display of complete document source metadata, meeting the party building traceability display requirements of central and local state-owned enterprises |
| Maximum number of recalled entries | Top 3 entries | Party building learning Q&A requires accurate matching of core study materials; too many entries will interfere with answer accuracy |
| Knowledge base synchronization cycle | Quarterly | Aligns with the quarterly update rhythm of party building documents, ensuring the timeliness of cited content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Incorrect rendering of document formatting in context citations: The citation area only displays plain text, without retaining structured information such as the original document's title and issuing unit. This occurs when the `detail` configuration item is not enabled, only returning main body fragments without loading full metadata fields.
- Failure to recall knowledge base files during questioning: No matching document content appears in the context citation area after submitting a question. This may be caused by a knowledge base synchronization cycle set too long, failing to synchronize the latest quarterly party building materials in time, or a maximum recalled entry count set too low, resulting in no matching documents being found.
- Unable to customize citation display: The context citation area is always visible, and cannot be hidden via the interface switch. This occurs when the `responseData` parameter is not configured correctly, forcing return of traceability information without retaining configuration space for custom switches.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a fertilizer and pesticide party building study document to the knowledge base, initiate a test question containing relevant professional terms, and check if the context citation area includes complete metadata such as title and issuing unit.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` parameter to a low value, enter a long-text test question, and confirm that cited content does not truncate key professional terms.
- Check the knowledge base synchronization logs in the FastGPT backend to confirm the synchronization cycle matches the quarterly update rhythm of party building documents.
- After submitting a test question, check if the context citation area can be hidden via the interface switch, confirming the `responseData` configuration is effective.
