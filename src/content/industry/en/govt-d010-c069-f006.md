# Party Building Learning Q&A for Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation: Citation Sources and Traceability

This document is written based on the configuration instructions for FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like
The Party Building learning Q&A data for hazardous chemicals storage and transportation mainly comes from the enterprise’s internal hazardous chemicals storage and transportation safety manuals, storage and transportation compliance specifications released by the local emergency management department, post operation SOPs, and quarterly updated safety ledgers. The data update rhythm is monthly synchronization of compliance clauses, and quarterly updates of post responsibility lists. Documents are stored in structured chapter format, including fields such as document number, effective date, responsible post, compliance clauses, etc. Some clauses are accompanied by graphic and text attachments of operation procedures. Units involved include cubic meters, hours, tons and other storage and transportation-related measurement standards.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "citation sources and traceability" link
Data sources are scattered and include external compliance documents and internal ledgers, which requires the citation traceability link to support associated recall of multi-source documents and avoid missing cross-source compliance content. The monthly and quarterly update rhythm requires regular triggering of incremental knowledge base synchronization to ensure that traceability content is consistent with the latest compliance requirements. Structured fields and measurement units require accurate matching of the responsible post, effective date and other information corresponding to the clauses during traceability, while accurately presenting storage and transportation-related measurement standards to avoid unit errors in responses. The multi-chapter document structure requires locating specific clauses by chapter during traceability, avoiding returning irrelevant full document content.

## How to set the configurations

| `responseData` | Enable source traceability fields | Ensures that responses are accompanied by structured information such as document number and effective date |

## Three common mistakes
- Responses contain content with unmatched measurement units, and the traceability fields are empty. The cause is that the `detail` configuration is not enabled, and measurement units and structured fields in the documents are not extracted.
- The number of referenced documents returned in responses exceeds the expected limit. The cause is that the single search limit configuration is set too high, and the recall quantity is not adjusted according to the scattered sources of hazardous chemicals storage and transportation documents.
- Responses do not use the compliance clauses ranked first in the knowledge base. The cause is that recall results are not sorted by document effective date, causing older compliance clauses to be returned first.

## How to confirm the configuration is correctly set
- Initiate a Party Building-related hazardous chemicals storage and transportation Q&A, check whether traceability fields such as document number and effective date are attached at the end of the response.
- Check whether the clauses referenced in the response match the measurement units and responsible post information in the documents.
- After adjusting the single search limit configuration, verify whether the number of referenced documents returned meets expectations.
- Manually update a compliance clause, trigger incremental synchronization, and check whether the new clause is correctly recalled and traced.
