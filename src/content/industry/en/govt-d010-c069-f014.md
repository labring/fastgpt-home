# Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation Party Building Learning Q&A: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What This Type of Data Looks Like
Data is sourced from internal party building knowledge bases of state-owned and public sector enterprises engaged in hazardous chemicals storage and transportation. It includes theoretical learning documents issued by superior party committees, corporate compliance party building training materials, and daily team learning records. Update cadence is synchronized within 1 to 3 working days after superior documents are released. Daily new learning content is entered as needed. Documents use structured storage, with five core fields: document title, publishing entity, publishing time, affiliated party building module (such as theoretical learning, compliance education), and main body content. Main body content is counted in characters. Publishing time uses ISO date format.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Conversation Logs and Auditing
Party building learning Q&A documents are linked to hazardous chemicals storage and transportation compliance clauses. Conversation logs must record cited specific document IDs and compliance chapters/sections to ensure audit traceability. Documents are updated on an irregular cycle. Logs must accurately record timestamps for conversation initiation and response to avoid audit deviations caused by document version changes. Structured document fields require logs to support filtering by dimensions such as publishing entity and party building module, to improve audit efficiency. Hazardous chemicals storage and transportation involves industry compliance supervision. Logs must retain complete user questions, AI responses, and cited fragments, with storage cycles meeting regulatory requirements.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The front end displays no historical records after refreshing the conversation window, but complete sessions can be queried in background logs. Cause: The binding logic between `chatId` and front-end session cache is not configured correctly. The front end does not associate the background session identifier, resulting in failure to pull corresponding data after refresh.
- The front end cannot display historical conversation records in login-free mode. Cause: The front-end display permission for the `detail` parameter is not enabled, or the `chatId` mapping rule for login-free sessions is not configured, resulting in failure to match and display historical records.
- A cannot read properties of undefined (reading) error occurs when calling the Q&A model. Cause: The generation logic for `responseChatItemId` is not configured correctly, resulting in missing association identifiers for response logs, interrupting the model call chain.
