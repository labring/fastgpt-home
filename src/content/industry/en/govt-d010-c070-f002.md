# Party Building Learning Q&A for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Vector Models and Indexes

## What data for this category looks like
Party building learning data for power generation equipment manufacturing enterprises mainly comes from internal party building ledgers, three meetings and one lesson records, monthly standardized learning materials, party member activity archives, and party building materials integrated with production frontline work. Data update cycles include real-time temporary party building notices, monthly learning documents, and quarterly party member assessment and activity summary documents. Document structures include plain text learning materials, activity record sheets with participant count and completion duration fields, and party member performance-related documents with assessment scores measured in points.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexing
Real-time temporary party building notices require support for high-frequency incremental indexing to avoid excessive system resource usage from full reindexing. Activity record sheets with participant count and completion duration, plus documents with assessment scores, need structured data vector processing to preserve field associations within tables during chunking. Party building materials combined with production work include specialized fields like equipment numbers and production batches; these must retain original units and semantics to prevent semantic drift in vector encoding. Long-text party course materials need proper chunking to avoid individual chunks exceeding the vector model’s token limit.
