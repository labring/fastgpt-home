# Party Building Learning Q&A for Power Generation Equipment Manufacturing: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What this category of data looks like
Power generation equipment manufacturing party building learning Q&A data mainly comes from official documents issued by superior party committees, internal branch three meetings and one lesson records, party member training courseware, annual party building compilation materials, and party building theme documents combined with production scenarios.
Update rhythm is adjusted per superior deployment and internal training plans. Official documents are updated quarterly. Temporary learning materials are released as needed.
Document types include numbered official documents, chaptered PPT courseware, structured Excel ledgers, and PDF manuals. Fields include document number, release date, learning theme, and associated equipment type. Metrics include study duration, participant count, and document page count.

## Constraints on the document parsing and chunking link
Fixed format of official documents requires retaining metadata during parsing, to avoid losing key information such as document number and issuing unit after chunking.
Embedded equipment diagrams in PPT courseware and PDF manuals need complete text extraction, to preserve the binding relationship between party building knowledge points and production scenarios.
Structured Excel ledgers contain multi-dimensional fields. Extracting only visible columns will cause missing associated data, which affects retrieval accuracy.
Large annual party building compilation files have large volume and long parsing time, requiring longer processing cycles.
Additionally, party building content combined with power generation equipment production often includes long passages. Chunking needs to balance completeness and retrieval granularity, to avoid content breakage or overly fragmented chunks.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A timeout error triggers when parsing large party building PDF courseware, and the interface displays request timeout. Cause: The `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` parameter is not adjusted, and the default value is used, which fails to complete parsing of large documents.
- Phenomenon: After uploading an Excel party member study ledger, the number of data entries returned during retrieval is far less than the source file, and entries associated with equipment types cannot be filtered. Cause: Complete field extraction configuration is not enabled, and only visible columns are extracted, resulting in loss of associated fields and limited retrieval scope.
- Phenomenon: After uploading a PDF party building document embedded with a steam turbine diagram, the chunking result loses the text description next to the diagram. Cause: The `customPdfParse` custom parsing mode is not enabled, and the default parsing only extracts plain text and ignores image-text associated content.
