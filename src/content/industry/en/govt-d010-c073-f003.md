# Party Building Learning Q&A for State-Owned Industrial Funds: Multi-Turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for state-owned industrial fund party building learning Q&A is primarily sourced from the fund’s internal party building archives, annual learning plans and policy documents issued by the superior party committee, and party building standard materials required by industry supervision. Data updates follow a rhythm of monthly releases of current learning materials, and quarterly updates of policy documents. Each individual document uses a chapter structure, with three core fields: title, body text, and citation source. Documents are categorized as whole units of "article", and the length of a single body text typically falls between 500 and 3000 characters.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
The chapter structure of documents requires multi-turn dialogue to maintain thematic relevance across context, preventing disjointed questions and answers from straying from the learning theme. Monthly updated materials require prompts to clearly state the latest data update cycle, ensuring responses are based on currently valid content. Fields that include citation sources require the dialogue process to trace the basis of responses, avoiding fabricated content. Compliance requirements for party building content of state-owned industrial funds mandate that multi-turn dialogue be strictly limited to the party building learning scope, with no unauthorized content allowed. These characteristics jointly constrain the context length of multi-turn dialogue, the boundary setting of prompts, and the traceability requirements of responses.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The conversation page crashes, and the interface displays request timeout or 500 error. Cause: The context length of `messages` is not limited, causing the conversation data carried in a single request to exceed the service’s carrying limit.
- Symptom: The AI response deviates from the party building learning scope, and the guiding prompt does not fulfill its limiting role. Cause: No dedicated theme variable is configured via `variables`, and the response boundary is not clearly defined.
- Symptom: Recalled party building documents do not match the current question, and the number of results exceeds the set value. Cause: No reasonable context recall count is set, introducing unrelated learning materials.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a continuous conversation that includes a prior learning topic, check whether the conversation interface retains the last 3 rounds of context records.
- Enter a non-party building related question, check whether the AI prompts that it can only answer content related to state-owned industrial fund party building learning.
- Initiate multiple independent conversations, confirm that the session identifiers of each conversation are unique.
- Test a conversation of more than 10 consecutive turns, check whether the interface does not display loading failures or crashes.
