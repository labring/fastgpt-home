# Party Building Study Q&A for State-Owned Industrial Funds: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Party building study Q&A data for state-owned industrial funds mainly comes from internal party building archives, guidelines for state-owned enterprise party building work released by the state-owned assets regulatory authority, and study materials issued by superior party committees. Some content is linked to party building linkage requirements of its own investment projects, such as party building supervision points during project implementation. Data is synchronized as needed; core policy documents are updated upon official release, and daily study materials are collected and organized monthly. Documents are mostly structured text, with clear hierarchical titles, publishing entity, effective date, topic classification and other fields. Some supporting meeting minutes and special project party building reports in PDF format are also available. Individual document lengths range from hundreds to tens of thousands of words. Some documents have annotation fields linked to project numbers. This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0 configuration rules.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation source and traceability link
The multi-source nature of party building data requires the traceability system to distinguish display formats between internal archives and external guidance documents to avoid confusion. The unique field of linked project numbers requires traceability results to display corresponding project information simultaneously, facilitating internal verification of the linkage between party building study and business operations. The on-demand update feature of study materials requires the data source synchronization rule to support flexible triggering mechanisms, ensuring that the latest content is included in the retrieval scope in a timely manner. Structured hierarchical titles require traceability positioning to be precise to specific sections, improving the accuracy of study references.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: No links in Notion format sources are displayed in traceability results. Cause: Data source synchronization rules supporting external unstructured links are not configured; only structured links from internal archives are supported by default.
- Phenomenon: After entering a Chinese question, no traceability results for English party building literature in the knowledge base are returned. Cause: Multi-language matching retrieval rules are not configured; currently only semantic retrieval of Chinese text is supported.
- Phenomenon: After creating a knowledge base, questions unrelated to party building content return results. Cause: A reasonable similarity threshold for filtering low-relevance results is not set, resulting in retrieval of irrelevant document fragments.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Initiate a party building-related question, check whether clear source annotations, including document name and release date, are attached at the end of the returned results.
- Check whether the linked project number field is included in the traceability results, matching the unique information in industrial fund documents.
- Adjust the question content to a non-party building related topic, confirm that irrelevant document fragments are not retrieved.
- Test uploading a party building study link in Notion format, confirm that it can be synchronized normally and displayed in traceability results.
