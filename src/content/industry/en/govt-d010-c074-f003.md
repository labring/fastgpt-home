# Party Building Learning Q&A for State-owned Asset Management Companies: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
State-owned asset management company party building learning data draws primarily from superior state-owned assets party building guidance documents, internal party building archive libraries, and party member learning ledgers. Teams update superior policy documents immediately as required, and synchronize internal learning materials every quarter. Most documents use official document formatting with hierarchical headings, and include fields such as issuing unit, release date, and applicable scope. Some assessment rules include structured table content, and the word count of individual documents varies widely.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The need to update superior party building guidance documents immediately requires multi-turn dialogue to link to the latest policy content, and prompts to synchronize latest rules regularly. Internal party building materials include structured table content. Multi-turn dialogue must support recognition and extraction of structured data, and prompts must specify the extraction format for structured information. The wide variation in word count of individual documents means multi-turn dialogue context windows must adapt to long text input, and prompts must limit responses to content related to state-owned asset management company party building scenarios.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: Historical context is lost during multi-turn dialogue, and responses cannot link to previous party building policy questions. Cause: The maximum context window configuration value is lower than the total length of historical `messages` in actual multi-turn dialogue.
- Phenomenon: Response content outside the scope of state-owned asset management company party building appears in conversations. Cause: The prompt prefix does not limit the business scenario, or the knowledge base bound by `variables` does not filter exclusive party building data.
- Phenomenon: The preview interface works normally but an error occurs in the formal environment, prompting that the associated knowledge base cannot be loaded. Cause: The `chatId` configuration in the formal environment does not correctly link to the exclusive knowledge base, or the knowledge base permissions have not been synchronized to the formal deployment node.
