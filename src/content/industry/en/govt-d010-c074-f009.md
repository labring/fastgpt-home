# Party Building Learning Q&A for State-owned Asset Management Companies: Model Access and Configuration

## What this type of data looks like
Party building learning data for state-owned asset management companies comes primarily from internal party building ledgers, policy documents issued by superior party committees, themed education learning materials, and party member study experience archives. Data is updated monthly with the latest superior policies, and every quarter with internal learning dynamics and assessment records.
Document formats include chaptered formal policy texts, standardized question and answer banks, and individual study experience entries. Fields include document number, issuing unit, release date, applicable hierarchy, and core viewpoint summary. Document lengths vary widely, from several hundred-word Q&A entries to complete policy compilations of tens of thousands of words.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on model access and configuration
The multi-source nature of party building data requires model access to support multi-source knowledge base synchronization. This avoids Q&A deviations caused by data fragmentation.
A high proportion of long-text policy documents requires context window configuration to support long-text recall and association.
The precise wording of official policies requires model-generated content to strictly align with established specifications, reducing randomness in outputs.
Field differences across documents require clear retrieval priority configuration during setup, to ensure Q&A accuracy.
Compliance requirements for state-owned asset management companies also mean model calls must follow internal permission control rules, to prevent sensitive information leaks.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Scenario: After adding a locally deployed large model, testing works normally, but the model does not appear in the FastGPT backend model list. Cause: The access whitelist allowed by the platform was not added to the `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` configuration, so the platform cannot recognize the service.
- Scenario: After submitting a party building policy Q&A, the returned result does not match official expressions. Cause: The `temperature` value is set too high, leading generated content to deviate from established official policy standards.
- Scenario: After attempting to use text-to-image functionality to process party building-related materials, the model returns no valid response. Cause: The `vision` parameter does not need to be enabled for the party building learning Q&A scenario. Enabling it by mistake occupies the model's core computing power, causing Q&A functionality to malfunction.
