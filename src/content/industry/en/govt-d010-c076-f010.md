# Party Building Learning Q&A for State-owned Capital Operation Companies: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data for state-owned capital operation companies mainly comes from policy documents issued by higher-level party committees, internal company party building records, minutes of three meetings and one lesson, and themed education learning materials. The update schedule adjusts with key work priorities. Full archives are updated quarterly as routine. Temporary policy files are synced immediately upon release. Document structures usually include fields such as document number, issuing unit, release date, and content category. The word count of single documents varies widely. There are short notices of hundreds of words, as well as theoretical learning compilations of tens of thousands of words.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall Link
These data characteristics create multiple constraints for the retrieval and recall link:
First, data sources are scattered and include clear classification fields. Retrieval scope must be limited to the exclusive party building dataset to avoid mixing in business documents.
Second, document length varies significantly. Excessively long single pieces of content cause context overflow. The total amount of content recalled in a single pass must be controlled.
Third, update frequency is not fixed. The retrieval system must support incremental synchronization and real-time recall of the latest content.
Fourth, the formal nature of party building content requires retrieval results to match official expressions, avoiding generalized recall of irrelevant content.

## Configuration Settings
This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0. All configuration items use officially specified parameters, as detailed below:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Adapts to the formal writing style of party building documents, avoids recalling irrelevant generalized content, and covers core policy knowledge points |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Balances context length and knowledge coverage. Party building documents have concentrated content; excessive recall causes model context window overflow |
| `searchMode` | Keyword + semantic hybrid retrieval | Adapts to the content structure of party building documents, which have both clear policy expressions and theoretical interpretations, balancing precise matching and semantic understanding |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Performs secondary sorting on recall results, corrects deviations from single semantic retrieval, and matches the hierarchical logic and importance of party building content |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically expands official synonyms and standard expressions related to party building, improving recall accuracy for policy documents |
| `collectionIds` | Bind the exclusive party building dataset ID of the state-owned capital operation company | Isolates party building data from different units, avoids cross-institutional content confusion, and ensures the retrieval scope only covers the target dataset |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: No retrieval results are returned after adding the party building knowledge base, with an empty result array returned. Cause: The `collectionIds` binding to the exclusive dataset was not configured correctly, so the retrieval scope did not cover the target documents.
- Symptom: A timeout error occurs when adding a knowledge base after deploying a large model locally. Cause: The `similarity` threshold or `limit` count was not adjusted, leading to excessive recalled content exceeding the model's context window and causing a timeout.
- Symptom: Retrieval results include non-party building business documents. Cause: The binding scope of `collectionIds` was not strictly limited, or the classification filtering logic of `searchMode` was not enabled, leading to generalized recall of irrelevant content.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Enter the FastGPT dataset management page, confirm that the `collectionIds` of the target party building dataset are correctly bound to the retrieval configuration.
- Submit a test question related to party building, check whether the number of retrieval results matches the `limit` configuration value.
- Adjust the `similarity` threshold, verify that the relevance of retrieval results shows a reasonable trend as the threshold changes.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, compare the retrieval sorting results before and after enabling to confirm that the reranking logic is working properly.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
