# Party Building Learning Q&A for Urban Public Transport: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data sources for urban public transport party building learning include party building documents issued by the public transport enterprise party committee, monthly study minutes of party branches, party member study records from front-line teams, and policy materials synchronized from superior party building platforms.
Update frequency varies by document type: Superior policy documents are synchronized in real time. Monthly study minutes are updated each month. Daily team study records are archived weekly.
Most documents are structured text, with fields including study topic, issuing unit, release date, study key points, and assessment requirements. Some documents are associated with business identifiers such as bus routes and stops.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the "Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall" Link
The hierarchical dispersion of party building data sources requires support for multi-collection cross-database retrieval. The `collectionIds` configuration must be used to associate multiple business-related knowledge base collections.
The feature that documents are associated with public transport business identifiers requires retrieval to match both party building topics and business keywords such as routes and stops. The `searchMode` parameter must be adjusted to adapt to hybrid retrieval logic.
Documents with different update frequencies require differentiated incremental and full synchronization strategies to avoid invalid full updates consuming system resources.
Coexisting short team study records and long branch minutes require adjustment of chunking rules to fit different text lengths and ensure semantic integrity.
Party building learning Q&A must strictly match original policy texts, so the relevance threshold for recall results must meet the strict requirements of party building learning, to avoid mixing irrelevant content.

## How to Configure the Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `collectionIds` | Fill in the collection IDs of party building documents, branch study minutes, and front-line team study records in order | Adapt to cross-collection retrieval needs for multi-level party building data sources, covering all party building learning materials |
| `searchMode` | hybrid | Support simultaneous matching of semantic similarity and keyword matching, adapting to the hybrid retrieval logic of party building topics and public transport business identifiers |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Ensure semantic relevance between recall results and party building questions, avoid mixing irrelevant content, and meet the strict requirements of party building learning |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Balance the amount of recalled information and context length, avoid excessive results overwhelming the model, while covering sufficient learning materials |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Perform secondary ranking on initial recall results, improve the priority of high-relevance content, and meet the accuracy requirements of party building Q&A pairs |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Automatically expand synonyms and related expressions of questions, covering different question formats in party building learning, such as matching "three meetings and one lesson" with "branch meeting system" |

This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: After enabling `usingReRank`, the rerank marker in retrieval return results is false, or reranked results are not sorted by relevance. Cause: The association key for the rerank model is not configured in the FastGPT backend, or the input format of the rerank model does not match the current knowledge base chunking format, causing the rerank process to fail to trigger normally.
- Symptom: When selecting variable application in the "Knowledge Base Search" node, there are no optional options in the reference variable dropdown menu. Cause: No string-type variable for the corresponding knowledge base ID is created in global variables or workflow variables, or the variable's scope does not cover the current workflow node.
- Symptom: The number of retrieval results is far lower than the configured `limit` value, and a large number of irrelevant non-party building content is included. Cause: `searchMode` is not correctly set to hybrid mode, only semantic retrieval is used without keyword filtering, causing confusion between party building topics and public transport business content, leading to recall of irrelevant documents.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Correct
- Enter the FastGPT knowledge base management page, check that the collections configured in `collectionIds` include the three types of data sources: party building documents, branch study minutes, and front-line team study records, and confirm that the collection IDs are correct.
- Submit a test question related to party building, such as "What is the three meetings and one lesson system for public transport party branches?", check the relevance of the retrieval results, and confirm that high-relevance content ranks first.
- View the retrieval log, confirm that the status of `usingReRank` is true, and the reranked results are sorted as expected.
- Try to dynamically specify the knowledge base through variables, check whether the reference variable dropdown menu displays the configured global or workflow variables, and confirm that the variable scope is correct.
