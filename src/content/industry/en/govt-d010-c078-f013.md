# Urban Water Utilities Party Building Learning Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
Data sources for urban water utilities party building learning Q&A include internal party building knowledge bases of urban water investment and water utilities, industry party building guidance documents issued by superior party committees, and study records and archived materials from grassroots party branches.
Update schedule follows a fixed rhythm: superior party building requirement documents are synchronized monthly, and grassroots party branch activity records and archived study reflections are updated quarterly.
Document structure is divided into three modules: party building theory, key points of party building in the water utilities industry, and party branch work standards.
Fields included are document title, publishing entity, publishing time, main body content, and associated water utilities operation and maintenance party building guidance number.
Publishing time uses standard date format. Main body content is measured in characters. Associated guidance number is a numeric string.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding workflows
The data includes internal grassroots branch records and documents issued by superior party committees. This requires sharing and embedding configurations to include identity authentication, to restrict access from unauthorized users.
The associated water utilities operation and maintenance party building guidance number requires embedded Q&A systems to retain field associations, allowing users to jump to corresponding business documents.
The modular document structure allows sharing configurations to limit the Q&A scope, focusing on the urban water utilities party building scenario.
The monthly and quarterly update schedule requires embedded knowledge bases to support scheduled cache refreshes, to avoid outdated content.
Additionally, party building content for water utilities must be integrated with daily operation and maintenance scenarios. Embedded Q&A systems must support jumping to associated business guidance documents, so relevant field mapping settings must be retained during configuration.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Symptom: After iframe embedding, the main application console displays the error "iframe actively interrupts main application code running in sub-application". Cause: Cross-domain cookie passing is not configured, causing session conflicts between the FastGPT embedded page and the internal water utilities system, triggering browser cross-domain interception.
- Symptom: The identity authentication option cannot be found on the general settings page of the FastGPT backend. Cause: The dedicated details page of the target urban water utilities party building knowledge base is not accessed. Authentication configuration for versions v4.9.11 and above must be accessed via the sharing menu in the knowledge base details page.
- Symptom: The AI and user avatars in the embedded Q&A window are not replaced with the water utilities party building brand style. Cause: Brand icon files that meet the size and format requirements are not uploaded, causing avatar loading failures.
