# Party Building Learning Q&A for Urban Gas Industry: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data for the urban gas industry comes primarily from internal study ledgers archived by the enterprise’s party committee office, policy interpretation documents issued by superior party organizations, and transcribed texts of internal party lecture recordings. Updates occur on a weekly schedule for new study tasks and policy highlights. Most individual documents are chaptered Word or PDF files, with fields including study topic, issuing unit, release date, core highlights, and assessment requirements. Individual document lengths typically range from 5,000 to 10,000 characters.

## Constraints for Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The chaptered structure of party building learning data requires multi-turn dialogue to accurately match content from corresponding chapters, avoiding irrelevant cross-chapter information interference. The weekly update frequency means prompts must explicitly prioritize the latest study materials to ensure response timeliness. Dedicated fields in the data require dialogue configurations to bind dedicated data for specific party branches or stations, preventing mixing of study content across different entities. The long document format limits the upper limit of the multi-turn dialogue context window, preventing content overflow that causes loss of critical information.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Answers to follow-up questions in multi-turn dialogue do not follow the previous conversation context, resulting in content disconnection. Cause: Context passing rules for the `messages` parameter are not configured correctly, so dialogue history is excluded from the retrieval scope.
- Symptom: Prompt settings do not take effect, and returned party building content does not meet preset requirements. Cause: The prompt does not explicitly limit usage to only this enterprise’s urban gas industry party building study data, and does not specify priority matching fields.
- Symptom: Uploaded party building study PPT attachments cannot be retrieved. Cause: The parsing function for the corresponding document format was not enabled, and the prompt did not specify priority access to attachment content.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
A test query may be submitted with the prompt "Please explain gas safety-related requirements in combination with this month’s party building study content". The returned content can be checked to confirm inclusion of the latest study highlights from the current month.
A multi-turn dialogue may be initiated: first submit the question "What was the theme of last week’s party lecture", then follow up with "What are the gas station assessment requirements corresponding to this theme". The second response can be checked to confirm it follows the previous topic context.
The `variables` parameter binding configuration can be reviewed to confirm association with the enterprise party branch ID field, ensuring dialogues from different branches match their dedicated data.
The `chatId` configuration can be checked to confirm each dialogue session has a unique ID, preventing context mixing across different sessions.
Confirm the currently used FastGPT version is v4.17.0, to ensure configuration functions operate correctly.
