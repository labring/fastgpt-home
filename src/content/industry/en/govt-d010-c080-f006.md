# Party Building Learning Q&A for Urban Investment Platforms: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Party building learning Q&A data for urban investment platforms mainly comes from internal party building archive systems, official documents issued by superior party committees, branch three meetings and one lesson records, and internal learning materials. Documents are categorized by party building themes, with structures including fields such as title, body, issuing unit, and release date. The character count of single documents varies widely. Data updates are synchronized with monthly party building work progress, with no fixed batch update cycle. Updates are only triggered when new party building documents are added or learning materials are updated.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on citation sources and traceability
The multi-source nature of urban investment platform party building data requires the citation traceability link to accurately associate the issuing entity of the document, to avoid confusing same-name documents issued by party committees at different levels. The non-fixed update rhythm means traceability must support real-time matching of the latest version of party building materials, and cannot rely on fixed cache expiration times. Document fields include release date and issuing unit, so cited content must display these metadata to ensure complete traceability information. The wide character count range of single documents affects the length of cited fragments, so the matching range of traceability fragments must be adjusted based on content importance.

## Three common mistakes
1. Phenomenon: The returned cited document does not match the actually retrieved party building document. Cause: Failed to correctly associate the issuing unit and release date fields of the document, leading to confusion of same-name documents.
2. Phenomenon: Citation content is returned even when the citation switch is not enabled. Cause: The default value of the `detail` parameter is enabled in the version configuration, and the non-essential traceability output switch was not manually adjusted.
3. Phenomenon: The streamed returned citation information is not synchronized with the Q&A analysis results. Cause: The traceability information parsing logic was not bound to the token stream of streaming output, leading to delayed or misaligned traceability information.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Initiate test questions related to party building, check whether the returned results include document metadata information.
- Check the `responseData` field in the API return, confirm whether it contains the required traceability-related content.
- Manually disable the citation traceability configuration, verify that no additional citation fragments are included in the returned results.
