# Party Building Learning Q&A for Urban Investment Platforms: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for urban investment platform party building learning Q&A comes primarily from policy documents issued by superior party committees, internal three meetings and one lesson records, monthly party building learning key points, and internal Q&A banks. The data update schedule synchronizes the latest party building deployment content from superior authorities monthly, and updates internal learning cases and Q&A banks quarterly. Each document has a fixed structure, including title, issuing unit, release date, main content, and corresponding Q&A analysis. Individual Q&A content lengths range from 100 to 5000 characters, with no additional non-standard fields.

## Constraints Imposed on the Sharing and Embedding Workflow
Because party building content involves internal management specifications and some sensitive information, the sharing and embedding workflow must strictly restrict access permissions, and only allow internal employees of urban investment platforms to access. The fixed document structure requires that embedded Q&A retains the original context association, and arbitrary truncation of policy documents or learning key points is not recommended. The monthly and quarterly update schedule requires that embedded knowledge bases are synchronized regularly to avoid displaying outdated content. Additionally, official websites of urban investment platforms often combine internal office systems and public disclosure platforms, so embedded components must adapt to layout requirements for both scenarios.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Symptom: After embedding in a Vue3 project, calling the FastGPT interface returns a 403 status code, or external personnel can directly access the sharing link. Cause: The sharing authentication configuration is not enabled, or the internal account permission binding for the urban investment platform is not completed.
- Symptom: After embedding via iframe, Q&A content returned by the model cannot be retrieved, and the front-end page only displays loading failure. Cause: The corresponding party building knowledge base is not bound via `shareId`, or the front-end does not correctly listen to the iframe message event.
- Symptom: The returned Q&A content after embedding does not match internal party building materials, and irrelevant business information appears. Cause: The `shareId` of other categories is used, resulting in calls to non-party building knowledge bases.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Access the generated sharing link, verify that only internal accounts of the urban investment platform can log in and access, and external IP addresses cannot open the page.
- Embed an iframe component in a Vue3 project, enter party building-related questions, confirm that the page loads normally and returns corresponding Q&A content.
- Check the FastGPT backend logs, confirm that the `shareId` used in the interface call matches the exclusive ID of the party building knowledge base, with no parameter errors.
