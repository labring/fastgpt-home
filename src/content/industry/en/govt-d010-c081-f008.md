# Party Building Learning Q&A for Basic Chemical Industry: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data from state-owned and public enterprises in the basic chemical industry comes from internal party building records, team study records, monthly themed party day materials, and party member assessment archives. Data updates follow the schedule of daily party building activities. For batch uploads, materials are mostly provided as integrated compressed packages. Document types include Word study manuals, Excel party member points and training records, and PDF meeting minutes. Some documents contain multi-sheet tables and fixed headers and footers. Fields include study topic, number of participants, study duration, and assessment score. Units include person-times, hours, points, and other relevant units.

## Constraints Imposed on the Document Parsing and Chunking Link
Multi-sheet Excel records from basic chemical industry party building documents require the parsing process to identify business associations between different sheets to avoid mixing data across sheets. Long paragraphs of study minutes and party member reflections require chunking to retain contextual logic and avoid breaking core topics. Documents with fixed headers and footers featuring party building logos require the parsing process to filter redundant repeated content and reduce retrieval interference. Large-volume compressed packages uploaded in batches require the parsing process to support large file processing and timeout fault tolerance. These characteristics directly determine that document parsing and chunking configurations need targeted adjustments to adapt to the structure and update scenarios of party building documents.

## How to Configure the Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Basic chemical industry party building documents are mostly long-paragraph study records and structured records. This length balances contextual integrity and retrieval accuracy |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by title + paragraph | Party building documents have clear thematic title hierarchies. Splitting this way preserves complete content for individual topics |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 500 MB | Adapts to the volume of batch-uploaded team study record compressed packages to avoid upload failures |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 600 seconds | Large party building PDF manuals require longer processing time to avoid mid-process timeout interruptions |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Filters repeated header and footer logos in party building PDFs to reduce redundant retrieval content |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual configuration | Adjust chunking parameters separately for different types of party building documents such as records, manuals, and minutes |

This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After importing an Excel table dataset, retrieval results only show scattered cells with no complete table correspondence. Cause: Custom parsing configuration was not enabled for the table dataset, resulting in lost binding between headers and data rows.
- Phenomenon: A timeout error with status code 504 is returned when parsing large party building study manuals. Cause: The `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` setting is lower than the actual required parsing time, and does not adapt to the processing rhythm of large-volume documents.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results after chunking contain a large number of repeated party building theme header content. Cause: The `customPdfParse` configuration was not enabled, and fixed repeated header and footer information in party building PDFs was not filtered.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload a single party member points Excel record, check if the parsed data retains the binding relationship between headers and corresponding rows, and that party member points records can be retrieved normally.
- Upload a themed party day minute document of around 1000 characters, check if the chunked content completely retains key information such as meeting theme and core agenda, with no sentence breakage.
- Upload a single 400 MB party building PDF manual, check if the parsing progress completes within 600 seconds with no timeout errors.
- Test the retrieval matching degree of chunked content with different vector models, confirm that the results meet the retrieval needs of party building topics.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be evaluated against the relevant samples.
