# Party Building Study Q&A for Infrastructure Construction: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Party building study data in the infrastructure construction sector mainly comes from the party committee archives of central and local state-owned enterprise construction units, study records of temporary party branches on construction sites, and standardized party building study materials issued by superior party committees. The data update schedule is to synchronize the latest policy documents from superior authorities every month, and update on-site project frontline party building study records every quarter. Most documents are structured, chaptered text, containing fields such as document number, issuing unit, release date, and applicable construction section scope. The length of a single document ranges from 5,000 to 20,000 characters.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "multi-turn dialogue and prompt" link
The long document length and the presence of construction section limitation fields require that the multi-turn dialogue context length be limited within a processable range, to avoid information loss caused by truncation triggered by overly long text. The high data update frequency requires adding an indicator of the latest knowledge base synchronization in the multi-turn dialogue prompts, to ensure that responses match currently valid party building policies. The applicable construction section field requires that prompts be bound to the exclusive study scope of the corresponding project, to prevent incorrect invocation of cross-section party building content.

## How to configure the settings
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0. The specific parameters are as follows:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Maximum Context | 8000–10000 characters | Most single-party building study documents for infrastructure construction range from 5000 to 20000 characters. Limiting context length avoids truncation of core study content |
| `messages` | Retain the last 10 rounds of interactions by conversation turn | Multi-turn party building Q&A needs to associate section information from previous questions. Retaining the last 10 rounds covers common context requirements |
| `variables` | Bind construction section ID and project party building lead ID | Party building study needs to match the exclusive study requirements of the corresponding section. Using variables filters non-applicable content |
| Question Optimization | Enabled | Inquiries about infrastructure party building often contain technical terms and section references. Optimization improves Q&A matching accuracy |
| Single Search Limit | Top 6 results | Valid reference content for party building study Q&A is mostly concentrated in the top 5-7 results. Limiting the number of results avoids interference from redundant information |
| `chatId` | Bound to grouping by project temporary party branch | Party building study content for different sections is independent. Grouping by `chatId` isolates conversation contexts of different projects |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: The party building response returned after calling the model fails to output standardized answers as preset, for example, failing to use the officially specified expression format. Cause: The prompt is not configured in the `messages` parameter during conversation initialization, so the model does not load preset constraint rules.
- Issue: Complete historical conversation records cannot be queried, or the storage location of conversation data is unclear. Cause: `chatId` is not correctly bound for grouped storage, leading to scattered conversation data that cannot be retrieved uniformly.
- Issue: After uploading PPTs or documents related to engineering party building, subsequent conversations cannot associate content from the attachments. Cause: The processing priority of attachments is not clearly defined in the prompt, and attachment content is not bound and associated with the party building knowledge base.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate an inquiry containing a specific construction section name, and check whether the model response associates exclusive party building study content for the corresponding section.
- Initiate consecutive multi-turn inquiries that associate context, and check whether the model can correctly reference information from previous conversations.
- View conversation grouping records, and confirm that conversation data for different projects has been isolated and grouped by `chatId`.
- Upload party building-related attachments and initiate an inquiry, and check whether the model can complete the Q&A by combining content from the attachments.

The values provided here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
