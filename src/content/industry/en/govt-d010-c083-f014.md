# Party Building Learning Q&A for Infrastructure Construction: Conversation Logs and Audits

## What the data for this category looks like
Party building learning data in the infrastructure construction sector primarily originates from three categories: official documents issued by higher-level party committees, temporary learning materials at project sites, and daily party member learning records. The data update rhythm varies by source: official documents from higher levels are updated in real time upon release, on-site materials are updated weekly, and party members’ records are archived monthly. A single document includes fields such as title, body text, issuing unit, release time, and associated project number. The content is mostly text, with some attached image attachments. The size of a single document ranges from hundreds of KB to tens of MB.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "conversation logs and audits" link
The party building learning data for this category has scattered sources, frequent updates, and includes project-associated metadata, which brings three core constraints to the conversation logs and audits link. First, the project number field must be associated to divide the audit scope by construction project dimension and avoid cross-project log confusion. Second, incremental log filtering must be supported to adapt to frequently updated learning materials and reduce full data scan overhead during audits. Third, complete document reference paths and release metadata must be captured to ensure that the source and version of specific learning materials can be traced during audits, meeting the traceability requirements of party building archives for domestic state-owned enterprises and public institutions.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: Response speed slows after multiple rounds of questions in the conversation window, and knowledge base retrieval takes more than 1 minute. Cause: `chatId` is not bound by project dimension, resulting in excessive reference fragments of infrastructure construction party building documents accumulated in the conversation context, and the automatic cleanup mechanism is not triggered.
- Symptom: Port 3000 cannot be accessed after deployment, and the log shows a database connection failure. Cause: The database storage path for conversation logs is not configured, or the log storage quota of more than 1000 MB is not reserved, resulting in database initialization failure.
- Symptom: The specific content of the referenced party building document is not displayed in the logs. Cause: The `detail` full record mode is not enabled, only basic session metadata is recorded, and document reference details in the conversation are not captured.
