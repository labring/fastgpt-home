# Party Building Learning Q&A for Bulk Commodity Trading: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Party building learning data in the bulk commodity trading field comes from three main sources: internal party building ledgers of state-owned and state-controlled enterprises, party building policies related to bulk commodity circulation released by industry competent authorities, and branch study and discussion records.
Data update follows this rhythm: industry policy documents are updated once per month, and internal study records are synchronized once per week.
Each single document includes five fields: document title, issuing unit, release date, main content, and affiliated branch. Main content is mostly paragraph-based, and individual document lengths mostly fall between 2000 and 5000 characters.

## Constraints on Citation Sources and Traceability
Decentralized data sources require precise distinction between internal ledger and external policy traceability tags, to avoid mixing party building study content from different levels.
Differences in update rhythms require the traceability system to support incremental data pulling across different cycles, preventing expired content from being cited.
Document fields include issuing unit and affiliated branch. Full display of these metadata during traceability is required to meet compliance standards for party building studies.
The relatively long length of individual documents requires precise positioning to specific paragraphs during citation traceability, with only the source of the corresponding content displayed.

## Three Common Misconfiguration Issues
- Phenomenon: No optional content appears in the "citation variable" dropdown menu when configuring a dynamic knowledge base. Cause: The unique identifier parameter of the bulk commodity trading party building dedicated knowledge base has not been entered in global variables in advance.
- Phenomenon: Redundant input and response content from knowledge base searches are attached to tool call responses. Cause: The citation display switch at the end of the answer has not been turned off.
- Phenomenon: Source fields of different party building documents cannot be distinguished in logs. Cause: Full metadata collection for the `detail` parameter has not been enabled.
