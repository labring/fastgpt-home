# Party Building Learning Q&A for Commodity Trading: Model Access and Configuration
## What the data for this category looks like
Party building learning data in the commodity trading scenario comes from the enterprise’s internal party building archive, industry association party building guidance documents, and official policy interpretation materials related to commodity trading compliance. Data updates are adjusted according to the schedule of enterprise party building activities and the release rhythm of industry policies, with no fixed cycle. Document structures mostly combine structured entries and unstructured text, including fields such as policy document number, applicable trading category, issuing unit, release date, learning points, and original text content. The release date uses the YYYY-MM-DD format, the policy document number has a fixed prefix identifier, and the applicable trading category field marks the corresponding commodity category.

## Constraints on model access and configuration
Multi-source and heterogeneous data sources require model access to support unified vector mapping and indexing rules across data sources, to avoid format conflicts in party building content from different sources. Structured fields such as applicable trading category and policy document number included in documents require retaining field association logic during configuration, to ensure the model can accurately match the party building Q&A needs of the commodity trading scenario. The non-fixed update rhythm requires supporting flexible adjustment of the data synchronization cycle in configuration, without relying on fixed scheduled tasks. Long policy original texts and case content require the model’s context window to adapt to long text processing, to avoid truncation of key information. Party building Q&A must strictly align with official expressions, requiring model output to comply with party building specifications, and avoid generating interpretations that deviate from official content.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Basis for This Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Party building documents for commodity trading mostly contain long policy original texts and associated cases, requiring complete context coverage to ensure sufficient basis for Q&A |
| `maxTokens` | 2000–3000 token | Party building Q&A requires outputting complete learning interpretations and policy-related content, to avoid truncation of key expressions |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500–2000 token | It is necessary to retain complete policy entries cited in the original text, to ensure the compliance and accuracy of Q&A |
| `temperature` | 0.3–0.5 | Party building Q&A requires strict standardization, to avoid generating personalized content that deviates from official expressions |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in the exclusive interface address of the deployed model | Dedicated connection to the specified large model instance, to ensure stable call links |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Fill in the valid access key corresponding to the model | Ensure the security and permission legitimacy of interface calls |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The model returns results with forced display of thinking process, and the setting cannot turn it off. Cause: The `toolChoice` parameter is not configured correctly, or the thinking process switch logic of the model itself is not adapted.
- Phenomenon: The Q&A result does not associate the applicable category field exclusive to the commodity trading scenario, and the content is general. Cause: No index rules for structured fields are configured during the data access stage, causing the model to fail to match scenario-specific party building content.
- Phenomenon: The interface call returns a 401 permission error. Cause: The `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is filled in incorrectly, or the permission scope corresponding to the key does not cover the current call scenario.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- A party building question related to the commodity trading scenario, such as "How to implement party building compliance requirements in the commodity trading sector", is used to verify whether the returned result associates the corresponding policy entry and applicable category field.
- The model call log of FastGPT v4.17.0 is reviewed to confirm that the `maxContext` parameter takes effect, and no error prompts related to context truncation appear.
- The `temperature` parameter is adjusted to 0.1 and 0.7, the change in the rigor of the returned content is observed, and the parameter configuration is confirmed to take effect normally.
- The validity of the interface key is verified, a test call is initiated, and no permission-related error returns are confirmed.
