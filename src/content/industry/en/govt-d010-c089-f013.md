# Party Building Learning Q&A for Construction Machinery: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Party building learning data in the construction machinery sector comes from internal Party building archives of state-owned equipment manufacturing enterprises, study materials uniformly distributed by industry party committees, and Party building learning records from frontline teams. Updates occur quarterly, synchronizing the latest study requirements from superior party committees and newly added internal Party building materials. Most documents are structured formal texts, with fields including document title, publishing entity, effective date, and core study points. Units are marked as articles, study hours, and topics.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
The structured nature of internal Party building archives and industry study materials requires embedded content to retain associated display of document fields. This prevents loss of key information such as publishing entity and effective date after splitting.
The quarterly update cycle requires the embedded data source’s synchronization cycle to match the quarterly update frequency. This stops embedded content from becoming outdated.
Scattered learning records from frontline teams require the sharing function to support content aggregation by topic. It also requires limiting the character length of a single shared item to fit small-screen display on team terminals.
Additionally, Party building content in construction machinery scenarios often covers study points combining equipment maintenance and safety production. Embedding must retain precise matching logic for core points to avoid content deviation.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing link validity period | 7 days | Matches the quarterly update cycle to avoid long-term shared content becoming outdated |
| Embedded content display fields | Title, core study points | Retains key associated information of Party building documents, avoids content fragmentation |
| Single shared content length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to small-screen display scenarios of team terminals, avoids content overflow |
| Number of recalled items per share | Top 5 items | Controls the volume of shared content, adapts to the fast learning needs of frontline teams |
| Data source synchronization cycle | Every 90 days | Matches the quarterly data update cycle to ensure content timeliness |
| `shareId` | Bound to the exclusive ID of the construction machinery Party building knowledge base | Accurately associates learning data of the corresponding field, avoids cross-domain recall |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The embedded Q&A page displays the error "Failed to load Q&A content". The cause is that `shareId` was not filled correctly, and it was not associated with the exclusive construction machinery Party building knowledge base.
- Shared Q&A results include irrelevant equipment maintenance content. The cause is that the display fields of embedded content were not limited, and non-Party building documents were recalled, resulting in content deviation.
- The embedded Q&A page loads slowly. The cause is that the set single shared content length exceeds the terminal's carrying limit, leading to excessive data transmission volume.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Open the embedded Q&A page, confirm that the page displays content including the Party building document title and publishing entity.
- Generate a sharing link, verify that the link validity period matches the preset 7 days.
- Enter a Party building-related question, confirm that the returned results only associate Party building learning content in the construction machinery field.
- In the FastGPT v4.17.0 deployment environment, check the system logs for no error messages of `shareId` binding failure or permission interception.
