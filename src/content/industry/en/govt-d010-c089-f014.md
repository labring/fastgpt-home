# Party Building Learning Q&A for Construction Machinery: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for this category originates from a dedicated party building learning document library for state-owned and public enterprise equipment manufacturing sectors. It covers party constitution and regulations, industry party building policies, and party building learning materials tailored to construction machinery operation and maintenance scenarios. The system synchronizes official party building documents and industry party building updates quarterly, with no fixed upper limit on the number of documents updated per batch. Each individual document includes document identifier, publishing entity, publishing time, content category, and associated equipment model fields, stored in plain text format. Some documents include embedded fragments of party building event check-in records.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the conversation logs and auditing workflow
Conversation logs for party building learning Q&A must be associated with specific construction machinery equipment scenarios. Logs must record the equipment model and operation team information at the time a conversation is initiated, to prevent generic logs from failing to trace back to corresponding business scenarios. Since the data includes embedded party building activity record fragments, logs must also synchronize the embedded position of the original document, to ensure the specific content referenced by a conversation can be traced back during audits. Document updates follow a quarterly rhythm, so conversation logs must retain historical records for at least one full update cycle, to prevent conversations from older scenarios from failing to match latest reference materials after new documents are released. Additionally, the standardized field requirement for associated equipment models means device codes must undergo format validation when logs are stored, to avoid audit classification failures due to inconsistent coding.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The aiproxy_pg container fails to start when launching with docker-compose, with logs indicating a database connection failure. Cause: Database connection parameters for conversation log storage were not configured correctly, causing the auditing module to fail initialization.
- Symptom: Only partial content is displayed in conversation logs, and the associated equipment model field is empty. Cause: The binding rule for associated device information collection was not enabled, causing the `chatId` field to fail to correctly associate with the device code.
- Symptom: After a large model call via OneAPI fails, the corresponding error message cannot be viewed in the audit logs. Cause: The `detail` parameter for full log recording was not enabled, only simplified conversation summaries were stored, and detailed call failure errors were not captured.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
Initiate a party building learning Q&A conversation associated with a specific equipment model, and check whether log details include complete input and response content recorded by the `detail` parameter.
Check whether each conversation in the log list includes the associated equipment model field, confirming that the `chatId` field has correctly bound the corresponding device code.
Access the log storage directory to verify that archive files matching the configured storage period are automatically generated, confirming that the archiving rule has taken effect.
Attempt to access logs for a specific team using an unauthorized account, to verify that the retrieval permission configuration has taken effect.
