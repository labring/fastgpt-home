# Party Building Learning Q&A for Refined Oil Sales: Sharing and Embedding

This document is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Data is sourced from internal party building management platforms within the petroleum and petrochemical system, official documents issued by superior party committees, and learning ledgers of grassroots party branches. Update cycles align with superior policy adjustments and grassroots monthly learning plans. Documents cover three categories: original policy texts, learning interpretations, and practical operation guidelines. No fixed unified format is used. Fields include topic classification, publishing entity, effective date, and applicable scenarios such as pre-shift learning at gas stations and party member activities at oil depots. Single document length varies widely, ranging from hundreds to thousands of characters.

## Constraints for Sharing and Embedding Workflow
Internal party building data includes sensitive policies and grassroots work details. Apply strict access restrictions during sharing and embedding, limiting access to internally authorized terminals only. Split and configure shared content separately for documents of different applicable scenarios, such as gas stations and oil depots, to avoid cross-scenario content leakage. Make additional typesetting adjustments after embedding, and retain the original document hierarchy, as no fixed document formats exist. Support batch configuration for embedded deployment, given the large number of grassroots stations, and ensure compatibility with interfaces of station internal learning systems.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing access permissions | Internally authorized access | Refined oil sales party building data includes internal policies and work details, requiring strict restrictions on access from unauthorized terminals |
| Scenario filtering toggle | Enabled | Shared content must be split by scenarios such as gas stations and oil depots, matching the applicable scenario fields of the data |
| Sharing link validity period | 30 days | Matches the monthly update rhythm of party building learning content, preventing access to outdated content |
| Embedded code loading timeout threshold | 8–12 seconds | Adapts to differences in network environments at grassroots stations, balancing loading speed and stability |
| `shareId` binding configuration | Bind the exclusive knowledge base corresponding to the scenario | Ensure shared content only includes party building Q&A data for the corresponding scenario, avoiding cross-scenario content leakage |
| Guest login access toggle | Disabled | Internal party building data requires authorized access, preventing sensitive information from being leaked via guest login links |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Symptom: Guest login sharing links can directly access the FastGPT management backend login page. Cause: The sharing service port and management page login port were not configured separately, causing the sharing service to reuse login port permissions.
- Symptom: Embedded Q&A pages display messy document typesetting, with some title levels lost. Cause: The scenario filtering toggle was not enabled, and full party building data with multiple formats was embedded, without adapting to differences in document structures.
- Symptom: No matching content is returned for Q&A results after embedding into the station's learning system. Cause: `shareId` was not bound to the knowledge base corresponding to the scenario, and shared content is full unfiltered party building data that does not match the station's scenario.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Open the sharing link, verify that only party building Q&A content for the preset scenario can be accessed, and switching to documents from other scenarios is not possible.
- Attempt to open the sharing link from an unauthorized terminal, confirm that access is denied and a permission prompt is displayed.
- Embed the code on a test terminal at a grassroots station, verify that page loading time falls within the 8–12 second threshold, with no timeout errors.
- Modify the sharing link validity period, confirm that the link cannot be accessed normally after expiration.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against local deployment samples.
