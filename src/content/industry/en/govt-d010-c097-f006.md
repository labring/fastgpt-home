# Party Building Learning Q&A for Nuclear Power Operations: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
The Party Building learning Q&A data for the nuclear power operations sector originates primarily from official documents archived in the enterprise’s internal Party Building management system. These include Party Committee Central Group study minutes, branch "three meetings and one lesson" records, Party member education manuals, and superior-level Party Building work notices. The data is updated weekly to reflect daily branch study activities, and monthly to add updated superior-level Party Building policy documents. Each document typically contains structured fields such as document title, issuing unit, release date, and main body content. Document character lengths vary widely, ranging from several hundred-word special notices to several thousand-word systematic study materials.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Citation and Traceability
The varied document lengths, structured fields, and regular update schedule of nuclear power operations Party Building data create multiple constraints for the citation and traceability process. Long-form study materials require precise extraction of the original text segments matching the Q&A, to prevent truncation of key policy statements or core study points. Structured fields require that traceability results display metadata including issuing unit and release date, to fully present both the document’s metadata and main content. Frequently updated policy documents require the traceability system to quickly match the latest available content, to avoid referencing outdated files. Short-form notice documents must also ensure full recall of the original text when retrieved, to prevent information loss.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Some Q&A results do not display a source view entry, or all results force display of traceability content. Cause: No differentiated visibility rules are configured for different types of Party Building documents, and only a global unified traceability display status is set.
- Phenomenon: Some Party Building learning Q&A directly return the complete original text, without generating corresponding Q&A pairs. Cause: The character length of a single document exceeds the chunking threshold, or the chunk overlap setting is unreasonable, resulting in split fragments failing to match valid Q&A keywords.
- Phenomenon: An error is returned when testing the model call, but the traceability function operates normally during formal operation. Cause: The timeout threshold set during the test phase is too low, failing to adapt to the recall response time of nuclear power Party Building documents, and reasonable timeout parameters are automatically adapted during formal operation.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Initiate a Q&A targeting superior-level Party Building policies, and check whether the returned results include metadata such as document title and issuing unit.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` parameter, then verify whether the length of the citation fragment meets expectations, with no key content truncated.
- Configure differentiated visibility rules, and verify whether different types of Party Building documents correspond to the correct traceability display status.
- Initiate a Q&A targeting short-form Party Building notices, and confirm that the recall result fully covers the original text content with no information missing.
