# Party Building Learning Q&A for Nuclear Power Operations: Deployment and Upgrade

## What data for this category looks like
Party building learning data mainly comes from internal party building management systems at nuclear power bases, official documents issued by party committees, and learning records from each operation branch. The update rhythm follows a monthly schedule for branch monthly learning plans and learning reflections, and a quarterly schedule for superior party building policy interpretations and industry compliance requirements. Individual document lengths vary widely, including policy notices of hundreds of words, lecture slides of thousands of words, and transcribed learning records of tens of thousands of words. Document fields include title, release level, release date, and associated production team number. There is no uniform unit, and some documents include audio transcription content.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the deployment and upgrade phase
Party building data includes structured fields with associated production team numbers. During deployment, configure the mapping rules between this field and knowledge base metadata to ensure that learning content can be filtered by team dimension in subsequent operations. Document lengths vary widely, ranging from hundreds to tens of thousands of words. During deployment, adapt the long document chunking logic to avoid excessively long chunks affecting retrieval efficiency. The monthly update rhythm requires retaining incremental synchronization configuration entries during upgrades to avoid full re-import occupying operational resources. Some documents come from audio transcriptions, so enable parsing support for the corresponding formats during deployment to ensure complete and accurate text extraction.

## Three common mistakes
- Code execution nodes throw runtime errors with status code 500. The cause is failure to adapt to the intranet isolation rules of the nuclear power base, and failure to configure correct proxy parameters, resulting in inability to access internal party building data sources.
- No party building documents are displayed after knowledge base synchronization. The cause is failure to correctly configure metadata field mapping, and failure to associate the production team number field, resulting in documents being automatically filtered by the system.
- Incremental synchronization tasks time out and interrupt after upgrade. The cause is failure to adjust the value of `DB_MAX_LINK`, with the number of links during full re-import exceeding the threshold, resulting in database connection overload.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Access the configured `FE_DOMAIN`, verify that the frontend page loads normally with no domain resolution or certificate errors.
- Run the knowledge base synchronization task, check the synchronization log to confirm that all party building documents have been successfully parsed and written to the knowledge base.
- Submit a test question related to party building, verify that the returned results include internal party building policies and learning content, with no empty results or irrelevant content.
- Check the connection status of `REDIS_URL`, confirm that the cache service is running normally, and no session data is lost or has anomalies.
