# Party Building Learning Q&A for Civil Aviation Airports: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What data for this use case looks like
Data for civil aviation airport party building learning Q&A comes from the airport's own party building archive library and public documents issued by the superior civil aviation party building competent department. The update rhythm follows adjustments to superior policies, with at least one update per month. Documents use standardized party and government official document format, including standard fields such as title, main recipient, main text, attachment note, etc. Each document includes release unit and effective date identifiers.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
The standardized format of civil aviation party building documents requires that the file structure cannot be arbitrarily split during multi-turn dialogue, and complete official document logic must be retained. The exclusive civil aviation grassroots party building scenario limits the applicable scope of Q&A. Prompt engineering must clearly distinguish between general party building content and specific requirements of civil aviation industry party building. The monthly updated data source requires that the dialogue system's knowledge base synchronization cycle matches the update rhythm to avoid outdated content. Context of civil aviation party building exclusive terms must be retained during multi-turn interaction to prevent the model from confusing general concepts with industry-specific expressions.

## Three common mistakes
- Opening the conversation page displays "No permission to operate this conversation record". The cause is that `chatId` is not bound to the exclusive permission group of the airport party organization, resulting in failed session ownership verification.
- A "File not uploaded" prompt is returned when document reading is triggered during conversation. The cause is that the unique identifier of the corresponding civil aviation party building document is not injected via `variables`, so the model cannot locate the target content in the knowledge base.
- No Word download entry appears after the conversation is completed. The cause is that the `messages`-based session content export trigger rule is not configured, and complete conversation data is not bound to the export task.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Initiate an initial question containing civil aviation party building exclusive terms, check whether the conversation context fully retains the question content.
- Call the interface related to `chatId` to view session ownership, confirm that it matches the permission group of the target party organization.
- Trigger the document reading operation, verify whether the model can correctly read the content of the corresponding civil aviation party building document.
- After completing one round of multi-turn conversation, check whether a clickable Word download link is generated.
