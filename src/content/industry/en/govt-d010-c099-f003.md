# Party Building Learning Q&A for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What this category of data looks like
Data sources include internal party building archives of petroleum and petrochemical enterprises, party building learning materials supporting exploration and development projects, and party building policy documents released by industry authorities. Updates synchronize latest policies and project-specific learning content on a regular basis. Most documents are long text with hierarchical headings, containing original policy texts, core learning points, and case analyses. Available fields include document title, publishing entity, publishing date, and affiliated business sector. Character count is used as the content statistics unit.

## Constraints imposed on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
First, individual documents have long lengths. Multi-turn dialogue context transmission must avoid truncation of key information. Reasonably control the length of historical conversations and knowledge base matching content passed in. Second, documents include the affiliated business sector field. Prompts must explicitly bind to the oil and gas exploration and development business scenario to prevent mismatch between general party building content and project-specific needs. Third, data updates follow a regular schedule. Prompts must include retrieval guidance for latest policies. Multi-turn dialogue context must support filtering latest learning materials by publishing date. Fourth, multi-source data is mixed. Prompts must explicitly specify the data source scope to ensure responses are based only on content combining party building in the petroleum and petrochemical industry and exploration and development work.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Prompts cannot extract party building learning content with time ranges such as this year or this month. Cause: Prompts do not bind time dimension association rules under the oil and gas exploration and development scenario, and do not explicitly require combining project cycles or industry policy release times.
- Phenomenon: Multi-turn dialogue cannot continue after setting the context count to 0. Cause: The `messages` parameter is not configured correctly for historical conversation transmission, leading to loss of context information with each question, and preventing coherent Q&A.
- Phenomenon: The knowledge base configured in the workflow cannot retrieve corresponding party building content. Cause: The business tag is not bound via the `variables` parameter, leading to retrieved content mixing general party building and non-oil and gas exploration and development documents.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a party building question related to the oil and gas exploration and development scenario, check whether the response links to the industry business scenario.
- Initiate consecutive multi-turn questions, check whether the `messages` parameter transmits historical conversation content, and whether the response follows contextual logic.
- Adjust the context length parameter, check whether long document content is fully passed in without truncation.
- Verify that the `chatId` parameter is generated per project, check whether conversation contexts across different projects are isolated.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
