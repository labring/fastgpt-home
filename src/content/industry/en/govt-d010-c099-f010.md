# Party Building Learning Q&A for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What this category’s data looks like
Party building learning data for the oil and gas exploration and development sector comes primarily from internal Party building documents of petroleum and petrochemical state-owned enterprises, compilations of Party building case studies from frontline production sites, and learning materials distributed by superior Party organizations.
Data updates follow a fixed quarterly schedule, with temporary additions of Party activity records from newly commissioned well sites.
Document structures include fields such as work area number, well site name, learning theme, and activity date.
Most individual documents range from 1000 to 5000 characters in length.
Some documents combined with production data also include business-related information such as operation cycles and geological parameters.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on knowledge base retrieval and recall
Data includes clear business-related fields. Retrieval must support filtering by work area, well site, and other dimensions to avoid mixing irrelevant Party building content across work areas.
Individual documents have relatively long lengths. Retrieval chunking must adapt to long text structures to prevent semantic segmentation from damaging the integrity of Party building learning content.
Update rhythms include both fixed cycles and temporary additions. The recall mechanism must prioritize newly updated documents while retaining historically compliant content.
All data sources are internal classified or semi-classified documents. Retrieval scope must be strictly limited to the specified knowledge base set, and cross-category calls to other datasets are not allowed.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When calling the application, only content within the knowledge base is returned. External information cannot be retrieved even if a network-enabled AI model is configured. Cause: The knowledge base-specific retrieval mode is enabled by default, and `searchMode` is not configured as a cross-source retrieval mode.
- Phenomenon: Non-oil and gas exploration Party building content appears in retrieval results. Cause: The `collectionIds` configuration to limit the knowledge base scope is not set, leading to retrieval across category datasets.
- Phenomenon: Insufficient retrieval results fail to cover the complete context of Party building Q&A. Cause: The `limit` value is set too low, failing to adapt to the long text characteristics of oil and gas exploration Party building documents.
