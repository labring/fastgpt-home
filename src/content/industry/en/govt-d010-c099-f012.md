# Party Building Learning Q&A for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development: Deployment and Upgrade

## What the data for this category looks like
Three main sources supply the data: internal party building management platforms, policy documents issued by superior party committees, and archived party building activity materials from frontline exploration and development teams.
Superior documents are synchronized immediately upon release. Internal study manuals are updated every quarter. Frontline cases are archived monthly as activities conclude.
Documents include structured policy texts, unstructured study manuals, and case compilations. Fields include issuing unit, release date, and applicable exploration operation segments. Individual document lengths vary significantly.

## Constraints Imposed on Deployment and Upgrade by These Characteristics
Multi-source, scattered data sources require deployment of a unified document parsing adapter. The adapter must adapt to multiple party building material formats, including policy texts, study manuals, and case compilations, to avoid parsing failures.
Data sources with differing update schedules require adjustment of incremental synchronization cron job parameters during upgrades. Parameters must match the immediate synchronization of superior documents, quarterly internal manual updates, and monthly frontline case archiving to ensure data timeliness.
Wide variation in document length requires deployment of text processing rules that cover the full length range. This avoids chunking or storage errors.
Documents with operation segment fields require deployment of field-level indexes. This ensures accurate subsequent filtering and retrieval based on exploration and development segments.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- A 404 error is returned when starting the FastGPT service, and the party building Q&A module fails to load. Cause: Authentication information for `PG_URL` or `REDIS_URL` is not filled out correctly, or the service node cannot access the configured database address.
- Only a small number of matching results are returned when retrieving party building Q&A content, and documents from all exploration and development segments are not covered. Cause: Field-level indexes are not configured, and the retrieval scope is not filtered based on the operation segment field, resulting in limited retrieval results.
- A large number of duplicate document entries appear during incremental synchronization of party building materials. Cause: Cron job parameters are not adjusted to match the data source update schedule, leading to repeated triggering of synchronization tasks, and document unique identifiers are not verified.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- The frontend domain configured via `FE_DOMAIN` is accessed, and the party building Q&A module is checked for normal loading, with no blank or error pages.
- A test oil and gas exploration and development party building document is uploaded, parsing and import operations are performed, and confirmation is made that the document is successfully stored in the specified database.
- A test query related to party building is submitted, and confirmation is made that the retrieval results include matching document content, and that the number of returned results matches expectations.
- Backend logs are checked to confirm that there are no abnormal error reports for database connections, cache read/write operations, and other processes.
