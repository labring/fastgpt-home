## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the source citation and traceability workflow
The fixed issuing unit and release date fields for Party building data require traceability information to include these metadata, ensuring the officiality and traceability of cited content. The monthly update rhythm requires knowledge base sync to match the cycle, avoiding citation of expired learning materials. The fixed structure and fields of documents require precise association of corresponding metadata when citing, rather than only extracting unmarked body content. Chunking requirements for some long documents require cited passages to retain contextual connections, avoiding out-of-context quotes that harm the accuracy of learning content.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Garbled text appears in cited snippets shown in the interface. Cause: No encoding adaptation is applied to specialized terms and abbreviations in oilfield Party building documents (such as work zone codes, Party building-specific terms), leading to character conversion errors during parsing.
- No knowledge base source information is displayed in the response. Cause: The source field return configuration is not enabled, so the interface does not return traceability fields such as issuing unit and release date.
- Cited content exceeds the answer length and causes messy formatting. Cause: The maximum cited token count value is not restricted, leading to overly long cited passages that damage the overall readability of the Q&A.
