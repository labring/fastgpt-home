# Party Building Learning Q&A for Port and Shipping Industry: Vector Models and Indexes

## What this category's data looks like
Party building learning Q&A data for the port and shipping industry comes mainly from official documents on the port group’s party building intranet, monthly branch study plans, meeting minutes of "three meetings and one lesson", front-line party members’ study reflections, and policy documents issued by higher-level party committees.
Data updates follow a weekly sync for branch dynamics, and monthly updates for party committee study arrangements. Most documents are in PDF or Word format. Their structure includes title, main text, issuing unit, and release date.
Content often includes party building-specific terms such as "three meetings and one lesson" and "party member pioneer post", and also contains industry-specific expressions such as port berth scheduling and container operations.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexing
The mixed use of party building-specific terms and industry professional expressions requires vector models to retain cross-domain semantic connections, and avoid losing single-domain semantics.
Diverse document formats and fixed party building sections require indexes to support multi-format parsing and structured semantic extraction.
The weekly and monthly incremental update rhythm requires indexes to have incremental indexing capabilities, to avoid performance losses from full reconstruction.
Growth in the number of branches leads to expanded data volume, requiring the index’s storage capacity to scale as needed, while retaining context carrying capacity for single-round Q&A.

## How to set the configuration
This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the paragraph logic of party building documents and port professional terms, avoids slicing through coherent expressions such as "three meetings and one lesson" and "berth scheduling" |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retains semantic connections across chunks, prevents professional terms from being unrecognizable after being truncated by chunking |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The interface shows a connection failure for the locally deployed VLLM embedding model, and logs contain 504 timeout errors. Cause: The port of the VLLM-deployed Qwen3-Embedding-8B is not exposed to the network where FastGPT is located, and the correct API access address is not configured.
- Phenomenon: The knowledge base disk usage statistics show three parts: original files, split chunks, and vector data, but actual usage is much higher than expected. Cause: The automatic import switch for non-party building content is not turned off, causing irrelevant port business files to be included in the vector storage.
- Phenomenon: When launching a party building Q&A, the number of recalled relevant documents is less than the configured single search limit. Cause: The chunk length is too short, causing party building-specific terms to be split, and the vector model cannot recognize complete semantic connections.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Upload a test document that contains both party building terms and port professional expressions, manually check the split chunk content, and confirm that core terms are not truncated.
- Launch a test question related to party building, check that the number of recalled documents matches the configured single search limit, and all content is related to the party building theme.
- Check the disk storage path, confirm that the storage logic of vector data, split chunk files, and original files meets the configuration requirements, with no redundant file accumulation.
- View the model connection logs, confirm there are no timeout or authentication errors, and the vector generation process completes normally.
