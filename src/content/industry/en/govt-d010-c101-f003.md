# Party Building Learning Q&A for Port and Shipping Industry: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What data for this category looks like
Party building learning data for the port and shipping industry mainly comes from official documents released by the group’s party affairs department, study records from subordinate branches, party building requirement documents from superior transportation systems, internal training materials and meeting minutes. Data is updated in monthly centralized batches, with supplementary content added for new policies on an ongoing basis. Most documents use structured formats, including titles, body paragraphs, and attachment links. Fields cover publishing department, publish date, topic category, and applicable positions. Document sizes typically range from hundreds of KB to several MB.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
The position classification field in party building data requires multi-turn dialogue to adjust answer professionalism based on user role. Avoid overly general statements for frontline crew and shore-based management positions. Attachment links and long text content in documents require multi-turn dialogue to retain contextual association, and avoid losing key policy paragraphs. The monthly update rhythm requires configuring a regular synchronization mechanism to ensure Q&A content always matches the latest official statements. Embedded video transcription content requires adapting chunk length for long text processing, to avoid exceeding model context limits.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Phenomenon: Chinese and English opening lines cannot function simultaneously in multi-turn dialogue, only a single language version is displayed. Cause: No language switching trigger rule configured in `variables`, no binding of user language usage scenarios.
- Phenomenon: Embedded images fail to load normally in imported Markdown format party building documents. Cause: No image domain name auto-completion rule configured in the document synchronization process, domain name replacement only triggered during Word import.
- Phenomenon: Context is truncated after importing long documents, making it impossible to fully answer associated party building policy questions. Cause: No reasonable chunk length and overlap length configured, leading to key policy paragraphs being split and lost.
