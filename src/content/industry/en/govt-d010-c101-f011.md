# Party Building Learning Q&A for Port Shipping: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for port shipping party building learning Q&A mainly come from internal party committee databases, branch meeting minutes, party member learning ledgers, and policy documents issued by superior party organizations. Update rhythms fall into two categories: fixed cycle and temporary trigger. Monthly updates cover branch learning ledgers and policy compilations. Temporary party building notices and post-specific learning requirements are uploaded immediately. Document structure includes fields such as document title, issuing unit, release date, applicable posts, learning theme, study hour requirement, and assessment key points. Common applicable post categories include terminal operation posts, vessel management posts, and administrative logistics posts. Study hour requirements are measured in hours.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on forms and interactions
The post-adaptable field in documents requires form interactions to support filtering Q&A materials by post category. This prevents pushing learning content for non-applicable posts. The mixed update rhythm of data sources requires form configurations to include manual refresh and scheduled sync options. This ensures Q&A materials align with the latest party building requirements. Numeric fields such as study hour requirements require adding validity checks during form input. This prevents submission of invalid values. Long-text meeting minute documents require form interactions that support long input and segmented parsing logic.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: When adding team members, entering the other party's account shows "username invalid". Cause: No port party building-specific account whitelist rules configured. Only internal party building work accounts are allowed to initiate Q&A interactions.
- Phenomenon: When importing party building documents into the knowledge base, pages keep refreshing and cannot be selected. Cause: Did not adapt to the long-text structure of port party building documents. The default chunking logic cannot correctly parse multi-chapter large meeting minutes.
- Phenomenon: Speech input on iOS devices cannot recognize port party building-specific terms. Cause: No speech recognition enhancement rules configured for port party building professional expressions. General speech models cannot accurately recognize content such as "terminal dispatch party member responsibility zone".

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Submit a test question that includes post information. Confirm that only party building learning materials for the applicable post are pushed.
- Manually trigger data source sync. Wait for sync to complete, then check if the document update time in the knowledge base matches the latest party building materials.
- Enter an invalid value such as a negative study hour count. Confirm that the form displays a corresponding error prompt.
- Initiate long-text question input. Confirm that the input box does not trigger forced truncation or errors.
