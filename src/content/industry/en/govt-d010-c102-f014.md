# Party Building Learning Q&A for Refinery and Chemical Production: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What this category of data looks like
Party Building learning Q&A data for refinery and chemical production scenarios is sourced from internal enterprise Party Building knowledge bases, industry Party Building guidance document compilations, and Party Building specifications linked to production scenarios. Updates are synchronized on demand, with content updates completed within 7 working days after major policy releases. Document structure includes title, publishing entity, effective date, and main body content. Fields include unique identifier, content text, associated business scenario tags. Main body length is recorded in characters. Some documents include associated tags related to production safety.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the conversation logs and auditing link
The business scenario tags associated with the data require conversation logs to synchronously record production scenario tags involved in Q&A, to facilitate quick location of compliance inspection scope during audits.
The non-fixed update rhythm requires logs to record the knowledge base document version and synchronization time for each round of Q&A calls, to ensure the timeliness of traced content can be verified during audits.
The effective date field included in documents requires the auditing link to verify whether Party Building documents used in conversations are within their effective cycle, to avoid using expired policy content.
The unique identifier field requires logs to bind `chatId` and the document's unique identifier, to achieve precise traceability of the data source for a single round of conversation.
Production-related associated tags require logs to fully retain context, to prevent key compliance information from being lost and affecting audit accuracy.

## How to set the configuration
This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Generate a unique value per user per session | Ensure logs for each round of conversation can be independently traced, avoiding confusion between logs from different sessions |
| `detail` | Enable full log recording | Retain full conversation context and document reference information, meeting complete traceability requirements for audits |
| Conversation log storage period | 180 days | Meets conventional retention period requirements for Party Building audits in Chinese state-owned and public enterprises, balancing storage costs and compliance |
| Log associated document identifier | Bind the document's unique ID field | Achieve precise mapping between Q&A content and original Party Building documents, facilitating verification of content compliance during audits |
| `responseChatItemId` | Generate a unique identifier per round of reply | Distinguish between multiple rounds of replies within a single session, facilitating location of audit nodes for specific replies |
| Audit log export permission | Restricted to administrator roles only | Prevent unauthorized personnel from tampering with or leaking Party Building-related conversation logs, meeting confidentiality requirements |

## Three common errors
- Symptom: A MongoDB connection failure prompt appears when accessing the FastGPT interface, making it impossible to view conversation logs. Cause: MongoDB storage parameters for knowledge base synchronization were not configured correctly, preventing log data from being written to the database.
- Symptom: Some conversation replies are not recorded in the log after the client's SSE connection is closed. Cause: The connection was interrupted before `responseChatItemId` generation was completed, leading to incomplete log writing.
- Symptom: Pulling conversation history by application dimension results in an unprocessable excessive data volume. Cause: Log grouping rules based on `chatId` plus user identifier were not configured, making it impossible to quickly filter data by user or session during audits.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Enter the FastGPT log management page, check whether the `chatId` of a single session matches the session identifier and is unique.
- Export a section of conversation logs, confirm that the `detail` field includes complete context and document reference information.
- Test interrupting the SSE connection, then re-enter the session, confirm that the full conversation history has been recorded.
- Verify that the log storage period meets preset compliance requirements, and confirm that only administrators can access audit logs.
