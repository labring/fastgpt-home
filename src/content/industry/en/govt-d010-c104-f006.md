# Coal Chemical Industry Party Building Learning Q&A: Source Citation and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
The data sources for coal chemical industry party building learning Q&A are internal group party building knowledge bases, industry party building guidance documents, and specialized party building materials combined with coal chemical industry safety production. The update rhythm follows the internal group document release cycle, with no uniform fixed cycle. The structure of each individual document includes title, body paragraphs, publishing entity, and release date fields. Each piece of data comes with a unique source identifier for subsequent traceability. Most document content contains industry-specific terminology and policy statements, and the overall length varies widely.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "source citation and traceability" link
The exclusive publishing entity and date fields of coal chemical industry party building data require the traceability link to fully display this information to ensure the authority of citations. The existence of the unique source identifier requires the configuration to support precise matching of individual data entries to avoid traceability confusion. The wide variation in document length requires chunking and recall configurations to adapt to long paragraph content, avoiding splitting that destroys the integrity of policy statements. The feature that the update rhythm follows internal release adjustments requires traceability information to synchronize with the knowledge base update status to ensure the timeliness of cited content.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The number of model context entries displayed on the page does not match the number actually sent to the inference interface. Cause: The configuration logic between `quoteMaxToken` and maximum context is not correctly matched, leading to abnormal truncation rules.
- Phenomenon: Unable to remove or customize citation identifiers in responses. Cause: `responseData` was incorrectly set to disabled, or traceability fields were not correctly called in the response template.
- Phenomenon: Citation limit prompts are triggered frequently. Cause: The single search limit configuration is too low, and it does not match the actual entry scale of the coal chemical industry party building knowledge base, leading to recall exceeding the limit.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Correct
- Launch a test query related to coal chemical industry party building, check whether traceability fields such as publishing entity and release date are attached at the end of the response.
- View the inference log, verify that the number of context entries passed to the interface matches the configured maximum context value.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, observe whether the display length of the cited fragment in the response meets expectations.
- Test toggling the citation display switch, confirm that citation identifiers in the response can be normally displayed or hidden.
