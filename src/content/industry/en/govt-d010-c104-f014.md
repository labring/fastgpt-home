# Coal Chemical Industry Party Building Learning Q&A: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for coal chemical industry party building learning Q&A comes from internal enterprise party building archived materials. These include monthly updated party committee study notices, quarterly organized branch study minutes, and standardized party building knowledge base entries. Individual document body lengths vary widely. Each document has structured metadata: title, body, release date, affiliated party organization, and more. Metadata fields include document ID, title, release time (formatted as YYYY-MM-DD), and affiliated branch. Body content is measured in characters, and release time is measured in days. Data updates follow a consistent schedule: new study materials are added monthly, and archived updates are completed quarterly. This ensures the timeliness and completeness of materials.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on conversation logs and auditing
The data characteristics of coal chemical industry party building learning Q&A directly constrain the configuration logic for conversation logs and auditing.
First, document bodies are long, and metadata includes affiliated branch and release time. Audit logs must support filtering by branch and time dimension. They must also precisely associate conversations with corresponding document fragments, to avoid redundant or irrelevant associations.
Second, data updates follow a consistent schedule. Log retention cycles must align with document archiving cycles. This ensures interactive records for full study cycles can be traced.
Third, individual document content is professional, and includes statements linking party building and production. Logs must fully record interactive details, to avoid information loss that would impact audit compliance.

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: After configuring a text extraction node in the workflow, no `responseChatItemId` tag appears for the corresponding session in conversation logs. Multi-turn interactions cannot be traced by session. Cause: Session ID binding logic is not configured in the workflow node. This leads to interactive logs being disconnected from their sessions, and group audit cannot be completed.
- Phenomenon: Log query results only show simplified interactive summaries. Full AI reply content and associated document fragments cannot be viewed. Cause: The `detail` log switch is not enabled. Only basic interactive information is recorded, which cannot meet the full-chain traceability requirements of audits.
- Phenomenon: Log retention period is too short. Historical conversations beyond the set period cannot be queried. Cause: The retention period does not match the archiving cycle of coal chemical industry party building documents. The set retention period does not meet enterprise audit compliance requirements.
