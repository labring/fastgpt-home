# Party Building Learning Q&A for Coal Transportation and Sales: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What This Category’s Data Looks Like
Party building learning Q&A data for the coal transportation and sales sector comes from three main sources: internal enterprise party building management systems, official party building documents issued by party committees, and industry party building guidance documents.
Update follows a fixed schedule: monthly sync for that month’s central group study materials and branch three meetings and one lesson content, quarterly sync for annual party building summary and assessment archives.
Most individual documents range from 1,000 to 10,000 characters. Standard fields include document title, publishing entity, publication date, document number, and main text. Some documents include participation record fields for party building activities.

## Constraints on Citation and Traceability
The data characteristics of coal transportation and sales party building learning Q&A create multiple constraints for the citation and traceability process.
Official internal documents require traceability information to precisely match structured fields such as document number and publishing entity. This avoids vague, generalized citations.
The monthly and quarterly update schedule requires the knowledge base incremental sync logic to separate update cycles for study materials and summary archives. This prevents responses from citing expired documents.
Long individual document lengths require traceability to limit the length of cited passages. This avoids exceeding context window limits.
Structured document fields require configuration to extract specific fields for traceability display. Displaying only file names cannot support accurate traceability.

## How to Configure

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Party building documents for coal transportation and sales have a wide range of individual document lengths. Limiting the token count of a single cited passage prevents context window overflow |
| `responseData` | Enabled | Structured traceability data must be returned to meet compliance traceability requirements for coal transportation and sales party building scenarios |
| `detail` | Enabled | Full context of cited passages must be displayed to facilitate verification of the accuracy of party building learning content |
| Citation source display fields | Document title, publishing entity, document number | Core identifying fields for coal transportation and sales party building documents, enabling accurate location of cited sources |
| Incremental sync cycle | Sync study materials monthly, sync summary archives quarterly | Matches the update schedule of coal transportation and sales party building data, ensuring the timeliness of cited content |
| Single recall limit | Top 3 results | Document classification in coal transportation and sales party building knowledge bases is clear. Limiting the number of recalled results avoids redundant information interfering with responses |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Traceability content appears in responses even after citation display is disabled. Cause: The knowledge base cache configuration was not updated synchronously, or there is a compatibility issue with the switch logic in FastGPT v4.17.0.
- Phenomenon: Returned traceability documents do not match the actually cited content. Cause: The document’s unique identifier field was not associated during the recall phase, leading to matching with incorrect document versions.
- Phenomenon: Cited passages exceed the preset length. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` configuration value is not adapted to the average length of coal transportation and sales party building documents, or the chunk configuration is not correctly linked to traceability fields.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Initiate a query related to party building learning, and check if the traceability module under the response displays the specified configured fields.
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` configuration value, and verify if the length of cited passages in the response matches the expected adjusted result.
- Manually modify the update time of a party building document, trigger a knowledge base sync, then verify if new queries prioritize citing the updated content.
- Disable the `responseData` configuration, and verify if structured traceability data is no longer included in responses.
