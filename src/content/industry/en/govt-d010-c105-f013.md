# Coal Transportation and Sales Party Building Learning Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category’s data looks like
Party building learning Q&A data for the coal transportation and sales sector comes from four main sources: internal enterprise party building management systems, industry party building policy documents issued by superior party committees, daily branch study records, and party member training archives.
Updates follow a monthly baseline schedule. Immediate supplementary updates are made when special party building policy adjustments occur in the energy sector.
Each document has a fixed structure with four core fields: title, issuing unit, release date, and main content. Some documents include extracted text from study courseware.
Unified field formats apply: release dates use the YYYY-MM-DD format, document numbers carry the exclusive `Coal Party Issued` prefix, study duration is measured in hours, and meeting attendance is counted in individuals.

## Constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding workflows
Since the data originates from internal confidential or sensitive party building management materials, the sharing and embedding workflow must strictly control access permissions. This prevents unauthorized external access to internal documents.
The fixed document structure and field formats require embedded pages to retain official release metadata. Omitting this metadata will reduce the authority of the party building Q&A content.
The monthly update schedule requires the embedded knowledge base to sync regularly. Without regular syncing, embedded page content will lag behind the latest party building study materials.
Exclusive party building terminology and industry policy content for coal transportation and sales require embedded modules to retain full context. This prevents semantic deviations in Q&A responses.

## Three common configuration errors
- Embedded pages pop up a universal login window instead of supporting WeChat Work scan-code login. This occurs because enterprise-specific login verification rules are not configured, and the platform’s default universal login method is used.
- Embedded pages fail to load the Q&A module normally. This occurs because the `shareId` parameter is not passed correctly, or the knowledge base bound to the parameter does not have sharing permissions enabled.
- Embedded pages display full fragments of original party building documents. This occurs because the reference content display toggle was accidentally enabled, violating confidentiality configuration requirements.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Copy the generated share link, open it in an internal enterprise browser, and verify that the Q&A module loads normally and returns party building-related content.
- Check the `shareId` parameter in the embedded code, confirm that it matches the exclusive ID of the target coal transportation and sales party building knowledge base.
- Test the share function on the embedded page, verify that the generated link has the preset 7-day validity period.
- Review the displayed content on the embedded page, confirm that only the title and release date are shown, with no original document fragments.
