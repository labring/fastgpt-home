# Coal Transportation and Sales Party Building Learning Q&A: Conversation Logs and Auditing
## What this category of data looks like
Data sources for coal transportation and sales party building learning Q&A include job-specific training materials archived by the party building office of coal transportation and sales enterprises, party building policy documents issued by the superior state-owned assets supervision and administration commission party committee, and general party building learning materials.
Update schedule: Superior policy documents are stored upon release. Internal job-specific training materials are updated once per quarter. General learning materials are updated once per month.
Each data entry includes a unique identifier, document number, issuing entity, release date, applicable job type (such as transportation scheduling, coal shipment, contract management), original text content, and associated party building knowledge point tags. Fields are primarily text, date, and enumeration types. Units include articles, copies, and entries.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on conversation logs and auditing
Data is associated with specific transportation and sales jobs, so conversation logs must be bound to job identifiers to avoid mixing cross-job learning records. This ensures audits can accurately match learning content corresponding to the job.
Update rhythms vary: superior policy documents are stored in real time, internal training materials are updated quarterly, and general learning materials are updated monthly. Audit filtering must support classification by issuing entity and update time, to facilitate tracing conversation records linked to learning materials from different sources.
Each data entry includes applicable job tags. Log audits must verify the match between the job that initiated the conversation and the applicable scope of the document, to ensure learning content complies with job compliance requirements.
Party building content involves compliance requirements for state-owned assets supervision. Log retention must comply with industry regulatory cycle requirements, and complete conversation context must be recorded to facilitate subsequent traceability, verification, and rectification.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Conversation log retention period | 12 months | Complies with state-owned assets supervision requirements for retention of party building-related data, and matches the management cycle of party building archives for coal transportation and sales enterprises |
| `chatId` binding rule | Bind to transportation and sales job ID | Coal transportation and sales party building Q&A requires distinguishing learning content for different jobs. Associating `chatId` with job IDs enables accurate positioning of job-related logs |
| Log detail collection switch | Enable the `detail` parameter | Records complete conversation context, meets the need to trace learning processes and verify Q&A compliance during audits, and adapts to the log collection rules of FastGPT v4.17.0 |
| Historical record association source | Support `responseChatItemId` to associate document ID | Matches the document number of party building learning materials, facilitating tracing specific conversations by learning material |

| Log filtering dimensions | Classify by job and release time | Matches the classification characteristics of coal transportation and sales party building data, improving audit efficiency |

## Three common configuration errors
- Conversation logs are not generated after calling the built-in party building Q&A plugin in a workflow. The cause is failing to connect the workflow node's conversation to the global log collection link, and failing to bind the `chatId` field.
- When querying historical records via an interface, only logs from API sources are returned, and party building learning documents cannot be associated. The cause is failing to configure the binding rule between `responseChatItemId` and party building document ID, and failing to enable full-source log collection.
- Detailed logs for MCP services in workflows cannot be viewed. The cause is failing to enable the `detail` parameter, and failing to configure the detailed log collection path, resulting in only basic session information being recorded.

## How to verify successful configuration
- A party building learning Q&A conversation for the transportation scheduling job is initiated, and the log list is checked for a `chatId` record bound to the corresponding job ID.
- The historical record query interface is called, and the returned results are verified to include the `responseChatItemId` field, with the field value associated with the document number of party building learning materials.
- The log file for a single conversation is exported, and the `detail` parameter is confirmed to include complete conversation context, Q&A content, and associated document information.
- The log retention settings are viewed, and party building conversation logs older than 12 months are confirmed to have not been automatically cleaned up, complying with regulatory requirements.
