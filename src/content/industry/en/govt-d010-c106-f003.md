# Coal-electric Integration Party Building Study Q&A: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for coal-electric integration party building study Q&A include official party building documents issued by the group party committee, monthly study ledgers of each party branch, and party building guidance materials from the higher-level state-owned assets supervision and administration commission.
Documents have a fixed structure with five fields: topic, issuing unit, release date, core study content, and assessment rules. Release date uses the YYYY-MM-DD format. Most individual core study content pieces range from 800 to 2000 characters.
Data update rhythm follows this schedule: monthly sync of party branch study updates, quarterly update of party building assessment norms, and annual review of the latest higher-level policy interpretations.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering link
Fixed document fields require prompts to explicitly limit the AI to only use fields such as topic and core content from documents when generating responses. This prevents the generation of irrelevant information.
The per-document length range requires that the multi-turn dialogue context window be controlled within a reasonable range. This avoids response timeouts caused by loading overly long content.
Monthly updated dynamic data requires the dialogue system to support real-time synchronization of the latest party branch study content. Multi-turn dialogue historical context must regularly clean redundant information to avoid old data interfering with current Q&A.
Quarterly updates to party building assessment norms require prompts to link to the latest assessment rules. This ensures responses align with current policy requirements.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Intermediate generated content from AI conversations is displayed directly on the page without being processed by a text splicing component. Cause: No output interception rule is configured in the workflow, causing AI-generated content to be returned directly to the front-end dialog box.
- Phenomenon: Core party building study knowledge points and assessment requirements cannot be marked in conversation logs. Cause: Custom field mapping for conversation logs is not enabled, and the document's assessment rules field is not synchronized to the log annotation items.
- Phenomenon: After knowledge base retrieval results are passed to AI conversations, the document's release date and topic information cannot be correctly associated. Cause: Retrieval data is not passed in accordance with the document's fixed field format, causing the AI to fail to recognize associated metadata.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Launch a session with multi-turn party building study questions, check that only the most recent 6 rounds of dialogue content are retained in the context window, with no redundant historical data.
- Submit a question related to assessment rules, verify that the AI response only uses fixed field content from documents and does not generate irrelevant information.
- View workflow run logs, confirm that AI dialogue output is intercepted by the text splicing component and not returned directly to the front-end dialog box.
- Check conversation log annotation items, confirm that the document's release date and topic fields are synchronized and displayed.
