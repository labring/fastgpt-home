# Party Building Learning Q&A for Power Grid Enterprises: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Q&A Category Looks Like
The data for power grid enterprise party building learning Q&A comes from the enterprise’s internal party building knowledge base, party building normative documents released by superior power industry party committees and the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority, and internal party building training courseware. The update schedule is to synchronize the latest superior documents monthly, and iterate internal training courseware content quarterly. Each single document includes structured fields such as title, main body content, applicable scenario tags, release date, and designated management department. The release date uses the standard YYYY-MM-DD format. The document number is an alphanumeric identifier field. All data is internal private content of the enterprise and not publicly disclosed.

## Constraints Imposed on Sharing and Embedding
Internal private data sources require embedding environments to be limited to the enterprise intranet or configured with a valid whitelist, preventing unauthorized external access to sensitive party building content. Monthly updated document structures require embedded Q&A systems to support scheduled knowledge base synchronization, avoiding content lag behind the latest party building requirements. The presence of structured fields requires embedding configurations to support filtering retrieval scope by tags, release date and other fields, ensuring returned content complies with compliance requirements. The standardized format of individual documents requires Q&A returned content after embedding to have unified typesetting, adapting to the display needs of internal enterprise learning scenarios.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: A `localhost:3000/chat/` domain error occurs after embedding into a VUE project, and the service cannot load normally. Cause: The correct embedded domain list was not configured. The default generated share link uses the local debugging domain, and was not replaced with the power grid enterprise intranet or record-filed domain.
- Symptom: After opening the login-free window, the history records cannot be displayed on the front end, but the conversation records can be queried in the system log. Cause: Server-side history storage was not enabled, only front-end local storage was used, resulting in record loss after page refresh or environment switching.
- Symptom: After embedding an iframe into a mini-program, the Q&A interface cannot be displayed, and a domain verification failure prompt is shown. Cause: The exclusive domain of the mini-program was not added to the embedded domain list, and the verification rules that meet internal requirements were not adjusted, resulting in access being blocked.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Copy the generated share link, open it in the power grid enterprise intranet environment, and verify that the party building learning Q&A interface loads normally.
- Initiate a party building-related Q&A, close the page and reopen it, and verify that the conversation history is displayed normally.
- Deploy the embedded code to the internal enterprise VUE project and mini-program, and verify the loading status and interactive functions separately.
- Check the system log to confirm that the source domain of the Q&A request matches the configured embedded domain list.
