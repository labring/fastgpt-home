# Party Building Learning Q&A for Grain Reserve: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What this category’s data looks like
Grain reserve sector Party building learning data mainly comes from official policy documents of grain and material reserve bureaus at all levels, Party Day activity records of directly affiliated reserve depots, Party branch study reflection documents, and Party building learning materials issued by superior Party committees. Update frequency is irregular: superior policy documents are updated quarterly, internal Party Day activities and study records are added monthly. Most individual documents use Markdown format, with fields including issuing unit, document number, affiliated Party branch, and associated business keywords. Some long documents cover specific measures for reserve depot safety Party building, with a length of thousands of characters. This page is written for FastGPT v4.17.0.

## Constraints on knowledge base retrieval and recall
The multi-source, decentralized nature of grain reserve Party building data requires limiting retrieval scope by business category to avoid retrieving irrelevant general Party building content. Individual document lengths vary widely. Some long documents related to grain storage safety Party building can reach thousands of characters, which may trigger the system’s default single-document length limit and cause import or chunking failures. A document base of over 50,000 entries increases full-library retrieval computational load, directly leading to slower search responses. The data includes both structured policy document numbers and unstructured study reflections. A single semantic retrieval cannot balance keyword matching accuracy. Multiple retrieval modes must be combined to ensure retrieval quality.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: A maxLength error is returned when calling the API to import Markdown documents, and document upload via the interface is unresponsive. Cause: No appropriate chunking parameters are configured for long grain reserve Party building documents (individual documents can reach thousands of characters), exceeding the system’s default single-document length limit.
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base search response time exceeds 10 seconds, and the number of retrieval results is far lower than expected. Cause: The `collectionIds` scope is not limited, and full-library retrieval of 50,000+ documents creates excessive computational load. `usingReRank` is also not enabled to filter invalid results.
- Phenomenon: Exported knowledge base backups cannot be split by business category, and can only be downloaded as a whole. Cause: Independent dataset collections were not created in advance based on grain reserve Party building Party branches and policy types, so backup granularity cannot match business needs.
