# Party Building Learning Q&A for Rail Transit Equipment: Model Access and Configuration

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data for rail transit equipment comes primarily from internal enterprise party building ledgers, policy documents issued by higher-level party committees, study records and meeting minutes from workshop teams. Two update schedules apply: policy documents from higher-level party committees are updated monthly, and daily study updates from workshop teams are updated weekly. Most documents follow formal text structures with hierarchical section headings. Some scenarios include PDF study materials and PPT attachments. Fields include document number, issuing unit, release date, applicable positions (such as locomotive maintenance, vehicle operation and maintenance, final assembly and debugging). Single study duration is measured in hours, and individual document size is measured in MB.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Model Access and Configuration
Decentralized, diverse-format party building data requires the model access link to support multi-source data import and multi-format parsing. Differences in update frequencies require the configuration link to support flexible settings for incremental updates and scheduled synchronization. The presence of position fields requires the retrieval link to filter associated content by position attributes, preventing delivery of study materials irrelevant to frontline production positions. Strict requirements for party building content require limiting the randomness of model generation, ensuring responses align with official expression standards. Additionally, party building content in rail transit equipment scenarios often ties to production contexts. For example, safety party building key points in locomotive operation and maintenance require retrieval content to be strongly linked to position scenarios, avoiding generic statements.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: No response after calling the model, with request timeout displayed on the interface. Cause: `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` is not configured as the enterprise internal proxy address, or `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is invalid, preventing connection to the large model.
- Phenomenon: 500 error returned when importing party building documents. Cause: Long paragraph structure of rail transit equipment documents is not adapted, triggering an error due to context overflow during model parsing.
- Phenomenon: Responses include irrelevant content outside the scope of party building. Cause: `temperature` parameter is set too high, causing the model to generate expressions that do not meet scenario requirements.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- The API interface corresponding to `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` is tested. A normal model response confirms `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is valid.
- A party building study document from a rail transit equipment enterprise is imported. Parsing succeeds with no 500 error.
- A party building-related question is submitted. Returned content includes accurately cited document fragments and meets strict party building content requirements.
- The `temperature` parameter is adjusted. Changes in response expressions are observed, confirming the parameter takes effect.
