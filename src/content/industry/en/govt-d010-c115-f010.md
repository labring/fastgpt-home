# Party Building Learning Q&A for Rail Transit Equipment: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data for rail transit equipment manufacturing enterprises comes from multiple sources. These include standardized learning documents issued by superior party committees, internal enterprise party building records, party day activity records from workshop teams, and party building integration case documents created during equipment maintenance.

There are three update frequency categories:
- Superior documents are synchronized quarterly
- Internal activity records are updated weekly
- Maintenance case documents are synchronized in real time alongside equipment operation and maintenance progress

Most documents are semi-structured. They contain fields such as title, main body text, associated equipment batch, production workshop number, publishing department, and publishing date. Some fields have clear units: for example, number of participants (unit: person) and study completion duration (unit: hour).

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
The characteristics of rail transit equipment party building data create multiple constraints for the retrieval and recall link.
1. Fields such as associated equipment batch and workshop number require retrieval to support business field filtering. This prevents returning generic party building content that is irrelevant to the current equipment scenario.
2. The semi-structured document format requires balancing semantic retrieval and structured field matching. No single semantic recall logic can fully meet the needs.
3. Different update cycles require configuring incremental update tasks for the knowledge base. High-frequency updated workshop activity records and low-frequency updated superior documents must be distinguished. This avoids redundant retrieval or content lag.
4. Document length varies widely. Some documents are only a few dozen words of study points, while others are thousands of words of activity summaries. Chunking and recall logic must be adapted to different text lengths.

## How to Set Configurations

| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | `0.72–0.80` | Most party building documents for rail transit equipment are standardized text. This threshold filters low-correlation generic party building content and retains results strongly relevant to equipment scenarios |
| `limit` | Top 6 entries | Answers for party building learning Q&A need to focus on core points. Too many results will make the context too long, affecting model understanding |
| `searchMode` | `semantic + filter` | Precise filtering must be performed using business fields such as equipment model and workshop number. Semantic retrieval is also needed to match study topics |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | A large amount of content with the same theme but different scenarios exists in rail transit equipment party building documents. Reranking improves the matching degree between results and queries |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Party building learning queries often use colloquial expressions. Extended queries cover more synonymous expressions, such as "maintenance party building" corresponding to "party building integration in equipment maintenance" |
| `collectionIds` | Specify the exclusive knowledge base collection for rail transit equipment party building | This avoids retrieving party building documents of other categories, ensuring results are scenario-specific |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Paragraph merging or excessive single-paragraph splitting occurs after knowledge base chunking. Cause: The semi-structured characteristics of rail transit equipment party building documents are not considered, and chunking rules are not correctly configured. As a result, structured fields such as equipment model and workshop number in the documents cannot be recognized as separation criteria.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results do not include English party building documents in the knowledge base after submitting a Chinese query. Cause: Cross-language retrieval adaptation is not enabled, and English documents are not vectorized using the same text understanding model as Chinese documents, leading to mismatched semantic spaces.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results return a large amount of irrelevant generic party building content that is not associated with the current equipment workshop or model. Cause: Filtering rules for `searchMode` are not configured, and retrieval conditions for associated fields are not specified, resulting in an overly large recall range.

- Submit a query containing a specific equipment model, and check whether the retrieval results include party building documents associated with that model.
- Adjust the value range of the `similarity` parameter, observe changes in the relevance of retrieval results, and confirm that the threshold configuration takes effect.
