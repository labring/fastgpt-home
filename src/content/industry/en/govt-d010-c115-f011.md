# Party Building Learning Q&A for Rail Transit Equipment: Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Party building learning data for the rail transit equipment sector comes from internal party building work ledgers, industry party building guidance manuals, and standard documents released by local Party organizations. Data is updated on a quarterly basis. Most individual documents are structured plain text, with fields including party building theme, publishing entity, effective date, and applicable job category. Units used are characters and standard date format. Documents are categorized and archived by production team, operation and maintenance post, overhaul post, and similar groups.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Forms and Interactions Link
The characteristic of data being categorized by job position requires form interactions to support filtering available party building learning content by categories such as production team and operation and maintenance post. This prevents irrelevant party building content from disrupting learning scenarios. The structured fields of individual documents require forms to support retrieving and matching data by dimensions such as theme and effective date. The quarterly update rhythm requires the interaction link to be configured with a trigger node for regular knowledge base synchronization, ensuring the timeliness of called party building content. The long-text structure of documents requires form input controls to adapt to long-text parsing and display, avoiding content truncation.

## Three Common Mistakes
- A message indicating no knowledge base selected appears in chat conversations, but debugging preview works normally. Phenomenon: The interface prompts that the knowledge base is not bound after formal deployment, but calls work normally during the debugging phase. Cause: The access permissions of the knowledge base were not synchronized during the deployment process, or the knowledge base binding parameters in the environment variables were not updated correctly.
- The AI model dropdown list in the workflow is empty. Phenomenon: No selectable options appear in the model selection bar of the question classification node. Cause: Available AI model keys were not configured in the platform backend, or model permissions were not bound to the current application.
- Custom input controls cannot be added. Phenomenon: Only 3 built-in controls are displayed in the form editing interface, with no custom entry. Cause: The custom form permission switch for the application was not enabled, or the current version does not support this function (confirm that FastGPT v4.17.0 supports it).
