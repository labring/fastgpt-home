# Party Building Learning Q&A for Communication Towers and Infrastructure: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Data related to party building learning Q&A for communication towers and infrastructure comes from internal enterprise operation and maintenance logs, and archived materials from party building learning platforms.
Update cadence is monthly for inspection data, and quarterly for party building learning materials.
Document structure includes fields such as unique tower identifier, inspection time, learning topic, conversation session identifier, and AI reply content.
Statistical units are entries, articles, and times. Most single party building learning materials range from 500 to 2000 characters in length.

## Constraints imposed on conversation logs and auditing
Since the data includes unique tower identifiers and party building conversation session identifiers, the auditing link must support precise traceability of conversation records by site and session.
Since inspection data is updated monthly and party building materials are updated quarterly, log storage must retain records covering a complete business cycle.
Since conversation content includes learning remarks and AI replies, auditing must support content verification and desensitization to prevent sensitive information leaks.
Category-specific fields must be bound to general conversation logs to ensure audit data can be linked to specific business scenarios.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log retention period | 6 months | Matches monthly inspection and quarterly party building material update cycles, covers a complete business audit cycle |
| Conversation association field | Bind `chatId` and tower ID | Enables precise traceability of conversations to specific sites and learning sessions, matches category-specific identifiers for this category's data |
| Log detail toggle | Enable `detail` | Fully records conversation context and AI reply details, meets full-process traceability requirements for auditing |
| Log retrieval limit | Top 1000 entries | Adapts to batch query needs for single audits, avoids resource overload |
| Response log recording | Enable `responseChatItemId` | Associates reply identifiers for each conversation round, facilitates locating abnormal reply content |
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: Audit logs only display single-round conversation content, and complete context is not retained. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled. Only basic conversation metadata is stored, and context association information is not recorded.
- Phenomenon: Logs cannot be linked to specific communication tower sites. Cause: The binding field between conversations and tower IDs is not configured. Only the general `chatId` is recorded, and category-specific site identifiers are not added.
- Phenomenon: Running logs cannot be viewed after Docker deployment. Cause: The log mount directory is not specified. Default logs are stored inside the container, and are not mapped to a host-accessible path.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a party building learning-related conversation, and check whether the log detail page includes complete context and the `responseChatItemId` identifier.
- Enter the tower ID in the log retrieval interface, and confirm that all conversation sessions for the corresponding site can be filtered.
- Check whether the log retention period meets the preset cycle, by searching for historical sessions from 3 months ago for verification.
- View the Docker container mount directory, and confirm that log files have been synchronously generated to the specified host path.

The values listed above are common starting points. Adjust them based on your own sample data and operational requirements.
