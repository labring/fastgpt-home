# Party Building Learning Q&A for Metal Material Processing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources include internal party building archives of metal material processing enterprises, study guidelines issued by industry competent authorities, and daily team study records.
Update rhythm syncs with adjustments to industry party building policies and revisions of internal study materials.
Most documents are structured text or standardized formats transcribed from scanned copies, with fields including topic, issuing entity, release date, core study points, and more.
Some documents add dedicated scenario-adapted content combined with production safety regulations.
The length of individual documents ranges from 500 to 2000 characters, with a wide span.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation sources and traceability link
The characteristics of party building learning data in the metal material processing field impose multiple constraints on the citation and traceability link.
First, data sources include internal archives and industry policy documents. Issuing entity and release date must be accurately matched to avoid misquoting cross-enterprise or expired documents.
Second, some documents combine production safety regulations. Original document scenario identifiers must be retained to clearly correspond to production team study needs during traceability.
Third, the update rhythm syncs with policy adjustments. Document version information must be linked to ensure all cited content is currently valid.
Additionally, the wide span of document lengths requires avoiding truncation of core study points to ensure the integrity of traced content.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Calling the chat API without returning citation source identifiers: The symptom is that the chat response does not include information such as the cited document's ID or issuing entity. The cause is that the `detail` configuration item is not enabled, and the citation detail return function is not activated.
- Cited document content being truncated: The symptom is that the cited party building study content in the response is incomplete, missing core points combined with production content. The cause is that the `quoteMaxToken` setting is too small, not matching the actual document length.
- Confused citation source scenarios: The symptom is that general party building documents and party building materials combined with production safety are not displayed separately. The cause is that the document category grouping configuration is not enabled, and corresponding scenario sources are not filtered by document fields.

## How to confirm configurations are correctly set
- Initiate a query that includes production-combined party building content, check if the returned results include fields such as the citation source's issuing entity and release date.
- Check if the `responseData` field in the API response includes complete citation detail information.
- Adjust the query content, test citation returns for both short and long documents respectively, verify that no content truncation occurs.
- View the display format of citation sources, confirm that they are grouped by document category.
