# Party Building Learning Q&A for Iron and Steel Smelting: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
Party building learning data for state-owned and state-controlled enterprises in the iron and steel smelting industry is sourced from internal party regulations archived by the enterprise’s party committee office, special industry party building guidance documents, internal branch study minutes, and latest policy interpretation documents. Updates occur as one centralized batch each quarter, with supplementary archiving for new policies released by national and superior party committees on an ongoing basis. Most documents use a long-text structure, including fields such as document title, issuing authority, effective date, and applicable scope. The length of individual documents varies widely, with units primarily counted as "articles" or "copies".

## What constraints these characteristics impose on multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering
Long-text party building documents require multi-turn dialogue context retention strategies to adapt to content length, preventing truncation of key policy clauses. Uniform document field structures require prompts to clearly specify the format of extracted content, ensuring responses follow official expression standards. Quarterly update cycles require the knowledge base synchronization cycle to match the update frequency, avoiding calls to outdated content. For party building learning needs tied to iron and steel smelting production scenarios, prompts must associate production scenario tags. This aligns Q&A content with specific work contexts and improves learning relevance.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind to the unique identifier of the party member account | Ensure multi-turn dialogue context is linked to the corresponding learning subject, avoiding cross-account session confusion |
| `messages` | Retain the last 10 rounds of dialogue content | Adapt to multi-round follow-up questions for studying key points in party building learning, filtering out redundant old conversations |
| Maximum context length | 8000–12000 characters | Match the average length of individual party building learning documents, preventing truncation of key policy statements |
| `variables` | Inject current smelting production scenario tags | Align Q&A content with specific iron and steel smelting work scenarios, improving learning relevance |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- The dialogue history area shows blank content or a prompt that history records cannot be loaded when no login status is active. Cause: The `chatId` binding for user identity is not configured, so the system cannot associate stored data for the corresponding session, making historical dialogue content unreadable.
- When a form input node is nested as an application within a workflow, the dialog box remains displayed continuously. Cause: Popup hiding rules for a single workflow are not synchronized in the nested configuration, leading to incorrect coverage of popup logic.
- When calling other party building Q&A plug-ins, dialogue logs are not written to the storage node. Cause: The `messages` parameter is not passed from the main workflow to the nested plug-in, so the plug-in cannot generate complete session log records.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate two consecutive party building learning-related questions, confirm the system can associate the context of the two dialogue rounds and continue the response.
- Trigger a call after configuring the nested application, confirm the form input node’s dialog box status matches the single workflow settings.
- Check the session log storage node, confirm dialogue history is fully recorded.
- Inject the smelting scenario tag and initiate a question, confirm the response content aligns with the specific work context of iron and steel smelting.
