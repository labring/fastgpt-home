# Party Building Learning Q&A for Railway Operations: Source Citation and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources include internal party building archives of railway operation units, standardized party building learning materials updated quarterly, and temporarily released political study notices. Documents are primarily structured chapters, with fixed fields including topic titles, body paragraphs, publishing entities, and release dates. The length of individual documents varies widely: some party lesson courseware can reach thousands of characters, while some standardized Q&A pairs are short texts. The primary update frequency is regular quarterly updates, and temporary study materials can be added at any time.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "source citation and traceability" link?
The structured metadata fields of railway operation party building learning data require that the traceability link must bind metadata such as publishing entities and release dates, to ensure the completeness and authority of traceability information. The wide variation in document length requires that retrieval and splitting must balance the chapter integrity of long documents and the complete recall of short Q&A pairs, to avoid truncation or omission. The high update frequency of temporary study materials requires that the traceability system must support high-frequency synchronization, to ensure that cited content always matches the latest released party building learning materials.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The service remains unresponsive for a long time after startup, stuck in the retrieval stage. Cause: No reasonable adjustment to the `quoteMaxToken` value, causing the system to occupy excessive resources when loading long document traceability data.
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base citation variables cannot be selected in workflow nodes, and field missing errors occur during runtime. Cause: The `detail` parameter is not enabled, causing the system to not load metadata fields of original documents for calling.
- Phenomenon: The generated answer does not cite the original text of the retrieved Q&A pairs, only producing generalized content. Cause: The `responseData` parameter is not configured correctly, causing the system to not include retrieved knowledge base content in the answer context.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Initiate a query related to party building, check whether metadata such as the publishing entity and release date of the original document is displayed at the end of the answer.
- Enter the node configuration interface of the workflow, confirm that the knowledge base citation variable appears in the optional variable list.
- Adjust the value of `quoteMaxToken`, verify whether the retrieved content fully retains the original text fragments.
- Temporarily turn off the traceability-related switches and initiate a query, confirm that no source-related additional information is displayed in the answer.
