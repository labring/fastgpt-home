# Party Building Learning Q&A for Expressway Operations: Source Citation and Traceability

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category primarily comes from internal party building management systems of expressway operation units, official documents issued by superior transportation party committees, monthly study minutes of branches at each operation station, and frontline party lesson courseware.
Update frequency aligns with the release schedule of superior documents. Daily study materials are updated monthly.
Most single documents use structured formats, with fields including title, main text, issuing unit, release date, and affiliated operation station. Some documents include attached policy interpretation attachments. Field units are based on characters and megabytes (MB).

## Constraints for source citation and traceability
Coexisting structured data from internal party building systems and unstructured documents from official superior documents requires traceability to support both structured field matching and unstructured text anchoring.
Exclusive study materials from different operation stations require traceability results to link to corresponding station identifiers. This helps frontline staff locate study content for their region.
Attached policy interpretation attachments require traceability links to directly jump to the original attachment address.
Unfixed data update frequency requires traceability results to mark the document’s last update time. This ensures the timeliness of cited content meets party building study archiving standards.

## Configuration Settings
Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | Adapts to the length of single main text sections in party building study documents, ensuring cited content is complete and stays within context limits |
| `responseData` | Includes source title, issuing unit, release date | Meets archiving requirements for party building traceability, providing complete source identifiers |
| `detail` | Enabled | Displays the complete source path of documents, making it easier to verify content origins |
| Single recall limit | Top 6 entries | Party building materials for expressway operations are mostly short documents; controlling recall entries avoids redundancy |
| Attachment link access permission | Internal network whitelist authorization | Adapts to the storage environment of internal party building documents for expressway operation units, ensuring internal staff can access content normally |

## Three common configuration errors
1.  Phenomenon: Q&A results do not return English literature sources from the knowledge base. The displayed citation list only includes Chinese documents. Cause: The knowledge base only uploaded Chinese party building study materials, and no embedding and recall rules for English content were configured.
2.  Phenomenon: Clicking a citation source link results in a 404 error or failed access. Cause: Links from the internal party building system were not added to FastGPT’s accessible whitelist, or links expired without being refreshed when the document was updated.
3.  Phenomenon: The query is unrelated to party building study content, but the system still returns random knowledge base fragments as citations. Cause: No matching degree filtering rules were set, leading to recall of low-relevance content.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Initiate a query targeting party building study content. Confirm that returned results include the document’s title, issuing unit, and release date.
- Click a citation source link. Confirm successful redirection to the original document address. Internal links must be accessible within the unit’s network.
- Adjust query keywords. Observe whether the number of recalled citations matches the configured single recall limit.
- Review Q&A logs. Confirm that the token count of cited content does not exceed the `quoteMaxToken` setting.
