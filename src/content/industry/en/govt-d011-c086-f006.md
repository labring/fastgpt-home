# Document Assistant for Work Brief Compilation: Citation Source and Traceability

## What the data for this category looks like
The data source for work brief compilation is special progress weekly reports, periodic research records, cross-department collaboration minutes, and meeting resolution summaries submitted by various internal business departments. The update rhythm is adjusted based on special task cycles or fixed submission cycles, with no unified fixed frequency. The structure of a single document includes standardized titles, submitting department identifiers, submission dates, core body content, and attachment association fields. The statistical units for fields are "issue" and "document". The body content mostly uses structured itemized expressions, with no large volumes of unstructured redundant paragraphs.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Citation Source and Traceability
The scattered sources of work brief compilation require associating metadata of the submitting department and submission date during traceability to avoid confusion of cross-department content. The non-fixed update rhythm requires pulling only newly submitted documents during incremental synchronization to reduce invalid recalls. The structured itemized body structure requires splitting by individual work progress items when chunking, to ensure cited fragments correspond to specific submission entries. Unique identifier fields such as submission numbers can serve as traceability anchors to improve the accuracy of citation matching. The formal nature of internal official documents requires cited fragments to retain the contextual boundaries of the original submission, to avoid information distortion caused by truncation.

| `detail` | Enabled | Internal official documents require retaining complete content of cited fragments to avoid truncation of key work progress information |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: After enabling reranking for knowledge base search, the returned citation fragments are empty, and valid content cannot be recalled. Cause: The reranking threshold is set too high, and the single search limit is set too low to cover the recall range of structured items in work brief compilation.
- Phenomenon: After exporting a workflow and importing it into a new environment, the associated citation traceability configuration cannot load normally. Cause: The metadata association plugin referenced in the configuration was not exported along with the workflow, or the new environment does not have the corresponding plugin permissions configured.
- Phenomenon: The generated work brief citation fragment ends with "No permission to operate this conversation record", and the citation source cannot be displayed normally. Cause: The metadata permission configuration for `responseData` is not enabled, causing the system to fail to read the access permission identifier of the original document and complete citation display.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Initiate a query that includes work brief compilation content, and check if the returned citation fragments include metadata fields such as submitting department and submission date.
- Adjust the state of the reranking switch, and compare the sorting changes of citation fragments in query results to confirm that the reranking function is working properly.
- Check the system logs to confirm that the value of `quoteMaxToken` does not exceed the chunk length limit of the current knowledge base, and that citation fragments are not abnormally truncated.
- Import a test workflow configuration, and check that the associated traceability plugin loads normally with no missing errors.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
