# Internal Office and Employee Services for Pension, Work Injury, and Unemployment Benefit Verification: Tool Calling and Plugins

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources include social insurance management systems, personal benefit records, labor relations and work injury identification systems, and the electronic social security card platform. Data is updated daily.
Daily associated documents include benefit application forms, payment period certification forms, retirement approval forms, work injury identification materials, and unemployment insurance benefit application materials.
Data fields include insured person identity identifiers, cumulative payment duration, work injury identification grade, unemployment insurance participation status, and others. Field formats align with the output standards of each business system. No additional custom statistical values are included.

## Constraints Imposed on Tool Calling and Plugins by These Characteristics
Multi-source data access requires plugins to support docking with multiple social insurance business system interfaces. Calls must match the daily data update rhythm to avoid obtaining expired data.
Associated documents have diverse formats. Tool calling must adapt to different business material formats to complete structured extraction.
Field formats depend on business system standards. Plugin configuration must strictly match preset field names to prevent data parsing errors.
The benefit verification process involves multiple stages of data verification. Tool calling must support connecting multiple business system interfaces in sequence to ensure complete data flow.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After calling a social insurance business tool, the running data in the conversation log is empty.
  Cause: Multi-source data docking parameters are not configured correctly, so valid data cannot be retrieved from the social insurance system. Or field verification rules do not match the business system format, resulting in data being filtered and discarded.
- Phenomenon: When chaining social insurance verification sub-processes in a workflow, the sub-processes cannot execute completely.
  Cause: The main workflow does not correctly pass dependent parameters. Or the `functionCall` configuration of the sub-process does not cover all complete business stages, resulting in process interruption.
- Phenomenon: After a custom plugin runs, the output download address keeps jumping before displaying the final result.
  Cause: A reasonable single call duration limit is not set. The tool repeatedly triggers retries while waiting for multi-source data to return, causing frequent updates to the output status.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Manually trigger a tool call, and check whether the conversation log contains valid data fields returned by the social insurance business system.
- Upload one daily associated benefit application material, and confirm that the plugin can complete structured extraction and extract required fields.
- Run the chained workflow, and confirm that the sub-process can execute completely to the final stage.
- Compare call results from different times, and confirm that business logic changes match updated data.
