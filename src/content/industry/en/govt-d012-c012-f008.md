# Internal Office and Employee Services for Senior Care Facility Oversight: Document Parsing and Chunking

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents for senior care facility oversight typically fall into five categories: filing materials, operating subsidy applications, service quality inspection checklists, safety hazard rectification ledgers, and elderly admission records. Data mainly comes from senior care information platforms, civil affairs statistics systems, fire inspection records, and facility operation ledgers, with a monthly update frequency. Document structures combine standardized forms and paragraphs, and include dedicated fields such as filing number, bed count, inspection date, rectification requirements, and subsidy application amount. Document formats vary slightly across different sources.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the document parsing and chunking stage?
Batch documents updated monthly require parsing processes to be stable and capable of batch processing, to avoid overall efficiency losses from overly long single-parsing times. Multi-source documents with inconsistent formats need parsing modules to adapt to different standards, ensuring key oversight fields are not omitted. Senior care-related documents contain large numbers of structured tables and precise numerical fields. Chunking must preserve the integrity of individual records, avoiding broken field associations after splitting. Additionally, parsing accuracy for oversight documents directly links to core businesses such as subsidy distribution and safety inspections. Parsing error rates must be reduced, and critical information loss due to improper chunking must be prevented.

## How to configure settings
This guide follows the configuration logic of FastGPT v4.17.0. Specific configuration items and recommendations are listed below:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Senior care oversight documents mostly contain individual service records, rectification items, or subsidy application details. This length fully preserves the contextual association of a single piece of information |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by paragraph + title | Documents include multiple structures such as filing forms, inspection checklists, and rectification ledgers. Splitting by title preserves the independent attributes of different categorized documents |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 300–600 seconds | When batch processing monthly operation ledgers and elderly admission records, single-file parsing takes a long time. This range covers most scenarios |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Senior care oversight documents are mostly standardized PDF forms. Custom parsing can accurately extract dedicated fields such as filing number and bed count |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 500 MB | Facility operation ledgers may contain multi-page scans and summary tables. This upper limit meets most batch upload needs |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual mode | Different document types require adjustment of chunking parameters. Manual configuration allows flexible adaptation to scenarios such as filing materials and inspection records |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Calling the file parsing API shows a successful call but no returned results. The cause is incorrect configuration of `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, leading to an empty result being returned before parsing completes.
- Split-related error logs appear during parsing. The cause is setting `chunkSplitter` to fixed character splitting, which fails to correctly identify split boundaries when encountering long tables in senior care documents.
- Document parsing speed is too slow. The cause is setting `chunkSize` too small, which generates too many chunks for a single document and increases the burden of parsing and subsequent indexing.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single senior care facility filing material, check if the parsing result includes core oversight fields such as filing number and bed count.
- Call the API to batch upload 3 monthly operation ledgers, compare the number of chunks across different parameters, and confirm the expected chunk length.
- View parsing logs, confirm that `customPdfParse` triggers normally, with no timeout errors or field loss prompts.
- Upload a service quality inspection checklist containing a long table, check if chunks preserve the row and column association information of the table.
