# Internal Office and Employee Services for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: HTTP Interfaces and External Systems

## What the data for this category looks like
The core data for this category includes standardized business documents: establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and similar items. Data sources include the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. Data is updated daily. Data fields cover entity name, residence address, shareholder information, license type, processing status, and other items. Some documents require linked electronic signature and identity verification fields. The overall data structure is built around the full process of business entity admission.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the HTTP Interfaces and External Systems link
The multi-data source feature requires interfaces to adapt to authentication rules and data formats of different systems, to avoid call failures caused by format incompatibility. Daily updated data sources require interface call rhythm to match the update frequency, to prevent frequent calls from triggering system rate limits. Standardized document field differences require interface parameters to cover core required items such as entity name, residence, and shareholder information, to ensure business requests can be correctly identified. The electronic signature and identity verification link requires encrypted fields to be transmitted, to ensure data transmission security. Dynamic links such as corrections and changes in business processes require interfaces to support dynamic parameter adjustments, to adapt to requirements of different processing stages. Interface return results must clearly correspond to business status, to allow internal office staff to quickly locate issues.

## Three common misconfigurations
- A call returns a 403 or quota insufficient error. This occurs when the token format in the `Authorization` header is not correctly set, or when call frequency exceeds the government system interface quota.
- Business fields returned by the interface are empty. This occurs when required corresponding parameters are not configured in `variables`, causing the system to fail to match the corresponding processing request.
- Business data fails to load after connecting to an external front-end platform. This occurs when response data parsing rules are not correctly configured, and valid business fields are not extracted from `responseData`.

## How to confirm configuration is complete
- Initiate a test call to verify the authentication logic of the `Authorization` header, confirm that the interface can connect normally.
- Pass preset business fields, check whether core business information is returned in `responseData`, confirm that parameter configuration matches the business data structure.
- Adjust call frequency, verify whether the system triggers rate limit restrictions, confirm that the call rhythm configuration is reasonable.
- After connecting to internal office tools, confirm that business data returned by the interface can be displayed normally, confirm that the parsing rules take effect.
