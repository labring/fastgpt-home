# Internal Office and Employee Services for Market Entity Registration and Licensing: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily business documents for market entity registration and licensing include establishment registration applications, articles of association, shareholder resolutions, residence certificates, business licenses, food business license applications, and more. Core fields cover entity name, residence address, shareholder information, license type, and others. Data sources are the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, the electronic business license system, and the residence information database. Update frequency is once per day. The business process requires verifying name, residence, shareholder materials and other information, determining pre- or post-license conditions, and handling correction, modification, and cancellation applications.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "multi-turn dialogue and prompt engineering" link
The fixed field structure of business documents requires multi-turn dialogue to accurately guide the extraction of specified information and avoid redundant input. The daily updates of multi-source data require the dialogue link to call the latest data sources in real time to ensure the accuracy of verified information. The step-by-step business process, such as name verification, material verification, license judgment, requires multi-turn dialogue to split context by link to avoid cross-link information interference. The time-consuming verification links require dialogue to gradually guide employees to confirm key information, reduce repeated communication, and quickly respond to correction notices.

| `chatId` | Bind to the current handling session, retain until the end of the process | Matches the single-handling logic of market entity registration, meets the need for fixed-process single dialogue, and avoids context confusion |

| `variables` | Preset core field variables such as entity name and residence address | Automatically extracts business information confirmed in dialogue, reducing repeated data entry operations |

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Response delay of more than 3 seconds when initiating a dialogue for the first time, with normal dialogue speed in subsequent interactions. Cause: `chatId` is not bound to the current session, and the model is reinitialized and full business data sources are loaded each time a dialogue is initiated, resulting in excessive initial loading time.
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant license options automatically pop up during the dialogue that do not match the current handling type. Cause: The `messages` context is not split by handling link, and redundant information from historical handling is mixed in, leading the prompt to generate incorrect guiding content.
- Phenomenon: The residence information obtained in the dialogue does not match the latest public data. Cause: The daily-updated data source synchronization mechanism is not configured, and expired data from the residence information database is called, affecting verification accuracy.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Initiate a dialogue for the first handling process, check that there is no additional initial loading delay, and confirm that `chatId` is bound to the current session.
- Enter core field information from multiple business documents, check that the dialogue only extracts content for the current handling link, with no redundant cross-link guidance.
- View the data source configuration page, confirm that the full-process electronic enterprise registration system, National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and other daily-updated data sources have been associated.
- Simulate a scenario with no input for an extended period, confirm that a reasonable waiting prompt is triggered after 60 seconds, and no unresponsive situation occurs.
