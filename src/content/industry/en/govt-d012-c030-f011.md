# Internal Office and Employee Services for Construction Engineering Quality and Safety Supervision: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from engineering quality and safety supervision systems, construction site real-name platforms, supervision logs, and test reports. The update frequency is weekly. Daily documents include supervision plans, supervision inspection records, hidden danger rectification notices, quality problem handling forms, and completion acceptance supervision reports. Fields cover project numbers, on-site locations, rectification deadlines, acceptance item details, and more. Units are mostly construction-related ones such as meters, square meters, and workdays. The data structure takes individual project supervision items as the core unit.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Weekly updated data requires forms to support bulk import and regular synchronization, to avoid duplicate entry of weekly supervision data from multiple sources. Multiple data sources require forms to support multi-field associated verification, to ensure that project numbers from the supervision system match on-site personnel information from the real-name platform. The category documents have many professional fields, so forms need to provide structured input options to reduce errors from free text entry. The hidden danger rectification tracking link requires forms to associate status flow fields, to achieve complete retention of rectification records. The completion acceptance verification link requires forms to support multi-dimensional data aggregation display, to facilitate quick verification of various acceptance conditions.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Core fields are empty after form submission. This occurs because required field verification is not enabled, and mandatory entry rules are not set for key fields such as project number and on-site location.
- Format errors occur when bulk importing supervision documents. This occurs because the import template is not configured according to the fixed field structure of category documents, resulting in mismatched data fields.
- Status stagnation occurs in the hidden danger rectification tracking link. This occurs because the rectification completion status field is not associated, and the rectification process records cannot be automatically updated.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- The form editing page is accessed, and the preset participating entity options are verified to include construction contractor unit, supervision unit, project owner unit, and housing and urban-rural development quality and safety station. Custom options may be adjusted or added as needed.
- A blank form is simulated for submission, and the system is verified to pop up a prompt for incomplete required fields, confirming that the field verification rules are active.
- A format-matched supervision plan document is imported, and fields are verified to be automatically filled correctly after import, with no format errors present.
- A hidden danger rectification record is simulated for submission, and the form is verified to normally associate the rectification status field and complete the status flow.
