# Internal Office and Employee Services for Investment Project Approval and Post-Evaluation: Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources for this category include SASAC investment supervision systems, enterprise ERP and project management systems, industry market data, audit and financial statements. Update frequency is quarterly. Daily documents include annual investment plans, project proposals, feasibility study reports, due diligence reports, asset appraisal reports, and post-investment evaluation reports. Data fields are set up to cover the full process of investment project approval and post-investment management. Field formats differ across business scenarios. Split corresponding fields based on actual business requirements.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Cross-system docking of multi-source heterogeneous data requires forms to support automatic filling of fields from different systems, to avoid manual entry errors. Quarterly updated industry and financial data requires form data synchronization cycles to match the update rhythm, to prevent loading outdated data. Classified storage of multiple types of business documents requires forms to support attachment upload and retrieval entrances divided by document type, to improve operational efficiency. Error consequences related to state-owned assets require forms to have strict required field verification, operation logging, and permission control mechanisms. Configurations must also match the business rules of central enterprise board investment decisions and negative list management.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The global variable "Select Knowledge Base" cannot be dynamically assigned. The corresponding classified document cannot be associated when the form is submitted. Cause: The knowledge base folder is not bound to the `formInput` field, causing the variable to fail to read the classified document pool.
- Phenomenon: The data analysis operation cannot be fixed after selecting the corresponding work order. The workflow does not trigger the preset logic. Cause: The data analysis node is not bound to the work order subject selected by `userSelect`, and the operation is not embedded in the workflow trigger logic.
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base search cannot filter specified folders. Search results include documents of unrelated types. Cause: Folders are not classified by document type, and the search scope is not limited to the corresponding business document library.

## How to Verify a Successful Configuration
- Check that required fields automatically match the fields of the corresponding business document when submitting the form, with no missing or mismatched items.
- Test that the optional scope of the `userSelect` component includes typical business entities, with no extra or missing options.
- Try selecting a specified folder during knowledge base search, confirm that only business documents of the corresponding type are displayed.
- Trigger the preset workflow fixed node, check that the data analysis or investment decision material organization operation is automatically executed.
- Upload large business documents, confirm that the upload progress is normal and no capacity limit error is triggered.
