# Internal Office and Employee Services for Government Procurement Projects: Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources include the national government procurement website, local government procurement websites, the fiscal integration system, the procurer budget system, and supplier credit records. Updates occur daily.
Daily processed documents cover procurement requirements, procurement plans, bidding documents, submission documents, bid evaluation reports, winning bid announcements, procurement contracts, and responses to inquiries.
Fields cover project budget, qualification review conditions, scoring rules, contract terms, and other relevant items. Most document structures combine structured tables and standardized text. Some fields must adhere to fixed format requirements set by the government procurement law framework.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Forms and Interactions
Multi-source data updated daily requires forms to support real-time synchronization of field information from channels such as the fiscal integration system and supplier credit records. This prevents use of outdated budget amounts or invalid supplier qualification data.
Diverse document types with mixed structures require forms to switch and adapt to field templates for different scenarios, including procurement requirements and responses to inquiries. This reduces repeated configuration across documents.
Field format requirements that match government procurement regulatory rules require pre-compliance checks during the interaction phase. This prevents entry of content that does not meet regulatory requirements.
Additionally, the most time-consuming steps of preparing procurement requirements and scoring rules require forms to support rapid reuse of common fields. This lowers the cost of repeated data entry.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `formInput` | Enable multi-field linkage validation | Government procurement forms must match field associations across multiple data sources, such as budget fields linked to amount limits in procurement requirements |
| `userSelect` | Restrict roles to procurers, review experts, regulatory staff, and similar roles | Government procurement interactions must strictly distinguish operating roles to ensure operational permissions comply with regulatory requirements |
| Maximum characters per single submission | 800–1200 characters | Standard paragraph lengths for documents such as bidding documents and scoring rules fall within this range, preventing content truncation |
| Timeout threshold | 600 seconds | Multi-source data synchronization takes a relatively long time. This duration covers conventional cross-system data retrieval |
| Scenario template reuse | Enabled | Adapt form templates for multiple scenarios such as procurement requirements and response to inquiries, reducing repeated configuration |
| Core field mandatory validation | Enable based on business scenario | Core fields such as qualification review conditions and project budget require mandatory validation to avoid risks caused by missing entries |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Calling a model triggers a 400 error. This occurs only when selecting the GPT-4o mini model and the input includes a numeric field query. The cause is that numeric field format validation is not properly configured in form interactions, leading to model call parameters that do not meet interface requirements.
- After mounting an LLM model, generated responses are unrelated to the government procurement knowledge base. The cause is that the form interaction phase does not limit the retrieval scope to only government procurement-related documents, leading to retrieval calls to non-target data sources.
- After deploying speech-to-text functionality, the Whisper model cannot be called normally. The cause is that the speech input channel for `formInput` is not enabled in the form configuration, and the locally deployed model port is not properly associated.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Open the form configuration page of FastGPT v4.17.0, and check if the role list for `userSelect` includes government procurement-related roles such as procurers, review experts, and regulatory staff.
- Submit a test form to trigger field validation, and confirm that corresponding prompts appear when core fields such as project budget and qualification conditions are left blank.
- Call the speech input function, and confirm that transcribed text can be normally passed to the model and associated with government procurement knowledge base content, with no error prompts.
- Switch between different scenario templates, and confirm that form fields can automatically adapt to different document types such as procurement requirements and responses to inquiries.
