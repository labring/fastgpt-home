# Internal Office and Employee Services for Legal Compliance and Major Risk Management: Model Integration and Configuration

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category comes from enterprise contract management systems, compliance risk databases, legal and regulatory repositories, case management systems, and reporting platforms. Documents processed daily include compliance risk lists, contract legal review opinions, legal opinions on major matters, compliance review forms, and violation investigation reports.
The data update frequency is monthly. Most documents are a mix of structured and semi-structured content, with fields such as compliance clauses, risk levels, and handling opinions. There are no uniform fixed units, as values vary by document type.
This content is written based on the configuration logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Model Integration and Configuration
Decentralized data sources covering structured and unstructured content from multiple systems require model integration configurations to support unified vector mapping for multi-source data.
Monthly update frequency requires configurations that allow flexible setup of incremental sync and full update trigger logic, to align with the regular update rhythm of compliance data.
Compliance documents contain specialized legal terminology and risk judgment fields. Model context length configurations must be adapted for long text processing to avoid truncation of critical compliance content.
Field differences across different documents require configurations that support custom content extraction rules, to prevent non-compliance information from interfering with model output.
Severe consequences of errors in compliance scenarios require configurations with stable failure retry mechanisms, to avoid legal risks caused by incorrect model output.

## How to Set the Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Compliance documents are mostly long texts, need to accommodate complete compliance review opinions and risk list content |
| `maxTokens` | 2000–3000 token | Ensure generated compliance review opinions and risk analysis content have sufficient length without exceeding limits |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500–2000 token | Match the length of specialized clauses in compliance documents, ensure completeness of referenced content |
| `temperature` | 0.1–0.3 | Compliance scenarios require precise output, reduce randomness to avoid generating content that violates regulations |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Support automatic calling of compliance-related tools, adapt to retrieval and analysis of multi-source data |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in the address provided by the deployment service provider | Ensure network connectivity and data transmission security for model integration |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Fill in the key provided by the model service provider | Complete identity verification for model calls, ensure access permissions |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Interface errors are returned when calling the DeepSeek model, and logs show connection timeouts. Cause: `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` are not configured correctly, interrupting the model integration link.
- Symptom: A `cannot read properties of undefined` error appears when testing the Qwen model. Cause: The association configuration between the model and channel is not properly bound, causing the system to fail to recognize the model's call parameters.
- Symptom: Significant fluctuations in retrieval accuracy occur when performing vector retrieval on compliance documents. Cause: The appropriate vector model is not selected based on the plain text attribute of compliance documents. Misusing a multimodal vector model leads to semantic matching deviations.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Run a model call test, check if the returned content includes specialized compliance-related terminology and logic, and adjust the values of relevant parameters based on the scenario.
- View vector retrieval results, confirm that the returned documents come from specified data sources such as compliance risk databases and contract management systems, and adjust context configurations based on retrieval needs.
- Check interface logs, confirm there are no parameter errors of the `cannot read properties of undefined` type, and verify that the binding configuration between the model and channel is correct.
- Test the incremental update process, confirm that monthly updated data can be synced to the vector library normally, and verify that the configured update trigger logic meets expectations.
