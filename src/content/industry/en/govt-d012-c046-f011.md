# Internal Office and Employee Services for Social Assistance and Minimum Living Security Review: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data is primarily sourced from daily office documents of entities including civil affairs bureau assistance sections, subdistrict and township civil affairs offices, community neighborhood committees, and verification centers. It includes minimum living security application forms, family economic status verification reports, household survey forms, democratic appraisal records, public notice materials, and assistance approval forms. Data sources include low-income population dynamic monitoring platforms, resident family economic status verification systems, and cross-department shared data from public security, human resources and social security, medical insurance, housing and urban-rural development, tax authorities, and other departments. The update frequency is monthly. Core fields focus on family economic status, member relationships, and assistance eligibility. The document structure centers on form materials covering the full application, verification, and approval process.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Multi-department data sources require forms and interactions to support automatic pulling and verification of multi-source data, reducing manual entry errors. The monthly update frequency means forms must support regular synchronization of the latest monitoring data to avoid expired information. Core fields focus on family economy and member relationships, so the interaction interface must include mandatory verification and associated prompts for these fields. Document requirements for household surveys and public notice links require forms to support uploading multiple types of supporting materials and linking them to approval process nodes. Cross-department data dependency requires the interaction interface to clearly display data sources and verification status, enabling office staff to easily verify information.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The phenomenon is that when importing minimum living security application-related documents into the knowledge base, the page refreshes in a loop and the target page cannot be selected. The cause is that a reasonable pagination loading threshold is not set for multi-page form documents, leading to continuous refreshes triggered by large amounts of data.
- The phenomenon is that when pulling cross-department data, the number of returned results does not match expectations, and complete family property information cannot be matched. The cause is that the correct permission range is not configured via `userSelect`, leading to some shared data interfaces not being called normally.
- The phenomenon is that an error indicating empty fields appears after form submission, but the fields were fully filled out during entry. The cause is that field linked verification rules are not configured via `formInput`, leading to associated fields not automatically synchronizing already entered data.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Import a standard minimum living security application form document, check whether the economic status data of the corresponding family is automatically pulled, and verify whether the multi-source data linked function takes effect.
- Switch accounts with different office roles, check whether only authorized roles can view cross-department shared data, and verify whether permission configurations meet position requirements.
- Submit a test form, check whether process nodes are split according to links such as application, verification, household survey, etc., and verify whether process configurations conform to business logic.
- Upload multiple different types of supporting materials, check whether the upload limit and single file size limit meet business needs, and verify whether material upload configurations take effect.
