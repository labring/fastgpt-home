# Internal Office and Employee Services for Road Transport Business Licensing: Model Integration and Configuration

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Data sources include the road transport administration management system, the national public service platform for dynamic information of road transport vehicles, and the vehicle and practitioner database. The update frequency is daily.
Documents processed daily include business license applications, vehicle technical files, driver qualification materials, work safety management systems, road transport business licenses, and more. Document fields include business license number, vehicle license plate number, qualification certificate number, safety system clauses, and others. Each document has a fixed structure, with fields closely related to transport industry business licensing operations.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Model Integration and Configuration
Daily data updates require the model context configuration to adapt to real-time synchronized business data. This avoids verification errors caused by delayed data.
Multiple types of fixed-structure business documents require the model to accurately identify the correspondence between document fields and business rules. This places high demands on the model's context understanding ability and output rigor.
The verification link takes up a large share of total business process time. The model must quickly process associated information from multiple documents. Therefore, the configuration must balance processing efficiency and content accuracy.
Format differences across data sources require the model integration configuration to support data import from multiple systems. This avoids data loss or errors caused by format mismatches.

## How to Set the Configuration
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale for This Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Adapts to multiple associated documents related to road transport business licensing, ensuring the model can fully read all materials including applications, vehicle files, and personnel qualifications |
| `temperature` | 0.1–0.3 | Reduces the randomness of model output, ensures the rigor of content in approval verification and opinion generation links, and complies with the normative requirements of administrative licensing |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Automatically calls tools to connect data from the road transport administration management system and vehicle dynamic platform, quickly completing real-time verification of vehicle and personnel qualifications |
| `maxTokens` | 2000 token | Controls the output length of a single round of question and answer, adapts to the conventional format of administrative licensing approval opinions, and avoids overly long output or exceeding system limits |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500 token | Limits the length of document fragments referenced by the model, ensuring that referenced content accurately corresponds to business verification points, and avoids redundant information interfering with approval judgments |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in according to the deployment environment | Adapts to the interface call path of the internal office system, ensuring that model requests can be normally sent to the deployed AI service |

## Three Common Mistakes
- When calling the model test, `Api response error: /api/core/ai/model/test?model=xxx` is returned. The cause is that `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` or `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is not configured correctly, or the filled model name does not match the actually deployed model.
- After enabling the thought output switch, the question and answer result still contains content wrapped in `<think>` tags. The cause is that the model's thought output format is not adapted correctly, or the configured parameters are not correctly associated with the model's thought switch logic.
- When attempting to call a text-to-image large model to generate visual materials, there is no response. The cause is that the corresponding configuration is not enabled, or the interface call requirements of the text-to-image model are not adapted.
