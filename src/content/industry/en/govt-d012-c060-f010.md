# Internal Office and Employee Services for Warehousing and Logistics: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What the data for this category looks like
The data sources for internal office and employee services in warehousing and logistics mainly come from warehouse area operation SOPs, equipment operation and maintenance manuals, employee position manuals, archived daily work orders, and warehouse layout documents. The data update rhythm is triggered by warehouse area adjustments, new equipment launches, and position rule changes, with no fixed cycle. Most documents are a mix of structured and semi-structured content, including fields such as warehouse area number, equipment model, operation duration, and number of materials, with units including square meters, hours, pieces, and others.

## What constraints these characteristics bring to the knowledge base retrieval and recall link
The semi-structured mixed documents and specific field units in warehousing and logistics will create three types of constraints for retrieval and recall. First, documents contain precise fields such as warehouse area number and equipment model. Targeted retrieval by specified fields is required to avoid irrelevant content interfering with search results. Second, data updates have no fixed cycle. Incremental synchronization configuration must be adapted to ensure that the latest warehouse area rules and equipment operation and maintenance requirements are added to the knowledge base in a timely manner. Third, document length varies widely, ranging from short position operation prompts to complete operation SOPs with thousands of characters. Chunking and recall matching logic for different lengths must be adapted.

## How to set the configurations
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0, adapted to the retrieval and recall needs of internal office knowledge bases for warehousing and logistics.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.72–0.78 | Most warehousing and logistics documents require precise field matching. This range balances precise recall and coverage, calibrated based on actual testing |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Knowledge base content for warehousing and logistics is mostly categorized by warehouse area and equipment. The top 8 entries can cover typical operation rules for a single scenario |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Warehousing and logistics documents contain a large number of professional terms and fields. Reranking optimizes the relevance ranking of results |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Employee questions are often in colloquial language, such as "forklift charging precautions". Optimization allows matching with structured SOP documents |
| `collectionIds` | Bind to dedicated warehousing and logistics collections | Distinguish from other knowledge bases such as administration and finance to avoid cross-category content interfering with retrieval |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Failure to batch download associated warehousing operation documents in search results: The batch download permission for knowledge base files is not enabled, or the bound dedicated knowledge base collection does not have download permissions enabled.
- Warehouse layout diagrams in image format cannot be correctly recalled: The image question answering splitting configuration is not enabled, and only text content is retrieved and recalled.
- After calling an external interface to upload HTML-format operation documents, the search results do not match the title content: The title and content fields were not correctly passed during upload, or the `collectionIds` of the corresponding knowledge base collection were not specified.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Check whether the `collectionIds` bound to the knowledge base only include document collections related to warehousing and logistics, to avoid mixing in content from other categories.
- Verify whether the value range of the `similarity` parameter meets the recall accuracy requirements of the current business. Randomly selected employee questions can be used for testing and adjustment.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, check whether the ranking of search results matches the professional terms and field matching degree, to confirm that the reranking function is active.
- Test uploading a single HTML-format operation document, confirm that the title and content fields can be correctly retrieved, and verify that the upload logic is normal.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
