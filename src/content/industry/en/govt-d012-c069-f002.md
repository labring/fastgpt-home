# Internal Office and Employee Services for Hazardous Chemicals Storage and Transportation: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Internal office data for hazardous chemicals storage and transportation mainly comes from storage and transportation ledgers, shift inspection records, equipment operation and maintenance logs, industry safety specification documents, and emergency response archives.
Ledgers are structured data. They include hazardous chemical name, unique ID, storage location, inventory (unit: ton or liter), and inbound and outbound time.
Inspection records are semi-structured text. They include inspection points, equipment status, abnormality descriptions, and inspection time.
Safety specification documents are long-form documents with chapter-based clauses.
Update rhythms vary significantly: ledgers and operation logs are updated in real time or daily, inspection records are updated per shift, and industry specifications are updated quarterly or annually.

## Constraints Imposed on Vector Models and Indexes by These Characteristics
Multi-source heterogeneous data formats require vector modeling to balance metadata mapping for structured fields and semantic extraction for unstructured text. This avoids vector deviation caused by format differences.
Differences in update frequency require index strategies to distinguish full and incremental updates. Real-time data needs support for high-frequency incremental synchronization. Static specification documents can use periodic full updates.
Unit information attached to fields (such as tons, liters) affects semantic similarity calculation. Unit associations must be retained during chunking and modeling. This prevents matching errors between similar hazardous chemicals caused by unit confusion.
The existence of long documents requires chunking strategies to balance context completeness and vector generation efficiency under the `maxToken` limit.

## How to Set Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the single-section length of hazardous chemical safety specifications and the length of single entries in inspection records, avoiding exceeding the `maxToken` limit |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retains contextual associations for storage and transportation processes and inspection sequences, preventing semantic fragmentation after chunking |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Distinguishes semantic similarity of hazardous chemical-specific terminology, reducing the probability of matching irrelevant results |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Adapts to the information density of internal office scenarios, avoiding excessive results exceeding the context window |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Adapts to the context limits of mainstream localized vector models, supporting complete vector generation for long documents |
| `defaultToken` | 2048 | Balances the information needs and response speed of daily employee queries, adapting to the token consumption of single questions |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- After importing a document, the displayed knowledge base capacity value does not match the expected value. The cause is that chunk parameters are not configured according to the actual character length of hazardous chemicals storage and transportation documents. This leads to chaotic chunking logic and deviations in token statistics.
- The number of search results returned does not match the set single search limit. Some queries return no results. The cause is that semantic expansion is not enabled for hazardous chemical-specific terminology, leading to failed term matching.
- Timeout errors occur during vector generation. The cause is that model quantization mode is not enabled in the GPU environment. Long document vector calculation cannot be processed efficiently on a 1080ti graphics card.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Import a single hazardous chemical safety specification document. Check the chunk preview interface, and confirm that the chunk length falls within the preset 800–1200 character range.
- Initiate a query containing hazardous chemical-specific terminology. Check the similarity scores of returned results, and confirm that the scores fall within the preset 0.75–0.85 threshold range.
- Batch import one week of inspection records. Confirm that there are no timeout errors during the incremental index update process, and that the update time meets expectations.
