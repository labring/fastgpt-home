# Internal Office and Employee Services for State-owned Capital Operation Companies: Vector Models and Indexing
This page applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What data for this use case looks like
Data for internal office and employee services at state-owned capital operation companies primarily comes from internal OA systems, personnel management platforms, state-owned assets supervision submission documents, monthly business meeting minutes, and employee handbooks.
Update cycles cover multiple periods: daily office notices and employee attendance records are updated daily. Monthly business reports and state-owned assets supervision submission materials are updated monthly. Employee handbooks and organizational structure documents are updated annually.
Documents include structured fields such as employee ID numbers, department codes, and state-owned assets transfer progress numbers, semi-structured meeting minutes and notices, and unstructured long research reports.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on vector models and indexing
Multi-cycle data updates require indexing to support incremental synchronization, avoiding computing resource consumption caused by full reindexing.
Structured fields including state-owned assets supervision exclusive codes and employee ID numbers must be associated with metadata fields in the vector index, to ensure retrieval results can be quickly filtered by business dimensions.
Long unstructured research reports and monthly meeting minutes require adaptation to long text chunking rules, to prevent single chunks from exceeding the vector model's token limit.
Some confidential or sensitive internal documents must have desensitization marking completed during the indexing phase, to ensure vectors are only generated from desensitized content during retrieval.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the average text length of internal meeting minutes and research reports, avoiding single chunks exceeding the token limit of most open-source embedding models |
| Overlap Length | 50–80 characters | Retains contextual association between chunks, preventing semantic breakage after long text is split |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Adapts to the maximum input token limit of qwen3-embedding-8b, ensuring chunked content can generate vectors normally |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85, calibrated via actual testing | Filters low-relevance retrieval results, matching the semantic similarity distribution of internal office documents |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Controls the number of returned results, adapting to internal employee information retrieval scenarios |
| Maximum Context | 3000–4000 tokens | Limits the context length passed to the large model, avoiding exceeding the model's input limit |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The interface displays the "Indexing" status for a long time after file upload, with no progress updates. Cause: The appropriate `maxToken` parameter is not configured, and long text chunks exceed the embedding model's token limit, causing the vector generation process to time out and get stuck.
- Phenomenon: After configuring a local M3E as the indexing model, retrieval results are empty or have extremely low relevance. Cause: No matching chunk length is set, causing chunked content to not match the text granularity used in the model's training, leading to deviations in vector semantics.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results include unredacted state-owned assets supervision confidential content. Cause: No metadata filtering rules are associated during the indexing phase, and no marking is done for sensitive fields, resulting in results not being filtered by business permissions during retrieval.

## How to confirm configurations are properly set
- Upload a typical internal meeting minute document, check if the number of chunks displayed on the interface matches the configured chunking rules.
- Initiate a simulated employee inquiry query, check if the number of retrieval results matches the set single search limit.
- Check the vector model's running logs to confirm that the token count of chunked content does not exceed the configured limit.
- Initiate a retrieval for confidential internal documents, confirm that the results do not include unredacted sensitive content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
