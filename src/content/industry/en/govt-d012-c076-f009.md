# Internal Office and Employee Services for State-owned Capital Operation Companies: Model Integration and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like

State-owned capital operation company internal office and employee service data comes primarily from three system types: state-owned assets supervision report systems, group internal OA office platforms, and employee service management systems.
Data sources include monthly or quarterly state-owned assets operation reports, daily office notices, employee attendance records, expense reimbursement applications, subsidiary operation data, and more.
Document structure has two categories: structured report fields and unstructured office documents.
Structured fields include state-owned assets codes, employee IDs, department numbers, revenue amounts, and similar items. Units include ten thousand yuan, person-times, natural days, and others.
Unstructured documents are mostly long texts such as group regulations, supervision requirements, and office guides.
Update frequencies vary: state-owned assets reports are updated monthly or quarterly, employee attendance and reimbursement data is updated daily, and office notices are updated in real time.

## Constraints on Model Integration and Configuration

Structured fields from state-owned assets supervision reports and standardized employee service data require model integration to distinguish embedding methods for structured data and unstructured documents. Mixed embedding reduces retrieval accuracy.
Configure regular vector database update tasks for state-owned assets reports updated monthly or quarterly. Adjust synchronization cycles for employee data updated daily to maintain information timeliness.
Long-text state-owned assets regulation documents consume more context quota. Set reasonable context parameter thresholds to avoid exceeding the model's supported range.
Adapt internal office tool calls to state-owned assets system permission verification logic. This ensures identity and data access compliance during tool calls meets state-owned assets supervision requirements.

## Configuration Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Adapts to context requirements for long documents of state-owned capital operation companies, such as annual operation reports and supervision regulations, and avoids truncating core content |
| `temperature` | 0.2–0.4 | Ensures rigor in internal office question-and-answer responses, and reduces the probability of generating non-compliant or uncertain content |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in the dedicated access address according to the deployment node | Adapts to data link security requirements of state-owned assets systems, and ensures tool call data does not cross compliance boundaries |
| `toolChoice` | auto | Supports automatic calling of internal office tools such as attendance query and reimbursement process query, and adapts to daily employee service needs |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500 characters | Matches typical internal document paragraph length, and ensures referenced state-owned assets regulations or office guide content is complete |
| `maxTokens` | 2000–3000 token | Limits single response output length, and avoids generating redundant content beyond internal office scenario needs |

## Three Common Misconfigurations

- A 403 error occurs when calling aiproxy to access the Doubao model. The cause is that the dedicated `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is not configured, and the FastGPT deployment node IP is not added to the state-owned assets system's security whitelist.
- A context limit exceeded error appears during the tool call reaction process. The cause is that the `maxContext` parameter is not adjusted for internal long documents, leading to input content exceeding the model's supported context length limit.
- Access to the MiniMax model fails. The cause is that the model's dedicated interface address is not filled into `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT`, and the correct model call key is not configured.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Properly Set

- Access the model channel management page of FastGPT v4.17.0, and check whether `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` are filled consistently with the deployment documentation.
- Initiate an internal office-related test query, such as "query the abnormal attendance situation of group employees this month", confirm that the tool call is successful and returned results meet expectations.
- Upload a typical state-owned assets operation regulation document, test whether context references during question-and-answer are complete, with no content truncation.
- Adjust the `temperature` parameter to 0.3 and initiate a test, confirm that response rigor has no obvious deviation.
