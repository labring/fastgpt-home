# Internal Office and Employee Services for Basic Chemical Manufacturing: Vector Models and Indexing
This page applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What data for this category looks like
Data sources for internal office and employee services in basic chemical manufacturing cover production process specifications, equipment operation and maintenance manuals, safety operation regulations, employee training materials, supply ledgers, and more. Update rhythms vary significantly: process specifications are updated every 6 months to 1 year with process iterations, operation and maintenance manuals are adjusted in real time with equipment modifications, safety regulations are revised regularly per regulatory requirements, and employee training materials are updated irregularly alongside training plans. Document structures include long texts (complete process manuals can span dozens of pages) and short texts (such as post safety reminders). Fields include document number, version number, effective date, affiliated workshop, corresponding equipment model, and more. Units include units, cubic meters, hours, person-times, and more.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexing
The multiple data types, varied update rhythms, and structured field features of basic chemical internal office data create multiple constraints for the vector models and indexing process. A high proportion of long texts requires chunking strategies that balance semantic integrity and vector embedding efficiency. Varied update frequencies across different documents require indexes to support incremental synchronization and versioned management to avoid mixing old and new data. Structured fields such as affiliated workshop and equipment model must be bound to unstructured text content for indexing, to improve retrieval accuracy in professional scenarios. Some frequently updated operation and maintenance documents require shortened vector index refresh intervals to ensure data timeliness.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Suggested Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Most internal documents for basic chemical manufacturing are long-format process and operation and maintenance texts; this range preserves semantic integrity of technical terms and operation steps |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Avoids semantic breaks after chunking long texts, adapts to coherence of professional content such as equipment numbers and process parameters that appear across chunks |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85 | Chemical technical terms have high recognition accuracy; this range filters low-relevance retrieval results and improves accuracy of internal services |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8–12 entries | Core information in internal office scenarios is concentrated in a small number of highly relevant results, avoiding redundant data occupying context space |
| `maxToken` | 4000–6000 token | Adapts to context length after concatenating long documents, aligns with average information density of basic chemical manufacturing documents |
| `defaultToken` | 1500–2000 token | Matches reasonable vector output length for a single chunk, avoids out-of-limit errors during embedding |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Vectorization task errors, with logs prompting rate limit exceeded. Cause: Failure to adjust chunking thread count based on average document length, or excessively small chunk length leading to excessive data per batch, exceeding interface call limits.
- Knowledge base retrieval response time exceeds reasonable range, while other scenarios with the same model perform normally. Cause: Single search limit set too high, resulting in excessive volume of vector retrieval results, increasing processing time in subsequent links.
- Dataset entry count increases repeatedly without manual operations. Cause: Versioned indexing not enabled, or incremental synchronization trigger logic fails to filter processed document versions, leading to repeated indexing of old data.

## How to confirm configurations are properly set
- Upload a single typical chemical process document, verify the chunked text length roughly matches the configured chunk length.
- Initiate a retrieval test, confirm the similarity scores of returned results fall within the set threshold range.
- Check vector embedding logs, confirm the token count of a single chunk does not exceed the set ranges of `maxToken` and `defaultToken`.
- Manually trigger an incremental synchronization, confirm the dataset entry count does not experience irregular repeated growth.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
