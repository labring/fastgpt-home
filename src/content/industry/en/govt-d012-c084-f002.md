# Internal Office and Employee Services for Commodity Trading: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Internal office data for commodity trading primarily comes from purchase and sales contracts, inventory ledgers, logistics waybills, employee approval documents, and business reports. Data sources cover business and administrative ends, with significantly different update frequencies: purchase and sales contracts are added on demand, inventory ledgers are updated daily, and employee handbook-style documents are adjusted quarterly. Most documents are structured forms and semi-structured text, containing fields such as contract number, product category, transaction quantity, unit price, delivery cycle, and others. Units include tons, yuan per ton, working days, and similar metrics.

## Constraints on Vector Models and Indexes
The multi-source nature, differentiated update frequencies, and structured characteristics of commodity trading data impose multiple constraints on the vector indexing process. Scenarios with mixed structured forms and semi-structured text require retaining semantic associations between fields, avoiding chunking that disrupts business logic. Daily updated inventory ledgers and on-demand added contract data require indexes to support flexible switching between incremental synchronization and full reconstruction. Field differences across multiple product categories require associating business classification tags during indexing to avoid invalid cross-category recall. Wide variations in document length require adapting chunking strategies for long and short texts to ensure consistency in vector representations.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Index building process hangs, with the interface displaying "index construction timed out" or status code 504. Cause: Failed to adapt to the daily incremental update rhythm of commodity trading ledgers. Full index operations did not enable incremental synchronization configuration, resulting in excessive data volume and timeout.
- Symptom: After adding a custom embedding model, index generation succeeds, but search tests return "vector dimension mismatch" or empty results. Cause: Failed to align the output dimension of the embedding model with the vector storage dimension configuration in FastGPT. The field dimensions of commodity trading business data do not match the model output.
- Symptom: After importing files such as purchase and sales contract PDFs and warehouse receipt images, the knowledge base shows no corresponding text content. Cause: The OCR parsing switch for the corresponding file format was not enabled, or the PDF forms and electronic warehouse receipt formats commonly used in commodity trading were not supported.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a sample commodity trading purchase and sales contract document, check if the chunked text retains core business fields with no obvious truncation.
- Trigger an incremental index synchronization, check backend logs to confirm the update rhythm meets business requirements.
- Input common internal employee business queries, check that the number of recall results matches the configured single search limit, and that semantic matching meets expected standards.
- View vector storage metadata, confirm that the storage path of the original document matches the configured storage location.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
