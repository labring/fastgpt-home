# Internal Office and Employee Services for Nuclear Power Operations: Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Internal office data for nuclear power operations comes primarily from four categories: operation logs, equipment maintenance procedures, personnel qualification ledgers, and emergency response plans.
Operation logs are updated daily per shift. Equipment procedures and plans are reviewed and updated quarterly. Qualification ledgers are adjusted in real time as personnel changes occur.
Document structures include structured ledger fields such as equipment ID, operating duration, and maintenance count, long-text procedures (up to tens of thousands of characters per document), and short-item emergency guidelines.
Data units include power units, duration units, operation count units, and similar units.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Multi-turn Dialogue and Prompt Engineering
The daily update of operation logs requires dialogue contexts to associate real-time latest working condition data, avoiding calls to outdated shift records.
The presence of long-text maintenance procedures and plans requires that the context window for multi-turn dialogue reserve sufficient space to store complete historical interactions and document fragments, preventing key operation steps from being truncated.
The multi-field attribute of structured equipment ledgers requires prompts to clearly specify field matching rules, avoiding confusion of parameter information from different units during multi-turn interactions.
The high-frequency call demand for short-item emergency guidelines requires the dialogue process to quickly locate guideline content for targeted scenarios, without occupying excessive context resources.
The real-time storage requirement for voice interaction logs requires dialogue contexts to associate both text transcription content and original voice fragments, requiring separate configuration of storage and calling rules for voice data.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Citation sources are forcibly displayed at the end of dialogue responses and cannot be manually turned off. Cause: The citation source display configuration item was not adjusted, and the default compliance configuration was retained.
- Phenomenon: The prompt "No permission to operate this dialogue record" appears during interaction. Cause: No permission verification rules bound to `chatId` were configured, leading to interception of cross-session context access.
- Phenomenon: Clicking opening shortcut buttons yields no response. Cause: The questions bound to the buttons were not associated with the initial dialogue content of the `messages` parameter, causing the trigger logic to fail.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Initiate a dialogue containing multi-turn equipment parameter queries, verify whether the context retains equipment number information from historical interactions.
- View the cited source at the end of the response content, confirm whether it matches the configuration item settings.
- Click the preset opening shortcut buttons, confirm whether the corresponding question is automatically sent and the response process is triggered.
- Test cross-session dialogue access, confirm that no permission error messages appear.
