# Internal Office and Employee Services for Communication Towers and Infrastructure: Forms and Interactions

## What data for this category looks like
Operation and maintenance data for communication towers and infrastructure mainly comes from base station inspection systems, power environment monitoring platforms, and tower operation and maintenance ledger systems. Data update rhythms fall into two categories: Single inspection records are submitted immediately after inspection is completed, and base station operating parameters are synchronized every hour. Each data document includes fields such as base station ID, tower number, equipment installation height, inspection time, hidden danger level, and rectification responsible person. The base station ID is a 15-digit numeric code, height is measured in meters, inspection duration is measured in minutes, and hidden danger level is a fixed enumeration value.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
The need for multi-source data linkage requires forms to automatically associate existing ledger data to avoid duplicate errors from manual input. Frequently updated operating parameters require that after a form is submitted, it must be synchronized to the backend system in real time to ensure work order records match actual operation and maintenance status. Fields include fixed codes, enumeration values, and numerical values with units, which requires forms to have built-in format verification rules to prevent invalid input. At the same time, the rectification responsible person field must match the post permissions of the internal organizational structure to ensure that only accounts of the corresponding team can be selected.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Some associated fields are empty after form submission, and the interface prompts "No matching data found". Cause: The automatic filling rule for `formInput` is not configured, and the manually entered base station ID cannot be found in the operation and maintenance ledger system.
- Phenomenon: When selecting a rectification responsible person, the prompt "Invalid username" appears after entering the account. Cause: The optional scope of `userSelect` associated with the internal organizational structure is not configured, and the entered account does not belong to the operation and maintenance team or inspection responsibility post.
- Phenomenon: The form submission prompts "Input format does not meet requirements". Cause: No field format verification rules are configured, and the manually entered inspection time is not filled in the YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM format, which does not match the preset input format.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Manually enter a base station ID registered in the operation and maintenance ledger system, and check whether the form automatically fills in associated fields such as tower number and inspection template.
- Open the rectification responsible person selector, confirm that the optional scope only includes accounts of operation and maintenance teams and related posts, and unauthorized accounts cannot be selected.
- Enter a value that does not meet the parameter range (such as filling 20 meters for tower height), and check whether the corresponding verification prompt pops up.
- Submit a test work order, check whether the submission success status is displayed within 30 seconds, and whether the work order record is synchronized to the operation and maintenance ledger system.
