# Internal Office and Employee Services for Metal Material Processing: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Business data for metal material processing comes primarily from manufacturing execution systems, laboratory information management systems for physical and chemical testing, and inventory management systems. Data update rhythm is divided by business batches: production process data is uploaded in real time with processing cycles, physical and chemical test data is synchronized after single batch inspection, and inventory data is updated daily at the end of the business day. A single business document includes fields such as batch number, material grade, yield strength, tensile strength, processing thickness, processing width, and total batch weight. Most field units are megapascals (MPa), millimeters (mm), tons (t), or kilograms (kg).

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
The characteristics of metal material processing business data impose multiple constraints on the forms and interactions link. Multi-source batched data requires forms to support associating fields from different systems on demand, to avoid duplicate entry. Highly specialized fields such as material grade and mechanical performance parameters need preset fixed enumeration options and unit matching rules, to reduce manual input errors. The requirement for uniqueness of single-batch data requires checking for duplicate batch numbers before form submission. Real-time updated production process data requires forms to support semi-real-time field synchronization, to maintain timeliness of the entry interface. Form fields vary across different processing procedures, requiring support for dynamically switching displayed form modules by procedure.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The front-end page can display FastGPT interaction results, but the back-end business system cannot obtain the reply data. Cause: Only the front-end rendering logic is configured, and the callback interface for synchronizing reply content to the back-end is not enabled.
- Phenomenon: The workflow AI node judges that the procurement information submitted by the form is incomplete, while all required fields have actually been filled in. Cause: The system prompt did not specify exclusive procurement fields for metal material processing, and only general rules were used, which cannot match the category's business logic.
- Phenomenon: Numerical verification fails when submitting the form, with the prompt "unit mismatch". Cause: No binding rules between fields and units are preset, allowing users to input values and units separately, resulting in inconsistent data formats.
