# Internal Office and Employee Services for Railway Operations: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for internal office and employee services in railway operations mainly comes from train dispatching manuals, employee attendance regulations, equipment operation and maintenance logs, passenger service process documents, and real-time dispatching logs. Daily management documents are updated quarterly or annually. Authorized staff sync dispatching logs and operation and maintenance logs daily. Documents include structured fields such as document number, publishing department, and effective date, as well as unstructured content like operation steps and fault handling workflows. Some business data uses specialized units such as kilometers, minutes, and passenger trips.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexing
Indexes must support incremental synchronization to handle real-time updates to train dispatching logs, avoiding performance loss from full reconstruction. Many specialized business terms such as shunting joint control and window maintenance are used in this domain. Vector models must adapt to professional transportation industry semantics, otherwise semantic matching deviations will occur. The mixed structured and unstructured document structure requires retaining associated metadata like document number and effective date during chunking, to avoid losing context during retrieval. Data volume grows gradually as railway lines expand, so index sharding must reserve horizontal expansion space to adapt to subsequent data increments.

## How to configure the settings
The following configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0, and are adapted for the internal office scenario of railway operations deployed on an internal network.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Railway operation documents contain long process descriptions. This range preserves complete business logic and avoids semantic fragmentation |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retains context continuity after chunking long documents, ensuring cross-chunk semantic coherence during retrieval |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.80 | Filters low-match irrelevant results while retaining business documents that meet matching standards but contain many specialized terms |
| `maxToken` | 8192 token | Adapts to the input upper limit of mainstream vector models such as bge-large, avoiding truncation of critical business content |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8 entries | Balances retrieval efficiency and result comprehensiveness, covering internal office documents from multiple sources |
| `defaultToken` | Calibrated via actual testing | Hardware resources vary across deployment environments, so adjustments should be made based on actual call latency |

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: The exported knowledge base dataset.csv only contains the index field, with no content field. Cause: The switch to synchronize source document content during export was not enabled, or the content field of the source document was not correctly bound during chunk configuration.
- Phenomenon: A startup error occurs after adding a locally deployed vector model, with logs indicating that video memory usage exceeds the limit. Cause: The number of concurrent model calls was not restricted, and tasks were not correctly allocated across multiple graphics cards, leading to overload on a single 3090 card.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval results cannot be associated with expected MongoDB table fields. Cause: The content field of the corresponding MongoDB collection was not specified in the knowledge base configuration, or the field mapping relationship was configured incorrectly.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Import a sample railway operation and maintenance log, check if the chunked text retains complete equipment numbers and operation steps, with no obvious semantic fragmentation.
- Initiate a query related to internal office work, verify that the retrieval result fields include metadata such as document number and publishing department, confirming that the associated configuration takes effect.
- Check the vector model call logs, confirm that the `maxToken` setting did not trigger content truncation, and that the returned embedding vector dimensions match the model's preset values.
- Test incremental import of real-time dispatching logs, check if the index automatically synchronizes updates, with no performance lag caused by full reconstruction.

The values listed above are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
