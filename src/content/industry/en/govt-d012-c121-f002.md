# Internal Office and Employee Services for Expressway Operations: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The internal office and employee service data for expressway operations comes primarily from three sources: daily internal office documents, including charging operation specifications, road administration incident handling manuals, and employee training materials; real-time records of front-line operations and charging work, including inspection logs, lane operation data, and charging ledgers; and meeting minutes and notices for cross-department collaboration.

The update rhythm varies significantly: office documents are updated on demand or monthly, inspection and charging data is updated hourly or in real time, and ledger data is updated daily. The data includes both structured and unstructured formats. Structured data contains fields such as road section number, lane number, inspection frequency, and processing duration. Unstructured documents are mostly in long text format.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Vector Models and Indexes
Mixed structured and unstructured data requires setting differentiated vectorization weights for different fields to avoid information bias caused by generic chunking. Real-time updated inspection and charging data requires indexes to support incremental updates, and full reconstruction cannot be relied on, otherwise retrieval latency will occur. Long-text office documents and short-entry inspection records coexist, so flexible adjustment of chunking rules is needed to adapt to content of different lengths. Fields with specific units such as road section number and timestamp must undergo standardized preprocessing before vectorization to ensure consistency in the vector space.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapt to the conventional content length of documents such as expressway operation inspection logs and operation manuals, avoid overly fragmented or overly long chunks |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Retain contextual association between chunks, cover cross-chunk logic of long documents, avoid key information breakage |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.72–0.85 | Match the accuracy requirements of different types of documents such as charging specifications and road administration ledgers, filter low-quality matching results |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Control the volume of returned results, adapt to the precise retrieval needs of internal employees, avoid interference from redundant information |
| `maxToken` | 4000 token | Limit the total length of single-round context, comply with the large model input specifications of FastGPT v4.17.0 |
| `defaultToken` | 200 token | Set the default answer generation length, adapt to the brief reply needs of daily employee inquiries |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: After packaging and running the Docker image, the vector scores of all retrieval results are completely consistent. Cause: The persistent directory of the vector index was not correctly mounted in the image, causing initial test data to be regenerated each time the service started, and the actual business index was not loaded.
- Phenomenon: Mixed retrieval takes more than 10 seconds, and single retrieval response times out. Cause: The retrieval weight was not adjusted for the mixed structured and unstructured data of expressway operations, and the data source range of single search was not restricted, resulting in full index scanning.
- Phenomenon: The index construction process stalls at the last set of files and fails to complete all builds. Cause: The `maxToken` parameter was not configured, causing chunk processing of ultra-long documents to exceed the system memory threshold and suspend the process.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Properly Set Up
- Upload a single expressway inspection log document, check whether the chunk length and overlap ratio meet the configuration requirements.
- Simulate employee inquiries about road administration incident handling procedures, verify whether the similarity scores of retrieval results fall within the preset interval.
- Start the Docker container to run the service, compare the consistency of retrieval results between the local environment and the container, confirm that the index mounting is normal.
- Test the mixed retrieval scenario, check whether the response time meets business expectations, and adjust relevant configuration parameters.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
