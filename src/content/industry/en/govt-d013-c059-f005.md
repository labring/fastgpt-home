# Employee Service Desk for Personnel Policy Q&A: Tool Calling and Plugins

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Personnel policy Q&A data is sourced from internal enterprise personnel management systems, official personnel policy document libraries, and revised employee handbooks. Updates occur irregularly alongside personnel policy adjustments, with no fixed schedule. Most documents follow a chaptered, clause-based structure, including fields such as policy number, effective date, applicable job scope, execution standards, and application procedures. Effective dates use the YYYY-MM-DD format. Applicable job scope lists specific job names. Individual document lengths vary widely, ranging from hundreds to tens of thousands of words.

## Constraints on Tool Calling and Plugins From These Characteristics
Personnel policy Q&A data is scattered across multiple internal systems and standalone documents, with version differences. The tool calling link must configure connection permissions for pulling data from multiple sources, and add version verification logic to ensure returned policies are the latest valid versions. Policy documents include structured fields such as effective date and applicable job scope. Tool calling must support content filtering by effective date and job parameters, to avoid returning expired or mismatched policy clauses. Individual policy document lengths vary greatly. Context processing for tool calling must adapt to different text lengths, while also avoiding redundant content that interferes with Q&A accuracy.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The tool calling module continuously outputs operation logs, and the conversation interface displays a large number of pulling and processing records. Cause: The log output configuration item for tool calling is not disabled, and the full execution process is printed each time the tool runs.
- Symptom: Policy content returned by the tool has overlapping fields, for example, effective date and applicable job scope content are repeatedly spliced. Cause: No splitting logic is configured for structured fields of policy documents, resulting in multiple segments of policy text being spliced without being distinguished by field.
- Symptom: No results are returned more than 10 seconds after calling the tool, or a timeout status code is returned. Cause: No reasonable tool calling timeout threshold is set, or the number of policy documents for a single search is not limited, leading to excessive time spent pulling and processing data.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Complete
- A personnel policy query for a specific job position may be manually triggered, and the returned policy may be verified to include the applicable scope of that job position.
- The tool calling configuration panel may be reviewed, and `toolChoice` may be confirmed to match the current scenario.
- The tool calling timeout threshold may be checked against the actual time spent pulling data, and adjusted as needed.
- Tool log output may be disabled, and the conversation interface may be verified to no longer display redundant operation records.
