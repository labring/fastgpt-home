# Employee Service Desk for Personnel Policy Q&A: Model Integration and Configuration

## What the data for this category looks like
Personnel policy Q&A data originates from official internal enterprise personnel management manuals, official announcements released by the HR department, and temporary policy adjustment notices. The update schedule combines annual routine revisions and immediate pushes of temporary policies, with no fixed update cycle. Document structure is split into modules such as recruitment, compensation, attendance, leave, and separation. Each module contains independent clauses, with fields including policy number, effective date, scope of application, original clause text, and more. There are no uniform standardized numerical units, and some clauses include supplementary explanations tailored to specific scenarios.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on model integration and configuration
The structured module split requires retaining sufficient context during model integration, to avoid truncating cross-module associated policy clauses. The non-fixed update schedule requires configuring on-demand or scheduled document synchronization mechanisms, to ensure the timeliness of retrieved content. The effective date and scope of application fields require combining these metadata for filtering during model calls, to avoid returning expired or inapplicable content. The lack of uniform numerical units in text descriptions requires configuring accurate semantic matching rules, to reduce retrieval deviations caused by wording differences.

## Configuration settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | Personnel policy content often contains complete logic across multiple chapters, so sufficient context must be retained to ensure accuracy of clause matching |
| `maxTokens` | `2000–3000 token` | Single-round responses need to cover 1 to 3 relevant policy clauses, to avoid content truncation caused by exceeding the model's output limit |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `1500 token` | Complete excerpts of original policy text must be cited, to ensure traceability and authority of responses |
| `temperature` | `0.1–0.3` | Personnel policy Q&A requires strict accuracy, so excessive randomization should be avoided to prevent statements inconsistent with policies |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Enterprise internal compliant proxy address or official compliant node | Complies with data security transmission requirements for state-owned enterprises and government agencies, to prevent sensitive policy content from being leaked |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Access key bound to enterprise-specific permissions | Strictly controls model call permissions, to prevent unauthorized access to internal policy data |

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Issue: Model test returns a 500 status code. Cause: `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` or `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is not configured correctly, preventing requests from reaching the compliant model node.
- Issue: Retrieval results include expired policy clauses. Cause: Context filtering rules based on effective dates are not configured, leading the model to fail to filter outdated content during calls.
- Issue: Policy excerpts cited in responses are incomplete. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is set too small, making it impossible to fully load the target original policy text.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Manually input a known internal personnel policy-related question, click test call, and check if the returned results include accurate policy clauses and effective information.
- Review the model call logs in FastGPT v4.17.0, confirm that the actual values of configuration parameters such as `maxContext` and `maxTokens` match the preset values.
- Trigger a manual document synchronization task, verify that the latest updated policy content can be retrieved normally.
- Configure permission verification rules, verify that unauthorized accounts cannot call this personnel policy Q&A model service.
