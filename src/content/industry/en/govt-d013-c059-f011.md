# Employee Service Desk for Personnel Policy Q&A: Forms and Interactions

Reference only; verify against the target FastGPT release and deployment documentation.

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Personnel policy Q&A data primarily comes from official internal enterprise documents including personnel management handbooks, compensation and welfare policies, attendance and leave regulations, and similar official materials. The update schedule aligns with adjustments to enterprise personnel policies, typically annual routine updates or synchronized updates during major policy changes. Most documents use a chapter-based structure, with fields such as policy name, effective date, scope of application, specific clauses, and exception notes. Some clauses include quantitative units such as days, amounts, or times.

## Constraints on Forms and Interactions
The chapter-based structure of documents requires the interaction flow to support precise matching of corresponding clauses using keywords, to avoid returning irrelevant content. The policy effective date field requires the interaction flow to automatically filter expired clauses and only return currently valid policy content. The presence of quantitative units requires forms and interactions to accurately identify and return corresponding values to prevent unit confusion. The data update frequency requires support for incremental uploads or quick replacement of individual documents, without rebuilding the entire knowledge base.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Search Limit | Top 5 results | Personnel policy document clauses are concentrated, and excessive results will distract user attention |
| Reranking | Enabled | Policy clauses have priority differences, reranking places content that best matches the user's question first |
| `formInput` | Enabled | Adapts to the fixed interaction process of the employee service desk, supports users to submit queries via forms |
| `userSelect` | Enabled | Supports employees to select preset policy categories to narrow the search scope |
| Maximum Context | 800–1200 characters | Individual personnel policy clauses have moderate length, this range covers complete core content |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Phenomenon: The `failed to create post p` error occurs after uploading a personnel policy TXT document. Cause: The document contains unescaped special characters or non-compliant line break formats, causing knowledge base construction to fail.
- Phenomenon: Empty results are returned when referencing knowledge base variables in the interaction form. Cause: The variable binding path is not configured correctly, or the variable has not been indexed in the corresponding knowledge base.
- Phenomenon: Abnormal behavior occurs when a single robot chat window supports both active push and question-and-answer interaction. Cause: The trigger logic of the interaction process is not configured correctly, leading to conflicts between push and question-and-answer processes.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload a test personnel policy document, run a retrieval query, and confirm the number of returned results matches the configured single search limit.
- Submit a question with colloquial wording, confirm that question optimization is triggered and accurate policy clauses are returned.
- Enable the `formInput` component, submit a custom query, and confirm that form data is properly passed to the retrieval process.
- Enable the `userSelect` component, select a preset policy category, and confirm that the search scope is narrowed accordingly.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
