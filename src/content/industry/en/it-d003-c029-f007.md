# Security and Compliance Material Review for Content Copyright and Authorization Management: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category primarily comes from contract systems, copyright registration records, content libraries, and complaint inboxes. Typical processing stakeholders include copyright operations, legal, and content procurement staff. Data flows across the full lifecycle of copyright authorization and infringement compliance.

Commonly processed documents include authorization contracts, copyright ledgers, takedown notices, and infringement complaint materials. Each individual data entry corresponds to a single copyright authorization, ledger update, or infringement complaint workflow. Data updates are tied to individual business workflows, with updates occurring per order or monthly.

Document fields include authorized party, licensee, authorization scope, validity period, complainant information, infringing content links, and more. There is no unified fixed unit; values adjust based on individual business documents.

## How These Characteristics Impact Conversation Logs and Auditing
Data sources are scattered and linked to multiple business systems. Conversation logs must be bound to corresponding business document identifiers. This ensures traceability to specific authorization or complaint workflows during audits.

Update frequencies vary, and some workflows have large individual data volumes. Conversation logs must support segmented storage by business order. This avoids overloading single log entries.

Document fields contain sensitive business information. The auditing process must desensitize fields like authorization scope and complaint content in logs. This prevents sensitive data leaks.

Infringement complaint materials are linked to specific content links. Logs must fully record the call and review trajectory of corresponding content. This ensures compliance reviews are traceable.

## Configuration Guidelines
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log retention period | Align with business order association period + 30 days | Adapts to the per-order or monthly update frequency of this category, ensures audit periods cover complete business workflows, and matches the log management logic of FastGPT v4.17.0 |
| Enable `detail` log collection | Enabled | Fully record authorization scope verification and infringement material review steps in conversations, meeting full traceability requirements for compliance audits |
| Maximum single log storage size | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the typical document length of authorization contracts and complaint materials, avoiding storage overload |
| Bind `chatId` | Associate with business order number | Ensure logs can be traced to specific copyright authorization or complaint workflows, enabling precise audits |
| Log desensitization rules | Desensitize authorization scope and complaint content fields | Matches the sensitive data processing needs of this category and complies with compliance requirements |
| Log export permissions | Restricted to legal and copyright operations roles | Matches the typical stakeholder permission structure of this category, preventing unauthorized access |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: The `responseChatItemId` field appears empty when exporting audit logs. Cause: The `detail` log collection switch is not enabled, and sub-item identifiers for conversation interactions are not fully recorded, resulting in missing associated fields.
- Issue: Specified conversation records cannot be deleted after deployment via Docker Compose. Cause: `chatId` is not bound to business order numbers, and generic logs that are deleted are not linked to specific copyright business workflows, making it impossible to precisely clean logs required for compliance.
- Issue: An error "No permission to operate this conversation record" is displayed when operating conversation records. Cause: Role permission matching is not configured, and access permissions for audit logs associated with the corresponding `chatId` are not opened for copyright operations or legal roles, resulting in unauthorized access blocking.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Manually create a test copyright authorization business order, associate it with `chatId`, then initiate a conversation. Check if the log contains complete `detail` interaction records.
- View the log storage directory to confirm that the character count of single logs falls within the preset range, with no overloaded storage.
- Switch to a non-authorized role, attempt to access the audit logs associated with the corresponding `chatId`, and confirm that permission blocking takes effect.
- Export audit logs, check that sensitive fields have been desensitized, and no original business content is leaked.
