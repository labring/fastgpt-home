# Security and Compliance Material Review for Creator Incentive Settlement: Permissions and Data Isolation

This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What data looks like for this category
Daily documents for this category typically fall into four categories: incentive rules, settlement details, appeal records, and payment lists. Data sources include content data, advertising revenue sharing systems, financial systems, and creator account backends. Update frequency is mostly monthly or aligned with single event cycles. Data fields usually include creator unique identifiers, settlement cycles, detail entries, payment statuses, appeal reasons, and processing results. There are no unified fixed units. Some fields are associated with synchronization identifiers across multiple systems.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on permissions and data isolation
Multi-source data synchronization requires permission isolation to cover access boundaries of different systems, to prevent cross-system data leaks. The monthly or event-cycle update rhythm means permission configurations do not need frequent changes, but must accurately match the scope of operations within a single settlement cycle. Sensitive documents include payment lists and appeal records, which involve creator privacy and financial compliance. Access permissions must be strictly divided by job role. Errors can lead to settlement disputes and incorrect or missed payments. Permission configuration errors will directly disrupt normal business operations, so unauthorized access to settlement data must be avoided.

## How to set configurations

| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teamId` permission binding | Assign corresponding `teamId` permissions based on operations, finance, and data roles | Matches the requirement to divide data access permissions by job role for this category |
| Sensitive data visibility scope | Only display settlement documents associated with the current `teamId` | Prevents cross-team viewing of unauthorized payment lists or appeal records |
| Session timeout setting | 600 seconds | Adapts to the single operation duration of monthly settlement processes |
| Permission inheritance configuration | Enable sub-team permission inheritance | Adapts to layered operational team management structures |
| Sensitive field desensitization rules | Mask payment account numbers and creator contact information | Protects creator privacy and compliance |
| Data return limit | Top 100 entries | Matches the operation scenario of single settlement detail verification |

## Three common configuration mistakes
- The interface displays "No permission to operate this conversation record". This occurs when the current operating account is not bound to the permission group of the corresponding `teamId`, and the access scope of settlement data is not matched.
- Deployed applications cannot be used by team members. This occurs when the team access permission of the application is not configured via `teamId`, and only personal private permissions are enabled.
- A permission error occurs when testing a locally deployed model. This occurs when the model call permission is not bound to the resource pool of the current `teamId`, and unauthorized model resources are called across teams.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Switch test accounts with different roles, verify that only settlement documents matching the job role permissions are accessible, and payment lists from other teams cannot be viewed.
- Check the application's permission configuration, confirm that the access group corresponding to the `teamId` has been associated, and unauthorized accounts cannot access the application conversation interface.
- Initiate a settlement detail query, verify that sensitive fields have been desensitized, and full payment account numbers or creator contact information are not displayed.
- Wait 600 seconds without any operation, confirm that the session automatically disconnects, and re-authentication of permissions is required to resume operations.
