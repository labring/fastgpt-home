# Security and Compliance Material Review for Change Management and Release Windows: Permissions and Data Isolation

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category is generated across the full production environment change workflow. Daily documents include change request forms, risk assessment reports, launch window schedules, and rollback plan documents. Data comes from internal enterprise change management systems, CI/CD pipelines, business monitoring platforms, and business calendars. Update frequency is weekly or each time a change is triggered. Data fields include change number, impact scope, approval status, window time period, rollback trigger conditions, and similar items. No additional statistical percentage data is included.

## Constraints imposed on permissions and data isolation
Multiple data sources require permission configurations to match the ownership dimensions of different data. This prevents unauthorized access across teams and environments. The non-fixed update frequency means permission rules must support dynamic synchronization. This ensures permissions take effect promptly when a change is triggered. Documents contain sensitive production environment risk information. Distinct access scopes must be applied to approvers, executors, and decision-makers. This prevents unauthorized disclosure of risk assessments and rollback plans. Permission isolation must also align with the enterprise’s internal role permission system. This avoids unauthorized elevated access.

## How to configure settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Practice | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teamId` | Bind to the business team ID associated with the change | Match the team ownership dimension in data sources, ensure only team members can access corresponding change data |
| `tmbId` | Allocate template IDs by change project dimension | Isolate change documents and approval process data for different projects, avoid cross-project permission confusion |
| Access log retention | Retain for 30 days | Meet operational traceability requirements for compliance reviews, adapt to the non-fixed update frequency of change management |
| Sensitive field encryption | Encrypt and store risk assessment and rollback plan content | Protect sensitive risk information for production environment changes, prevent unauthorized disclosure |
| Permission change synchronization | Synchronize on change trigger events | Adapt to the weekly or per-change update rhythm of change management, ensure permissions take effect promptly |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Scenario: After binding a change management plugin in a workflow, the input and output parameter list is empty, with no error logs. Cause: Team permissions for the corresponding change project are not bound via `teamId`, causing the plugin to fail to pull valid change data.
- Scenario: An SQL permission error occurs when performing change approval-related operations after restarting the system. Cause: The permission configuration fails to synchronize the `teamId` binding relationship after system restart, leading to failed database access permission verification.
- Scenario: Cross-team members can directly obtain complete document content when viewing change plans that do not belong to their own team. Cause: `tmbId` isolation rules are not configured by change project dimension, causing cross-team data permissions to fail to take effect.

## How to verify correct configuration
- Submit a test change request for FastGPT v4.17.0. Use an account that does not belong to the associated team, and confirm that the request content cannot be viewed.
- Perform a change trigger operation. Check the system permission logs, and confirm that permission configurations are synchronized with the operation.
- View the sensitive fields of the change document. Confirm that risk assessment content cannot be directly viewed by unauthorized roles.
- Perform a change approval operation after restarting the system. Confirm that no SQL permission-related errors occur.
