# Security and Compliance Material Review for Customer Training and Usage Manuals: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data primarily originates from product help centers, customer organizational structures, training recordings, and ticket systems.
Daily produced documents include training slides, user manuals, training check-in sheets, and Q&A lists.
The data update cadence is once per customer service order or product version iteration.
Individual data entries typically contain fields such as applicable customer roles, operation step descriptions, version numbers, and interactive Q&A records.
There is no fixed unified unit; values adjust based on document type.

## Constraints on Conversation Logs and Auditing
This category of data is multi-source, scattered, and updated per order or version. Conversation logs and auditing must link multi-source data including product help centers and ticket systems. This ensures matching manuals and training content for the corresponding version can be retrieved during traceability.
Differences in operation permissions across customer roles require the auditing link to validate the role associated with `chatId`. This prevents unauthorized viewing.
The unstructured nature of training recordings and Q&A lists requires logs to record context for interaction nodes synchronously. This ensures complete communication links can be restored during auditing.
The per-order update attribute also requires auditing scopes to be divided by customer service order or product version. This avoids mixing data across orders.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session Association Identifier | `chatId` + `responseChatItemId` | Binds the complete chain of a single customer service session, ensures auditing can match viewing scenarios for corresponding training slides or user manuals |
| Log Detail Collection Scope | `detail` | Collects the first 2000 characters, calibrated via actual testing. Covers complete content of multi-round training Q&A and manual operation feedback, avoids truncating critical information |
| Permission Validation Rule | Validate customer role associated with `chatId` | Enables validation of permission matching between visitor and session-owning customer. Prevents unauthorized users from viewing sensitive training records |
| Log Update Trigger Timing | After session ends | Triggers log synchronization when customer service order is completed. Matches per-order update business cadence of this category |
| Historical Record Loading Limit | Load first 10 entries per request | Adapts to multi-round interactive training Q&A scenarios, avoids overloading page loading |
| Log Retention Period | 90 days | Meets basic periodic requirements for general compliance auditing, adapts to customer training service cycles |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Interface displays "No permission to operate this conversation record". Cause: Permission validation not configured to associate customer organizational structures with `chatId`, leading to mismatch between visitor role and session-owning customer.
- Symptom: Refreshing the conversation page shows a new conversation, while background systems retain historical records. Cause: `chatId` binding logic with local storage not configured, or version compatibility issues cause session identifier loss.
- Symptom: Login-free windows cannot view historical records, while corresponding records are queryable in the log backend. Cause: Login-free access permission configuration for the `detail` field not enabled, leading to temporary sessions failing to load associated logs.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Correct
- Access the session corresponding to `chatId`, verify normal loading of historical conversation content and complete logs for the `detail` field.
- Switch accounts with different customer roles, validate that permission validation takes effect, with non-privileged roles unable to view logs of non-owned sessions.
- Trigger and end a training session, check that corresponding log entries are generated in the backend, and that update timing matches configured requirements.
- Confirm current FastGPT version is v4.17.0, to ensure configuration parameters are compatible with interface functions.
