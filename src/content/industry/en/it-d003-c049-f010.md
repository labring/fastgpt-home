# Security and Compliance Material Review for Customer Training and Usage Manual: Citation Source and Traceability

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category includes training courseware, user manuals, training attendance sheets, and question and answer lists. Data sources are product help centers, customer organizational structures, recorded training sessions, and ticket systems. Updates are triggered per order or per product version. Document structures typically include three types of fields: role permission descriptions, operation step demonstrations, and frequently asked questions. Some recorded document attachments include duration and playback progress fields. Overall data formats combine structured text and unstructured audio/video transcription content.

## Constraints Imposed on Citation Source and Traceability
Data sources for this category are scattered, and include multiple formats such as text and recorded session transcriptions. Update frequency changes based on customer orders or product versions. The traceability link must bind corresponding versions and applicable roles to avoid citing expired content. Document structures vary widely, ranging from structured operation step lists to unstructured question and answer records. This requires adapting to fragment extraction for different formats. Real-time question data from ticket systems requires the traceability link to support quick association with the latest question and answer content. At the same time, citations of training materials must match the corresponding customer roles to prevent incorrect permission descriptions.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Adapts to the mixed structured and unstructured document characteristics of this category, balancing recall accuracy and coverage |
| Single Search Limit | Top 3–5 results | Matches the focused nature of single training material content, preventing excessive redundant irrelevant fragments from being introduced |
| Reranking | Enabled | Optimizes sorting accuracy for documents with similar operation steps in this category, improving traceability precision |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1000–1500 token | Adapts to the single-segment content length of training courseware and user manuals, ensuring cited fragments are complete and do not exceed context limits |
| `responseData` | Enable source fields | Binds document versions and applicable roles, meeting compliance traceability requirements for version verification and role matching |
| `detail` | Enable detailed traceability | Displays the specific location of document sources, making it easier for training instructors and customer success managers to verify content correctness |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Enabling reranking results in empty citations. Cause: Some short fragment question and answer records in this category are filtered by reranking thresholds, and valid citation fragments are not retained.
- Citation content times out before being returned. Cause: The `quoteMaxToken` parameter is not adjusted for long-text training courseware in this category, causing the system to wait beyond a reasonable range.
- Unable to customize citation templates and prompt words. Cause: The installed FastGPT version is lower than v4.17.0, and advanced configuration functions are not available.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Upload a user manual labeled with a version number, trigger citation traceability, and check if the returned result binds the corresponding product version field.
- Search for questions related to operation steps, confirm that the returned citation fragment sorting is logical and has no obvious misalignment after reranking is enabled.
- View the details of a single citation, confirm that the specific location and update time of the document are displayed, and the original material can be directly traced.
- Test searches for short fragment question and answer content, confirm that no valid citations are missing, and recall results meet expectations.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
