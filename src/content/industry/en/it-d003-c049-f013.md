# Security and Compliance Material Review for Customer Training and Usage Manuals: Permissions and Data Isolation

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category primarily comes from product help centers, customer organizational structures, training recordings, and ticket systems. Update frequency is per order or per version release. Daily documents include training slides, user manuals, training attendance sheets, and problem resolution checklists. Data fields include customer organization hierarchy, training session numbers, manual version numbers, and resolution record identifiers. Data is updated synchronously with individual customer service or product version iterations, covering all training and documentation materials from a customer’s first contact through to full product adoption.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Permissions and Data Isolation
The data for this category is tied to a customer’s exclusive end-to-end service workflow. Strict isolation must be maintained between different customers’ training materials and documents to prevent sensitive customer information leaks from cross-tenant access. Data updates occur per order or version iteration, meaning permission configurations must support dynamic adaptation to new training documents and manual versions without repeated initial setup. Multiple document types are supported, including recordings and text checklists, so permission rules must accommodate access control for varied data formats. If isolation fails, customers will be unable to access their exclusive training content, leading to user confusion and a surge in support tickets, which negatively impacts customer conversion outcomes. Unlike renewal health management, this category focuses on establishing initial usage capabilities. The stability of permission isolation directly determines a customer’s first-time usage experience.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teamId` | Bind to the exclusive team ID of the corresponding customer | Match the team division of the customer’s organizational structure, ensuring only members of the same team can access the customer’s training materials |
| `tmbId` | Bind to the member ID of the corresponding training instructor | Restrict only the specified instructor to edit training slides and usage manuals for the associated customer |
| Permission Isolation Scope | Configure per customer single order dimension | Cover the update rhythm of each order or version for this category, adapting to end-to-end data for individual customer services |
| Access Log Retention Duration | `30 days` | Adapt to the document update frequency of this category, meeting basic storage requirements for compliance audits |
| Document Access Permissions | Divide by training session | Restrict only customers who participated in the corresponding training to view exclusive manual content, aligning with access logic for training scenarios |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: After calling interfaces related to `teamId`, cross-customer teams can view other customers’ training materials. Cause: `teamId` was not bound to the document storage path, leading to ineffective permission validation.
- Phenomenon: When the knowledge base responds with content from customer training manuals, a citation identifier is automatically appended to the end and cannot be removed. Cause: Permission configurations failed to distinguish between customer-exclusive documents and public knowledge base content, resulting in full citation being applied by default.
- Phenomenon: A prompt reading "No permission to operate this conversation record" is displayed when attempting to access the team management page. Cause: The current user was not assigned the access permission for the corresponding `teamId`, leading to permission validation failure during page load.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Upload a test training slide, use an unauthorized account to attempt access, and confirm that a no permission prompt is returned.
- Review the document storage path, confirm that the path of each customer’s training material includes the corresponding `teamId` identifier.
- Call the `tmbId` verification interface, confirm that only the bound instructor account can edit documents for the associated customer.
- In FastGPT v4.17.0, trigger a document update operation, confirm that only authorized customer success managers can perform the update action.
