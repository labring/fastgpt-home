# Security and Compliance Material Review for Ad Creative Audit: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Business data for ad creative audit comes primarily from advertising platforms, landing pages, product qualifications, and advertising law compliance clauses. Update frequency is daily or real-time. Daily processed documents include single ad creative files, landing page screenshots, audit opinion forms, and modification trace records. Data fields include creative unique identifiers, audit times, audit results, associated advertising accounts, compliance check nodes, and no additional non-business-required statistical fields.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Conversation Logs and Auditing
Since data updates are real-time or daily, conversation logs must support real-time collection and storage to avoid time gaps in compliance traceability. Since daily processed documents include multiple types of business materials, logs must bind to the corresponding creative unique identifier to enable precise traceability of the full audit trail for a single creative. Since error consequences include compliance risks such as ad rejection or account restriction, logs must support export and deletion operations with permission isolation, and must be immutable. Since audit workflows include multiple steps such as copy checking and landing page verification, logs must fully record each audit operation and associated content to support full-process auditing.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` binding rule | Associate with ad creative unique identifier | Match the traceability requirement for single creative audits, implemented via the log module standard in FastGPT v4.17.0 |
| `responseChatItemId` association | Bind to audit node operation ID | Precisely locate each audit and verification step for a single creative |
| `detail` field storage | Retain full conversation content | Cover complete operation records for copy checking and landing page verification |
| Log retention period | 30–90 days | Meet standard traceability requirements for advertising compliance audits |
| Log export permissions | Only accessible to audit administrators | Prevent unauthorized disclosure of sensitive compliance data |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Issue: Conversation logs do not associate with ad creative unique identifiers, making it impossible to locate the corresponding audited creative. Cause: The binding rule between `chatId` and creative ID is not configured, causing logs to be disconnected from business data.
- Issue: After calling an external compliance verification plugin, conversation logs do not record plugin interaction content. Cause: Cross-application log synchronization configuration is not enabled, so only conversation content within the current workflow is recorded.
- Issue: AI thinking processes are not displayed in historical conversation records. Cause: The log display rule filters thinking process nodes in the `detail` field, so full conversation details are not retained.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Generate a test ad creative audit conversation, check if the bound creative unique identifier exists in the logs to confirm the `chatId` association configuration is active.
- Call an external compliance verification plugin, check if the plugin's input and output content is recorded in the logs to confirm cross-application log synchronization is enabled.
- View the historical conversation list, confirm that the `detail` field contains complete content including AI thinking processes and audit operations to confirm the display rule configuration is correct.
- Attempt to access the log export function with a regular account permission level to verify that the permission configuration only allows administrators to perform related operations.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
