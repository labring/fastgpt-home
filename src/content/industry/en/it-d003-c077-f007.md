# Security and Compliance Material Review for Computer Room Inspection and Environmental Monitoring: Conversation Logs and Audits

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data for this category typically comes from environmental monitoring systems, access control systems, UPS monitoring, and video inspection equipment, with update frequencies of real-time or daily archiving. Daily generated documents include inspection sheets, temperature and humidity records, alarm records, and on-duty logs.
Inspection sheets contain device numbers, inspection times, and temperature and humidity values (units: ℃, %RH). Alarm records mark trigger times, duration, and associated device IDs. On-duty logs record shift handover status and exception handling details.

## Constraints Imposed on Conversation Logs and Audits
Multi-source data access and differentiated update frequencies require that conversation logs be bound to unique identifiers of environmental monitoring, UPS, and other equipment to avoid cross-device log confusion.
The variety of daily document types and differences in field units require the audit process to classify and archive logs by document categories such as inspection sheets and alarm records.
High-frequency calls for real-time alarms and low-frequency queries for daily-updated inspection logs require configuring differentiated retention periods for different types of logs.
Additionally, permission isolation requirements for computer room operations require audits to support dividing log visibility ranges by operating subject, ensuring only authorized personnel can view conversation records for corresponding operations.

| Log retention period | Real-time alarm logs: 30 days; inspection conversation logs: 90 days | Matches the high-frequency traceability needs of environmental monitoring alarms and the monthly archiving requirements for inspection documents |

| `responseChatItemId` filtering | Only retain conversation entries containing temperature and humidity, alarm fields | Filter invalid conversations and narrow the scope of audit investigations |

| Log export fields | Include device number, trigger time, operation content | Satisfies traceability requirements for compliance reviews |
| `detail` level configuration | Enable full log details | Retain all conversation interaction details for in-depth audits |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The device ID field is empty in conversation logs, making it impossible to associate corresponding inspection records. Cause: The binding rule between `chatId` and device ID was not configured, resulting in logs not carrying key traceability information.
- Symptom: Alarm conversation logs cannot be retrieved within a specified time range, and logs show as missing. Cause: The log retention period was set to 15 days, alarm logs beyond the configured range were automatically cleaned up, and the retention duration was not adjusted via the docker configuration file.
- Symptom: Different operation and maintenance teams can view all conversation logs, with no permission isolation. Cause: The configuration for dividing log visibility ranges by operating subject was not enabled, resulting in failed permission control.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Enter the log management page of FastGPT v4.17.0, search for conversations of a specified device ID, and confirm that each log carries the corresponding device number and timestamp.
- Manually trigger an alarm conversation, check whether the log retention duration matches the configured 30-day/90-day period, and verify that the docker configuration takes effect.
- Switch accounts of different operation and maintenance teams, confirm that only the conversation logs of the current team can be viewed, and records of other teams cannot be accessed.
- Export a conversation log, check that it includes preset export fields such as device number, trigger time, and operation content.
- On the token statistics page, select a specified computer room application, and confirm that token usage data for the corresponding dimension can be viewed.
