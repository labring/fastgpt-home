# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for IT Service Desk Ticket Handling: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data comes from enterprise ITSM systems, phone or chat records, asset systems, and internal knowledge bases. Daily documents include service requests, incident tickets, processing records, and user satisfaction surveys. Fields include ticket number, acceptance time, handler, associated asset number, satisfaction score, and more. Data update frequency is daily or real-time synchronization. Each single piece of data is associated with complete information across the full problem acceptance, processing, and closure workflow. Each piece of data corresponds to only one single acceptance ticket, with no redundant duplicate fields.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding link
This category of data is associated with multiple systems and updated in real time. When embedding into business systems, ensure that the latest ticket and asset information is synchronized to avoid data lag. Fields include specific business identifiers. The sharing and embedding link must support carrying associated parameters passed in by the business system to match corresponding ticket data. Since the data covers the full acceptance workflow, permission range control for the embedding window is implemented by the external business system. The FastGPT no-login window only carries the sharing entry function, and requires the external system to pass corresponding permission identifiers to limit displayed content. At the same time, real-time updated ticket data must be obtained by the external business system through dynamic pulling to avoid information lag caused by fixed caching. Ensure that the passed business identifier parameters can accurately associate with the full workflow data of the corresponding ticket. The FastGPT no-login window itself does not have built-in data caching and dynamic pulling capabilities.

## Three common mistakes
- Failure to open the sharing entry related to IT service desk ticket handling. First check the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then refer to the browser request status; do not replace `shareId` on your own.
- Mismatch between the visible range of IT service desk ticket handling data and expectations. Check the third-party identity verification result, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- Original conversation not visible on another device for the same IT service desk ticket handling Q&A. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache, do not judge service abnormalities solely based on this phenomenon.

- Open the sharing entry from the IT service desk ticket handling business page, check the complete link generated by the system, browser requests, and access scope; troubleshoot external page icon resources and session access separately.
