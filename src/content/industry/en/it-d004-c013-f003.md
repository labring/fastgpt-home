# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Cloud Billing and Cost Accounting: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
Cloud billing and cost accounting data typically comes from cloud billing systems, tag data systems, order systems, and resource monitoring platforms. Daily generated documents include billing detail entries, cross-project cost allocation tables, resource utilization statistics reports, and cost optimization suggestion sheets. Data update frequencies are divided into daily and monthly categories. Fields include resource ID, expense items, attribution tags, allocation ratios, consumption duration, and more. Data association relies on resource tags and tenant IDs to ensure the binding relationship between expense data and corresponding resources and customers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
Data for cloud billing and cost accounting involves tenant-specific expense information. Permission scope must be strictly controlled during sharing and embedding to prevent cross-tenant data leaks. Data association relies on resource tags and tenant IDs. Field association relationships must be retained during embedding, and critical binding information between expenses and resources cannot be split. Data update frequencies are divided into daily and monthly categories. The caching strategy for embedded content must match the update cycle to prevent display of expired cost data. Additionally, daily document structures are complex, including detailed and summarized content. Embedded content must be configured to display full documents or partial entries based on the scenario to avoid information overload.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries related to cloud billing and cost accounting fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of cloud billing and cost accounting data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same cloud billing and cost accounting Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Open the share link, and check that the displayed billing data only includes expense details and allocation information for the currently authorized tenant, with no cross-tenant data.
- Confirm that the export button configuration for the login-free window meets requirements, with no hidden export functions.
- Use two different access identifiers to access the embedded page, and confirm that each can only view its own associated technical support conversations and cost data.
- Wait for the data update cycle, then refresh the embedded page, and confirm that the displayed billing data matches the latest data from the cloud billing system.
