# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Supplier and Equipment Delivery Management: Sharing and Embedding

This configuration applies to FastGPT v4.17.0 and above open source versions.

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for supplier and equipment delivery management typically comes from ERP systems, supplier shipping documents, logistics information, and device vendor backends. Daily associated documents include purchase orders, arrival acceptance forms, serial number lists, and invoice materials. Data is updated per order or weekly. Common data fields include purchase order numbers, device models, serial numbers, arrival dates, acceptance statuses, and supplier information. There is no unified cross-project standardized format, so data must be organized per individual project.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" Link
This category’s data is updated per order or weekly, and managed per individual project. As a result, sharing and embedding must rely on external business datasets for the corresponding project to avoid cross-project data confusion. Data fields include sensitive serial numbers and procurement information, so access scope for password-free sharing must be restricted. Only authorized access to the corresponding project is allowed (commercial edition supports third-party identity verification integration). Additionally, the core business process is model and serial number verification. The prompt text for sharing sessions must be configured separately by the embedded front end to match this scenario. General consultation prompt copy cannot be used.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to supplier and equipment delivery management fail to open. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check browser request status. Do not replace `shareId` yourself.
- The visibility range of supplier and equipment delivery management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions respectively. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same supplier and equipment delivery management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache. Do not judge service abnormalities solely based on this phenomenon.
