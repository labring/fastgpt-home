## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" Workflow
Since adaptation data is updated per project and most documents are project-specific, access scope and validity period must be limited when sharing. This prevents unauthorized users from accessing incomplete adaptation information. Data sources are scattered and require verification combined with test environments. Sharing links cannot include built-in real-time data import or refresh logic. Data updates must rely on knowledge bases and external systems. Consultation content for adaptation scenarios mostly focuses on specific document fragments, so full-text sharing is unnecessary. Specifying referenced fragments reduces information leakage risks. Additionally, localId for the same device and browser supports anonymous conversation records. Records cannot be retained across devices or after clearing browser cache.
