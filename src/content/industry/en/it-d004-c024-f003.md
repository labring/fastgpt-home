# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Feed Ad Creative Testing: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Relevant data for feed ad creative testing originates from advertising platforms, asset libraries, user interaction data, and conversion data. Daily generated documents include creative scripts, asset test sheets, delivery data reports, and iteration checklists. Fields cover asset ID, creative combination, test cycle, click-through rate, conversion cost, and other relevant metrics. Data updates occur on a daily or weekly schedule. Asset and delivery data for individual tests syncs with delivery progress. Iteration checklists are compiled weekly alongside creative adjustments.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Sharing and Embedding
Data for this category includes sensitive delivery strategies and asset details. Most data is project data for team collaboration. Sharing and embedding require strict access permission controls. Data sources are scattered, and update rhythms are flexible. Embedded pages must adapt to display formats for multiple document types. Caching policies must be adjusted to match data update frequencies. The core of creative testing is multi-asset combination comparison, unlike keyword management for search ads. Sharing and embedding for this category must support simultaneous display of scripts, test data, and asset files. Fragmented displays will impair decision-making. Additionally, most test data fields are business-specific. Embedded configurations must retain original field names and units to avoid post-conversion ambiguity.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entry for feed ad creative testing fails to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- Visibility of feed ad creative testing data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions. The sharing entry identifier itself does not grant business authorization.
- The same feed ad creative testing question-and-answer pair is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status. Retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing. Do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.
