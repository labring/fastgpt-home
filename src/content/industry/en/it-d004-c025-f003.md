# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Duty Scheduling and Handover: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category comes from scheduling systems, fault work orders, monitoring alerts, and organizational contact directories. Update frequency is daily or weekly. Regularly maintained documents include standardized duty schedules, unresolved handover records, escalation contact lists, and daily duty reports. Duty schedules contain fields for date, duty personnel, and contact information. Handover records include fields for pending follow-up items, priority, and completion deadlines. Escalation contact lists contain fields for job position and contact channel. All documents are structured or semi-structured text data.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Sharing and Embedding
Since data sources include high-real-time monitoring alerts and fault work orders, and most update frequencies are daily, data synchronization timeliness must be ensured during sharing and embedding. Because documents contain sensitive content such as personnel contact information and pending follow-up items, sharing authentication must match internal organizational permission rules. Fields for documents such as duty schedules and handover records are fixed and clearly structured, so original formatting must be retained during embedding to ensure information readability. When embedding across systems, compatibility with different formatted data source imports is required to avoid display errors caused by missing fields.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Duty scheduling and handover-related sharing links fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of duty scheduling and handover data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same duty scheduling and handover Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Access the generated embedding link, check whether sensitive information has been desensitized according to the configuration, and whether fields match the original document structure.
- Simulate data updates, refresh the embedding page, and confirm that the content synchronization logic meets expectations.
- Test that the sharing link can be accessed normally within the configured expiration period, and cannot load content after the expiration date.
- Trigger interaction instructions on the embedding page, confirm that the local system can normally respond to jump actions.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
