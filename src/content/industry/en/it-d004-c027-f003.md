# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Content Audit Rule Operations: Sharing and Embedding

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data sources for this category include the content audit backend, user reporting channels, regulatory policy documents, and violation sample libraries. Daily produced documents include audit rules, disposal guidelines, appeal records, and quality inspection reports. Audit rules contain fields such as rule ID, effective period, and trigger conditions. Appeal records contain fields such as appeal number, processing progress, and review conclusions.
Data update frequency is either daily synchronization or immediate update when policies change. The size of individual documents varies, and there is no fixed unified format.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" Link
Scattered data sources and inconsistent update rhythms for audit rule operations mean associated external business systems must support filtering the latest versions by update time. This prevents embedding expired rules and triggering compliance issues.
Daily documents include multiple types of structured content. The embedding link requires associated external business systems to adapt field display logic for different documents. For example, appeal records must display numbers and processing status, while audit rules must mark effective periods.
Additionally, the size of individual documents varies. Embedding components require associated external business systems to support adaptive content length, preventing layout overflow or content truncation.
Data associated with this work carries high compliance risks. Associated external business systems must retain traceable source identifiers for shared embedded content, to facilitate subsequent verification and responsibility definition.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to content audit rule operations fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of content audit rule operation data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same content audit rule operation question and answer cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Access the generated share link, confirm that the full content of the corresponding audit rule or appeal record loads without formatting errors.
- Confirm that the associated external business system has completed data updates, and access the share link to load the corresponding latest audit rule or appeal record content.
- Check that the source identifier and update time of the embedded component are displayed correctly, and can be traced back to the generation time of the corresponding document.
- Switch page containers of different widths, confirm that the embedded component styles adapt normally without content truncation or layout overflow.
- This configuration applies to platform versions that support login-free share links.
