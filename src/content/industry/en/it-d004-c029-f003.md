# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Content Copyright and Authorization Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category comes from contract systems, copyright registration records, content libraries, and complaint inboxes. Daily processed documents include authorization contracts, copyright ledgers, removal notices, and infringement complaint materials. Data updates follow the rhythm of single authorization business operations or monthly ledger maintenance. Core fields include authorization subject, authorization scope, valid period, and infringement association identifier. Most fields are non-quantitative information describing rights scope and time nodes.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Bring to the "Sharing and Embedding" Link
The core of this category’s data is rights ownership and authorization scope. Shared and embedded content must strictly match the authorization boundary to avoid displaying unauthorized content beyond permitted rights. Data update frequency is low, but the impact scope of single changes is clear. The caching strategy for the embedding link is controlled by external business systems or browsers, requiring a balance between real-time performance and access speed. Daily documents are mostly compliance materials. Field display on the embedded interface is controlled by external business systems, so only core compliance fields should be shown, and unauthorized contract details must not be leaked. For scenarios where intranet deployments are mapped to the public internet, additional constraints for browser cross-domain and external business system permission verification must be handled to avoid compliance risks.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to content copyright and authorization management fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of content copyright and authorization management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same content copyright and authorization management question and answer cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Copy the generated login-free share address, embed it into a test page via iframe, and confirm that core fields such as authorization scope and valid period are displayed normally after access.
- Access the embedded page via the share link, and check that the displayed content fields only include information within the authorized scope, with no unauthorized content.
- Modify the authorization data of the external business system, wait for the expiration time configured for the external cache or login-free window, and revisit the embedded page to confirm that the information is updated synchronously.
- Check the cross-domain whitelist configuration of the login-free window, and confirm that the public internet domain name of the embedded page has been added to the whitelist.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
