# Customer Technical Support and Pre-Sales Q&A for Creator Onboarding and Verification: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Creator onboarding and verification business data typically originates from creator backends, enterprise credit information systems, account history databases, and contract systems. Updates occur daily or are triggered by each application submission. Commonly used documents include onboarding application forms, scanned copies of identity qualification materials, audit records for verification, and cooperation agreement texts. Fields include qualification numbers, unique account identifiers, audit statuses, cooperation periods, and more. Data is only archived by application batch and audit node.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" workflow
Since data sources span multiple systems and include sensitive qualification information, the sharing and embedding process must strictly control access permissions to prevent unauthorized users from accessing enterprise credit information or content that has not completed the audit process. Data updates are triggered by application nodes, so embedded display content must be synchronized by external business systems to ensure displayed audit status matches actual progress. The core process of this category focuses on identity verification and access control, so shared content must only include publicly available documents that have passed audit, to prevent the spread of risk information related to fraudulent verification.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to creator onboarding and verification fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses; do not replace `shareId` yourself.
- The visible scope of creator onboarding and verification data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same creator onboarding and verification Q&A cannot be accessed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Directly access the generated sharing link, confirm that the page loads normally and displays documents and Q&A content for the corresponding category.
- Embed the link into an iframe tag of a third-party page, confirm that the embedded area has no blank space and the content loads completely.
- Submit a simulated onboarding application process, confirm that after the external business system updates, the sharing link can display the latest audit status information.
- Check the error prompt configuration, confirm that prompt texts in abnormal scenarios meet expectations and do not disclose internal configurations.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
