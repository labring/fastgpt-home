# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Creator Incentive Settlement: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data typically comes from content data systems, ad revenue sharing systems, financial systems, and creator account backends.
Daily produced documents include incentive rules, settlement details, appeal records, and payment lists.
Common data fields include creator unique identifier, corresponding content ID, revenue accounting caliber, settlement cycle, payment status, and appeal progress.
Data update rhythm is mostly monthly or triggered by individual operational activities. There is no fixed high-frequency update rhythm.
Single update data volume fluctuates based on activity scale.

## Constraints on the sharing and embedding link
Data sources cover multiple business systems. The sharing and embedding link must clarify access permission boundaries for each data source to prevent cross-system data leaks.
Documents include both structured and unstructured content. Embedded display must match the original document’s field display logic. Misalignment can cause misplaced key information such as revenue amounts and settlement cycles.
Data updates occur infrequently. The cache period for embedded content can be extended appropriately.
After incentive rules are updated, corresponding content in the FastGPT application must be updated, and the cache of the embedding carrier must be refreshed. This ensures displayed information matches the latest rules and avoids creator questions caused by delayed information.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries related to creator incentive settlement fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of creator incentive settlement data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same creator incentive settlement Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance. Do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service abnormalities.

## How to confirm configurations are correctly set
- If commercial edition identity verification is enabled, open the configured share link to verify if the configured third-party identity verification process is triggered. Confirm that the authentication rule has taken effect.
- Access the embedded page from a network environment other than the deployment machine. Confirm there are no loading errors and content displays normally.
- After modifying incentive rules or settlement details, update corresponding content in the FastGPT application and refresh the cache of the embedding carrier. Confirm that content on the embedded page has been synchronized and updated.
- Check field display on the embedded page. Confirm it matches the original document’s field logic and display order.
