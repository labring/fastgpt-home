# Customer Technical Support and Pre-Sales Q&A for Change Management and Release Windows: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
The core data for change management and release windows comes from change management systems, CI pipelines, monitoring data, and business calendars. Daily associated documents include change request forms, risk assessment reports, launch window schedules, and rollback plan documents. The data update frequency is weekly or triggered by each individual change. Fields include change unique identifier, execution time window, risk level, approval status, and rollback plan number. This data involves content related to production environment risk control, so access scope must be strictly controlled.

## What constraints these characteristics impose for the sharing and embedding process
The data sources for change management and release windows are scattered and include sensitive risk information, so the sharing and embedding link must prioritize permission compliance. The data update frequency is strongly tied to individual changes, so shared content must synchronize the latest status returned by external business systems to avoid displaying expired window plans or approval statuses. Most document structures consist of multiple associated materials, so when embedding, the corresponding document fragments must be accurately specified to avoid loading irrelevant content. At the same time, the permission system of the operation and maintenance team is tied to the change process, so the sharing scope must match the existing approval permissions to prevent unauthorized users from accessing unapproved change plans.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to change management and release windows fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of change management and release window data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of external systems separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same change management and release window question and answer cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache, do not judge service abnormalities solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Use an unauthorized device to open the share link, confirm that an identity verification interface pops up or a prompt of no access permission appears.
- Use an authorized device to open the share link, confirm that corresponding document content such as change request forms and risk assessment reports loads normally.
- Manually update the launch window time of one change document, wait for synchronization by the external business system, confirm that the content displayed on the share link has been updated synchronously.
- Check the network request in the browser console of the embedded page, confirm that the `shareId` parameter is correctly carried and there are no error messages.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against respective deployment samples.
