# Customer Training and Usage Manual: Technical Support, Pre-sales Q&A, and Sharing & Embedding

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data primarily originates from product help centers, customer organizational structures, training recordings, and ticket systems. Update cycles are adjusted per customer order or product version iteration. Daily produced documents include training slides, user usage manuals, training attendance sheets, and Q&A lists. Document structures typically include version identifiers, operation step numbers, and scenario classification fields. Units include minutes, entry counts, version numbers, and similar metrics. Data must align with the corresponding customer’s organizational permissions and the current product version.

## Constraints Imposed on Sharing and Embedding Workflows
Data sources for this category are scattered and have varying formats. A unified content format must be used during sharing and embedding to adapt to different embedding scenarios. Data update frequencies are adjusted per customer order or product version iteration. An external system must be used to associate documents with the latest version, to avoid outdated content. Documents include fields related to organizational permissions. An external permission system must be used to match the corresponding customer’s permission rules, to prevent unauthorized access. Additionally, Q&A lists have high real-time requirements. An external document update system must be used to trigger content synchronization, ensuring shared content matches the latest customer training materials.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to customer training and usage manuals fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of customer training and usage manual data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions respectively. The sharing entry identifier itself does not grant business authorization.
- The same customer training and usage manual Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status. Records cannot be guaranteed to be retained across devices or after cache clearing. Do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Directly access the external link of the embedded page, and confirm that the content matches the target training document.
- Open the embedded page in different network environments, and confirm that there is no prolonged loading or blank screen.
- Modify the content of the target document, wait for synchronization to complete, refresh the embedded page, and confirm that the content has been updated.
- Verify the access rules controlled by the external permission system, and confirm that unauthorized access cannot view the content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
