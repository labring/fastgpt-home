# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Customer Permission and Organizational Structure Configuration: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category comes from customer HR systems, identity directories, product backends, and audit logs. Daily maintained documents include permission matrices, organizational structure tables, role configuration records, and permission change orders. Data updates occur per order or irregularly, triggered by customer permission adjustments or organizational structure changes. Data fields include department hierarchy, role permission items, user affiliation bindings, change timestamps, and other related items. There are no unified fixed statistical fields; data is generated only based on the actual needs of individual customer service engagements.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" Workflow
The data characteristics of this category directly constrain the configuration logic of the sharing and embedding process. First, the data involves sensitive internal customer permission and organizational structure information. When sharing, strict matching of role and department permission divisions is required to prevent unauthorized access. Second, data updates have no fixed cycle. Sharing links must be set with a reasonable validity period to prevent displaying outdated permission configurations after expiration. Third, documents include sensitive content such as permission matrices and change records. When embedding into external systems, the range of visible fields must be limited to prevent sensitive information leaks. Fourth, pre-sales Q&A scenarios need to support login-free access, while retaining the rendering capability of reference links to meet information traceability requirements. Changes to permission configurations affect the validity of shared content. Ensure that shared links point to resources synchronized with the latest current configurations.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to customer permission and organizational structure configuration fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of customer permission and organizational structure configuration data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same customer permission and organizational structure configuration Q&A is not visible on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Correct
- Copy the sharing link generated by the login-free window, open it in a non-login environment, and verify that reference links render normally and support clickable jumps.
- Use an external account without corresponding permissions to access the embedded page, and confirm that sensitive permission configuration content is not accessible.
- Click preset jump keywords in the external system to verify that the local system triggers the corresponding page jump (external system implementation).
- Wait 7 days after the sharing link validity period expires, access the link again, and confirm that the page displays a prompt that the link is invalid.
