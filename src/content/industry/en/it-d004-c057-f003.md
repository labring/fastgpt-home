# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Ticket Handling and Issue Tiering: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
This category of data comes from ticket systems, chat logs, product logs, and customer contract SLAs. Updates occur daily or in real time. Daily documents include customer tickets, issue tiering tables, processing records, and service reports. Ticket fields include customer identifiers, issue descriptions, submission times, and tiering tags. The issue tiering table includes priority, response time limits, responsible team mappings, and similar content. Data originates around customer feedback entry points, focusing on full-process records from acceptance to tiering and assignment, and does not include content related to regression testing for product defect fixes.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" link
Data real-time requirements vary. Align calls made after embedding with the daily or real-time update rhythm of ticket data. Otherwise, tiering tags will not match actual ticket status. Tickets and tiering tables contain customer sensitive information and SLA details. Configure permission filtering rules when sharing and embedding to prevent unauthorized access. Data sources are scattered and field formats are inconsistent. Implement unified mapping rules during embedding to ensure consistent matching logic for tickets and tiering data from different sources. Additionally, this category of data links to multiple team responsibility divisions. Follow permission divisions strictly for post-embedding display to avoid cross-team access to sensitive content.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries associated with ticket handling and issue tiering fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of ticket handling and issue tiering data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions respectively; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same ticket handling and issue tiering Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Access the embedded link to verify that only ticket and tiering data within the scope authorized by the external business system is visible.
- Manually trigger data synchronization from the external business system, confirm that updated ticket content can be properly displayed on the embedded page.
- Check call logs to confirm that the `shareId` parameter is correctly included in the request, with no missing parameters.
- Simulate a customer feedback scenario to verify that the embedded page can associate processing records and tiering information for the corresponding ticket.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
