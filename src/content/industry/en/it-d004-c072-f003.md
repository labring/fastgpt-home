# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Data Collection Task Design: Sharing and Embedding

This documentation applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like
The core carriers of data for this category are four types of documents: collection plans, sample specifications, authorization statements, and task schedules. Data sources include customer requirements, public data sources, collection tools, and in-house sample libraries. Data updates follow a per-project cycle or weekly cadence. Fields include sample scope, collection fields, authorization boundaries, schedule nodes, etc. There are no unified fixed units; corresponding formats must be matched based on project requirements.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" link
Documents such as collection plans and authorization statements involve customer customization requirements and compliance information. The sharing and embedding link must strictly control visibility to avoid sensitive data leaks. The weekly or per-project data update cadence requires that task configurations in external business systems be associated with corresponding project identifiers to prevent cross-project data confusion. Customized field specification requirements require external business systems to retain original field mapping relationships to avoid data format errors. The pre-procedure for collection legality verification requires external business systems to prompt compliance verification steps at the embedding entrance to avoid infringement risks.

## Three Common Errors
- Failure to open the sharing entrance related to data collection task design. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check the browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible range of data collection task design data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of the external system respectively; the sharing entrance identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same data collection task design question-and-answer pair is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Open the embedded page, check that the displayed collection plan fields match the documents of the target project, and confirm that no field mappings are lost.
- Verify that access complies with the permission rules configured by the external business system, and confirm that permission verification takes effect.
- Wait beyond the set share link validity period, attempt to access the link, confirm that the link cannot be opened normally, and verify that the validity period configuration is correct.
- Open the embedded page in IE browser, confirm that the page loads normally, and verify that the compatibility configuration takes effect.
