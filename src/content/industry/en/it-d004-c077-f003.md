# Customer Technical Support and Pre-Sales Q&A for Computer Room Inspection and Environmental Monitoring: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
For use by IDC on-duty operations staff, facility engineers, and monitoring center personnel. Data sources include dynamic environment monitoring systems, access control systems, UPS monitoring equipment, and on-site video inspection records. Update frequency is daily or real-time. Daily documents include inspection sheets, temperature and humidity records, alarm records, and on-duty logs. Fields include device number, temperature and humidity values, alarm level, inspection time, and on-duty staff number. Units include degrees Celsius, percentage, volts, timestamps, and more.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
Because of high real-time data requirements, rely on the real-time synchronization capabilities of external business systems to ensure that shared content matches source-end data, and avoid data lag caused by static caching. The multi-field, multi-unit document structure requires retaining the original field format during embedding deployment to prevent unit confusion or data interpretation errors. The sensitivity of inspection and alarm data requires relying on the permission control capabilities of external business systems to limit the access scope of shared content, only authorizing designated personnel to access. The real-time update feature requires that embedding deployment code adapts to dynamic loading logic, adapting to data refresh requirements in different scenarios, while avoiding excessive system resource usage from frequent refreshes.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to computer room inspection and environmental monitoring fail to open. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not replace `shareId` independently.
- The visible scope of computer room inspection and environmental monitoring data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same computer room inspection and environmental monitoring question and answer cannot be viewed on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing; do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm configurations are set correctly
- Open the generated share link, check whether it includes the fields and units configured by the external business system, with no omissions or format errors.
- Deploy the embedding code to a test page, refresh the page and check whether data is synchronized with source-end inspection records and alarm information.
- Log in to the external business system backend and modify an unprocessed alarm record, confirm that the alarm list on the embedded page synchronizes the updated status.
- Try accessing via a login-free link, confirm that publicly available basic data configured by the external business system is displayed normally, with no sensitive variable leaks.
