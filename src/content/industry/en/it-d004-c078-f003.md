# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Cabinet Rack Installation and Cabling: Sharing and Embedding

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category primarily comes from DCIM systems, asset management systems, network topology diagrams, and customer construction work orders. Daily produced documents include rack installation application forms, cabinet wiring diagrams, port label sheets, and on-site construction records. Data updates occur once per order or once daily. Document fields typically include rack unit number, cable specification, port identifier, construction date, and other details. Some documents require matching physical unit standards such as U (rack unit) and meters. The data structure focuses on structured physical deployment information.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Sharing and Embedding Workflow
Because this category’s data includes sensitive physical deployment information such as rack unit numbers and port identifiers, configure authentication rules for the sharing and embedding workflow. Only authorized personnel may access the data.
Data updates occur at a relatively high frequency. Sync the latest construction records and wiring diagrams from external systems to embedded knowledge bases. This avoids using outdated deployment information.
Documents include specific physical units and structured fields. Embedded conversation tools must rely on the knowledge base for processing, and retain the original document’s format and field associations. This prevents loss of critical port and rack unit information during segmentation or parsing.
Additionally, embedding for technical support scenarios must support fast retrieval of dedicated documents for the corresponding cabinet. Match the knowledge base’s classification filtering rules to ensure returned content matches the cabinet being queried.
