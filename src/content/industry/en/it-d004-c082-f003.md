# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category mainly comes from advertising platforms, CRM systems, attribution SDKs, and form systems. The update frequency is typically daily or weekly. Regularly produced documents include attribution rule configuration tables, lead distribution tables, channel effect reports, and exception checklists. Core fields include channel identifier, lead unique ID, assigned sales account, conversion timestamp, and consumption amount. Data is stored in structured table format, with no semi-structured unstandardized content.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" workflow
Scattered data sources and differing update frequencies (daily and weekly) require the original business system to support pulling data within a specified time range, to avoid cross-cycle data confusion. Core fields contain structured content such as channel identifiers and lead IDs, so field mapping relationships must be retained to ensure that embedded attribution rules and lead distribution information match the original business system. Exception checklists involve sensitive information such as sales assignments, so permission checks must be configured to allow only authorized users to access via embedded links. The workflow must also support viewing needs for different roles, adapt display logic for scenarios such as growth operations and sales operations, which is defined by the original business system.

## Three common errors
- Sharing entries related to channel attribution and lead distribution fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not replace `shareId` on your own.
- Visibility of channel attribution and lead distribution data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same channel attribution and lead distribution Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; records cannot be guaranteed to be retained across devices or after cache clearing, do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm configurations are complete
- Copy the full sharing link generated from Application Details - Publish Channels - Non-login window, open it in an incognito browser, and verify that the corresponding content loads normally.
- Add test URL parameters (such as `channel=test&leadId=123`) to the end of the sharing link, open the embedded page, and check if the original business system can correctly obtain and display the corresponding parameter content.
- Switch to an unauthorized account to access the embedded page, and verify that the original business system or third-party verification will intercept access or prompt no access permission.
- Wait for the original business system to complete data update, reopen the embedded page, and check that the displayed content matches the original business system.
