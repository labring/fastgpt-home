# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Game Design and Numerical Balance Tuning: Sharing and Embedding

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Daily documents for this category include gameplay design docs, numerical balance tables, version requirement sheets, and balance test records. Data sources include player behavior data, publicly available competitor materials, battle logs, and version feedback. Data update frequency is mostly weekly or per version cycle. Numerical documents contain fields such as individual monster health values, skill cooldown durations, and resource output quantities. Gameplay design docs include level numbers, rule entries, and version adaptation notes. Core identifiers primarily use pure numerical values, durations, and entry numbers as units.

## Constraints for Sharing and Embedding Workflows
Documents in this category mostly consist of structured rules and numerical content, updated per version or weekly. When sharing or embedding, retain field relevance and original format to avoid losing numerical units or rule entries. Since data sources include undisclosed internal test data, match the sharing scope to the target audience’s permissions, and do not disclose undisclosed content. Additionally, content embedded in pre-sales Q&A scenarios must adapt quickly to different customer consultation scenarios. Flexible extraction of specified document fragments for sharing is supported, ensuring delivered content aligns with the customer’s specific questions.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to game design and numerical balance tuning fail to open. The full system-generated link and its validity period are first verified, then browser request status is checked; manual replacement of `shareId` is not permitted.
- The visible scope of game design and numerical balance tuning data does not match expectations. Third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions are verified separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same game design and numerical balance tuning Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, device, browser, and local identity status are first verified; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities are not determined solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- The share link is accessed, and custom variables are verified to be replaced with corresponding numerical values or version information as expected.
- Document fragments are extracted and embedded in a test page, and numerical units, rule entries, and format matching the original document are confirmed to be retained.
- After the original document is updated, the synchronization cycle is waited to end, and embedded content is verified to be updated to the latest version.
- The share link is confirmed to only be accessible to authorized audiences, to prevent unauthorized link leaks.
