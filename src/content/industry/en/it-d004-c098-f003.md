# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Community Contributor Governance: Sharing and Embedding

## Data Overview for This Category
Data sources for community contributor governance include code repositories, community forums, contribution statistics reports, and governance meeting minutes. Daily maintained documents include contribution guidelines, code of conduct, maintainer lists, and governance meeting minutes. Data is updated monthly or quarterly. Core fields include contributor unique identifiers, role levels, contribution counts, meeting resolution numbers, and other relevant fields. Data only covers community personnel roles, rules, and decision-making mechanisms. It does not include business transaction content, and access control must strictly follow governance permission rules.

## Constraints on the Sharing and Embedding Workflow
Community contributor governance data focuses primarily on personnel roles and rule documents, with monthly or quarterly update cycles, and strict permission rules must be enforced. This characteristic imposes multiple constraints during the sharing and embedding process:
- Shared content must only be bound to governance-related documents such as contribution guidelines and code of conduct. It must not be extended to unrelated business content.
- Sharing entry points must align with governance identity verification logic. Non-commercial editions only support anonymous local identities. Commercial editions can integrate with external systems to verify contributor identities.
- Due to the low data update frequency, shared link expiration times can be set to match the update cycle. Frequent re-generation is unnecessary.
- Community contributor consultation records are only retained via localId on the same device and browser. Retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entry points for community contributor governance fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its expiration time, then check browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of community contributor governance data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not grant business authorization.
- The same community contributor governance Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing. Do not use this phenomenon alone to conclude that the service is abnormal.
