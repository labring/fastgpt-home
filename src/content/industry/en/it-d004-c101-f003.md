# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Renewal Health Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
Data for renewal health management primarily comes from CRM systems, product usage statistics, customer service ticket logs, and contract management systems. Update frequency is typically weekly or monthly. Core documents include customer health reports, renewal forecast sheets, high-risk customer lists, and cross-department communication minutes.
Data fields cover customer unique identifiers, contract cycle milestones, product usage frequency in recent cycles, service response times, renewal-related ticket statuses, and more. Field units adapt to their attributes, with no unified fixed standard.

## Constraints imposed on sharing and embedding workflows
Scattered data sources and long update cycles require that shared embedded content clearly label the data time range, to avoid information bias caused by reliance on real-time dynamic data.
Sharing scope control for sensitive data such as high-risk customer lists and terminal permission verification logic must be implemented separately by the corresponding external business system. FastGPT commercial edition supports connecting sharing entrances to third-party identity verification systems, with external services returning success status and uid.
Different documents have large differences in field structure. Custom field mapping for embedding scenarios must be implemented separately by the corresponding external business system, to avoid display exceptions caused by field mismatches. Synchronized refresh of embedded content must be implemented separately by the corresponding external business system. Caching strategies can be configured to optimize display consistency.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Sharing entrances related to renewal health management fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of renewal health management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entrance identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same renewal health management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service abnormalities.

## How to confirm configuration is complete
- Copy the generated share link, open it on an unauthorized terminal, and verify that it meets access scope restriction requirements.
- Embed the component in the mini-program development tool, confirm that the page loads normally and there are no domain verification related errors.
- Wait for the cache refresh cycle configured by the corresponding external business system to expire, then check whether the data displayed on the embedded end matches the latest updated content in the background.
- After enabling guest mode, access the share link, confirm that the preset health report content can be viewed normally.
