# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Art Asset Production Management: Sharing and Embedding

This guide applies to FastGPT v4.17.0 and later versions.

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category primarily comes from art asset libraries, project management systems, engine resource packages, and outsourced delivery packages.
Daily associated documents include art requirement sheets, asset specification sheets, outsourced feedback sheets, and acceptance records.
Professional fields include triangle polygon count, resolution, resource format, rework times, and others.
Data update frequency is set to daily or weekly, and syncs with project progress.
It flows through the full delivery lifecycle of art assets including characters, scenes, and effects.
This workflow serves art PMs, concept artists, 3D artists, and outsourced project managers.

## Constraints for the Sharing and Embedding Link
Data sources for this category are scattered and linked to multiple types of business documents. The sharing and embedding link must clarify the permission scope of data sources to avoid unauthorized disclosure of outsourced delivery information.
Asset specifications, acceptance records, and other fields have professional unit and format requirements. Custom field display rules must be implemented by the associated external business system. The FastGPT sharing component only displays authorized content to ensure compliance with art production standards.
Data updates daily or weekly. Version locking or incremental sync configuration must be handled by the associated external business data source. The FastGPT sharing component only loads authorized content to prevent embedded content from mismatching the current project asset status.
Communication documents related to outsourcing must distinguish permission boundaries between internal collaboration and customer viewing to prevent sensitive information leaks.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to art asset production management fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of art asset production management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same art asset production management Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service abnormalities.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Copy the sharing link, open it in an incognito browser, and confirm only authorized asset specification and art requirement sheet fields are displayed.
- Replace with a valid sharing link, and confirm only authorized content for the corresponding project is displayed.
- Create a sharing link and configure quotas via Application Details - Release Channels - Guest Access Window, then initiate a call using the test link to confirm API quota binding takes effect.
- Wait 7 days, and confirm the sharing link automatically expires per the preset validity period.
