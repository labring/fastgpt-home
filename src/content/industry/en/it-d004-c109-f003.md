# Customer Technical Support and Pre-sales Q&A for Identity Account and Permission Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
This category’s data primarily originates from enterprise IAM systems, HR systems, business system logs, and approval systems. Updates trigger either one-time personnel changes or fixed daily schedules. Daily generated documents include account application forms, permission matrices, offboarding account recovery forms, and compliance audit reports. Document fields include account ID, permission scope, approval status, change time, with no additional custom units. All data is linked to the full lifecycle process of internal enterprise employee accounts.

## Constraints for Sharing and Embedding
This category’s data is tied to the full lifecycle of internal enterprise employee accounts, and updates frequently alongside personnel changes. When embedding or sharing, authentication logic must sync in real time with the enterprise IAM system to prevent permission expiration from local caching. Data sources include approval systems and business logs. Embedded interactions requiring association with approval status fields must be implemented separately by external business systems. FastGPT sharing only provides a data display carrier. Access scope restrictions for shared content such as permission matrices and audit reports must be implemented using a combination of the enterprise identity system and data permission configurations from external business systems. Data is only accessible to internal enterprise employees. Embedded scenarios must bind strictly to the enterprise identity system to block unauthorized external access, and must adapt to embedding container specifications for different business systems.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to identity account and permission management fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its expiration period, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of identity account and permission management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The share entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same identity account and permission management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Open the configured share link, use a non-internal enterprise account to attempt access, and verify whether the access request is blocked.
- Check whether the corresponding permission matrices and approval records displayed on the embedded page are updated synchronously after the associated external business system updates its business data.
- Call the embedded interface to trigger interactions, and verify whether the associated external business system can correctly receive business data related to the interaction.
- Check whether the page display of the embedding container meets the configuration requirements.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
