# Technical Documentation and API Q&A for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category primarily comes from advertising platforms, CRM systems, attribution SDKs, and form systems. Daily processed documents include attribution rules, lead distribution tables, channel reports, and exception reconciliation sheets. Data updates occur primarily on a daily or weekly basis. Each document contains fields such as channel identifiers, conversion nodes, attribution rules, and lead IDs. Units include impressions, clicks, conversions, and monetary amounts. Core processing steps include channel parameter verification, duplicate lead deduplication, and sales attribution confirmation.

## Constraints on Vector Models and Indexing Workflows
Dispersed data sources lead to inconsistent field formats, requiring adaptation to vector conversion logic for multi-source heterogeneous data. Daily or weekly batch updates require indexes to support incremental synchronization, avoiding excessive time spent on full reconstruction. Each document contains multiple types of business fields, requiring optimization of vector extraction weights for core fields such as channel identifiers and conversion data. The business logic of duplicate lead deduplication and sales attribution confirmation requires index recall results to strictly match business rules, avoiding irrelevant data interfering with retrieval accuracy.

## How to Configure Settings
This document is written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the field density of documents such as attribution rules and channel reports, avoiding including too many unrelated business details in a single chunk |
| Overlap Length | 100–200 characters | Retains critical context such as cross-chunk channel parameters and conversion nodes, preventing key information from being truncated |
| `defaultToken` | 4000 token | Matches the average length of single-batch lead distribution tables, balancing vector conversion accuracy and computational cost |
| `maxToken` | 8000 token | Covers the full content of exception reconciliation sheets and channel reports, ensuring complete recall of information |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Filters low-match non-target channel data, avoiding interference with the sales attribution confirmation process |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Controls retrieval return volume, adapting to the rapid verification needs of channel attribution |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Issue: In the open-source version, when configuring the indexing model, Alibaba-emb3 is mistakenly mixed with general embedding models. Cause: Failure to account for the multi-source heterogeneous characteristics of channel data, and failure to match the applicable scenario of the corresponding embedding model.
- Issue: Retrieval recalls relevant channel reports or lead distribution tables, but the large language model outputs a prompt stating that no relevant content was found. Cause: Failure to optimize vector extraction weights for core business fields such as channel identifiers and conversion nodes, resulting in insufficient match between recalled document vectors and query intent.
- Issue: After batch index updates, duplicate leads are not deduplicated in retrieval results. Cause: Failure to configure incremental synchronization rules according to the daily or weekly update rhythm, resulting in incorrect association and filtering of old and new duplicate data.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Review vector conversion logs to confirm that the number of chunks for each business document meets expectations, with no excessive truncation or redundant chunks.
- Initiate a retrieval test for specific channel parameters, verifying that the fields of recalled results match query intent to meet business requirements.
- Run an incremental synchronization test, confirming that updated lead data can be correctly associated with existing indexes, with no duplicate data remaining.
- Monitor retrieval request response times, confirming that they meet real-time business processing requirements, and adjust relevant configuration items to adapt to processing rhythm.
