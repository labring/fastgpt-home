# Technical Documentation and Interface Q&A for On-site Outsourced Operations and Maintenance Services: Document Parsing and Chunking

This configuration is based on the feature logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Typical Data for This Category Looks Like
Typical data for this category originates from customer ITSM systems, monitoring platforms, asset inventories, and on-duty records. Daily generated documents fall into four categories: on-site daily reports, service desk tickets, inspection reports, and SLA monthly reports.

Document structure varies significantly by type:
- On-site daily reports include fields such as duty period, problem description, and processing result
- Service desk tickets include business identifiers such as number, priority, and response time limit
- Inspection reports include operation and maintenance fields such as device number, status, and inspection time
- SLA monthly reports include service achievement statistics

Data update frequencies are divided into daily updates (on-site daily reports, service desk tickets) and monthly updates (SLA monthly reports).

## Constraints Imposed on Document Parsing and Chunking
Scattered data sources contain mixed structured and unstructured content. Parsing engines must adapt to both text paragraphs and table fields. Chunking must retain the association of business fields, and avoid splitting key identifiers.

Differences in update frequencies across documents require chunking strategies to match timeliness needs: daily updated documents need information granularity suitable for high-frequency queries, while monthly updated documents must retain the integrity of statistical logic.

On-site service documents often include professional operation and maintenance fields such as device numbers and response durations. Chunking must avoid splitting cross-paragraph business units. This prevents information fragmentation that prevents subsequent Q&A from associating complete business scenarios.

## How to Configure the Settings

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `chunkSize` | 800–1200 characters | Matches the information density of single business content such as on-site daily reports and tickets, avoids splitting key business fields |
| `chunkSplitter` | Split by paragraph + title | Adapts to the hierarchical structure of inspection reports and SLA monthly reports, retains document logical coherence |
| `customPdfParse` | Enabled | Adapts to mostly standardized PDF documents in on-site services, improves parsing accuracy for structured content |
| `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` | 120–180 seconds | Matches the parsing time of large SLA monthly reports and batch ticket documents, avoids timeout failures |
| `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` | 500 MB | Meets the needs of batch uploading inspection reports and monthly summary documents, supports batch archiving scenarios for on-site services |
| `chunkSettingMode` | Manual configuration | Adjust chunking parameters for different document types, adapts to the characteristics of multiple types of business documents for on-site services |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Connection failure error occurs when calling `customPdfParse` deployed in Docker Desktop. Attempting to use a local address still fails to connect. Cause: The parsing service inside the Docker container did not expose ports correctly, or the container network mode restricts local access.
- Phenomenon: After uploading documents such as on-site daily reports and tickets, the parsing result is empty or cannot recognize table fields. Cause: The `customPdfParse` configuration is not enabled. The default parsing engine has insufficient adaptability for structured tables.
- Phenomenon: Timeout error occurs when parsing SLA monthly reports. Cause: The value set for `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` is lower than the actual parsing time of the document, failing to adapt to the processing rhythm of large monthly summary documents.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Log in to the FastGPT backend, enter the document parsing configuration page, and check that the setting values of configuration items such as `PARSE_FILE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` and `UPLOAD_FILE_MAX_SIZE` match the preset parameters.
- Upload a typical on-site daily report document, review the field integrity in the parsing result, and confirm that key business identifiers are not split.
- Try uploading a single monthly summary document under 500 MB, verify that the upload and parsing process has no timeout errors.
- For different types of business documents, switch `chunkSettingMode` to manual configuration, adjust `chunkSize` and preview the chunking effect, confirm that it meets the business information granularity requirements.
