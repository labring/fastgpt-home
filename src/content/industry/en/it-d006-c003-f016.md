# Test Case and Defect Analysis for IT Asset Inventory: Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category primarily comes from asset management systems, procurement systems, financial fixed asset ledgers, and terminal management platforms. Four document types are routinely generated: asset ledgers, inventory sheets, requisition and return slips, and scrap application forms. Core fields include asset number, user, storage location, purchase date, and asset status. Update cycles are mostly monthly, quarterly, or annually. Data focuses on asset ownership, physical location, and usage subjects, with no additional non-core business fields associated.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Place on Forms and Interactions
Dispersed data sources require integration with external business systems to enable multi-source data import and verification, to avoid cross-system data inconsistency issues. Fixed update cycles require external business systems to configure scheduled inventory reminders, to match standard company inventory rhythms. Core fields focus on ownership, location, and user. Interaction layouts must prioritize high-frequency fields to reduce operator search time. The physical and ledger verification link requires batch import of actual inventory data for comparison with ledgers. This matching verification must be implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes. Severe error consequences require external business systems to implement mandatory field blocking and abnormal field highlighting functions, to avoid audit risks caused by missing core fields.
