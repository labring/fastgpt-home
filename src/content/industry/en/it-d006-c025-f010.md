# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Duty Scheduling and Handover: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for this category comes from scheduling systems, fault work orders, monitoring alerts, and organizational contact directories. Update frequency is daily or weekly. Daily produced documents include duty schedules, handover records, escalation contact lists, and daily duty reports. Fields include duty time slots, handover items, responsible person contact information, escalation paths, and unresolved work order statuses, among others. There is no unified fixed field format, with slight variations based on team operation and maintenance specifications.

## Constraints Imposed on Citation Sources and Traceability
Data sources are scattered and update frequencies vary. The traceability link must match unique identifiers across multiple systems to ensure accurate association. Document structures have no unified format. Differentiated retrieval and matching rules must be configured for different types of business documents. Update frequency is daily or weekly. The traceability time range must adapt to this rhythm to avoid missing recent scheduling adjustments or handover items. This category of data directly links to operation and maintenance response efficiency. The accuracy of traceability results directly affects fault handling efficiency. Core business fields must be strictly matched.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Adapts to the content length of single documents such as duty schedules and handover records, avoiding truncation of critical traceability information |
| `responseData` | `Field subset containing duty time slots, handover items, and escalation contacts` | Only retains business fields required for traceability, reducing data transmission and storage overhead |
| `detail` | Enabled | Fully displays details of handover records associated with traceability, meeting fault review needs of SRE teams |
| Maximum Context | `Top 10 associated records` | Covers daily or weekly duty data volume, matching the update frequency of this category |
| Citation Matching Rule | Associate by duty staff ID and time slot | Matches unique identifiers of scheduling systems and handover records to ensure traceability accuracy |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The symptom is that the knowledge base retrieves duty scheduling-related content, but only returns citation links without specific business information. The cause is that the `responseData` configuration does not include core business fields such as duty time slots and handover items.
- The symptom is an input error after referencing the plugin in a workflow, with a 400 status code returned. The cause is that the `quoteMaxToken` value exceeds the maximum token limit allowed by FastGPT v4.17.0.
- The symptom is empty traceability results for unresolved item handovers. The cause is that the matching rule associating by duty staff ID and time slot is not configured, resulting in failure to match corresponding handover records.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Launch a workflow that includes a duty scheduling query, check if the returned results include the fields configured in `responseData`.
- Review workflow logs to confirm no error prompts about `quoteMaxToken` exceeding limits appear.
- Simulate a scheduling conflict scenario, verify that traceability results are associated with correct handover records and escalation contact information.
- Adjust the `detail` configuration to disabled, confirm that the detail level of returned content meets business requirements.
