# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Content Copyright and Authorization Management: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data comes primarily from contract systems, copyright registration records, content libraries, and complaint inboxes. Daily processed documents include authorization contracts, copyright ledgers, removal notices, and infringement complaint materials. These cover fields such as rights subjects, authorization scope, validity periods, and complainant information. Data updates follow a per-order or monthly rhythm. It requires associating static and dynamic information across multiple systems. The size of a single data set changes based on the scale of authorized content.

## Constraints on Forms and Interactions
The data characteristics of this category impose multiple constraints on forms and interactions. Data sources are scattered. Forms must support receiving field data provided by external business systems. Cross-source data pulling and linkage are implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes. Update rhythms are flexible. Forms support single submission. Batch processing operations are implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow branches. This meets the needs of single authorization and monthly ledger batch processing. Document fields are closely linked. Forms must transmit field information to external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes. Field dependency verification is implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes. Infringement complaint materials have diverse formats. Forms must provide attachment upload entrances. Multi-type attachment upload and format verification are defined by external business systems. Error consequences are severe. Interaction links must transmit user input information to external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes. Pre-verification and secondary confirmation are implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When configuring the `formInput` node, the entrance for continued input remains visible after submission, and subsequent user input cannot be closed. Cause: The post-submission interaction logic of the `formInput` node is not correctly configured, causing the node to not end the interaction state as expected.
- Phenomenon: When configuring the `userSelect` node, the optional value list is empty, and associated fields of the copyright ledger cannot be selected. Cause: Field data from the contract system and copyright ledger is not pulled through external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes, resulting in no available options in the list.
- Phenomenon: Field information collected by the `formInput` node is not transmitted to subsequent workflow nodes, and subsequent execution cannot be triggered. Cause: The post-submission trigger logic of the `formInput` node is not correctly configured, causing input information to fail to complete transmission.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Submit single authorization information, check whether automatic verification prompts appear for required fields, and confirm that the `formInput` verification rules take effect.
- Trigger batch data import through an external business system, check whether the field information received by the form meets expectations, and confirm that the linkage logic is correct.
- Upload attachments in non-PDF/Word formats, check whether format verification prompts are triggered, and confirm that the attachment upload restrictions take effect.
- Select the rights subject option in `userSelect`, check whether only internally compliant subject lists are displayed, and confirm that the optional range configuration is correct.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
