# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Customer Permission and Organizational Structure Configuration: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
The core data for this category comes from customer HR systems, identity directories, product backends, and audit logs. Four document types are maintained daily: permission matrices, organizational structure tables, role configuration records, and permission change tickets. Data updates follow the cycle of a single customer implementation project, or are triggered irregularly alongside permission adjustments. Fields include department hierarchy identifiers, role permission scopes, change timestamps, and operator information. No standard fixed units are used. Text, numeric, and enumeration types are applied as appropriate for the business scenario.

## Constraints on forms and interactions
Multi-source data access requires external business systems or subsequent nodes to build association logic between forms and external identity directories and HR systems. This avoids manual duplicate data entry. Irregular data updates require external business systems or subsequent nodes to support form change history and version rollback. This prevents configuration confusion. Complex document structures require external business systems or subsequent nodes to build form interfaces split into modules for organizational structure, role permissions, and change records. This lowers operational barriers. Errors such as unauthorized access require external business systems or subsequent nodes to run pre-form permission mapping verification. This blocks invalid configurations. External business systems or subsequent nodes must also synchronize form operation records to audit log storage to ensure traceability.

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: An "invalid parameter input" prompt appears when submitting a permission configuration form. Cause: Required field verification for `formInput` is not implemented by external business systems or subsequent nodes. Null value submission triggers verification failure.
- Configured options are selected one by one in the user selection node `userSelect`. The workflow is checked to confirm whether it enters the corresponding branch. Filtering of records related to customer permission and organizational structure configuration must be additionally verified against external interface input, permissions, and return values.
- Phenomenon: No audit record is generated after a permission change is submitted. Cause: Change record synchronization configuration is not enabled by external business systems or subsequent nodes. This results in missing audit logs and fails to meet traceability requirements.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- An empty permission configuration form is submitted. External business systems or subsequent nodes are checked for required field prompts, to verify that the `formInput` verification rules are active.
- More than 50 user roles are selected. External business systems or subsequent nodes are checked for a single selection limit prompt, to verify that the `userSelect` restriction logic is active.
- An organizational structure table that violates hierarchy specifications is imported. External business systems or subsequent nodes are checked for blocked imports and returned verification information, to verify that the organizational structure import verification configuration is active.
- A permission change configuration is submitted. The product backend audit module of the external business system is checked for corresponding generated logs, to verify that the change record synchronization configuration is active.
