# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Advertising Compliance Audit Trail: Citation Sources and Traceability
## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data sources for advertising compliance audit trail include advertising platform backends, customer qualification documents, contract systems, and publicly available regulatory rules. Daily generated documents include advertising review forms, qualification attachments, delivery records, and rectification notices. Each individual data entry is associated with fields such as activity ID, qualification number, delivery time period, and rectification document number. Data is updated once per delivery campaign or monthly for archiving. Full chain evidence is adjusted alongside campaign progress and retained regularly.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Citation Sources and Traceability Link
Data from multiple sources requires distinguishing the priority of different data sources during traceability to avoid confusing the association between customer qualifications and delivery records. Fixed update rhythms require citations to be bound to the corresponding campaign cycle, and expired data cannot be cited across campaigns. Documents include attachments in multiple formats and structured tables, so traceability must support original text jumps for multiple file types. The most time-consuming archiving and sorting processes require the citation workflow to automatically associate data to reduce manual operations. Errors can lead to no evidence available during regulatory inspections, customer liability claims, or account penalties. Therefore, traceability must ensure the accuracy of association relationships.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | `800–1200 token` | Matches the average length of a single paragraph in compliance audit trail documents for FastGPT v4.17.0, to avoid truncating critical content |
| `responseData` | Return full citation metadata | Retain core traceability fields such as qualification numbers and delivery record IDs to meet compliance audit requirements |
| `detail` | Enable detailed traceability | Associate rectification notice original text with specific delivery record content to improve traceability accuracy |
| Maximum context rounds | `First 3 conversation turns` | Compliance audit trail approval chains typically do not exceed 3 turns, covering core approval workflows |
| Citation cache validity period | `24 hours` | Matches the monthly update frequency of compliance audit trail data, ensuring the timeliness of cited data |
| Citation display threshold | `Similarity ≥ 0.7` | Ensure semantic matching accuracy for compliance documents, avoiding incorrect association of unrelated content |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The reply only shows citation markers without body content. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is set too small, so compliance documents are truncated and only metadata is retained
- Symptom: The citation function cannot be disabled when accessing a password-free shared link. Cause: The front-end citation display switch is not disabled synchronously in the application configuration, and the shared link inherits the default citation-enabled configuration
- Symptom: No citation marker appears at the end of the reply paragraph. Cause: The `detail` configuration is not enabled, or the citation display switch is not activated

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Upload an advertising review form and delivery records, initiate a question related to compliance audit trails, and check whether a citation marker appears at the end of the reply
- Copy the password-free shared link, access the link, click the citation marker, and confirm that it jumps to the original document page corresponding to the content
- Initiate a question involving a multi-turn approval process, and check whether the reply associates compliance documents from previous conversations
- Adjust the `quoteMaxToken` parameter to a lower value, verify whether the reply body is truncated, and confirm that the configuration takes effect

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
