# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Ad Creative Review: Forms and Interactions

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data comes from advertising platforms, landing pages, product qualifications, and advertising law provisions. Update frequency is daily or real-time. Daily associated documents include ad creative files, landing page screenshots, review opinion sheets, and modification record ledgers. Data fields include creative unique identifiers, violation type tags, review status, submission time, and reviewer numbers. Units are mostly characters, items, or times. There is no unified fixed numerical unit; corresponding dimensions must be matched based on the specific review scenario.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Decentralized, real-time data sources require external business systems to quickly pull and verify multi-source data, preventing review deviations caused by data lag. The need to associate multiple types of documents requires external interaction interfaces to support attachment uploads. Associated field linkage must be implemented by external business systems; for example, after uploading a landing page screenshot, the external business system automatically matches the corresponding creative ID. There is no unified unit for fields, so external interaction interfaces must provide unit selection options, or external business systems must implement automatic adaptation, to prevent reviewers from making errors due to unit confusion. Real-time updated data requires external interaction interfaces to have instant save and version rollback functions, to avoid loss of modification records. The need for association and binding of multiple fields requires external business systems to automatically associate review opinions with creative data, reducing the workload of repeated data entry.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: A "Duplicate input information" error pops up when submitting a review form. Cause: Input deduplication rules are not configured in the external business system, leading to duplicate submission requests for the same creative review.
- Phenomenon: The model selection dropdown menu in the external interaction interface only displays a small number of available models. Cause: Permission filtering rules are not configured in the external model management system, and call permissions for all available models are not enabled.
- Phenomenon: Modification records cannot be automatically associated with review opinions after submitting a review form. Cause: Associated field linkage logic is not configured in the external business system, leading to failure to bind review opinions and creative data.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Upload multiple different types of ad creatives and landing page screenshots, confirm that the external business system can automatically identify and match the corresponding creative ID.
- Switch accounts with different permissions, check whether the optional range passed to the user selection node by the external business system conforms to role configuration, and only authorized reviewers can see corresponding options.
- After submitting the review form, check whether the corresponding modification record ledger is generated, confirm that review opinions and creative data are automatically associated and bound.
- Simulate network fluctuations, test whether the external interaction interface can complete submission normally within 300 seconds without error prompts.
