# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Delivery Effect Optimization: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Data for this category primarily comes from advertising platform APIs, attribution systems, CRM lead databases, and landing page event tracking data. Update frequency is mostly daily or real-time. Daily associated documents include daily delivery reports, weekly delivery reports, daily price adjustment records, material A/B test sheets, and optimization review ledgers. Fields typically include delivery account identifiers, material creative types, impressions, clicks, conversion costs, budget balances, and lead source channels. There are no unified fixed units. Matching measurement standards are used based on the data type.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Data sources are scattered, and update frequencies vary widely. External systems or subsequent nodes must support multi-data source parameter binding configuration. Real-time data verification logic for external business systems must also be integrated to avoid configuration deviations caused by delayed data.
There are various types of daily associated documents. External systems or subsequent nodes must configure field mapping rules to match the field structures of different documents such as price adjustment records and test sheets.
The business pain point of long time spent locating abnormal indicators requires the interaction link to integrate fast screening components from external business systems. These components can quickly locate abnormal items based on dimensions such as conversion costs and budget consumption.
At the same time, configuration verification steps from external business systems must be integrated into the interaction link to prevent subsequent delivery adjustments from failing due to missing fields.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The `userSelect` node has no delivery account options, and target advertising accounts cannot be selected. Cause: The API key of the corresponding advertising platform is not bound, or the key permissions do not cover the account reading scope.
- Phenomenon: After the real-time data synchronization form is submitted, the interface shows that the data has not been updated, but the debug preview works normally. Cause: The multi-data source verification logic of the external business system is not integrated, and the interface return status of the data source is not verified.
- Phenomenon: After the external system/subsequent node field mapping rules are configured, the submitted price adjustment record data is not automatically matched to the delivery effect optimization indicator fields. Cause: The selected template does not match the field structure of the actual document, and the mapping relationship is not manually adjusted.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Execute the custom indicator label verification configured in `formInput`, enter the indicator name of the corresponding advertising platform, and confirm that the form can normally receive input content.
- Trigger the option loading configured in `userSelect`, check whether preset options such as delivery accounts and material types are displayed.
- Submit test price adjustment record data, confirm that the external system/subsequent node can complete data matching according to the configured field mapping rules.
- In the connected external business system, configure the abnormal indicator screening component, select the conversion cost dimension, and confirm that the component can load the corresponding data dimension options.
