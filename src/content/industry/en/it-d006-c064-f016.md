# Test Cases and Defect Analysis for Bid Document Preparation: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
The core documents for this category are bid documents, qualification certificates, business deviation tables, and technical deviation tables. Data is sourced from tender announcements, bidding documents, enterprise qualification databases, and supplier quotations, with an update frequency of once per order. Common daily fields include preset format items, qualification entries, quotation information, and clause response content set by the tenderer. Strict matching with the tenderer’s fixed format requirements is mandatory; any missing field or format non-compliance will affect the final bid result. The most time-consuming daily steps are clause-by-clause response, qualification material verification, and seal and packaging inspection.

## What constraints these characteristics place on forms and interactions
Since data is sourced from single-tender announcements and documents and updated once per order, forms and interactions must support fast loading of single documents. Custom field matching must be completed via external system integration. Core document formats are strictly defined by the tenderer. The interaction link must collect required field information. Required field verification and format compliance checks are implemented by external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes to avoid missing fills or format errors. Qualification materials must accurately match entries in the enterprise qualification database. Interactions must support entry binding; automatic verification is completed by external business systems. The system must also adapt to parallel processing of multiple documents to ensure data from different tender orders is not mixed, and verification results meet the strict requirements of the bidding process.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: After the frontend submits the bid document form, the backend does not obtain the results of the external verification logic. Cause: The callback interface of the external verification logic is not configured correctly, and the frontend interaction and backend data link are disconnected.
- Phenomenon: The AI verification does not only verify the preset six procurement product information items as required, and additionally identifies irrelevant content. Cause: The system prompt of the workflow AI node does not strictly limit the verification scope, causing the model output to exceed preset rules.
- Phenomenon: Clause response items in bid documents are incorrectly categorized into irrelevant classifications, causing verification to not trigger. Cause: No classification mapping rules are configured for the clause response fields of bid documents, and the system prompt is not used to limit the model's recognition scope.
