# Online Troubleshooting and Retrospective for IT Procurement and Supplier Management: Conversation Logs and Auditing
This document was written based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like
This data typically comes from procurement systems, supplier quotation documents, budget spreadsheets, and contract management systems. Daily documents include procurement applications, price comparison records, contracts, and acceptance receipts. Data is updated per procurement order, either after each order is completed or synced monthly. Each individual data entry contains fields such as procurement order ID, associated supplier name, contract number, applied amount, and approval node records. Field content changes dynamically based on the complexity of the procurement process, with no unified fixed upper limit for field length.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on conversation logs and auditing workflows
Since this category of data is updated per order or monthly and originates from scattered sources, the conversation logs and auditing workflow first must bind `chatId` to a unique procurement order ID to avoid mixing logs across different procurement orders. Second, daily documents include multiple types of structured business files, so logs must be associated with operation records for corresponding procurement nodes. For example, conversation content from the price comparison stage must match price comparison records. Additionally, procurement cycles are relatively long, so log retention must cover the entire process cycle to ensure complete approval and communication chains can be traced during audits. Finally, data fields change with procurement complexity, so auditing configurations need to support dynamic matching of associated fields for different procurement orders.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session Binding Identifier | Bind `chatId` to procurement order ID | Ensures every conversation log is tied to a unique procurement order, making it easy to trace and audit per order |
| Single-Round Conversation Carrying Limit | First 50–200 entries | Matches typical conversation rounds in IT procurement workflows, covering key stages such as requirement confirmation, price comparison, and contract verification |
| Log Detail Toggle | Enable `detail` for full display | Fully records core business information in conversations, including procurement amounts, supplier names, and contract terms |
| Response Association Identifier | Bind `responseChatItemId` to price comparison record ID | Ensures procurement price comparison suggestions generated by the model correspond one-to-one with specific price comparison records |
| Log Retention Period | 12–24 months | Covers the full process cycle of most enterprise IT procurement workflows, meeting standard audit requirements |

## Three common configuration errors
- Phenomenon: After setting the single log carrying count to 6, the model cannot associate conversation content from the previous procurement price comparison stage. Cause: `chatId` was not bound to the corresponding procurement order, causing context to not be aggregated per procurement order.
- Phenomenon: Credential errors are returned when calling the log retrieval API. Cause: `responseChatItemId` was not configured to associate with the business credential field, causing the interface verification to fail to match valid business identifiers.
- Phenomenon: Conversation logs for a specific procurement order cannot be found during audits. Cause: Full recording of the `detail` field was not enabled, causing key business information to be truncated and making it impossible to locate the target log via search.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Manually create a test procurement order, initiate corresponding business conversations, and check if the unique ID of this procurement order is bound in the log list.
- Retrieve a single conversation log, confirm that the `detail` field contains core business information such as procurement amount and supplier name.
- Initiate multiple rounds of procurement price comparison conversations, check if the model can associate content from the previous round of price comparison records.
- View the log retention configuration, confirm that the retention period covers the full process cycle of the current test procurement order.
