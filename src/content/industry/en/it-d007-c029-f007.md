# Online Troubleshooting and Retrospective for Content Copyright and Authorization Management: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the data for this category looks like
Data sources for this category include contract systems, copyright registration information systems, internal content libraries, and complaint feedback inboxes. Daily processed documents include authorization contracts, copyright ledgers, removal notices, and infringement complaint materials. Each single data entry corresponds to one authorization or complaint matter. Update frequency is per order or monthly. Data fields include full authorized entity name, authorization scope clauses, validity period start and end times, complainant contact information, content unique identifier, and other relevant fields. There are no unified fixed numeric statistical fields. Archiving is performed per individual business matter only.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "conversation logs and auditing" workflow
Data sources are dispersed. Conversation logs must be linked to cross-system fields such as contract numbers and content unique identifiers to quickly locate the full-link conversation for a corresponding authorization or complaint matter. Single data entries have low update frequency. High-frequency real-time synchronization is not required. However, archiving must follow business matter dimensions to facilitate subsequent retrospective of infringement or authorization disputes. Daily processed documents are mostly compliance-related texts. Conversation logs must fully retain query and reply content related to authorization scope and validity periods. This ensures compliance details can be traced during auditing. Additionally, scenarios involve sensitive situations such as infringement complaints. Logs must have tamper-proof properties. Access is restricted to designated roles such as copyright and legal teams. Unlike content review scenarios, the core requirement here is post-hoc auditing and dispute retrospection.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `detail` | Enable full context | Covers core compliance troubleshooting scenarios including authorization scope and validity periods |
| `chatId` | Bind to contract number, content unique identifier | Links cross-system business data from contract systems and content libraries |
| `responseChatItemId` | Bind to complaint material number | Associates conversation reply content corresponding to infringement complaints |
| Log storage duration | 180 days | Matches the traceability period required for compliance audits |
| Retrieval result limit | Top 20 entries | Prevents excessive irrelevant data from disrupting troubleshooting efficiency |
| Retrieval permission configuration | Access restricted to copyright and legal roles | Controls access scope for sensitive compliance data |

## Three common misconfigurations
- Symptom: The user end of guest login channels shows no conversation history, but full content can be queried in backend conversation logs. Cause: The `chatId` of guest login sessions is not bound to user temporary identifiers. This causes the front end to fail to correctly associate corresponding session data.
- Symptom: An error "cannot read properties of undefined" appears when viewing session log details during model testing for new channels. Cause: The `responseChatItemId` binding field is not configured correctly. This causes the system to fail to read associated data for corresponding conversation replies.
- Symptom: Session logs cannot be located by authorization contract number or content unique identifier during retrieval. Cause: The `chatId` is not bound to business fields in contract systems or content libraries. This causes retrieval dimensions to not match business data.

## How to confirm configurations are correct
- Log in to a designated copyright or legal role account. Search for session logs corresponding to an authorization contract number. Confirm that matching results are returned.
- Initiate a conversation in a guest login channel. Check if the user end displays corresponding historical records. Confirm that session association configurations are active.
- Access the session details page. Confirm that full conversation context loads normally, with no error prompts.
- Use a non-authorized role account to attempt accessing this type of session log. Confirm that relevant content cannot be retrieved.
