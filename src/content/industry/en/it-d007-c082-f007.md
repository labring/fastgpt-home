# Online Troubleshooting and Retrospective for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
This category of data primarily originates from advertising platforms, CRM systems, attribution SDKs, and form systems, with most updates occurring on a daily or weekly basis. Commonly associated documents include attribution rule configuration tables, lead distribution tables, channel effect reports, and exception checklists. Data fields include channel identifiers, lead IDs, conversion timestamps, sales attribution IDs, and attribution parameter values, among others. There are no standardized fixed units, as formats vary based on the source system. Core processing steps involve channel parameter verification, duplicate lead deduplication, and sales attribution confirmation. Unlike landing page conversion optimization-focused sub-directions, this category focuses on validating lead sources and sales attribution. Data anomalies directly impact attribution accuracy and business decision-making.

## Constraints on Conversation Logs and Auditing
Decentralized data sources require conversation logs to cover calls to multiple system interfaces, and unify collection of channel parameters and lead identifiers across different formats. The daily or weekly update rhythm means audit logs must support batch retrieval by time range, and associate with attribution reports for the corresponding cycle. The need to link multiple documents requires audit logs to bind version snapshots of attribution rules and lead distribution tables, to avoid being unable to verify historical anomalies after configuration changes. Logs related to sales attribution must retain complete parameter verification chains for subsequent dispute checks and issue tracing.

## Configuration Guidelines
The following configurations are formulated for FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Stored bound to lead ID and channel identifier, retained for 30–180 days | Serves as the unique session identifier, associates core business fields, and matches the business update rhythm |
| `responseChatItemId` | Records the unique identifier of attribution results, archived synchronously with sessions | Used to trace the final lead attribution judgment link, supports dispute checks |
| `detail` | Stores original requests and verification logs, containing complete parameters of 1000–2000 characters | Covers full process details of channel parameter verification and duplicate lead deduplication |
| Log storage period | 30–180 days | Matches the daily/weekly business data update rhythm, balances audit requirements and storage costs |
| Multi-source data association | Enabled | Covers log collection from multiple sources including advertising platforms, CRM, attribution SDKs, and unifies audit standards |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: A call to the attribution interface returns a 400 invalid image error, and no corresponding parameter verification record appears in the conversation logs. Cause: The `detail` configuration item is not enabled, so the image parameter content of the original request is not stored, making it impossible to locate the anomaly source.
- Symptom: After deploying a separate container, conversation logs cannot be associated with the corresponding lead ID and channel identifier. Cause: The association between sessions and business fields is not bound via `chatId`, causing audit logs to become disconnected from business data.
- Symptom: Console.log logs from workflow code modules are not displayed in the audit panel. Cause: The multi-source data association configuration is not enabled, so log output inside the workflow is not collected, preventing full-link auditing.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Randomly select a historical session, verify that `chatId` is bound to the corresponding lead ID and channel identifier, and confirm the association is correct.
- View the stored content of the `detail` field, confirm that it includes original request parameters and verification logs, covering full process details.
- Check the log storage period setting, confirm it matches the daily or weekly update rhythm of the business and meets audit requirements.
- Trigger an abnormal call, verify that the conversation logs fully record the error information and parameter content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
