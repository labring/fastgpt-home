# Online Troubleshooting and Retrospective for Renewal Health Management: Conversation Logs and Auditing

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
Core data for renewal health management originates from CRM systems, product usage data, ticket statistics ledgers, and contract management systems. Daily output documents include customer health reports, renewal forecast sheets, high-risk customer lists, and cross-team communication minutes. Data is typically updated on a weekly or monthly basis. Core fields include customer unique identifiers, contract expiration dates, product usage frequency, ticket response times, renewal progress, and risk levels. The data structure must cover renewal-related behaviors across the full customer lifecycle, to support subsequent risk attribution and action follow-up.

## Constraints Imposed on the Conversation Logs and Auditing Link
Because data sources are scattered and update cycles are lengthy, conversation logs must integrate cross-system associated identifiers. This ensures every record can be traced back to the corresponding customer’s renewal-related behavior. The complexity of document structures requires logs to match core field indexing rules, to quickly filter conversation records for high-risk customers. The weekly or monthly update rhythm means auditing does not require real-time collection. But logs must be retained for a long enough period to cover full renewal cycle analysis. Additionally, the most time-consuming risk classification step requires logs to retain complete call chains and parameter configurations. This reduces the cost of retroactively completing data.

## How to Configure Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chatId` | Bind to the customer session ID from the high-risk customer list | Associate with the unique customer identifier in CRM, to quickly locate full-link conversation records for high-risk customers |
| `responseChatItemId` | Record the complete model response return ID | Precisely locate anomalies in individual replies, troubleshoot issues where Feishu bots show successful sends but no records exist in FastGPT |
| `detail` | Enable full log collection | Cover full-link details of product usage data and ticket statistics, to classify specific causes of renewal risks |
| Log retention period | 30–90 days | Match weekly or monthly update frequencies, meet auditing and retrospective needs across the full renewal cycle |
| Log retrieval indexes | Index by contract expiration date and ticket status | Match core fields of customer health reports, quickly filter conversation logs for high-risk customers |
| Log filtering rules | Filter sessions that are not business tests | Reduce invalid log storage usage, focus on real customer communications related to renewals |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- The symptom is failed loading of the FastGPT page deployed via Docker. The page spins and then throws an error, with no detailed stack trace for the `detail` field in logs. The cause is that full log collection is not enabled, so startup exceptions and configuration errors in the deployment environment cannot be captured.
- After configuring a Feishu bot, Feishu shows the send was successful, but no corresponding message appears in FastGPT conversation records. The cause is that `chatId` is not bound to the customer identifier of the Feishu session. This means logs cannot be associated with the high-risk customer list, and communication records cannot be traced.
- After sending a question, the system logs do not show differences between the actual called model parameters and the configured parameters. The cause is that collection of the `detail` field is not enabled. Model call context configurations are not recorded, so issues with inconsistent knowledge base model parameters cannot be troubleshooted.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Trigger a customer communication related to renewals. In FastGPT v4.17.0, confirm that the `chatId`, `responseChatItemId`, and `detail` fields all exist in the conversation logs and match the customer identifier.
- Check log storage settings. Confirm that the log retention period meets the 30–90 day requirement, and that historical renewal-related conversations older than one month can be retrieved.
- Send a test message via the simulated Feishu bot. Confirm that corresponding records exist in both Feishu and FastGPT, and that the customer identifier linked to `chatId` matches the high-risk customer list.
- Trigger a model call exception. Check that the logs contain complete error stacks and parameter configurations, and confirm that the `detail` field has collected full-link call details.
