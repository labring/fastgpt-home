# IT Procurement and Supplier Management Operations On-Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for IT procurement and supplier management comes from procurement systems, supplier quotations, budget spreadsheets, and contract systems. Daily business documents include procurement applications, price comparison records, contracts, and acceptance documents. Each document has dedicated business fields: procurement applications include application number, applying department, and budget limit; contracts include contract number, effective date, and payment terms. Data is updated per individual procurement process: sync once per completed order or once per month for full data. Documents from different procurement orders are independent but closely linked.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on the Sharing and Embedding Link
This category’s data characteristics impose three core constraints on the sharing and embedding link. First, data sources are scattered but closely linked. When embedding Q&A, ensure that context from multiple document types such as procurement applications, price comparison records, and contracts can be called to avoid breaking business logic. Second, document fields have strong business attributes. When slicing or retrieving, complete field information must be retained, otherwise accurate responses to procurement clauses and budget limits in Q&A will be affected. Third, content is only for internal IT department use. Sharing and embedding must restrict access scope, while adapting to the embedding specifications of internal operations ticket systems, to ensure that call permissions match business processes.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to IT procurement and supplier management fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of IT procurement and supplier management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same IT procurement and supplier management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache; do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.
