# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for SLA and Service Reports: Sharing and Embedding

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category comes from monitoring systems, ticketing systems, contract documents, and customer communication records. Update frequency is monthly or quarterly. Daily output documents include SLA clause text, monthly service summary reports, service event statistics entries, and customer review minutes. Data fields include service response cycles agreed in contracts, single event processing duration, report coverage cycles, and review event lists. No pre-set additional statistical values are included; only raw records are summarized and organized.

## Constraints on Sharing and Embedding
The multi-source nature, periodic updates, and non-standardized field characteristics of this category's data impose multiple constraints on the sharing and embedding process. First, the data includes contractually agreed clauses and customer communication records. Sharing links must be configured with identity authentication (supported in the commercial version), restricting access only to internal operations and customer success teams. Holding the `shareId` alone does not grant authorization. Second, for summary reports updated monthly or quarterly, embedded displays must support manually triggering data synchronization, or external business systems must be configured with scheduled synchronization rules to ensure displayed content matches the latest summary results. Third, fields have no unified preset format. Embedded displays require external systems to handle custom field format adaptation to avoid layout misalignment. Additionally, the data involves service compliance content. Embedded pages must be configured with access log recording (external implementation recommended) to facilitate subsequent traceability.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to SLA and service reports fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check the browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of SLA and service report data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier alone does not provide business authorization.
- The same SLA and service report Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache; do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Access the share link to verify that only internally authorized accounts that have passed identity authentication can view it, while unauthorized accounts cannot access it. Holding the `shareId` alone does not grant authorization.
- Open the embedded page on different devices (including mobile devices) to verify that the content layout is normal, with no misalignment or overflow.
- Trigger the data synchronization operation to verify that the displayed content matches the latest monthly/quarterly SLA report.
- View the access log (external implementation recommended) to confirm that all access records are properly retained.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
