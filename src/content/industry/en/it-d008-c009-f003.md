# Personal Data Protection Compliance for Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
This category's data primarily originates from business systems, App permission lists, privacy policies, and user request records. Update frequency is quarterly or following each product change. Core daily documents include personal information inventories, processing rules, impact assessment reports, and user authorization texts. Document fields mostly cover natural person identifying information identifiers, processing purposes, legal bases, retention periods, and similar attributes. There are no uniform fixed units; content granularity must be adjusted based on specific business scenarios.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding workflow
Since the data involves identifiable natural person privacy information, the sharing and embedding process must strictly restrict access scope and follow the principle of least privilege. Document update frequency is relatively high. Embedded compliance documents must have a synchronization mechanism configured via the corresponding knowledge base system to ensure embedded content stays consistent with the latest compliance documents. For operations on-call and ticket scenarios, embedded Q&A tools must integrate with internal teams’ identity permission systems (commercial edition only) to prevent unauthorized personnel from accessing tickets and documents containing personal information. Shared link validity periods and access scopes must also be standardized to meet compliance audit record requirements.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Shared links for personal data protection compliance fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of personal data protection compliance data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not grant business authorization.
- The same personal data protection compliance Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and this phenomenon alone cannot be used to judge service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Configuration is Complete
- Copy the generated shared link, access it using an account outside the internal compliance team, and verify that a no access permission prompt is displayed.
- View the knowledge base content loaded on the embedded page, and confirm that only compliance-related documents such as personal information inventories and processing rules are included, with no other unrelated data.
- Trigger one compliance ticket Q&A, check that the returned result includes the complete legal basis field, and that no content truncation occurs.
- Wait 7 days and access the original shared link, and verify that a link expired prompt is displayed.
