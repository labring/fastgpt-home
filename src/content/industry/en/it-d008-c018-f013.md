# Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Enterprise Software License Management: Permission and Data Isolation

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category originates from software asset management systems, procurement systems, terminal scan results, and vendor authorization files. Four types of documents are maintained regularly: license ledgers, software usage lists, procurement contracts, and compliance audit reports. Data is updated monthly or quarterly. Each data entry includes fields such as license number, authorized quantity, bound terminal count, and expiration date. Field units include units, pieces, and calendar days. Data from different sources corresponds to distinct business management responsibility boundaries.

## Constraints for Permission and Data Isolation
Data for this category originates from scattered sources and contains compliance-sensitive information. Different data sources correspond to separate permission boundaries. Access ranges for roles including IT asset managers, procurement staff, and legal compliance teams must be strictly separated. The monthly or quarterly update rhythm means permission configurations do not require frequent adjustments. However, data isolation rules must take effect immediately after changes. Documents such as license ledgers and compliance audit reports carry enterprise compliance risks. Only authorized roles may view content for the modules they oversee. In operations on-call and ticket Q&A scenarios, processing staff may only call business line data linked to the current ticket. This prevents cross-departmental data leaks.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `teamId` isolation dimension | Divide by business line | Data for this category corresponds to different software procurement and management entities by business line, matching business line permission boundaries |
| `tmbId` member permission binding | Configure by role groups | Separate access ranges for IT asset managers, procurement staff, and legal compliance staff, aligning with daily responsibility divisions |
| Permission check timeout | 600 seconds | Prevent permission check blocking during ticket processing, adapting to the real-time response requirements of operations on-call |
| Maximum number of data entries per call | Top 20 | Adapt to the single-page display volume of license ledgers, avoiding loading excessive sensitive data at one time |
| Permission cache validity period | 7 days | Align with the monthly/quarterly update rhythm of this category, avoiding frequent synchronization of permission data |
| Sensitive document filtering rules | Only display license ledgers and usage lists under the current `teamId` | Compliance audit reports are only accessible to legal roles, preventing unauthorized access |
| Ticket context isolation | Only retain data for the `teamId` associated with the current session | Prevent cross-business-line sensitive data leaks during ticket Q&A |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: Team data returned after calling `teamId`-related interfaces is not stored correctly, leading to permission check failures. Cause: The `teamId` is not bound to business line data, and returned interface data is not synchronized to the permission isolation module.
- Symptom: The knowledge base answer ends with "No permission to operate this conversation record", and the reference cannot be removed. Cause: No `teamId` isolation rule is configured for ticket context, leading to accidental association of cross-business-line session data.
- Symptom: The team management page fails to open. Cause: The permission management module corresponding to `teamId` is not enabled, or the role is not assigned access permission for the team management page.

## How to Verify a Successful Configuration
- Log in to FastGPT v4.17.0, enter the team management page, and confirm that `teamId` groups are divided by business line.
- Initiate an operations ticket for the corresponding business line, call `teamId`-related interfaces, and verify that returned data only includes license information for the current business line.
- Switch to an account with a non-legal role, attempt to access compliance audit reports, and confirm that a no permission prompt is displayed.
- Initiate a cross-business-line ticket test, verify that the session context does not load sensitive data from other business lines.
