# Operations On-Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Crowdsourced Annotator Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data primarily comes from crowdsourced platform backends, annotation quality statistics systems, internal training exam records, and financial settlement systems. Daily associated documents include annotator access rules, onboarding training materials, monthly performance statistics sheets, and payment settlement lists.
Data updates follow daily and monthly cadences. Daily data covers same-day annotation quality and staff attendance records. Monthly data includes performance rankings and settlement details.
Data fields include staff ID, annotation task type, quality score, assessment result, settlement amount, and more. Each field has clear business units and defined meanings.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Sharing and Embedding
This category’s data originates from multiple systems. When embedding, external systems must be used to isolate permissions across different data sources to prevent cross-system data confusion.
Daily-updated annotation quality data requires embedded content’s caching strategy to align with the daily update cadence, avoiding display of outdated information.
Settlement lists and performance sheets contain sensitive financial and staff information, so shared links must be configured with commercial third-party identity verification rules.
Data fields include business-specific staff IDs, quality scores, and more. Contextual recall must prioritize matching this category’s business fields via knowledge bases or workflows to ensure Q&A content aligns with annotator management scenarios.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Failed opening of the share entry for crowdsourced annotator management. First verify the full system-generated link and its expiry period, then check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- Visible range of crowdsourced annotator management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the share entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- Original conversation is not visible on another device for the same crowdsourced annotator management Q&A. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, so do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Successfully Applied
- Access the embedded component, enter test questions, confirm that returned content matches the annotator management data for the corresponding crowdsourcing project, with no cross-project irrelevant information.
- Attempt to access the embedded component using an account that has not passed commercial third-party identity verification, confirm that Q&A content cannot be loaded normally or valid data cannot be obtained.
- Wait for the caching strategy of the external embedded system to take effect, check if Q&A results match the latest daily annotation quality data, verify that the refresh logic is working.
- Test access requests from non-internal IP ranges, confirm that the embedded component cannot be loaded normally, verify that access restriction configurations are working.
