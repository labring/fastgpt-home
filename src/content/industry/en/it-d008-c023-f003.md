# Operations on-duty and internal ticket Q&A for IT budget and project portfolio management: Sharing and embedding

## What data for this category looks like
Data sources for this category include financial systems, project management systems, contract ledgers, and business department requirements. Daily output documents include annual budget sheets, project proposal documents, project portfolio dashboards, and investment review reports. Core fields include budget amount (unit: yuan), project cycle (unit: days), priority level, and revenue forecast. Data update frequency is usually monthly, quarterly, or annually. A single update involves data consolidation and verification across multiple systems. The overall structure tends to combine structured reports and combined dashboards.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" link
Data sources for this category are scattered, and update frequency is low. When embedding into operations on-duty and internal ticket systems, ensure that associated knowledge base sessions only point to budget and project portfolio-related documents, and avoid mixing unrelated content. Structured reports and combined dashboards have high size requirements. The embedding container must adapt to the layout of the original business interface, and avoid occupying core operation areas. Internal scenarios often involve confidential budget data, so non-authorized access must be restricted. Login and permission verification rules during embedding must match enterprise internal office specifications.

- The same IT budget and project portfolio management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device.
