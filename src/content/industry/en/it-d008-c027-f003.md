# Operations and Maintenance Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Content Audit Rule Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
The data sources for this category are the content audit backend, user reports, regulatory policy library, and audit sample library. Documents produced and maintained daily include audit rules, disposal guidelines, appeal records, and quality inspection reports.
Data update frequency is daily synchronization or immediate updates when regulatory policies change.
Document fields include rule number, effective period, sample label, appeal status, quality inspection score, and others. The core covers the full process of formulating, executing, and reviewing audit standards, with no additional statistical quantitative fields.
This documentation applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Sharing and Embedding
This category of data mostly involves compliance audit rules, internal appeal and quality inspection business data. Strict access control is required when embedding and sharing to avoid leakage of sensitive policies or user-related content.
Data update frequency is high, so embedded knowledge base content must sync with the backend for the latest rules and documents to ensure timeliness for operation staff.
For internal ticket and duty scenarios, the embedded chat window style can be adapted by external systems, and external systems can filter shareable document content by permissions.
In addition, audit samples and quality inspection reports involve sensitive business information, so the access scope of shared links must be limited by external systems to prevent unauthorized external data access.
Also, embedded content for operation and maintenance duty scenarios should prioritize associating frequently used document libraries to avoid loading redundant content that affects query efficiency.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Failed to open the sharing entry related to content audit rule management. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check the browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of content audit rule management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same content audit rule management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache, so do not judge service abnormalities solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm the Configuration is Complete
- Open the configured share link or embedded iframe, check if the displayed language matches the target user group.
- Enter query terms related to audit rules, confirm that the returned content only includes audit rules and disposal guideline documents, with no irrelevant data.
- Try to access the share link using a non-internal organization account, confirm that content cannot be loaded normally.
- Confirm that the embedded iframe can adapt to the pop-up or sidebar layout requirements of the internal system.
