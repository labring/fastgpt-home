# Operations on-duty and internal ticket Q&A for content copyright and authorization management: Sharing and embedding

## What data for this category looks like
This category's data comes from contract systems, copyright registration information, content libraries, and complaint inboxes. Daily processed documents include authorization contracts, copyright ledgers, removal notices, and infringement complaint materials. Each document contains fields such as authorized subject, authorization scope, valid period, and infringement fact description. Data updates follow a per-order or monthly rhythm: ledgers are updated immediately after a single authorization or complaint is completed, and full copyright expiration information is summarized monthly. Data fields focus on ownership and usage scope, with no unified business statistical unit. Records are archived only as single authorization or single complaint entries.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on "sharing and embedding"
Data sources are scattered and update rhythms are uneven. External systems (contract systems, complaint inboxes) must periodically sync incremental data to the FastGPT knowledge base to avoid lagging authorization scope information. Document fields focus on rights details, so embedded Q&A must strictly match fields such as authorized subject and scope to ensure accurate responses. Single-item data update frequency is low, so external ticket systems must trigger Q&A requests per single record, and FastGPT processes corresponding Q&A logic based on knowledge base content. Error consequences are severe, so cross-domain restrictions must be configured in line with the high-risk nature of this category. Cross-domain restriction configuration is completed by the internal operation and maintenance system to which the embedded page belongs. Access permission-related parameters can be configured for the release channel of FastGPT sharing links, and verification must be enabled in line with the high-risk nature of this category to avoid risks such as infringement compensation or content removal caused by inaccurate responses.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to content copyright and authorization management fail to open. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not replace `shareId` yourself.
- The visible scope of content copyright and authorization management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of external systems separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same content copyright and authorization management Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and service abnormalities cannot be judged solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Manually insert the configured iframe code into an internal test page, and check whether the robot interaction interface loads normally.
- Submit a test ticket containing authorization keywords, and verify whether the robot returns accurate information from the corresponding copyright ledger.
- Check the sync logs of external systems (contract systems, complaint inboxes) to confirm that incremental data has been successfully synced to the FastGPT knowledge base.
- Test the entry of FastGPT sharing links generated through different application details - release channels - guest login window, and verify that responses only match the configuration scope of the corresponding entry.
