# Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Creator Onboarding and Verification: Sharing and Embedding

This document is written for FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What the data for this category looks like
The data for this category comes primarily from creator backends, enterprise credit information systems, account history databases, and contract management systems. Daily business documents include onboarding application forms, scanned identity qualification documents, audit verification records, and cooperation agreement texts. Data updates run on a daily sync schedule or trigger when individual applications are submitted. Individual business data includes fields such as application number, qualification number, account status, and contract effective date. No unified fixed statistical numerical requirements apply.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
Sensitive business information such as identity qualifications and contracts is included in the data. The scope of embedded sharing must be strictly limited to internal office networks to prevent external access and sensitive content leaks. Data updates occur daily or when applications are submitted. Embedded conversation caching logic must match data update rhythms to avoid displaying expired audit or application information. External systems or browsers can implement this caching logic. Data sources are scattered and linked to unique ticket identifiers. Embedding configuration must bind the unique identifier of the corresponding application through an external system. This ensures conversation content corresponds one-to-one with specific tickets. Individual business document lengths vary. Embedded conversation context length must adapt to the average scale of business documents to avoid context overflow or information truncation.

## Three common errors
- Sharing entries related to creator onboarding and verification fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of creator onboarding and verification data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same creator onboarding and verification Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing; do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to confirm successful configuration
- Verify that the `shareId` used for the embedded conversation matches the system-generated value from the corresponding share link. The corresponding configuration can be traced via the share link.
- Access the embedded page from a non-internal office network, and confirm that interactive initiation fails or a permission prompt pops up.
- Trigger a reply that includes images, and confirm that the image preview function loads and displays normally.
- Submit a query involving sensitive qualifications, and confirm that the reply does not display unsensitized content such as original qualification numbers or enterprise credit information.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
