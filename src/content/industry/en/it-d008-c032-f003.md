# Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Creator Incentive Settlement: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
Typically, data related to creator incentive settlement comes from content data, ad revenue sharing systems, financial systems, and account backends. The update cadence is monthly or per single campaign cycle. Daily associated documents include incentive rule documents, settlement detail lists, creator appeal records, payment approval lists, and others. Data fields include creator unique identifier, settlement cycle, revenue share amount, appeal status, payment progress, and others. Units include yuan, number of users, calendar days, and others.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding workflow
Since data sources span multiple internal systems, embedded Q&A applications must connect to third-party systems via FastGPT Business Edition identity verification. Access is only allowed after external services return a successful status and uid, to prevent leakage of settlement data. The document field density of the associated knowledge base must match the field requirements for settlement-related documents, to ensure Q&A can accurately match settlement details, appeal records, and other content. The update frequency of documents in the associated knowledge base is fixed, but data volume fluctuates with campaigns. It is necessary to adapt to data recall volume across different cycles, while meeting the high response requirements for settlement dispute tickets.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries for creator incentive settlement fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- Visibility range of creator incentive settlement data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same creator incentive settlement Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Embed the application in the intranet and filed external test environment respectively, verify that the page loads normally and returns Q&A content related to creator incentive settlement.
- Check that the `shareId` extracted from the guest window link matches the corresponding sharing entry, avoid using incorrect sharing identifiers.
- Verify that the validity period configuration of the guest window meets the usage cycle of internal operations on-call, confirm that the timeout logic is normal.
- Simulate temporary network fluctuations, check whether the external calling system triggers the preset error retry logic, confirm that the configuration takes effect after no repeated errors occur.
