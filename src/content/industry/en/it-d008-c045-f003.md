# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Security Training and Phishing Drills: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
This category’s data typically comes from employee contact directories, email gateways, internal training platforms, and click tracking systems. Daily generated documents fall into four categories: training courseware, phishing drill plans, phishing email templates, and drill result reports. Data update cycles are mostly quarterly or annual. The core fields of document structure are standardized drill workflows, personnel group information, and click behavior records. There is no unified fixed unit, and fields are adjusted according to individual drill scenarios.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding workflows
Decentralized data sources and slow update cycles mean sharing and embedding configurations must adapt to static or low-frequency updated datasets. Frequent full synchronization should be avoided. Document types include unstructured courseware, templates, and structured reports. The embedding link must support rendering of mixed-format content. Personnel screening for drills and result review rely on accurate personnel and behavior data. Interactions after embedding must retain data permission controls to prevent unauthorized access. Errors are directly linked to employee data leaks and audit risks. The embedding link must support operation log retention and permission verification to ensure all interactions are traceable.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to security training and phishing drills fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of security training and phishing drill data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same security training and phishing drill Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status. Records cannot be guaranteed to be retained across devices or after cache clearing. Do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to verify a successful configuration
- Access the configured embedded page, verify that exclusive documents, templates, and result reports for security training and phishing drills can be loaded.
- Check background operation logs to confirm all embedded requests come from configured legitimate domains, with no access records from unauthorized sources.
- Test the connected external speech recognition service, confirm there are no permission error prompts, and that speech-to-text query operations can be completed normally.
- Initiate a query operation, verify that the front-end page displays the corresponding conversation records, and that storage rules meet business scenario requirements.

The values provided here are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
