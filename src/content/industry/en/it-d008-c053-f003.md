# Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Query for Customer Requirement Pool Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
Core data for customer requirement pool management comes from ticketing systems, customer interview records, CRM systems, and product roadmaps. Update frequency is weekly or per product release cycle. Daily produced documents include requirement pool lists, requirement evaluation forms, customer feedback records, and version planning descriptions. Common document fields include requirement ID, customer identifier, requirement description, priority, evaluation cost, and scheduling node. No mandatory additional statistical units are used. Data revolves around filtering, merging, and implementing feedback from multiple customers.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding workflow
Customer requirement pool data has scattered sources and inconsistent update cycles. External business systems connected via sharing and embedding must support synchronization of the latest version of requirement pool data to avoid displaying outdated content. Structured multi-field documents require embedded components that support on-demand filtering to display specified fields, preventing loading of redundant information. Data is linked to customer identifiers and ticket information. When sharing and embedding, the connected external business system must configure permission filtering rules to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive customer data. For on-call operations scenarios requiring immediate viewing, the connected external business system must support quick content refresh to adapt to real-time business rhythms.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Failure to open the sharing entry related to customer requirement pool management. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status. Do not replace `shareId` independently.
- Visibility of customer requirement pool management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same customer requirement pool management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache; do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Open the configured share link, check that the displayed fields are the pre-configured core fields, with no redundant customer privacy information.
- Manually update the requirement pool data, wait for the data synchronization rhythm of the connected external business system, and check whether the share link loads the latest requirement pool content.
- Switch to an unauthorized role account, attempt to access the share link, and confirm that content cannot be viewed or is blocked by the system.
- Adjust the display size of the embedded container, check that the display effect adapts to the layout requirements of the internal ticketing system.
