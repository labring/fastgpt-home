# Operations On-Call Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Capacity Planning and Resource Scaling: Sharing and Embedding

This document is compatible with FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category comes from cloud service provider bills, cluster monitoring systems, business traffic forecast reports, and stress test output reports. The update cycle is weekly or monthly. Associated business documents include capacity planning reports, expansion application forms, resource utilization summary tables, and budget calculation forms. These contain fields such as resource usage, peak load thresholds, applied quotas, and budget amounts, with different units of measurement. The data structure is relatively fixed, but it involves business-sensitive resource configuration and budget information. Access scope must be strictly controlled to fit the daily on-call duty and ticket processing scenarios of operations teams.

## Constraints on Sharing and Embedding Workflows
The data sources for this category are scattered, and it includes business-sensitive resource configuration and budget information. Therefore, access permissions must be strictly controlled when sharing to prevent unauthorized personnel from accessing core business data. The data update cycle is weekly or monthly, so the cache duration of shared links must match the update rhythm to avoid displaying outdated capacity planning content that affects ticket decision-making. The associated document structure includes multiple types of business fields with inconsistent units, so content filtering rules must be configured during embedding to only display fields and corresponding units required for the target scenario, reducing interference from redundant information. Operations on-call duty and ticket Q&A require association with real-time resource data, so dynamic pulling of the latest resource data via external business systems such as knowledge bases or workflows is necessary to ensure the accuracy and timeliness of Q&A content.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Failure to open the share entry related to capacity planning and resource scaling. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check the browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of capacity planning and resource scaling data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of external systems respectively; the share entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same capacity planning and resource scaling Q&A cannot be seen on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, so do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.
