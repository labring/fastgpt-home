# Advertising Compliance Recordkeeping: Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What data for this category looks like
This category's data primarily comes from advertising platform backends, customer qualification libraries, contract systems, and publicly available regulatory rules. Daily processed documents include advertising review forms, qualification attachments, delivery records, and rectification notices. Fields per document include activity ID, qualification number, delivery period, compliance status, and more. Data updates are triggered upon completion of a single advertising campaign or on a monthly cycle, tightly bound to business activities. There is no fixed frequency, but updates must align with campaign timelines. Data must be linked to activity subjects, compliance nodes, and retained evidence for subsequent regulatory audits and liability tracing.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding workflow
Core data for advertising compliance recordkeeping is tightly bound to individual advertising campaigns. When sharing or embedding, ensure recipients can associate with the complete evidence chain of the corresponding campaign. Since data is required for regulatory audits, sharing links must use valid `shareId` generated by the system to prevent unauthorized access. File sizes of qualification attachments and delivery records vary significantly, so embedding must adapt to the loading logic of different files. Data updates are triggered upon campaign completion. Embedded content must link to external business systems to sync the latest rectification status and archive records, while retaining traceability information of original data to ensure compliance. For internal ticket scenarios, sharing must be restricted to only internal operations and legal personnel. This permission verification must be implemented via a commercial third-party identity verification system.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Sharing entries related to advertising compliance recordkeeping fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of advertising compliance recordkeeping data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same advertising compliance recordkeeping Q&A cannot be accessed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache, do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.

## How to confirm configuration is complete
- Access the sharing link generated from App Details - Publish Channels - Non-login window, and verify that the bound advertising campaign-specific compliance data is displayed.
- If commercial edition third-party identity verification is configured, switch to an unauthorized role to access the sharing link, confirm that content cannot be viewed, to verify that permission configuration is effective.
- Upload qualification attachments of different sizes to the associated external business system, confirm that the embedding component loading process works normally, with no obvious lag or errors.
- Wait for the external business system to trigger data updates, refresh the embedding window, confirm that the latest rectification status or archive records have been synchronized and displayed.
