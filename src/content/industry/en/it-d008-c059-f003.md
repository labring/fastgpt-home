# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Ad Creative Review: Sharing and Embedding

## What this category of data looks like
Daily processed documents include ad creative files, landing page screenshots, review opinion sheets, and modification record ledgers. Data sources are ad placement platforms, landing page hosting services, product qualification filing repositories, and ad compliance clause repositories. Update frequency is daily or real-time push. Data fields include creative unique identifiers, violation type tags, review latency, modification traceability numbers, and other fields directly related to the review process. No redundant statistical items are included.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the sharing and embedding workflow
Multiple data sources and flexible update rhythms for ad creative review data mean embedded carriers must support dynamic pulling of the latest data from external business systems to avoid outdated review content. Review-related documents contain compliance-sensitive information, so access after embedding must be bound to the permissions of the internal ticket system, and cannot be made publicly available arbitrarily. There are differences in the size of creative files, so embedded carriers must support custom loading thresholds to adapt to the loading needs of different creative files and avoid loading timeouts. Additionally, internal operational on-call scenarios require embedded content to link with the internal ticket system: clicking will jump to the ticket page of the corresponding review task. This linkage is implemented by the internal ticket system to improve operational efficiency.

## How to configure settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sharing Entry `shareId` | Copy the complete share link generated by the system, do not replace the identifier manually | Identifies the sharing entry; ad creative review business records and data permissions still need to be handled by external systems |
| Share Link Validity Period | Test starting point: 7 days; adjust based on actual testing of ad creative review collaboration cycles and revocation requirements | Verify availability within the valid period and access behavior after expiration. This value is not an official default or industry statistic |
| QPM | Test starting point: 20 requests per user per minute; adjust based on actual load | Limits the access frequency of a single user, needs to be verified combined with request logs and actual load |
| Identity Verification | When identity permission requirements exist, first verify the business edition capabilities and the uid returned by the third-party identity service | Holding a share link does not equate to having business data permissions; third-party identity and data scope must be verified separately |
| Referenced Content Display | Select the display configuration of snippet, full text, or original text based on the sensitivity of ad creative review materials | Check displayed content within the authorized data range; do not use hidden interfaces as a substitute for data permission control |
| Conversation Record Verification | Test example: Prepare 10 ad creative review Q&A samples, check them in the same browser and another device respectively | Anonymous records rely on browser local identity, and cannot be guaranteed to be retained across devices or after cache clearing |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Failure to open the sharing entry related to ad creative review. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check the browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of ad creative review data does not match expectations. Verify the third-party identity verification result, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same ad creative review Q&A cannot be seen on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status. Records cannot be guaranteed to be retained across devices or after cache clearing, do not use this phenomenon alone to determine service abnormalities.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Open the generated share link, confirm that the basic page loads normally.
- Use an account not authorized by the internal operational system to access the share link, confirm that review data cannot be loaded.
- Adjust the window size in the target embedded carrier, confirm that the content can be fully displayed without overflow.
- Trigger data updates in the external business system, refresh the embedded page, confirm that the review data is in the latest state.

Note: The configuration values listed above are common starting points. They should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
