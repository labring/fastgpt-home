# Search Ad Keyword Management: Operations On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A - Sharing and Embedding

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Daily data for search ad keyword management centers on user-initiated search terms. Core documents include keyword lists, negative keyword tables, bid records, and search term reports. Data primarily comes from search ad backends, search term reports, ranking data, and landing page data. Most updates run on a daily or weekly cadence. Fields include keyword text, match type, bid amount, search volume, conversion cost, and more. Some documents require classification by delivery account and time period.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Failure to open the share entry for search ad keyword management. First verify the full system-generated link and its expiry date, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- Visible scope of search ad keyword management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The share entry identifier itself does not grant business authorization.
- Original conversation does not appear on a separate device for the same search ad keyword management Q&A. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status. Session retention is not guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache. Do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.
