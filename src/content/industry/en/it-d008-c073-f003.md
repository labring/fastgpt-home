# Operations on Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Dataset Delivery and Version Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
Usually, data originates from object storage, data warehouses, annotation platforms, and customer acceptance feedback. The update frequency is once per delivery batch or per version. Daily supporting documents include dataset descriptions, version records, delivery lists, and acceptance reports. Common data fields include version number, dataset storage path (unit: MB), total sample count (unit: entries), delivery date, acceptance result, and no additional custom units are required.

## How These Characteristics Impact Sharing and Embedding Workflows
Data sources are scattered and linked to multiple systems. The sharing process must clarify permission boundaries for cross-system calls to prevent mixing datasets from different batches. Update frequency varies with delivery batches. The embedding process requires external business systems to link to the sharing entry of the corresponding version to avoid calling outdated delivery packages. Supporting documents have a fixed structure and clearly defined fields. External business systems must link to the sharing entry of the corresponding version to ensure recipients receive matching dataset descriptions and acceptance information. Sample differences and delivery integrity must be traceable. Embedded Q&A must be linked to version records to ensure reproducibility of data usage.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to dataset delivery and version management fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of dataset delivery and version management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same dataset delivery and version management Q&A cannot be accessed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing, and do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Access the sharing link, confirm that conversation records are retained normally in the same device and browser, records are deleted after clearing browser cache, and external system-generated operation logs are preserved in the background.
- Link the sharing entry of the corresponding version via the external business system, verify that the returned dataset content matches the delivery list of the corresponding version.
- Check the external business system's permission configuration to confirm that only internal network segments can access the corresponding resources.
- Confirm that the link validity period falls within the preset on-duty cycle, and the link automatically expires after the set time.
- Test the Q&A loading duration of the external business system's embedded page to ensure normal response within the preset context range.
