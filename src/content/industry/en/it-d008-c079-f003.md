## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to architecture design and technical solutions fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of architecture design and technical solution data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same architecture design and technical solution Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearing. Do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Access the shared link, check that historical conversations are displayed normally, and confirm that the historical record switch has been configured according to business requirements.
- Load embedded content on the mini-program or target embedding page, verify that document types such as architecture diagrams and technical solutions can be rendered normally.
- Test the voice recognition function, confirm there are no permission errors, and check that the domain whitelist includes the domain of the current embedding page.
- Wait for the preset duration, verify that the login-free session expires or renews normally according to the configured validity period.
