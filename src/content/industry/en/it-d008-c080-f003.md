# Desktop Terminal Support for Operations On-Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents for desktop terminal support include terminal trouble tickets, installation manifests, software installation records, and remote assistance records. Data comes primarily from terminal management platforms, asset systems, user reporting channels, and software repositories. Update frequency is daily or triggered by user requests. Individual records contain fields such as device ID, fault type, processing time, peripheral model, and user workstation number. Some records include multi-column structured content or fragmented fault description text. The overall data structure varies slightly based on the operations and maintenance scenario.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" workflow
Data sources are scattered and update frequencies are flexible. Sharing and embedding must match the access rules of multiple external data sources to avoid cross-system permission conflicts. Field differences in individual records require custom filtering of embedded content to be implemented by the external display system. Only show core information required by operations and maintenance personnel to prevent leakage of sensitive device data. The mix of multi-column structured documents and fragmented text requires embedded content processing to adapt to content splitting across different formats. Control the length of returned results to avoid exceeding the adaptation range of terminal displays. Internal operations and maintenance scenarios have strict privacy requirements. Embedded access must be strictly limited to the enterprise IT department scope by the enterprise external permission control system. This prevents unauthorized personnel from accessing sensitive information related to terminal devices.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries related to desktop terminal support fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of desktop terminal support data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of the external system respectively. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same desktop terminal support Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache. Do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Copy the generated embedding code, paste it into a test workspace, and check whether matching terminal trouble ticket content loads correctly.
- Call the external embedding interface, pass in the `shareId` parameter obtained from the official share link, and check whether the returned content includes the preset core fields.
- Modify a record in the bound data source, wait for the cache to expire, refresh the embedded page, and confirm that the content has been updated.
- Attempt to access the embedded page using an unauthorized access subject, such as a device or account belonging to non-enterprise IT department personnel. Confirm that relevant operations and maintenance data cannot be obtained. For commercial edition scenarios, verify the third-party identity verification interception logic.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
