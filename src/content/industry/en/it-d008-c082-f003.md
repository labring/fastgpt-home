# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data comes primarily from advertising platforms, CRM systems, attribution SDKs, and form submission systems. Core documents for daily maintenance include attribution rule configuration tables, lead distribution tables, channel effect reports, and abnormal lead checklists. Data updates run on daily or weekly cycles. Fields include channel unique identifiers, lead IDs, sales owners, conversion timestamps, budget allocation amounts, and no additional custom units.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose During Sharing and Embedding
This category’s multi-source data nature requires embedded bots to link to documents from multiple data sources such as advertising platforms and CRMs. Embedding configurations must connect to external data sources or knowledge bases to define data scopes. This prevents unauthorized disclosure of unrelated internal data. This scope configuration is handled by external systems or knowledge bases.
Daily or weekly update cycles require the synchronization cycle of associated knowledge bases or external data sources to match the data update frequency. Information lag will occur if this alignment is not maintained. This synchronization cycle is handled by external systems or knowledge bases.
High field complexity requires limiting context window length during embedding. This avoids loading redundant data that slows page load speeds.
For internal operational scenarios, style adaptation and interactive logic compatibility for embedded components must be implemented separately by the internal ticket system. FastGPT sharing links only provide a basic interactive framework.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to channel attribution and lead distribution fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then cross-check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of channel attribution and lead distribution data does not match expectations. Cross-check third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions respectively. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same channel attribution and lead distribution question-and-answer entry is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify device, browser, and local identity statuses. Record retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Successful Configuration
- Access the configured iframe embedding address, and check if the knowledge base documents loaded by the page include exclusive business content such as attribution rules and lead distribution tables.
- Initiate a test query on the embedded page, verifying that returned results include business content related to operational on-call such as channel parameter verification and duplicate lead deduplication. This type of business logic is handled by external systems or associated workflows.
- Simulate the embedding scenario of the internal ticket system, checking if the component style matches the interface style of the existing system.
- After configuring identity verification (commercial edition) or external access control policies, use an unauthorized IP or identity to access the embedding address, verifying that the page fails to load normally.
