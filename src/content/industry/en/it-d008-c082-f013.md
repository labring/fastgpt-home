# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Query for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Permission and Data Isolation

## What this category of data looks like
Data for this category primarily comes from advertising platforms, CRM systems, attribution SDKs, and form systems. Daily maintained documents include attribution rules, lead distribution tables, channel reports, and exception checklists. Data update cycles are mostly daily or weekly. Fields include channel identifiers, lead unique identifiers, assigned sales IDs, conversion times, budget allocation values, and more. Field priorities and formats vary across different documents. Attribution rules are mostly structured configuration items, while channel reports contain aggregated business metrics.

## Constraints on Permission and Data Isolation Posed by These Characteristics
Multi-source data access requires permission isolation to cover access scopes across different systems, preventing cross-business-line data leaks. Data with different update frequencies must match corresponding permission verification rhythms. Daily-updated channel reports can use fixed permission scopes. Weekly-updated attribution rules need to support temporary permission adjustments. Sensitive fields such as budget allocation values and assigned sales IDs require separate permission controls to prevent unauthorized tampering or disclosure. In operational on-call and internal ticket scenarios, access to exception checklists for specific channels must be restricted to relevant personnel only, avoiding interference from unrelated individuals with business processes. Operational permissions for duplicate lead deduplication and sales assignment confirmation must be tied to role responsibilities, preventing unauthorized operations that could lead to sales disputes.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Issue: After configuring `teamId` or `tmbId`, a test prompt error appears. Forcing continuation allows the reference to run. Cause: Permission parameters for business lines or members were not correctly bound, leading to failed initial verification but no full verification trigger during subsequent reference.
- Issue: No team invitation function is available after local deployment. Cause: The team permission module was not enabled in the FastGPT v4.17.0 configuration file, or the deployment package does not include the corresponding functional components.
- Issue: Unable to export conversation content or call search APIs. Cause: Data export and API call permissions were not configured for the corresponding role, or the permission scope does not cover the corresponding business line data.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Log in to accounts with different roles, verify that only attribution data, lead distribution tables, and channel reports for the corresponding business line are accessible.
- Trigger a test call, confirm that no permission verification errors occur, and the returned data scope matches the `teamId` or `tmbId` binding range set in the configuration.
- Attempt to access sensitive fields, confirm that unauthorized roles cannot view complete budget or assigned sales information.
- Submit an internal ticket, verify that only operational on-call personnel receive exception ticket notifications for the corresponding channels.
