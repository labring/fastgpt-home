# Operations on Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Game Operation Activity Configuration: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Game operation activity configuration-related data typically comes from operation backends, player segmentation systems, payment data platforms, and activity log systems. Daily deliverables include activity plans, reward configuration tables, launch checklists, and activity review materials. Common fields include activity ID, reward type, distribution threshold, effective time period, etc. Units include person-times, yuan, hours, etc.

Data update frequency is mostly weekly or around holidays. Final verification of core configuration content must be completed before activity launch. Changes need to synchronize associated multi-system data to avoid configuration inconsistencies.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "sharing and embedding" link
Documents and data related to activity configurations involve sensitive business content such as activity effective time periods and reward values. Sharing must strictly restrict access permissions, only open to internal operations and planning personnel to prevent sensitive information leaks.

Since data updates are weekly or around holidays, external business systems need to be connected to regularly synchronize the latest activity configuration tables and log data. This avoids incorrect Q&A results caused by using expired information.

Activity configurations have many fields and are associated with multiple systems. External business systems must be connected to accurately identify exclusive fields such as activity ID and reward type, and must not confuse the field logic of general operation tickets.

At the same time, Q&A related to exception compensation needs to be associated with real-time activity logs. External business systems must be connected to ensure the timeliness of data calls, ensuring that on-duty personnel can respond to exception queries in a timely manner.

## Three common mistakes
- Failure to open the sharing entry related to game operation activity configuration. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check the browser request status; do not replace `shareId` on your own.
- The visible scope of game operation activity configuration data does not match expectations. Verify the third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of external systems respectively; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same game operation activity configuration Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache, do not judge the service as abnormal based solely on this phenomenon.
