# Operations on-call and internal ticket Q&A for user growth and retention operations: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents for this category include growth experiment plans, user segmentation tables, outreach copy, and retention analysis reports. Data sources include user behavior logs, push systems, experiment platforms, and CRM. Data update cycles are divided into daily and weekly: user segmentation tables are updated daily, and retention analysis reports are produced weekly. Document fields include user segmentation tags, outreach conversion rates, experimental control group data, and more. Units involve person-times, items, days, and others. There is no unified fixed statistical caliber, and adjustments must be made flexibly based on operational scenarios.

## Constraints imposed by these characteristics on the Sharing and Embedding link
Data sources for this category are scattered and update cycles vary. When embedding into operations on-call or internal ticket scenarios, ensure the latest version of operational documents is loaded to avoid data lag affecting operational decisions. Daily documents have diverse formats, so embedded components must adapt to outreach copy of different lengths and structured user segmentation data to ensure complete content display. Operational errors in this category will directly affect user retention and channel budgets. Therefore, the sharing and embedding link must include permission verification capabilities to prevent unauthorized access to internal sensitive operational data. Sharing links must also restrict internal access scope to avoid data leakage.

## Three common mistakes
- Sharing entries related to user growth and retention operations fail to open. First verify the complete system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of user growth and retention operation data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same user growth and retention operation Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. In anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance, and do not judge service abnormalities solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Open the preview page of the embedded component, adjust the browser window size, and verify whether the component style adapts to the current page layout.
- Copy the login-free sharing link, access it using a browser with a non-internal domain name, and confirm that the "View Original" button is not displayed.
- Initiate a ticket Q&A on the embedded page, and check whether the background log can obtain the system-generated `shareId` parameter value.
- Wait until the actual operational document update cycle ends, and verify whether the user segmentation data loaded by the embedded component matches the corresponding updated report.
