# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Community Contributor Governance: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for community contributor governance comes from code repositories, community forums, contribution statistics, and governance meeting minutes. Daily maintained documents include contribution guidelines, code of conduct, maintainer lists, and governance meeting records. Data update frequency is monthly or quarterly. The data structure is decentralized, covering different types of content such as role permission rules, contribution process descriptions, and personnel qualification information. Fields include role hierarchy, contribution frequency, permission scope, etc., with no fixed unit requirements.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "Sharing and Embedding" workflow
The data used for community contributor governance is decentralized and updated infrequently. For the sharing and embedding workflow, external systems are required to aggregate multiple data sources and perform regular synchronization. The FastGPT sharing component only handles content display. Since the data involves sensitive content such as role permissions and governance rules, access to embedded content must have its authorization scope controlled by a third-party identity verification system (supported in the commercial edition) to prevent unauthorized personnel from accessing internal governance information. Additionally, the document structure is diverse, so retrieval rules must be configurable per document type to ensure that content returned during operational on-call and ticket Q&A accurately matches the needs of governance scenarios. Since data updates are infrequent, external systems can set cache cycles to reduce synchronization overhead. The FastGPT sharing component supports a manual refresh entry, which requires coordination with external systems.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to community contributor governance fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its validity period, then check browser request statuses; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of community contributor governance data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same community contributor governance Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first verify the device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance, so do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.
