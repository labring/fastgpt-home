# Operational On-Call and Internal Ticket Q&A for Renewal Health Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What the data for this category looks like
Renewal health management data typically comes from CRM systems, product usage data, internal ticket statistics, and contract ledgers. Update frequencies are mostly weekly or monthly. Daily produced documents include customer health reports, renewal forecast sheets, high-risk customer lists, and communication notes. Core fields cover customer identifiers, contract cycle milestones, renewal quotas, risk levels, ticket response times, and more. There are no uniform fixed units. Adaptation is required based on the document scenario.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on sharing and embedding
Data sources are scattered and update cycles are long. Shared content must align with role permission rules of external systems to restrict cross-team access to sensitive contract information. Document structures vary. Sharing and embedding requires combining the data source template capabilities of external systems, which must be implemented separately by the external system, to adapt to the display logic of different documents. FastGPT share links do not natively provide data source template binding capabilities. Content such as high-risk customer lists involves sensitive data. Embedding scenarios must combine permission rules of external systems to restrict unauthorized access, while also adapting to embedding formats of different internal systems, such as iframe embedding requirements for ticket systems. Additionally, data update frequencies are fixed. Updates to shared content are supported by the update mechanism of external data sources. Content synchronization must be implemented separately by the external system. FastGPT share links do not have built-in content synchronization functionality.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Sharing entries related to renewal health management fail to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its expiration period, then check browser request statuses. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of renewal health management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of external systems separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same renewal health management Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance; do not use this phenomenon alone to judge service anomalies.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Open the generated non-login share link, and confirm that the displayed content matches the preset renewal health management documents.
- Attempt to create multiple non-login links, and confirm that the number of created links complies with the quota rules of the corresponding plan.
- Embed the test iframe code into the target internal ticket system, and confirm that the specified renewal health management document content loads normally.
- Verify the access permissions of the share link, and confirm that only authorized internal team members can view sensitive data. This requires adaptation combined with the identity verification rules of external systems.
