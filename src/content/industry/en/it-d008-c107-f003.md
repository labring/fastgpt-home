# Operations On-Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Account Ban and Appeal Review: Sharing and Embedding

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
The data for this category typically originates from risk control systems, audit records, user behavior logs, and report evidence. Updates occur daily or in real time. Daily associated documents include account ban records, appeal submission materials, review opinion documents, and user notification templates. Common fields include account unique identifiers, ban trigger rules, appeal reasons, audit results, and disposal timeliness details. Most data uses character-based identifiers and timestamp formats, with no unified quantitative statistical unit.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the Sharing and Embedding Workflow
Due to scattered data sources and high update frequency, the embedding workflow requires external business systems to implement logic for real-time pulling of latest risk control and audit data. The FastGPT sharing component only renders passed structured business data to avoid displaying expired content.
Associated document types are diverse, so external business systems must complete format conversion for multi-format data sources. The FastGPT sharing component only renders passed structured business data to ensure correct content association.
Account-specific focused display requires external business systems to filter data. The FastGPT sharing component only renders passed structured content to avoid mixing non-target category information.
Risks of errors such as false bans or returned violated accounts must be mitigated by configuring strict access permissions in the external access control system, limiting access to embedded content of the FastGPT sharing component only to internal operations and customer service teams.
Additionally, compliant sharing entrance configuration must be used to ensure embedded content only links to business data of this category, avoiding confusion with data sources from other scenarios.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entrance related to account ban and appeal review fails to open. First verify the full system-generated link and its expiration period, then check browser request status; do not manually replace `shareId`.
- Visibility range of account ban and appeal review data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and external system data permissions separately; the sharing entrance identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same account ban and appeal review Q&A is not visible on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check device, browser, and local identity status; record retention is not guaranteed across devices or after cache clearance, so this phenomenon alone cannot be used to judge service anomalies.
