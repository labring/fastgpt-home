# Operations Duty and Internal Ticket Q&A for Identity Account and Permission Management: Sharing and Embedding

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data primarily comes from enterprise IAM systems, HR systems, business system logs, and approval systems. Daily generated documents include account application forms, permission matrices, offboarding recovery forms, and compliance audit reports. Fields cover account ID, permission scope, approval status, effective time, and more. Data update frequency is either daily synchronization or real-time updates triggered by personnel onboarding, offboarding, position changes, and transfers. The size of individual documents ranges from dozens to hundreds of lines. While core fields have no unified format, all include permission association relationships and subject identification information.

## How Do These Characteristics Create Constraints for the Sharing and Embedding Link
This category of data contains sensitive permission information and has scattered sources. When embedding into operations duty and ticket systems, strict alignment with the enterprise’s internal identity system is required to prevent unauthorized access. Data update frequency is high, so the cache duration of shared links must match the data synchronization rhythm set by the external system. This prevents the display of expired permission matrices or offboarded account information. The size of individual documents varies widely, so embedding must support loading content of different lengths. External business systems must also support filtering shareable document fragments by permission, to ensure only authorized users can view corresponding content. Additionally, account permission content involved in ticket Q&A must link to internal approval processes. The embedding workflow must rely on external systems to retain necessary fields for operation traceability.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Sharing entries related to identity account and permission management fail to open. First verify the complete link generated by the system and its validity period, then check browser request status. Do not manually replace `shareId`.
- The visible scope of identity account and permission management data does not match expectations. Verify third-party identity verification results, workflow request parameters, and data permissions of the external system separately. The sharing entry identifier itself does not provide business authorization.
- The same identity account and permission management Q&A cannot be viewed on another device. For anonymous scenarios, first check the device, browser, and local identity status. Record retention cannot be guaranteed across devices or after clearing cache; do not judge service anomalies solely based on this phenomenon.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- After generating a commercial version shared link for a login-free window, use an unauthorized third-party identity to access, and verify that embedded content cannot be viewed.
- Modify permission data in the IAM system, wait for external data synchronization and cache refresh, and verify that the content displayed on the embedded page has been updated.
- Embed permission documents of different sizes, and verify that the external embedded page loads without lag or content truncation.
- Test the validity period of the login-free window shared link, and confirm that the link cannot be accessed normally after expiration.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
