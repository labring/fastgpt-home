# Drafting Requirements and Design Documents for Personal Information Protection Compliance: Forms and Interactions

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data for this category primarily comes from business systems, App permission lists, privacy policies, and user request records. It is maintained by privacy compliance teams, security consulting, or legal departments. Four core documents are regularly produced: personal information inventories, processing rules, impact assessment reports, and user authorization texts. Data is updated quarterly or whenever a product version changes. Core fields include information processing scenarios, legal bases, data subject scope, storage periods, and more. These must be updated alongside business adjustments to prevent gaps between compliance documents and actual operations.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Dispersed data sources requiring cross-system integration mean external business system forms must support bulk import of multi-source fields or associated references. Frequent document updates with version traceability requirements mean external business system interaction flows must retain edit histories and version markers. Fields involve natural person information, so legal compliance validation must be added to the input stage of external business systems. Scenario sorting and compliance confirmation take significant time, so external business system forms must provide preset options and bulk filling functions. Severe consequences of errors mean a compliance pre-check stage must be added before submission in external business systems to proactively avoid compliance risks.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: No compliance verification feedback appears on the first form submission, with error prompts only displaying after multiple submissions. Verification steps: Check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes have configured real-time verification trigger logic for the `formInput` node, and confirm whether verification logic loads before submission.
- Phenomenon: A large number of empty fields appear in bulk-imported personal information inventories. Verification steps: Check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes have configured required field verification rules for import templates, and confirm whether cross-system exported data fields are fully verified and blocked.
- Phenomenon: Form submission returns an error stating "No valid compliance basis matched". Verification steps: Check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes have configured preset option verification logic for the `userSelect` node, and confirm whether submitted content has passed preset option verification.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a test document containing complete compliance fields, and check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes automatically verify required items and prompt abnormalities.
- Select preset legal basis options, and confirm whether external business systems/subsequent nodes correctly associate corresponding compliance logic.
- Edit the test document, and check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes retain edit traces.
- Attempt to import a file that does not match the template format, and check whether external business systems/subsequent nodes block the import and provide clear prompts.
