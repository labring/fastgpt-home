# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Crowdsourced Annotator Management: Forms and Interactions

## What Data This Category Includes
Data sources for this category typically include personnel registration information from crowdsourcing platforms, annotation task completion quality statistics, internal training exam results, and settlement records from financial systems.
Two update cadences apply: basic personnel information is synchronized daily. Performance statistics and settlement data are updated monthly.
Commonly used documents include personnel access rules, training and assessment materials, performance statistics tables, and settlement lists.
Corresponding fields cover personnel qualifications, exam scores, task completion volume, settlement amounts, and other relevant items.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Cross-source data association requires linking fields across external business systems. This avoids manual duplicate entry and errors.
Differences in update frequencies require sync logic to separate daily and scheduled tasks. This aligns with daily basic personnel information updates and monthly performance and settlement data updates.
Diverse document fields require forms with flexible field types.
Validation rules must be implemented by external business systems or downstream nodes. These rules cover qualification review, performance statistics, and settlement verification scenarios.
Error consequences directly affect project quality and financial accuracy. Mandatory validation and error prompt mechanisms for business fields must be implemented by external business systems or downstream nodes. This reduces the risk of errors and omissions.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Information collected by `formInput` is not correctly passed to downstream nodes. Previously associated annotation task information does not take effect. Cause: Cross-system field linking logic is not implemented by the external business system.
- Select each configured option in the user selection node `userSelect`. Verify whether the workflow enters the corresponding branch. For filtering crowdsourced annotator management records, additionally verify the external interface's input, permissions, and return values.
- Phenomenon: Submitting a form with empty financial fields does not trigger a prompt. This results in blank amount entries in settlement lists. Cause: Financial field validation rules are not implemented by the external business system.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Complete
- Test information passing for the `formInput` node. After entering a person ID, confirm that the collected input information can be passed to downstream nodes. Cross-system field linking logic is implemented by the external business system.
- Select each configured option in the user selection node `userSelect`. Verify whether the workflow enters the corresponding branch. For filtering crowdsourced annotator management records, additionally verify the external interface's input, permissions, and return values.
- Submit a test form with empty required fields. Check whether validation prompts for the corresponding fields are displayed.
- View external system synchronization logs. Confirm that daily and monthly synchronization tasks execute normally at the preset times.
