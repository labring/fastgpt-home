# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Supplier and Equipment Arrival Management: Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category typically comes from ERP systems, supplier shipping invoices, logistics tracking information, and device manufacturer backends. Daily associated documents include purchase orders, arrival acceptance slips, serial number lists, and invoice materials. Updates follow per-order or per-week cycles. Core fields include supplier information, device model, serial number, arrival date, and acceptance status. Common units are physical measurement units such as sets, pieces, and individual units. This data is generated for specific integrated project deliveries, and differs from general enterprise IT procurement data. It must be bound to project numbers and delivery milestones.

## Constraints on Forms and Interactions From These Characteristics
Data sources are scattered and tied to project deliveries. External business systems must implement logic to automatically populate fields such as supplier and device model information by linking external data sources. FastGPT’s `formInput` node only collects input information to reduce manual entry errors. The per-order or per-week update cycle requires each form instance to use a unique project number, with independent data storage to avoid confusion. For core steps such as model verification and serial number registration, external business systems or subsequent workflow nodes must set strong field validation rules to match serial number formats from device manufacturer backends. External business systems or subsequent nodes must also configure form editing permissions, only granting access to integrators and warehouse administrators for corresponding projects to prevent unauthorized modification of core data.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Select configured options one by one in the `userSelect` node, and verify that the workflow enters the corresponding branch. Filtering supplier and equipment arrival management records requires additional verification of the external interface’s input, permissions, and return values.
- Symptom: A field empty error appears after form submission, even though all fields were filled out on the interface. Verification steps: Check if the external business system has configured pre-submission validation logic for `formInput` fields, and verify if browser cache affects submitted input content.
- Symptom: Some entries are rejected when importing a serial number list. Verification steps: Check if the external business system has configured serial number format validation rules matching device manufacturers, and verify the entry format of the imported list.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Manually enter a serial number formatted as 15 to 20 characters, and check if the external business system or subsequent nodes can correctly verify the serial number format.
- Open the `userSelect` supplier drop-down list, and confirm that preset partner entries are displayed.
- Attempt to leave required fields blank, and check if the external business system or subsequent nodes correctly lock the submit button.
- Import a test serial number list generated by an external business system, and confirm that the external business system or subsequent nodes can correctly verify and pass entries that meet the format requirements.
