# Drafting Requirements and Design Documents for Delivery Effect Optimization: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for delivery effect optimization comes from advertising platform backends, attribution systems, CRM lead databases, and landing page monitoring tools. Updates run on a daily or real-time schedule. Daily produced documents include daily delivery reports, temporary price adjustment records, material A/B test sheets, and optimization review reports. Common fields include impressions, clicks, conversion cost, and budget balance. Units include yuan, occurrences, and cost per thousand impressions (CPM). The typical length of a single core record ranges from 800 to 1200 characters.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on forms and interactions
Real-time data requirements mean forms must support fast retrieval of latest metrics, to avoid filling outdated information. Fixed document field structures require forms to pre-configure frequently used delivery optimization fields, to reduce manual input work. Frequent operations for troubleshooting abnormal metrics and adjusting budgets require forms to have simple interactions and clear validation logic. This prevents invalid submissions that increase customer acquisition costs. Cross-link reuse business needs mean form nodes must support calls from other business links, to adapt to multi-scenario document drafting workflows.

## Three common misconfigurations
- Attempting to connect nodes from other business links to form nodes results in failed jumps. The cause is that cross-link call permissions are not configured. This permission is managed by external business systems. FastGPT form nodes only support calls from nodes within the current workflow link by default.
- Subsequent nodes do not receive complete fields after a delivery effect optimization related form is submitted. Compare required fields, actual submission content, and node output mappings one by one, then check subsequent interface returns. Existing symptoms alone cannot determine the root cause.
- An exception occurs when form data is passed to subsequent nodes. The cause is that the data passing logic is not configured correctly. This logic is implemented by external business systems or subsequent nodes.

## How to confirm configurations are complete
- Verify that the pre-configured form fields match the core fields of daily optimization documents.
- Submit a test form with invalid numerical values, confirm that error prompts accurately match input exception types.
- Attempt to jump to the current form node via external business link management, verify that cross-link call configurations take effect.
- View the data results loaded by the form retrieved via external interfaces, confirm that the data update frequency meets business requirements.
