# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Channel Attribution and Lead Distribution: Forms and Interactions

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Core users of this category include growth operations specialists, sales operations specialists, and data analysts. Data is primarily sourced from advertising platforms, customer relationship management systems, attribution SDKs, and front-end form systems. Daily deliverable documents include attribution rule configuration tables, lead distribution tables, channel effect reports, and abnormal lead checklists. Data updates run on a daily or weekly cycle. Common fields include channel identifiers, lead IDs, conversion timestamps, affiliated sales IDs, budget allocation amounts, and more. There are no unified fixed units; matching must align with specific business scenarios.

## Constraints Imposed on Forms and Interactions
Dispersed data sources require forms to support mapping configuration for fields across multiple data sources. Forms must use field mapping templates for common data sources such as advertising platforms and CRMs, provided by external business systems, to adapt to field formats from different sources. Complex document structures require forms to display modules like attribution rules and lead allocation rules in separate sections, to prevent mixing fields from different business scenarios. Differences in update frequencies require forms to integrate with external systems to implement timed data synchronization logic. A temporary editing entry linked to an external abnormal check system must be reserved to support high-frequency abnormal processing needs. No unified unit requirements apply to fields. Forms can work with external systems or downstream nodes to support user-defined input or selection options for field units, to fit statistical logic for different businesses.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: The `formInput` node is not bound to the field mapping of the corresponding data source, resulting in empty data after submission. Cause: The preset data source template provided by the external system is not enabled, and the channel identifier field of the advertising platform is missed during manual configuration.
- Phenomenon: The lead deduplication rule configured in the external business system returns more results than expected after triggering. Cause: No matching threshold is set for deduplication fields, resulting in overly loose duplicate lead judgment logic.
- Phenomenon: Data update delay exceeds the preset duration after form submission, triggering an abnormal alert. Cause: Timed synchronization logic with external systems is not configured based on actual business scenarios, and real-time pulling is relied on, resulting in interface timeout.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Open the form configuration page of FastGPT v4.17.0, check that the field type of `formInput` matches the field format of the corresponding data source.
- Test the drop-down list of the `userSelect` node, confirm that it only contains valid personnel IDs of the current business team passed in by the external system.
- Submit a test data entry, check whether the data is passed to subsequent nodes or external business systems.
- Adjust the timed synchronization configuration linked to the external system, wait for the corresponding duration, and check whether the data has completed updating.
