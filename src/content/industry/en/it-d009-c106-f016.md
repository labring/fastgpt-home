# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Speech Annotation and Transcription: Forms and Interactions

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Core data for speech annotation and transcription comes from audio files, annotation platforms, proprietary vocabularies, and spot-check samples. Daily produced documents include transcription specifications, audio lists, quality inspection reports, and speaker annotation forms.
Data updates are typically completed daily or per batch. The most time-consuming steps focus on noise segment judgment, speaker differentiation, and proprietary vocabulary proofreading.
The core difference between this category and text annotation projects is that audio content must first be converted to structured text before subsequent annotation processes can begin. Errors can lead to recognition training deviations, transcription rework, or delivery delays.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Forms and Interactions
Bulk audio file sources require external business systems or subsequent workflows to support bulk upload and path mapping configurations. This reduces inefficient manual single-file entry. The form only collects input.
The fixed structure of proprietary vocabularies and speaker annotation forms requires external business systems or subsequent workflows to implement field matching and import verification functions. This ensures consistency between annotation rules and data formats. The form only collects input.
Daily or per-batch update frequencies require external business systems or subsequent workflows to support incremental trigger and batch processing configurations. This adapts to the team’s daily work rhythm. The form only collects input.
High-time-consuming steps such as noise segment judgment and speaker differentiation require external business systems or subsequent workflows to provide fast interaction components. This simplifies repetitive operations. The form only collects input.
The prerequisite requirement of structured text requires external business systems or subsequent workflows to implement format conversion verification functions. This ensures consistency between input and output. The form only collects input.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Form input node content passing failure: Collected input content is not sent to subsequent nodes after submission. The cause is that the input passing rule for the `formInput` node was not correctly configured in the workflow, and this rule must be handled by subsequent nodes.
- Partial files not recognized during bulk upload: Some audio files are not automatically loaded by external business systems or subsequent workflows after uploading the audio list. The cause is that the naming format of audio files does not match the field rules preset by the external business system or subsequent workflow.
- Select configured options one by one in the user selection node `userSelect`, verify whether the workflow enters the corresponding branch; filtering of speech annotation and transcription records should additionally verify the input, permissions, and return values of external interfaces.
