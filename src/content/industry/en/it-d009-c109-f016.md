# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Identity Account and Permission Management: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
This category of data originates primarily from enterprise IAM systems, HR systems, business system logs, and approval systems. Update cycles follow daily synchronization or real-time updates triggered by personnel onboarding, offboarding, transfers, or role changes. Standard daily deliverables include account application forms, permission matrices, offboarding account recovery forms, and audit reports. Fields covered include employee ID numbers, affiliated departments, applied permission scopes, approval statuses, account lifecycle stages, and more. All data must align strictly with internal enterprise personnel and business system ledgers, with no additional custom extended fields.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Dispersed data sources and lack of unified update trigger nodes require integration with external business systems to automatically pull and validate multi-source data, eliminating manual entry errors. Document fields are fixed and must align with enterprise ledgers, so forms must pre-configure standard fields and limit custom field counts. Update frequencies are triggered by personnel changes, so the interaction flow must integrate with external systems to deliver real-time status feedback. Form access must be restricted to enterprise-authorized personnel per internal employee requirements, and integration with external account lifecycle systems to trigger corresponding actions is necessary to prevent unauthorized access or residual offboarding accounts.

## Three common mistakes
- Scenario: After form submission, the prompt "Username invalid" appears, and account application cannot be completed. Verification steps: Check whether integration logic with the enterprise HR system’s employee ledger has been configured, and confirm whether the entered employee ID matches valid employee data in the HR system.
- Scenario: Permission configuration is not synchronized to the IAM system after form submission. Verification steps: Check whether the automatic synchronization interface between the form and the IAM system has been configured, and confirm whether the submitted form data is passed to subsequent nodes to trigger the permission change process.
- Scenario: The offboarding account recovery form does not automatically mark offboarding employee accounts. Verification steps: Check whether the offboarding data update trigger mechanism from the HR system has been bound, and confirm whether the form automatically obtains offboarding employee status data from the HR system.
