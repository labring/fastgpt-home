# Requirements and Design Document Drafting for Requirement Analysis and PRD Management: Forms and Interactions

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data typically comes from daily deliverables of internet product teams, business analysts, and product managers. Deliverables include requirement research notes, PRD documents, user story lists, and requirement change forms.
Data sources cover customer interview records, internal ticketing systems, public competitor pages, and analytics dashboards.
Update frequency follows weekly or per-version release cycles.
Core fields include business objectives, acceptance criteria, requirement boundaries, and change records.
No uniform mandatory units exist; adjust based on specific projects.

## Constraints Imposed on the Forms and Interactions Link
Daily documents use a multi-field structure, so forms must support multi-module grouping to adapt to different requirement item types.
Weekly or per-version updates require forms to support quick reuse of historical configurations and incremental editing.
Interview information classification and boundary condition confirmation are time-consuming, so external business systems must provide preset fields and validation logic to reduce manual organization costs.
High-frequency requirement change synchronization requires external business systems to support version traceability and change marking.
Acceptance-related fields need clear input specifications to avoid ambiguity that causes subsequent development rework and schedule delays.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: When configuring knowledge base association fields, no corresponding document content is returned after dynamically passing variables. Verification step: Check whether the requirement category selection field is bound to the external system’s association logic, and confirm that the variable mapping relationship is correct.
- Phenomenon: A field empty error prompt appears after form submission. Verification step: Check whether the external business system has configured required validation logic for the corresponding field, and confirm that core fields are set as required items.
- Phenomenon: Form loading timeout for requirement change forms. Verification step: Check the single entry limit value configured for the current form, confirm that it does not exceed the reasonable range of the test example, and verify the external system’s form rendering time data.

## How to Confirm Configuration Is Complete
- Access the form editing page, verify that the `formInput` input length limit configured by the external business system aligns with the field requirements of the business document.
- Activate the `userSelect` drop-down menu, confirm that preset project options match the current project’s associated categories.
- Submit a test requirement item, check that the change marking and historical version management functions configured by the external system display normally.
- Simulate multi-entry submission, confirm that the external system’s form loading speed meets daily use fluency standards.
