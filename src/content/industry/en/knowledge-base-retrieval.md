# Knowledge Base Retrieval for Financial Services

## Organize the materials

Financial teams often handle policies, contracts, reports, and audit records together. Organize them by business object and update cadence, then keep source and version details with each material for later review.

## Validate the parameters

Start with chunk size, recall count, and similarity threshold settings. Validate recall coverage and citation completeness against real questions, then tune the settings from the observed results.
