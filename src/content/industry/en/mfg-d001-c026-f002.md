# Supplier and Process Quality Audit for Non-Ferrous Metal Smelting and Electrolysis: Vector Models and Indexes
## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Typical data for this category comes from smelting, electrolysis, laboratory testing, and scheduling departments of copper, aluminum, lead-zinc, and other smelting enterprises. Daily documents include batching lists, smelting records, electrolytic cell status records, laboratory reports, anode and cathode quality records, and environmental protection ledgers. Data sources cover concentrate and auxiliary material analysis, DCS systems, cell voltage and current monitoring, laboratory testing, and energy and environmental monitoring modules. Update frequency adjusts in real time based on material batches, furnace heats, or cell status. Each individual data entry contains fields such as composition parameters, operating condition values, and test results. Some fields use industry-specific units.

## How These Characteristics Impact Vector Models and Index Workflows
This category’s data includes multi-source heterogeneous text and numeric records. Vector models must adapt to semantic extraction of industrial specialized terminology and unique units. High-frequency real-time updates impose synchronization pressure on incremental indexing. Indexing logic must support streaming writes. Individual documents have strong field correlations. For example, batching lists and smelting records have strong business correlations. Vector indexes must retain field-level semantic associations to avoid losing business context after splitting. Indexes for compliance documents such as environmental protection ledgers must retain the mapping between original data and vectors to meet traceability requirements. Additionally, data update frequency adjusts in real time based on batches, furnace heats, and cell status. Index sharding strategies must adapt to dynamic changes in data volume.

## How to Set Configurations
The following configurations are adapted for FastGPT v4.17.0:

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the average length of documents such as batching lists and cell status records, and avoids splitting that damages business semantics |
| Overlap Length | 100–200 characters | Retains business correlations between adjacent chunks, and prevents semantic breaks across chunks |
| `defaultToken` | 4096 | Adapts to the long-text processing needs of industrial documents, and covers the semantic scope of complete single-batch records |
| Similarity Threshold | Calibrated based on actual testing | Due to the large number of specialized terms, adjust based on actual business scenarios to avoid false matches or missed matches |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Balances recall efficiency and business coverage, and adapts to the associated retrieval needs of multi-source data |
| `maxToken` | 8192 | Supports splicing of recall results with long context, and meets the audit and analysis needs of complex operating conditions |

## Three Common Mistakes
- The interface displays vector similarity values exceeding 10000, which falls outside the standard 0-1 range. This occurs when similarity normalization logic is not configured correctly, or when an unadapted embedding model output format is used.
- The knowledge base remains in the "indexing" state for a long time after importing a dataset, with no progress updates. This happens when the index batch write threshold is not adjusted for this category’s high-frequency real-time data, leading to backlog in the write queue.
- An embedding model call error occurs, prompting that no available channels are present. This occurs when an embedding model channel adapted to industrial specialized semantics is not configured, or when call permissions for the corresponding model are not enabled.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Upload a single typical document, and verify that the chunking result retains business field integrity, with no forced truncation of key parameters.
- Enter supplier qualification or operating condition questions, and verify that the similarity value range of recall results meets business expectations, with no abnormally high values.
- Submit a real-time updated dataset, and verify that indexing progress adjusts dynamically with data volume, with no long-term stagnation.
- Call the embedding model interface, and verify that the returned vector format meets platform requirements, with no format errors or missing fields.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
