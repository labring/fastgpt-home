# Supplier and Process Quality Audit for Electric Motor and Generator Manufacturing: Citation Sources and Traceability

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
The core data for this category originates from winding, insulation, final assembly, and testing departments of electric motor factories and power equipment enterprises. Daily documents include winding process cards, insulation treatment records, dynamic balance reports, voltage withstand insulation test results, and type and factory inspection test reports. Data sources cover electromagnetic design data, copper wire and insulating material batches, winding equipment, balancing machines, electrical test benches, and ERP/MES systems. Updates trigger per individual product, per production batch, or each time a type modification occurs. Document fields include winding turns, insulation thickness, dynamic balance accuracy grade, voltage withstand test values, and more. Corresponding units follow professional measurement standards such as turns, millimeters, grams, volts, and others.

## Constraints Imposed by These Characteristics on Citation Sources and Traceability
The requirement to update data per individual unit or batch means traceability must bind unique identifiers such as batch numbers and equipment numbers. This prevents mixing documents across production batches. Field differences across multiple types of professional documents require precise matching of data from categories including processes, tests, and materials during citation. This stops semantic misassociation. The high-frequency update requirement means the traceability system must associate the latest document versions in real time. This avoids use of expired test or process data. Additionally, this category falls under constraints from electromagnetic, insulation, and rotating machinery balance requirements. Citation sources must cover all three document types, and cannot rely solely on a single type of test or process record.

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant cited content not present in the local knowledge base appears in conversations. Cause: The similarity threshold is not configured correctly, leading to recall of low-relevance documents, or no mixed retrieval filtering rules are set.
- Phenomenon: Cited content is excessively truncated, making complete winding turns or voltage withstand test data unviewable. Cause: `quoteMaxToken` is set too low, exceeding the content length of a single professional document, leading to truncation of core audit parameters.
- Phenomenon: Unable to accurately recall corresponding insulation treatment records via batch numbers. Cause: Batch numbers from ERP/MES systems are not bound as metadata to knowledge base chunks, making it impossible to match batch query conditions during traceability.

## How to Verify Correct Configuration
- Upload one winding process card and one voltage withstand test report, initiate a query about the insulation status of a motor with a specified batch number, and check whether the returned citations include the uploaded documents.
- View the citation display area in the conversation interface, confirm that the number of displayed citations matches the set number of displayed citations, and that content is not excessively truncated.
- Check the citation metadata to confirm that core audit fields such as batch numbers, equipment numbers, and test times are included.
- Initiate an unrelated general query such as office equipment procurement needs, and confirm that no motor-related knowledge base citations are returned.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
