# Process Document and Abnormal Report Drafting for Safety, Environmental Protection and Special Operations Management: Vector Models and Indexes

## What data looks like for this category
Data for this category comes from daily work outputs of manufacturing enterprise EHS departments and maintenance teams, plus external regulatory channels. It includes risk identification lists, work permits for hot work, confined space work, lifting work and other operations, online environmental monitoring records, gas detector readings, personnel qualification documents, equipment isolation lists, hazardous waste ledgers, emission monitoring data, accident and hidden danger rectification reports, and more.
Data updates occur immediately when a single job is completed. Online monitoring data is updated in real time or per regulatory required cycles. Qualification and ledger data is updated on regular inspection schedules. Each document contains both structured and unstructured fields, including job time, job type, guardian information, monitoring values, rectification closure status, and similar details.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexes
The heterogeneous nature of this category’s data, differences in update rhythms, and high confidence requirements create multiple constraints for the vector model and index link.
Multi-source heterogeneous data includes structured qualification fields, real-time monitoring values, and unstructured job text. Indexes must support vector encoding adaptation for mixed fields.
Single job documents have large content spans. Chunking strategies must balance semantic integrity and retrieval efficiency.
Mixed real-time and scheduled update rhythms require indexes to support flexible configuration of incremental and full updates.
Work permit documents are directly linked to safety and compliance decisions. Vector recall accuracy must meet the high requirements of business veto power, avoiding irrelevant content interfering with risk judgments.

## How to set configurations
These configurations are based on the FastGPT v4.17.0 interface and parameter logic.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapt to the semantic integrity of long texts such as work permits and rectification reports, avoid splitting risk identification logic across chunks |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Compensate for semantic gaps after long text chunking, ensure continuity of cross-chunk risk descriptions |
| `maxToken` | 4000–6000 token | Carry context information from multiple work permits and monitoring records, meet the complete basis requirements for abnormal report drafting |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Match the high confidence requirements of work permit documents, filter low-relevance non-compliant content |
| Single Search Limit | Top 8 results | Balance the comprehensiveness of retrieval recall and the efficiency of result processing, avoid excessive redundant data interfering with decision-making |
| Reranking | Enable private reranking model | Adapt to the mixed retrieval scenario of real-time monitoring data and job text in this category, improve accuracy |

The `defaultToken` parameter can be used to adjust the base vector encoding length of a single document, adapting to different types of unstructured text.

## Three common configuration errors
### Phenomenon 1
Retrieval returns a large number of historical monitoring data unrelated to the current job, and the number of results exceeds expectations.
Cause: The single search limit is set too high, and retrieval conditions are not filtered based on the job scope of this category.

### Phenomenon 2
After the incremental update task is triggered, the index progress stalls, and the log shows a `maxToken` over-limit error.
Cause: A reasonable value for the `maxToken` parameter is not configured, and the total token count of long text after chunking exceeds system limits.

### Phenomenon 3
Core content of risk identification is not recalled, and key compliance basis is missing when drafting abnormal reports.
Cause: The similarity threshold is set too high, filtering relevant documents that meet business confidence requirements.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Import a single typical work permit document, check the semantic integrity after chunking, confirm that no key risk descriptions are split.
- Trigger an incremental update task, verify that the index update time matches expectations, confirm that no long-term stall occurs.
- Enter a query related to job risk identification, check the relevance of retrieval results, adjust the similarity threshold to meet business confidence requirements.
- After enabling the reranking model, compare the sorting of retrieval results before and after enabling, confirm that the reranking logic adapts to the heterogeneous data scenario of this category.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
