# Process Quality and Finished Product Release: Drafting Process Documents and Exception Reports - Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for this category originates from manufacturing enterprise MES systems, online inspection equipment, laboratory systems, process parameter libraries, customer standards, and historical defect libraries. Daily documents include control plans, patrol inspection records, SPC reports, finished product inspection reports, nonconforming product review forms, release forms, and quality monthly reports. Data is updated per production batch and shift, or real-time parameters are collected continuously. Document fields include process tolerances, inspection frequencies, nonconforming product types, batch numbers, inspection times, and additional fields. Units include ppm, seconds, piece counts, and others, adjusted based on document type.

## Constraints Imposed on Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall
Data for this category is scattered and has varied update rhythms. Retrieval systems must support multi-data source incremental synchronization and batch-associated indexing logic. Document types are diverse, including standardized control plans and unstructured patrol inspection records. Different chunking rules must be applied to preserve field relevance. Real-time collected process parameters and inspection data require retrieval latency to align with production tempo, to avoid delaying release review efficiency. Historical defect libraries and customer standards must be bound to production permissions. Only personnel in eligible positions may retrieve data within authorized scope, to ensure compliance.

## Configuration Settings
These configurations are based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75–0.85 | Matches the professional term precision of process quality documents, avoids low-relevance results being included |
| `limit` | Top 8 entries | Balances retrieval recall volume and context window usage, fits the fast decision-making needs of release reviews |
| `searchMode` | `embedding` | Adapts to semantic matching requirements for unstructured text such as SPC reports and defect records |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Optimizes sorting accuracy for multiple results, reduces misjudgments in nonconforming product type identification |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Supplementary associative retrieval for process parameters and customer standards, covers cross-document quality judgment basis |
| `collectionIds` | Divided by production workshop/product line | Isolates quality data for different batches, ensures retrieval scope meets permission requirements |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Phenomenon: Retrieval return results have `similarity` scores generally close to 1, and have low relevance to actual quality issues. Cause: The `similarity` threshold was not adjusted for manufacturing quality document professional terms, or key field information such as process tolerances and defect types was lost during chunking.
- Phenomenon: Retrieval recall results are completely unrelated to the query description. For example, querying finished product release rules returns incoming material inspection records. Cause: `collectionIds` were not configured to divide datasets by product line or workshop, leading to retrieval scope covering unauthorized business data.
- Phenomenon: An English prompt is entered to retrieve an English knowledge base, but the final model output is in Chinese. Cause: The dataset configuration did not specify retrieval of only documents in the corresponding language, or the language consistency rule between retrieval and large model output was not bound.

## How to Verify Successful Configuration
- Review retrieval logs to confirm that the `collectionIds` of recalled results match the current workshop/product line being operated on.
- Test different types of quality issues to verify the rationality of result sorting after `usingReRank` is enabled.
- Check the `similarity` score distribution of retrieval results, confirm the score interval meets the precision requirements of the business scenario.
- Test cross-document retrieval to verify whether `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` can recall associated process parameters and customer standard content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
