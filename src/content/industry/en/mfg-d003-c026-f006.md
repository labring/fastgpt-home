# Process Specification and Technical Standard Q&A for Non-Ferrous Metal Smelting and Electrolysis: Citation Source and Traceability

## What this category of data consists of
This category of data primarily comes from smelting, electrolysis, laboratory testing, and scheduling departments of copper, aluminum, lead-zinc, and other smelting enterprises. Daily documents include batching lists, smelting records, electrolytic cell condition records, laboratory reports, anode and cathode quality records, and environmental protection ledgers. Data sources cover concentrate and auxiliary material analysis, DCS systems, cell voltage and current monitoring, laboratory testing, energy and environmental protection monitoring equipment. Update frequency changes in real time with material batches, furnace heats, and cell conditions. Individual documents typically include fields such as component proportions, process parameters, and test values, with units mostly being percentage, ampere, volt, ton, and similar units.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the citation source and traceability link?
Documents updated in real time or at high frequency require the traceability chain to quickly associate the latest production data, and avoid citing expired cell condition or smelting records. Multi-source heterogeneous data sources require the traceability system to distinguish the collection nodes and update times of different data, ensuring the timeliness of cited content. The high field complexity of individual documents requires accurate matching of parameters corresponding to the process link during traceability, to avoid confusing component proportions of different material batches. The binding of environmental protection ledgers and production data requires traceability to cover compliance records, to meet subsequent verification needs.

## Three common configuration errors
- Symptom: When `quoteMaxToken` is set above 3000, the large model does not receive context data. Cause: This configuration exceeds the platform's default context carrying threshold, triggering the truncation logic which causes the context to not be delivered.
- Symptom: Directly passing HTTP response data as a knowledge base citation, and the result does not take effect. Cause: The response data was not converted to the structured format required by the platform, so it cannot be indexed and recalled by the knowledge base.
- Symptom: After configuring the global knowledge base, the workflow does not call the specified knowledge base content. Cause: The target knowledge base ID was not passed in the workflow node, and the system uses the default knowledge base that is not bound to the business scenario by default.
