# Q&A on Process Procedures and Technical Standards for Mass Production of Automotive Parts: Source Citation and Traceability

## What This Category's Data Looks Like
Data for this category originates from the full production processes of automotive parts suppliers focused on braking, steering, chassis, and other components. Daily documents include control plans, process flow diagrams, work instructions, first-piece and patrol inspection records, SPC reports, PPAP materials, and 8D reports.
Data sources cover customer drawings and special characteristic lists, ERP/MES systems, measuring tool data, equipment process parameters, and supplier quality certificates. Updates occur per production batch, per shift patrol inspection, and via continuous collection.
Most document structures include clear fields such as process parameters, quality thresholds, and exception handling workflows. Units primarily use industry-standard measurements like millimeters and newton-meters.

## Constraints for Source Citation and Traceability
Data for this category is scattered across multiple production and management systems. When performing citation and traceability, unique identifiers from different data sources such as customer drawings, equipment parameters, and patrol inspection records must be linked to ensure sources can be mapped to specific production batches.
Frequently updated production data requires the traceability mechanism to support real-time synchronization, to avoid citing expired process parameters or exception handling records.
Professional process fields included in documents require precise matching of processes to corresponding document entries, to prevent parameter confusion.
Additionally, subject to the quality system requirements of vehicle customers, cited special characteristics and change records must be traceable back to original customer requirements, to ensure traceability results meet compliance standards.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When setting the number of cited entries, intermediate values between 100 and 900 cannot be selected, only fixed preset options are available. Cause: The traceability configuration module in FastGPT v4.17.0 does not offer an input box for custom intermediate values, only preset options are provided.
- Phenomenon: It is impossible to accurately estimate knowledge base storage costs or predict the occupied capacity of process documents. Cause: Total storage volume is not calculated based on the average character count and chunking rules for multiple types of documents in this category, and differences in long documents such as PPAP and 8D reports are ignored.
- Phenomenon: An error occurs when attempting to cite production data returned by an HTTP interface as a traceability source, and content association cannot be completed. Cause: Authorization and format parsing rules for the HTTP data source are not configured, so the system cannot identify valid fields in the returned content.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Check the traceability source list at the bottom of responses, confirm that core document entries for this category such as control plans and SPC reports are included, and verify that entries match the process of the current query.
- Trigger the real-time synchronization function, confirm that the latest patrol inspection records or equipment parameters can be retrieved within the preset time period, and adjust synchronization frequency according to production rhythm.
- Export the associated fields of traceability data, confirm that identifiers required for compliance such as customer drawing numbers and production batch numbers are included, and that field completeness meets the quality system requirements of vehicle customers.
- Adjust the `detail` parameter setting, verify that the cited sources of process parameters in responses fully match the document content.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
