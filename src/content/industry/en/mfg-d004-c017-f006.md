# Cross-Review of Technical Documentation for Coating and Painting: Source Citation and Traceability

## What the Data for This Category Looks Like
This category’s data covers the full workflow of coating lines for automotive, home appliance, construction machinery, metal furniture and other sectors. It forms organic or powder coatings to achieve protective and aesthetic effects, differing from bath solution data dependencies of processes such as electroplating. Data sources include coating supplier TDS/SDS, real-time collection data from spray booths and ovens, colorimeter and film thickness gauge test results, environmental monitoring records, and MES system data. Updates trigger with each batch of coating, each production shift, and continuous collection. Daily documentation includes coating process cards, coating batch sheets, paint mixing records, film thickness and color difference reports, oven curves, defect repair sheets, and VOCs logs. Fields include batch number, viscosity value, film thickness value, oven temperature range and others. Some documents require matching the time windows of pre-treatment, spraying, and curing to complete cross-referencing.

## Constraints on Source Citation and Traceability Posed by This Category’s Data Characteristics
This category’s data characteristics impose multiple constraints on the citation and traceability process. Multi-source heterogeneous production documents must be cross-referenced via batch numbers and timestamps to ensure that referenced coating batch sheets match the corresponding oven curves and defect repair sheets correctly. Frequently updated real-time collection data requires traceability to quickly locate the latest version of documents by production node, to avoid referencing expired data. Exclusive field units (such as film thickness μm, viscosity measured via No. 4 coating cup seconds) must be automatically verified and matched during traceability, to prevent citation deviations caused by unit mismatches. Compliance-related logs must retain a complete citation chain to support subsequent compliance tracing and issue troubleshooting.

## Configuration Setup
This configuration is based on FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 800–1200 token | The effective citation content length of a single coating document is moderate. This range covers complete process parameters or test results |
| `responseData` | Enabled | Original batch information and test data must be returned as citation content to ensure complete traceability information |
| `detail` | Full display | Detailed parameters of coating processes are critical for cross-review. The field content of referenced documents must be fully presented |
| Single search limit | Top 5 entries | Coating document associations focus on production batches and time windows. The top 5 entries cover most associated documents |
| Context window | 1500–2000 characters | Context of multiple documents linked to batches must be retained to avoid truncation of critical matching information |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Symptom: The AI response in the workflow does not reference the queried coating documents, and the citation fields are empty. Cause: The `responseData` configuration item is not enabled, so the system does not bind the queried raw data to the response.
- Symptom: Timeouts occur after submitting a cross-review request, and logs show context overflow. Cause: The values for `quoteMaxToken` or the context window are too large, exceeding the system’s carrying limit.
- Symptom: Referenced documents have unit mismatches, such as film thickness μm displayed as mm. Cause: No field unit verification logic is configured, so units are not aligned during cross-document citation.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
A coating process card and corresponding coating batch sheet may be manually imported, a cross-review request submitted, and the returned result checked for complete citation content of both documents.
The field units in cited content may be checked against original documents to confirm that the unit verification logic is active.
The value of `quoteMaxToken` may be adjusted, and the length of the returned citation content verified against the expected range.
Multi-batch production scenarios may be simulated, and the system checked for correct association of documents from different batches to avoid cross-batch citation errors.

The values provided are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
