# Tender Document and Quotation Preparation for Non-Ferrous Metal Smelting and Electrolysis: Forms and Interactions

## What the data for this category looks like
Daily documents for this category include batching lists, smelting records, electrolytic cell condition records, laboratory reports, anode and cathode quality records, and environmental protection ledgers. Data sources cover concentrate and auxiliary material analysis, DCS systems, cell voltage and current monitoring, laboratory testing, and energy and environmental protection monitoring equipment. Data update frequency changes with raw material batches, single furnace or single cell operations, and real-time working conditions. Fields include raw material batch numbers, furnace and cell numbers, composition parameters, current and voltage values, emission indicators, and more. There is no unified fixed format, and most content is stored classified by production link.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Data sources for this category are scattered and update rhythms vary. This requires forms to support quick calling and verification of multi-source data. Document types are diverse and fields have no unified format. This requires interactions to adapt to uploading of multiple file types and field mapping. Business links involve sensitive parameters such as furnace conditions and composition calculations. Incorrect input will cause production risks, so forms must be configured with strict verification rules. Tender quotations also require sequential confirmation of multi-layer data including raw materials, furnace conditions, and laboratory results. This requires interactions to trigger layer by layer according to business processes.

## How to set the configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Basis for This Approach |

| `formInput` verification rules | Bind required and range verification for business fields | Data for this category involves furnace conditions and composition parameters, and errors will cause production risks |
| User selection hierarchy | Trigger layer by layer according to raw materials - furnace conditions - laboratory results | Tender quotations require confirmation of data from each link in sequence |
| File upload | Support all default general formats | Daily documents include multiple types of files such as batching lists and laboratory reports |
| Form submission timeout | 600 seconds | Complex metal balance accounting requires sufficient processing time |
| Knowledge base folder selection | Bind dedicated classification folders for smelting business | The knowledge base for this category is classified by production link, facilitating quick retrieval |

## Three common misconfigurations
- A field empty error appears after form submission. The cause is that the required verification rule for `formInput` is not configured, so uncollected business parameters are not intercepted.
- Industrial table formats cannot be recognized during file upload. The cause is that default full format support is not enabled, and only general document formats are available, which cannot adapt to the CSV format of batching lists.
- Duplicate questions appear in multi-layer user selection. The cause is that the trigger logic for user selection is not configured layer by layer according to business processes, leading to repeated requests for data from the same link.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Use the form editor in FastGPT v4.17.0, enter the folder selection pop-up window, and confirm that only dedicated classification folders for smelting business are displayed.
- Upload a CSV-format batching list, and confirm that the form can automatically extract fields such as raw material batch numbers and composition parameters.
- Intentionally leave the required fields configured in `formInput` blank, such as the raw material batch number, and confirm that a corresponding verification prompt pops up.
- Trigger the multi-layer user selection process, select the raw material batch and furnace number in sequence, and confirm that the next layer of options is only loaded after the previous step of selection is completed.
