# Bid Document and Quotation Preparation for Welded Structural Component Manufacturing: Forms and Interactions

## What this category of data looks like
Core data for welded structural component manufacturing comes from design drawings, material quality certificates, welding equipment parameters, filler metal inventory, non-destructive testing systems, and personnel qualification ledgers. Daily generated documents include welding procedure qualification records, welding procedure specifications, filler metal usage records, welder qualification records, weld inspection reports, and repair orders.
Data update frequency follows per weld seam, per batch, or per shift. Fields include weld number, filler metal grade, welder certification number, inspection results, and more. Units include millimeters, kilograms, hours, and others. Each single record forms a complete associated chain around a single weld seam or a single batch production task.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on forms and interactions
Multi-source data association requirements require forms to support cross-ledger field mapping, to avoid duplicate manual data entry. Frequently updated weld and production data require forms to support real-time verification and incremental entry, to prevent expired data from being included in bid documents.
Rigid qualification and process constraints require forms to have built-in compliance verification rules, to intercept non-compliant filler metals, welders, and process parameters in advance. The fixed chapter structure of bid documents requires form fields to be accurately bound to document templates, to ensure quotation and process data are correctly filled into corresponding positions.
Single batch production batch data requirements require forms to support batch entry and submission, to improve preparation efficiency.

## Three common configuration errors
- After configuring preset welder qualification options for `userSelect`, the corresponding selectable values do not appear in the bid form, and blank fields appear during selection. The cause is that the binding rule between the personnel qualification ledger and `userSelect` was not correctly associated, resulting in preset values not being pulled from the data source.
- After configuring a third-party model endpoint, an error indicating that the guidance logic does not take effect is returned when calling the form to generate quotations. The cause is that the model identifier parameter was not updated synchronously, resulting in a mismatch between the called model and the preset guidance prompt.
- After entering more than 50 weld data entries in batch, a timeout error occurs when the form is submitted. The cause is that a reasonable `formInput` batch submission limit was not set, resulting in the single submission data volume exceeding the system processing threshold.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Open the bid document form, enter a preset weld number, and verify that the automatically populated filler metal grade and welder certification number match the filler metal inventory and personnel qualification ledger.
- After configuring a third-party model endpoint, enter a test guidance prompt, and confirm that the returned quotation calculation result conforms to the cost logic of welded structural component manufacturing.
- Enter 50 weld data entries in batch, submit the form, and confirm that there are no timeout or field missing errors.
- Enter an unqualified welder certification number, confirm that the form triggers a compliance prompt and intercepts the submission of non-compliant data.
