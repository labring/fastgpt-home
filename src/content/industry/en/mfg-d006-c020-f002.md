# Inspection and Process Record Audit for Safety, Environmental Protection and Special Work Management: Vector Models and Indexes

## What data looks like for this category
Data for this category comes from regulatory standards, online environmental monitoring equipment, gas detectors, personnel qualification files, equipment isolation lists, and supervision platforms. Daily documents include risk identification records, work permits for hot work, confined space work, lifting operations and similar tasks, environmental monitoring records, hazardous waste ledgers, emission monitoring data, and accident and hidden danger rectification reports. Data updates are triggered by the completion of individual jobs, real-time monitoring results, or regular inspections. Document fields include operation time, gas concentration values, qualification validity periods, isolation point numbers and similar details, with exclusive units such as ppm and days.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on vector models and indexes
This category of data includes both structured monitoring values and unstructured document content. Update frequency shifts based on jobs, real-time monitoring, or regular inspections. Vector models and indexes must adapt to mixed data formats and high-frequency update rhythms. Documents contain specialized operational terminology and exclusive units. Vector models must accurately capture industry semantic features. Work audits carry veto power, which demands high precision. Recall result accuracy must match business decision-making needs. False recalls or missed recalls must be avoided, as they can affect safety and compliance.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: Knowledge base search responses time out. Logs indicate single retrieval exceeds 60 seconds. Cause: Incremental indexing is not configured for high-frequency work data. Full index reconstruction consumes excessive computing resources.
- Phenomenon: Irrelevant product quality inspection records appear in retrieval results. Non-safety and environmental protection documents are not filtered. Cause: No index filtering rules based on data source or document type are configured. Cross-category data is recalled.
- Phenomenon: Custom index creation fails. The interface prompts field format mismatch. Cause: General document index templates are reused directly. The exclusive units and field structure of this category of data are not adapted to.

## How to confirm proper configuration
- Use the index management module of FastGPT v4.17.0 to run a single retrieval test. Verify that all returned document types relate to safety, environmental protection and special operations. Adjust the similarity threshold to the range that meets business requirements.
- Simulate document upload after a single job is completed. Check whether the index completes incremental updates within a reasonable time. Confirm the update rhythm matches actual business frequency.
- View the encoding logs of the vector model. Confirm that specialized terminology and exclusive units are correctly identified. No semantic encoding deviation should be present.
- Check whether the `maxToken` configuration adapts to the longest currently imported document length. Avoid context truncation issues.
