# Inspection and Process Record Audit for Cement Clinker and Grinding: Vector Models and Indexes

## What Data for This Category Looks Like
Data for cement clinker and grinding scenarios covers daily business documents from cement plant mining, raw material, calcination, grinding, quality, and energy departments. These documents include raw meal blending plans, kiln operation records, laboratory reports, quality control notices, equipment inspection records, and energy reports.
Data sources include online composition analysis systems, DCS control systems, laboratories, kiln and grinding equipment, raw and fuel ledgers, and energy metering devices. Update frequencies fall into three categories: real-time, shift-based, and daily.
Documents include numeric records with fixed fields and operation text in flexible formats.

## Constraints on Vector Models and Indexing Workflows
Data sources for this category are scattered, and update rhythms vary significantly. Real-time DCS data and offline laboratory reports require separate adaptation to incremental and batch indexing needs.
Documents contain industrial technical terms such as free calcium oxide, specific surface area, and thermal control systems. Vector models must adapt to process industry semantics.
Cross-departmental business document association requires indexes to support joint recall of cross-department documents.
Data with different update frequencies needs configured differentiated index synchronization rules. This avoids data lag or index redundancy.

## Configuration Settings
| Configuration Item | Recommended Approach | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Accommodates documents of varying lengths such as raw meal blending plans and kiln operation records, balances semantic completeness of vector segments and index storage efficiency |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Preserves key associated information such as blending parameters and thermal parameters across chunks, avoids semantic breaks during cross-document association |
| `defaultToken` | Calibrated against industry terminology libraries | Adapts token splitting rules for cement industry-specific terms, reduces incorrect splitting of professional terminology |
| `maxToken` | 4000–6000 tokens | Supports integrating context from multiple laboratory reports and energy reports, meets cross-document association needs for inspection and audit |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 results | Balances recall precision and retrieval efficiency, adapts to batch audit scenarios for multi-source data |
| Reranking Model | dengcao/Qwen3-Embedding-8B:F16 | Adapts to cement industry professional semantics, improves matching precision between inspection records and audit standards |

## Three Common Misconfigurations
- Vector recall results show low matching accuracy with professional semantics. This happens when an index model is selected without adapting to cement industry professional terminology, and only a general vector index is used. This leads to insufficient matching precision for keywords such as free calcium oxide and thermal control systems.
- Token statistics displayed in the interface have anomalies. This happens when `defaultToken` is not used to calibrate professional term splitting, resulting in incorrect splitting of proper nouns.
- Index storage usage exceeds expected levels. This happens when chunk length is not adjusted based on the document length of raw meal blending plans, generating excessive redundant vector segments.

## How to Confirm Proper Configuration
- Review index update logs in FastGPT v4.17.0 to confirm that synchronization frequency for real-time data and offline documents matches configured requirements.
- Input professional keywords from kiln operation records, check that recall results include business documents from the corresponding department, and verify semantic matching precision.
- Adjust the chunk length parameter, observe changes in the number of generated vector segments, and confirm expected storage efficiency is achieved.
- View the token statistics interface, verify that splitting results for professional terms conform to calibrated rules.
