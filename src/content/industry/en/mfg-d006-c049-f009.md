# Inspection and Process Record Review for In-Process Quality and Final Product Release: Model Access and Configuration

## What This Category of Data Looks Like
This category of data comes from manufacturing execution systems (MES), testing equipment, laboratory systems, process parameter records, customer quality standards, and historical defect databases. Data updates run per production batch or shift, or are generated through continuous collection. Daily output documents include control plans, patrol inspection records, SPC reports, final product inspection reports, nonconforming product review forms, release forms, and monthly quality reports. Each document includes fields such as inspection items, parameter thresholds, defect types, and judgment results. No unified fixed units exist, as units vary based on the type of inspection item.

## Constraints Imposed on Model Access and Configuration
Dispersed data sources and highly varied update rhythms require multi-source data access configurations to adapt to data sources with different collection frequencies. Diverse document types and non-uniform field formats require models to support multi-structure text parsing. Correlation analysis between historical defect databases and real-time inspection data requires models to handle cross-document correlation queries. Closed-loop association between nonconforming product reviews and release forms requires models to retain context to link historical judgment results. Standard conversion of inspection items requires models to accurately match process parameters with customer requirements. Context length must be limited to avoid interference from redundant information, while multi-model switching support is needed to adapt to the needs of different inspection scenarios.

## How to Configure Settings
The following configurations are based on the interface and parameter logic of FastGPT v4.17.0.

| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maxContext` | `8000–12000 token` | Adapts to the total text length of multi-source inspection records and historical defect databases, avoiding exceeding the model's context window |
| `maxTokens` | 2000–3000 token | Meets requirements for complete judgment and review output of a single batch of final product inspection reports |
| `quoteMaxToken` | 1500–2000 token | Limits the length of referenced inspection document fragments to ensure associated information is precise and non-redundant |
| `temperature` | 0.3–0.5 | Balances the rigor of inspection judgments and result consistency, reducing random deviations |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in according to the local area network address of the deployed model | Adapts to local or local area network deployed model access scenarios |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Fill in according to the key provided by the model service provider | Completes identity verification for model access |

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- Incorrect entry of `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` or `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT`, resulting in 401 status code returns or connection timeouts. Cause: The identity verification key and address of the deployed model are not correctly matched, or access parameters for different models are confused.
- Incorrect switching of model call configurations when setting up multi-model access, leading to the called model not matching the expected one. Cause: `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` are not updated according to the requirements of different models, or corresponding configurations are not completed in the model selection interface.
- Abnormal similarity filtering configuration, resulting in unexpected numbers of returned results. Cause: The semantic characteristics of this category of inspection data are not adapted, threshold configurations outside the 0-1 interval are incorrectly used, or adjustments are not made by referring to the semantic similarity distribution of historical inspection data.

## How to Confirm Configurations Are Correctly Set
- A parsing request for a single batch of final product inspection reports may be submitted, and inspection item judgment results output by the model may be verified against the original documents.
- Model call logs may be reviewed to confirm that the configurations of `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` have taken effect, with no connection timeout or identity verification errors.
- Multi-model switching may be tested to confirm that call parameter configurations for different models are correct and returned results meet expectations.
- Multi-source data correlation queries may be tested to confirm that the model can correctly associate information from historical defect databases and current inspection records.
- Release channel access configurations may be checked to confirm that the authorization scope meets business requirements.

The values given are common starting points and should be measured against the reader's own samples.
