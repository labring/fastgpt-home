# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Pharmaceutical Dosage Production: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What this category’s data looks like
Typical users of equipment inspection and maintenance data for pharmaceutical dosage production are production, quality assurance, quality control, and warehouse departments of pharmaceutical dosage manufacturers.
Data sources include approved formulation processes, MES electronic batch records, LIMS systems, quality management systems, and equipment and environmental monitoring data.
Document types cover master production documents, batch production records, cleaning records, deviation/CAPA/change documents, batch release audit forms, and others.
Fields include equipment number, operating parameters, deviation descriptions, material batch numbers, and more.
Units include degrees Celsius (℃), megapascals (MPa), hours (h), and others.
Data is updated per production batch, each key process step, and at regular intervals per equipment monitoring plans.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the "knowledge base retrieval and recall" link
This category’s data is tied to production batches and must comply with regulatory requirements.
Errors in retrieval results may lead to batch non-release or recall, triggering regulatory penalties.
Retrieval must associate batch and equipment number information to avoid retrieving non-compliant content across batches.
Document structures include hierarchical approval and execution records.
Retrieval needs to distinguish the priority of different document types. This ensures equipment parameters directly related to the current issue are retrieved first.
Data update frequency is high. A real-time or near-real-time index update mechanism must be configured to keep retrieval results synchronized with the latest production and inspection records.
Data fields include many professional parameters and compliance descriptions. Low-match irrelevant content must be filtered to avoid interfering with maintenance decisions.

## Configuration settings (based on FastGPT v4.17.0)
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `similarity` | 0.75-0.85 | Pharmaceutical dosage production data must meet compliance requirements. This threshold filters low-match irrelevant content and ensures retrieval result rigor |
| `limit` | Top 6-10 results | Equipment inspection and maintenance issues tie to a small number of key equipment parameters. Too many retrieval results increase context load and reduce response efficiency |
| `usingReRank` | Enabled | Equipment-related documents have many similar operating parameter descriptions. Reranking reorders results by semantic relevance to improve accuracy |
| `datasetSearchUsingExtensionQuery` | Enabled | Inspection and maintenance questions often link equipment numbers to historical faults. Extended queries cover more relevant documents and reduce missed detections |
| `collectionIds` | Bind dedicated datasets for equipment inspection and maintenance records | Isolate non-relevant quality release documents. This avoids mixing irrelevant content during retrieval, narrows scope, and improves efficiency |

## Three common errors
- The symptom is a knowledge base question-and-answer split error. The cause is failure to split document chunks by equipment number and production batch. This breaks associations between equipment parameters and batch information within chunks, making accurate question matching impossible.
- The symptom is a 503 error prompt when uploading files via the dialog box. Uploads to the knowledge base work normally. The cause is equipment documents containing uncompleted GMP format attachments, triggering temporary service rate limiting.
- The symptom is low accuracy for complex maintenance question responses. The cause is failure to enable `usingReRank` and use an excessively high `limit` value. This introduces large amounts of non-critical redundant parameter content into the context.

## How to confirm the configuration is properly set
- Retrieve inspection records for a specified equipment number. Confirm retrieval results include content linked to the corresponding batch’s electronic batch records.
- Upload a single cleaning record. Verify split document chunks include the equipment number and cleaning time fields.
- Compare retrieval result sorting with and without `usingReRank` enabled. Confirm the reranking function operates correctly.
- Check dataset binding status. Confirm only document collections related to equipment inspection and maintenance are included.

The values provided are common starting points and should be evaluated against samples relevant to the specific deployment.
