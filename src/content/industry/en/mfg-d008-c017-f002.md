# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Spraying and Coating: Vector Models and Indexes

## What the data for this category looks like
Core data for spraying and coating scenarios covers coating lines for automotive, home appliances, construction machinery, and metal furniture. Daily documents include coating process cards, coating batch sheets, paint mixing records, film thickness and color difference reports, oven curves, defect repair sheets, and VOCs ledgers. Data comes from coating supplier TDS/SDS documents, real-time collection data from spray booths and ovens, colorimeters, film thickness gauges, environmental monitoring equipment, and MES systems. Update cadence aligns with each coating batch, each shift’s production nodes, and continuous equipment operation collection. Document fields contain professional parameters such as film thickness, color difference ΔE value, oven temperature, and coating viscosity. Some records use tabular parameter formats, others use textual process descriptions, with no unified general structure.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the vector model and indexing link
This category has scattered data sources and widely varying update frequencies. Continuously collected equipment data needs incremental indexing to avoid full scans slowing retrieval speed. Document structures are inconsistent, with both tabular parameter records and textual process descriptions. Chunking must balance parameter integrity and contextual relevance. The vector model must accurately capture semantic associations of professional parameters such as film thickness and color difference ΔE value, and adapt to professional terminology in industrial process texts. Retrieval results must link reference data across the full pre-treatment, spraying, and curing workflows. Single recall results must cover the complete process logic required for maintenance decisions, and avoid missing key links.

## How to set configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Value Range | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunk Length | 800–1200 characters | Adapts to the paragraph length of documents such as coating process cards and oven curves, and retains the complete process window for pre-treatment, spraying, and curing |
| Overlap Length | 100–150 characters | Prevents chunking from breaking continuous process parameters, such as the connection between paint mixing viscosity and corresponding spraying pressure |
| Similarity Threshold | 0.75–0.85 | Distinguishes similar descriptions of coating defects, such as the characteristic differences between orange peel and sagging, and avoids mismatching irrelevant documents |
| Single Search Limit | Top 10 entries | Key reference data in coating scenarios concentrates within 10 entries, and avoids redundant results interfering with maintenance decisions |
| `maxToken` | 4096 token | Adapts to the context window of mainstream large language models, and fully loads associated content from process cards and defect reports |
| `defaultToken` | 512 token | Used for text truncation in vector models, and balances the integrity of professional parameters and retrieval speed |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Symptom: Knowledge base search responses time out, and logs show vector matching takes more than 60 seconds. Cause: Incremental indexing is not configured for per-batch coating and per-shift batch sheets and equipment data. Full indexing scans all historical data on low-configured hardware.
- Symptom: Search results include large numbers of irrelevant VOCs ledger documents, and cannot retrieve maintenance solutions for coating defects. Cause: The similarity threshold is set too low, and matching rules are not adjusted using the weight of process parameters in the coating scenario.
- Symptom: After question-answering splitting, oven temperature and corresponding film thickness requirements appear in different text chunks. Cause: Chunk length is set too short, breaking continuous process-associated parameters and destroying the complete logic of maintenance solutions.
