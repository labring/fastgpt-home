# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Furniture and Wood Product Manufacturing: Forms and Interactions

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Daily documents for these entities typically include order breakdown drawings, cutting lists, edge banding and drilling programs, coating process documents, hardware material lists, trial assembly and packaging records. Data mainly originates from order design systems, sheet and lumber batch ledgers, CNC cutting and drilling equipment logs, ERP/MES systems, and moisture content test records. Data updates align with individual orders, daily production batches, and material batch cycles. Most fields cover panel dimensions, material moisture content, equipment operating parameters, hardware models, and similar items. Units include millimeters, percentages, cubic meters, and others.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Forms and Interactions
Data for this category comes from multiple dispersed sources and updates per order or batch. The forms and interactions link must support associating structured data exported from multiple systems, such as order breakdown drawings and cutting lists. Custom order-driven requirements mean form fields must support personalized configuration to adapt to hardware lists, panel parameters, and other content for different orders. Multi-dimensional equipment operation and material test data require the interaction link to quickly associate corresponding documents and parameters, avoiding duplicate data entry. Additionally, the large number of panels linked to on-site installation dimensions means interactions must support bulk selection or upload of associated files to reduce operation steps.

| Attachment upload limit | 1000 MB per file, maximum 10 files per submission | Documents such as order breakdown drawings and coating process files have large sizes, adapting to upload requirements |

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: No follow-up feedback appears after submitting an inspection and maintenance form, and some fields on the interface display as empty. Cause: The trigger action after form submission is not configured, or the associated customer service channel has not completed authorization configuration.
- Phenomenon: The `formInput` component configured in the workflow cannot be displayed on the conversation interface, and interaction content fails to load. Cause: The `formInput` component is not added to the rendering list of the conversation front end, or the configured display conditions do not match correctly.
- Phenomenon: A prompt indicating the file exceeds the limit appears after uploading an order breakdown drawing or process document. Cause: The attachment upload limit configuration item is not adjusted, causing the uploaded file size to exceed the preset threshold.

## How to Verify the Configuration Is Correct
- Enter the configuration page, check that the preset fields of the `formInput` component match the order fields of the current category, and confirm that the custom added fields meet production requirements.
- Initiate a test conversation, trigger the `userSelect` component, verify that the preset equipment options load normally and can be selected, and confirm that manually entered supplementary options are properly received by the system.
- Upload a small order breakdown drawing file, verify that the upload progress and prompts align with the configured attachment limit requirements, with no error pop-ups.
- Submit simulated form data, confirm that the system triggers the preset follow-up actions after receiving the data, with no abnormal error messages displayed.
