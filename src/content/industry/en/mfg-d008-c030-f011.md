# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Rubber Mixing and Product Vulcanization: Forms and Interactions
This document applies to FastGPT v4.17.0.

## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from the recipe system, raw material batch management module, internal mixer, Mooney viscometer, vulcanizing press, and laboratory testing equipment. Daily documents include recipe sheets, weighing records, mixing curves, rubber compound quick test reports, vulcanization process cards, mold usage records, and finished product performance reports.
Data update frequency is per raw material batch, per mixed rubber compound batch, per vulcanized product mold run. Fields include mixing temperature, vulcanization duration, rubber Mooney value, mold number, and other parameters, with clear physical units attached.

## What constraints these characteristics impose on the forms and interactions link
Because data is updated frequently per batch and mold run, forms must support real-time retrieval of preset process parameters for the current batch to avoid lag errors from manual entry. Category document fields vary significantly, so forms must support custom configuration of fields and units to meet entry requirements for different documents such as recipe sheets and mixing curves.
Errors can lead to scrap of rubber compounds or products, so forms must add required field validation and real-time prompts, and must be linked to historical equipment inspection records to help maintenance personnel quickly locate abnormalities.

## Three common mistakes
- Phenomenon: After configuring the global variable as a knowledge base selection, conditional judgment cannot be set in the judge. Reason: The conditional call permission for the global variable is not enabled, or the knowledge base is not bound to the optional scope of the corresponding global variable.
- Phenomenon: After setting the `userSelect` component, the selection prompt pops up repeatedly in a single round of conversation. Reason: The single-trigger logic of the component is not configured, causing the selection items to reload every interaction.
- Phenomenon: The retrieval results include a large number of irrelevant maintenance documents from other categories. Reason: The exclusive knowledge base of this category is not bound, and the retrieval scope covers materials from non-target categories.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Manually create an inspection form without filling in the mixing temperature, and check whether the required field validation prompt is triggered.
- Select the preset vulcanizing press over-vulcanization fault option, and check whether the retrieval results only return maintenance solutions for this category.
- View the binding list of global variables, confirm that the exclusive knowledge base documents for mixing and vulcanization have been associated.
- Initiate a simulated inspection request, check that the response duration does not exceed the preset threshold.
