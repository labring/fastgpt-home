## What data for this category looks like
Data for this category comes from online composition analysis systems, DCS distributed control systems, laboratories, kiln and grinding equipment, raw material fuel ledgers, and energy metering devices. Daily generated documents include raw material batching plans, kiln operation records, laboratory reports, quality control notices, equipment inspection records, and energy reports. Data update cycles cover real-time, per-shift, and daily frequencies, covering business links across mining, raw material, calcination, and grinding departments.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the tool calling and plugins workflow
Multiple scattered data sources and varied update cycles require tool calling to support multiple data acquisition methods such as real-time pulling and scheduled synchronization. Business documents covering multiple fields including composition, temperature, and inspection records require plugins to support parsing structured and unstructured content in different formats. The industry has clear consequences for errors, so tool calling must be configured with fault tolerance mechanisms and complete log retention. The business focuses on thermal stability and raw material homogenization, so tool calling logic must prioritize core needs of clinker calcination and grinding links.

## Three common configuration mistakes
- Symptom: `messages is empty` error returned when calling openAPI. Cause: The request body is not properly populated with context documents related to equipment inspection, or the specific user inspection question content is not included.
- Symptom: Tool call results do not match exclusive equipment data for cement clinker grinding. Cause: Question optimization configuration is not enabled, leading to unoptimized questions that fail to match knowledge base documents for this category, or `functionCall` is not set to enabled.
- Symptom: No valid log retention after tool call failure. Cause: Log retention period is configured too short to cover business troubleshooting cycles, or the log collection switch is not turned on.

- Navigate to the system configuration page, confirm that the settings for `toolChoice` and `functionCall` match the recommended values in the table.
