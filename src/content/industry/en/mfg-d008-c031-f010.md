# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Cement Clinker and Grinding: Knowledge Base Retrieval and Recall

## What data in this category consists of
Data comes from the mining, raw material, calcination, grinding, quality control, and energy departments of cement enterprises. It covers business documents including raw material batching plans, kiln operation records, laboratory reports, quality control notices, equipment inspection records, and energy reports. Data sources are online composition analysis systems, DCS control systems, laboratories, kiln and grinding equipment, raw and fuel ledgers, and energy metering devices. Update frequencies include real-time, per-shift, and daily. Document fields include material composition values, equipment operating temperatures, inspection cycles, and energy consumption values. Units include degrees Celsius, tons, kilowatt-hours, and other standard industrial units.

## Constraints on knowledge base retrieval and recall
Differences in data formats across multiple sources require unifying field and unit identifiers before documents are added to the repository. This prevents loss of valid information due to format mismatches during retrieval. Real-time, per-shift, and daily update frequencies require retrieval scopes to cover datasets with different update cycles. This ensures that core documents such as the latest kiln operation records and quality control notices are recalled. Documents related to equipment inspection and kiln/grinding equipment make up a large share of the total. Retrieval must prioritize matching keywords for equipment inspection and maintenance to improve the recall rate of relevant results. Documents including core business materials such as raw material batching plans require accurate retrieval results. Incorrect recalls could disrupt production links including clinker quality control and kiln system operation.

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: After setting `similarity` to 0.77, some documents matched by professional terms are judged as irrelevant, and the model returns no relevant information. Cause: The semantic similarity calculation for professional terms in this category has deviations. A threshold of 0.77 is too high, filtering out some documents with sufficient matching degrees.
- Symptom: Semantic retrieval returns more than 10 results, and the content most matching the query is not filtered out. Cause: The `usingReRank` configuration is not enabled, and no reranking sorting is performed on the recall results, leading to cluttered results.
- Symptom: After uploading equipment inspection records in Excel format, the table content cannot be read during retrieval. Cause: The knowledge base is not configured to support parsing of Excel files, and the index switch for the corresponding file type is not enabled.

## How to confirm the configuration is correct
- Upload typical documents of this category, perform queries related to equipment inspection, and check whether the returned results include exclusive documents such as raw material batching plans and kiln operation records.
- Adjust the `similarity` configuration, compare recall results under different thresholds, and confirm that the matching degree meets business requirements.
- After enabling `usingReRank`, verify that the order of recall results matches the relevance to the query.
- Upload equipment inspection records in Excel format, perform a query, and confirm that the table content can be correctly retrieved.
