# Cement Clinker and Grinding Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A: Forms and Interactions

## What this category’s data looks like
Data for this category comes from online composition analysis, DCS systems, laboratories, kiln and grinding equipment, raw and fuel ledgers, and energy metering devices.
Data update frequencies fall into three groups: real-time, per shift, and daily.
Daily documents include raw material batching plans, kiln operation records, test reports, quality control notices, equipment inspection records, and energy reports.
Data fields cover raw and fuel composition, kiln thermal parameters, clinker mineral composition, cement strength indicators, and other relevant metrics. All fields must match standardized metering formats used in industrial sites.

## Constraints on forms and interactions from these characteristics
Data sources are scattered, and update frequencies vary widely. Forms must support linked field entry across multiple data sources to avoid duplicate entry of inspection and test data across different time periods.
There are many types of documents. Forms must adapt to the field structures of different documents. For example, parameters for raw material batching plans and fields for equipment inspection records must be configured separately.
Units of measurement used in industrial sites must be strictly matched. Forms must include built-in standardized unit options to prevent entry errors.
Real-time data entry must support quick submission to avoid parameter deviations caused by delays. Batch import and validation must be supported for shift and daily summary data.

## Three Common Configuration Mistakes
- After configuring input guidance and the professional thesaurus, the preset inspection and maintenance questions do not display in the conversation interface. This occurs when terms for raw material batching and kiln operations from the cement clinker and grinding category are not imported into the thesaurus, or when guidance terms are not bound to the category’s business scenarios.
- Voice input on the guest login page fails to display sent content, or the press-and-hold timer stays at 00:00. This happens when the configuration does not adapt to network delay scenarios common in industrial sites, or when the timeout threshold for the input component does not match the site’s network environment.
- When submitting batch inspection records, no prompt appears after exceeding the set limit, leading to partial data not being saved. This occurs when batch submission validation rules for `formInput` are not configured, or when the limit value does not match the summary volume of shift inspection records.

## How to Verify Proper Configuration
- Open the form configuration page, confirm that entry fields include category-specific fields such as raw material batching, kiln inspection, and test results.

- Test the voice input function, confirm that input content displays normally and can be submitted when simulating network delay scenarios common in industrial sites.
