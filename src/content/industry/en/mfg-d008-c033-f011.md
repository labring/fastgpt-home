# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for High-Volume Automotive Parts Manufacturing: Forms and Interactions

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Data sources for this category include customer drawings, special characteristic lists, ERP/MES systems, measuring tools, equipment process parameters, and supplier quality certificates. Data update frequency follows per batch, per shift, and continuous collection cycles. Daily documents include control plans, process flowcharts, work instructions, first-piece and patrol inspection records, SPC reports, PPAP materials, and 8D reports. Fields cover process numbers, equipment codes, inspection items, abnormality descriptions, rectification measures, batch numbers, special characteristic parameters, and more. Units use common industrial measurement standards such as length, torque, and temperature.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on Forms and Interactions
Dispersed data sources and frequent update cycles require use of the `formInput` component to support flexible adaptation for multi-source field automatic synchronization or manual entry. Diverse daily document types with strong associated constraints require the form to support on-demand loading of corresponding document field groups, avoiding redundant fields that interfere with operations. The strong binding between batches and special characteristics requires the form to associate batch numbers with corresponding special characteristic parameters, ensuring that inspection and maintenance information can be traced back to the corresponding production batch. Constrained by vehicle customer quality systems, the form must retain complete operation traceability fields including submitter, submission time, and rectification status. It must also adapt to multi-layered question tree interaction logic to meet business needs for step-by-step equipment abnormality troubleshooting.

## Three Common Mistakes
- Phenomenon: When searching for equipment inspection-related documents, it is not possible to filter knowledge base content within a specified folder, and a large number of irrelevant results are returned. Cause: The search scope is not configured to be limited to the specified business folder, and default retrieval covers the full knowledge base.
- Phenomenon: After the form is submitted, uploaded inspection photos and rectification reports cannot be associated with the corresponding equipment and production batches. Cause: The file upload field is not bound to the equipment number and batch number fields of the form, resulting in untraceable information.
- Phenomenon: The multi-layered question tree form cannot automatically jump to subsequent follow-up field groups based on the selected abnormality type, and all fields must be filled out manually. Cause: The question branch jump logic is not configured, and the association relationship between the abnormality type and subsequent field groups is not bound.
