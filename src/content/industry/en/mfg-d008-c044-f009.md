# Pulp and Paper Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A: Model Integration and Configuration

## What Data Looks Like for This Category
Business data for the pulp and paper category originates from wood pulp and waste paper batches, DCS/QCS systems, on-paper machine measurement equipment, laboratories, energy systems, and water treatment systems. Daily documents include stock preparation sheets, beating records, paper machine operation logs, online quality curves, laboratory reports, sheet break records, and product change records. Data update frequencies cover three dimensions: real-time, per shift, and per roll. Fields include pulp properties, paper machine operating parameters, paper quality indicators, and equipment operating status. No fixed single document size applies.

## What Constraints Do These Characteristics Impose on Model Integration and Configuration
Data sources for this category are scattered, and update frequencies vary significantly. The model integration link must support batch reading and real-time synchronization of multi-source data. Unstructured data such as online quality curves and sheet break records coexists with structured data such as stock preparation sheets and beating records. Processing logic adapted to mixed-format data must be configured. High-speed continuous web production results in high business data density. The model context window must match the update rhythm of business data to avoid missing key equipment inspection and maintenance information due to context overflow.

## How to Set Configurations
| Configuration Item | Recommended Setting | Rationale |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| `maxContext` | 8000–12000 token | Matches the total length of on-shift stock preparation sheets, beating records, and paper machine operation logs, preventing context overflow that causes loss of key equipment inspection and maintenance information |
| `temperature` | 0.2–0.4 | Equipment inspection and maintenance Q&A requires precision and accuracy. A lower temperature reduces answer randomness, aligning with reliability requirements for industrial scenarios |
| `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` | Fill in the official access address of the deployment region | Adapts to cross-node synchronization requirements for multi-source industrial data, ensuring stability of model calls |
| `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` | Exclusive token generated according to internal enterprise permissions | Ensures security of model integration, preventing unauthorized calls from affecting production data |
| `maxTokens` | 1000–1500 token | Controls the output length of single-round responses, matching the conciseness requirements for equipment maintenance plans, avoiding redundant information that interferes with on-site operations |

## Three Common Configuration Errors
- Phenomenon: A 404 error occurs when calling the model after configuring a third-party access address. Cause: The actual access endpoint of the model is not correctly matched, causing the request to fail recognition by the target service.
- Phenomenon: Setting the `temperature` parameter too high leads to inconsistent maintenance plan recommendations in equipment inspection Q&A. Cause: A high temperature parameter amplifies the randomness of model responses, which does not meet the precision requirements of industrial scenarios.
- Phenomenon: Configuring the `maxContext` parameter too small prevents operation records for multiple paper machine rolls from being fully passed to the model, resulting in sheet break-related information being omitted from inspection results. Cause: The total length of multiple historical records in pulp and paper business is not accommodated, leading to insufficient context window.
