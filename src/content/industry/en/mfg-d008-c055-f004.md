# Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A for Foundry (Sand Molding, Precision Casting, Die Casting): Workflow Orchestration

## What This Category's Data Looks Like
This category's data comes from ERP material master data, melting furnace thermometers, spectrometers, MES heat records, mold management systems, customer drawings and technical specifications. Update frequency is per heat, per batch, or daily. Daily documents include casting process cards, batching lists, heat records, mold histories, first-piece inspection records, material test reports, and nonconforming product review forms. Fields include alloy batching ratio, furnace temperature, pouring temperature, mold cycle, defect type, and similar items. No additional statistical figures are included.

## What Constraints These Characteristics Impose on the "Workflow Orchestration" Link
This category's data updates per heat, per batch, or daily. Workflows must support heat number and mold cycle as core trigger conditions. They must integrate multi-source data connections, including ERP material master data, melting temperature measurement data, mold histories, and more. Daily documents have many fields with high correlation. Workflows must support matching and validation of structured fields such as ratios, temperatures, and defect types. Workflow nodes must transfer associations between structured data and unstructured documents. They must also handle traceability associations for nonconforming product review forms. Additionally, workflows must support serial or parallel orchestration of multiple nodes to cover the full process of equipment inspection and maintenance Q&A, and avoid expired data or association errors.

## Three Common Errors
- Symptom: Intermediate result fields passed between workflow nodes are empty. Cause: Core identifiers such as heat number and mold cycle were not passed correctly via `variables`, causing the retrieval node to fail to match the corresponding category data.
- Symptom: After enabling knowledge base plugin question optimization, when the final answer is not closed, retrieval results deviate from process parameters. Cause: No association rules for question optimization were configured in the workflow, causing the retrieval logic to fail to match professional terminology.
- Symptom: After embedding into a business system, the workflow cannot initiate requests normally. Cause: No identity association parameters between the business system and FastGPT were configured, causing requests to lack valid user identifiers and unable to trigger the workflow.

## How to Confirm the Configuration Is Complete
- Trigger the workflow and pass the specified heat number, then check if the `variables` parameter in the node logs includes the corresponding heat and mold cycle information.
- View the return results of the knowledge base retrieval node, and confirm that only documents such as the current heat's process card and heat record are included.
- Call the workflow interface and pass the business system's user identifier, then confirm that the request returns normal maintenance Q&A results.
- Check the dependency relationships of `nodeId` in the workflow, and confirm that each node executes in the expected order.
