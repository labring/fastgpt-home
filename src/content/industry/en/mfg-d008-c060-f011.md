# Beverage and Bottled Water Equipment Inspection and Maintenance Q&A: Forms and Interactions

## What data looks like for this category
Daily documents for beverage and bottled water production include recipe sheets, water treatment records, blending records, sterilization or aseptic filling records, packaging material inspections, and finished product inspections. Data sources cover water source and raw material testing, recipe systems, online sugar and acidity monitoring, filling equipment, visual inspection systems, and LIMS platforms. Data update cycles cover per batch, per shift, and continuous collection. Fields include concentrate dosage, water treatment turbidity, filling liquid level, sealing pressure, product batch number, and others. Corresponding units are mostly kg, NTU, mm, kPa; the batch number field has no quantitative unit.

## What constraints do these characteristics impose on the "forms and interactions" link
Data sources for this category are scattered, and update cycles vary significantly. Continuously collected equipment data must be linked to forms in real time. Multi-batch documents must be managed by production stage and shift. The high-speed filling production characteristic requires form response speed to adapt to real-time inspection needs. Error consequences in concentrate dosing, aseptic control, and other links are severe, so forms must include strict field validation rules. Additionally, frequent product switching requires form options to be quickly adjusted with production recipes to avoid mismatched packaging materials or batch numbers.

## Three common mistakes
- Symptom: A "value out of range" error pops up when submitting the form. Cause: The legal range of the field was not set according to the standard parameters of beverage and bottled water equipment, causing valid inputs to be blocked.
- Symptom: Search results include expired sterilization or filling records. Cause: No rule was configured to filter knowledge base documents by production batch, resulting in mixed cross-shift data.
- Symptom: Single-choice options do not update automatically with the product batch number. Cause: The batch number field was not bound to the batch number parameter of the knowledge base, making it impossible to match packaging material inspection records for the corresponding batch.

## How to confirm the configuration is complete
- Input a dosage value outside the 0–500 kg range, and confirm that the corresponding error prompt displays in the form.
- Switch the production shift option, and confirm that the knowledge base options update synchronously to the document list for the corresponding shift.
- Load online detection data for the current batch, and confirm that the form automatically fills the corresponding sugar and acidity fields.
- Submit a test form, and confirm that search results only include valid documents for the current production stage.

The values provided here are common starting points, and should be evaluated against the reader's own samples.
