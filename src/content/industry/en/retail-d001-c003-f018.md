# Shopify fulfillment: product questions with hybrid retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT hybrid retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the hybrid retrieval step

Start the Shopify product test with four details actually written in the proposed order attachment: a summer-limited T-shirt, size L, blue and one piece. These support a question about the purchase, without supplying material or intended-user information. Inspect whether hybrid candidates keep the product name attached to its attributes, then rerank using a complete size-and-colour question. An attribute detached from its product is not usable evidence merely because it appears near the top. Keep purchase details separate from product-care evidence in the review checklist. Finding all four order fields does not supply material or washing instructions. A result can be relevant to the purchased item while still lacking the particular attribute asked about.

## Three common errors

The example naming a winter sweater in the order and a spring jacket on the shipping label needs two separately identified descriptions. Confirm that both passages were recalled, then inspect whether final citations merge the names into one product. Different text alone does not establish an actual wrong shipment: the order identity and versions still need checking. Reranking operates on existing candidates, so a missing label passage requires a recall-scope check before a ranking-model change.

## How to verify the setup

An order attachment containing only name and quantity still lacks evidence for material or washing instructions. Inspect whether the answer borrows attributes from another item, then repeat the test after adding the corresponding product information. Preserve before-and-after candidates and citations for the same question to show when evidence entered the chain. Apply filtering under its documented conditions; a higher score cannot replace the material or care information that has not been supplied.
