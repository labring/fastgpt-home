# Temu semi-managed sellers: product questions with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Divider structure, location checking and a packaging draft concern distinct review subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

In this proposed Temu product fixture, a declared specification describes a storage box with removable dividers. A location record says that the full carton awaits review. An undated-status packaging draft uses fixed compartments without establishing that it replaces the specification. A selection question about removing the dividers should rely on the structural wording in the specification. Inventory-review progress and the draft document state concern different matters; combining their vocabulary would incorrectly create a single confirmed product description. Removable dividers describe a connection property, whereas fixed compartments in a draft might describe an arrangement rather than that connection. The supplied text does not establish identical structural subjects. Inspect the component intended by the draft and its confirmation record before treating the wording as a specification discrepancy.

| Task check | Material condition | Evidence action |

## Three common errors

Citation review should address two concrete risks. A populated citation containing only the location record cannot support a claim about divider structure, because that attribute is absent from the passage. A citation using fixed compartments from the packaging draft requires a check of the applicable product and replacement relationship. Modification time alone cannot establish that the draft overrides the specification. Preserve the wording difference and document roles while seeking confirmation, without announcing an actual structural conflict or an inventory incident from these materials. A third error makes the location review state the cause of a divider-structure problem. Look for an actual investigation connecting that location, product and structural issue. Nearby status wording alone cannot supply a causal explanation for differences between product descriptions.

## How to verify the setup

Separate tests should ask for the evidence about divider structure and the work described as awaiting review. The structural answer should trace to the removable-divider wording, while the status answer should trace to the location record. Then supply only the location passage to the structural question and inspect whether the missing structural basis is acknowledged. If references are empty, investigate the current material scope and passage-transfer chain. That result does not establish that the specification is absent or that the dividers cannot be removed.
