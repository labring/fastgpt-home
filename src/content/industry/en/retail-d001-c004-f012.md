# Temu semi-managed sellers: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. A separately purchased item note must remain associated with the set-content description. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed Temu product page is headed desktop organizer set. Its body groups a tray with clips, while the next line says hooks are purchased separately. A question about hooks needs the actual composition and note, not the set title alone. Parsing may mistake the separate-purchase note for the next product heading, while chunking may retain only set and hooks without their relationship. Compare original hierarchy and actual parsed output first, then establish which group the hook note qualifies. Set terminology cannot establish every accessory is included.

## Three common errors

One common error leaves the note in a short block without its subject, allowing adjacent names to support an inclusion claim. Another merges clips and hooks as one component, changing tray with clips into tray with hooks. Inspect component names, composition and condition references to distinguish separated notes from merged entities. A hook-related index still requires the corresponding original data; hit counts cannot establish contents. Missing single-item details also cannot supply a separately purchased specification or current stock status that the page did not state.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should search tray, clips and purchased separately, then ask whether the set contains hooks. Inspect whether returned blocks explain complete relationships. The answer should preserve tray with clips and separately purchased hooks without expanding the heading into a complete-accessory promise. If an actual contents list is added, inspect processing and index versions before repeating the question against new citations. Upload success cannot establish that the earlier mistaken relationship disappeared. Keep old blocks beside new returns, particularly checking that similarly named components are no longer substituted for one another.
