# TikTok Shop US operations: product questions with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For product questions in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Connection method, an included lead and no-cabling claims are different product statements. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed TikTok Shop original says to connect a desktop device using the accessory lead and refers interface details to a separate instruction. A promotional draft says a tidier desktop. A no-cabling question cannot obtain wireless capability from that creative phrase. An entry summarizing it as no cabling would turn an editorial angle into a technical attribute. Distinguish device, lead and interface-instruction subjects, retaining each target original. The existence of a lead also cannot establish every interface specification or compatibility with devices that the source did not identify.

## Three common errors

First, a tidy-desktop draft may be indexed as a wireless device; inspect whether the original actually supplies that capability. Second, an interface question may return only the lead sentence; inspect whether the target contains interface details rather than treating component presence as a specification. Third, a similarly named entry may use instructions for another device; compare device identity and applicability. These require attribute provenance, content coverage and object correspondence respectively. Promotional wording, entry length and ranking score cannot settle the technical conclusion without its supporting source.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask whether a lead is needed, where interface evidence appears and which device the description covers. The supplied sentence can identify lead use, while interface details remain dependent on the applicable instruction. Do not add wireless, universal or verified-compatible claims. After adding material, inspect actual original data and indexes before comparing same-question citations, preserving editorial and technical source identities. Several matching entries still require every target read. Repeated discovery of one connection sentence does not create additional interface or compatibility evidence for the answer.
