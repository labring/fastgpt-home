# TikTok Shop US operations: product questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A product listing provides a starting point for a selection question, while its descriptions and supporting product evidence remain separate inputs. A proposed record may supply appearance and use wording but leave material instructions unavailable. Collect the provided passage and the property being asked about through the form. Inspect downstream transfer for an advertising description turned into a verified technical parameter, and check that the unsupported property remains unresolved.

## Three common errors

Where Return Grading material or return feedback is available, establish its product identity and scope before adding it to this selection question. A person may choose to inspect the original product instructions or read the particular content supported by that return record. Test the connected destinations separately. A return-status description does not automatically represent every product, and a selection neither updates the listing nor establishes suitability for an intended use. For example, if the proposed listing leaves material composition empty while the answer claims that composition was verified, trace that claim to the submitted sources. Inspect whether an unsupported description entered through the question and was treated as product evidence. After identifying its origin, repeat the same question with the composition source still absent.

## How to verify the setup

Identify a provider and receiving step for missing product instructions. After they arrive, repeat the property question and verify that the new passage reaches the answer. If the source is an image, conversion into readable content requires its own implementation; a form does not parse or authenticate images by itself. Moving a conclusion to a public listing or service system requires the corresponding review and actual update record beyond generated text.
