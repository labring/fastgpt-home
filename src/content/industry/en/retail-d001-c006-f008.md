# General export declarations: product questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Commodity names and packing descriptions in export records can show how the goods are documented. They do not supply a complete specification for an intended use. A proposed input may retain the invoice name, packing-list description and requested use condition separately for form collection and reading. Inspect whether each passage keeps its origin, particularly whether a transport-packaging description becomes a definitive statement about material, performance or suitability.

## Three common errors

When procurement asks for detail beyond the documents, such as manufacturer guidance for a model’s use, establish what evidence was actually collected before offering a choice to obtain specifications or read only the trade records. Test the received values and destination of each option. The document-only route cannot supply missing technical information. Matching trade text also does not establish product acceptance, classification or supplier qualification, which require their own review. Packing text may count cartons while the selection question asks about the composition of the product itself. Treating a carton count as the contents of a product set confuses two objects. Inspect the quantity heading and surrounding passage, preserve it as packaging information, then obtain a product-contents description and check whether the revised answer actually uses that additional source.

## How to verify the setup

Keep the property question fixed while comparing submission, downstream input and the final answer, checking that quoted or packing text stays within its source scope. After manufacturer material arrives, repeat the test with its new citation and outstanding conditions retained. Writing a selection result to trade or procurement systems requires business permissions, an operation contract and actual records. Completing the source question does not establish completed declaration, procurement or product review.
