# General export declarations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. A transport-disassembly note must remain associated with the illustrated machine. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed export product page illustrates a worktable and lists a top with legs. A continuation across the page break says the legs are removed for transport and packed separately with the goods. A question about assembled arrival needs the transport setting and included-but-separate relationship preserved. Removed does not mean omitted, while a worktable illustration does not establish an assembled delivery. Parsing should verify that the continuation still belongs to this product rather than assigning the second half to the next product merely because it starts on another page.

## Three common errors

One common error separates packed with the goods from legs and the answer interprets removal as exclusion. Another retrieves only the illustrated structure and treats it as arrival form. Compare page continuation, parsed order and actual blocks to distinguish lost component relationships from mixed use states. Assembled and separate-part search wording should still trace to original transport conditions; the question vocabulary cannot supply them. Installation service is also absent, so the material cannot establish who assembles the table after arrival or that an assembly arrangement already exists.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should search legs, for transport and packed separately with the goods, followed by the complete arrival-form question. Citations should explain both removal and continued inclusion. The answer needs to distinguish structure, transport packaging and assembly arrangements not supplied. After obtaining a packing list, inspect applicable product, actual processing and index state before retesting. Upload success neither repairs page-break membership by itself nor proves actual received contents. Compare original, parsed output and returned blocks, preserving earlier incorrect blocks as evidence for locating the specific relationship failure.
