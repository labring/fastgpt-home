# General import declarations: product questions with hybrid retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT hybrid retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the hybrid retrieval step

The proposed import invoice states “standard-model dark-blue goods.” Mark the original wording for model and colour, then inspect whether fused candidates keep those details attached to the same product. Retain the requested product in the complete question and observe whether a similar model passage moves ahead during reranking. The answer should trace its description to the invoice, without extending that sentence into performance or market-admission claims.

## Three common errors

One hundred pieces and ninety-nine pieces occur in the proposed commercial invoice and packing list respectively. A quantity check needs the product and total-piece wording from both sources, beyond the side most similar to the question. Preserve order and provenance, then inspect whether citations retain the meaning of total quantity. Batch and version correspondence still require confirmation. Different values raise a review point; ranking cannot establish a short shipment or a customs-declaration error. Retain both quantity statements with their units, without silently normalizing ninety-nine into one hundred. Even a short final response should leave both retrieved values traceable. Similar surrounding product text cannot erase a numerical difference between the supplied sources.

## How to verify the setup

The available invoice contains no evidence about the specified market-admission standard. Product name, colour and quantity cannot replace it. Use this missing-source question to inspect whether reranking encourages a general product description to be presented as conformity evidence. After obtaining the relevant standard and applicable material, repeat the question and inspect citation of the added evidence. Validate minimum relevance only under its documented conditions, without converting rank or score into an established admission outcome.
