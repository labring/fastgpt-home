# Amazon FBA preparation: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Flat packing and unfolded display need their separate form-setting sections retained. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed FBA product page says a folding storage basket can unfold. A packing section requires flat form for inbound handling, a display section says open it for presentation and a final note says these are state descriptions rather than included-item lists. Unfolding capability and package contents are separate questions. Parsing needs section headings and the note preserved. A display state cannot establish shipment form, and opening the basket cannot establish an included support accessory. The source describes states without supplying a bonus or accessory list.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original or chunk check | Specific source relationship | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section headings | Structure, packing and display | Check setting membership. |
| State conditions | Flat inbound, open for display | Do not treat as same-setting conflict. |
| Included-item boundary | State description is not contents list | Keep final note associated. |
| Separate queries | Unfolding, inbound form and contents | Inspect evidence returned for each question. |
| Section-level update | Revised packing and display version | Check actual processing and citations. |

## Three common errors

One common error puts flat and open into a headingless block and the answer declares a contradiction. Another separates the final note, allowing state descriptions to become package composition. Compare original section membership, setting words and chunk conditions to distinguish different purposes for the same subject. Unfolding and inbound search entries still require their own original data; shared keywords cannot establish included components. Retain source identity and version as well, since a new packing section combined with an earlier display section could otherwise create a comparison the document never made.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask whether the basket unfolds, which form applies inbound and whether included items are listed. Returned blocks should support the respective targets. Preserve unfolding capability, flat inbound form and the list gap without merging them into a package promise. After updating packing text, inspect processing and index state before repeating the original questions against cited versions. Uploading a new page does not establish that older sections stopped returning. Preserve original-to-parsed comparisons to check that settings and the final note remain attached after the update rather than becoming detached again.
