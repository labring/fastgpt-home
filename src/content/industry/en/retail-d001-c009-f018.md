# Amazon FBM fulfillment: product questions with hybrid retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Amazon FBM fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT hybrid retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the hybrid retrieval step

The proposed FBM picking-list example gives “forest green” and “enlarged three-person version” for a camping tent. Colour and size questions should trace those phrases to that item. Compare candidates for literal attributes and a complete product question, then inspect whether reranking preserves the association. This is a reading of self-drafted picking text: “three-person” does not supply load-bearing or usage results, and the size wording should not migrate into an answer about another version.

## Three common errors

The portable coffee machine is identified as X-zero-zero-one on the picking list and X-zero-zero-two on shipment confirmation. Check that candidates contain both identifiers before inspecting order and final citations. They are not interchangeable synonyms. Documented order identity and versions are needed to interpret the difference. A ranking model can change the order of existing candidates without deciding that an actual incorrect shipment took place. Preserve each literal model identifier with its file identity, without automatically standardizing the identifiers in the answer. Identify the linking material still needed as well. This separates a recorded wording difference from an assertion about what was actually shipped to the customer.

## How to verify the setup

The picking attachment provides no smartwatch battery-life information. Use that question to inspect whether the answer borrows a duration from another product. A highly ranked watch-name passage still supplies no battery-life evidence. After obtaining the corresponding specification, repeat the question and inspect candidate inclusion, filtering under the applicable conditions and complete citation of the new passage. Similar model names or score changes do not establish that battery life was verified.
