# JD POP seller operations: product questions with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For product questions in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Removing a battery cover does not itself establish battery-replacement capability. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed JD POP product note says the battery cover can be removed and refers battery-replacement conditions to an instruction. An appearance page describes convenient viewing inside. A question about user replacement has a cover action and missing conditions, while viewing supplies no replacement procedure. An entry saying user-replaceable battery would expand component access into a maintenance capability. Preserve cover, battery and instruction subjects. Being able to see the battery cannot establish supplied replacement steps or applicability to every user of the product.

## Three common errors

First, a cover action may be indexed as battery replacement; inspect the object of the original verb. Second, a replacement question may return only the appearance page; inspect whether the target supplies conditions rather than a visual description. Third, a similarly named entry may adopt instructions for another model; inspect product identity and version applicability. These concern action subject, content relevance and model correspondence. An entry title cannot decide the maintenance conclusion, and missing instructions cannot be filled with invented tools, procedures or completed operational results.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask what the cover permits, what the appearance page describes and where replacement conditions are documented. Inspect citations for merged subjects. Existing text can support cover removal and viewing, while battery replacement still depends on the applicable instruction. After obtaining it, inspect actual original data and entries before repeating questions against added conditions. File presence does not establish retargeting. Several entries for one original still cover that same passage; their count cannot establish that all maintenance conditions have been supplied or verified for the current product.
