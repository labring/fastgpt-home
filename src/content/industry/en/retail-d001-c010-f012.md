# JD POP seller operations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Desktop and wall-mounted rows of similarly named products need their variant hierarchy retained. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed JD POP page has a common storage-rack heading. Its table says the desktop variant includes a base, while mounting hardware for the wall variant is listed separately. A note requires reading contents by the corresponding variant. A generic contents question therefore needs variant differences retained rather than both rows merged into one accessory set. Inspect subrow identities during parsing and whether bases, mounting hardware and their variants remain traceable after chunking. A shared series heading does not establish identical contents for both products.

## Three common errors

One common error detaches the wall row from its variant heading and attaches separately listed hardware to the desktop product. Another returns base and mounting-hardware terms without qualifiers, allowing the answer to combine both variants. Compare original rows, parsed hierarchy and actual blocks to distinguish shifted scope from assembled contents. A series-level search entry still requires the specific original data supporting each item. Covering a series in search wording cannot establish complete accessory scope for every variant. If the question omits the variant, preserve that selection gap.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask about desktop, wall-mounted and unspecified-variant contents separately. Returned blocks need their variant qualifiers. The answer should attribute the base and separately listed hardware correctly without inventing the wall-hardware list. After revising one variant, inspect actual processing and index state and retest both variants to detect imported older rows. Uploading one revision cannot establish changed contents for the other variant or actual answer use of the update. Preserve each original row beside its block to locate whether a failure affects one variant or the whole hierarchy.
