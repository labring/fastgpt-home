# Convenience fresh-food orders: product questions with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. A large-portion photo example and an order unit do not supply actual serving specifications. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed fresh-food photo note says the large portion is illustrative, while an order table records boxes and refers serving size to a product booklet. A question about each box matching the pictured portion lacks actual size evidence. An entry cannot attach large portion to the sale item from the image note, and a box unit cannot be converted into serving contents without support. Preserve photography source, ordered subject and booklet reference separately. A common dish name cannot establish equivalence between a visual example and the specifications used for this order.

## Three common errors

First, an illustrative portion may be indexed as a sales specification; inspect the actual booklet entry. Second, an each-box question may return only box-based registration; inspect whether original data includes serving detail rather than a unit alone. Third, specifications from another menu period may be used here; inspect item, period and applicable version. These require attribute provenance, information coverage and subject-time correspondence. A visual or entry name cannot determine actual contents, and the exercise must not add weights, quantities or findings about the physical food.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask what the note means, how orders are recorded and which source confirms actual portions. Keep entry and citation scope visible. The illustration and box unit can be explained, while serving detail still needs the booklet. After adding it, inspect item applicability and actual processing before testing whether indexes reach the new entry. Upload does not establish adoption. Several entries containing box or large portion still need every original read; repeated photography notes cannot be counted as independent specification evidence or as confirmation of the order contents.
