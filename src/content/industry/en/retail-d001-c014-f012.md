# Convenience fresh-food orders: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Preserve product rows, separate footnotes and missing observations with their subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed fresh-food table labels a plain wrap as a whole portion and a sauce packet as separately packed. A footnote says the pictured halves are for display only. The question asks whether delivery includes a halved wrap. Parsing needs headings, both product rows and footnote attribution retained. Separate packing describes the sauce, while halving describes the display. Whole portion supplies no observation of an actual delivery. This is ordinary text simulating a table and caption, without demonstrated image understanding or inspection of real food.

## Three common errors

First, the sauce-packing cell may join the wrap row; compare original table, parsed text and chunks for row correspondence. Second, the halving footnote may lose display-only scope; inspect its photo reference and adjacent wording instead of turning display into product configuration. Third, updated whole-portion wording may still yield an older divided-portion excerpt; inspect original version, processing and index state with actual citations rather than upload presence. These concern table relationships, footnote scope and update adoption. Coexisting old and new text does not itself establish contradictory current product information.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the wrap row states, how sauce is packed and what the halving note describes, retaining the path from original through parsed text to reference. Repeat the same questions after chunk changes for traceable conditions; additional indexes cannot replace a row relationship absent from parsing. Without an actual delivery record, the delivery configuration remains unknown. The supplied text cannot confirm either a halved delivery or an undivided delivery. This exercise decides no saleability and places no order. Successful parsing concerns the proposed text structure only, proving no actual image processing or physical product condition beyond the material that was independently supplied.
