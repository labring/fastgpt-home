# Letter-of-credit documents: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Chunk continued item headings, abbreviations and attachment restrictions with the correct subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed product list in a document-review setting starts with a stand body, continues with a fastening plate as an accessory and excludes pictured tools in a note. A local abbreviation shortens fastening plate to plate. The question asks about included installation tools. Parsing must reconnect the continuation to the current product while preserving the exclusion. Tool appearance in a caption adds no supplied component, and a local abbreviation identifies no arbitrary plate-like accessory elsewhere. No actual documentary requirements are introduced; the test concerns the proposed list, caption and their structural references.

## Three common errors

First, a continued accessory may lose its parent item; inspect page order, headings and parsed row attribution. Second, the tool exclusion may form an unassigned fragment; inspect the caption relationship and retain not included with its product. Third, plate may inherit meaning in another product section; check abbreviation scope and chunk identity instead of treating the same word as one part. These concern continuation, exclusion and terminology scope. Chunk length or an index title cannot decide actual composition or restore a structural relationship missing from the parsed original.

## How to verify the setup

Ask separately about body, fastening plate and pictured tools, retaining originals, parsed text, chunks and adopted quotations. Repeat full term, abbreviation and natural questions after changes, inspecting subjects without guaranteeing equal retrieval. Missing exclusion text requires structural repair before indexing. After a list update, inspect processing and citation version rather than upload success. This exercise presents no documents and determines no formal review result. The exercise does not establish actual packing contents. Those contents require evidence beyond a successful comparison of the proposed product text and the excerpts returned for it.
