# Franchisee onboarding: product questions with hybrid retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT hybrid retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the hybrid retrieval step

The franchise-training product example lists a special coffee with coffee beans, milk and syrup in the proposed SOP attachment. Candidates should keep the drink name with that ingredient list before reranking a complete product question. This checks the manual’s wording without inferring an unsupplied recipe ratio, nutritional claim or effect. If preparation steps also enter candidates, inspect whether their length displaces the requested ingredient passage, retaining a citation to the corresponding manual.

## Three common errors

“Seasonal edition” in the SOP and “regular supply” in an attendance-sheet note have different source roles. Confirm that both were recalled, then inspect the product, period and version to which they refer. A note matching the question more closely cannot automatically change the SOP scope, and wording alone does not establish actual operating arrangements. Record whether reranking and citation trimming preserve both qualifications for the subsequent source review. Keep the attendance note’s supply statement separate from the SOP rule until their relationship is checked. A document appearing newer still needs version evidence. Neither retrieval order nor its filename establishes which wording currently applies to this particular product and period.

## How to verify the setup

The new item lacks detailed selection parameters and target-customer information. A training record’s existence does not supply that evidence. Inspect whether an audience answer borrows another drink’s description, then add the corresponding new-product material and repeat the question. Record new candidates, retention after reranking and traceable final citations separately. Check minimum relevance only where it applies; a related passage’s high score does not establish a confirmed audience position.
