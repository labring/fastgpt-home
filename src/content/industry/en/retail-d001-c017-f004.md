# Franchise fee settlement: product questions with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. A blank badge holder and separately arranged printing need separate index subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed franchise-related supplies note lists a blank badge holder with printing separately arranged. A service remark allows recording the store name. A question about the collected item already carrying that name needs blank state, collected information and printing action separated. The remark supplies no printing result. An entry saying named-store badge would convert a separately arranged service into a completed product attribute. Their shared opening-document context cannot create a customized item, and the proposed text contains no amounts or basis for reconstructing commercial terms.

## Three common errors

First, blank may be indexed as already printed; compare the supplies sentence with the added attribute. Second, a store-name question may target only the registration remark; inspect whether it concerns information collection or completed printing. Third, a finished-item record for another store may be used here; inspect store and item identities. These require state, action-type and subject-linkage checks. A store name or matching entry cannot replace result evidence, and registered information cannot establish that the separately arranged task was completed for this particular item.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask about the item initial state, the action allowed by the remark and evidence of printing for this store. Preserve blank, registration and separate arrangement in citations, leaving a named finished item dependent on its record without invented delivery. After a store record is added, inspect original data and index mapping before same-question comparison; upload success does not establish an updated entry. Several routes to a registration passage still establish registration only. Their number cannot create independent evidence that printing occurred or that the item received carries the store name.
