# Franchise fee settlement: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Keep included services, the exclusion of equipment procurement and illustrative footnotes tied to their subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed franchise-service list includes an opening guide, excludes equipment procurement from this item and labels a pictured checkout counter as illustrative only. The question asks whether the service includes the counter. Parsing needs the relationship between this service item and the equipment-procurement exclusion, together with footnote identity, preserved rather than adding pictured objects to deliverables. A guide may discuss checkout equipment without supplying it. No actual fee, deposit or responsibility terms are introduced. The exercise concerns the stated scope and relationships of a hypothetical list rather than a real commercial package or completed equipment transaction.

## Three common errors

First, the exclusion of equipment procurement may detach into another service section; compare list hierarchy and chunks to identify this item. Second, the counter caption may lose illustrative only; inspect caption and main-text references rather than turning display into delivery. Third, an equipment name inside the guide may become an included service component; inspect chapter purpose and actual list. These concern hierarchy, footnote scope and content type. Several related excerpts cannot combine into an unsupported package or equipment commitment when their original roles describe different kinds of information.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the service includes, which equipment-procurement work is outside this item and what the image illustrates, retaining parsed text and quotations. After chunk changes, repeat guide, counter and natural inclusion questions for source correspondence. A missing parsed equipment-procurement exclusion cannot be repaired by additional indexes. An updated list requires processing, original-data and actual-reference checks rather than upload success. This exercise interprets no actual charge or refund entitlement and proves no purchased equipment or delivered service. Real scope and execution need their own applicable evidence beyond the proposed text and its successfully parsed structural relationships.
