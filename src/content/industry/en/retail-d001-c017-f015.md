# Franchise fee settlement: product questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. A service line and equipment references may be retrieved while the equipment subjects still require the actual supply list. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A product-selection question inside franchise settlement material first needs to distinguish a service line from equipment detail. In this proposed invoice, the entry says franchise service item and refers equipment to a supply list. A payment-plan note says equipment matters are listed separately. These are two documentary references, with no equipment entries themselves. Searching for equipment names may still return the invoice and note. Identify their actual contribution: the project identity and a pointer to another source. Repeated references to the same missing list do not establish a configuration or add an equipment item to the supplied wording.

## Three common errors

For a follow-up asking which machine this item includes, this item initially refers to the known service line. Question optimization that inserts a machine name changes that subject. Retrieval of similar equipment vocabulary would still need an applicable supply source. A separate failure occurs when passage trimming retains equipment but drops see the supply list and listed separately, allowing the answer to describe equipment as already included. Compare the original question, optimized wording, complete candidate and actual citation. This locates whether an unsupported entity entered during rewriting, retrieval interpretation or answer composition, without assuming that every matching noun describes a supplied machine.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should inspect three distinct reading results: the kind of item the invoice establishes, the material to which the payment note delegates equipment details and whether the available text names any equipment with its configuration. The first two can trace to their respective statements; the third still needs the list text. If the actual supply list is later obtained, establish its relationship to the same service project before reading equipment subjects, configuration and exclusions, then trace its presence in candidates and citations. The earlier invoice remains useful for project identity. Adjoining old and new passages cannot establish that payment or equipment delivery actually occurred.
