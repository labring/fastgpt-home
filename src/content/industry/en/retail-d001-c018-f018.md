# Regional dealer stock: product questions with hybrid retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT hybrid retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the hybrid retrieval step

The proposed regional replenishment request uses “medium” for suggested replenishment volume and “standard carton” for packaging. Retrieval and reranking should preserve these qualitative descriptions, without converting them into an invented piece or carton count. Check that both passages for the requested model enter candidates, then inspect whether the complete question retains support for volume and packaging. An actual order quantity requires corresponding business evidence, beyond interpreting these sample terms.

## Three common errors

“Sufficient” in the request and “low” in the turnover sheet require product, timing and measurement-scope checks. The words may describe different scopes and do not alone establish an inventory-record error. Retain both statements and source identities in candidate inspection, then check whether reranking leaves only the side closest to the question. Investigate recall scope if one side is missing; where both exist, inspect order and citations for conditions affecting comparability. Inventory-state labels cannot be directly converted into a recommended purchase quantity. If the question turns into an ordering decision, inspect the quantity and condition evidence separately. “Low” is not a supplied numerical stock value from which the model can calculate replenishment.

## How to verify the setup

No shelf-life information has been supplied. The request therefore lacks evidence for whether the model meets near-term sales requirements, which also need their own source. Inspect whether the answer infers suitability from sufficient inventory or invents a date. After adding corresponding material, repeat the question and inspect new candidates, filtering and final citations separately. A reranking adjustment cannot create a shelf-life record or replace examination of the specific sales conditions.
