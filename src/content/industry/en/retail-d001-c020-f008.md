# Certificates of origin: product questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An origin statement in an application and a product’s material or use instructions are different reading objects. A proposed test can supply the application passage while noting that only an invoice item name is available, without manufacturer composition guidance. After submission, inspect whether the next step retains those origins. A stated origin must not become a performance claim, and application wording does not establish an issued certificate.

## Three common errors

For a product-property follow-up, a source owner may choose production guidance, review of the application text or deferral. Test each path against the evidence required by the question. Reading the application does not replace obtaining product instructions. If a supplementary-source choice yields no new material, inspect actual received values and retain the gap. The selection does not decide origin qualification or product quality; those require their own evidence and responsible review. A proposed production statement may identify an assembly location while the question asks about material composition. If the answer infers composition from location, trace its citation to the exact passage. Separate requests for process information and a material list. Inspect the contents of what arrives; a document title resembling a cost statement does not establish that the required composition evidence is present.

## How to verify the setup

After obtaining material, repeat the property question and compare the added passage, submission and final citations, keeping conclusions within the source’s scope. If production guidance remains insufficient, identify the remaining requirement and its provider. Amending or submitting a certificate application needs a separate authorized business operation and real result. Continued question answering or interaction does not establish certificate issuance, product acceptance or completed customer clearance.
