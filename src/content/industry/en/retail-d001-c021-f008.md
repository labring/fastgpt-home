# Brand member operations: product questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Member product answers preserve benefit applicability, product differences and information versions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Suppose a member page limits a benefit to a specified series, a product card lists material without dimensions and an older group message concerns another campaign. Collect item, benefit question and source, let a person choose specification review or applicability evidence, then inspect original wording received downstream. Collecting a member question establishes no eligibility, and a user choice is not a result from a membership database.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Member-product workflow | Proposed qualification | Inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benefit series | Specified range | No all-item claim |
| Product size | Absent from card | No fit inference |
| Historical message | Another campaign | Check version/currency |
| Choice route | Specification or benefit | Actual source wording |
| Benefit issuance | Separate business interface | Eligibility and authority |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

A series restriction extended to all items needs benefit scope checked. Missing dimensions followed by a fit claim need size evidence checked. An older campaign applied to the current benefit needs version checked. These examine scope, specification gaps and currency. No real eligibility certification or purchase outcome is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Trace both routes sentence by sentence to sources and leave absent dimensions open. Added dimensions complete specification evidence alone; eligibility remains separate. Keep the older message in comparison material. Benefit issuance, member validation and order changes need independent business interfaces and authority. Orchestration transfers collected information without making those business decisions. Separate available material information from missing dimensions, placing the benefit wording beside its applicable series for source review.
