# Brand member operations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Preserve sections, participation scope and illustrative benefits in member-service text. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed member-service page separates an online-event section offering material after signup from an in-store experience section with places to verify. A caption marks pictured gifts as examples only. The question asks whether online signup includes the experience and gifts. Parsing needs section hierarchy and caption qualifiers, rather than merging adjacent material into online benefits. Receiving a document does not include another event. No actual member rule or commercial entitlement is supplied; the exercise concerns the structure and source of statements on a hypothetical service page.

## Three common errors

First, in-store experience may merge into the online item; compare headings and parsed rows for scope attribution. Second, a pictured gift may lose example only; inspect caption and service relationships instead of treating display as an included item. Third, signup may detach from receiving material; inspect the prerequisite-action relationship within chunks, without expanding access to every visitor. These concern section identity, illustration and participation condition. Adjacent excerpts cannot combine into a package of benefits that none of the original sections independently establishes for the current online service.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the online section, store section and gift caption each state, retaining hierarchy, parsed text and quotations. Repeat signup, experience and natural inclusion questions for preserved headings and qualifiers. After updates, inspect original data, processing and index state rather than assuming an added file changes actual references. Missing event confirmation remains unresolved without proving participation impossible. This test grants no benefit, collects no member data and sends no notification. It determines no actual permission validity or real service outcome beyond the proposed page and the structural evidence preserved for its reading.
