# Brand member operations: product questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A new membership-product rule test says, “The member gift set contains a trial pack; the full-size item is selected separately.” Inspect candidates for trial and full-size terms before comparing modes with the complete contents question. The membership label supplies a hypothetical context, without real personal data. A product-keyword match cannot convert the optional full-size item into established gift-set contents.

## Three common errors

A proposed outreach plan says, “Full-size display illustration; this does not describe the gift-set configuration.” It is ordinary text without an actual image. Using a displayed item as the set’s contents misuses that source. Retain the rule, plan wording and actual citation together. Inspect token trimming for a lost exclusion or separate-selection condition, rather than an isolated full-size noun. Joint retrieval of an outreach plan and a product rule does not supply a new gift-set list. A third error extends a full-size display in one contact plan to every member activity. Check the plan activity and period against this bundle rule. A single display neither covers other campaigns nor supplies the current contents list.

## How to verify the setup

No trial-pack specification was supplied. The rule and display wording cannot provide capacity or material. For a size follow-up, inspect whether optimization retains the trial pack instead of importing full-size specifications. After obtaining the corresponding product explanation, repeat the question and trace the new citation, recording source and retrieval-setting changes separately. Check minimum relevance under semantic or reranking conditions. A score does not establish complete gift attributes or any real member’s purchase or authorization state.
