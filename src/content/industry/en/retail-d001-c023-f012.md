# Brand repair services: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Preserve component hierarchy, replaceable markers and neighbouring-model notes in repair-product text. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed brand-repair component tree puts a separately replaceable trim piece under the housing and a connector socket under the board with conditions in another instruction. A neighbouring-model sidebar describes a complete board assembly. The question concerns separate replacement of the socket. Parsing must keep the replaceable marker with the trim piece, the unresolved instruction with the socket and the sidebar with its other model. Sharing a page transfers no capability to every component or construction approach across models. No actual repair steps or operational authority are supplied by the proposed tree.

## Three common errors

First, flattening the tree may apply separate replacement to the socket; inspect parent-child relationships and marker attribution in parsed text. Second, the instruction reference may become an unassigned fragment; inspect the socket relationship instead of filling missing feasibility. Third, another-model sidebar may merge into the current product; compare model, component and layout with actual citation identity. These concern hierarchy, condition source and model correspondence. Page proximity or additional indexes do not establish repairability or provide missing tools or procedures for the part actually asked about.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about the trim marker, socket conditions and sidebar model, retaining original hierarchy, parsed text and references. Repeat full part names and natural replacement questions after chunk changes for correctly assigned restrictions, without promising exact matching. Added instructions need current model and version checked before processing and citation adoption; upload success does not establish that the current source has been adopted. Missing conditions remain unresolved rather than establishing impossible replacement. This lesson includes no physical repair or model-effect test, decides no warranty responsibility and establishes no restored real function beyond applicable evidence supplied for the actual device.
