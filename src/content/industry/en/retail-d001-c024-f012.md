# Brand concession operations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Parse orientation notes, installation illustrations and accessory fields with their subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed counter product page titles the base vertical, allows the display paper to lie horizontally, lists blank paper separately and marks an illustration as placement only. The question asks whether the product is horizontal and includes paper. Parsing needs base and paper subjects retained: paper orientation changes no base design, and separate provision establishes no inclusion. A chunk keeping only horizontal and pictured paper could invent a different product. The illustration is simulated as ordinary text, without demonstrated image recognition or actual installation of the object.

## Three common errors

First, the horizontal sentence may lose display paper as its subject; compare source grammar with parsed wording. Second, separately provided paper may become an unassigned accessory fragment; inspect field, heading and base identity so absence is not filled as inclusion. Third, a placement illustration may become a specification; inspect caption purpose and main text for display actions. These concern subject, accessory state and illustration identity. Adjacent fragments establish neither a horizontal product nor a paper-included set, and visual appearance determines no actual processing route for the supplied file.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about base title, paper orientation and accessory state, tracing each answer to its own wording. After chunk edits, repeat vertical-base, horizontal-paper and natural inclusion questions without promising precise matching. Missing accessory text needs parsing inspection rather than guessed indexes. Updated pages require processing, original-data and reference-version checks, not upload presence. This exercise tests no physical installation or material dimensions, purchases no counter sign and establishes no actual approved store placement. Those outcomes need applicable evidence beyond the proposed layout and the textual relationships retained in its returned excerpts.
