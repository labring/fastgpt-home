# Brand outlet operations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Keep outlet state labels, care references and other-design captions separate. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed outlet page labels a design from an earlier season, refers fabric details to an unavailable inner page and captions a neighbouring image as a new-design cuff example. The question asks whether the earlier design has that cuff and verified fabric. Parsing must retain the other-design caption. Season identifies timing rather than material or condition. Chunking cannot join the new cuff to the current design or treat an inner-page directory entry as read content. No real brand or material performance is supplied; the exercise concerns textual subjects and page relationships only.

## Three common errors

First, a new-design caption may cross columns into earlier-design text; compare layout, design identity and parsed wording. Second, an inner-page reference may become a complete fabric description; inspect actual original coverage and obtain the matching page. Third, an earlier-season label may summarize into damaged or unsuitable; read its meaning and seek actual condition evidence rather than converting timing into quality. These concern design, reference and attribute scope. Neighbouring pages or related terms cannot supply absent product details or extend an observation that no current source actually states.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the label establishes, which design the image concerns and where fabric evidence is, retaining original, parsed text and citations. After chunk changes, repeat season, cuff and natural fabric questions for source subjects without assuming exact matching. Added inner pages need current design and version checked before processing and actual index-state adoption. Upload success is no adoption evidence. Missing detail establishes no nonconforming material. This test includes no real fitting or inspection, changes no price or discount and derives no actual quality or business state from season alone.
