# Dealer ordering events: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Retain applicability across collection introductions, design differences and colour-card notes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed ordering catalogue introduces a lightweight collection, gives design A a removable hood and design B no hood, and labels colour cards as approximate display only. The question asks whether B has A’s hood and exactly matches the card. Parsing needs design rows and colour qualifiers retained instead of applying a collection introduction or A’s feature to every item. Approximate display supplies no observed colour-difference result. No actual material or colour standard is introduced; the exercise checks the respective scope of introduction, item table and footnote in the proposed catalogue.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Material check | Conditions in this test | Implementation evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design row | A and B separate | Inspect no-hood retention. |
| Collection introduction | Lightweight | Do not make measured result. |
| Colour-card condition | Approximate display | Retain with caption. |
| Updated design | Current model table | Inspect citation version. |
| Actual selection | No order confirmation | Do not claim selected design. |

## Three common errors

First, A’s removable hood may cross into B; compare model rows and parsed output for retained no hood. Second, a colour footnote may detach from the card; inspect its reference instead of converting approximate into exact correspondence. Third, lightweight introduction may become a measured design weight; check meaning and actual item evidence rather than deriving measurements from promotional text. These concern design identity, display conditions and attribute evidence. Related excerpts from one collection cannot establish a physical test or complete product capability that no original sentence supports.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about both hood configurations, the colour-card purpose and the introduction, retaining structure, parsed text and actual quotations. After chunk changes, repeat design-name, removable-hood and natural colour questions for subject correspondence without assuming precise matching. An updated table requires processing and index-state checks; old-table adoption needs actual references inspected. Missing measurements establish neither weight nor colour difference. This page performs no fitting, inspection or ordering and derives no real dealer selection or supply confirmation from a clearly written hypothetical catalogue or its successfully parsed rows.
