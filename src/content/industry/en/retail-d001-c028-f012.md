# Brand livestream operations: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Separate quoted remarks, script suggestions and specification fields on livestream product cards. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed brand-livestream card has a specification requiring the lid to be tightened, a script suggesting one-handed lifting and a quoted viewer asking whether it can be inverted. The question concerns inverted use. Parsing needs specification, suggestion and question identities. Asking establishes no capability, and lifting demonstrates no inversion. Losing quotation or section labels could turn a question into an asserted attribute. These hypothetical texts supply no real sealing or experiment data, and the lid-tightening statement supports no inferred physical performance for the whole cup beyond its own wording.

## Three common errors

First, a viewer question may lose whether and become an inversion statement; compare quotation area, grammar and parsed text for retained question identity. Second, planned one-handed lifting may become specification; inspect section purpose and actual product evidence rather than deriving capability from a script. Third, tightening may detach from the lid; inspect action-subject relationship without leaving an unassigned condition or broadening it to whole-product effects. These concern quotation, content type and condition. Action words sharing a page establish no actual physical performance for the item under discussion.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the specification requires, what the script proposes and what the viewer asks, tracing each answer to its section. After chunk changes, repeat tightening, one-handed and natural inversion questions for actual quotations, retaining questions as questions without promising retrieval. Updated cards require processing and original-version checks; upload alone establishes no adopted condition. Missing testing remains unresolved. This page runs no stream and performs no inversion experiment. It guarantees no safety or performance and presents no proposed viewer question as real customer feedback or a measured product result.
