# Retail packaging reporting: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Preserve unresolved material, display labels and local scope across supplies-page columns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed supplies page has a handled bag with material pending in the left column, a paper-bag style reference on the right and a shared note saying illustrations do not prove physical material. The question asks whether the left item is confirmed paper. Parsing must retain column identity and the common note instead of joining paper and bag through proximity. Unresolved material does not establish non-paper composition. This exercise introduces no actual reporting scheme or environmental property, and its illustrations are simulated in ordinary text without material testing or image-processing results.

## Three common errors

First, reading order may join the right style to the left item; compare layout, parsed sequence and block identity. Second, the shared note may attach only to the right column; inspect which illustrations it covers so the left retains its evidence limitation. Third, a summary may drop material pending; compare originals and actual references for the missing-support state. These concern reading order, note coverage and status. A paper-related index term does not establish the item’s actual material when the source provides only a separate style reference and no current material confirmation.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about both columns, illustration purpose and unresolved items, retaining original layout, parsed text and citations. After chunk changes, repeat handled-bag, paper-bag and natural material questions without guaranteeing equal matching. A parser that merges columns needs structural repair before indexing. Added material pages require product, processing and reference-version checks; upload is no adopted evidence. This test performs no material inspection and determines no compliance or environmental outcome. It submits no supplies report and establishes no physical property beyond what the actual applicable source text supports for the item.
