# Retail checkout and loyalty: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Retail checkout and loyalty, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Keep voucher sample markings, blank media and backend-service wording in separate sections. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed checkout catalogue separates a layout-only voucher that cannot be redeemed, a blank card and a service whose rule import remains for discussion. The question asks whether buying the card includes effective campaign benefits. Parsing must retain section identities: a displayed sample is no redeemable voucher, blank media supplies no entitlement and discussed import is no delivered service. Joining short cells cannot create a configured promotion. Card artwork supplies neither a benefit value nor member identity or backend action. These are hypothetical ordinary texts rather than real campaign or customer records.

## Three common errors

First, the sample’s redemption exclusion may become an unassigned fragment; compare section and original data to retain purpose. Second, rule-import service wording may become card contents; inspect service-versus-material boundaries instead of making proposed work a physical inclusion. Third, an earlier effective-voucher page may serve a similarly named block; inspect event, card and version rather than transferring benefits through artwork. These concern exclusion, service hierarchy and source identity. Complete-looking chunks or multiple indexes do not establish that a campaign is effective when the original subjects and states remain separate.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the sample does, what the blank card contains and which stage the service has, retaining sections, parsed text and references. Repeat sample, blank-card and natural benefit questions after changes for attached restrictions. Missing exclusion parsing requires text repair before any redemption conclusion. Updated rule pages need event and processing checks with actual citations; upload does not establish backend activation. This exercise issues no voucher, binds no member and executes no redemption. It establishes no real benefits or effects without independent applicable evidence for an actual campaign and its configuration.
