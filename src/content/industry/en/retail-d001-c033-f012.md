# Trade and FX administration: product questions with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Trace trade-product subjects, local aliases and packing fields by their original columns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed trade-product table identifies raw glass sheets as the item, defines sheet as a local abbreviation and assigns wooden racks to packaging. A footnote refers collection-party details to a separate transaction page. The question concerns racks as product components and whether the receiver is also the producer. Parsing needs columns and local terminology preserved. Missing transaction text cannot supply a party. Packaging describes arrangement without becoming the traded item, and a company name in a title establishes neither production identity nor the current collection relationship for this proposed transaction.

## Three common errors

First, wooden packaging may merge into product contents; compare headings, parsed columns and blocks for the item relationship. Second, the local abbreviation may carry into a different material page; inspect its definition scope instead of equating terms with one product. Third, a transaction reference may become a claim that party details were read; inspect the actual page and current correspondence, retaining absence. These concern columns, terminology and source coverage. A single product table cannot establish every trade relationship or provide fund-handling conditions missing from its own wording.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the product is, what the rack does and where party evidence is, retaining original-to-text-to-reference correspondence. Repeat full name, local alias and natural composition questions after chunk changes for headings and references without assuming exact retrieval. Updated trade or product pages need transaction, processing and citation-version checks; upload establishes no completed review. These materials contain no actual purchase, collection or production proof. The exercise proposes no exchange arrangement, determines no qualification and performs no real financial action beyond inspecting the supported scope of the hypothetical product text.
