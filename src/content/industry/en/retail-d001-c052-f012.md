# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: document chunking for product questions

## What the documents look like

Pinduoduo low-price campaign operations revolve around low-price resource slots, campaign inventory, and refund-rate swings. Four document families carry most of the evidence: campaign registration forms, product cost calculation sheets, after-sales refund orders, and platform internal messages. These materials update daily or once per campaign, and they drive inventory estimation, abnormal-refund tracking, and campaign-price gross-margin checks.

The chunking step has to cut stable fields out of those materials: campaign price, registration quantity, cost items, refund status. A product question then has to be traceable back to a specific material and version. Because the same product appears in several campaign sessions at different prices, chunk boundaries must keep the session scope attached to the price and quantity it governs.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

In FastGPT, a knowledge base is made of library, collection, and data. The smallest search unit is the library; collections only classify data and do not affect search results. Vector source data lives in the MongoDB `dataset.datas` table, and an `indexes` field records the corresponding vector IDs, so one group of data can map to multiple vectors. During retrieval, vectors are recalled first, then the vector IDs are used to find source content in MongoDB; matches to the same source group are merged and the highest vector score is kept.

Segmentation must keep conditions, descriptions, and the content they constrain together. If a price condition in a campaign registration form is separated from the product range it applies to, a selection question cannot return a complete basis. Semantic retrieval depends on vector distance; full-text retrieval suits precise lookups such as numbers, models, and proper nouns; hybrid retrieval merges multiple result paths through RRF; ReRank performs a second ordering of candidate text. None of these steps performs permission or entity isolation, and retrieval results do not replace authorization checks.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a successful upload as an updated retrieval state. After material changes, the actual training and index status must be checked; the upload action itself does not indicate that new segments can be recalled.
2. Treating semantic recall or a relevance score as a permission or entity filter. A similarity setting does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records, and retrieval results still require manual checking of the authorization boundary.
3. Describing RRF after hybrid retrieval as a separate switch that can be turned on. RRF is a result fusion process, not a configuration item; reranking requires a model and must be enabled according to settings.

## How to verify the setup

Hypothetical exercise sample: the source text is "Campaign registration form: product A, campaign number HD-0715, registration price 9.9 yuan, registration quantity 500 units, applicable session July 15 to July 17. Product cost calculation sheet: product A unit cost 8.2 yuan, including logistics 1.1 yuan." The draft sentence to be judged is "Product A can register 500 units at 9.9 yuan in session HD-0715, and the gross margin is negative." The judgment compares whether registration price, cost items, and quantity share the same subject, the same session, and the same field.

1. Input the draft sentence and the source text, and compare whether product A's campaign number, registration price, and cost items agree. If total cost exceeds the registration price, the draft sentence's gross-margin judgment is supported; if the cost field is missing, record it as unsupported.
2. Search "HD-0715" and "product A registration price" separately, and compare whether returned segments contain the campaign number, registration price, and session range. If a segment keeps the condition sentence intact, the check condition is met; if condition and price are separated, record a segmentation problem.
3. Input an empty-result downstream scenario, using the explicit paper input [] to check the downstream display design, and observe whether the system handles it as an empty citation. Do not claim that a fictional rule's retrieval necessarily returns []. If the downstream design does not define empty-result behavior, record it as pending verification.
