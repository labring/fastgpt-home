# Livestream sample inspections: product questions with model connections

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Livestream sample inspections, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Separate sample findings, credential subjects and restricted scope without inventing sale eligibility. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Suppose a sample sheet records tactile observations, a test report covers another specification, supplier credentials identify an entity, and the market scope of a restricted-products list needs checking. Inspect the texts and subjects actually received by the model. A tactile opinion establishes neither safety compliance nor sale eligibility. This exercise supplies no actual test metric or platform admission result. Document count and response speed do not prove a sample/report match.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

- If a report’s specification field is omitted and the answer approves this sample, compare the report page with actual input to locate lost subject information.

- If supplier credentials prove product performance, inspect their entity-identification purpose and separate test evidence. Entity records replace no sample result.

- If the list’s market scope disappears and all channels become eligible, compare the rule scope with the proposed destination. Missing corresponding rules remain unresolved.

## How to verify the setup

Hold the sample sheet and report fixed and ask which evidence supports observation, testing and sale eligibility separately. A same-specification report should update only its supported conclusion. Model or parameter comparisons need the same sources and questions, with actual input and citations retained. Test image reports separately without accuracy or certification promises. Actual selection still requires responsible review of testing, channel rules and authority; this exercise lists no product for sale.
