# Group-buy presale orders: vector indexes for product questions

## What the documents look like

Group-buy presale operations collect orders first and organize procurement and fulfillment afterward. The materials handled in product information and selection Q&A are the presale product table, order-collection results, the procurement plan, and the order-cutoff notice. These materials update daily and per session, and product specifications, supplier details, and arrival information can change around each cutoff. The people asking selection questions are typically platform operations, procurement planning, and supply chain scheduling staff, and their questions concern the specification, supplier, planned quantity, and cutoff arrangement of a product in a given presale session. Because updates are frequent, ingestion and indexing need to follow the material version, and any cited original text must correspond to a specific session and a specific product.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Knowledge bases associated for retrieval must use the same vector model; check the actual model and input type first. One original data block can be associated with multiple indexes, and the index and the original content are checked separately. Index content is built from complete and focused information, and retrieval coverage and index length are weighed together against samples. Image retrieval also depends on whether ingestion generated an image description or an image vector, and the query side alone does not decide this. After a model or index change, compare cited original text using the same set of questions, and do not promise results from a vector score alone.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: the source material is three sentences. "The presale product for this session is 'Chunsee Ponkan, 5-jin pack,' the supplier is batch A, and the planned procurement quantity is determined by the order-collection result." "The order cutoff is 20:00 on the day after this session opens, and no new orders are accepted after cutoff." "The procurement plan is compiled by the procurement planning role after cutoff, and warehouse routing is arranged separately." One draft sentence to be assessed is: "This product can still accept additional orders after cutoff, and the procurement planning role adjusts procurement accordingly." The comparison object is the three source sentences above and this draft sentence, and the assessment scope is limited to this session's presale product information. The draft sentence and the second source sentence take mutually exclusive values on the field of whether new orders are accepted after cutoff, so the draft is unsupported.

Retrieval and relevance do not perform permission or entity isolation. Knowledge bases with different vector models cannot be associated together; check identifiers, exclude those that do not match, and record the actual observation. RRF is a fusion process for mixed results and is not an independent switch. Image retrieval requires corresponding image input and model capability, and reranking requires a model and is enabled according to settings.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval as authorization. Retrieving a product record does not mean that record has been approved for this session's procurement or handover. The draft sentence "can still accept additional orders after cutoff" has no corresponding support in the source text, and a retrieval hit does not change that gap.
2. Associating knowledge bases with different vector models together. The documentation states that associated knowledge bases must use the same vector model. When a knowledge base has an inconsistent model identifier, exclude it, and do not run cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.
3. Treating image retrieval as a default path. Image retrieval requires image input and model capability, and whether an image description or image vector index was generated at ingestion must be checked separately. Uploading an image on the query side does not replace the ingestion index.
