# Cross-border settlements: product questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A product identifier in settlement material can locate a transaction entry without supplying use or performance instructions. A proposed record may contain an identifier in the statement and an item name in an ERP order, with their correspondence still unconfirmed. After collecting both passages and the selection question, inspect whether the next input preserves their origins. Similar names must not become an assumed identical product, and settlement or exchange records cannot supply unstated product guidance.

## Three common errors

A source owner may choose to obtain a catalogue, inspect order mapping or defer the property answer. Test what each branch receives. If the catalogue-request route returns only the original settlement entry, inspect the connection and actual input rather than claim completed supplementation. Where order currency, settlement currency or rate date appear, preserve their separate meanings; financial-record descriptions must not be merged into product attributes. A settlement currency does not establish product origin or the markets in which an item is suitable for sale. If a product question becomes a regional-compliance conclusion, trace the regional information to either product evidence or settlement wording. Remove the unsupported inference and obtain the relevant product source. Completing a reading of receipt entries does not establish product-property or market-admission review.

## How to verify the setup

After catalogue or product guidance arrives, repeat the original question and inspect values and citations, establishing correspondence from evidence while retaining absent use conditions. This source check supports only what was supplied, without unsupported conclusions about earnings, tax or payment outcomes. Product-master changes, stock queries or other business updates require separate authorization and actual responses. A completed form does not establish confirmed product matching or an executed external operation.
