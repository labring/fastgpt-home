# Cross-border settlements: product questions with model connections

## What the documents look like

For product questions in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Identify which product questions settlement entries support, separating model connectivity from complete specification evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Cross-border settlement material may include product entries, while its primary purpose concerns funds collection and reconciliation. A new proposed attachment supplies only a product name and settlement classification, without specifications or use conditions. Inspect delivery of those originals to the model before checking whether output converts a financial classification into product performance. The current source cannot verify such a selection conclusion. It needs the relevant product documentation; a successful service response does not establish that specification evidence is complete.

## Three common errors

- If settlement entries are presented as read without reaching the request, inspect export version, source selection and model input. Retain service responses separately from evidence of field delivery.

- If similarly named settlement and ERP entries become the same product, compare identities and answer, then obtain product or order correspondence. Name resemblance cannot establish that mapping.

- If a financial category becomes a specification or capability-for-use claim, trace each addition. Without technical conditions the judgment remains unverifiable; add product evidence and repeat the question.

## How to verify the setup

Suitability for a use requires the use requirement, technical conditions or applicable description; a financial entry does not provide them. Trace any added dimension, capability or use conclusion sentence by sentence and record unsupported statements. After adding the product source, hold question and application settings constant, retaining the service response, actual input and originals adopted in the answer separately. The text test does not validate unused image or other capabilities. A changed input form requires the corresponding test rather than an assumption about coverage.
