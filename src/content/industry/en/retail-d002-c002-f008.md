# MCN operations: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An after-sales clause in a creator contract describes agreed responsibilities between cooperation parties. An individual consumer order needs a separate record set. A proposed input can state that the responsibility passage is supplied while evidence for the current consumer return is absent. Pass that passage and inquiry through the form to the next step. Inspect whether the answer distinguishes contractual explanation from an order lookup, without turning an assigned responsibility into an already processed refund.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input for this page | Observed acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Responsibility reading | The creator contract’s service-responsibility passage | It is not rewritten as an order-processing result |
| Order evidence | The current inquiry and missing return proof | The recipient preserves the contract/order distinction |
| Handoff choices | Obtain order records, read terms or contact service operations | Record each destination and its received content |
| Rule supplementation | Consumer return policy absent from the contract | Citations use the policy rather than commission terms |
| Operation result | Actual customer-service or order-system response | Verify created records, approval and operation status separately |

## Three common errors

A violation record mentioning product quality does not independently establish the consumer’s after-sales entitlement. First collect its relationship to the contract. A source owner can then choose to request order evidence, review the cooperation terms or hand the issue to the actual service operator. Test every connected destination and the content sent on the handoff path. Selecting handoff does not establish a created ticket, and responsibility wording is not business approval. For example, a consumer refund-progress question may reach a step supplied only with creator commission terms. Inspect the relationship between the current question and received material. Keep the commission explanation within its actual scope, obtain processing evidence for this order and compare both answers. Contractual allocation of settlement work does not establish receipt of a consumer refund.

## How to verify the setup

If the available contract covers commission settlement but omits consumer return and refund rules, identify the rules needed, their provider and receiving step. After supplementation, repeat the inquiry and inspect downstream input and citations, keeping commission terms separate from consumer policy. Submission to a customer-service or order system requires an actual interface, authorization and business response. This answer can describe obtained evidence and outstanding work, without claiming an unverified operation.
