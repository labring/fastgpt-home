# Shopify fulfillment: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An independent-store order status, shipping label and carrier record should retain separate origins in the flow. A proposed text sample can state that a label was generated while carrier-pickup evidence remains absent. After form submission, inspect the next step’s actual input and check that label generation has not become collected by carrier. A merchant’s configured fulfillment method or shipping-rate information also cannot independently establish delivery of this particular order.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input for this page | Observed acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label and pickup | A generated label with carrier-receipt evidence missing | The next input keeps these states separate |
| Form receipt | Order source and the original service inquiry | The recipient receives text with its source identity |
| After-sales branches | Obtain carrier records, inspect a refund or read terms | Each choice follows only its connected route |
| Receipt and policy gaps | Delivery proof and applicable return rules | Retest the inquiry and preserve unresolved facts |
| Actual operation | A return, refund or fulfillment-change response | Verify the business record separately from conversational completion |

## Three common errors

An order record and a partial-refund record can describe different aspects of one transaction. Establish their relationship before selecting a service route. Proposed choices may request carrier evidence, inspect the refund source or continue reading service terms. Test each connected recipient and its values. A choice arranges the next reading or handoff; it does not decide refund sufficiency or proof of receipt, and must not establish an unconfirmed order change. Also test the order correspondence of a refund record. Supplying processing evidence for another order still leaves this inquiry without progress evidence. Compare the subjects of the order details and refund material, remove the unrelated record and pass the current evidence gap to the receiving person. Inspect whether the answer stops carrying forward that incorrect processing state.

## How to verify the setup

Product information and a delivery address leave gaps when the applicable return policy or receipt evidence is unavailable. Assign those gaps to defined source providers. After completion, repeat the inquiry and inspect transfer of historical input and new material, checking whether the answer mistakes an address for delivery proof. Creating a return, refund or fulfillment change requires separate verification of the business interface, permissions and actual record beyond the end of this conversation.
