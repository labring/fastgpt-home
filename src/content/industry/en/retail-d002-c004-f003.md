# Temu semi-managed sellers: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test sharing boundaries with a specified warehouse issue summary, retaining separate evidence for stock, logistics and platform handling. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service-collaboration entry provides an issue summary for one warehouse, excluding other stock tables and internal procurement calculations. Inspect product, warehouse and period through the complete link, then test restricted sources. Retain paraphrased quantities or conditions to investigate grants; one product does not establish one universal scope. The entry identifier verifies no warehouse personnel and grants no external operation rights. Confirm the intended summary through actual reads.

## Three common errors

- If a specified-warehouse summary exposes another quantity or internal calculation, retain product, warehouse, period and full response. One product does not authorize all warehouse sources.

- If a computer-stock to phone-dispatch question lacks order history, inspect warehouse and prior question, then obtain the source. Anonymous identity does not inherit subjects from similar products.

- If stock becomes dispatched or platform-handled, compare inventory, carrier and handling originals. Separate unavailable results from omitted input; a reply synchronizes no WMS and submits no declaration.

## How to verify the setup

Test the intended summary, another warehouse’s table and internal calculation for reads and denials, recording originals and version-specific access conditions. Required identity validation needs separate commercial capability and return checks. Distinguish an available carrier record omitted from input from an unobtained original, investigating transfer or collecting evidence respectively. Replies may organize required material without synchronizing WMS, submitting platform handling or editing declarations. Confirm fulfillment and resolution against actual systems and the responsible owner.
