## What the documents look like

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose a platform ticket acknowledges documents only, a seller warehouse records carrier handoff and buyer receipt evidence is absent. Use chatId to align the conversation with the current order, then inspect subjects and history actually delivered to the model. Even after checking detail and responseData under the official contract, inspect returned scope. Present fields establish no complete coverage across platform, seller and carrier.

## Three common errors

Platform intake reported as resolved dispute needs actual handling findings checked. Seller handoff reported as buyer receipt needs event parties checked. An older order explanation applied here needs order identity and version checked. These examine stage, roles and historical ownership without supplying a real dispute or delivery outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Retain redacted identity, input and original answer, checking historical sources separately for debug and production. Missing receipt stays unreviewed, while omitted detail proves no absent delivery. Added platform findings update their handling state alone. Logistics execution, service decisions and business writes depend on real authorized procedures and evidence. The log shows the material chain used to form an answer. Link platform findings and logistics events separately to this order; seller-warehouse handoff cannot stand for buyer confirmation of receipt.
