# General export declarations: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Retain the contractual delivery time separately from actual fulfillment records in the export workflow. A proposed text test may supply the delivery clause while transport and receipt evidence remain unavailable. Collect the inquiry scope and passage through the form, then check that an agreed time has not become an observed shipment or receipt. Where declarations or packing records are supplied, answer only within their statements; a complete document set does not establish fulfilled service responsibilities.

## Three common errors

For a delivery-status follow-up, proposed human choices can obtain transport records, inspect the contract commitment or hand the issue to the fulfillment owner. Verify which sources reach each branch and check for mixed transactions or time points. Interpret a recorded status against its actual subject. Selecting handoff establishes a route, not the recipient’s accepted task, completed delivery or agreement to a service remedy; those need separate business evidence. A receipt statement for another shipment cannot support this delivery merely because its date is close. Compare the contract’s subject, transport-document identity and recipient, recording the unresolved correspondence. Remove the other shipment’s evidence and verify that the supplementation route receives the current delivery question rather than proceeding directly to an answer asserting delivery.

## How to verify the setup

Assign missing transport, receipt or service evidence to a provider and receiving step. After completion, repeat the inquiry and inspect whether the added content reaches the answer and resolves the original gap. Creating a task in logistics, trade or service systems requires an interface and authorization check, followed by the actual created record and response. Continuing conversation or a completed submission cannot replace fulfillment and service-operation receipts.
