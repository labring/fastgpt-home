# Amazon FBA preparation: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Follow planned, in-transit, received and claim records without converting inventory stages into a service conclusion. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A proposed inventory exercise lists cartons planned for dispatch and cartons received, which describe different events. Before asking what to do about the missing ones, identify whether the question concerns an undispatched plan, transit awaiting receipt or an observed receiving difference. Preserve period and shipment in the prompt instead of labeling the difference lost. If the answer treats every unreceived carton as compensable, inspect delivery and receiving evidence in input. Without those sources, the claim judgment remains unverifiable.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Question handoff | Identify missing ones and those cartons | Original and optimized question correspondence |

## Three common errors

- If the dispatch-plan versus receipt difference becomes lost or compensable stock, inspect shipment, period and carrier events beside the conclusion. Unreceived stock alone establishes no responsibility.

- If a new receiving event still yields an old difference or another shipment, retain update, actual history and optimized question. Distinguish omitted delivery from overwrite after arrival.

- If a claim attachment becomes an accepted or awarded claim, trace the actual handling result. Application wording supplies no such evidence; retain the gap rather than using conversation completion as handling proof.

## How to verify the setup

A claim draft may organize known differences and missing documents, but application wording does not establish platform acceptance or responsibility. Record the inventory version and receiving events used at each turn; repeat the question in a new conversation with complete material. An application attachment without a handling result leaves that result unresolved. Mention of compensation cannot promote an application into an award. Confirm the eventual business status against actual inventory and handling records.
