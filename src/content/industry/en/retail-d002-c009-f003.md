# Amazon FBM fulfillment: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Amazon FBM fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect shared non-delivery sources while separating shipping confirmation, carrier tracking and the buyer’s issue. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service entry provides one buyer’s tracking summary, excluding full service messages, other addresses and internal return settings. Inspect the receiver through the complete link, then request restricted sources, retaining originals and full responses. Another order’s address requires resource-grant investigation. Shared carrier or similar tracking numbers do not make it this order’s source. A link locates a conversation without authenticating buyer identity; test intended reads and denials.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-share check | Seller-fulfilled tracking sources | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary scope | Order tracking excludes other addresses | Original in receiving environment |
| Restricted sources | Messages and internal return settings excluded | Full responses and denials |
| Event boundary | Separate confirmation, collection and delivery | No immediate loss conclusion |
| Device history | Order and prior question across environments | Provide missing material |
| Actual handling | Answer edits no address or submits no claim | Tracking delivery and business outcome checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If tracking repeats another address or internal return settings, retain the full response. Shared carrier or similar numbers do not establish that the source belongs to this order.

- If phone non-delivery follow-up lacks the computer’s order, inspect history and obtain the prior question. Anonymous continuity cannot borrow another parcel’s tracking.

- If confirmation becomes collection, delivery or definite loss, inspect corresponding carrier events. A confirmation supplies no complete outcome; retain gaps rather than equating a normal reply with handling.

## How to verify the setup

Test the intended summary, another order and return settings for reads and denials, retaining originals and current-version limits. Required third-party identity needs commercial capability and return checks. Distinguish available tracking omitted from input from an unobtained event, investigating transfer or collecting material respectively. Replies may organize enquiries without confirming delivery, amending addresses or submitting claims. Actual non-delivery handling and customer outcomes require operational records rather than a completed share conversation being labeled resolved service.
