# JD POP seller operations: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Share a specified service summary while testing the boundary around address attachments and other order records. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service share provides an order-progress summary while excluding buyer-address attachments and other disputes. Use the complete channel-generated link in the receiving environment, asking about both the summary and restricted sources. If a full address or another order’s facts enter the answer, retain the response and inspect scope. An undisplayed attachment can still be paraphrased. A link locates an entry without identifying its recipient or authorizing every shop source.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-entry check | POP summary scope | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intended information | Progress for a specified order | Full response in receiving environment |
| Address boundary | Exclude addresses and other orders | Inspect paraphrases and denials |
| Anonymous follow-up | Identify registration and completion referents | Clarify order and original question across devices |
| Stage evidence | Registration supplies no repair result | Separate absent source and omitted delivery |
| Business responsibility | Answer updates no ticket or liability decision | Inspect actual records and entry conditions |

## Three common errors

- If a service summary includes complete addresses or another dispute, retain the response and scope. An undisplayed appendix proves no isolation, and a link authenticates no buyer.

- If completion follow-up on another device lacks the repair order, inspect actual history and stage, then obtain the earlier question. Anonymous identity does not automatically cross devices.

- If registration or explanation becomes repair completion, adjudication or liability confirmation, trace actual results and input. Separate unavailable sources from omissions; the answer updates no ticket.

## How to verify the setup

Test the intended summary, another order and address sources with the receiving identity, recording reads, denials and full responses. Inspect validity, access and usage conditions for the actual version. Identity validation needs commercial-edition capability and official returns; the link identifier is not a buyer account. Sharing can explain recorded steps without updating a ticket or establishing adjudication, performance or liability. Those business conclusions require actual sources instead of acceptance of a fluent service answer.
