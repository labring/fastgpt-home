# JD POP seller operations: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Preserve the order, repair item and agreed scope during warranty follow-ups, without treating a service record as proof of breach. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A proposed service exercise provides a warranty record noting a registered repair and items awaiting confirmation, then asks whether this constitutes breach. The record establishes service handling, without independently proving performance or breach. Identify the order, agreement and required evidence. An answer assigning liability merely because repair is mentioned requires checking whether history includes applicable terms and facts. A prompt may organize differences and gaps; a confident tone cannot replace their evidentiary basis.

## Three common errors

- If registered repair becomes performance or breach, compare applicable terms, service record and answer to trace liability support. Repair itself adjudicates neither.

- If dispatch timing becomes a repair deadline, retain both questions and source passages, checking order, task and optimized reference. A different historical obligation must not enter this turn.

- If no completion entry becomes definite noncompletion, retain unverifiable status and obtain results. An existing result omitted from input needs delivery checks; absent records are no proof of the opposite event.

## How to verify the setup

Record service registration, buyer request, merchant agreement and actual completion evidence separately, checking what each follow-up adopts. Absence of a completion entry does not prove noncompletion; retain the presently unverifiable status and obtain the result. A communication suggestion is not a platform adjudication and does not automatically update a ticket. Visual evidence needs its actual input-path check. Text conversation does not validate photo contents or establish the eventual dispute outcome.
