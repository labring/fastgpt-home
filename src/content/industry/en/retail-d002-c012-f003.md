# Warehouse picking and packing: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Warehouse picking and packing, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect wave and label boundaries in a shared parcel summary, separating packing, recheck and actual delivery. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service entry supplies one parcel’s handling summary, excluding complete label addresses, other waves and internal consumables sources. Inspect parcel identity through the complete link, then request restricted sources, retaining full responses. The summary explains supplied records without identifying a buyer or warehouse employee. Forwarding grants no universal wave access. Verify originals and denials rather than assuming undisplayed tables or images cannot be paraphrased.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Parcel-share check | Warehouse-record scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary identity | This parcel and corresponding wave | Original in receiving environment |
| Restricted sources | Exclude addresses, other waves and consumables | Full responses and denials |
| Device handoff | Parcel and prior question behind this item | Provide missing material |
| Event distinction | Label and recheck are not delivery | Dispatch and carrier originals |
| Actual handling | Answer scans, ships or compensates nothing | Outcomes and entry conditions |

## Three common errors

- If this summary reads another wave’s address or internal materials, retain source identity and full response. Shared warehouse work supplies no sharing basis.

- If a phone dispatch question lacks the computer’s parcel and review question, inspect history and obtain material. Anonymous identity cannot automatically reuse another parcel’s result.

- If generated labels or recheck notes become actual delivery, inspect dispatch and carrier events. Recorded stages establish no subsequent action; answers pack, scan and confirm nothing.

## How to verify the setup

Test the parcel summary, another wave and restricted labels for reads and denials, recording originals and version-specific limits. Distinguish an update omitted from input from an unobtained dispatch event; longer validity supplies neither evidence. Required identity validation needs commercial capability and return checks. Explanation may organize records to inspect without changing waves, printing labels or compensating. Actual shipping, damage and service outcomes need corresponding sources rather than an ended share conversation being labeled finished handling.
