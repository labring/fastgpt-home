# Franchise store supervision: conversation logs for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Franchise supervision differs from direct-operated fresh-food ordering because the head office supervises how franchisees carry out standards. The records relevant to this page are the conversation logs that form between the head office and franchise store owners around ordering, order fulfillment, and after-sales questions. The category brief lists the main data sources as the supervision app, POS sales, the franchise system, and store photos. The daily documents include store visit forms, franchise contracts, rectification notices, and display standards. These materials update weekly or monthly, so a time gap commonly exists between a conversation log and the business document it relates to. Confirming that both fall in the same period is the first step before any comparison.

This page handles conversation logs and auditing inside order fulfillment and after-sales question answering. A retrieval result or a submitted form is not a business authorization. A log record existing and a business action being completed or approved are separate facts.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Conversation identity needs chatId to match the target conversation with the business record. Sessions from different entry points cannot be counted together. When run details are needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect the fields actually returned in responseData. Business data that is not returned cannot be assumed to be fully retained.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample (material for comparison, not actually executed):

Source text one: "In September 2024, a regional supervisor issued a rectification notice to a franchise store owner through the supervision app, requiring feedback on a display-standard nonconformity at a store by September 20." Source text two: "In the same period, the franchise system shows the store's order in a pending-review state." Draft sentence: "The store has confirmed rectification is complete, and the order has been approved."

Comparison points: the subject in source text one is the regional supervisor and the franchise store owner, the object is display rectification, the state is "feedback required," and no confirmation of rectification is recorded. The state in source text two is "pending review," and pending review does not show approval. The draft sentence states unsupported states as completed and approved.

## Three common errors

1. Treating "pending review" as "approved." In the sample, the franchise system shows the order in a pending-review state, and the draft sentence writes "has been approved." The check compares the status field of the same order in the same period; only a value that explicitly states approval satisfies the condition.
2. Treating "feedback required" as "rectification confirmed." Source text one only requires feedback by September 20 and does not record the feedback result. The draft sentence writes "has confirmed rectification is complete," which is unsupported.
3. Treating log existence as completed compliance auditing. Log retention, reconciliation, and compliance requirements are carried out by a complete business solution separately. Log existence does not equal completed compliance auditing.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input source text one and source text two, compare "has confirmed rectification is complete" in the draft sentence with "feedback required" in source text one, and compare "has been approved" with "pending review" in source text two. The condition is satisfied only when the material explicitly records the corresponding state value; otherwise record it as unsupported.
2. Input the hypothetical chatId "JC-202409-001," set detail to true per the official contract, and inspect whether responseData returns the required run fields. If they are not returned, record it as not fully retained and do not assume retention.
3. Input the hypothetical last message "Has rectification been confirmed" and compare whether the returned content matches the states in source texts one and two. If the returned content contains approval or completion wording not recorded in the material, record it as inconsistent with the material and preserve the evidence gap.
