# Franchise store supervision: after-sales questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Retrieve left-right locations, correction scope and feedback stage together in store follow-up. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A proposed franchise follow-up has a notice requesting labels on the left shelf, a reply completing the right display and a service question about the left now. Both statements can coexist. Right-side completion does not refute a left-side gap. Retrieve location and source identity together, using the literal left-label phrase and full question. Searching completed alone may favour the right. No later left-side record is supplied. A shared store or task does not establish store-wide completion, and records for different sides are not automatically contradictory statements about the same object.

## Three common errors

First, right-side feedback may answer for the left; compare candidate location, notice subject and original instead of relying on a store match. Second, optimization may drop left; retain original and rewritten questions to inspect search subject. Third, a reference may keep completed while trimming right; inspect actual text under its token limit rather than judging completeness from a filename. These concern subject, question qualification and reference clipping. Threshold or mode changes supply no actual left-side handling outcome missing from the available material.

## How to verify the setup

Ask where the notice applies, where the reply reports completion and which left-side evidence is still needed, retaining candidates and adopted references. Compare semantic, full-text and hybrid results with the right-side passage as a discrimination example rather than treating high scores as relevance proof. Later feedback needs the same store and item checked before repeating; updates cover only its location. This exercise conducts no visit or acceptance and claims no real passed correction. Missing follow-up remains pending rather than an inferred refusal or completed task.
