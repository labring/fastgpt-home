# Letter-of-credit documents: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

In a credit-related fulfillment inquiry, handing over documents and the bank’s processing result require separate sources. A proposed input can supply the presentation statement while noting that the corresponding response is unavailable. Collect the passage, source identity and question, then inspect whether the next step preserves that state. Document submission must not become acceptance or completed payment, and contractual commitments do not replace the result of this processing instance.

## Three common errors

If an inquiry combines goods delivery with document handling, design separate choices to obtain transport receipt, inspect a bank response or contact the actual operator. Test each recipient and check for records from different transactions mixed into one explanation. Selecting an operator arranges communication only. Without receipt confirmation or a business record, it does not establish completed handling, allocate responsibility or approve an after-sales remedy. If a bank response from an earlier transaction reaches the answer with the current presentation statement, inspect the response’s subject against the current source identity. Remove the earlier response and retest the missing-response case, observing whether the relevant evidence is requested. Explain the response’s processing state only after establishing its correspondence to this inquiry.

## How to verify the setup

After supplementation, repeat the inquiry and compare submitted text, downstream values and citations to establish whether the added source is transport evidence, a processing response or another document. Cost or settlement questions remain unresolved without their details; complete presentation documents do not establish an operating conclusion. An external status query or action requires verified interface scope, authorization and a real response. A completed workflow is insufficient proof of bank or carrier processing.
