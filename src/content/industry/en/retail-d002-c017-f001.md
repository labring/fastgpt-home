# Franchise fee settlement: after-sales questions with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Invoice status, actual fund events and unavailable query material must be interpreted separately. The contract party and the evidence returned for this inquiry determine what can be said about settlement. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A franchise-settlement inquiry can inspect states described by plans, invoices, notices and bank material without amounts. Proposed wording contains invoice and deduction notices, not fund-execution evidence. The real external contract must define subjects, method and lookup conditions; complete financial access is not assumed. An invoice or sent notice supports those records rather than receipt, deduction or approved handling.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-reading check | Proposed material | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State distinction | Invoice separate from bank source | Invoice is not receipt |
| Party mismatch | Another store’s ledger enters this contract | Inspect request conditions and remove it |
| Empty-return meaning | Response supplies no material | Not inferred as absent business activity |
| Evidence retest | Corresponding financial source for this party | Explanation actually uses the record |
| Formal handling | Separate deduction, adjustment and confirmation | Query is not financial approval |

## Three common errors

Invoice mistaken for received funds: An invoice described as received funds requires separate invoice wording and corresponding bank records. Another store’s ledger returned: Another store’s ledger returned for this contract needs party and condition checks before removal. Empty query mistaken for absent business: An empty response described as no fund activity requires authority, query conditions and the service definition. These concern state, subject and coverage, without turning unavailable sources into nonexistent business.

## How to verify the setup

Retain party, source status, actual request and return scope. Repeat after corresponding financial evidence arrives and inspect its use. Check headers separately from business parameters and body applicability against the contract without public authentication. Accounting adjustments, deductions and settlement confirmation require separate permission and real receipts. Reading establishes neither passed financial review nor a determination of contractual validity or responsibility.
