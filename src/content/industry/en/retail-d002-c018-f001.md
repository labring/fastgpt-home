# Regional dealer stock: after-sales questions with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Dealer identity, collection status and order-level dispatch require supported correspondence. An aggregate stock source cannot independently explain execution of this particular replenishment request or replace its missing business records. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A dealer-replenishment service inquiry can ask what a request, stock record, dispatch source and collection plan each establish. Proposed material supplies demand and plans without current dispatch or bank evidence. A request does not establish fulfillment. Obtain a real contract defining dealer, item and period lookup without assuming connected brand and ERP systems or combining parties through shared item names.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-reading check | Proposed material | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan and execution | Collection arrangement separate from receipt | Plan does not establish funds received |
| Party scope | Dealer and transmitted subject | Other-party outcomes not borrowed |
| Record granularity | Item aggregate versus order dispatch | Obtain order relationship and execution source |
| Actual reading | Contract conditions beside real request | Return covers the present question |
| Further handling | Separate replenishment, adjustment and confirmation | Reading is not fulfillment or reconciliation |

## Three common errors

Plan mistaken for funds received: A collection plan described as received funds requires source-status and receipt checks. Wrong dealer response: Another dealer’s response needs party correspondence and sent-value checks. Item aggregate mistaken for order dispatch: An item stock aggregate treated as this order’s dispatch needs order linkage and the actual dispatch record. These inspect execution, subject and granularity; a normal response establishes neither completed supply nor missing business relationships.

## How to verify the setup

Retain dealer, order demand, source time, actual request and response. Repeat after corresponding execution evidence arrives, keeping conclusions within this subject. Check parameters, headers and any appropriate body against the external contract without public credentials. Replenishment submission, stock changes and financial confirmation require separate permission and receipts. Reading establishes neither ordering, deduction nor reconciliation, and absent responses do not prove absent handling.
