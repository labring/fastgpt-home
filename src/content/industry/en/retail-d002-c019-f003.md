# On-demand retail fulfillment: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in On-demand retail fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Preserve order identity in shared instant-order progress, separating picking, rider acceptance and customer receipt. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration entry shows this order’s picking text and pending rider acceptance, excluding other-store stock, unrelated orders and rider personal information. Inspect recipient passages through the complete link, preserving store, order and source time before restricted-source tests. Entry credentials establish no actual customer or delivery identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. Explain only the stage supported by the source. A valid link and completed conversation supply no actual rider acceptance or customer receipt record.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Instant-order progress share | This order’s visible scope | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Picking and pending acceptance | Store, order and time |
| Restricted sources | Other stores, orders and personal data | Full answer and denial |
| Across devices | Order behind the delivery question | Actual history and supplementation |
| Different stages | Inspect picking, acceptance and receipt | Evidence for each actual stage |
| Business execution | Sharing dispatches, closes and changes nothing | Records, authority and receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this progress returns other-store stock or unrelated rider information, retain source identity and full answers. Shared channels or goods authorize no cross-order sharing, including paraphrases.

- If a phone asks about delivery without the computer’s order identity, inspect history and obtain the original question. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow the latest order status.

- If completed picking becomes rider collection or customer receipt, obtain this order’s acceptance and reception evidence separately. Earlier and later stages cannot prove one another completed.

## How to verify the setup

Test this summary, other-store sources and unrelated orders, recording passages, restrictions and denials. Distinguish obtained rider records omitted from input from absent customer-receipt evidence. Renewal and repeated follow-ups create no event. Repeat object-free questions across devices and inspect requests for store, order and time. Sharing dispatches nobody, changes no stock and closes no order. Actual delivery and receipt need business authority, records and receipts. Keep unverified stages pending rather than equating an answered question with a resolved order.
