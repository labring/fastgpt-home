# Certificates of origin: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A document-service inquiry can first separate evidence of application submission, issuance and delivery. A proposed text may state that application material was submitted while an issuance response and shipping proof remain unavailable. Inspect the next step after form transfer so submitted has not become issued. Even a complete invoice and packing list supplies transaction information only; it does not replace records from the issuing organization or delivery stage.

## Three common errors

If a customer reports not receiving a certificate, establish which sources are actually available before offering an issuance-response request, shipping-record check or operator handoff. Test each connection and received content. Choosing a shipping query does not presume dispatch of an original document. If versions differ, identify the relevant application first to avoid mixing an older certificate or another batch into the answer. A workflow choice does not determine documentary validity. Test a version mismatch in which the operator’s note concerns the current application but the attached certificate points to an earlier record. Compare the subjects side by side and identify whether this inquiry lacks issuance feedback or dispatch evidence. Have the source owner request the corresponding material; the existence of a certificate does not resolve the application identity.

## How to verify the setup

Repeat the inquiry after supplementation, checking that the new evidence reaches the answer and that unresolved stages remain explicit. An amendment, replacement dispatch or external query requires verified scope, authorization and an actual response. Ending a form does not establish accepted processing. Keep the state supported by available documents separate from matters awaiting the operator’s confirmation, without inferring issuance, delivery or clearance from an ordinary work note.
