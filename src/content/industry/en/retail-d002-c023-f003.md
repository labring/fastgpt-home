# Brand repair services: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Preserve item and work-order identity in repair shares, separating descriptions, inspection, parts and actual repair outcomes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed repair summary states that intake was received, inspection is missing or reshipment remains unconfirmed, excluding other customers’ orders and internal disputes. Inspect recipient passages through the complete link, preserving item and work-order stage before restricted-source tests. Entry credentials establish no ownership or customer identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. A customer’s unusable description is a statement rather than a responsibility finding. Parts issue and created reshipments cannot together establish completed repair or receipt.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Repair-progress share | Order stage and restriction | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Received intake and pending inspection or reshipment | Item and work-order passages |
| Restricted sources | Other inspections and internal disputes | Full answer and denial |
| Device handoff | Work order behind repair and shipping | Actual history and supplementation |
| Stage evidence | Parts and creation prove no repair or collection | Process and carrier sources |
| Formal handling | Sharing approves no replacement or closure | Authority and actual receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this summary exposes another customer’s inspection or internal disputes, retain source identity and full answers. Shared models or batches authorize no cross-customer sharing.

- If a phone asks about replacement and shipping without item and prior question, inspect history and obtain the work order. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute recent repair status.

- If issued parts or created shipments become completed repair and carrier collection, obtain the actual repair process and collection source separately. Earlier records execute no later stage.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about this progress, other-customer sources and internal comments separately, retaining passages, versions and denials. Separate obtained inspection omitted from input from inspection without an actual record. Renewal or longer dialogue cannot verify a defect. Repeat object-free follow-ups across devices and inspect requests for item and work order. Sharing approves no replacement, performs no repair and closes no application. Actual handling needs authority, process records and receipts. Unsupported stages remain open rather than answered service establishing responsibility or performance.
