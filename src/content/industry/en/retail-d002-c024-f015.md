# Brand concession operations: after-sales questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Retrieve counter reservation, collection method and transport wording for the specific aftercare item. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A proposed counter case reports a missing handle on a collection bag, reserves a replacement at the counter and points a mall-wide notice to separate delivery arrangements. The question asks whether the replacement was mailed. The reservation records a counter location, and the notice does not identify this item. A missing handle does not establish that the whole bag is unusable. Retrieve collection, counter reservation and dispatch as different actions. Compare the full mailing question with the reserved-at-counter wording rather than turning a related transport notice into an actual shipment record for this specific replacement.

## Three common errors

First, reservation may become dispatch in the answer; inspect the store’s action and item. Second, optimization may turn collection support into delivery support; compare task and collection method without inventing transport arrangements. Third, an earlier supplier bag shipment may answer for this replacement; inspect recipient, event and version. These concern action, service method and source identity. A shared mall or bag name does not establish a common logistics event, and an existing notice does not establish an executed backend arrangement or current handling outcome for the customer item.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the store reserved, how collection was originally arranged and whom the notice covers, expanding actual references. Compare semantic, full-text and hybrid results on the same questions, retaining counter and separate-reference qualifiers under token limits. Added handoff evidence needs this item and actual collection method checked; dispatch does not establish customer receipt. This exercise arranges no mailing, changes no collection method and decides no service responsibility. Missing item records remain unresolved rather than a refusal to dispatch or completed delivery inferred from general material.
