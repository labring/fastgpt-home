# Brand outlet operations: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect style, purchase-condition and internal-approval boundaries in a store service entry without deriving outcomes from a clearance label. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service entry opens confirmed purchase information for one style, excluding internal margin calculations and another store’s approvals. Inspect the receiving environment through the complete channel link, asking about both the purchased item and restricted sources. Paraphrased calculation text requires retaining the full response and investigating grants; an undisplayed table can still be readable. Clearance participation does not expand source rights, and holding a link establishes no internal approval authority.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-share check | Outlet purchase-information scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permitted source | Confirmed description for purchased style | Original visible to receiver |
| Internal restriction | Margin and other-store approval excluded | Full response including table paraphrases |
| Cross-device request | Identify the item in packaging and exchange questions | Clarify style, prior question and terms |
| Gap separation | Inspect purchase source, history and delivery | No exchange conclusion from label |
| Handling state | Separate advice from executed refund or exchange | Inspect documents and owner confirmation |

| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |

## Three common errors

- If the purchased-style entry reads internal margins or another approval, retain full response and resource scope. An undisplayed table may remain readable; clearance expands no grant.

- If packaging-to-exchange follow-up loses style and prior question across devices, inspect input and clarify identity. Anonymous records provide no automatic continuity or exchange terms from a label.

- If absent returns become completed performance or advice becomes an executed refund, inspect actual documents. Separate missing records from absent events; sharing changes no product or ticket.

## How to verify the setup

Test the purchased style, another store and internal calculations for permitted and denied reads, retaining original wording, denial scope and version-specific access conditions. Required customer identity validation needs applicable commercial capability; a link identifier is not an authenticated account. Suggestions execute no exchange, refund or product-state change. Absence of a return entry also does not establish completed performance. Actual handling needs documents and owner confirmation rather than a completed conversation being labeled resolved service.
