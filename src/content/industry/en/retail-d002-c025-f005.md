# Brand outlet operations: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Separate product condition, campaign terms and handling records in outlet service follow-ups so labels do not replace actual agreements. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A proposed service exercise begins with a product attachment labeled past-season, then asks whether this item can still be handled. The label describes inventory background without supplying return or warranty terms. Clarify product, purchase conditions and actual request. An answer refusing service because the item is clearance requires checking applicable terms in history; the label cannot verify that conclusion. The absence of a return record also does not establish completed performance: distinguish an absent record from a handling event that did not occur.

## Three common errors

- If a past-season or clearance label alone causes service refusal, inspect purchase conditions and actual request beside the answer. Inventory background supplies no return or warranty agreement.

- If packaging review becomes a quality complaint or another style, compare the customer wording, questions and product identity. Locate failed request handoff rather than deciding through further turns.

- If absence of a return entry becomes completed performance, distinguish unavailable records from events that did not occur. Obtain handling results; replies execute neither refunds nor exchanges and update no ticket.

## How to verify the setup

Compare follow-ups with only the seasonal label, added conversation and an actual handling result, checking retained gaps and sources. Campaign wording can describe scope without establishing customer acceptance of every term or platform adjudication. The answer organizes known facts and further checks; refunds, exchanges and ticket updates require actual execution evidence. Confirm agreements and handling state with the responsible owner instead of treating a smoothly ended conversation as completed service.
