# Brand outlet operations: conversation logs for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Brand outlet operations exist to clear out-of-season inventory, and the price structure and gross-margin room stay tight. In order fulfillment and after-sales question handling, conversation logs often touch four document types at once: discount approvals, slow-moving inventory lists, price tags, and gross-margin calculation sheets. The people involved include brand outlet stores, channel clearance teams, and finance business partners. Errors in this setting damage the price structure, leave inventory sitting, or turn margin into a loss. Data sources are ERP inventory age, POS sales, outlet mall campaigns, and historical discounts, updated weekly or monthly.

The role of conversation logs and auditing at this step is to fix the materials, actions, and request states that appeared in one fulfillment or after-sales exchange so a handover can be checked. The existence of a log shows that a conversation happened. It does not show that a discount was approved, that a price tag was replaced, or that margin was reviewed.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Check the target conversation against the business record by chatId, and do not mix sessions from different entry points into one count. When the conversation interface run detail is needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData. Compare the log against the fields the interface actually returns; business data that was not returned cannot be treated as fully retained. Identity fields in a test mode may differ from a formal call, so check inputs, history, and returned content separately. Reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements belong to a complete business plan; the presence of a log does not mean a compliance audit is finished.

Hypothetical drill sample: a brand outlet store channel clearance team submits a discount approval material on 10 March 2025. The source text reads: "SKU A-1042, inventory age 210 days, current price tag 299 yuan, discount requested to 199 yuan, approval status is pending review; approver: unsigned." In an after-sales conversation the same day, the proposed sentence is: "This SKU discount is approved and can be fulfilled at 199 yuan." The subject, object, status, and validity scope needed for a judgment are all inside these two source texts. Pending review and unsigned do not amount to approval.

## Three common errors

1. Treating pending review as approved. The sample source text states "approval status is pending review; approver: unsigned," while the proposed sentence calls it approved. Check: input the proposed sentence and the approval material source text, compare the status field value, and only a status of approved with a signed approver satisfies the fulfillment condition.

2. Treating the existence of a log as a completed audit. The official document states that detail=true returns responseData, but business data not returned cannot be treated as fully retained. Check: input one request with detail=true, compare the responseData fields against the business field checklist, and record missing fields as evidence gaps.

3. Treating retrieval or a form as an authorized action. The userSelect and inputForm returned by an interactive node are display configuration only, and a collected form value does not equal business verification or approval. Check: input the params returned by the interactive node, compare whether they contain an approval conclusion field, and record a missing field as pending verification.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the proposed sentence "This SKU discount is approved and can be fulfilled at 199 yuan," input the approval material source text and compare the approval status and approver fields. Only a status of approved with a signed approver satisfies the condition. In the sample both are pending review and unsigned, so the judgment is unsupported.

2. For the same chatId, issue a request with detail=true and compare whether responseData contains the corresponding node response and quoteList. Observe the difference between returned fields and the business field checklist, and leave missing parts unknown.

3. For the userSelect options returned by the interactive node, input the option value and key and compare whether they contain an authorization conclusion. If they do not, record them as collected form values; business verification and approval require separate implementation.
