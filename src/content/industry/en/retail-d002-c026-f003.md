# Brand-owned store operations: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Preserve store and shift in shared operational progress without treating schedules, photographs or transfer requests as completed site work. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration summary lists missing display wording, unconfirmed staffing and a received transfer request for this shift, excluding personal staff records, other-store sources and internal performance comments. Inspect recipient passages through the complete link, preserving store and shift before restricted-source tests. Entry credentials establish no actual manager or regional identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. Image descriptions, schedules and requests support their recorded stages. Validity refreshes neither sites nor stock, and visible summaries establish no completed store work.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Owned-store progress share | Current public scope | Inspection evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collaboration summary | Missing display, pending staffing and request | Store and shift passages |
| Restricted sources | Personnel, other stores and performance | Full answer and denial |
| Device follow-up | Store and shift behind people and goods | History and supplementation |
| Execution stage | Schedules and requests prove no attendance or receipt | Shift and reception evidence |
| Subsequent handling | Sharing schedules or transfers nothing | Authority, records and receipts |

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |

| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |

| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |

| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this summary returns other-store staffing or internal performance comments, retain source identity and full answers. A shared brand authorizes no cross-store disclosure, including paraphrases.

- If a phone asks about arrived people and goods without store and shift, inspect history and obtain the prior question. Anonymous identity cannot automatically fill gaps from an earlier shift.

- If schedules and transfer requests become attendance and received goods, obtain actual shift and reception records separately. Arrangements execute no subsequent action.

## How to verify the setup

Test current summaries, other-store sources and internal comments separately for reads and denials, retaining passages, versions and restrictions. Distinguish obtained site wording omitted from input from a shift lacking execution records. Renewal creates no attendance or transport event. Repeat object-free follow-ups across devices and inspect requests for store and shift. Sharing schedules nobody, transfers nothing and confirms no performance. Actual handling needs authority, execution records and receipts. Keep unverified tasks open rather than displayed summaries or ended discussion establishing fulfilled operating results.
