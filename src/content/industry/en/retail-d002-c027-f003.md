# Dealer ordering events: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect dealer and event in post-event progress shares without turning samples, drafts or requests into completed orders. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration entry reports received dealer sources and unverified order acceptance, excluding other contracts and internal credit judgments. Inspect recipient passages and event through the complete link before restricted-source tests. Event credentials establish no actual dealer identity or ordering eligibility; required checks depend on verified commercial capability. Handbook wording and displayed samples serve different purposes without supplying brand acceptance. A prepared agreement proves neither signatures nor applicable terms. Preserve those source stages rather than expanding the progress summary into a transaction conclusion.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Event-progress share | Dealer sources and stages | Inspection evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Received sources and unverified acceptance | Dealer and event |
| Restricted sources | Other agreements and internal credit | Full answer and denial |
| Device follow-up | Dealer and question behind ordering | Actual history and supplementation |
| Commercial stage | Samples and drafts prove no acceptance | Actual evidence and permission |
| Execution boundary | Sharing orders, signs and approves nothing | Authority, records and receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this summary exposes another contract or internal credit, retain party passages and full answers. A shared event or brand authorizes no cross-customer disclosure.

- If a phone asks about confirmed ordering without dealer and prior question, inspect history and obtain them. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute recent dealer records.

- If samples or draft agreements become accepted orders or approved credit, obtain actual acceptance and authority evidence separately. Reading sources executes no business.

## How to verify the setup

Test public progress, other-customer sources and internal comments separately for reads and denials, recording event, passages and versions. Separate obtained acceptance omitted from input from acceptance without available evidence; renewal or longer dialogue supplies no commercial decision. Repeat object-free questions across devices and inspect requests for dealer and event. The entry submits no order, confirms no contract and approves no credit. Actual handling needs authority, records and receipts. Open stages cannot become brand or dealer commitments through a completed conversation.
