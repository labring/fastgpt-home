# Dealer ordering events: after-sales questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Retrieve sample sizes, confirmed-order rows and arrival observations by their actual subjects. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A proposed dealer aftercare packet labels a display garment medium, leaves final-order sizing for confirmation and asks whether the received goods are also medium. No arrival label or inspection is supplied. Retrieve sample and order identities with the full receiving question. Sample size does not cover production goods merely through the same product name. Pending final sizing does not establish misdelivery. Compare sample-medium, final-size and complete questions to inspect which actual candidates support each subject rather than treating all sizing material as current delivery evidence.

## Three common errors

First, sample size may become an arrival fact; inspect candidate object and current delivery correspondence. Second, optimization may replace received size with available sample sizes; compare question targets rather than narrowing to an easier catalogue query. Third, another event’s confirmed order may be adopted; inspect event, item and order row rather than transferring through design or dealer names. These concern evidence object, question task and source event. Scores or several size documents cannot combine into a physical observation absent from the actual current packet.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the sample labels, what the final row lacks and where arrival evidence is, inspecting actual citations. Compare semantic, full-text and hybrid results using identical questions with sample and pending qualifiers retained under token limits. Added labels or observations need transaction and item checked, updating only observed scope rather than an entire batch. This exercise decides no return conditions and executes no replenishment. Missing arrival records remain unresolved; they establish neither a mistaken customer nor a fault-free current delivery from related sample information.
