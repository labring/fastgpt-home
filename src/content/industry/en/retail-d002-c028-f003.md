# Brand livestream operations: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect session and product limits in collaboration shares, tracing drafts, comments and actual broadcasts separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration summary lists an unconfirmed script and a received product card, excluding internal review comments, other sessions and audience personal data. Inspect recipient passages, session and version through the complete link before restricted-source requests. Entry credentials establish no actual presenter or operations identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. Product claims still need original qualifications. A script proves no spoken content, and purchase comments replace no transaction evidence. Sharing describes received sources rather than events absent from the actual record.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Livestream-collaboration share | Current progress scope | Verification evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Unconfirmed script and received card | Session, version and passage |
| Restricted sources | Other comments and personal data | Full answer and denial |
| Device follow-up | Session and sentence behind spoken content | Actual history and supplementation |
| Outcome evidence | Drafts and comments prove no broadcast or sale | Approval and transaction records |
| Execution boundary | Sharing operates no account or order | Authority and actual receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this summary exposes another session’s comments or audience material, retain source identity and full answers. Shared goods or presenters authorize no cross-session disclosure.

- If a phone asks whether wording was spoken without session and passage, inspect history and obtain the prior question. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute the previous broadcast.

- If drafts or comments become actual broadcast, approved promotion or transactions, obtain session records, approval and transaction evidence separately. A displayed summary supplies none of those events.

## How to verify the setup

Request public progress, other-session sources and internal comments, recording passages, versions and denials. Separate obtained product evidence omitted from input from a session without broadcast records; renewal creates no outcome. Repeat object-free questions across devices and inspect requests for session, goods and wording. Sharing operates no live account, approves no promotion and changes no order. Formal execution needs authority and actual records. Unsupported claims remain unresolved rather than accessible links or ended discussions establishing completed session business.
