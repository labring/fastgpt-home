# Supermarket supplier accounts: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect batch and period in supplier-settlement shares, separating arrival acknowledgment, acceptance opinions and mutual reconciliation confirmation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration summary lists arrived goods, missing acceptance opinions and unconfirmed reconciliation, excluding internal settlement comments, other agreements and actual finance vouchers. Inspect recipient passages and current subject through the complete link before restricted-source tests. Entry credentials establish no actual supplier identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. Arrival, passed acceptance and settlement agreement have different sources. Listing all three cannot expand one occurred stage into completed handling, and contractual existence supplies no actual execution outcome absent from those records.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supplier-settlement share | Batch and period scope | Inspection action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Arrival with pending acceptance and reconciliation | Subject and stage passages |
| Restricted sources | Other agreements and internal settlement | Full answer and denial |
| Device follow-up | Batch and acceptance stage questioned | History, batch and period |
| Confirmation distinction | Acknowledgment and drafts prove no approval | Current actual evidence |
| Execution boundary | Sharing inspects, adjusts and pays nothing | Authority, records and receipts |

## Three common errors

- If this summary exposes another agreement or internal settlement wording, retain party identity and full answers. A shared retailer authorizes no cross-supplier disclosure.

- If a phone asks whether the batch passed without batch and prior reconciliation question, inspect history and obtain specifics. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute an earlier opinion.

- If arrival acknowledgment or a prepared statement becomes passed inspection or mutual agreement, obtain current batch acceptance and period confirmation separately. Receiving sources differs from decisions.

## How to verify the setup

Request public progress, other-supplier sources and internal comments, recording passages, versions and denials. Separate obtained period confirmation omitted from input from parties that have not confirmed; renewal creates no settlement outcome. Repeat object-free questions across devices and inspect requests for batch, period and stage. Sharing inspects nothing, adjusts no settlement and handles no funds. Actual operations need authority, records and receipts. Unsupported stages remain unresolved rather than an answered question establishing formal acceptance by either supplier or retailer.
