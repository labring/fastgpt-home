# Supermarket supplier accounts: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Supplier-entry service review traces entity, goods receipt and settlement wording in the correct conversation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose an entry checklist concerns a supplier branch, a supermarket note acknowledges goods receipt only, and a settlement objection remains under review. Before answering whether the matter is settled, locate the current chatId and relevant entity, then inspect input, history and the actual answer. Obtain runtime detail under the official contract. Returned fields do not prove that all contracts, settlement sheets and fund evidence were present in context. No actual amount is supplied.

## Three common errors

Branch material applied to another legal entity needs contracting identity checked. Goods receipt reported as settled accounts needs settlement and fund evidence checked. A pending objection reported as mutual acceptance needs original wording and confirmation checked. These concern subject, stage and opinion status.

## How to verify the setup

Mark the input passage supporting each conclusion, examining debug and production identity and history separately. An unavailable settlement sheet remains unreviewed; missing detail proves no absent transaction. Added identity changes correspondence alone. Reconciliation, settlement and fund confirmation depend on actual business processes and evidence. Reviewing logs supplies no payment authority. Retain the object acknowledged by the supermarket, leaving the branch-to-contracting-entity relationship open without evidence.
