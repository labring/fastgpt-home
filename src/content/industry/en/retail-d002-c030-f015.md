# Supermarket supplier accounts: after-sales questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Separate carton identifiers, inner product codes and physical correspondence in supplier aftercare. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A proposed supplier case asks whether different packaging identification and product wording mean wrong goods. A note identifies the carton code as a grouped-package marker and refers inner product codes to another page. Neither that page nor current physical inspection is supplied. Retrieve packaging level with the full question. A grouped identifier and item description are not automatically comparable fields, and different wording alone does not establish misdelivery. Compare carton, product-code and full questions to identify missing correspondence rather than inventing the identity of actual goods from the proposed label excerpts.

## Three common errors

First, carton wording may prove inner goods correct; inspect candidate packaging scope because a label purpose replaces no observation. Second, optimization may turn different into nonconforming product; compare question strength without promoting an unresolved relationship to a known error. Third, another product’s inner-code page may be used here; inspect product, batch and original identity. These concern evidence scope, question inference and source correspondence. Similar identifiers, relevance scores or complete-looking titles cannot determine a real wrong-goods outcome for a batch absent from the actual supplied evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the carton marker does, whether the inner source exists in input and which physical evidence is missing, expanding actual references. Compare semantic, full-text and hybrid results on identical questions with grouped and separately attached retained under token limits. Added inner pages need product and batch matched before checking actual delivery to answering; upload is no comparison result. This exercise inspects no goods, changes no codes and settles no accounts. Missing pages remain unresolved, proving neither wrong goods nor an absent inner product identifier.
