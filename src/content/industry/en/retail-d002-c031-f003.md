# Retail packaging reporting: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect store and period in shared supply-report progress, separating procurement, sales, preparation and processing receipts. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed share states that sources are missing and a report draft is prepared, excluding operating details, other-store ledgers and internal comments, without regulatory requirements. Inspect recipient passages, store and period through the complete link before restricted-source tests. Entry credentials establish no actual filing identity; required validation depends on verified commercial capability. Procurement explains acquisition rather than sales or use. A draft concerns organized wording rather than sending, acceptance or approval. Preserve these stages and source gaps instead of expanding a readable summary into official completion.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supply-report progress share | Public and restricted material | Inspection object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public summary | Missing sources and prepared draft | Store, period and passage |
| Restricted sources | Operating details, other stores and comments | Full answer and denial |
| Device follow-up | Store and period behind completed filing | Actual history and supplementation |
| Evidence stage | Procurement is not sales; draft is not acceptance | Sources and processing receipts |
| Handling boundary | Sharing generates or submits nothing | Authority, execution and receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If progress exposes operating details or other-store comments, retain full answers and source scope. Similar supplies authorize no cross-store disclosure.

- If a phone asks about completed filing without store and period, inspect history and obtain the prior question. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute an earlier receipt.

- If ledgers or drafts become verified sales or accepted reports, inspect actual sales sources and current processing receipts separately. Complete-looking names supply no business evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Test public summaries, restricted ledgers and other-store sources for reads and denials, recording passages, periods and versions. Separate obtained processing evidence omitted from input from a filing without receipts; longer validity creates no acceptance. Repeat object-free questions across devices and inspect requests for store and period. Sharing generates no statistics, submits no report and determines no compliance. Handling needs business permission, execution and receipts. Unsupported stages remain open rather than an accessible summary or ended discussion establishing formally passed reporting.
