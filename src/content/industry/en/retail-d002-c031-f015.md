# Retail packaging reporting: after-sales questions with retrieval

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT retrieval. Retrieve display use, carrying use and unverified material together in supplies aftercare. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the retrieval step

A proposed supplies case describes a sample bag for repeated display, leaves actual collection use unconfirmed and asks whether it can repeatedly carry goods. Repeating a display action does not establish load-bearing capability, durability or reuse performance. Retrieve the object of repeated and the unresolved scope, comparing literal display wording with the full carrying question. A shared repetition term supplies no positive answer. No physical testing or current-use confirmation is provided, and the example introduces no environmental attribute or regulatory conclusion about an actual bag or the conditions of its use.

## Three common errors

First, display wording may become carrying capability; inspect candidate purpose and original action rather than deriving performance from everyday similarity. Second, optimization may add recyclable or environmentally preferable; compare questions for attributes absent from the source. Third, an older paper-bag use page may answer for a bag with unresolved material; inspect material, subject and version. These concern action object, added properties and applicability. Relevance, shared naming or a help page does not establish real load-bearing capability without the required actual product evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what is repeated, which use remains pending and which attributes the packet supplies, inspecting actual references. Compare semantic, full-text and hybrid retrieval on the same questions, retaining display and carrying-pending qualifiers under token limits. Added use material needs current bag and conditions checked before repeating, without extending another material or one observation to every use. This exercise tests no object and recommends no real loading or environmental position. It purchases nothing and files no report. Missing confirmation establishes neither impossible use nor approved use.
