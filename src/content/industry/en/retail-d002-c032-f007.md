# Retail checkout and loyalty: conversation logs for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Retail checkout and loyalty operations draw on the POS system, the member CRM, the payment platform and the price-tag system. Records update daily or per shift. The working document set for after-sales questions consists of promotional price tags, member points rules, the daily checkout report and exceptional refund slips. Price and member entitlement are the two recurring points of dispute, and the consequences of error are shortfalls or overages in the till and disputes over member points. At the conversation-logs and audit step, the task is to align one conversation with the corresponding business record and to check identity, time and fields.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Conversation identity is established through chatId, which locates the target conversation for comparison against the business record. Conversations arriving through different entry points must not be counted together. When intermediate values are required, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData. The fields actually returned by the log and the interface must be compared against the fields the business record requires; business data that is not returned cannot be treated as fully retained. Identity fields in test mode may differ from those in a formal call, so inputs, history and returned content are checked separately. Reconciliation, retention and compliance obligations belong to a complete business solution; the existence of a log does not by itself complete a compliance audit.

Hypothetical drill sample: a store checkout supervisor submits a pending request at shift handover on 10 March 2025, reading: "Export the conversation record for chatId C-0310A, morning shift, 10 March, to reconcile one exceptional refund slip." The material available for comparison states that the daily checkout report records refund slip number R-0310-07 for that shift, and that the amount matches the payment platform transaction; the member points rules state that the points for this refund should be reversed; the remarks field of the exceptional refund slip is blank. The authorization boundary of the request is limited to this store and this shift, excluding other stores and other dates, and the export action is to be performed by a person holding audit permission. The material does not state whether this supervisor holds export permission.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Item | Relevant setting or check on this page | Hypothetical trial starting value and comparison method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation identity | Locate the target conversation by chatId; compare appId against the business record | Start from C-0310A and compare character by character against the shift number in the daily checkout report |
| Run detail | Set detail when intermediate values are needed; inspect responseData | Start from detail=true and compare the field range against a detail=false return |
| Conversation list | Distinguish API conversations from page conversations by source | Start from source=api and compare the count against the page conversation list |
| Conversation records | Retrieve the record list by chatId and dataId | Start from pageSize=10 and compare against the number of entries in the daily checkout report |
| Feedback fields | Read userGoodFeedback and userBadFeedback as required | Start from loadCustomFeedbacks=true and compare against the default read result |
| Interactive node | With detail=true, read the configuration from data.interactive | Start from stream=false and compare against the event stream at stream=true |

These starting values are chosen for this hypothetical trial so that an observable difference can be formed against another value; they are not official defaults.

## Three common errors

1. Treating chatId as a business document number. The input is chatId=C-0310A and the comparison object is the shift number in the daily checkout report. The condition is met only when the material states an explicit correspondence between the two; when the material does not state it, record the gap.
2. Treating the responseData returned under detail as complete business data. The input is the return under detail=true and the comparison object is the fields in the daily checkout report and the payment platform transaction. A field that does not appear in the return is unsupported and cannot be judged missing or conflicting.
3. Treating the submission of an export request as granted authorization. The input is the pending request above and the comparison object is whether the material states this person's export permission. When the material does not state it, the request remains pending verification and does not constitute granted authorization.

## How to verify the setup

1. Identity check: input chatId=C-0310A and the appId, compare the shift number in the daily checkout report against the conversation identifier in the record. The condition is met when the two agree and belong to the same store and the same shift; when they disagree, record the result as pending verification.
2. Field check: input the responseData under detail=true, compare the fields appearing there against the fields required by the daily checkout report and the payment platform transaction, and mark each field as returned or not returned. A field that is not returned remains unknown.
3. Permission check: input this supervisor's export request and compare whether the material states the audit permission and its authorized scope. The condition is met when the material states it and the scope covers this shift; when the material does not state it, record the evidence gap and wait for supplementary material before deciding.

This page addresses only retail checkout and loyalty, order fulfilment and after-sales questions, and conversation logs and audit. At operational handover, the authorization status of materials, actions and requests cannot be inferred from retrieval or from a form.
