# Trade returns and claims: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Trade returns and claims, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Return-shipment claim logs preserve requests, acceptance and actual transport events. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose a buyer requests return shipment, a carrier note acknowledges an inquiry only and the other party supplies no compensation position. Locate chatId with current contract and goods identity, then inspect requesting party, carrier and history in actual input. Check detail and responseData under the official contract while inspecting returned coverage. Present fields do not establish that all contracts and logistics documents reached context. No actual compensation amount is supplied.

## Three common errors

A return request reported as mutual agreement needs explicit acceptance checked. A carrier reply reported as dispatch needs actual transport evidence checked. An older claim outcome applied here needs contract and goods identity checked. These examine opinion, event and ownership without determining responsibility from retained logs.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect identity and history separately for debug and production. Separate request from acceptance evidence and retain the carrier inquiry wording. Added transport material changes logistics stage alone while compensation needs independent support; omitted detail proves no absent return movement. Return operations, claims and business writes need authorized procedures and receipts. Log review traces answer evidence without promising or deciding for either party. Identify the requesting buyer and the carrier's limited acknowledgment, separating compensation acceptance from funds evidence and checking contract and goods correspondence when documents arrive.
