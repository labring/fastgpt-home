# Marketplace promotions: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Marketplace promotions, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Inspect campaign-enquiry access to registration, stock and approval text while preserving customer requests and backend processing stages. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service-enquiry entry provides campaign participation information, excluding internal approval appendices and another campaign’s stock-locking table. Inspect actual originals through the complete link, testing permitted conditions and restricted sources. Retain any exposed locking plan to investigate scope; shared classification does not authorize the recipient. Participation wording describes applicability without establishing a successful order registration, available stock or completed approval.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Enquiry-entry check | Participation material | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permitted note | Conditions and applicable subjects | Original in receiving environment |
| Internal boundary | Exclude approval and other stock plans | Full responses and denials |
| Campaign reference | Identity and version across devices | Clarify conditions when history is missing |
| Currency location | Separate old conditions, updates and delivery | Obtain currently applicable source |
| Business execution | Answer registers or locks nothing | Inspect actual handling and entry limits |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If participation sharing exposes internal approval or another stock plan, retain originals and full response. Shared campaign classification grants no complete source access.

- If a phone question about current usability lacks campaign and condition version, inspect history and obtain sources. Anonymous records do not cross devices, and older conditions need applicability checks.

- If registration or planned locking becomes successful registration or secured stock, compare actual backend outcomes. Readable conditions and a valid link establish neither execution nor business approval.

## How to verify the setup

Test the permitted note, internal approval and another campaign for reads and denials, retaining full responses and versions. Inspect expiry, per-user limits and usage responsibility for the actual deployment. Known recipient identity requires separate commercial-edition validation capability. A shared answer performs no registration, stock lock, refund or label change. Confirm customer handling against actual records rather than deriving satisfied business conditions or resolved service from a valid link and readable participation text.
