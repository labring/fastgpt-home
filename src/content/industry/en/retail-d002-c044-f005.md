# Marketplace advertising: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Marketplace advertising, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Separate order-attribution labels from fulfillment and service events while preserving the current order and source roles. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

An attribution table’s conversion-channel label supports reading that field, without establishing shipment or completed after-sales handling. A proposed on-site search advertising label can test whether the prompt preserves this scope. If an answer converts successful conversion into completed service, trace its event source. Without that record, the service conclusion cannot be verified. The attribution field’s definition and period also need inspection; a channel label is not evidence for the entire customer journey.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Conversation or implementation check | Support provided by advertising sources | Acceptance trace |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Service gap | Order F lacks a service record | Trace generated steps and status individually |

## Three common errors

- If a conversion-channel field becomes dispatch or completed service, compare attribution wording and answer with order-event evidence. Conversion supplies no complete customer journey.

- If expected and attributed channels differ and one source is declared wrong, preserve definitions, periods and stages. Inspect whether optimization changed expectation into an event.

- If follow-up borrows another order or invents service steps without records, inspect original question, history and returned sources. Restore the current subject and keep missing tickets unresolved.

## How to verify the setup

Order F has conversion evidence but no service record. A service-detail question needs the relevant case or event material. Since the model may still answer, inspect generated steps and completion states sentence by sentence. After adding the source, record whether it actually enters the next turn and repeat the original question and a follow-up. Retain channel interpretation separately from service evidence. Neither a continuing conversation nor a conversion label validates the complete customer experience.
