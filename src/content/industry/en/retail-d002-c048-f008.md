# Parcel labels and handoffs: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Parcel labels and handoffs, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Parcel support should distinguish label generation, inclusion in a handover list and carrier scanning. A proposed input may list the parcel for handover while noting that corresponding pickup-scan evidence is unavailable. After form submission, inspect whether the receiving step keeps those states separate. A list entry does not establish carrier receipt, and a sender’s preparation record does not replace evidence of subsequent transport or delivery.

## Three common errors

For a parcel-location inquiry, proposed choices can obtain carrier records, inspect sender handover material or contact the actual logistics owner. Test each destination and parcel-source identity, preventing another batch’s scan from entering the explanation. If a pickup query returns only label wording, inspect connections and input mapping. Filling an answer with existing text is not a newly observed status. A person’s selection also does not establish loss or allocate responsibility. A parcel listed on a handover sheet may still have no corresponding object in the supplied scan records. Compare label identity, the handover entry and the scan records’ coverage, then refer the difference to the actual operator. Until the relevant scan evidence is supplied, the answer can describe the sheet without asserting that the carrier collected that parcel.

## How to verify the setup

After supplementary records arrive, repeat the inquiry and verify their parcel and time scope before checking citations. Describe scanning, handover and customer receipt according to their separate evidence, retaining any missing stage. Reshipment, compensation or rerouting requires business confirmation, an authorized interface and an actual operation result. Receiving material or finishing the conversation does not establish an updated transport state or resolved service responsibility.
