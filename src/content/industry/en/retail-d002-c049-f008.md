# Wholesale stall trading: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Wholesale stall trading, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Stall fulfilment separates held goods, collector identity and handoff evidence before choosing a follow-up route. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed stall slip holds goods for collection while the collector needs checking. A neighbouring stall note records someone collecting other goods. Receive this stall and item identity, then pass the slip and missing identity evidence to a route for further details or handoff records. A paper hold does not establish reserved live stock. Another stall’s collection is not this event. Preserve subject and pending state downstream; connected branches authenticate no person.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Stall route | Proposed state | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold slip | Awaiting collection | Not delivery evidence |
| Collector | Identity unresolved | Retain gap |
| Other stall | Other goods collected | Check subject |
| Branch input | Slip and missing evidence | Compare received input |
| Actual handoff | Business verification | Authority and receipt |

## Three common errors

A hold promoted to delivery needs this item’s handoff. Another stall’s collection needs stall and item identity checked. Pending identity promoted to permission to release needs the outstanding item compared with actual authority. These concern stage, subject and release evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Compare entrance material, branch input and answer. Ask where the goods are held, who needs checking and whether a handoff covers this item. Added evidence updates its own issue. Stock reservation, dispatch and handoff require business authority and receipts; running a flow arranges no collection. Keep the goods-hold slip separate from collector confirmation. When further evidence arrives, verify the stall, goods identity and current collection matter before reconsidering the proposed handover, using only the supplied records.
