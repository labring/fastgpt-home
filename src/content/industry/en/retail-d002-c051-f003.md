# Douyin storefront operations: shared access for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Douyin storefront shelf operations center on product cards and the store experience score. The documents handled during order fulfillment and after-sales question answering are product qualification pages, experience score detail sheets, shipping violation appeals, and store announcements. These materials update daily or per order, and fulfillment milestones and after-sales responsibility judgments depend on the shipping, receipt, and appeal status of a single order.

When a shared access step hands these materials or a question-answering entry point to a collaborator, the authorization state of each item matters first. A qualification page can be used for internal cross-checking. A shipping violation appeal is pending material before submission. The publication scope of a store announcement determines which parties can see it. Treating a no-login link or a retrieval result as business authorization conflates material visibility with operating permission.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created from the publish channel and the system-generated full link is copied. The link identifier only grants access and does not act as business authorization. The identity verification field holds the root address of a POST request. Share link initialization, conversation start, and conversation end each send a request to the corresponding interface, and the server returns success and uid. The uid is used to pull and save conversation records. The anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and same browser; cross-device records require separate identity and session management design. Retrieval and similarity do not carry permission or entity isolation, and the same-model association condition of a retrieval library does not extend to external systems.

Hypothetical paper test case: the source text of one material reads "Store announcement: during the Double Eleven period, this store's shipping time limit is adjusted to within seventy-two hours after payment, applicable to orders paid between October 20 and November 5." Another material reads "Experience score detail: order A was paid on October 25 and picked up by the carrier on October 30." The proposed copy sentence is "This order meets the announcement's shipping requirement, and the announcement and experience score detail can be shared with the collaborator as fulfillment evidence." The pending request is "Please deliver the two materials above to the outsourced customer service through a no-login link for after-sales question answering." The judgment requires the same subject, same period, and same field: the announcement's applicable scope covers order A's payment date, and the comparison between the pickup time and the seventy-two-hour measure determines whether the proposed copy sentence holds. The sharing request itself is a pending request and does not constitute authorized execution.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the no-login link identifier as business authorization. Link accessibility means the material is visible; it does not mean the collaborator has permission to process a shipping violation appeal or modify a store announcement. The check is to input the link and the collaborator identity, compare the operations actually executable against the authorization list, and judge it as an error only when an out-of-scope action appears.

2. Treating a pending request as an executed share. The proposed copy sentence and the pending request are in a pending state, unsigned, unapproved, and unexecuted. The check is to input the request text and the approval record, compare their status fields, and record it as unsupported when the approval record is absent.

3. Using a retrieval result in place of identity verification. Retrieval returns relevant fragments and does not judge whether the collaborator has the right to view a qualification page or experience score detail. The check is to input the retrieval result and the identity verification return value, compare the uid against the target material scope, and treat a retrieval hit as not constituting a permission basis.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the proposed copy sentence in the hypothetical sample, input the announcement's applicable scope and order A's payment date, compare the seventy-two-hour measure with the pickup time, and the proposed copy sentence holds only when the pickup time falls within the measure; when it exceeds the measure, the proposed copy sentence is unsupported.

2. For the sharing request, input the request text and the authorization boundary, compare the share target with the material scope, and the completion of link creation and copying only means an access entry point exists; it does not mean the outsourced customer service has obtained after-sales processing permission.

3. For the identity verification configuration, input the verification root address and the returned success and uid, compare whether the uid matches the format that excludes specific characters and stays within two hundred fifty-five bytes, and record a failed return as pending verification without inferring a specific failure cause.
