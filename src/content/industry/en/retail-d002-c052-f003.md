# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Pinduoduo seller campaigns, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test sharing boundaries with a campaign-item service summary, distinguishing refund requests, delivery signatures and actual handling results. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed service entry provides the buyer with one order’s handling summary, excluding product-cost calculations, other buyers’ refund requests and internal platform messages. Inspect the intended order and restricted questions through the complete channel link, retaining full responses. Exposed cost conditions or another order’s text require scope investigation. One campaign provides no universal sharing basis. A link identifier replaces neither buyer identity nor authorization for all campaign or supply-chain sources.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Service-share check | Campaign-store sources | Acceptance record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order summary | Scope intended for this buyer | Original in receiving environment |
| Restricted sources | Exclude costs, other orders and internal messages | Full responses and denial scope |
| Stage distinction | Separate application, signature and refund result | No inferred dispute resolution |
| Anonymous continuity | Order and prior question across devices | Provide source when history is missing |
| Business result | Advice issues no refund or stock change | Actual records and entry conditions |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If a summary exposes costs, another request or internal messages, retain passages and full response to inspect resources. One campaign supplies no sharing basis or authenticated buyer identity.

- If a phone payment follow-up lacks the computer’s order and application stage, inspect actual history and obtain the source. Anonymous sessions do not transfer automatically.

- If application or delivery signature becomes a completed refund or resolved dispute, inspect actual outcomes. Neither signature nor absent returns establishes complete performance; the answer edits no stock or order.

## How to verify the setup

Test the intended summary, another order’s request and internal calculations for reads and denials, recording visibility and version-specific validity, user limits and usage conditions. Required buyer authentication needs commercial third-party capability and return checks rather than identity inferred from the link. Replies organize steps without issuing refunds, writing orders or changing stock. Verify results against actual platform or owner records. Absence of a return entry cannot establish completed performance, and an ended sharing session does not establish resolved service.
