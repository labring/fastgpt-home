# Franchise filing management: after-sales questions with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Franchise filing management, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. A franchise fulfilment query separates archived material from filing receipts and handling evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A hypothetical text readout says this store’s agreement attachments are archived but a filing receipt is absent. An older record concerns another store’s completed handover. An HTTP node can connect an authorized archive query, with address, method and store selection checked against its actual contract. An archive record establishes neither a filing outcome nor physical delivery. Preserve the store and missing receipt before answering; older-store evidence does not transfer.

## Three common errors

Archiving promoted to completed filing needs evidence for this matter. Another store’s result needs request and returned-store identity compared. An unattached receipt promoted to no filing needs source coverage checked; a missing attachment proves no absent event. These concern state, subject and inference.

## How to verify the setup

Retain redacted request scope, raw content and answer, tracing this store’s evidence. Check service authentication separately from business read permission. A response grants no access to every archive. Handover, filing and external submission need separate processes and actual receipts.
