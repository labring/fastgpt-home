# Franchise filing management: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Franchise filing management, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Franchise-service review distinguishes the question, the answer and an external filing result. Someone saying “complete” in history does not establish verified handling evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

In a proposed conversation, store staff ask whether sending documents counts as completion. The assistant asks for that store’s result. Someone later says “it is done” without a source. Link the conversation identifier to the store and entry route, then inspect speakers under the actual detail-response contract. Searching for completion words cannot establish answer correctness. Do not assume external attachments or filing records are returned; missing original results still need collection.

## Three common errors

Mixed speakers: A question or staff assertion treated as an assistant-verified conclusion needs role and context checks. Mixed stores: Another franchise location’s history requires inspection of the actual business subject linked to the conversation. Sending promoted to completed filing needs the corresponding outcome evidence. These inspect speaker, subject and process stage rather than infer an external result from a message timestamp.

## How to verify the setup

Retain query scope, conversation identifier, actual response and outstanding evidence. Check whether the store’s follow-up still receives a qualified answer. After adding the corresponding result, inspect actual source use instead of repeating the unsupported staff assertion. History locates the answer sequence; external handling, identity determination and compliance audit require separate records. A log establishes neither a completed filing nor passed review.
