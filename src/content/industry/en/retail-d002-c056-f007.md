# Producer export tax processes: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Producer export tax processes, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Export-service logs need the questioned period and source version. A past explanation of a declaration process does not establish a result for this business event. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A proposed customer first asks about missing prior-period details, then switches to current-period declaration material while the assistant reuses the earlier explanation. Link the conversation identifier to the actual client entry and inspect messages and source clues returned by the detail contract. A historical explanation does not automatically cover the new period. An attachment title without its contents does not establish that the original was reviewed. This exercise examines answer evidence without tax eligibility or calculation advice.

## Three common errors

Carried-over period: An old explanation used as the current result needs question-period and cited-source checks. Title promoted to document reading requires returned contents and evidence of original access. Test history promoted to a client record requires conversation-origin and business-linkage checks. These inspect temporal scope, visible evidence and record environment rather than keyword presence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain query conditions and returned messages. Repeat the same gap after adding current-period wording and inspect whether that source now supports the explanation. A process description still does not establish a completed declaration. External submission, financial handling and outcome confirmation need other real records. Logs locate the answer and its evidence gap without becoming a formal business voucher or proof of authorized tax treatment.
