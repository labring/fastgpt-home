# Fresh-food supplier claims: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Fresh-food supplier claims, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Fresh-produce claim review identifies exactly what the supplier acknowledged. Receipt of an anomaly notice and agreement to a remedy require different evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A proposed service exchange quotes the supplier: “Photos received; awaiting physical inspection.” The assistant tells the customer the claim was accepted. Link the conversation identifier to the delivery event and inspect the complete passage under the actual detail response. Receiving photographs supports receipt of information, not a negotiated outcome. If images themselves are absent, do not claim that the log shows damaged goods. An evidence gap also does not establish that the claim failed.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Claim-service investigation | Proposed supplier reply | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information receipt | Photos received | Not accepted compensation |
| Pending action | Physical inspection awaited | Retain in quotation |
| Image content | Response may omit photographs | No invented damage observation |
| Event identity | Multiple anomalies for supplier | This delivery’s relationship |
| Negotiated outcome | Explicit handling response absent | Retest within added evidence |

## Three common errors

Receipt promoted to agreement needs the full supplier reply beside the assistant conclusion, including pending inspection. Image receipt promoted to a physical finding needs actual inspection evidence. An old anomaly from the same supplier requires delivery-subject and quotation-ownership checks. These inspect position, evidence type and event identity; a matching supplier name does not make them one case.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the question, supplier passage and answer sequence. Repeat after adding this inspection or an explicit response, and limit the conclusion to its coverage. A reply acknowledging receipt alone leaves negotiation unresolved. Deductions, replacement, settlement changes and dispute closure need separate business records. History traces answer formation without accepting a claim for the supplier or equating a closed conversation with a closed claim.
