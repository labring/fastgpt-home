# Store cold-chain receiving: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Preserve batches and observation times across temperature, receipt and rejection records so follow-up questions do not assign responsibility prematurely. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A proposed service exercise begins with a transport-temperature record identifying a vehicle and recording period, then asks whether this batch should be rejected. The vehicle record has not been linked to the store’s delivery batch; receipt measurements and applicable acceptance conditions are also absent. Specify the required relationships. If one transit observation becomes a conclusion that the entire arrival fails, trace input scope. Without batch correspondence the conclusion remains unverifiable; further questions cannot join unrelated events into fact.

## Three common errors

- If an unlinked vehicle-temperature record becomes a batch-rejection decision, inspect vehicle period, batch and acceptance conditions. One transit observation does not establish an entire failed arrival.

- If its relationship to the earlier note uses another batch’s temperature series, compare questions and identities with vehicle trip and receipt time. More context does not establish event attribution.

- If a rejection reason becomes supplier agreement or resolved disagreement, inspect actual handling results. A recorded reason establishes no consent; distinguish unobtained photos from delivery omissions as well.

## How to verify the setup

A rejection note records a stated reason that may support the current handling summary, without establishing supplier agreement or resolved disagreement. Trace cited measurements and documents at each turn, then repeat the question with complete sources in a new conversation. Obtain missing inspection photos separately; photos already obtained but absent from their input path require delivery investigation. Text questions do not replace on-site inspection, and unresolved opinions cannot become completed quality testing or service compensation.
