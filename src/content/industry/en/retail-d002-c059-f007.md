# Store cold-chain receiving: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Cold-chain arrival disputes separate sensor records, staff descriptions and actual acceptance. A log message timestamp is not a physical handoff timestamp. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A proposed conversation first names a compartment-record title, then says “arrived at the store.” The assistant describes cold-chain conditions as confirmed. Link the conversation identifier to this delivery and inspect whether the detail response includes record contents and staff wording. A title does not identify the measured carton or establish acceptance. Message timing locates conversation events without becoming arrival, unloading or measurement timing. No temperatures or standards are supplied in this example.

## Three common errors

Title promoted to test evidence needs actual returned contents and measurement-subject checks before declaring conformity. Arrival promoted to acceptance needs the batch’s formal conclusion. Message time promoted to handoff time requires the actual transfer record. These inspect visible content, business stage and time meaning independently of a conversation’s existence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain source titles, visible contents and surrounding messages. Ask which evidence supports each answer. Adding handoff material can correct the arrival stage without confirming missing measurement or acceptance. Rejection, return transport, stock changes and responsibility decisions need subsequent records and actual authority. Log querying reviews the answer’s basis without establishing cold-chain performance or automatically deciding the dispute.
