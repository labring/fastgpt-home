# Fruit-store procurement: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Fruit-store procurement, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Fruit after-sales review follows the actual observation passage. Purchase requirements, photograph descriptions and testing conclusions retain different evidence status in history. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A proposed customer says the received fruit looks yellow. The assistant cites a purchase requirement for green fruit and calls the delivery conforming. Link the conversation identifier, store and supply batch, then inspect returned messages and sources. A requirement describes terms and cannot disprove the customer’s observation. If photographs are absent from the actual response, attachment names do not establish visual inspection. This reviews answer formation without replacing product assessment.

## Three common errors

Requirement promoted to observation needs the acceptance passage and the customer description with their separate origins. An earlier batch’s test used here needs report-subject and delivery-linkage checks. Unseen photograph described as inspected needs actual image contents or access evidence. These inspect fact status, batch coverage and visibility rather than infer evidence from a matching fruit name.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the complaint, answer and citation scope. Ask whether the available material actually determines this item’s condition. After adding current-batch inspection, examine whether the answer distinguishes observation from judgment and stays within this delivery. Returns, loss attribution and supplier handling need separate records. Customer receipt wording, assistant conformity claims and ended conversations do not establish completed after-sales handling.
