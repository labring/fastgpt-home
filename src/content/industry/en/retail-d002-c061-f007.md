# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: after-sales questions with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Fresh-food weighing and sorting, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Weighing-dispute history retains the customer’s measurement basis and actual review actions. An adjustment arrangement establishes neither reweighing nor completed compensation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A proposed customer asks whether the label includes packaging. Staff say a reweighing request was registered, but the assistant summarizes that net quantity was verified. Link the conversation identifier to the item and task, then inspect the question, registration passage and summary sequence. Registration establishes neither performed weighing nor a confirmed packaging basis. Missing label contents remain a gap rather than an inference from the product name. No metrology criteria or conversions are supplied.

## Three common errors

Question converted to fact needs the original interrogative message and label explanation. Registration promoted to completed reweighing needs this item’s actual weighing record. Adjustment reply promoted to execution needs the corresponding handling outcome. These inspect message status, field action and subsequent business stage independently of chat availability.

## How to verify the setup

Retain surrounding messages and actual returned fields. Repeat after adding the label or reweighing source and inspect whether the answer stays within that evidence. A confirmed measurement basis still does not establish executed compensation. Field weighing, financial adjustments and dispute closure require separate business records. Traceable history locates the answer without establishing correct quantity or a resolved customer issue.
