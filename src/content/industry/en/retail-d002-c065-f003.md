# Livestream violation appeals: shared access for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Livestream violation appeals address penalties that have already been issued. The materials center on the violation notice, the livestream recording, product qualification files, and the appeal statement. In order fulfillment and after-sales question handling, compliance staff and livestream operations need to synchronize appeal materials, penalty status, and order-handling positions with collaborators. The shared access and embedding step hands a conversation entry point to a collaborator while the materials themselves stay in the original system. Materials update irregularly, with each violation triggering a round of supplementation. Creating, copying, and configuring a share link belongs to the publish-channel workflow. A link identifier does not constitute business authorization, and a retrieval result does not constitute authorization either.

A hypothetical paper exercise sample is used throughout this page. A store compliance team receives a platform violation notice, number A-2024-0715, with the penalized object being a product link, the penalty status being product livestream ban, and the penalty date being July 15, 2024. The same batch contains one livestream recording from July 15, one product qualification file, and one draft appeal statement. The sentence under review reads: "The product livestream ban took effect on July 15, and customer service may suspend after-sales question transfer for this product on that basis." The sentence asserts two facts: the effective time of the penalty and the authorization for customer service action. The materials state the penalty status and the date, and do not state an effective time or a transfer authorization.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created from application details, publish channel. The system generates the complete link, and the name is used only for record display. Anonymous users rely on a localId to pull conversation records from the cloud. This only holds on the same device and same browser; switching devices or clearing browser cache loses the records. The system permits pulling only the most recent 30 days and 20 records. Expiration time, QPM, point cap, identity verification, real-time running status, viewing quoted segments, viewing full quoted text, and downloading or opening source originals are configurable items, and some parameters are supported only in the commercial edition. Identity verification is a POST root address. Initialization, conversation start, and conversation end each send a request to a specific interface under that address. The server returns success and uid, and uid is used to pull and save conversation records. The conversation result reporting interface has no specified return value, and its response includes totalPoints and token.

For the sample, the penalty status is product livestream ban and the date is July 15, 2024. The materials do not state an effective time or a transfer authorization. A pending review is not proof that an operation permission is missing. The draft sentence treats the effective time and the transfer authorization as established, while the source materials leave both unstated.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a share link identifier as business authorization. A link can be created and copied, and this does not mean the link holder obtains appeal material access or order-handling permission. Compare the link holder against the material authorization scope, and observe whether unauthorized reading appears.
2. Treating a retrieval result as a permission filter. Similarity and relevance do not perform entity isolation, and a retrieval hit does not indicate the user is permitted to view that record. Compare the query against the ownership of returned records, and observe whether unauthorized records are returned.
3. Writing a pending status as already effective. In the sample, the penalty status is product livestream ban and the date is July 15, and the materials do not state an effective time or a transfer authorization. Compare the draft sentence against the source materials, and observe whether facts not stated in the materials appear.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample source text and the draft sentence "The product livestream ban took effect on July 15, and customer service may suspend after-sales question transfer for this product on that basis." Compare the penalty status and date against the effective time and transfer authorization fields. The materials state only the status and the date, and do not state the effective time or the authorization. This part of the draft sentence is unsupported and needs to be returned for supplementation or changed to a pending-verification wording.
2. Input the sample materials and a handover request: "Ask the collaborator to view the July 15 recording through the share link and handle after-sales for this product." Compare the operation in the request against the read scope the share link can provide. The condition is met when the collaborator can read only authorized materials, and the order-handling action requires a separate system implementation. The link itself does not execute that action.
3. Input an empty-result continuation scenario, with a paper input of []. Compare whether the downstream design handles the empty-result branch. The condition is met when the downstream clearly distinguishes empty results from non-empty results, and does not claim that a fabricated-rule retrieval necessarily returns []. Libraries using different vector models cannot be associated together. Check the identifier first, exclude those that do not match, and record the actual observation.
