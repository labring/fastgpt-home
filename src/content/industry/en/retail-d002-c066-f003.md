# Short-video affiliate sales: shared access for after-sales questions

## What the documents look like

Short-video affiliate sales rely on long-term content exposure and product-link conversion. Order fulfillment and after-sales question handling therefore work from consultations and transaction records that accumulate across days, without a live explanation step. The main data comes from the selected affiliate program, short-video playback data, product transaction data, and platform rules. Everyday materials include short-video scripts, product lists for attached links, commission plans, and violation feedback. Content operations staff, creator business development staff, and product distribution specialists hand these materials over to one another, and the handover needs a clear distinction between retrieval results, form entries, and records that actually carry an authorization decision.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created under the publishing channel, and the system generates a complete link that can be copied. The link identifier itself does not function as a business authorization. Identity verification is limited to the commercial edition, and the official interface response uses `success` plus a returned `uid` to decide whether the user may continue. That `uid` is used to pull and save conversation records. The anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and the same browser, so records across devices need separate identity and session management. The conversation result reporting interface has no specified return value; its response values match the chat interface format with one additional `token`, and `totalPoints` and `token` are the fields worth attention.

Hypothetical paper exercise: a content operations member of a short-video distribution team records in paper materials that the attached-link product list names product A with status "pending review" and a validity range of 1 March 2025 to 31 March 2025; the commission plan states that the commission rate for this product is to be confirmed; the violation feedback column is empty. A pending handover request reads: "Embed the share link for product A into the after-sales question page and allow the creator to use it directly." The request does not state the authorizing party or the scope of authorization.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the link identifier as a business authorization. A link can be copied and opened, which does not mean product A has passed review or that the commission rate is confirmed. For the check, take the list text and the handover request as input, compare whether the authorizing party and the scope of authorization are stated, and record the item as pending verification when they are not stated.
2. Reading a pending-review status as executable. The list states "pending review" while the handover request asks for direct use; the subject and period match, and the field is the same, but the values are mutually exclusive. For the check, compare the status field; a pending-review entry does not support a conclusion that authorization has been granted.
3. Treating retrieval or a form entry as authorized execution. A retrieval result returning product A, or a form that accepts a share link, does not constitute a passed review or an effective commission. For the check, compare the nature of the action; business verification, approval, and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation.

## How to verify the setup

1. Take the list text and the handover request from the hypothetical exercise as input, and compare the authorizing party, the scope of authorization, and the status field of product A. The condition for continuing is met only when the status field clearly states that review has passed and the authorization scope covers this request. When that is not stated, record the item as pending verification.
2. Take the hypothetical response from the identity verification interface as input, and compare whether `success` is `true` and whether the returned `uid` is a string that excludes "|", "/", and "\" and stays within 255 bytes. When `success` is not `true`, the configured message is shown and the user is not allowed to continue.
3. Take the hypothetical `responseData` from the conversation result reporting interface as input, and compare whether `totalPoints` and `token` are consistent with the chat interface format. The interface has no specified return value, so no conclusion about a completed deduction or balance change follows from this comparison.

This page covers only short-video affiliate sales / order fulfillment and after-sales question handling / sharing and embedding. During operations handover, the state of materials, actions, and requests needs to be distinguished, and retrieval results or form entries cannot be treated as authorized execution.
