# Group-buy presale orders: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Group-buy presale orders, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Separate grouped orders, procurement and cutoff times from completed fulfillment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Suppose a presale list is proposed, grouped orders await cancellation checks, procurement is unconfirmed, and a cutoff notice is a draft. Follow-ups about extra orders and pickup need the notice state and grouping time actually supplied. Procurement plans establish no arrival. This exercise supplies no actual volume, cutoff date or arrival promise. An earlier generated date is no new evidence.

## Three common errors

- If a later answer repeats an earlier guessed cutoff date, compare the notice and actual history. An absent date must remain absent.

- If pending cancellations disappear and all grouped orders become purchased, inspect source scope, cancellation state and procurement confirmation.

- If procurement planning becomes ready pickup, inspect supplier confirmation, arrival and pickup notice separately. Preserve intermediate stages.

## How to verify the setup

Add a confirmed notice and arrival note to a fixed grouping sample across turns. Ask which extra orders are currently permitted and what supports pickup. Compare new and continuing conversations, retaining actual input. Instructions should separate plan from execution and notice time from order time. This exercise publishes no cutoff and places no purchase; actual actions require authority.
