# Cross-border settlements: after-sales questions with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An after-sales entry in cross-border settlement material first needs an order relationship and a clear description of the financial event recorded. A proposed test can supply refund wording from a platform statement and a related reconciliation entry without presuming they are the same processing instance. After collecting passages, source dates and the inquiry, inspect their separate origins. A settlement reversal, merchant refund and actual customer receipt must not become one presumed completed state.

## Three common errors

Follow-up choices can obtain payment-processing records, inspect the platform-entry relationship or read service rules. Test the content each actually receives. If a payment query receives only a settlement summary, inspect the route and values. That summary cannot replace a receipt-of-funds record. A person’s confirmation that passages may be related supplies a review lead only; it does not establish refund execution, service responsibility or a financial outcome. A reversal note for another settlement batch cannot resolve whether this customer received payment. Compare the batch correspondence, order explanation and receipt record, marking entries whose relationship remains unestablished for the operator to inspect. After supplementation, check whether the answer cites the current record, rather than carrying forward the earlier conclusion about receipt of funds.

## How to verify the setup

After supplementation, repeat the order question and trace added sources to the citations, checking whether identities and scopes align. Assign missing refund proof or applicable policy to a provider; an exchange-rate sheet or service-fee invoice does not support a service conclusion by itself. Any later processing request or external update requires authorization, interface response and a real record. Completed conversational routing does not establish completed funds processing or a closed after-sales case.
