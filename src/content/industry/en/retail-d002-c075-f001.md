# Creator sales campaigns: after-sales questions with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Creator sales campaigns, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Creator-session service requests verify collaboration scope, logistics sources and returned content. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

Suppose collaboration wording covers one creator session only, a service record says goods reached a warehouse, and an interface index lists an attachment without its body. Select address, method and parameters from the real contract, checking session and item identity passed by earlier nodes. Inject credentials safely and inspect authentication separately from collaboration authority. A successful interface return establishes its defined response without proving fulfillment of a service promise.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

Session-only display enlarged into a continuing service commitment needs collaboration wording checked. Warehouse handoff reported as delivery needs logistics evidence. An attachment name reported as reviewed text needs actual response content. These concern scope, fulfillment stage and data coverage; no real order or measured outcome is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Retain redacted requests, raw responses and cited passages, verifying Body structure and variable mapping against the contract. Assess missing debug identity under official guidance without inferring an absent business record. Retest after attachment content arrives. Promise confirmation, shipment operations and service decisions need business processes; HTTP access grants no such authority. Locate session collaboration and service matters separately, keeping each proposed commitment traceable to that session source.
