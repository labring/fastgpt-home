# Creator sales campaigns: after-sales questions with shared access

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Creator sales campaigns, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Limit creator-cooperation service notes to the intended session, inspecting sample, livestream and after-sales deduction sources separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed collaboration entry supplies a sample-issue summary for one creator session, excluding other creators’ agreements and complete store transaction details. Inspect actual originals through the complete link, testing intended and other-session questions. Retain paraphrased external terms to investigate grants. Shared involvement in creator commerce provides no sharing authorization. The link opens a conversation without giving its holder rights to every agreement, commission source or transaction record.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Collaboration-entry check | Creator-session sources | Acceptance clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session summary | Permitted sample-issue scope | Original in receiving environment |
| Other material | Exclude external agreements and transaction details | Full response including paraphrased terms |
| Stage evidence | Separate samples, livestream and deductions | No universal completion claim |
| Across devices | Clarify session and task | Inspect history and result sources |
| Business action | Advice deducts or settles nothing | Entry limits and actual execution |

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If this session entry repeats another agreement or complete transaction details, retain terms and full response to inspect grants. Shared commerce work does not authorize all collaborators.

- If an after-sales follow-up on another device lacks session and sample question, inspect history and source identity. Anonymous sessions do not automatically inherit another session’s outcome.

- If sample dispatch, reports or requests become completed performance and deductions, inspect livestream, service and settlement events separately. One stage establishes no complete process; replies execute neither samples nor deductions.

## How to verify the setup

Test the permitted summary, another session’s agreement and restricted details for reads and denials, recording visible originals and scope. Inspect validity, per-user limits and usage responsibility by version. Required external identity validation needs commercial capability and return checks, not inferred identity from a link. Replies can organize outstanding checks without sending samples, deducting commission or settling accounts. Cooperation responsibilities and actual service outcomes need original records and owner confirmation rather than a completed conversation.
