# Store price and display checks: after-sales questions with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For after-sales questions in Store price and display checks, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Carry price-label periods, product scope and attributed store replies into follow-up service questions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A hypothetical conversation starts with an audit note saying an old price label awaits removal and its promotion period has ended. A customer then asks whether a recent purchase follows the displayed terms. The note supplies neither this transaction’s receipt nor a store decision. The prompt should request purchase time, product and label correspondence before organizing what can be explained. A recent purchase must not refer automatically to another customer’s historical order. A label still displayed does not establish continuing promotional applicability. This exercise examines text and references without deciding transaction rights.

## Three common errors

- If the next request omits the ended-period sentence but answers that the offer still applies, retain the request and history to locate the lost period restriction.

- If question optimization substitutes an earlier order from another store, compare the original question, optimized question and order identity. Trace the changed store and product.

- If a proposed store follow-up becomes customer acceptance, inspect its author, actual reply and customer response. A planned action supplies no mutual outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Compare the same follow-up with empty history, the audit note alone and the note plus this purchase’s evidence. Record remaining gaps. Inspect product and transaction time when the receipt arrives; upload time is a separate event. Attribute later store statements to their speaker. A source sentence obtained but omitted calls for context investigation; an unobtained receipt calls for collection. More history or a longer answer cannot create customer acceptance. This exercise changes no price, issues no refund and promises no remedy.
