# B2B marketplaces: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Indoor storage and avoiding high humidity are supplied conditions; load capacity lacks evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed B2B copy brief describes a shelving unit for indoor storage and says to avoid high humidity. A selection note asks for sample organization but supplies no load-capacity statement. Drafting a warehouse introduction needs the use, environmental limitation and missing load evidence together in input. Organizing samples does not establish support for arbitrary sample weight, and indoors does not mean every warehouse environment is suitable. The model needs the limitation to describe the supplied scope rather than borrowing heavy-duty or moisture-resistance promises from a general impression of the industry.

## Three common errors

One common error is input trimming away avoid high humidity, leaving a broad warehouse-use claim. Another is prior conversation about a heavy-duty rack transferring its attribute to this unit. Inspect source qualifications and historical product identity separately, retaining whether actual input contains load evidence. Reply length should accommodate both limitations and unresolved specifications; more output space does not provide performance evidence. Check actual model context capacity independently, while inspecting the citation-token limit against retention of the environmental sentence. A fluent longer draft cannot resolve a source gap. A third error expands indoor storage into suitability for every warehouse environment. Compare environmental wording in the draft with source limits and locate that scope increase. Specific settings need applicable support; indoors cannot stand in for every other condition.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance can request a use sentence, a conditions sentence and a list of specifications still needing confirmation, then trace each to supplied text. Retain indoor storage and the sample-organization need, preserve avoidance of high humidity and leave capacity unconfirmed without claiming testing. Compare the same request with empty history and earlier heavy-duty discussion to detect transferred attributes. If a fixed format supports copy review, verify complete parsing of limitations and unresolved specifications. A smooth headline alone is insufficient, and a missing limitation field must not be read as evidence that no limitation applies.
