# B2B marketplaces: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Distinguish a buyer’s customization request, supplied product statements and evidence still needed for copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A new B2B writing exercise uses quotation text recording a buyer’s requested customization, without a finished-product specification. Define the prompt’s distinction between a request and an established capability. Inspect the originals and supplied history for the source of any capability claim in the draft. A buyer’s expectation alone cannot verify a supplier function. Retain the evidence gap instead of promoting a proposal to fact through repeated follow-up questions.

## Three common errors

- If requested customization becomes an established supplier function, compare quotation requirements and capability wording with product evidence. Expectations establish neither technical performance nor delivery capability.

- If this company or item becomes a historical supplier, retain original question, actual history and optimized wording. Trace invented identity before reusing any earlier draft.

- If added specifications fail to reach this turn and structured copy retains earlier values, inspect actual input and sources for each value. Parsing does not establish refreshed evidence; restore delivery and retest.

## How to verify the setup

A proposed reading checklist can separate explicit source statements, buyer requirements and documents still needed; these are review categories, not new software fields. Choose a response format for the actual downstream requirement. Where json_schema is used, inspect configuration and parsing, then trace each value. Complete structure cannot fill an absent product condition. After adding a source, also record what actually enters the current turn rather than assuming it is included.
