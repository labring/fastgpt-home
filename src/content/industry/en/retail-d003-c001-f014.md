# B2B marketplaces: copy drafting with model connections

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

The quotation phrase describing appearance as open to specification discussion can support an invitation to enquire. Its discussion status supplies no finalized product, tested function or obtained certification. Inspect preservation of that qualifier, then review draft verbs: discussing, supplying, possessing and guaranteeing make different commitments. Even unchanged product nouns can accompany a shift from negotiation to established capability. Compare the draft with the contributor’s wording to separate restatement from added assertions. Obtain documentation for each new attribute rather than filling an individual product’s evidence gap with company or brand background.

## Three common errors

- If quotation wording never reaches the model but review is claimed, compare application material with the actual request. Investigate omission; a service response does not establish reading.

- If company scope becomes product certification or performance, retain scope, product description and draft, tracing documentary support. Conducting a business category does not substantiate that product claim.

- If the quotation recipient becomes the entire target audience, inspect positioning evidence and applicable product. Keep absent conditions unresolved and recheck after supplementation; parameters cannot supply proof.

## How to verify the setup

An answer may still invent positioning when the audience and market scope were not supplied. Trace each related statement. A quotation recipient does not identify every intended user, and business scope does not establish a particular product use. After adding positioning or product material, repeat the original question and settings, recording delivery of the new text and its use in the draft. The check covers supplied input only and does not validate other model capabilities.
