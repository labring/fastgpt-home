# MCN operations: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. An exaggerated demonstration scene cannot become an observed product-performance claim. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed MCN training brief asks for an explanation of folding and storage steps. A creative outline adds fast camera cuts to show a tidy desk, marked illustrative. Input needs the distinction between the actual teaching task and the shooting treatment. A camera transition is not a measured organization-speed result and does not establish the same outcome for every user. Spoken copy can describe the steps but must not turn an illustrative scene into a performance promise, a genuine user testimonial or a claim that verification has already taken place.

## Three common errors

One failure is a citation losing the illustrative note and fast cutting becoming fast organization in the copy. Another is an exaggerated headline from history replacing the current step-explanation task. Compare outline, note and actual input to inspect whether visual treatment became performance evidence. More reply space adds expression, not observations. Actual model context capacity needs separate checking, and retaining earlier creative material must not push the current qualification out of the supplied input. The failure location matters because shortening the reply does not restore a lost note. A third error presents the planned tidy-desk scene as an experience obtained by a real user. Compare shooting plans with actual experience evidence, inspecting tense and speaker in the draft. A creative outline cannot supply a testimonial or an event already experienced.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance can separate the step explanation from shooting instructions. The former should concern folding and storage; the latter should retain its illustrative identity. Repeat the drafting request without and with the exaggerated headline, checking for added speed, effect or testimonial claims. If a fixed format separates spoken copy and production notes, verify that content does not cross between them and that the qualification parses and remains visible to review. With no test evidence supplied, the draft stays within operating steps and planned creative treatment rather than presenting a planned scene as an experienced result.
