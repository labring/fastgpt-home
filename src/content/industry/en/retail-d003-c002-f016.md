# MCN operations: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

MCN copy preparation can collect a session topic, the creator’s contractual cooperation scope and the proposed product source. A hypothetical input separates an allowed promotion category from absent material for this particular item. Inspect the text actually received by the drafting step after submission. Collecting values does not establish review of contractual authority, ingredients or advertising evidence, and it does not approve publication of the content.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope distinction | Contract category beside item-specific evidence | Category is not expanded into product promises |
| Passage transfer | Submitted cooperation-scope passage | Drafting receives the same wording |
| Misconnected choice | Source request reaches drafting | Repair selection-to-recipient correspondence |
| Evidence wait | Session product material not supplied | Inspect waiting and continuation after supplementation |
| Publication | Separate editorial approval and account operation | Form completion is not publication evidence |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

If the source passage was submitted but only a schedule heading reaches drafting, trace where the passage was lost, repair transfer and repeat the original topic. Another test selects a request for product material while the connection still reaches drafting. Retain the selection and actual recipient, repair the route and check waiting and continuation. A branch label cannot supply a document that is absent. If a contract category becomes a promise about a particular product’s use, inspect that model’s guidance and the category passage. A category supplies no use evidence; remove the specific promise without its source.

## How to verify the setup

Trace original input, submission, receiving step and drafted text, checking that claims remain bounded by supplied descriptions and that missing product evidence stays explicit. An interaction node records workflow state and waits; completion continues downstream. Source validation, editorial approval and account publication need their actual downstream or external arrangements. Completing the form does not establish contractual review or external publication.
