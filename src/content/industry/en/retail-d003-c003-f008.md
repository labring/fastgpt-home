# Shopify fulfillment: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Separate immediate downloads, physical preparation and optional parts instead of turning digital access into stocked goods. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

This page invents product material: a download page offers immediate access to instructions, a physical kit awaits warehouse preparation confirmation, and accessories require separate selection. Collect digital-document purpose, physical-item identity and composition wording, then choose access-method or accessory-description review. Downloadable instructions establish no prepared kit, and optional parts are not included by default. No actual order or delivery-time figure is supplied. Accessible webpages do not establish immediate delivery of every item.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Storefront-fulfillment copy route | Invented subject | Inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate download | Instructions only | Not physical dispatch |
| Physical kit | Preparation unconfirmed | No guaranteed delivery |
| Optional parts | Separate selection | Not standard contents |
| Document entry | Readable link | Not connected order system |
| Fulfillment | Separate dispatch/writes | Authority and receipt |

## Three common errors

- If immediate document access becomes immediate physical dispatch, compare the page subject with warehouse wording, separating information access from fulfillment.

- If pending preparation becomes guaranteed receipt on a date, inspect warehouse confirmation and logistics evidence. A preparation state supplies no delivery promise.

- If pictured optional parts become standard kit contents, inspect composition wording and actual selections. A display image determines no delivery scope.

## How to verify the setup

Preserve digital and physical identities on both routes, comparing submitted passages, actual downstream input and adopted copy. Added download links resolve access alone without making goods available. Preparation evidence supplies neither delivery nor accessories. Successful document reads establish no connected Shopify order operation. Actual publication, selection, shipping and system writes require authorized interfaces and receipts. The workflow organizes writing evidence without dispatching goods or confirming customer receipt.
