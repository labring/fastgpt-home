# Temu semi-managed sellers: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT context limits. A removable lid describes structure and does not establish the cup body use conditions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed Temu product note says the lid is removable, while a use remark says the cup body is unsuitable for high-temperature settings. Specific heating-applicability material is absent. A convenience-copy request needs the lid and body subjects plus the restriction together in input. Removing a lid establishes a structural action, not permission to heat the body or use any treatment. The body restriction also cannot become a claim about lid composition. The draft may explain removal, while other use conditions remain dependent on applicable source material rather than inference from the component design.

## Three common errors

One failure is a summary retaining convenient removal and then adding suitability for high temperature. Another is earlier discussion of a different cup transferring its use attributes to this item. Inspect current subjects and restrictions to distinguish omitted source wording from cross-product inheritance. Reply length should accommodate the use condition, while actual context capacity needs independent verification. Neither supplies missing heating material. Inspect the actual passage against the reference-token limit rather than judging evidence adequacy from a short headline or from how much text the model can generate. A third error adds heating steps or treatment duration to make instructions look complete. Trace each added action to material for this cup. Unsupported steps remain unresolved; a removable lid cannot provide the missing basis for heating instructions.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should inspect structural description, body restriction and unresolved use cases separately. Removable must remain attached to the lid and the high-temperature qualification to the body. Compare the same request without history and with another cup discussion to detect transferred properties. If fixed output separates component, action and restriction, verify correspondence and successful parsing. Heating questions lacking specific material remain unresolved without added temperatures, durations or safety promises. The finished wording also must not present this proposed text exercise as an actual physical-use test or observed product result.
