# Temu semi-managed sellers: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Semi-managed product drafting can collect declaration wording beside the scope of an overseas inventory source. These explain the item and the source’s coverage separately. Proposed inputs retain both passages while lacking current warehouse availability evidence. Submitted values support wording preparation without establishing stock or accepted declaration. A human choice should arrange the next reading step rather than ask the user to select a business fact into existence.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product passage | Relevant declaration wording | No declaration-acceptance claim added |
| Supply scope | Overseas warehouse applicability | Not replaced by shop identity |
| Stock gap | Current availability evidence absent | No in-stock promise drafted |
| Waiting choice | Person requests inventory evidence | Inspect selection, connection and continuation |
| Business write | Actual inventory or listing operation | Evidence kept separate from interaction completion |

## Three common errors

Replacing the warehouse name with the shop name during handoff confuses supply scope. Inspect both passages and received text, repair the substitution and retest. Also select waiting for availability evidence and inspect the actual continuation. If the route still drafts an in-stock claim, check selection and connections and remove that unsupported wording. Receiving a warehouse name does not establish verified inventory. If a supplied warehouse name becomes a current in-stock claim, obtain availability evidence for this item and warehouse and inspect its source version. Correct identity is not verified inventory; remove the stock promise while the evidence is absent.

## How to verify the setup

Keep the product passage, warehouse scope and missing availability evidence together, comparing submissions with the final draft. After supplementation, repeat the original topic and inspect whether the new statement reaches drafting. Field checks, platform approval and inventory writes require actual downstream or external design. Continuing after interaction does not establish declaration, procurement or listing actions, nor does it confirm a changed stock state.
