# TikTok Shop US operations: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

TikTok Shop copy preparation can collect listing text, the intended wording scope and available inspection evidence. Proposed material separates a customer return statement as an unresolved lead rather than a product feature. The form collects and passes values without automatically establishing support for advertising claims. If a product property is absent, preserve that gap instead of turning a desired expression in the question into an evidenced selling point.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source distinction | Listing separate from customer statements | Unresolved lead is not a product feature |
| Status transfer | Statement accompanied by its unresolved note | Received input retains source status |
| Wrong route | Retain wording reaches feature addition | Inspect connections and added-sentence evidence |
| Property gap | Product detail absent from sources | Desired wording does not supply a fact |
| Publication | Actual advertising review and account procedure | Drafting is not platform acceptance |

## Three common errors

Losing the unresolved-status note while passing a customer statement can promote the statement to fact. Compare its source characterization with received text and restore it before drafting. Also test a choice to retain existing listing wording that is connected to adding new features. Inspect the selection, recipient and evidence for added sentences; a choice replaces neither product inspection nor advertising review. If one returned-item inspection becomes a model-wide feature, inspect the item, observation passage and product-source coverage. A case observation does not establish properties of every item; remove the expansion without model evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain published wording, inspection material, unresolved leads and the draft, tracing each included property to supplied evidence. Test before and after supplementation. A user selection follows its connected branch and is not a product-database selector. Additional validation, approval and publication belong to their actual downstream procedures. Form completion or draft generation does not establish platform acceptance or resolution of a return dispute.
