# General export declarations: copy drafting with model connections

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Use a brand field and two model entries to inspect connectivity, source delivery and writing support. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

The proposed invoice’s brand field is a point for checking source delivery, not support for a full brand introduction. Inspect whether the actual model request contains the field and surrounding product text, then check for invented company history in the draft. A service response describes only part of the call path. Retain original input and generated output separately, and inspect application parameters independently from channel configuration. An unexpected writing tone alone does not establish connection failure. Inspect an invoice brand field separately from brand history, usage authorization and marketing endorsement. Record the original location and subject, then check whether the draft turns a completed field into an authorization claim. A generated brand introduction is not authorization evidence. For supplementary documents, inspect their relationship to the current product and contributor so that another supplier’s history does not become this draft’s material. The test defines usable sources rather than merely observing willingness to generate an introduction.

## Three common errors

- If a full brand introduction appears without the invoice brand field in the request, retain input and added statements. Investigate omitted material and background sources beyond successful connectivity.

- If both model descriptions arrive but are merged or one is selected, compare declaration, invoice and output. Locate overwrite without deciding document validity through a channel change.

- If a populated brand field becomes authorization or endorsement, trace the relevant evidence, product and contributor. The field supplies no grant, and another supplier’s background cannot fill it.

## How to verify the setup

This example lacks a core-benefit source. Model settings cannot provide functions or results absent from the material. Before obtaining a product description, inspect whether the draft preserves that gap; after adding it, use the same settings to compare source delivery and output. The test uses text and does not validate image, tool or other input capabilities. Record connectivity, delivered originals and writing scope separately, without treating one successful check as completion of the entire drafting task.
