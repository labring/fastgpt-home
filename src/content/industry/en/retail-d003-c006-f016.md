# General export declarations: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Export copy input should separate the contract’s product description from packing-list wording so that packaging quantities do not become specifications. Proposed material supplies both passages while lacking manufacturer use guidance. A form can collect text, source identities and the topic for drafting, without adding technical properties to trade documents. Complete invoices and contracts do not independently support licensing, quality or performance claims.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Object distinction | Contract goods and packing passages | Not merged into product specifications |
| Context transfer | Quantity with its source column | Packaging purpose survives downstream |
| Source request | Manufacturer choice receives old contract | Inspect version, selection and received values |
| Claim scope | Use guidance absent | No certification or performance claim drafted |
| Business effect | Separate contract or declaration record | Interaction does not replace approval or acceptance |

## Three common errors

If the packing column is dropped and only quantity wording reaches drafting, inspect the changed meaning and restore its context before retesting. Another choice requests manufacturer guidance but still passes the old contract passage. Inspect selection, received values and source versions, repairing the handoff before drafting. Connected nodes do not establish that the additional manufacturer evidence participated in the present text. If the draft turns complete trade documents into product certification, inspect the actual evidence for the stated certification and its applicable subject. Contracts, invoices and packing lists do not supply it; remove the claim when the source is absent.

## How to verify the setup

Trace topic, entered values, received sources and copy, retaining the absent use guidance and checking that document completeness never becomes product certification. After adding material, repeat the original topic and inspect use of the new source. Validation, contract approval, customs submission or publication require downstream or external procedures. Interaction completion establishes the handoff result rather than effectiveness of a transaction or declaration.
