# General import declarations: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. A storage location supports inventory position, not product-origin evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed import product note says the displayed location is a storage site, while an inventory brief says enquiries can be made with that warehouse. Origin evidence is not supplied. A request for copy describing products from that place needs the storage role and missing origin material in input. Location can help describe where inventory is held, not where goods were manufactured. An enquiry route also cannot establish continuous stock availability. First inspect what the place modifies in the source, rather than promoting a location word into origin or supply commitments.

## Three common errors

One common error is a summary retaining the place name while omitting storage, allowing the draft to say locally manufactured. Another is a historical brand story about a different batch transferring origin to the current product. Compare actual input location roles, goods identity and source scope to distinguish summary distortion from historical inheritance. Check model context capability independently and allow reply space for unresolved evidence. Additional space still cannot create a missing origin document; a coherent story is not a substitute for a source that establishes the product-origin relationship. A third error turns a warehouse enquiry route into continuous stock availability. Check for inventory evidence applicable to the item and current time. A contact route explains how to enquire, not saleable status, and cannot add a supply promise to a location introduction.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance can separate an inventory-location sentence from the product-origin item still needing verification. The former must not become a manufacturing statement, and an enquiry route must not become a stock guarantee. Compare the task with and without the earlier brand story to identify added geographic attributes. Fixed output for page editing should retain location role, applicable goods and required material through actual parsing. Any later origin document needs its specific subject and scope checked, without extending one entry to every item or treating this fixture as an origin-compliance determination.
