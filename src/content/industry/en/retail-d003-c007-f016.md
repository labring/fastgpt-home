# General import declarations: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Import product copy can collect commodity wording, transport identifiers and the intended-use question with their separate origins. Proposed material supplies invoice and bill-of-lading passages while explicitly lacking manufacturer model and composition evidence. The form passes these inputs downstream without supplying product performance. A use description requires product guidance; arranged transport does not establish sale eligibility or suitability for a particular use.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source identity | Invoice and transport passages retained separately | Their purposes are not merged |
| Mark handoff | Transport identifier labelled as a model | Repair its label before drafting again |
| Use branch | Commodity-only choice reaches use description | Inspect evidence for added sentences |
| Product gap | Model and composition sources absent | No technical properties supplied automatically |
| Further approval | Separate product review and publication | Submission does not establish approval |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

If a shipping mark becomes a model during handoff, inspect its original purpose and the label attached to drafting input, then repair and retest. Also select organizing supplied commodity wording while its connection reaches a use-description step. Inspect selection, recipient and added suitability sentences, retaining unsupported uses as missing evidence rather than allowing a branch name to establish a fact. If an invoice naming goods becomes a composition claim, inspect manufacturer composition wording and correspondence to this item. Trade naming does not supply material composition; retain the missing property without its source.

## How to verify the setup

Trace invoice wording, transport marks, source gaps and corresponding draft sentences. After obtaining manufacturer guidance, repeat the topic and inspect actual transfer of the addition. List indispensable materials and test missing-input behavior without portraying the form as a business database or automatic validator. Product review, procurement admission and publication approval require actual downstream procedures; completion does not establish import processing, product approval or publication.
