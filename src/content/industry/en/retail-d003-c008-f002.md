# Amazon FBA preparation: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Internal inbound-carton markings and consumer-package copy address different readers. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed FBA writing exercise describes outer-carton markings for warehouse identification only and says consumer-package wording needs separate review. A retail-packaging request needs warehouse purpose and the separate-review condition together in input. Internal markings help staff identify cartons; they are not consumer benefits, and handling abbreviations cannot become product functions. Consumer wording is absent. The model may identify information still needed, but warehouse convenience cannot be described as a user experience already obtained from the product or as a validated retail message.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Physical subject | Outer carton differs from product | Check attribute transfer. |
| Retail gap | Consumer wording needs review | Keep unresolved without inventing benefits. |
| Protection history | Transport-protection discussion | Do not convert into product durability. |
| Editorial format | Audience, subject and unresolved content | Verify field correspondence and parsing. |

## Three common errors

One failure is a citation losing warehouse only and internal markings being copied into retail copy. Another is earlier transport-protection discussion turning into durability of the product itself. Inspect intended reader, physical subject and writing task for each input passage to distinguish lost purpose from transferred carton attributes. Check actual model capacity and reply length separately. Inspect the reference-token limit against retention of the purpose sentence. Additional consumer storytelling does not provide the absent consumer-package basis or correct a mismatch between warehouse handling and product performance. A third error places warehouse-identification wording in a consumer-specification field. Inspect audience-content pairs after output parsing and verify the intended editing subject. Complete input scope does not establish that downstream fields received the right material.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance can separate warehouse-identification wording, supplied material actually usable for retail and package content still requiring review. Ensure these categories do not fill one another. Compare the same request without history and with transport-protection discussion to detect added durability promises. If fixed output feeds packaging editing, verify audience, carton subject and product subject fields remain complete and correctly related after parsing. When consumer wording is later supplied, check its applicable version; merely placing new text in context cannot establish that physical packaging has already been revised.
