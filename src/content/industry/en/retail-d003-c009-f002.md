# Amazon FBM fulfillment: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Amazon FBM fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT context limits. A preorder notice and draft schedule cannot become a current stock promise. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed FBM marketing note allows recording preorder interest while current stock awaits confirmation. A schedule header says draft. A purchase-reminder request needs interest, pending confirmation and draft status preserved in input. Registering interest does not establish order acceptance or reserved inventory, and a draft schedule does not establish a dispatch time. The copy can explain an enquiry or registration route, but supply conditions remain unconfirmed. Making a purchase action sound clear cannot justify adding an immediate-stock promise that the source does not provide.

## Three common errors

One common error is a summary retaining registration while dropping interest, producing a direct-order message. Another is an earlier in-stock announcement being inherited as the current state. Inspect registration subject, current stock evidence and schedule identity to distinguish trimmed wording from mixed periods. Reply length should accommodate pending confirmation, while actual model context capacity needs its own check. An older message appearing in context does not make it currently applicable. Inspect the reference limit for retained state words rather than checking only that the product name was returned. A third error presents a draft schedule as a confirmed dispatch arrangement. Check schedule version and confirmation evidence, then inspect state verbs in the reminder. Recording interest, confirming stock and arranging dispatch remain separate events.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should draft registration, supply-state and schedule wording separately, preserving interest recording, stock awaiting confirmation and a draft arrangement. Do not add quantity or timing guarantees. Compare the same task with empty history and the earlier stock announcement to trace any added promise to its input passage. If fixed output supports a page or service handoff, verify complete parsing of state, applicable period and confirmation action. Any newer inventory material still needs its subject and time checked; an updated plan cannot become a statement that dispatch already occurred.
