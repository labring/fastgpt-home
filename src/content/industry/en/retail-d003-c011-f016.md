# Refund-only disputes: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Refund-only disputes, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Using refund-only material for copy preparation requires separating customer statements, warehouse records and proposed service wording. Hypothetical inputs supply the complaint question and dispatch passage while lacking a platform decision. A form can collect these for internal drafting without determining facts or responsibility. It must not turn a complaint into an established defect or expand an individual dispute into a service promise covering all orders.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed input or selection | Observable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statement origin | Application wording identified as customer claims | Not promoted to a verified defect |
| Scope boundary | Individual case distinct from general wording | Not expanded to every order |
| Lost status | Recipient omits the source characterization | Restore it against complete input |
| Processing evidence | Source choice produces a resolved-service claim | Inspect route and remove unsupported judgment |
| Business handling | Separate refund and publication results | Submission is not completed handling |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Losing the note that a statement comes from the customer may turn it into a verification finding. Inspect complete input and downstream text, restoring its status before drafting. Also select obtaining processing evidence while the connected path produces a resolved-service claim. Inspect the selection, received sources and added judgments, removing unsupported conclusions rather than inferring dispute resolution from a completed form. If submitted form data is interpreted as receipt of a refund, inspect this order’s payment-processing evidence and actual response. Application wording and finished drafting do not replace receipt results; retain the financial state as unresolved without that evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the question, business record, absent decision and draft, tracing wording to evidence and applicable scope. Test missing and supplemented interactions; a selection node executes its connected branch rather than automatically approving a case. Validation, refund decisions and publication require actual downstream arrangements. Submission establishes neither refund receipt, responsibility determination nor a published page.
