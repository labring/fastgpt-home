# Franchise store supervision: multi-turn prompts for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Franchise supervision copy drafting draws on four document types: store visit forms, franchise agreements, rectification notices, and display standards. These materials are produced by headquarters, regional supervisors, and franchise owners at different points in time. Their formats vary, and their update rhythm is mainly weekly and monthly. When drafting copy, the model needs to confirm facts such as product names, display positions, promotion conditions, and rectification status from these materials, then organize them into sentences suitable for external use. Fields absent from the materials, such as whether a product has received headquarters approval for promotion or whether a rectification has passed re-inspection, are unsupported information that the model must not supply. The key difference from direct-operated convenience stores is that headquarters supervises franchisee execution of standards; the rights and obligations boundary in copy must match the original text of the agreement and rectification notice.

The four document types also differ in what they record. A store visit form records observations at a point in time, such as a price tag display value. A franchise agreement records the framework governing the relationship. A rectification notice records a requirement, a deadline, and a re-inspection status. Display standards record placement requirements. Copy drafting must keep these roles separate rather than merging them into a single assertion.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Multi-turn dialogue is used during drafting to supplement materials step by step, ask about missing fields, and correct wording. The prompt needs to state the material scope, the answering task, and how to handle missing evidence. The memory turns setting determines how many earlier rounds are carried in; too few rounds may fail to use product information already confirmed earlier, while too many rounds may bring in unrelated content. Question optimization handles reference resolution in continuous conversation; when an earlier round mentions a product and a later round uses a pronoun, the optimized question needs to be checked to confirm it still points to the same object. Model and length settings require checking whether the context length is sufficient to hold excerpts from the store visit form and display standards. The response format follows downstream needs; if downstream reads a fixed structure, select `json_schema` and fill in the corresponding format requirement, then check whether the returned structure matches.

## Three common errors

1. Reading "re-inspection record is empty" in the rectification notice as "rectification completed." Input is the rectification notice original text and the re-inspection record field. The comparison object is the required completion date against the re-inspection status. The observable result is that the re-inspection record remains empty, and the phrase "completed rectification" in the draft sentence is unsupported by the materials.
2. Expanding "material content is subject to headquarters' written notice for the current period" in the franchise agreement into "headquarters has approved this copy." Input is clause 7 of the agreement and the draft sentence. The comparison object is whether the agreement records approval of this specific copy. The observable result is that the agreement only governs the source of materials and does not record approval status for this sentence, which remains unsupported.
3. Treating "price tag shows 2 yuan" in the store visit form as directly consistent with the system price. Input is the store visit form excerpt and the price inconsistency record in the rectification notice. The comparison object is the same product, the same period, and the same price field. The observable result is that the store visit form records only the price tag display value and does not record the system price, so consistency cannot be determined and remains pending verification.
