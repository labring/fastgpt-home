# Franchise store supervision: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Franchise-supervision copy distinguishes observations, proposed correction and actual completion. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Suppose a supervision note records a shelf-display observation only, a proposed correction remains under discussion and a photograph concerns another store. Collect store identity, observed wording and photo correspondence, then choose material review or correction evidence. Submission passes information to writing after interaction. The node supplies no inspection database; business approval and evidence validation require downstream processes.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supervision-copy form | Proposed material | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display observation | Note level | Not formal violation |
| Correction advice | Discussion pending | Not completed |
| Photo subject | Another store | Check correspondence |
| Choice branches | Material or correction | Original received wording |
| External handling | Separate publication/work orders | Authority and receipt |

## Three common errors

An observation rewritten as a formal violation needs applicable rules and a finding checked. Proposed correction reported as completed needs site-execution evidence checked. Another store photograph applied here needs subject correspondence checked. These concern observation level, action stage and evidence ownership independently of completed fields.

## How to verify the setup

Compare proposed copy sentence by sentence with source wording and photo notes. A photograph without correspondence supports no claim about this store. Added correction evidence updates that status alone while penalties or certification need real authority and process. External publication, correction work orders and store writes require business interfaces and receipts. The form gathers traceable material without replacing an operational judgment through submission. Locate observations separately from formal findings, retaining proposed-correction wording through copy review and checking the photograph's store independently.
