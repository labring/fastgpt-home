# Franchisee onboarding: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect simulation, written assessment and opening practice as separate stages across current material and older cohorts. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed opening-training note records completion of a checkout simulation, with real-equipment practice scheduled separately. An assessment records only written identification. Historical copy describes hands-on work by another store cohort; its outcome does not belong to this run. Inspect whether the actual request includes the current material and cohort before examining historical store and activity subjects. Simulation, written assessment and equipment practice are separate stages. Their joint mention or an attendance record does not establish completed practice or independent operating competence.

## Three common errors

- If the separately scheduled equipment stage disappears and simulation becomes completed hands-on work, compare the full note, actual request and draft to locate the missing condition.

- If another cohort’s practice enters a follow-up about this run, hold current material constant across history conditions and inspect cohort, store and activity identity.

- If written identification becomes demonstrated operating competence, inspect the corresponding practice evidence. Parsing an assessment summary does not establish practical competence without the corresponding demonstration record.

## How to verify the setup

Ask separately what this run completed, which stage remains scheduled and what the assessment supports, tracing sentences to actual input. When an equipment record is added, verify cohort identity before repeating the questions. Inspect model context capability and configuration separately from this request’s scope, and citation-token limits separately from reply length. Extending output supplies no practice evidence. Keep training stages distinct from formal store opening, which requires its own record. These are hypothetical texts, without real learner results.
