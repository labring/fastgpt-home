# Franchise fee settlement: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect fee descriptions, service-list references and deposit-review status without borrowing another store’s outcome. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed franchise-service explanation uses an invoice item labelled training service and a note referring to a service schedule that remains absent. A deposit ledger records this matter as under review. No service schedule or review outcome is supplied; history concerns another store whose settlement ended. Inspect current store and subject identity in the actual input, preserving item description, schedule pointer and review stage. A service name does not enumerate the whole package or establish completed teaching. No real amount or settlement condition is provided.

## Three common errors

- If the missing-schedule condition is lost and copy lists a complete package, trace each service name rather than constructing a catalogue from the invoice item.

- If another store’s historical settlement enters this matter’s explanation, hold the current ledger constant across history conditions and inspect store, subject and status referents.

- If the invoice item becomes completed training, inspect actual course records. Naming a service and delivering it require different evidence, even in a well-formed structured result.

## How to verify the setup

Ask separately about the service item, actual contents and review progress, tracing each sentence to material received this turn. Inspect the schedule’s subject when it arrives and the review decision’s state separately; these additions are not one outcome. Check citation tokens separately from reply limits. Model capability and context configuration need their own basis; one successful request does not establish the full window. The text explains supplied records, while fee handling, course delivery and formal settlement retain their own evidence.
