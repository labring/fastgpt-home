# Brand member operations: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Preserve signup audience and current-event purpose separately from historical campaign copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed member-invitation brief sends event material to registered participants only and excludes store-experience booking from this round. Historical copy offered an in-store experience for another event. Actual requests need both restrictions instead of compressing registered people into every member or reusing the old booking call. Sending material is a communication purpose, not evidence of places or arrangements. The draft may describe current information without turning a store mention in long background into a bookable benefit or claiming an actual campaign action from the hypothetical brief.

## Three common errors

First, the signup restriction may be absent from input; compare the full brief with the actual request for delivered audience wording. Second, historical booking may enter this round; hold current material constant with empty history and inspect event and service identity. Third, a shorter reply may omit no booking this round; compare the draft with both restrictions and allow expression space. These concern input coverage, historical applicability and output scope. More reply budget creates neither an experience source nor real permission to send the material to any particular audience.

## How to verify the setup

Ask whom to invite, what the material serves and what this round excludes, comparing request with copy. Token-limited references need registered-only and no-booking clauses retained before checking adoption. A reply allowance is not the model’s full context window; inspect actual capability separately. Added store wording must match this event rather than borrow historical scope. This exercise sends no message and changes no member authority. It determines no actual permission validity and presents no unprovided benefit as delivered, keeping the copy within the current supplied evidence.
