# Omnichannel stock transfers: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Keep warehouse arrival, store arrival and regional scope distinct in transfer-service copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed channel-service brief tracks goods to a regional warehouse, leaves store receipt for separate checking and describes the east zone only. Current store copy must not compress warehouse into store or drop geography to offer support everywhere. A request retaining only tracking and a region name still lacks the full boundary. Inspect actual retained sentences after retrieved material enters context. Uploading documents does not establish that the writing step received the note. The draft must not invent transport commitments or an extended service endpoint absent from the material provided for the proposed writing task.

## Three common errors

First, a reference budget may trim store receipt separately checked; compare the note with actual input to locate retrieval-versus-writing loss. Second, the draft may turn the regional warehouse into the customer’s store; inspect location and adopted sentence so fluent wording changes no endpoint. Third, shorter copy may drop east zone; inspect output coverage and length instead of broadening through simplification. These concern qualifier delivery, place meaning and regional expression. Adjusting budgets does not establish an actual service upgrade or that an unprovided destination is included.

## How to verify the setup

Ask where tracking ends, what remains to verify and which zone the sources cover, comparing input and copy sentence by sentence. Check actual model capacity, recording reference allowance separately from reply length. Missing input conditions need transfer repair first. Added store or regional sources require identity checks before rewriting, without extending one region to every location. This exercise transfers no goods and notifies no store. It guarantees no real arrival; the text may describe only the service scope supported by the material actually delivered to the writing step.
