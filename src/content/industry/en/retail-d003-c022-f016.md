# Omnichannel stock transfers: interactive forms for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Omnichannel stock transfers for a brand retailer move goods between stores and warehouses. This category does not handle marketing outreach, so the working materials describe stock positions and transfer scope rather than campaign audiences. Four sources feed the work: ERP inventory, store POS records, e-commerce orders, and WMS data. The routine documents are transfer requests, inventory snapshots, store shortage lists, and transfer logistics notes.

For copy drafting, these documents decide which stock states and transfer ranges a proposed sentence may cite. A transfer request states the requesting party, the releasing party, the item, the requested quantity, the request date, and a status value. An inventory snapshot states the adjustable quantity for an item on a given date. A store shortage list records that an item was short at a store on a given date. A transfer logistics note describes movement of goods. The supplied material gives daily and weekly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time behavior, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from those frequencies.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The interactive forms step collects values a person enters and passes them to later nodes after submission. The Form Input node records workflow state, enters an interaction state, and continues downward once the user completes the interaction. The User Selection node behaves the same way at trigger time and then evaluates the user's choice to run a branch. Neither node supplies a business database function, a personnel directory, or a business data selector. Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations require separate implementation in an external system or a later node. The documents describe interaction waiting, state recording, and hiding of the dialogue box; they do not describe a control switch for those behaviors, a submission count, automatic judgment, or snapshot guarantees.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample, used only for reasoning about this page. the source material reads "Transfer request T-2041, requesting party a store in the East China region, releasing party a warehouse in the South China region, item a spring coat style, requested quantity 60 pieces, request date 8 April, status pending review." "The warehouse inventory snapshot dated 7 April shows 45 pieces adjustable for that style." "The store shortage list dated 8 April records that the style was short at that store." Proposed draft sentence under review: "The store has completed transfer inbound of 60 pieces of that spring coat style."

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Type | Assumed starting point and reason for this exercise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer request number | Form Input field | Collects text so it can be compared character by character with the source material |
| Item and quantity | Form Input field | Collects item name and piece count for comparison with the snapshot adjustable quantity |
| Request status | User Selection branch | Takes its value from the material status; pending review does not enter a completion branch |
| Proposed draft sentence | Form Input field | Collects the sentence to be judged so a later comparison can be made |
| Interaction waiting | Existing node behavior | Records state and waits for submission after triggering; no separate switch is documented |
| Review conclusion | Manual review action | A person fills this in after comparing the material with the proposed sentence |

The starting values above are exercise inputs chosen because the sample material states them; they are not official defaults. The comparison method is direct value matching between the collected field and the corresponding statement in the source material.

## Three common errors

1. Treating "pending review" as a completed transfer. The check compares the request status value with the completion fact asserted in the proposed sentence. When the status reads pending review and the sentence asserts completed inbound, the two do not agree, and the sentence is unsupported by the material.
2. Using the snapshot adjustable quantity of 45 pieces to support a 60-piece transfer. The check compares the requested quantity with the snapshot adjustable quantity. When the requested quantity exceeds the adjustable quantity, the transfer is unsupported by stock evidence for that quantity range.
3. Treating an interactive node as an approval or database-write function. The check compares the node description with the business action required. Approval and write-back need separate system implementation, so a node that only collects values or branches on a choice cannot supply them.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the hypothetical source material and the proposed sentence "The store has completed transfer inbound of 60 pieces of that spring coat style." Compare the request status "pending review" with the completion fact "completed inbound." If the two do not agree, the proposed sentence is not supported by the material. The expected observation is a mismatch; this page does not claim the check was run.
2. Enter the requested quantity of 60 pieces and the snapshot adjustable quantity of 45 pieces. Compare the two values. If the requested quantity is greater than the adjustable quantity, the transfer is not supported by stock evidence within that quantity range. The expected observation is a quantity gap of 15 pieces.
3. Enter the store shortage list record and the proposed sentence. Compare the shortage fact with the inbound completion fact. If the material records only a shortage and does not record inbound, completion of inbound remains unverified. The expected observation is a missing inbound record, and the evidence gap stays open.

These three checks test the named primary task of copy drafting: whether a proposed sentence is supported by the supplied transfer documents. They do not test identity certification, report analysis, or any other task. A relevance score is not a category, entity, or permission filter, and retrieval is not authorization. Where a document field is not provided, its value is unknown. A pending request is not an approval, an offered option is not execution, and an unsigned termination is not a completed termination.
