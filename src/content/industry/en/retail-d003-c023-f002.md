# Brand repair services: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Keep service description, observed symptoms and unresolved items distinct in structured repair copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed repair brief offers appearance assessment, records a case where an indicator lights and schedules functional testing separately. The writing exercise uses service scope, observed case and pending items as three headings, not new software fields. Actual input needs those source roles retained. A case observation cannot fill service capability, and generated structure cannot erase scheduled testing. Appearance assessment supplies no current repair outcome. No complete inspection or actual restoration record is provided, so the proposed text must not announce either from its layout alone.

## Three common errors

First, the scope heading may turn lights up into a repair guarantee; trace whether the sentence belongs to a case or service description. Second, structured output may omit pending items; inspect both field presence and content rather than parsing success alone. Third, separate testing may be absent from the request; compare original and delivered text before increasing reply length. These concern source placement, structural transfer and input gaps. Tidy formatting creates no actual service capability or supporting result missing from the original brief and its case material.

## How to verify the setup

Ask each heading what the service offers, what the case observed and which test remains, retaining request, originals and structured draft. Parseable output with an empty pending field still needs content inspection. Missing source outcomes remain missing after larger budgets. Check actual context capability separately from reply settings and repeat with the same complete material when needed. This exercise diagnoses nothing, repairs no device and decides no warranty responsibility. It claims no real repair effect, and the case observation cannot become normal function across every device.
