# Brand outlet operations: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Inspect store and item scope during clearance-copy revisions, preserving differences between earlier and current campaign conditions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A writing exercise begins with a clearance-team note covering selected past-season clothing and participating stores. A follow-up asks to emphasize the whole shop as last time. Separate historical copy from current material; earlier wording does not establish present campaign conditions. If the new draft includes every shop item, compare store notes, historical sentence and output to investigate mixed scope. Specify supplied conditions and unresolved claims: repeated imitation expands neither product participation nor approved scope.

## Three common errors

- If historical whole-shop wording overwrites current selected stores, compare current note, earlier phrase and output to locate expansion. Previous copy establishes no present approval.

- If a candidate coat borrows another item’s material or wearing occasion, inspect style, history and optimized question. Collect absent evidence or investigate omitted delivery separately.

- If stock clearance becomes a defect, scarcity or performance claim, trace descriptions and applicable approval. Inventory background supplies no such support, and drafting publishes no campaign or label change.

## How to verify the setup

Test the task with changed store scope and product continuation, recording whether every added phrase comes from current sources or historical wording. Stock clearance establishes neither a defect nor scarcity, performance or commercial results. After obtaining product descriptions and applicable approval, recheck adopted originals and retain unresolved items. A draft may be reviewed by the responsible owner, but generation publishes no activity, changes no label and establishes neither promotional approval nor achieved outcomes.
