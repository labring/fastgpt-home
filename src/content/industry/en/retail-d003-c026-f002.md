# Brand-owned store operations: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Separate a change enquiry, stock condition and completed service in direct-store copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed direct-store service card accepts enquiries about a change, but confirmation that it can be carried out requires checking this store’s stock first. A reception note records the customer’s requested colour, while historical copy reports a completed change at another branch. The current question-and-answer draft has neither stock confirmation nor completion evidence. Keep branch scope and the stock-check condition for confirming the change in actual input. Supplying enquiry alone removes the reason a commitment needs qualification. The other branch’s completion cannot supply a result for this store or establish that the requested colour is available.

## Three common errors

First, references may trim the stock condition; trace the original service sentence into the actual request with its action. Second, the draft may turn a recorded colour into an available exchange; inspect actor and outcome and seek separate stock evidence, leaving availability unresolved when absent. Third, another branch’s completed case may enter this answer; hold the current card fixed and compare empty history for branch identity. These test input coverage, action stage and historical object. Extra reply space cannot create a stock source or verify a completed change.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what can be enquired about, what needs checking first and whether current completion evidence exists, comparing request and copy. Record retained reference and history text separately; reply length governs expression space while actual model context capacity needs its own check. A later stock sheet requires the correct store and colour before revision. This exercise queries no live inventory and processes no exchange. A finished answer does not establish that the service was delivered, so the current copy must explain consultation and checking without turning an unprovided result into an accomplished event.
