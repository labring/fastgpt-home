# Dealer ordering events: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Retain pending customization review, sample-logo purpose and optional accessories without production promises. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Invented event material describes a sample garment whose logo is for display only. A factory reply says customization requires artwork review, and an accessory list marks optional choices. Collect series, sample purpose and original passages, then choose customization evidence or accessory scope. A display image grants no general brand-mark rights, and review of artwork is no accepted production order. No actual order, specification parameter or price is supplied. Entered demand establishes no purchase commitment.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Dealer-event promotion form | Invented restriction | Drafting check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customization reply | Artwork review pending | No universal-production promise |
| Sample logo | Display only | Not usage authority |
| Accessories | Optional | Not included in every set |
| Choice route | Customization or composition | Inspect actual passages |
| Purchasing | Separate business acceptance | Entered demand is no transaction |

## Three common errors

- If pending artwork review becomes production capability for every design, compare the factory reply and current request, retaining the unreviewed condition.

- If a sample logo becomes permission for dealer use, inspect display purpose and actual usage rights. Image existence replaces no authority evidence.

- If optional accessories become included in every set, compare the component list and confirmed choices. A garment image supplies no inclusion scope.

## How to verify the setup

Compare submitted material and actual input on both routes for series, purpose and gaps. Ask the writer to identify sources for each customization or inclusion promise. Factory replies cover their request alone without extending to other artwork, while accessory choices require separate evidence. Business review, mark use, formal ordering and record writes need authority and receipts. The form transfers material without certifying supply capability or completing a transaction. After parsing, inspect conditions sentence by sentence rather than allowing polished promotional copy to hide unresolved matters.
