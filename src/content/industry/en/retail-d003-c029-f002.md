# After-sales repairs and reships: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Replacement copy must keep a cover plate separate from the whole latch repair. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed repair note plans a replacement cover plate. The parts page excludes the latch, and a communication memo leaves the receiving address to be checked. The customer explanation therefore has a component scope and a pending action, without dispatch or completed repair evidence. Keep the latch exclusion beside the replacement object in actual context. A request reduced to cover may invite whole-assembly wording, while a fluent closing can incorrectly convert an unverified address into arranged delivery. Neither expansion is supported by the proposed documents supplied for this writing task.

## Three common errors

First, references may cut off the exclusion; compare the parts page with the actual request to check whether the latch-exclusion clause reached the writing request. Second, planned replacement may become dispatched; compare work-note stage and email verbs, seeking separate shipment evidence instead of supplying a completed state. Third, historical conversation may import a whole-lock repair; inspect component and case identity using empty-history comparison. These distinguish scope, stage and historical ownership. Longer replies or larger context cannot establish that a repair was carried out or that another component belongs in this replacement.

## How to verify the setup

Trace each noun and action in the email: what part is proposed, what is excluded and what still needs checking. Inspect reference allowance, history and reply length separately from actual model context capacity. This exercise fills no real address and sends no email. Later shipment material requires the same case, component and state before updating the copy. Current wording may explain proposed scope and pending checks, without concluding from a cover-plate action that the latch works, the whole device has recovered or responsibility has been determined for an actual repair.
