# Supermarket supplier accounts: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Separate document assistance, mutual reconciliation and settlement in supplier-service copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed supplier-service memo assists in organising an index of original delivery documents. A service note assigns formal reconciliation to both parties later, while history contains a settlement notice for another account. Current introductory copy must keep document indexing separate from agreement that accounts match and from settlement itself. Retain the action objects and stages in actual input rather than reducing the service to document assistance alone. An old notice may inform tone but supplies neither a voucher nor a commitment for the account discussed by the current proposed brief.

## Three common errors

First, copy may expand directory preparation into completed reconciliation; inspect action and document object and seek both parties’ checking record. Second, shorter text may omit the separate-checking condition; trace it from service note to actual request and then inspect reply coverage. Third, historical settlement may migrate into current service copy; compare account and period using generation without that history. These test action evidence, qualifier transfer and record ownership. Larger reply space cannot create a settlement fact, confirm the parties’ agreement or supply a missing notice for the current account.

## How to verify the setup

Require the copy to explain the assistance, who checks what and which results lack current evidence, tracing each statement to supplied material. Record references, history and reply allowances separately and verify actual model context capacity. No amount or real account is provided here, and the exercise submits, reconciles and settles nothing. A later notice needs subject, period and exact-state checks before revision. Its absence from input does not establish an unsettled account; current evidence supports organisation and subsequent checking scope without deciding the actual business outcome.
