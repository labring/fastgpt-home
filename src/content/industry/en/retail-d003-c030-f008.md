# Supermarket supplier accounts: workflow routing for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Supplier entry settlement for supermarkets and convenience stores centres on purchase-and-sale or consignment payables. A supplier may have no resident counter operation, so the settlement record is built from documents rather than from on-site activity. The main data sources are ERP purchasing, the supplier portal, POS sales and the finance system, with weekly or monthly updates. The documents handled day to day are purchase contracts, delivery acceptance notes, rebate agreements and reconciliation statements.

Copy drafting for goods and marketing text draws its subject, object, status and validity scope from these materials. Whether a proposed sentence is supported depends on what the material actually states. A purchase contract can name the parties and the settlement method. A delivery acceptance note can record a date, a delivered quantity and an accepted quantity. A rebate agreement can set out a calculation method. None of these documents automatically supplies an effective date, a settlement basis or a supply period unless the text says so.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The Form Input node triggers an interactive state, records the workflow state, and after the user completes required fields and submits, collects the entered information and passes it to later nodes. The User Selection node also enters an interactive state when triggered, and when the user makes a choice, judges that choice and executes the corresponding "yes" branch. Waiting for interaction, recording state and hiding the dialogue box are existing node behaviours. No separate global state-recording switch is documented. The collected information must be explicitly passed to later retrieval, judgement or interface nodes. Order writing, document backfill and external state synchronisation require a separate business interface or later process.

The same-vector-model requirement for retrieval libraries applies only to vector retrieval association. It does not extend to business registration websites or other external systems.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. The material text supplied for checking consists of three sentences. "This purchase contract is signed between Supermarket A and Food Supplier B; the settlement method is purchase and sale, with a payment period of thirty days." "The delivery acceptance note records: on 10 March 2025, Supplier B delivered 200 boxes of biscuits, of which 180 boxes passed acceptance." "The rebate agreement provides: the annual rebate for the biscuit category is calculated at two percent of the tax-inclusive purchase amount." One draft sentence to be judged is: "The annual rebate for Supplier B's biscuit category is calculated at two percent of the tax-inclusive purchase amount, and the accepted quantity is based on the delivery acceptance note." The judgement scope is limited to the same subject, same period and same field across these three sentences.

## Three common errors

1. Reading "the rebate agreement provides that the annual rebate is calculated at two percent of the tax-inclusive purchase amount" as an already effective rebate. Agreement signature and rebate effectiveness are different facts, and the material states no effectiveness clause. Check: input the rebate agreement sentence, compare whether the draft sentence writes an effective status. The condition is met when the draft sentence states only the calculation method.

2. Treating the 180 boxes that passed acceptance on the delivery acceptance note as the settlement quantity. The material states only the accepted quantity and does not state the settlement basis. Check: input the delivery acceptance note sentence, compare whether the draft sentence treats the accepted quantity as the settlement quantity. The condition is met when the draft sentence retains "the accepted quantity is based on the delivery acceptance note."

3. Extending the thirty-day payment period in the purchase contract into a supply period. A quotation or payment period does not indicate a supply period. Check: input the purchase contract sentence, compare whether a supply-period expression appears in the draft sentence. The condition is met when no such expression appears. All three remain pending verification and do not constitute denial or conflict.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the three hypothetical material sentences and the draft sentence, then compare whether the rebate percentage, accepted quantity and payment period fields in the draft sentence share the same subject, same period and same field as the source text. The condition is met when the draft sentence adds no effectiveness or supply-period expression.

2. Input an explicit paper empty result [] and check whether the downstream node design handles an empty result, without claiming that a fictional rule retrieval necessarily returns []. Observe whether the failure branch records the gap.

3. Input one association request across libraries using different vector models, check the identifiers and exclude those that do not conform, and record the actual observation. Do not run a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recall, and do not infer a runtime result.
