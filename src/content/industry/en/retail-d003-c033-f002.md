# Trade and FX administration: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Trade-service copy must separate correspondence summaries from fund handling and review decisions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed trade-directory service scope organises summaries of correspondence. A handover memo sends material to the responsible person for checking, and an exclusion says the service handles no funds. External copy can describe organisation and transfer without offering payment handling or claiming approval. All three texts need to reach the actual request; a service title alone loses both scope and the pending reviewer. These are independently proposed ordinary documents, establishing no institutional procedure or software ability to carry out a financial, registration or approval process.

## Three common errors

First, summary preparation may expand into handling receipts and payments; trace service verbs and the exclusion to identify the added action. Second, transfer for checking may become approval; compare memo verbs and draft stage, seeking a decision source instead of supplying an unsupported conclusion. Third, history may import another party’s service scope; inspect entity identity using an empty-history request. These distinguish action, decision and object. Extra reply space creates neither authority to act nor evidence that a responsible party has completed its review of the material.

## How to verify the setup

Mark the object processed, receiving reviewer and explicit exclusion in the overview, tracing every statement to input. Record reference limits, reply allowance and actual model context capacity separately. Restore missing conditions before writing instead of inferring authority from long background. This exercise handles no funds, submits no application and gives no financial compliance advice or directory-eligibility decision. Later scope material requires the correct entity and current version. Failure to retrieve a decision does not establish that an actual application was rejected or that no processing has ever occurred.
