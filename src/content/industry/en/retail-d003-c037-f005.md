# Peak-sale fulfillment: multi-turn prompts for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Peak-sale fulfillment planning rotates around short-term peak capacity and the arrangement of people, vehicles and sites. The working materials are peak-sale forecast tables, staffing rosters, courier support plans and exception daily reports. Their update rhythm is per event and per day. For copy drafting, these materials serve activity pages, fulfillment service descriptions or internal scheduling notices, and the drafter converts forecast peaks, temporary staffing arrangements and courier vehicle coordination into text.

Each field carries a subject, an object and a validity scope. A forecast table gives an order-volume range for one event. A roster gives headcount for one date and one shift. A courier support plan gives a pickup arrangement for one carrier in one time window. A promotional sentence that reaches beyond these scopes lacks material support. The relevant industry facts for this page are the short peak window, the temporary nature of staffing, and the carrier-specific pickup commitment. These three shape what a draft may state.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

The prompt must state the material scope, the drafting task and the handling of missing evidence, and it must not let the model supply business facts. For history, check the context actually carried in and distinguish a new session from a continued session. For question optimization, check reference resolution and question expansion when a knowledge base runs continuous dialogue, and do not assume every short question qualifies. For model and length, check the context and reply settings the model supports, and test long documents combined with follow-up questions. For reply format, choose the format according to downstream consumption; when selecting `json_schema`, fill in the corresponding format requirement and verify the returned structure.

Hypothetical drill sample: a fulfillment team prepares fulfillment service copy for a peak-sale activity page. The material text available for checking consists of three sentences. Sentence one: "The forecast order peak for this peak-sale event is 80,000 orders, concentrated from 0:00 to 4:00 on November 1." Sentence two: "Temporary sorting staffing for that window is 120 people, rotating across three shifts." Sentence three: "Partner courier A commits 20 pickup vehicles in that window, covering the East China region." The draft sentence to be judged is: "This peak-sale event offers unlimited pickup across all time windows in East China, with nationwide next-day delivery." The subject of this draft sentence is the fulfillment team, the object is East China pickup and nationwide next-day delivery, the state is a commitment, and the validity scope is "all time windows", "unlimited" and "nationwide". The material records only 20 vehicles from 0:00 to 4:00 on November 1 and the East China region, and records nothing about all time windows, unlimited volume or nationwide next-day delivery. The phrases "all time windows, unlimited" and "nationwide next-day delivery" have no material support.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Relevant setting or check on this page | Relation to the sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI model | Select a model that supports the needed context length and multimodal ability; model capability follows what the configuration dialog displays | Long forecast tables and rosters need sufficient context length |
| Memory turns | Controls how many earlier chat turns are referenced at most; use the default first when there is no clear requirement | Check the history turns actually carried in during multi-turn follow-up |
| Reply limit | When opened, limits reply length; raise it appropriately for plans or longer explanations | Check truncation when drafting needs a more complete output |
| Temperature | Lower values give steadier answers; copy and creative suggestions may raise it appropriately | Promotional sentence drafting needs steady restatement of material facts |
| Reply format | Keep default for ordinary Q&A; when a downstream flow needs a fixed format, select `json_schema` and fill in the format requirement | If a downstream node reads structured copy fields, verify the returned structure |
| Thinking configuration | Some models support thinking intensity; complex questions may use a higher intensity, and default uses the model default | Long material comparison may use a higher thinking intensity, not required |

The values above are starting points for this hypothetical trial. The reason for choosing them is that a material-comparison task needs steady output. The comparison method is to change the setting under the same prompt and observe whether the draft sentence still exceeds the material scope.

## Three common errors

1. Writing a local time window and a local region from the material as all time windows and nationwide scope. The input is the three material sentences above and the draft sentence; the comparison is between "all time windows", "unlimited" and "nationwide" in the draft and "0:00 to 4:00", "20 vehicles" and "East China region" in the material; the observable result is that the draft contains a scope extension not recorded in the material.
2. Treating a pending or unconfirmed scheduling arrangement as an effective commitment. The input is the status fields in the roster and the courier support plan; the comparison is whether "commits 20 pickup vehicles" has been signed or has taken effect; the observable result is that the material gives only the commitment wording and records no signing or effective date.
3. Treating a similarity score as a permission or entity filter. The input is the `score` field and `quoteList` returned by retrieval; the comparison is between the score level and whether the record belongs to the target business scope; the observable result is that the score indicates relevance only, not that the record has been authorized or belongs to the target entity.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the draft sentence "This peak-sale event offers unlimited pickup across all time windows in East China, with nationwide next-day delivery", input the three material sentences above and compare the scope words in the draft with the material records. The expected observation is that "all time windows", "unlimited" and "nationwide" have no material support and require revision or additional material.
2. For the handover request "Please generate fulfillment service copy from the material and publish it directly", input the material text and the request, and compare "publish it directly" with whether the material contains publication authorization. The expected observation is that the material records no publication authorization, so the request remains pending verification and cannot be treated as approved.
3. For empty-result handling, input an explicit paper retrieval result of `[]` and compare the downstream node design when `quoteList` is empty. The expected observation is that the downstream should give a missing-evidence notice and must not supply business facts; this is a design check and does not claim an actual test.
