# Tmall flagship operations: interactive forms for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Tmall flagship store product and marketing copy drafting draws on the merchant backend, the Tmall rules center, ERP order tables, and customer service chat records. Compared with other platform stores, brand authorization, flagship store identity, and Tmall points compensation rules carry heavier weight in copy-related workflows. Daily documents include the product publishing page, brand authorization letter, delivery commitment record, and after-sales work order.

Copy drafting inputs are scattered across the product publishing page and authorization materials. A drafter needs to place the source wording and the proposed sentence in the same review setting, then judge sentence by sentence whether the wording has material support. The form input node collects filled-in information and passes it to later nodes after submission; it does not itself carry business database functions. The user selection node executes a branch according to the user's choice, which suits choosing among multiple copy directions.

The material update frequency given for this category is daily and per order. This page does not derive batch synchronization, real-time, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from that frequency.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The form input node is a user interaction node. When triggered, the conversation enters an interaction state, records the workflow state, and waits for the user to complete the interaction; after completion it continues downward. The user selection node likewise enters an interaction state, and after the user makes a choice it executes the corresponding branch. Both nodes are user interaction nodes, can be added repeatedly, and trigger execution.

Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations are implemented in external systems or later nodes as needed. Forms collect filled values; business verification and external synchronization require separate implementation.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a flagship store operator prepares one product copy sentence. The source wording supplied for review is "This store is the brand's Tmall flagship store, with authorization valid until December 31, 2026" and "The product page states a delivery commitment of within 48 hours after payment." The proposed sentence to be judged is "This store has obtained long-term authorization from the brand, and all products can be shipped within 24 hours." The material gives an authorization end date and a 48-hour delivery commitment; the proposed sentence writes the authorization as long-term and the delivery as 24 hours. Both expressions are inconsistent with the material. This sample is used only for the review demonstration on this page and points to no real store.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the form input node as a business database. The node only collects filled information and passes it to later nodes; business verification and external synchronization require separate implementation.
2. Treating the user selection node as a personnel directory or business data selector. This node executes a branch according to the user's choice and does not perform entity isolation or permission judgment.
3. Treating "long-term authorization" and "shipped within 24 hours" in the proposed sentence as facts already supported by the material. The material gives an authorization end date and a 48-hour commitment; both expressions are inconsistent with the material and remain unsupported.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the two source sentences and one proposed sentence from the hypothetical sample above, and compare the proposed sentence with the material on two fields: authorization period and delivery time limit. The proposed sentence writes "long-term authorization," while the material writes authorization valid until December 31, 2026; the proposed sentence writes "within 24 hours," while the material writes within 48 hours. When the two values are inconsistent, judge that the proposed sentence is not supported by the material.
2. Fill the required fields in the form input node, submit, and check whether later nodes receive the filled values. When a field is missing, check whether the node remains in the interaction state; after completion, check whether it continues downward. The observed phenomena are the interaction state record and subsequent execution, with no preset submission count or automatic judgment.
3. Select one copy direction in the user selection node and check whether the corresponding branch executes. After selection the conversation continues downward; without a selection it remains in the interaction state. This check only verifies branch execution and does not involve permissions or entity isolation.
