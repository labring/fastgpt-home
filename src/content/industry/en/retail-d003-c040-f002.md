# Customer-service knowledge: copy drafting with context limits

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Customer-service knowledge, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Support knowledge copy needs the version actually delivered to writing, not merely a newer uploaded page. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed old support answer says a component includes a fixed base. A revision memo suspends reuse while new material awaits checking; only the new page title is supplied, without its body. Current update copy cannot infer base configuration from that title or keep using the old answer because its wording is complete. Actual requests need the suspension condition. A file entering a folder does not establish delivery of its body to the model. The present evidence supports checking and withholding old wording, without reporting a completed parameter update.

## Three common errors

First, a new title may become a confirmed configuration; inspect actual reference body and locate whether a configuration source exists. Second, history may reintroduce the old answer; hold the revision memo fixed and compare empty history to identify the base statement’s version. Third, a shorter reply may omit no reuse; compare actual request and copy to distinguish delivered-but-unexpressed conditions from missing input. These concern body evidence, historical version and restriction coverage. Extra reply space cannot create product content not supplied in the proposed new page.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which parameters can currently be confirmed, whether the old answer is reusable and where the new body appears, tracing adopted statements. Record reference, history and reply allowances separately and check actual model context capacity. Later body text needs the correct component and version before revision, with inspection of actual input rather than an assumption that upload means reading. This exercise updates no live support system, tests no product and promises no knowledge accuracy. Missing new body remains pending; retrieval failure does not establish that formal material does not exist.
