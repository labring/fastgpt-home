# Product listing reviews: multi-turn prompts for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Platform product title and detail compliance handles advertising truthfulness and qualification display rather than parcel delivery. The working materials are product title tables, detail page assets, advertising prohibited-word lists, qualification and testing reports, and brand asset libraries drawn from platform rule centers and advertising compliance checklists. Updates arrive weekly and with each new product launch. For copy drafting, the model must decide whether a proposed claim is supported by parameters, reports, or authorized assets, and must preserve any evidence gap rather than filling it.

This differs from platform shipping operations, which handle delivery rather than promotional truthfulness. The consequence of error here is product delisting, complaints, or administrative penalty risk. The three most time-consuming steps in the broader category are checking absolute claims, comparing physical parameters, and replacing non-compliant assets.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Drafting depends on multi-turn dialogue retaining the scope of the source material. The prompt must state the document scope, the answering task, and how to handle missing evidence. History determines the context carried forward, and a new session differs from a continued session in what is supplied. Short questions need coreference resolution and question expansion; this applies to scenarios requiring continuous knowledge base dialogue. Model context length and reply limits affect the combination of long documents plus follow-up questions. Reply format follows the downstream consumer; when json_schema is selected, the corresponding format requirement must be filled in and the returned structure checked.

Hypothetical paper test case: the source text reads "The inner liner of this insulated bottle is marked 316 stainless steel, accompanied by testing report number QC-2024-0317; the report states the inner liner material is 316 stainless steel, the test date is 17 March 2024, and the valid scope is limited to that batch of 500 units." The draft sentence to be assessed is "The inner liners of the entire series of this insulated bottle are all 316 stainless steel and have passed authoritative quality testing." The comparison is between "entire series" in the draft and "that batch of 500 units" in the source, and between "authoritative quality testing" and the institution qualification description not stated in the source.

## Three common errors

1. Treating "entire series" in the draft sentence as already supported by the source. The source limits scope to that batch of 500 units, so the subject scope does not match and the claim is unsupported.
2. Treating "authoritative quality testing" as stated in the source. The source does not describe the institution's qualification, so this description remains to be verified.
3. Treating retrieval similarity as a permission or entity filter. Similarity does not exclude unrelated or unauthorized records, and retrieval does not constitute authorization.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the source text above and the draft sentence, compare "entire series" against "that batch of 500 units" for subject scope, and observe whether the output marks the claim as unsupported.
2. Input the same source and "authoritative quality testing", compare whether the source states the institution's qualification, and observe whether the output marks it as pending verification.
3. Input an empty-result downstream scenario using the paper input [] and check the downstream design, observing whether the downstream handles it as missing evidence and preserves the gap. All three record actual observations without claiming that testing has occurred.
