# Multi-store management: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Multi-store management, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Keep single items/bundles, pickup/shipping and permitted image pages distinct across store copy. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

This page invents material for two stores. One card offers a single item for pickup; another proposes a bundle with shipping conditions unconfirmed. A brand image is approved for one specified page in the first store. Collect store, composition and fulfillment wording, then choose bundle or collection-language review. Shared product names merge neither bundles nor delivery methods. No real store name, price or launch outcome is supplied. Visible images grant no cross-store reuse.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multi-store copy handoff | Invented difference | Inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composition | Single item versus proposed bundle | No accessories from shared names |
| Collection | Pickup versus unconfirmed shipping | No promise transfer |
| Image | Specified store/page | No universal reuse |
| Writing route | Composition or fulfillment | Inspect corresponding store input |
| Publication | Separate authority | Not completed by interaction |

## Three common errors

- If a single-item card becomes a complete bundle elsewhere, inspect component lists, store identity and received input. Pictures cannot supply missing accessory evidence.

- If pickup wording becomes home delivery across stores, inspect collection method and the corresponding store terms. Fluent copy creates no shipping conditions.

- If page-specific image permission becomes universal use, compare the approved page with the intended draft and retain purpose and publication scope.

## How to verify the setup

Follow composition and fulfillment routes into the appropriate store’s writing step, retaining submission, receipt and adopted sentences. Bundle evidence updates that item and store only without confirming shipping. New image permission still needs page matching; a shared brand covers no account automatically. Publication, image use and store operations require authorized procedures and receipts. Orchestration supplies neither a cross-store product directory nor an access system. This exercise hands over invented copy evidence without operating stores.
