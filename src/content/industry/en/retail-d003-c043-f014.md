# Multi-store management: model connections for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Multi-store matrix management draws on platform merchant backends, ERP channel pricing, brand anti-counterfeiting systems, and distributor reports. The working documents are store authorization lists, price control tables, cross-territory complaint forms, and monthly sales reports. Copy drafting for products and marketing extracts verifiable statements from these materials and produces text for review. Cross-store authorization and price order are the central concerns, so price descriptions, channel scope, and authorization status in drafted copy must match the source materials. The brief gives daily and monthly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization or real-time triggers from them.

A store authorization list records which store holds which authorization type and through which date. A price control table records a suggested retail price and a floor for promotional pricing. A cross-territory complaint form records a complaint identifier, the store involved, and a status such as pending verification. These three document types are the inputs this page works with. A drafted sentence is checked against them, and any statement that goes beyond the recorded scope is a drafting error rather than a model configuration issue.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Model access depends on AI Proxy. The environment variable `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` holds the service root address and does not append `/v1`. `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` holds a non-empty administrator token and cannot be replaced by an application API key. The system needs at least one language model and one index model to operate. A model ID is globally unique, and model mapping translates the model FastGPT requests into the upstream model actually called. Model status probing applies only to currently enabled system models; a probe retries at most three times after a first failure, so a single round makes at most four attempts, and only four failed attempts mark the model abnormal. The three health states are normal, high latency, and abnormal. A relevance score does not act as a permission or entity filter, and knowledge bases on different vector models cannot be associated together.

Hypothetical paper exercise. The source materials read: "This store is an authorized distributor of the brand; authorization is valid through December 31, 2026." "The suggested retail price of this product is 199 yuan; the promotional price is not lower than 179 yuan." "Cross-territory complaint number CK-2026-0317 involves store A; status pending verification." The draft sentence under review reads: "This store is an officially authorized brand store, the lowest price across all platforms at 179 yuan, guaranteed authentic." The draft sentence widens the authorization from "distributor" to "officially authorized store," converts a promotional price floor into a lowest-price claim, and asserts authenticity with no corresponding statement in the materials.

## Three common errors

1. Writing "authorized distributor" from the materials as "officially authorized store." Input is the source sentence and the draft sentence; compare the authorization subject wording; observe whether the draft sentence widens the authorization scope.
2. Writing "promotional price not lower than 179 yuan" as "lowest price across all platforms at 179 yuan." Input is the price control table text and the draft sentence; compare the price wording; observe whether a price floor has been rewritten as an absolute lowest price.
3. Treating "status pending verification" on a cross-territory complaint as a confirmed violation. Input is the complaint form text and the draft sentence; compare the status field; observe whether a pending status has been written as an established fact.

## How to verify the setup

1. Check the authorization wording. Input the source sentence "This store is an authorized distributor of the brand; authorization is valid through December 31, 2026" and the draft sentence "This store is an officially authorized brand store." Compare the authorization subject and validity period. The condition is met when the draft sentence matches the source; when it does not, record the difference and do not supply authorization terms that are absent.
2. Check the price wording. Input the price control table text "suggested retail price 199 yuan; promotional price not lower than 179 yuan" and the draft sentence "lowest price across all platforms at 179 yuan." Compare the price description. The condition is met when the draft sentence stays within the source range; when it exceeds the range, keep the source wording and do not infer prices at other stores.
3. Check the complaint status. Input the cross-territory complaint "status pending verification" and the draft sentence "guaranteed authentic." Compare the status field. The condition is met when the draft sentence does not turn a pending status into a confirmed one; when the status is missing, record it as unsupported and do not classify it as a denial or a conflict.

Model probe results use green for normal with latency no greater than 30 seconds, yellow for high latency above 30 seconds, and red for abnormal after four consecutive failures. These states check model availability and do not judge whether copy content is correct. When a retrieval result is empty, a paper input of `[]` can be used to check the downstream design; this page does not claim that a fabricated rule search always returns empty.
