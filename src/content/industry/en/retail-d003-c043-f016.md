# Multi-store management: interactive forms for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Copy drafting for a platform multi-store matrix draws on merchant back-office records from each platform, ERP channel prices, brand anti-counterfeit systems, and dealer reports. The distinguishing concern in this category is cross-store authorization and price order, which sits alongside single-store traffic efficiency. The documents that matter at drafting time are the store authorization list, the price control table, the diversion complaint form, and the monthly sales report. The brief gives daily and monthly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, or real-time mechanisms from those frequencies.

For drafting, the relevant work is aligning the subject, object, status, and validity scope recorded in those documents with the sentence being drafted. A promotional sentence is supported only when the material it rests on states the same subject, the same object, the same period, and the same field. The store authorization list supplies who may sell what, through which store, and for how long. The price control table supplies price floors and the period in which they apply. The diversion complaint form and the monthly sales report carry status information that may bear on a draft, but they do not by themselves establish authorization or price compliance.

A hypothetical paper exercise sample, labelled as such: the authorization list states, "Subject A authorizes store S to sell product M from January 2025 to December 2025, with an authorization scope of online retail." The price control table states, "The retail price floor for product M at store S is 199 yuan, applicable from January 2025 to December 2025." The draft sentence under review reads, "Store S sold product M at 199 yuan throughout 2025 as authorized retail." The items to be judged are whether the subject, object, period, and price floor in the draft sentence each match the two source documents. The sample does not state an actual transaction price; that field remains unknown.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The form input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user completes the interaction. The node collects the information the user fills in and passes it to subsequent nodes. It does not carry business database functions of its own. The user selection node is also a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state and the workflow state is recorded; when the user makes a selection, the node judges that selection and executes the corresponding branch. It does not constitute a personnel directory or a business data selector.

Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations are implemented in external systems or subsequent nodes as needed. A form submission shows that filled information was collected and passed downstream. It does not show that an approval occurred, that a database write occurred, or that an external system was synchronized. An approval does not happen automatically because a form was submitted. A pending request is not an approval. An offered option is not an execution. An unsigned termination is not a completed termination.

For the paper exercise, the draft sentence and the two source documents are the inputs to a human comparison step. The comparison covers four fields: subject, object, period, and price floor. Where the source documents do not state a field, that field stays unknown and is recorded as an evidence gap. Missing evidence remains missing; it is not converted into a negative finding or a conflict. Retrieval association and relevance scores do not perform permission or entity isolation, and retrieval is not authorization.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a form submission as a completed business approval. Submission shows that filled information was collected and passed to subsequent nodes. Approval is an action that requires separate system implementation, and the two are not interchangeable.
2. Treating the user selection node as a business data selector. The node judges the user selection and executes a branch. It does not constitute a personnel directory or a business data selector.
3. Treating retrieval association as permission isolation. Semantic recall and relevance scores do not carry permission or entity isolation, and retrieval is not authorization.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the two source sentences and the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample above. Compare the subject, object, period, and price floor fields. The draft is supported when all four fields correspond to the same subject, the same period, and the same field with no mutually exclusive values.
2. Input the draft phrase "sold at 199 yuan" against the source phrase "retail price floor is 199 yuan." Compare the actual transaction price field. The source does not state an actual transaction price, so that field remains unknown and is not judged as a negative finding or a conflict.
3. Input a draft handover request that does not state an authorization period. Compare the period field in the store authorization list. When the period is missing, record the evidence gap. Do not supply an authorization or effective clause to obtain a definite answer.

The above is a paper comparison design. It does not claim that a test was run, that a deployment occurred, or that an approval was granted.
