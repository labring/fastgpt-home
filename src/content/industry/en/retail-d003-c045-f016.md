# Seller ratings management: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Seller ratings management, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Review and rectification material can support service wording when statements, plans and actual verification remain distinct. The example uses no scores or outcome figures. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Rating-related copy preparation can collect a topic, diagnostic passage and rectification scope. Proposed inputs contain claims and planned correction without current verification or complete experience evidence. Preserve gaps downstream rather than deriving improved service from plans. Choices arrange evidence or source organization without querying scores automatically or generalizing one review across products. Store and period must travel with the wording.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Copy-input check | Proposed material | Receiving and wording evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store-period scope | Passage limited to one store-period | Not expanded to every subject |
| Rectification state | Plan without verification | Not rewritten as improved |
| Statement status | Customer claim separate from inspection | No direct product-fact finding |
| Evidence destination | Obtain verification or organize text | Downstream actually receives the source |
| Formal release | Separate reply approval and update | Draft is not a business outcome |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Lost scope expanding one store-period needs comparison of entered and received text. Planned correction described as improvement needs actual follow-up. Customer statements treated as product findings need corresponding inspection. These concern coverage, execution and evidence status rather than filled values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain question, source identity, route and received content. Repeat with this verification and inspect actual use. The node records state, waits and continues without a business database or content approval. Validation, public reply, publication and staff authority require downstream permission and receipts. Drafting completion establishes neither improved experience, responsibility nor an updated page.
