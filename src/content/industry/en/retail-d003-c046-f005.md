# Marketplace category access: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Marketplace category access, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Retain licensed entity, category and dates when adding qualification evidence across turns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Suppose a business registration identifies an entity, a trademark authorization covers selected categories, and a permit application note records receipt for review. An initial brand introduction followed by stronger qualification wording should inspect the permit evidence actually supplied. Receipt is distinct from issuance. Category authority does not cover every product, and registration proves no platform category admission. This invented exercise contains no actual certificate number, validity date or review decision. Missing fields stay unresolved instead of being completed through instructions.

## Three common errors

- If a follow-up sees only an earlier generated complete-materials summary without the receipt note, inspect history selection and summary provenance. Generated text proves no issued permit.

- If selected-category authority becomes shop-wide coverage, compare the licensed products with the proposed wording and retain uncovered categories.

- If a permit expiry date becomes a platform receipt date, inspect field meaning and original dates. Adjacent dates can describe different events.

## How to verify the setup

Add entity records, category authority and permit state in turns while holding the question about permissible qualification wording fixed. Retain actual input and adopted sentences, then repeat in a fresh conversation with final documents only. Instructions should state when numbers or dates are absent; inspect history selection in the actual configuration. Platform admission wording needs corresponding checked platform evidence. A copy draft submits no application and certifies no compliance.
