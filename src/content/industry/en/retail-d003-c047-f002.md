# Marketplace returns: context limits for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

In marketplace refund and return handling, work follows individual orders and continues across the day. A case does not have to be tied to a promise made in one livestream session. The working materials are refund applications, return shipping labels, quality inspection photos and refund approvals. Copy drafting for product and marketing text depends on these materials to judge whether a promotional sentence has support.

Material status directly limits what can be written. A refund application under review shows only that review is pending. A return shipping label shows the parcel was handed to the carrier. A quality inspection photo reflects only the moment it was taken. When refund approval is not complete, the amount and the responsibility are not settled. Updates arrive per order and per day, so materials for one order can arrive in stages. Drafting may work from a partial version of the record.

## Constraints at the context limits step

The length of content a model can reference in a single request follows the actual model capability. The reply limit constrains output length only and does not stand in for the full context window. The knowledge base reference limit is controlled by tokens, so a long single material may be trimmed; the question sample should be used to check whether the referenced content is complete. Memory rounds decide whether earlier conversation is carried in, so the same question under empty history and under history produces different input, and this condition must be fixed during checking. When a fixed reply format such as json_schema is used, field completeness and successful parsing need separate verification; acceptable text length does not mean the structure is usable. Semantic recall and relevance scores affect ordering and filtering only. They do not perform permission or entity isolation. A retrieval library and a vector model identifier that do not match cannot be associated together.

Hypothetical drill sample: the material text supplied for checking reads, "Refund application RF-2026-0412 states: order A-7731, product is a portable juicer cup, reason for application is water seepage at the base of the cup body, application date 12 April 2026, status pending review. The return shipping label shows the buyer shipped the item on 14 April 2026, logistics status in transit. The quality inspection photo was taken on 16 April 2026 and shows water stains at the base of the cup body, with no inspection conclusion attached." The draft sentence to be judged is, "This product has been confirmed to have a water seepage quality defect, and a full refund is supported." This sentence asserts a quality confirmation and a refund conclusion. In the materials, the refund application is pending review, the inspection photo carries no conclusion, and the shipment is still in transit.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a refund application under review as a confirmed quality conclusion. The input is RF-2026-0412 with status pending review, and an inspection photo with no conclusion attached. The comparison object is the phrase "has been confirmed to have a water seepage quality defect" in the draft sentence. The observable result is that the material carries no confirmation conclusion, so that promotional sentence lacks support.
2. Treating a return shipping label in transit as a completed return. The input is shipment on 14 April 2026 with status in transit. The comparison object is the responsibility and amount conditions needed for "a full refund is supported." The observable result is that refund approval is not complete and the conclusion remains to be verified.
3. Using a semantic recall or relevance score in place of permission and entity checking. The input is a retrieval scope under the same vector model identifier. The comparison object is the material subject, order A-7731, and the object the draft sentence points to. The observable result is that a relevance score does not constitute authorization or entity filtering, and subject consistency needs separate checking.

## How to verify the setup

1. Submit the sample material text together with the draft sentence "This product has been confirmed to have a water seepage quality defect, and a full refund is supported." Compare whether the quality confirmation and refund conclusion in the draft sentence have support in the material fields. The observable result: when the refund application is pending review and the inspection photo has no conclusion attached, the sentence should be marked as lacking support.
2. Submit the same question once under empty history and once with a small amount of history. Compare the two inputs and the referenced results. The observable result: check whether the referenced content is trimmed as history changes; if it is trimmed, adjust the reference limit or shorten the material.
3. Use the explicit paper input [] to check empty-result handling. Compare the output fields and prompts the downstream node produces when the reference is empty. The observable result: whether the downstream handles the empty reference as designed. Do not assume that a retrieval of a fictional rule will necessarily return empty.
