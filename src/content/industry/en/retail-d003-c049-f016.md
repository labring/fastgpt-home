# Wholesale stall trading: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Wholesale stall trading, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Stall, handwritten-order and receipt sources support their own facts. Copy preparation retains party roles and readable-content gaps before making statements about goods or supply. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Wholesale-stall copy can collect business wording, item text and intended audience without amounts or actual vouchers. Proposed sources contain tenancy, handwriting and receipt passages, not current stock or complete product guidance. The form passes origins without automatically reading handwriting or identifying manufacturers. Choices arrange readable text or product evidence rather than manufacturing identity from an address or stock from a receipt.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Copy-input check | Proposed material | Receiving and wording evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business role | Tenant separate from production origin | Not directly described as manufacturer |
| Readable content | Original handwriting and reliable text | Automatic recognition not presumed |
| Supply state | Receipt separate from current stock | Payment is not stock availability |
| Evidence destination | Readable passage or product guidance | Actually reaches drafting |
| Formal business | Separate confirmation, finance and release | Draft is not performance |

## Three common errors

A tenant described as manufacturer needs business wording and actual product origin. Unclear handwriting producing a definite item needs reliable readable evidence. A payment receipt described as present supply needs corresponding stock or confirmation. These concern identity, content and state rather than filled values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain audience, source identity, selected route and draft evidence. Repeat after corresponding product or readable material arrives and inspect use. The node records state, waits and continues without a business database. Validation, procurement confirmation, finance and publication need downstream authority and receipts. Drafting establishes neither prepared goods, performed transactions nor a live introduction.
