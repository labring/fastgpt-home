# Complaints and official replies: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Complaints and official replies, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Complaint and draft-reply material can inform service wording when source status remains visible. Drafting does not establish verified allegations or acceptance by an external institution. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Complaint-related service wording can collect the topic, customer passage and rectification scope without legal reply requirements. Proposed material lacks current external handling. Pass gaps to drafting without deriving facts or responsibility from a complaint. Choices arrange order evidence or source organization rather than deciding disputes or deriving external acceptance from a finished draft. Preserve the order and event covered by each passage.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Copy-input check | Proposed material | Receiving and wording evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statement status | Claim separate from actual verification | Source qualification retained |
| Reply stage | Draft without external acceptance | Obtain current actual response |
| Case scope | One order separate from all services | Not expanded into universal promises |
| Evidence route | Order source or organized text | Actually reaches corresponding drafting |
| Formal submission | Separate closure, reply and release | Copy is not external acceptance |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Lost claim status needs complete input and actual evidence checks. A draft reply described as accepted needs this external response. One order expanded into universal service promises needs subject and applicability checks. These concern status, stage and coverage rather than one human choice.

## How to verify the setup

Retain topic, passages, destinations and draft origins. Repeat with corresponding evidence and inspect use. The node records interaction, waits and continues without approval or legal judgment. Validation, external submission, closure and publication require downstream authority and receipts. Drafting establishes neither determined responsibility, resolved matters nor approved promotion.
