# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Pinduoduo seller campaigns, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Platform campaign copy separates registration, review feedback and product applicability. Submitted material does not establish a live campaign. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed registration says review is pending. An internal message only acknowledges receiving material, but the draft claims the entire store participates. Collect product scope and message passages. A person may obtain actual feedback or inspect registration subjects. Supply no prices, discounts or performance claims. Receipt establishes intake, not approval or a live campaign; cost material also does not establish permission for product promises.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Campaign-copy drafting | Proposed state | Check requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration passage | Review pending | Not approved |
| Internal message | Receipt only | Actual feedback needed |
| Participation scope | One product, not whole store | Original form subject |
| Public status | No launch record | Not a live campaign |
| Drafting handoff | Gaps remain visible | New reply changes covered conclusions |

## Three common errors

Receipt promoted to approval needs this review’s actual feedback. One product expanded to the store needs registration coverage beside received wording. Registration promoted to launch requires actual public status or publication records. These inspect message status, scope and stage independently of a human choice.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect the registration received by feedback and subject paths. Added review feedback changes only its explicit conclusion while missing public visibility stays unresolved. A request for more material preserves pending status. Formal registration, stock settings and copy publication need separate authority and receipts. A finished draft establishes neither successful participation nor sales or exposure gains.
