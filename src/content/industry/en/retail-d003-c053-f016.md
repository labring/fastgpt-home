# Channel receivables: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Channel receivables, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Collection, credit and payment material need their parties, conditions and communication stages preserved in explanatory copy. Public examples contain no amounts, deadlines or actual debtor records. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Receivables-explanation copy can collect contractual scope, notice passages and intended audience without amounts, deadlines or actual debtor records. Proposed input lacks bank confirmation or counterparty agreement. Preserve gaps instead of deriving receipt from a notice. Choices arrange sources without credit or legal judgments. An aging-sheet title supplies no calculation definition; pass party and period to drafting rather than inventing them from an introduction.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Copy-input check | Proposed material | Receiving and wording evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agreement scope | Credit party and passage | Recipient retains conditions |
| Communication outcome | Notice sent without agreement | Obtain current actual reply |
| Contract mapping | Other-transaction bank source | Inspect relationship and remove |
| Public audience | General wording without debtor data | Actually reaches drafting |
| Formal operation | Separate confirmation, sending and release | Draft is not legal determination |

## Three common errors

Lost credit applicability needs comparison of submitted and received text. A sent notice described as agreement needs this actual reply. Another contract’s bank material needs transaction checks before removal. These concern scope, communication outcome and identity rather than one human choice.

## How to verify the setup

Retain audience, contract, source status, route and draft citations. Repeat with corresponding passages and inspect use. The node waits and continues without processing funds or freezing dispatch. Validation, receipt confirmation, external sending and publication require authority and receipts. Drafting establishes neither received funds, responsibility nor justified handling.
