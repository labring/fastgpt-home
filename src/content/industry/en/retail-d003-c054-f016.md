# Channel diversion reviews: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Channel diversion reviews, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Channel complaints, photographs and handling notices can inform wording within their own scope. Observations do not independently establish violations, responsibility or completed penalties. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Channel-review copy can collect complaint wording, code-source descriptions and readable image evidence without actual codes or commercial figures. Proposed input lacks complete findings. Preserve gaps instead of deriving violations from complaints. Choices arrange passages or subject checks without automatic image reading or responsibility decisions. Pass channel, item and observation scope to drafting rather than completing an investigation through similar names.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Copy-input check | Proposed material | Receiving and wording evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complaint status | Statement separate from findings | Source qualification retained |
| Observation scope | One photograph separate from all channels | Not expanded globally |
| Handling stage | Notice separate from operation record | Not rewritten as penalized |
| Readable material | Evidence for text formed from images | Recognition not presumed |
| Formal confirmation | Separate sending, writing and release | Draft is not an investigation finding |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Lost complaint status needs full-source and actual-investigation checks. One photograph expanded across channels needs subject and coverage evidence. A proposed notice described as executed penalty needs this business record. These concern status, scope and stage rather than filled values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain topic, source identity, destination and draft evidence. Repeat with corresponding case material and trace sentences to passages. Waiting and continuation create neither penalties nor publication. Validation, investigation confirmation, external sending and writes require authority and receipts. Drafting establishes neither violations, legal responsibility nor completed handling, and hypothetical material is not an actual customer case.
