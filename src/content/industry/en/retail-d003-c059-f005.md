# Store cold-chain receiving: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Trace cold-chain product copy across turns without converting transport records or earlier-batch conclusions into benefits. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A writing exercise supplies a delivery description containing only a food name, packaging and suggested preparation, then asks to emphasize quality. Separate provided facts from absent quality evidence. Neither name nor preparation establishes nutrition, certification or quality outcomes. Compare originals with added draft statements to identify unverifiable commitments. Without independent quality material, successive turns may change expression but cannot promote a desired emphasis into fact.

## Three common errors

- If name, packaging and preparation become nutrition, certification or quality commitments, compare source and added sentence, naming missing evidence. Repeated emphasis creates no fact.

- If one transport observation becomes a whole-journey guarantee, retain object, period and citation to inspect expanded scope. One record establishes neither every item nor every stage.

- If the draft borrows another batch’s acceptance or turns pending review into confirmation, compare batch identities, history and optimized question. Obtain current-batch evidence and inspect its adoption.

## How to verify the setup

Compare input without added evidence, with a same-batch observation and with a changed batch, checking adopted sources and remaining gaps. Acceptance comments marked pending recheck cannot become confirmed superior quality. After obtaining a formal product description, repeat the task to inspect added conditions and prevent older history overriding them. The responsible owner still confirms promotional support. Writing performs no on-site inspection, publishes no page and establishes no verified business outcome.
