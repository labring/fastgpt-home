# Store cold-chain receiving: copy drafting with model connections

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

This page invents two documents for a cold-chain copy exercise. A delivery note supplies a product name, packaging form and a steaming suggestion. An acceptance attachment supplies a grilling suggestion. Neither document supplies nutrition testing, a quality grade or certification evidence. The name identifies the item, packaging describes the supplied form, and each preparation suggestion retains its source subject and conditions. These are usable, bounded writing materials without a dedicated benefits field. Inspect delivery of both documents and their contents, then check whether a suggestion becomes a product guarantee. Smoother copy changes none of the source evidence. This is a paper exercise, not an actual food inspection or nutrition assessment.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Steaming suggestion | Steaming suggestion in the same delivery note | No conversion into an exclusive preparation claim |

| Nutrition and certification | Neither the delivery note nor acceptance attachment proves them | Trace additional commitments to separate evidence |

## Three common errors

- If the acceptance attachment is absent but joint preparation review is claimed, retain the delivery note, acceptance-attachment inventory and actual request. Locate omission of the document containing the grilling suggestion before claiming a two-source result.

- If the delivery note’s steaming suggestion and the acceptance attachment’s grilling suggestion both arrive but the output excludes one, compare both sources and the response for false exclusion. Model selection does not adjudicate suitable preparation.

- If name and packaging become nutrition or certification conclusions, trace added statements and evidence. The supplied name and packaging remain usable material without a prepared benefits field. Any additional nutrition or certification statement still needs its own evidence.

## How to verify the setup

The absence of a prepared selling-points field does not establish absence of all writing material. Identify facts supplied by the delivery note and acceptance attachment separately before reviewing additions in the draft. Restating a name and package is different from asserting nutrition, quality or audience benefits, which need their own sources. After adding documentation, hold the question and settings constant, checking source delivery and the statements adopted. More fluent generation does not establish sufficient factual support.
