# Fruit-store procurement: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Fruit-store procurement, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Fruit-gift descriptions preserve optional accessories, tasted subjects and draft status. Workflow handling cannot extend one sample’s material into commitments for every delivery. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed product wording supplies fruit only, offers a separate gift bag, describes one delivered tasting sample and leaves display copy under review. Collect passages and batch coverage. A person can obtain combination confirmation or inspect tasting origins. An option is not included delivery, and sample impressions do not establish every batch’s performance. Inspect retained qualifications at drafting instead of merging all material under the same fruit name into approved copy.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Fruit-description drafting | Proposed source status | Downstream check |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Three common errors

Optional packaging promoted to inclusion needs combination wording beside transmitted input. One sample extended to all supply requires observation subjects and batch relationships. Pending copy promoted to release readiness needs actual review evidence. These inspect composition, observation coverage and publication stage independently of ended workflow execution.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual recipients on combination and origin paths. Added current-batch correspondence changes supported descriptions only while packaging and review remain pending. A product title alone does not alter composition. Purchasing, packing and publication need separate authority and receipts. Continued nodes establish neither packed orders nor customer receipt and cannot create quality guarantees from sample wording.
