# Group-buy presale orders: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Group-buy presale orders, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. A presale-copy form retains interest, missing group conditions and an awaited arrival reply. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed material collects interest only, leaves group conditions for review and awaits the supplier’s arrival arrangements. Collect product identity and supplied wording before a person requests conditions or drafts an interest notice. Product and contact entries establish no order. Missing conditions cannot be borrowed from an older campaign. Retain all three states downstream; complete promotional wording supplies no source, and another product’s reply does not apply here.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Presale-copy form | Task stage | Inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration purpose | Interest only | Not an order |
| Group conditions | Unresolved | Borrow no old campaign |
| Arrival | Supplier reply pending | Not a fixed arrangement |
| Added material | Conditions and reply | Check identity separately |
| Transaction | Separate order/notice | Authority and receipt |

## Three common errors

Interest registration promoted to an order needs actual confirmation. Older group conditions applied here need event identity and originals checked. Awaited arrival wording promoted to a fixed arrangement needs this product’s reply. These concern transaction stage, scope and supply evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Compare form text, downstream input and draft sentence by sentence. Check identity separately for new conditions or replies, retaining remaining gaps. Interaction forms create no group purchase, order or supplier notice. Actual transactions and sending require separate authority and business receipts. Give arrival wording and group-opening conditions separate source references in the proposed copy. An unanswered supplier question remains pending, allowing a reviewer to identify which statement still lacks support before collecting orders.
