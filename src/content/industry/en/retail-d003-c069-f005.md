# Dealer rebate calculation: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Dealer rebate calculation, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Preserve policy conditions, reviewed sales and invoicing stages in rebate-copy turns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Suppose a rebate policy covers one campaign, achieved sales await return checks, reconciliation lacks distributor confirmation, and no credit-note evidence is supplied. A campaign introduction followed by a results question should use the actual policy and period. Earlier copy establishes no achieved target. No actual rate or rebate amount is provided; missing invoice evidence supports no completed settlement.

## Three common errors

- If a follow-up ignores pending returns and declares all targets met, compare the actual sales history and source condition for summary omissions.

- If one campaign’s policy applies to every distributor, inspect campaign subjects, period and distributor identity. General wording expands no policy.

- If unconfirmed reconciliation becomes settled and invoiced rebates, inspect mutual confirmation and invoice evidence without skipping stages.

## How to verify the setup

Add policy, sales and reconciliation positions in turns, repeating questions about permissible promotion and unconfirmed results. Retain adopted sentences and repeat final material in a fresh conversation. Instructions should retain conditions rather than invent earnings for attractive copy. Actual accounting, invoices and distribution remain responsible business work; copy performs no settlement. When a credit note arrives, inspect its matching transaction and adjustment subject. Its existence alone establishes no completed payment.
