# Cross-border settlements: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Separate the support provided by sales records from popularity and product-positioning claims. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

The proposed settlement statement supplies sales quantity and sales amount for Product G, while the writing request asks for popularity claims. Separate those scopes first: the record can support a description of the recorded sales, while popularity needs an explicit comparison basis. Define the current settlement material as the prompt’s source and inspect the actual input for this turn. Check that performance attributed to another product in history does not enter this draft. An absent evaluation criterion should remain unresolved through further revisions.

## Three common errors

- If product G’s settlement sales become definite popularity, inspect comparison criteria and periods beside the added conclusion. Recorded sales supply no evaluation basis automatically.

- If one earlier value overwrites another record for H, inspect settlement, reconciliation and optimized question. Preserve period, currency and definitions before judging a difference erroneous.

- If I lacks positioning but borrows G or H’s users and use, compare subjects, history and draft. Locate cross-product borrowing; settlement information supplies no product features or audience evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Sales information for Product I supplies no evidence for its product characteristics or target audience. During further copy revisions, inspect whether the answer invents an audience, use or distinctive benefit from the product name or sales record. Mark each unsupported statement. After obtaining the product description and positioning sources, record the material actually entering the new turn and review the draft again. Where the copy has a structured downstream format, acceptance must distinguish parseable fields from evidence supporting their contents.
