# Cross-border settlements: copy drafting with model connections

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Inspect use of financial entries in explanatory copy without converting fee or sales records into product benefits. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

A new proposed service-fee invoice describes a charging purpose, while a settlement attachment lists product entries. They can support explanatory copy about reconciliation material, without supplying product functions, audience or market-performance evidence. Inspect the passages delivered in the actual request before reviewing additions in the draft. A definite product advantage generated from financial records alone cannot be verified from this input. Obtain the product source rather than treating fluent generation as evidence that the writing material is sufficient.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Connection or implementation check | Financial-copy source scope | Inspection object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source delivery | Supply charge purpose and product entry separately | Passages actually included in the request |
| Writing task | Explain reconciliation material | No inferred function or market result |
| Different measures | Separate fee classification from sales wording | No merged certification or benefit conclusion |
| Audience gap | A payer does not define all positioning | Obtain product and audience sources |
| Retest under the same settings | Associate added sources with the original question | Retain actual input and adopted sentences separately |

## Three common errors

- If settlement-only input explains a service-fee invoice, inspect request and adopted statements. Establish whether the invoice was obtained and delivered rather than borrowing related financial words.

- If both sources arrive but a fee category becomes certification or product results, compare invoice purpose, sales entry and draft. Locate scope expansion; parameter changes create no evidence.

- If the payer becomes every intended user or a sale becomes a product advantage, inspect positioning and product descriptions. Financial parties and sales records do not substantiate those conclusions.

## How to verify the setup

Audience-specific product copy needs positioning, a product description or applicable conditions. The payer named in a financial source does not identify every intended user, and a recorded sale does not prove a product advantage. After adding the relevant material, repeat the original question under the same settings, retaining actual input and sentences used in the draft. Test capabilities needed by the real input. A text result does not validate an image route never exercised, and acceptance of the copy still depends on source scope.
