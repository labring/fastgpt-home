# Cross-border payment reviews: model connections for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Cross-border standalone-site payment risk control operates around card-network disputes and receiving accounts. The working materials are payment chargeback notices, fraudulent-order lists, invoices, and proof of carrier delivery. Copy drafting for products and marketing draws checkable wording from these materials: the delivery status of a specific order, the issuing entity and amount range on a specific invoice. Industry material describes this data as updated daily and per order. This page does not derive real-time triggers, batch synchronization, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from that description.

A draft sentence about a specific order must be traceable to a specific field in a specific document. Where a field is blank, the draft cannot convert that blank into a positive fact. Where two documents carry different entities, the draft cannot assert a relationship the materials do not state.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Copy drafting depends on a model reading and restating source wording. Model connection therefore begins with confirming that the AI Proxy dependency is available. Per the v4.17.2 documents, both the App and an Admin service that reuses App/Service configuration must set `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and a non-empty `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN`; missing either causes startup validation to fail. Legacy OpenAI environment variables and single-model custom request addresses do not substitute for these two settings.

Model capability is checked against the inputs the application actually needs. Multimodal input, tool selection, and thinking configuration are each verified separately. Parameters are set in the application or node configuration; a same-named field on a compatible chat interface is not assumed to override orchestration.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a payment risk-control file states, "Order A-2210 was delivered by the carrier on 4 March 2026; the recipient field is blank. The corresponding invoice was issued by entity B, amount 480 USD, invoice date 1 March 2026." The draft sentence under review is, "This order has completed delivery and obtained a compliant invoice; the payment obligation is confirmed." This sample is a paper exercise and points to no real customer or legal identity number. The review scope is limited to the same entity, the same period, and the same field. A blank recipient field is an unstated item and does not constitute a negation of the delivery fact. The correspondence between the invoice entity and the order entity is not stated in the material.

## Three common errors

1. Reading a blank recipient field as "not delivered." The input is the sample material above; the comparison is between the blank recipient field and the delivery fact as two different fields; the observable result is that the draft sentence still states delivery is complete.
2. Treating an invoice issued by entity B as automatically the compliant invoice for order A-2210. The input is the invoice entity and the order entity; the comparison is whether they are the same entity; the observable result is that the material does not state the correspondence while the draft writes it as confirmed.
3. Using a similarity setting to exclude unauthorized records. The input is the retrieval configuration; the comparison is between relevance and permission; the observable result is that retrieval does not perform authorization judgment.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample material above and the draft sentence; compare "has completed delivery" in the draft against the blank recipient field in the source; the condition is met only when the draft is changed to state that delivery status is pending verification.
2. Input invoice entity B and order A-2210; compare whether the two are the same entity; the condition is met only when the material states the correspondence or the draft marks it as pending verification.
3. Input an unrelated query and observe the returned result and the failure branch, recording the actual observation. An empty result can be checked against the downstream design using the explicit paper input `[]`; this page does not claim that a fictional rule retrieval must return empty.

Each verification item states an expected condition rather than a completed test. Where the material does not state a field, the gap remains a gap. A pending review does not demonstrate absence of operating permission; signing, reaching cooperation, and contract effectiveness are three separate facts.
