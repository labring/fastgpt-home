# Creator sales campaigns: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Creator sales campaigns, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Creator-collaboration copy separates plans to invite, sample dispatch and confirmed scheduling. Carry unresolved stages into drafting; sending a sample does not establish a sent invitation or an agreed event. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed material contains a plan to invite a creator for a special event, a dispatched sample and an unconfirmed schedule. No evidence that the invitation was sent is supplied. Collect these passages with the covered product. A person may seek collaboration confirmation or organize a sample introduction. Sample dispatch establishes its sending action, not receipt, willingness to cooperate or a future livestream. Draft agreement arrangements cannot become public commitments. Preserve the proposed invitation and pending schedule when passing input to drafting.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Creator-announcement drafting | Proposed source status | Handoff requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation plan | Proposed invitation; cooperation unconfirmed | No claim of sent invitation or finalized event |
| Sample dispatch | Sent only | Not receipt or reply |
| Timing arrangement | Still pending | Not a formal announcement |
| Sample scope | Specific product | Not the whole product line |
| Drafting route | Confirmation or introduction | Corresponding passages received |

## Three common errors

Sample dispatch promoted to collaboration needs the actual creator reply. Draft timing promoted to a scheduled event needs explicit confirmation. One sample expanded to a product line requires the sample subject beside copy coverage. These inspect relationship, stage and scope without deriving a commercial decision from node completion.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect inputs on confirmation and introduction branches, retaining product identity and pending wording. A creator reply changes only the cooperation it confirms; sample receipt, timing and other products may remain unresolved. Public release, agreement execution and sample dispatch need separate authority and receipts. A completed draft establishes neither release readiness nor promised selling performance.
