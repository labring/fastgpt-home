# Creator commission settlement: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Creator commission settlement, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Commission-settlement wording preserves distinctions among statements, refund deductions and actual handling. A drafting workflow does not create financial commitments. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed settlement wording contains commission-setting passages and an unresolved statement. Refund-deduction material concerns one transaction, and invoice material lacks handling confirmation. Collect these source statuses. A person can obtain transaction correspondence or actual processing evidence. Supply no amounts, rates or timing promises. Settings describe an arrangement without proving applicability to every transaction, and prepared statements do not establish executed payment. Carry gaps downstream with the passages.

## Three common errors

Settings promoted to payable entitlement need actual applicability and confirmation evidence. One deduction expanded to the whole statement needs transaction ownership. An invoice or statement promoted to completed settlement needs corresponding execution records. These inspect applicability, coverage and stage without substituting document titles.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect transaction scope and unresolved state received by both branches. Added correspondence changes only that transaction’s wording. Without execution evidence, the draft cannot become a settled-payment notice; an invoice title does not change this. Formal settlement, deductions and accounting writes need separate authority and receipts. Output establishes drafting, not entitlement or completed financial review.
