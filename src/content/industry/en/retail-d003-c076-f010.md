# Creator commission settlement: citation tracing for copy drafting

## What the documents look like

Commission settlement in this category happens after a sale closes. The material that supports settlement comes from the selected alliance backend, order after-sales data, the finance system and creator contracts. Copy drafting for products and marketing campaigns needs to cite commission ratios, settlement cycles, refund deduction rules and invoicing information from those materials. Every promotional statement in a draft should have a checkable source. The underlying data updates weekly or monthly, so a draft must state which version and effective scope of the material it relies on. A copy error can trigger creator disputes, incorrect finance entries or a stalled cooperation, which is why source tracing runs through the whole drafting step.

Two facts shape this page. Settlement evidence arrives only after transactions and after-sales outcomes are known, so a draft cannot treat a commission ratio or deduction rule as settled until the cited material covers the relevant order scope. The same evidence is shared across finance settlement, alliance operations and creator business roles, so a citation that satisfies one reader may still be incomplete for another. The remaining brief dimensions do not change the citation tracing step and are left aside here.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

The knowledge base search node can associate one or more knowledge bases that use the same vector model. Its citation output is an array and its length may be zero, which means the output path still runs even when nothing is retrieved. Citations must be passed item by item to the answering step, and the answer text is checked against the cited source wording. Retrieved results pass through similarity filtering and a citation limit, so the final citation order does not necessarily follow a single vector similarity ranking. A relevance score is a filtering threshold; it does not perform permission or entity isolation. The same-vector-model requirement applies to knowledge base association only and does not extend to business registration sites or other external systems.

A retrieval step is not an authorization step. Whether the drafting role may read a given settlement record is decided outside the search node, and the citation array alone does not establish that the reader had access.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: the source wording available for checking consists of three sentences. "The commission ratio for this creator cooperation is fifteen percent, and the settlement cycle is thirty days after confirmed receipt." "When an order is refunded, the corresponding commission is deducted in the next month's settlement statement." "This agreement takes effect from the date both parties sign, and the cooperation term runs to 31 December 2025." One draft sentence to be judged reads: "This product carries a fifteen percent commission, settles thirty days after confirmed receipt, deducts refunded order commission in the next month's statement, and takes effect on signing." The comparison object is the three source sentences against the draft sentence. The comparison scope covers four fields: commission ratio, settlement cycle, refund deduction and effectiveness condition. The comparison date is the drafting day. The authorization boundary is that only these three sentences may be used, with no other document or oral statement introduced.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the draft phrase "takes effect on signing" as a verified fact. The source wording states effectiveness from the date both parties sign, and the draft omits the signing parties and the date scope, so the phrase is unsupported. The effectiveness condition field must be checked and the draft revised.
2. Treating minimum relevance as a permission filter. That value is a similarity threshold. It does not exclude unauthorized records and does not separate one creator's settlement data from another's. Material access scope must be confirmed separately.
3. Treating an empty citation array as a retrieval failure. Citation content length may be zero. When the array is empty, check whether the data has finished training and whether the question lacks a subject, record the gap, and do not infer a system error.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the three source sentences and the draft sentence above, then compare the commission ratio, settlement cycle, refund deduction and effectiveness condition fields. When all four fields have a source correspondence and the wording matches, the draft sentence meets the citation tracing condition. When any field is missing or its wording exceeds the source scope, record it as unsupported.
2. Enter a question covering only the refund deduction field, then compare the retrieved citation array against the second source sentence. When the citation array contains that sentence and the answer introduces no other deduction rule, the condition is met. When the citation array is empty, record the empty result and check the downstream handling design rather than treating it as a failure.
3. Enter an explicitly paper-only empty array as the citation input, then compare the output of the downstream answering step. When the downstream step fabricates no commission ratio or effectiveness condition, the empty-result handling condition is met. When the downstream output contains content absent from the source material, record it as a citation gap.

These checks describe what to compare and what remains unknown. No test, deployment or approval has been performed, and any configuration value above is a hypothetical starting point for this exercise only.
