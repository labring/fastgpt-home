# Import and export permits: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Import and export permits, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. License-service copy gives separate sources for preparation and handling results. Contracts and product descriptions cannot establish an obtained permit. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed contract describes intended imported goods. A product-description excerpt states only their use, while the preparation checklist lacks a corresponding permit outcome. Collect the passages and subjects. A person can obtain fuller product wording or inspect this application’s evidence. This drafts a material-service explanation without deciding permit categories or supplying handling rules. A signed contract does not establish permission, and a listed document name does not establish an actual covering file.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

Checklist promoted to held document needs the real file and its subject. A use phrase promoted to a definitive category needs genuine specialist determination evidence. Another product’s result applied here requires subject correspondence. These inspect existence, decision basis and coverage without deriving legal requirements from workflow output.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual inputs on description and handling paths, keeping intended goods and gaps identifiable. Adding this document changes only the status it covers, not permission for other products. Formal applications, authority handling and public release require actual authority and receipts in separate steps. A draft establishes neither completed licensing nor an import, export or admission commitment.
