# Chain-store site selection: copy drafting with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Chain-store site selection, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Store-opening copy separates site evaluation, lease intent and actual opening. A proposed schedule cannot automatically become a public commitment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed site assessment says evaluation should continue, a lease-intent document lacks formal confirmation and the opening schedule remains a draft. Collect location and status passages. A person can seek site evidence or schedule confirmation. Renovation-budget material is only document context without amounts. Continued evaluation does not establish a selected site, and an intent document does not establish an effective lease. Preserve planned status instead of changing it to an opened store after a choice.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Store-opening copy | Proposed status passage | Corresponding evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Site opinion | Evaluation continues | Not selected location |
| Lease intent | Formally unconfirmed | Not effective lease |
| Draft schedule | Proposed arrangement | Not an opening notice |
| Location transfer | Source matches recipient | Exclude another address |
| Business execution | Announcements and construction separate | Draft is not an operating store |

## Three common errors

Intent promoted to a settled site needs actual confirmation. Draft timing promoted to an opening notice needs current approval and execution status. Another address inserted into the copy requires source-location and downstream-input checks. These inspect decision, stage and subject without filling gaps through editing.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual recipients on site and timing paths. Added location confirmation changes only what it establishes. Unconfirmed timing remains qualified, and construction material cannot substitute for operating status. Formal leasing, construction and public announcements need separate authority and receipts. Node output establishes a draft, not permission to operate or certainty that plans will occur.
