# Chain-store closure accounts: copy drafting with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Chain-store closure accounts, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Track remaining-service arrangements through closure-copy revisions without promoting proposed transfers to completed settlement. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A closure-copy exercise begins with a hypothetical memo proposing a neighboring store as a continuing contact, with the handover still unconfirmed. A second note temporarily retains the original store’s enquiry desk. The next request asks for a shorter notice. There is no receiving-store confirmation or record of completed customer handling. The prompt must preserve proposal and confirmation gaps while separating a contact channel from service performance. A shortened notice saying services transferred would overstate the supported stage. Fluent wording cannot replace the receiving party’s actual confirmation.

## Three common errors

- If shortening omits the pending-confirmation sentence and announces a completed handover, retain the submitted history and prior draft to locate the condition lost during compression.

- If the temporary enquiry desk becomes full continuing service in the optimized question, compare both questions with the memo’s action scope. A contact channel does not establish performance arrangements.

- If another store’s closure summary supplies settlement here, inspect store identity, settlement subject and supporting record. This store’s missing settlement evidence remains a gap.

## How to verify the setup

Compare a new conversation containing only the two notes with continued revisions in the original conversation. Inspect preservation of the proposed handover. When a neighboring-store reply arrives, check covered services, period and responsible party. One contact arrangement establishes neither all services nor account settlement. For a structured notice draft, validate parsing before tracing each status word to a source; complete structure may still misstate stages. This produces reviewable copy without publishing to customers or changing stored-value and settlement records.
