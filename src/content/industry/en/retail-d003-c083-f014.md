# Store counts and loss control: copy drafting with model connections

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Store counts and loss control, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Keep stock discrepancies and investigation stages distinct in inventory copy drafts. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Suppose a stock sheet awaits a repeat count, an analyst suspects missed scans, and a write-off request remains unsigned. Inventory copy should retain these stages. Inspect the text actually received by the connected model and the authors of the analysis and approval records. This paper exercise supplies neither surveillance footage nor actual stock figures or a responsible individual. A draft may state the discrepancy and missing evidence without presenting a suspicion as an investigation finding. A responding service establishes no completed count, attribution or approval.

## Three common errors

- If only the stock sheet reaches the request but missed scans become a confirmed cause, inspect the request and cited wording to distinguish omitted evidence from an invented conclusion.

- If the storekeeper’s suspicion becomes finance confirmation, compare the note’s author, verb and review response. A role name supplies no acceptance.

- If an earlier approved write-off authorizes the current discrepancy, inspect store, counting round and approval subject. The earlier decision has its own scope.

## How to verify the setup

Add the analysis and current approval record to the fixed stock sheet in stages, repeating questions about state and permissible wording. Retain actual inputs and outputs and trace additions to supplied text. Test scanned sheets or footage through their actual input paths separately. Text connectivity establishes no image or video support. Model-service credentials grant neither finance approval nor inventory-write authority; the responsible reviewer must check signatures and distribution scope before using the copy.
