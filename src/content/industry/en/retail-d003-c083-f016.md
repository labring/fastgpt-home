# Store counts and loss control: copy drafting with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For copy drafting in Store counts and loss control, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Stock-count copy distinguishes observations, discrepancies and evidence for their causes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Suppose a note says book and physical stock await comparison, a first count lacks review and a site photograph comes from another date. Collect store, count matter and source version, then choose recount evidence or image review before writing. The node collects information and records interaction without a ledger or cause-determination function. Test missing store identity and completed input. No loss amount or personal responsibility is supplied.

## Three common errors

An unresolved comparison rewritten as confirmed loss needs actual review checked. A discrepancy reported as theft needs causal evidence checked. An older photograph applied to this count needs date and subject checked. These concern finding stage, cause and evidence ownership; complete wording proves no explained discrepancy.

## How to verify the setup

Trace each proposed sentence to the note. Retain the first-count label and verifiable date beside the image. Added review changes the relevant difference alone while cause remains separate, without implying accountability was decided. Stock adjustments, loss recognition and responsibility handling depend on authorized business procedures, facts and receipts. The form gathers writing material without making financial or personnel judgments. Index first counts separately from review evidence, preserving verifiable photo dates so proposed statements about loss or cause reveal both their sources and remaining gaps.
