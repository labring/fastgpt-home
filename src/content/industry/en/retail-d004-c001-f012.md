# B2B marketplaces: document comparison with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Do delivery scope, change conditions and warehouse roles remain connected after parsing? The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

The proposed contract limits delivery to the agreed warehouse, with a following paragraph requiring separate confirmation for a change of receiving location. Begin by locating both statements in the parsed output, then inspect whether their relationship survives chunking. A warehouse name alone supplies no basis for the change condition. Compare a literal clause query with a natural question about changing the receiving location, retaining the text returned by each. These are proposed inspection steps, with no parsing result assumed in advance.

## Three common errors

- If the warehouse name separates from the confirmation condition for changing destination, compare original, parsed output and block for a traceable relationship.

- If quotation dispatch and contract receiving warehouses become conflicting clauses, inspect their roles rather than judging unequal names.

- If an absent acceptance method becomes a specific clause, inspect the complete original first. A clause present but missing from parsing is a processing gap; chunking cannot supply absent business evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Parsing cannot supply an acceptance procedure absent from the contract. Inspect the complete original first: an absent clause belongs on the document-request list; a clause present in the original but missing from parsed output calls for retaining that failed passage and inspecting the processing path. After adding material, check training or index state and repeat the same acceptance question to trace the new clause. Upload, parsing completion and retrieval of supporting evidence are separate acceptance objects, each requiring its actual inputs and outputs.
