# MCN operations: document comparison with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A new proposed influencer agreement offers a chunking test: “Brand materials may be used in this stream; later promotion requires a separate agreement.” The two clauses qualify purpose and timing. Parsing must not reduce them to unrestricted availability of brand material. Ask separately about use in this stream and the evidence needed for later promotion, tracing the original conditions rather than treating one search hit as support for both situations.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Chunk completeness | Separate agreement before later promotion | Keep timing and purpose conditions associated. |
| Original data and indexes | Independent agreement and handling-form originals | An index summary is not a responsibility decision. |

## Three common errors

A proposed violation-handling form records that the material appeared in later promotion. That is a recorded statement, not an established contractual breach. Inspect retention of the later-promotion context and examine contract and handling-form originals separately. Indexes provide search routes; they must not merge both sources into an existing responsibility decision. Document identity, version and the actual applicable relationship still require their own confirmation. Check the event statement separately from the agreement’s purpose and timing conditions. One recorded event is not a complete authorization chain. The versions actually used for comparison should remain traceable in their original data, without being inferred from index wording.

## How to verify the setup

If the contract refers to a brand-material list that was not supplied, parsing its body cannot enumerate every item on the list. Record the missing attachment and its clause relationship, then inspect parsing and indexes after supplementation. Repeat the same scope question and trace the new citation to the attachment. An upload record without corresponding original data does not validate availability of the list. This example checks text handoff without granting authorization or deciding responsibility.
