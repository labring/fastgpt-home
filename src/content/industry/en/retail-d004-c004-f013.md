# Temu semi-managed sellers: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect declaration, overseas-stock and procurement sources by user, preserving distinct platform and warehouse scopes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise contains a product-declaration attachment, overseas-stock table and procurement calculation. Operations reviews declaration, a warehouse reviews its stock, and the supplier sees its own description. Test actual accounts resource by resource; one product authorizes no universal sharing. If a warehouse identity reads internal procurement conditions, retain response and grant configuration. Platform and warehouse sources may concern the same item without giving every collaborator all reading or modification rights.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Permission check | Half-managed collaboration sources | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task sources | Separate declaration, stock and calculations | Account reads and operations by resource |
| Warehouse boundary | Relevant and unrelated warehouses | Full response with internal conditions |
| Member mapping | Warehouse and supplier are business labels | Owner-confirmed account scope |
| External identity | Inspect platform, WMS and logistics separately | No universal grant from one interface |
| Content identity | Wrong warehouse quantity needs period checks | Separate grants and stock execution |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If warehouse or supplier labels become members, inspect actual accounts and allowed operations. Business correspondence grants no rights automatically; the owner resolves missing mappings.

- If a warehouse identity reads internal procurement calculations, retain full response and grant configuration. One product relationship establishes no universal sharing.

- If platform or WMS readability becomes universal authorization, inspect external identity and internal resources independently. Working reads also establish neither saleability nor declaration acceptance; those need actual outcomes.

## How to verify the setup

Ask operations, the relevant warehouse, supplier and unrelated warehouse about the same item, recording originals, operations and denials. Inspect full responses for paraphrased restricted calculations beyond attachment displays. Correct grants with another warehouse’s quantity in the answer need subject and period checks rather than wider rights. Keep resource scope separate from unresolved content. Actual sales availability, stock synchronization and fulfillment execution require business records; they cannot be inferred from a readable table.
