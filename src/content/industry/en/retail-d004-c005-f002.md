# TikTok Shop US operations: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Image use during cooperation and replacement of withdrawn material need separate reading. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed TikTok Shop agreement permits product images only during cooperation and requires replacement of withdrawn material. A cleanup sheet marks an old image awaiting replacement, while earlier conversation used long-term retention wording. A current question about continuing to use the old image needs cooperation period, withdrawal and pending replacement together in input. The cleanup sheet does not establish whether cooperation remains active or the image still applies. Historical wording cannot replace the current qualification, and pending replacement cannot become a completed image change.

## Three common errors

One common error is retaining permission to use images while trimming away its time scope. Another is summarizing awaiting replacement as replaced, merging different states. Compare the complete clause, supplied passage and status summary to locate the change. If an answer with history adopts long-term retention, check whether that wording concerns the same material and has explicit confirmation. Actual model context capacity cannot be inferred from the reply limit. Space for a longer answer also cannot provide evidence for the cooperation state missing from the supplied material.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should identify the image, applicable period, cleanup progress and cooperation status still requiring confirmation before drawing a use conclusion. Repeat the same question without and with history to see whether long-term wording overrides the current qualification. If output feeds a material-cleanup process through a fixed format, verify that pending replacement still belongs to the old image and that conditions and gaps parse successfully. Any later confirmation needs its own material and period scope, so a result for one picture is not extended to all older assets.
