# General export declarations: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. A pre-shipment review draft and formally confirmed documents require their document stages retained. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed export clause requires a review draft before shipment and separate confirmation of formal documents. A packing-list header says review draft, and an email reply says received. A question about document confirmation needs the draft identity, formal-confirmation condition and receipt action together in input. Receiving a file and confirming its contents are different matters. No actual shipment record is supplied either, so before shipment cannot be reversed into proof that shipment occurred, and the receipt reply cannot become formal acceptance of the document contents.

## Three common errors

One failure condition is a citation losing the header and the answer treating draft content as formal documentation. Another is earlier discussion of what may be submitted becoming a statement that confirmation occurred. Inspect document stage, action and subject in actual input, then locate the change against complete sources. A missing formal document cannot be created by allocating more context. Reply length controls expression space, not evidence availability. A long review draft still requires inspection of the actual supplied passage for the separate-confirmation qualification. A third error treats the received email as formal confirmation. Inspect actual confirmation material and its corresponding document, separating file receipt from content acceptance. The existence of a reply cannot fill the formal-confirmation gap.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance questions should distinguish the stage reached by the draft, the action evidenced by the email and the confirmation material still needed. Retain the scope of submitted or received wording and leave formal confirmation unresolved instead of declaring the file process complete. Repeat the same question with earlier discussion present, preserving both actual inputs to check changed attribution. If a fixed response supports document preparation, verify that stage, observed action and follow-up all parse completely. The output must not turn documentation progress into an actual physical shipment result.
