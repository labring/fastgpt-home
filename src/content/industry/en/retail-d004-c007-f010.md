# General import declarations: document comparison with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Compare consignee, notification and delivery-location fields by their stated roles. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed import packet names Seaside Trading as the receiving party, a warehouse-service provider as the notification contact and the warehouse address as the destination for documents in an email. An earlier warehouse contact sheet is also retained. A question about replacing the receiving party needs those field roles separated. Party identity, notification contact and document destination do not overwrite one another. Preserve field headings with quoted wording. An email destination establishes no party amendment, while an older contact sheet alone cannot establish the current warehouse location.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Transaction-source check | Material and action scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party field | Seaside Trading | Read transaction wording. |
| Notification role | Warehouse-service provider | Do not replace receiving party. |
| Email object | Documents were sent | Do not infer goods delivery. |
| Address version | Earlier contact sheet | Inspect current instructions. |
| Amendment evidence | Not supplied for current transaction | Check transaction and subject. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Associated knowledge bases` | Knowledge bases associated with one search node must use the same embedding model. |
| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |
| `Citation input handoff` | Inspect citations actually received by the answer step. |
| `Citation display check` | Trace displayed sources to their originals. |
| `Document-validity check` | Inspect source identity and applicable version separately. |

## Three common errors

First, the notification contact may become the receiving party; compare transaction and transport fields and seek a current amendment. Second, delivery of documents may become delivery of goods; inspect the emailed object and obtain the applicable goods-handover record. Third, an older warehouse address may become the current destination; inspect contact-sheet identity, version and current instructions. These checks concern role, action object and location version. Repeated company names or addresses do not establish a changed operational relationship or completed goods transfer between the named organizations.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask who receives goods, who receives notification and what the email sent, tracing each answer to actual reference content. A company name without its field heading may be insufficient to identify the role, so inspect the supplied excerpt rather than completing it. Added amendment text needs a transaction and subject match; failure to obtain it does not establish rejection. This proposed-text comparison proves no actual receipt, clearance or release. Those outcomes require their own records regardless of how many related documents can be read.
