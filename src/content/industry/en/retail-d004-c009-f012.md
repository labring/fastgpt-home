# Amazon FBM fulfillment: document comparison with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Amazon FBM fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Stock confirmation before dispatch arrangements and separate area checks must remain conditional. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed FBM service clause says dispatch is arranged after stock confirmation. Its next paragraph continues that the arrangement excludes special areas requiring separate checks. A question about all locations needs the phrase this arrangement traced back to the earlier action, rather than treated as a subjectless general note. Parsing must preserve paragraph order, and chunk review should inspect whether the stock prerequisite and area scope remain traceable together. No area list is supplied, so the exercise cannot define which actual locations fall under the exception.

## Three common errors

One failure leaves the stock condition in an earlier block while dispatch arranged appears alone and becomes a stock promise. Another separates the area exception so that retrieval supplies only the affirmative action. Compare original adjacency and actual blocks to locate loss of prerequisite and scope independently. An all-locations search entry cannot fill the original range. Inspect indexes and original data separately, since a searchable entry does not establish that the exception text reached the answer or that the resulting statement remains conditional.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should search stock confirmation, special areas and whether every location can receive direct dispatch. Inspect whether citations reconstruct the complete condition. The answer should retain dispatch after stock confirmation and separate area checking without adding current stock or an actual dispatch result. After revision, inspect processing and index versions before comparing the same returned passages. Upload success does not establish a repaired continuation relationship. If area detail is on a missing page, record the gap rather than reading absence of the page as absence of any exception.
