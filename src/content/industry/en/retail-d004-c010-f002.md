# JD POP seller operations: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Repair-intake scope and a method determined after inspection do not establish a selected repair plan. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed JD POP guide requires checking intake scope before sending an item for repair and deciding the repair method after inspection. An intake instruction says main unit only for this case, with cables not sent. A question about returning the whole set for immediate replacement needs the current scope, cable exclusion and inspection prerequisite together. Intake and replacement decisions concern different stages. No inspection finding or actual repair plan is supplied, so arranging intake cannot establish direct replacement. Excluding cables from this shipment also does not establish that cables require no service of any kind.

## Three common errors

One common error is a citation losing the cable exclusion and the answer treating the full set as the intake subject. Another is earlier replacement discussion bypassing the current inspection prerequisite. Inspect intake subjects and decision sequence in actual input, keeping the guide distinct from historical suggestions. Check actual model context capacity. A short reply must not retain only send the main unit while losing the later verification action; a longer reply likewise cannot provide an inspection finding absent from the sources. Identify that missing evidence explicitly.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask what is accepted, what is excluded from this shipment and when the repair method is determined. Match each output to its sentence, then compare the same question with empty history and earlier replacement discussion. If fixed output supports the service case, verify complete parsing of main unit, cable exclusion and the method still requiring inspection. Later inspection material needs its item and recommendation scope checked. An inspection suggestion cannot automatically become completed repair, and the text comparison should not decide responsibility or service charges for the customer.
