## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed JD POP attachment says the main unit is replaced when conditions on the referenced page are met. An adjoining parenthesis excludes accessories unless separately confirmed. The condition page is absent. A whole-set replacement question needs main unit, accessories and separate-confirmation exception kept distinct. Ignoring the parenthesis during parsing or retaining only replace main unit during chunking could expand a local clause into a whole-set arrangement. The supplied wording establishes a prerequisite exists, but cannot provide its missing content or establish that this case meets it.

## Three common errors

One common error separates accessories excluded from unless separately confirmed into different blocks, making either an absolute exclusion or complete inclusion appear supported. Another summarizes when conditions are met as conditions already met. Compare parenthesis completeness, original-block subject and condition-page reference to distinguish separated exceptions from invented facts. A whole-set search entry still needs the source qualification and cannot expand service scope. The clause also does not establish an executed replacement; absent an actual handling record, preserve that distinction in the resulting answer.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should query main unit, accessories, separate confirmation and the complete whole-set question. Inspect returned blocks for the full parenthesis and prerequisite. The answer should identify current wording and missing confirmation material without deciding eligibility. If the condition page is obtained, check clause applicability, processing and index state before repeating the question. Upload success does not establish answer use of the added page. Where only the earlier main clause returns, retain originals and citations to locate excluded input scope or omission of an already retrieved condition.
