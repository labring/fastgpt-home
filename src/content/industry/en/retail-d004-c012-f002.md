# Warehouse picking and packing: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Warehouse picking and packing, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Keep package relationships and supplementary conditions together in current input. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed warehouse packet lists a main unit and sealing ring in one package, excludes the ring from a display sample and asks whether the formal order contains it. Longer background discusses displaying the sample. Actual answer input needs order-versus-sample identity together with the respective inclusion and exclusion. Keeping only the ring exclusion may apply a display condition to the order. Summarizing the two texts as a conflict also removes scopes that can coexist. Shorter input or output alone cannot identify an incorrect business record.

## Three common errors

First, the packed-together sentence may lose the formal-order identity; inspect list, subject and appendix relationships in the actual request, locating missing text. Second, earlier sample discussion may govern the current order question; use the same question with empty history and retain actual inputs rather than comparing answers alone. Third, the answer may state inclusion but omit the sample exception; inspect sentence scope and response length so both subjects can be expressed. These concern delivered material, historical reference and condition expression. More output space supplies no missing evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about the formal order, the sample and why their scopes differ, comparing delivered text with each answer. When knowledge references are limited by tokens, inspect the actual retained list and note rather than counting returned records. Check the chosen model’s real context capability; a reply limit is not its complete input window. Missing order identity requires corresponding evidence first. This proposed-text test packs no goods and establishes neither complete real orders nor completed warehouse operations, which would need independent applicable records beyond the input and answer comparison.
