# Warehouse picking and packing: document comparison with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Warehouse picking and packing, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Verify what review signatures, reprinted labels and package-content records actually cover. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed warehouse packet specifies a cup and lid as a set, contains a review field limited to the cup’s appearance and notes that a label was reprinted because the address was unclear. The question asks whether a signature proves a complete set and delivery to a new address. Review scope is limited, and the reprint reason establishes no address change. A weighing slip belongs to another parcel. Preserve field and parcel attribution rather than interpreting several paper traces as evidence that every stage of the operation was completed.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Transaction-source check | Material and action scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed field | Cup appearance only | Inspect actual review scope. |
| Set contents | Cup and lid | Seek composition and quantity evidence. |
| Reprint reason | Unclear writing | Do not infer changed address. |
| Weighing identity | Other-parcel slip | Check parcel attribution. |
| Operation completion | Task listing is not execution | Seek corresponding operation records. |

| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |

## Three common errors

First, an appearance signature may establish lid quantity and set completeness; read the signed field and seek applicable quantity and composition records. Second, label reprinting may become address modification; compare previous and new wording with actual amendment evidence, since unclear writing concerns presentation. Third, another parcel’s weighing slip may prove this parcel is complete; inspect parcel identity and what weighing supports. Weight alone does not identify every component. These require operation scope, change object and record-attribution checks rather than an aggregate impression of paperwork completeness.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the signature covers, why the label was reprinted and whose weighing slip is supplied, retaining the review limitation and parcel identity in actual references. A task sheet alone establishes no executed review; a label alone establishes no complete set. Added operation records support only their stated inspection items, without extending one signature to every condition. Empty references require investigation of source scope and transfer. Missing returned material cannot establish an omitted item, and the comparison proves no completed dispatch or delivery from these proposed documents.
