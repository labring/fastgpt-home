# Warehouse picking and packing: document comparison with document chunking

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Warehouse picking and packing, this page examines FastGPT document chunking. Fragile-mark handling and follow-up after damage are different operational conditions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

A proposed warehouse agreement says to separate items when a fragile mark is present. The next section says discovered damage triggers pausing sealing and recording the issue, with a note that a mark alone does not establish damage. A question about pausing sealing needs section roles, triggers and the note together. Fragile is a handling signal, while damage is an observed subject requiring its own evidence. The two cannot merge into stopping every marked package. Parsing that lists actions without their premises would remove the relationships needed for the comparison.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original or chunk check | Specific source relationship | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mark trigger | Fragile mark means separate placement | Check condition-action pair. |
| Damage trigger | Discovered damage pauses sealing and is recorded | Check action scope. |
| Distinguishing note | Mark does not establish damage | Keep note associated. |
| Event identity | Procedure is not execution record | Do not infer an actual incident. |

## Three common errors

One common error puts separate placement and pause sealing in one block while losing their respective triggers. Another isolates the mark-is-not-damage note so the answer treats a warning as an incident record. Compare original structure and actual blocks for condition-action pairs. Different fragile and damage search entries may point to corresponding originals, but entry names cannot establish that damage actually occurred. This proposed procedure also cannot be described as a warehouse execution record merely because the text gives a complete sequence of possible actions.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should search fragile mark, discovered damage and whether a fragile mark alone pauses sealing. Inspect which trigger the returned wording retains. The answer should distinguish separate placement from pausing and recording after damage, while identifying the absence of an actual incident in this fixture. After procedure revisions, inspect processing, indexes and citation versions before repeating questions. Uploading a new procedure does not establish removal of older triggers. Preserve old blocks to locate continued use of their relationships rather than declaring operational logic updated from file presence alone.
