# Franchise store supervision: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect contracts and visit sources by head office, regional supervisor and owner, without expanding access through shared regional membership. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise pairs a store’s franchise appendix, visit record and correction-photo description. Head office reviews terms, regional staff follow the relevant store, and the owner reads their own matters. Test actual accounts and operations rather than turning regional groups or titles into roles. Franchise relationships identify business parties without opening other contracts or every photo. Retain store, visit period and originals for each read instead of merging scope through matching correction headings.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Permission check | Franchise-visit relationship | Acceptance object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party correspondence | Office, supervisor and owner are business labels | Actual account-resource mapping |
| Store boundary | Separate own contract and other-store photos | Full response with restricted text |
| Period and version | Visit time and applicable standards | No scope merging from matching headings |
| External source | Inspect supervision-app authentication | No substitute for grants or acceptance |
| Business approval | Reading amends no contract or purchase | Actual responsibility and records |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If head-office or owner titles become members, inspect actual accounts and contract or visit resources. The owner confirms missing mapping; business relationships create no automatic grant.

- If one store reads another contract or correction-photo description, retain full response. Shared region grants no cross-store access, including paraphrases of undisplayed attachments.

- If a supervision interface establishes universal access or accepted correction, inspect authentication, internal resources and actual confirmation separately. Reading replaces no business result.

## How to verify the setup

Ask head office, assigned supervisor, relevant store and unrelated-store identities about the same correction, recording originals, operations and denials. Correct access with another store’s standard needs subject and version checks rather than wider grants. Retest the same identity scope after adding photos or reviews, retaining absent material. Contract amendment, visits and correction acceptance need separate confirmation. Resource reading approves no purchase and changes no franchise obligation; those actions require actual responsibility and records.
