# Letter-of-credit documents: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access to credit, invoice and presentation sources, separating textual differences, bank comments and business authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise pairs beneficiary wording, a seller appendix and a bank presentation comment for trade, document and finance readers. List the passages needed before checking actual accounts and permitted operations; job titles create no membership or maintenance rights. Amendments, invoices and bank comments may carry different dates. Reading one does not open the others. A textual difference identifies an unresolved point rather than establishing bank acceptance or compliance through successful access.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Comparison access check | Document source | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member mapping | Trade, document and finance duties | Accounts and permitted operations |
| Case scope | Beneficiary and seller appendix | Case identity and passage |
| Restricted sources | Other credits and internal comments | Full response and denial |
| Amendment relationship | Credit amendment and invoice dates | Obtained version and input |
| Processing boundary | Query versus bank acceptance | Authentication and actual reply |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a trade contact becomes a member with universal document-maintenance rights, inspect the account and operations. The owner confirms missing mapping; a title grants nothing.

- If another case’s credit or internal bank comment appears, retain the passage, case and full response. A common beneficiary name authorizes no cross-case access.

- If a presentation-query response proves internal access or bank acceptance, inspect external authentication, source permissions and actual handling replies separately. A response establishes no outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Test the same case’s beneficiary text, amendment and invoice with allowed accounts, then request restricted sources through unrelated-case identities. Keep denial or leakage passages. Correct grants with missing amendments require a distinction between unobtained sources and obtained passages omitted from input; wider rights cannot supply evidence. Retest the original scope after receiving bank comments and preserve version relationships. Reading and textual support execute no presentation, credit amendment or payment confirmation; each needs authority and actual processing records.
