# Franchisee onboarding: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Retain prerequisites, missing items and responsible roles in opening-check comparisons. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed opening-coaching packet schedules review after display photos are complete, records attendance at a checkout introduction and has a manager message allowing material preparation first. The question asks whether opening review was passed. Actual input needs after completion, arrange review and preparation preserved as different stages. Attendance concerns another activity. If long history mentions another store’s passed review, that is no current result. The packet supplies no inspection outcome, and sharing one conversation cannot compress preparation, attendance and acceptance into a single completed state.

## Three common errors

First, review after photos are complete may become review completed; compare original conditions with actual request and preserve the later action. Second, another store’s historical result may become current acceptance; inspect store and review item and compare the same question with empty history. Third, an answer may list attendance while omitting missing photos; inspect task coverage and reply length instead of replacing review support with participation. These concern prerequisite, historical subject and source-gap expression. A longer response adds no inspection event absent from the underlying material.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which photos are missing, how far the manager’s arrangement goes and which activity attendance covers, comparing actual input with answer wording. Under a token budget, inspect retained prerequisites and gaps instead of merely whether a short conclusion can be generated; check the model window separately. Missing photos and current review records remain unresolved after reference or reply adjustments. This page schedules no visit, approves no opening and does not assess any employee’s practical competence. A proposed preparation checklist also establishes no actual completion record without independent applicable evidence for the real event.
