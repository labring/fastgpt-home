# Franchisee onboarding: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Allocate reading around training, site checks and corrections, testing head-office, supervisor and store source scope. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise pairs a training appendix, store checklist and correction note, identifying questions for head office, supervisors and store managers. Inspect actual accounts and permitted operations before combining sources. SOPs, photographs and attendance lists may be maintained separately; supervisory duties open no universal access. Even sources from one store need dates and versions. Signatures, images and matching wording supply no automatic conclusion about site execution or performance of obligations beyond the evidence actually obtained.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supervision comparison access | Training and site sources | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duty mapping | Head-office, supervisor and store questions | Account, resources and operations |
| Version relationship | Training appendix and applicable SOP | Actual dates and passages |
| Store restriction | Other photographs and internal rules | Full response and denial |
| External system | Training login versus internal reading | Separate identity and permission |
| Execution scope | Attendance does not prove correction | Actual record and responsible judgment |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a supervisor title becomes universal store maintenance, inspect the account, resources and operations. The owner confirms missing membership mapping; staffing arrangements create no grant.

- If one store reads another store’s photographs or internal head-office rules, retain source descriptions and full responses. A shared brand authorizes no cross-store access.

- If training-system login proves SOP access and completed correction, inspect external identity, internal permissions and actual correction evidence separately. Attendance replaces no execution.

## How to verify the setup

List allowed and prohibited readers for the training appendix, inspection and correction sources. Test the same passages for reads and denials, retaining actual versions. Correct permissions with an older SOP need current-input and date checks rather than wider grants. Distinguish unobtained site material from obtained material omitted from the request, then repeat. Reading arranges no training, submits no correction and establishes no store qualification. Those outcomes require actual business records and a responsible person’s judgment.
