# Franchise fee settlement: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Retain request category, payment identity and handling-state wording in deposit comparisons. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed franchise packet has a deposit ledger awaiting material, a service-fee notice saying an explanation was sent and a store request for deposit-handling progress. The current question concerns the deposit only. Actual input needs payment identity retained across all three sources, rather than combining a sent service-fee explanation with completed deposit handling. Long history may include another store’s returned payment, which supplies no current outcome. These hypothetical texts contain no amounts or actual review result and support category and progress checks only, without assuming that related settlement documents describe the same matter.

## Three common errors

First, summarization may merge service fee and deposit into a generic charge; inspect original names, source identity and current request to restore lost categories. Second, another store’s result may become this application’s state; check store and request and compare delivered material under the same question with empty history. Third, output may keep sent while dropping material pending; compare each state with its object and retain open actions when adjusting length. These concern category, historical attribution and state expression. Fluent response supplies no evidence of the current handling result.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the ledger lacks, what the notice sent and what the request concerns, tracing every sentence to actual input. Reference-token budgeting needs categories and pending conditions retained, rather than judging completeness by record count or summary length. Check the real model window separately from reply allowance. If the source has no outcome, additional budget creates no approval or payment. This exercise determines no return entitlement and performs no settlement. Material sent establishes no refunded funds; actual handling and payment still require their matching operational records beyond the proposed packet.
