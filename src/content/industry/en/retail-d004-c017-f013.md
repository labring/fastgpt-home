# Franchise fee settlement: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access to franchise payment plans, invoicing and receipt sources without turning financial wording into funds or breach judgments. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes franchise payment terms, invoicing wording and a store receipt note, without amounts or actual vouchers. Operations reviews agreed stages, finance checks current funds evidence, and the store reads its sources. Map tasks to actual accounts and allowed operations. Plans, invoices and bank sources may be managed separately; a finance title opens no universal franchise access. Issued invoices and outstanding collection can coexist. Readable wording establishes neither performance, liability for unpaid funds nor mutually accepted settlement.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Franchise-finance access | Source status | Inspection object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task mapping | Operations, finance and store needs | Actual accounts and operations |
| Different stages | Plan, invoice and receipt | Passages and current record |
| Other-store restriction | Separate collection and violation records | Full answer and denial |
| Period scope | Earlier terms do not supply current evidence | Input version and date |
| Handling responsibility | Reading collects no funds or decides breach | Execution sources and confirmation |

## Three common errors

- If a franchise contact becomes a member with universal finance maintenance, inspect account, store and resource operations. The owner confirms scope; a contractual signature grants nothing.

- If this comparison returns another store’s collection or internal violation records, retain source identity and full answers. One franchise network authorizes no cross-store sharing, including paraphrases.

- If finance connectivity establishes internal-voucher access or received funds, inspect external authentication, resource rights and this receipt separately. An invoice or plan replaces no funds evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Have operations, finance, this store and an unrelated store query the same contract and restricted vouchers. Retain passages, operations and denials. Correct grants with an older payment plan need input, period and version checks rather than broader rights. Separate obtained vouchers omitted from input from absent receipt records, then repeat within the original scope. Access collects nothing, issues no invoice and determines no breach. Accounting and responsibility need execution sources and confirmation by the responsible person.
