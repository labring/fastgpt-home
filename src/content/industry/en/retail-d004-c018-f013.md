# Regional dealer stock: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect permissions for dealer replenishment, collection plans and stock sources, separating parties, periods and execution evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes a dealer request, a brand collection plan and warehouse stock wording. Sales checks this customer’s demand, finance checks funds sources, and warehouse staff identify goods. Confirm actual members and operations before combining documents; contact names create no cross-department permissions. Requested quantities, collection plans and point-in-time stock answer different questions. Matching goods establish neither the same dealer nor period. Readable sources prove no accepted order or allocatable stock beyond their actual coverage.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |

| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a sales contact gains universal dealer maintenance, inspect the account, customer mapping and operations. The owner confirms scope; commercial contact creates no authorization.

- If this request returns another customer’s collection or internal credit text, retain party passages and full responses. Shared brands or goods authorize no mutual access.

- If brand ordering connectivity establishes warehouse-source access or executed replenishment, inspect interface authentication, internal reading and current-order results separately. A plan supplies no funds receipt.

## How to verify the setup

Test the same material with sales, finance, warehouse and unrelated-customer identities, recording passages, operations and denials by scope. Correct accounts with older stock dates need actual-input and definition checks rather than broader rights. Distinguish unobtained funds vouchers from obtained sources omitted from reading, then repeat within the original scope. Access accepts no replenishment, reserves no inventory and handles no collection. Supply and funds stages still need corresponding business sources and owner confirmation rather than a complete-looking document set.
