# On-demand retail fulfillment: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in On-demand retail fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Match exclusions, substitution proposals and historical replies to the correct retail round. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed immediate-retail packet excludes products containing nuts, proposes sesame crisps and supplies an ingredient page that does not state the relevant nut conditions. History contains acceptance of substitution on an earlier order. The question asks whether the current proposal is accepted and satisfies the note. Retain exclusion, ingredient gap and earlier-order identity. Names alone determine no applicable ingredient relationship, and old consent does not renew. This exercise checks input and evidential support without making a composition, dietary suitability or allergy-safety judgment about real products or the proposed substitute.

| Ingredient gap | Relevant conditions absent | Retain unresolved state. |
| Input budget | Note and gap together | Inspect more than returned page identity. |

## Three common errors

First, summarization may omit the exclusion; compare the current note with actual request and retain the customer’s scope. Second, earlier consent may migrate to the present proposal; inspect order, substitute and historical reference, including the same question with empty history. Third, the answer may assert satisfaction while dropping the ingredient gap; trace support and allow space for unresolved conditions. These concern condition delivery, consent attribution and insufficient-evidence expression. Additional tokens supply no ingredient evidence that is absent from the source and establish no safe or accepted substitution.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the customer excludes, what is proposed and what the current page explicitly establishes, retaining actual input and response conditions. Budget checks need both the note and ingredient gap available; a returned product page is not necessarily complete evidence. Inspect actual model window, reference limit and response length separately. Missing ingredient material remains a source requirement. This test executes no substitution or delivery and guarantees no dietary compliance. Real composition and applicability judgments require matching evidence and responsible verification beyond the proposed text and its model-generated summary.
