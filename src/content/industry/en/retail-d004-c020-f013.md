# Certificates of origin: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access to certificate applications, customer requirements and production sources by actual user, without deriving grants from party labels. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A proposed comparison allocates application and invoice review to document staff, production wording to plant staff, and only necessary correction information to the customer. Inspect reading and operations with actual accounts. One shipment does not imply universal sharing. If a customer identity retrieves a production appendix, retain the passage and grant scope to investigate boundary failure. Material useful for origin analysis does not mean every collaborator should see its complete production contents.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Permission check | Origin-source allocation | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document review | Application and invoice | Account reads and operation scope |
| Production appendix | Process text limited to appropriate personnel | Negative test with customer identity |
| Business name | Exporter is not a team role | Owner-confirmed account correspondence |
| External material | Company-system production source | Separate authentication and resource grants |
| Full-response inspection | Restricted information may be paraphrased | Retain response beyond the attachment list |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If an exporter name becomes team membership or qualification, inspect actual accounts and business proof separately. A party in an application creates neither member rights nor origin eligibility.

- If a customer reads restricted production passages, retain originals and full response to inspect grants. One shipment creates no automatic sharing, including paraphrased content.

- If a production-interface call authorizes the customer entry, test external scope and internal reads separately. Readable material replaces neither origin rules nor actual certificate issuance.

## How to verify the setup

Ask document, production and customer identities about the same application sources, recording visible originals, denials and operation limits. If restricted process information is paraphrased into an answer, inspect the full response even when the appendix is absent from the attachment list. After permission testing, applicable rules, product scope and supporting evidence still need content review. Readable material establishes neither qualification nor issued certification, and does not itself fulfill the customer’s clearance requirements.
