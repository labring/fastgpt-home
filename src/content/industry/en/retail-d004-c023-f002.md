## What the documents look like

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed brand-repair packet records receipt of a main unit without its adapter, a draft inspecting only the power connector and a note returning empty packaging. The question asks whether the complete set was repaired and the unit returned. Actual input needs without adapter, connector only and empty packaging retained, instead of combining receipt, inspection and return fragments into full completion. Another device’s adapter in history supplies no current component. No repair result or unit-return record is given, and a readable receipt establishes no responsibility or warranty conclusion.

## Three common errors

First, connector-only inspection may summarize into complete repair; inspect the actual request for examination scope and document stage. Second, returned empty packaging may become a returned main unit; compare return object and device identity, checking whether history lost the packaging qualifier. Third, output may keep received while omitting the absent adapter; inspect component coverage and reply length, distinguishing unit and accessory states. These concern inspection scope, returned object and component expression. Additional reply space creates no repair finding or missing operational evidence for any of those claims.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what was received, which part was inspected and what was returned, comparing actual input and answer sentence by sentence. Under token limits, inspect whether each qualifier remains with its excerpt; receiving three documents establishes no complete-set evidence.
