# Brand concession operations: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Preserve counter identity, participation scope and material-receipt objects in mall-event comparisons. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed mall-counter packet limits an invitation to display participation without on-site sales, has the current counter reporting materials sent and gives another counter a confirmed sales arrangement. The question concerns the current counter’s permission. Actual input needs the exclusion and other-counter identity rather than expanding participation into every activity. Materials sent establishes no sales arrangement. Long dialogue may continue the other counter’s confirmation under the wrong subject. These are hypothetical letters, without real mall rules; the test checks their stated actions and subjects rather than asserting actual commercial approval.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Context-material check | Input and expression conditions | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event exclusion | No on-site sales | Inspect request retention. |
| Counter identity | Current and other counter | Do not merge through history. |
| Material action | Sent only | Do not make it sales permission. |
| Response scope | Display and sales separate | Allow qualifier expression. |
| Confirmation gap | Current counter unresolved | Keep pending without inferring refusal. |

## Three common errors

First, citation trimming may drop the sales exclusion; compare the invitation with actual request and inspect delivery of the condition. Second, history summarization may turn the other counter into the present one; inspect counter, event and reference, comparing the same question with empty history. Third, the answer may use materials sent as sales confirmation; trace action object and evidence while allowing space to distinguish delivery from arrangements. These concern exclusion, counter attribution and conclusion support. More background must not dilute a limitation that remains applicable in the supplied invitation.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what participation covers, what the reply establishes and which counter has which confirmation, comparing delivered text with output. Token-budget checks need exclusion wording and counter labels, rather than event-name relevance alone. Inspect actual model context separately; reply length decides no invitation applicability. If current-counter sales evidence is absent, retain the gap without inferring a refused request. This exercise submits no event participation and conducts no sale. Delivery of materials also establishes no real counter approval beyond the evidence actually supplied for the proposed comparison and its stated scope.
