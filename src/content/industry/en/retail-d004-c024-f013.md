# Brand concession operations: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Authorize mall contracts, activity notices and settlement sources separately, testing counter, brand and mall readers against actual resources. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison can propose concession terms, a mall activity notice, receipt wording and a prepared monthly statement without fee values. The brand reviews terms, the counter checks its records, and mall collaborators read permitted sources. Inspect actual accounts and operations; a mall contact does not automatically join the brand team. Central cashier records and internal brand sources may update differently. A notice establishes no executed activity, while textual differences establish neither valid charges, reconciled accounts nor breach through successful access.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Counter comparison access | Mall and brand sources | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member scope | Brand, counter and mall collaboration | Actual accounts and operations |
| Period and subject | Separate contract, notice, receipts and statement | Counter passages and dates |
| Restricted counter | Other sales and internal fee comments | Full return and denial |
| Cashier distinction | Mall POS supplies no brand rights | Authentication and resource reading |
| Settlement confirmation | Draft does not prove mutual agreement | Current records and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a counter manager or mall contact becomes a universal brand-contract maintainer, inspect account, counter mapping and operations. Duties grant no team role; the owner confirms missing correspondence.

- If this comparison exposes other-counter sales or internal fee comments, retain counter identity and full returns. Sharing one mall authorizes no cross-counter access, including summaries.

- If mall POS authentication proves brand-statement access or mutual acceptance, inspect external permissions, internal resources and current confirmation separately. A prepared draft supplies no formal settlement basis.

## How to verify the setup

Query the same sources as brand, counter, mall collaborator and unrelated-counter identities, retaining passages, operations and denials. Correct grants with another period’s receipts need input and source-ownership checks rather than broader finance access. Separate absent monthly confirmation from obtained confirmation omitted from this request, then repeat within the original scope. Comparison joins no activity, adjusts no fees and settles no account. Handling needs applicable terms, records and owner confirmation; visible mall cashier data establishes no completed brand-account synchronization.
