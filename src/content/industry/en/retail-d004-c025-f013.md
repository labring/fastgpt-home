# Brand outlet operations: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

The discount-approval attachment labels parties as brand owner and operator. Those statements can be checked as text, but they neither replace the full transaction documents nor grant the current account access to every related source. List the approval and gross-profit resources separately and inspect each member’s reading scope. Outlet store, clearance team and finance business partner describe business roles rather than configured grants. Retain which original passage the actual account can see.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Permission or implementation check | Outlet comparison resource | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party labels | Brand owner and operator are attachment text | Not a grant or complete contract |
| Independent resources | Inspect approval and calculation rights separately | Original scope visible to the actual account |
| Measure difference | Check product and period separately | Permission success does not choose a discount basis |
| Approval scope | A title does not define every business power | Obtain evidence from the responsible owner |
| Denied-access verification | Test the same source with authorized and denied identities | Record actual reads and denials |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |

## Three common errors

- If brand, operator or finance labels become existing grants, inspect accounts, approval and calculation resources. Titles supply neither team roles nor complete business authority.

- If a permitted identity lacks an original or a denied identity reads it, retain actual results and configuration separately. Labels or relevance cannot reconstruct the missing calculation.

- If readable tables become universal approval authority or an adopted discount, inspect responsibility, period and product. External ERP authentication remains separate; access cannot choose a business measure.

## How to verify the setup

An approver title without a stated approval-authority scope needs evidence from the responsible owner. Reading the form in an application does not establish that the named person has every approval power. External-system sources also require separate interface authorization. After obtaining the scope, test resource reading with identities intended to be authorized and denied, recording actual visibility. One successful read cannot confirm both business-approval responsibilities and resource-access boundaries.
