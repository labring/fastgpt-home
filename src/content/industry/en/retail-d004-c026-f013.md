# Brand-owned store operations: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access to store sales, staffing and display sources; brand employment establishes no universal access across stores. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes staffing text, display-image descriptions and sales summaries. Managers inspect their store, regional readers inspect permitted stores, and head office reads applicable internal rules. List actual accounts and operations. Direct brand employment opens no universal store access and creates no role from a job title. Staffing, images and sales update at different intervals. A store heading verifies no site, planned staffing proves no attendance, and readable sources cannot jointly establish performance or fulfilled obligations beyond their actual evidence.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Owned-store comparison access | Sources and duties | Inspection object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member scope | Manager, region and head-office tasks | Account, store and operations |
| Update differences | Separate staffing, images and sales | Actual source and date |
| Other-store restriction | Staffing and performance comments | Full answer and denial |
| System distinction | POS, CRM and internal sources | Separate identity and reading checks |
| Execution evidence | Plans and images supply no attendance | Shift records and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a regional manager becomes a universal store maintainer, inspect the account, store scope and operations. Duties differ from team grants; the owner confirms missing mapping.

- If this comparison returns other-store staffing or internal performance comments, retain source identity and full answers. Direct ownership under one brand authorizes no cross-store disclosure.

- If POS or CRM login establishes internal-image access and attendance, inspect external identity, resource reading and actual shift records separately. A schedule supplies no execution evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Query the same restricted sources as store manager, regional, head-office and unrelated-store identities, recording passages, operations and denials. Correct grants with another store’s display need actual-source and input mapping checked rather than wider image access. Separate unobtained site descriptions from obtained passages omitted from the request, then repeat within original permissions. Comparison changes no schedule, records no attendance and adjusts no performance. Sales, attendance and site conditions need corresponding business records and owner confirmation rather than visible passages establishing executed store work.
