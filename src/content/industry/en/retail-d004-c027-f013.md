# Dealer ordering events: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access around event handbooks, samples and dealer terms; reading display sources grants no order or credit approval. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes event-handbook applicability, a sample list and a dealer appendix without commercial amounts. Brand staff read this event’s rules, dealers read their terms, and support reads permitted explanations. Confirm actual accounts and operations. Displayed samples differ from accepted orders, and an agreement naming goods establishes no universal dealer eligibility. Readable handbooks support their contents rather than turning commercial contact or attendance into team membership, internal-credit access or order-maintenance rights without confirmed authority for the actual resources.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Ordering-event access | Sources and parties | Actual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personnel mapping | Brand, dealer and support tasks | Accounts and operations |
| Event scope | Separate handbook, samples and agreement | Applicability and version |
| Other-customer restriction | Agreements and internal credit | Full answer and denial |
| External platform | Login grants no internal-contract rights | Separate identity and resources |
| Business acceptance | Samples are not accepted orders | Actual records and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If an attendee contact becomes a universal dealer-agreement maintainer, inspect account, party and operations. Invitations create no team role; the owner confirms necessary scope.

- If this comparison returns another customer’s agreement or internal credit comments, retain party passages and full answers. Shared events or goods authorize no mutual access.

- If ordering-platform login establishes internal-contract access or accepted orders, inspect external identity, FastGPT resources and actual acceptance evidence separately. Samples and handbooks execute no orders.

## How to verify the setup

Request the same restricted sources as brand, dealer, support and unrelated-attendee identities, retaining passages, operations and denials. Correct grants with another event’s handbook need actual-input event and version checks rather than broader dealer access. Separate unobtained formal orders from obtained originals omitted from this request, then retest the same identities after supplementation. Comparison accepts no order, confirms no credit and submits no contract. Outcomes need actual records and owner confirmation; complete samples or readable agreements establish no completed sale.
