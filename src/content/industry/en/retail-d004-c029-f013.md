# After-sales repairs and reships: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Authorize reshipment requests, parts dispatch and carrier sources separately, checking actual scopes for service, warehouse and repair readers. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes a reshipment request, parts-dispatch note and carrier handoff wording. Service checks the request, warehouse readers identify parts, and repair staff inspect related observations, without actual addresses. Identify order, item and operations before mapping each original to accounts. A named contact grants nothing. Dispatch still needs evidence before carrier acceptance, and repair observations establish no breach. Reading the sources supports their wording rather than settled responsibility or disclosure of customer information to every collaborator.

## Three common errors

- If a reshipment contact becomes a member editing every warehouse and service order, inspect account, resources and operations. The owner confirms scope; request wording creates no team grant.

- If this request exposes another customer’s address or internal repair dispute, retain source identity and full answers. Matching parts authorize no cross-customer access, including paraphrases.

- If carrier connectivity proves internal-dispatch access or completed reshipment, inspect authentication, source reading and this reception separately. Dispatch and transport stages cannot substitute for one another.

## How to verify the setup

Test the same restricted sources as service, warehouse, repair and unrelated-customer identities, retaining passages and denials. Correct grants with another item’s inspection need input-subject and source checks rather than a wider order library. Separate obtained carrier material omitted from the request from absent current handoff evidence, then retest original accounts. Reading approves no reshipment, completes no repair and confirms no customer receipt. Actions and responsibility need records and confirmation; an existing reshipment cannot become delivered goods or a resolved dispute.
