# Supermarket supplier accounts: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect contract, inspection and reconciliation access, tracing terms, arrival acknowledgment and passed acceptance separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes purchasing terms, arrival acknowledgment, an acceptance opinion and a prepared reconciliation without amounts. Suppliers read their terms, stores inspect this batch, and finance checks current sources. Confirm accounts and operations. Terms describe expected supply rather than actual arrival, and an arrival signature does not automatically establish passed acceptance. Even sources sharing supplier and goods need period and origin relationships. Reading establishes neither supplier admission, agreed settlement nor responsibility beyond the actual evidence for each separate stage.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Retailer-settlement access | Stage-specific source | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party mapping | Supplier, store and finance tasks | Accounts and resource operations |
| Acceptance distinction | Terms, arrival and passed inspection | Batch passages and dates |
| Restricted sources | Other terms and internal comments | Full answer and denial |
| Portal distinction | Login grants no internal inspection rights | Separate identity and resources |
| Settlement agreement | Draft supplies no mutual confirmation | Current actual records |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a supplier contact becomes a universal contract or finance maintainer, inspect account and party mapping with resource operations. Commercial relationships configure no team rights.

- If this comparison returns another supplier’s agreement or internal settlement comments, retain party passages and full answers. Shared retailers or goods authorize no cross-customer access.

- If portal login establishes internal-inspection access or confirmed settlement, inspect external identity, resource permissions and current confirmation separately. Arrival signatures and drafts prove no acceptance or mutual agreement.

## How to verify the setup

Request the same restricted sources as supplier, store, finance and unrelated-supplier identities, retaining passages, versions and denials. Correct grants with another batch’s acceptance need actual-input subjects and dates checked rather than broader records. Separate unobtained acceptance conclusions from obtained conclusions omitted from input, then repeat within original scope. Comparison approves no admission, inspection or funds. Those actions need actual authority and records; complete sources or normal responses establish neither passed goods, balanced accounts nor breach.
