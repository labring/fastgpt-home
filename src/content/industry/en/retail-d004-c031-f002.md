# Retail packaging reporting: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Keep material categories, procurement-versus-use and reporting periods separate in supplies reports. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A proposed supplies-report packet registers plastic and paper bags separately, records paper bags used for display and leaves current-period use material pending in a draft. History contains a previous-period plastic-bag purchasing summary. The question concerns verified current plastic-bag use. Actual input needs material, purchase-versus-use action and period retained. The word bag cannot merge categories, and a display paper bag establishes no plastic consumption. This exercise introduces no real reporting rule, filing requirement or penalty figure; it checks the scopes and relationships of ordinary hypothetical records rather than asserting a regulatory conclusion.

## Three common errors

First, input compression may merge paper and plastic bags; compare procurement categories with actual request and restore material identity. Second, previous-period purchasing may migrate into current use; inspect period, action and original dialogue, including the same question with empty history. Third, output may announce verified totals while dropping pending use material; inspect conclusion coverage and reply length so the gap remains visible. These concern classification, period-and-action attribution and source-gap expression. A larger window performs no unrecorded counting and creates no current usage evidence absent from the proposed packet.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which material, which action and which period each sentence concerns, comparing actual input and output. Reference budgeting needs categories and pending status retained, rather than treating document count as complete statistics. Inspect the real model window separately from response length. Missing current-use records remain missing after budget changes. Added hypothetical records must match period and category and remain labelled practice. This page calculates no actual usage, submits no report and makes no compliance judgment. A procurement summary also establishes no completed real report without its own applicable source and execution evidence.
