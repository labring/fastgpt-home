# Retail packaging reporting: document comparison with access controls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect access to supply procurement, sales and report sources, verifying data status and filing stages separately without regulatory conclusions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A comparison exercise proposes a procurement ledger, sales definition, prepared report and use commitment, without actual operating figures or regulatory requirements. Purchasing reads item origins, stores inspect local records, and report staff read permitted sources. Confirm accounts and operations. Procurement, inbound stock and sales have different definitions, while commitments supply no actual-use outcome. Readable material establishes obtained wording rather than submitted or accepted reports, satisfied requirements or combined evidence across stores and periods sharing a supply name.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supply-report access | Source status | Inspection evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personnel mapping | Purchasing, store and report tasks | Account scope and operations |
| Data meaning | Separate procurement, inbound and sales | Definitions and period passages |
| Store restriction | Operating statistics and comments | Full answer and denial |
| Filing identity | External filing grants no internal reads | Authentication and resource rights |
| Report outcome | Drafts and commitments prove no acceptance | Actual receipt and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a report owner becomes a universal store-ledger maintainer, inspect account, store, period and resource operations. Duties grant no team role automatically.

- If this comparison exposes another store’s statistics or internal report comments, retain source scope and full answers. Supplies with a shared purpose authorize no cross-store access.

- If external filing login establishes internal-ledger access or accepted reports, inspect authentication, resource permissions and current processing receipts separately. Drafts and commitments supply no actual outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Request the same restricted sources as purchasing, store, report and unrelated-store identities, recording passages, definitions and denials. Correct grants with inbound stock described as sales need actual-input and meaning checks rather than broader resources. Separate unobtained sales definitions from obtained sources omitted from the request, then retest original accounts. Reading generates no actual statistics, submits no report and establishes no compliance. Handling needs applicable evidence, execution records and owner confirmation rather than complete material or successful requests establishing those outcomes.
