# Retail checkout and loyalty: context limits for document comparison

## What the documents look like

Retail checkout and loyalty material comparison in this category depends on four document types: promotion price tags, membership points rules, checkout daily reports, and anomalous refund slips. These materials originate from the POS system, membership CRM, payment platform, and price tag system, with daily or per-shift update frequency. Store checkout supervisors, CRM operations staff, and finance auditors need to reconcile these materials within the same comparison task, confirming whether promotion prices, points entitlements, and refund records remain consistent. The subject, object, status, and effective scope recorded in the materials form the basis for judgment. A promotion price tag states the applicable store and effective period. A membership points rule states the points calculation method and applicable membership tier. A checkout daily report states the shift transaction summary. An anomalous refund slip states the refund reason and authorization status. These fields are distributed across different documents, and comparison requires confirming the value relationship of the same subject, same period, and same field item by item.

In the context and token step, the relevant constraints involve the content length the model can reference, the knowledge base citation limit, the historical input range, and structured output handling. The model maximum context is checked against actual model capability, and the reply limit controls single-response length; these are distinct concepts. The knowledge base citation limit uses tokens as its unit, with each search citing at most n tokens of content. A long single passage may be truncated, and the actual cited content needs to be checked against the question sample. Historical input affects retrieval behavior in continuous conversation, and the same question may produce different input under empty history and with history. Structured output requires verifying field completeness and successful parsing.

Hypothetical drill sample: A store promotion price tag states in its original text: "From June 1 to June 30, 2025, Class A members purchasing designated cooking oil enjoy a 20% discount, limited to 2 barrels per card." The membership points rule states in its original text: "Class A member consumption points accrue at 1 point per 1 yuan actually paid; promotional items do not participate in points." The checkout daily report states in its original text: "June 15 shift, Class A member Zhang purchased 2 barrels of designated cooking oil, actual payment 96 yuan, points increase 0 yuan." The anomalous refund slip states in its original text: "June 15, Zhang applied to refund 1 barrel of cooking oil, refund amount 24 yuan, refund reason: duplicate purchase, authorization status is pending review." Draft sentence to be judged: "Zhang purchased cooking oil on June 15 and received 96 points; after the refund the points have been deducted." This draft sentence is inconsistent with the points rule original text "promotional items do not participate in points" and the checkout daily report "points increase 0 yuan." The refund slip authorization status is pending review, which cannot be treated as a completed refund or points deduction.

## Constraints at the context limits step

The model maximum context and the reply limit serve different functions. The maximum context governs the content length a single request can reference. The reply limit governs the length of a single answer. Setting a reply limit does not expand the referenceable content, and a large maximum context does not guarantee the reply will include every field.

The knowledge base citation limit is measured in tokens. When a single passage of material is long, the cited content may be truncated, and the comparison conclusion may lack the field needed for judgment. The actual cited content needs to be checked against the question sample rather than assumed to cover all four documents.

Historical input affects retrieval scope. The same question under empty history and with history may produce different retrieval input, and the cited content may differ accordingly. Verification needs to compare the actual cited content under both conditions rather than assume the first hit or full recall.

Structured output handling requires verifying field completeness and successful parsing. A fixed reply format such as json_schema needs its output fields checked against the comparison conclusion requirements, and parsing success needs to be observed rather than inferred from text length.

The minimum relevance score is a filter threshold that takes effect only under semantic retrieval or result reranking. It does not perform permission or entity isolation and does not exclude unrelated stores or unauthorized records. Retrieval is not authorization.

Thinking budget is used for the model thinking process and does not serve as the citation token limit.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the reply limit as the full context window. The reply limit controls single-response length, while the maximum context controls the content length a single request can reference; these are different. Check method: Input the four materials in full, compare the content the model can actually reference against the reply limit value, and observe whether the reply is truncated due to the limit.

2. Treating the minimum relevance score as permission or entity filtering. The minimum relevance score is a filter threshold effective only under semantic retrieval or result reranking, and it does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records. Check method: Input a query containing an unrelated store, observe whether returned results are filtered only due to low relevance, and confirm no permission filtering logic is present.

3. Treating a pending-review refund slip as a completed refund. The refund slip authorization status is pending review, and it cannot be inferred that the refund has been executed or points have been deducted. Check method: Input the refund slip original text and the draft sentence, compare the authorization status field against the draft sentence wording, and observe whether the draft sentence writes pending review as completed.
