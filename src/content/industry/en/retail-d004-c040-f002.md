# Customer-service knowledge: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Customer-service knowledge, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect the recording duty and the party deciding service acceptance in the actual comparison input. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A hypothetical current support agreement makes the store responsible for forwarding repair requests while a designated service provider replies separately about acceptance. A knowledge note offers help recording a request. Conversation history concerns another store whose inspection was arranged. Before comparing the current documents, inspect whether the complete clause, store identity and provider identity reach the request. A quotation retaining only forwarding omits the decision boundary. Recording, forwarding and accepting service are separate actions. Help recording a request establishes no repair commitment by the store, and another store’s arrangement does not belong to this matter.

## Three common errors

- If the provider’s separate decision is clipped from the input, compare the original clause, actual citation and request to locate the missing party. A complete answer does not establish complete source delivery.

- If a follow-up about this store borrows another store’s arrangement, hold the current clause fixed across empty and populated histories and inspect store and matter references.

- If a parseable responsibility table assigns repair acceptance to the recording party, trace each subject to the clause. A valid structure does not establish correct role mapping.

## How to verify the setup

Ask who records, who forwards and who still decides acceptance, tracing answer sentences to actual input. When the provider’s reply arrives, check store and matter identity before identifying the duty or stage newly supported. Inspect citation tokens separately from reply length, using the actual model configuration and accepted input scope for context capability. Shorter output or a larger window supplies no absent service clause. These are hypothetical role texts without an actual service arrangement.
