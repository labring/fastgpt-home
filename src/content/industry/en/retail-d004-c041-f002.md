# Marketplace promotions: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Marketplace promotions, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Preserve permission to submit creative separately from fee and activation terms without borrowing a prior event’s authorization. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A hypothetical event clause permits submission of creative for this occasion while fees and activation timing require a separate confirmation letter. A note records prepared material only. History includes an activation confirmation for a previous event, without a letter for this one. Input retaining only permission to submit may expand a limited permission into settled fees and execution timing. Inspect whether event identity, permitted action and the separate-letter prerequisite remain together in the actual request. Preparing creative establishes neither submission nor a running event. No real fee figure or payment arrangement is provided.

## Three common errors

- If the separate confirmation disappears from input while the comparison declares settled fees and timing, compare the full clause with material actually delivered to locate the clipped prerequisite.

- If a follow-up borrows the previous event’s letter, hold current sources fixed across history conditions and inspect event, version and applicable period.

- If prepared creative becomes execution evidence in a table, inspect the recorded action and actual execution material. Parseable fields create no new event.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what submission is permitted, where the fee basis is stated and which material confirms activation. Trace each answer to actual input. When a current letter arrives, inspect event, covered matter and period before repeating the questions. Later receipt does not establish later activation. Inspect citation-token budgets separately from reply limits and the actual model’s accepted context scope. Verify that necessary prerequisites arrived and were used. Longer answers or a responsive workflow alone do not establish that result.
