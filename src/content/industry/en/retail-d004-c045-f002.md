# Seller ratings management: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Seller ratings management, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Compare reply drafting, publication approval and internal notes while retaining store and review identity. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

A hypothetical review-maintenance commission asks a contractor to organize disputed points and draft a reply, with the store confirming each public reply. An internal note proposes possible causes; no publication confirmation is supplied. Earlier conversation concerns another delivery review to which the store replied. Keeping only the drafting language can discard the approval prerequisite. A suggested cause in an internal note cannot become a factual statement to a customer merely through comparison. The current review and its commission attachments define the task. Missing evidence leaves statements awaiting confirmation, without establishing fault, withdrawal or publication.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| --- | --- | --- |
| Drafting task | Organize disputes and draft replies | Retain individual publication approval. |
| Public approval | Current confirmation absent | Keep the prerequisite in input. |
| Earlier review | A different delivery review | Separate status by review identity. |
| Internal note | Possible causes only | Preserve tentative wording. |
| Added confirmation | Specific wording and version | Repeat the question to check permission. |

## Three common errors

The first error loses individual confirmation from the request. Compare the complete commission, citation and actual input to inspect an expansion from drafting into permission to publish. The second imports another review’s handling status. Label review identities and compare the same question with empty and populated histories to locate the borrowed status. The third places a tentative internal explanation in a public-reply field. Trace each sentence to its original uncertainty and supporting evidence after parsing. Authorization scope, review identity and factual support require different inspections; one record cannot settle all three.

## How to verify the setup

Ask who drafts, who confirms and which current review has a confirmation record. Trace the delivered clause and answer citations. When confirmation arrives, inspect the approved wording and version rather than merely a signature image. Use the citation-token budget to check retention of prerequisites, separately recording reply length and the actual model context scope. Shorter replies do not supply publication permission. A larger window does not make another review’s history applicable here. These are invented documents without actual publication, review withdrawal or experience-score changes.
