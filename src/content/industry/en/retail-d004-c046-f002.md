# Marketplace category access: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Marketplace category access, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect document inventories, entity scope and supplementation notices without turning receipt into an admission decision. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented category-document commission requires a directory for the current business entity, with missing items supplemented by the material provider and platform review performed separately. Its attachment lists received filenames without a review result. History includes a supplementation notice for another entity. A similarly named current document still needs inspection of its contents. A receipt inventory cannot establish entity applicability, validity period or review status from a filename alone. Clipping the separate-review sentence at the end of a long attachment may turn an organization task into an admission commitment. This exercise defines no actual platform admission rule.

## Three common errors

The first error omits the separate-review sentence from a citation. Check the full page ending, citation position and actual request for an expansion of the contractor’s authority. The second imports an older notice addressed to another entity. Compare entity names and notice subjects, then input with and without history; matching filenames do not transfer status. The third fills a proof field from a received-file label. Read the relevant entity, period and current review basis inside the document after parsing. These inspections distinguish assigned scope, notice identity and evidential contents.

## How to verify the setup

List what arrived for the current entity, what requires supplementation and which material supports review status. When a file arrives, inspect its entity, period and purpose before repeating the questions. Upload time does not determine validity time. Inspect delivery of restrictions against citation-token budgets, separately from model context and reply limits, without assuming numeric settings. Preserve receipt inventories, document contents and review results as distinct stages. Missing result material leaves status unconfirmed; it establishes neither a formal rejection nor admission.
