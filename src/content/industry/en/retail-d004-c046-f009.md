# Marketplace category access: document comparison with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Marketplace category access, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Entity documents, trademarks and authorization chains concern different parties and scopes. Reading them does not independently determine marketplace-category admission or legal sufficiency. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A category-source comparison can inspect relationships among entity, trademark and authorization passages without platform rules or legal conclusions. Proposed input lacks current platform acceptance. Limit a tool to supplied passages, retaining party, item scope and version. Complete titles establish neither a complete chain nor item permission from business scope. Compare required input with actual values; descriptions guarantee neither execution nor admission.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Transaction-comparison check | Proposed material | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party roles | Authorizer and document holder | Original roles not merged |
| Applicable scope | Item permission separate from category | No automatic expansion |
| Submission stage | Submitted without current acceptance | Obtain processing response |
| Tool input | Party, version and actual passage | Request retains origins |
| Formal admission | Separate submission, update and access | Reading is not institutional judgment |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Tool description` | Describe purpose, inputs and non-applicable situations. |
| `Tool parameters` | Inspect module and plugin parameters separately. |
| `Required parameters` | The model may not invoke a tool without suitable required inputs. |
| `Tool connections` | Available tools must connect to the calling module. |
| `Tool-call termination` | Termination ends the call without a later AI summary. |

## Three common errors

Confusing the authorizing party with a document holder needs original-role checks. Single-item permission expanded across categories needs applicability evidence. Submitted material described as admitted needs this processing response. These concern party, scope and stage rather than titles or selected values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain source identity, actual request and cited return. Repeat after corresponding evidence arrives and trace differences. Missing suitable required inputs may prevent invocation; collect them through interaction and inspect requests, starting with a small tool set. Termination prevents another AI summary. Submission, credential updates and permission operations need business authority and receipts. Comparison establishes neither legal sufficiency, platform admission nor accepted products.
