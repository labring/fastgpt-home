# Parcel labels and handoffs: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Parcel labels and handoffs, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Preserve the separate purposes of a pickup list, physical handover and exception records without treating a label as pickup. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented pickup supplement uses a label list for preliminary scheduling, counts physical parcels against a bilateral handover page and lists exceptions separately. Only a created-label list and a driver-arrival message are attached. No handover page is supplied. History concerns another batch handed over yesterday. Inspect whether the latter half of the supplement and its exception scope enter the context. Label creation, driver arrival and physical transfer need different evidence. A list row count does not determine transferred parcels. This exercise supplies no actual quantity and determines no transport liability or signature effect.

## Three common errors

The first error drops the bilateral-handover condition from a citation. Compare supplement paragraphs and actual input for promotion of a scheduling list into transfer evidence. The second imports yesterday’s batch status into today’s follow-up. Hold current batch identity and list fixed across histories, tracing arrival, collection and handover separately. The third merges separately listed exceptions into normal handover rows. Compare the exception record, linked list items and output groups after parsing. These checks locate a missing prerequisite, wrong batch and lost exception identity rather than trusting an orderly total table.

## How to verify the setup

Identify evidence for label creation, arrival and physical handover for this batch. When a handover page arrives, inspect the parties’ recorded objects and exception links before repeating the questions. Supplement receipt time does not replace event time. Inspect clause delivery against citation budgets separately from reply length and model context scope. Confirm actual request contents instead of assuming a displayed allowance means complete delivery. Without the handover page, physical transfer remains unknown; missing evidence does not establish that transfer never occurred.
