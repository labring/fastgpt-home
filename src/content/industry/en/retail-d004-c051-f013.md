# Douyin storefront operations: access controls for document comparison

## What the documents look like

Douyin storefront operations revolve around product cards and store experience score, independent of any single live session. The working materials for this category include product qualification pages, experience score details, shipping violation appeals, and store announcements. Their update rhythm is daily and per order. Contract and transaction material comparison in this category places qualification pages, appeal records, and announcement content into one review scope, confirming subject, object, status, and effective range item by item.

These materials sit across the Douyin store workbench, the Douyin e-commerce learning center, Selected Alliance data, and logistics interfaces. The central question for access controls and data isolation is who may read and operate which materials within which scope. A retrieval tool itself does not perform authorization.

## Constraints at the access controls step

FastGPT configures permissions across four resource levels: team, app, dataset, and collection. Dataset permission is always the threshold; collection permission cannot bypass dataset permission. A team administrator does not bypass collection-level permission, and datasets and collections remain governed by their own collaborator configuration. Retrieval recall filters by the files the current member may read, and unauthorized files are not recalled. That filtering takes effect only when authentication runs under the current member identity.

### Hypothetical paper exercise sample

Material text one (product qualification page excerpt): "This store submitted on 1 March 2025 the product qualification page for a certain brand skincare essence, valid until 28 February 2026, with the qualification subject being a certain trading company in Hangzhou."

Material text two (shipping violation appeal excerpt): "This store filed an appeal on 10 April 2025 regarding the delayed shipping violation for order number ending 7732. The appeal status is pending review, and the appeal materials include the pickup time record returned by the logistics interface."

Material text three (store announcement excerpt): "This store adjusts the shipping timeliness statement from 1 May 2025, applicable to orders created on or after 1 May 2025."

Draft sentence to be assessed: "The skincare essence qualification page above is valid from 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2026 and can support the continued listing of this product in the shelf scenario; the shipping violation appeal has been submitted, and the delayed shipping violation has been cleared."

This draft sentence contains two points requiring review. The qualification page text states only the validity start and end and the qualification subject; it does not state any rule linking that qualification to continued shelf listing. The draft treats validity as equivalent to a listing support condition, which is unsupported. The appeal text states the status as pending review. Pending review does not establish that the violation has been cleared. The draft treats pending review as equivalent to violation clearance, which is a status misjudgment.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Relation to this page's documents | Check method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dataset manage permission | Collaborator configuration of the dataset holding qualification pages, appeals, and announcements | Confirm whether the target member holds dataset manage permission |
| Collection-level permission switch | Whether an individual qualification page or appeal file has independent collaborator configuration | Inspect the permission entry state in the dataset detail page file list |
| Collection read permission | Whether a member can view a qualification page or appeal text | Open the same file with an account holding read permission and one without |
| Collection write permission | Whether a member can modify qualification page or appeal material content | Attempt an edit on the file detail page and record the actual result |
| Retrieval recall filtering | Whether unauthorized files are recalled when authentication runs under the current member identity | Run a retrieval with an account lacking read permission and record the files actually recalled |
| Collaborator full overwrite | Whether the submitted collaborator list contains the current creator | Compare the submitted list against the creator record |

Hypothetical starting point for this page: use dataset manage permission as the starting permission for this review. The reason for choosing it is that contract and transaction material comparison must cover qualification, appeal, and announcement files, and manage permission is the prerequisite for configuring collection-level permission. Compare against the visible content of an account with no permission.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval relevance as permission filtering. A relevance or similarity setting in retrieval does not perform permission or entity isolation, and a file without read permission should not be recalled because of a retrieval setting. The erroneous practice is assuming that raising relevance excludes unrelated or unauthorized files.

2. Treating a pending-review appeal as a cleared violation. The appeal text states the status as pending review; pending review does not establish a lack of operation permission, nor does it establish that the violation has been cleared. The erroneous practice is writing "the violation has been cleared" directly in a draft sentence.

3. Treating qualification validity as a listing support condition. The qualification page text states only the validity start and end and the qualification subject; it does not state any rule linking them to continued shelf listing. The erroneous practice is equating validity directly with support for continued listing.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the product qualification page text and the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample. Compare the draft sentence "can support the continued listing of this product in the shelf scenario" against the validity start and end and the qualification subject stated in the source. The source states no rule linking the qualification to listing. That part of the draft is unsupported. Satisfied condition: the draft retains only the validity and subject information stated in the source and removes the unsupported listing support conclusion.

2. Input the shipping violation appeal text and the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample. Compare the draft sentence "the delayed shipping violation has been cleared" against the source statement "the appeal status is pending review." Pending review is not violation clearance. Satisfied condition: the draft reads "the appeal has been submitted, with status pending review" and does not state that the violation has been cleared.

3. Input the store announcement text and the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample. Compare whether the draft extends the announcement's applicable scope to orders created before 1 May 2025. The announcement text states applicability to orders created on or after 1 May 2025. Satisfied condition: the draft explicitly marks the applicable scope as orders created on or after 1 May 2025 and does not extend it to earlier orders.
