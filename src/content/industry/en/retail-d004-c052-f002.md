# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Pinduoduo seller campaigns, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect pending application, price-note scope and execution prerequisites without importing a prior activity round. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented activity-preparation agreement organizes proposed products, makes prices subject to a current-round confirmation page and schedules creative after a selection notice. Only a candidate list and internal price-discussion notes are supplied. Current confirmation and selection notice are absent; history records selection in an earlier round. This task calculates no actual price or discount and defines no platform eligibility rule. A candidate row records proposed scope only. A price-discussion field does not become a public price simply because it reaches the model. The current-round identity and notice prerequisite must remain in the long clause.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Activity-preparation context | Material scope | Corresponding check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate list | Proposed products only | Does not establish selection. |
| Price note | Internal discussion | Seek current-round confirmation. |
| Scheduling prerequisite | After a selection notice | Retain the condition in input. |
| Historical record | Selected in a previous round | Check current round and subject. |
| Added notice | Covered products and stage | Do not automatically settle price. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Model context` | Use the actual model’s accepted context scope. |
| `Reply length` | The reply limit differs from the context window. |
| `Knowledge-reference limit` | Knowledge citation limits are token-based. |
| `History-input check` | Compare empty-history and populated-history inputs. |
| `Structured-output handoff` | Validate parsing, then inspect support for field values. |

## Three common errors

The first error drops after receipt of notice from input. Compare the full preparation agreement and actual citation to locate scheduling described as already authorized. The second imports an earlier round’s selection into a current follow-up. Hold the candidate list fixed across history conditions and inspect round and notice subjects. The third fills a confirmed-price field from an internal note. Trace the note’s purpose, current confirmation page and output field after parsing. Missing conditions, wrong rounds and price-document levels need different evidence; additional candidate rows cannot establish selection.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what is proposed, which page supports prices and which document supports subsequent scheduling, expanding actual input. A current notice updates covered products and stages only; prices still need their corresponding confirmation. Supplementation does not settle every pending item at once. Inspect citation-token budgets separately from reply length and actual model context configuration. Shorter answers do not prove retention of prerequisites. This exercise applies to no activity, sets no price and performs no campaign. An absent notice does not establish formal rejection.
