# Channel receivables: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Channel receivables, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Compare balance definitions, dispute notes and contact duties while separating account entries from payment evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented channel-reconciliation mandate permits inquiries about differences, leaves disputed items for separate review and says the summary does not confirm settlement. Attachments contain an account summary and an acknowledgement email without item-level agreement or receipt of funds. History concerns a different customer’s payment. This task computes no real balance, determines no debt and gives no collection-law advice. Acknowledgement records receipt of an email only. Summary entries must retain dispute qualifications instead of becoming bilateral findings. The actual context needs the disputed-item and settlement restrictions before answering about payment stages.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Reconciliation context | Invented purpose | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact scope | Inquiry about differences | Add no debt determination. |
| Dispute note | Separate item review | Retain footer and covered entries. |
| Acknowledgement | Receipt of communication | Does not establish settlement. |
| Historical payment | Another customer’s matter | Check current counterparty and ownership. |
| Added agreement | Confirmation of covered entries | Seek funds-receipt evidence separately. |

## Three common errors

The first error loses the separate-review footnote from a citation. Compare the summary footer, mandate and actual request for tentative entries promoted to agreed receivables. The second borrows another customer’s payment history. Hold the current counterparty fixed across history conditions and trace payment subject and matter identifiers. The third fills a settlement-proof field from an acknowledgement email. Read the communication’s action and corresponding funds-receipt evidence after parsing. Definition, counterparty and evidence purpose need separate inspections. Missing originals do not establish refusal to pay.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which difference may be discussed, which entries remain disputed and which record supports payment status. Inspect actual delivery item by item. Added agreement changes only the corresponding entry’s confirmation stage; funds receipt needs records for this entity and matter. Inspect retention of notes against citation-token budgets separately from reply limits and the model’s accepted context. A complete generated table does not establish complete input. This invented exercise contacts nobody, moves no funds and determines no settlement. Missing records do not establish that payment has not arrived.
