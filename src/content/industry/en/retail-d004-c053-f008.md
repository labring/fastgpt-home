# Channel receivables: document comparison with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Channel receivables, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Carry reconciliation terms, rebate review and document receipt separately without confirming payment or offset. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A hypothetical reconciliation term covers this season’s shipments, leaves rebate matters under separate review and includes an email acknowledging receipt of the list only. Check current material against contract scope and carry the rebate gap onward. Receipt establishes arrival of documents without balance agreement or completed payment. A settlement for an older period supplies no current outcome. Organize clauses and missing evidence separately while preserving period, matter and speaker instead of one closed state.

## Three common errors

Rebate review merged into an agreed balance needs the original term and confirmation scope inspected. An older outcome applied here needs period identity checked. List receipt promoted to funds received needs actual payment evidence. These concern matter, period and event type.

## How to verify the setup

Compare original clauses, node inputs and results item by item. Added rebate material updates its own matter. Reply length cannot replace source delivery. Actual reconciliation agreement, payment and offsets need separate authority and records; comparison describes relationships in the supplied text. Record current-season receivables, the rebate question and acknowledgment of the received list separately. For a later reply, identify exactly which item was acknowledged before considering any proposed conclusion about the balance due.
