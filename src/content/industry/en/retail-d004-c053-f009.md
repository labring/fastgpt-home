# Channel receivables: document comparison with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Channel receivables, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Aging descriptions, collection notices, credit terms and freeze notices need separate reading. Existing documents do not independently establish payment, shipment state or justified handling. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A receivables-source comparison can collect credit, collection and freeze passages without amounts, deadlines or handling requirements. Proposed inputs lack current bank and freeze-execution records. Limit a tool to supplied text with channel, contract and source time. An aging title provides no calculation definition, and collection wording is not counterparty agreement. Differences do not determine responsibility, credit or legal consequences.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Transaction-comparison check | Proposed material | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract subject | Other-channel source introduced | Inspect original relationship |
| Communication result | Notice sent without agreement | Obtain actual counterparty reply |
| Freeze stage | Notice separate from stopped dispatch | Obtain current operation record |
| Definition scope | Aging wording without calculation evidence | No guessed figures or responsibility |
| Formal handling | Separate confirmation, freeze and write | Comparison is not funds received |

## Three common errors

Another channel’s evidence needs contractual relationship checks. A sent notice described as agreed needs the actual reply. A proposed freeze described as stopped shipments needs this operation’s record. These concern subject, communication outcome and execution rather than results supplied by a tool return.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual requests, invocation and citations. Repeat with corresponding bank or business evidence, keeping interpretation traceable. Missing suitable required input may prevent calls; collect it through interaction and inspect requests with a small tool set. Termination leaves no later AI summary. Receipt confirmation, freezes and accounting writes need authority and receipts. Comparison establishes neither funds received, halted dispatch nor justified handling.
