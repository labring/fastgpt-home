# Channel diversion reviews: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Channel diversion reviews, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect source leads, territory restrictions and written confirmation without promoting a report summary into a contractual-breach finding. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented channel-check commission organizes sample-movement leads, makes territory applicability subject to written confirmation for this lot and excludes direct breach findings from leads alone. Attachments contain a reported purchase-location summary and package photograph without current territory confirmation or full movement records. History resolved the origin of another lot. Preserve the reported nature, lot identity and written-confirmation prerequisite in the actual context. Purchase location does not determine supply path, and similar packaging does not independently identify a channel. This exercise interprets no real distribution policy, contract effect or penalty rule.

## Three common errors

The first error clips the restriction against direct findings from context. Compare the full commission, actual citation and conclusion for promotion of a summary into a breach outcome. The second borrows another lot’s resolved origin. Hold the current photograph and lot fixed across history conditions and trace the subject of the origin finding. The third fills a supply-channel field from purchase location. Read the reported statement, movement records and current territory confirmation after parsing, separating a location lead from path evidence. Populated fields do not settle evidential status, lot identity and route correspondence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask who supplied the lead, which lot it concerns and which territory or matter the written confirmation covers. Inspect delivery of these restrictions. Added movement records support covered paths after product and lot checks; a breach finding still needs corresponding terms and evidence. More material alone does not establish one. Inspect citation tokens separately from reply length and accepted model context, without assuming a larger window retains the ending automatically. This exercise investigates no real channel, contacts no dealer and imposes no penalty. Absent movement records do not prove a route was unused.
