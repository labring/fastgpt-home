# Producer export tax processes: document comparison with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Producer export tax processes, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Export contract goods and the reporting period of declaration details require separate comparisons. A material-organizing workflow does not determine tax treatment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed export contract names an assembled component set, while a sales ledger lists individual parts. The declaration excerpt does not explain the grouping. Collect the contract passage, ledger names and relevant period as proposed inputs. A person can request correspondence evidence or inspect the period. The workflow passes text and choices without deciding that differently named goods belong to one shipment or that available declaration material establishes confirmed details. This exercise supplies neither tax eligibility nor calculations.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Export-material comparison | Proposed distinction | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract goods | Assembled component set | No automatic splitting or merging |
| Sales ledger | Separately listed parts | Evidence linking them to contract |
| Reporting period | Ledger period unresolved | Scope retained downstream |
| Declaration draft | Prepared wording only | Not a confirmed result |
| Follow-up route | Mapping or period inspection | Compare actual received passages |

## Three common errors

Forced name matching needs actual evidence linking the parts to the contracted set. A different period’s ledger routed into this comparison requires submitted-period and received-passage checks. A prepared declaration draft described as confirmed needs the corresponding handling evidence. These inspect goods relationships, material scope and result status independently of matching report titles.

## How to verify the setup

Connect correspondence collection and period review to their respective follow-up paths. Compare source wording, chosen destination and actual downstream input. Added mapping evidence can support the goods relationship while leaving the handling outcome unchanged. Keep an unclear period unresolved. External declaration, material amendment and result readback require actual authority and business records. Submission or completed branching establishes neither a filed declaration nor a particular tax outcome.
