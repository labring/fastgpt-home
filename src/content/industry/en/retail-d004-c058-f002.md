# Fresh-food supplier claims: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Fresh-food supplier claims, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Compare a supplier-objection notice, unresolved causes and quantity attachments before allowing a claim draft to exceed source support. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented supplier-claim agreement organizes objections, leaves cause and responsibility for separate checking against corresponding records from both parties and uses attachment quantities for comparison only. Only an arrival-objection notice and quantity excerpt are supplied, without agreed causes or a current-lot determination. History concerns an accepted objection for another lot. Raising an objection, checking quantity and determining cause or responsibility are separate steps. A quantity excerpt does not itself establish actual loss. Input must preserve separate checking and the attachment’s provisional purpose rather than portray an organized packet as supplier agreement. No claim amount or actual liability finding is supplied.

## Three common errors

The first error clips the quantity-comparison restriction from a citation. Compare the clause, full attachment and actual request for figures promoted into determined loss. The second borrows another lot’s accepted objection. Hold arrival and lot identifiers fixed across histories and trace the supplier reply’s subject. The third fills a settled-responsibility field from an unresolved cause. Read both parties’ records and the cause wording after parsing, distinguishing allegations from corresponding confirmations. Quantity purpose, lot reference and causal support require different evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what objection was raised, what the quantity attachment supports and which corresponding records are absent for cause or responsibility. Trace draft sentences to input. Added bilateral confirmation updates expressly covered lots and items only, leaving remaining matters unconfirmed. Inspect retention of attachment purpose against citation tokens separately from reply length and actual model context scope. Successful drafting does not establish a resolved dispute. This exercise sends no claim, determines no loss and moves no funds. Missing material does not establish the supplier’s refusal of responsibility.
