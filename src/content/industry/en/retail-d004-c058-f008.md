# Fresh-food supplier claims: document comparison with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Fresh-food supplier claims, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Fresh-produce claims need distinct anomaly wording, image identity and supplier acknowledgment. A deduction notice does not replace a mutual handling conclusion. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed anomaly note describes bruising on some leafy vegetables. Photographs show only the outer carton, and a claim-reconciliation draft awaits the supplier’s response. Collect these excerpts with delivery correspondence. A person may request product inspection or the response for this claim. A contractual requirement to submit anomaly material does not establish accepted compensation, and carton images do not describe every item inside. Preserve the pending response downstream instead of equating organized material with supplier agreement.

## Three common errors

Evidence overreach: A carton image used to describe all vegetables needs image-scope and corresponding inspection checks. Unilateral notice promoted to agreement needs the supplier’s actual response. Erased pending status: If the recipient receives only a claim conclusion, compare it with the submitted passage and restore what remains unresolved. These concern coverage, parties’ positions and transfer completeness.

## How to verify the setup

Connect inspection collection and response collection separately. Inspect the recorded choice, delivery subject and gaps received downstream. Before retesting an added reply, identify which anomaly it addresses. Acknowledgment of receiving a notice does not establish acceptance of the claim. Deductions, payment adjustments and dispute closure require actual business receipts in separately connected steps. A completed path establishes that workflow handling occurred, not that a commercial agreement was reached.
