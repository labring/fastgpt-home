# Store cold-chain receiving: document comparison with context limits

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT context limits. Inspect temperature records, pending arrival checks and receipt notes while retaining time and subject restrictions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the context limits step

An invented cold-chain handover clause supplies vehicle-temperature records for review, requires separate inspection of arriving goods and says a parcel-receipt signature does not replace quality confirmation. Only a transport excerpt and a receipt note marked pending inspection are supplied. No inspection page for this lot appears; history concerns an accepted delivery on another vehicle run. Equipment records, parcel receipt and goods quality need different evidence. A signature does not establish completed inspection. Actual input must retain separate inspection, the confirmation restriction and the excerpt’s covered period. No actual temperature, quality standard or test result is supplied.

## Three common errors

The first error clips the restriction against replacing quality confirmation from a citation. Compare the complete clause, excerpt and actual request for receipt promoted into a quality finding. The second borrows another vehicle run’s inspection history. Hold current run and lot fixed across history conditions and trace the inspection record’s subject. The third turns a transport excerpt into a full-journey state in a structured table. Check original coverage and excerpt position after parsing, retaining uncovered periods. Action type, vehicle-lot reference and time coverage require separate checks; a signature or partial trace cannot fill them.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which document records parcel receipt, which records inspection of this lot and which period the transport excerpt covers. Trace actual input sentence by sentence. Added inspection evidence updates covered quality information after run, lot and item checks; equipment records retain their own subject and period. Inspect citation tokens separately from reply limits and actual model context scope. A complete answer does not establish delivery of the ending clause or the entire record. This hypothetical exercise tests no food, performs no receipt and determines no transport liability. An absent inspection page does not establish that goods failed inspection.
