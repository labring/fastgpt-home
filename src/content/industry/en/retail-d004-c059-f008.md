# Store cold-chain receiving: document comparison with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Cold-chain temperature records, arrival signatures and rejection wording have distinct subjects. Contractual requirements do not establish acceptance of this batch. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed delivery note covers a refrigerated vehicle, while the temperature excerpt names only the compartment and a rejection passage concerns one carton. Collect coverage and actual wording without sample temperatures. A compartment record does not establish that each item satisfies contractual conditions. A person can choose carton-level evidence or rejection-subject review. The branch arranges inspection; it does not turn an arrival signature into acceptance of the whole load or replace actual measurement and procurement judgment.

## Three common errors

Measurement-subject substitution: A compartment record described as carton testing needs object and carton-level evidence. Arrival signature promoted to acceptance needs the batch’s actual acceptance conclusion. Local rejection expanded to the vehicle: Compare the one-carton rejection scope with downstream wording before describing the whole load as returned. These inspect measurement subject, business stage and handling coverage.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the vehicle context, carton-source explanation and choice record. Inspect actual inputs on the measurement and rejection branches. Adding that carton’s conclusion can support its own conditions without resolving other cartons. Rejection registration, return transport and inventory handling require separate business records. Workflow output and readable temperature material establish neither accepted goods nor real cold-chain performance.
