# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: document comparison with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For document comparison in Fresh-food weighing and sorting, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Weighing and sorting material needs separate item, packaging-basis and adjustment-status checks. A sorting task does not establish performed weighing or completed compensation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed sorting task concerns loose goods. The weighing-label excerpt includes packaging, and an adjustment request records only a customer objection. Contract comparison cannot treat that text as comparable net content or invent a conversion. Collect the measurement basis and objection passage. A person can request weighing evidence or adjustment handling. Keep the task, field record and customer request in their distinct stages rather than merging them because they name the same item.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Sorting transaction check | Proposed input | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label basis | Packaging included | Not directly net goods |
| Task ownership | Possible other-task label | Item and origin relationship |
| Customer objection | Adjustment request only | Not completed handling |
| Input handoff | Packaging and objection passages | Details retained downstream |
| Operational outcome | Weighing and adjustment evidence | Node completion is not resolution |

## Three common errors

Mixed quantity basis: A packaged measurement treated as net goods needs the packaging basis and actual weighing explanation. Misattached item: A different task’s label requires task-subject and label-origin checks. Request promoted to compensation: An adjustment application described as handled needs the actual processing record. These inspect measurement meaning, record ownership and execution status.

## How to verify the setup

Follow the weighing-evidence choice and confirm that packaging information survives handoff. Inspect the adjustment path to see whether the request remains unresolved. After adding a real handling result, retest only the corresponding item and objection without extending the answer to other batches. Field weighing, financial adjustments and stock changes need separately connected evidence. This comparison supplies neither a legal metrology conclusion nor proof of a resolved customer objection.
