# E-commerce warehouse receiving: document chunking for document comparison

## What the documents look like

E-commerce warehouse receiving and putaway handles the first physical-and-book confirmation when goods enter the warehouse. The main data sources are WMS, ERP procurement, supplier delivery notes and barcode scanning. The daily documents are purchase arrival notes, receiving inspection notes, putaway tasks and discrepancy reports. These materials update per batch and per day.

For document comparison, parsing and chunking must keep the arrival quantity, the inspection conclusion and the location assignment of the same batch mutually traceable. Compared with in-warehouse picking and packing, receiving materials are produced before the inventory movement. If chunking separates the batch number on the delivery note, the received quantity on the inspection note and the discrepancy reason on the discrepancy report, later comparison loses the anchor of the same object. This page addresses only document parsing and chunking within contract and transaction material comparison. It does not construct business functions beyond retrieval or forms.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

The actors in receiving materials are suppliers, receiving clerks and WMS administrators. The objects are purchase arrival notes, receiving inspection notes, putaway tasks and discrepancy reports. Chunking must preserve the document identity and version that can be traced back to the source text, inspect paragraphs according to the specific material structure, and avoid separating a condition, an explanation or a qualification from the content it constrains. Original data and retrieval indexes must be distinguished; one group of original data can correspond to multiple indexes. After a material change, the actual training and indexing state must be checked. A successful upload does not mean retrieval has been updated.

Hypothetical drill sample: a paper purchase arrival note reads "Supplier: Xinghai Parts; purchase order number: PO-2026-0412; arrival date: April 12, 2026; material: bearing housing type A; arrival quantity: 120 pieces; delivery note number: SH-0412-07." A paper receiving inspection note reads "Inspection note number: YS-2026-0412-03; corresponding purchase order number: PO-2026-0412; actually received quantity: 118 pieces; discrepancy quantity: 2 pieces; inspection status is pending discrepancy handling." A draft sentence awaiting judgment reads "The 120 pieces in this arrival have all passed inspection and been put into storage." Check whether the subject, object, quantity and status of this draft sentence agree with the two source texts.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the arrival quantity of 120 pieces on the arrival note directly as the inspection conclusion. The error is ignoring that the inspection note records 118 pieces actually received, a discrepancy of 2 pieces and a status of pending discrepancy handling. Comparison requires the same subject, the same period and the same field; only mutually exclusive values on the same field constitute a contradiction.
2. Treating the draft sentence "all passed inspection and been put into storage" as supported. The source text states only pending discrepancy handling and does not state passed inspection or completed putaway. This wording is unsupported and the evidence gap must be recorded.
3. Treating retrieval or a form as authorized execution. A same-vector-model requirement for a retrieval library cannot be extended to other external systems sharing the same model. Semantic recall and relevance do not handle permission or entity isolation. Pending review does not demonstrate a lack of operating permission.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample source texts and the draft sentence. Compare the 120 pieces in the draft sentence with the 118 pieces actually received on the inspection note. If the two disagree on the same field, judge the draft sentence unsupported and record the discrepancy quantity of 2 pieces and the pending discrepancy handling status.
2. Input "bearing housing type A" and "PO-2026-0412" for separate searches. Compare whether the returned chunks retain the order number, material and quantity together. If a chunk retains only the material and loses the order number, record insufficient chunk completeness; do not guarantee a first-hit result.
3. Input the paper empty result [] to check the downstream design. Compare how the system handles the no-hit case. If the returned content is unrelated to the sample, record the actual observation; do not assert that a fictional rule necessarily returns empty. For libraries across different vector models, first check the identifier and exclude non-conforming ones; do not run cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.
