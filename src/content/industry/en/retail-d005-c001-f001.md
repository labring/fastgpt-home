# B2B marketplaces: procurement with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For procurement in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A procurement reader must distinguish statements in certification documents and a quotation from information an external service can supply. In the proposed text example, one document records raw-material production qualifications and the other lists specifications and pricing. These statements define a lookup need, not an existing qualification-checking API. Before using a FastGPT HTTP node, inspect the implemented interface contract, address, method, authentication and actual input values. Retain the original records returned by the business service.

## Three common errors

A registered address in City A and a shipping origin in City B describe different locations. They are not inherently contradictory supplier statements. If the proposed lookup concerns an address, specify which location is requested and retain the certification and quotation sources separately. When inspecting Params, Body or upstream variables, keep their business meanings distinct. A successful response still needs its field definitions checked to establish which location question it answers. If an absent test-mode chatId is interpreted as a failed supplier query, inspect the call mode and actual business conditions. That identity field is absent during testing and cannot replace the address response as query evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Certification documents alone do not establish historical supply performance. Identify an authorized provider for those records and prepare the inputs required by its actual interface. Missing chatId or responseChatItemId in test mode is separate from an absent performance report; conversation variables cannot supply business evidence. After obtaining records, repeat the lookup and inspect both returned content and its receiving step. Supplier admission and order approval require their own business process.
