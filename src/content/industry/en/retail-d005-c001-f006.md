# B2B marketplaces: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Scheduled supplier checks must distinguish the trigger from obtaining the source. Proposed certification documents record production qualifications and a quotation lists material specifications, but they do not provide a connected lookup service. Verify an available application entry or external scheduler for the deployed version, then define the authorized interface and its inputs. Retain a real trigger, request and response showing the originals actually read. A schedule configuration does not establish that the supplier meets procurement conditions.

## Three common errors

A registered address in City A and shipping origin in City B are not inherently contradictory. Repeating a trigger does not resolve their business meanings. Specify the address object and source version for each lookup, and inspect whether actual requests use the same sources. Any repeated request, write or retry requires explicitly implemented deduplication and idempotency, followed by observation of repeated runs. An entry in a task interface does not establish updated supplier information.

## How to verify the setup

If certification documents lack historical supply and performance records, running again on time cannot fill that gap. Identify the authorized record provider and receiving path, then reuse the original task input after obtaining the source and inspect the real response and business receipt. Record no trigger, call failure and absent evidence separately. Supplier admission, approval and ordering still need business confirmation; a successful scheduled response is not a receipt for those operations.
