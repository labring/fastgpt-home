# B2B marketplaces: procurement with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Sample finishes, a long-term supply introduction and a current replenishment commitment need different evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed B2B procurement packet includes a sample sheet offering a powder-coated finish, a supplier introduction describing long-term supply services and a replenishment enquiry saying the current batch awaits confirmation. A question about immediate replenishment to the sample needs their roles kept separate. The introduction is a service statement, the sample sheet lists an option and the enquiry supplies no current commitment. Citations must preserve those subjects and scopes. Their common supplier context cannot combine an option, a capability description and an unconfirmed enquiry into an established delivery arrangement.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Procurement source check | Proposed material and scope | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample option | Coated finish optional | Do not make it a confirmed supply specification. |
| Service statement | Long-term supply introduction | Inspect statement scope. |
| Current batch | Enquiry awaits confirmation | Seek applicable supply conditions. |
| Cited subject | Earlier enquiry may concern another material | Check subject and version. |
| Citation coverage | Introduction, sample and enquiry | Inspect which text actually reached the answer. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Associated knowledge bases` | Knowledge bases associated with one search node must use the same embedding model. |
| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |
| `Citation input handoff` | Inspect citations actually received by the answer step. |
| `Citation display check` | Trace displayed sources to their originals. |
| `Document-validity check` | Inspect source identity and applicable version separately. |

## Three common errors

First, a long-term supply introduction may become an immediate commitment for this batch; seek the applicable supply confirmation and conditions. Second, an optional sample finish may become the confirmed production specification; inspect final specification evidence rather than the sample alone. Third, an older enquiry for another material may be cited for the current coated item; inspect source subject and version. These require transaction confirmation, specification support and identity checks respectively. Additional introduction pages cannot replace any of those missing evidence objects or establish the requested batch outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask separately about sample options, the supplier service statement and the stage reached by the current batch enquiry. Compare each with actual citation content. The answer may describe the finish option and service introduction while retaining unresolved current conditions without adding delivery timing. If references contain only the introduction, inspect whether enquiry and sample sentences actually reached the answer; empty references require scope and transfer checks. Neither citation quantity nor wording relevance establishes supplier qualifications, real stock availability or completed fulfilment from this proposed packet.
