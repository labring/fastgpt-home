# MCN operations: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Periodic MCN procurement review can be limited to reading cooperation-source gaps rather than approving suppliers. Proposed input lists the contracting party, absent training material and its owner while no external reading service is configured. Verify the available scheduling entry in this deployment and whether application scheduling or an external scheduler triggers it. Reaching a scheduled time does not automatically create access to the MCN backend or contract ledger.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed arrangement | Execution evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger owner | Application scheduling or external scheduler specified | Deployment entry and actual start record |
| Source reading | Training gap and contracting party | Actual response from configured service |
| Empty execution | Timestamp without reading result | Inspect the post-trigger transition |
| Repeated registration | Same gap sent to business handling repeatedly | Separate deduplication and receipt checks |
| Run conclusion | Runs before and after source addition | Task existence is not this run’s success |

## Three common errors

A trigger timestamp without actual input and reading response cannot establish that this cooperation was reviewed. Inspect the transition from scheduling to reading. If the same gap is repeatedly registered externally, compare triggered subjects and business results; deduplication or idempotency requires explicit interface or downstream design. The presence of a reminder record is also distinct from its actual receipt.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect runs with no service connection, a reading response that still lacks evidence and a later run after supplementation, retaining contract version and return scope. Check HTTP method, headers, appropriate body and references against the real contract without exposing authentication. Review, supplier approval and ledger writes require separate permissions and receipts. A periodic task’s existence does not establish successful completion of this review.
