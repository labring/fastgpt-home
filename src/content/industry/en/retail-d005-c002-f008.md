# MCN operations: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An MCN procurement workflow can begin with the cooperation evidence needed for this inquiry. Proposed inputs separate the creator, contractual scope and absent training material. After form submission, inspect what the next reading step actually receives rather than inferring procurement qualification from an account schedule. Permitted cooperation wording and admission to this procurement need their respective evidence. Preserve that distinction before asking the source owner to investigate.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Step | Proposed material | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Separate needs | Cooperation scope and training gap | Scheduling does not establish qualification |
| Identity handoff | Creator name beside account nickname | Unsupported correspondence remains unresolved |
| Human route | Obtain training or organize the contract | Each connected yes branch reaches its recipient |
| Added evidence | Training passage and the same inquiry | Output actually uses the new source |
| External approval | Separate procurement registration and approval | Workflow end does not replace business receipts |

## Three common errors

Offer human choices to obtain training records or organize the existing contract, testing the connected yes branch of each. If the training choice reaches contract organization, inspect selection, recipient and transmitted sources; the existing contract does not replace the requested evidence. Another failure substitutes an account nickname for the creator’s name. Without supported correspondence, inspect the entered text and retain the identity gap instead of importing another person’s qualifications. If attendance becomes verified qualification for this cooperation, inspect its participation evidence separately from required training content and contractual scope. Attendance cannot supply competence or this cooperation’s missing source evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Trace the need, submitted content, branch destination and output evidence before and after training material is added. Interaction state is recorded by the node’s behavior, not an invented global switch establishing completed review. External record lookup, procurement registration and cooperation approval need actual business interfaces, permission and receipts. Reaching this workflow’s end does not establish supplier admission, effective agreement or an updated schedule.
