# Shopify fulfillment: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A Shopify replenishment workflow can collect order demand, the location to inspect and the missing availability source. Proposed text identifies the ordered item and location without providing stock or treating a label as preparation evidence. Inspect whether the receiving step preserves demand rather than inventing availability, including its order origin. Passing a storefront description does not establish the identifier used by an external stock service.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Step | Proposed material | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand meaning | Ordered item and requested units | Not converted into available stock |
| Location gap | Location named without availability evidence | Need for supplementation remains explicit |
| Wrong connection | Location request reaches ordering | Inspect selection and recipient |
| Identifier mapping | Storefront wording and stock-system identity | Correspondence is not guessed from names |
| Business writing | Separate procurement, reservation and order updates | Actual results evidenced independently |

| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |

## Three common errors

Test separate choices to obtain location evidence, refer supply checks to procurement or organize supplied orders only. If requesting location evidence reaches an ordering step, inspect the recipient and selection route before retaining the unverified location. Another failure drops the quantity’s meaning and treats ordered units as available inventory. Compare original and received passages, restoring the quantity as demand before continuing. If a shipping label becomes reserved stock, inspect its passage separately from this location’s inventory source. Label creation establishes that transport material, not an actual reservation for this item.

## How to verify the setup

Retain demand wording, location sources, actual branch and explanation. After obtaining availability material, repeat the question and inspect unresolved items rather than assuming availability. Waiting for human interaction and continuing does not establish stock approval. Procurement creation, reservation and order writes require separately configured interfaces or procedures, with permission, responses and actual records. Completed organization establishes neither a purchase order nor a stock deduction.
