# Temu semi-managed sellers: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Preserve scope across temporary packaging names, material descriptions and confirmed specifications. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed Temu packaging packet uses soft bag as an informal name, describes a thin-paper dust cover not intended to carry weight and leaves its formal name unconfirmed. A draft index instead says load-bearing carry bag. A question about lifting goods needs the actual purpose. The informal name supplies no carrying evidence. An index can help locate the material, but must be inspected with its linked original. It cannot turn conversational naming into an established material function or replace confirmation of the specification with a search description.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informal-name scope | Conversation note says soft bag without material | Trace to thin-paper dust cover and inspect added carrying meaning. |
| Purpose exclusion | Material page explicitly excludes carrying weight | A lifting question still needs this exclusion rather than a bag title alone. |
| Confirmation state | Formal name pending rather than final specification | Match any added name confirmation to this thin-paper item. |
| Older source | Separate fabric-bag page differs from thin paper | Inspect the actual target without transferring fabric use to paper. |
| Retest subject | Keep lifting and dust-cover questions separate | Record each returned page and whether the carrying exclusion remains. |

## Three common errors

First, soft bag may expand into load-bearing capability; compare index and original for added purpose and the explicit exclusion. Second, an index may omit the formal-name-pending state; inspect block status and identity instead of treating a pending note as final specification. Third, a lifting question may return an older fabric-bag source; compare material, use and version rather than adopting it through the word bag. These concern functional meaning, confirmation state and linked source. Vector scores cannot replace the applicability evidence needed for the proposed packaging use.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about informal naming, material purpose and formal-name status, then expand actual returned originals. After editing indexes, repeat the same lifting and dust-cover questions to inspect their source targets without assuming exact matching. Missing carrying evidence leaves the conclusion unsupported. Failure to retrieve a material record does not establish that the material does not exist. These proposed text fixtures include no physical carrying or image test and promise no procurement effect. Actual specification and supply need applicable confirmation, while changing an index executes no purchase or physical verification beyond the recorded source comparison.
