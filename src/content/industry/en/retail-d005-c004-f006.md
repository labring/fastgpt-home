# Temu semi-managed sellers: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A periodic overseas-warehouse procurement check can take declaration wording, warehouse identity and inventory-sheet version as this run’s inputs. Proposed arrangements lack transfer-availability evidence and assume no WMS connection. Verify the scheduling entry and trigger owner for the actual deployment before specifying external reading and permissions. Periodic startup determines when execution begins, without establishing its business sources or transferable stock.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Check | Proposed arrangement | Execution evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warehouse subject | Current warehouse and source version | Response is not for another warehouse |
| Connection state | WMS reading not yet configured | Startup is not business-data access |
| Failure retained | Run starts while reading fails | Reason and missing coverage remain visible |
| Rerun responsibility | Restart may repeat writes | Interface or downstream deduplication designed separately |
| Actual handling | Separate replenishment and transfer status | Startup does not establish warehouse processing |

## Three common errors

Reading another warehouse’s records cannot support the target warehouse. Inspect triggered inputs, HTTP references and returned identity. A startup record with a failed reading needs its failure and missing coverage retained, rather than an earlier response presented as current completion. A rerun may repeat business writes, so inspect deduplication in the actual interface and downstream handling without promising that the scheduling node supplies it.

## How to verify the setup

Compare trigger time, real request and response against this run’s warehouse and source version, then inspect changes after transfer evidence is added. Configure method, headers, appropriate body and references under the external contract, keeping credentials private. Replenishment creation, warehouse adjustment and platform declaration require separate authority, acceptance and business state. Startup proves neither completed warehouse processing nor zero inventory when reading returns no evidence.
