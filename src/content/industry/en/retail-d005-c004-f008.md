# Temu semi-managed sellers: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A semi-managed procurement workflow can receive declaration wording, the overseas warehouse and absent availability evidence separately. Proposed inputs supply a source version while explicitly lacking transfer confirmation. Downstream reading must retain that scope rather than infer transferability from a sheet’s existence. Warehouse and goods correspondence need supplied text. Arranging nodes does not establish a WMS connection or completed stock verification.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Step | Proposed material | What to inspect |

## Three common errors

Changing a warehouse option’s visible wording while still passing the previous warehouse’s source creates a subject mismatch. Inspect selection, received text and version origin, repair the connection and test each destination. Another test refers missing transfer evidence to an operator but produces a completed-transfer statement. Inspect handover content and the resulting judgment, retaining pending handling rather than inferring execution from a traversed route. If connected source nodes are reported as a queried WMS, inspect the actual business-interface connection, transmitted request and warehouse return. Passing existing sheets does not replace evidence of an external reading.

## How to verify the setup

Compare declaration passages, warehouse identity, the transfer gap and actual output. After corresponding confirmation arrives, repeat the need and inspect whether downstream input includes it. Interaction state is recorded by the triggered node while waiting, without an invented global review switch. Procurement creation, warehouse changes or platform submission require actual business steps, permission and receipts; completion of the choice does not establish those external updates.
