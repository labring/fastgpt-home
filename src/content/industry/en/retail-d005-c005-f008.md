# TikTok Shop US operations: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A TikTok Shop procurement workflow can distinguish listing wording, return inspection and a requested stock source. Proposed inputs supply the first two while explicitly lacking stock evidence. Return grading does not establish procurement or sale availability. Preserve each material’s product identity and source status during handoff, limiting interpretation to supplied records rather than extending one inspection across present inventory or guessing cross-system relationships from a product name.

## Three common errors

Inspection wording for another product cannot answer the current procurement question. Trace submission origins and product correspondence, removing unrelated text. Also test human choices to obtain stock evidence or read listing wording only. If the stock request still receives only the listing, inspect actual inputs. A person’s selection does not establish a stock response, and downstream reading cannot derive availability from the published description. If return-inspection wording becomes currently procurable stock, inspect the individual item and present inventory coverage against their passages. Grading does not establish warehouse availability; retain the unknown without its source.

## How to verify the setup

Retain all three source scopes and branch results, repeating the same procurement question after stock evidence arrives. Inspect use of the new material and remaining unknowns. User choices continue through connected yes branches; recorded interaction state is not passed business review. Procurement registration, stock changes or relisting require external handling, permission, responses and operation records. A functioning workflow does not establish platform acceptance of those actions.
