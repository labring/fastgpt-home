# General import declarations: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An import procurement workflow can establish what the supplier contract, goods description and arrival statement each provide. Proposed inputs retain the contracting party and transport passage while lacking warehouse receipt evidence. Separate supplier identity from the transport subject downstream; supplied import documents do not establish available received stock. Current supply questions require corresponding sources rather than deriving a delivery commitment from arranged transport.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Step | Proposed material | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party distinction | Supplier separate from transport subject | Identities are not substituted |
| Abbreviation defect | Full name replaced without evidence | Inspect received values and retain mapping gap |
| Receipt route | Warehouse choice receives arrival notice only | Scope remains explicitly insufficient |
| Corresponding addition | New record for this transaction | Downstream actually uses that source |
| Business acceptance | Separate procurement registration or profile change | Continuation does not establish handling |

## Three common errors

Reducing the supplier’s full name to an unsupported abbreviation can introduce another party’s records. Inspect original wording and received values. Test separate choices to obtain warehouse receipt evidence or read contractual terms. If the receipt request receives only an arrival notice, identify the scope gap; the word arrival does not establish receipt of this order into the warehouse. If general invoice wording becomes a manufacturer model, inspect manufacturer correspondence and the original commodity scope. Transport and trade descriptions do not supply model mapping or a basis for procurement selection without that source.

## How to verify the setup

Retain party identity, transport sources, warehouse gaps and branch output. Repeat after adding the corresponding record and inspect its actual downstream receipt. Interaction-state recording is node behavior rather than completed supplier review or warehouse approval. External receipt queries, procurement registration and profile changes require actual interfaces, authority and business receipts. Continuing the workflow establishes neither import processing, procurement acceptance nor payment. For this test, retain the full contracting-party passage alongside the transport subject so the receiving person can inspect their correspondence directly, rather than relying on an abbreviation.
