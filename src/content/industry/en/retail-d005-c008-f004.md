# Amazon FBA preparation: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Link liner samples, bulk options and packaging designs to their own originals. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed FBA procurement packet describes a soft-foam liner sample, leaves a rigid card insert for separate bulk confirmation and limits an outer-carton catalogue to short cups. A draft index says the long-cup rigid insert is settled. The question asks whether that insert can be adopted directly for long cups. No such confirmation is supplied, and the sample material differs. Inspect material, cup design and state in each original block. Inclusion in a knowledge base establishes no universal product applicability, and a sample’s existence establishes no selected bulk specification.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample material | Soft foam | Do not index as rigid card. |
| Cup-design scope | Short cups | Do not transfer to long cups. |
| Bulk state | Pending separate confirmation | Do not call it settled. |
| Linked block | Material, design and state | Read each attribute. |
| Physical fit | No test supplied | Keep unresolved. |

## Three common errors

First, a soft-foam source may be indexed as rigid card; compare material terms with the linked block for added meaning. Second, short-cup applicability may expand to long cups; inspect catalogue subject and current design instead of inferring fit from the same product family. Third, a requirement for separate confirmation may become an already-settled outcome; seek actual confirmation and version while retaining the unresolved state. These concern material, applicability and confirmation status. Vector scores or index quantity cannot replace the actual packaging evidence needed for the intended item and its proposed bulk arrangement.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the sample uses, which design the catalogue covers and how far bulk confirmation has progressed, retaining indexes and actual quotations. Repeat foam, rigid-insert and cup-design questions after edits, inspecting targets without promising matching performance. Missing long-cup evidence remains unresolved. Failure to retrieve long-cup evidence does not establish that production is impossible. These are text fixtures without physical packing or image-retrieval tests. Model and index changes still require original-source inspection and execute no preparation. Actual packing and warehouse receipt outcomes require their own evidence beyond the proposed source relationships and retrieved wording.
