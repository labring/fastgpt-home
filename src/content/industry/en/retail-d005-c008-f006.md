# Amazon FBA preparation: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Scheduled FBA preparation checks distinguish plans, carton documentation and warehouse receiving. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Suppose preparation material contains a draft shipment plan, carton labels awaiting review and a warehouse-receipt note for another consignment. Confirm the current-version trigger route and source interface, configuring requests under the real contract instead of assuming scheduled access to platform inventory. No actual shipment quantity, receiving state or sales result is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

A draft plan reported as dispatched needs carrier evidence checked. Unreviewed labels described as ready to use need specification and review support checked. Another consignment receipt reported as this shipment sale stock needs correspondence and actual availability checked. These concern preparation versus action, label applicability and subject stage. Trigger success proves no warehousing event.

## How to verify the setup

Compare trigger time, input, response and business material item by item, locating carton identity beside label wording. Added receiving evidence updates warehouse stage alone while sale availability remains separate. Downstream deduplication, idempotency, plan submission and stock writes need explicit design, authority and receipts. The scheduled chain arranges document comparison without performing shipment operations or confirming a listing. Retain the draft-plan label, locate carton identity beside label wording and inspect receiving evidence separately from sale-availability evidence.
