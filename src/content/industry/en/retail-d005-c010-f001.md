# JD POP seller operations: procurement with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For procurement in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A JD POP supplier inquiry can focus on the origins of product information and a shipping-time agreement. Proposed inputs supply product material and the agreement’s scope while lacking a commitment for this preparation instance. Establish whether the real external service provides agreement records or stock evidence before checking address, method and conditions. A warranty repair record remains a separate source and does not establish present inability to supply.

## Three common errors

An agreement for another supplier or earlier cooperation scope may be misused when party and version conditions are omitted. Inspect the complete passage, transmitted values and response coverage before comparison. Another failure puts one product description in the body and a different subject in parameters. Check their actual contractual roles separately, repair the request and repeat instead of accepting an arbitrary response as admission evidence. If repair within warranty is used to establish present inability to supply, inspect the repair subject, recorded stage and current supply statement. One after-sales case does not replace evidence of present supply capacity.

## How to verify the setup

Trace product material, agreement identity, the missing supply statement and the final answer, limiting interpretation to obtained records. Absent responseChatItemId in test mode follows the documented testing condition and is distinct from an incorrectly transmitted party. Keep authentication headers private. Supplier-profile changes, procurement approval or inventory deductions require separate external permissions and actual results; completing a document query does not establish their execution.
