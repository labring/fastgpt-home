# JD POP seller operations: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Scheduled POP purchasing checks trace catalog and variant differences without approving listings or stock. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Suppose a supplier changes packaging wording, the existing catalog remains old and availability for one variant awaits a reply. Confirm the actual-version application trigger or external scheduler and identify the configured data interface. Use its real method, headers and variables; scheduling creates no supplier-system connection. No actual stock or purchasing outcome is supplied.

## Three common errors

New wording reported as updated knowledge needs processing and retrievable content checked. An unanswered variant reported as available needs supply evidence checked. A read operation reported as completed listing needs a publishing receipt checked. These examine version adoption, supply state and business action independently of a running job.

## How to verify the setup

Compare trigger time, input, raw return and version. Keep packaging wording beside variant identity and preserve differences when the old catalog remains. New replies update evidence for their variant alone. Downstream deduplication, idempotency, purchasing and listing writes need explicit design, authority and receipts. Scheduled checking identifies gaps without making supply promises or approving product content; the reviewer still needs source text for each proposed change. Place packaging versions beside supplied variants, retaining unprocessed catalog differences in review gaps instead of comparing job state alone.
