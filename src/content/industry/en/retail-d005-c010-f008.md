# JD POP seller operations: procurement with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A JD POP procurement workflow can collect product information, supply-related terms and outstanding questions. Proposed input also contains a warranty repair record but lacks a current supply response. Repair concerns another service question and does not establish present breach or inability to supply. Inspect source scopes downstream. Agreement applicability and item identity require textual correspondence rather than inferring complete evidence from document titles.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Step | Proposed material | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Case scope | Repair separate from current supply | Repair does not establish supply breach |
| Party handoff | Agreement applicability passage | Cooperation scope survives downstream |
| Missing-source route | Supply choice receives repair heading | Inspect sources and connection, then retest |
| Added response | Current supply statement for the item | Output actually cites the addition |
| Business approval | Separate profile change or ordering process | Choice is not procurement permission |

## Three common errors

Test separate choices to obtain current supply guidance, inspect terms or refer the matter to an operator. If the supply branch receives only a repair heading, inspect transmitted sources and connections, identifying the missing supply evidence. Losing the agreement’s applicable party may import another cooperation’s scope; restore that scope against the original passage before continuing. An executed branch does not establish passed source review. If repair becomes breach of this supply commitment, inspect warranty service scope and current supply evidence. An after-sales case is not this procurement’s execution result, and repair occurrence alone establishes no breach.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, agreement, repair-case status and supply gap. Repeat after the corresponding response arrives, checking actual citation of the addition. Interaction-state recording is node behavior, and human choices arrange connected paths without approving procurement. Supplier-profile changes, ordering and external synchronization require actual procedures, authority and operation receipts. Completed source organization establishes neither supplier admission nor acceptance of an order.
