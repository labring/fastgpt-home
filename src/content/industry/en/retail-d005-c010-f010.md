# JD POP seller operations: procurement with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Repair-spare catalogues, recovered-item records and new-product replenishment need distinct scopes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed JD POP procurement packet describes repair replacement parts, records a recovered main unit received pending review and leaves a new-product complete-unit combination unconfirmed. A question about confirmed supply of new units needs these roles separated. The catalogue concerns repair use, receipt supplies no saleable finding and the new combination remains open. A received main unit cannot become new stock, while a spare-parts catalogue cannot assemble a complete new configuration. Similar product names across service and procurement sources do not establish that their objects are interchangeable.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Procurement source check | Proposed material and scope | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material purpose | Repair replacement | Do not turn into a new complete unit. |
| Recovery state | Received pending review | Seek actual checks and state. |
| New contents | Combination unconfirmed | Obtain configuration list. |
| Supply action | Demand differs from commitment | Check current confirmation subject. |
| Title identity | New-product title may accompany recovery text | Read text and applicable item. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Associated knowledge bases` | Knowledge bases associated with one search node must use the same embedding model. |
| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |
| `Citation input handoff` | Inspect citations actually received by the answer step. |
| `Citation display check` | Trace displayed sources to their originals. |
| `Document-validity check` | Inspect source identity and applicable version separately. |

## Three common errors

First, repair spares may become contents of a new complete unit; inspect a new-product configuration list. Second, recovered receipt may become saleable status; inspect item-specific checks and state confirmation. Third, a complete-unit demand draft may become a supplier commitment; inspect the current supply confirmation and its subject. These require contents, material-state and procurement-action evidence from different sources. Neither recovered-item registration nor spare naming can replace new-supply evidence, and pending review cannot establish definite inability to sell without an actual finding.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask what the spare catalogue supports, which stage the recovered-item entry records and what the new demand still lacks. Compare actual citation wording, retaining repair use, receipt pending review and an unconfirmed combination without real stock or quality conclusions. A new-product title accompanying recovered-item text needs identity inspection; empty references cannot establish that no supplier exists. Later configuration and confirmation material must match product and state before use. One component record cannot establish complete supply capability or turn a procurement proposal into an executed delivery.
