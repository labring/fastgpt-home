# Warehouse picking and packing: procurement with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Warehouse picking and packing, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

Warehouse procurement can examine the relationship between packaging demand and picking or recheck records. Proposed material supplies a wave list of items awaiting packing, while the consumables request lacks stock evidence and weighing data is unavailable. Before an HTTP lookup, inspect how the real WMS contract identifies consumables and the warehouse. An item name on a shipping label is not automatically a packaging identifier, and planned picking does not establish packaging readiness.

## Three common errors

Using item quantities as consumable demand makes the response insufficient for this procurement question. Trace the wave passage, packaging request and transmitted conditions, separating products from packaging objects. A second failure asserts abnormal parcel weight despite absent weighing records. Inspect whether the actual response contains relevant measurements; neither an empty value nor a generated label replaces weighing evidence. Reassess the question after the corresponding record is supplied. If a recheck note covering the picking list becomes a weighed parcel, inspect the check’s subject and this parcel’s device source. List verification and actual measurement are different evidence and cannot replace one another through document names.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the wave, consumables request and availability evidence, comparing sent conditions with the scope of the returned record. Check headers, parameters and any appropriate body against the contract while keeping authentication private. Missing identity fields during testing do not alone establish a business fault. Consumables procurement, warehouse changes or exception handling require separate authorization and actual receipts. A normal query response establishes neither completed packing nor an inventory change.
