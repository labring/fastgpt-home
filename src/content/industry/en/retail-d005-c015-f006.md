# Letter-of-credit documents: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Scheduled documentary-credit checks locate missing documents and version differences without deciding conformity. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Suppose purchasing material includes an unreviewed draft bill of lading, credit wording from another version and an email requesting supplier documents. Confirm the actual-version application trigger or external scheduler, then identify the configured file-reading interface. Use its real method, headers, body and variables. Triggering creates no bank connection. No actual amount, bank decision or completed presentation is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Scheduled credit-document check | Paper material | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft bill | Nature unresolved | Not formal document |
| Credit version | Another version | Check applicability |
| Document request | Supplier asked | Not presented |
| Triggered reading | Configured interface | Actual return |
| Formal handling | Separate bank/business review | Receipt and authority |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Trigger responsibility` | Inspect the available trigger and responsible component. |
| `Data reads` | External read interfaces require explicit configuration. |
| `HTTP requests` | Construct requests and check variables against the external contract. |
| `Repeated-trigger responsibility` | Implement deduplication, idempotency and writes explicitly. |
| `Execution records` | Inspect actual triggers, inputs and business responses. |

## Three common errors

A draft bill reported as a formal document needs document nature checked. Another credit version applied here needs version and entity checked. A request for documents reported as completed presentation needs submission acknowledgment checked. These examine material stage, applicability and a handling event; scheduling records determine neither conformity nor acceptance.

## How to verify the setup

Compare trigger time, input, return and missing documents for the same paper matter. Mark source text unreviewed when only an index returns, and verify version again after content arrives. Downstream deduplication, idempotency, formal presentation and business writes need explicit design and evidence. The schedule runs a configured comparison chain without making decisions for a bank or responsible business reviewer. Retain the document-request recipient and identify versions for both the draft bill and credit wording.
