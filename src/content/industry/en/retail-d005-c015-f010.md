# Letter-of-credit documents: procurement with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Beneficiary naming, manufacturer descriptions and goods details require separate attribution. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed procurement packet associated with a letter of credit names Trader A as beneficiary, describes Factory B as producing sensor products and refers current models to a separate detail list. A question about who supplies which specifications needs these roles preserved. The notice identifies a beneficiary, the introduction describes a production category and the list text is missing. Trader cannot become manufacturer, and a sensor-producing factory cannot establish the models in this batch. Party and goods relationships require actual material rather than document titles or retrieval relevance.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Procurement source check | Proposed material and scope | Evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notice role | Trader named beneficiary | Do not turn into manufacturer. |
| Production scope | Sensor product category | Do not invent batch models. |
| Detail gap | Current models separately referenced | Obtain actual list. |
| Transaction linkage | Other-transaction list may be imported | Check documents and version. |
| Conclusion boundary | Source roles differ from transaction judgments | Retain evidence scope. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Associated knowledge bases` | Knowledge bases associated with one search node must use the same embedding model. |
| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |
| `Citation input handoff` | Inspect citations actually received by the answer step. |
| `Citation display check` | Trace displayed sources to their originals. |
| `Document-validity check` | Inspect source identity and applicable version separately. |

## Three common errors

First, beneficiary identity may become actual manufacturer identity; inspect the documented relationships for this transaction. Second, the factory category may become batch-specific specifications; obtain model detail and applicable goods evidence. Third, a list for another transaction may be used here; inspect document linkage and version. These require roles, specifications and transaction identity separately. The notice and introduction cannot assemble a supply chain not stated in the sources, and these excerpts do not determine a bank-document review or transaction-compliance outcome for the parties.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask whom the notice names, what the factory describes and whether current detail is available. Trace each answer to its source. Retain missing specifications and unresolved relationships without adding commitments or fulfilment. If citations contain only the factory introduction, inspect whether notice and reference wording reached the answer; a list link still needs its text read. Added material must match the transaction before use. One relationship cannot cover all business between the parties, and empty references cannot establish that a named party had no involvement in the matter.
