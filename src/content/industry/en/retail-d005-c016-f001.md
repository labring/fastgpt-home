# Franchisee onboarding: procurement with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Separate rack components, delivery location and installation from illustrative training diagrams. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

An invented opening-supply packet offers rack components with installation to be discussed separately. A delivery note specifies the doorway only, and a training diagram is illustrative rather than a measurement of this store. Inspect the real interface’s coverage of store, materials and services before querying. A shared rack name establishes no complete installation promise. No actual dimensions, installation parameters or purchasing charges are supplied, and the diagram proves no site or equipment inspection. Reading organizes purchasing gaps without approving opening.

## Three common errors

- If components become installation-inclusive supply, compare goods and service wording. Missing installation terms remain for discussion despite a complete-rack picture.

- If doorway delivery becomes onsite installation, inspect location, actual handoff and work records. Delivery expands into no construction action.

- If a training diagram proves local fit, inspect its purpose and actual site measurements. A reference drawing replaces no space evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Check address, method, Params, Headers and applicable Body under the real contract, injecting credentials safely and retaining redacted content. Identify rack, service and site sources separately. A catalog name proves no read installation instructions. Added components resolve composition alone; delivery evidence supplies neither fit nor installation. Supply confirmation, surveying, purchasing and construction require authority and receipts. The HTTP node assumes no control of equipment work, stock or franchise accounts.
