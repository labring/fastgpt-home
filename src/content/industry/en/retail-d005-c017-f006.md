# Franchise fee settlement: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A periodic procurement-related settlement check can inspect contractual plans and requested business evidence without amounts. Proposed arrangements list invoice, ledger and deduction wording, not bank processing records. Complete titles do not establish received or paid funds. Verify scheduling entry and trigger owner, then define configured interfaces for this party’s sources. Startup does not connect finance automatically or grant accounting authority from a contract.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

Another store’s ledger requires party and transmitted-subject checks; complete returns still cannot support this contract. Absent bank evidence must not become receipt through an earlier explanation or notice. Retain this run’s gap. If repeated triggers register several requests, inspect each receipt. Idempotency, duplicate prevention and accounting writes need explicit interface or downstream design rather than assumed financial controls.

## How to verify the setup

Retain party, source meanings, actual request and return scope. Repeat after corresponding evidence arrives, keeping plans separate from execution. Check HTTP method, headers, appropriate body and references under the external contract without exposing credentials. Purchase approval, deductions, accounting changes and settlement confirmation need separate authority and actual states. Output establishes neither completed fund handling nor passed financial review.
