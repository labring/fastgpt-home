# Regional dealer stock: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A periodic dealer-replenishment check can read requests, stock sources and outstanding supply questions. Proposed inputs supply demand and a collection plan without this run’s dispatch or bank evidence. Plans do not establish procurement availability. Specify the actual application or external trigger owner; external data still needs a configured interface. ERP-to-ordering connectivity and matching warehouse definitions are not assumed.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Periodic check | Proposed arrangement or gap | Execution evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand scope | Request and plan without execution records | Availability is not presumed |
| Party and period | Other-dealer earlier material introduced | Inspect time and actual request |
| Reading failure | Task runs without obtaining sources | Failed coverage remains explicit |
| Repeated replenishment | Rerun may submit again | Separate business receipts and prevention |
| Actual state | Separate ordering, adjustment and confirmation | Reading is not acceptance or reconciliation |

## Three common errors

Reusing another dealer or earlier period requires party, source-time and actual-request checks; complete returns may still answer the wrong question. If reading fails despite a run label, retain failed coverage instead of declaring reviewed demand. A rerun may submit replenishment again. Inspect real business receipts and implement deduplication or idempotency explicitly rather than inferring duplicate prevention from scheduled execution.

## How to verify the setup

Retain dealer, item, demand status, actual input and response. Repeat after corresponding stock or dispatch sources arrive and inspect use of the addition. Check HTTP method, headers, appropriate body and references under the contract without public credentials. Purchase creation, stock changes and collection confirmation require business permission and real states. Periodic reading establishes neither accepted replenishment nor reconciled finance.
