# Regional dealer stock: procurement with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Regional low-stock labels, store requests and model confirmation do not establish replenishment quantities. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed regional-dealer procurement packet labels regional stock low, allows stores to submit replenishment requests and leaves the specific supply model to separate checking. A question about directly arranging this store order lacks store quantities, an accepted request and a confirmed model. Citations must preserve statistical level and subject. Regional low cannot become this store out of stock, and a state label cannot calculate how much to buy. The supplied text also does not establish that a supplier accepted the proposed replenishment or allocated any goods.

## Three common errors

First, a regional summary may become a store stocktake; inspect the applicable store inventory record. Second, permission to request may become an approved replenishment; inspect this request and its acceptance state. Third, model checking may disappear while a confirmation for another specification is reused; inspect the model covered by the supply original. These require inventory level, process action and specification identity respectively. A shared dealer name cannot replace the checks, and relevance or ranking cannot decide an order quantity without actual quantity and applicability evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Acceptance should ask what level the summary covers, what the store may currently do and what supply still requires. Preserve store and model gaps in actual citations. A quantity decision needs the missing store-level evidence rather than invented orders or available stock. If references contain only the regional summary, inspect whether store material entered scope; empty references cannot establish absence of stock. Later store and model documents need statistical time and specification checks before answering current procurement. One low-stock state cannot establish persistent shortage or a general finding about supplier capability.
