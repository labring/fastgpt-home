# On-demand retail fulfillment: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in On-demand retail fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A periodic on-demand procurement check can inspect shortage wording, store stock and unmet demand without executing substitution or refund. Proposed arrangements supply shortage and picking passages while lacking current availability. Verify deployment entry, trigger owner and configured reading contract. Frequent order updates do not establish this run’s completeness, and startup does not connect riders, platforms and stock automatically or explain procurement causes from delivery exceptions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Periodic check | Proposed arrangement or gap | Execution evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand distinction | Shortage and picking separate from stock | No inferred procurement cause |
| Stock time | Other-time response used for the present | Inspect input and coverage |
| Present gap | Startup without reading results | No verified-preparation claim |
| Repeated demand | Several triggers register externally | Separate receipt, idempotency and prevention |
| Handling result | Separate purchase, substitution or refund | Reading is not platform approval |

## Three common errors

A stock response from another time cannot support current store demand. Inspect input time, store and response coverage. A startup record without stock results leaves a gap instead of verified preparation. Repeated triggers registering demand require actual receipt checks. Deduplication, idempotency and procurement writes need separate design rather than assumed avoidance of repeated handling.

## How to verify the setup

Retain order, store, source time, actual input and response. Repeat after current stock evidence arrives and inspect remaining unknowns. Check method, headers, appropriate body and references under the actual contract, keeping authentication private. Purchasing, substitutions, refunds and closure require separate permission and states. Successful periodic reading establishes neither prepared goods, buyer receipt nor approved platform remedies.
