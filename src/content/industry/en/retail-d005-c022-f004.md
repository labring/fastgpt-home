# Omnichannel stock transfers: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Index borrowed racks, consumable purchases and storage records by item purpose. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed channel-procurement packet requests rack foot pads, records borrowing the rack body and retains earlier pads as samples. A draft index says the complete rack is for sale and pads are in stock. The question asks about directly buying both. Consumable demand, borrowing and sample retention establish neither rack sale nor saleable pad inventory. Inspect item and purpose in the linked block instead of combining every rack-related page into a supply list. A storage location also supplies no evidence that a retained sample can fulfil an actual order.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rack purpose | Borrowed | Do not index as sale. |
| Pad demand | Purchase request | Do not make it stock record. |
| Sample status | Retained reference | Do not make it saleable. |
| Component linkage | Rack and pads | Inspect actual target. |
| Supply evidence | Currently absent | Retain pending confirmation. |

## Three common errors

First, borrowing may become sale in the index; compare purpose wording with the linked block instead of changing transaction type. Second, a retained sample may become saleable inventory; inspect storage object and actual supply evidence, since existence establishes no available stock. Third, an earlier whole-rack index may override the foot-pad request; compare component, application and version and inspect actual returns. These concern purpose, inventory state and part targeting. Vector scores or similar rack names cannot replace confirmation of the particular goods and intended procurement arrangement.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what is requested, what is borrowed and why samples are retained, saving proposed indexes, block identities and actual quotations. After edits, repeat rack, pad and sample questions to inspect scope without assuming every wording retrieves correctly. Missing supply material remains unresolved without proving the supplier cannot sell. This text test verifies no physical stock. Model and index changes execute no purchase, transfer or ownership change. Actual availability and supply need independent applicable evidence beyond the relationship between the proposed words and the sources returned for them.
