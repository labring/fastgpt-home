# Brand repair services: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Keep loan tools, consumables and verification notes separate in procurement indexes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed brand-repair procurement packet loans a test pen, sells probe caps separately and notes that verification records are stored elsewhere. A draft index offers a verified complete tool set. The question concerns direct purchase of the set. The sources establish a loan tool and separate consumable without set-sale confirmation or a verification result. Trace tool and consumable indexes to their own blocks. Mentioning an external record establishes neither its existence nor applicability, and use of a tool for inspection supplies no observed performance or qualification for the actual device.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool purpose | Loan | Do not index as sale. |
| Consumable composition | Probe cap only | Do not expand to complete tool. |
| Record pointer | Stored elsewhere, unread | Seek actual evidence. |
| Object version | Current tool | Inspect verification correspondence. |
| Actual effect | No device test | Do not promise diagnostic performance. |

## Three common errors

First, loan may become sale in the index; compare tool wording with proposed meaning for changed transaction purpose. Second, a separately purchased cap may become a complete tool; inspect composition and actual supply scope. Third, records stored elsewhere may become verified; seek record identity, tool and version instead of filling a result from a note. These concern purpose, composition and evidence. Retrieval similarity and focused indexes cannot confirm actual tool condition, and a consumable source cannot replace the separate evidence required for sale of the tool itself.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what is borrowed, what is sold and where records are, retaining indexes, linked originals and actual quotations. After changes, repeat pen and cap questions to inspect their sources. Consumable-only results leave complete-set supply and verification unresolved; a missing record proves no absence of previous verification. These are hypothetical texts without device tests or connections. Model and index changes compare evidence identity only, promising no fit or diagnostic effect. They convert no loan into sale. Actual procurement still needs applicable corresponding confirmation beyond the proposed index-to-source relationship.
