# Brand-owned store operations: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Keep badge plates, name stickers and role lists distinct in company-store indexes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed company-store procurement packet supplies a blank badge plate, leaves name-sticker wording for confirmation and notes temporary display assistance as a role. A draft index offers a finished manager badge. The question asks about ordering a named manager badge now. The plate has no name, the sticker is unconfirmed and temporary assistance establishes no managerial title. Inspect material, lettering and role originals separately. Shared store context cannot combine them into an official identity or completed production, and a role note also supplies no system authorization for the named person.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material state | Blank plate | Do not add printed name. |
| Wording confirmation | Name pending | Retain unproduced state. |
| Role meaning | Temporary assistance | Do not elevate to manager. |
| Store attribution | Other-store printed badge | Check name and version. |
| Real action | No grant or purchase | Do not claim execution. |

## Three common errors

First, a blank plate index may add a finished name; inspect wording confirmation and composition evidence while retaining the unproduced state. Second, temporary assistance may become manager; check role wording and actual identity rather than deriving a position from a badge label. Third, another store’s printed-badge index may serve the current material; compare store, name and version instead of transferring through the same plate design. These concern finished-item state, role meaning and source attribution. A retrieval hit confirms neither a job appointment nor completed production or applicable current purchasing evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the plate supplies, which state the name has and what task the note describes, expanding actual references and index relationships. After edits, repeat blank-plate and finished-badge questions without promising exact matching for every alias. Plate-only results leave name and current-role evidence unresolved, not impossible production or refused access. These fixtures contain no real personnel data or manufacturing test. Model and index changes appoint nobody, grant no rights and place no order. Actual finished goods still require applicable confirmation beyond the proposed material and role descriptions.
