# Brand-owned store operations: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Direct-store replenishment review checks store demand and transfer origins. Staffing and daily reports do not automatically establish a purchase requirement. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed store material says display needs replenishment, a daily report covers an earlier shift and transfer paperwork lacks receipt. Periodic review can seek current demand and stock evidence without equating a display gap with no inventory. Verify deployed trigger and read entry, then inspect whether this run obtained the same store’s material. Updated rosters describe staffing, not purchase confirmation, and earlier shifts do not establish present goods status.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Direct-store replenishment review | Proposed source status | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display gap | Not proof of no stock | Actual inventory |
| Daily shift | Earlier period | Current applicability |
| Transfer paperwork | Receipt absent | Not purchase confirmation |
| Triggered read | Current same-store sources | Actual return |
| Formal action | Purchasing and notices separate | Plan is not occurrence |

### Documented conditions

| `Repeated-trigger responsibility` | Implement deduplication, idempotency and writes explicitly. |
| `Execution records` | Inspect actual triggers, inputs and business responses. |

## Three common errors

Display gap promoted to no stock needs actual inventory evidence. Earlier-shift data promoted to current conditions needs source timing. A transfer record promoted to confirmed procurement needs the real decision. These inspect demand, temporal coverage and execution independently of configured tasks.

## How to verify the setup

Retain each run’s passages and actual return. Added current-store stock changes corresponding item judgments only while unconfirmed transfers remain pending. Purchasing, transfer writes and store notices need separate authority and receipts. Inspect repeated handling under the real system. A scheduled task establishes neither occurred replenishment nor resolution of display issues.
