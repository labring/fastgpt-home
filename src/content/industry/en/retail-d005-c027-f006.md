# Dealer ordering events: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Ordering-event preparation review separates candidate styles, credit applications and confirmed contracts. Updated display samples do not establish completed purchases. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed preparation material contains display samples, candidate orders, contracts awaiting replies and pending credit applications. Periodic review can inspect confirmations for this event without turning sample lists into purchase orders. Establish the available deployed trigger or call and inspect event ownership of acquired material. Matching styles or company names cannot supply contract relationships, and credit applications do not authorize procurement execution.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Ordering-event procurement preparation | Proposed stage | Current check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display sample | Not a formal order | Event confirmation |
| Candidate styles | Reply pending | Not decided procurement |
| Contract origin | Event correspondence needed | Exclude another event |
| Credit application | Not execution authority | Actual approval |
| Periodic result | Evidenced entries only | Not universal sales completion |

## Three common errors

Display promoted to demand needs actual current-event order confirmation. Another event’s contract reused here needs source-event checks. Application promoted to authority needs real approval. These inspect demand, subject and authority independently of periodically collected files.

## How to verify the setup

Retain current-event input, trigger records and pending replies. Added style confirmation changes that entry only while other styles remain candidates. Prior runs cannot substitute for new evidence. Approval for another event cannot authorize this purchase. Signing, purchasing and payment need separate authority and receipts. Check repeated runs and external writes against actual implementation. Task execution establishes review, without promising sales, supply or credit outcomes.
