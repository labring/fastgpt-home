# Brand livestream operations: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Brand-livestream preparation checks scheduled products against actual demand. Updated scripts do not automatically create purchase orders or stock commitments. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed script adds a gift, the product card remains old, scheduling awaits confirmation and preparation wording lacks the gift’s source. Periodic review inspects version relationships and actual demand rather than create procurement from a script sentence. Verify the deployed trigger and preserve each acquired subject. Old cards do not establish new merchandise, proposed timing does not establish broadcasting and updated assets do not establish available stock.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Livestream preparation review | Proposed version difference | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Script gift | New wording | Actual demand confirmation |
| Product card | Old version | Not new merchandise evidence |
| Schedule state | Confirmation pending | Not prepared goods |
| Stock origin | Missing | Assets do not establish availability |
| Formal preparation | Purchase and receiving separate | Review is not a fulfillment promise |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Trigger responsibility` | Inspect the available trigger and responsible component. |
| `Data reads` | External read interfaces require explicit configuration. |
| `HTTP requests` | Construct requests and check variables against the external contract. |
| `Repeated-trigger responsibility` | Implement deduplication, idempotency and writes explicitly. |
| `Execution records` | Inspect actual triggers, inputs and business responses. |

## Three common errors

Script promoted to purchase demand needs the real business decision. An old card applied to new merchandise requires version and origin checks. Pending scheduling promoted to prepared goods needs actual preparation records. These inspect demand evidence, source relationships and work stage independently of periodic execution.

## How to verify the setup

Retain before-and-after passages. Added gift-demand confirmation changes that scope only while missing cards or stock stay unresolved. Failed reads cannot reuse old versions as new evidence. Purchasing, receiving and broadcast release need separate authority and receipts. Deduplication follows the actual system. Periodic review establishes neither prepared gifts nor promised broadcast or fulfillment performance.
