# After-sales repairs and reships: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Index repair cover plates, trim rings and function notes by component position. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed repair-mailing procurement packet lists a rear dust-cover plate, separates a trim ring in a text legend and excludes a latch from the plate. A draft index calls it a locking trim-ring assembly. The question asks about buying that assembly. The originals separate plate and ring without a latch, so position and function matter. Covering an opening establishes no locking structure, and adjacency in a legend establishes no complete set. The legend is hypothetical text, without assumed image ingestion or searchable visual processing for the actual material.

## Three common errors

First, a plate index may add a latch; compare the original exclusion with proposed composition and unsupported function. Second, a trim ring and plate may merge into an assembly; inspect parts and set confirmation instead of deriving composition from nearby positions. Third, an older front-cover index may answer the rear-cover question; compare position, part identity and version. These concern function, composition and source target. Vector scores establish no identical physical structure, and changing models creates no part facts or confirmation absent from the linked original data.

## How to verify the setup

Ask where the plate sits, whether it contains a latch and how the ring is listed, retaining actual references and index relationships. After edits, repeat rear-cover and locking-set questions for preserved qualifiers without promising exact matching. Missing assembly evidence remains unresolved without proving production impossible. No physical or image-path test is supplied; actual image retrieval would need its ingestion conditions checked separately. This page creates no replacement order or purchase and establishes no restored repair function. Adoption still requires applicable evidence for the actual parts and intended arrangement.
