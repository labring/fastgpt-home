# Supermarket supplier accounts: procurement with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Index supplier cartons, inner-bag lists and confirmation appendices by packaging level. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A proposed supermarket supplier packet puts a white label on the outer carton, describes a clear unprinted inner bag and confirms only the carton description in an email. A draft index says both packaging levels are confirmed with white labels. The question concerns all packaging. The originals distinguish levels and limit confirmation; a shared product or email subject cannot establish every level. Link indexes to specific blocks, retaining unprinted bag and carton-only scope. Color, printing and confirmation cannot be merged into a new specification unsupported by any source sentence.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Original-data/index check | Specific subject relationship | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging level | Carton and inner bag | Attribute separately. |
| Printing condition | Inner bag unprinted | Do not add white label. |
| Confirmation scope | Carton only | Retain qualifier in index. |
| Older-bag source | Historical printed page | Inspect version and layout. |
| Actual adoption | Full scope unconfirmed | Keep pending without inferring refusal. |

## Three common errors

First, a carton label may migrate to the inner bag through indexing; compare packaging level and original attributes for cross-object additions. Second, carton-only confirmation may become complete confirmation; inspect the email object and actual appendix rather than summarizing from its subject. Third, an older printed-bag source may answer for the current clear bag; compare material, layout and version. These concern packaging hierarchy, confirmation scope and block identity. Vector scores or the same supplier name establish no identical specification and supply no missing confirmation for the inner packaging level.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about carton, inner bag and email scope, inspecting actual references and index targets. After changes, repeat label and unprinted-bag questions to record preserved levels without promising exact matching. Carton-only results leave inner-bag and complete-scope evidence unresolved, not nonconformity or a refused supply request. These proposed texts contain no real packing, delivery or image verification. Model and index changes place no purchase and decide no acceptance or settlement outcome. Actual adoption still needs corresponding confirmation beyond the retrieved wording and the proposed index relationship to its original source.
