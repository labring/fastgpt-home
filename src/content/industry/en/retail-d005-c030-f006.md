# Supermarket supplier accounts: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Supplier preparation review separates contractual demand, delivery acceptance and current confirmation. Settlement files do not establish an approved next purchase. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed purchasing lists intended replenishment. Contract passages limit scope, acceptance belongs to a previous batch and reconciliation remains unconfirmed. Periodic review can inspect current demand and correspondence without applying old acceptance to new purchases. Establish the actual deployed trigger, then inspect supplier and period read this run. Rebate applicability cannot supply product demand, and unsigned reconciliation establishes neither current procurement nor fund handling.

| Delivery acceptance | Previous batch | Not current goods |

## Three common errors

Earlier batch used for current goods needs batch correspondence. An agreement promoted to purchase demand needs the actual business decision. Unsigned wording promoted to settled handling needs a real period outcome. These inspect source, demand and execution independently of periodic reading.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the trigger and actual input. Added current confirmation changes covered products only while acceptance and settlement remain unresolved. Updated files do not establish those actions. If the supplier merely acknowledges receiving the plan, the purchase remains unconfirmed. Purchasing, acceptance and accounting writes need separate authority and receipts. Inspect repeated triggering and writes under actual implementation. Review completion establishes neither supplied goods, settlement nor completed supplier performance.
