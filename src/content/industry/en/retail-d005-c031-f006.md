# Retail packaging reporting: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Disposable-supplies procurement review separates purchases, stock and actual use. A historical report cannot replace current store demand. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed preparation contains previous purchase and sales evidence, a draft half-year report and no current store balance. Periodic review can obtain stock and demand origins without treating purchased goods as still available or deriving the next purchase from sales vouchers. Verify actual deployed trigger and reads, retaining store and period. A commitment states wording, not current use or purchasing decisions, and supplies no compliance conclusion.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Supplies procurement review | Proposed source status | State check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earlier purchases | Not current balance | Actual stock |
| Sales voucher | Not next purchase decision | Demand origin |
| Report draft | Historical period | Not current confirmation |
| Commitment | Action evidence absent | Not performed measures |
| Actual action | Purchase and reporting separate | Plan is not replenished stock |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Trigger responsibility` | Inspect the available trigger and responsible component. |
| `Data reads` | External read interfaces require explicit configuration. |
| `HTTP requests` | Construct requests and check variables against the external contract. |
| `Repeated-trigger responsibility` | Implement deduplication, idempotency and writes explicitly. |
| `Execution records` | Inspect actual triggers, inputs and business responses. |

## Three common errors

Past purchases promoted to current stock need actual inventory evidence. Historical reports promoted to present demand need temporal scope. Commitment promoted to performed measures needs action records. These inspect stock meaning, timing and execution independently of review intervals.

## How to verify the setup

Retain acquired passages and gaps per run. Added current-store evidence changes supported demand explanations only. The same draft is neither new evidence nor completed confirmation. Purchasing, receiving and formal reporting need separate authority and receipts. Check deduplication and external writes against the real system. A task establishes neither sufficient supplies nor a submitted or approved report.
