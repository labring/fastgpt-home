# Trade and FX administration: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Trade-procurement source review checks actual contract-to-collection relationships. Updated registration files do not establish execution conditions for this purchase. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed procurement files contain a trade contract and registration material, a collection record with unresolved transaction identity and an invoice lacking current correspondence. Periodic review can seek mappings without replacing transaction relationships with company names or making financial and tax judgments. Verify the real deployed trigger and read entry, retaining acquired origins. Updated registration describes changed material, not approved purchasing, processed funds or arrived goods.

## Three common errors

Entity substituted for transaction needs genuine collection correspondence. Registration promoted to authority needs actual purchasing authorization. Invoice promoted to delivery needs current receiving evidence. These inspect relationships, decisions and goods stage independently of periodic execution.

## How to verify the setup

Retain passages, trigger details and real returns. Added mapping changes that explanation alone while registration and procurement states need their own sources. A changed title does not establish substantive review. Purchasing, fund actions and external submission need separate authority and receipts. Check deduplication and writes under the real system. Task output establishes neither appropriate financial action nor a completed transaction.
