# Export tax rebate filings: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Export tax rebate filings, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Declaration-related procurement review checks purchase evidence against export batches. Material preparation and handling outcomes remain separate tracks. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed preparation contains purchase evidence, export material and an unresolved declaration draft, while current collection correspondence is missing. Periodic review can track added relationships without matching transactions by document names or deriving purchasing eligibility from a prepared declaration. Establish actual trigger and read capability, retaining this run’s period and subject. The review organizes relationships without calculating outcomes or determining tax applicability for the responsible party.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Declaration procurement review | Proposed gap | Current check |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Evidence period | Current applicability needed | Exclude another period |
| Declaration draft | Preparation unresolved | Not an obtained result |

## Three common errors

Forced purchase-export mapping needs real batch correspondence. Prior-period evidence reused here requires period and subject checks. Preparation promoted to a handling outcome needs this event’s actual reply. These inspect transaction relationships, temporal scope and process stage independently of task existence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain input and result. Added purchase-export correspondence changes supported relationships only while collection and processing remain unresolved. Unchanged files are not new progress. Formal procurement confirmation, declaration and accounting need separate authority and receipts. Inspect repeated triggers and writes under actual implementation. Periodic review establishes neither declaration outcomes nor eligibility or fund-handling commitments.
