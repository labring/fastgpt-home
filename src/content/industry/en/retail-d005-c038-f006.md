# Fresh-food concession counters: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Fresh-food concession counters, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Scheduled concession-purchasing checks distinguish counter ownership, supply varieties and store receipt. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Suppose supply wording covers a concession counter only, a variety list awaits confirmation and a store receipt summary belongs to the directly operated department. Confirm the actual-version trigger route and data interface, carrying counter, variety and source identity as input. Configure reading under the real contract instead of assuming scheduled access to store business databases. No actual purchase quantity, waste or revenue share is invented.

## Three common errors

Concession wording applied to directly operated stock needs business-entity identity checked. Unconfirmed varieties reported as fully available need supplier wording checked. Another department receipt applied to the counter needs department and event checked. These examine subject, supply certainty and receiving ownership independently of a trigger.

## How to verify the setup

Compare trigger time, input, raw return and business state item by item. Place counter identity beside source wording and leave source body unreviewed when titles alone return. Added variety replies update their supply evidence alone while receipt remains separate. Downstream deduplication, idempotency and purchasing or stock writes need explicit design, authority and receipts. The timed chain finds differences without placing orders or accepting concession settlement. Identify concession and directly operated departments beside sources, preserving pending variety confirmation and restricting receipt summaries to their department while settlement acceptance needs separate evidence.
