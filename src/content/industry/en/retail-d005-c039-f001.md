# Tmall flagship operations: procurement with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Tmall flagship operations, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Brand authorization, listing wording and shipping commitments have separate coverage. Procurement reading needs actual supply evidence rather than substituting customer-service statements for verified outcomes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A Tmall procurement reading can inspect authorization, listing material and shipping commitments while identifying current supply gaps. Proposed sources lack available stock or supplier confirmation; a shop name does not establish complete permission. Check party, item and applicability against the real contract without assuming platform-to-ERP synchronization. Service records describe statements rather than product inspection, supply acceptance or execution.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Procurement-reading check | Proposed material | Actual supporting evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority applicability | Item, period and passage | Other subjects not substituted |
| Supply state | Commitment separate from stock source | No direct in-stock claim |
| Inspection evidence | Service statement without actual findings | Not promoted to quality conclusion |
| Request coverage | Party, version and sent conditions | Return supports the present question |
| Further operation | Separate entry, reservation or change | Reading is not platform acceptance |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Request address and method` | The request address and method follow the external contract. |
| `Params and Headers` | Inspect parameters, headers and authentication separately. |
| Body | Where a JSON body is used, validate its structure against the external contract. |
| `Variable references` | Variables can come from global, system or upstream outputs. |
| `Debug identity fields` | Test mode lacks chatId and responseChatItemId. |

## Three common errors

Authorization for another item or period needs passage and condition checks. A shipping commitment described as current stock needs availability evidence. A service retelling promoted to a quality conclusion needs actual inspection support. These concern permission scope, supply state and source status rather than a normal response.

## How to verify the setup

Retain subject, version, actual request and return. Repeat after corresponding supply or inspection sources arrive and inspect use of the addition. Check parameters separately from headers and body applicability under the contract, keeping authentication private. Procurement entries, stock reservation and product changes need permission and actual receipts. Reading establishes neither supplier admission, prepared goods nor platform acceptance.
