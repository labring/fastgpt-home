# Seller ratings management: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Seller ratings management, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Review-related procurement checks need packaging evidence and actual purchasing decisions. Complaints or experience material cannot establish improved outcomes from replacement materials. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed review mentions torn outer packaging, a rectification ledger plans new cartons, procurement confirmation is absent and experience material concerns an earlier period. Periodic review can follow inspection and purchase evidence without deciding supply from a complaint. Establish available deployed triggers and reads, retaining current passages. Customer statements are not investigated causes, and planned materials establish neither purchases nor current demand or effects inferred from old-period data.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Review-related procurement check | Proposed source state | Current check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer review | Packaging assertion | Actual inspection separately |
| Rectification ledger | Cartons planned | Not purchase record |
| Experience material | Earlier period | Not current scope |
| Triggered read | Current passages | No evidence is not progress |
| Formal replacement | Purchasing execution separate | No promised improvement |

## Three common errors

Assertion promoted to a finding needs corresponding inspection. Planned cartons promoted to ordering need real decisions and records. Earlier experience applied to this run requires period and subject checks. These inspect fact status, execution and coverage independently of periodic runs.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger details, passages and results. Added inspection changes supported issues only while procurement stays pending. Missing new material cannot turn prior conclusions into progress. Purchasing, packaging replacement and external replies need separate authority and receipts. Inspect deduplication under the actual system. Completed review establishes neither changed packaging nor promised review improvements or business performance.
