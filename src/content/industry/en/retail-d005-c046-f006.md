# Marketplace category access: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Marketplace category access, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Category-procurement preparation follows credential entities, pending renewal and covered products. Acquired files do not establish admission of the current merchandise. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed supplier material contains a credential cover, an authorization chain awaiting renewal and product wording without applicability scope. Periodic review can obtain contents and correspondence without deriving universal purchasing permission from a cover. Verify the actual trigger and read contract, preserving acquired versions. Pending renewal is not renewed status, changed filenames do not establish validity and material receipt does not establish a platform decision. This checks relationships without admission rules or legal judgments.

## Three common errors

Wrong entity requires actual authorization relationships before using another party’s credential. Pending renewal promoted to completion needs contents and handling evidence. Material receipt promoted to admission needs the current real outcome. These inspect entity, state and decision independently of periodic review.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger records, acquired versions and gaps. Added authorization chains change covered relationships only while product applicability remains unresolved. A cover alone supports no content conclusion. Purchasing, credential handling and product admission need separate authority and receipts. Deduplication and writes follow actual implementation. Task output establishes neither renewal nor purchasing release and provides no compliance guarantee.
