# Channel receivables: scheduled workflows for procurement

## What the documents look like

Channel receivables collection happens after credit sales, so the working records centre on credit limits and cash recovery rather than on matching buyers with sellers. The finance system produces an accounts receivable ageing table. Bank receipts show what has actually arrived. Distributor orders and sales contracts form the basis for shipment and for the credit terms granted. A credit agreement sets the limit and the payment period. A shipment freeze notice records how an over-limit situation was handled. The parties involved are typically brand finance staff, sales back-office staff, and the distributor owner. The supplied material gives weekly and monthly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronisation or real-time triggering from those frequencies.

For the scheduled workflows step in procurement and supplier records, these documents matter because an automated request can only read what an interface has already been configured to reach. The ageing table and the credit agreement are the inputs that a recurring job would reference. The freeze notice is a business action that sits downstream of any judgement about credit status.

A hypothetical paper exercise sample is used throughout this page. A brand finance team retains a credit agreement whose text reads: "Distributor A is granted a credit limit of 500,000 yuan, a payment period of 30 days, and shipments are suspended once the limit is exhausted." The same batch retains an ageing table whose text reads: "As of the hypothetical date, Distributor A has an outstanding receivable balance of 480,000 yuan, of which 80,000 yuan is 10 days overdue." The proposed sentence under review is: "Distributor A has exceeded its credit limit and shipment should be frozen automatically." This request concerns a credit-status judgement and a shipment freeze, both of which require a business system implementation. This page only checks how the materials and the request correspond.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Scheduled workflows and automation here read external data on a cycle, send requests, and record results. The responsibility for triggering must be stated explicitly: either the application's scheduling capability or an external scheduler, checked against the actual version in use for available entry points. External data is read through an interface that has already been configured; a scheduled trigger by itself does not establish a connection to a business system. HTTP requests are checked against the external interface documentation for method, headers, body, and variable inputs. Deduplication, idempotency, and business writes require explicit design in the interface or in a later flow. Execution records are checked for trigger time, actual input, returned result, and business status; the existence of a task does not substitute for successful execution. Retrieved values or values collected by a form do not equal authorised execution. Credit approval, shipment freezing, database writes, and external synchronisation each need their own downstream implementation.

In the sample, the credit agreement and the ageing table are paper materials. A trigger firing produces no read action, so the check is whether an interface has been configured. The proposed sentence asserts an over-limit state, but the sample text states only limit, payment period, balance, and overdue amount, with no credit-status field recorded. The request points at a shipment freeze, which a business system must implement; the request itself is not execution.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the scheduled trigger as an established business system connection. In the sample, the credit agreement and ageing table are paper materials, and a trigger firing produces no read action. The check is whether the interface has been configured.

2. Treating the proposed sentence as a supported conclusion. The sample text gives limit, payment period, balance, and overdue amount, and does not record a credit-status field. The over-limit judgement remains to be verified.

3. Treating the request being sent as a completed shipment freeze. The sample request points at a shipment freeze, and that action requires a business system implementation. The request alone does not constitute execution.

Corresponding checks: first, inspect whether the trigger responsibility setting matches the sample cycle, and observe whether any trigger record exists without a configured interface. Second, inspect whether the fields cited by the proposed sentence appear in the sample text, and observe whether the over-limit field is absent. Third, inspect the request's returned result against the business status, and observe whether a separate execution record exists for the shipment freeze.

## How to verify the setup

- Verification one: input the sample credit agreement text and ageing table text, and compare the fields cited by the proposed sentence "Distributor A has exceeded its credit limit and shipment should be frozen automatically." The sample text contains limit, payment period, balance, and overdue amount, and does not contain a credit-status field. The sentence is unsupported, and the evidence gap should be recorded.

- Verification two: input the sample request and compare the request body fields against the two material identifiers. The request body references the ageing table identifier and the credit agreement identifier. If the return value extraction JSON path points at the balance field, the extracted result corresponds to the sample balance of 480,000 yuan. If it points at a field that does not appear, the extraction is empty and the interface response structure needs checking.

- Verification three: input the sample trigger time and execution record, and compare task existence against successful execution. The record needs trigger time, actual input, returned result, and business status. If the record has only a trigger time and no returned result, the execution status remains to be verified. If the returned result does not match the sample materials, the source of the input variables needs checking. All of the above is paper-based checking and has not been run.
