# Franchise filing management: scheduled workflows for procurement

## What the documents look like

Franchise filing management works with franchise contracts, operations manuals, filing materials, and information disclosure documents. The data sources named in the brief are the commercial franchise information management system, enterprise licenses, store lists, and contract ledgers. Filing materials and disclosure documents update annually or irregularly. Inventory procurement and supplier records belong to the day-to-day operating documents of the franchise system, and they differ from the site engineering materials that head-office location selection handles.

Scheduled tasks and automation in this category mainly carry document expiry reminders, ledger comparison triggers, and external interface calls. The trigger itself changes no filing status and no authorization status. This page addresses one task only: franchise filing management / inventory procurement and supplier records / scheduled tasks and automation. It does not establish business functions without a documented basis.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A scheduled trigger only decides when a process starts. It does not establish a connection to a business system, and it produces no approval and no write effect. External data must be read through a configured HTTP request. The method, headers, body, and variable inputs are checked against the external interface documentation. Deduplication, idempotency, and business writes require explicit design in the interface or in a subsequent flow. Execution records should be checked for trigger time, actual input, returned result, and business status. The existence of a task does not replace successful execution.

Hypothetical drill sample: on 10 March 2025, a legal staff member at a franchisor headquarters prepares a supplier record handover request. The source text reads: "Party A and Party B signed the Equipment Procurement Framework Agreement on 1 June 2024, valid until 31 May 2025. As of 10 March 2025, the agreement is within its validity period and no termination notice has been received." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "This supplier agreement has been terminated and can be removed from the filing record list." The authorization boundary of this request is internal legal review. It does not include external submission or system writes.

The retrieval library and relevance scores do not carry out permission or entity isolation. Retrieval is not authorization. A similarity setting does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a scheduled trigger as a business write. Input: the scheduled entry configuration. Compare the trigger record with the business system status. If the business system has no corresponding change record, the trigger produced no write.
2. Treating retrieval or a form as authorized execution. Input: the handover request. Compare the authorization boundary in the request with the conclusion in the draft sentence. If the draft sentence exceeds the internal legal review scope, the conclusion has no authorization support.
3. Treating a pending review as a completed termination. Input: the source text "no termination notice has been received." Compare the draft sentence "has been terminated." The two statements are mutually exclusive on the same agreement status field, so the draft sentence contradicts the source text.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample source text and the draft sentence. Compare the agreement validity end date of 31 May 2025 with the base date of 10 March 2025. If the base date is earlier than the expiry date and no termination notice exists, the draft sentence "has been terminated" is unsupported.
2. Input the authorization boundary "internal legal review" from the handover request. Compare whether the draft sentence contains an execution action to remove the record from the list. If the draft sentence requires removal, it exceeds the authorization boundary, and the evidence gap must be recorded.
3. Input the HTTP request configuration. Compare the request method with the external interface documentation. If the method, headers, or body do not match the documentation, the request configuration does not meet the condition. Whether the returned result matches the business status remains to be observed in practice.

A pending request is not approval. An offered option is not execution. An unsigned termination is not a completed termination. Missing evidence remains missing. Where a document field is not provided, the value is unknown.
