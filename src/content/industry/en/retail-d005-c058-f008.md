# Fresh-food supplier claims: workflow routing for procurement

## What the documents look like

Fresh-food supplier claims pursue quality responsibility at the receiving stage and do not cover natural in-store shrinkage. That distinction shapes the material set around batch evidence captured during acceptance. The primary sources are the quality inspection system, WMS receiving records, supplier contracts and financial reconciliation. Day-to-day documents include the quality exception form, photo evidence, deduction notices and claim reconciliation statements. Updates occur per batch and per month: batch evidence supports an individual claim, while monthly reconciliation supports a period summary. Securing evidence, calculating the deduction, and negotiating replacement or compensation are the most time-consuming steps. Workflow routing therefore has to place evidence collection, amount checking and negotiation outcomes on separately verifiable nodes.

A hypothetical paper exercise sample: Supplier A delivered one batch of spinach on 10 March 2025. The WMS receiving record shows 200 boxes arrived. The quality inspection system issued a quality exception form that day stating that 30 boxes had wilted leaves, with photo evidence number ZJ-0310-07. The supplier contract states that quality exceptions are deducted against the batch quantity. The draft sentence to be assessed is: "Supplier A has confirmed full responsibility for the deduction on all 30 boxes of spinach, and the deduction notice has taken effect."

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The Form Input node is a user-interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user completes the interaction. Required fields are collected and, after submission, the information the user entered is passed to subsequent nodes. The User Selection node behaves the same way at the state level: it enters an interaction state, records the workflow state, and after the user makes a selection it evaluates that selection and executes the corresponding "yes" branch. The interaction state and state recording are behaviors of these nodes; no separate global state-recording switch is documented.

Collected values must be explicitly passed to downstream retrieval, judgment or interface nodes, and outputs must be compared item by item against inputs. Order writes, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process; the nodes themselves do not perform those business actions. Retrieval locates material and does not perform authorization judgment. A relevance score is not a category, entity or permission filter, and a similarity setting does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records. Retrieval is not authorization.

At this step the practical boundary is that a form collects values and a selection routes a branch. Whether a deduction notice is effective, whether a replacement shipment occurred, and whether a reconciliation record was written are downstream business facts that need their own stated implementation. A pending request is not approval, an offered option is not execution, and an unsigned termination is not a completed termination.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration or check item | Purpose on this page | Basis and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form Input node | Collect the quality exception form number, batch date and exception quantity | After submission, information is passed to subsequent nodes |
| User Selection node | Choose the replacement or compensation direction | After selection, the corresponding "yes" branch executes |
| Interaction state check | Confirm the conversation enters interaction and waits for submission | Documented node behavior; no global switch |
| Output handoff check | Compare form output with downstream node input | Item-by-item comparison; no assumed automatic write |
| Retrieval scope check | Confirm material sits inside the accessible target range | Retrieval does not constitute authorization |
| External action split | Deduction notice effect and reconciliation backfill connect to a separate interface | Nodes do not perform external business actions |

A hypothetical starting point for this exercise: set the exception quantity field as required, because that value feeds directly into the deduction calculation and must be compared with the quantity stated on the quality exception form before it is passed onward. This is a hypothetical starting value chosen for this paper exercise, not a documented default.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as deduction confirmation. Submission completes information collection only. Whether the deduction notice has taken effect is an external business fact, and the sample contains no supplier confirmation record.
2. Treating the selected branch as already executed. Choosing the replacement direction only triggers the corresponding branch. Whether a replacement shipment occurred must be checked separately.
3. Using a retrieval result in place of an authorization judgment. Retrieving the quality exception form does not mean the record may be used for an external deduction. The access range and business authorization must be checked.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the quality exception form text from the sample above together with the draft sentence. Compare the draft sentence's claim that the supplier "has confirmed full responsibility for the deduction" against the exception quantity stated in the source text and the contract clause. The source text records no supplier confirmation, so that sentence is not supported and remains pending verification.
2. Input the field values after form submission and the values received by the downstream node. Compare whether the two sets match item by item. The handoff condition is satisfied when they match and no field is lost.
3. Input an explicit paper empty result, written as `[]`, and compare the downstream node's branch design when no retrieval result is returned. Confirm the flow does not break on the empty result and does not produce a fabricated conclusion. This checks the downstream design only; it does not claim that any particular query returns an empty result.

Each check states what to compare and what would satisfy the condition. No test, deployment, approval, refund or other business action is claimed to have occurred.
