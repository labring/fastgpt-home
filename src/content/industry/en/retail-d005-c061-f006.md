# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: scheduled workflows for procurement

## What the documents look like

Weighing and sorting happen inside the warehouse fulfilment step, and the central data are weight and specification. The working document set for this category consists of sorting task sheets, weighing labels, short-weight compensation forms, and quality inspection records. These arrive from the WMS, electronic scales, the order system, and inspection photographs. When something goes wrong, the direct consequences are short-shipment compensation, gross-margin loss, and poor customer reviews, so any scheduled workflow touching inventory procurement and supplier records must stay traceable to a specific document and a specific time.

The supplied material gives an update frequency of per order and daily. This page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, or a real-time trigger mechanism from that frequency. The feature under discussion is scheduled tasks and automation, and the task scope is inventory procurement and supplier records. Documents such as quality inspection records and short-weight compensation forms carry states that matter later: a form that has not been generated, a contract still under review, a label recording a weight. Scheduled automation reads and writes around these states, and the states themselves are business facts that a schedule cannot create.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A scheduled trigger only starts a request at a set time. It does not by itself establish a connection to a business system. External data has to be read through an already-configured interface. Deduplication, idempotency, and business writes have to be explicitly designed in the interface or in a later flow; this page does not claim they exist automatically. Execution records must be checked against trigger time, real input, returned result, and business status. The existence of a task does not replace a successful execution. Retrieval or a form does not constitute authorization, and a pending review does not demonstrate that an operation permission is absent.

Hypothetical paper exercise: On 10 March 2026, a quality inspection supervisor at a front warehouse submits a handover request. The source material reads: "Supplier A, spinach batch arriving 9 March 2026, weighing label records 500 grams, quality inspection record notes slight leaf wilting, short-weight compensation form not yet generated, procurement contract under review." The proposed sentence is: "The short-weight compensation for this spinach batch from Supplier A has completed approval, and purchasing may continue at the 500-gram standard." The question to decide is whether the proposed sentence is supported by the material. It is not. The material states the compensation form is not yet generated and the contract is under review, while the proposed sentence asserts a completed approval. These are mutually exclusive values for the same field, same subject, same period. The gap is recorded as pending verification.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a scheduled trigger as an established business system connection. Check the input: the trigger configuration and the interface configuration. Compare: whether a configured read interface exists. Observable result: when only a trigger record exists and no interface read record exists, the connection state is unknown.
2. Treating a contract under review as effective authorization. Check the input: the sample text "procurement contract under review." Compare: the proposed sentence "has completed approval." Observable result: the two values for the same field are mutually exclusive, and the proposed sentence is not supported.
3. Treating a retrieval hit as a permission filter. Check the input: the records returned by retrieval. Compare: the subject owning the record against the requesting subject. Observable result: a relevance score does not exclude unrelated subjects, and the identifier must be checked separately.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input: the hypothetical sample source text and the proposed sentence. Compare: the approval state described in the proposed sentence against "not yet generated" and "under review" in the source. Observable result: the proposed sentence is not supported, and the gap is recorded as pending verification.
2. Input: a paper input of `[]` as an empty result. Compare: whether the downstream design accepts an empty result. Observable result: the downstream produces no fabricated record, and the empty-result handling path is explicit.
3. Input: two requests carrying the same batch identifier. Compare: the deduplication result on the interface side against the two returns. Observable result: the repeated request produces no second business write, or it is explicitly recorded as not deduplicated.

Libraries built on different vector models cannot be associated together. The identifier must first be checked and non-matching items excluded; do not run a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recall. Image retrieval requires an image input and model capability, and reranking requires the model and must be enabled per its settings. Optional paths in the documented flow do not mean every request executes all of them.
