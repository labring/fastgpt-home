# Short-video affiliate sales: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Short-video affiliate sales, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Periodic preparation for shoppable-video procurement preserves script-version and product-list relationships. Distribution arrangements do not establish a placed purchase order. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed script promotes a bundle, while the linked-product list names a single unit and procurement wording says a supplier remains to be selected. Review whether correspondence material has arrived without turning sales wording into purchase specifications. Use the deployment’s actual trigger entry and inspect whether script and list belong together. A commission plan is only related document context here, without amounts or an inference that suppliers, stock or orders are confirmed.

## Three common errors

Copied specification needs the product list and supplier explanation before treating bundle wording as a single-unit purchase. Crossed versions require this input and source identifiers when a new script uses an old list. Preparation promoted to ordering needs an actual purchase record. These inspect goods meaning, document correspondence and execution stage despite a successful external response.

## How to verify the setup

Retain both passages acquired on each run and the unresolved supplier choice. After adding specification correspondence, repeat and inspect the limited change. A missing list or failed read remains a collection task, not confirmed merchandise. Ordering, stock verification and shoppable-link publication need separate business actions. Authority, repeated handling and receipts follow the real system; task execution establishes neither sale eligibility nor a published link.
