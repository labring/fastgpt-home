# Group-buy leader operations: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Group-buy leader operations, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Group-leader procurement review separates community demand, pickup arrangements and actual purchasing. Collected lists do not establish supplier acceptance. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed community notice invites demand registration. A pickup list contains tentative names, and a leader agreement describes cooperation scope. Periodic review can inspect whether genuine confirmation has arrived without promoting a list to a purchase commitment. Supply no amounts or performance figures. Establish the available trigger and read conditions, preserve notice and list passages, and state when the run only organized material without confirming suppliers or dispatch.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Group-leader procurement check | Proposed source status | Confirmation needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community notice | Demand registration invited | Not a confirmed order |
| Pickup names | Tentative list | Not received goods |
| Cooperation agreement | Collaboration scope | Not ordering authority |
| Added confirmation | This actual purchase | Remaining demand stays tentative |
| Trigger result | Material review only | Separate supply and notice evidence |

## Three common errors

Registration promoted to an order needs actual purchase confirmation. A tentative pickup list promoted to available goods needs corresponding receiving or supply evidence. Cooperation scope promoted to ordering authority needs actual authorization. These inspect demand status, goods stage and operational authority rather than substitute the same leader’s name.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger details, returns and outstanding confirmations. Repeat after adding this purchase confirmation and preserve remaining tentative demand. Unchanged sources must not become newly completed evidence, and repeated triggering is not repeated successful procurement. External ordering, supplier acknowledgment and pickup notices need separate receipts. Deduplication and business writes require actual implementation; the task plan itself is not a purchasing action.
