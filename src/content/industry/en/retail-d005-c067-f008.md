# Group-buy leader operations: workflow routing for procurement

## What the documents look like

Community group-buy leader operations manage community nodes and commissions, and do not perform cold-chain quality inspection directly. For the inventory procurement and supplier records task, the working documents are leader agreements, commission tables, pickup lists and community announcements. These come from the group-buy backend, leader community groups, order details and after-sales records. The update rhythm is daily or per group, which affects the time range used when reconciling materials and does not imply batch synchronization or a real-time mechanism.

Workflow routing in this task separates material collection, human judgment and downstream interface actions. The form input node is a user interaction node: when it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user completes the interaction. It collects required information and passes that information to later nodes after submission. The user selection node is also a user interaction node, and after the user makes a choice it executes the corresponding branch. Both nodes collect values and route branches. They do not complete business verification, approval or external synchronization.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Interaction nodes handle collection and branching only. Order writes, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process. A retrieval result or a submitted form does not constitute authorization to execute. Semantic recall and relevance scores do not carry permission or entity isolation. Libraries built on different vector models cannot be associated together; identifiers must be checked first and non-matching items excluded, and no cross-model retrieval is performed to compare missed recalls.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: the source material reads, "The signature page of the leader agreement states that Party A is a community group-buy platform and Party B is leader Zhang, with a signing date of March 1, 2025; the commission table states that leader Zhang's group has a commission rate of five percent for March 2025, settled monthly; the pickup list states that on March 10, 2025 the group picked up 120 items." The draft sentence to be judged is, "Leader Zhang's agreement has taken effect, and the platform has completed the March settlement according to the commission table." This draft sentence merges three facts: signing, taking effect and settlement. The source states only the signing date and does not state an effective clause. The commission table states only a rate and a cycle, and does not state that settlement was completed. Judgment requires the same subject, the same period and the same field. A signing date and an effective clause are different fields; when the effective clause is not stated, the claim is unsupported rather than negated or contradictory. Settlement completion requires a settlement record field, and its absence is recorded as a gap.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as completed authorization. The form input node collects required information and passes it to later nodes; it does not perform approval or writes. If a draft sentence reads "the agreement takes effect after submission," the input is the signature page source, the comparison field is the effective clause, and when the source does not state it the conclusion is unsupported.
2. Treating a user selection branch as a business determination. The user selection node executes a branch after a choice; it does not judge whether materials are genuine. If "materials complete" is used to conclude "commission settled," the input is the commission table source, the comparison field is the settlement record, and when it is missing the result is recorded as a gap.
3. Treating a retrieval hit as proof of permission. Retrieval and relevance do not carry entity isolation. If retrieving a leader agreement is used to conclude that the leader may view the commission table, the input is the retrieval result and the comparison object is the authorization field; these are different subject fields and the conclusion remains unverified.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the leader agreement signature page source from the hypothetical exercise sample and the draft sentence "Leader Zhang's agreement has taken effect." Compare the signing date field and the effective clause field. When the signing date is stated and the effective clause is not stated, the draft sentence is unsupported and the gap is recorded.
2. Input the commission table source and the draft sentence "the platform has completed the March settlement according to the commission table." Compare the commission rate, settlement cycle and settlement record fields. When the rate and cycle are stated and the settlement record is not stated, settlement completion is unsupported and is not called a negation.
3. Input the pickup list source and the request "start replenishment according to the pickup list." Compare the pickup date, quantity and replenishment authorization fields. When the date and quantity are stated and replenishment authorization is not stated, the request remains pending verification and no replenishment action is executed. An empty-result handoff can be checked against a downstream design using the explicit paper input [], without claiming that retrieval necessarily returns [].
