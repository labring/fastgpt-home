# Group-buy presale orders: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Group-buy presale orders, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Periodic pre-sale consolidation review checks cut-off coverage and procurement-plan status. A consolidated result is not supplier confirmation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed pre-sale sheet includes unconfirmed combinations. The cut-off notice covers vegetables, the consolidated list also includes fruit, and procurement remains a draft. Periodic reading can inspect supported coverage without assuming every entry becomes a valid order at a scheduled time. Verify trigger entry and source reading in the actual deployment. Updated files do not establish confirmed demand, and consolidation output does not establish completed procurement or supplier acceptance.

## Three common errors

Expanded cut-off coverage needs the notice passage beside consolidated categories. Draft promoted to commitment needs supplier confirmation. Erased pending status requires comparison of this run’s input and output. These inspect scope, the other party’s position and transfer completeness rather than rely on periodic execution.

## How to verify the setup

Retain input versions and run results. Add the fruit-specific notice and inspect only that coverage change while leaving supplier confirmation unresolved. Earlier runs cannot fill unconfirmed combinations. Ordering, stock reservation and community notification need separate steps and receipts. Check repeated handling against the actual implementation. A passed material review establishes neither executed procurement nor guaranteed supply.
