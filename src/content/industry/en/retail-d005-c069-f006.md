# Dealer rebate calculation: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Dealer rebate calculation, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Rebate-related procurement review compares policy applicability with transaction scope. A scheduled task does not calculate entitlement or confirm accounting execution. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed policy excerpt covers purchases in one channel, while an achievement sheet mixes other channels and an unsigned reconciliation draft lacks current confirmation. Periodic review can organize scope differences and evidence gaps without thresholds, rates or amounts. A report title does not establish rebate entitlement. Inspect the policy and transaction passages actually read after triggering; prior-period policy cannot silently become current evidence. Procurement changes and reconciliation execution need other records.

## Three common errors

Mixed channels require policy applicability beside transaction origins. Policy carried across periods needs the acquired version and its period relationship. Unsigned reconciliation promoted to handled rebate needs current confirmation and processing evidence. These inspect coverage, version and execution independently of task output.

## How to verify the setup

Retain policy passages and scope differences before and after triggering. Repeat with channel-classification evidence and check that out-of-scope transactions remain distinct. Classification alone does not confirm reconciliation. Procurement amendments, formal calculation and accounting writes require separate authority and receipts. Inspect repeated handling under the actual external-trigger design without deriving a payment schedule or promise from the review interval.
