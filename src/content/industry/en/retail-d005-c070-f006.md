# Cross-border product labels: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Cross-border product labels, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Cross-border procurement preparation follows label versions and inspected subjects. Periodically obtaining files does not establish permission to sell the goods. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed English label changes the packaging description, while test material still concerns an older packaging sample and safety wording omits the current variant. Periodic review can list mismatches and missing sources without deciding regulations or platform admission. Verify the available trigger and files actually read, then inspect their subject passages. A report and label existing together does not make them the same version, and successful reading does not authorize procurement release.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Cross-border procurement review | Proposed distinction | Correspondence check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label version | New packaging wording | Relationship to inspected sample |
| Test subject | Older packaging | Not automatically current version |
| Safety material | Current variant omitted | Actual coverage evidence |
| Triggered acquisition | Current files obtained | Not approval evidence |
| Formal business | Replacement, approval and release separately checked | No admission promise |

## Three common errors

Crossed versions need evidence linking the new label and inspected sample. Expanded variant coverage requires the original safety scope beside output wording. Acquisition promoted to approval needs actual approval or handling evidence. These inspect correspondence, applicability and result status rather than complete-looking filenames.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger records and each document’s subject. Repeat after adding current-variant correspondence and change only the supported relationship. Missing testing or label material remains assigned for collection without reusing old conclusions. Label replacement, procurement approval and sale publication need separate authority and receipts. Repeated reading and business writes follow actual implementation. Source review supplies neither a compliance opinion nor a promise of permission to sell.
