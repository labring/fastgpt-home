# Cross-border payment reviews: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Cross-border payment reviews, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Payment-risk procurement review preserves order identity and the separate scope of payment disputes and logistics evidence. A list label does not establish the underlying transaction fact. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Proposed procurement wording refers to an order list. A chargeback notice concerns only one order, while a logistics receipt does not explain its recipient’s relationship to that order. Periodic review can inspect missing correspondence without deciding fraud from a list title or automatically cancelling or releasing procurement. Verify the actual trigger and read contract, then inspect input coverage. Invoice titles, logistics records and payment status cannot substitute for one another; no risk thresholds or payment rules are supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Payment-dispute procurement review | Proposed gap | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order list | Classification is not fact | Actual case evidence |
| Chargeback notice | One order only | Not the whole list |
| Logistics receipt | Recipient relationship unclear | Actual order correspondence |
| Triggered read | Retain actual response | Not cancelled procurement |
| Payment handling | Holds and release separately checked | Review does not decide disposition |

## Three common errors

Label promoted to fact needs actual case evidence before calling fraud established. Mislinked logistics needs order and recipient correspondence. Reading promoted to disposition needs an actual handling receipt before declaring procurement cancelled. These inspect assertion evidence, event ownership and action stage rather than rely on scheduled execution.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger origin, order mapping and actual response. Add this logistics relationship and change only the covered interpretation. Leave payment disputes unresolved while their evidence remains missing; previous labels are not new evidence. Payment handling, procurement holds or releases and external notices need separate authority and receipts. Inspect repeated handling under the actual design. Complete-looking material does not establish eliminated transaction risk.
