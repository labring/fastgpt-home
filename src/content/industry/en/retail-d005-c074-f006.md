# Cross-border resale returns: procurement with scheduled workflows

## What the documents look like

For procurement in Cross-border resale returns, this page examines FastGPT scheduled workflows. Returned-goods preparation separates inspection coverage, refurbishment records and resale lists. Periodically updated material does not establish completed refurbishment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

A proposed return inspection leaves the shell condition unresolved. A preparation list names planned replacement parts, while a resale sheet remains a draft. Periodic review inspects whether actual evidence arrived without describing planned replacement as performed repair or treating a resale title as listing permission. Verify trigger and read scope, preserving the item named in each source. Another returned item’s scrap record cannot explain this item’s destination or decide parts procurement.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Refurbishment procurement preparation | Proposed state | Corresponding source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shell inspection | Unresolved | Not automatically intact |
| Parts arrangement | Replacement planned | Actual refurbishment record |
| Scrap subject | Possible other return | This item’s origin relationship |
| Resale sheet | Draft | Separate listing decision |
| Formal execution | Parts, inventory and release separately checked | Update is not completion |

| `Repeated-trigger responsibility` | Implement deduplication, idempotency and writes explicitly. |

## Three common errors

Plan promoted to repair needs the actual refurbishment record. Another item’s scrap disposition requires subject and origin checks. Draft list promoted to sale permission needs actual business approval evidence. These inspect work stage, item ownership and decision status independently of source updates.

## How to verify the setup

Retain trigger results and item correspondence. Repeat after adding an actual refurbishment record and inspect the limited repair-state change. Keep shell inspection or listing decisions unresolved until their own evidence arrives. Parts procurement, inventory changes and resale publication need separate authority and receipts. Inspect deduplication and repeated writes under the real design. This review supplies neither a quality guarantee nor a resale commitment.
