# Creator sales campaigns: scheduled workflows for procurement

## What the documents look like

Creator special sales campaigns depend on external creator fulfilment and commission settlement rather than internal brand scheduling. Brand commerce staff, MCN agencies and creator managers work around selected alliance data, creator marketplace data, creator history and store transaction details. This page addresses one task: inventory procurement and supplier records inside that category, with scheduled workflows and automation as the feature under review. The working documents are creator cooperation agreements, commission setting forms, sample shipment forms and live campaign reports. The supplied brief gives update frequency only at the level of per campaign and per week, so no batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time or transaction-triggered mechanism follows from it.

These characteristics place a boundary on what a scheduled workflow can do. A scheduled task can read configured material and initiate a request. Business authorization, approval and database writes require their own downstream implementation. Retrieval and form collection gather values; they do not carry permission to act on those values.

## Constraints at the scheduled workflows step

Trigger responsibility needs to be stated explicitly: whether the application-side scheduled capability or an external scheduler performs the trigger, checked against the actual version in use. External data is read through a configured interface, and a scheduled trigger by itself does not establish a connection to a business system. HTTP requests are checked against the external interface document for method, headers, body and variable inputs. Deduplication, idempotency and business writes need explicit design in the interface or a later flow, and are not automatically present. Execution records are compared against trigger time, actual input, returned result and business status; the existence of a task does not substitute for a successful execution.

Hypothetical drill sample: a brand commerce staff member holds two items for review. Item one is a creator cooperation agreement whose text reads: "Party A commissions Party B to complete two special live sessions between 1 June 2025 and 30 June 2025; samples are sent by Party A; commission is settled according to store transaction details." Item two is a sample shipment form whose text reads: "Recipient: receiving person designated by Party B; shipment quantity: three samples per session; shipment status is pending dispatch." A handover request awaiting a decision reads: "Please have the scheduled task automatically confirm that the cooperation has taken effect on 1 June and synchronize supplier inventory." This request treats a pending-dispatch status as completed shipment, treats the agreement period as an effective cooperation, and lacks a supported authorization boundary.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a pending-dispatch status as completed shipment. Item two states only "shipment status is pending dispatch" and gives no dispatch time or receipt result. That status cannot support automatic confirmation that shipment is complete.
2. Treating the agreement period as an effective cooperation. Item one states only a commissioned period and gives no signature, effectiveness or authorization clause. It cannot support triggering an automatic confirmation that the cooperation has taken effect.
3. Treating retrieval or a form as authorized execution. Retrieval and forms collect values; business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization each need their own downstream implementation. The request phrase "synchronize supplier inventory" has no corresponding downstream design.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the text of item one and the text of item two. Compare the two values "pending dispatch" and "completed shipment". Shipment completion is supported only when item two shows a dispatch time and a receipt result; the current record is unsupported.
2. Input the text of item one and the pending handover request. Compare the two facts "agreement period" and "effective cooperation". Effective cooperation is supported only when item one shows a signature or effectiveness clause; the current record remains to be verified.
3. Input the pending handover request and the configuration table. Compare the phrase "synchronize supplier inventory" in the request against the downstream implementation field. The action can be executed only when an explicit downstream interface or write design exists; the current record is a gap, and the missing evidence is retained as missing.

These checks concern only this page's scope: creator special sales campaigns, inventory procurement and supplier records, scheduled workflows and automation. Which materials, operations and requests sit at which authorization state during handover cannot be resolved by treating retrieval or a form as authorized execution.
