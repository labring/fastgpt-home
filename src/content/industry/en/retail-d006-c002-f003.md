# MCN operations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with review and scheduling exercises while preserving assumptions and boundaries around actual account sources. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share pairs content-review comments with a schedule, labeled a classroom case without actual creator records. Inspect visible conditions through the complete link, then request a restricted handling original. Retain exposed account wording to investigate grants. If exercise comments become a platform finding, separately inspect source identity in input. Visible classroom material establishes neither backend isolation nor actual occurrence of scheduling or review events.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Review and schedule exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom identity | Comments and schedule labeled proposed | No claim of a platform finding |
| Actual handling | Operational violation note excluded | Returned passages and grant scope |
| Browser handoff | Delivery of prior comment and assumption | Provide exercise when missing |
| Permission boundary | Review exercise grants no publication permission | Obtain actual normative evidence |
| Subsequent system | Separate solving, publication, account change and scoring | Inspect execution and identity conditions |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If a classroom entry reads an actual violation note, retain passages and full response to inspect grants. Avoiding creator data in the exercise does not isolate other resources.

- If a new browser lacks the exercise behind a broadcasting question, inspect history and obtain the original. Anonymous identity provides no automatic handoff or borrowed review outcome.

- If hypothetical review comments become platform findings or publishing permission, compare exercise identity, input and output. Explanation supplies no actual content-approval record.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised comments and restricted handling sources, recording originals, denials and versions. Inspect usage and identity capability by version, including commercial-edition scope and returns for third-party validation where required. Solving an exercise can distinguish review comments from scheduling without publishing a livestream, changing an account or writing grades. Learning records and business actions require independent execution checks. Preserve assumptions rather than treating completed training as approved content publication.
