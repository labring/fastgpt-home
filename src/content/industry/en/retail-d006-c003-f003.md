# Shopify fulfillment: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Validate a training share with fulfillment-stage exercises while separating classroom orders, actual shipping sources and anonymous learning history. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training entry uses order and label fragments to explain creation, dispatch and carrier events without actual addresses. Inspect classroom identity through the complete link, then request restricted shipping templates or payment sources. Retain exposed operating text and resource scope to investigate grants. Avoiding real addresses in the exercise does not isolate other sources. Its stages must remain distinguishable from actual parcel fulfillment throughout the explanation.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Fulfillment-stage exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom identity | Order and label fragments are proposed | No claim of an actual parcel result |
| Actual sources | Shipping and payment material excluded | Full response and resource scope |
| Browser change | Reference to prior order and conditions | Provide absent exercise |
| Policy gap | Logistics fragments supply no refund terms | Obtain applicable material |
| Subsequent execution | Separate solving, refunds, edits and scoring | Grade writing and identity conditions |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If fulfillment exercises expose actual shipping or payment sources, retain full response and scope. Removing addresses from classroom text does not isolate backend material.

- If refund follow-up in another browser lacks the exercise order, inspect history and obtain request and conditions. Anonymous sessions inherit neither an order nor a policy automatically.

- If logistics fragments become refund terms or actual fulfillment, inspect purchase conditions and events. Classroom stages supply no such support, and explanation executes no refund or order edit.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised stages and restricted actual material, recording originals, denials and versions alongside applicable usage conditions. Required third-party identity needs separate commercial scope and return checks. Solving an exercise can explain event verification without issuing refunds, buying labels or editing orders. Scoring and learning records require subsequent execution evidence; a generated answer establishes no written grade. Preserve classroom identity and gaps so training text cannot become an actual customer-handling result.
