# Temu semi-managed sellers: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Let learners read declaration, stock and fulfillment stages while testing classroom identity and boundaries around actual warehouse sources. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share pairs declaration and overseas-stock fragments, identifying proposed submission and exercise records without actual warehouse quantities. Inspect headings and classroom limitation through the complete link, then request restricted procurement or another warehouse’s sources. Retain exposed originals and full responses to investigate grants. An exercise without actual data does not isolate backend material. Its declaration cannot become platform acceptance; preserve source identity and stage throughout the explanation.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Declaration and stock exercise | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom identity | Separate proposed submission and exercise stock | No claim of actual platform acceptance |
| Operational restriction | Procurement and other warehouse sources excluded | Full response and grant scope |
| Browser history | Delivery of product, warehouse and period | Provide absent exercise text |
| Operating gap | Stock fragment is not saleable quantity | Obtain rules, allocations and actual result |
| Subsequent action | Separate solving, submission, stock edits and scores | Actual execution and identity conditions |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If classroom stock sharing retrieves actual procurement or another warehouse, retain passages and full response. Avoiding real quantities in the exercise establishes no restricted backend scope.

- If a changed browser lacks product, warehouse or period for saleability, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically fill gaps from another warehouse.

- If proposed submission or exercise stock becomes accepted or currently saleable, inspect classroom identity and actual rules or allocations. Fragments supply no operational outcome; link validity refreshes no stock.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised periods and restricted originals for reads and denials, retaining passages and versions alongside required identity and usage conditions. Third-party validation needs commercial scope and return checks where required. Explanations can organize missing relationships without submitting declarations, changing stock or writing grades. Learning records and backend actions need subsequent execution tests; generated answers do not establish success. Preserve classroom timing and source limitations so an exercise never becomes a real-time operational result.
