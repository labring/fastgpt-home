# TikTok Shop US operations: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace translated replies, original wording and issue classification independently in service training. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed TikTok Shop service exercise contains the buyer wording the lid feels loose, a Chinese practice translation asserting existing damage, a note marking an assembly issue for verification and guidance to clarify the symptom first. The question asks whether the translation supports announcing confirmed damage. The original expresses a feeling, the classification remains provisional and the guidance requests clarification. Retain both language identities. Greater certainty in a translation cannot retroactively become an observation supplied by the buyer or evidence of an actual diagnosed product condition.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Teaching identity and evidence scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original certainty | Feels loose | Do not make it confirmed damage. |
| Translation identity | Practice rendering | Compare with original sentence. |
| Classification support | Assembly cause unverified | Do not infer cause from title. |
| Guidance action | Clarify first | Do not call it a completed exchange. |
| Added dialogue | Still hypothetical practice | Retain source identity. |

## Three common errors

First, feels loose may become already damaged; compare certainty and observed object across the two texts, preserving what needs clarification. Second, a provisional classification may become an established assembly cause; inspect supporting evidence and actual verification instead of deriving causation from a note title. Third, clarify first may become a completed exchange with a reply; inspect actual dialogue records, since an instruction records no event. These concern language strength, causal evidence and conversational action. Fluent translation cannot replace correspondence to the original sentence or the missing operational record.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the buyer said, what the translation added and which stages the note and guidance describe, checking actual references and language identity. Translation-only input requires investigation of original wording reaching the answer and establishes no verified meaning. Guidance alone supplies no buyer response. If further hypothetical dialogue is added, keep it labelled as practice instead of an actual complaint. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. This page diagnoses no fault, arranges no compensation and establishes no completed conversation or solved case from the proposed classroom texts.
