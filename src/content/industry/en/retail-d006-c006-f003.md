# General export declarations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Validate a training entry with exercise documents while preserving handout versions, learner sessions and the boundary around operational originals. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training entry uses a de-identified packing exercise with net- and gross-weight labels, without opening operational contracts. Use the complete publishing-channel address and inspect the handout visible in the learner’s environment. If a question retrieves an operational appendix outside the exercise, retain the full response and investigate grants. Removing names from a handout does not establish backend isolation, and a functioning entry does not substitute for account or resource-scope verification.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-entry check | Packing-exercise scope | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exercise source | Keep fields without opening operational contracts | Originals readable by the learner |
| Boundary question | Operational appendix excluded from the exercise | Full response and grant scope |
| Browser change | Actual delivery of the previous exercise | Provide source or manage sessions separately |
| Version condition | Test corrected and earlier handouts | Adopted originals and entry limits |
| Subsequent record | Separate explanation from grade writing | Inspect scoring and actual execution |

## Three common errors

- If packing exercises retrieve operational contracts, retain question and full response to inspect grants. Removing names from a handout does not isolate actual appendices.

- If another browser lacks the previous question and answer, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute another document question.

- If earlier labels overwrite corrected handouts, retain versions and citations to inspect selection and delivery. Solving a course establishes neither actual filing confirmation nor written grades.

## How to verify the setup

Test visibility of corrected and earlier handouts, recording the version cited. Inspect expiry, access limits and usage responsibility for the actual entry. Where identity validation is required, verify version capability separately rather than treating the link identifier as learner identity. An explanation does not establish a written grade or a processed declaration; verify subsequent scoring and writing independently. Preserve the exercise label so proposed classroom data is never presented as an actual operational case.
