# General import declarations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with import-document roles while separating classroom material from actual filing evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training entry distinguishes consignee, notify party and payer in bill and invoice text without actual company data. Inspect the exercise and limitations through the complete link, then request restricted contract or payment sources. Fictional data does not isolate other resources, and classroom explanation determines no actual duty or filing outcome. Record entry identity, exercise version and readable sources rather than checking only whether the answer names a correct role.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-entry check | Import-role exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role original | Separate receipt, notice and payment | Exercise and limits visible to learner |
| Actual source | Contract and payment evidence excluded | Full response and grant scope |
| Historical subject | Person meant by the previous task | Provide exercise across browsers |
| Authorization boundary | Classroom roles grant no filing rights | Owner and system evidence separately |
| Subsequent action | Separate solving, payment and grading | Actual execution for each |

## Three common errors

- If a role exercise reads actual contracts or payment appendices, retain originals and full response. Removing company names does not restrict backend material.

- If another browser lacks the subject behind the previous task, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically guess an operator account.

- If classroom roles become actual filing or payment authorization, compare hypothetical limits and output. Real rights need owner and system evidence absent from the exercise.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised roles and restricted originals, recording passages, denials and versions alongside applicable identity conditions. A new exercise visible in one browser establishes no cross-device history. Provide earlier material or manage sessions separately where needed. Explanation submits no declaration, executes no payment and writes no grade. Learning records and business actions need independent execution checks; preserve classroom status rather than treating finished reading as completed import or training administration.
