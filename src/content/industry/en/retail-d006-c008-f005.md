# Amazon FBA preparation: staff training with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Use fragile-item guidance to inspect task boundaries and packing conditions in training follow-ups. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

The training attachment supplies only the instructions to handle fragile items with care and avoid squeezing. An answer can explain those statements, without expanding them into a complete packing procedure. For a follow-up about operations, inspect actual history and current sources, checking that a previous product-copywriting claim does not become a work instruction. Record which instructions can be traced and which steps remain undocumented, keeping this lesson tied to the fragile-item attachment.

## Three common errors

- If gentle-handling guidance becomes a complete packing SOP, trace steps to an actual manual. One fragility note supplies no full opening, filling and sealing procedure.

- If coexistence of bubble wrap and foam becomes a mandatory choice, retain both sources, positions and questions. Locate the added exclusion rather than selecting a procedure through follow-up.

- If earphone noise-reduction copy enters glassware training, inspect historical products and current originals. Retain the carried-over statement; marketing features cannot replace operational guidance.

## How to verify the setup

The glass cup lacks a complete training manual. One fragile-item warning does not cover every operating step. In follow-ups about opening, filling or sealing a package, inspect whether the answer supplies procedures absent from the source or imports them from history. After obtaining the manual, retain its version and inspect the originals actually received in the new turn. Repeat the initial question and an exception question, tracing each operation to its basis. Prior conversational explanation does not automatically become the current training rule.
