# Amazon FBA preparation: workflow routing for staff training

## What the documents look like

FBA preparation moves inventory into platform warehouses, so the operating risk concentrates on inbound receiving and inventory age. The working materials for this category are the FBA inbound plan, carton labels, inventory reports and compensation claims. These materials refresh daily and weekly, and store service and internal training schedules handovers around that rhythm.

For store service and internal training, workflow routing has to answer a narrow question: when an operations person hands an inbound plan, a carton label task or a compensation claim to a store or a trainee, which materials, actions and requests sit at which authorization state. The Form Input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and the workflow continues after the user completes the interaction. The node collects the required information the user fills in and passes it to later nodes. The User Selection node is also a user interaction node; when the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the corresponding branch. Both nodes complete information collection and branch direction only. They do not constitute approval, database writes or external synchronization.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Hypothetical paper exercise, not actually run. Material text available for checking: "Store training handover form: subject is the East China FBA preparation operations group; object is the store service specialist; matter is inbound plan and carton label production training; status is pending review; effective scope is the March 2025 batch." A draft sentence awaiting judgment: "This handover form has been approved, and the store may modify carton labels on its own and submit the inbound shipment."

The constraints come from the documented behavior of the nodes and from the division of labor around business actions. Interaction nodes record state and wait for the user to complete the interaction before continuing, which is documented behavior. Order writes, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process. The draft sentence turns "pending review" into "approved" and assigns carton label modification and inbound submission to the store. Neither has material support.

The judgment rule requires the same subject, the same period and the same field. The handover form status field reads "pending review." The draft sentence states "approved." These are mutually exclusive values in the same field, so the pair is contradictory. The authorization scope field does not state that the store may modify carton labels, so that part is unsupported. A pending review does not by itself show that an operating permission is absent; it only shows that the review step has not been recorded as complete. Missing evidence stays missing.

## Three common errors

1. Treating "pending review" as "approved." Input is the handover form status field. Comparison object is the draft sentence wording "approved." The two values are mutually exclusive in the same field, and the observable result is a contradiction.
2. Treating information collected by a form as authorized execution. Input is the required information collected by the Form Input node. Comparison object is the carton label modification permission, which the material does not state. The observable result is that the permission is unsupported.
3. Treating the "yes" branch of the User Selection node as approval. Input is the branch direction taken by the User Selection node. Comparison object is the authorization scope field on the handover form. The observable result is that branch execution does not equal an effective approval.

2. Take the material text "effective scope is the March 2025 batch" and the draft sentence "the store may modify carton labels on its own." Compare the authorization scope field.
