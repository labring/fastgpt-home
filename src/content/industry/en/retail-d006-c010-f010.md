# JD POP seller operations: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Keep hypotheses, troubleshooting guidance and findings at their stated evidence levels. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed JD POP service lesson describes fluctuating headphone volume, an instructor note saying connection may be involved and guidance to ask whether the customer tried another device. The learner answer says a device was changed and the cause is connection. No customer reply or inspection finding is supplied. Preserve symptom, possible cause and proposed question separately. Mentioning connection establishes no diagnosis, and a whether question in guidance cannot become an operation that the customer has already performed or evidence that such an operation produced a result.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Teaching identity and evidence scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Symptom wording | Fluctuating volume | Do not invent cause. |
| Hypothesis wording | May be involved | Seek actual diagnostic support. |
| Question state | Whether device changed | Do not make it completed action. |
| Submission identity | Learning output | Trace added facts. |
| Outcome limit | No improvement or resolution supplied | Do not infer measured effect. |

## Three common errors

First, a possible connection issue may become a confirmed cause; inspect tentative wording and actual diagnostic support instead of treating a note as a finding. Second, whether another device was tried may become a completed action; inspect the customer reply and named device, since a question records no action. Third, the learner answer may become new factual evidence; compare the scenario and submission and trace each addition. These require causal support, question-answer state and source identity. A complete-sounding answer cannot fill missing observations through a citation to the learner’s own text.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what symptom is described, which possibility the instructor proposes and what should be clarified next, tracing actual references. A note alone establishes no reviewed case or solved issue. Guidance alone contains no customer action record. Added hypothetical replies remain test material instead of real inspection results. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. The page prescribes no actual repair procedure and decides no warranty responsibility. It also claims no benefit from switching devices; real diagnosis and handling outcomes require applicable technical and operational evidence beyond the proposed lesson.
