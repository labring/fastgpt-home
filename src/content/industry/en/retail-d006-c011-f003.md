# Refund-only disputes: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Refund-only disputes, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with shipping and receipt exercises, without treating classroom evidence as platform adjudication or actual payment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training entry supplies outbound, receipt and refund-request fragments without actual orders, asking what each can support. Inspect classroom labels and sources through the complete link, then request restricted chats or appeal originals. No real buyer information in the exercise does not isolate other material. A readable signature also establishes neither item condition nor platform decision. Preserve exercise identity and source object instead of merely asking for repeated status words.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Shipping and receipt exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom material | Separate outbound, signature and request | Identity and addressed subject |
| Operational restriction | Formal chats and appeals excluded | Full response and grants |
| Browser handoff | Delivery of prior order and assumptions | Provide absent exercise |
| Evidence scope | Signature decides no condition or refund | Obtain outcomes and applicable evidence |
| Subsequent system | Separate solving, refunds, evidence and scores | Actual execution and entry conditions |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If a classroom entry returns actual chats or another case’s evidence, retain passages and full response. De-identification replaces no resource test.

- If another browser lacks the order and assumptions behind prior handling, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity supplies neither a platform rule nor another case automatically.

- If a signature fragment becomes normal goods, accepted appeal or completed refund, compare source scope with actual outcomes. Classroom records decide none of these matters; retain gaps.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised material and restricted actual sources, recording readable passages, denials and versions alongside entry and identity conditions. After changes, inspect current input rather than using generation date as evidence of refreshed exercise content. Solving performs no refund, evidence upload or grade writing. Learning records and business actions need separate execution checks. Preserve proposed identity and missing conditions instead of turning ended classroom discussion into a resolved actual dispute.
