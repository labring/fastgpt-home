# Convenience fresh-food orders: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Fresh-food ordering training can collect the learner’s procedural question, spoilage passage and near-expiry handling wording, with temperature material as a separate source. Proposed text reports spoilage without corresponding temperature evidence, supplying neither a cause nor a storage threshold. Inspect source status downstream: ordering arrangements, handling and measurements answer different questions. A spoilage form alone establishes neither batch quality nor correct store practice.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question scope | Ordering separate from handling wording | No quality-identification claim |
| Causal gap | Spoilage present; temperature source absent | Occurrence does not establish cause |
| Shift mismatch | Another shift’s record passed downstream | Inspect time and batch before removal |
| Added judgment | Spoilage wording becomes a cause | Repair against the passage and retest |
| Business owner | Separate recognition and actual handling | Submission is not business approval |

## Three common errors

Test choices to read ordering instructions, obtain temperature material or refer batch checks to the owner. If the source route passes another shift’s record, inspect timing and batch correspondence while retaining the present gap. If a spoilage statement becomes a causal conclusion downstream, compare the passage and added judgment, repair and retest. A learner’s selection does not establish reviewed business evidence. If an order form becomes arrived goods in the training answer, inspect the demand passage and this batch’s actual receipt evidence. Planned ordering differs from goods receipt; do not supply an arrival outcome without the corresponding record.

## How to verify the setup

Trace question, submission, received scope and answer origins. Repeat with corresponding batch material, preserving record-versus-cause distinctions. Waiting and continuation do not automatically retrieve device or warehouse data. Training recognition, actual ordering, food handling and record writes require responsible-party authority and evidence. Completion supports inspection of the handoff without constituting quality identification, approved handling or passed competence.
