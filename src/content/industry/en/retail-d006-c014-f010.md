# Convenience fresh-food orders: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace fresh-food category prompts, question cards and learner observations by product subject. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed fresh-food lesson instructs learners to read the product label, asks which batch a record belongs to and records a learner observation that the outer bag remains unopened. A different product’s sample label refers to its own instructions. The exercise asks which sources are still needed for the current sandwich. The packet supports locating label and batch evidence, but an unopened bag contains no complete label text and another product’s example supplies no current conditions. Declaring storage correct would exceed the observations and material actually given in this classroom scenario.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Teaching identity and evidence scope | Acceptance action |

| Label subject | Other product does not replace current one | Check product identity. |
| Observation scope | Bag unopened | Do not infer correct storage. |
| Question state | Batch awaits answer | Seek actual response. |

## Three common errors

First, another product’s sample label may apply to the sandwich; inspect product and label identity instead of transferring through similar packaging. Second, an unopened bag may establish correct storage; inspect current conditions and actual observations, since a packaging action decides no product state. Third, a question asking which batch may become verified batch identity; seek the exercise reply and corresponding record. A prompt supplies no answer. These require label applicability, observation dimension and question-answer state rather than a general impression that the teaching packet already contains all necessary evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the instruction requires reading, what the learner observed and what the question still seeks, inspecting actual references. Another product’s label leaves the current label unresolved and decides no saleability. An observation alone supplies no storage conditions. Added hypothetical labels and replies need the exercise’s goods and batch matched and remain labelled practice. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. This page prescribes no storage standard and executes no product disposition. It treats no classroom answer as an actual food-condition finding or completed training-assessment result for the learner.
