# Letter-of-credit documents: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with proposed document passages, separating textual findings, bank comments and formal examination outcomes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

Training can propose beneficiary, seller and supplementary-notice passages for learners to identify correspondence and missing sources, without examination rules or customer information. Make exercise identity visible through the classroom link, then request restricted operational documents. Matching names support that textual comparison rather than eligibility, authority or bank acceptance. Different currency wording identifies an unresolved difference. Access to an exercise supplies no formal transaction decision and should preserve the limited meaning of each passage.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Document-training share | Exercise boundary | Actual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed content | Beneficiary, seller and supplement | Exercise identity and version |
| Operational restriction | Customer sources and bank comments | Full response and denial |
| Across browsers | Delivery of prior passages | Obtain absent originals |
| Text comparison | Matches and differences decide no examination | Exercise limits and processing records |
| Subsequent execution | Separate solving, presentation and scores | Actual learning and business records |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If classroom sharing returns actual customer documents or internal bank comments, retain originals and full answers. Sanitized exercises establish no isolation of operational sources.

- If another browser asks whether the prior exercise can be presented without its passages, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically supply missing material.

- If a learner’s textual match becomes passed examination or completed presentation, compare exercise limits and actual processing evidence. Reading replaces neither a bank decision nor execution.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial passages, a revised seller statement and restricted actual sources separately, recording versions, responses and denials. Verify required identity conditions under actual capability rather than treating a classroom credential as customer identity. Distinguish supplied passages omitted from input from an incomplete exercise. Expiry updates no documents. Solving submits nothing and writes no formal score; learning records and transaction processing require separate execution evidence. Keep outstanding originals visible instead of describing the business as completed.
