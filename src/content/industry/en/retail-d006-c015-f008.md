# Letter-of-credit documents: workflow routing for staff training

## What the documents look like

Letter-of-credit document examination and presentation serves the paying conditions set by the issuing bank. The material set for each transaction includes the letter of credit, commercial invoice, bill of lading, insurance policy and draft. The comparison focus is consistency across documents rather than tax matching, which distinguishes this category from export tax rebate filing. Data arrives from the issuing bank notice, UCP600 clauses, freight forwarder bills of lading and bank presentation records, and the material set refreshes with each transaction.

In a store service and internal training setting, staff need to keep material status, operation status and authorization status separate. A retrieved record or a completed form entry is not a finished business action. Document examiners, bank international settlement staff and exporters hand work between these states, so the workflow must show what has been collected, what has been decided and what still requires a downstream interface or manual step.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The form input node enters an interaction state when triggered, collects required information, and passes the submitted information to later nodes. The user selection node enters an interaction state when triggered, and after the user makes a choice it evaluates that choice and executes the corresponding "yes" branch. The recorded interaction state is behavior of the interaction node; no separate global state-recording switch is documented. Collected information must be explicitly passed to later retrieval, judgment or interface nodes, and outputs must be compared item by item against inputs. Order writing, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process; the form itself does not perform these actions.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a store receives a letter of credit notice. The material text reads "Issuing bank: Bank A; Applicant: Company B; Beneficiary: Company C; Goods: cotton men's shirts; Latest shipment date: 30 June 2025; Presentation period: within 21 days after shipment." A draft sentence states "This transaction has passed document examination and completed presentation." The pending request is "Please update the above material status to presented." This sample is used only for paper comparison and does not refer to a real customer or real document.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as business completion. The form input node only collects and passes information. The draft sentence "This transaction has passed document examination and completed presentation" lacks independent records of examination and presentation, so the status cannot be updated on that basis.

2. Treating a user selection branch as authorized execution. The user selection node executes the "yes" branch, which indicates only that the path was chosen. It does not indicate that order writing or external synchronization has occurred.

3. Treating retrieval results as a permission or entity filter. A similarity setting does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records, and retrieval by itself does not constitute authorization.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample material text and the draft sentence above. Compare whether the draft sentence has independent records of examination and presentation, and observe whether a checkable examination conclusion and presentation evidence exist. When they are absent, record the item as pending verification.

2. Input the pending request "Please update the above material status to presented." Compare whether the request states an authorizing party and an operation boundary, and observe whether the form or selection node only completes information collection. When authorization is not stated, preserve the evidence gap.

3. Check the output handoff. Compare beneficiary, goods and shipment date collected by the form against the inputs of later nodes, item by item, and observe whether the fields match. When they differ or are missing, record the actual observation without inferring a system error or an automatic business action.

This page addresses only letter-of-credit document examination and presentation, store service and internal training, and workflow routing. During operational handoff, staff must distinguish which materials, operations and requests are in which authorization state, and must not treat retrieval or a form entry as authorized execution.
