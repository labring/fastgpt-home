# Letter-of-credit documents: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace format examples, pending checks and instructor comments separately in document-review training. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed document-review lesson has an example demonstrating file order, a practice checklist leaving an item-wording appendix for verification and an instructor comment praising clear arrangement. The learner declares every document compliant with the current requirements. The example teaches sequence, the comment addresses arrangement and the appendix remains missing. Preserve those purposes instead of treating clear presentation as content conformity or assuming full review because no error was mentioned. The exercise contains no actual credit or bank-processing record and supports no formal review opinion about real documents.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Teaching identity and evidence scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example purpose | Arrangement only | Do not make it content answer. |
| Comment object | Clear arrangement | Do not expand to complete approval. |
| Explicit gap | Item-wording appendix | Retain in summary. |
| Item-level review | Actual support absent | Do not invent review opinion. |
| Earlier exercise | Does not replace current appendix | Check exercise attribution. |

## Three common errors

First, a format example may become the correct content answer; inspect its purpose and the exercise’s actual requirements, since form supplies no facts. Second, praise for arrangement may become approval under every requirement; identify the comment’s object and seek item-level content checks. Third, an abstract may omit the pending appendix and declare coverage complete; compare checklist gaps with actual reference contents and obtain the matching appendix. These concern example hierarchy, evaluation scope and material coverage. Positive wording cannot remove an explicitly unresolved source by making the summary sound complete.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the example teaches, what the comment evaluates and what the checklist still lacks, tracing actual references. Instructor comments alone leave exercise requirements and appendix unresolved; invent no discrepancy or readiness assurance. Added appendices need the same exercise and amendment context matched rather than borrowing an earlier example. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. This page accepts no documents for a bank and performs no presentation. A complete learner essay or tidy layout also establishes no passed ability assessment without explicit applicable evidence of an actual evaluation and result.
