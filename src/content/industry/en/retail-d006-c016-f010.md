# Franchisee onboarding: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Distinguish sample schedules, learner suggestions and actual assignments in opening coaching. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed opening-coaching lesson has a sample sequence moving from shelf familiarization to checkout practice, a learner suggestion to reverse it and a note asking for discussion of the reason. An assignment form has no named responsible person. A question about manager approval and completed staffing needs the voices and purposes separated. The sample structures practice, the suggestion proposes change and the note invites discussion. An empty form assigns nobody. Their combined narrative establishes no approved store arrangement, and mentioning a manager supplies no statement from that person or evidence of approval.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Teaching identity and evidence scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speaker | Learner suggests | Do not make it manager approval. |
| Sample structure | Practice sequence | Do not make it universal policy. |
| Assignment content | Form uncompleted | Do not infer staffing complete. |
| Store correspondence | Arrangement unresolved | Inspect matter and identity. |
| Learning result | Competence evidence absent | Do not claim mastery. |

| `Citation output` | The output is an array; an empty array can continue. |

## Three common errors

First, a learner suggestion may become manager approval; inspect speaker attribution and actual confirmation instead of inferring authority from shared attendance. Second, the sample sequence may become a rule for every store; read teaching purpose and the applicable local arrangement rather than elevating practice structure into policy. Third, an empty assignment form may become scheduled personnel; inspect completed contents and actual responsibility mapping, since headings allocate no tasks. These concern confirming subject, applicability and record content. The visual appearance of a schedule cannot replace actual organization or a missing approval event.

## How to verify the setup

Ask who suggests the change, why the example uses its sequence and what the form currently contains, checking roles and states in actual references. A sample alone leaves local arrangements unresolved without proving rejection or mandatory adoption. Added hypothetical confirmation and assignments must remain labelled practice and match store and matter. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. A teaching answer performs no staffing, training or opening approval. It also establishes no checkout competence; actual arrangements and learning results each need independent applicable evidence beyond the lesson’s sample documents.
