# Franchise fee settlement: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training entry with proposed invoicing and reconciliation questions without turning reading practice into actual finance confirmation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

Training can propose payment plans, invoicing text and unresolved reconciliation, without amounts, account numbers or actual vouchers. Learners identify what stage each source supports. Make classroom identity visible through the complete link, then request restricted finance sources. Plans, invoices and receipt concern different observations that can coexist without substituting for each other. Fluent answers establish no balanced account. Training-entry eligibility and operational voucher access need separate checks; attendance grants no universal franchise permissions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Finance-training share | Exercise-source boundary | Actual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed questions | Plans, invoices and reconciliation without vouchers | Classroom identity and stages |
| Operational restriction | Bank vouchers and other-store collection | Full answer and denial |
| Browser history | Prior period and question | Obtain absent material |
| Role distinction | Reading confirms no funds or authority | Limits and execution evidence |
| Record execution | Solving writes no voucher or score | Separate learning and accounting checks |

## Three common errors

- If a classroom link returns actual bank vouchers or other-store collection, retain passages and full answers. Amount-free exercises establish no operational-source isolation.

- If another browser asks whether a prior period was settled without the exercise, inspect history and obtain its period and question. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow an earlier example.

- If identifying plans and invoices confirms funds or grants financial operations, compare exercise limits and actual execution evidence. Reading collects nothing and assigns no role.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised invoicing text and restricted vouchers, recording versions, passages and denials. If answers still treat invoicing as receipt, inspect current material and its stage; entry renewal supplies no funds information. Test required identity conditions under verified capability, without treating a classroom credential as an operational role. Solving writes no voucher, handles no funds and automatically records no score. Learning records and accounting need separate execution checks, while missing actual sources remain unsupported by classroom discussion.
