# Franchise fee settlement: multi-turn prompts for staff training

## What the documents look like
Franchise fee and deposit settlement handles money and breach deductions. It does not handle government franchise filing. The main materials are finance system records, franchise contracts, bank statements, and store violation records. Daily documents include franchise fee invoices, deposit ledgers, deduction notices, and contract payment schedules.

The parties are the franchisee and the chain finance team. The object is a specific fee or deposit. The status may be paid, pending payment, deducted, or pending refund. The valid scope is defined by the contract payment period together with the deduction reason. Updates occur monthly and at each deduction. This page does not infer batch synchronization, full synchronization, or real-time triggering from that cadence.

Multi-turn prompts let store service and internal training staff check these materials through continuous question and answer. The prompt must state the material scope, the answering task, and how to handle missing evidence. It must not let the model write business facts that the materials do not contain.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample, not a real record. Material text one: "Franchise contract A states that franchisee Jia shall pay a franchise fee of 50,000 yuan before 31 March 2025." Material text two: "The deposit ledger shows franchisee Jia's deposit of 20,000 yuan has status pending refund." Material text three: "Deduction notice B states that 3,000 yuan is deducted for a store violation, the reason being non-compliant display, dated 2 April 2025."

Draft sentence to be judged: "Franchisee Jia's franchise fee is settled, the 20,000 yuan deposit has been refunded, and the 3,000 yuan deduction has been executed." The differences from the material text are as follows. Text one states only a due date, not payment. Text two states pending refund, not refunded. Text three is a deduction notice, not an executed deduction.

## Three common errors
1. Treating the due date in material text one as a payment fact and writing "franchise fee is settled" in the draft. Input is text one. The comparison is between "due date" and "paid status". The observable result is a paid conclusion appearing in the draft that the text does not state.
2. Treating the pending refund status in material text two as refunded and writing "deposit has been refunded". Input is text two. The comparison is between "pending refund" and "refunded". The observable result is the draft rewriting pending refund as refunded.
3. Treating the deduction notice in material text three as an executed deduction and writing "deduction has been executed". Input is text three. The comparison is between "notice date" and "execution result". The observable result is the draft treating a notice as execution.

## How to verify the setup
1. Input material texts one to three, plus the draft sentence "Franchisee Jia's franchise fee is settled, the 20,000 yuan deposit has been refunded, and the 3,000 yuan deduction has been executed." Compare each conclusion in the draft with the stated status in the matching text. The condition is met when the draft marks all three items as unsupported or pending verification, with no assertion of paid, refunded, or executed.
2. Input material text two and the follow-up question "What is the deposit status now?" Compare whether the answer restates "pending refund". The condition is met when the answer presents only pending refund and adds no refund date, refund method, or approval status.
3. Input material text three and the follow-up question "Has the deduction been executed?" Compare whether the answer distinguishes notice from execution. The condition is met when the answer states the material is a deduction notice, that the execution result is not stated, and records the evidence gap.
