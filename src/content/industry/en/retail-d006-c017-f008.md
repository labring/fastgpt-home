# Franchise fee settlement: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Franchise-settlement training can practice distinctions among contractual plans, invoices, ledger entries and bank evidence without amounts. Proposed input supplies plans and notices while lacking operation receipts. The form passes passages and parties to explanation without reconciling accounts. Answers remain within the supplied wording; an issued invoice is not received payment, and a sent notice is not executed deduction.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |

| Party mismatch | Another store’s ledger introduced | Inspect contractual identity and remove it |

## Three common errors

Test choices to obtain a corresponding voucher or read the contract only. If the voucher route receives only a notice heading, inspect actual transferred values and retain the execution gap. Another store’s ledger also needs party and source checks before removal. Downstream continuation does not establish approved accounting relationships, and a learner’s selection does not replace financial authority. If invoice wording recording issuance becomes received funds, inspect that statement separately from this party’s bank evidence. An invoice supplies no receipt event; remove the financial conclusion while its evidence is absent.

## How to verify the setup

Retain parties, source meanings and answer origins. Repeat with corresponding business records and inspect the distinction between plans and execution. Interaction state follows the node, without a global switch declaring financial review complete. Actual deductions, accounting changes or settlement confirmation need separate procedures, authority and real receipts. Training completion establishes neither completed settlement nor a determination of contractual validity or justified handling.
