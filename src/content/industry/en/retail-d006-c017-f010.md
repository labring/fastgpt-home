# Franchise fee settlement: staff training with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace category cards, blank receipts and approval examples by their teaching purposes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed franchise-settlement lesson separates deposits from service fees on a category card, leaves purpose blank on a receipt template and shows a process sending complete material for review. The learner labels the blank receipt an approved deposit refund. No actual receipt, request or approval event is supplied. The card teaches category separation, the empty form identifies no payment and the example describes a later action. Preserve teaching and event boundaries instead of creating a transaction from a familiar process or allowing the learner’s category choice to prove an approval outcome as well.

| Blank-form state | Purpose uncompleted | Do not make it actual receipt. |

## Three common errors

First, an uncompleted purpose field may become a deposit; inspect actual purpose evidence instead of selecting one category from the teaching card. Second, an example of sending for review may become passed approval; read stage and condition, seeking an actual review record before discussing results. Third, a blank receipt may prove payment received; inspect document identity and completed content, since a template is no receipt event. These concern category support, process state and transaction existence. A shared settlement topic cannot establish that the missing business facts have already occurred.

## How to verify the setup

Ask which categories the card separates, what the form lacks and when the example action occurs, tracing actual references. A category card alone leaves transaction purpose and state unresolved and decides no return entitlement. A process example supplies no applicant or reviewer. Added hypothetical receipts or results must remain labelled practice and match the intended matter, without becoming actual customer payment records. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation. This page supplies no charging or refund rule and performs no settlement. Correct classroom identification also establishes no completed real-business review without its own evidence.
