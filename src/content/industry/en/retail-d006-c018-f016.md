# Regional dealer stock: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Dealer-replenishment training distinguishes demand interest, territory and goods in transit. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Suppose an exercise sheet expresses store replenishment interest, territory rules concern another dealer and transit wording lacks destination-store receipt. Collect current store, item and source, then let a trainee choose territory review or receiving evidence. Test missing store identity and completed input, checking what reaches downstream nodes. A choice node routes interaction without replacing a stock ledger or dealer-authority database.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Territory rules | Another dealer | Entity/geography |
| Transit wording | No store receipt | Not sale stock |
| Missing store | Compare completed input | Actual handoff |
| Real replenishment | Authorized process | Business acknowledgment |

## Three common errors

Entered interest reported as an order needs an order acknowledgment. Another territory rule applied here needs dealer and geographic scope checked. Transit reported as sale stock needs receipt and availability state checked. These examine transaction stage, scope and inventory events; a completed training form proves no real replenishment.

## How to verify the setup

Have the trainee locate open matters in source wording, inspecting connection and information handoff on both routes. Added receipt changes receiving stage alone while territory permission stays separate and orders still need their own evidence. Ordering, transfers and stock writes require authorized business procedures. Classroom records describe entry and comparison without establishing successful execution from a click. Place dealer territory beside target-store correspondence and ask the trainee to identify this store's missing order or receiving evidence.
