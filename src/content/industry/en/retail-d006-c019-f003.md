# On-demand retail fulfillment: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in On-demand retail fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with proposed instant-order passages, separating inventory meaning, work stages and actual delivery. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes an order, picking text and pending acceptance solely for reading subjects and stages, without actual orders or rider information. Inspect the classroom label through the complete link, then request restricted operational stock and orders. Inventory time does not update with an order, and picking differs from rider acceptance. Learners identify missing passages rather than supplying delivery through certainty. Classroom eligibility and store permissions differ; manager or rider names in an exercise create no actual member or business authority.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Instant-order training share | Classroom reading scope | Actual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed material | Order, picking and pending acceptance | Classroom identity and stage |
| Operational restriction | Stock, orders and delivery personal data | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior order and exercise | Obtain absent history |
| Different capabilities | Identifying picking creates no dispatch authority | Limits and execution evidence |
| Subsequent records | Solving dispatches, changes and scores nothing | Separate learning and delivery checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual stock or delivery personal data, retain passages and full answers. Fictional questions establish no isolation of other sources.

- If another browser asks whether the prior order finished without its question, inspect history and obtain order and exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow an earlier shift or recent state.

- If identifying picking becomes actual acceptance, delivery or dispatch rights, compare exercise limits and current execution evidence. Reading creates no delivery event or operation authority.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised acceptance text and restricted operational sources separately, retaining versions, readable passages and denials. Supplied revisions omitted from input need transmission checks. Unprovided reception evidence remains missing rather than supplied by longer validity. Verify identity conditions under actual capability, without treating classroom credentials as store-operation authority. Solving dispatches nobody, changes no stock and automatically records no score. Learning records and actual delivery need separate execution checks; ended classroom discussion establishes no completed operational order.
