# Certificates of origin: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Validate a training share using clearly proposed origin exercises, distinguishing classroom conditions, official sources and learner identity. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share pairs process wording with hypothetical conditions, visibly labeled as a reading exercise, excluding actual company production appendices. Inspect the material visible through the complete channel link, then ask about formal sources to test the boundary. If hypothetical conditions become official rules in the answer, retain exercise wording and response to locate loss of source identity during input or generation. A functioning link does not establish suitability for actual operations.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Origin-exercise condition | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source identity | Process and conditions labeled proposed | No claim of official rules |
| Restricted source | Actual company production appendix | Full response and denial scope |
| Device change | Delivery of earlier question and limitation | Provide exercise original when history is missing |
| Version condition | Test earlier and revised exercises | Readable material and entry limits |
| Subsequent result | Separate explanation, grade and certificate operation | Execution evidence for each |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If an origin classroom entry retrieves actual production appendices, retain originals and full response to inspect grants. Readable exercises establish no isolated company sources.

- If another device lacks the process and assumptions behind a passing question, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically supply the source or origin conclusion.

- If hypothetical conditions become official rules or eligibility evidence, compare classroom labels, input and output for lost identity. Missing formal rules remain gaps; share settings create no norm.

## How to verify the setup

Test readable exercises against restricted production originals, then compare visibility of earlier and revised conditions, retaining full responses and versions. Inspect expiry, per-user limits and usage responsibility for the actual version, with separate checks of third-party identity capability. Explanations can describe how to obtain rules without deciding actual qualification or writing grades. Learning records and certificate operations require separate execution tests. Exercise answers must not masquerade as confirmed origin evidence.
