# Certificates of origin: staff training with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Distinguish rule originals, company descriptions and classroom assumptions in origin-document training, tracing what each answer can support. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A training exercise supplies a company description saying that one production step occurs at its plant, then asks whether this qualifies. Preserve the rule being asked about and identify the absent rule original. A production-step statement does not establish satisfaction of origin conditions. If the answer declares qualification, inspect whether history supplied applicable conditions. Without that source, training can explain how to find evidence, but cannot turn a proposed description into an eligibility decision or formal certificate.

## Three common errors

- If one plant operation becomes confirmed origin eligibility, inspect actual applicable rules and conditions. Without the original rule, training can organize checks but cannot decide qualification.

- If hypothetical classroom conditions become official norms, compare labels, actual input and response to locate lost identity. A reading exercise supplies no formal certificate basis.

- If a new product or destination inherits prior conditions, compare questions and subject identities. Retained history does not establish applicability; obtain current evidence and retest.

## How to verify the setup

Compare questions with no rule, added exercise conditions and a changed subject, recording evidence, hypothetical status and unresolved items at each turn. Real training requiring official rules needs the applicable version and owner confirmation. A longer response cannot supply an unobtained norm. Answers can organize verification paths and required materials without claiming an actual origin review passed. If a learning system needs structured output, validate fields and parsing separately; fluent text does not establish a written learning record.
