# Brand member operations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed membership exercises for source reading without treating labels, notice drafts or answer quality as actual operating permission. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes activity rules, label definitions and an unsent notice for learners to identify conditions, source status and gaps, without actual member information. Show the version through the complete link, then request restricted CRM sources. An active-member label supports classification inside the exercise rather than actual eligibility or contact permission. An unsent draft remains proposed content. Training access and operational roles differ, so correct answers grant no label maintenance, points changes or sending tasks.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Membership-training share | Classroom scope | Verification object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed content | Rules, labels and unsent notice | Version, conditions and gaps |
| Operational restriction | CRM records and internal analysis | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior terms and notice draft | Obtain absent exercise |
| Permission boundary | Understanding obtains no consent or sending right | Limits and actual permission |
| Execution records | Solving changes no labels or scores | Separate learning and business checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual member records or internal analysis, retain passages and full answers. Proposed names establish no operational-source isolation.

- If another browser asks whether the prior draft can be sent without terms and wording, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow older conditions.

- If identifying labels and drafts becomes consent, completed contact or learner sending rights, compare classroom limits and actual permission records. Reading obtains no consent and configures no role.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised applicability and restricted member sources, recording passages, denials and versions. Supplied rules omitted from input need actual-request checks. Exercises lacking eligibility evidence remain incomplete; longer entry validity creates no basis. Verify classroom identity conditions under actual capability without treating entry credentials as an operating role. Solving changes no labels, sends no messages and automatically records no score. Learning results and formal handling need separate execution checks; ended discussion proves no actual brand activity took place.
