# Brand member operations: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Member-operations training can use general rules and hypothetical text only, collecting the learner’s question, activity plan and authorization gap without real member data. The form passes material downstream without deriving sending permission from a tag or coupon usage from configured rules. Questions about actual authority must retain the source owner and unresolved evidence rather than selecting unverified permission into existence.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public scope | General rules and proposed activity text | No real member records |
| Authority gap | Permission route receives plan heading | Plan is not permission |
| Activity mapping | Another activity’s usage wording introduced | Inspect identity and scope |
| Execution distinction | Plan versus sending; configuration versus usage | Existence does not establish execution |
| Actual handling | Separate sending, writing and recognition | State does not replace authority checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Test branches for reading rules or obtaining authorization material. If the latter receives only a campaign heading, inspect actual transferred values; a plan does not replace permission evidence. Another activity’s usage statement also needs identity and scope checks before interpretation. Preserve plans versus sending and coupon configuration versus usage as distinct received statements. If a planned notification becomes received communication, inspect this activity’s actual sending and receipt evidence separately. A plan establishes neither event, and general rules do not supply a reception outcome.

## How to verify the setup

Trace the question, public general material, chosen route and explanation. Repeat after corresponding guidance arrives and inspect actual use of the addition. Recorded interaction state and waiting do not establish completed permission checks. Real sending, tag writes, coupon handling and staff recognition require actual downstream authority and receipts. Training completion establishes neither contact, effects nor passed privacy review.
