# Omnichannel stock transfers: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a classroom share with proposed transfer questions about locations, balances and movements without executing stock business through reading. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes a two-location request, inventory balance text and pending receipt for learners to identify roles and times without actual warehouse information. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request restricted operational sources. Balances concern a time and movement quantities an event; similar labels or numeric forms do not make them interchangeable. Without movement and receipt records, answers retain gaps. Entry access concerns classroom interaction rather than actual stock changes or authority to execute transfers.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Transfer-training share | Proposed exercise scope | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location roles | Request, balance and pending receipt | Locations, time and gaps |
| Operational restriction | Warehouse stock and allocation | Full answer and denial |
| Browser handoff | Prior roles and question | Obtain absent history |
| Quantity meaning | Balance is not event quantity | Limits and event sources |
| Execution distinction | Solving creates no transfer or score | Separate learning and business checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If classroom sharing returns actual warehouse stock or allocation sources, retain passages and full answers. Renamed locations establish no operational isolation.

- If another browser asks about the prior destination without locations and exercise, inspect history and obtain sending and receiving roles. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute recent records.

- If recognizing a balance becomes posted movement or learner transfer rights, compare exercise limits and actual event evidence. Reading changes no stock and configures no operating role.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised location roles and restricted warehouse sources, recording passages, denials and versions. Supplied new roles omitted from input need transmission mapping checked. Exercises without receipt records remain incomplete rather than completed through validity. Verify classroom identity under actual capability while keeping entry credentials separate from warehouse accounts. Solving creates no transfer, posts no stock and automatically records no score. Learning and business actions need separate execution evidence; successful discussion establishes neither synchronized channel stock nor arrived goods.
