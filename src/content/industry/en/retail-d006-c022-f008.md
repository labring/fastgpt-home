# Omnichannel stock transfers: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Stock-transfer training can collect a store shortage, transfer request and snapshot passage while explicitly lacking receipt evidence. Preserve demand, request and execution downstream instead of turning an application into arrival. Proposed text separates sending and receiving locations to teach their roles. Shared items across channels do not establish matching definitions, and the workflow does not automatically synchronize stock or reconcile goods from those passages.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand and execution | Shortage and request separate from arrival | Request is not receipt |
| Location roles | Sending becomes receiving downstream | Repair against passages and values |
| Receipt gap | Source request passes logistics heading only | Heading is not confirmation |
| Added passage | Receipt for this transfer | Actually reaches the reading step |
| Formal operation | Separate writing, reconciliation and assessment | Workflow is not permission or synchronization |

## Three common errors

Test choices to obtain transport evidence, inspect stock scope or explain the request only. If the sending location becomes the receiving location during transfer, inspect source roles and received values, repair and retest. If a receipt request passes only a logistics heading, retain the receipt gap. A transport record’s existence is not confirmed store receipt, and a learner’s selection is not transfer approval. If a snapshot balance becomes this transfer’s movement, inspect its moment, subject and the actual movement evidence. Stock held differs from stock moved; an absent process record cannot be replaced by a balance.

## How to verify the setup

Compare item, location roles, source time and explanation origins. Repeat with receipt material for this transfer and inspect its actual destination. Interaction records state, waits and continues without establishing warehouse processing. Transfer creation, stock writes, reconciliation and assessment need separate business procedures, real permissions and receipts. Completion establishes neither synchronized inventory nor the learner’s authority to operate.
