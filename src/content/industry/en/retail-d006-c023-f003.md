# Brand repair services: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed repair exercises to inspect a training entry and source recognition without performing inspection or granting repair approval. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes a defect statement, warranty passage and parts note so learners identify who said what and which inspection is missing, without customer records. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request restricted operational orders. Statements, observations and outcomes supply different evidence. Matching two passages supports reading rather than warranty applicability or responsibility. Training access creates no repair-approval or customer-maintenance authority, and job titles in examples cannot automatically establish team roles beyond an explicitly confirmed account and resource scope.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Repair-training share | Classroom-source boundary | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed exercise | Separate defect, warranty and parts | Statement origins and gaps |
| Operational restriction | Inspections and other repairs | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior item and applicable conditions | Obtain absent passages |
| Judgment scope | Understanding decides no inspection or approval | Limits and actual sources |
| Record action | Solving assigns no repair or score | Separate learning and handling checks |

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual inspections or another customer’s repairs, retain passages and full answers. A fictional story establishes no isolation of other sources.

- If another browser asks about replacement without the prior item and conditions, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically apply another item’s repair conclusion.

- If understanding warranty or parts becomes passed inspection or approved replacement, compare classroom limits and actual handling evidence. Solving makes no business decision and configures no approval role.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised item wording and restricted work orders, recording passages, denials and versions. Separate supplied inspection text omitted from input from an exercise lacking inspection. Longer validity supplies no conclusion. Verify identity conditions under actual capability without treating classroom credentials as customer or repair-role evidence. Solving assigns no repair, approves no replacement and automatically records no score. Learning and formal handling need separate execution checks; retain unresolved sources instead of describing answered exercises as established defects and responsibility.
