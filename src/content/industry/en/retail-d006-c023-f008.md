# Brand repair services: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Repair training can collect a question, warranty passage and repair report while identifying absent inspection and approval evidence. Proposed text establishes neither a verified defect from submission nor a broken promise from repair within warranty. Preserve distinctions among customer statements, inspection and approval downstream. A warranty card’s existence does not establish permission for every action or a legal responsibility conclusion.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source status | Reported symptoms separate from inspection and approval | No direct defect or responsibility judgment |
| Heading gap | Inspection route receives work-order title | Inspect content and obtain findings |
| Item mismatch | Another item’s inspection introduced | Check correspondence and remove it |
| Inflated state | Exchange application becomes approval | Restore status against the passage |
| Handling authority | Separate repair, closure and assessment | Ending waiting is not permission |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |

| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |

## Three common errors

Test choices to obtain inspection wording or explain the warranty only. If inspection receives only a work-order heading, inspect actual content rather than replacing findings with a title. Another item’s inspection also needs item and work-order correspondence before removal. If an exchange application becomes approval, restore its status against the passage; continuation does not establish executed replacement.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, work-order identities, source status and citations. Repeat after corresponding evidence arrives and inspect actual use of new records. Interaction-state recording and waiting are not completed business review. Repair, exchange, closure and staff assessment need separate downstream authority and receipts. Training completion establishes neither a determined defect or responsibility nor the learner’s handling permission.
