# Brand concession operations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed counter exercises for contract and statement reading without substituting classroom interaction for activity or finance handling. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A counter exercise proposes three passages: participation requires separate confirmation, the notice was sent, and the monthly statement awaits review. Learners identify the contract, mall notice and draft as different origins. Sending describes communication rather than brand participation; preparing a statement supplies no settlement agreement. Include no actual sales or fee values. Through the complete share link, inspect each passage with its origin, then request restricted counter records to test whether mall cashier and brand-account sources are being confused.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Counter-training share | Classroom-source scope | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed material | Contract, activity and statement stages | Counter, period and gaps |
| Operational restriction | Sales and internal settlement comments | Full answer and denial |
| Browser handoff | Prior mall, counter and period | Obtain absent passages |
| Duty distinction | Understanding grants no contract or finance approval | Limits and actual confirmation |
| Subsequent records | Solving registers, settles and scores nothing | Separate learning and operations checks |

## Three common errors

- If classroom sharing returns actual counter sales or internal settlement comments, retain passages and full answers. Proposed receipt wording establishes no operational isolation.

- If another browser asks whether the mall confirmed without counter, period and question, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow an earlier statement’s status.

- If recognizing notices or drafts becomes registration, mutual agreement or learner approval rights, compare exercise limits and actual confirmation evidence. Reading executes no business and creates no role.

## How to verify the setup

For acceptance, change the notice to another counter and inspect whether the learner identifies missing participation evidence for this counter. Then supply a new period’s draft and check that earlier agreement is not reused. Locate revisions in received text; absent revisions need input checks, while a draft without mutual comments remains incomplete. Request operational records separately and preserve denial or leakage. Classroom credentials establish no mall-operations identity. Explanation registers nothing, settles nothing and saves no score automatically; verify handling and learning records separately.
