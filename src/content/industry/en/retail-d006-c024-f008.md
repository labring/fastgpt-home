# Brand concession operations: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand concession operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Concession training can collect contractual applicability, a mall activity letter and the learner’s question without amounts. Proposed material contains arrangements and receipt wording without settlement confirmation. Pass these sources to reading without turning an activity letter into participation or one sales receipt into verified period sales and settlement. Contracting parties and activity scope need to travel with their passages.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrangement boundary | Activity letter separate from participation | Letter does not establish execution |
| Sales scope | One receipt beside period-source gap | Not expanded into verified period totals |
| Settlement route | Only a receipt heading received | Inspect content and obtain evidence |
| Concession identity | Another concession’s contract introduced | Check party and version before removal |
| Formal handling | Separate enrollment, settlement and recognition | Interaction is not approval or reconciliation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Test choices to read the activity, obtain settlement evidence or refer party checks to the operator. If settlement receives only a receipt heading, inspect actual material and retain the period gap. Another concession’s contract also needs party and version checks before removal. A shared mall name does not establish identical contractual or activity conditions, and a learner’s choice does not approve business handling. If an activity letter describes arrangements but the answer claims this concession participated, inspect its passage separately from this concession’s actual participation record. A shared mall letter does not establish every concession’s execution; retain the gap.

## How to verify the setup

Trace question, submitted values, applicability and explanation origins. Repeat after this concession’s period record arrives and inspect its actual use. Interaction state and waiting are node behavior, not confirmed sales or finance. Activity enrollment, settlement writes, amendments and training recognition require separate downstream or external permissions and actual receipts. Workflow completion establishes neither executed participation nor reconciled accounts.
