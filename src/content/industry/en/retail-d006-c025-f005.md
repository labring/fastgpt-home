# Brand outlet operations: staff training with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Inspect training follow-ups about stock age and clearance while preserving measure definitions, periods and classroom assumptions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

A proposed training note separates receipt date, time in store and latest sale date. Ask learners to explain them, then choose which style to clear. The note supplies no formal selection rule or business-decision basis, so preserve that gap. An answer choosing the earliest date requires tracing the measure actually defined. Different time dimensions are not directly interchangeable. Classroom sorting can discuss a method without becoming the store’s approved clearance conclusion.

## Three common errors

- If receipt, store duration and latest-sale dates become a chronological clearance decision, inspect definitions and actual rules. Distinct time measures are not interchangeable.

- If hypothetical classroom conditions become approved store policy, retain proposed labels, history and output to trace lost identity. Exercise ranking establishes no business confirmation.

- If another inventory period inherits the earlier style choice, compare tables, periods and optimized question. Restore the current subject and conditions; response space does not decide the result.

## How to verify the setup

Compare input with no rule, an added assumption and a changed period, recording field definitions behind the explanation and conditions needing owner confirmation. Missing margin or discount material cannot justify invented values or a profitability judgment from stock ordering alone. Real training needs applicable policy and confirmed examples; keep the exercise label. Grade records or list updates require separate structure, parsing and execution checks. A completed conversation does not establish an updated learning or business record.
