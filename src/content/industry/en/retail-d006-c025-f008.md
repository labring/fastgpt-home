# Brand outlet operations: staff training with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Outlet training distinguishes display samples, disclosed defects and actual sale rules. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Suppose a training card describes a display sample, includes a defect-location note and leaves sale rules awaiting store confirmation. Collect item and store identity, choose sample-purpose review or sale evidence, then inspect wording actually received downstream. The node records interaction and continues without supplying an employee-access directory or deciding quality and sale eligibility. No discount, price or real transaction is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Outlet-training workflow | Hypothetical material | Learning check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display sample | Limited purpose | Not new sale stock |
| Defect note | Keep location | No defect-free wording |
| Sale rule | Store confirmation pending | List gap |
| Training choice | Purpose or evidence | Inspect handoff |
| Listing event | Separate system action | Real receipt |

## Three common errors

A display sample reported as new sale stock needs purpose and condition checked. A disclosed defect rewritten as defect-free needs original wording checked. Completed training reported as a listed item needs a real listing receipt checked. These concern nature, retained content and a business event.

## How to verify the setup

Ask the trainee to locate the defect and missing rule in the card, inspecting output handoff on both routes. Added rules change evidence for this item and store alone, without generalizing to others. Actual pricing, inspection and listing require authorized store procedures and system operations. Training orchestration produces traceable learning and evidence-completion records without proving sale eligibility from course completion. Locate both the sample label and defect note, and have the trainee identify the sale rule that remains unavailable.
