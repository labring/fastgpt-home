# Brand-owned store operations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed store exercises for staffing and site-source reading while preserving access boundaries between classroom and actual operations. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes staffing, image descriptions and transfer passages so learners identify store, shift, time and missing records, without actual personnel or store sources. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request restricted sales and staff material. Planned attendance proves no actual presence; an image supports its described observation rather than a complete site check. Explaining those distinctions creates no operating right. Employment, training participation and team-resource permissions require separate checks rather than a manager label in an exercise creating a role.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Owned-store training share | Classroom and operational sources | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed question | Staffing, images and transfers | Store, shift, time and gaps |
| Operational restriction | Personnel, sales and performance comments | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior store, shift and passage | Obtain absent exercise |
| Capability distinction | Reading proves no attendance or approval right | Limits and actual sources |
| Record execution | Solving records or changes nothing | Separate learning and business checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If classroom sharing returns actual staff, other-store sales or performance comments, retain passages and full answers. Renamed exercises establish no isolation of operational sources.

- If another browser asks about completed inspection without the question and shift, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically substitute the latest photograph.

- If identifying schedules or images becomes attendance, verified sites or learner approval rights, compare exercise limits and actual execution evidence. Reading completes no store task and grants no role.

## How to verify the setup

Ask about initial questions, revised photograph dates and restricted operational sources, retaining passages, denials and versions. Supplied new times omitted from input need transmission checks; absent attendance or review passages remain missing. Renewal supplies no site evidence. Verify classroom identity conditions under actual capability without treating entry credentials as operational-role proof. Solving records no attendance, changes no staffing and automatically writes no score. Learning and business require separate execution checks; ended discussion establishes neither accepted site conditions nor achieved performance targets.
