# Brand-owned store operations: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. A staff schedule, display image and transfer record support different stages of store work. Their subjects must remain visible when a training step receives them. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A brand-owned store training form can collect a question, schedule, display material and transfer passage. Proposed inputs lack actual attendance or transfer receipt, whose gaps must survive downstream. Collecting text does not query attendance automatically or establish current display conformity from a photograph. Choices arrange evidence or reading, while actual retrieval and business receipt need implemented connections.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff stage | Schedule separate from attendance | Plan does not establish arrival |
| Image origin | Present and other-store identities | Mismatched image removed |
| Transfer state | Request without receipt evidence | Not rewritten as received |
| Evidence handoff | Corresponding shift or transfer passage | Downstream actually receives it |
| Formal business | Separate change, writing and recognition | Interaction is not synchronization |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Schedule mistaken for attendance: planned staffing needs real shift-attendance evidence. Wrong photograph subject: another store’s image requires store and caption checks. Misstated transfer stage: a request described as received needs this transfer’s receipt record. These inspect personnel execution, source identity and goods stage rather than deriving results from filled values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain store, shift, source status and actual received content. Repeat after corresponding attendance or receipt passages arrive and inspect their use. The node records state, waits and continues without formal recognition. Validation, schedule changes, stock writes and staff authority require business permission and real receipts. Submission establishes neither completed store operations nor synchronized data.
