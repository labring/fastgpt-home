# Dealer ordering events: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed event exercises for handbook and sample reading without deriving actual ordering eligibility or sales from classroom answers. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes handbook applicability, sample-display wording and an unconfirmed order so learners distinguish display from acceptance, without actual dealers or amounts. Inspect the exercise version through the complete link, then request restricted agreements and credit sources. Naming goods establishes textual presence rather than ordering eligibility or approved terms. A training entry is not the ordering platform, and proposed roles create no actual members. Answers retain missing acceptance evidence instead of turning a complete-looking source set into a completed transaction.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Event-training share | Classroom scope | Verification action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed sources | Handbook, samples and unconfirmed order | Version and acceptance gap |
| Operational restriction | Dealer agreements and internal credit | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior event and question | Obtain absent history |
| Version test | Inapplicable handbook and added acceptance | Actual input passages |
| Execution distinction | Solving orders, approves and scores nothing | Separate learning and ordering checks |

## Three common errors

- If classroom sharing returns actual dealer agreements or internal credit, retain passages and full answers. Proposed customer names establish no operational isolation.

- If another browser asks about prior goods without handbook event and question, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow another event’s order outcome.

- If recognizing samples and terms becomes accepted orders, approved credit or learner approval rights, compare classroom limits and actual decisions. Reading completes no sale and creates no permission.

## How to verify the setup

Change the handbook to an inapplicable event version and inspect whether answers identify missing current conditions. Then add a proposed acceptance passage and check whether it actually reaches input. Exercises without approval evidence remain incomplete rather than supplied by renewal. Request operational sources separately and preserve denial or leakage; classroom credentials establish no dealer identity. Solving submits no order, approves no credit and automatically saves no score. Learning records and actual ordering need separate execution checks; completed training establishes no completed commercial stages.
