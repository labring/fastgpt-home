# Dealer ordering events: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Ordering guidance, sample lists and credit material need their own subjects and versions. A training explanation keeps product display, contractual ordering and authority distinct. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

An ordering-event training form can collect the question, handbook passage, sample scope and missing credit material without amounts. Proposed sources lack a transaction or approval outcome. Pass supplied and outstanding material to reading. A sample list describes displayed items rather than actual orders, and a contract’s existence does not establish confirmed conditions. A human choice is neither a dealer directory nor financial approval.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display and execution | Sample list separate from actual order | Not rewritten as ordered |
| Credit subject | Another dealer’s material introduced | Inspect identity and remove it |
| Handbook version | Applicability travels with passage | Recipient retains scope |
| Supplement retest | Corresponding order or credit passage | Actually reaches explanation |
| Formal confirmation | Separate submission, contract and authority | State is not transaction approval |

## Three common errors

Samples mistaken for orders need this transaction’s actual ordering evidence. Another dealer’s credit source needs party checks before removal. Lost handbook applicability requires comparison of entered and received wording to restore version scope. These inspect execution, identity and version; choosing continuation supplies none of the business facts.

## How to verify the setup

Trace question, source identities, branch and received text. Repeat after corresponding credit or order evidence arrives and inspect its use. Waiting and continuation are not passed training. Validation, order submission, contractual confirmation and staff authority need actual downstream or external permission and receipts. Completed learning establishes neither a transaction, credit approval nor a dealer’s purchase commitment.
