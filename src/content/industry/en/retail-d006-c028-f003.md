# Brand livestream operations: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed livestream exercises for qualifications and source reading without turning classroom scripts or comments into actual promotion or transactions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes product qualifications, an unbroadcast script and comments so learners identify supported sentences and missing verification, without actual accounts or audience data. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request restricted assets. Future scripts and expressed comments replace no broadcast or transactions. Proposed roles identify source origins rather than actual team members. Understanding qualifications establishes neither publication eligibility nor verified performance. Preserve each source’s identity instead of expanding an exercise into an operational conclusion beyond its actual passages.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Livestream-training share | Proposed-source boundary | Actual test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom material | Qualifications, unbroadcast script and comments | Origins and gaps |
| Operational restriction | Audience data and internal review | Full answer and denial |
| Browser handoff | Prior script and session | Obtain absent history |
| Qualification revision | Specific difference between new card and old sentence | Received passage and identified mismatch |
| Business distinction | Solving broadcasts, updates and scores nothing | Separate learning and operations checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual audience information or internal product reviews, retain passages and full answers. Proposed comments establish no operational-source isolation.

- If another browser asks whether prior wording was broadcast without its session, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically confirm broadcast from recent content.

- If recognizing cards or comments becomes approved promotion, transactions or publishing rights, compare exercise limits and actual records. Reading executes no operation and grants no account authority.

## How to verify the setup

Change one product qualification, then supply a script retaining the earlier sentence. Inspect whether learners identify the specific mismatch rather than declaring everything inapplicable. Check that revised passages actually reach input, separating omitted sources from absent exercise evidence. Request restricted operational sources separately and preserve denial or leakage; classroom credentials establish no platform identity. Solving broadcasts nothing, updates no goods and automatically saves no score. Learning records and formal operations need separate execution checks; ended classroom discussion establishes no completed promotion.
