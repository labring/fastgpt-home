# Brand livestream operations: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Scripts, product guidance and comments have different evidential status. Training keeps planned speech, supported product statements and actual session outcomes traceable to their sources. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A brand-livestream training form can collect the session question, script passage and product guidance, keeping comments as statements. Proposed material lacks actual broadcast or transaction evidence, so a complete script does not establish spoken content. Preserve source status and session downstream rather than assuming automatic backend access. Choices arrange guidance or draft reading, not product selection or approved promotional claims.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speech stage | Script separate from actual broadcast | Complete draft is not spoken content |
| Product scope | Qualification in the guidance passage | Recipient retains the limitation |
| Comment status | Viewer statement separate from transaction source | No direct sales conclusion |
| Added material | This session’s broadcast or product evidence | Explanation actually uses it |
| Formal release | Separate approval, account and recognition | Form is not execution or effects |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Script mistaken for execution needs the corresponding broadcast source. Lost product qualifications require comparison of submitted and received passages to restore scope. Comments mistaken for transactions need actual session transaction evidence. These inspect execution, property scope and business records rather than treating document names as proof.

## How to verify the setup

Retain session, source status, chosen path and actual received text. Repeat after corresponding broadcast or product evidence arrives and inspect use of the addition. Recorded waiting and continuation create neither publication nor transaction records. Validation, content approval, account release and staff recognition need downstream or external authority and receipts. Completed training establishes neither an executed session nor operating effects.
