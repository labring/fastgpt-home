# After-sales repairs and reships: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Use proposed reshipment exercises for stage reading, inspecting request, dispatch and carrier sources without turning practice into actual service. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes three stages: a created request, warehouse handoff pending and absent recipient evidence. Learners identify the next source needed without actual addresses or orders. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request operational sources to test separation. Dispatch and repair wording answer different questions; reshipment establishes no breach. Learners identify support from passages rather than receiving warehouse authority through proposed roles. The entry carries reading practice without executing reshipment or confirming customer receipt beyond the source stage.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Reshipment-training share | Proposed stage sources | Actual test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom question | Created request, pending handoff and missing receipt | Next required evidence |
| Operational restriction | Addresses and internal warehouse records | Full answer and denial |
| Browser follow-up | Prior stage and passages | Obtain absent history |
| Added source | Collection does not supply customer receipt | Received passage and answer |
| Execution distinction | Solving dispatches, judges and scores nothing | Separate learning and service checks |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual addresses or internal warehouse records, retain passages and full answers. Proposed order names establish no isolation of other sources.

- If another browser asks about prior shipping without the question’s stage, inspect history and obtain the material. Anonymous identity cannot automatically supply an earlier example’s logistics.

- If reading dispatch or carrier wording becomes customer receipt, completed repair or learner reshipment approval, compare exercise limits and actual process. Reading creates no event and grants no role.

## How to verify the setup

Start with pending handoff, then add a proposed collection passage. Inspect whether answers update only to collection without guessing delivery, and locate the new passage in received text. Carrier evidence cannot fill absent customer receipt. Omitted originals need transmission checks. Request operational sources separately and preserve denial or leakage; classroom credentials establish no recipient identity. Solving dispatches nothing, determines no responsibility and automatically records no score. Learning and service records need separate execution checks; smooth practice establishes no completed actual reshipment.
