# After-sales repairs and reships: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Repair applications, parts dispatch and reshipment records describe different stages. The learner needs the particular item and the evidence still missing between each source and a completed service outcome. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Repair-and-reship training can collect the item question, inspection passage and shipment identities. Proposed input contains an application and parts-dispatch wording without current findings or reship collection evidence. Preserve those gaps downstream. A form does not identify defects from submission or turn parts leaving stock into installation. Choices arrange inspection or dispatch sources; business execution needs actual later procedures.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item mapping | Another item’s findings introduced | Inspect identity and remove it |
| Repair stage | Parts dispatch separate from installation | Not rewritten as completed repair |
| Transport stage | Reship creation separate from collection | Inspect the carrier evidence |
| Evidence handoff | This order’s inspection or collection passage | Actually reaches downstream |
| Business operation | Separate repair, reship and authority | Interaction is not completed service |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Another item’s inspection requires item-to-application correspondence checks. Parts dispatch described as completed repair needs actual repair-process evidence. A created reshipment described as carrier collection needs this shipment’s collection source. These concern identity, repair execution and transport stage, none supplied by one choice.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, source status, route destination and received text. Repeat after corresponding inspection or collection passages arrive and inspect their use. The node records state, waits and continues without automatically creating business work orders. Validation, repair, reshipping and staff authority need downstream or external permissions and receipts. Training completion establishes neither defect responsibility nor completed service.
