# Supermarket supplier accounts: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training share with proposed retailer-settlement questions, identifying contractual terms, arrival and acceptance conclusions separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A classroom exercise proposes supply terms, arrival acknowledgment and unresolved reconciliation so learners state each source’s coverage, without actual suppliers or amounts. Inspect the version through the complete link, then request restricted contracts and finance material. Arrival acknowledgment concerns reception rather than passed acceptance; preparing a statement proves no mutual agreement. Missing acceptance passages remain unresolved. Contractual terms or smooth classroom execution establish no business performance, and learner identity automatically creates no purchasing or finance approval role beyond confirmed actual permissions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

- If an exercise returns actual settlement vouchers or another supplier’s contract, retain passages and full answers. Proposed party names establish no operational isolation.

- If another browser asks about confirmed batches without exercise and period, inspect history and obtain them. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow an earlier example’s confirmation.

- If understanding terms or acknowledgment becomes passed acceptance, mutual agreement or learner approval rights, compare exercise limits and actual conclusions. Reading performs no formal inspection and configures no role.

## How to verify the setup

Provide only arrival acknowledgment first and inspect whether the answer retains missing acceptance. Add a proposed acceptance conclusion and verify receipt and change only to this batch’s acceptance, without also confirming period reconciliation. Separate omitted input from absent exercise records. Request restricted operational sources separately and preserve denial or leakage; classroom credentials establish no supplier identity. Solving inspects nothing, settles nothing and automatically records no score. Learning and actual purchasing require separate execution checks; one added source cannot complete every supply and funds task.
