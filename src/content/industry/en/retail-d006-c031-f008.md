# Retail packaging reporting: workflow routing for staff training

## What the documents look like

Retail packaging reporting for supermarkets and convenience stores centres on single-use plastic product reporting and environmental supervision. The main data sources are the Ministry of Commerce reporting system, POS plastic bag sales, procurement systems and store ledgers. The documents handled day to day are plastic bag procurement ledgers, sales vouchers, half-year reports and self-discipline commitments. This category differs from checkout membership and promotion work because it revolves around disposable plastic product reporting and environmental regulation, not member marketing.

For store service and internal training, headquarters compliance and administrative procurement staff need to organise half-year submission requirements, recycling verification and store operating rules into a reusable workflow. The submission cadence is mainly half-yearly, while sales vouchers arise per transaction. The two material types have different time granularities, so collection and verification steps need to be set separately during orchestration. Error consequences include rectification within a deadline, fines of ten thousand to one hundred thousand yuan, and regulatory interviews. Authorisation boundaries and evidence completeness matter more than the degree of automation.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

When the Form Input node triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state and records the workflow state. After the user completes the required information and submits, the node collects the submitted form information and passes it to later nodes. The User Selection node also enters an interaction state, records the workflow state, and after the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the "yes" branch.

For store service and internal training, orchestration places training sign-in, material handover confirmation and pending-request submission into these interaction nodes, so that a person fills in or selects before the flow continues. Retrieval or form collection produces input values only. Order writing, document backfill and external status synchronisation require a separate business interface or later process, and interaction nodes cannot complete them on their own. A pending review status does not indicate a lack of operating permission, and a pending approval request is not an approved execution.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: on 10 June 2025, a compliance post at a supermarket chain headquarters issues an internal training and material handover notice to three stores. The notice text reads: "Each store shall submit the plastic bag procurement ledger and sales voucher summary for the first half of 2025 before 20 June 2025, and complete store operation training sign-in; after confirmation by the store manager, materials are handed to the headquarters compliance post, and archiving may proceed only after the headquarters compliance post passes review." In the same batch, a store submission sentence reads: "This store's ledger has been submitted, and the current period reporting obligation is deemed complete." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "Submitting the ledger completes the current period reporting." This sample is used only for verification on this page and does not mean that a real submission or approval has occurred.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Purpose on this page | Setting or verification method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form Input node | Collect store training sign-in and material handover confirmation | Set as required fields; after submission pass values to the later verification node |
| User Selection node | Store manager selects whether materials are complete | Connect the "yes" branch to the later archiving verification flow |
| Interaction state check | Confirm the conversation enters the interaction state and records the workflow state | Compare the state record and input values before and after submission item by item |
| Output handover check | Pass collected information to later retrieval or decision nodes | Compare node output against form input item by item |
| External action separation | Order writing, document backfill, external synchronisation | State explicitly that a separate business interface is required; do not declare completion inside the interaction node |
| Manual verification action | Headquarters compliance post reviews materials | Compare item by item against the review-pass conditions in the notice text |

## Three common errors

1. Treating store ledger submission as completion of the reporting obligation. In the sample, the notice text requires that "after confirmation by the store manager, materials are handed to the headquarters compliance post, and archiving may proceed only after the headquarters compliance post passes review." Submission is a handover action; passing review is the archiving condition. The draft sentence omits the review step.
2. Treating the "yes" branch of the User Selection node as approval. Executing the "yes" branch of the User Selection node means the user made a choice. Approval requires the headquarters compliance post to verify materials against the notice conditions and confirm separately.
3. Treating retrieval or form input as authorised execution. The Form Input node collects required information and passes it to later nodes. Order writing, document backfill and external synchronisation require a separate business interface. The collected value itself does not constitute business authorisation.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample notice text and the store submission sentence, then compare whether the values of the two fields "submit the ledger" and "archive only after passing review" are mutually exclusive. The draft sentence "submitting the ledger completes the current period reporting" shares the same subject, the same period and the same field with the notice text, and the values contradict each other, so it is judged unsupported.
2. Input the record where the store manager selects "materials complete" in the User Selection node, then compare whether that choice and the headquarters compliance post's review-pass record are the same fact. They belong to different stages. The selection result does not constitute passing review, and the evidence gap must be recorded.
3. Input the output value after submission of the Form Input node, compare whether that value is used by later nodes for retrieval or decision, and check whether order writing, document backfill and external synchronisation are handled by a separate interface. If the downstream only has a retrieval call and no business interface, the related business actions remain unexecuted.
