# Retail packaging reporting: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Retail packaging reporting, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Procurement ledgers, sales evidence, prepared reports and commitments support different statements. This training reads their actual scope without asserting regulatory requirements or submission outcomes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Packaging-report training can collect the question, procurement-ledger scope and prepared-report passages without regulatory criteria or filing requirements. Proposed sources contain ledgers and commitments, not complete sales or submission receipts. Preserve those gaps downstream rather than deriving execution from a pledge. Choices arrange sources or explanation, while actual retrieval and formal submission need business procedures rather than automatic compliance reporting.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ledger meaning | Procurement separate from sales | Receipt into stock is not sale |
| Report stage | Preparation separate from submission and acceptance | Inspect actual receipts |
| Commitment boundary | Promise separate from conduct evidence | No direct performance conclusion |
| Added source | Corresponding sales or processing passage | Actually reaches explanation |
| Formal responsibility | Separate submission, retention and recognition | Learning is not compliance determination |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Procurement mistaken for sales needs the corresponding sales source and definition. A prepared report described as accepted needs actual submission and processing receipts. Commitment wording treated as an execution record needs business evidence. These concern data meaning, filing stage and conduct, none established by nonempty values.

## How to verify the setup

Retain coverage, source status, route and received text. Repeat after corresponding sales or receipts arrive, limiting interpretation to obtained evidence. The node records state, waits and continues without creating ledgers automatically. Validation, submission, retention and recognition require actual responsibility and records. Completed learning establishes neither accepted reports, complete materials nor compliant business.
