# Trade and FX administration: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

Trade and FX administration work in this category centers on foreign-exchange settlement and the authenticity of the underlying trade background. The daily materials are collection receipts, trade contracts, invoices, and foreign-exchange business registration records. This differs from letter-of-credit document examination, which reviews a full document set under the credit. Here the focus is whether the collecting party matches the counterparty and whether the trade background can be explained. In store service and internal training, materials enter the reconciliation process on a per-transaction basis and on a monthly basis. The share-and-embed step determines what materials, operations, and requests are in what authorization state when a conversation entry point is handed to store staff or trainees.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A no-login window is created from the publication channel, and the system-generated full link is copied. The link identifier only opens the conversation entry point and does not serve as a business authorization credential. The anonymous method depends on the same device and the same browser; cross-device records require separate identity and session management design. Identity verification is limited to the commercial edition scope and follows the official interface return of a success state and uid. Expiration time, QPM, and the points cap are usage limits on the share entry point, and costs are attributed to the team that owns the application. The conversation result reporting interface has no specified return value, and its response matches the chat interface format with one additional token.

Hypothetical exercise sample. An internal training page at a foreign-trade company plans to embed a conversation entry point so trainees can look up a checklist of settlement materials. The source text provided for comparison is: "Trade contract number HT-2024-031; the collection receipt shows the payer as ALPHA TRADING, while the contract signing party is BETA IMPORT, and the two names do not match; the invoice amount is USD 12,000 and the receipt amount is USD 12,000." One draft sentence to be judged is: "The collecting party for this transaction matches the contract signing party, the trade background has been verified, and settlement can proceed." According to the source text, the collecting party and the contract signing party names do not match, so the "match" judgment in the draft sentence is unsupported. The phrase "the trade background has been verified" has no corresponding field in the source text and is also unsupported. This draft sentence should not be used as an example conclusion on the training page.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Expiration time | Usage scope of the training page | Set for this training period and compare with the actual time the link stops working |
| QPM | Maximum visits per user per minute | Set a hypothetical starting value for this exercise based on training concurrency and compare with observed visit frequency |
| Points cap | Maximum billing data for the share entry point | Check cost attribution to the team and compare with this training budget |

## Three common errors

1. Treating the link identifier as business authorization. The no-login window shareId only opens the conversation entry point. Comparing the contract number with the collection receipt in the source text belongs to a different step. A working link does not mean the collecting party has been checked.
2. Treating the identity verification success as trade background verification. The verification interface only returns whether the user may continue. Whether the collecting party and the contract signing party match in the source text requires manual comparison of contract and receipt fields.
3. Treating anonymous conversation records as cross-device retrievable. The anonymous method depends on the same device and same browser, and switching devices loses records. A training scenario needs separate identity and session management design.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the source text and the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample. Compare the "match" judgment in the draft sentence with the two party names in the source text. Observe the result: when the names do not match, that judgment does not hold. Use a paper input of [] to check the downstream design for empty results, without claiming that a fabricated rule search necessarily returns empty.
2. Input the share link and authToken. Compare the success and uid returned by the verification interface. Observe the result: only when success is true and uid is a string without the specified characters and no longer than 255 bytes is the user allowed to continue; when the uid format does not conform, the Invalid UID error is returned.
3. Input this training's visit frequency and points consumption. Compare the QPM setting with observed values and the points cap with accumulated consumption. Observe the result: when the setting is exceeded, the link cannot continue to be used. Retrieval relevance does not handle permission or entity isolation, libraries across different vector models cannot be associated together, and only identifiers are checked and non-conforming items are excluded, with actual observations recorded.
