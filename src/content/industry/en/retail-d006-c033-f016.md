# Trade and FX administration: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Trade contracts, receipt evidence and registration material have separate subjects and stages. The example teaches source reading without financial amounts, policy advice or determinations of business eligibility. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Trade-and-FX training can collect contract passages, voucher descriptions and registration-source identities without amounts, rates or legal requirements. Proposed input contains documents rather than current bank confirmation. Preserve terms versus actual records downstream. Choices arrange passages or scope explanation without connecting online banking automatically; registration material establishes neither completed banking nor approved authority.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-source check | Proposed input | Observable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction relationship | Contract and present voucher identities | Other outcomes not borrowed |
| Purpose scope | Voucher description passed with passage | Recipient retains its purpose |
| Registration stage | Material separate from banking execution | Inspect current operation confirmation |
| Added evidence | Corresponding handling source and question | Explanation uses the actual addition |
| Formal business | Separate banking, retention and recognition | Learning is not a compliance finding |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Another transaction’s voucher requires party and contract correspondence checks. Lost voucher-purpose scope requires comparison of submitted and received wording. Registration described as this banking result needs corresponding operation confirmation. These concern subject, scope and stage rather than complete-looking document names.

## How to verify the setup

Retain contract, voucher status, version, chosen destination and received text. Repeat after current confirmation arrives and inspect use of the addition. Interaction waits and continues without accounting writes. Validation, banking, retention and staff recognition need actual downstream permission and receipts. Completed training establishes neither compliance, processed funds nor contractual responsibility.
