# Fresh-food concession counters: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

A fresh-food concession counter in a hypermarket is often run by a supplier who stations staff on site and is paid through a sales-share arrangement. Store service and internal training therefore involve three parties at once: the hypermarket fresh-food department, the concession supplier, and the store manager. The working documents are the concession contract, the daily sales report, the shrinkage record, and the food purchase inspection record. These documents update daily or per batch, and the sharing step hands them to on-site staff for study or handover. The material itself carries a subject, a batch, and an authorization state, and those attributes limit how far a shared item can be used.

Point-of-sale sales, supplier delivery notes, and store inventory counts come from different sources. A shared copy of training material does not replace the store's own check of the underlying vouchers. The concession contract defines the supplier and counter, the inspection record defines batch and quantity, and the shrinkage record defines a state that may still be pending. None of these three facts can be substituted for another.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created from the publish channel and the system generates a complete link that can be copied. The link identifier only grants access to the window. It is not a business authorization, and it does not show that the recipient is entitled to use a specific contract, batch, or shrinkage record.

Identity verification is limited to the commercial edition. It works through a POST root address, and the system decides whether to allow the user to continue by reading the `success` field returned by the interface. The returned `uid` is the user's unique credential and must be a string that does not contain the characters "|", "/", or "\", with a byte length of no more than 255. Otherwise the system returns an `Invalid UID` error. The `uid` is used to pull and save conversation records.

The anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and the same browser. Records are lost when the device changes or the browser cache is cleared. For this reason the system only allows a user to pull the 20 records from the last 30 days. Retrieval and similarity scores do not carry permission or entity isolation. A same-model retrieval association inside a knowledge base does not extend to an external system.

Hypothetical paper exercise: a store fresh-food department plans to share a training item with a newly stationed supplier employee. The source material reads: "Concession contract number A-001, supplier Alpha, counter is the chilled pork counter, contract valid until 31 December 2025. The purchase inspection record dated 5 March 2025 lists batch B-0305, quantity 40 kilograms, and the inspector has signed. Shrinkage record D-0306 records 2 kilograms of shrinkage on the same day, with status pending store manager review." The proposed sentence reads: "This counter has completed inspection and shrinkage approval for all batches on 5 March 2025 and can be used directly for external training." The pending request is to embed the proposed sentence in a no-login window share link for the stationed employee to study.

## Configuration and implementation checks

Hypothetical starting value for this exercise: set the expiration time to 30 June 2025. The reason is that the exercise contract is valid until 31 December 2025 and the training window is shorter than the contract period, so the value must be compared with the contract end date to confirm whether it covers the training cycle. This value is only a starting point for this exercise and is not an official default.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the link identifier as a business authorization. In the exercise the link only grants access to the window. The authorization state of contract A-001 and inspection record B-0305 must be checked separately. A link that opens does not show that the stationed employee is permitted to use the material.
2. Writing a pending state as completed. In the exercise shrinkage record D-0306 has status pending store manager review, and the proposed sentence states that approval is completed. These are mutually exclusive values on the same field, and the proposed sentence is not supported.
3. Treating retrieval or similarity as a permission filter. In the exercise, retrieving a material fragment does not show that the fragment is visible to the current `uid`. The `uid` returned by identity verification and the authorization scope of the material must each be checked.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the exercise source material and the proposed sentence, then compare whether "pending store manager review" and "approval completed" refer to the same status field of the same shrinkage record D-0306. If the status field values are mutually exclusive, the proposed sentence does not hold. If the material does not state the approval result, record the item as pending verification.
2. Input the exercise contract A-001 with validity until 31 December 2025, then compare whether the hypothetical expiration time of 30 June 2025 falls inside the contract validity period. If it falls inside, the link use scope matches the contract period. If it falls outside, record the gap and check whether the contract has been renewed.
3. Input the batch and quantity of 40 kilograms from inspection record B-0305, then compare whether the 2 kilograms in shrinkage record D-0306 belong to the same batch. If the batches match, the shrinkage and inspection can correspond. If the batch is not stated, record the item as not supported and do not infer a contradiction.
