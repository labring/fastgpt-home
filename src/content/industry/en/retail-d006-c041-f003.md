# Marketplace promotions: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Marketplace promotions, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training entry with registration and inventory-stage exercises without treating classroom tables as executed campaigns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share pairs a registration exercise with stock-locking wording, explicitly labeled for reading. Learners distinguish planned registration, submitted and awaiting confirmation. Inspect visible headings and stages through the complete link, then request a restricted approval original. Retain exposed approval text to investigate grants; if exercise stages become actual campaign execution, separately inspect source identity. Accessible anonymous material establishes neither operational applicability nor an executed activity.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Registration and stock exercise | Actual acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage original | Separate plan, submission and pending confirmation | Headings and classroom identity |
| Actual source | Approval originals excluded | Full response and resource grants |
| History gap | Product and campaign meant by stock | Provide earlier exercise across browsers |
| Evidence boundary | Locking plan does not establish available inventory | No actual execution inferred |
| Subsequent system | Separate explanation, registration and scoring | Inspect identity conditions and actual writing |

## Three common errors

- If registration exercises retrieve actual approval or stock originals, retain response and scope to inspect grants. Readable classroom material establishes no isolation.

- If another browser lacks product and campaign for inventory sufficiency, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot assume the earlier question or operational stock.

- If proposed registration or locking becomes approval or available stock, compare classroom stage and actual outcomes. Plans establish no execution; explanation submits no registration or approval writeback.

## How to verify the setup

Test initial exercises, revised stages and restricted actual registration sources, recording readable originals, denials and versions. Inspect usage and identity capability separately, including commercial-edition limits where relevant. A link identifier does not establish learner identity. Explanations can organize required material without submitting registration, changing stock or writing approval. Scoring and learning records need subsequent execution checks. Ended classroom discussion establishes neither written grades nor campaign acceptance; retain assumptions and unresolved conditions.
