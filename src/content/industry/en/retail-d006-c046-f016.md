# Marketplace category access: interactive forms for staff training

## What the documents look like

Marketplace category access happens before a listing goes live or a store is onboarded. The review object is the operating entity's qualifications and the chain of authorization behind the brand. The working documents are the business license, the trademark registration certificate, the brand authorization chain and the industry license. These are submitted by category recruitment operations, store onboarding specialists or a brand-side channel manager, and they are resubmitted at each onboarding rather than on a fixed schedule.

In store service and internal training, the form step collects the field values from those documents and passes them to later nodes. When the form input node triggers, the conversation enters an interactive state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user completes the interaction. The user selection node evaluates the user's choice and runs the corresponding branch. Neither node carries a business database of its own; field validation, approval, write-back and bulk operations belong to an external system or to later nodes.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The materials carry a subject, an object, a status and a validity range. A missing field in any of these affects the later judgment. The form input node collects what the user fills in and passes it downstream after submission; it does not decide whether an authorization is valid and does not verify whether a business license is active. The user selection node only routes by the choice made, and it is not a personnel directory or a business data selector.

The following is a hypothetical drill sample used to explain the checking method. It does not point to any real entity.

Material text (hypothetical): "The business license subject is 'Chenye Trading (hypothetical)', the unified social credit code ends in 0000X, and the registration status is active." "Trademark registration certificate No. 0000000 (hypothetical), registrant 'Chenye Trading (hypothetical)', valid until 2027-06-30." "The brand authorization letter states the authorizer is 'Chenye Trading (hypothetical)', the authorized party is 'Qinghe Store (hypothetical)', the authorized category is household appliances, the authorization period runs from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31, and the signature block shows no seals from either party."

Draft sentence (to be judged): "Qinghe Store has obtained complete authorization for the household appliance category and can submit for onboarding directly."

This draft sentence touches subject, object, status and validity range. The signature block of the authorization letter shows no seals, so whether the authorization has taken effect is unsupported. The active business license and the trademark certificate validity are separate fields and do not substitute for judging whether the authorization took effect. A form can collect these fields; whether onboarding is granted is decided by a later business step.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the authorization start and end collected by the form as proof that the authorization has taken effect. The input is that the signature block of the authorization letter in the sample shows no seals; the comparison object is whether the authorization has taken effect; the observable result is that the field remains pending verification and no direct-onboarding conclusion can be drawn.
2. Treating an active business license as covering every field of the authorization chain. The input is the active registration status on the business license; the comparison object is the authorized party and category on the authorization letter; the observable result is that when the subject and object do not match, the two cannot be merged into one judgment.
3. Treating the branch result of the user selection node as an approval conclusion. The input is the choice "authorization effective"; the comparison object is the signature status in the material text; the observable result is that the branch only decides the flow direction and approval still requires the external system.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical material text and the draft sentence; compare "complete authorization" in the draft sentence against the signature block status of the authorization letter. The condition is met when the draft sentence is marked pending verification and no direct-onboarding conclusion is output.
2. Input the authorization period 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 from the sample; compare the period stated in the draft sentence against that range. The condition is met when a period outside the range is marked unsupported and only matching fields within the same period proceed to further checking.
3. Input the paper empty result []; compare how the downstream design handles an empty value. The condition is met when the downstream shows no value obtained and keeps a record of the gap, without inferring that retrieval always returns empty or always returns a hit.

All of the above is paper checking. No test, deployment or approval was actually executed. Missing evidence remains missing, and a pending-review state does not constitute a valid authorization.
