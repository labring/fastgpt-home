# Wholesale stall trading: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Wholesale stall trading, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Expose only approved training text while checking payment and lease attachments separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

Suppose a stall-training note says handwritten sales need checking, payment receipts are incomplete, and lease terms are for internal teaching. Invented text detached from real traders can test a shared entry that separates sales registration, payment and lease conditions. Opening the page establishes no universal receipt access or permission to circulate a contract. Replies should identify unresolved materials. A sales slip proves no completed payment. This exercise includes no actual amount or payment action.

## Three common errors

- If the publisher reads a receipt but a visitor cannot, inspect the same attachment through the intended learner identity and entry logs. An administrator test cannot replace visitor evidence.

- If a shared answer converts a sale registration into paid funds, compare receipt evidence and the corresponding sale. Missing payment evidence remains unresolved.

- If embedding exposes internal lease terms, inspect sample selection, approved audience and original-file permissions. An embedded entry does not expand contract publication rights.

## How to verify the setup

Define the audience and approved sample text before configuring sharing or embedding for the actual version, without invented parameters. Ask through publisher and learner paths and inspect answers, citations and attachment addresses for excess disclosure. If training access must end, verify access after the actual closure. This paper exercise trades nothing and issues no payment request. Actual receipts and leases still need owner approval and appropriate access control.
