# Douyin storefront operations: workflow routing for staff training

## What the documents look like

Douyin storefront shelf operations center on product cards and the store experience score, which separates this work from live-room operations that follow a single broadcast rhythm. The four document types handled day to day are product qualification pages, experience score details, shipping violation appeals, and store announcements. These come from the Douyin store workbench, the Douyin e-commerce learning center, Selected Alliance data, and logistics interfaces. The supplied material gives daily and per-order update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time processing, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from those two values. Documented error consequences include traffic restriction, violation point deductions, and consumer compensation. For handover requests inside store service and internal training, the first question is what authorization state the material and the operation occupy. Retrieval and form collection do not by themselves constitute authorization to execute.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The Form Input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user completes the interaction. After the user fills required information and submits, the node collects the form information and passes it to later nodes. The User Selection node is also a user interaction node; when the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the corresponding branch. Interaction waiting, state recording, and dialog hiding follow the documented description. This page does not define a global state-recording switch and does not invent submission counts, automatic judgment, or snapshot guarantees.

Order writes, document backfill, and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later flow. Retrieval and relevance scores do not carry permission or entity isolation. Libraries built on different vector models cannot be associated together; verify identifiers first and exclude non-matching items rather than running a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.

Hypothetical paper exercise: a store service group at a Douyin storefront merchant uses a paper record in an internal training handover. The source text reads: "Product qualification page A was submitted for review on March 1, 2026, status pending review; shipping violation appeal B was registered on March 2, 2026, handling status pending; store announcement C was published on March 3, 2026, valid until March 31, 2026." The sentence under evaluation reads: "Qualification page A has passed review, appeal B has been fully handled, and announcement C is within its validity period." This sample is used only for paper comparison.

## Three common errors

1. Treating required information collected by a form as business authorization. In the sample, qualification page A has status pending review. Pending review does not demonstrate a lack of operating permission, and it does not equal review approval. Using a collected field directly as grounds for execution crosses the authorization boundary.
2. Treating the "yes" branch of the User Selection node as automatic judgment. The documentation states that the node evaluates the user's choice and executes the corresponding branch; branch connections must be verified one by one. Reading branch execution as the system completing a business judgment automatically omits the manual verification step.
3. Treating retrieval relevance as permission or entity isolation. A relevance score is not a category, entity, or permission filter, and retrieval does not constitute authorization. Libraries on different vector models cannot be associated together; verify identifiers first and exclude non-matching items.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample source text and the sentence under evaluation, then compare the review status field of qualification page A. The source states pending review; the sentence states passed review. Same subject, same field, mutually exclusive values, so the sentence is judged contradictory. When this condition holds, the sentence must be changed to pending review to match the source.
2. Input the sample source text and the sentence under evaluation, then compare the handling status field of appeal B. The source states pending; the sentence states fully handled. The sentence is judged contradictory. When this condition holds, the sentence must retain pending, or mark the evidence gap as awaiting verification.
3. Input the sample source text and the sentence under evaluation, then compare the validity period of announcement C. The source states valid until March 31, 2026; the sentence says the announcement is within its validity period. If no comparison date is given, no judgment is possible. When this condition holds, a comparison date must be supplied before the same-field comparison can reach a conclusion.

All three verification steps are paper expectations. They were not executed, and no test, approval, or acceptance is claimed.
