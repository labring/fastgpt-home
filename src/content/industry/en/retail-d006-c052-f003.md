# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Pinduoduo seller campaigns, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training entry with campaign-registration and refund-reading exercises, preserving the boundary around actual store material. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share uses registration and refund fragments without real orders, practicing stage and product correspondence. Inspect exercise identity through the complete link, then request restricted cost material or formal platform messages. Retain exposed operating wording to investigate grants. Avoiding actual data in the exercise does not isolate other resources. If a hypothetical application becomes an approved campaign, inspect source and stage separately; an accessible entry replaces no verification.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Campaign and refund exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom identity | Registration and refund fragments labeled proposed | No invented campaign approval |
| Operational restriction | Costs and formal messages excluded | Full response and resource grants |
| Across devices | Delivery of exercise, period and product | Provide absent exercise text |
| Abnormality definition | Status fragment supplies no complete measure | No actual rate or responsibility inferred |
| Subsequent action | Separate solving, scoring and business updates | Execution evidence for each |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If campaign-refund exercises read actual costs or internal messages, retain full response and grants. No real classroom orders does not isolate operating sources.

- If another device lacks period and product for an abnormal-refund question, inspect history and obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot automatically borrow another question’s status.

- If application fragments become an actual abnormality rate or responsibility finding, inspect definitions, full samples and outcomes. Proposed fragments supply no such evidence and execute no refunds.

## How to verify the setup

Test earlier exercises, revised stages and restricted operational sources, recording originals, denials and version-specific limits. Inspect required identity validation within commercial-edition capability. Classroom explanation can describe checking an application against its result without registering campaigns, executing refunds or changing stock. Scoring, grades and business-list writing need separate execution checks; generated answers establish no updates. Retain hypothetical status and missing conditions so training wording cannot become a conclusion about the actual store’s operations.
