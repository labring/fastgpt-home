# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: interactive forms for staff training

## What the documents look like

Pinduoduo low-price campaign operations revolve around campaign resource slots, campaign inventory, and refund-rate fluctuation. Compared with Tmall flagship store operations, the enrollment rhythm for low-price resource slots is denser, and the tolerance for error in campaign inventory and cost calculation is narrower. Day-to-day documents concentrate on campaign enrollment forms, product cost calculation sheets, after-sales refund slips, and platform in-site messages, updated daily or per campaign. In the store service and internal training task, these documents mostly enter the workflow as form entries and selection interactions. Missing fields or misread statuses lead directly to stockout penalties, loss-making order acceptance, and elevated after-sales rates.

The form input node and the user selection node are the two interactive nodes that carry this material. Neither node includes business database functions. Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations require an external system or a subsequent node. The enrollment form, cost calculation sheet, refund slip, and in-site message serve here only as collected or selected inputs. Retrieval or a submitted form does not constitute authorized execution.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

When the form input node is triggered, the conversation enters an interactive state, the workflow state is recorded, and after the user completes the required information and submits, the collected information is passed to subsequent nodes. When the user selection node is triggered, the conversation also enters an interactive state; after the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the "yes" branch. Both nodes wait for interaction before continuing downstream. The documents listed above are inputs at this step, not approvals.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a store submits an internal training handover packet. The source text reads: "This store plans to join a low-price resource slot campaign from June 10 to June 12, 2025. The enrollment form has been filled with the campaign price and estimated inventory. The cost calculation sheet notes that the campaign price is below the per-unit cost. The after-sales refund slip shows that the previous campaign's refund rate was above the usual level. The in-site message states that the enrollment deadline is June 9 at 18:00." The draft sentence to be judged is: "This campaign price has passed internal approval and training can be arranged and enrollment executed." The material only states that the enrollment form was filled, the cost calculation sheet noted a low price, the refund slip showed a high refund rate, and the in-site message gave a deadline. It does not state the approving party, approval time, or authorization scope. The phrase "has passed internal approval" in the draft sentence is unsupported.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration or check item | Relevant setting or implementation check on this page | Relation to the sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form input node | Enters interactive state when triggered, records workflow state, collects information after submission and passes it to subsequent nodes | Collects enrollment form and cost calculation sheet fields |
| User selection node | Enters interactive state when triggered, evaluates the choice and executes the "yes" branch | Handles the choice of whether to continue training or enrollment |
| Required information check | Lists the information that cannot be missing for this task; tests missing and completed cases separately | Checks whether campaign price, inventory, and deadline are complete |
| Interactive state waiting | Continues downstream execution after interaction completes | Does not advance subsequent nodes before submission |
| Downstream processing division | Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations are implemented in an external system or subsequent node | Approval and enrollment execution are not completed inside the node |

Hypothetical starting value for this paper comparison: set the prompt threshold for "campaign price below per-unit cost" as the starting point for this comparison. The reason is that the cost calculation sheet in the sample already notes this state. The comparison method is a field-by-field check of the cost calculation sheet source text against the enrollment form campaign price. This value is only a hypothetical starting point for this exercise, not an official default.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as completed approval. In the sample, the enrollment form is filled and the in-site message gives a deadline. The submission action only shows that information collection is complete. The approving party and authorization scope are not stated.

2. Treating execution of a user selection branch as business execution. Selecting "yes" only triggers the corresponding branch. Whether enrollment succeeds and whether inventory is locked still require an external system.

3. Treating a refund rate above the usual level as a direct finding of loss-making order acceptance. The refund slip only shows the previous campaign's refund rate state. Evidence of mutually exclusive values for the same period and the same field, compared with this campaign's price and gross margin, is absent.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample source text and the draft sentence. Compare whether the draft sentence contains an approval conclusion not stated in the material. If "has passed internal approval" appears, the condition is not met.

2. Input the enrollment form and cost calculation sheet fields from the sample. Compare whether the campaign price and per-unit cost are for the same period and the same field. When the cost calculation sheet notes a low price and the enrollment form campaign price matches, the check condition is met. The approval status remains to be verified.

3. Input the in-site message deadline and the enrollment form submission status. Compare whether the submission time falls before the deadline. When the material does not state a submission time, record it as a gap and do not judge the enrollment as valid.

These checks test whether the proposed copy sentence stays within the supplied source wording and its stated limits. They do not test identity certification, report analysis, or any other task. Where the material does not state a field, the value remains unknown. A pending request is not approval, an offered option is not execution, and an unsigned termination is not a completed termination. Retrieval is not authorization. The form and selection nodes collect values; business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation.
