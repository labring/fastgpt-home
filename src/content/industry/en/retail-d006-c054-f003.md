# Channel diversion reviews: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

Channel diversion review works on regional and price order, not on rebate amounts. The materials are diversion complaint forms, anti-counterfeit code records, penalty notices, and inspection photographs. Sources include the anti-counterfeit code system, e-commerce platform prices, distributor shipment records, and market inspections. In the store-service and internal-training setting, these materials become a question-and-answer entry that regional managers and store staff can open to check handling positions. Material updates arrive weekly or irregularly. The share entry itself does not carry bulk synchronization duties; it carries questions, answers, and record return.

Two features of this category shape the sharing step. Complaint forms carry a status field, and that status governs what a draft may state. Anti-counterfeit code records and shipment records carry region and date scope, and that scope governs which batch a statement may cover. A share link created and copied from the publish channel is a delivery address, not a business authorization. Identity verification, where configured, is performed by a third-party system that returns a success flag and a uid; the uid is used to pull and save conversation records. The anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and the same browser, so records across devices require separate identity and session design. Parameters such as expiry time, QPM, and points cap are set for the intended scope, and some parameters are limited by edition.

## Constraints at the shared access step

Three constraints matter for this page. First, the share link identifier does not constitute business authorization. Creating and copying a link places a question-and-answer entry; it does not approve a penalty, confirm a violation, or release a shipment. Second, retrieval results and form entries are not authorization execution. A retrieved complaint form or a filled field does not by itself permit a downstream business action. Third, the conversation record boundary is documented: the anonymous local identifier works on the same device and same browser, and pulling records is limited to the most recent 30 days and 20 records. Records across devices need separate design.

Assume the following paper exercise. A brand channel-control team plans to share a diversion complaint query entry with regional managers. The material text reads: complaint number A-001; the complained party is a distributor; the complaint reason is cross-region low-price sale; the status is pending verification. The anti-counterfeit code record shows the code segment of this batch belongs to the East China region, and the shipment record date is March 1. The proposed handover sentence reads: "This distributor's cross-region low-price sale is confirmed; please issue the penalty directly using the penalty notice template." The judgment point is that the complaint form status is pending verification, the materials carry no confirmation conclusion, and the draft sentence writes a pending status as confirmed. That draft is unsupported by the materials.

## Three common errors

1. Writing a pending complaint status as confirmed. Input: the complaint form text "status is pending verification." Comparison: the draft sentence "is confirmed." Observable result: the draft sentence exceeds the support of the material.
2. Treating the share link identifier as business authorization. Input: the link creation and copy operation. Comparison: the authorization boundary description. Observable result: link availability does not equal penalty executability.
3. Treating the points cap as a penalty amount cap. Input: the points cap parameter. Comparison: the official cost-attribution description. Observable result: the points cap constrains billing data only.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the draft sentence in the exercise, input the complaint form text and the draft sentence, compare the status field, and if the draft contains "confirmed" while the source reads "pending verification," judge it unsupported. If the draft instead preserves the pending status, the draft stays within the material.
2. For identity verification, input the authToken and the third-party interface return, compare success and uid, and if success is true and the uid format is compliant, identity verification passes. If success is not true, the system shows the error message. If the interface returns no uid, the uid requirement is unmet and the result remains unknown until a compliant uid is supplied.
3. For the conversation record boundary, input two scenarios: same device and same browser, and switched device. Compare the record pull result. If records are not visible after switching devices, that matches the documented boundary condition, and cross-device records require separate identity and session design.

These checks address the named primary task of drafting and sharing review material. They do not certify identities, approve penalties, or confirm that any business action occurred. Where the material is silent, the evidence gap remains.
