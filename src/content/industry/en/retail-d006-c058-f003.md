# Fresh-food supplier claims: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

Fresh-food supplier claims pursue quality liability for goods on arrival. They sit apart from near-expiry markdown handling, which addresses natural in-store loss. The working materials are quality exception forms, photographic evidence, deduction notices and claim reconciliation statements. Typical participants are fresh procurement, quality inspection and supplier business staff. Poor handling produces supplier disputes, absorbed spoilage cost or interrupted supply. Records update per batch and per month.

For store service and internal training, these materials must reach store staff and be embedded in daily operations. Sharing a link distributes access. It does not create claim authority and it does not execute a deduction. That distinction governs everything below.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created under the publishing channel, and the system generates the complete link. The link name is used for record display only and cannot serve as business authorization. Identity verification is limited to the commercial edition, and release depends on the returned success value and uid. Anonymous records depend on the same device and the same browser; carrying records across devices requires separate identity and session management.

Hypothetical drill sample: a store quality inspector submits a quality exception form reading, "On 10 March 2025, store A received batch B-0310 spinach; acceptance inspection found leaf rot at approximately 15 percent; three photographs attached; deduction requested." A proposed handover request reads, "Send this claim share link to the supplier business contact; once they click it, the deduction is confirmed." The subject, object and status needed for judgment are present in the source text. The authorization boundary for deduction confirmation is not stated.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the link identifier as deduction authority. The input is the share link; the comparison object is the quality exception form text. When the source text states no authorization clause, clicking the link produces no deduction effect.
2. Treating successful identity verification as claim approval. The input is the returned success and uid; the comparison object is the approval record. The fields differ, and successful release only means the conversation may begin.
3. Treating anonymous records as available across devices. The input is the same device and same browser; the comparison object is another device. Record loss is a documented boundary, and separate identity management is required.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample text and the proposed handover request. Compare whether the deduction confirmation states an authorizing subject and effective conditions. When the source text does not state them, record the item as pending verification rather than authorized.
2. Input the identity verification response fields success and uid. Compare whether uid excludes the specified characters and stays within 255 bytes. A conforming uid permits release; a non-conforming uid is observed through the Invalid UID error.
3. Input the same device and same browser against another device. Compare whether conversation records can be pulled. The same device can pull 20 records from the last 30 days; records are lost across devices, and separate identity management is required.
