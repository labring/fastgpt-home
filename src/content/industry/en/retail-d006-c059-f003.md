# Store cold-chain receiving: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Test a training entry with measurement-object exercises while protecting actual inspection sources and checking follow-up conditions across environments. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training entry supplies separate descriptions of vehicle-environment and product-surface measurements solely for identifying objects, without actual photos or site results. Inspect headings, times and objects through the complete link, then ask for a restricted acceptance note. Retain any exposed site wording and source to investigate grants. De-identifying an exercise does not isolate operational resources. If the answer merges the objects, also inspect lost headings, recording access and content-handoff issues separately.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-entry check | Measurement-reading exercise | Actual inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading and object | Separate environment and product surface | Delivery of originals with headings |
| Operational source | Site acceptance excluded from classroom access | Full response to a boundary question |
| Device handoff | Inspect tablet and computer history | Provide exercise when history is missing |
| Conditional boundary | Different objects and absent formal standards | Validity period supplies no conclusion |
| Subsequent system | Separate explanation, scoring and receiving update | Classroom identity and execution evidence |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |

| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If measurement-reading entries return actual acceptance records, retain site wording and full response to inspect resources. De-identified exercises establish no restricted batch scope.

- If computer follow-up lacks the tablet’s two objects, inspect history and obtain both passages. Anonymous sessions cannot guess environment or product-surface references.

- If objects merge into a direct comparison or completed inspection, compare headings, times and conditions. Classroom records supply no formal standard, measurement execution or food approval.

## How to verify the setup

Test visibility of earlier exercises, revisions and unrelated operational records, retaining full responses and denials. Inspect usage conditions by version. Where third-party identity is needed, verify commercial-edition scope and returned identity rather than calling the link identifier a learner account. Preserve classroom status in explanations, without inspection conclusions. Scoring, learning records and receiving-system updates need separate execution tests. A completed shared conversation performs no site measurement and approves no food batch.
