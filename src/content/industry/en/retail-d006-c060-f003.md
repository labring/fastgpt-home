# Fruit-store procurement: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

Fruit-store procurement runs on purchase orders, acceptance records, pesticide-residue test reports and shrinkage logs. The working inputs are wholesale-market price movement, supplier quotations, store sales and test reports. Inquiry and price comparison, acceptance grading and shrinkage control consume most of the working hours, and mistakes surface as spoilage losses, food-safety complaints and stockouts at the store. These materials update daily or per batch, so a share action in store service and internal training must attach to a specific batch and a specific date range. One shared link cannot serve as a long-term authorization.

The eight-dimension brief describes this category at a general level. It does not supply a named store, a real supplier, a real test-report number or a real acceptance result. Any figure used below is a clearly labelled hypothetical starting point for a paper exercise.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created under the publish channel, and the complete link generated by the system is copied for use. The link identifier is an access entry only; it is not a business authorization. The expiry time and QPM are set for the intended scope of use and checked against the actual version in use. QPM means the maximum number of visits per user per minute. The points cap is checked against the usage limit of the share entry and the team that bears the cost, and some parameters are restricted by version. Identity verification is restricted to the commercial edition and is judged by the success state and uid returned by the official interface. The anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and the same browser; records across devices need separate identity and session design. Retrieval and form fields collect values, while business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization each need their own downstream implementation.

Hypothetical paper exercise: a store fresh-produce supervisor submits an internal training material handover request on 10 March 2025. The material text reads: "Mandarin batch A arrived on 10 March, the acceptance record shows grade two, the pesticide-residue test report number is pending supplier submission, and the shrinkage log for that day records 3 kilograms." The proposed judgment sentence is: "This batch has completed all acceptance steps and can be used for store training sharing." The authorization boundary of the request is internal store training use, with no external publication.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a shared link as a business authorization. The input is the share link, the comparison object is the authorization boundary in the handover request, and the observable result is that link availability does not show the batch acceptance is complete.
2. Treating a pending test report as already supplied. The input is the material text "number pending supplier submission," the comparison object is the phrase "completed all acceptance steps," and the observable result is that the two do not agree.
3. Treating the anonymous local identifier as a cross-device identity. The input is the same share link opened on a different device, the comparison object is the documented same-device same-browser condition, and the observable result is that records may be lost.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the material text and the proposed judgment sentence from the hypothetical sample. Compare "grade two," "number pending supplier submission" and "shrinkage log 3 kilograms" against "completed all acceptance steps." The observable result is that the proposed sentence is not supported and the evidence gap must be recorded. The pending test report remains missing evidence, and the acceptance record does not by itself show that grading, residue testing and shrinkage entry are all closed.
2. Enter the share link with authToken concatenated and the identity verification root address. Compare the success value and uid returned by the interface. The observable result is that continuation is allowed only when success is true and the uid format is compliant. A uid containing "|", "/" or "\", or exceeding 255 bytes in length, returns the Invalid UID error. The finish interface has no specified return value and reports totalPoints and token.
3. Enter the expiry time of 10 April 2025 and the request date of 10 March 2025. Compare the two dates. The observable result is that the request date precedes the expiry time, so the link is usable within the hypothetical scope. This comparison does not show that any batch acceptance, test report or training publication was completed.
