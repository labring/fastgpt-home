# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Fresh-food weighing and sorting, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Check exercise answers, net-weight meaning and operating boundaries in a shared sorting-training page. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A hypothetical weighing handout says this exercise first weighs goods with their crate, while the empty-crate weight is missing. Another page explains net weight versus the combined reading without supplying a usable deduction. A shared course must retain that missing input. Inspect the course version and exercise actually opened. The words already weighed describe an exercise assumption; they establish no live scale measurement for actual goods. An embedded question entry is separate from weighing, calibration and recording a learner’s result, each requiring its own implementation and evidence.

## Three common errors

- If an older exercise’s crate record answers the new one, inspect complete link, exercise version and actual citation. A shared handout name cannot fill the present gap.

- If another browser receives follow the previous exercise without its material, inspect actual input and anonymous-session conditions. Request the exercise instead of assuming a deduction value.

- If an explanation becomes completed live weighing, inspect exercise marking, operator records and actual measurements. A textual answer creates no equipment-test evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Ask through the course link and embedded entry whether net weight is supported, retaining the explanation of the missing crate input. After adding a hypothetical crate note, check container and exercise identity before repeating the question. Another container’s relationship cannot be transferred. Supply the exercise again on a new device and inspect preservation of its teaching status. If a training ledger needs the answer, verify submission, receipt and recorded result separately. An accessible question entry establishes no completed grading record.
