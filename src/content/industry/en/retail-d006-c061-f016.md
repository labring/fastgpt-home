# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Fresh-food weighing and sorting, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Retain units, label reprints and equipment checks separately; a complete label proves no reweighing. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

An invented lesson packet describes one weight unit on a scale and another on a label template, without readings. An operation note records a label reprint only, and a shift sheet assigns follow-up of equipment confirmation. Collect batch, original units and actions, then choose unit or operation-record review. No measured weight, conversion value or calibration finding is supplied. Reprinting is not reweighing, and follow-up duties grant no equipment-certification authority. The exercise examines text handoff only.

## Three common errors

- If downstream input retains a weight field but loses its unit, compare scale wording, label template and actual received input. Compute no conversion and assume no common basis.

- If a label reprint becomes completed reweighing, inspect the recorded action and measurement evidence. Printing supplies no new measured result.

- If equipment follow-up becomes completed calibration, compare responsibility wording with device evidence. A staff name replaces no metrological confirmation.

## How to verify the setup

Test missing-unit and completed-unit inputs on both routes, retaining batch and action wording. Ask the learner which evidence remains absent. A template identifies units without supplying real values, and shift handover supplies no device state. Actual weighing, calibration, label writes and inventory records require equipment, authorized processes and evidence. This page defines no measurement procedure or statutory error allowance. Form interaction performs none of those operations and establishes no passed practical assessment.
