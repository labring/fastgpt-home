# E-commerce warehouse receiving: interactive forms for staff training

## What the documents look like

Receiving and putaway handles the first accounting-versus-physical confirmation when goods enter the warehouse. Compared with in-warehouse picking and packing, this step confirms what has actually arrived against what was purchased. The documents involved are the purchase arrival note, the receiving inspection sheet, the putaway task, and the discrepancy report. Data comes from the WMS, ERP purchasing, supplier delivery notes, and barcode scanning. The typical roles are the e-commerce warehouse, the receiving clerk, and the WMS administrator. When this step goes wrong, the consequences are inventory that does not match the books, later picking errors, and disputes with suppliers. Data updates per batch and per day.

For store service and internal training, the question is which materials, operations, and requests are in what authorization state at the point of operational handover. A form collects the values that are filled in and passes them to later nodes after submission. The form does not carry business database functions of its own. Retrieval and forms do not constitute authorized execution. These two points shape everything below.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The form input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution waits until the user completes the interaction, then continues downward. The user selection node is also a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, waits for the user to make a choice, evaluates that choice, and executes the "yes" branch. Both nodes can be added repeatedly and trigger execution.

Field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations are implemented as needed in external systems or later nodes. The nodes themselves do not perform these actions. When required information is missing, the interaction stays in the waiting state and only submits after the missing values are supplied, then passes values downward. Retrieval results do not distinguish business entities or permission scope, and similarity does not exclude unrelated or unauthorized records.

Assume a drill sample for checking. The store training handover material text reads: "This batch purchase arrival note number PO-2026-0417, arrival date 17 April 2026, supplier delivery note quantity 120 pieces, counted by the store receiving clerk on the same day." "Receiving inspection sheet inspection quantity 118 pieces, discrepancy 2 pieces, discrepancy reason pending supplier confirmation." "Putaway task has been assigned location A-03, status pending execution." The draft sentence to be judged reads: "This batch arrival inspection is complete, the discrepancy is confirmed, the putaway task has finished execution, and the case can be closed."

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as completed business approval. In the sample, the discrepancy reason is still pending supplier confirmation. Submission only means the interaction is complete. Approval and case closure require an external system or later nodes.

2. Treating the user selection branch decision as a personnel directory or business data selector. The node only evaluates the choice and executes the branch. It does not perform entity or permission filtering.

3. Treating retrieval similarity as a permission or entity isolation mechanism. Similarity does not exclude unrelated or unauthorized records, and retrieval does not constitute authorized execution.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample material and the draft sentence, then compare "discrepancy is confirmed," "putaway task has finished execution," and "case can be closed" in the draft against the source states. The source only states that the discrepancy reason is pending confirmation and that the putaway task status is pending execution. The draft goes beyond what the source supports and is judged unsupported.

2. Input two sets of form fills, one with all four required fields and one missing the inspection quantity, then compare submission behavior. With all fields present, the form submits and passes values downward. With a missing field, the interaction stays in the waiting state and only submits after the missing value is supplied.

3. Input discrepancy quantities 118 and 120 and compare the user selection branch. When the two are unequal, the non-0 branch executes.
