# Livestream violation appeals: workflow routing for staff training

## What the documents look like

Livestream violation appeals deal with penalties that have already been issued. The material set is built around the violation notice, the livestream recording, product qualification documents and the appeal statement, and the work requires reconstructing the evidence chain. In store service and internal training, store compliance, livestream operations and legal teams share these materials. Errors can lead to a product being banned from livestreaming, account traffic restrictions, or deduction of the security deposit. Data updates are irregular, and each violation triggers one round of material preparation.

The material formats differ. The violation notice states the penalized subject and the violation type. The recording must be located to a specific segment. Product qualification documents support the compliance position of the product. The appeal statement is a document still to be drafted. Workflow routing handles how these materials move through store and training steps, collects operation information, and passes it to later nodes; business verification and external synchronization belong to downstream implementation.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

When the form input node is triggered, the conversation enters an interactive state, collects required information, and after submission passes that information to later nodes. The user selection node executes the corresponding "yes" branch after the user makes a choice. Both node types record workflow state, and the dialog box is hidden during interaction. Retrieval or form collection does not constitute authorized execution, and the authorization status of a store handover request must be confirmed separately.

Hypothetical drill sample: a store submits a handover request. The source material reads: "The violation notice states that the penalized subject is Store A, the violation type is missing product qualification, and the penalty date is March 10, 2025; the attached recording file marks the segment start and end times as 20:14 to 20:19 on that day; in the product qualification library, the qualification status of this product is pending review." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "Store A completed the qualification resubmission on March 10, 2025 and received platform approval." This draft sentence involves two facts, completion of qualification resubmission and platform approval. The source text gives only a pending review status, and neither fact is supported.

The hypothetical retrieval starting point of 0.5 is only a comparison baseline for this paper drill. A similarity score does not carry permission or entity isolation functions, and retrieval does not constitute authorization.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a pending review status as completed. The sample source text states that the product qualification status is pending review. If the draft sentence reads "completed the qualification resubmission and received platform approval," the input and the source status do not agree. The comparison object is the qualification status field, and the observable result is that the draft sentence contradicts the source value.

2. Treating form submission as authorized execution. After the store submits the handover request, if document backfill or external status synchronization is triggered directly, the input is the information collected by the form, the comparison object is whether a downstream business interface exists, and the observable result is that without such an interface the action has no execution basis.

3. Treating retrieval results as permission filtering. The sample query returns several entries. If this is used to conclude that a store has the right to view all records, the input is the returned list, the comparison object is the authorization boundary statement, and the observable result is that the retrieval results contain no permission field, so authorization cannot be judged from them.

## How to verify the setup

- Verification one: Input the sample source material and the draft sentence. Compare "completed the qualification resubmission and received platform approval" in the draft sentence against "qualification status is pending review" in the source. The observable result is that the two values are mutually exclusive, the draft sentence is unsupported, and it should be recorded as pending confirmation.

- Verification two: Input the store handover request. Compare the form output fields against the later node input fields. The observable result is that the fields correspond item by item and the information is passed completely; if any field is unmapped, record the gap.

- Verification three: Input the sample query. Compare the returned entries against the expected entries. The observable result is that the returned entries contain no permission field, and the similarity score is not used as a basis for authorization; if the return is empty, use the paper input [] to check the downstream design, without claiming that retrieval necessarily returns empty.
