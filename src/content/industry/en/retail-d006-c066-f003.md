# Short-video affiliate sales: shared access for staff training

## What the documents look like

Short-video affiliate sales rely on long-term content exposure and product link conversion, without depending on live explanation. In store service and internal training scenarios, the sharing and embedding step must handle four document types: short-video scripts, affiliate product lists, commission plans, and violation feedback, with daily and weekly update frequencies. During store handover, the current authorization state of materials must be made clear: whether scripts are confirmed, whether product lists are checked, whether commission plans are effective, and whether violation feedback is processed. A share link provides only an access entry point and cannot serve as a business authorization credential.

## Constraints at the shared access step

The no-login window is created from the publishing channel and the complete system-generated link is copied; the link identifier cannot serve as business authorization. Expiration time and QPM are set according to the scope of use and checked against the actual version, where QPM means the maximum number of visits per user per minute. The points cap is used to check the usage limit and cost attribution of the sharing entry, and some parameters are restricted by version. Identity verification is limited by the commercial edition scope and relies on the official interface returning a success status and uid. Regarding conversation record boundaries, the anonymous local identifier depends on the same device and same browser, and cross-device records require separately designed identity and session management.

During internal store training handover, it is necessary to distinguish existing node behavior, actually configurable fields, manual verification actions, and business actions that require separate system implementation. Retrieval or form collection values do not constitute authorized execution, and business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization each require their own downstream implementation.

Hypothetical drill sample: the original store training material text reads "This share link is used for new employee short-video affiliate sales operation drills, valid until June 30, 2025, for internal store training use only. The affiliate product list is based on the June 20, 2025 version." The draft sentence to be judged is "This link has been authorized for external distribution promotion."

## Three common errors

1. Treating the share link as business authorization. The draft sentence states "has been authorized for external distribution promotion," while the original material text only says "for internal store training use only." The two purpose fields are mutually exclusive, and the draft sentence is unsupported.
2. Treating the identity verification interface returning success as completion of business approval. success being true only indicates that verification passed and the user may continue operating. The original material text records no approval field, and the "has been authorized" in the draft sentence lacks evidentiary support.
3. Treating the validity period in the material as the authorization validity period. The original text "valid until June 30, 2025" describes the link's usable period and does not state how the authorization state changes after that date. The draft sentence conflates link availability with external promotion authorization, which remains to be verified.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical drill sample original text and the draft sentence, compare the purpose fields "for internal store training use only" and "external distribution promotion," and when the two are mutually exclusive, judge the draft sentence as unsupported and record the evidence gap.
2. Input the expiration time field in the share link configuration, compare the original material text "valid until June 30, 2025" with the current date; if past that date the link cannot be used, and if not past it, continue checking the purpose field.
3. Input the success and uid returned by the identity verification interface, check whether success is true; when success is not true, display the error message and do not continue operating; when success is true, it only indicates that verification passed and does not indicate completion of business approval.
