# Group-buy leader operations: staff training with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Group-buy leader operations, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Retain commission conditions and pickup-confirmation subjects across leader-training turns. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Suppose a leader agreement requires reconciliation before settlement, a commission sheet awaits review, a pickup list has only leader entries, and an announcement is a draft. Questions about settlement followed by pickup progress need inspection of original text in actual history. Draft notices and earlier answers replace no agreement or customer acknowledgment. This invented exercise supplies no real commission or pickup quantity.

## Three common errors

- If only a notice summary remains but settlement becomes definite, compare actual history, the agreement and pending-review state for omissions.

- If leader registration becomes customer-confirmed pickup, inspect the recorder and customer receipt. Follow-ups supply no confirming person.

- If earlier commission conditions extend to another settlement period, inspect agreement version and period. A new question expands no old condition.

## How to verify the setup

Add the agreement, sheet and pickup receipt across turns while asking who confirmed what. Repeat in a fresh conversation with final materials and retain actual requests. Instructions should state periods, speakers and gaps; inspect actual history selection without invented limits. External notices and commission payments need separate authority. Training text performs neither. Retain pickup-record time separately from the settlement period; one pickup entry cannot establish an entire period’s commission.
