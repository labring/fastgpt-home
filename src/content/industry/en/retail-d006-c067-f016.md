# Group-buy leader operations: staff training with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Group-buy leader operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. A group-leader training form separates list exercises, group notices and actual fulfilment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A proposed training page practices checking an example list, with no actual notice sent. Historical practice concerns another cohort. Collect cohort and exercise question so learners may request subject details or continue the example. A person column does not establish collected member data, and training completion establishes no sent notice. Preserve cohort and exercise purpose downstream instead of importing an older completion state.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Leader-training form | Exercise scope | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example list | Checking practice | Not real member data |
| Cohort | Current versus older | Inspect identity |
| Actual notice | Not sent | Add no completion |
| Choice | Continue or obtain detail | Not a send action |
| Business execution | Group authority and receipt | Verify separately |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

An example list promoted to real data needs purpose and source checked. Another cohort’s result needs cohort identity checked. A continue option promoted to a sent notice needs an actual sending record. These concern material type, subject and execution.

## How to verify the setup

Compare the submitted exercise with received input and ask what this run covers and leaves undone. Extra example notes change reading scope only. Waiting and continuation access no real group or messages. Actual notices and fulfilment need separate permission and evidence; training forms perform no business registration. Keep the example list visibly identified as illustrative. Actual notification recipients need evidence from the current activity; record the missing list explicitly instead of copying an older group into the proposed action.
