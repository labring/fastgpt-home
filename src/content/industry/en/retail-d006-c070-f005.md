# Cross-border product labels: staff training with multi-turn prompts

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Cross-border product labels, this page examines FastGPT multi-turn prompts. Retain market, product and test scope across label-training turns; translation grants no clearance. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the multi-turn prompts step

Suppose an English label is a review draft, a report covers specified samples, an MSDS has missing pages, and a restricted-products list applies to one market. A meaning question followed by a sale-eligibility question needs fresh inspection of subject and actual evidence. Translation establishes no clearance. These invented passages contain no actual testing or legal finding. Missing pages and market boundaries remain explicit despite earlier explanations.

## Three common errors

- If sample testing expands to every model, inspect product identifiers and report scope. Continuous history expands no sample.

- If an MSDS page gap vanishes in a summary, compare the original inventory and actual passages. Earlier explanations cannot supply missing pages.

- If one market’s list determines another market’s eligibility, compare destination and rule version. Shared translated names establish no shared applicability.

## How to verify the setup

Add label, report and list separately while asking what each supports. Compare fresh and continuing conversations and retain actual history. Instructions should state missing fields and applicable scope. Actual label compliance requires current destination rules and professional review separately. This exercise reads documents without approving or listing products.
