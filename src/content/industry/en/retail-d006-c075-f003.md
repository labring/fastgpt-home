# Creator sales campaigns: staff training with shared access

## What the documents look like

For staff training in Creator sales campaigns, this page examines FastGPT shared access. Let commercial learners distinguish sample and performance terms while testing exercise identity and restricted agreements in a training share. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the shared access step

A proposed training share pairs sample-dispatch wording with a livestream schedule, clearly labeled a reading exercise without actual creator agreements. Inspect visible content through the complete link, then request a restricted formal agreement. Retain exposed commercial wording and resource configuration to investigate grants. If a hypothetical schedule becomes a confirmed booking, separately inspect source identity in input and response. Readable classroom material establishes neither isolated agreements nor an event that occurred.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Training-share check | Creator-term exercise | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exercise identity | Sample and schedule wording labeled proposed | No invented confirmed booking |
| Agreement restriction | Actual commercial terms excluded | Returned passages and configuration |
| History handoff | Terms and limitation across devices | Provide exercise when prior question is absent |
| Distinct obligations | Sample dispatch is not livestream execution | No complete performance from one source |
| Subsequent system | Separate solving, samples, schedules and scores | Execution evidence for each |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Login-free window` | Copy the complete generated sharing link. |
| `Expiry and QPM` | QPM is the per-user requests-per-minute limit. |
| `Credit limit` | Inspect usage and charging conditions by entry and edition. |
| `Identity verification` | Third-party authentication has commercial-edition restrictions. |
| `Conversation-history boundary` | Anonymous identity depends on the same device and browser. |

## Three common errors

- If sample-schedule exercises read actual commercial agreements, retain terms and full response to inspect resources. No real agreement in class does not isolate backend contracts.

- If computer performance follow-up lacks phone sample terms, inspect history and classroom limitations, then obtain the exercise. Anonymous identity cannot inherit another session’s obligations.

- If samples or hypothetical schedules become completed livestream performance, compare obligations and execution evidence. Classroom records establish no performance, publication or settlement.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect initial exercises, revised schedules and restricted agreements, recording visible originals, denials and versions alongside access and usage conditions. Required third-party identity capability needs commercial-edition scope and return tests. Solving an exercise publishes no schedule, sends no sample and writes no grade. Business actions and learning records need downstream execution checks. Preserve proposed status and unresolved conditions rather than turning a training answer into completed creator performance or a settled cooperation account.
