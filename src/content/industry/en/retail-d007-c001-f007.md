# B2B marketplaces: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

The proposed platform settlement statement lists transaction totals and fees for a period, which a management report needs to summarize. Conversation logs help inspect what that particular request read and returned. Associate the target conversation with the statement identity, then inspect runtime details and actual responseData fields against the interface contract. Fee definitions, the aggregation method and matching periods still require business records and implemented calculations; the presence of logs does not establish them.

## Three common errors

A final settlement amount in the contract differs from the amount in the platform statement. Inspect both passages and their applicable scope before checking whether they entered the same conversation. Retain user input, retrieved citations, external responses and model interpretation separately to locate how each value was used. The phrase settlement amount in a log does not establish matching definitions. A field absent from the response cannot be presumed retained in the background; the cause needs further evidence. If a test exchange is added to the formal settlement conversation, inspect entry point, chatId and original history, keeping the two call contexts separate. A test explanation is not evidence of this financial operation or formal reconciliation.

## How to verify the setup

Settlement totals alone cannot support sales shares by product category; classification and corresponding sales records are missing. Register that business gap separately from technical logs, identify its provider and inspect the original question’s actual input and response after completion. Check identity fields in their test or actual-call context. Reconciliation conclusions, vouchers and audit retention require an implemented business process. A successful conversation or searchable log does not establish a verified report.
