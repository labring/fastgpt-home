# MCN operations: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

An MCN operating review can first compare the subjects and periods covered by account material, schedules and contracts. Proposed sources describe a planned session without execution evidence, so scheduling establishes neither execution nor results. Before reading account records through HTTP, obtain a real service contract covering address, method and query scope. No particular MCN endpoint is presumed, and a display name does not establish the contracting party.

## Three common errors

A request may use the schedule period while the response covers another interval. Inspect the original period statement, transmitted values and response coverage before interpreting this review. Another failure treats completed training as completed account operations. Compare returned passages with the conclusion and retain these as different records. A successful exchange cannot supply execution counts or effects when its evidence does not cover the question. A further proposed passage can state that a session was planned and later cancelled, beside a contract naming only cooperation categories. Establish whether the cancellation concerns this session; the contract explains scope without supplying actual session duration or account performance. If the external response contains training instead, identify the source mismatch before including it in an operations list. Notification of cancellation also differs from proof that the session never occurred. The record owner may need to provide the actual session source and explain what its coverage includes. Keep those materials attached to their own subjects rather than assigning one account’s outcome to every scheduled creator. The report should identify the unresolved execution question, who can supply its evidence and which source would change the explanation, without manufacturing a complete operating history from a schedule and contract.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the cooperation subject, review interval, actual request and returned scope. After execution evidence is supplied, repeat the question and limit interpretation to obtained facts. Check parameters, headers and any appropriate body against the external contract, keeping authentication protected. Account operations, ledger changes or external report submission require separate authority and results. Reading completion establishes neither reconciliation nor absence of activity when records are missing.
