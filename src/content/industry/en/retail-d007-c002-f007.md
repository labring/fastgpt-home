# MCN operations: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. MCN review traces agency aggregates, creator correspondence and action stage through logs. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose an agency aggregate records content submission only, creator mapping awaits review and settlement wording requests confirmation from both parties. Locate this chatId and project, then inspect whether actual input and history retain these conditions. Obtain runtime detail under the official contract. Returned fields establish no complete context containing individual records, contracts or fund evidence. No performance measure or actual amount is supplied.

## Three common errors

Agency submission reported as individual publication needs subject and publishing evidence checked. An unreviewed mapping presented as confirmed ownership needs creator correspondence checked. Requested confirmation reported as settled accounts needs acceptance and fund evidence checked. These concern aggregation level, identity reliability and action stage.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect identity and history separately for debug and production, locating aggregate wording and creator evidence independently. Added identity corrects ownership alone while performance and settlement still need separate support. An absent individual item means unobserved detail in this return, without proving no collaboration. Publication, performance recognition and payment depend on authorized business processes. Log review neither calculates nor approves those outcomes. Keep aggregate coverage beside source wording and leave creator mapping gaps explicit so individual conclusions remain within supplied evidence.
