# Shopify fulfillment: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A Shopify report review should define the periods and states covered by orders and refunds. Proposed material supplies order details and a refund passage without a complete corresponding list. Before an external HTTP reading, inspect which conditions the real contract supports and map the supplied scope without assuming automatic completeness. An order’s current state differs from events during a period, and settled status does not establish absence of after-sales issues.

## Three common errors

Filtering only by order creation time cannot support a review of the refund-processing period. Inspect period meanings, request conditions and actual coverage. An empty result also needs its subject, authorization and contractual meaning checked before asserting no refunds. Missing records do not establish that an event never occurred. Without returned inventory evidence, order and refund passages cannot establish completed deductions or replenishment. A separate ordinary-text example can describe an order placed during the review period and a refund requested later. Reviewing what later happened to orders from this period requires following those orders, while reviewing refund activity during the period requires the relevant processing sources. Those questions should not silently share one list. Ask the record owner which population the returned material represents and retain the question beside its actual coverage. If a response is only an item-level aggregate, check whether any supplied evidence links it back to individual orders before explaining a particular transaction. The report must keep the order’s present state separate from its earlier state and from the timing of a refund request. Missing linkage is a source requirement, rather than permission to extend one transaction’s status to every order containing the same product.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the question, subject coverage, sent conditions and the response’s list scope. Preserve absent completeness evidence and repeat after corresponding records arrive. Check headers separately from business parameters and structure any appropriate body under the external contract without exposing authentication. Procurement, refund entries or report writes require separate authority and actual states. Completing HTTP reading does not establish reconciliation among orders, payments and stock.
