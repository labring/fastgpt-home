# General import declarations: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like
General import declaration work centres on the customs value, freight and insurance charges, and the payment of duties and taxes. The main data sources are the International Trade Single Window, the customs tariff, the freight forwarder's arrival notice, and foreign exchange payment vouchers. Records update once per declaration. The documents handled day to day are the import declaration form, the commercial invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading, and the contract. Operating reviews and exception retrospectives need to align the same shipment across these documents by subject, period, and field. The database and operations layer keeps those records searchable, comparable, and recoverable. Because errors in import declaration lead to duty recovery with late fees, cargo detention at port, or administrative penalties, retrospective review places high demands on checking document versions and field consistency.

## Constraints at the database operations step
The database carries business source data and the vector retrieval backend separately, and the corresponding service is checked against the actual deployment. Connection and load checks use the connection counts, errors, and latencies observed in the specific deployment, and do not copy untested concurrency thresholds. Backup and recovery are verified in two separate steps: that a backup is produced, and that an actual restore succeeds, with a comparable sample and record retained. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample is checked; process liveness alone is not sufficient evidence. The same-vector-model requirement for retrieval association applies only within one vector model and does not extend to business registry sites or other external systems. Semantic recall and relevance scores do not carry permission or entity isolation.

Hypothetical drill sample: On 10 March 2025, an importer's customs manager submits a retrospective request, attaching three sentences of commercial invoice source text — "Invoice No. INV-2025-0310, issue date 5 March 2025, seller Company A, buyer Company B, goods valves, quantity 100 pieces, unit price USD 20, trade term FOB." "Bill of lading No. BL-2025-0308, port of loading Busan, port of discharge Qingdao, freight collect." "Contract No. CT-2025-001, signed 28 February 2025, agreed trade term FOB." Proposed sentence for assessment: "The customs value of this declaration should include freight." The request is in a pending-review state, which does not indicate that any database write or external synchronisation has been authorised.

## Three common errors
1. Treating the pending-review retrospective request as authorisation to perform a database write. Input: the pending-review request in the sample. Comparison point: the request's status field. Observable result: the status has not become approved, so a write is not performed.
2. Treating a semantic recall result as a basis for permission or entity isolation. Input: the similar records returned by retrieval. Comparison point: the subject that owns the record and the authorised scope. Observable result: a high similarity score does not indicate that the subject is authorised to view that record.
3. Treating process liveness after a configuration change as completed business recovery. Input: the service state after the change. Comparison point: the query result for the same shipment's business sample. Observable result: if the process is alive but the sample records are inconsistent, the check is still incomplete.

## How to verify the setup
1. Input the commercial invoice, bill of lading, and contract source text from the sample. Compare the subject, period, and trade term fields across the three documents. Observable result: the subject and period for the same shipment are consistent, all trade terms are FOB, and the freight-collect field corresponds to the information the proposed sentence requires, so the checking condition is met.
2. Input the proposed sentence "The customs value of this declaration should include freight." The comparison point is whether the sample source text states the customs value composition for this declaration. Observable result: the sample does not provide the declaration's customs value field, so the proposed sentence is unsupported and the evidence gap must be recorded.
3. Input the pending-review request from the sample. The comparison point is the request status and the authorisation boundary. Observable result: the status is pending review, which does not indicate authorisation to perform a database write, external synchronisation, or business action, so those operations remain unexecuted.

This page addresses only general import declarations / operating reviews and exception retrospectives / database operations. During operational handover, the authorisation state of materials, operations, and requests is recorded; retrieval and forms are not treated as authorisation to execute.
