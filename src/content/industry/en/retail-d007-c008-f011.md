# Amazon FBA preparation: operating reviews with database operations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT database operations. Validate recovery against inventory snapshots and later receiving events, preserving distinct responsibilities for original reports and indexes. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the database operations step

An operations sample pairs an early-week inventory report with a later receiving event, retaining shipment, version and record date. Identify responsibilities of original-data and vector services in the actual deployment, then read both recovered sources. A searchable shipment title without the receiving statement needs comparison of the original and returned content to inspect coverage. An index does not establish recovery of the later text, and successful database connection does not guarantee completeness of inventory material.

## Three common errors

- If the shipment title is searchable but the later receiving statement is unreadable, retain source, date and response to inspect original coverage. An index does not restore an event.

- If updated originals exist but only the early snapshot is cited, inspect indexes, retrieval and answer input separately. Locate stale stages without inferring current stock from scores.

- If local recovery becomes reconstructed platform or forwarder status, inspect backup inclusion and retain excluded sources as gaps. Technical reading proves neither physical receipt nor handled compensation.

## How to verify the setup

For the same carton and question, compare originals, citations and answers before and after change. Record connection errors and elapsed time under the actual workload instead of untested capacity thresholds. Restore and read a report and receiving event from backup, retaining gaps and versions. Technical recovery establishes neither physical receipt, revised inventory nor processed compensation. Operations records should state only verified material coverage; business facts still require actual platform reports and event sources.
