# Amazon FBM fulfillment: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Amazon FBM fulfillment is handled by the seller, who also sets the return address. Platform warehouse inspection is not part of this flow. The daily materials are order pick sheets, shipment confirmation records, return address settings, and logistics tracking tables. These materials are produced per order and summarized by day. Customer service email and carrier systems each hold their own fragments of the same case.

For operating reports and exception review, the conversation log and audit work compares one order's handling record in customer service conversations against those documents. The comparison asks whether the conversation record and the paper record point to the same subject, the same period, and the same field. In the documented chat interface, chatId identifies a session under one application, and dataId identifies one conversation under that session. Together they decide which log a record belongs to. Industry material gives a per-order and daily update rhythm; this page uses that only to understand material pacing, and does not infer batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time behavior, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from it.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A conversation log shows that a request and a response were recorded. It does not show that a return address took effect, that a carrier accepted a parcel, or that a refund was executed. A relevance score is a similarity measure. It is not a category, entity, or permission filter, and it does not separate records belonging to different stores or different customers. Retrieval is not authorization.

Run detail requires setting detail according to the documented contract. In non-stream mode the complete result is held in responseData; in stream mode results are distinguished by event. Log existence and completion of a compliance audit are two different things. Reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements belong to a full business solution implemented elsewhere.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: the source material has three sentences. Sentence one, from customer service email: "Order A-1001, buyer reports non-delivery, carrier tracking number TRK-7788, last trace shows in transit." Sentence two, from the same row of the logistics tracking table: "A-1001, TRK-7788, status: in transit, update date March 12." Sentence three, from the return address settings page: "The return address for the store owning A-1001 is US-CA-01, effective date March 1." The draft sentence to be judged is: "A-1001 has completed refund, and return address US-CA-01 has expired." That draft asserts two facts, completed refund and expired address, and the material contains no record for either.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a similarity score in the log as a basis for permission or entity filtering. The score expresses retrieval relevance only and does not exclude records from other stores. Check: send the same query, compare the dataset_id in the returned items against the target library identifier, and observe whether items from a non-target library are mixed in.
2. Treating a refund request awaiting review as a completed refund. A request under review is a pending state, and refund execution needs a downstream implementation. Check: search the material for a refund completion field; if only a request record exists, judge that fact unsupported.
3. Inferring missed recall after jointly associating libraries built on different vector models. Different models cannot be jointly associated; compare identifiers and exclude non-conforming ones. Check: compare the model identifier of each library, record the non-conforming ones, and keep the actual observation without inferring a run result.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the three source sentences of the hypothetical sample and the draft sentence, then compare "has completed refund" in the draft against whether a refund completion field exists in the material. If the material holds only a non-delivery report and an in-transit status, the sentence is unsupported and must be marked for verification.
2. Input "return address US-CA-01 has expired" from the hypothetical sample and the effective date March 1 recorded on the return address settings page, then compare whether an expiry date or expiry operation record exists. If neither appears, the expiry fact is unsupported.
3. Input chatId fbm-case-0312 and dataId rec-0312-01, then compare whether appId, chatId, and dataId in the returned record match the request. A match confirms log attribution; a mismatch means the difference is recorded and the gap kept. All of the above is paper comparison; no interface was actually called.
