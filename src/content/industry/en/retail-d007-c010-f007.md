# JD POP seller operations: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

JD POP third-party store operations center on open platform dispute rules and warranty-period service document handling. The daily materials include the product information sheet, the shipping time commitment, the dispute handling form, and the warranty-period service record. Data comes from the JD merchant backend, the JD help center, the JD open platform rules, and logistics tracking, with updates occurring daily and per order.

Operating reports and exception reviews require aligning the conversation record of every dispute and every warranty service against the underlying business documents. When materials are missing, responsibility cannot be determined, and the common consequences are a sustained transaction dispute, expanded after-sales responsibility, and platform demerit points. The role of conversation logs and auditing at this step is to retain session and run records as checkable input for review. The existence of a log does not by itself constitute a completed compliance audit.

Two features of this category shape the task. First, store operations work from open platform dispute rules and warranty-period service records, so the review must connect a specific order and its documents to a specific conversation. Second, updates occur daily and per order, so the review unit is the individual order rather than an aggregate batch. Conversation identity must be checked against chatId to match the target conversation with the business record, and sessions from different entry points must not be counted together.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

When run details are needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData. Business data that is not returned cannot be assumed to have been retained in full. Identity fields in test mode may differ from formal calls, so inputs, history, and returned content need to be checked separately. Reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements are implemented through a complete business plan outside this feature.

Hypothetical drill sample: A POP store after-sales supervisor processes a warranty-period service record on 13 October 2024. The source materials read as follows. The product information sheet states that the order product is a Model A electric kettle with a 12-month warranty period. The shipping time commitment states dispatch within 48 hours of order placement. The dispute handling form states that the buyer claims the kettle body had a dent on arrival, and the logistics tracking shows a signed delivery time of 10 October. A draft handover request reads: "Please merge and archive this session's conversation log with the above dispute handling form, and confirm warranty responsibility." The subject of this request is the store after-sales supervisor, the object is the session under the specified chatId together with the dispute handling form, the status is pending archive, the effective scope is this order, and the authorization boundary is limited to this store's materials for this order.

## Three common errors

1. Counting sessions from different entry points together by chatId. In the sample, if page sessions and API sessions are merged for statistics, the input is two chatIds from different sources, the comparison object is the official source parameter that distinguishes them, and the observable result is that the session list total does not match the count of business documents.

2. Asserting that run details are complete without setting detail. In the sample, if detail is false, the input is a response that does not return responseData, the comparison object is the documented behavior of the detail field, and the observable result is that node run records cannot be checked and the retention status of business data remains unknown.

3. Treating the existence of a log as a completed compliance audit. In the sample, the archive request covers only merging the log with the dispute handling form; the input is the log file and the form, the comparison object is the reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements of a complete business plan, and the observable result is that with no authorization execution record the archive status remains pending verification.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample source materials and the draft handover request, compare whether the subject, object, status, effective scope, and authorization boundary in the request match the materials, and observe the result: if the archive request does not state an authorization execution record, judge it as pending verification and do not judge it as completed archiving.

2. Input the sample chatId with a session list request using source=api, compare whether the chatId in the returned list matches the sample, and observe the result: if the chatId in the list differs from the sample, record it as a session ownership mismatch and recheck.

3. Input the sample dataId with a conversation run details request using detail=true, compare whether responseData contains the corresponding node run record, and observe the result: if responseData is empty or lacks the corresponding node, record it as run details not obtained and do not infer that business data has been retained in full.
