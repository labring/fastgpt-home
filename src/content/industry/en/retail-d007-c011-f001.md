# Refund-only disputes: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Refund-only disputes, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A refund-only review must distinguish application, appeal submission and actual handling, without turning an application into a completed remedy. Proposed sources contain the claim and dispatch or receipt passages while lacking a platform decision and complete scoped list. An actual HTTP contract must define retrievable states, subjects and periods before references are checked. After-sales, carrier and warehouse systems are not assumed to be connected, and a customer statement is not verified product evidence.

## Three common errors

Application-period conditions cannot directly support a conclusion about cases completed in that interval. Inspect event meaning and response coverage. Another order’s receipt also needs correspondence checks instead of filling this case’s gap. Empty results may concern unsuitable conditions; before checking the service definition, they establish neither absent disputes, absent appeals nor lack of platform handling. Retain the particular evidence still needed. Another ordinary-text example can state that the customer applied, the merchant submitted an explanation and the platform result is absent. Merchant submission is not platform acceptance, and a customer application is not receipt of a refund. Reviewing processing progress requires separate source status for statements, submissions and business outcomes. A narrower review of available materials cannot silently become a judgment about responsibility across cases. Later evidence still needs correspondence to this case and to its actual business time. Do not fill an absent stage with another order’s document merely to create a complete-looking chain. The report should identify the stage reached by each supplied record, which connection remains unestablished and what evidence would support the next conclusion, without borrowing authority from an external response that contains several different source types.

## How to verify the setup

Record event stage, order, actual request and the absent decision. Repeat after corresponding handling evidence arrives and limit conclusions to retrieved records. Check parameters separately from authentication headers and structure a body where appropriate under the contract, without publishing credentials. Refunds, claims and report writes need separate permission and actual business states. Reading establishes neither platform agreement with either party nor complete dispute reconciliation.
