# Warehouse picking and packing: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Warehouse picking, checking, and packing handles outbound orders. It does not handle supplier arrivals. The working records for this stage come from the WMS, the order system, the electronic waybill platform, and weighing equipment. Day-to-day documents include wave picking lists, checking records, waybills, and packaging consumable sheets. Data updates per order and per hour. An operating review or exception review needs to link the scanning and checking actions tied to one wave picking list, the waybill generation record, and the packaging consumable issue record into one traceable chain.

Conversation logs and audit at this step let a checker or a packing supervisor look back at one conversation by chatId: its input, its history, and its run details. The review then compares whether the picking list and checking record linked to that conversation match the WMS. The conversation API does not return the actual consumed Token value. When that value is needed, set detail=true and calculate the tokens value inside responseData manually. The model, temperature, and similar parameter fields passed to the conversation API have no effect; the orchestration decides these. Retrieval or a collected form value does not constitute authorization. Reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements belong to a complete business plan handled elsewhere. The existence of a log does not mean a compliance audit is complete.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Conversation log retention and querying rely on the fields of the official interface, not on retrieval similarity. Fetching the conversation list uses appId, offset, pageSize, and source; source=api returns only conversations created through the API and does not fetch conversations from the page. Fetching the dialogue list uses appId, chatId, offset, pageSize, and loadCustomFeedbacks. Fetching the run details of a single dialogue uses appId, chatId, and dataId. When intermediate values are needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect module status and complete results inside responseData.

A relevance score is not a category, entity, or permission filter. Similarity expresses the degree of textual relatedness; it does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records. Retrieval is not authorization. Forms collect values; business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation. A pending review means the process has not finished. It does not establish that an operating permission is missing.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: A warehouse checker processes wave picking list PL-0610-A on 10 June 2025. It covers order SO-8821, goods being six glass cups. Checking record RC-0610-17 states that scanning is complete. The draft sentence under review reads: "The checker has confirmed that all six glass cups are fully packed, the waybill has been printed, and the consumable issue is correct." The source text records only that scanning is complete. It does not record packing completion, waybill printing, or the consumable issue result. The subject, object, status, and valid scope of the source cover the scanning action alone. The draft sentence exceeds the source scope.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval similarity as a permission filter. Similarity only expresses textual relatedness and does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records. Input: one ordinary query. Compare whether the returned quoteList contains picking lists from other orders. Observe whether records outside the target chatId appear.

2. Treating a form submission as business execution. A form collects values and does not complete approval or a database write. Input: one checking form submission. Compare whether the order status in the WMS changes. Observe whether the status remains not checked after submission.

3. Treating a pending review as missing permission. A pending review only means the process is unfinished and does not establish that an operating permission is absent. Input: one pending checking request. Compare its authorization record with the operation log. Observe whether an explicit permission-denied record appears.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the chatId and dataId from the hypothetical sample and call the single-dialogue run-detail interface. Compare whether the returned moduleName and runningTime correspond to this checking action. Observe whether the module response for this conversation appears in responseData.

2. Input the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample and compare it field by field against the source wording "scanning is complete" for subject, object, and status. Observe whether the draft sentence exceeds the source scope. When it exceeds the scope, record it as pending verification and do not treat it as completed.

3. Input source=api and pageSize=20 and call the conversation list interface. Compare whether the returned list contains only API-source conversations. Observe whether page conversations are mixed in. If the return is empty, record an empty result and do not infer that no conversation exists.
