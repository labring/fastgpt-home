## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A store-supervision review can locate the conversation that interpreted particular visit material. Proposed evidence contains standards and rectification discussion without later site verification. Use chatId with store and source identity to inspect that exchange, without assuming retention of photographs, correction or the complete supervision process. Standard interpretation, findings and accepted correction need separate evidence; an ended answer does not establish a compliant store.

## Three common errors

Combining similar display questions from different stores requires conversation, input and returned-subject checks. Similar wording does not justify borrowing a result. If responseData lacks follow-up verification, it cannot prove correction. Request detail under the official contract and inspect the actual return instead of inferring completeness from a configured parameter or guessing site status from absent fields. If a visible log becomes satisfied supervision-retention requirements, compare actually retrievable standards, images and follow-up coverage with the project’s retention arrangement. Retain absent sources as gaps; a visible exchange does not replace the complete auditing design.

## How to verify the setup

Retain this store’s conversation, standard version, notice scope and follow-up gap, then inspect explanation changes after corresponding evidence arrives. Compare testing and production inputs, history and returns separately without assigning a formal identity to a test record. Closure, penalties, retention and compliance auditing require the complete business arrangement. Reviewing a log establishes conversation evidence rather than completed supervision or business operations.
