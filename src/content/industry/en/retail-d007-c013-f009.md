# Franchise store supervision: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A franchise-store review can separate visit findings, a rectification notice and later verification. Proposed text supplies display standards and visit passages while explicitly lacking a follow-up check. A proposed reading tool organizes those records without turning a sent notice into completed correction. Describe its subject as this store visit and retain source identities and applicability. Neither a photograph nor the tool’s name establishes compliance or an existing supervision-audit capability.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

If required rectification feedback never reaches the actual request, invocation may not occur. Inspect parameters and source passages, collect the feedback through interaction and retry without promising automatic questioning. A photograph from another store also cannot support this visit. Trace its caption, store identity and returned citation; complete-looking output may still compare the wrong objects and establishes neither a violation nor accepted correction. If a rectification notice recording sending becomes store receipt, inspect the sent passage separately from this store’s actual reception evidence. Sending does not establish receipt and still cannot replace later site verification.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual input, invocation and origins. After a corresponding follow-up arrives, repeat the question and distinguish finding, requested action and verification. Start with a small connected reading-tool set; parameter descriptions do not establish business execution. Termination ends this call without a subsequent AI summary. Penalties, rectification closure and supervision-record writes require separate permission and actual receipts; textual review does not establish those operations.
