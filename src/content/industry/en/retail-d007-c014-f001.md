# Convenience fresh-food orders: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Preserve order units, rejected rows and replacement requests without inferring fulfilled demand from summaries. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

An invented fresh-food packet orders cartons, lists accepted and rejected rows in receiving notes, and contains a store request for replacement without supplier confirmation. Inspect how the real service returns units and row states. Do not add ordered and received quantities or count rejected rows as accepted. No actual quantity, conversion ratio or quality finding is supplied, and rejection reasons remain absent. A replacement request establishes no second arrival.

## Three common errors

- If carton orders merge directly with item-level receipt fields, inspect units, packaging evidence and original rows. A shared product name supplies no conversion or demand-gap calculation.

- If rejected rows become accepted inventory, compare row state, actual receipt evidence and returned scope. Total list rows do not determine received quantities.

- If a replacement request becomes another completed delivery, inspect requester, supplier confirmation and actual handoff. A demand action creates no fulfillment event.

## How to verify the setup

Check store, order matter and material scope under the real interface contract, injecting authentication safely and inspecting parameters and applicable body separately. Retain raw response and report citations. If only summaries return, list missing units and rejection details without inventing source rows. Packaging instructions correct counting relationships alone; supplier replies add no receipt. Actual order changes, replacements and stock entries need business authority and records. Reading decides no food condition, purchase or inventory write.
