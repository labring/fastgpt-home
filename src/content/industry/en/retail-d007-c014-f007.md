# Convenience fresh-food orders: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A fresh-food log review can identify whether the conversation read ordering, spoilage or temperature material. Proposed evidence interprets order and spoilage wording without corresponding batch temperature sources. chatId locates the exchange and inputs; it does not establish measurement or complete device-data retention. Mentioning temperature establishes neither quality identification, a determined cause nor verified store practice. A temperature-record heading still needs correspondence to this batch’s measurement; reading the heading does not establish that device data was obtained.

## Three common errors

Using another shift’s conversation for this batch requires history, source-time and coverage checks. A recent record is not necessarily applicable. If only answer text is returned, request detail under the official contract and inspect responseData. Missing device evidence requires its actual source rather than inferred measurements from absent fields. Log creation time also does not automatically identify handling or measurement time.

## How to verify the setup

Retain shift, batch, conversation identity, reading scope and device gap. Reinspect explanations after corresponding evidence arrives, comparing testing and production inputs and history separately. Order changes, spoilage entries and actual handling require business permission and real records. Retention and compliance requirements need the complete arrangement; a conversation log is neither a food-quality conclusion nor completed auditing.
