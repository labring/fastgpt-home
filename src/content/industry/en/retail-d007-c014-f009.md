# Convenience fresh-food orders: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A fresh-food ordering review can separate order arrangements, spoilage statements, near-expiry handling and temperature records. Proposed text supplies ordering and spoilage passages without corresponding batch temperature evidence. Spoilage alone does not establish improper temperature handling. Limit a proposed tool to reading those statements and gaps, retaining store, batch and source status. An order describes demand rather than actual receipt or sales, and a product name does not establish storage conditions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Review step | Proposed material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordering scope | Demand arrangement rather than receipt result | Arrangement is not execution |
| Batch evidence | Values present without correspondence passage | Inspect invocation after obtaining linkage |
| Causal error | Expiry handling beside spoilage | Adjacent records do not establish cause |
| Temperature gap | This store’s batch source absent | Another store’s source does not replace it |
| Business handling | Separate order or spoilage procedure | Reading is not quality identification |

## Three common errors

A populated request without batch-correspondence evidence may still lack suitable required input. Inspect actual values and passages, obtaining the relationship before reading. Treating near-expiry handling as a spoilage cause also requires inspection of the recorded action and subject; adjacent records do not establish causation. Another store’s temperature passage cannot fill this store’s gap, and complete output does not establish complete evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain demand, handling and observation sources separately, inspecting actual invocation and citations before and after corresponding batch material is added. Start with a small connected tool set, collecting missing sources through interaction without promising automatic detection. Termination prevents a later AI summary of these results. Order adjustments, spoilage entries or food-handling decisions require actual business authority and evidence; textual review is neither quality identification nor execution evidence.
