# Letter-of-credit documents: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Letter-of-credit examination and presentation centers on documentary consistency and serves the bank's payment conditions. The working papers for one transaction include the letter of credit, the commercial invoice, the bill of lading, the insurance policy, and the draft. The data sources are the issuing bank's advice, the UCP600 articles, the forwarder's bill of lading, and the bank's presentation records. These materials update once per transaction. A documentary discrepancy can lead to refusal of payment, deductions, or delayed receipt of funds. Operating reports and exception reviews must map the field values in these documents to the presentation record, and the database and operations layer is responsible for storing that material and keeping it retrievable.

The documents differ from export tax-refund filings in one important respect: the review serves the bank's payment conditions, so the emphasis falls on consistency among documents rather than on tax matching. Three facts shape this page. Field values from the letter of credit, invoice, bill of lading, insurance policy, and draft must be compared against the presentation record for the same transaction. The outcome of a review depends on what the source documents actually state, so a missing field stays missing. A request to change a record's status is a separate matter from the underlying business fact, and the two must not be merged.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Database connection configuration is checked against the chosen deployment plan. A configuration value is not inferred from the variable name. Business source-data storage and the vector retrieval backend have separate responsibilities and are checked against the actual deployment. Connection counts and load are judged from observed connections, errors, and latency rather than from untested concurrency thresholds. Backup generation and restore are verified separately, and a checkable sample and its record are retained for each. After a change to database or service configuration, the same business sample is re-checked; a running process does not by itself show that the configuration took effect. A same-model retrieval association is limited to associations inside one model and does not extend to business-registry sites or other external systems. Semantic recall and relevance scores carry no permission or entity-isolation duty. Neither retrieval results nor values collected by a form constitute authorization to act.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. Under one letter of credit, the issuing bank's advice names Company A as beneficiary, describes the goods as a shipment of lamps, and sets the presentation period at twenty-one days after the shipment date. The commercial invoice states an invoice amount of USD 80,000, and the bill of lading states a shipment date of 1 March 2024. A draft sentence for an operating exception review reads: "This presentation has completed collection of proceeds and needs no follow-up." A handover request reads: "Please mark the above presentation record as proceeds received and close the exception." The source text states no information about receipt of proceeds, so both the draft sentence and the handover request lack support.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the draft sentence "this presentation has completed collection of proceeds" as an occurred fact. The source text states no information about receipt of proceeds, so the sentence is unsupported. Each field is compared against the letter of credit, invoice, and bill of lading text, and a missing field is recorded as a gap.

2. Treating the handover request "mark as proceeds received and close the exception" as already executed. The request is pending. A pending review does not show that authorization was granted, so the presence of an authorization record is checked; when none is provided, the item stays pending verification.

3. Treating a retrieval result or a form value as grounds for authorization. Neither retrieval results nor form fields constitute authorization to act. The authorization boundary is checked separately, and when it is not stated, the gap is recorded.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample text and the draft sentence. Compare whether the draft sentence states the party receiving proceeds, the amount, and the value date. None of these appear in the source text. The observed result is unsupported; it is not recorded as a negation or a conflict.

2. Input the handover request. Compare whether an authorization record is attached. The request carries no authorization. The observed result is pending verification; it is not recorded as approved or executed.

3. Input the same presentation sample before and after the configuration change. Compare whether the field values match. If they match, the observed result is that the post-change sample is re-checkable; if they differ, the difference is recorded and the evidence gap is retained.

This page addresses one scope only: letter-of-credit examination and presentation, operating reports and exception reviews, database and operations. At the point of operational handover, the authorization state of materials, actions, and requests is a separate question, and retrieval results or form values are not treated as authorization to act.
