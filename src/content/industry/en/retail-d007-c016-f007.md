## Constraints at the conversation logs step

An onboarding log review can establish whether a conversation only explained an SOP or actually received store assessment and opening material. Proposed evidence contains attendance and handbook discussion without assessment execution or site checks. chatId locates the exchange without establishing competence or a completed business checklist. Inspect actual inputs and returns rather than assuming assessment fields or the entire training history were retained.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Object | Proposed record | Evidence to inspect |

## Three common errors

An earlier handbook conversation mixed into current training requires identity, version and source checks. A recent answer does not establish applicability. If responseData contains no assessment record, it cannot prove a pass. Request detail under the official contract and inspect the return, obtaining business evidence separately. Testing identity differences must not turn a test answer into formal staff assessment.

## How to verify the setup

Reinspect explanation after business material arrives without declaring completion from record existence. Opening approval, recognition, profile writes and retention need the complete procedure.
