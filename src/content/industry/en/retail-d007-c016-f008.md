# Franchisee onboarding: workflow routing for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Franchisee training and opening coaching is a transfer of operating capability. It differs from franchise registration management, which is a legal disclosure activity. The supplied brief identifies the main data sources as the training system, the supervisor app, store photographs and the headquarters SOP library. The daily documents are training sign-in sheets, opening checklists, SOP manuals and assessment scores. The typical parties are the chain training department, regional supervisors and franchise store owners. In operating reporting and exception review these materials serve one purpose: to turn each store's monthly training and opening status into checkable records and to locate the step where a deviation occurred.

The training sign-in sheet records who attended which course and when. The opening checklist records the item-by-item status of store materials and actions. The SOP manual provides the action baseline. Assessment scores reflect how well a trainee has mastered the material. The fields, definitions and update rhythms of these four document types are not uniform. Routing must carry each type separately. One type cannot be assumed to substitute for another. The brief labels the update frequency as per store, per month. This page does not derive batch synchronization or real-time triggering from that label.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The form input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state and the workflow state is recorded. After the user fills the required information and submits, the node collects the form information and passes it to subsequent nodes. The user selection node also enters the interaction state. After the user makes a choice, the corresponding branch executes. The shared constraint of both nodes is that interaction waiting and state recording are the node's own behavior. The documents do not describe a global state switch, a submission count limit or an automatic judgment mechanism.

During routing, collected values must be passed explicitly to subsequent retrieval, judgment or interface nodes, and outputs must be compared against inputs item by item. Order writing, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process. The form and selection nodes do not perform those business actions themselves. Retrieval is used to fetch material and does not carry authorization or entity isolation. A similarity setting does not exclude unrelated business or unauthorized records.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a regional supervisor in a chain training department submits an opening coaching handover request. The attached material text reads: "Training sign-in sheet: 10 March 2025, Store A, cashier course, 4 attendees signed in; Opening checklist: 12 March 2025, Store A, 9 of 12 material items verified; Assessment score: Store A cashier course average 72 points." The proposed determination sentence is: "Store A opening coaching is complete and can be closed." The check is whether the subject, object, date range and completion status have material support.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as business completion. In the sample, the sign-in sheet and checklist record only verified item counts. The proposed determination sentence writes "9 items verified" as "opening coaching is complete," and the completion status field does not support that. The original text does not state a completion status, so the result is recorded as unsupported or pending verification, and the evidence gap is retained.

2. Treating a user selection branch as authorized execution. The user selection node executes the "yes" branch. The documents do not state that this branch can replace approval or a write. A pending review status does not demonstrate that operating authority is absent.

3. Using retrieval results in place of permission verification. Retrieval only fetches material. Similarity is not a permission or entity filter. Libraries on different vector models cannot be associated together. Check the identifiers first and exclude non-matching ones.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample original text and the proposed determination sentence, and compare whether "9 items verified" and "complete" share the same subject, the same period and the same field. Where the original text does not state a completion status, record the result as unsupported or pending verification and retain the evidence gap.

2. Input the selection branch request from the sample and compare whether the branch executed after the choice matches the branch described in the documents. The documents state that user selection executes the "yes" branch. Branch connections must be verified one by one; no claim is made that this was actually tested.

3. Input an explicit paper empty result [] and check how the downstream handoff design handles the empty value. Do not claim that a hypothetical rule retrieval necessarily returns []. Also check the retrieval library identifier and the vector model identifier, exclude non-matching ones, and record the actual observation. Do not run a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.
