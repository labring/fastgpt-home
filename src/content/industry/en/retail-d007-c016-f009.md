# Franchisee onboarding: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Franchisee onboarding, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A franchisee-onboarding review can ask what attendance, assessment and opening checks each establish. Proposed material supplies attendance and SOP passages without assessment details or opening verification. Complete attendance does not establish operational competence. Limit a reading tool to the supplied scope, retaining store, trainee and applicable SOP version. An assessment title does not establish that its result was read or that opening was approved.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Review step | Proposed material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence separation | Attendance, SOP and actual assessment | Attendance is not competence |
| Standard boundary | Headquarters SOP without store execution record | Not evidence of completed checks |
| Heading gap | Assessment title without content | Inspect request and obtain the passage |
| Store identity | Another store’s image introduced | Cannot fill this store’s opening gap |
| Formal recognition | Separate training or opening approval | Output does not replace business receipts |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Tool description` | Describe purpose, inputs and non-applicable situations. |
| `Tool parameters` | Inspect module and plugin parameters separately. |
| `Required parameters` | The model may not invoke a tool without suitable required inputs. |
| `Tool connections` | Available tools must connect to the calling module. |
| `Tool-call termination` | Termination ends the call without a later AI summary. |

## Three common errors

Using a general headquarters SOP as evidence of this store’s completed checks confuses standards with execution. Inspect the actual passage and returned citation. A required assessment supplied only as a heading may still be unsuitable input or result in no call. Inspect the request’s origin and obtain relevant content rather than treating a nonempty value as completed assessment. Another store’s photograph cannot establish this store’s opening state.

## How to verify the setup

Retain attendance, instructions, assessment and opening gaps separately, tracing actual calls and output sources before and after corresponding records arrive. Connect a small required tool set without assuming complete access to the training library. Termination ends this call without a later AI summary. Training recognition, opening approval and record writes require separate authority and receipts; reading text does not establish passed competence or readiness checks.
