## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A franchise-settlement log review can distinguish contract explanation from actual accounting handling. Proposed discussion concerns invoice, ledger and deduction-notice text without bank evidence or an operation receipt, and includes no amounts. chatId can locate the exchange and citations without turning invoice discussion into received funds or establishing complete retention of financial-system fields. ## Three common errors

Another party’s reconciliation conversation requires identity, contractual-subject and source checks before removal from this store’s review. Shared brands or malls do not justify combination. If responseData lacks deduction results, it cannot establish execution. Request detail under the official contract and inspect coverage instead of equating populated parameters with complete evidence. A recommendation is neither financial approval nor actual deduction.

## How to verify the setup

Retain party, period, source identities, actual return and bank gap. Inspect testing and production histories separately without using tests as formal receipts. Revisit explanation after corresponding business evidence arrives. Financial reconciliation, adjustments, permissions and retention require the complete arrangement. Conversation review establishes neither completed settlement, approved contractual handling nor finished compliance auditing.
