# Franchise fee settlement: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A franchise-settlement review can distinguish contractual payment plans, invoices, deposit ledgers and deduction notices without numerical amounts. Proposed inputs describe an issued invoice and a notice lacking corresponding bank evidence. Issuing an invoice does not establish receipt, and a notice does not establish executed deduction. Describe a reading tool as locating supplied entries and gaps, retaining parties and origins without assuming a connected financial system.

## Three common errors

A ledger from another store cannot support this contract’s conclusion. Inspect contractual identity, ledger subject and returned citations. Another failure populates required bank evidence with an unsourced document name. Trace submitted material and obtain the corresponding record; a value is not a supplied voucher. Deduction wording replaces neither execution nor a bank result, and textual differences do not determine whether a deduction is justified.

## How to verify the setup

Retain plans, notices, accounting entries and bank evidence separately, inspecting actual invocation and supporting passages before and after corresponding records arrive. Start with a small tool set. Missing suitable required inputs may prevent a call, without promising automatic supplementation. Termination prevents another AI summary. Accounting changes, deductions and reconciliation confirmation require separate authority and receipts; organizing text establishes neither completed settlement nor financial approval.
