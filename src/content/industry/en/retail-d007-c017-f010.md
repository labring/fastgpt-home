# Franchise fee settlement: citation tracing for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Franchise fee and deposit settlement deals with money movement and breach deductions. It is a different matter from franchise filing administration, which handles government registration. The working data comes from the finance system, franchise contracts, bank statements and store violation records. The everyday documents are franchise fee invoices, deposit ledgers, deduction notices and contract payment schedules. Citation tracing exists so that every conclusion in an operating report or exception review can be traced back to a specific material and version.

Updates arrive monthly and on each deduction. This page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time or transaction-triggered mechanisms from that rhythm. Three steps consume the most time: checking contract payment terms, confirming the reason for a deduction, and issuing invoices. Tracing demand concentrates in those same three places.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

The knowledge base search node selects one or more knowledge bases that share the same vector model and outputs a citation array whose length may be zero. When nothing is found, the output path still completes. The citation limit is measured in tokens. Minimum relevance is a filter threshold that applies only in semantic search or when result reranking is used. Hybrid search merges multiple retrieval paths through RRF. Reranking requires a rerank model and must be enabled in settings. Image retrieval requires corresponding image input and model capability. Retrieval and similarity scores do not carry permission or entity isolation, and they do not constitute authorization. Knowledge bases built on different vector models cannot be associated together; the identifiers must be checked and non-matching ones excluded.

Hypothetical exercise sample. The source material reads: "Party A shall pay the current month's franchise fee by the tenth day of each month, and overdue amounts accrue a daily penalty." "The deposit ledger shows a balance of RMB 50,000 as of June 30." "The June deduction notice states the reason as store violation record No. 2024-06-011." The draft sentence under review is: "This franchisee's June franchise fee has been settled in full, and the deposit balance is RMB 50,000." The source material supports the ledger balance and the deduction reason. It does not state the payment status of the June franchise fee, so the first half of the draft sentence is unsupported. The second half matches the ledger on the same subject, the same period and the same field, and can be cited to the June 30 deposit ledger balance.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval hit as authorization. In the sample, the deduction notice states only the reason. Retrieving that notice does not mean the deduction was approved or executed; a separate system implementation or manual approval record is required.

2. Treating a pending review as missing permission. When the draft sentence is pending review, this does not establish that the operator lacks permission, and it does not establish that approval was granted.

3. Treating a quotation validity period as a supply period. Date fields in the contract payment schedule and the supply period are different facts. A contradiction exists only when the same field carries explicitly mutually exclusive values.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample source material and the draft sentence. Compare whether "the June franchise fee has been settled in full" matches the source on the same subject, the same period and the same field. The source does not state payment status, so the result should be unsupported and must not be marked as settled.

2. Input the sample ledger text. Compare "the deposit balance is RMB 50,000" against the June 30 balance field in the ledger. Same subject, same period and same field match, so the result may cite the ledger as source while retaining the date range.

3. Input the paper empty array []. Compare whether the downstream node still executes. The citation output length may be zero, so the result should be that the path completes; do not assert that retrieval must return empty or must raise an error.
