## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A dealer-report log review can identify whether a conversation explained demand, inventory definitions or actual dispatch. Proposed evidence contains a replenishment request and collection plan without dispatch or bank execution sources. Use chatId with dealer and period to locate the exchange without assuming calculated turnover, financial reconciliation or complete ERP retention. An answer must trace what it actually read rather than treating plans as events.

## Three common errors

Another dealer’s exchange about the same item requires conversation, input-scope and returned-subject checks. A product name does not justify borrowing a conclusion. If responseData lacks calculation evidence or business records, final wording cannot prove verified metrics. Inspect detail under the official contract and its actual return, retaining missing definitions and receipts rather than assuming complete operating data. If a collection plan mentioned in the log becomes received funds, inspect the actual passage status and corresponding bank record. Discussing a plan supplies no receipt event; retain unresolved status without returned business evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Retain dealer, period, sources and output scope, revisiting the explanation after corresponding records arrive. Compare testing and production histories and inputs separately. Stock adjustments, replenishment ordering, financial confirmation and retention need the complete procedure. Conversation evidence helps inspect origins without establishing created orders, changed stock or a completed operating audit.
