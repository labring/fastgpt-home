# Certificates of origin: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A certificate-application log review can distinguish source explanation, submission and actual issuance. Proposed discussion reads application and production passages without issuance or dispatch receipts. chatId locates this exchange without establishing retention of the issuing party’s complete records. An answer saying material was checked does not establish issuance. Retrieval, submission and processing need separate evidence; log existence supplies no validity or clearance determination. Inspect the proposed statement that application text was read while issuance feedback remains absent; an informal phrase in the exchange cannot establish the stage reached by the issuing party.

## Three common errors

An earlier certificate’s conversation mixed into the current application requires identity, question and version checks. Shared product wording does not justify reusing its outcome. If responseData lacks issuance evidence or a business receipt, it cannot establish completion. Request detail under the official contract and inspect returns, retaining gaps. Dispatch cannot be inferred from advice, and a test answer is not formal application acceptance.

## How to verify the setup

Retain application identity, source scope, actual return and issuance or dispatch gaps. Inspect testing and production histories separately. Revisit explanation after corresponding evidence arrives without borrowing another batch’s record. Amendments, dispatch, retention and compliance auditing need the complete procedure. This log review establishes exchange evidence rather than issuance, delivery or external acceptance.
