## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A member-operations log review can use general rules and hypothetical activity text without personal data in public examples. Proposed discussion concerns plans, coupon rules and an authorization gap without sending or usage results. chatId helps inspect the exchange and inputs rather than establishing automatic retention of behavior, permission and every contact record. A recommendation to send establishes neither business authority nor execution.

## Three common errors

Another activity’s conversation requires identity, question and source-scope checks. Similar wording does not justify borrowing its result. If responseData lacks permission or operation evidence, it cannot establish completed authority checks. Inspect detail and actual returns under the official contract, obtaining business records separately. Record existence also differs from required retention, and a test identity is not formal personal authorization.

## How to verify the setup

Retain activity, conversation identity, public-source scope and absent results. Compare testing and production inputs, history and returns without real personal examples. Sending, tagging, coupon handling, retention and compliance auditing need the complete arrangement. Reviewing an exchange establishes neither effects, passed permission checks, reviewed data processing nor actual account operations.
