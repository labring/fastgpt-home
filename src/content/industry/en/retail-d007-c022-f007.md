# Omnichannel stock transfers: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A transfer log review can separate discussion of demand from warehouse execution. Proposed exchange interprets a request and stock snapshot without transport or receipt evidence. Use chatId with item and sending or receiving locations to locate the conversation, without assuming channel stock was synchronized into the log. Requests, balances, movements and receipts have distinct scopes; completed explanation is not physical transfer or arrival.

## Three common errors

Confused sending and receiving roles require original-input, citation and subject checks. Shared item names do not establish a common location. If responseData lacks external writing or receipt records, chat cannot prove changed stock. Request detail under the official contract and inspect real content, obtaining business evidence from the operator. Keep testing history separate from formal operations rather than combining identities into a chain.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, location roles, source time, conversation and return scope. Reinspect explanation after corresponding evidence arrives, checking testing and production histories independently. Run time is not goods-movement time. Transfer creation, stock writing, reconciliation and retention need the complete business procedure. Log review establishes neither synchronized channels, store receipt nor a completed audit.
