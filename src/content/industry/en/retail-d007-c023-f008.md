# Brand repair services: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Keep unreproduced symptoms, uninstalled parts and partial retests separate from full repair. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Invented repair material says a noise was not reproduced during this inspection, a parts issue awaits installation and a retest list covers buttons without the noise. Collect work-order identity, reported symptom and each recorded action, then choose installation evidence or retest coverage. Nonreproduction describes this inspection without proving the customer never experienced a fault. Issued parts are not installed parts, and button checks establish no resolved noise. No actual device parameter, fault finding or repair outcome is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Repair-review evidence routing | Invented action | Inspection scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nonreproduction note | No noise observed this time | No fault-free inference |
| Parts issue | Installation pending | Not completed replacement |
| Retest list | Buttons only | No noise/all-machine expansion |
| Evidence route | Work step or retest | Inspect actual handoff |
| Physical repair | Separate technician process | Independent execution record |

## Three common errors

- If temporary nonreproduction becomes a fault-free machine, compare inspection scope, customer-reported symptom and original finding. One unobserved event proves no permanent absence.

- If issued parts become completed replacement, inspect issue purpose, installation records and device correspondence. A material movement is no completed repair step.

- If passed button checks become resolved noise or an all-machine pass, compare tested items with uncovered symptoms and retain the limited scope.

## How to verify the setup

Keep material/work stages separate from retest items on both routes. Compare submitted wording, received states and adopted report sentences. Installation evidence updates installation alone; noise still needs its own retest. Customer descriptions supply no technical finding. List gaps item by item rather than smoothing stages into a complete table. Actual repairs, parts accounting, case closure and customer replies need authority and receipts. Orchestration organizes review material without repairing equipment or making a technician’s determination.
