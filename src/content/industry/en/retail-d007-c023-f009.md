# Brand repair services: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

A repair-services review can separate warranty material, a repair work order, inspection wording and exchange approval. Proposed input supplies warranty scope and a repair-submission record without inspection details or an exchange result. Repair submission does not establish a verified defect. Limit a reading tool to supplied passages and identities. Repair within warranty is not inherently a broken promise, and a warranty card does not establish approval of all handling.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Review step | Proposed material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record status | Warranty separate from repair submission | No automatic defect or broken-promise conclusion |
| Inflated state | Exchange application treated as approval | Trace actual request and passage |
| Heading gap | Inspection supplied without content | Inspect invocation and obtain the text |
| Item mismatch | Another item’s inspection fills this work order | Remove it and retain the gap |
| Handling result | Separate repair, exchange or write operation | Output is not completed business |

## Three common errors

Turning an exchange application into approval inflates business state. Inspect source status, actual parameters and citations, obtaining handling evidence. A required inspection supplied only as a heading may remain unsuitable input; inspect actual invocation and collect the passage. Another item’s inspection cannot fill this work order’s gap. Complete-looking output establishes neither a defect nor responsibility.

## How to verify the setup

Retain commitment scope, reported symptoms, inspection evidence and absent approval separately. Repeat after corresponding work-order material arrives, tracing actual origins. Connect a small required reading-tool set without promising automatic follow-up. Termination ends this call without a later AI summary. Work-order closure, exchange execution or after-sales writes need separate authority and real receipts. Textual review makes no legal determination and establishes neither repair, replacement nor payment completion.
