# Brand concession operations: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

A brand concession inside a shopping mall accepts the mall's unified cashiering and percentage-based settlement. Sales records originate at the mall POS, while the brand ERP, mall membership campaigns and the finance system each keep their own records. Operating reports and exception reviews bring four document types under one comparison basis: the joint-operation concession contract, the mall campaign letter, the sales receipt, and the monthly reconciliation statement. The supplied brief gives daily and monthly update frequency; this page does not infer batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time behavior or transaction-triggered mechanisms from that frequency.

No supplied material describes an automatic write relationship between the mall POS and the brand ERP. Generation of the monthly reconciliation statement is likewise a business action that requires a separate system implementation. What the database operations step can carry is storage, retrieval and presentation of these materials, together with preservation of the original records needed for manual comparison.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Materials arrive from several systems, so field naming and time basis may not agree. The database must be checked against the actual deployment for the business data store and the vector retrieval backend connection configuration; configuration values are not inferred from variable names. Retrieval results reflect similarity only. They do not constitute a permission decision and do not constitute entity isolation between concessions, brands or malls.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. A brand concession signs a joint-operation concession contract with a mall. The contract text states: "The settlement period is the calendar month; the concession rate is twenty percent of the month's tax-inclusive sales; reconciliation uses the mall POS aggregate as the basis." The mall campaign letter states: "This letter confirms only the campaign window and the venue location; fees and settlement method are set out in a separate agreement." The proposed copy sentence reads: "The concession amount for this concession for this month has been confirmed under the campaign letter and settlement is complete." The pending request reads: "Please confirm, on the basis of the available materials, that this month's concession settlement is complete."

The proposed sentence treats the campaign letter as the settlement basis. The source text confirms only the window and location, and places fees and settlement method in a separate agreement, so the sentence is not supported. The pending request lacks the monthly reconciliation statement and evidence of completed settlement; this is an evidence gap and cannot be judged as complete.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Hypothetical starting value for this exercise | Reason for the choice and comparison method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business data connection | Complete according to the actual deployment | Check connection availability and read/write scope; do not infer purpose from the name |
| Vector retrieval backend connection | Complete according to the actual deployment | Confirm the retrieval store and the vector model identifier agree; exclude those that do not |
| Log output level | Reuse the value already present in the deployment | Compare against logs from before the change and observe whether errors and durations shift |
| Backup and restore sample | One designated monthly reconciliation statement | Verify backup generation and actual restore separately, keeping a comparison record |
| Post-change review sample | The same monthly reconciliation statement | Compare the same field before and after the change; do not rely on process liveness alone |

The starting values in this table are settings for this hypothetical exercise, not official defaults. The retrieval store and vector model identifier must be checked first. Stores across different vector models cannot be associated together; check the identifier, exclude those that do not match, and do not run cross-model retrieval to compare missed recall. An empty-result handoff can be checked with a clearly stated paper input of an empty array against the downstream design; this does not assert that a fictional-rule retrieval necessarily returns empty.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the campaign letter as the settlement basis. The campaign letter confirms only the window and the venue, with fees and settlement method in a separate agreement. A draft sentence that writes "settlement is complete" on that basis has the campaign letter as input, the settlement-basis field as the comparison object, and the missing settlement evidence as the observable result.
2. Treating pending review as missing operating permission. Pending review states only the current status and does not demonstrate that permission is absent. The input is the status field, the comparison object is the permission record, and the observable result is that the permission conclusion has no basis.
3. Treating a retrieval result as authorization. A similar material returned by retrieval does not mean the current subject may use that material. The input is the retrieval result, the comparison object is the authorization record, and the observable result is that the authorization conclusion is not supported.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the contract text from the hypothetical sample together with the proposed sentence. Compare whether the proposed sentence cites the settlement period and concession rate stated in the contract. The observable result is that the sentence does not cite a settlement basis not stated in the campaign letter, and the judgment is not supported.
2. Input the pending request together with the list of available materials. Compare the evidence the request requires against the list. The observable result is the absence of the monthly reconciliation statement and the completed-settlement record, and the judgment is an evidence gap.
3. Input the records of the same monthly reconciliation statement before and after the configuration change. Compare the value of the same field. The observable result is that the field agrees and no new error logs appear, and the post-change review passes.

All judgments above are paper reasoning and were not actually executed. Signing, reaching cooperation and contract effectiveness are three distinct facts. A quotation validity period does not indicate a supply period. Clauses not stated remain unknown.
