# Brand-owned store operations: operating reviews with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Keep order entry, staffing plans and unresolved cancellations separate from completed sales. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed company-store review records sales orders with cancellations still to check, proposes extra evening staff and places a new display rack. The draft calls daily sales and service complete. Entry does not resolve cancellations, proposed staffing establishes no attendance and rack placement records no transaction or service outcome. Cite each action separately instead of combining preparation into operating completion. No sales, conversion or staffing effects are supplied. The exercise checks which source supports each proposed statement rather than reporting or calculating an actual store result.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Material check | Conditions in this test | Implementation evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction qualification | Cancellations pending | Retain in citation. |
| Staff state | Extra help proposed | Seek actual shift work. |
| Display attribution | Store and period | Exclude another store. |
| Action distinction | Placement is not trade | Trace every claim. |
| Report update | Supported matters only | Do not close all pending work. |

## Three common errors

First, order entry may lose cancellations pending; compare the full daily report with adopted references and retain transaction gaps. Second, a proposed rota may become completed service; seek actual shift and work records because arrangements establish no execution. Third, another store’s display result may apply here; check store, period and original identity rather than transferring through a campaign name. These concern transaction state, staff action and store attribution. More references cannot replace the independent operational evidence needed before the current report announces completed sales or service.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the daily report leaves open, where staffing stands and whose display is described, expanding actual citations. A rack record supports placement, not complete sales. Empty references require scope and transfer investigation without proving no trade occurred. Added cancellation or shift material needs current store and period checked, changing supported facts only. This exercise schedules nobody, posts no accounts and notifies no store. It calculates no real effect. Formal review still needs corresponding execution evidence rather than treating collected preparation material as closure of every business matter.
