# Dealer ordering events: operating reviews with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace additional interest, colour confirmation and credit-file receipt independently. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed dealer-order review asks to add an indigo design, leaves colour confirmation open in a contract note and acknowledges receipt of credit material. The draft says every additional order is settled. Interest is not confirmation, colour remains unresolved and received documents contain no credit decision. Preserve all three states in citations. A shared dealer cannot combine interest, contract and receipt into an approved transaction. No limit, price or order quantity is supplied. The exercise checks the stage supported by hypothetical records rather than an actual financing or procurement outcome.

## Three common errors

First, a wish to add goods may become an order; obtain current-event confirmation for the item instead of adopting an interest-form title. Second, pending colour may disappear and leave complete specification; compare the original note with the report for gaps. Third, credit-file receipt may become authority to execute; inspect the actual decision and its scope, since acknowledgement is no approval. These concern ordering action, specification coverage and credit stage. A common party or more files cannot substantiate the claim that all commitments have been finalized.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the interest proposes, what the contract lacks and what the credit reply acknowledges, expanding actual quotations. An interest form supports a wish without a real purchasing commitment. Empty references require scope and transfer checks, not a claim of no interest. Added confirmation needs current event and design matched, updating that entry while credit stays unresolved. This exercise approves no credit, places no order and handles no funds. It guarantees no supply. Formal results need their own applicable records beyond the proposed report and its associated sources.
