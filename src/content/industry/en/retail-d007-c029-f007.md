# After-sales repairs and reships: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Repair-review logs trace inspection advice, handling authority and replacement-shipment events. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose a repair summary recommends component replacement only, shipment material says spare parts are prepared, and history concerns another fault. Use chatId to locate the conversation and repair matter, then inspect the fault, recommendation and history actually delivered to the model. Check detail and responseData under the official contract while distinguishing returned scope. Runtime detail does not establish complete retention of inspection reports or logistics attachments.

## Three common errors

Advice reported as repaired needs handling and inspection evidence checked. Prepared parts reported as signed-for goods need logistics stages checked. Another fault outcome applied here needs fault identity and version checked. These separate recommendation from action, event stage and historical ownership.

## How to verify the setup

Retain redacted conversation identity, input, raw return and final answer. Examine identity and history separately for debug and production requests. Added logistics material changes shipment assessment alone; the repair finding still needs this inspection. Actual repair approval, replacement dispatch and work-order writes require business procedures and authority. Log review traces evidence without establishing completed repair or closed service. Keep this inspection beside its fault description and link replacement parcels separately to avoid merging repair and shipment findings.
