# After-sales repairs and reships: operating reviews with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace replacement-order creation, dispatch preparation and customer confirmation by stage. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed repair-mailing review creates a replacement order, leaves dispatch material under review and records a customer agreeing to a new cover plate. The draft calls the complaint solved. Creation establishes no shipment, unresolved documents record no actual dispatch and accepting a proposal means neither receipt nor installation. Cite part and request identity instead of joining preparation into restored use. Receipt for another order supplies no current outcome. No actual repair effect or cost is described; the exercise checks completion boundaries in the proposed report rather than a real closed case.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Material check | Conditions in this test | Implementation evidence |

| Document action | Order created | Do not equate with dispatch. |

| Customer position | Agrees to proposal | Do not replace outcome acceptance. |

## Three common errors

First, order creation may become dispatched goods; obtain current actual dispatch and handoff evidence. Second, agreement to replacement may become confirmed resolution; read the message object and seek subsequent customer feedback. Third, another request’s plate receipt may be used here; compare request, device and parcel instead of transferring through a shared part name. These concern shipment action, the other party’s position and attribution. Complete-looking process records cannot establish closed service without the distinct current execution and outcome evidence required for that claim.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what the order records, what dispatch still lacks and which proposal the customer accepts, expanding actual references. Agreement alone supports that position, not shipped or restored use. Empty references require scope and transfer checks without proving no conversation. Added current logistics or feedback needs subject correspondence, updating only covered stages; dispatch remains different from receipt. This exercise creates no replacement, makes no purchase and closes no case. It determines no fit. Actual resolution still requires applicable execution and customer-outcome records beyond the proposed documents and their quotations.
