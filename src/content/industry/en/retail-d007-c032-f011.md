# Retail checkout and loyalty: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Retail checkout and loyalty operations produce four recurring document types: promotional price tags, membership points rules, daily checkout reports, and exception refund forms. The data originates in POS systems, membership CRM, payment platforms, and price tag systems. The category centers on price and membership benefit accuracy; it does not manage short-shelf-life inventory, which separates it from convenience-store fresh-food ordering. Errors surface as price complaints, cash shortfalls or overages, and membership benefit disputes. For database operations, the task is to map the fields in these four document types to storage and retrieval configuration so that operating reports can be reconciled and exception reviews can be traced.

A daily or per-shift rhythm matters here because checkout reports and refund forms are produced within a shift, while membership points rules and promotional price tags change on their own cycle. The database operations step must keep business record storage separate from vector retrieval backends, and it must support reconciliation of the same business sample before and after any configuration change.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Database operations must distinguish business source-data storage from the vector retrieval backend. MONGODB_URI points to the main business database. PG_URL, OCEANBASE_URL, OPENGAUSS_URL, SEEKDB_URL, and MILVUS_ADDRESS are selected according to the actual deployment. REDIS_URL serves caching and stream recovery. Backup and recovery require separate verification of backup generation and actual restore, with checkable samples and records retained. After a change to the database or service configuration, the same business sample must be re-checked; process liveness alone is not sufficient evidence.

Retrieval association is constrained by the vector model identifier. Libraries using different vector models cannot be associated together. The identifier must be checked first, and non-matching libraries excluded. A relevance score does not carry permission or entity-isolation responsibility; retrieval itself does not constitute authorization. Forms collect values; business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation.

Hypothetical drill sample: On 10 March 2025, a store checkout supervisor submits a handover request. The source material reads: "The 9 March daily checkout report shows a membership points deduction amount of 1,200 yuan; exception refund form number R20250309007 records a refund amount of 300 yuan with status pending review; promotional price tag rule PR-0309 applies from 9 March to 10 March." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "R20250309007 has completed the refund and has been synchronized to membership CRM." The authorization boundary for this request: the checkout supervisor may view this store's daily report and refund form; refund execution and CRM synchronization are handled separately by financial audit and CRM operations.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a pending-review refund form as a completed refund. Input: R20250309007 with status "pending review." Comparison object: the conditions for "completed refund." Observable result: the status field has not changed to completed. Pending review does not constitute refund execution.

2. Treating a retrieval relevance score as a permission filter. Input: a retrieval request. Comparison object: the authorization boundary showing the checkout supervisor may view this store's records. Observable result: whether the retrieval result contains records outside this store's scope. A relevance score does not carry entity-isolation responsibility.

3. Associating libraries across different vector models. Input: two libraries with different vector model identifiers. Comparison object: whether the model identifiers match. Observable result: when identifiers do not match, exclude the non-matching library and record the actual observation; do not run a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recall.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the hypothetical sample sentence "R20250309007 has completed the refund and has been synchronized to membership CRM": input is the source material and the draft sentence. Comparison object is the refund form status field and the CRM synchronization record. Observable result: when the status remains pending review and no CRM synchronization record exists, the draft sentence is unsupported.

2. For promotional price tag rule PR-0309 in the hypothetical sample: input is the price tag rule text and the daily checkout report. Comparison object is the applicable period 9 March to 10 March against the report date. Observable result: when the report date falls within the applicable period, the rule can be reconciled; when it falls outside, record the evidence gap.

3. For the membership points deduction amount of 1,200 yuan in the hypothetical sample: input is the daily checkout report text and the membership points rules. Comparison object is the deduction amount field against the range the rules allow. Observable result: when the amount field matches the rules, it can be reconciled; when the rules do not state a range, retain it as pending verification.

An empty result can be checked against a clearly stated paper input of [] for downstream design; a fictional rule retrieval cannot be claimed to return [] with certainty. Retrieval checks describe observations and failure branches; they do not guarantee a first hit, full recall, or that an unrelated query returns empty. Optional paths listed in an overall document flow do not mean every request executes all of them. Image retrieval requires a corresponding image input and model capability; reranking requires a model and must be enabled per configuration. Unstated evidence, or evidence covering only a related stage, is called unsupported or pending verification. Pending review does not prove the absence of operating permission. Signing, reaching cooperation, and contract effectiveness are three distinct facts. A quotation validity period does not indicate a supply period.
