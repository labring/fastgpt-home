# Export tax rebate filings: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Export tax rebate filings, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Export-tax review separates prepared material, filing and evidence of actual handling. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A hypothetical export checklist says shipping documents were gathered, a customs summary awaits comparison, and a historical handling notice is attached. Collect period and document identity, let a person choose current-file completion or difference review, then pass the selected original text downstream. A complete-looking checklist describes collected scope without proving this period was filed. No actual tax amount or approval finding is supplied in this exercise.

## Three common errors

Collection reported as filing needs formal submission evidence. A historical notice applied to this period needs period and entity identity. A pending customs comparison reported as no difference needs the qualifying source wording. These examine process stage, time ownership and uncertainty, independently of node execution counts.

## How to verify the setup

Check each choice route and the versions actually received downstream. An added document closes its own evidence gap while current handling remains unresolved without its notice. Formal tax decisions require the competent process and evidence. External filing and spreadsheet writes need separate interfaces, permission and execution acknowledgments. Recorded interaction state explains the handoff without supplying a tax outcome. List shipping-document locations separately from current-period submission evidence so collection does not obscure a filing gap.
