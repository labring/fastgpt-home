# Export tax rebate filings: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Export tax rebate filing takes place after goods have left the country, and its work is the matching of tax vouchers. Data arrives from the electronic tax bureau, the export rebate filing system, customs electronic messages and the accounting ledger. The documents handled day to day are the export tax exemption and rebate filing form, the export customs declaration, the purchase invoice and the foreign exchange receipt voucher. Matching invoices against customs declarations, generating filing data and handling doubt feedback are the time-consuming steps. Errors lead to delayed rebates, ineligibility for rebate and tax risk. For operating reports and exception review, this means the original document status must remain checkable. Database and operations work centres on reading, retaining and comparing those documents.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Connection settings are checked against the actual deployment plan, and a configuration value is not assigned a purpose from its variable name. Business source data storage and the vector retrieval backend have separate responsibilities, and the corresponding services are checked against the actual deployment. Connection counts and load are judged from errors and latency observed under the operator's own load, and untested thresholds are not copied. Backup creation and actual restore are verified separately, with checkable samples and records retained. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample is rechecked; a running process alone is not sufficient.

Hypothetical drill sample: a finance staff member at a foreign trade enterprise, in March 2025, assembles a batch of customs declarations for exports in January 2025 together with the corresponding purchase invoices. The source text for checking consists of three sentences. First: "Export customs declaration number XXXX, export date 10 January 2025, goods have been exported." Second: "The corresponding purchase invoice has been issued, invoice date 5 January 2025." Third: "The foreign exchange receipt voucher shows the payment for this transaction arrived on 20 February 2025." The draft sentence to be judged is: "This batch of export business has completed rebate filing and the rebate can be confirmed." That draft sentence concerns filing status and rebate confirmation. The material shows only export, invoicing and receipt of payment. It does not state that a filing was submitted, that review passed, or that funds were returned. The sentence is unsupported, and the evidence gap is retained.

## Three common errors

1. Treating "goods have been exported, the invoice has been issued, payment has arrived" in the material as "rebate filing is complete and the rebate can be confirmed." The input is the three source sentences and the draft sentence. The comparison is whether filing status and rebate confirmation have supporting evidence. The observable result is that the material does not state a filing submission or a passed review, so the draft sentence is unsupported.

2. Inferring the purpose of a database connection value from its variable name. The input is the connection address and pool value filled in the actual deployment. The comparison is whether that address points to where customs declarations, invoices and receipt vouchers are actually stored. The observable result is that a similar name does not show the target is correct; an actual read of the sample is needed.

3. Treating the existence of a backup file as restore capability. The input is the backup creation record and the restore operation. The comparison is whether the restored sample data matches the original documents. The observable result is that creating a backup alone does not constitute restore verification; an actual restore and sample comparison are required.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the three source sentences and the draft sentence in the hypothetical sample, the input is the source text and the draft sentence. The comparison is whether the filing status and rebate confirmation stated in the draft sentence have material support. The observable result is that the material shows only export, invoicing and receipt of payment, with no filing submission or passed review stated, so the judgement is unsupported and the gap is retained.

2. For the database connection configuration, the input is the connection address and pool value in the actual deployment. The comparison is whether that configuration matches the connection waits and errors observed under the operator's own load. The observable result is that the check condition is met only when connection waits and errors stay within an acceptable range and the sample read is consistent.

3. For backup and restore, the input is the backup creation record and the restore operation. The comparison is whether the restored sample data matches the original documents. The observable result is that the condition is met only when the restored sample is checkable and the records are complete; a backup file existing alone does not meet it.
