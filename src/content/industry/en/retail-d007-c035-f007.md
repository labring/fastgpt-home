# Trade returns and claims: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Trade returns and claims, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Return-claim review checks speakers, batches and visible attachment contents. Customer assertions and document titles cannot establish supplier confirmation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

In a proposed exchange, the customer says quality material was sent and a supplier reply is pending. Earlier history concerns a closed batch, but the assistant calls this claim settled. Link the conversation identifier to the current business event and inspect speakers in actual detail returns. Customer assertions cannot become supplier agreement, and an attachment title cannot establish read quality findings. Preserve the pending reply rather than borrow outcomes from historical keywords.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Return-review log | Proposed message clue | Corresponding evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer wording | Sent; reply pending | Not supplier agreement |
| Historical batch | Earlier case closed | Not current outcome |
| Attachment visibility | Possibly title only | Contents and access |
| Added response | Current explicit matters only | Receipt is not agreement |
| Real result | Logistics and finance separate | Conversation is not closure |

## Three common errors

Assertion promoted to agreement needs message roles and the actual supplier response. A closed earlier batch applied here requires historical subjects and origins. A title promoted to observed damage needs contents and access evidence. These inspect position, identity and visibility independently of readable history.

## How to verify the setup

Retain query scope, actual returns and the full message chain. Add this supplier’s reply and inspect whether the explanation stays within it. Acknowledged receipt still does not establish accepted compensation. Return receiving, handling decisions and accounting adjustments need separate business evidence. Logs trace answer formation without deciding for suppliers or equating ended conversations with ended logistics or disputes.
