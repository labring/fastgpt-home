# Peak-sale fulfillment: operating reviews with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Peak-sale fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace wave labels, covered zones and unmatched receipts without claiming warehouse-wide assurance. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed peak-sale review defines Blue Line as an internal warehouse group, limits an assurance letter to the east zone and leaves receipts unmatched in an anomaly report. The draft gives those orders complete assurance and closes exceptions. A grouping label is no carrier route, one zone covers no entire warehouse and unmatched documents identify no current-order result. Preserve label definitions and boundaries in citations. No timing, order counts or assurance effects are supplied. This exercise checks the report’s use of sources rather than actual logistics performance or a real operational promise.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Label definition | Blue Line warehouse group | Do not make carrier route. |
| Coverage zone | East only | Inspect order location. |
| Receipt correspondence | Unmatched | Do not close anomaly. |

## Three common errors

First, Blue Line may become a transport route; trace the scheduling definition and actual logistics evidence instead of inventing an external event from an internal label. Second, east-zone coverage may become warehouse-wide; inspect letter scope and order location rather than expanding through a shared event. Third, unmatched receipts may become closed anomalies; seek order correspondence and actual handling outcomes. These concern name meaning, geographical coverage and execution evidence. More documents or a finished report cannot establish that a proposed assurance was delivered for the orders under review.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what Blue Line means, which zone the letter covers and what each receipt matches, expanding actual quotations. A schedule supports grouping without dispatch. Empty references establish no absent assurance arrangement. Added matched receipts need order and zone checked, updating their stage without clearing other exceptions. This exercise schedules nothing, contacts no carrier and makes no external promise. It calculates no actual performance. Assurance and fulfilment need their own applicable records instead of document titles substituted for the real event and outcome evidence that the current report still lacks.
