# Tmall flagship operations: operating reviews with citation tracing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Tmall flagship operations, this page examines FastGPT citation tracing. Trace authorized display scope, publication drafts and promise examples separately in flagship reviews. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

A proposed flagship review limits authorization to logo display, leaves a product page as an unpublished draft and marks a shipping script as an example with order conditions to verify. The report calls the page effective and every promise fulfilled. Display authorization covers no entire description, drafts supply no publication and examples contain no current shipment. Preserve source roles instead of deriving whole-page approval from branding or real fulfilment from familiar wording. No actual platform criterion, price or performance is supplied by this hypothetical material or its proposed report.

## Three common errors

First, display permission may become reviewed advertising content; inspect authorized subjects and actual product support. Second, a visible draft may become a live page; obtain real publication state instead of inferring an external page from file presence. Third, a script example may prove current dispatch; inspect order conditions and actual handoff because templates record no event. These concern scope, publication stage and fulfilment evidence. Matching titles or repeated completed-looking words cannot combine into operational completion when sources concern different roles and still lack the corresponding real outcomes.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what authorization covers, where the draft stands and what the example lacks, expanding actual citations. Authorization alone supports its stated scope, with publication and dispatch unresolved. Empty references establish no absence of permission; inspect scope and transfer. Added current-order records need product and period matched, changing corresponding claims rather than the entire store. This exercise edits no page, ships nothing and closes no service case. It decides no platform admission. Actual activation and fulfilment need their own sources rather than a report output treated as execution evidence.
