# Tmall flagship operations: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Operating reports and exception reviews for Tmall flagship store daily operations draw on four document families: product listing pages, brand authorization letters, delivery commitment records, and after-sales work orders. Compared with JD POP stores, Tmall flagship stores place more weight on brand authorization, flagship store identity, and Tmall points compensation rules, and those differences decide which fields an operating report reconciles and which objects an exception review checks.

Brand authorization letters are qualification material; delivery commitment records are fulfillment material. Their fields differ and one cannot substitute for the other in a report. Daily and per-order update timing appears in the source material only as a time label on the data origin, and this page does not derive batch synchronization, full synchronization, real-time processing, or transaction-triggered mechanisms from it.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Database operations at this stage covers connections, storage responsibility, backup and recovery, and post-change re-checks. It does not perform authorization decisions. Material that a retrieval or a form has collected carries an authorization state that must be checked separately. A value collected by a form is not a business authorization, and a retrieval hit is not a right to execute. A similarity score returned by vector retrieval is a relevance measure. It does not act as a category, entity, or permission filter, and it cannot be used to exclude unrelated merchants or unauthorized records. Knowledge base reference limits use the unit stated in the documentation. Same-model retrieval association, empty-reference output, and context limits keep their documented conditions. Libraries that use different vector models cannot be jointly associated; the identifier is checked first, non-matching libraries are excluded, and the actual observation is recorded.

Hypothetical drill sample: a brand e-commerce department runs a monthly operating report and exception review for a Tmall flagship store covering 1 March 2026 through 31 March 2026, using product listing pages, brand authorization letters, delivery commitment records, and after-sales work orders. The material supplied for checking reads: "The brand authorization letter states an authorization period from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026, and the authorized object is Tmall flagship store entity A." "The delivery commitment record states that the order was promised to ship within 48 hours, and the actual ship time was later than the promised time." The draft sentence to be judged reads: "All delayed shipments for this store in March have completed compensation." The draft sentence lacks the subject, time, and evidence of completed compensation, so it is unsupported and the evidence gap remains.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a brand authorization letter that is inside its validity period as proof that compensation has been completed. The input is the authorization letter text above; the comparison object is the compensation subject, time, and evidence required for completed compensation; the observable result is that the authorization period field cannot yield a compensation state, and the draft sentence lacks evidence.
2. Writing "later than the promised time" from a delivery commitment record as completed compensation. The input is the delivery commitment record text above; the comparison object is evidence of completed compensation; the observable result is that the record states only a ship time and carries no compensation action.
3. Using a vector similarity score to exclude unauthorized records. The input is the relevance score returned by retrieval; the comparison object is the authorization state field; the observable result is that the score contains no category, entity, or permission information, so the exclusion has no basis.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the brand authorization letter text above together with the draft sentence. Compare the authorization period, the authorized object, and the fields required for completed compensation. The draft sentence meets the condition only when a compensation subject, a time, and evidence all appear. At present only the authorization period is visible, so the judgement is unsupported.
2. Input the delivery commitment record text above. Compare the promised time, the actual ship time, and compensation evidence. A completed-compensation judgement is met only when a completed-compensation record also appears. At present only a time difference is visible, so the judgement is pending verification.
3. Input the relevance score returned by retrieval and the authorization state field. Compare whether the two belong to the same judgement dimension. The authorization state field can serve as a basis for exclusion only when it is explicitly stated. The score alone does not meet the condition, so the actual observation is recorded and the gap is kept.
