# Multi-store management: citation tracing for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Multi-store matrix management for platform commerce centers on cross-store authorization and price order. The working materials differ in origin and refresh rhythm. A store authorization list records which stores hold brand authorization. A price control table records agreed prices by channel. A diversion complaint form records dealer or consumer reports of abnormal low pricing or unauthorized selling. A monthly sales report aggregates operating data by store. Authorization status and price agreements require item-by-item checking per store.

Citation tracing for operating reports and exception reviews requires every conclusion to point back to a specific original passage. The authorization list, price table, complaint form and sales report form a verification chain. When the original text for any link is missing, the related conclusion can only be recorded as pending verification.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

Retrieval output is a citation array whose length may be zero. An empty array means the search returned no content and the workflow still proceeds; it does not show that a downstream step received a source or that business activity ended. Citation content must be handed to the answering step for item-by-item comparison against the original text. Fluent wording does not make a source reliable.

Knowledge bases using different vector models cannot be associated together. Before association, check the vector model identifier of each selected knowledge base, exclude those that do not match, and record what was observed. The minimum relevance score is a filtering threshold. It does not perform permission or entity isolation and cannot be used to exclude unauthorized store records.

Hypothetical drill sample: A brand e-commerce lead assembles materials. The original text reads "Store A authorization is valid until December 31, 2026, covering online flagship store sales," "Store B authorization expired on June 30, 2025, with no renewal," and "The price control table states a suggested retail price of 199 for a certain model at Store A, valid January 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026." The draft sentence is "Store B currently holds valid authorization and may follow Store A's pricing system." This draft conflicts with the original record that Store B's authorization expired without renewal and is therefore unsupported.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval return as authorization confirmation. A retrieval hit on the authorization list only shows the material was recalled. Whether Store A is within its validity period and whether Store B renewed still require comparing original dates and status fields. The input is the retrieval citation array, the comparison is against the authorization list's original validity period, and the observed result is whether the draft sentence matches the original or remains unsupported.

2. Using minimum relevance to exclude unauthorized records. Minimum relevance is a relevance filtering threshold. The input is a relevance value, the comparison is against the set threshold, and the observed result is that low-scoring items are filtered. This process does not judge store authorization status.

3. Treating an empty citation array as a system error. The input is the retrieval output, the comparison is against the array length, and the observed result is that a zero length still allows the workflow to proceed. It cannot be inferred that downstream received a source or that business activity ended.

## How to verify the setup

1. Take the original text from the hypothetical sample stating Store B's authorization expired without renewal. Input the draft sentence "Store B currently holds valid authorization." Compare the draft against the original authorization status field. The observed result is that the two do not match, and the draft is recorded as unsupported.

2. Take the price control table original validity period of January 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026. Input the draft sentence "may follow Store A's pricing system." Compare the period stated in the draft against the price table validity period. The observed result is that when the period is not stated or exceeds the validity period, it is recorded as pending verification.

3. Use the paper input [] to check the downstream handoff design. Compare whether the workflow continues when the array length is zero. The observed result is that the chain proceeds and produces no source conclusion; this check does not claim that a fictional rule search necessarily returns an empty array.
