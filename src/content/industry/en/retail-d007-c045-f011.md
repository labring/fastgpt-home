# Seller ratings management: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Platform shop rating and experience score maintenance follows consumer feedback that arrives after fulfilment and after-sales service. Source material includes platform experience scores, marketplace service indicators, after-sales review records and customer service system exports. The working documents are negative review handling records, experience score detail sheets, service diagnostic reports and remediation ledgers. These materials form on a daily or weekly cycle and feed operating reports and exception reviews.

At the database operations step, those documents become queryable records for recheck and traceability. Completeness, field definitions and time range determine whether a review conclusion can be checked. Where material is missing, the related conclusion stays pending verification.

Two characteristics shape this step. First, the business records and the vector retrieval backend have different responsibilities and must be confirmed separately against the actual deployment. Second, a similarity score returned from retrieval is not a business entity filter and is not a permission filter; it does not separate unrelated shops or unauthorized records. Retrieval is not authorization. A form collects values; business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization need their own downstream implementation.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Database connections, cache and vector retrieval backends are checked against the deployment plan, and configuration values are not inferred from variable names. Connection counts, errors and latency are observed under the deployment's own load; untested concurrency thresholds are not copied. Backup generation and actual restore are verified separately, with checkable samples and records retained. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample is checked; process liveness alone is not a pass condition.

A pending review entry in a remediation ledger is a pending-verification state. It does not indicate approval and does not indicate execution. An unsigned reviewer field is not a completed termination or a completed change. The experience score detail sheet updates daily or weekly; this page does not infer batch synchronization, real-time or transaction-triggered mechanisms.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a shop operator records three verbatim entries in a remediation ledger. Entry one: "On 10 March 2025, order A received a negative review; customer service contacted the buyer on 11 March to request supporting evidence; the buyer has not replied." Entry two: "On 12 March 2025, the service diagnostic report showed the experience score down 0.2 points week over week, attributed to logistics fulfilment." Entry three: "On 13 March 2025, the remediation ledger registered one pending-review entry requesting that the negative review attribution be changed from logistics to product description; the reviewer has not signed." Draft sentence to be judged: "The shop has completed the negative review attribution remediation and the experience score has recovered." The subject is the shop, the object is negative review attribution and experience score, the state is completed, and the effective scope covers the three entries above.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval similarity score as a business entity or permission filter. Input is a negative review handling record query; the comparison object is the business entity of returned records; the observable result is that score level does not change record ownership. The error is concluding that unrelated records have been excluded.

2. Treating a pending-review record as completed remediation. Input is entry three above; the comparison object is the reviewer signature field; the observable result is that the signature field is empty. The error is the draft sentence claiming remediation is complete.

3. Treating process liveness as a passed configuration change. Input is service state after a database configuration change; the comparison object is the query result for the same business sample; the observable result is that the process runs while sample fields do not present as expected. The error is judging the change successful from process liveness alone.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the draft sentence "The shop has completed the negative review attribution remediation and the experience score has recovered," input the three entries above and compare against the state fields and effective scope in the source text. Entry one shows the buyer has not replied, entry two shows the experience score declined, and entry three shows the reviewer has not signed. The observable result is that the stated "completed" state does not match the source states, and the sentence is not supported.

2. For the authorization state of the pending-review record, input entry three and a handover request "please execute this attribution change," and compare the reviewer signature field against the execution action. The observable result is that the signature field is empty; the request remains pending verification and does not constitute authorized execution.

3. For retrieval results, input a negative review handling record query and compare returned records against the querying entity. The observable result is that similarity scores do not distinguish business entities, and record ownership requires manual confirmation. An empty result can be checked against downstream design with the paper input `[]`; this does not assert that a fictional rule query always returns empty.

After a configuration change, check the same business sample; process liveness is not a pass condition. Verify backup generation and actual restore separately, retaining checkable samples and records. Where material is missing, related conclusions remain pending verification.
