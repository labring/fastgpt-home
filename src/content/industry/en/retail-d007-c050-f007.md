# Complaints and official replies: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Complaints and official replies are directed at platforms or regulatory bodies, so the material set is formal: complaint tickets, regulatory inquiry letters, order evidence, and rectification reports. Compared with after-sales repair and reshipment work, this category depends on formal evidence and rectification wording, and errors can lead to administrative investigation, platform penalties, or brand exposure. Data arrives irregularly, case by case.

Conversation logs serve to reconstruct the transaction communication process. Customer service chat, the order system, the platform complaint center, and the 12315 platform each leave records, and operating reports and exception reviews need those records grouped under the same case by conversation identity. The existence of a log only shows that the system wrote a record; reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements are handled by a complete business solution separately. A log record does not mean a compliance audit is complete.

Three facts shape this task. First, complaint and regulatory material is formal evidence, so a drafted sentence must trace back to specific source wording rather than to a plausible sequence of events. Second, the same case can appear across several entry points, so conversation identity must be checked before records are combined. Third, the interface returns only the fields it returns; business data that is absent from the response cannot be assumed to be retained elsewhere.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

The conversation identifier chatId is used to check the target conversation against business records, and conversations from different entry points are not mixed. When the conversation interface run detail is needed, detail is set according to the official contract; in non-stream mode the result is held in responseData, and in stream mode it is distinguished through event. Which fields the log and interface actually return must be checked against the returned result, and business data that is not returned cannot be presumed to be fully retained.

Identity fields in test mode may differ from formal calls, so input, history, and returned content are checked separately. Retrieval and relevance do not carry permission or entity isolation duties, and the same-vector-model requirement for a retrieval library applies to that library itself and does not extend to business registration websites or other external systems. Four kinds of things are recorded separately: behavior a node already has, fields that are actually configurable, manual checking actions, and business actions that require a separate system implementation.

Hypothetical drill sample

Source text one: A customer service supervisor at an e-commerce store received a platform complaint center ticket on 10 March 2025. The ticket content is "the buyer reports that a vacuum flask ordered on 2 March 2025 arrived with a cracked lid, and requests a refund and an explanation of the handling plan."

Source text two: The customer service chat record shows that on 3 March 2025 the buyer sent a photo of the cracked lid, and customer service replied "recorded, will forward to after-sales for verification."

Source text three: The order system records the order status as "signed for" and the refund application status as "pending review."

Draft sentence to be judged: "The store completed the refund on 10 March 2025 and submitted the rectification report."

Evidence needed for the judgment: neither a refund completion record nor a rectification report submission receipt appears in the material. Pending review does not equal approval, so the draft sentence's "completed the refund" and "submitted the rectification report" are not supported and should be recorded as pending verification.

## Three common errors

1. Treating pending review as a completed refund. Source text three shows the refund application status as "pending review," while the draft sentence says "completed the refund." The error is equating pending review with approval. The check compares the refund status field in the order system against the wording of the draft sentence; if the status remains pending review, the draft sentence does not hold.

2. Treating the existence of a log as a completed compliance audit. Source text two contains only the customer service reply "recorded, will forward to after-sales for verification," with no rectification report submission receipt. The error is equating a system-written record with a completed business action. The check compares whether a rectification report submission receipt appears in the material; if it does not appear, the item is recorded as an evidence gap.

3. Mixing conversations from different entry points. The sample conversation was created through the API, so checking against the page conversation list may fail to retrieve it. The error is not checking the source field. The check compares the chatId returned in the conversation list against the sample chatId; if they do not match, the wrong source has been used.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input source texts one through three and the draft sentence, compare "completed the refund" in the draft sentence against the refund application status "pending review" in source text three, and observe whether the status field and the draft sentence wording disagree. If they disagree, the draft sentence is judged unsupported and recorded as pending verification.

2. Input the sample chatId, call the conversation list interface with source set to api, compare the chatId in the returned list against the sample chatId, and observe whether the two match. If they match, the target conversation is locatable under the API source; if they do not match, record a source mismatch.

3. Input the sample conversation, issue requests with detail=true and detail=false respectively, compare whether the responseData field appears in the two returns, and observe whether that field appears when detail=true. If it appears, the run detail is returned according to the contract; if it does not appear, record a missing field and keep that gap.

All three verification items are paper-based check designs and were not actually executed. They do not indicate that any test, deployment, approval, or refund has taken place.
