# Douyin storefront operations: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Douyin storefront operations center on product cards and store experience score, and the operating rhythm differs from live-room operations, which depend on the pacing of a single broadcast. For this category, daily data arrives on a daily and per-order basis. The main data sources are the Douyin store workbench, the Douyin e-commerce learning center, Selected Alliance data, and logistics interfaces. Operating reports and exception reviews work with product qualification pages, experience score details, shipping violation appeals, and store announcements. In the database and operations layer, reports must be reconcilable by order and by day, exception reviews must point to specific materials, and the status and authorization boundary of those materials cannot be inferred automatically from a retrieval result or from a filled form.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Database and operations work must separate business source-data storage from the vector retrieval backend, and connection settings must be checked against the actual deployment plan rather than guessed from a variable name. Connection pooling, caching, and the vector store each carry different responsibilities, and a retrieval result cannot stand in for a permission decision. Once data and vector duties are separated, backup and recovery must be verified separately: backup generation and actual restoration are two different checks, and each needs a sample and a record that can be compared. After changing a database or service setting, the same business sample should be rechecked; a running process alone does not show that results are correct.

Hypothetical drill sample: a storefront operations team records in its review materials dated 10 June 2025 that the product qualification page shows a test report for a summer sun-protection jacket valid from 1 May 2025 to 30 April 2026; the experience score details show that on 9 June this product lost 0.5 points for a shipping timeout; the shipping violation appeal record shows the status "pending review"; and the store announcement board published a notice on 10 June about delayed shipping. The draft sentence to be judged is: "The product's test report has expired, the experience score deduction appeal has succeeded, and the announcement has confirmed responsibility for the delayed shipping." The subject, object, status, and validity range in this draft sentence must be checked item by item against the materials above.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval result as authorization to act. The input is the "pending review" appeal record in the sample above; the comparison object is the draft sentence "the appeal has succeeded"; the observable result is that the record status does not match the draft sentence. A pending review does not show that the appeal succeeded, and it does not show that the operator lacks permission.
2. Treating a filled form as business verification. The input is the validity field on the product qualification page; the comparison object is the draft sentence "has expired"; the observable result is that the validity period covers 30 April 2026, which conflicts with "has expired." Forms collect values, and business verification needs a downstream implementation.
3. Treating a published announcement as confirmation of responsibility. The input is the 10 June store announcement; the comparison object is the draft sentence "has confirmed responsibility for the delayed shipping"; the observable result is that the announcement only describes the delay and states no attribution of responsibility. Publication and responsibility confirmation are separate facts.

## How to verify the setup

1. Using the test report validity range "1 May 2025 to 30 April 2026" from the sample, take the draft sentence "the product's test report has expired" as input, compare the end date of validity with the date in the draft sentence, and observe that the validity period covers the draft sentence date, so the draft sentence is not supported.
2. Using the shipping violation appeal record status "pending review" from the sample, take the draft sentence "the experience score deduction appeal has succeeded" as input, compare the record status with the draft sentence, and observe that the status is pending review, so the appeal success claim is not supported and the evidence gap must remain recorded.
3. Using the 10 June store announcement from the sample, take the draft sentence "the announcement has confirmed responsibility for the delayed shipping" as input, compare the announcement content with the draft sentence, and observe that the announcement states no responsibility confirmation, so this judgment remains unverified. Where an empty result must be carried downstream, use the explicit paper input `[]` to check the downstream design; do not claim that a fictional rule retrieval necessarily returns `[]`.
