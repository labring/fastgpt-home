# Producer export tax processes: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Producer export tax refunds combine the offset of domestic sales tax with the export refund calculation. This differs from the calculation path used by trading companies, and the difference shapes how conversation logs are checked during operating report review and exception analysis. Within one period, the domestic sales ledger, export customs declarations, the producer export tax refund declaration detail table and export contracts may come from different systems. Values appearing in a conversation log must correspond to a specific source document. The primary data sources are the electronic tax bureau, ERP sales, customs electronic information and financial accounts. The documents handled routinely are the producer export tax refund declaration detail table, export contracts, the sales detail ledger and export customs declarations. The most time-consuming steps are collecting self-produced export records, calculating the offset and refund, and handling free-on-board price differences.

For this page, the operating report review and exception analysis task depends on being able to trace a log entry back to a named document, a period, and a field. A log entry records what was said in a conversation. It does not by itself perform reconciliation, retention or compliance work. Those steps belong to a separate business process.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

In this task, conversation logs support operating report checking and exception replay. Log identity relies on chatId to match a target conversation against a business record, and conversations from different entry points are not combined into one reconciliation basis. When run details for a conversation interface are needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData. When comparing log content against fields actually returned by the interface, business data that was not returned cannot be treated as fully retained. Identity fields in test mode may differ, so input, history and returned content are each checked separately. Reconciliation, retention and compliance requirements are carried out through a complete business plan. The presence of a log does not mean a compliance audit has been completed.

Hypothetical drill sample: A tax accountant at an export producer holds a producer export tax refund declaration detail table during an operating report review in July 2025. The table header records a period of June 2025, with total export sales at free-on-board price of RMB 1,200,000. One entry corresponds to export contract HT-2025-0612 and customs declaration BG-2025-0618, and the sales detail ledger records this entry at RMB 480,000. The draft sentence under review is: "The producer's June 2025 export tax refund declaration detail table matches the sales detail ledger in amount, and the export business has been fully checked." The question is whether this draft sentence is supported by the material.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the existence of a log as a completed compliance audit. A log records conversation content; business reconciliation, retention and compliance requirements must be carried out separately. Check: input the log record, compare whether a corresponding business plan exists, and observe whether compliance steps remain unaddressed.

2. Combining conversations from different entry points. chatId distinguishes a target conversation, and conversations from different sources are not merged into one statistic. Check: input chatIds from two sources, compare the business record ownership, and observe whether they are counted under the same review basis.

3. Treating a field that was not returned as fully retained. Business data not returned by the interface cannot be assumed to exist. Check: input the actual returned result, compare against the list of required fields, and observe whether missing fields are marked as unsupported.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the draft sentence in the hypothetical sample, input the original text of the producer export tax refund declaration detail table and the original text of the sales detail ledger, and compare the period, customs declaration number and amount fields. When all three fields agree, the draft sentence is supported by the material. When any field is missing or only related-stage evidence exists, record it as unsupported or pending verification. No actual test is claimed.

2. For the chatId check, input the hypothetical conversation identifier CHK-202507-01 and the business record, and compare the conversation source against the business ownership. When records under the same chatId agree, the condition is met. When conversations from different entry points are combined, record the observed deviation.

3. For the detail setting, input a request with detail=true and the returned responseData, and compare the module run detail fields against the required check items. When the fields are returned completely, the condition is met. When fields are missing, record the gap and do not assume full retention.
