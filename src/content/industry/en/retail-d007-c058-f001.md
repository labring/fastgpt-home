# Fresh-food supplier claims: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Fresh-food supplier claims, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Cite document receipt, supplier position and execution separately in claim review. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

A hypothetical query body records measurement documents received while the supplier’s opinion is pending. An attachment list names current-batch photographs without a handling outcome. Inspect batch, speaker and actual readable attachments when connecting a real source. A listed name does not establish complete returned image content. Document receipt is not acceptance of liability, and an awaited opinion does not establish refusal. Organize current evidence and gaps without importing another matter’s outcome.

## Three common errors

Receipt promoted to acceptance needs the supplier’s original reply. An attachment listing promoted to full content needs the actual response inspected. An older outcome used here needs batch and matter identity checked. These concern meaning, coverage and subject, independent of parseability.

## How to verify the setup

Compare contracted response type, raw body and citations; retain the gap if an attachment was not returned. A new opinion updates its corresponding matter only. Verify authentication, material access and claim-handling authority separately. A read sends no claim or closure; execution requires actual business evidence.
