# Fresh-food supplier claims: citation tracing for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Fresh-food supplier claims pursue quality responsibility for goods on arrival. They do not handle natural in-store shrinkage. That boundary decides which sources an operating report or exception review has to reconcile: the quality inspection system, WMS receiving records, the supplier contract, and financial reconciliation. Day-to-day documents cluster around quality exception forms, photo evidence, deduction notices, and claim reconciliation statements. Fixing evidence, calculating deductions, and negotiating replacement or compensation are the most time-consuming steps. The review question is narrower: which material does a given claim conclusion cite, what state is that material in, and does that state support the wording being used.

Materials update per batch and per month. This page does not derive batch synchronization, real-time triggers, or full-coverage mechanisms from that frequency.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

Citation content is output as an array, and its length may be zero. Even when nothing is retrieved, that output path still runs. A claim review page therefore cannot treat an empty citation as an abnormal termination, and it cannot treat a retrieval hit as business authorization. Knowledge base search can associate only knowledge bases that share the same vector model. That is a retrieval-layer condition and does not extend to business registration sites or external systems. The minimum relevance value is a filter threshold; it does not perform permission or entity isolation. Hybrid retrieval merges multiple result paths through RRF. Re-ranking requires a model and must be enabled in settings. Image retrieval requires corresponding image input and model capability. Optional paths shown in an overall flow diagram do not mean every request executes all of them.

Hypothetical drill sample: the material text to be checked consists of three sentences. First, the quality exception form states: "12 March 2025, batch A-0312, spinach on arrival, inspection judged the proportion of wilted leaves to be over the limit, responsibility attribution pending supplier confirmation." Second, the deduction notice states: "For batch A-0312, proposed deduction amount to be determined after quality inspection re-check, notice status pending review." Third, the claim reconciliation statement states: "Supplier contract S-2024-08 provides that quality responsibility on arrival rests with the supplier; the reconciliation scope for this period is March 2025." One draft sentence awaiting judgment reads: "Batch A-0312 deduction has been completed and the supplier has confirmed responsibility." Comparing that draft against the source text: responsibility attribution is pending confirmation, and the deduction notice is pending review. Neither completed deduction nor confirmed responsibility appears in the source. The draft sentence is therefore unsupported.

## Three common errors

1. Writing a pending-review deduction notice as a completed deduction. Input is the deduction notice text above. Compare the two states "pending review" and "completed." Observable result: when the draft claims completed deduction, it does not match the status field in the source text.
2. Writing responsibility attribution pending confirmation as supplier-confirmed responsibility. Input is the quality exception form text above. Compare "pending supplier confirmation" with "confirmed." Observable result: when the draft contains a confirmed wording, the source text does not support it.
3. Treating a retrieval hit as business authorization or completed claim execution. Input is the citation array returned by the search node. Compare the citation content against authorization status fields. Observable result: the citation array only shows that content was retrieved; it does not show that deduction, compensation, or termination has been completed.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the quality exception form text above and the draft sentence "the supplier has confirmed responsibility." Compare the responsibility attribution field value. Observable result: the draft does not match the source wording "pending supplier confirmation" and is judged unsupported.
2. Input the deduction notice text above and the draft sentence "deduction completed." Compare the notice status field value. Observable result: the source reads "pending review," the draft lacks support, and the item is judged pending verification.
3. Input an explicit paper retrieval output `[]` and check the downstream answering-step design. Observable result: the empty array is handled as a length of zero and the path runs through. Do not infer that a fictional rule query necessarily returns empty. Cross-model bases cannot be jointly associated; check identifiers first, exclude non-conforming bases, and record actual observations. Cases that are unstated or supported only by an adjacent-stage document remain unsupported or pending verification.

This page addresses only fresh-food supplier claims, operating reports and exception reviews, and citation tracing. When operations hand off materials, actions, or requests, the authorization state of each must be recorded; retrieval or a form is not authorization to execute.
