# Store cold-chain receiving: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Store cold-chain receiving, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Cold-chain review tools distinguish handoff records, measurement location and actual acceptance evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

Suppose a store handoff sheet records package transfer only, a measurement note concerns the vehicle interior and item acceptance findings are absent. Describe the tool as organizing this batch, specifying batch identity, measurement subject and evidence limits. Connect available tools to the calling module and inspect required parameters. Parameter descriptions guarantee neither real data nor invocation. No temperature threshold, actual measurement or food-safety finding is supplied.

| Measurement subject | Vehicle interior | Not item measurement |

## Three common errors

Package transfer reported as accepted goods needs actual acceptance evidence checked. Vehicle-interior measurement described as item measurement needs location and subject checked. Older-batch findings applied here need batch identity checked. These distinguish transfer from judgment, measurement subject and material ownership; a tool creates no missing inspection.

## How to verify the setup

Retain actual input, request and response, tracing each answer statement to its source. Keep absent inspection unresolved and assess real terminal output if calling stops. Added measurement-location wording corrects the subject relationship alone. Physical acceptance, quality handling and stock entry require authorized business procedures. Tool return supplies no authority to judge conformity or accept goods. Link measurement locations to their stated subjects and keep package handoff separate from current-batch inspection findings.
