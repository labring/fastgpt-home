# Livestream after-sales service: operating reviews with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Livestream after-sales service, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Bulk live-sale service review separates queue intake, individual handling and fund events. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose a consolidated list records inquiries received, handling advice covers one order and fund receipts are absent. Locate chatId and the current session matter, then inspect list-to-order correspondence in actual input and history. Check runtime detail against the official contract. Returned fields establish neither full retention of session orders nor delivery of every handling finding into context. No real resolution rate or amount is supplied.

## Three common errors

Inquiry intake reported as resolved needs actual handling evidence checked. One-order advice extended across the list needs coverage checked. Handling advice reported as credited funds needs fund-event evidence checked. These concern intake stage, aggregation level and financial execution; orderly summaries prove no per-order closure.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect identity and history separately for debug and production. Keep advice beside its order and leave unhandled entries explicit. Added individual receipts update that order alone. An omitted order means unobserved detail in this return without proving no service matter. Bulk handling, refunds and business writes need independent authority, interfaces and receipts. Logs trace evidence without executing or approving outcomes. Keep each listed inquiry traceable to its order, marking the coverage of individual advice and leaving unhandled entries open without generalizing across the session.
