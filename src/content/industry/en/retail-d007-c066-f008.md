# Short-video affiliate sales: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Short-video affiliate sales, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. A shoppable-content report distinguishes script preparation, product relationships and public visibility. A finished draft does not establish publication. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed report mixes a reviewed script, an unresolved product list and unchecked violation feedback in one progress section, without public-page evidence. Collect those status passages and let a person inspect product correspondence or feedback subjects. Script review supports preparation of that version, not a published video or functioning product link. A product list also establishes neither stock nor confirmed distribution arrangements. Carry these gaps into report drafting.

## Three common errors

Review promoted to publication needs an actual public page or publication record. A misattached product needs script-list relationship passages beside received text. One feedback item generalized to every video needs the actual covered content. These inspect publication stage, product relationships and feedback coverage rather than infer from report headings.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect whether each choice passes corresponding material downstream. Added public evidence changes only that version’s visibility, leaving unresolved feedback and product relationships explicit. Publication, product linking and distribution handling need separate authority and receipts. Completed workflow execution supplies no conversion-effect conclusion and does not derive business outcomes from content quantities or complete-looking assets.
