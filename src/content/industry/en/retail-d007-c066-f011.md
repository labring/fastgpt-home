# Short-video affiliate sales: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Short-video affiliate sales rely on long-term content exposure and product-link conversion rather than live explanation. The main data sources are the selected alliance, short-video playback data, product transaction data, and platform rules. Content operators, creator business development staff, and product distribution specialists handle four document types: short-video scripts, linked-product lists, commission plans, and violation feedback. Scripts and lists record the linking relationship, commission plans record revenue-share arrangements, and violation feedback records platform handling information. The completeness of fields in these four document types determines whether a review conclusion can be tied to a specific subject, product, and time window.

For operating reports and exception reviews, this means a database operations step must separate business source-data storage from the vector retrieval backend and check the corresponding service against the actual deployment. Connection settings are checked against the chosen deployment plan; configuration values are not inferred from names. Backup and recovery are verified separately, covering both backup generation and actual restoration, with checkable samples and records retained. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample is rechecked rather than relying only on process liveness. Retrieval and relevance scores do not carry authorization or entity isolation.

## Constraints at the database operations step

The database operations step must distinguish business source-data storage from the vector retrieval backend. Connection and load are observed with the deployment's own load, covering connections, errors, and latency; untested concurrency thresholds are not copied from elsewhere. Backup generation and actual recovery are validated as two separate operations, each retaining a checkable sample and record. When a database or service configuration changes, the same business sample is rechecked. A retrieval library's same-vector-model requirement is limited to the documented scope and does not extend to external systems such as business-registration websites.

A hypothetical drill sample is used below. On 10 March 2025, a content operator submits one short-video script and one linked-product list. The script's first line reads "Linked product: A." The list's first line reads "Linked products: A, B." The commission plan contains the line "Product A commission rate: filled in." The violation feedback records a title violation for product B. The proposed judgment sentence is: "This video script and linked-product list are consistent on the product object." The subject is the content operator; the objects are product A and product B; the states are script submitted, list submitted, commission plan partially filled, and violation feedback recorded; the effective scope is the batch submitted on 10 March 2025. The original texts available for checking are the script's first line, the list's first line, and the commission plan line above.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval or a submitted form as an authorized execution. The error appears when the submission of a linked-product list is taken as evidence that a product is listed or a commission is in effect. The check takes the list submission record and the commission plan's fill status as input, compares them against whether a platform-side listing or effect record exists, and observes that when the submission record exists but the effect record is absent, the claim is unsupported.

2. Treating an unstated field as a negative conclusion. The error appears when product B is absent from the commission plan and is therefore judged to have no commission arrangement. The check takes the commission plan text and the product B identifier as input, compares whether product B appears in the plan, and observes that when product B does not appear, the matter is left pending verification rather than judged negative.

3. Jointly associating libraries with different vector models. The error appears when two libraries with different vector models are directly associated and compared for missed recall. The check takes the vector model identifiers of the two libraries as input, compares whether the identifiers match, and observes that when they differ, the non-matching library is excluded and the actual observation is recorded, without performing a cross-model retrieval comparison.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the proposed judgment sentence in the hypothetical sample, the input is the script's first line and the list's first line, the comparison is whether the product object identifiers match, and the observation is that the script marks product A while the list marks products A and B. Under this observation, the sentence "consistent on the product object" is unsupported, and the difference for product B must be recorded.

2. For the commission plan's fill status, the input is the commission plan text, the comparison covers the commission fields for product A and product B, and the observation is that product A is filled in while product B does not appear. Under this observation, the commission arrangement for product B is left pending verification rather than judged absent.

3. For the link between violation feedback and the script, the input is the violation feedback record and the script's product identifier, the comparison is whether the violation object is a linked product of the script, and the observation is that the violation feedback records product B while the script links product A. Under this observation, the two do not form a violation association on the same object, and the evidence gap is retained.
