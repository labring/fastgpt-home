# Group-buy leader operations: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Group-buy leader operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. A group-leader report separates announcements, pickup arrangements and actual receipt. A cooperation agreement does not establish fulfillment in every community. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed report says an announcement was sent, pickup names await confirmation and receipt evidence is missing, yet concludes service was delivered. Collect community scope and source status for each entry. A person can obtain receipt evidence or inspect announcement coverage. Sending supports a communication action, not members’ awareness or received goods. A leader agreement describes cooperation without proving individual pickup execution. No commission amounts or performance figures are supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Group-leader report | Proposed source state | Separate checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcement | Sent only | Not members’ awareness |
| Pickup names | Confirmation pending | Actual delivery record |
| Community scope | One area evidenced | Not every area |
| Cooperation agreement | Arrangement, not execution | No inferred pickup |
| Evidence route | Pass passages by pickup point | Update evidenced entries only |

## Three common errors

Notice promoted to receipt needs actual communication evidence. Pickup names promoted to fulfillment need the corresponding delivery record. One community’s material expanded to all areas requires original coverage beside downstream wording. These inspect information arrival, goods stage and scope without merging everything under the same leader.

## How to verify the setup

Follow the receipt branch and inspect its community subject and passages. On the coverage branch, retain the actual announcement population. Adding one pickup point’s delivery changes only that point while other tentative lists remain unresolved. Group sending, procurement delivery and reconciliation need separate authority and receipts. Recorded choices establish neither message receipt nor received goods, and the report does not automatically become fulfillment evidence.
