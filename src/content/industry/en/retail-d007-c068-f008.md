# Group-buy presale orders: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Group-buy presale orders, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Pre-sale reporting distinguishes registration, consolidation and supplier confirmation. The workflow preserves cut-off coverage separately from actually confirmed demand. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed review says demand was consolidated, procurement awaits confirmation and added fruit was absent from the original cut-off notice. Collect these passages. A person can inspect the addition’s coverage or obtain supplier confirmation without promoting consolidation to fulfillment. For this pre-sale round, pass the corresponding notice and list together. A completed earlier round does not establish the current state, and an existing plan does not establish supplier acceptance.

## Three common errors

Consolidation promoted to confirmation needs the actual supplier reply. An addition’s unresolved status erased from the report requires notice and input comparison. A prior-round outcome used here needs source-event and received-text checks. These inspect supplier position, coverage differences and event identity rather than infer from a finished report.

## How to verify the setup

Pass addition review and supplier collection to their respective recipients. Adding this round’s fruit explanation changes coverage without confirming supply. A supplier acknowledging demand receipt still leaves handling pending. Purchasing, customer notices and reserved stock need actual records in separate steps. Output establishes neither executed pre-sale handling nor conversion performance; keep unknown states instead of filling them with earlier material.
