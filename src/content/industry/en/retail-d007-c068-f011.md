# Group-buy presale orders: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Group-buy presale order collection takes orders first and organizes procurement and fulfillment afterward. This differs from leader relationship management, which centers on maintaining leader relationships. Data sources include mini-program orders, supplier inventory, leader feedback, and warehouse-delivery routing. Operating reports and exception reviews depend on four document types: the presale product table, the order-collection result, the procurement plan, and the cut-off notice.

These materials update daily or per collection round, and report definitions need to align with the cut-off point in time. The database and operations constraint comes from the time ordering of the data itself: order status changes before and after cut-off, the aggregation scope of the order-collection result, and the correspondence between the procurement plan and the order-collection result all require the storage layer to retain verifiable original records.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Reports and reviews read orders, collection results, and procurement data that have already been written to the database. Operations must keep this data traceable across the cut-off, procurement-release, and sorting stages. Database connection configuration is checked against the chosen deployment plan, and configuration values are not inferred from variable names. Business raw-data storage and vector retrieval backends have different responsibilities; check the corresponding service against the actual deployment.

For connection and load, observe connection counts, errors, and latency under the system's own load. Do not copy untested concurrency thresholds. Backups and recovery are verified separately: backup generation and actual recovery each need a verifiable sample and record. After a database or service configuration change, check the same business sample; process liveness alone does not satisfy this. Values collected by retrieval or forms do not constitute authorization. Business review, approval, and external synchronization require their corresponding downstream implementation.

Hypothetical drill sample: A platform operator, after the cut-off on 10 June 2025, needs to check the correspondence between one order-collection result and the procurement plan. The source text reads: "Presale product table number YY-0610, cut-off time 20:00 on 10 June 2025; the order-collection result records a group-formation quantity of 320 units for this product; the procurement plan records a procurement quantity of 300 units, with status pending review." The draft sentence to be judged is: "This product has completed procurement at 320 units." This request is in pending-review status, and no completed approval or procurement execution is stated.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the group-formation quantity in the order-collection result directly as the procurement execution quantity. The order-collection result records an order aggregation; the procurement plan may be in pending-review status, and the two are not the same fact. When checking, compare the collection quantity and procurement quantity fields under the same product number, and inspect the procurement plan status field. A pending-review status does not constitute completed procurement.

2. Using retrieval or form submission in place of an authorization judgment. A retrieval returning related records, or a form collecting a procurement quantity value, does not mean approval passed or procurement was executed. Check whether an approval record exists and whether its status is completed; when missing, record the evidence gap.

3. Checking only process liveness after a configuration change. A running process does not mean the order-collection result and procurement plan read by reports remain consistent. Re-read the same business sample and compare field values, observing whether a difference appears.

## How to verify the setup

- Verification 1: Input the source text from the hypothetical sample and the draft sentence "This product has completed procurement at 320 units." Compare the procurement-completion wording in the draft sentence with the procurement plan status field "pending review" in the material. The condition is met when: the material states no completed approval or procurement execution record, so the completion wording in the draft sentence is unsupported and should be recorded as pending verification, not judged as completed.

- Verification 2: Input the order-collection result group-formation quantity of 320 units and the procurement plan procurement quantity of 300 units under the same product number YY-0610. Compare the two field values and their respective document sources. The condition is met when: the two values differ, so the report should not equate the collection quantity with the procurement quantity, and should present them separately with source documents noted.

- Verification 3: Input the same order-collection result record before and after a database or service configuration change. Compare the field values and record counts read before and after the change. The condition is met when: the field values of the same sample after the change match those before, or any difference corresponds to an explicit configuration change description; process liveness alone does not satisfy this condition.

If a report query returns an empty result, a paper input of an empty set can be used to check the downstream display design, without inferring that retrieval rules necessarily return empty. Libraries across different vector models cannot be associated together; first check identifiers and exclude non-matching ones, record actual observations, and do not perform cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.
