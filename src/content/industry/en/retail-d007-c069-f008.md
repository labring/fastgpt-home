# Dealer rebate calculation: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Dealer rebate calculation, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Rebate-report organization separates policy applicability, achievement evidence and mutual confirmation. Complete-looking tables establish neither calculation nor accounting execution. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed report includes policy wording, channel-achievement material and an unconfirmed reconciliation draft, yet describes everything as handled. Collect applicability periods and channel descriptions. A person may inspect policy coverage or obtain mutual confirmation. This organizes sources without amounts, rates or financial calculations. Prepared achievement material does not establish policy applicability, and a related credit document alone does not establish this rebate’s processing. Preserve each passage’s actual subject downstream.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Rebate-report organization | Proposed material | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy applicability | Period and channel limits | Keep out-of-scope items separate |
| Achievement material | Prepared | Not an established calculation |
| Reconciliation draft | Unconfirmed unilateral text | Actual mutual reply |
| Related document | Does not prove processing | Current handling outcome |
| Workflow handoff | Period and channel preserved | Gaps remain explicit |

## Three common errors

Merged coverage needs channel identity beside policy scope. Preparation promoted to confirmation needs actual mutual period acknowledgment. A document promoted to posting needs this transaction’s real accounting result. These inspect applicability, parties’ positions and execution; matching document titles do not establish business relationships.

## How to verify the setup

Compare periods and channels received by both paths. Added scope material changes the applicability explanation without closing missing confirmation or handling outcomes. Formal calculation, voucher processing and public reporting need separate authority and receipts. Node output establishes neither entitlement, completed accounting nor reconciliation of every transaction.
