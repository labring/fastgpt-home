# Cross-border settlements: conversation logs for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Cross-border settlement and foreign exchange work concerns the collection of funds from completed orders. The working materials are platform settlement statements, bank transaction records, payment provider back-office records, and ERP orders. Fraud and chargeback handling sits outside this scope. The people doing the work are cross-border seller finance staff, tax accountants, and platform operations staff. Mistakes surface in profit calculation, tax filing, and cash shortfalls. The supplied brief gives weekly and monthly update frequencies; this page does not extend those into batch synchronization, full synchronization, or real-time triggers.

This page addresses one task only: conversation logs and audit during operating report review and exception review. The question is which conversation and which dialogue record contain a given settlement or foreign exchange inquiry, which run fields were returned, and what authorization state those records occupy. Retrieval and form value collection do not constitute authorized execution.

Two features of the material shape this task. First, a conversation is identified by chatId, and sessions from different entry points are not pooled. Second, run detail is requested through the documented detail setting, and the returned module fields must be checked item by item. Business data that was not returned cannot be assumed to have been retained in full. Reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements are handled by a separate complete business plan; the existence of a log does not mean a compliance audit was completed. A similarity score does not perform permission or entity isolation.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

When checking a target dialogue against a business record, use chatId and do not combine sessions from different entry points. When run detail is needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect the module fields inside responseData. Compare the fields actually returned by the interface against the log; business data that is absent from the response is not evidence of complete retention. Identity fields in test mode may differ from production calls, so check the input, the history, and the returned content separately.

Hypothetical paper exercise: a cross-border seller finance staff member reviews a service fee discrepancy on a February platform settlement statement in March 2025. The source material consists of three sentences. Sentence one: "This statement covers the settlement period 2025-02-01 to 2025-02-28, with total deductions of 1,200 USD." Sentence two: "The receipt reconciliation table records 1,180 USD received for this period." Sentence three: "The service fee invoice was issued for 20 USD, status issued." One pending handover request reads: "Please confirm that the difference between this statement's deductions and the amount received has been automatically collected by the system and that the audit has been completed." The scope of judgment is limited to the above subject, period, and fields. Evidence for automatic system collection and completed audit is missing from the material.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a missing chatId as use of context. The official text states that when chatId is not passed, the database context is not used and context is built entirely from messages. Check by submitting the same messages under both chatId states, comparing the returned history content, and observing whether database session records appear.

2. Treating a similarity score as permission or entity filtering. In the official example, score is a retrieval result field. Check by submitting a cross-business inquiry, comparing quoteList and score, and observing whether unauthorized records appear; a high or low score does not show that a record has been authorized.

3. Treating an interactive node response as completed business execution. The official text states that the interactive node returns a display configuration containing only type and params. Check by submitting a request with an interactive node, comparing the choices content, and observing whether only display fields are present; collected form values are not approval or a database write.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input sentence one and sentence two of the hypothetical sample, then compare whether the 1,200 USD statement deduction and the 1,180 USD reconciliation receipt share the same subject, period, and field. These are different fields, and the 20 USD difference is not supported by the material; record it as pending verification.

2. Input sentence three of the hypothetical sample, then compare the 20 USD invoice amount against the status "issued." The status shows only that an invoice was issued, which is a different fact from whether the deduction difference has been collected. It is not evidence of automatic collection.

3. Input the pending handover request, then compare its claim that automatic collection and audit completion occurred against what the material actually states. The material does not state that action, so the judgment is unsupported. Preserve the evidence gap and do not supply authorization or effective clauses.
