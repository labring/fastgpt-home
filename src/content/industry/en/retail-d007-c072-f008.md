# Overseas stock replenishment: operating reviews with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Overseas stock replenishment, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. An overseas-warehouse report separates replenishment planning, warehouse receipt and last-mile delivery. One transport record does not establish the entire chain’s completion. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed report contains an inbound appointment and old-stock age material. Last-mile wording concerns earlier dispatched goods, yet the report calls current replenishment available in the warehouse. Collect warehouse, source-batch and event subjects. A person may inspect stock origins or obtain current receiving evidence. Supply no actual stock quantities or turnover effects. Earlier inventory does not establish new arrivals, and matching item names cannot link old delivery material to this plan.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Overseas-warehouse report check | Proposed subject | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appointment | Receipt unproven | Current warehouse receiving record |
| Inventory age | Earlier stock | Not current replenishment |
| Last-mile delivery | Earlier dispatched goods | Not inbound direction |
| Usable state | Receipt alone insufficient | Corresponding business evidence |
| Workflow update | Warehouse and batch preserved | Not full-chain completion |

## Three common errors

Appointment promoted to availability needs actual receiving and usable-state evidence. Old goods promoted to replenishment require inventory source-batch checks. Last-mile delivery applied to inbound receipt needs logistics-event and direction checks. These inspect stage, origin and chain direction rather than fill gaps with the same warehouse name.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect warehouse and batch descriptions received by both choices. Added current receiving material changes that stage alone; usable inventory needs its own evidence. Keep exclusion of last-mile material that still concerns earlier dispatch. Purchasing, international transport and inventory writes need separate authority and receipts. Output establishes neither a completed chain nor actual warehouse performance from complete-looking plans and logistics files.
