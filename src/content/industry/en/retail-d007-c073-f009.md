# Cross-border payment reviews: operating reviews with tool calls

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Cross-border payment reviews, this page examines FastGPT tool calls. Payment-risk review separates an attempted transaction from evidence of merchant receipt. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the tool calls step

Hypothetical material records initiation of a payment attempt, lacks merchant receipt evidence and includes an outcome for another matter. Describe the tool purpose, required matter identity and limits when evidence is missing, then connect the configured plugin to its calling module. Check required parameters before assuming invocation; unsuitable or missing information may leave the tool uncalled. This paper exercise executes no payment and supplies no risk clearance.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Payment-record tool | Hypothetical material | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matter identity | Payment attempt | Required parameters |
| Fund stage | Receipt evidence absent | Do not report receipt |
| Earlier outcome | Another matter | Separate identity/period |
| Response scope | May be an index | Inspect raw return |
| Handling authority | Separate business system | No tool-based clearance |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Tool description` | Describe purpose, inputs and non-applicable situations. |
| `Tool parameters` | Inspect module and plugin parameters separately. |
| `Required parameters` | The model may not invoke a tool without suitable required inputs. |
| `Tool connections` | Available tools must connect to the calling module. |
| `Tool-call termination` | Termination ends the call without a later AI summary. |

## Three common errors

An attempt reported as received funds needs transaction stage and receipt evidence checked. An earlier outcome imported here needs matter identity and period checked. An index reported as fully reviewed content needs actual response coverage inspected. These trace event, ownership and returned content.

## How to verify the setup

Retain input, invocation parameters, raw return and final answer, aligning each claim with its business source. If termination is configured, inspect actual terminal output without expecting a later model summary. Payment execution, fund confirmation and risk handling require their business systems and authority; tool success describes the returned call state.
