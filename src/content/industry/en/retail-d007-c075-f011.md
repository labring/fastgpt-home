# Creator sales campaigns: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Creator special sales campaigns depend on external creator fulfilment and commission settlement. Each session produces its own transaction detail, commission setting record and after-sales commission deduction data, and the cooperation cycle advances session by session. Operating reports bring the creator cooperation agreement, the commission setting sheet, the sample dispatch sheet and the live session report under one time reference for cross-checking. These materials come from different stages, and their field definitions may differ, so a check must hold the same subject, the same session and the same field. Data updates per session or per week, and report generation relies on transaction and settlement records already written to the database.

The category differs from brand self-broadcast because the core is external creator fulfilment and commission settlement rather than internal brand scheduling. The documents involved are the creator cooperation agreement, the commission setting sheet, the sample dispatch sheet and the live session report. A mismatch in commission or sample records can produce settlement disputes and damage to brand reputation, which is why the review step needs traceable, comparable samples rather than process liveness alone.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Operating reports and anomaly reviews depend on stable database connections, recoverable backups and re-checkable changes. Database connection settings must be checked against the actual deployment plan, and a configuration value cannot be inferred from its variable name. Business source data and the vector retrieval backend serve different roles and must be checked against their respective services.

Connection counts and concurrency thresholds must be observed against the deployment's own load; values that have not been tested in that environment cannot be copied in. Backup generation and actual recovery must be verified separately, with a comparable sample and record retained. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample must be used for re-checking; a running process does not constitute evidence that business data is correct.

Hypothetical drill sample

Source text one: "Creator A and Brand B agreed on a special session for 18 June 2025, commission rate 20%, slot fee 5,000 yuan, after-sales commission deduction calculated on the actual return amount."

Source text two: "Sample dispatch sheet number SP-0618, three samples sent, signed for on 10 June 2025."

Draft sentence to be assessed: "Creator A's 18 June special session commission has been settled in full at 20%."

The draft sentence concerns settlement status. The source text does not state whether settlement was completed; it states only the agreed commission rate. A check must separate the agreed rate from the fact of completed settlement.

## Three common errors

1. Treating an agreed commission rate as completed settlement. The input is source text one, and the comparison object is the settlement status field. Where the material does not state that settlement is complete, the draft sentence is not supported.
2. Treating sample receipt as completed live session fulfilment. The input is source text two, and the comparison object is the actual outbound record in the live session report. The signing date and the live session date are separate facts and cannot substitute for one another.
3. Treating a running database process as correct data. The input is the service state after a change, and the comparison object is the field values of the same special session transaction detail. A running process does not show that business data was unaffected.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input source text one and the draft sentence, and compare the commission rate field with the settlement status field. Where the material states only the rate, the draft sentence's settlement completion claim is not supported, and the evidence gap must be recorded.
2. Input source text two, and compare the signing date on the sample dispatch sheet with the session date in the live session report. Where the two differ, whether the samples were used for that session must be verified, and a direct association cannot be made.
3. Input the same special session transaction detail before and after the change, and compare the same field values. Where a difference appears, record the differing field and the sample scope; where no difference appears, this shows only that no change was observed within that sample scope and does not constitute a conclusion that all data is correct.

The primary task on this page is operating reports and anomaly reviews for creator special sales campaigns at the database operations step. Retrieval and forms collect values or return results; they do not grant authorization, and business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation. A pending request is not approval, an offered option is not execution, and an unsigned termination is not a completed termination. Where the material does not state a fact, that fact remains unknown, and the evidence gap is retained rather than filled with an assumed authorization or effective clause.
