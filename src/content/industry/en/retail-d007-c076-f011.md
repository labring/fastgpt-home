# Creator commission settlement: database operations for operating reviews

## What the documents look like

Operating reports and exception reviews for creator commission settlement and commission deduction draw on four core materials: commission setting records, settlement statements, refund commission deduction details, and invoices. Commission settlement occurs after a transaction closes, so the report has to align three threads to the same entity, the same period, and the same field: valid promotion orders, after-sales refund commission deductions, and invoicing and payment. Sources include the selected alliance backend, order after-sales data, the finance system, and creator contracts.

For database and operations work, the task is to keep these materials traceable through storage, retrieval, backup, and change review, and to let report definitions be traced line by line back to source records during exception review. A commission setting record captures a rate and a validity window; it does not by itself authorize a settlement action. Retrieval of a record is not authorization to execute against it.

Hypothetical drill sample (paper exercise, no real party): a commission setting record reads "Entity: Xinghai Culture Communication (fictional); object: creator account HX-0731 (fictional); status: commission rate 12%, applicable period 1 March 2026 to 31 March 2026; authorization boundary: limited to livestream closed orders for this account within March." A draft sentence under review reads: "All orders for this creator in March 2026 settle at 12%, and refunded orders are not deducted." The material states the applicable period and the authorization boundary. It does not state a refund deduction rule, so the refund portion is unsupported.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Settlement materials span multiple systems. Business source data and the vector retrieval backend carry different responsibilities, so at deployment each connection is checked against the actual plan and configuration values are not inferred from names. Connection and load behavior is observed against the deployment's own load: connection counts, errors, and latency. Concurrency thresholds that were not tested are not copied in.

Backup and recovery are verified separately: backup generation is one check, and actual restore is another, each retaining a sample and a record that can be compared. After a database or service configuration change, the same business sample is re-checked. A running process is not evidence that the report definition is unchanged.

This page addresses only creator commission settlement and deduction / operating reports and exception review / database and operations. What matters at handover is which materials, operations, and requests are in what authorization state. A retrieved record, a filled form, or a pending request does not constitute approval or execution. An unsigned termination is not a completed termination, and an offered option is not an executed action.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval or a form as authorized execution. The commission setting record states only the applicable period and authorization boundary. Retrieving that record does not authorize a refund deduction rule and does not approve settlement execution.
2. Treating an unstated field as a negative finding. The draft sentence's "refunded orders are not deducted" has no corresponding clause in the material. It is unsupported, and it cannot be written as a confirmed no-deduction rule or as a conflict with the material.
3. Substituting process liveness for change review. After a database or service configuration change, confirming only that the service process is running, without comparing settlement amounts and deduction details against the same business sample, leaves the report definition's stability unknown.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample text and the draft sentence. Compare "applicable period 1 March 2026 to 31 March 2026" against the draft's "all orders in March 2026." If the draft expands the authorization boundary from this account's livestream closed orders to all orders, the sentence exceeds the material's scope.
2. Input the hypothetical sample text. Compare whether the material states a refund deduction rule. When the material does not state one, the draft's "refunded orders are not deducted" is unsupported. Record the evidence gap and defer judgment until a clause is supplied.
3. Input the same settlement statement sample from before and after a configuration change. Compare commission amounts and deduction details for the same entity, the same period, and the same field. Only when both results agree and trace back to source records is the post-change review condition met. When results differ, retain a difference record and do not infer a cause.
