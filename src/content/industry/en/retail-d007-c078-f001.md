# Chain-store site selection: operating reviews with HTTP integrations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Chain-store site selection, this page examines FastGPT HTTP integrations. Site-review requests preserve interest, candidate locations and actual opening events. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the HTTP integrations step

Suppose a candidate-site list records inspection interest only, a leasing email awaits confirmation of conditions and historical opening photographs concern another address. Pass site and matter identity under the real interface contract, checking address, method and variables with safely configured credentials. Inspect query authentication separately from signing authority. Readable material establishes no headquarters approval to open. No area, rent or business forecast is invented.

## Three common errors

Candidate interest reported as signed needs an actual contract checked. Pending conditions reported as mutual acceptance need email wording checked. Another address photograph reported as current operation needs address and event checked. These concern stage, opinion state and evidence subject independently of response success.

## How to verify the setup

Retain redacted requests, raw responses and source locations. Address variants need actual correspondence rather than guesses from abbreviations. Mark image content unreviewed when only filenames return. An added contract updates signing assessment alone while operation still needs separate evidence. Signing, opening approval and project writes require authorized procedures and interfaces. HTTP reading supports review of existing material without creating those business outcomes. Attach verified address correspondence to site abbreviations, linking lease conditions separately from opening images so review can name the missing contract, acceptance or operating evidence.
