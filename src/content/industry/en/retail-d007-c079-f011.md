# Central chain procurement: operating reviews with database operations

## What the documents look like

For operating reviews in Central chain procurement, this page examines FastGPT database operations. Separate headquarters ordering, dispatch and branch receipts without inventing packaging conversions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the database operations step

Suppose a procurement agreement uses cartons, branch requests use individual items, opening instructions are absent, and a dispatch sheet is preparation only. Preserve counting subjects and planning states in storage tests. Quantities for a shared product cannot simply be merged across documents. No actual procurement count, conversion ratio or inventory is supplied. Search returns prove no branch receipt.

## Three common errors

- If carton/item distinctions disappear after persistence, compare original contract/request fields with restored text. Numeric presence establishes no additive quantities.

- If dispatch preparation becomes branch acknowledgment, inspect receipts and delivery stages. Connectivity supplies no receiving result.

- If older packaging terms support a new purchase, compare agreement version, product and packaging conditions. Missing current evidence stays unresolved.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect persistence separately for agreements, branch requests and dispatch originals, then check retrieval matches and citations. Repeat packaging and receipt questions after actual restoration. Check installed connections and record observed load errors and timing without guessed concurrency capability. Keep documents fixed across changes. Tests place no purchase, allocate no inventory and promise no distribution capability.
