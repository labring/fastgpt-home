# TikTok Shop US operations: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Livestream-material indexes must distinguish capture records, demonstration purpose and permission for advertising reuse. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed TikTok Shop material records a store demonstration filmed for this explanation only. Its English note says demo footage was captured in store, with reuse approval pending. A question about advertising use needs permission evidence beyond filming location and demonstration purpose. Index wording can support questions about the demo or pending advertising reuse while mapping to originals containing the restriction. Captured records completed filming; it cannot be compressed into approved advertising use, because those completed states would concern different actions and require different supporting material.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change captured into approved; compare the capture verb with the permission condition to identify the added approval conclusion. Second, the current-explanation limit may disappear; inspect the complete registration page mapped by the index for any broadened purpose. Third, similar bilingual titles may import another clip’s permission; inspect clip identity and actual original data, tracing permission text separately for each item. These concern action stage, use scope and material identity. A shared store or language cannot establish that two clips carry the same permission.

## How to verify the setup

Test questions about what was captured, its present purpose and evidence for advertising reuse, retaining index-to-source mappings. Two indexes returning one registration remain one source. Semantic proximity guarantees neither complete recall nor permission to publish; check the same-model condition separately when multiple bases share one retrieval node. This exercise publishes nothing and decides no actual permission validity. Missing reuse evidence stays pending. Failure to retrieve it does not establish that a rights holder refused reuse or that approval has never been given for the footage elsewhere.
