# General export declarations: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Check bill roles, weight fields and translation revisions without treating wording changes as declaration amendments. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An invented export packet contains a bill excerpt labeling consignee and notify party. Chinese packing wording separates gross and net weight without values, and a translation note requests terminology review only. Collect original roles and labels by document, then choose recipient-role or weight-label review. Inspect whether downstream input retains sources. A notify party is not automatically the consignee, and weight labels cannot be exchanged. Revising translation establishes no amendment to actual customs records. No real bill, weight or customs outcome is supplied.

## Three common errors

- If notify party becomes the actual recipient in Chinese, compare both bill roles and their subjects. A shared contact name does not merge identities.

- If gross weight becomes net weight downstream, inspect original labels and their scope. Parseable documents establish no correct measurement basis.

- If translation comments become amended customs records, inspect the correction target, translation version and formal submission evidence. Editing text performs no external-system action.

## How to verify the setup

Ask who is notified, which field is gross and which document the correction changes. Compare actual input on both routes. Added identity mapping resolves roles alone; measurement explanations supply neither values nor declaration state. Retain original terms beside translated passages without inventing official field names or customs rules. Formal declarations, original-document amendments and business writes need authorized interfaces and receipts. Language workflow transfers traceable sources without conducting a declaration or confirming that any external record changed.
