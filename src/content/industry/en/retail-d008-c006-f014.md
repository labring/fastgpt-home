# General export declarations: multilingual work with model connections

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Inspect parties, measurement units and packing scope in parallel trade text without treating matching wording as matching declarations. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

A bilingual exercise lists exporter, consignee and notify party separately, retaining each role beside its English name. Connection testing should inspect delivery of the paired roles and names instead of comparing company spelling alone. If the answer translates notify party as consignee, retain the actual request, original role and returned sentence. The same company name may carry different documentary roles. This text comparison establishes neither a party’s qualification nor acceptance of the shipment’s declaration information.

## Three common errors

- If notify party becomes consignee, compare original role, actual request and output. Locate changed relationships during input or generation; identical company spelling does not erase documentary roles.

- If inner-package quantities attach to outer cartons, retain hierarchy, numbers and units while inspecting input structure. With both supplied, locate generated correspondence errors separately.

- If absent English use or specification text becomes purported original document wording, label the draft and obtain counterparts. Product names supply neither declaration codes nor undocumented conditions.

## How to verify the setup

Use separate questions for roles, units and packing hierarchy, retaining the passages cited and unresolved items. An English product name can be drafted without supplying undocumented specifications or declaration codes. After obtaining corresponding text, hold the question and settings constant to inspect delivery and scope. If actual material depends on a scanned image, validate that input capability separately. The present bilingual text result does not establish reading of a stamp, diagram or image-based field.
