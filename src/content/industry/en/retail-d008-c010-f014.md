# JD POP seller operations: multilingual work with model connections

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Inspect the limits of parallel product and service wording so translations do not expand charging information or repair registration into extra promises. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

A proposed product attachment distinguishes supports charging from includes a charger; the actual Chinese original supplies only the former. Inspect whether English input preserves that limit. If output turns capability into an included accessory, compare request and original wording. The statements concern function and package contents, which do not become equivalent because both mention charging. Without packing information, this comparison cannot confirm inclusion of an accessory, and the product name cannot fill that gap.

## Three common errors

- If charging support becomes an included charger, retain function wording and the packing gap beside actual input. Neither a name nor shared charging terminology supplies an accessory.

- If registered repair becomes completed, trace whether completion came from an original or generation. Registration-only evidence requires correction; a draft is no existing platform outcome.

- If both counterparts exist but only one reaches the request, inspect selection and actual input. Retain omission rather than claiming complete review or diagnosing capability from one-sided output.

## How to verify the setup

Ask separately about function, included items and service stage, recording the Chinese condition behind every English judgment and unresolved item. Repeat the questions after adding packing or repair-result information, inspecting delivery and adopted scope so an earlier draft does not overwrite the new source. Label photos need their own reading check; text results do not cover pictured instructions. Keep draft translations distinguishable from confirmed material before the responsible owner verifies actual product and service information.
