# Convenience fresh-food orders: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Convenience fresh-food orders, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Fresh-display indexes must distinguish a temperature-zone sign from measured food condition. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed fresh-display material defines a cold-zone sign as a location marker and records no food temperature. Its English note says cold-zone sign for location, no product reading recorded. A question about a food item meeting a temperature condition lacks both its reading and the applicable condition in this source set. An index must not turn cold-zone into a product temperature-test record. Ask separately about the sign and measurement evidence; shared cold vocabulary cannot promote a location description into a food-quality conclusion or an actual observation of the item.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change a location sign into a test result; compare sign and reading objects and identify the new evidence type. Second, an answer may turn no reading into compliant; inspect negation scope and adopted wording without filling measured status. Third, a record for another counter may attach to this item; inspect counter, item and observation time instead of joining readings through similar display names. These concern evidence type, missing-evidence meaning and observed object. Textual relevance provides no actual food-suitability or safety conclusion from a sign’s name.

## How to verify the setup

Test location identification separately from item measurement, retaining index-to-original mappings and naming the missing evidence for temperature questions. Bilingual indexes for one note are not two measurement samples. Inspect model conditions separately when multiple bases share a retrieval node. This exercise measures no food, sets no temperature standard and performs no site disposition. An unrecorded reading does not establish that measurement never occurred, and retrieval failure does not establish noncompliance. Later observation material requires the same item and observation point before writing only what that material explicitly supports.
