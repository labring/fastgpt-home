# Franchise fee settlement: model connections for multilingual work

## What the documents look like

Franchise fee and deposit settlement centers on money movements and deduction events. It is a different matter from government franchise filing administration. The working materials are franchise fee invoices, deposit ledgers, deduction notices, and contractual payment schedules. Source systems include the finance system, franchise contracts, bank statements, and store violation records. In cross-border and multilingual retrieval, one deduction notice may appear in several languages, so retrieval depends on connected models to perform cross-language semantic matching.

The generic brief rates this category description as roughly accurate, so the details above remain conditional rather than confirmed. The brief lists monthly and per-deduction update frequencies for the source data. Those frequencies do not change the model connection step described here and are not treated as a synchronization mechanism.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Model connections depend on AI Proxy. Both the App and an Admin service that reuses App/Service configuration must set AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT and a non-empty AIPROXY_API_TOKEN. Missing either one causes startup validation to fail. AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT takes the service root address and does not append /v1. The system needs at least one language model and one index model to work normally.

Retrieval and similarity do not carry authorization or entity isolation duties. A shared vector model is the precondition for knowledge bases to be associated with each other. This requirement does not extend to business registration sites or other external systems. Libraries on different vector models cannot be associated together; the identifier must be checked first and non-matching libraries excluded, with the actual observation recorded.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. A franchise management department keeps a Chinese deduction notice reading: "To Franchisee A of the East China region: because the March 2026 monthly reconciliation was not completed under Article 7 of the contract, RMB 8,000 is proposed for deduction from the deposit ledger; the deduction reason is overdue reconciliation; status is pending review by the franchise management department." It also keeps an English deposit ledger extract reading: "Party A, deposit ledger, March 2026, pending review." The draft sentence to be judged is: "The franchisee's deposit deduction has been completed and settlement is closed."

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval hit as authorized execution. Given the hypothetical sample's deduction notice marked pending review, judging the deduction complete only because the notice was retrieved treats a retrieval result as a business action. The notice states a pending-review status and does not state that the deduction was executed.
2. Treating pending review as missing operating permission. The English ledger extract in the hypothetical sample says only pending review and does not state who holds operating permission. A conclusion that a particular party lacks permission does not follow.
3. Treating a similarity score as an entity or permission filter. If retrieval in the hypothetical sample returns records of other franchisees, a high score does not make those records belong to the same entity. The identifier must be checked first.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample's Chinese deduction notice and English ledger extract, then compare whether the model returns text blocks related to deposit deduction and pending review. If the returned content matches the original subject, period, and fields, treat retrieval as usable. If it returns records of other franchisees, record the result as pending verification.
2. Input the draft sentence "The franchisee's deposit deduction has been completed and settlement is closed" and compare it against the sample source text. The source states pending review; the draft states the deduction is complete. The two take mutually exclusive values on the same field, so the draft sentence is unsupported.
3. Input an empty-result scenario and check the downstream design against a paper input of []. The document states that nothing is pushed when the value is empty. A claim that a fictional-rule search must return [] cannot be made. If the actual return is non-empty, record the observation and do not infer a cause.

Model probe retries and alerts follow the documented conditions, and the page shows an empty state when automatic probing is not enabled. Probe configuration and the alert webhook are separate settings and do not take effect automatically because retrieval configuration is complete.
