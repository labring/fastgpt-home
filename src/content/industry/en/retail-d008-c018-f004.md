# Regional dealer stock: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Dealer-stock material needs regional interest separated from exclusive arrangements without expanded territory rights. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed dealer-stock correspondence expresses interest in a northern-region trial, with coverage awaiting confirmation by both parties. The English page says trial interest and states no exclusivity. A question about exclusive northern coverage has neither an exclusive arrangement nor a confirmed scope in these materials. Indexes can retain trial interest and pending territory, without shortening the region name into exclusive territory. The document records communication, not a formal grant. Inspect the interested party and region in each language instead of treating a shared place name as authority to act.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change trial interest into exclusive territory; compare interest wording and the exclusivity conclusion for added rights. Second, index phrasing may remove bilateral confirmation; compare Chinese coverage and English explanation and retain the unresolved state in the answer. Third, another dealer’s regional note may map into this one; inspect communicating party and original identity rather than join scope through the same region name. These concern behaviour, confirmation stage and participant. They decide no actual contract validity, dealership eligibility or authority merely by matching a region and business term.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what interest was expressed, who confirms coverage and which original states exclusivity, retaining returned indexes and originals. Several indexes for one memo are not multiple-party confirmation. Check embedding-model conditions for associated bases within the actual retrieval node. This exercise grants no dealership, arranges no trial and promises no supply. No exclusivity stated describes this page’s content and does not rule out arrangements elsewhere. Failure to retrieve formal material leaves a gap, rather than establishing non-exclusive business or the other party’s lack of qualification in the real relationship.
