# Certificates of origin: multilingual work with model connections

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Certificates of origin, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Inspect country roles, addresses and product descriptions in parallel certificate material without treating language comparison as an origin decision. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

A proposed bilingual attachment separates country of export, destination country and company address, preserving their English headings. Inspect delivery of these relationships in the request. A country appearing in an address does not establish the product’s origin. If the answer substitutes export country or company location for origin, compare the original heading and returned sentence to locate the unsupported extension. Origin evidence must be obtained separately; matching country names, working connectivity or fluent wording supplies no such basis.

## Three common errors

- If export country or company address becomes product origin, compare heading, request and response to locate changed location roles. Matching place names supply no origin basis.

- If selection includes use wording but the request omits it, retain both stages and investigate transfer. This turn cannot validate reading of the use or alone establish a translation error.

- If generated English use wording becomes purported existing certificate text, inspect the actual counterpart and retain draft identity. Obtain evidence, then repeat subject and version checks.

## How to verify the setup

Compare actual input and output sentence by sentence for country roles, addresses and intended use, recording draft suggestions separately from provided counterparts. A stamped scan requires separate inspection of image input or completeness of extracted text; a text call does not validate the stamp or every field. Translation does not execute certificate correction or formal issuance. Configuration checks help locate call and input problems, while language accuracy still requires actual sources and applicable requirements.
