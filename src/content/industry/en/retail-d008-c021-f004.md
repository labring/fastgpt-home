# Brand member operations: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Member-event indexes need information registration, event enrolment and attendance treated as separate actions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed member material records willingness to receive event information, explicitly not event enrolment. The English page says information opt-in only and attendance unrecorded. A question about participants has neither enrolment nor attendance evidence in that preference. Opt-in concerns communication here. Indexes need the information purpose and no-enrolment limit, rather than being named an attendee list. No real person is included. This paper exercise examines action objects: registration vocabulary can describe a communication preference or an event signup, and only the original sentence determines which action was actually recorded.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change information opt-in into event enrolment; inspect the object of agreement and Chinese exclusion for an action substitution. Second, attendance unrecorded may become did not attend; preserve the recording gap instead of creating a negative observation. Third, another event’s list may enter this registration; inspect event round and source identity instead of joining attendance through a shared member category. These concern action object, record gap and event ownership. Multilingual hits establish no actual member behaviour beyond the specific action in the original proposed text.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what information may be received, whether enrolment is documented and whether an attendance record exists, tracing returned indexes and originals. Multiple indexes for one form remain one preference source. Check the same-model condition within a retrieval node’s associated bases, without treating a language switch as complete recall or sufficient permission. This exercise gathers no list, sends no material and decides no real consent validity. Failure to retrieve a signup record does not determine whether signup occurred. An unrecorded attendance state does not determine whether the person attended. Independent evidence for this event is needed before answering another stage.
