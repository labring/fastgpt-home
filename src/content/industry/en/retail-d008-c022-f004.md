# Omnichannel stock transfers: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Channel-transfer indexes need warehouse allocation separated from store receipt without compressing the transport chain. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed channel-transfer material allocates preparation to a regional warehouse and leaves store receipt pending a return note. The English memo says allocated to regional warehouse, store receipt pending. A store-arrival question has allocation evidence, not proof of transport or receipt. Index wording may describe warehouse allocation and pending store evidence while mapping to the original, but allocated cannot become delivered. No real stock or interface is supplied. The paper exercise checks whether places and stages still correspond across languages without creating completed movement from an administrative allocation.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change allocated into delivered; compare the allocation action and original place for an added transport conclusion. Second, regional warehouse may become store; inspect recipient and location level rather than let fluent translation move the endpoint. Third, loss of pending may produce received; inspect the complete memo and a separate store receipt instead of replacing it with warehouse preparation. These concern action, place and completion evidence. Multiple indexes do not demonstrate execution of multiple transport stages or make allocation a substitute for a receiving record.

## How to verify the setup

Ask allocation destination, evidence of store status and the batch covered by a receipt separately, retaining actual index-to-original mappings. Later store material needs the same batch and location before use. Check model consistency locally for bases within one retrieval node when applicable. This exercise moves no inventory, issues no transfer instruction and guarantees no delivery. Failure to retrieve a receipt does not establish non-receipt, while an allocation sheet does not establish dispatch. Relevance can locate material, but each business stage still needs its own supporting source text before appearing as fact.
