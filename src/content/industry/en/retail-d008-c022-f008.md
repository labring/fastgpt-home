# Omnichannel stock transfers: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Bilingual transfer handoff preserves transit, reserved stock and channel ownership. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Suppose an English transfer notice says goods remain in transit, a Chinese note reserves the batch for a store and a screenshot concerns an online channel. Collect original passages, batch and channel correspondence, then let a person choose terminology review or arrival evidence. Inspect states actually passed downstream after submission. Reserved stock establishes no sale availability, and transit cannot become available destination stock through translation. No actual transfer is executed in this paper exercise.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Bilingual stock transfer | Hypothetical state | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transit notice | Not arrived | Receiving evidence |
| Reserved stock | Availability restricted | Preserve restriction |
| Online screenshot | Another channel | Stock-pool identity |
| Person chooses | Terms or arrival | Inspect received input |
| Stock operations | Separate system | Authority and receipt |

| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Transit translated as arrived needs transport and receiving evidence. Reservation rewritten as sale availability needs the restricting wording checked. Online-channel stock applied to a store balance needs channel identity checked. These concern stage, availability and subject independently of continued nodes.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual input on both choice routes and preserve the same batch qualification in both languages. Added arrival evidence changes receipt stage alone; sale availability still needs authority and business state. Identify the channel beside each screenshot to prevent same-name items crossing stock pools. Transfers, release and stock writes require real systems, permission and receipts. List missing release evidence beside reserved stock, with destination receipt and online balance linked to their separate original sources.
