# Brand repair services: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Repair indexes must separate visual review from functional testing and avoid turning reviewed into repaired. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

Proposed repair material says appearance notes have been reviewed while an operation test awaits arrangement. The English page states appearance notes reviewed, operation test pending. A question about restored use has no functional result or completed-repair evidence in that review. Indexes may describe checked appearance records and pending testing without naming the device repaired. Reviewed acts on notes, not a complete device inspection. Preserve object and outstanding action in both languages rather than merge everything under broad repair or inspection vocabulary and infer a completed technical outcome.

## Three common errors

First, an index may change notes reviewed into device repaired; inspect the reviewed object and action and seek separate repair evidence. Second, omission of operation test pending may produce functions normally; compare the full original and adopted statement, leaving the test unresolved without evidence. Third, another device’s test page may map to this case; inspect individual identity and record version rather than join results through the same model. These concern action object, test stage and individual ownership. A repair occurring during warranty does not by itself establish a conflict between contract terms.

## How to verify the setup

Ask what was reviewed, the test stage and which device owns any result, retaining index-to-original relationships. Several questions returning one note add no test repetitions. Check the same-model condition within associated bases at one retrieval node separately. A later scanned written test record requires inspection of actual text parsing rather than an assumed image path. This exercise repairs or runs no device and decides no actual safety. Failure to retrieve a result establishes neither an unperformed test nor a definite fault; matching independent evidence is needed before reporting real functional status.
