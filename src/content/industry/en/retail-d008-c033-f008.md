# Trade and FX administration: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Foreign-exchange material handoff preserves the registered entity, receipt stage and missing evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Suppose an English email supplies documents for preliminary review only, a Chinese register concerns a related company, and a receipt note contains an application sent to a bank. Collect original wording, entity identity and matter correspondence before a person chooses meaning review or identity completion. Inspect qualifications actually passed downstream. Preliminary review establishes no completed handling. No bank acknowledgment or real transaction outcome is supplied, and a related company in a register establishes no qualification for this entity.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Foreign-exchange handoff | Proposed material | Downstream check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English email | Preliminary review only | No approval wording |
| Registered entity | Related company | Establish correspondence |
| Bank application | No credit evidence | Keep unresolved |
| Person chooses | Meaning or identity | Inspect branch connections |
| Formal handling | External process | Actual acknowledgment |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Review translated as approval needs the email wording checked. The related entity applied here needs identity and correspondence checked. A receipt application reported as credited funds needs bank evidence checked. These test meaning, subject and event stage independently of a user choice or connected nodes.

## How to verify the setup

Exercise both routes and leave missing receipts explicit. Added identity resolves correspondence alone. Preserve source wording beside the proposed bilingual answer so a reviewer can locate each limitation. Formal handling, fund confirmation and external writes require their institutions and business interfaces; an interaction does not decide them. Place entity correspondence beside the register excerpt and keep the missing bank acknowledgment separately identified.
