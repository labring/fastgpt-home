# Trade returns and claims: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Trade returns and claims, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Claim wording, translated passages and return-shipment material retain their subjects and stages. A language change must not enlarge what the source establishes about handling or responsibility. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A trade-return workflow can collect original wording, an existing translation and source identities. Proposed inputs separate a claim request from a processing response without a responsibility decision or completed return. Inspect what original and translated passages reach the next step rather than assuming built-in translation validation. Choices arrange source review or supplementation; fluent language establishes neither a business relationship nor verified terminology.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original relationship | Claim passage and existing translation | Recipient retains the same case |
| Stage distinction | Request without processing acceptance | Not translated as approved |
| Report scope | Observation separate from responsibility | Trace the actual supporting evidence |
| Choice route | Review original or obtain handling source | Connected yes branch receives material |
| Business operation | Separate return, claim or write process | Organization is not institutional acceptance |

## Three common errors

A request translated as accepted needs the original passage and actual processing response. Another transaction’s return material requires contract and goods correspondence checks. A quality observation expanded into responsibility needs the report’s scope and evidence for that judgment. These concern stage, identity and source status, none supplied by a branch.

## How to verify the setup

Retain originals, translations, term guidance and received text. Repeat after this case’s handling evidence arrives and inspect traceability to the passage. Test each connected yes branch; interaction-state recording does not resolve the dispute. Return declaration, claim submission or profile writes need separate business permission and real receipts. Multilingual organization establishes neither institutional acceptance nor returned goods.
