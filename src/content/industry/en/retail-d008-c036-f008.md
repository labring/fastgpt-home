# Multi-warehouse inventory: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Multi-warehouse inventory, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Warehouse names, inventory terms and synchronization stages travel with their source passages. A translated label does not establish availability or completed cross-system updates. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A multi-warehouse workflow can collect Chinese allocation wording, foreign-language stock text and terminology sources. Proposed material contains inventory and a synchronization request without completion feedback. Preserve location roles and source time downstream instead of converting stock held into availability. Definitions need business evidence rather than automatic synonym recognition. Choices arrange definition checks or receipts, not selection from a warehouse database.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location roles | Sending and receiving in both passages | Roles are not swapped |
| Stock definition | Held separate from allocatable | Similar wording is not equivalence |
| Synchronization stage | Sent request without completion | Inspect actual return and record |
| Definition route | Review terminology or obtain receipt | Recipient gets the corresponding source |
| External state | Separate query, transfer and writing | Interaction is not stock synchronization |

## Three common errors

Swapped sending and receiving roles require both passages and location correspondence. Stock held described as allocatable needs the source definition and scope. A sent request described as synchronized needs this actual response and business record. These concern role, meaning and execution without deriving relationships from matching labels.

## How to verify the setup

Compare originals, translations, definitions and received values. Repeat with corresponding evidence and inspect actual use. Test connected yes branches and their inputs; ending interaction does not establish synchronization. External lookup, transfers and stock writes require actual interfaces, authority and receipts. Completed workflow establishes neither matching warehouse definitions nor goods movement.
