# Peak-sale fulfillment: document chunking for multilingual work

## What the documents look like

Peak-sale warehouse and delivery scheduling turns on short-term peak capacity and the arrangement of people, vehicles and dock space. The working document set for this category is the peak-sale forecast table, staff shift rosters, courier assurance plans and daily exception reports. These materials are refreshed per campaign and per day, and they draw on historical peak-sale orders, platform campaign registrations, WMS capacity data and courier pickup capability. In cross-border operations the same assurance plan may carry Chinese and English passages side by side, so document parsing and chunking has to preserve both the original structure and a document identity that lets a retrieved fragment point back to a specific source and version.

The forecast table holds quantities, the roster holds shifts and named staff, and the assurance plan holds vehicle assignments and pickup windows. These values exist as concrete fields. When chunking separates a condition from the content it constrains, a retrieved fragment loses the basis for any judgment about it. The update rhythm of per campaign and per day matters here because a revised assurance plan and its earlier version can both sit in the same library, and a fragment that cannot be traced to a version cannot support an operational handover.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

Parsing and chunking has to keep raw data distinct from retrieval indexes. One raw data group can map to several indexes; the index field records vector identifiers, retrieval first recalls vectors, then looks up the raw data by identifier and merges, taking the highest score for that group. Segmentation should keep structure and semantics complete so that a condition, its explanation and the content it constrains stay inside one data group. Multilingual retrieval leans on semantic recall for cross-language matching, while identifiers, model numbers and proper nouns lean on keyword hits from full-text retrieval. Hybrid retrieval fuses multiple result paths through RRF, reranking applies a second ordering to candidate text, and similarity filtering takes effect only under semantic retrieval or reranking. The citation limit is expressed in tokens, and the minimum relevance value is a filter threshold rather than a final ordering rule. Retrieval itself does not perform authorization. Whether a handover material, an operation or a request carries a particular authorization state has to be checked separately.

Image paths in the documented retrieval flow are optional and depend on the input and the model capability; reranking likewise depends on a rerank model being available and enabled. A single request does not execute every path shown in the overall flow.

## Configuration and implementation checks

Hypothetical drill sample: a cross-border warehouse and delivery team prepares a peak-sale scheduling handover. The material is one courier assurance plan with mixed Chinese and English text. The source text contains three sentences: "During this peak-sale period, the daily pickup cutoff at the East China warehouse is adjusted to 22:00. The temporary staff roster is confirmed by the warehouse manager three days before the campaign. The exception report must be submitted to the supply chain duty group before 10:00 the next day." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "The pickup cutoff at the East China warehouse during the peak-sale period has been confirmed by the warehouse manager." ## Three common errors

1. Treating a successful upload as a completed retrieval update. After a material changes, the actual training and index state has to be checked; the upload action and index activation are two separate stages. The check takes the changed material and a retrieval request as input, compares whether the returned content carries the new version field, and identifies the error when old-version content is returned.

2. Treating a similarity score as a permission or entity filter. The minimum relevance value only filters low-relevance results; it does not exclude unrelated business lines or unauthorized records. The check takes a retrieval request spanning multiple subjects as input, compares whether the returned content carries fields belonging to a non-authorized subject, and identifies the error when such content appears.

3. Treating the fused hybrid result as a single vector ordering. RRF fuses multiple recall paths, and a fragment hit by several paths usually ranks higher. The check takes a query containing both an identifier and natural language as input, compares the returned order against the order from semantic retrieval alone, and identifies the error when the two orders diverge and the fusion effect is not accounted for.

## How to verify the setup

- Verification one: input the three hypothetical source sentences and the draft sentence "The pickup cutoff at the East China warehouse during the peak-sale period has been confirmed by the warehouse manager," then compare the subject of the draft against the source. The source states that the pickup cutoff adjustment applies to the East China warehouse, while the confirmation actor is the warehouse manager; these are different subjects. The draft sentence is not supported by the source. The observable condition is that the draft subject differs from the source subject, which marks the draft as pending verification.

- Verification two: input the search terms "22:00" and "pickup cutoff," then compare whether the returned fragment contains the complete conditional sentence. The source binds the time to the warehouse and the campaign period. The observable condition is that the returned fragment carries only the time while missing the warehouse and the campaign scope, which marks the segmentation as incomplete.

- Verification three: input the hypothetical source text and a roster containing Chinese only, then compare the returned identifiers for the two materials under semantic recall. The document states that cross-language understanding depends on model capability. The observable condition is that an English passage is not recalled by a Chinese query; record the actual observation without inferring a shared association result across different models.

Empty-result handling: use an explicit paper input of an empty array to check the downstream design, without claiming that a fictional rule search returns an empty set. For libraries using different vector models, first check the identifiers and exclude those that do not conform, and do not run a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls. A pending review state does not demonstrate a lack of operating permission, and signing, reaching cooperation and contract effectiveness are three distinct facts.
