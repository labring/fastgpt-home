# Tmall flagship operations: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Tmall flagship operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Authorization scope, shipping commitments and service statements remain different evidence across languages. The workflow preserves their subjects and stages for subsequent reading. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A Tmall multilingual workflow can collect product-page, brand-authorization and service passages with existing translations. Proposed input lacks delivery or handling receipts for this order, so shipping commitments do not establish performance. Preserve brand, item and applicability rather than deriving permission from a shop name. Choices arrange source review or order evidence, not automatic selection of platform records or complete rule review.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority applicability | Brand, item and original scope | Translation retains its subject boundary |
| Delivery stage | Commitment separate from actual receipt | Not rewritten as performed |
| Statement status | Service retelling separate from inspection | No direct product-fact conclusion |
| Evidence route | Review original or obtain order source | Recipient retains this order |
| Formal handling | Separate changes, service and writing | Workflow is not platform acceptance |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Lost authorization applicability needs comparison of the original and received wording. A promised date translated as delivery needs this order’s receipt evidence. Reported service statements treated as product facts need provider and inspection-source checks. These concern authority scope, execution and source status rather than certainty expressed in one language.

## How to verify the setup

Retain originals, translations, order identity and received values. Repeat with corresponding delivery or inspection and inspect actual use. Test each connected yes branch; interaction state is not publication approval. Product changes, after-sales handling, access and external writes need business procedures and receipts. Organization establishes neither platform acceptance, completed orders nor determined responsibility.
