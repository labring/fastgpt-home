# Customer-service knowledge: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Customer-service knowledge, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. FAQ wording, scripts and escalation records retain applicability after translation. A supplied answer and a completed business resolution need separate source evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A customer-service multilingual workflow can collect FAQ, product and translated passages, retaining escalation identity separately. Proposed material contains recommended wording, not this handling outcome. Inspect actual sources downstream rather than deriving resolution from fluent answers. Choices arrange original reading, product evidence or work-order checks, without automatic validation of every translation or direct access to the business database.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicability | FAQ passage and translation | Recipient retains its scope |
| Script status | Advice separate from certain service results | Inspect actual supporting evidence |
| Work-order stage | Escalation without closure record | Not translated as resolved |
| Evidence destination | Original, product or work-order source | Connected branch actually receives it |
| Formal update | Separate publication, operation and writing | Organization is not business completion |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Lost FAQ conditions need original-to-received comparisons. Suggested wording turned into a definite service result needs actual policy scope and this instance’s evidence. Escalation described as resolution needs the corresponding handling record. These concern applicability, statement status and business stage, none supplied by a choice.

## How to verify the setup

Retain originals, translations, product and work-order correspondence. Repeat with actual handling or product material and inspect its use. Test connected yes branches; recorded interaction does not establish complete rule review. FAQ updates, work-order actions and writes require separate authority and receipts. Workflow completion establishes neither publication, resolved customer problems nor approved commitments.
