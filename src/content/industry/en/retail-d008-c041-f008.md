# Marketplace promotions: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Marketplace promotions, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Promotion registration, approval scope and stock-lock wording remain separate. Multilingual reading preserves submitted arrangements and the actual acceptance or reservation still needing evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A promotion-registration workflow can collect application, approval-scope and stock-lock passages with existing translations, without amounts or discount rates. Proposed sources lack current platform acceptance and actual reservation. Preserve those gaps. Choices arrange activity reading, approval or stock evidence, with actual connection checks rather than assumed marketing-interface access or applicability to every item sharing an activity name.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration stage | Submission without platform reply | Not translated as approved |
| Approval scope | Item and period applicability | Scope survives both received passages |
| Reservation state | Lock arrangement separate from actual change | Inspect execution record |
| Choice handoff | Obtain approval or stock evidence | Branch receives this activity’s source |
| Formal handling | Separate submission, writing and publication | Interaction is not campaign execution |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Submission translated as acceptance needs this processing response. Lost approved items or periods require comparison of both passages to restore scope. A planned lock described as actual inventory change needs the corresponding execution record. These concern platform stage, applicability and business state rather than certainty expressed by translated words.

## How to verify the setup

Retain activity, item, originals, translations and received text. Repeat after approval or stock evidence arrives and inspect its actual use. Recorded waiting is not promotion approval. Submission, stock writes, pricing handling or publication require actual business connections, permission and receipts. Multilingual organization establishes neither platform acceptance, reserved goods nor executed campaigns.
