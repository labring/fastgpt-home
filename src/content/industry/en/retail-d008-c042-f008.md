# Product listing reviews: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Product listing reviews, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Titles, assets and inspection passages retain their original scope across languages. A selected term does not establish a product claim or replace the actual requirements supplied for review. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A product-title workflow can collect original wording, an existing translation and item-specific inspection, checking correspondence without legal conclusions or prohibited-word rules. Proposed material lacks complete applicable requirements. Pass that gap downstream rather than treating rewording as approved promotion. Choices arrange original reading, product evidence or responsible-party scope checks without automatic advertising approval or certification inferred from asset names.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multilingual workflow check | Proposed material | Actual handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expression strength | Conditional original and translation | No expanded definite performance |
| Inspection scope | Item and report sample | Other-item report not substituted |
| Requirement gap | Word list lacks complete applicability | Not treated as approval |
| Evidence route | Review original or obtain source | Recipient retains the same subject |
| Formal release | Separate changes, assets and writing | Organization is not compliance determination |

## Three common errors

Conditional wording translated as definite performance needs supporting evidence and its limitation. Another item’s report applied to this title needs item and sample-scope checks. A word list treated as compliance approval needs actual applicable requirements and approval records. These concern expression, evidence identity and business judgment rather than a branch selection.

## How to verify the setup

Retain originals, translations, versions and received values. Repeat with corresponding product or requirement material and trace each sentence. Test each connected yes branch; interaction state is not completed review. Title changes, publication and writes need separate authority and receipts. Organization explains source transfer rather than established performance, platform acceptance or satisfied legal requirements.
