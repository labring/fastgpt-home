# Marketplace category access: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Marketplace category access, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Distinguish document collection, entity identity and category admission while preserving missing-item markers. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A hypothetical category-document inventory records receipt of the business entity’s identity papers, with a brand authorization page still to be supplied. It states that the table is for collecting material. The English counterpart retains the distinction between received and awaiting submission. A historical checklist covers another category. An entry that changes documents collected into category approved crosses from preparation to a decision. Entity identity, brand authorization and category scope need their own corresponding sources. Their association with one company does not make the documents interchangeable. No actual admission requirement or legal rule is specified here; the exercise follows the boundaries of the supplied inventory.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Category-language check | Inventory information | Specific action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt scope | Entity papers received | Confirm only the listed material’s arrival. |
| Missing item | Brand authorization page absent | Retain its pending state and subject. |
| Category scope | Historical table for another category | Inspect original scope and actual matter. |
| Document purpose | Material-collection table | Do not turn it into an admission outcome. |
| Additional sources | Authorization and current receipt | Check brand/entity scope and category decision separately. |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Embedding model` | Knowledge bases associated with one search node must use the same embedding model. |
| `Original data and indexes` | One original data block can have multiple indexes. |
| `Index content` | Index wording should be complete and focused. |
| `Image indexes` | Where image retrieval is used, inspect descriptions or image vectors. |
| `Version and result check` | After a change, reuse questions to compare originals. |

## Three common errors

- If receipt of identity papers becomes brand authorization in an entry, inspect the material name, authorization subject and missing-page marker. Identify the absent corresponding page.

- If another category’s checklist supports approval here, inspect category identity and actual processing evidence. A shared entity establishes no shared scope.

- If a collection table becomes an admission decision in the answer, compare its stated purpose, actual citation and decision receipt to locate the source of that conclusion.

## How to verify the setup

Ask in both languages which items arrived, which remain missing and whether a decision covers this category. Inspect the index targets and cited sentences. When authorization arrives, check brand, entity and scope; when a decision receipt arrives, check its category and matter separately. Additional language entries do not supply absent pages or perform a review. For bases associated within one search node, inspect actual embedding-model consistency before examining semantic and document identity. This example offers no legal assessment or assurance of platform qualification.
