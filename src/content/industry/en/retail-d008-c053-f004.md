# Channel receivables: multilingual work with vector indexes

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Channel receivables, this page examines FastGPT vector indexes. Separate a statement being read, its figures being accepted and payment actually received. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the vector indexes step

A hypothetical channel-account email says the counterparty has read the current statement, with discrepancies still to be checked and a payment-arrangement reply pending. An internal follow-up note supplies no receipt of funds. An English entry changing statement read into balance agreed would promote reading to acceptance. Summarizing the email as paid would add receipt of payment as well. The statement, discrepancy review, proposed arrangements and bank receipt need distinct evidence even when they concern the same customer. No real balance, amount or payment date is supplied, and no contractual payment duty is assessed.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Account translation check | Recorded wording | Inspection action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read versus agreed | Current statement has been read | Retain unresolved discrepancies. |
| Payment plan | Arrangement reply pending | Add neither a promise nor receipt of funds. |
| Payment event | No receipt supplied | Find actual evidence for this matter. |
| Speaker | Internal follow-up versus counterparty email | Check authorship before attributing commitments. |
| New document | Confirmation, reply or receipt | Update conclusions separately for the same matter. |

## Three common errors

- If reading the statement becomes acceptance of its balance in an entry, inspect the email verb, unresolved discrepancies and actual confirmation to locate the added agreement.

- If an awaited payment-arrangement reply becomes funds received, compare the correspondence with a receipt of payment. Plans do not establish the event.

- If an internal follow-up note becomes the customer’s promise, inspect authorship and attributed speaker. Internal remarks cannot make a commitment for the counterparty.

## How to verify the setup

Ask in both languages what the counterparty read, whether discrepancies were settled, who made a payment statement and whether receipt evidence exists. Follow each original. When a confirmation, reply or receipt arrives, check customer and matter identity before updating only its supported conclusion. Adding indexes reconciles no account, collects no debt and writes no ledger. Check actual embedding consistency within the associating search node. The translated terms agreed and paid must point to their respective supporting records; retrieval similarity cannot bridge business stages.
