# Short-video affiliate sales: multilingual work with model connections

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Short-video affiliate sales, this page examines FastGPT model connections. Preserve script suggestions, product mapping and commission conditions without inventing earnings. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the model connections step

Suppose a video script suggests demonstrating a feature, a linked-product list maps to another specification, commission conditions await confirmation, and platform feedback requests clarification. A multilingual connection test should preserve each subject and state. Suggested uses establish no product guarantee; a commission plan is no earned revenue. This paper exercise contains no real link, rate, order count or platform finding. Answers are limited to sourced comparison and missing information.

## Three common errors

- If suggested wording is omitted and translation guarantees an outcome, inspect the original sentence, actual input and modality. Affirmative wording cannot supply missing conditions.

- If another linked specification becomes the scripted product, compare product identity and specification. Similar translated names do not establish correct mapping.

- If conditional commission becomes received income, inspect conditions, orders and settlement evidence. Translation creates no performed settlement.

## How to verify the setup

Keep bilingual passages fixed while inspecting the script subject, then adding product mapping and commission conditions. Repeat questions about currently supportable statements and retain adopted original/translated sentences. Compare models using the same material. Link access and video analysis need separate tests. For structured returns, check mapping and state evidence before parsing. Actual product linking, accounting and platform replies require authority; this exercise publishes no video and configures no commission.
