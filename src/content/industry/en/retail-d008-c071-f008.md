# Cross-border settlements: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Retain statement perspective, date roles and amendment links without inferring spendable funds from translation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

This exercise invents an English platform statement, a Chinese merchant review and an amendment note. English wording identifies amounts payable by the platform; Chinese text lists platform-collected service items separately. No bank credit record is supplied. Statement period and bank-processing date remain unmapped, and the amendment identifies no superseded draft. Collect original perspectives and document relationships, then choose date mapping or amendment-source review. Payables establish no credited funds, date roles cannot be exchanged, and proposed corrections cancel no earlier draft. No actual amount, exchange rate or funding advice is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Bilingual settlement links | Invented restriction | Source inspection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform payable | Direction toward merchant | Not credited funds |
| Collected charge | Different fund direction | No automatic netting |
| Date roles | Period versus bank event | Do not exchange field meaning |
| Amendment note | Original link absent | Recency is no replacement |
| Accounting | No execution record | Separate authority/evidence |

| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |

## Three common errors

- If platform payables and platform-collected charges merge into one fund direction, compare payer, recipient and original rows. Do not net documents or calculate a balance merely because both concern settlement.

- If statement period becomes actual bank-credit date, inspect field-event meaning and bank evidence. Parseable date formats prove no fund timing.

- If an amendment note becomes a replacement draft, inspect original identity, affected rows and explicit adoption evidence. Upload recency determines no replacement.

## How to verify the setup

Retain issuer, date role and unresolved links on both routes, comparing submitted and actual received content without invented external field names. Bank evidence changes its event alone without accepting an amendment; amendment correspondence executes no funds. Translation should trace to the same source row, leaving unsupported differences open. Formal reconciliation, charges and accounting writes need business authority and records. The workflow performs no offsets or currency exchange and determines no withdrawable amount.
