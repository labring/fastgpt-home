# Creator sales campaigns: document chunking for multilingual work

## What the documents look like

Creator sales campaigns rest on external creator fulfillment and commission settlement, which separates this category from brand-run livestreaming. The working materials are creator cooperation agreements, commission setup sheets, sample shipment sheets, and livestream performance reports. In cross-border work these materials commonly hold Chinese and English text side by side, and a single agreement may carry a Chinese body with English attachments.

Document parsing and chunking has to split agreements, commission sheets, shipment sheets, and performance reports along their own structures so that clauses, amounts, sample details, and session figures each become retrievable units. Materials change on a per-session and weekly rhythm, and parsing and indexing status needs rechecking whenever material changes.

Three category facts shape this step. Commission terms tie a rate to specific sessions, so a clause and its applicable session must stay together. Sample shipment sheets bind specific items to a specific creator and a specific broadcast, so item lines and their usage restrictions belong in one block. Performance reports carry session figures and settlement bases, so turnover and return amounts need to remain readable as one settlement context. When any of these is split apart, a retrieved fragment cannot point back to the full condition that governs it.

## Constraints at the document chunking step

Segmentation completeness directly determines retrieval quality. If a commission rate and its applicable session, or a sample item and its corresponding creator, or a session figure and its settlement basis, land in different data blocks, matching one block still fails to recover the complete condition. One group of source data can map to multiple indexes, and this multi-vector mapping balances content length against semantic richness. Semantic retrieval supports cross-language understanding, so a Chinese question can match an English knowledge point, but precision depends on model training quality, keywords, and sentence completeness. Queries for numbers, models, and proper nouns suit full-text retrieval or hybrid retrieval. Hybrid retrieval merges multiple result paths through RRF, and a rerank model reorders candidate text and returns a score between 0 and 1. The reference cap is measured in tokens. The minimum relevance value takes effect only under semantic retrieval or result reranking. Both are filtering and trimming settings and carry no permission or entity isolation role.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a brand business contact and a creator agent discuss one cross-border session. The source text reads: "The commission rate under this agreement is settled at fifteen percent of the actual turnover of the session, and the applicable session is the session of 8 March 2025." "The three samples listed on the sample shipment sheet are for display in that livestream only and may not be resold." "The livestream performance report shows the turnover and return amount of that session, and settlement uses the net amount after returns as the base." The draft sentence to be judged is: "The commission has been settled in full based on turnover." Comparing that sentence with the source: the source states the settlement rate, the applicable session, and the net-amount base. It does not state that settlement is complete, and it does not state a settled amount. The finding is unsupported, and settlement evidence plus a net-amount calculation record would be required.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration or check item | Hypothetical starting point for this exercise | Reason and comparison object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search mode | Hybrid retrieval | Materials carry Chinese and English clauses plus identifiers, so semantic and keyword coverage both matter; compared with semantic retrieval alone for recall breadth |
| Reference cap | Hypothetical starting point of 3000 tokens | Agreement clauses and performance data blocks run long; compared with a 1500-token starting point for whether conditional sentences are truncated |
| Minimum relevance | Hypothetical starting point of 0.4 | Filters low-relevance fragments; compared with a 0.2 starting point for false recall against missed recall |
| Question optimization | Enabled for multi-turn follow-up scenarios | Coreference resolution in continuous dialogue; compared with the disabled state for completion effect and added latency |
| Result reranking | Enabled when candidates are numerous | Rerank scores support filtering; compared with vector scores alone for ordering stability |
| Segmentation check | Split along clause, amount, and session boundaries | Compared with fixed-length splitting for whether conditions and constraints stay in one block |

## Three common errors

1. Treating a successful upload as retrieval already updated. After material changes, the actual training and indexing status needs checking; the upload action and index effectiveness are two separate matters.
2. Treating minimum relevance as permission or entity filtering. That value is a relevance threshold; it does not exclude unrelated business or unauthorized records, and retrieval results still need manual review against the business scope.
3. Associating knowledge bases across different vector models. Libraries under different vector models cannot be associated together; identifiers should be checked first and non-matching ones excluded, with actual observations recorded rather than runtime outcomes inferred.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the agreement source text from the hypothetical sample together with the draft sentence "The commission has been settled in full based on turnover," then compare whether the source states settlement completion and a settled amount. The source states neither, so the finding is unsupported and settlement evidence would be required.
2. Input "the three samples listed on the sample shipment sheet" and "may not be resold," then compare whether the restriction stays in the same block as the sample item after chunking. If the restriction is split away, matching the sample item cannot recover the limiting condition.
3. Input the Chinese question "what base is the commission for that session settled on," then compare whether the retrieved content includes the phrase "net amount after returns." If retrieval carries only turnover without the net-amount base, segmentation or indexing has not covered that condition, and block boundaries plus index generation status need checking. For an empty result, verify the downstream design against a clearly stated paper input; no claim is made that a fictional rule must return an empty result.
