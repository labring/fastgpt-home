# Creator commission settlement: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Creator commission settlement, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Inspect charge direction, revision state and issuer in bilingual creator statements without calculating payables. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

An invented bilingual packet contains a platform-issued charge note, a creator request to revise a statement and an agency forwarding note. English wording describes a charge collected by the platform; the Chinese draft omits direction. The revision request lacks recipient acceptance. Collect each issuer, recipient and passage, then choose charge-direction review or revision mapping. A forwarding agency is not the issuer, and requested amendments establish no effective replacement statement. No actual currency, rate or amount is supplied; translation calculates neither payables nor deductions.

## Three common errors

- If a platform-collected charge becomes commission paid by the platform, compare payer and recipient in the original and downstream explanation. Similar fee columns cannot merge directions.

- If a creator revision request becomes a replacement statement, inspect its purpose, original statement identity and explicit adoption evidence. Receipt of comments does not update a version.

- If an agency forwarding note becomes agency-issued confirmation, compare authorship with forwarding and locate the platform or creator’s actual position.

## How to verify the setup

Review direction first, then follow the revision route using the same packet. Compare submitted and received authors, verbs and version relationships. A new statement needs identification of what it replaces and which rows were accepted; receiving a file establishes no settled account. Keep missing direction unresolved rather than guessing meanings from signs or translated labels. Actual accounting corrections, fund handling and external dispatch need business authority and receipts. The workflow hands over traceable bilingual text without performing those operations.
