# Import and export permits: multilingual work with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For multilingual work in Import and export permits, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Preserve holder identity and issuance-versus-validity fields when routing bilingual permit text. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

This exercise invents an English certificate excerpt and a Chinese handling note. The excerpt labels holder, issued and valid until without actual names or dates. The note says an agent submits documents while correspondence between applicant and holder remains unresolved. Goods wording covers listed models only. Collect passages by document and field, then choose date-meaning review or entity correspondence. A submitting agent does not automatically become the holder. Issuance and validity expiry are different events. This page determines neither permit category nor the validity of an actual document.

## Three common errors

- If holder becomes the submitting agent in Chinese downstream input, compare the holder field, agency note and received entity to locate the substituted role.

- If valid until becomes application-receipt time, compare field meanings and adopted wording across the invented documents. Parseable dates cannot exchange event labels.

- If the model restriction disappears from the goods passage downstream, inspect input excerpts and citation scope. A shared certificate title cannot extend coverage to other products.

## How to verify the setup

On both routes retain document identity, original field and Chinese interpretation. Ask who is named as holder, which field identifies expiry and which model is covered. Added entity mapping resolves that relationship alone; dates and approval still need their own originals. Compare submitted and received content for translation or field loss without inventing interface field names or official document translations. Formal submission, validity review and external writes require responsible business procedures and authority. Language routing issues no certificate and confirms no qualification.
