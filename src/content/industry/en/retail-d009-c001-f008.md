# B2B marketplaces: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in B2B marketplaces, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. B2B handover separates contact identity, interest and formal authority from transaction commitment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Suppose a platform lead names an inquiry contact, the buyer remains in evaluation and a successor is assigned supplier-document organization. Collect matter, entities and original wording, then choose buyer correspondence or successor-access review. The node records interaction and passes information without supplying a buyer organization directory or generating account access from a name. No real transaction or payment is supplied.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| B2B transaction handover | Proposed material | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry contact | Authority unverified | Real authorization |
| Buyer evaluation | Not confirmed purchasing | Preserve interest |
| Document assignment | Open work | Not account access |
| Person chooses | Mapping or permission | Downstream qualification |
| Formal transaction | Separate confirmation | Actual acceptance evidence |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

A contact reported as an authorized signatory needs actual authority checked. Evaluation interest reported as confirmed purchasing needs buyer wording checked. Document assignment reported as permission to change transactions needs platform access checked. These concern representation, transaction stage and operation boundary independently of continued workflow.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect entities and qualifications received on both routes, keeping unverified representation beside contact material. Added buyer identity resolves correspondence alone while signing and platform operations remain separate. Formal offer acceptance, transaction confirmation and business writes require independent authority, interfaces and receipts. Handover orchestration organizes open tasks without accepting commercial conditions for another party or implying agreement because a person has taken over the work. Separate contact names from representation authority, keeping buyer evaluation wording beside the matter and leaving signing permission open after document organization finishes.
