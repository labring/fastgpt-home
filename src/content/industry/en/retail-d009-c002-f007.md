# MCN operations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

MCN handover should first establish which conversation concerns which creator and cooperation material. Proposed input distinguishes contract reading, schedule checks and an account-operation request, while lacking an actual account receipt. Use chatId alongside source identities to locate the target conversation. Its existence does not establish transferred publishing authority, and mentioning an account does not establish complete retention of its operational records.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Object | Proposed handover material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation | Creator, question and chatId | Other entry-point records are not mixed |
| Source version | Present contract and schedule | Earlier cooperation scope is not carried forward |
| Runtime detail | detail requested under the contract | Inspect actual responseData |
| Operation gap | No publishing receipt supplied | Obtain business evidence without guessing fields |
| Business handover | Permission and retention arrangements | Log existence is not formal transfer |

## Three common errors

Combining conversations from different entry points may carry an earlier cooperation scope into a new task. Inspect conversation identity, the question and contract version. If returned data contains no account-operation field, the log cannot prove publication. Compare actual fields with the handover checklist and obtain a business record separately. Where runtime detail is required, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData rather than assuming it was returned. If mentioning a contract in the log becomes complete handover retention, inspect whether its passage, version and this schedule can actually be retrieved. A document name in conversation does not establish retention of its text.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the target conversation, source version, returned content and absent account receipt, identifying the next person’s required checks. Inspect testing and production identities, history and responses separately, without filling a missing test identity from another conversation. Permission confirmation, retention and compliance auditing need the complete business arrangement. Conversation evidence does not establish cooperation approval, account handover or publication.
