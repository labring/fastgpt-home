# MCN operations: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Creator-management handover separates training attendance, account scheduling and handling tasks. Submitted records do not establish settled cooperation authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed handover says a creator attended training, account scheduling awaits personal confirmation and a violation form was forwarded. Collect passages with the relevant account. A person may obtain a scheduling reply or inspect forwarding progress. Attendance does not grant account access, and a schedule does not establish acceptance. Preserve pending and forwarded status downstream without merging different accounts because they share a creator name.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Creator handover workflow | Proposed passage | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training attendance | Attended only | Not account authority |
| Account schedule | Personal confirmation pending | Actual reply |
| Handling form | Forwarded | Not resolved |
| Account identity | Separate accounts per creator | Receiving subject |
| Subsequent business | Actions and signing separately checked | Nodes do not grant authority |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

Training promoted to authority needs the actual authorization relationship. Proposed scheduling promoted to acceptance needs this person’s reply. Forwarding promoted to closure needs the actual handling outcome. These inspect authority, the other party’s position and execution stage independently of workflow completion.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect account subjects and passages received on scheduling and progress paths. Added personal confirmation changes only its scheduling scope while missing handling remains pending. A reply for a different account cannot confirm this proposed slot. Account actions, cooperation signing and formal case handling require separate authority and receipts. A recorded choice and continued execution establish neither granted access nor completion of every cooperation matter.
