# MCN operations: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in MCN operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer access by creator account and unresolved task, verifying incoming-agent availability and former-agent revocation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists creator accounts, current schedules and matters awaiting supplementary material, alongside excluded accounts. Inspect the incoming identity’s access to relevant contracts, training and notice versions, retaining account correspondence. Historical schedules without current handling originals do not establish readiness. Retain denial and handover references to inspect resource mapping. A shared agent title grants no agency-wide creator access; the responsible owner confirms each transfer boundary.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover-permission check | Creator account and active matter | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account scope | Separate included and excluded creators | Owner-confirmed resource boundary |
| Current originals | Contract, training and notice versions | Incoming reads and denials |
| Former agent | Schedules and attachments requiring revocation | Full responses for the old account |
| External identity | Inspect platform and agency interfaces separately | No synchronized disablement inferred |
| Subsequent matters | Handover handles no account or publication | Retain external revocation and business approval |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If historical schedules establish current readiness for a new agent, compare handover references and notice versions with actual account resources. Old material supplies no current handling basis.

- If the former agent still reads revoked notices or contracts, retain full response and account scope. Signatures or archiving establish no actual denial.

- If membership change becomes disabled platform or agency interfaces, inspect external accounts, sessions and operations independently. Internal roles neither terminate external access automatically nor publish schedules.

## How to verify the setup

Use the incoming identity for required current-task reads and former or unrelated identities for negative tests on the same source. Record originals and operations. If a permitted answer imports training from an excluded creator, inspect input and version instead of widening grants. Handover performs no platform handling or schedule publication, and technical operations are not business approval of terms. Retain unresolved resources and external revocation actions without labeling every account matter completed after an agent changes roles.
