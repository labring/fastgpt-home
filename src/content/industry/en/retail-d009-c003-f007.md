# Shopify fulfillment: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A Shopify operations handover can list order conversations separately from fulfillment changes. Proposed material says a conversation reviewed label and refund sources but supplies no storefront operation receipt. chatId helps locate its question and answer evidence without establishing a write to Shopify. Mentioning an order also does not establish that every order field or complete payment record was retained in the conversation log.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Object | Proposed handover material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry identity | Support conversation separate from testing | Test answer is not official handling |
| Conversation scope | Review of label and refund material | No storefront write presumed |
| Detail response | Actual return after requesting detail | Missing responseData evidence remains explicit |
| Payment evidence | Refund discussed without receipt documentation | Obtain the corresponding business record |
| Transfer requirements | Separate reconciliation, access and retention design | Log is not audit completion |

## Three common errors

Mixing support and internal-test entry points can turn a test answer into apparent official handling. Inspect chatId, input history and actual responses. Another failure requests runtime detail while responseData lacks the required material. Check the returned content and contract rather than inferring completeness from detail. Proof of refund receipt needs a corresponding business document; discussing a refund does not establish payment-side processing.

## How to verify the setup

Retain entry point, conversation identity, source version and missing fulfillment receipt so the receiving person can inspect the connection between answer and operation. Testing identities may differ; interpret empty values in their mode instead of borrowing another conversation’s identity. Order reconciliation, access transfer and retention need the complete arrangement. A log establishes neither a completed order audit, executed refund nor revoked access.
