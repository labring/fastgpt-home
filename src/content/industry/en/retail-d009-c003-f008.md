# Shopify fulfillment: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Shopify fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Independent-store fulfillment handover separates labels, physical handoff and refund outcomes. Template descriptions do not establish delivered or refunded orders. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed order notes leave the address unresolved. A label exists, refund material shows only an application and a shipping template does not identify this order’s applicability. Collect order relationships and statuses. A person can obtain address evidence or inspect refund handling. Label creation does not establish carrier handoff, and template existence does not establish suitable shipping terms. Preserve gaps without authenticating customers or replacing real addresses through this handover.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Store fulfillment handover | Proposed source status | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order address | Unresolved | Keep origin |
| Created label | Not carrier handoff | Actual order record |
| Shipping template | Applicability unspecified | Conditions and relationship |
| Refund application | Execution absent | Not returned funds |
| System writes | Address, dispatch and refund separately connected | Handover is not backend change |

## Three common errors

Label promoted to dispatch needs the actual carrier transfer. Template promoted to applicability needs its conditions and order relationship. Refund application promoted to payment execution needs the corresponding handling record. These inspect logistics, coverage and fund stage independently of ended handover.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect whether address and refund paths receive the same order. Added carrier evidence changes logistics alone while refunds remain unresolved. Keep the origin of a spoken address instead of assuming verification. Formal address amendments, dispatch and refunds need separate authority and receipts. Continued nodes establish neither delivery nor returned funds and do not establish a write to the store backend.
