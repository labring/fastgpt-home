# Temu semi-managed sellers: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Temu semi-managed sellers, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A semi-managed operations handover can begin with separate identities for product-declaration discussions and warehouse queries. Proposed material supplies declaration wording and an inventory-sheet version while lacking a warehouse adjustment receipt. Locate the target conversation with chatId and inspect actual inputs and returns. Mentioning a warehouse does not establish retention of all warehouse changes, and interpreting declaration text is not platform approval.

## Three common errors

Attaching a conversation based on an earlier inventory sheet to the current warehouse handover can distort the receiving person’s assessment. Inspect its original question, source date and returned subject. If responseData contains no warehouse operation record, it cannot prove a transfer; obtain corresponding WMS evidence separately. Request and inspect runtime detail under the official contract without equating a visible conversation history with complete business audit information. If an empty test identity is interpreted as failed overseas-warehouse handling, inspect call mode, actual input and business return. Test-field differences do not diagnose WMS operations or justify borrowing another conversation’s identity as a handling record.

## How to verify the setup

Retain warehouse, conversation identity, supplied sources and absent operation evidence, distinguishing completed explanation from warehouse processing. Check testing and production inputs, history and returns separately instead of merging empty test identities. Access removal, stock reconciliation and retention need an actual business arrangement. This log inspection does not establish accepted declarations, warehouse adjustment or completed procurement preparation.
