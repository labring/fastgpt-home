# Temu semi-managed sellers: citation tracing for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Semi-managed sellers on Temu maintain overseas inventory and arrange shipment themselves, rather than only supplying goods to the platform. Operations handover therefore touches four recurring materials: product declaration forms, overseas inventory sheets, price declarations, and platform fulfillment orders. These materials update daily or per order, so the version of a source at the moment of handover becomes a central point of checking.

Citation tracing in this setting means enabling the receiving operator to return to the specific source text and confirm the subject, object, status, and validity scope that a given operational request relies on. The materials involved come from Temu Seller Center, overseas warehouse WMS records, logistics tracking, and procurement cost sheets. The entities typically involved are cross-border suppliers, Temu seller operations staff, and overseas warehouse service providers. Errors in this area can lead to products being unavailable for sale, shipment delays, or platform penalties.

The four document types carry different kinds of information. A product declaration form records submission status and batch scope. An overseas inventory sheet records a quantity snapshot at a stated date. A price declaration records a price and its applicable period. A platform fulfillment order records an order-level obligation. Citation tracing must keep these identities separate, because a statement drawn from one material cannot be used to assert a fact that belongs to another.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

The knowledge base search node outputs a citation content array whose length may be zero. An empty array means the output path still runs; it does not indicate that the workflow has errored or stopped. Knowledge bases selected together must share the same vector model. The minimum relevance setting takes effect only under semantic retrieval or when result reranking is used; it is a filtering threshold and does not perform permission or entity isolation. Retrieval itself does not constitute authorization.

A hypothetical paper exercise sample is set out here for checking purposes only. In a handover packet for a semi-managed store operation, the product declaration form states: "Subject: Store Operations Group A; Object: SKU-1001 declaration information; Status is submitted, pending platform review; Validity scope: March 2025 batch." The overseas inventory sheet states: "SKU-1001 available stock at the North America warehouse: 40 units; data date: 12 March 2025." The draft sentence to be assessed reads: "Based on the above declaration form, SKU-1001 has obtained platform sales permission, and the receiving operator may list and ship it directly."

The draft sentence is not supported by the cited materials. The declaration form records a submission action, not a review outcome. The inventory sheet records a quantity as of a stated date, not a shipping authorization. Neither platform review conclusion nor shipping authorization appears in the supplied text, so both remain open items requiring separate verification.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| --- | --- | --- |
| Associated knowledge bases | Select only bases whose vector model identifiers match | Compare the vector model identifier of each candidate base against the base holding the handover materials; exclude any that differ |
| Search mode | Semantic retrieval | Compared with full-text retrieval and hybrid retrieval, the question in this exercise is a natural-language description |
| Minimum relevance | Hypothetical starting value 0.5 for this exercise | Observed together with the citation limit to see how much is recalled; this is not an official default |
| Citation limit | Hypothetical starting value 2000 tokens for this exercise | Compared against candidate segment lengths so a single long segment does not consume the whole citation allowance |
| Citation content output | Carried as an array, empty permitted | Compared against the input format of the downstream answer step |
| Material currency check | Treat the March 2025 batch as the current version | Compare against earlier document versions to identify source differences |

The search node returns citation content only. It does not verify operational permissions, and it does not write to any downstream system. Any business verification, approval, database write, or external synchronization requires its own stated downstream implementation. No scheduled trigger or hidden control is assumed here.

## Three common errors

1. Reading "submitted, pending platform review" as an already-granted sales permission. That field records completion of the submission action; the review conclusion is not stated in the material.
2. Treating a retrieval hit as a basis for authorization. The search node returns citation content and does not check operational permissions.
3. Associating knowledge bases that use different vector models. Sharing the same vector model is a precondition for joint association; bases whose identifiers do not match should be excluded first.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the declaration form text above together with the draft sentence, and compare whether "submitted, pending platform review" and "has obtained platform sales permission" are mutually exclusive values of the same field. They point to different facts, so the draft sentence is not supported.
2. Enter the inventory sheet text above and compare the data date of 12 March 2025 against the period covered by the handover request. The available quantity is a snapshot as of that date; whether it can support shipment requires separate verification.
3. Enter the empty citation array `[]` and observe whether the downstream answer step still runs. This checks the design for carrying an empty result; it does not mean any retrieval rule necessarily returns an empty array.

All three judgments preserve the evidence gaps. The platform review conclusion and the shipping authorization are not stated in the materials and remain pending verification.
