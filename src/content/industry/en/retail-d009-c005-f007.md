# TikTok Shop US operations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in TikTok Shop US operations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A TikTok Shop handover can separate listing discussion, return-material interpretation and proposed account operations instead of treating them as completed handling. Proposed evidence includes the conversation and cited sources without listing or after-sales approval receipts. Use chatId alongside business material to identify the exchange. A listing mentioned in discussion does not establish a page change or automatic retention of every product-inspection source.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Object | Proposed handover material | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business separation | Page discussion, return reading and account action | Not merged into completed handling |
| Shop mismatch | Similar product exchange from another shop | Inspect entry and source before removal |
| Runtime evidence | Answer present without call details | Inspect responseData under the contract |
| Approval gap | Recommendation without platform approval record | Listing permission is not presumed |
| Transfer scope | Separate operation, access and retention evidence | Conversation review is not formal handover |

## Three common errors

A similar product conversation from another shop may enter the handover incorrectly. Inspect entry point, product material and conversation identity before removing the mismatch. If only answer text is returned, request runtime detail under the official contract and inspect responseData rather than treating an answer as complete call evidence. A recommendation to publish cannot establish platform approval or execution when the relevant business record is absent.

## How to verify the setup

Retain conversation identity, source origins, actual return coverage and missing account-operation evidence for follow-up. Inspect testing and production histories and identities separately; an absent test identity alone does not establish a failed platform operation. Account access, retention and compliance auditing need the complete arrangement. Reviewing the conversation establishes neither listing, refund approval nor formal completion of handover.
