# General export declarations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Compare the material received with the sources used in the answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

An export handover can locate the conversation about the contract, invoice and packing list before listing absent business receipts. Proposed material records an explanation of commodity wording without a declaration-submission or counterparty-confirmation receipt. chatId helps associate the exchange with its sources, without establishing complete external synchronization. Contract text appearing in a log proves neither an approved amendment nor customs acceptance.

## Three common errors

Combining different transactions for the same customer may attach packing wording to the wrong contract. Inspect conversation identity, the original question and source correspondence, retaining unresolved links. If runtime data contains no declaration-operation field, an answer saying that material was checked does not establish submission. Request detail and inspect responseData coverage, obtaining absent receipts from the operator rather than inferring state from unreturned fields. If conversation-record time becomes declaration-submission time, inspect the actual declaration receipt and its business event separately. Exchange order does not establish acceptance progress; a log supports the conversation chronology it actually records.

## How to verify the setup

Hand over the target conversation, input sources, returned explanation and missing business receipts separately so the receiving person can trace the passages. Inspect testing and production history, inputs and returns in their own contexts without mixing identities. External confirmation, retention and compliance auditing need a complete arrangement. This conversation review establishes only available exchange evidence; amendments, customs acceptance and payment handling require their own actual records.
