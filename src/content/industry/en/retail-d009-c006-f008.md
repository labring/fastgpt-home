# General export declarations: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in General export declarations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Export-declaration handover preserves the relationship among contracts, packing material and declaration drafts. Prepared files do not establish completed handling. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A proposed contract names an assembled set, packing wording lists accessories separately and declaration text awaits name correspondence. Collect passages and the business event. A person can obtain goods mapping or inspect handling status. Supply no customs requirements, amounts or declaration figures. Different names need actual correspondence evidence, while complete preparation establishes neither submission, receipt nor a final outcome.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Export handover workflow | Proposed source subject | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract goods | Assembled set name | Actual accessory relationship |
| Packing wording | Accessories separately listed | No automatic merging |
| Declaration text | Unresolved draft | Not actual submission |
| Handling record | Current outcome missing | Exclude another batch |
| Workflow update | Mapping is not outcome | No inferred departure |

## Three common errors

Forced name mapping needs evidence linking accessories to contracted goods. Draft promoted to submission needs the actual submission record. Another batch’s result applied here requires business ownership. These inspect goods relationships, action stage and subject without filling gaps through workflow receipt.

## How to verify the setup

Compare passages received by correspondence and handling paths. Added naming explanations change relationships only; handling still needs this event’s records. A receipt acknowledging documents cannot become a completed-declaration result. Formal declaration, logistics and external readback require actual authority and receipts in separately connected steps. Continued nodes establish neither a customs outcome nor departed goods.
