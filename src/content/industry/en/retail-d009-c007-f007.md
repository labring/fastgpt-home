# General import declarations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in General import declarations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Import-handover logs separate agent submission, customs handling and account authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose handover material says an agent received documents, omits customs handling state and assigns a successor document organization only. Locate chatId and the current import matter, then inspect agency role, goods correspondence and history actually passed as input. Check detail and responseData under the official contract. Returned text guarantees no full retention of customs attachments, and existing records establish no account authority.

## Three common errors

Agent receipt reported as customs release needs institutional roles and the real acknowledgment checked. Document work reported as declaration access needs permission evidence checked. An older batch state applied here needs goods identity checked. These concern handling level, authority and ownership. No real customs decision or duty amount is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect identity, history and return separately for debug and production requests, identifying the responsible party beside each passage. Missing handling results remain unreviewed without proving an absent event. Added release evidence updates that batch stage alone while account access stays separate. Declaration execution and business writes require authorized processes; logs perform none of those operations. Identify the receiving agent, carry goods identity through current sources and keep customs acknowledgment separate from missing account permission.
