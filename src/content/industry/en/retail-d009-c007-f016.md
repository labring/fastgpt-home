# General import declarations: interactive forms for operations handover

## What the documents look like

General trade import declaration work centres on dutiable value, freight and insurance charges, and tax payment. The main data sources are the International Trade Single Window, the customs tariff, the freight forwarder's arrival notice, and foreign exchange payment documents. The documents handled per declaration are the import declaration form, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and contract. These materials update per declaration.

For operations handover, the authorization state of each material determines whether a handover request can continue. An arrival notice states that goods have arrived; it does not show that a customs declaration engagement is in force. Contract signature, contract effectiveness, and authorization to perform declaration operations are three separate facts. The form input node collects the values a user fills in and passes them to later nodes after submission; it does not provide a business database. The user selection node judges the user's choice and executes a branch; it is not a personnel directory or a business data selector. Both nodes enter an interactive state when triggered, record the workflow state, wait for the interaction, and continue downward after the interaction completes.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Handover management must separate behaviour a node already has, fields that can actually be configured, manual comparison actions, and business actions that require another system. The form input node collects field values; field validation, business approval, write-back, and batch operations are implemented in an external system or a later node as needed. The user selection node judges a choice and executes a branch; it does not grant permissions. The interactive state records the workflow state and waits for the interaction. The documentation does not describe submission-count control, automatic judgement, or snapshot guarantees, so these are not written into configuration. Retrieval results or form results do not constitute authorization to execute. A similarity or relevance setting does not perform subject isolation or permission filtering. Libraries built on different vector models cannot be associated together; check the identifier and exclude non-matching libraries rather than running a cross-model retrieval to compare missed recalls.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a handover request between an importer and a customs broker. The material text reads: "The bill of lading names Company A as consignee; the commercial invoice is issued by Company B; the contract is signed by Company A and Company C, dated 1 March 2024, and the contract states no effectiveness condition." The sentence to be assessed is: "Company A has completed customs declaration engagement authorization to Company B and may execute handover of import declaration form completion." This sample is used only for paper comparison on this page and does not refer to any real enterprise or real business.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the bill of lading consignee as the subject of customs declaration engagement authorization. The input is the sample text "the bill of lading names Company A as consignee"; the comparison object is "has completed customs declaration engagement authorization to Company B" in the sentence to be assessed; the observable result is that the bill of lading states only a consignee and states no engagement authorization, so the assessment is unsupported.
2. Treating contract signature as contract effectiveness. The input is "the contract is signed by Company A and Company C, dated 1 March 2024, and the contract states no effectiveness condition"; the comparison object is "authorization is in force"; the observable result is that signature and effectiveness are different facts, the effectiveness status is absent, and the item is recorded as pending verification.
3. Treating a user selection branch as a grant of permission. The input is the option "continue handover"; the comparison object is whether authorization evidence exists; the observable result is that the node only executes a branch and produces no authorization, which still requires external implementation.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample text "the bill of lading names Company A as consignee; the commercial invoice is issued by Company B; the contract is signed by Company A and Company C"; the comparison object is the sentence to be assessed, "Company A has completed customs declaration engagement authorization to Company B"; the observable result is that the three subjects differ and no authorization statement appears, so the assessment does not hold and the gap is retained.
2. Input the sample statement that the contract "states no effectiveness condition"; the comparison object is "authorization is in force"; the observable result is that the effectiveness condition is absent, which counts as unsupported and cannot be recorded as a denial or a conflict.
3. Input a paper empty result []; the comparison object is whether the downstream design handles an empty result and a missing field separately; the observable result is that the downstream records a gap and does not claim that a fabricated rule retrieval necessarily returns empty. Retrieval checks record only observations and failure branches and do not guarantee a first hit or full recall.
