# Amazon FBA preparation: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Amazon FBA preparation, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. FBA preparation handover checks inbound plans, carton labels and actual stock separately. Created plans establish neither warehouse receipt nor approved compensation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed handover contains an inbound plan, a carton-label version awaiting review, an old-stock report and a compensation application. Collect source batches and states. A person can inspect labels or obtain receiving evidence without assuming account-warehouse integration. Plans establish arrangements, not actual receipt. Existing stock does not prove this shipment arrived, and an application does not establish accepted compensation.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| FBA preparation handover | Proposed material | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound plan | Arrangement only | Not warehouse receipt |
| Carton-label version | Plan relationship unresolved | Compare actual received content |
| Stock origin | Earlier goods | Not current arrival |
| Compensation application | Outcome absent | Not approved |
| Business writes | Replacement, dispatch and updates separate | Nodes do not establish availability |

## Three common errors

Wrong label version needs the source plan beside received content. Old stock promoted to inbound receipt needs this batch’s receiving evidence. Compensation application promoted to approval needs the actual reply. These inspect version ownership, goods stage and the other party’s position independently of document completeness.

## How to verify the setup

Compare plan subjects on label and receiving paths. Added batch receipt changes that stage alone while compensation still needs its result. Acknowledged application receipt remains pending handling. Label replacement, dispatch, inventory writes and compensation processing need separate authority and receipts. Continued nodes establish neither available goods nor promised platform outcomes or compensation effects.
