# JD POP seller operations: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Store-service handover separates shipping terms, repair records and dispute handling. Repair during a warranty period alone establishes neither conflicting promises nor a resolved dispute. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed handover contains shipping terms, a repair-intake record and an open dispute, without this order’s performance evidence. Collect the terms and order relationship. A person can obtain receipt evidence or inspect repair progress. Intake establishes acceptance for handling, not completed repair; a contractual delivery date is not actual receipt. Supply no platform rules or default warranty terms, and infer no promise conflict merely from repair during a warranty period.

## Three common errors

Terms promoted to performance need actual handoff and receipt. Repair intake promoted to restoration needs a real handling outcome. A repair record promoted to dispute closure needs this customer’s actual confirmation. These inspect fulfillment, repair and the parties’ dispute positions without substituting evidence.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual input on receipt and repair paths. Added repair outcomes change that stage while missing customer confirmation remains pending. An arrangement alone does not change execution state. Formal service actions, case closure and notices need separate authority and receipts. Continued nodes establish neither fulfilled promises nor a breach or liability finding inferred from repair records.
