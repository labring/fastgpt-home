# JD POP seller operations: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in JD POP seller operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer shop sources by unresolved disputes and warranty tasks, testing both operators’ resource scopes and operations. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists one dispute awaiting evidence and one archived warranty case; the incoming operator handles the former. Identify needed address, timing and service sources, then inspect actual reading and operations. Access only to the archived case does not establish availability of current evidence. Retain the handover list, order actually read and denials to inspect correspondence. A shop job title does not automatically configure access to every order and service source.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover-permission check | Order and service scope | Actual acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence task | Separate current dispute and archived warranty | Order and denial for the new account |
| Required resource | Address, timing and service records | Individual reads and permitted operations |
| Former operator | Addresses and attachments requiring revocation | Actual responses beyond a signature |
| Original platform identity | Test merchant backend and interface separately | No synchronization inferred from team changes |
| Business boundary | Operation right is not approval to amend terms | Retain owner confirmations and unresolved items |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If archived warranty access establishes current evidence readiness, inspect open order, address and timing sources beside denials and handover references. A shop title gives no universal rights.

- If former operators read buyer addresses or dispute appendices, retain full responses and configuration. Signatures, archiving and classifications establish no denial; inspect external backends too.

- If correct grants still reuse an old warranty case, inspect history, order and version. Wider access does not correct crossed tasks, and operations authorize no amended terms or liability decision.

## How to verify the setup

Record necessary reads for the incoming identity on the current dispute, expected denials for the former identity and negative tests on unrelated orders. If grants are correct but the answer adopts an old warranty case, inspect history and source version; widening rights does not resolve crossed business tasks. Operation rights also do not confer business approval to amend terms or adjudicate liability. Retain unresolved resources and owner confirmations without presenting a completed meeting as completed dispute, warranty or ticket handling.
