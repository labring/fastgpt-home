# Franchise store supervision: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer supervisor access by open regional corrections, inspecting former rights and external visit accounts. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists stores awaiting recheck, completed visits and another region’s contracts. The incoming supervisor follows the first. Inspect access to current notices, photo descriptions and applicable standards, retaining store and period. Titles or region names create no system role. Historical reading establishes no complete current evidence. The owner confirms necessary scope instead of treating regional handover as universal access to owners’ historical information and contracts.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Regional-handover check | Store and period scope | Actual verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current stores | Separate pending recheck and closed visits | Versions read by new account |
| Regional boundary | Other contracts do not transfer automatically | Owner-confirmed resources |
| Former supervisor | Revoke contracts and photo sources | Full responses and denials |
| External identity | Inspect supervision-app accounts and interfaces | No invalidation inferred from reassignment |
| Record retention | Kept evidence grants no former access | Source gaps and responsibility |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If completed visits establish recheck readiness, compare store list, period and actual returns. Earlier photos supply no current confirmation.

- If former supervisors read revoked contracts or photo wording, retain full response and configuration. Signatures and archiving establish no denied identity.

- If internal reassignment invalidates external supervision accounts by assumption, inspect accounts and operations independently. Reading supplies no business authority to amend contracts or accept corrections.

## How to verify the setup

Use the incoming identity for open-store sources and former or excluded-region identities for negative tests, recording reads and operations. Correct scope with another store’s standards needs input and version checks rather than wider grants. Evidence retention differs from former-personnel visibility: kept records confer no continued access. List resource gaps and external revocations. Handover performs no visit, acceptance or publication of results; assigned responsibility still requires actual records and confirmation.
