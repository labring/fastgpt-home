# Franchise store supervision: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Franchise store supervision, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. The receiving person needs the store, applicable display standard and the stage reached after a rectification notice. The handover keeps finding, notification and later verification tied to their own evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A supervision handover can collect this store’s finding, applicable standard passage and rectification gap. Proposed material contains a finding and notice without this follow-up check. The form passes text downstream rather than verifying the site. Human choices arrange source collection or organization, not selection from a staff directory. Actual recipients and scope depend on connections and business design, and interaction completion does not establish accepted handover.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store identity | Visit passage and present store | Other-store sources not mixed |
| Standard scope | Submitted version applicability | Scope survives received wording |
| Notice stage | Sent without reception evidence | Not rewritten as received |
| Human destination | Obtain follow-up or organize text | Connected recipient gets the intended source |
| Formal transfer | Separate confirmation and closure | Form is not a business receipt |

## Three common errors

Mixed store identity: Remove another store’s visit passage after inspecting store identity and source. Lost standard applicability: If a standard’s version scope is lost, compare submitted and received wording and restore applicability before reading. Sending mistaken for reception: If a sent notice becomes received material, obtain this store’s actual reception evidence; notice existence cannot supply receipt. These inspect subject, scope and handover stage separately rather than deriving all three from one choice.

## How to verify the setup

Trace question, entered values, branch destination and received text. Repeat after this follow-up arrives and inspect actual use of the addition. The node records interaction state, waits and continues without automatically writing to a supervision system. Validation, formal transfer and rectification closure need their actual downstream or external arrangements and receipts. Form completion establishes neither corrected premises nor confirmation by the receiving person.
