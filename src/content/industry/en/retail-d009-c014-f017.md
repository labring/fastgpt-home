# Convenience fresh-food orders: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Convenience-store fresh food is ordered by the store at short shelf-life and high frequency, and it is not handled as a concession-revenue-share counter. The materials that matter at handover are the fresh-food order form, the damage and loss form, the near-expiry handling sheet, and the temperature record. Each maps to a different subject: order quantity, loss registration, near-expiry disposal, and cold-chain state. These materials move between the store manager, the fresh-food buyer, and the regional supervisor. At handover, a material may be complete, awaiting review, or merely proposed; these are three different states and the paperwork must be read field by field. Update frequency is daily or per shift. This page does not rely on batch synchronization or automatic triggers, and none should be inferred.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Around an upgrade, whether the order form submission, the loss-form confirmation, the near-expiry sheet execution, and the temperature record entry sit in the same authorization state cannot be inferred from a retrieval result or from a form having been filled in. A similarity score does not act as a permission filter, and a form collecting values does not mean the business step was approved. Retrieval is not authorization. The following is a hypothetical paper exercise.

Material text one (hypothetical): Shift of 10 March 2026, store A manager submits a fresh-food order form, item rice ball, quantity 40 units, status awaiting regional supervisor review. Material text two (hypothetical): Same day, same store, the damage and loss form records 6 rice balls scrapped, reason expiry, status registered. Material text three (hypothetical): Same day, the temperature record shows the refrigerated cabinet at 4 degrees Celsius, recorded by the on-duty clerk, with no countersignature attached.

Draft sentence to be judged (hypothetical): The regional supervisor has approved the above order form, and handover is complete.

Judging point: the status in material text one is awaiting review. No approving party, approval time, or approval action appears. The approval asserted in the draft sentence is not supported by the given materials, and the claim that handover is complete likewise lacks a basis. The draft sentence and the source do not form mutually exclusive values in the same field, so this can only be recorded as pending verification.

## Three common errors

1. Treating an order form awaiting review as approved. Input is material text one; the compared field is status; the observable result is that the status remains awaiting review and no approval record appears.
2. Treating a loss form marked registered as executed disposal. Input is material text two; the compared fields are registration and execution; the observable result is that only a registered status is present and no execution confirmation appears.
3. Treating a filled-in temperature record as a completed cold-chain countersignature. Input is material text three; the compared fields are recorder and countersignature; the observable result is that the record exists and the countersignature is absent.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input material text one and the draft sentence; compare the status field and the approving party. If the status is awaiting review and no approving party appears, the draft sentence is not supported.
2. Input material text two; compare the registered status with the execution status. If only registered is visible, the disposal execution remains pending verification.
3. Input material text three; compare the recorder with the countersignature. If the signature is absent, the countersignature state remains pending verification.

All three items are limited to the paper materials above. No test, deployment, approval, refund, or other business action is claimed to have occurred. Where a document field is not provided, it remains unknown.
