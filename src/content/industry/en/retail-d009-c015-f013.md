# Letter-of-credit documents: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Letter-of-credit documents, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer access around open presentation tasks, testing incoming, former and bank-query identities separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists supplementary documents, an earlier credit and a recent bank question. The incoming person follows missing passages. A filename establishes neither new-account access nor removal of the former person’s rights. List resources by case, version and operation. Historical comments may support tracing without continued access across all customers. Bank-query identities and team identities may be managed separately, so signatures cannot replace actual access results on either side.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Presentation handover access | Open case material | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task scope | Supplement list and bank question | Actual new-account passages |
| Version check | Earlier credit cannot replace amendment | Return and source dates |
| Former staff | Customer documents and internal comments | Same-source reads and denials |
| External identity | Separate bank-query management | Account and interface revocation |
| Business closure | Handover does not submit supplements | Handling record and owner confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If an earlier credit establishes complete supplementary sources, compare the task list, amendment relationship and actual return. Check receipt of the recent bank question instead of relying on one case name.

- If former staff still read revoked customer documents or internal comments, retain full responses and grant scope for the same source. Archiving proves no denial.

- If an internal role change ends bank-query access by assumption, inspect external accounts and interface grants. Retention and continued reading differ; revocation confirms no completed presentation.

## How to verify the setup

Have the incoming account read sources needed for this open case, then test the same restricted material with former staff and unrelated-customer identities. Record accounts, passages and operations. Correct access with old questions needs actual-input and date checks rather than broader rights. List external revocation, retained history and missing bank replies separately. Handover submits no supplement, requests no amendment and accepts no bank comment. Closing the task needs this case’s handling evidence and owner confirmation.
