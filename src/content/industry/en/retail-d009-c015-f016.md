# Letter-of-credit documents: interactive forms for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Letter-of-credit document examination serves the paying conditions of the issuing bank. The working focus is consistency across the presented set rather than tax matching. A single transaction can involve the credit itself, a commercial invoice, a bill of lading, an insurance policy and a draft. Source material arrives through the issuing bank's advice, UCP600 clauses, the freight forwarder's bill of lading and the bank's presentation records. A mismatch across the set can lead to refusal, charges or delayed collection of foreign exchange. Document examiners, bank international settlement staff and exporters hand over materials and operating rights between stages. Values collected in a form and the business authorization state of a presentation are separate matters and must be tracked separately.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The form input node is a user-interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interactive state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution waits until the user completes the interaction. After the user fills the required information and submits, the node collects the submitted form values and passes them to later nodes. The user selection node is also a user-interaction node with the same interactive-state behavior; when the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the corresponding branch. Neither node carries its own business database function. Field validation, business approval, write-back and batch operations require an external system or later nodes.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. Under a letter of credit, the issuing bank's advice states: "Latest shipment date 30 June 2025; presentation period 21 days after shipment." The freight forwarder's bill of lading states: "Date of shipment 25 June 2025." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "The presentation deadline for this transaction is 16 July 2025." This sentence must be compared against the credit text: 25 June 2025 plus 21 days yields 16 July 2025, which matches the draft sentence. If the bill of lading does not state a shipment date, the presentation deadline cannot be determined from the available material.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Credit number | Form input, required | Collects the credit number from the issuing bank's advice and passes it to later nodes after submission |
| Commercial invoice amount | Form input, required | Collects the invoice amount value for later comparison against credit terms |
| Bill of lading shipment date | Form input, required | Collects the shipment date stated on the bill of lading for the presentation-deadline calculation |
| Presentation deadline result | Form input, required | Collects the date calculated from the shipment date and the presentation period |
| Discrepancy handling choice | User selection | Executes the "accept" or "return" branch according to the user's choice; does not indicate that approval is complete |
| Handover confirmation | User selection | Executes the "continue" or "pause" branch according to the user's choice; does not indicate that authorization has taken effect |

## Three common errors

1. Treating a submitted form value as a business authorization result. The form input node only collects the information that was filled in and passes it to later nodes; it does not carry its own business database function. Entering a calculated presentation deadline does not mean the bank has accepted the presentation.
2. Treating a user selection branch as an executed approval. The user selection node executes a branch according to the user's choice; it is not a personnel directory or a business data selector. Choosing the "accept" branch does not mean a discrepancy has been formally approved.
3. Treating retrieval or a form as a permission-isolation mechanism. Semantic recall and relevance scores are not responsible for permission or entity isolation. A same-vector-model requirement for a retrieval library cannot be extended to mean that business-registration sites or other external systems must also use the same model.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the credit text "Latest shipment date 30 June 2025; presentation period 21 days after shipment" and the bill of lading text "Date of shipment 25 June 2025" from the hypothetical exercise sample, then compare the draft sentence "The presentation deadline for this transaction is 16 July 2025." The condition is met when the date in the draft sentence agrees with the result of adding 21 days to the shipment date. If the bill of lading does not state a shipment date, record this as an evidence gap and do not judge the draft sentence to hold.
2. Input the draft sentence to be assessed from the hypothetical exercise sample and compare it against the presentation-period clause in the credit text. The condition is met when the date in the draft sentence falls within the presentation period stated in the credit and agrees with the result of adding 21 days to the shipment date. If the credit does not state a presentation period, record this as unsupported.
3. Input the handover request from the hypothetical exercise sample and compare it against the branch execution conditions of the user selection node. The condition is met when the workflow continues downward after the "continue" branch is selected and stops to wait after the "pause" branch is selected. Do not claim that approval or authorization has actually been completed.
