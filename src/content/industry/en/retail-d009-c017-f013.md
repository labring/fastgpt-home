# Franchise fee settlement: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer resources around open reconciliation tasks, separating voucher retention, former-staff revocation and external finance identities. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists current unresolved reconciliation, earlier payment terms and received invoicing text. The incoming finance reader needs this store’s missing sources. List period, store and voucher identity before confirming new-account reads and operations. Retaining historical vouchers neither preserves the former operator’s rights nor supplies current funds evidence to a successor. Payment arrangements and cash stages differ. A handover marked organized cannot become balanced accounts or mutual confirmation without the corresponding actual records.

## Configuration and implementation checks

## Three common errors

- If an earlier plan supplies complete current funds evidence, compare the open list, period and actual returns. Confirm receipt of current vouchers rather than borrowing historical values or dates.

- If the former operator reads revoked vouchers or other-store violation material, retain full same-source answers and grant scope. Archiving and signatures establish no denial.

- If an internal transfer ends bank or finance-service identities by assumption, inspect external accounts and interfaces. Revocation handles no funds and confirms no responsibility for open tasks.

## How to verify the setup

Test current tasks with the incoming account, then request the same restricted sources as former staff and an unrelated franchise. Record passages and operations. Correct accounts with an older period need input and source-ownership checks rather than expanded rights. Verify external revocation separately from historical retention and retest the original scope after new receipts. Handover creates no vouchers, confirms no collection and closes no reconciliation. Tasks lacking current evidence remain unresolved rather than completed through a changed personnel list.
