# Franchise fee settlement: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Franchise fee settlement, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. The contract party and each document’s status travel with the passages. An invoice, a deduction notice and a bank record answer different questions for the person receiving settlement material. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A settlement handover can collect contracting party, plans and requested evidence without amounts. Proposed material contains invoice and notice wording, not bank execution records. Pass that status intact without automatically reconciling finance. Choices can arrange contract reading or evidence collection, but are not accounting approval or authority to deduct funds and alter a ledger merely by continuing.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State distinction | Invoice separate from bank record | Issuance is not receipt |
| Party mismatch | Another store’s ledger enters the contract | Inspect origin and remove it |
| Voucher route | Source request passes a notice heading | Inspect connection and received content |
| Authority scope | Continue reading or obtain material | Choice is not deduction permission |
| Formal confirmation | Separate reconciliation, handling and transfer | Interaction is not a real receipt |

## Three common errors

Invoice mistaken for funds received: An issued invoice described as received funds requires separate invoice and bank evidence. Another store’s ledger: Another store’s ledger entering this contract needs party and source checks before removal. Notice heading substituted for evidence: If a voucher request receives only a notice heading, inspect connections and received values, retaining the execution gap. These concern status, subject and transfer; filled content does not establish resolved accounts.

## How to verify the setup

Retain party, source meanings, chosen destination and received text. Repeat after corresponding evidence arrives and inspect actual use. Recorded interaction state and continuation are not settlement approval. Validation, accounting handling, reconciliation and retention need downstream or external authority and actual receipts. Submission establishes neither completed funds, justified contractual handling nor acceptance of handover.
