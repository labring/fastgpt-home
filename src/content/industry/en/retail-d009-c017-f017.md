# Franchise fee settlement: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Franchise fee and deposit settlement works from financial systems, franchise contracts, bank statements, and store violation records. The daily documents are franchise fee invoices, deposit ledgers, deduction notices, and contract payment schedules. Settlement concerns money received and paid plus breach deductions; it does not handle government franchise filing. Reconciling contract payment terms, confirming deduction grounds, and issuing invoices make up most of the workload. The supplied material gives an update frequency of monthly and at each deduction. This page does not infer batch synchronization or real-time triggers from that frequency.

For operations handover, these documents serve as inputs awaiting reconciliation. A deposit ledger, a deduction notice, and a payment schedule each carry their own fields, and a field value in one document does not transfer to another. Forms collect values. Business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization each require their own downstream implementation. Retrieval is not authorization, and a similarity setting does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Operations permission and handover management must distinguish what authorization state a material, action, or request is in. Contracts, ledgers, deduction notices, and payment schedules are reconciliation inputs. A pending review status is not an approval. An offered option is not an execution. An unsigned termination is not a completed termination.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a franchise management department hands over a deposit ledger on 10 March 2026. The ledger records the object "East China Region Store A," covers the period January to February 2026, and the field "deposit balance" reads "RMB 80,000." On the same page the field "deduction status" reads "pending review." A separate franchise contract payment schedule records that the same store's January 2026 franchise fee due date is "15 January 2026," and the field "invoice issuance status" reads "not issued." The draft handover request sentence reads: "The deposit deduction for this store is complete; please confirm as handover recipient and synchronize to the financial system."

At the upgrades step, version and dependency checks come before any verification of the sample above. ## Three common errors

1. Treating "deduction status is pending review" as a completed deduction. Pending review is not approval, and the draft handover sentence's "deduction is complete" has no support in the ledger. The input is the ledger field; the comparison is against the request sentence; the observable result is an unsupported claim.
2. Treating a retrieval result or a filled form as authorized execution. Retrieval does not constitute authorization, a form only collects values, and synchronizing to the financial system requires a separately connected downstream implementation. The input is the retrieval or form output; the comparison is against the required authorization state; the observable result is a missing downstream action.
3. Merging "invoice issuance status: not issued" with the payment schedule due date into one fact. The due date and the issuance status are different fields and cannot be inferred from each other. The input is both fields; the comparison is field identity; the observable result is a recorded "not issued" as pending verification.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the ledger text above and the draft handover request sentence. Compare the "deduction status" field value against the request sentence wording. The ledger reads "pending review" and the request says "deduction is complete," so the two do not agree and the claim is judged unsupported. This is a paper comparison, not a test that was run.
2. Input the payment schedule text. Compare the "invoice issuance status" field against the due date field. The two are recorded separately and do not form a contradiction; the "not issued" value is recorded as pending verification.
3. Input an empty-result carry-over sample: assume retrieval returns `[]`. Check how the downstream design handles an empty result. Do not claim that a fictional rule necessarily returns `[]`.
