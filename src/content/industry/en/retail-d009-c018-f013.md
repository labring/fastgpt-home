# Regional dealer stock: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer resources around open replenishment and collection, inspecting historical customer sources, ordering identities and actual versions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists this dealer’s unconfirmed replenishment, unresolved collection and an earlier stock appendix. The incoming person needs current task sources. List dealer, period and actual resources, with owner-confirmed new-account scope rather than universal access from regional sales duties. Historical retention supports tracing without continuing former rights or supplying present stock. Collection arrangements differ from funds, and requests differ from accepted orders. A handover marked transferred establishes none of those processing outcomes through its status alone.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| --- | --- | --- |
| Open list | Unconfirmed replenishment and collection | New-account source scope |
| Historical version | Old stock and plans supply no current evidence | Dates and return |
| Former staff | Revoke customer and regional sources | Same-source answer and denial |
| External identity | Brand ordering and dealer ERP | Separate account and interface checks |
| Closure condition | Transfer does not process orders or funds | Actual records and owner confirmation |

## Three common errors

- If old stock and a collection plan complete current tasks, compare the open list, dates and actual returns. Keep missing current sources visible rather than borrowing stages from one customer.

- If former staff read revoked customer sources or another region’s internal credit, retain full same-source answers and scope. Archiving and contact changes establish no denial.

- If changed customer ownership terminates brand-ordering or dealer-ERP identities by assumption, inspect external accounts and interfaces separately. Revocation processes neither orders nor funds.

## How to verify the setup

Test this customer’s open sources with the incoming account, then request the same restricted material as former staff and an unrelated region. Retain passages, accounts and operations. Older collection conditions need this history and input version checked rather than additional grants. Document external revocation separately from retained history and retest the original scope after current evidence arrives. Handover submits no replenishment, confirms no receipt and reserves no stock. Closure needs actual records and owner confirmation; unsupported tasks remain unresolved.
