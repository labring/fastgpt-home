# Regional dealer stock: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Regional dealer stock, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Demand, inventory definitions and collection arrangements retain their original meanings after handover. The next reader needs the dealer, applicable period and the execution sources still outstanding. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A dealer-source handover can collect replenishment demand, inventory definitions and a collection plan while identifying absent execution records. Proposed material lacks dispatch and bank receipts. The form passes entered text without converting plans into handling. Choices arrange source collection or explanation; actual recipients depend on connections. Shared products across dealers establish neither identical definitions nor interchangeable records.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dealer relationship | Present party and shared product sources | Other-party stock not substituted |
| Plan and execution | Collection plan distinct from receipt | Not rewritten as received funds |
| Definition handoff | Inventory meaning and applicability | Heading does not replace the explanation |
| Evidence receipt | New dispatch or collection passage | Actually reaches the recipient |
| Business transfer | Separate ordering, writing and confirmation | Submission is not formal reconciliation |

## Three common errors

Wrong dealer stock: Another dealer’s stock requires party and item correspondence checks. Collection plan mistaken for receipt: A collection plan described as received funds needs its source status and actual receipt evidence. Lost inventory definitions: If inventory definitions disappear and the recipient gets only an aggregate heading, compare submission with received text and restore applicability. These inspect subject, execution and definitions separately rather than deriving sources from a selection.

## How to verify the setup

Retain dealer, period, source status, branch destination and received content. Repeat after corresponding dispatch or collection material arrives and inspect use of the addition. Interaction-state recording and continuation are not procurement registration. Validation, ordering, stock writes and formal transfer require actual business authority and receipts. An ended form establishes neither verified metrics, changed stock nor reconciled finance.
