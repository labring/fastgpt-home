# On-demand retail fulfillment: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in On-demand retail fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Order timing, warehouse picking and rider collection remain distinct during transfer. Qualifications attached to an unresolved statement must survive so the receiving person can see exactly what remains unknown. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

An on-demand handover can collect this order’s question, store material and rider progress with their source times. Proposed inputs contain picking and shortage wording without final delivery or handling evidence. The form passes them downstream without establishing buyer receipt from a completed shift handover. Human choices arrange sources or explanation rather than automatic order selection; actual receipt and responsibility need the business procedure.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time mapping | Earlier stock separate from this order’s moment | No projected ordering state |
| Stage distinction | Picking separate from rider collection | Warehouse record is not collection |
| Unresolved status | Submission carries its qualification | Recipient does not get a definite finding |
| Added source | Delivery material for this order | Actually reaches downstream reading |
| Formal handling | Separate transfer, substitution and closure | Choice is not responsibility or execution |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Wrong stock moment: Earlier stock treated as ordering-time availability requires time and order correspondence checks. Picking mistaken for rider collection: Picking described as rider collection needs separate picking and actual handover evidence for this order. Lost unresolved qualification: If an unresolved-status note disappears and the recipient gets a definite conclusion, compare original submission and restore its status. These inspect time, stage and statement character rather than deriving verification from continuation.

## How to verify the setup

Retain order, store, source scope, branch destination and received text. Repeat after this order’s delivery evidence arrives and inspect its actual use. The node records interaction, waits and continues without automatic refunds or stock changes. Validation, formal transfer, substitution and closure need actual downstream or external authority and receipts. An ended form establishes neither executed handling nor determined responsibility.
