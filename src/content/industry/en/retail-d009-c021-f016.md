# Brand member operations: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Brand member operations, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. This example uses general activity wording and examines applicability, permission origins and notification receipt. Public materials contain no actual member records, while business evidence retains its separate status. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A member-operations handover can use general rules, hypothetical demand and missing authorization wording without real personal records. The form collects and passes material rather than establishing contact from a campaign plan. Choices arrange rule reading or source collection; actual roles and permission need business design. The selection node is neither a member database nor staff directory, and interaction state does not complete authority checks.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activity scope | Activity and period covered by rules | Other-activity rules not reused |
| Authority origin | Tag name without permission evidence | Gap remains explicit |
| Notification stage | Proposed sending separate from receipt | Plan does not become received |
| Public material | General text without personal records | Received scope matches this example |
| Formal business | Separate sending, retention and transfer | Interaction is not passed authority review |

| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Wrong activity rules: Another activity’s rules need identity and applicability checks. Tag mistaken for authority: A tag name treated as sending permission needs actual authorization evidence and a retained gap. Planned notification mistaken for receipt: Planned notification received as completed contact needs separate sending and reception records. These inspect subject, authority origin and execution stage, none supplied by a choice or entered value.

## How to verify the setup

Retain public general material, activity, selected destination and received text. Repeat after corresponding guidance arrives and inspect use of the addition. The node waits and continues without automatically sending or tagging. Validation, formal transfer, sending and retention need downstream or external business authority and real receipts. Form completion establishes neither contact effects nor passed personal-data review.
