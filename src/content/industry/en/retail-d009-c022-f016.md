# Omnichannel stock transfers: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Omnichannel stock transfers, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Sending and receiving roles, balances and physical receipt need their own source relationships. The handover preserves those distinctions before a person explains the state of this particular transfer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A stock-transfer handover can collect item, sending and receiving locations and inventory scope while explicitly lacking receipt evidence. Proposed sources contain a request, snapshot and transport wording. Preserve demand, balance, movement and receipt downstream rather than treating transferred documents as synchronized stock. Choices arrange evidence or explanation, not a business database; actual acceptance, approval and writes depend on implemented business connections.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location roles | Sending and receiving passages | Not swapped at reception |
| Quantity meaning | Balance separate from movement | Balance does not supply movement quantity |
| Receipt stage | Transport present; reception absent | Logistics document is not receipt |
| Evidence handoff | Corresponding new passage | Reaches the selected next step |
| Formal transfer | Separate creation, writing and reconciliation | Form is not stock confirmation |

## Three common errors

Confused sending and receiving: If sending becomes receiving, restore roles against the location passages. Balance mistaken for movement: If a snapshot balance becomes this movement’s quantity, inspect actual movement records and timing separately. Transport mistaken for receipt: If a logistics document becomes store receipt, obtain this transfer’s reception evidence. These inspect roles, quantity meaning and stage without assembling completion from one item name or choice.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, locations, source time, branch destination and received passages. Repeat with corresponding evidence and inspect actual use. The node records state, waits and continues without external acceptance. Validation, transfer creation, stock writes, reconciliation and formal handover need separate authority and real receipts. Form completion establishes neither moved goods nor matched records and stock.
