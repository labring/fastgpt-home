# Brand repair services: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Brand repair services, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Item and work-order identity, inspection content and exchange status travel separately. The receiving step needs actual passages and the outstanding handling evidence before explaining this service case. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

A repair handover form can collect item and work order, reported symptoms, warranty passage and missing inspection text without promoting claims to defect findings. Proposed inputs lack inspection details and exchange approval. Pass those gaps to reading. Choices arrange evidence or source review rather than automatically selecting after-sales database records. Actual handling authority and responsibility need business design; ended interaction is not accepted repair or replacement.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover check | Proposed input | Receiving evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item and work order | Another item’s inspection introduced | Check correspondence and remove it |
| Content gap | Inspection supplied as heading only | Obtain the relevant passage |
| Inflated state | Exchange application becomes approval | Inspect status and approval source |
| Added material | This order’s inspection or handling text | Actually reaches downstream reading |
| Formal handling | Separate repair, exchange and transfer | Submission is not responsibility determination |

## Three common errors

Wrong inspection subject: Another item’s inspection requires item and work-order correspondence before removal. Inspection heading mistaken for findings: An inspection heading treated as findings needs the actual received passage and missing content. Exchange application mistaken for approval: An exchange application rewritten as approval requires the original status and corresponding approval evidence. These inspect subject, evidence content and business stage, none supplied by complete-looking titles or continuing interaction.

## How to verify the setup

Retain work-order identity, source status, chosen path and received text. Repeat after this inspection or handling evidence arrives and inspect its handoff. Waiting and continuation do not automatically write to an after-sales system. Validation, repair, exchange, closure and formal transfer need separate downstream or external authority and receipts. Submission establishes neither responsibility, financial handling nor personnel permissions.
