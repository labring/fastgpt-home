# Brand outlet operations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Brand outlet operations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Outlet handover needs the actual approval scope, list meaning and label execution evidence. A conversation can locate the explanation while leaving business outcomes separately evidenced. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

An outlet handover can locate the exchange explaining slow-moving stock, discount approval and labels without amounts or rates. Proposed discussion lacks current execution receipts. Use chatId with item, activity and version, without assuming applicability from mentioned approval. Inspect actual returned fields instead of claiming complete retention of approvals, stock or profit calculations. A label also does not establish cashier-system application. Historical discount material describes its earlier applicability; the receiving person still needs evidence relating this item to the present activity.

## Three common errors

An approval conversation for another activity needs question and applicability checks. Inventory-age wording described as availability needs actual input and definition tracing. A conversation treated as an applied cashier change needs this operation’s business record. These inspect subject, source meaning and execution rather than deriving them from a visible log.

## How to verify the setup

Retain conversation, source version, actual return and execution gap. Compare testing and production inputs, history and identities separately. Where runtime detail is needed, inspect detail and responseData under the official contract without assuming completeness from a request. Pricing authority, stock changes, retention and auditing need the complete arrangement. Exchange review establishes neither effective approval, verified finance nor transferred staff permission.
