# Brand outlet operations: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Brand outlet discount operations clear out-of-season inventory and do not maintain a new-arrival full-price image. The working documents are discount approvals, slow-moving lists, price tags and gross-margin worksheets, drawing on ERP inventory age, POS sales, outlet mall campaigns and historical discounts. At the deployment and upgrade step of operations-permission and handover management, three of these materials carry the state that decides what a handover may do. A discount approval carries approval status. A slow-moving list carries an SKU scope. A price tag carries a price effective range. Together they determine which actions are already authorized at handover and which remain pending review. The supplied brief gives weekly and monthly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization or real-time triggers from them.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Before and after an upgrade, the authorization state of the same discount approvals, slow-moving lists, price tags and gross-margin worksheets can differ. The deployment side checks dependencies and backups against the official deployment and upgrade steps, and recovery capability is verified in the operator's own environment. `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` is the AI Proxy service root address and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` is the administrator token; both are required in App and Admin, and a missing item causes startup validation to fail. Retrieval and form collection produce values and do not constitute authorized execution. Approval passing, signature completion and contract effectiveness are three separate facts.

Hypothetical drill sample: a brand outlet clearance group submits a discount approval on 2 March 2026 whose text reads: "Apply a 30 percent discount clearance to 28 out-of-season SKUs, SKU-OL-031 through SKU-OL-058, valid 5 March 2026 to 20 March 2026; the approver field is blank." The accompanying slow-moving list states that all 28 SKUs have inventory age over 180 days. The handover request to be judged is: "This discount approval is executable; hand it over to the store price-tag printing step." The approver field is blank, so the current material does not establish approval for execution and the request is unsupported. Check the approval status and record separately; the blank alone establishes neither incomplete approval nor rejection.

## Three common errors

1. Reading the blank approver field in the approval as authorization. The input is the sample text, the comparison object is the approval status field, and the observable result is that the handover request is marked unsupported.
2. Treating the slow-moving list's SKU scope as the printable price-tag scope. The input is the 28 SKUs on the list, the comparison object is the authorized scope declared at the price-tag step, and the observable result is that a scope mismatch sends the price-tag step back for reconciliation.
3. Treating completion of an upgrade script as handover taking effect. The input is the upgrade script execution record, the comparison object is the actual state of the approval and price tag, and the observable result is that script completion does not change the fact of the blank approver field.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the sample text "the approver field is blank", compare the approval status field against the value "approved", and observe that the two do not match, so the handover request is judged unsupported.
2. Input the 28 SKUs from the slow-moving list and the SKU scope declared at the price-tag step, compare whether the two match item by item, and observe that a difference is recorded as a gap without judging the price tag authorized.
3. Input the actual configured values of `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` after the upgrade, compare whether both are non-empty and consistent with the server, and observe that a missing item causes startup validation to fail, requiring completion before the sample handover request is rechecked.
