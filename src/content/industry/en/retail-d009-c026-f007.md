# Brand-owned store operations: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Brand-owned store operations, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. A store conversation can explain scheduling, display and transfer material. Attendance and physical movement retain their own sources instead of being established by a recent answer. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A brand-owned store handover can identify the store, shift and sources behind a conversation. Proposed exchange contains schedule, image and transfer wording without actual attendance or receipt evidence. Use chatId with business subjects without assuming complete POS, CRM or stock synchronization into logs. An ended exchange establishes no completed arrangement. Inspect actual returned fields rather than presuming retention of missing data.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Log-handover check | Proposed record | Evidence to revisit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store origin | Other-store display exchange introduced | Inspect entry and image identity |
| Attendance stage | Schedule without actual attendance | Not rewritten as present staff |
| Movement time | Log runtime separate from business event | Inspect this transfer’s record |
| Detail scope | Contract request and actual return | Complete retention not assumed |
| Formal confirmation | Separate changes, access and shift transfer | Log is not business acceptance |

## Three common errors

Another store’s display exchange needs entry-point and photograph-origin checks. A schedule described as attendance needs original-input and actual attendance evidence. Log time treated as goods-movement time needs this operation’s business record separately. These inspect source identity, personnel execution and event timing, none supplied by a shared brand or recent conversation.

## How to verify the setup

Retain store, shift, source identity, actual return and gaps, checking testing and production histories separately. Request runtime detail under the official contract and inspect responseData without equating configuration with obtained content. Schedule changes, stock writes, access and retention need the complete arrangement. Reviewing logs establishes neither site verification, stock synchronization nor formal acceptance by the receiving shift.
