# Dealer ordering events: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Dealer ordering events, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Sample, contract and credit discussions retain dealer identity. The handover distinguishes available explanations from order acceptance and credit decisions that need their own business evidence. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

An ordering-event handover can locate discussion of this dealer, handbook and samples without commercial amounts. Proposed exchange contains sample and contract wording, not order acceptance or credit outcomes. chatId associates questions with inputs rather than establishing a transaction or complete financial-approval retention. Inspect returned fields and source versions; mentioning a document does not establish retention of its complete text.

## Three common errors

Another dealer’s exchange needs question and contractual-identity checks. Sample material described as an order needs actual ordering evidence. Credit advice treated as permission needs this instance’s formal decision. These concern subject, execution and authority, none supplied by a shared item or completed conversation.

## How to verify the setup

Retain the target exchange, source version, actual return and outstanding result. Inspect testing and production inputs, history and identity separately. For runtime detail, check detail and responseData under the official contract without inferring complete sources from a successful request. Ordering, confirmation, access transfer and retention need the complete business arrangement. Review establishes neither a purchase commitment, approved credit nor formal handover acceptance.
