# Brand livestream operations: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Brand livestream operations, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect unbroadcast content and product evidence during session handover, testing former accounts and external publication identities separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A session handover exercise lists scripts awaiting review, a revised product card and earlier comments, without actual audience data. Identify session, product and version before owner-confirmed new-account reads and maintenance. Historical scripts support tracing without continued former access or current applicability. Transferring an unbroadcast draft describes work arrangements. Given to the presenter cannot become already broadcast or approved promotion. Publication eligibility also needs separate verification rather than being transferred through an asset list or inherited from a familiar product name.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Session handover access | Current review sources | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current task | Unbroadcast script and revised card | Necessary successor passages |
| Earlier session | History supplies no current conditions | Version and actual return |
| Former operations | Revoke scripts and restricted comments | Same-source answer and denial |
| External publication | Separate platform identity and eligibility | Account and business permission |
| Broadcast outcome | Handover broadcasts, publishes and verifies nothing | Actual records and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If an earlier script completes current content, compare tasks, qualifications and actual returns. Obtain the revised card rather than borrowing versions from one product.

- If former operations staff read revoked scripts or restricted comments, retain same-source full answers and scope. Session completion and archiving establish no denial.

- If internal staffing changes terminate live-platform identity or transfer publication eligibility, inspect external accounts and business permission separately. Asset transfer publishes nothing and verifies no product claim.

## How to verify the setup

Have the successor read current session tasks, then request the same restricted material as former staff and unrelated sessions. Record passages, operations and denials. Correct access with old qualifications needs actual-input version checks rather than universal product assets. Document external publication revocation, retained history and session gaps separately, then retest the original scope after supplementation. Handover schedules no broadcast, publishes no content and verifies no transaction. Actual broadcast and outcomes need records; unresolved conditions cannot become executed operations through transferred team access.
