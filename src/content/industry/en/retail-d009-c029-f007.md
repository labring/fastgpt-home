# After-sales repairs and reships: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Repair, parts and reshipment need supported relationships to the same item. A log explains available exchanges while leaving inspection, installation and carrier collection independently evidenced. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A repair-and-reship handover can locate the target conversation for this item and work order. Proposed discussion contains applications, parts dispatch and shipment text without inspection or collection outcomes. Use chatId with work order and origins without deriving completed service from conversation completion. Inspect actual returns rather than assuming retention of inspection, repair and logistics fields or a verified defect.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Log-handover check | Proposed record | Evidence to revisit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work-order subject | Another item’s inspection exchange | Inspect source and identity |
| Repair process | Parts dispatch separate from installation | Not rewritten as repaired |
| Transport result | Shipment creation separate from collection | Obtain current actual evidence |
| Detail scope | Returned runtime fields and gaps | Complete retention not presumed |
| Formal handling | Separate repair, dispatch and retention | Review is not completed handling |

## Three common errors

Another item’s inspection exchange needs source-identity checks. Parts dispatch described as completed repair needs the actual repair process. A created reshipment treated as carrier collection needs this shipment’s evidence. These inspect subject, repair and transport stages rather than deriving them from headings or recent exchanges.

## How to verify the setup

Retain item, work order, source status, actual return and gaps. Inspect testing and production histories separately. For runtime detail, check detail and responseData under the official contract without presuming complete fields from parameters. Repair, reshipment, closure, access and retention need a complete business procedure. Reviewing logs establishes neither responsibility, delivery nor formal completion by the receiving operator.
