# After-sales repairs and reships: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in After-sales repairs and reships, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer resources around pending reshipment and carrier checks, testing former operators, warehouse and external logistics identities separately. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists created reshipment requests, parts awaiting handoff and unconfirmed customer receipt, without personal data. Relate request, warehouse and carrier sources to this item and order before necessary new-account reads and operations. Retained history preserves no former reading rights or current handoff event. Warehouse dispatch and customer receipt still have a carrier stage between them. A completed work handover cannot make that entire process finished or establish responsibility for the dispute without its separate actual evidence.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Reshipment handover access | Current open sources | Acceptance action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current task | Request, parts and unverified receipt | Item, order and passages |
| Historical distinction | Old logistics supply no current handoff | Actual return and stage |
| Former operator | Revoke customer and warehouse sources | Same-source answer and denial |
| External identity | Separate warehouse and carrier checks | Accounts, interfaces and retention |
| Closure condition | Transferred grants prove no delivery | Actual process and outcome |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If a created request completes reshipment evidence, compare open tasks, parts and actual returns for this dispatch and handoff. Earlier logistics cannot supply the current event.

- If former operators read revoked customer handling or internal warehouse text, retain full same-source answers and scope. Archiving and changed names establish no denial.

- If changed internal operators terminate warehouse and carrier accounts by assumption, inspect external identities and interfaces separately. Retention preserves no operating right; revocation confirms no customer receipt.

## How to verify the setup

Have the successor read current tasks, then request the same restricted sources as former staff and unrelated customers. Record passages, operations and denials. Correct access with a prior order’s state needs current-order and history-input checks rather than broader customer scope. List external revocation, retained history and current gaps separately, then repeat under original permissions after handoff evidence arrives. Handover creates no reshipment, dispatches nothing and confirms no delivery. Closure and responsibility require actual processes and outcomes rather than transferred grants establishing completed goods and service.
