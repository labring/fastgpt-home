# Supermarket supplier accounts: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

Supplier handover distinguishes contractual explanation, delivery acceptance and settlement confirmation. The exchange needs identifiable sources rather than inferred financial outcomes from discussed documents. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A supermarket-supplier handover can locate discussion of this contract, acceptance and reconciliation without public amounts. ## Three common errors

Another contract’s exchange needs party and source checks. Delivery acceptance described as completed fund handling needs actual settlement evidence for the period. A prepared reconciliation treated as mutual agreement needs the formal confirmation record. These concern subject, business stage and confirmation status, none established by a recent answer.

## How to verify the setup

Retain contract, supplier, source status, actual return and missing records. Inspect testing and production histories separately. Request runtime detail under the official contract and inspect responseData without assuming complete evidence from configuration. Settlement, reconciliation, access and retention need the complete arrangement. Log review establishes exchange evidence rather than completed funds, matched accounts or formal handover acceptance.
