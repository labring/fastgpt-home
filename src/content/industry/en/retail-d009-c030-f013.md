# Supermarket supplier accounts: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Supermarket supplier accounts, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer resources around open acceptance and reconciliation, preserving batch, period and former-access distinctions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists pending batch acceptance, unconfirmed current reconciliation and an earlier settlement note. Identify supplier, batch and period before necessary new-account reads and operations. Successor finance or purchasing duties open no universal resources. Historical settlement supports tracing without supplying this batch’s acceptance or current mutual confirmation, and former reading needs independent grants. A signature transfers work responsibility rather than establishing passed inspection, balanced accounts or completed funds processing. Preserve those distinctions while identifying the actual sources still missing from each task.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Settlement handover access | Current review sources | Inspection action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Batch and period | Pending acceptance and reconciliation | Necessary successor passages |
| Earlier settlement | Supplies no current conclusion | Actual return and identity |
| Former operator | Revoke contracts and internal settlement | Same-source answer and denial |
| External identity | Supplier portal and finance system | Accounts, interfaces and retention |
| Business closure | Transferred rights inspect or reconcile nothing | Actual records and confirmation |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If earlier settlement completes current acceptance and reconciliation, compare open tasks, batch, period and actual returns. Keep absent current conclusions visible instead of borrowing events from a shared supplier.

- If former staff read revoked contracts or internal settlement comments, retain full same-source answers and scope. Archiving and changed lists establish no denial.

- If internal duty handover terminates supplier-portal or finance identities by assumption, inspect external accounts and interfaces separately. Revocation inspects or pays nothing; historical retention needs separate checks.

## How to verify the setup

Have the successor read current open tasks, then request the same restricted sources as former staff and unrelated suppliers. Retain passages, operations and denials. Correct access returning an earlier batch opinion needs actual-input and source-ownership checks rather than wider inspection access. Document external revocation, current gaps and retained history separately, then retest original scope after confirmation arrives. Handover approves no supply, confirms no acceptance and closes no reconciliation. Closure needs records and owner confirmation rather than transferred grants proving completed business stages.
