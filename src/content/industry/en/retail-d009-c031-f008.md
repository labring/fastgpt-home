# Retail packaging reporting: workflow routing for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Retail packaging and single-use item reporting sits around disposable plastic product reporting and environmental supervision, which separates it from cashier membership and promotion work. The main materials come from the Ministry of Commerce reporting system, POS plastic bag sales, the procurement system and store ledgers. Everyday documents include plastic bag procurement ledgers, sales vouchers, semi-annual reports and self-discipline commitments. Two update rhythms apply: the semi-annual report follows a reporting cycle, while sales vouchers arise with each transaction. The most time-consuming steps are counting usage, checking sales vouchers and reporting recovery information.

At operations handover, these materials raise an authorization question. Who may submit the semi-annual report, who may check sales vouchers and who may sign the self-discipline commitment all need a clear authorization state at the moment of transfer. A procurement ledger entry, a sales voucher or a draft report describes a record; it does not by itself state who is permitted to act on it.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

The form input node belongs to the user interaction nodes. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution waits until the user completes the interaction. The node collects the required form information the user submits and passes it to later nodes. The user selection node also belongs to the user interaction nodes. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state and the workflow state is recorded. When the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the corresponding branch, so branch connections need to be verified one by one.

Recording the interaction state is behaviour of the interaction nodes. No separate global state-recording switch is described. Information collected through a form must be explicitly passed to a later retrieval, judgement or interface node, and output must be compared against input item by item. Order writing, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or a later process. Retrieval or a form submission does not constitute authorized execution. At operations handover, the authorization state of a material, an operation or a request cannot be replaced by a retrieval result or a form submission.

Hypothetical drill sample: a compliance role at a supermarket headquarters submits a draft semi-annual report on 1 July 2025. The material text reads: "This store's plastic bag sales volume for the first half of 2025 is 12,000 units; the procurement ledger records a procured quantity of 11,500 units; the difference of 500 units is pending verification." A second material reads: "The store ledger records a recovered quantity of 3,000 units; the recovery voucher number is HZ-2025-0301." A draft sentence to be assessed reads: "This store has completed the first-half 2025 plastic bag usage count and reported the recovery situation." Authorization boundary: the compliance role holds draft submission rights only; reporting requires approval by the headquarters compliance lead, and no approval record is provided.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Configuration item | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form input node | Interaction node | Enters an interaction state on trigger, collects required information, passes it to later nodes after submission |
| User selection node | Interaction node | Executes the corresponding branch after the user makes a choice; verify branch connections one by one |
| Interaction state check | Manual check | State recording is interaction-node behaviour; no separate global state-recording switch is described |
| Output handover check | Manual check | Pass collected information explicitly to later retrieval, judgement or interface nodes and compare item by item |
| External action separation | Implementation check | Order writing, document backfill and external status synchronization require a separate business interface or later process |

## Three common errors

1. Treating a form submission as completed authorization. In the sample, the compliance role submits a draft. If a draft sentence states that reporting has been completed on that basis, draft submission and approved reporting are conflated. The check takes the draft sentence and the authorization boundary as input, compares whether "submission" and "reporting" refer to the same actor and the same action, and observes whether the wording equates a draft submission with reporting.
2. Treating a retrieval result as a permission credential. In the sample, a sales voucher is retrieved. If the compliance role is judged to hold reporting rights on that basis, retrieval is standing in for authorization. The check takes the retrieval result and the authorization boundary as input, compares whether the retrieval action and the authorization action occupy the same field, and observes whether approval is replaced by an inference drawn from retrieval.
3. Writing a pending difference as a settled conclusion. In the sample, the sales volume of 12,000 units and the procurement quantity of 11,500 units differ by 500 units pending verification. If a draft sentence states that the procurement ledger and the sales voucher agree, the pending state is dropped. The check takes both material texts and the draft sentence as input, compares the values of the same field for the same period, and observes whether a pending-verification difference is written as agreement or as a settled conclusion.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample, "This store has completed the first-half 2025 plastic bag usage count and reported the recovery situation," and compare it against the authorization boundary in which the compliance role holds draft submission rights only, reporting requires approval, and no approval record is provided. Observe whether the draft sentence writes draft submission as completed reporting. The condition is met when the draft sentence clearly separates submission from reporting and preserves the approval-record gap.
2. Input the sales volume of 12,000 units and the procurement quantity of 11,500 units from the hypothetical sample, and compare the values of the same field for the same period across the two materials. Observe whether the difference of 500 units is written as pending verification. The condition is met when the difference keeps its pending-verification state and is not written as agreement or as a settled conclusion.
3. Input the recovered quantity of 3,000 units and the recovery voucher number HZ-2025-0301 from the hypothetical sample, and compare whether the recovery situation is described in the draft sentence as already reported. Observe the authorization boundary in which reporting requires approval and no approval record is provided. The condition is met when the description of the recovery situation keeps its pending-approval state and is not written as completed reporting.
