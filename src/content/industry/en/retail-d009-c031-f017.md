# Retail packaging reporting: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like
This category covers disposable plastic bags and single-use items reporting for supermarkets and convenience stores. It is a compliance and environmental-regulation track, separate from membership marketing or checkout promotion data. The main sources are the commerce department reporting system, POS plastic bag sales records, the procurement system, and store ledgers. Routine documents are the plastic bag procurement ledger, sales vouchers, the half-year report, and the self-discipline commitment.

Two update rhythms coexist. The half-year report and daily sales records mean the same quantity figure can originate from different periods. During a handover, the object and its valid range must be confirmed before any figure is treated as usable. The consequences of error include a deadline for corrective action, a regulatory interview, and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand yuan. Every link in the permission handover has to trace back to a specific material and an authorization boundary.

## Constraints at the upgrades step
Before and after an upgrade, the read and submit permissions for the procurement ledger, sales vouchers, the half-year report, and the self-discipline commitment can change with the version. Deployment work starts by checking the current version, the target version, and the matching upgrade notes. Current behavior is not inferred from an old configuration name.

Retrieval and forms only collect information. Business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization each need their own downstream implementation. A retrieval hit or a completed form is not an authorization execution.

Hypothetical drill sample: a supermarket headquarters compliance role prepares handover materials before a version upgrade. The ledger text reads "Store A plastic bag procurement ledger for the first half of 2026, procurement quantity 120,000 bags, registered by Li, status pending review." The sales voucher text reads "Store A plastic bag sales receipt summary for June 2026, sales quantity 118,000 bags, status archived." The proposed handover request reads "Transfer the reporting permission for Store A's first-half procurement ledger and sales voucher to the new compliance role, and authorize that role to submit the half-year report." The request states an intention to transfer. The two materials carry the statuses pending review and archived. The half-year report has not been generated. Whether the authorization takes effect depends on later approval, so the handover form cannot record it as completed.

## Three common errors
1. Treating a pending-review ledger as approved data and submitting it. The input is the ledger text with status pending review. The comparison object is the archived sales voucher. The observable result is that the two status fields hold different values. Pending review is not approval, and the half-year report cannot be filled from it.
2. Treating the proposed handover request as an effective authorization. The input is the request sentence that authorizes submission of the half-year report. The comparison object is whether an approval record exists. The observable result is that the request text and the approval record are separate facts. Until approval is granted, the submission action has no authorization basis.
3. Treating a retrieval hit as completed permission isolation. The input is a ledger fragment returned by retrieval. The comparison object is the authorization scope recorded on the handover form. The observable result is that the retrieval result carries no subject permission information. A similarity score is not a permission or entity filter, so the authorization scope needs a separate check.

## How to verify the setup
1. For the ledger text in the hypothetical sample, input "Store A plastic bag procurement ledger for the first half of 2026, status pending review" together with the sales voucher text "Store A plastic bag sales receipt summary for June 2026, status archived." Compare the status fields and the period fields of the two materials. The condition is met when the result shows a status mismatch and different period scopes. Record it as pending verification, not as approved.
2. For the proposed handover request "Transfer the reporting permission for Store A's first-half procurement ledger and sales voucher to the new compliance role, and authorize that role to submit the half-year report," input the request sentence and the approval record field. Compare the request content with the approval status. The condition is met when the result shows the approval record is absent. Record it as not authorized, not as a completed handover.
3. For the retrieval step, input the ledger and voucher materials above as paper inputs. Compare the retrieval return with the authorization scope on the handover form. The condition is met when the result shows the retrieved fragments carry no subject permission identifier. If the return is empty, use the explicit paper input `[]` to check the downstream design, without claiming that a fictional rule retrieval always returns `[]`. Libraries on different vector models cannot be associated together. Check the identifier only, exclude non-matching items, and record the actual observation.
