# Retail checkout and loyalty: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Retail checkout and loyalty, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Checkout rules, labels and refund exceptions retain activity scope and actual payment stages. Public examples use hypothetical transaction text and contain no real member data. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A checkout-and-loyalty handover can use general rules and hypothetical transactions to locate points, label or refund discussions without amounts or personal records. Use chatId with activity and source version without deriving writes from mentioned points changes. Inspect actual returned scope rather than assuming complete POS, CRM and payment retention or passed personal-access checks from a visible conversation.

## Three common errors

Missing activity applicability in input described as universal rules needs source and history checks. A label treated as this payment result needs the actual transaction evidence. A refund request described as returned funds needs corresponding payment processing. These concern scope, transaction and execution, none supplied by a shared activity name or completed exchange.

## How to verify the setup

Retain public material, activity, actual return and business gaps. Compare testing and production histories separately. For runtime detail, inspect detail and responseData under the official contract without presuming completeness from parameters. Refunds, member writes, retention and formal handover need the complete arrangement. Log review establishes neither processed funds, effective rules nor the recipient’s business authority.
