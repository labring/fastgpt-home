# Retail checkout and loyalty: operations handover with workflow routing

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Retail checkout and loyalty, this page examines FastGPT workflow routing. Checkout-promotion handover separates training demonstrations, rule scope and real account authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

Proposed shift material includes a demonstration-account screenshot, a store-specific member promotion note and equipment awaiting transfer. Collect store, role and open work, let a person choose rule checking or equipment completion, and pass the information to the relevant downstream path. A demonstration account provides no production access; campaign wording proves no configuration. Verify waiting and continuation through actual interaction records without inventing a global shift-handover switch.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Checkout handover | Proposed material | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demonstration account | Training screenshot | Real authorization |
| Campaign store | Specified scope | No broader applicability |
| Equipment list | Transfer pending | Receipt evidence |
| Choice branches | Rules or equipment | Compare inputs/outputs |
| Shift closure | Business procedure | No state-based inference |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input node` | Submitted form values pass to subsequent steps. |
| `User-selection node` | Execution follows the connected selected branch. |
| `Interaction-state check` | Inspect waiting and post-submission states. |
| `Output handoff check` | Compare values passed between steps. |
| `External-operation responsibility` | External business operations require an implementation. |

## Three common errors

A demonstration screenshot used as authorization needs real account approval checked. A store promotion extended elsewhere needs applicability checked. An equipment list described as completed delivery needs signed receipt checked. These test authority, scope and physical transfer; no real checkout or membership outcome is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Trace original input and received fields along each route. A missing equipment receipt stays open when rule details arrive, and completed wording opens no account. Promotion configuration, access transfer and equipment receipt require business systems or actual handover procedures. The workflow organizes review without closing a whole shift from complete-looking documents. Keep equipment receipt and account authorization references separate so shift review can inspect the evidence for each. Label the demonstration environment beside its screenshot and keep equipment identity next to the receipt entry.
