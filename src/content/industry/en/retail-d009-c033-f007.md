# Trade and FX administration: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Trade and FX administration, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Trade, receipt and registration discussions must correspond to the transaction. A log supports inspection of supplied material without establishing bank execution, eligibility or authoritative policy interpretation. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A trade-and-FX source handover can locate discussion of contracts, vouchers and registration without amounts, rates or legal requirements. Proposed exchange lacks current bank confirmation. Use chatId with contract and source identity rather than deriving processed funds from mentioned receipt. Inspect actual fields without presuming complete bank or registration retention, eligibility or responsibility from document names.

## Three common errors

Another transaction’s exchange needs party and voucher-relationship checks. Receipt wording treated as complete banking requires the corresponding actual confirmation. Registration material described as this executed operation needs its operation evidence separately. These concern subject, evidence scope and stage rather than outcomes from conversation time or headings.

## How to verify the setup

Retain contract, source status, actual return and requested confirmation, inspecting testing and production histories separately. Request runtime detail under the official contract and check responseData without assuming complete fields from success. Banking, accounting, retention and handover confirmation need the complete arrangement. Review establishes exchange evidence rather than compliance, completed fund handling or formal recipient acceptance.
