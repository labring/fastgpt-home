# Trade returns and claims: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Trade returns and claims, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Return-claim handover separates original-warehouse receipt, damage evidence and declaration status. Neither a planned return nor carrier handoff establishes receipt at that warehouse. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed handover wording says goods are intended for return to the original warehouse. Quality evidence is missing and declaration wording remains a draft. A form can collect passages and item correspondence. A person may request quality evidence or review return arrangements; these are proposed business inputs, not existing software parameters. Inspect received text for retained intent and draft status without inferring accepted compensation or supplying customs rules and legal conclusions.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Return-shipment handover form | Proposed passage | Input and business check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original-warehouse receipt | Return intended only | Separate receipt evidence for this item |
| Quality gap | Inspection missing | Not confirmed damage |
| Declaration status | Draft | Not completed handling |
| Human choice | Quality or arrangement review | Passage on connected branch |
| Business result | Warehouse receipt, declaration and agreement checked separately | Carrier handoff is not original-warehouse receipt |

## Three common errors

Planned return promoted to receipt at the original warehouse needs that warehouse’s actual receiving evidence for this item; carrier handoff establishes only the start of return transport. Missing quality material promoted to confirmed damage needs corresponding inspection. A draft promoted to completed declaration needs real submission and handling evidence. These inspect warehouse receipt, factual basis and process status without filling gaps through form completion.

## How to verify the setup

Compare submitted passages with the subject, destination and gaps received downstream, then follow the chosen connected branch. Added quality wording changes only supported judgments; original-warehouse receipt, declaration handling and the other party’s agreement still need their own evidence. Formal return transport, declaration and claim confirmation require separate authority and receipts. Interaction waiting and continuation supply neither freight integration nor agreement. Completed form handover establishes neither receipt at the original warehouse nor ended business.
