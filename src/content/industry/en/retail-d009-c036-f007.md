# Multi-warehouse inventory: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Multi-warehouse inventory, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Multi-warehouse history separates updated synchronization notes, sending and receiving roles, and record environments. Available conversations do not establish synchronized inventory. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Proposed staff messages say a synchronization sheet was updated, sending-warehouse notes remain unresolved and the assistant calls both warehouses reconciled. A test conversation demonstrates similar wording. Link history to its real business environment before inspecting returned passages and speakers. Updated text does not establish external stock writes, and test answers do not establish production execution. Business records omitted from actual responses need separate collection rather than assumed completeness.

## Three common errors

Text promoted to synchronization needs actual system outcomes. A sending record described as receiving requires the source role beside answer wording. Test history promoted to formal handover needs environment and business linkage. These inspect execution, roles and record origin independently of shared warehouse names.

## How to verify the setup

Retain conversation identifiers, environments and actual returns. Corrected roles change their explanation only while inventory needs separate receipts. An old sheet update cannot establish synchronization. Stock writes, authority changes and handover closure need separate business records. Logs establish neither revocation nor reconciled stock, change no warehouse automatically and cannot turn absent returned data into zero real inventory.
