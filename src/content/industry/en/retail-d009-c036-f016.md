# Multi-warehouse inventory: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Multi-warehouse inventory, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. A multi-warehouse handover form preserves transfer roles and inventory timing. Submitted synchronization wording does not establish receipt in another warehouse. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed handover says the sending warehouse awaits dispatch and the receiving warehouse awaits receipt. The synchronization excerpt is only a read-time stock snapshot. Collect warehouse-role passages, snapshot status and gaps. A person may inspect dispatch or obtain receiving evidence. A choice neither selects real stock nor authenticates staff, and no warehouse integration is assumed. Actual downstream receipt must preserve roles and timing rather than merge them during organization.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multi-warehouse handover check | Proposed role and status | Downstream requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sending warehouse | Awaiting dispatch | Not transmitted as receiver |
| Receiving warehouse | Receipt pending | Not already arrived |
| Stock snapshot | Read-time balance | Not this movement |
| Human choice | Dispatch or receiving evidence | Correct actual warehouse subject |
| Business write | Synchronization and closure separately checked | Nodes do not reconcile stock |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Reversed role: A sending-warehouse passage transmitted as the receiving warehouse needs submitted-role and received-value comparison. Snapshot promoted to movement requires actual dispatch or receipt events. Pending receipt promoted to arrival needs the receiving warehouse’s corresponding record. These inspect roles, quantity meaning and stage independently of the word synchronization.

## How to verify the setup

Compare each submitted element with actual downstream input and follow the selected branch to its warehouse subject. Added dispatch evidence changes dispatch only while receipt stays pending. Stock synchronization, transfer writes and anomaly closure need separate authority and receipts. Recorded interaction and continued nodes establish workflow action, not goods movement or reconciled stock across warehouses.
