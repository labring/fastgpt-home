# Peak-sale fulfillment: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Peak-sale fulfillment, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Peak-sale scheduling handover preserves forecasts, staffing arrangements and pending anomalies. Submitting a plan does not establish dispatched orders. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed shift wording forecasts concentrated dispatch. Staffing is a proposed arrangement, while daily anomalies remain unchecked. Collect these statuses and work subjects. A person can obtain anomaly evidence or inspect shift acceptance without treating choices as a real staffing system. Forecasts are estimates, rosters do not establish execution and delivery-assurance plans do not establish individual order handoffs. Include pending work in the handover content so downstream steps can continue handling it.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Peak-sale handover form | Proposed source status | Transfer check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forecast | Concentrated dispatch expected | Not actual occurrence |
| Staffing arrangement | Proposed staff | Not actual attendance |
| Daily anomaly | Unchecked | Pending work retained |
| Assurance plan | Not an order handoff | Actual logistics record |
| Choice destination | Anomaly or shift review | Corresponding work passage received |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Forecast promoted to actual work needs execution records. Staffing promoted to attendance needs actual shift-receipt evidence. An assurance plan promoted to dispatch needs the corresponding logistics handoff. These inspect estimation, staff action and fulfillment stage independently of a submitted form.

## How to verify the setup

Retain submitted passages, the choice and actual received text. Inspect whether anomalies remain identifiable and whether the connected branch reaches the corresponding work. Added dispatch evidence changes that entry alone without confirming every shift or anomaly. Staff recognition, warehouse writes and external commitments need separate authority and receipts. Waiting and continuation establish neither execution to plan nor peak-sale performance.
