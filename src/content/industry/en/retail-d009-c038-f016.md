# Fresh-food concession counters: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Fresh-food concession counters, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Fresh-concession handover separates contractual scope, purchase inspection and unresolved losses. A daily report does not establish completed shift fulfillment. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed counter handover includes a daily-report title, missing purchase inspection and a draft loss explanation. The concession excerpt defines responsibility scope only. Collect the counter and passages. A person may obtain inspection or inspect loss origins without deciding responsibility through a choice. A report title does not establish performed food inspection, and terms cannot supply shift execution. No safety criteria, loss amounts or business effects are provided; preserve unverified status downstream.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract scope | Responsibility terms | Not performed shift duty |
| Purchase inspection | Evidence missing | Not completed inspection |
| Loss explanation | Draft under review | No determined cause |
| Daily-report subject | Named counter | Exclude another location |
| Subsequent business | Acceptance, stock and reconciliation separate | Interaction is not an outcome |

## Three common errors

Contract promoted to fulfilled duty needs actual shift inspection. A draft promoted to confirmed loss needs corresponding evidence. Another counter’s daily report used here requires original-subject and received-text checks. These inspect terms versus actions, fact status and subject coverage without merging concessions in the same mall.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect whether submission and actual receipt retain counter scope and gaps on both connected branches. Added inspection wording changes only covered incoming goods, not loss attribution or other items. Formal acceptance, stock handling and mutual reconciliation need separate authority and receipts. Completed interaction establishes neither food conformity, determined responsibility nor closure of all shift work.
