# Tmall flagship operations: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Tmall flagship store operations revolve around four recurring materials: the product publishing page, the brand authorization letter, the shipping commitment record, and the after-sales work order. Brand authorization and flagship store identity sit at the center of store qualification, and the brief lists product delisting, delayed-shipment compensation, and store rating decline as common consequences of error. The stated update frequency is daily and per order. This page addresses the operations-permission and handover-management task inside the deployment-and-upgrade step: it checks what authorization state each material, action, or request actually occupies. Retrieval and form completion do not constitute authorization execution.

Two industry facts shape this task. First, flagship identity depends on a brand authorization letter naming a specific authorized party, so a handover request that names a different party cannot be treated as covered by that letter. Second, the shipping commitment record and after-sales work order carry per-order status, so a status label such as pending review describes one stage of one document, not the presence or absence of an operations permission.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Version checking comes first: confirm the current version, the target version, and the corresponding upgrade notes, and do not infer current behavior from an old configuration name. `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` takes the AI Proxy root address, without a single-model address or an appended chat-completion path. `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` must match the AI Proxy server configuration and must not be empty; it does not replace an application invoke credential. Dependencies and backups are checked against the official deployment and upgrade steps, and recovery capability must be verified in the operator's own environment. After upgrade, the same sample is used to recheck import, retrieval, and application invocation. Completing the steps does not promise zero interruption.

Hypothetical drill sample: an excerpt of handover materials for a Tmall flagship store reads as follows. The brand authorization letter states that the authorizing party is "A Brand Management Co., Ltd.", the authorized party is "B E-Commerce Co., Ltd.", the authorized store is "A Brand Tmall Flagship Store", the authorization is valid until March 31, 2027, and the authorization scope covers product publishing and after-sales handling for that store. The handover request states: "Transfer the product publishing page edit permission and the after-sales work order handling permission to C Agency Operations Co., Ltd., effective May 1, 2026." The draft sentence to be assessed reads: "C Agency Operations Co., Ltd. obtains the store's product publishing and after-sales handling permissions from May 1, 2026." Check point: the authorized party in the letter is B, while the draft sentence names C, so the subjects differ; the letter does not name C and does not state that the permissions may be sub-delegated. The request remains pending verification and is not supported.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval hit as authorization. Input: a knowledge-base search for "product publishing permission". Comparison: the retrieval result against the authorized party named in the brand authorization letter. Observable result: retrieval returns B's material while the draft sentence names C; the two do not match, so the request stays pending verification.

2. Treating a completed form as an effective permission. Input: the phrase "effective May 1, 2026" in the handover request. Comparison: that phrase against the authorization letter's validity period and authorization scope. Observable result: the letter does not name C and contains no sub-delegation clause, so the effective date lacks an authorization basis and cannot be judged effective.

3. Treating pending review as missing operations permission. Input: an after-sales work order labeled "pending review". Comparison: that label against the work order handling permission record. Observable result: pending review shows only that review is incomplete; it does not negate an operations permission, and the permission state remains unsupported.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample text and the draft sentence. Comparison: the draft sentence subject "C Agency Operations Co., Ltd." against the authorized party "B E-Commerce Co., Ltd." in the letter. Condition met: the label "authorized" applies only when the two match; in this sample they do not match, so the label is "pending verification".

2. Input the effective date "May 1, 2026" from the handover request. Comparison: that date against the letter's validity "until March 31, 2027" and its authorization scope. Condition met: effectiveness can be judged only when the letter names that subject and states a sub-delegation clause; this sample states neither, so the evidence gap is preserved.

3. Input the same handover materials after the upgrade. Comparison: import, retrieval, and application invocation results before and after the upgrade. Condition met: matching results are recorded as passing; differences are recorded as difference items, without judging zero interruption. An empty-result continuation can use the explicit paper input `[]` to check the downstream design; this does not claim that a fictional-rule search necessarily returns `[]`.
