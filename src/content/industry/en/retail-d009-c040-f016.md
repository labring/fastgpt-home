# Customer-service knowledge: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Customer-service knowledge, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Knowledge-maintenance handover preserves distinctions among old answers, current parameters and escalation status. A submitted form does not automatically update stored knowledge. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed handover says an old FAQ is still cited, parameter wording changed but awaits review, and an escalation was received. Collect old wording, the new explanation and its product subject. A person can inspect parameters or obtain escalation outcomes. These are proposed materials, without assuming direct knowledge writes or reindexing. A script template does not establish publication. Distinguish suggested changes from effective content instead of inferring removal of old answers from ended handover.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Knowledge-maintenance handover | Proposed pending work | Receiving requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old FAQ | Still cited | Retain wording to review |
| New parameters | Changed; review pending | Product applicability |
| Script template | Publication unproven | Actual update record |
| Escalation | Receipt only | Real handling result |
| Subsequent changes | Knowledge writes and indexing separate | Submission is not synchronization |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

Suggestion promoted to effective change needs actual update records. Another model’s parameters applied here require applicability and received-passage checks. Escalation receipt promoted to resolution needs its actual handling result. These inspect content state, subject and business stage independently of choices.

## How to verify the setup

Compare whether submitted and received text preserve both old and new wording. Follow the parameter or outcome branch to its product. Parameter confirmation changes only supported recommendations while publication still needs evidence. Knowledge changes, indexing and escalation closure require separate authority and receipts. Interaction records establish neither synchronized material nor correct future answers to every phrasing.
