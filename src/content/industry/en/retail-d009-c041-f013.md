# Marketplace promotions: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Marketplace promotions, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer resources by current campaign and platform, verifying former-operator revocation and necessary access for the recipient. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists current registration, a previous campaign review and another platform’s inventory appendix; the incoming person follows current registration only. Inspect access to applicable notes and required stock sources, retaining campaign, platform and version. Receiving only the previous review cannot establish current readiness. Compare handover references and actual response. Shared titles or products do not transfer all historical approvals and platforms; the business owner confirms scope.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover-permission check | Campaign, platform and resource | Acceptance record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current work | Separate current registration and earlier review | Campaign and version read by new account |
| Platform restriction | Another stock appendix needs a separate decision | Owner-confirmed transfer boundary |
| Former account | Test approval and calculation revocation independently | Full response for the former identity |
| Distinct events | Separate expiry, archiving and revocation | Actual result for each |
| Business approval | Operation right is not campaign-change approval | Execution records and unresolved items |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If an earlier review establishes current registration readiness, inspect activity, platform and version beside handover references. Shared product names supply no current source.

- If former operators read approval or internal calculations, retain full responses and configuration. New access proves no old denial; cross-platform scope needs its own decision.

- If expiry, archiving or team changes become terminated external access, test each result and backend identity separately. They are not substitutes, and technical operations approve no campaign change.

## How to verify the setup

Record necessary current-campaign access for the incoming identity, expected denials for the former one and negative tests on excluded platforms. Correct access with earlier conditions in the answer needs input and version checks rather than indiscriminately wider grants. Allowed operations are not business approval to change a campaign or stock; actual execution needs separate confirmation. Retain unresolved resources without presenting completed personnel transfer as accepted registration, locked inventory or satisfied operating conditions.
