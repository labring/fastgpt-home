# Marketplace promotions: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Marketplace promotion sign-up and price management turns on price, inventory and campaign timing. The working materials come from platform marketing back ends, ERP inventory and historical transaction prices. The recurring documents are the campaign sign-up form, the promotional price approval form, the inventory lock form and the campaign review form. This page handles one slice of that work: operations permission and handover management at the deployment and upgrade step. It decides what authorization state each material, action and request actually occupies.

The brief marks this category as roughly accurate rather than confirmed. Document updates are described as weekly and per campaign. Nothing here infers batch synchronization, full synchronization or transaction-triggered mechanisms from that cadence.

Two features of this category shape the handover task. First, the materials are separate documents with separate status fields, so a sign-up record, a price approval and an inventory lock each carry their own state and none of them inherits another's. Second, the paper trail is what an incoming operator receives, so the handover question is whether the outgoing operator held the authority the incoming operator is being asked to act on.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Handover judgments require the same subject, the same period and the same field. A pending-review state does not demonstrate that an operator lacks permission. A quotation validity window does not state a supply term. Retrieval and form collection do not by themselves constitute authorized execution.

Hypothetical drill sample. Material text one: store operator A submitted the Spring Campaign Sign-Up Form on 2 March 2026, for item A, campaign window 8 March to 12 March, sign-up status "pending review." Material text two: the Promotional Price Approval Form submitted the same day states item A's campaign net price at 199 yuan, approval status "pending review," approver field empty. Material text three: the Inventory Lock Form shows 500 units of item A locked, lock status "locked," lock expiry 7 March.

Handover request sentence to be assessed: "A has obtained sign-up and price authority for item A's spring campaign and may hand the sign-up form, approval form and lock form to B for listing execution."

## Three common errors

1. Reading the Promotional Price Approval Form's "pending review" as granted price authority. The input is the approval status field; the comparison object is the sample text's "pending review" together with the empty approver field; the observable result is that the status field has not changed to approved.
2. Treating the Inventory Lock Form's "locked" as sign-up and price authority. The input is the lock status field; the comparison object is the locked object and the expiry date; the observable result is that this field describes inventory occupation only and states no sign-up or price permission.
3. Treating a retrieval hit or a form submission as authorized execution. The input is a retrieval result or a form record; the comparison object is whether the sample text states an authorizing subject and an effective scope; the observable result is that neither states an authorization conclusion.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the three sample material sentences and the handover request sentence. Compare the sign-up status field against the phrase "has obtained authority." Observable result: while the sign-up status reads "pending review," the request sentence is not supported by the material and must be recorded as pending verification.
2. Input the approval status field and the approver field. Compare "pending review" and the empty approver field against the phrase "has obtained price authority." Observable result: neither field states an approval conclusion, so that part of the judgment is unsupported.
3. Input the Inventory Lock Form's lock status and expiry date. Compare the field meanings against the scope of sign-up and price authority. Observable result: the form states a lock through 7 March, which cannot support a lock remaining in place across the 8 to 12 March campaign window, and cannot supply sign-up or price authority. Period coverage and the two authorities each remain pending verification; no conclusion is drawn that the system releases the lock automatically.
