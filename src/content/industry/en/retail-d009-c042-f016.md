# Product listing reviews: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Product listing reviews, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Title-and-detail handover preserves copy versions, source coverage and pending comments. A prohibited-words list does not establish approval of an entire page. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed title wording changes a product description, but detail assets remain old and inspection covers only a submitted specimen. Collect versions and unresolved passages. A person can inspect asset correspondence or obtain quality evidence. A word list is related material without replacing complete review or supplying legal judgments. Proposed titles are not published titles, and sample evidence does not cover every item. Preserve these boundaries downstream.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Title-and-detail handover | Proposed distinction | Transfer check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed title | Unpublished | Retain suggested status |
| Detail assets | Old version | Actual correspondence |
| Inspection scope | Submitted subject only | Not every item |
| Word list | Not complete review | Actual review record |
| Formal page | Replacement and release separate | Form does not update page |

## Three common errors

Mixed asset versions need submission and actual-recipient comparison. Expanded report coverage requires the inspected subject and applicability before covering every product. A word-list check promoted to whole-page approval needs the actual review record. These inspect version, evidence scope and approval status independently of file completeness.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect actual input on asset and evidence paths. Added version explanation changes correspondence alone while review and publication need their own sources. A title-only addition cannot establish the applicability of all detail content. Formal review, page replacement and release require separate authority and receipts. Interaction establishes neither legal or platform compliance nor an automatically changed product page.
