# Multi-store management: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Multi-store management, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Multi-store handover preserves authorization coverage and complaint subjects. An existing list does not establish every operator’s access to all stores. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed handover includes store authorization names, an unchecked channel complaint and one store’s monthly note, without evidence of the current operator’s access. Collect each store subject and pending action. A person can inspect authorization scope or obtain complaint origins; choices do not authenticate a store or switch real accounts. One store cannot represent the matrix, and similar names cannot merge subjects. Internal price-control material remains related context without figures.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Multi-store handover check | Proposed scope | Relationship needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorization list | Names are not current access | Actual operator authority |
| Complaint case | Specific store | Exclude another store |
| Monthly note | Report is not handling outcome | Corresponding business record |
| Choice path | Authority or origin review | No real account switching |
| Result scope | Current store only | Not the entire matrix |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Form input` | Collect input and pass values after submission. |
| `Required-information check` | Design required business inputs and missing-input handling. |
| `User selection` | The selected option maps to a connected branch. |
| `Interaction state` | Inspect waiting and completed interaction separately. |
| `Downstream responsibility` | Verify validation, approval and write results separately. |

## Three common errors

List promoted to access needs the actual operator-authorization relationship. Another store’s complaint used here requires complaint-subject and received-text checks. A monthly report promoted to resolved handling needs the actual outcome. These inspect authority, business ownership and execution independently of a choice.

## How to verify the setup

Compare store descriptions in submitted and received text. Follow authorization or complaint paths to the corresponding task. Added complaint wording changes that case while operational access still needs independent verification. Account actions, external replies and closure require separate authority and receipts. Recorded interaction establishes neither granted access nor completed handling and does not extend one store’s outcome across the matrix.
