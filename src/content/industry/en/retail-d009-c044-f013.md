# Marketplace advertising: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Marketplace advertising, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Transfer operator access by active plans, checking revocation for former agencies, historical reports and external advertising identities. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A handover exercise lists an active plan and a completed brand review; the incoming operator follows the former. Identify keyword, audience and attribution resources required, then read current versions with the new account. Reading only the completed plan does not establish availability of current material. Retain handover references and the actual plan to inspect mapping or grants. Shared channel names and operator titles cannot open every historical brand; the owner confirms transfer scope individually.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Handover-permission check | Active and historical resources | Actual acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current plan | Current keyword, audience and attribution sources | Reads by the incoming account |
| Historical boundary | Completed plans and other brands need decisions | Owner-confirmed transfer scope |
| Former agency | Retest plans, reports and appendices | Full responses and denials |
| External identity | Inspect backend accounts and interface sessions | No synchronized invalidation inferred from role change |
| Subsequent action | Personnel change executes no bid or backend disablement | Retain gaps and business approval |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If completed plans establish active-campaign readiness, inspect current keyword, audience and attribution versions beside references and returns. A channel title grants no universal brand scope.

- If all historical brands transfer through shared agency work, confirm necessity resource by resource and negatively test unrelated identities. Collaboration supplies no cross-brand authorization.

- If former agencies retain audiences or internal change implies disabled backend sessions, retain internal responses and external checks separately. Archiving revokes nothing automatically; handover changes no bid.

## How to verify the setup

Test necessary active-plan reads for the incoming operator and denials for former and unrelated-brand identities, recording operations and results. Correct grants with a completed period in the answer need history and subject checks; wider access does not resolve mixed plans. Handover changes no bid and disables no backend identity automatically. Technical operation rights are not business approval. Retain unresolved access and external-revocation items without presenting a personnel change as executed advertising adjustment.
