# Marketplace category access: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Category qualification access on an e-commerce platform occurs before a product is listed or a store is onboarded. The core materials are the operating entity's credentials and the chain of authorization behind the brand. A business license identifies the operating entity. A trademark registration certificate identifies who holds the brand right. A brand authorization chain links the brand owner to the onboarding entity level by level. An industry license covers the operating permission for a specific category.

These materials are held separately by recruitment operations staff, store onboarding specialists and brand-side channel managers. Collecting the authorization chain, matching the operating category and supplementing licenses are the most time-consuming steps. Missing materials or a broken authorization chain lead to onboarding rejection, an inability to publish in the category, and occupied deposit funds. The materials are updated irregularly and are re-checked at each onboarding.

At the deployment and upgrade step, the handover task is to confirm which materials are in a handover-ready state, which operations have already completed, and which requests are still pending. A qualification document does not by itself constitute platform authorization. Retrieving a business license does not mean the entity has obtained category access. A form collects field values; business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization each require their stated downstream implementation.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

During a handover, the material list, operation records and pending requests left by the previous operator must be checked item by item. A submitted onboarding application may be under review or already approved. An authorization chain file may be an original or awaiting supplementation. A license may or may not still be within its validity period. A pending-review state does not demonstrate that operating permission is absent. Signing, reaching cooperation and contract effectiveness are three separate facts.

Hypothetical exercise sample: an onboarding specialist hands over a material package. The business license original reads "Unified Social Credit Code 91330100MA2XXXXX, entity name Hangzhou X Trading Co., Ltd., business scope includes daily goods sales, registration status active." The trademark registration certificate original reads "No. 12345678, registrant Hangzhou X Trading Co., Ltd., approved goods Class 21, valid until March 1, 2030." The brand authorization chain file reads "The brand owner authorizes Hangzhou X Trading Co., Ltd. to sell Class 21 goods on the e-commerce platform, authorization period January 1, 2025 to December 31, 2026, authorization nature ordinary authorization." The draft sentence to be assessed: "This entity has obtained platform category access for all Class 21 goods, and the authorization chain is complete with no supplementation required."

## Configuration and implementation checks

The configuration below centers on the verification items for this sample. The values are starting points for this hypothetical exercise, chosen for comparison against the dates and ranges in the sample materials. They are not official default values.

| Configuration item | Hypothetical starting point | Description and comparison method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorization chain validity check | December 31, 2026 | Compare with the authorization chain original end date to judge whether it remains valid at handover |
| Trademark certificate validity check | March 1, 2030 | Compare with the trademark certificate original validity date to confirm it has not expired |
| Business license registration status check | Active | Compare with the registration status field in the license original; a non-active value is flagged for verification |
| Category scope match | Class 21 | Compare with the approved goods class on the trademark certificate and the authorized class in the authorization chain |
| Pending request status marker | Under review | Compare with the current onboarding application status; under review is not treated as approved |
| Material supplementation list | Authorization chain original | Compare with the handover list; a missing original is recorded as a gap |

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval hit as authorization confirmation. Input a business license; retrieval returns a record for that entity. The comparison is whether the entity name and Unified Social Credit Code match; the observable result is that a record exists. The existence of a record does not mean category access has been granted, and the authorization state still requires the actual approval result of the onboarding application.

2. Writing a pending-review request as completed. Input an onboarding application record; compare its status field against "approved." The observable result is a status of under review. Pending review is not approval, and the draft sentence cannot state that access has been obtained.

3. Merging the trademark certificate validity date with the authorization chain validity date into one field. Input the trademark certificate validity date March 1, 2030 and the authorization chain validity date December 31, 2026; compare the date fields of two different documents. The observable result is that the two differ. Only mutually exclusive values within the same field constitute a contradiction. A date difference between different documents is two independent facts and does not by itself establish that the authorization chain is invalid.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the draft sentence from the hypothetical sample: "This entity has obtained platform category access for all Class 21 goods, and the authorization chain is complete with no supplementation required." Compare "has obtained access" in the draft against the actual status field of the onboarding application. The observable result: if the status field shows under review, the draft sentence is unsupported and must be changed to "the onboarding application has been submitted and is under review." If the status field shows approved and the authorization chain is within its validity period, the access portion of the draft is supported, but "no supplementation required" still needs comparison against the supplementation list.

2. Input the authorization chain original date December 31, 2026 and the trademark certificate date March 1, 2030 from the hypothetical sample. Compare both dates against the handover date. The observable result: if both dates are later than the handover date, neither material has a validity gap. If the authorization chain date is earlier than the handover date, record that the authorization chain has expired and a new authorization file must be obtained from the brand owner.

3. Input the material package list from the hypothetical sample and compare the listed files against the four material types: business license, trademark registration certificate, brand authorization chain, industry license. The observable result: if all four types have corresponding files and readable status, the handover materials are complete. If the industry license is missing, record that gap and do not infer that the entity lacks operating qualification; mark it as pending supplementation only.
