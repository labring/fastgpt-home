# Parcel labels and handoffs: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Parcel handoff takes place at the point where a package leaves the warehouse, and the central record is the carrier scan. The working materials for this category are electronic waybills, handoff manifests, pickup scan records and exception-item registers. These materials come from the courier system, warehouse shipment records, service standards and carrier statements, and they refresh per batch and per day.

For an operations permissions and handoff task, the materials matter because each one carries a different authorization state. A waybill print request, a manifest submission and a pickup scan record are separate objects. The materials record field values. They do not show that an operation was approved. A manifest number identifies a batch. A scan count states how many records were scanned. An exception register states that an item is pending verification. None of these fields is an approval, and none of them is an execution record.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

The upgrade path for this version involves image updates for the main service and the commercial service, plus manual migration scripts that appear under version upgrade in the administration area. Two manual tasks are documented: `20261008_migrate_chunk_training` and `20261009_migrate_dataset_rebuild_status`. The second depends on the first, and neither blocks service startup. Before running them, all older instances of the main service and the commercial service should be confirmed stopped and old task leases expired. These are preconditions stated in the upgrade notes, not automatic checks.

Two AI Proxy settings are required. `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` must both be set, and a missing value in either one causes startup validation to fail. Older single-model address settings do not replace them. This matters for handoff work because the application must start before any import, retrieval or application call can be reviewed after upgrade.

Retrieval and forms collect values. Business verification, approval, database writes and external synchronization each need their own downstream implementation. A similarity score does not carry permission or entity isolation duties. A form submission is a request, not an approval. A pending review is not an executed handoff.

Hypothetical drill sample, clearly labelled: a warehouse shipment group prepares handoff materials before an upgrade. The source text reads: "This batch contains 120 electronic waybills, handoff manifest number JC-0417, pickup scan records show 118 scanned entries, exception register shows 2 entries pending verification." The draft sentence under review reads: "This batch of 120 parcels has completed pickup handoff, authorizing the shipment group to perform subsequent operations." The phrases "has completed pickup handoff" and "authorizing" do not appear in the source material. The material states a scan count and a pending-verification status. The subject, object, status and validity scope needed for the draft claim are partly absent.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a scan count in the material as evidence of completed handoff. Input: the JC-0417 material line "118 scanned entries." Compare against the draft sentence "has completed pickup handoff." Observable result: the material does not state a completion status, so the claim is unsupported.
2. Treating a form submission as authorized execution. Input: a handoff request submitted by the shipment group. Compare against approval records. Observable result: a pending-review state does not demonstrate that operating permission was granted, and the authorization state remains pending verification.
3. Treating a similarity score as a permission filter. Input: a relevance value returned by retrieval. Compare against the authorization field of the target record. Observable result: relevance does not exclude unauthorized records, and permission must be checked separately.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical source text "This batch contains 120 electronic waybills, handoff manifest number JC-0417, pickup scan records show 118 scanned entries, exception register shows 2 entries pending verification." Compare against the draft sentence "This batch of 120 parcels has completed pickup handoff, authorizing the shipment group to perform subsequent operations." Expected: the material does not state completion or authorization, so the judgment is unsupported and the evidence gap is recorded.
2. Input the same material line "exception register shows 2 entries pending verification." Compare against any statement claiming full completion. Expected: a pending-verification status is inconsistent with a full-completion claim, and that claim does not hold.
3. Input a hypothetical empty result `[]`. Compare against the downstream design for handling empty results. Expected: check whether the downstream design distinguishes an empty result from a lack of permission, without asserting that a fictional rule retrieval necessarily returns an empty array.
