# Wholesale stall trading: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Wholesale stall trading runs on purchase notes, handwritten sales slips, stall lease contracts and payment receipts. These records sit alongside stall inventory, WeChat collection records and logistics consignment notes. Bargaining and writing the slip, arranging consignment, and chasing the final balance consume most of the working day. Slips are frequently handwritten, and one payment can be settled in several instalments. For deployment and upgrades, the consequence is concrete: after a version switch, the recorded permission ownership and handover state must be checked against each original document. Lists and search results inside the system reflect data rows only. They do not reflect the verbal authorisation given at the stall.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

An upgrade begins by comparing the current version with the target version and the matching upgrade notes. Behaviour is not inferred from an old configuration name. V4.17.2 contains two manually triggered upgrade scripts: the historical chunk training task migration, and the knowledge base rebuild status migration that depends on the first. Neither blocks service startup. Both are executed after confirming that all old-version App and Pro instances have fully stopped and old task leases have expired.

At this step, member permissions, application read permission and log visibility belong to deployment configuration. Signing on a slip, the lease contract taking effect, and settlement of the payment belong to business facts. A search hit on a receipt shows that the data row exists. It does not show that the final balance has been collected or that the stall has been handed over.

Hypothetical exercise sample: a stall handover occurs on 10 March 2026. The source material reads: "Received today a deposit of 2,000 yuan from a buyer surnamed Wang; the remaining 8,000 yuan to be settled on the day of collection," and "Stall lease contract runs until 31 December 2026; the tenant field is Li." The draft sentence to be judged is: "The stall payment has been settled in full and the stall operating permission has been handed over." The comparison objects are the deposit receipt and the lease contract. The fields needed are settlement status and tenant. Neither document states that the remaining balance was paid, and neither states that handover was completed. The draft sentence is not supported.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a search hit as authorisation. The input is the deposit receipt in the sample. Judging from it that the final balance has been settled treats the existence of a data row as a business fact. The receipt records only the deposit of 2,000 yuan and the agreed remaining 8,000 yuan.
2. Treating a pending review as absence of permission. The member edit dialog fetches full member information before it opens. A dialog in the open state shows only that information is loading. It does not show that the member lacks operating permission.
3. Merging signing, contract effectiveness and completed handover into one fact. In the sample lease contract, the tenant field is Li and the term runs to 31 December 2026. These facts are checked separately. A stated lease term does not indicate that handover is complete.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the two source sentences and the draft sentence "The stall payment has been settled in full and the stall operating permission has been handed over." Compare whether the draft sentence has explicit records of settlement and handover. Neither document states them, so the sentence is judged unsupported and the evidence gap is recorded.
2. Input the hypothetical configuration `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT=http://aiproxy:3000` and a non-empty `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN`. Compare whether startup validation passes. When either item is missing, startup validation fails, which is an observable event.
3. Input the sample receipt and lease contract. Compare whether search results and manual reconciliation conclusions agree. Search results are used only to locate data rows; permission and handover status follow the original documents. When they disagree, the original document record is retained.
