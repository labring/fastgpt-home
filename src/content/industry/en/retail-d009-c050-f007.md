# Complaints and official replies: conversation logs for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Complaints and official replies are directed at platforms or regulatory bodies, so they require formal evidence and rectification text. This differs from after-sales repair and reshipment work, which centers on routine ticket handling. The main data comes from the 12315 platform, platform complaint centers, customer service chat, and order systems. Everyday documents include complaint tickets, regulatory inquiry letters, order evidence, and rectification reports. Data updates are irregular, once per case, so conversation logs and auditing focus on per-case traceability rather than batch synchronization.

Typical roles are customer service supervisors, legal and compliance staff, and store owners. Errors can lead to administrative investigation, platform penalties, and brand reputation issues. Operations permissions and handover management need to distinguish who may view conversations, who may submit materials, and who may reply to the outside on behalf of the store. The conversation log itself is a record of runs. Business reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements are implemented through a complete business plan, and the existence of a log does not mean a compliance audit is complete.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

The chat interface locates a session by chatId and a conversation by dataId. During handover, check whether the target conversation and the business record point to the same chatId. Sessions created through different entry points cannot be counted together. When run details are needed, set detail according to the official contract and inspect responseData. When detail is true, module status and complete results are returned; in non-stream mode the result is stored in responseData. The log records the fields the interface actually returns. Business data that is not returned cannot be presumed to be fully retained.

Retrieval and similarity do not carry permission or entity isolation. Similarity is a basis for ordering retrieval results and cannot serve as a permission filter or a judgment of business ownership. authProxy is used to proxy team member identity and must be enabled by the team owner; the proxied identity still needs permission for the target application and session. Forms collect values only. Business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization each need their own downstream implementation.

Hypothetical drill sample: a store customer service supervisor hands over on 10 March 2025. The handover material reads: "Complaint ticket TS-0310-07, concerning an order refund dispute for store A, current status awaiting supplementary evidence; no official number has been received for the regulatory inquiry letter; the customer service chat record was created by the customer service account, chatId api-0310-a." The handover request sentence to be judged is: "Transfer all conversation logs with chatId api-0310-a to the successor, and treat the ticket's evidence preparation as complete." The phrase "treat the evidence preparation as complete" lacks an authorization basis. The ticket status is awaiting supplementary evidence, and the material does not state that evidence is complete.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the same chatId as a complete evidence chain for the whole ticket. In the sample, chatId api-0310-a only shows session ownership. The ticket status is awaiting supplementary evidence, and the material does not state that evidence is complete. The existence of a conversation log does not equal completed evidence preparation.
2. Treating similarity or retrieval results as a permission judgment. Retrieval returns relevant fragments and does not judge whether a fragment belongs to store A or whether the successor may view it. Permission needs a separate check against authProxy and the target application and session permissions.
3. Writing a pending or supplementary status as completed. In the sample the ticket is awaiting supplementary evidence, and no official number has been received for the regulatory inquiry letter. Both are unsupported and cannot be written as submitted or replied.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input chatId api-0310-a and ticket TS-0310-07 from the sample, and compare whether they point to the same session and the same order dispute. If the chatId matches while the ticket status remains awaiting supplementary evidence, the log is traceable but evidence preparation is not complete; this observation satisfies the check condition.
2. Input the sentence to be judged, "Transfer all conversation logs with chatId api-0310-a to the successor, and treat the ticket's evidence preparation as complete," and compare "treat the evidence preparation as complete" with the recorded status "awaiting supplementary evidence." If the material does not state that evidence is complete or that authorization is complete, the sentence exceeds the authorization boundary; this observation satisfies the check condition.
3. Input a request with detail true and stream false, and compare the fields actually returned in responseData with the fields listed in the handover checklist. If the checklist lists a field not stated in the material or not returned by the interface, mark that item as unsupported; this observation satisfies the check condition.
