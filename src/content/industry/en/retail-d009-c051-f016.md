# Douyin storefront operations: interactive forms for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Douyin storefront operations does not depend on the pace of a single livestream session. Day-to-day attention goes to product card performance and store experience score. The main data sources for this category are the Douyin store workbench, the Douyin e-commerce learning center, Selected Alliance data and logistics interfaces. Data updates daily and per order. The documents handled routinely are product qualification pages, experience score details, shipping violation appeals and store announcements. The most time-consuming work concentrates on maintaining the experience score, handling out-of-stock and no-stock situations, and supplementing product qualifications.

For operations permission and handover management, these characteristics mean the interactive forms step revolves around four material types: qualifications, experience score, appeals and announcements. Material status and operating permission need separate verification. A qualification page records validity dates for a category. Experience score details record deduction entries within a stated window. A shipping violation appeal carries a review status. A store announcement records what has been published. None of these documents, by itself, records whether handover has been completed or whether an appeal has passed.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The Form Input node is a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user finishes the interaction. The User Selection node is also a user interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interaction state; when the user makes a selection, the node evaluates the selection and executes the corresponding branch. These nodes collect the information that is filled in and pass it to subsequent nodes. The nodes do not carry a business database function of their own. Field validation, business approval, database writes and batch operations are implemented in external systems or later nodes as needed. Retrieval or form collection does not constitute authorization to execute.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample. A Douyin store operations staff member submits a handover request. The source material reads: "The category qualification listed on the product qualification page is valid until 31 March 2026; the experience score details show two shipping violation deduction entries in the last 30 days; the shipping violation appeal is currently in pending review status; one out-of-stock notice has been published in the store announcement area." The proposed determination sentence reads: "All operating permissions for this store have been handed over, and the appeal has passed." What needs to be checked: the material states a qualification validity date, deduction entries, a pending review appeal status and a published announcement. It does not state that permission handover is complete or that the appeal has passed.

## Three common errors

1. Treating form submission as completed authorization. In the sample, the appeal status is pending review. Pending review does not show that the appeal passed, and it does not show that operating permission is lacking. To check, input the source material and the proposed determination sentence, compare whether "pending review" and "passed" are the same field with the same value, and only treat the condition as satisfied when the material explicitly states that it passed.

2. Treating retrieval results as permission isolation. Retrieval and semantic recall do not handle permission or entity isolation, and a similarity setting does not exclude unrelated business or unauthorized records. To check, input the sample material and the retrieval query, compare whether the returned records match the query subject, and treat a record as reference material only when both the identifier and the subject match.

3. Treating qualification validity as completed permission handover. The material states only that the qualification is valid until 31 March 2026. It does not state the handover status. To check, input that date and the proposed determination sentence, compare whether the material states a handover completion field, and record an evidence gap when it does not.

## How to verify the setup

1. Against the sample source material and the proposed determination sentence, input the four field types collected by the form: qualification, deduction, appeal and announcement. Compare whether the phrase "all operating permissions have been handed over" in the proposed sentence corresponds to a handover field stated in the material. When the material does not state it, judge the claim as unsupported rather than as negated or contradictory.

2. Against the appeal status, input "pending review" and the proposed sentence "the appeal has passed." Compare whether the same field carries mutually exclusive values. Only when the material explicitly states that it passed is the condition satisfied; otherwise record it as pending verification.

3. Against the qualification validity date, input 31 March 2026 and the current check date, and compare the date range. The condition is satisfied only when the check date falls within the valid period. At the same time, check whether permission handover is stated separately; when it is not stated, retain the evidence gap.
