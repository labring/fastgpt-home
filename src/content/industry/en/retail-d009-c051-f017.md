# Douyin storefront operations: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Shelf operations for a Douyin storefront runs on four recurring record types: product qualification pages, experience score details, shipping violation appeals, and store announcements. The brief gives their update cadence as daily and per order. That cadence matters here because handover work at the deployment and upgrade stage depends on knowing which version of a record was in force when an authorization was granted, and on separating three things that are easy to blur together: the state of a document, the operation being requested, and the boundary of the authorization covering it.

The brief also notes that shelf operations differs from livestream operations in that it does not depend on a single broadcast rhythm and pays more attention to product cards and store experience scores. For handover, the practical consequence is that authority over qualification pages, experience score details, appeal records, and announcements may sit with different people, and a handover package has to state each one separately rather than describe a single blanket operator role.

This page covers only the reconciliation between handover materials, operation requests, and authorization state. It does not construct business functions that the supplied documents do not describe.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Deployment and upgrade work at this stage involves version reconciliation, configuration confirmation, and handover records. Two constraints follow from the supplied technical text and shape every check below.

First, retrieval and similarity do not carry permission or entity isolation duties. A search result that returns a qualification page record is not evidence that the requester may act on that page. Forms collect values; business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization each require their own downstream implementation. Neither a form submission nor a retrieval hit becomes an approval on its own.

Second, the upgrade notes for this version describe manual migration scripts that do not block service startup, and they instruct that the historical chunk training task migration be triggered only after all older FastGPT App and Pro instances have fully stopped and old task leases have expired. The dataset rebuild status migration depends on the training task migration having completed. These are conditions stated in the release notes, not automatic sequences.

Hypothetical paper exercise. A Douyin storefront merchant hands over shelf operations authority on 1 March 2026. Two source sentences are supplied for comparison.

Store announcement text: "Effective 1 March 2026, the product qualification page is updated by Operator A and reviewed by Operator B. The experience score details are exported daily and then transferred."

Shipping violation appeal text: "Appeal number A-031, status pending review, submitter Operator A, submission date 28 February 2026."

A draft sentence under review reads: "Operator B has obtained final approval authority over the product qualification page, and appeal A-031 has passed and completed compensation."

This is a paper exercise only. No configuration was deployed, no appeal was decided, and no compensation occurred.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a pending appeal as passed. Input: appeal A-031 with status "pending review" from the exercise text. Comparison: the draft sentence asserting the appeal "has passed and completed compensation." Observable result: the status field in the source and the status asserted in the draft are mutually exclusive, so the draft is unsupported by the supplied text.

2. Treating a review duty as approval authority. Input: the announcement sentence assigning Operator B to review the qualification page. Comparison: the draft sentence asserting Operator B "has obtained final approval authority." Observable result: review and approval are different fields, and the announcement states only review, so the draft exceeds the source wording and remains unverified.

3. Treating a retrieval hit as an authorization basis. Input: a retrieved qualification page record. Comparison: the authorization record for the requested handover operation. Observable result: the retrieval result contains no authorization field, so the evidence gap is recorded and the update is not executed.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the draft sentence "Operator B has obtained final approval authority over the product qualification page," input the store announcement text, compare the "reviewed by" field against the "approval authority" field, and observe that the source does not state approval authority. The result is recorded as pending verification.

2. For the draft sentence "appeal A-031 has passed and completed compensation," input the appeal text, compare the "pending review" status against the asserted "passed" status, and observe that the two values are mutually exclusive and the source states pending review. The result is recorded as unsupported.

3. For the handover request "update the qualification page directly according to the retrieval result," input the retrieval hit record and the authorization record, compare the operation target against the authorization scope, and observe that the retrieval result carries no authorization field. The evidence gap is recorded and the update is not executed.

None of these checks was run against a live system. They describe what to compare and what would count as a satisfied condition, and they leave missing evidence as missing.
