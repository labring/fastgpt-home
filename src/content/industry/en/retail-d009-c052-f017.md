# Pinduoduo seller campaigns: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Pinduoduo low-price campaign operations revolve around campaign resource slots, campaign inventory, and refund-rate swings. Compared with Tmall flagship store operations, the work concentrates more on low-price resource slots and campaign inventory rhythm. The main data sources are the Pinduoduo merchant backend, campaign resource-slot data, ERP inventory, and logistics delivery-confirmation data. The daily documents are the campaign registration form, the product cost calculation sheet, after-sales refund orders, and platform site messages. Data updates daily and per campaign. That cadence means that during a handover the campaign registration form and the cost calculation sheet may change version with each campaign, so handover material must state the matching campaign session and date. Otherwise a previous session's inventory estimate or price conclusion can be carried into the next session.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

The upgrades step handles operations permissions and handover management: who may view or modify the campaign registration form, the cost calculation sheet, refund orders, and site messages, and which actions have already been authorized at handover versus which remain pending. On the FastGPT side, the relevant constraints come from environment variables and the upgrade notes. `AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT` and `AIPROXY_API_TOKEN` are required; missing either one causes startup validation to fail. Upgrading to V4.17.2 requires updating the fastgpt-app and fastgpt-pro image tags, and there are two manually triggered scripts. Before running them, confirm that all old-version instances have fully stopped and that old task leases have expired. Retrieval or form entry does not itself constitute authorization; whether a handover request takes effect depends on downstream permission configuration and human approval.

Hypothetical drill sample: a Pinduoduo campaign operations group prepares on 8 October 2026 to hand over a low-price campaign dated 10 October. The source material reads: "The campaign registration form (session 20261010) has been filled in by operator A and submitted to the platform; status is pending platform review. The product cost calculation sheet (session 20261010) was updated by buyer B on 8 October; the calculated gross margin is 8%. One refund application in the after-sales refund orders, dated 7 October, has status pending review." The draft sentence to be judged is: "Operator A has obtained all operations permissions for this campaign and can directly modify campaign inventory and execute the handover." That sentence conflates the registration form's submission status with a grant of permission. A registration form pending review does not mean operator A holds inventory-modification permission, and the handover request has not been approved.

## Three common errors

1. Treating the registration form's "pending platform review" as operator A having obtained inventory-modification permission. The input is the registration form status sentence in the sample; the comparison object is the permission grant record; the observable result is whether both point to the same subject and the same permission. When the status is pending review, the permission remains unsupported.
2. Using the 8% gross margin from the cost calculation sheet updated on 8 October directly for the 10 October campaign without checking the session and price validity period. The input is the calculation sheet date and session; the comparison object is campaign session 20261010; the observable result is whether the date and session match.
3. Treating the submission of the handover request as a completed handover. The input is the handover request in the draft sentence; the comparison object is the approval record; the observable result is whether clear evidence of completed approval exists. When only a submission action is present, the handover status is pending verification.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the sample sentence "The campaign registration form (session 20261010) has been filled in by operator A and submitted to the platform; status is pending platform review," input that source text and the draft sentence "Operator A has obtained all operations permissions for this campaign." Compare the registration form's status field with the subject, object, and status in the permission grant record. The draft sentence is supported only when the permission record clearly shows operator A holds modification permission for campaign inventory; otherwise record it as unsupported.
2. For the 8% gross margin in the cost calculation sheet, input the sheet date of 8 October and session 20261010, and compare the campaign execution date of 10 October with the sheet's session. The margin may be used for that campaign only when the session matches and the price validity period covers the execution date; otherwise record a gap.
3. For the handover request, input the draft sentence and the approval record, and compare the request content with the approval result. The handover is treated as complete only when the approval record shows the handover completed and permissions transferred; a pending-review status remains pending verification. Retrieval or form entry does not replace authorized execution, and relevance settings do not perform permission filtering.
