# Franchise filing management: citation tracing for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Franchise filing management works with legal disclosure and the commerce department system. It does not handle specific site engineering. The documents handled day to day include the franchise contract, the operations manual, filing materials and information disclosure documents. Citation tracing for these materials requires checking the original version, the subject and the valid scope. Materials update on an irregular and annual basis, so a citation must confirm that the cited material belongs to the currently effective version.

This category differs from headquarters site selection and store opening because the work concerns legal disclosure and the commerce department system. The main data sources are the commercial franchise information management system, enterprise licenses, store lists and contract ledgers. Typical roles are the franchisor headquarters, franchise legal staff and the commerce supervisor contact. Errors can lead to an order to file, franchise contract disputes and administrative penalties. The three most time-consuming steps are preparing filing materials, checking the two stores one year condition, and updating franchise information.

## Constraints at the citation tracing step

The knowledge base search node can associate one or more knowledge bases that use the same vector model. Citation content is output as an array, and its length can be zero. A zero-length output means the output path still runs even when no content is found. The retrieval output must be passed to the answering step, where each answer is checked against the cited original text. Semantic recall and relevance scores do not carry out permission or entity isolation. Retrieval is information lookup, and it does not constitute authorization, approval or business execution.

The following is a hypothetical exercise sample. It does not involve a real customer or a legitimate identity number.

Material text one: "Party A is the franchisor headquarters and holds a filing receipt from the commerce competent department. The filing subject is a certain catering management company limited. The filing scope is the conclusion of franchise contracts and information disclosure."

Material text two: "Party B is the franchise legal position. The authorization period runs from March 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025. The authorized matters are reviewing information disclosure documents and updating the operations manual."

Draft sentence to be judged: "The franchise legal position has the authority to approve the signing of new franchisee contracts and to complete the commerce department filing change."

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval output as an authorization credential. Error symptom: the draft sentence states that the legal position has the authority to approve contract signing, while material text two only records reviewing information disclosure documents and updating the operations manual, so the authorized matters field does not match. Check: input material text two and the draft sentence, compare the authorized matters field, and find that approval of signing is not supported.

2. Treating a pending review status as a completed filing change. Error symptom: material text one records the filing scope and does not record that the change has been completed. Check: input material text one and the draft sentence, compare the filing status field, and find that the change fact is not supported.

3. Associating knowledge bases that use different vector models. Error symptom: the vector model identifier is not checked during association. Check: input the vector model identifiers of the two knowledge bases, compare whether they are the same, and exclude the association if they differ.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input material text one, material text two and the draft sentence. Compare the authorized matters in the draft sentence with the authorized matters field in material text two. If the approval of signing described in the draft sentence does not appear in the authorized matters, judge it as not supported.

2. Input material text one and the draft sentence. Compare the completed filing change status in the draft sentence with the filing scope field in material text one. If material text one does not record that the change is completed, judge it as pending verification.

3. Input the vector model identifiers of the two knowledge bases to be associated. Compare whether the two identifiers are the same. If they differ, exclude the joint association, record the actual observation, and do not perform a cross-model retrieval comparison.

This page addresses only franchise filing management, operations handover, and citation tracing. Which materials, operations and requests are in what authorization state during an operations handover cannot be treated as authorized execution through retrieval or a form. A pending review does not prove the absence of operating authority. Signing, reaching cooperation and contract effectiveness are three distinct facts. The retrieval scope must fall within the target search range to which access is held, or within an explicit manual input; when evidence is missing, record the gap.
