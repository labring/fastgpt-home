# Producer export tax processes: operations handover with upgrades

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Producer export tax processes, this page examines FastGPT upgrades. Check entity, reporting period and restored attachments without inferring tax outcomes from drafts. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Suppose a tax-detail sheet is awaiting review, export-declaration text identifies a reporting period, and a sales-ledger extract lacks domestic-sales material. These invented passages can test whether upgrade recovery preserves entity, period and gaps. A readable draft proves no submitted return, approval or refund. Export material cannot replace domestic-sales records. This page provides no tax calculation, actual amount or filing date. It inspects document recovery and answer provenance without determining tax treatment.

## Three common errors

- If a restored summary turns pending review into approval, compare the original state with the content actually restored and inspect lost conditions.

- If export material from an earlier reporting period supports a current-period answer, compare entity, period and declared subject. Shared attachment names do not merge periods.

- If the model channel works while a ledger attachment fails, inspect original storage, retrieval and file access separately. Channel connectivity proves no recovered business record.

## How to verify the setup

Check prerequisites against the fixed v4.17.2 baseline and target-deployment documentation. Record current dependencies and settings, then verify what a backup actually restores. Inspect actual AIPROXY service addressing and matching credentials without publishing values. Repeat period, state and missing-input questions on the same draft after upgrading and test authorized attachment access separately. Promise no interruption-free transition; a live process is insufficient recovery evidence. Responsible personnel retain actual filing work.
