# Fruit-store procurement: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Fruit-store procurement, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Fruit-purchasing handover collects grade, supply replies and authority without granting purchasing access. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Suppose sample wording shows appearance grade only, the supplier has not answered arrival arrangements and a successor is assigned contact work only. Collect store, variety and handover matter, then choose supply evidence or purchasing-access review. Test missing variety and completed input, comparing downstream content with the source. A choice node serves no supplier directory, and submitting a form approves no purchase.

## Three common errors

A sample grade reported as whole-batch quality needs batch inspection checked. Contact assignment reported as purchasing authority needs real permission checked. Unanswered arrival arrangements stated as confirmed supply need a supplier reply checked. These distinguish sample from batch, work from authority and commitment certainty. No real quantity or quality outcome is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Keep the sample label beside variety identity and separate contact work from permission evidence, leaving arrival arrangements unresolved. Added batch material supports that batch alone without generalizing across supply. Orders, acceptance and stock writes require actual business interfaces, authority and receipts. The handover form transfers information without deciding receipt for a store or automatically granting a successor account. Separate appearance-sample purpose from current-batch evidence, retaining contact-only assignments and unresolved arrival arrangements without inferring batch quality from a shared variety name.
