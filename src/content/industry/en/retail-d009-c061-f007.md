# Fresh-food weighing and sorting: conversation logs for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Fresh-food weighing and sorting sits inside warehouse fulfillment rather than the pre-sale group-buy aggregation stage. The work centers on weight and specification, and the materials that record it are the sorting task sheet, the weighing label, the short-weight compensation form, and the quality inspection record. Data arrives from the WMS, electronic scales, the order system, and inspection photos. These materials are produced per order and per day, and they move between sorters and quality inspection supervisors during handover.

The category difference matters for this page because the handover question is about materials and authority, not about sales volume. A short shipment can trigger compensation, margin loss, and negative customer reviews, so handover needs a careful check of which material is in which state and which operator holds which permission. The operational permission and handover task is where those two questions meet.

For conversation logs and auditing, FastGPT links a session and a dialogue through identifiers such as appId, chatId, and dataId. During handover, the target conversation and the business record must be checked against the same object. Sessions from different entry points cannot be combined into one total. Where run details are required, detail is set according to the official contract and the fields actually returned in responseData are inspected. Business data that is not returned cannot be treated as fully retained. The existence of a log does not by itself complete a compliance audit; reconciliation, retention, and compliance requirements belong to the full business solution. Retrieval and forms do not perform authorization, and a relevance score is not a permission or entity filter.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

A hypothetical drill sample is given here for comparison purposes only. On 3 June 2025, a front warehouse sorting task sheet A-0603 states: "Sorter Li is responsible for weighing order 12; quality inspection supervisor Wang reviews it." On the same day, short-weight compensation form B-0603 states: "Requested object: order 12; status is pending review; applicant: Li." The proposed handover request reads: "Transfer the weighing-label review permission for A-0603 to Wang and confirm that B-0603 has passed." This request involves both a permission transfer and a status confirmation, and it remains to be judged.

The constraints at this step follow from the documents. The conversation log identifies a session through chatId and a dialogue through dataId, and the application through appId. Handover must first check whether the target conversation and the business record point to the same object. A pending review status on a compensation form is not an approval, and an offered option is not an executed action. A retrieval hit on A-0603 is not a permission transfer record. The authProxy setting, where the team owner enables it when creating or editing a key, proxies team member identity and still requires the target application and session permissions. Where authProxy is not enabled, the request is handled as an ordinary identity. The interactive node returns only type and params to the external caller; internal runtime fields such as entryNodeIds, memoryEdges, nodeOutputs, and nodeResponseId are not returned. These conditions shape what can be checked at handover, and what remains unknown.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieval result as the basis for authorization. The input is the proposed handover request in the drill sample. If a retrieval hit on A-0603 is used to conclude that the permission has been transferred, the object of comparison is the permission transfer record itself, and the observable result is that the retrieval hit and the authorization record do not match. This is an error.

2. Treating pending review as passed. The input is the status field of B-0603, which reads pending review. If the handover conclusion states that it has passed, the object of comparison is the compensation form status field, and the observable result is that the status text and the conclusion do not agree. This is an error.

3. Mixing sessions with different chatId values. The input is the chatId in the two requests. If the handover combines histories from different chatId values into one total, the object of comparison is the chatId field, and the observable result is that the field values do not match. This is an error.

## How to verify the setup

1. For the proposed handover request in the drill sample, the input is the appId and chatId in the request body. The object of comparison is the target application and session identifier. The condition is satisfied when both match the records associated with A-0603; otherwise the item is recorded as pending verification.

2. For the status of B-0603, the input is the compensation form text "status is pending review." The object of comparison is the status description in the handover conclusion. The condition is satisfied when the conclusion preserves the pending review wording; a passed wording does not satisfy the condition.

3. For the permission transfer, the input is the authProxy switch record and the key permission description. The object of comparison is the permission scope in the transfer request. The condition is satisfied when an explicit authorization record exists; a retrieval hit or a filled form alone does not satisfy the condition.

Where a document field is not provided, the value remains unknown. A pending request is not an approval, an offered option is not an execution, and an unsigned termination is not a completed termination. The relevance score attached to a retrieval result does not exclude unrelated businesses or unauthorized records, and retrieval does not perform authorization. Forms collect values; business verification, approval, database writes, and external synchronization require their stated downstream implementation. No scheduled trigger or hidden control is assumed. Where no documented error code or actual observation exists, the check describes what to compare and what remains unknown rather than asserting a deterministic failure cause or result.
