# E-commerce warehouse receiving: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in E-commerce warehouse receiving, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Receiving-handover logs distinguish dock receipt, putaway and operation authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Suppose a handover sheet records dock receipt only, a putaway task awaits execution and a successor is assigned document organization. Use chatId to locate the conversation and current receiving matter, inspecting whether actual input and history preserve task stage. Check detail and responseData against the official contract when needed. Present fields guarantee no complete retention of business attachments, and existing logs establish no successor account permission.

## Three common errors

Dock receipt reported as putaway needs location-execution evidence checked. Document work reported as stock-adjustment access needs real authority checked. An older receiving batch applied here needs batch and item identity checked. These concern stage, operation scope and historical ownership. No real inventory outcome is supplied.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect identity and history separately for debug and production. Place receiving wording beside the location task for comparison. Added putaway evidence updates execution stage alone while availability remains separate; missing detail proves no absent physical arrival. Putaway, account grants and stock writes depend on business systems and receipts. The log traces which material an answer used without confirming those operations. Link dock receipt to current goods, locating putaway tasks separately from execution receipts and separating document duties from access so review preserves each distinct state.
