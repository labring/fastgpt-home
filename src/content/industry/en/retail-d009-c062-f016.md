# E-commerce warehouse receiving: operations handover with interactive forms

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in E-commerce warehouse receiving, this page examines FastGPT interactive forms. Inbound handover forms separate temporary placement, formal shelving and pending discrepancies. A chosen route does not perform warehouse actions. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

Proposed handover says material occupies a temporary area, a formal bin remains unspecified and a discrepancy was passed to the next shift without an outcome. Collect material, warehouse and states. A person can obtain location evidence or route discrepancy review. Temporary placement is not formal shelving, and passing work does not close it. Preserve warehouse identity and gaps downstream; choices neither authenticate warehouse identities nor write inventory.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Inbound handover form | Proposed location state | Actual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporary area | Placement record | Not a formal bin |
| Missing bin | Still unresolved | Item shelving evidence |
| Warehouse subject | Submission matches recipient | Exclude another warehouse |
| Discrepancy handoff | Outcome absent | Not closure |
| Business writes | Receiving and adjustment separate | Choice is not execution |

## Three common errors

Temporary placement promoted to shelving needs actual item-location evidence. Another warehouse’s wording applied here requires submitted-subject and received-text comparison. Shift handover promoted to discrepancy closure needs the real outcome. These inspect location meaning, subject and business stage independently of connected branches.

## How to verify the setup

Compare submitted elements with actual received input. Added warehouse-location evidence changes its material only while discrepancies remain unresolved. Choosing review does not perform stock adjustment. Receiving, shelving and discrepancy writes need separate authority and receipts. Waiting and continuation establish interaction rather than reconciled stock or universal goods availability from existing records.
