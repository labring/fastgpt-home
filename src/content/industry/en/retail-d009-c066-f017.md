# Short-video affiliate sales: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Short-video affiliate distribution relies on long-term content exposure and product-link conversion, and it does not depend on live explanation. Selected Alliance, short-video play data, product transaction data and platform rules form the main data sources. Content operators, creator business development staff and product distribution specialists handle short-video scripts, product lists for attached carts, commission plans and violation feedback on daily and weekly frequencies. At the deployment and upgrade step, the key effect is that these four document types carry different authorization states. Treating the existence of a document as the existence of a permission causes a handover to execute an unauthorized action as though it were authorized. The supplied material gives daily and weekly update frequencies; this page does not derive batch synchronization, real-time or transaction-triggered mechanisms from them.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: a content operator submits a handover request before an upgrade window, and the source material consists of three sentences. Sentence one: "Product list v3 was submitted by the product distribution specialist on 2 March 2026, and its status is pending review." Sentence two: "Commission plan A takes effect on 5 March 2026, and its applicable product scope is the entries in list v3 that have passed review." Sentence three: "Violation feedback form R-07 records a title violation, its handling status is appeal submitted, and the appeal result has not been backfilled." The draft sentence to be assessed is: "List v3 has completed review, commission plan A has taken effect for all cart videos, and the appeal has passed." Assessment conclusion: the first sentence supports only "submitted, pending review" and does not support "completed review"; the second sentence supports only the effective date and the applicable scope and does not support "taken effect for all cart videos"; the third sentence supports only "appeal submitted, result not backfilled" and does not support "appeal has passed." All three are unsupported rather than contradictory with the source, and they remain evidence gaps.

## Three common errors

1. Reading "submitted, pending review" as "completed review." The input is the first sample sentence, the compared field is the review status, and the observed result is that "pending review" and "completed review" do not match, so the claim is unsupported.
2. Reading "effective date and applicable scope" as "taken effect for all cart videos." The input is the second sample sentence, the compared field is the applicable scope, and the observed result is that "entries in list v3 that have passed review" and "all cart videos" do not match, so the claim is unsupported.
3. Reading "appeal submitted, result not backfilled" as "appeal has passed." The input is the third sample sentence, the compared field is the appeal result, and the observed result is that "not backfilled" and "has passed" do not match, so the claim is unsupported.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the first sample sentence and the first part of the draft sentence, compare the review status field, and observe "pending review." The draft wording "completed review" is unsupported at that point; if the wording "completed review" appears, a corresponding review record must be provided.
2. Input the second sample sentence and the middle part of the draft sentence, compare the applicable scope field, and observe "entries in list v3 that have passed review." The draft wording "all cart videos" is unsupported at that point; if the wording "all" appears, an authorization record covering every video must be provided.
3. Input the third sample sentence and the final part of the draft sentence, compare the appeal result field, and observe "not backfilled." The draft wording "appeal has passed" is unsupported at that point; if the wording "has passed" appears, an appeal result backfill record must be provided. All three steps are paper checks. No deployment, approval, refund or other business action is claimed to have occurred, and no test is claimed to have been run.
