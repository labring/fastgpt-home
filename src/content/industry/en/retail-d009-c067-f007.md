# Group-buy leader operations: conversation logs for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Group-buy leader operations manages community nodes and commissions, and does not perform cold-chain quality inspection. Working documents include leader agreements, commission tables, pickup lists and community announcements. Data comes from the group-buy backend, leader community groups, order details and after-sales records. Updates occur daily or per group. Leader attrition, wrong or missing deliveries, and community complaints are the main consequences.

Conversation logs and audit serve handover traceability in this category. When a leader is replaced, permissions are transferred, or commissions are reconciled, the operation needs to confirm which leader, which community node and which agreement scope a given conversation belongs to. The log itself records conversation content and runtime information, and does not complete an authorization judgment on its own.

The conversation interface identifies a session under one application by chatId, and identifies a conversation under a session by dataId. When retrieving the session list, source=api returns only sessions created through the API and does not return page sessions. Clearing application sessions clears only sessions created by API Key and does not affect sessions from online use or share links. These boundaries show that the scope of a log search result is limited by entry source, and records from one source cannot be treated as all records.

When conversation run details are needed, set detail=true according to the official contract. In non-stream mode the result is stored in responseData; in stream mode it is distinguished by event. Fields differ between modules in responseData, and business data that is not returned cannot be presumed to be fully retained. The retrieval similarity score indicates only the degree of recall relevance and does not perform permission or entity isolation. Retrieval is not authorization.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

The following hypothetical drill sample is used for field and scope checking. It is not drawn from any real customer, company or legitimate identity number.

Material text for checking: Leader agreement number T-2024-031, community node Qinghe Community, leader Li, agreement validity from 1 March 2024 to 31 August 2024. Backend log shows a session with chatId C-7781 and a conversation with dataId D-9902, occurring on 15 September 2024, with content concerning a commission settlement inquiry.

Draft sentence to be judged: "This conversation falls within the validity period of agreement T-2024-031, and Li is authorized to handle it." Comparing the agreement validity period with the conversation date, 15 September 2024 is later than 31 August 2024, so the draft sentence is not supported.

The stage here is operations handover: the materials, operations and requests involved in a handover sit at a particular authorization state, and neither retrieval nor a form counts as an authorization execution. A pending request is not approval; an offered option is not execution; an unsigned termination is not a completed termination.

## Three common errors

1. Treating retrieval similarity as a permission filter. The input is a score value, the comparison object is the leader agreement and authorization scope, and the observable result is that a high score does not mean the leader is authorized to handle that conversation.
2. Treating API-source sessions as all sessions. The input is the list returned with source=api, the comparison object is online use and share-link sources, and the observable result is that the list does not include records from other sources.
3. Treating the existence of a log as a completed audit. The input is the record corresponding to dataId, the comparison object is reconciliation, retention and compliance requirements, and the observable result is that the record's existence does not mean the business audit is complete.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input chatId C-7781 and dataId D-9902 from the hypothetical sample, compare the session and conversation identifiers in the returned record, and observe whether the result matches the backend log. If the return is an empty array [], check whether the downstream design handles the empty result as such, and do not claim that a fictional rule necessarily returns empty.
2. Input a request with detail=true, compare whether the corresponding module field appears in responseData, and observe whether the required run information is included. If the field does not appear, record the gap and do not presume the data has been retained.
3. Input the draft sentence and the agreement validity period, compare the conversation date 15 September 2024 with the agreement end date 31 August 2024, and observe whether the result supports the judgment. When the date falls outside the range, the judgment is not supported, and the evidence gap is kept.

These checks test the named primary task of operations handover logging: confirming which leader, node and agreement scope a conversation belongs to, and whether the authorization claim is supported by the supplied material. They do not test identity certification, report analysis or any other task. Where a field is not provided in the source document, the value remains unknown. Where a request is pending, it is not an approval. Where an option is offered, it is not an execution. Where a termination is unsigned, it is not a completed termination.
