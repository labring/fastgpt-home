# Dealer rebate calculation: workflow routing for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Rebate calculation happens after a sale is complete. The work centers on policy definitions and financial treatment rather than on inventory movement. The main data sources are ERP sales records, dealer remittances, contract ledgers and the finance system. The daily documents are rebate policies, volume achievement tables, reconciliation statements and red-letter invoices. Monthly and quarterly update rhythms mean handover work regularly crosses periods, and the same rebate may sit at different stages in the volume achievement table, the reconciliation statement and the finance system.

Workflow routing in this category strings material collection, permission confirmation and handover requests into a traceable interaction. Each node needs a clear source for its input and a clear destination for its output. The form input node and the user selection node are user interaction nodes. When either is triggered, the conversation enters an interaction state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues after the user finishes interacting. The form input node collects the required information the user submits and passes it to later nodes. The user selection node evaluates the user's choice and executes the corresponding branch. These behaviors collect and pass information. They do not constitute authorization approval or business execution.

## Constraints at the workflow routing step

A handover request in rebate calculation, such as passing a dealer's rebate reconciliation materials to the next stage, is information collected through a form. Whether that information is later written into the finance system or synchronized to an external state requires a separate business interface or a downstream process. Retrieval results carry relevance scores and do not act as a permission filter, an entity filter or a category filter. Libraries that share the same vector model are a precondition for joint association; libraries using different vector models cannot be associated together.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: the source material reads "The March 2025 volume achievement table (number XS-2025-03) shows dealer A in the East China region reached an achievement rate of 92%; the reconciliation statement for the same period (number DZ-2025-03) records a rebate amount payable of 46,000 yuan with status pending review; the rebate policy (number ZC-2025-01) provides that an achievement rate above 90% earns a 2% rebate." A draft sentence to be assessed reads "Dealer A's March 2025 rebate has passed review and can proceed to invoice offset." The draft sentence conflicts with the source material's pending review status.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a form submission as authorized execution. In the sample, the draft sentence says the rebate has passed review while the source material records pending review. Submitting a form completes information collection and does not constitute a passed review.
2. Treating a user selection branch as a business approval. Executing the yes branch on the user selection node indicates workflow direction only. It does not indicate that a rebate application has been approved.
3. Treating retrieval relevance as a permission filter. Retrieval results are returned by relevance and do not distinguish dealer ownership or operating permissions. Permission judgment requires a separate verification step.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the sample source material and the draft sentence. Compare the draft sentence's "passed review" with the reconciliation statement's "pending review." When the two statuses do not match, judge that the draft sentence is not supported by the material and record the evidence gap.
2. Enter the form submission values. Compare the submitted fields against the required field list. When the fields are complete and correspond to the material numbers, confirm that information collection is complete; when fields are missing, record the gap.
3. Enter an empty result `[]`. Check the receiving design for how that empty result passes into downstream nodes, and confirm that downstream nodes have a defined handling path for empty input. Do not claim that a fictional rule retrieval necessarily returns an empty result.
