# Cross-border settlements: operations handover with access controls

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT access controls. Inspect finance-handover duties, actual accounts and settlement-resource grants without inferring permission from written responsibilities. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the access controls step

A new handover note assigns settlement checking to finance, order background to operations and rate explanations to accounting. Those responsibility labels are not configured system roles. Identify real accounts and resources before checking reading and operations on settlement, rate and reconciliation sources independently. A duty without an account mapping cannot verify the receiving person’s actions. Inspect grant evidence rather than having a model turn a job description into a permission result.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Permission or implementation check | Finance-handover object | Actual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duty label | Finance, operations and accounting duties | Not automatic system roles |
| Account mapping | Actual identities and required resources | Inspect reading and operations separately |
| Same source | Reading and maintenance may differ | Test per account against the design |
| Service authentication | Separate administrator token from business grants | No inferred ability to modify financial content |
| Transfer scope | New grants, revocation and external sources | Record allowed, denied and unresolved results |

### Documented conditions

| Documented item / implementation check | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Team roles` | Inspect roles for the actual member identity. |
| `Resource permissions` | Inspect reading, editing and management by resource. |
| `Document classification` | Classification and tags do not replace authorization. |
| `External-system permissions` | Verify external-interface authentication separately. |
| `Per-account validation` | Test the same source with authorized and unauthorized accounts. |

## Three common errors

- If finance, operations and rate-maintenance duties become configured roles, inspect actual accounts and resource operations. Duty labels establish no concrete incoming capability.

- If a denied identity reads rates or reconciliation, retain content and configuration. A valid service administrator Token grants no business-user maintenance rights.

- If internal contract or settlement reads become external bank access, inspect payment authentication and actual returns. Test new access and former revocation separately; ended conversation establishes no complete transfer.

## How to verify the setup

Handover also needs removal of former access and a defined route for external bank or payment records. Test new and revoked access separately, recording readable originals, denied operations and absent sources. Reading a contract or settlement passage does not grant the receiving person external statements. Accept by account, resource and operation result, retaining unresolved scope. Neither the end of a handover conversation nor a model’s restatement of duties establishes that every grant was implemented.
