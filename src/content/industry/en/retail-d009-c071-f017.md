# Cross-border settlements: operations handover with upgrades

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Cross-border settlements, this page examines FastGPT upgrades. Inspect correspondence among handover-source versions, recovery reads and post-upgrade citations. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

A new proposed handover checklist lists a settlement statement, exchange-rate sheet and reconciliation sheet, marking one rate-sheet version as unconfirmed. Before upgrading, identify the originals and index scope actually held, then record current and target deployment versions with their instructions. A fixed documentation baseline does not establish the runtime version. Keep the unconfirmed source unresolved so that an answer change after upgrading is not silently caused by substitution of another rate sheet.

## Configuration and implementation checks

| Upgrade or implementation check | Handover checklist condition | Acceptance object |

| Connection and permission | Inspect root address, authentication and account access separately | Retain call and resource-read results independently |

## Three common errors

- If a documentation baseline becomes the running environment or an unidentified rate table becomes current, inspect actual deployment, source identity and target instructions before attributing answer changes to upgrade.

- If normal AI Proxy authentication becomes transferred finance rights, test business accounts against settlement and rate resources independently. Service connection grants no personnel access.

- If backup exists but reconciliation is unreadable or old rates remain cited, retain recovered originals, indexes and answer input. Recovery cannot confirm applicability of an unverified rate source.

## How to verify the setup

A checklist entry for backup does not establish recoverability of all three source types. Read a recovered sample and inspect whether originals, indexes and application answers still correspond to the same version. The unconfirmed exchange-rate sheet needs evidence from its owner; a recovery result does not establish its applicability. Record recovered content, unresolved version evidence and account-access results separately. Keep open issues visible after upgrade steps finish, without promising uninterrupted service.
