# Overseas stock replenishment: operations handover with conversation logs

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Overseas stock replenishment, this page examines FastGPT conversation logs. Overseas-warehouse handover review separates dispatch, carrier and destination roles from account authority. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the conversation logs step

Proposed material records handoff from the dispatch warehouse to a carrier, lacks an overseas-warehouse receipt and passes work to a successor. Locate the current conversation and replenishment subject, then inspect roles in input and history. Carrier handoff establishes no destination receipt. Transferring work grants no system access. Partial node text leaves business evidence unobserved; its absence from detail does not prove no receipt or replenishment event.

## Three common errors

Dispatch promoted to destination receipt needs original roles and receiving evidence checked. A successor identity promoted to account access needs actual authority. Missing detail promoted to no business event needs returned coverage checked. These concern acting party, permission and inference.

## How to verify the setup

Compare identity, input, history and return separately for debug and production calls. Obtain required detail under the official contract, then trace business sources item by item. A receipt or authorization updates its own matter. Warehousing, stock writes and role access need independent system processes; a log alone does not close handover.
