# Creator commission settlement: upgrades for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Commission settlement in this category happens after a sale closes. The objects being reconciled are valid promotion orders and post-sale commission deductions, and the working materials come from the alliance backend, order after-sales data, the finance system, and creator contracts. The document set handled day to day consists of commission setting records, settlement statements, refund deduction details, and invoices, with a weekly and monthly update rhythm.

The constraint that matters for operations handover is that these materials record amounts and states. They do not record who is allowed to act. A settlement statement shows what was earned and what was deducted; it does not show which role may approve a payout or issue an invoice. Retrieving a statement, or completing a form, is not the same as holding settlement authority or executing a deduction. Authority ownership and handover authorization have to be checked separately against the handover record itself.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

An upgrade begins by confirming the current version, the target version, and the matching upgrade notes. Current behavior is not inferred from an older configuration name. This release carries manually triggered upgrade scripts visible under the admin version upgrade area. The historical chunk training task migration requires that all old-version App and Pro instances in the cluster have fully stopped and that old task leases have expired before it is run. The dataset rebuild status migration depends on the earlier migration having completed. Both are manual triggers and do not block service start.

After the upgrade, the same sample is used to recheck import, retrieval, and application invocation. Completing the steps is not a zero-downtime commitment. Authorization states inside handover materials during the upgrade window are recorded separately; system availability does not carry them forward automatically.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample: an internal handover record states that "the August 2026 settlement statement for Creator A has been checked by the finance settlement role; two refunds in the refund deduction details are marked pending confirmation; commission setting records keep the original ratio; the invoice has not been issued." A handover request in the same batch states: "Please take over and continue processing the August settlement from the above materials and issue the invoice." The subject is Creator A, the objects are the August statement and refund details, the states are pending confirmation and not issued, and the effective scope is August. The item to be determined is whether the receiving person holds invoice issuance authority. The materials do not state an authorization clause, so this remains to be verified.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a retrieved August settlement statement as proof that the receiving person holds invoice issuance authority. The input is the retrieval result, the comparison is the authorization field in the material, the observable result is that the field is empty, and the determination is pending verification.
2. Treating "please continue processing" in the handover request as completed authorization. The input is the request text, the comparison is whether the request names an authorizing subject and scope, the observable result is that neither is stated, and the determination is pending verification.
3. Treating the appearance of an upgrade script on the admin version upgrade page as execution. The input is the page display, the comparison is the script execution record, the observable result is that no execution record exists, and the determination is not executed.

## How to verify the setup

1. Input the hypothetical sample text together with the proposed sentence "Please take over and continue processing the August settlement from the above materials and issue the invoice." Compare the authorization fields in the material against the claim in the proposed sentence. The observable result is that the authorization fields are not stated, so the proposed sentence is not supported.
2. Input the manual trigger conditions from the upgrade notes together with the hypothetical start that old instances are stopped. Compare whether the conditions are met. The observable result is that conditions are met but no execution record exists, so the determination is pending execution.
3. Input the hypothetical starting value of AIPROXY_API_ENDPOINT together with the requirement in the upgrade notes. Compare whether it is non-empty and has no `/v1` appended. The observable result is that it conforms, so this item passes.

Each check compares one field against one claim or condition. A retrieval result, a form entry, or a page display does not carry authority, and no check above asserts that an approval, refund, or other business action has occurred.
