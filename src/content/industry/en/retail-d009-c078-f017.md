# Chain-store site selection: operations handover with upgrades

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Chain-store site selection, this page examines FastGPT upgrades. Handover version, restored sources and account scope without changing leasing intent or unmeasured-space limits. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

A hypothetical site note records viewing the front area while scheduling measurement of the back area separately. A landlord email expresses willingness to continue discussions, without a signed lease or opening record. Before an upgrade, retain the cited note, address and version. Afterward, repeat the same questions to inspect restoration and use of the sources. An accessible page returning only frontage wording still supplies no back-area dimensions. Reading an email also establishes no lease. Floor-plan or image paths need their own input tests; this text sample does not verify complete plan reading. No real lease, fee or operating approval is provided.

## Three common errors

- If the upgraded answer expands frontage observation into measurement of the whole site, compare the original note, restored text and actual request for the separately scheduled back area.

- If a restored older candidate supplies the current address, inspect address, spatial coverage and version. A file name or update timestamp alone cannot establish the match.

- If an administrator connection becomes access for every handover member, inspect team identity, resource grants and actual account returns. Technical dependencies and business access need separate tests.

## How to verify the setup

Inspect current and target versions with their official instructions, then retain backup and dependency evidence applicable to this deployment. After restoration, ask what was viewed, which area remains unmeasured and what the landlord said. Compare actual citations with the originals. A service change needs the AI Proxy root and matching administrator tokens checked, without treating connection configuration as lease-document authorization. Backup creation, actual restored material and citation by the application are distinct evidence. Record the tests rather than promising unaffected site work from service startup.
