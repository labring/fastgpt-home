# Central chain procurement: operations handover with upgrades

## What the documents look like

For operations handover in Central chain procurement, this page examines FastGPT upgrades. Preserve unit relationships, unpacking gaps and actual receipt across a centralized-procurement upgrade. The examples are hypothetical.

## Constraints at the upgrades step

A hypothetical procurement request uses individual units for store demand, cartons for warehouse preparation and leaves unpacking rules to be checked. A distribution note supplies no receipt for this round’s stores. An upgrade test should retain both originals and prior citations so the rebuilt service still distinguishes demand units from preparation units. A carton catalogue establishes no permission or ability to split packs, and restoring a distribution plan proves no store receipt. Source storage, vector retrieval and application calls are separate layers; a healthy connection in one layer cannot establish recovery of the others. No real inventory or executable supply arrangement is provided.

## Three common errors

- If the restored answer permits individual-unit distribution after losing the unpacking condition, inspect the original file, restored text and citation. Locate whether restoration or retrieval dropped the restriction.

- If an older delivery sheet becomes receipt by every current store, inspect store, batch and actual receiving event. A plan or matching document name supplies no receipt.

- If vector-service availability becomes complete business-data restoration, inspect original storage, retrieval returns and application samples separately. Service liveness cannot establish their contents.

## How to verify the setup

Review current and target-version instructions and dependencies before retaining a backup and testing restored content. Repeat the same questions about demand units, missing rules and store-specific receipt evidence, comparing original identities and actual citations. For AI Proxy changes, inspect the service root and administrator authentication at both ends; verify application credentials separately. Recovery evidence covers only the samples and environment actually tested. It promises no uninterrupted service and cannot turn completed upgrade steps into completed distribution.
