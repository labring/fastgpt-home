# Chain-store closure accounts: interactive forms for operations handover

## What the documents look like

Chain-store closure settlement handles exit and asset recovery, running in the opposite direction from head-office site selection and store opening. The supplied brief lists the main data sources as POS inventory, the fixed-asset system, lease contracts and financial ledgers. The documents handled day to day are closure approvals, inventory clearance sheets, lease termination agreements and asset count sheets. The update frequency is irregular, per store. This combination means handover material typically spans several systems and several signing stages, and the same store can carry different statuses in different documents. For operations permission and handover management at the interactive-forms step, material source, current status and authorization scope need to be recorded separately so later nodes can read them.

The brief rates its own confidence as broadly indicative, so these characteristics are treated as context rather than fixed rules. Where a document field is not supplied, its value stays unknown.

## Constraints at the interactive forms step

The Form Input node is a user-interaction node. When it triggers, the conversation enters an interactive state, the workflow state is recorded, and execution continues downward after the user completes the interaction. After the user fills the required information and submits, the node collects what was entered and passes it to later nodes. The User Selection node is also a user-interaction node with the same interactive-state behaviour; when the user makes a choice, the node evaluates that choice and executes the "yes" branch. Its basic use is to pose a question requiring a decision and design different workflow paths from the user's feedback.

Neither node carries a business database, an approval capability or a permission decision. Field validation, business approval, write-back and batch operations are implemented in external systems or later nodes as needed. Retrieval and form collection do not constitute authorization execution. A similarity or relevance score does not act as a permission filter or an entity-isolation filter. Retrieval is not authorization.

Hypothetical paper exercise sample, not pointing to any real party. Material text one: "Store A closure approval form, number HYP-001, application date 1 March 2024, status is awaiting regional operations review." Material text two: "Store A lease termination agreement, number HYP-002, signing parties are lessor and lessee, signing status: lessee signed, lessor not signed, effectiveness conditioned on signature by both parties." Draft sentence to be assessed: "Store A has completed closure approval and terminated the lease, so asset clearance handover can proceed." This sentence writes a pending review as completed and writes a one-sided signature as a completed termination. It is unsupported.

## Three common errors

1. Treating a pending-review status as approved. In the sample, HYP-001 reads "awaiting regional operations review" while the draft sentence says "has completed closure approval". Input is the two material texts plus the draft sentence; the comparison is the approval-status field; the observable result is that the status values do not match, so the draft sentence is unsupported.

2. Treating a one-sided signature as a terminated lease. In the sample, HYP-002 has no lessor signature and effectiveness is conditioned on signature by both parties. The comparison is the signing-status field; the observable result is that the signing parties are incomplete, so termination is not completed.

3. Treating a form submission or a retrieval hit as authorized execution. The nodes collect information and pass it to later nodes and do not perform approval. The comparison is between "can proceed" in the draft sentence and the node's documented responsibility; the observable result is that the authorization basis is absent.

## How to verify the setup

1. Enter the sample material texts and the draft sentence. Compare the value of the approval-status field. When "awaiting review" and "completed" appear as different values, the draft sentence is judged unsupported, and the evidence gap is recorded.

2. Enter the HYP-002 signing status. Compare whether the signing parties are complete. When the lessor has not signed, termination is judged not completed and remains pending verification.

3. Enter an empty-result continuation scenario using the paper input [] to check the downstream design. Observe whether the downstream handles it as missing data. Do not assume that a rule not described in the documents returns an empty result.
