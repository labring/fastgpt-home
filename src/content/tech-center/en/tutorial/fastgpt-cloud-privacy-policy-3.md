# FastGPT Cloud Privacy Compliance Technical Details

### Collected Data Categories
Two distinct data categories are collected during FastGPT Cloud Service usage:
1.  User-provided personal information: Collected during registration or active service use, including full name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
2.  Operational metadata: Generated during service sessions, including operation logs, IP addresses, and device models.
3.  Session tracking data: Collected via cookies or similar technologies to optimize service experience during user visits.

### Permitted Third-Party Disclosure Rules
The following table outlines all authorized scenarios for disclosing user personal information to third parties:
| Disclosure Scenario | Mandatory Requirements |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Explicit user consent | Requires prior affirmative consent from the user |
| Legal regulatory requirement | Mandated by applicable local, national, or international law |
| Legitimate interest protection | Necessary to safeguard FastGPT Cloud or other user legitimate interests |
| Affiliate/partner sharing | Must include appropriate confidentiality measures to ensure information security |

### Data Protection & Service Protocols
Standardized security controls are implemented to protect collected personal information, including encryption and access restrictions. Regular security assessments are conducted for all collected, stored, and processed personal data. In the event of a data breach or security incident, an emergency response plan is activated immediately, with prompt notification per applicable legal requirements.

No user data is utilized for additional backup storage or model training. User-initiated data deletions within the FastGPT Cloud Service are physical deletions that cannot be recovered; any non-physical deletion operations are clearly disclosed within the service interface.

User data rights include access to personal information, correction of inaccurate data, permanent deletion of data, refusal of personal information collection (with potential limitation of core service feature access), and request to suspend personal information processing (with potential inability to continue service use).

The privacy policy may be updated periodically, with changes posted on the FastGPT Cloud Service page. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of updated policy terms. Cross-border data transfers may occur as needed to deliver the service, with servers located across multiple countries or regions, and adequate protection measures implemented for such transfers. Protection of minor personal information requires guardian supervision for minor users, with appropriate handling of personal information by guardians.
