# FastGPT Cloud Service Usage Terms Reference

**Core Service Specifications**
This section outlines standard FastGPT cloud service provisions. The service includes internet-based information technology services: storage, computing, and network transmission. The platform may send periodic updates via in-app messages, email, or SMS. Official technical support and customer service are provided to assist with service utilization. A guaranteed monthly service availability of no less than 99% is provided for all subscribed plans.

**Account Administration Requirements**
To access the FastGPT cloud service, users must first complete an account registration. All registration information must be true, accurate, and complete, and users are required to keep this information updated at all times. Users are solely responsible for safeguarding their account credentials and all activity conducted under their registered account. If unauthorized account use is detected, users must immediately change their account password or contact support. The company reserves the right to conduct routine account reviews; access to the service may be suspended or terminated if abnormal or illegal activity is identified.

**Compliance & Prohibited Usage**
Users must comply with all applicable laws and this agreement, and bear full responsibility for all published content and usage-related consequences. Malicious account registration via methods including profiteering, speculation, or arbitrage is prohibited. Distribution of illegal, harmful, or malicious software or information is also restricted. The following categories of prohibited content are strictly enforced:
| Prohibited Content Category | Defined Prohibited Actions |
|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Exploitation and Abuse | Child sexual exploitation/abuse, child grooming |
| Non-Consensual Intimate Content | Non-consensual intimate activity description/promotion |
| Commercial Sexual Services | Commercial sexual activity/service promotion |
| Human Trafficking | Trafficking recruitment, transport, forced exploitation |
| Self-Harm & Suicide | Self-harm/suicide description, promotion, or glorification |
| Violent Content | Graphic violence, terrorist content, violent advocacy |
| Hate Speech & Discrimination | Targeted attacks based on identity, abusive imagery |
| Deceptive Public Harm Content | False health/safety/election integrity content |
| Malicious Software Activities | Malicious executable distribution, DDoS attacks |
Any account found generating or distributing prohibited content will be immediately banned, with no refund of remaining account balance.
