# Access FastGPT Community Support Channels

## Community Support Overview
FastGPT is an open source project driven by user contributors and community participants. All technical questions, feedback, and collaboration opportunities are supported through official community channels designed to connect users with fellow contributors and development resources. The project’s community framework ensures that users can access support and share insights without relying on centralized, closed channels.

## Official Lark Community Group
The real-time community discussion channel for FastGPT is the Lark group. To join this group, scan the provided QR code:

## Structured Feedback Submission
For formal bug reports, issue tracking, or feature requests, use the official FastGPT GitHub Issues portal. This channel provides a documented framework for all submissions, ensuring they are reviewed by the relevant contributing developers. To submit feedback via this portal:
1.  Navigate to the official submission page using the following URL: `https://github.com/labring/fastgpt/issues/new/choose`
2.  Complete the required on-page form with clear, specific details related to your submission
3.  Submit the form to send your feedback to the FastGPT community and contributing developers.
